# PIJ editorial rules (Pete's)

These are the standing rules for every desk. They come from Pete's original
Personal Intelligence Journal brief and his decisions on 15 Nov 2026.

## The bar
- Curated, substantive, evidence-led. Explain **what happened, why it matters, what changed, what happens next**.
- No filler, sensationalism or recycled headlines. A desk reports **only** when something clears the bar.
- Every desk reports one of three states: **story**, **quiet** (checked, nothing worth reporting) or **failed** (could not check). Silence must always be trustworthy.
- Never invent a figure, date, quote or URL. If it is not in a source you saw, it does not go in.

## Sources and balance
- Core sources are in `sources.json` (approved by Pete). Tier 1 = facts (wires, official data, primary sources). Tier 2 = context. Tier 3 = perspective, always paired left and right, always labelled as perspective.
- A political claim needs a Tier 1 source or two independent outlets from different sides; otherwise mark it `reported`.
- Sources marked `Unsure` (and any aggregator) are tip-offs only. A story whose only sources are tip-offs does **not** run: mention it in the desk note as "unconfirmed: …" until an official or core source confirms it. This matters most on the prop desk, where rule changes must come from the firm itself (site, help centre or official announcement). Posts in the inbox marked `Discord · <firm>` come from the firm's own announcements channel, which Pete follows; treat them as official (Tier 1) and lead with them, since firms post there before their help pages update. Exception: `Discord · The United Muppetiers #latest-gossip` is a fan gossip feed: tier 3, `rumour` at best, never a sole source. Promotions channels (discount codes) only clear the bar if a deal is unusually large for a watchlist firm. Lucid and TPT have no followable Discord channel, so check their sites and help pages directly.
- Headlines found outside the core list count as `reported` until a core source confirms them.
- Strictly nonpartisan on politics. Describe and attribute; no verdicts on parties or politicians. Pete leans right and wants balance, not an echo.

## Desk specifics
1. **Manchester United & Football**: manager, transfers, injuries, tactics, results, wider football. Transfer gossip is `rumour` unless a reliable outlet confirms.
2. **Futures & Prop Firms**: watchlist ONLY: Lucid Trading, Topstep, Tradeify, Take Profit Trader, Apex Trader Funding. Other firms only for industry-wide changes (CME, regulators, data fees). Rule changes: effective dates, who is grandfathered, practical impact.
3. **Trading Technology**: TradingView, Pine Script, Quantower, non-repainting indicators, AI-assisted trading research and automation.
4. **Artificial Intelligence**: OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, agents, coding, autonomous workflows, image/video generation, AI gaming. Benchmarks from a company are company claims.
5. **Business & E-commerce**: DTC, Meta ads, creative strategy, CRO, acquisition, AI-powered small businesses. Practical implications.
6. **Christianity & Theology**: biblical scholarship, apologetics, early church history, charismatic Christianity, global Christianity, biblical archaeology. Separate evidence from interpretation.
7. **World Affairs & Politics**: US, UK and Australia first: governments, elections, policy, economics, immigration, public opinion, international relations. Polls need pollster, dates, sample and margin of error.
8. **Health & Wellness**: nutrition, fitness, hormones, testosterone, sleep, longevity, holistic health, emerging research. Women's health is welcome. **Evidence grade every item**: A (large RCTs / meta-analyses), B (single RCT or strong cohort), C (small, observational, animal, preprint) with study type, population and key limitation.
9. **Gaming & PlayStation**: GTA VI, PS5/PS5 Pro stock and deals, performance, PSSR, PS6, next-gen Xbox, Warzone, EA FC. Rumours labelled.
10. **Expand My World**: one worthwhile discovery outside the usual interests (paper, technology, thinker, interview, idea). Skip rather than pad.

## Shape of a story
- Headline: plain, specific, ≤ 100 characters.
- Why: 1–2 sentences, the "so what" for Pete.
- Points: 3–4 short factual bullets.
- Next: the next catalyst with a date.
- Confidence: confirmed | official data | reported | claim | rumour.
- Every story carries real source links.

## Markets box ("Before the open", weekdays)
- Today's scheduled US releases and Fed events in **Pacific Time**, from official calendars (BLS, Federal Reserve) or company investor-relations pages.
- Anything overnight that could move Nasdaq futures. RTH opens 6:30 AM PT.

## Tone
- British spelling is fine (Pete is English). Direct, no hype, no padding.
