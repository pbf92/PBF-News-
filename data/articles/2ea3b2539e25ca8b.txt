TITLE: GTA 6 'blackouts begin' on PS5 as consoles shut down, Sony and Rockstar issue statements
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

PlayStation 5 owners are reporting technical issues after playing Grand Theft Auto VI for several hours, believing the game isn’t fit for some current-gen hardware.
GTA VI is Rockstar Games’ most impressive game to date and pushes the boundary of what’s technically achievable on the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
After playing 70 hours of GTA VI for my review, and an additional 30-ish hours since I’d confidently say it’s one of the best games ever made, though you’d expect it to be after we waited so long for it.
Even Rockstar Games isn’t above criticism, though. GTA VI’s open-world is a technological marvel, and as such it’s become a severe stress-test for people’s consoles.

“THE PS5 BLACKOUT HAS STARTED” began an X post by Modyfikator89. “I called it back in early August. First came error reports and sudden shutdowns. Now we’re seeing graphics artefacts live on stream and reports of consoles dying.”
The post was accompanied by footage of the user standing next to a random car. Despite there being nothing of note happening in the game, the console “is already pulling around 220 W at the wall.”
We all knew GTA VI would be a taxing game, even without 60fps, but we wouldn’t have expected it to cripple consoles as reports claim, especially after first-hand experience.

GTA 6 nearly bricked my PlayStation, but for the most part it’s well-optimised
I played GTA VI in its entirety on a launch-day PlayStation 5 console and can honestly say it was a great experience.
There was only one instance where the open-world obviously proved to be too much for my console. There was a singular, scary crash and the occasional dip in framerate, but for the most part everything went off without a hitch.
With that in mind, what’s happening to everyone else’s console? There are a lot of theories on what could be causing this PlayStation 5 ‘blackout’, but many users have been quick to defend GTA VI.
Users have cited long-standing issues with the way our PlayStation 5s were manufactured, but don’t believe the claims that standing your PS5 vertically will shorten its life; that was debunked ages ago and, again, my PS5 has stood vertically since the day of purchase and is still kicking – touch wood…
A general lack of maintenance could be one of the big problems. The PlayStation 5 can gather a lot of dust through its vents, which makes running taxing games like GTA VI all the more difficult.
There could be loads of reasons as to why PlayStation 5 consoles are dying, though, so let’s not blame GTA VI too soon just because it’s the next big thing.
GAMINGbible has reached out to both Rockstar Games and Sony for comments on the technical problems, but has yet to receive a response.
