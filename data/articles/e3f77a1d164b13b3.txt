BridgeWise Files for a Tel Aviv IPO Instead of Crossing the Atlantic: Report
Israeli fintech BridgeWise has filed a prospectus for an initial public offering on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange, Globes reported today (Sunday). The move came after the company, which develops artificial intelligence tools for investment analysis, has raised $46.6 million from private investors.

An Unusual Move for an Israeli Tech Company
The report further outlined that the company plans to raise tens of millions of dollars in the offering.Gaby Diamant, Co-founder and CEO of Bridgewise
IBI Underwriting and Issuances will lead the IPO, Globes said, with Brazilian investment bank BTG Pactual acting as global distributor.
The public listing move, however, did not come as a surprise, as BridgeWise’s plans to go public were reported last month.
Founded in 2019 and based in Ramat Gan, the company closed a $21 million Series A round in 2024 led by SIX Group. In February, it bought Chicago-based Context Analytics in a deal estimated at about $13 million, which linked it to X, and it announced a partnership with the social platform in May.
Earlier in August, Finance Magnates reported on BridgeWise’s partnership with Match-Trade Technologies to offer its analysis to brokers on the Match-Trader platform, following an earlier integration with FXBO's forex CRM. In January, it appointed Thomas Kareklas to lead expansion into the CFD broker segment and updated its Bridget AI platform to support CFD brokers. Devexperts has also integrated Bridget into its Devexa trading assistant.
Will the Numbers Attract Investors?
BridgeWise's revenue reached about $5.8 million in the first half of 2026, against $1.2 million a year earlier, a rise of about 368%, according to the financial statements attached to the prospectus.
The company, however, recorded a loss of about $10.9 million in the period, and operating cash flow was negative by about $6.5 million.
It also reported annual recurring revenue of about $16 million in October, up 419% in a year. Globes noted that the metric reflects annualised revenue from recurring contracts, not revenue already received. Part of the growth came from the February acquisition of Context Analytics. Excluding that deal, growth stood at 146%.
The prospectus projects annual revenue of $120 million to $140 million in 2029, compared with about $3.1 million in 2025. BridgeWise expects to reach net profit in 2028.
