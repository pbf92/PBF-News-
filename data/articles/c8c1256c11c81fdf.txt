TITLE: Shopify Keeps Sliding After Its Q3 Miss: Why Piper Sandler Still Sees 30% Upside
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Key Takeaways
- Shopify closed at $132.31 on Nov. 24, 26% below its October high, three weeks after third-quarter merchandise volume fell short of estimates.
- Piper Sandler reiterated its Overweight rating and $172 target that day, 30% above the stock, pointing to more large brands adopting the AI-shopping standard Shopify developed with Google.
- Shop Pay volume grew 50% last quarter against 6% for PayPal's total payment volume, and Affirm raised its full-year outlook.
- Piper's $172 is 53x 2028 earnings estimates, roughly the multiple the stock already pays for 2027, so the upside rests on those estimates holding.
Shopify (SHOP:NASDAQ) closed at $132.31 on Nov. 24, 26% below its Oct. 12 high, after a third quarter that fell just short. That same day, Piper Sandler reiterated its Overweight rating and $172 price target, 30% above the close.
Piper's case is that more big brands are adopting the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), the AI-shopping standard Shopify developed with Google. Its tracker has UCP on about 15% of the top 2,500 e-commerce sites, up from about 14% in September, and recent Fortune 1000 additions include PayPal (PYPL:NASDAQ), whose checkout button competes with Shop Pay. Shop Pay volume grew 50% in the third quarter, against 6% for PayPal's total payment volume. Affirm (AFRM:NASDAQ), which runs Shop Pay Installments, grew volume 34% to $14.4 billion in the quarter to Sept. 30 and raised its full-year outlook the same week Shopify reported.
Shopify's Good Quarter Wasn't Good Enough
On Nov. 3, Shopify reported revenue up 32% to $3.74 billion, roughly in line with estimates. Gross merchandise volume was the problem: $119.15 billion against the $120.08 billion analysts expected. Jefferies called the quarter "robust, but shy of lofty expectations."
The outlook didn't help either. Shopify guided fourth-quarter revenue growth to the low thirties, but gross profit growth only to the high twenties. Subscription gross margin also fell to 79% from about 82%, which CFO Jeff Hoffmeister tied mostly to added AI spending on Sidekick, Shopify's merchant assistant.

Piper's $172 Still Sits Below the Street
Piper trimmed its target to $172 from $180 after the report, so the Nov. 24 note held the line. The Street's average target of $177 sits above Piper's and has barely moved in a year, from $175 to $177, with a dip to $147 in July.
