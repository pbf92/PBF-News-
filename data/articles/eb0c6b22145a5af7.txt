Ready Capital approves 1-for-10 reverse stock split
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- Ready Capital announced on Monday that it approved a 1-for-10 reverse stock split of the company's outstanding common stock, scheduled to take effect on December 7, 2026.
- The reverse split will reduce outstanding common shares from approximately 159.1M to 15.9M, based on shares outstanding as of November 23, 2026.
- The company will also reduce its authorized common share count from 500M to 50M following the split, while maintaining the $0.0001 par value per share.
- Split-adjusted trading is expected to begin at the market open on December 8, 2026.
- The conversion rate of its 6.25% Series C cumulative convertible preferred stock and the change-of-control share cap of its 6.50% Series E cumulative redeemable preferred stock will be adjusted proportionately.
- The split will not change the outstanding preferred share counts, liquidation preferences, or dividend rates.
- No fractional shares will be issued; eligible shareholders will receive cash in lieu based on the pre-split closing price, adjusted for the reverse split.
- The reverse split will not materially change shareholders' ownership percentages, except for minor adjustments due to fractional shares.
