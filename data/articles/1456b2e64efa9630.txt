Fulcrum Therapeutics Declares Special Dividend of Up to $275.7 Million
Fulcrum Therapeutics declared a special cash dividend of up to approximately $275.7 million, or an estimated $3.6095 per share, in connection with its proposed merger with Slate Medicines.
Key Highlights:
- Stockholders of record on December 7, 2026, are eligible for the dividend; payment to the transfer agent is scheduled for December 10.
- The final dividend depends on Fulcrum’s Net Cash at merger closing and may differ from the current estimate.
- The estimate includes about $7.0 million from a contingent sale of early-stage HbF assets; without those escrowed proceeds, the estimate falls to $268.7 million, or $3.5178 per share.
- Fulcrum expects the merger to close on or about December 11, 2026, subject to stockholder approval and other closing conditions.
- Stockholders are scheduled to vote on the merger at a special meeting on December 3, 2026.
Original SEC Filing: Fulcrum Therapeutics, Inc. [ FULC ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
