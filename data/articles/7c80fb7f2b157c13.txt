TITLE: Light & Wonder Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $3.8M
OUTLET: TradingView

Light & Wonder Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $3.8M
Jamie Odell, a director who held the shares directly, sold 15,813 shares on Nov. 23, 2026, at $79.10 per share and 8,687 shares on Nov. 24 in two transactions at a weighted average price of $79.67 per share, for total proceeds of $1,942,883. After the transactions, Odell directly owned 212,293 shares and indirectly owned 8,275 shares through the New Dusk Pty Ltd (Odell Family Trust) and 10,000 shares through the Jamie and Caroline Odell Superannuation Fund.
Antonia Korsanos, a director who held the shares directly as CHESS Depositary Interests, sold 15,813 shares on Nov. 23, 2026, at $79.10 per share and 8,687 shares on Nov. 24 in two transactions at a weighted average price of $79.67 per share, for total proceeds of $1,942,883. Following the transactions, Korsanos directly owned 218,197 shares, and her child held 313 shares; Korsanos disclaimed beneficial ownership of the shares held by her child.
