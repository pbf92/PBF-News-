TITLE: RIMOWA and Rockstar Games Unveil Exclusive 'Grand Theft Auto VI' PlayStation 5 Case
OUTLET: Hypebeast

RIMOWA has partnered with Rockstar Games to celebrate the highly anticipated launch of Grand Theft Auto VI
The collaborative project features a special edition case housing a custom PlayStation 5 console and its controllers
The bespoke luggage set is strictly limited to an exclusive Friends & Family edition
RIMOWA has officially partnered with Rockstar Games to celebrate the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI with a special edition custom PlayStation 5 case. The exclusive collaboration merges the luxury luggage manufacturer’s signature travel aesthetic with the highly anticipated next chapter of the iconic video game franchise.
Serving as a tribute to the upcoming blockbuster release, the bespoke luggage houses a custom gaming setup designed for Vice City. Inside the special edition case, recipients will find a custom PlayStation 5 console alongside its controllers. To complete the ultimate travel gaming suite, the set also includes the GTA VI game itself.
While the collaboration successfully translates the fictional destination of Vice City into a tangible accessory, the release remains strictly exclusive. The collaborative effort has been produced entirely as a Friends & Family edition, intended to celebrate the game’s launch among a select few.
As a Friends & Family exclusive, the RIMOWA x Rockstar Games Grand Theft Auto VI case will not be available for public retail.
