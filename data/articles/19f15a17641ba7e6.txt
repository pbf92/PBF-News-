TITLE: M61 shut in both directions after serious crash
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

The M61 is shut following a crash in Lancashire tonight.
The motorway is closed to traffic in both directions between Junction 8 (Chorley) and Junction 9.
Emergency services are at the scene, with National Highways first confirming the closure at around 7.30pm.
A National Highways spokesperson said: "The M61 northbound is closed in both directions between J8 Chorley and J9 M65 junction due to a serious collision. All emergency services are in attendance. Delays are building in the area."
Inrix, the traffic data company, said: "M61 in both directions blocked, queueing traffic due to obstruction on the road between J8 A674 Millennium Way (Chorley) and J9 M65 J2 (Clayton Brook)."
This is a live incident. We will bring you further updates as they become available on our dedicated feed below.
Crash may involve HGV
We are receiving unconfirmed reports that the crash involves a pick-up truck.
Diversion routes in place as motorway closes
The M61 has been closed this evening following the serious crash.
There are multiple diversions now in place for motorists.
Northbound traffic is being diverted via the solid square symbol on road signs:
- Leave the M61 northbound at Junction 8 and join the A674 Millennium Way towards Chorley.
- At the roundabout, take the third exit onto the A6 Preston Road northbound. At the next roundabout, take the first exit and continue along the A6 through Whittle-le-Woods and Clayton-le-Woods, continuing ahead at the A6/B5256 roundabout.
- At the A6/Four Oaks Road roundabout, take the second exit onto Four Oaks Road towards Walton Summit.
- At the next roundabout, turn right onto Tramway Lane. At the Tramway Lane/Spectre Way roundabout, take the second exit onto the M61/M65 link road.
- At Clayton Brook Interchange, take the first exit to rejoin the M61 northbound at Junction 9 towards Lancaster and the M6.
Southbound traffic is being diverted via the hollow triangle symbol on road signs:
- Leave the M61 southbound at Junction 9 and take the fourth exit at Clayton Brook Interchange onto the M61/M65 link road towards Walton Summit.
- At the Tramway Lane/Spectre Way roundabout, continue straight onto Tramway Lane. At the next roundabout, turn left onto Four Oaks Road.
- At the next roundabout, turn left onto the A6 Preston Road southbound towards Chorley. Continue through Clayton-le-Woods and Whittle-le-Woods, taking the second exit at the A6/B5256 roundabout to remain on the A6.
- On approaching the A6/A674 roundabout, keep left and merge onto the A674 Millennium Way towards the M61.
- At Junction 8, take the third exit to rejoin the M61 southbound towards Manchester and Bolton.
Reports of 29 minute delays on motorway
According to the AA route planner, the M61 is seeing 'severe' delays lasting up to 29 minutes.
