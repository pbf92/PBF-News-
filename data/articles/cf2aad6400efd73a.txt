TITLE: Baby Jesus pictured with nipple tape for London 'drag nativity'
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

Posters advertising a 'drag nativity’ hosted at a council-owned venue in London have shown the infant Jesus with nipple tape.
Polish drag group Slav 4 U have described their performance as being “unapologetically camp”. The baby Jesus was depicted as a drag performer with the black tape, a moustache, lipstick and a golden halo.
Playing on the traditional Polish Christmas nativity play called Jaselka, the ‘Yassselka’ event has sold-out the Rose Lipman Building, owned by Green-led Hackney Council.
After a CitizenGo petition was set up calling on the show to be cancelled, the group removed its image of Jesus and dropped the ‘Yassselka’ title.
The petition has now reached 72,000 signatures. It states that the show is a “deliberate insult to what Christians hold most sacred, and timed for the very season we spend preparing our hearts to welcome Him.”
It adds: “Polish Catholics in London, families who carried their faith through war and communism, are being mocked through their own traditions.”
A social media post from Christian Concern called the poster “shocking” and “sexualises” baby Jesus, adding “only Christianity is subjected to this defilement.”
Sharing the new poster on Instagram, Slav 4 U said the event would be “a purely festive celebration” with “love” and “friendship”. A number of comments thanked the group for the change in branding.
