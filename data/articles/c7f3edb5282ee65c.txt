X3 Higher Moment Fund Gains Board Influence at Perfect Moment with 9.9% Stake
X3 Higher Moment Fund LLC disclosed beneficial ownership of a 9.9% stake in Perfect Moment Ltd.'s common stock. On November 16, 2026, Perfect Moment's board appointed Toby Maitland Hudson, an executive at the fund's investment manager, as a director. The appointment gives the fund an affiliated voice in board discussions while it also has a lending relationship with Perfect Moment.
Investor Intent
Hudson's appointment marks the fund's immediate governance development at Perfect Moment. As a director, he can participate in and influence board discussions and decisions.
X3 Higher Moment Fund says it will continue reviewing its investment. It may buy or sell Perfect Moment securities, hold discussions with company leaders, directors, shareholders or others, and change or enforce its rights as a lender.
Investor's Background
X3 Higher Moment Fund LLC is a Delaware limited liability company. The fund invests in securities and other financial instruments. Its listed business address is in Minneapolis, Minnesota.
