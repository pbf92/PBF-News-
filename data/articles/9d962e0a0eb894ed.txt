Pochettino still awaits first win as Manchester United coach after 2-2 draw
Photo source: The Japan Times Sports
A goalkeeping error by Senne Lammens helped Sporting Lisbon salvage a 2-2 Champions League draw on Wednesday, leaving Mauricio Pochettino still waiting for his first win as Manchester United coach.
United conceded two penalties in the first half, with Luis Suarez converting the second. Bruno Fernandes equalized from the spot early in the second half and scored twice against his former side.
This is reported by The Japan Times.
Suarez completed his brace after poor handling by United goalkeeper Lammens, canceling out Fernandes’ double.
