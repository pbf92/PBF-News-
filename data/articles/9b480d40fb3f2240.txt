TITLE: Gamers take sick days, plan schedules ahead of GTA 6 release - ABC News
OUTLET: Breaking News, Latest News and Videos

Gamers are making plans and some are even calling in sick in order to play the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) starting at midnight on Thursday, Nov. 19.
The new video game has been 13 years in the making -- the previous game, Grand Theft Auto V, came out in 2013. The hype has reached an all-time high, with some industry experts saying this could be the biggest entertainment property launch ever.
"The release of GTA 6 is the largest release in the games industry's history and possibly, in the entertainment history," Joost van Dreunen, an associate adjunct professor at NYU Stern School of Business, told ABC News. "Once midnight hits, it's going to be a frenzy. And so, relatively speaking, there's nothing really like it."
GTA 6, developed by Rockstar Games, has already topped Sony and Microsoft's pre-order charts ahead of its release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with the game promising a mix of escapism and nostalgia.
Brodie Shea, a student at New York University and video gamer, said the excitement around GTA 6 is due in part to the game's high level of detail.
"Just the attention to detail, to the environment, to the [non-player characters]," he told ABC News. "And I think that's really what everyone's excited for, including myself with this new release, is really how far does that attention to details go?"
Across social media, some gamers have said they are preparing to take a sick day or two to play the new game, a trend among enthusiasts nicknamed "GTA flu."
Content creator and gamer Bud "Gata Bud" Burruss said he has already taken time off and mapped out his schedule for the next few days.
"I'm taking the two days in the week prior. I'm talking the three days in Thanksgiving week that are not company holidays. And then I'm obviously taking Thanksgiving and Black Friday for a total of nine days," Burruss told ABC News.
Residents of Miami, which inspired the fictional Vice City in the world of GTA, are also leaning into the game buzz.
Felix Jarquin, a Miami resident and video gamer, told ABC News he and his friends are excited to play the new GTA 6 together.
"I already have a group of friends that we are going to be playing through together, trying to see if we can get to each other's houses, like kind of a little race," Jarquin said.
The Miami Heat is also joining in on the fun, with the NBA team temporarily rebranding as the Vice City Heat for Wednesday's game against the Milwaukee Bucks, complete with custom Vice City uniforms and a unique court design for a GTA-themed game, called "A Night in Vice City."
For gamers looking to play at midnight, Sony is reminding PlayStation 5 users that there will be a required software update before a user can play GTA 6. Users should download the update prior to midnight so they're ready to play at launch.
