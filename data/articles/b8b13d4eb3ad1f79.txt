TITLE: Divots, howlers and bad decisions - dissecting Man Utd's draw in Lisbon
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
Manchester United were held to a draw in a chaotic and controversial Champions League encounter with Sporting in Lisbon with Mauricio Pochettino denied a first win as United head coach despite his side turning around a first-half deficit.
They were behind at the break after goalkeeper Senne Lammens conceded from a penalty he gave away – having saved another just minutes before.
United then had a goal disallowed before scoring a penalty of their own through Bruno Fernandes, who then put them in front with the help of a large divot on the pitch.
There was more drama as Lammens dropped the ball for Sporting's equaliser before Pochettino's side had what they thought was a winner ruled out.
Polish referee Szymon Marciniak was at the centre of much of the drama with midfielder Youri Tielemans sent off in stoppage time.
BBC Sport breaks down each incident after a crazy night.
Lammens saves penalty (30 mins)
Lammens saved a poor penalty from Sporting's Luis Suarez, given after Lisandro Martinez left a leg out which caught Geny Catamo.
The Belgium keeper got down to hold on to Suarez's low effort which looked set to be a huge boost for the underperforming United number one.
Sporting score second penalty (31 mins)
Lammens went from hero to zero in the space of 76 seconds as he rushed out of his goal and took out Maxi Araujo in the United box.
Noussair Mazraoui was caught out by a ball over the top but it was reckless from Lammens and this time Suarez buried the spot-kick.
Former United midfielder Owen Hargreaves told TNT Sports: "There is no way Senne Lammens can get there. Just stay in the goal."
United 'goal' ruled out (40 mins)
Amad Diallo thought he had equalised for United before half-time when he headed in after his first attempted was turned on to the cross bar.
However, referee Marciniak blew his whistle for a foul against Youri Tielemans.
Replays showed Tielemans won the ball and pulled out of the challenge after he played the ball through to Amad.
BBC Sport's Dale Johnson: "Marciniak believed that Goncalo Inacio had been fouled by Youri Tielemens on the edge of the area as the Manchester United midfielder played a pass through.
"Rather than immediately blow his whistle, Marciniak held off to let the move play out.
"But when Diallo hit the bar with his first effort, Marciniak blew his whistle.
"Play was dead at that point, so when Diallo then nodded the rebound over the line the VAR was locked out.
"However, the chances that the VAR would have intervened were very slim.
"Tielemens, who was booked, caught Inacio's foot under his body sliding to make the pass, causing the Sporting defender's ankle to buckle."
Former Manchester United midfielder Ashley Young said on BBC Radio 5 Live: "It's an absolute disgrace. I don't know how the referee is seeing that Tielemans has kicked the player.
"On first viewing, I thought his foot must have hit him on the follow through but when you look at it again, Tielemans actually tries to take his foot away.
"He doesn't touch him because he knows the midfielder is coming towards him.
"It is one of the worst decisions I've seen with a referee giving that decision."
Fernandes scores penalty (47 mins)
United got back on level terms after a foul on Martinez in the Sporting area by Eduardo Quaresma led to a penalty. Bruno Fernandes converted.
Former Manchester City defender Nedum Onuoha told BBC Sport: "Martinez wins that penalty, but it is a penalty."
Divot assist for Fernandes (49 mins)
United took the lead with two quick-fire goals but in bizarre circumstances as Fernandes' speculative strike looked like it would be easily collected by Kaique Pereira only to hit a divot on the pitch and fly up past the Sporting goalkeeper.
Former Rangers striker Ally McCoist on TNT Sports: "I think it hits a divot and it jumps over the goalkeeper! That is absolutely incredible."
Hargreaves added on TNT Sports: "Unbelievable. It is like a golf divot. There are holes in this pitch absolutely everywhere.
"Bruno Fernandes created a divot, does he get an assist for that?"
Lammens howler gifts equaliser (80 mins)
Lammens made another error, spilling a high ball under little pressure from Suarez. The Sporting striker then reacted quickly to find the target with an overhead kick to make it 2-2, the goal given after a quick VAR check.
McCoist: "I am baffled because the forward does what you expect him to do.
"The goalkeeper, instead of concentrating on the ball, he thinks about the player."
United winner chalked off (82 mins)
United thought they had taken the lead as Sporting keeper Kaique appeared to push the ball into his own net from a Fernandes corner.
However, after a VAR intervention, the 'goal' was then disallowed for a foul on the goalkeeper by Patrick Dorgu.
Onuoha: "First that was an absolutely disastrous goal for United to concede for the equaliser. it comes from a nothing ball into the box, too.
"For the United goal that has just been ruled out, the keeper has pushed it into his own net before Dorgu has touched him."
Young added on BBC Radio 5 Live: "The keeper comes out and flaps at it before he is even touched. How that has gone to VAR is beyond me.
"If a referee is on the pitch and being sent to the screen, if he's given the goal then stand by your decision."
Tielemans sent off (90+3 mins)
Midfielder Tielemans was then sent off for a late challenge on Sergi Altimira. The Belgium international's second yellow compounds the controversial one he received in the first half.
United have now received five red cards in all competitions this season - two more than any other Premier League side.
Young added: "The referee's performance was just simply 'wow'.
"The decisions that he gave. Youri Tielemans getting booked to eventually getting sent off later because you booked him when it should never have been a booking and it should have been an equalising goal.
"His decision-making today is nothing more than a disgrace."
