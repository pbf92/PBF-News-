TITLE: Google Requires AI Agents to Use Governed Access to Workspace Data
OUTLET: CDO Magazine

Artificial Intelligence
Written by: Tathagata Sen
Updated 5:35 PM EST, November 27, 2026
Google is introducing new safeguards for AI agents that access information in Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets and other Workspace applications, according to a November 24 announcement.
The company has made its Workspace Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers and command-line interface (CLI) available for general use. This would allow AI agents to connect to Workspace apps and interact with their data through a standardized interface.
The CLI also lets developers and AI agents access Workspace services. Technical enforcement of this requirement begins February 1, 2027.
Google is also expanding controls that let organizations manage and monitor how external AI tools access Workspace data.
For chief data officers (CDOs), the announcement highlights the need to manage AI agents as users of enterprise data. Each agent needs a defined identity, access limited to the information required for its tasks and an audit trail of its activity.
Taking a leaf from Google’s book, CDOs should apply the same principles across other enterprise systems, internal APIs and third-party platforms, ensuring that AI agents have controlled access to data and their activities are monitored, which are central to operationalizing responsible AI governance.
