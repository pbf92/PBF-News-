TITLE: Hell yeah, there's a zoo in GTA 6
OUTLET: PC Gamer

If you're looking for how to find the zoo in GTA 6, it's probably because you haven't yet been tearing along a particular highway in a stolen luxury car that you're suddenly slamming the brakes on because, hold on, they put a zoo in this? A whole zoo? With animals and everything?
The answer to those questions is yes: Rockstar put in a whole zoo with animals and everything. If that realization is as exciting for you as it was for me, I'll tell you where to find the zoo below.
How to find the zoo in GTA 6
The Wildland Zoo is located on Waxman Island, tucked in the waterway between Vice City proper and Vice Beach. The zoo is on the northeastern side of the island, and if you've made it to Chapter 3 in the story, it's just under 1.5 miles of driving away from home, making it a quick trip if you want to check it out.
The zoo has a main entrance and parking lot on its eastern side, but there's a small drop-off loop branching off a service road on its western end where the only thing separating you from the park is a fence you can easily climb over for faster entry. If, hypothetically, I'd been making an unreasonable amount of trips to the zoo, that'd be the main way I'd be getting in. Hypothetically.
What can you do in the GTA 6 zoo?
Well, look at animals, mostly. You know, like you do at a zoo. The Wildland Zoo has a convenient park guide displayed throughout the area, which details its selection of animal exhibits and other features.
Native Florida wildlife make up the bulk of the zoo's animal inhabitants, with waterfowl and reptiles filling three marsh areas and a swamp enclosure. But the main attractions are a trio of primate enclosures hosting chimpanzees and macaques and a tiger exhibit with three big, beautiful Bengals.
In case you're wondering, yes: It's possible to enter the tiger enclosure. The Bengals weren't as thrilled to see me as I was to see them, however.

There's also "Parrot Paradise," a small structure full of tropical birds that have adopted an expansive lexicon of imitated human speech. Mostly, it was an excuse for Rockstar to record people saying a bunch of lewd things while pretending to be parrots. Money well spent, in my opinion.
It's maybe not the widest collection of animals, but it's a delightful way for the studio to get some extra mileage out of the modeling, rigging, and animation work it did for Leonida's wildlife. My only real complaint is that—despite being on the Park Guide—the zoo's gift shop isn't actually open for browsing and buying souvenirs. I bet you could get some primo ugly shirts in there. Maybe someday.
