TITLE: GTA VI Statistics (2026) – Player Count, Sales, & Revenue
OUTLET: DemandSage

Grand Theft Auto VI was released on November 19, 2026. The game sold 32.5 million pre-release units and recorded $3.79 billion in retail sales on day one (As per Take-Two news)
The game launched 13 years after GTA V and has a 99 score on Metacritic. It also became the most pre-ordered game in both PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store history.
Let’s look at the numbers behind one of the biggest game launches ever. We will cover data available from reliable sources and press releases.
Note: Take-Two and Rockstar have not yet released official GTA VI revenue and player figures. All player numbers below are third-party estimates and are labelled as such. We will update this page when official data is published.
GTA VI Statistics – Key Takeaways
- GTA VI was released on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- The game sold 32.5 million units before release and generated $3.79 billion in retail sales.
- Pre-orders alone generated an estimated $1.65 billion or more (Sensor Tower).
- The Standard Edition costs $79.99, and the Ultimate Edition costs $99.99.
- GTA VI holds a 99 Metascore, tied with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest score on Metacritic.
- The game needs up to 212 GB of storage.
- Launch streams peaked at 7,915,475 concurrent viewers across all platforms (Streams Charts).
- Trailer 1 holds the Guinness World Record for the most-viewed video game reveal on YouTube in 24 hours, with 90,421,491 views.
About Grand Theft Auto VI
GTA VI Release Date
Rockstar Games released GTA VI on Thursday, November 19, 2026. The game was unlocked at midnight in available regions.
GTA 6’s release was already halted twice before. The first trailer was dropped on December 5, 2023, and it was set to be released in 2025. Then, it was supposed to launch on May 26, 2026, after the second trailer release.
Then Rockstar Games finalized the release date on November 6, 2025, and pre-orders began on June 24, 2026, with a release timeline for November 19, 2026.
It took 13 years for Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive to release the sixth edition of Grand Theft Auto. GTA V is already on the list of the most-played games on PC, and over 230 million copies have been sold all over the world.
Source: Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive
GTA 6 PC Release Date
For now, GTA VI has been released on consoles like PS5 and Xbox. There is no official confirmation of the game’s release on PC.
According to a Games Beat Insider Conversation, Rockstar is planning to release the PC and online version in the first three months of 2027.
This delay is much shorter than GTA IV and GTA V because PC represents 45% to 50% of sales of major titles. GTA V took 19 months before launching the PC version.
GTA 6 Player Count
There are no official records published on live player counts or daily active players. Steam can’t track it either, as it is not released on PC.
However, the third-party tracker ActivePlayer estimates around 11-15 million active players as of November 20, 2026. The number might be slightly higher since almost X3 of this estimate’s copies have been sold and players are on track to complete the main story mode.
Right before the launch of the game, a PCMag survey revealed that 12.5 million Americans are planning to adjust their work schedule or take PTO to play GTA 6.
The Independent UK survey said that an estimated 2.8 million adults will play at the launch of GTA VI.
Source: Activeplayer.io, PCMag, Independent UK
Note: Any live GTA 6 player count you see online is an estimate or model, not confirmed data. We will add official numbers when Rockstar or Take-Two releases their data.

GTA 6 Sales & Revenue in Numbers
Take-Two released a press release through Business Wire and revealed the latest numbers on November 20, 2026. The game sold 32.5 million units before the launch and generated retail sales of $3.79 billion in Day One.
According to Sensor Tower GTA 6 Sales Tracker, the game sold 24.58 million units by the evening of the launch day. The units sold generated revenue of $2.36 billion (from pre-orders + launch day sales).
PlayStation accounts for 75% of total sales, which accumulates to around $1.77 billion. Xbox sales generated $588 million in revenue.
Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick predicted $8 to $8.2 billion in net bookings from GTA VI in fiscal year 2027.
Source: Business Wire, Sensor Tower, Take-Two SEC Filing

GTA 6 Making Cost
Take-Two has not disclosed GTA VI’s development budget. A Twitter post by Reuters reports an estimated $1 billion or more in production costs.
Even if the making cost is around $1 billion, that would make this the most expensive entertainment project ever.
Rockstar North’s financial statements show it spent over $2.1 billion on salaries and wages since 2019. This covers all of the studio’s work, not only GTA VI.
Source: Reuters on X, TweakTown
GTA 6 File Size and Supported Devices
According to Rockstar support, GTA 6 needs at least 212 GB of storage. The exact storage depends on the gaming console you are using.
Before its launch, Rockstar already rolled out two updates, which took up to 2 GB of storage.
GTA VI only supports two gaming consoles for now: PlayStation and Xbox. These are the supported devices of these consoles:
- PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 5 Pro
- Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S
- Xbox One (via cloud gaming only)
GTA 6 is only available as a printed digital download code. There is no disc version available, and there might not be one in the future, as the game requires more than 210 GB of storage. This is twice the capacity of standard Ultra HD Blu-ray game discs.
Important Note: GTA VI is officially available on these retail stores: Xbox Store, PlayStation Store, and Rockstar Store. They sell an authorized “code-in-box” edition, so only purchase from these sources. Do not purchase from any unverified source looking for a discount.
GTA 6 Live Streaming Statistics
According to Streams Charts, GTA VI peaked at 7,915,475 concurrent viewers across livestreaming platforms. That makes it the biggest game launch in streaming history.
That is more than 4.5 times the previous record of 1.74 million, set by Valorant’s closed beta in April 2020.
Here’s the peak viewership by platform:
- GTA VI generated 125.47 million hours watched in its first 24 hours. Twitch and YouTube together made up 76.8% of that, and Kick 22.1%.
- On Twitch, GTA VI reached the highest peak viewership of any gaming category ever. It passed League of Legends (3.11 million) and Fortnite (3.04 million).
- Overall Twitch viewership was up 111% compared with the previous week.
- IlloJuan had the highest single-streamer peak on day one, with 562K viewers.
Source: Variety, Stream Charts
