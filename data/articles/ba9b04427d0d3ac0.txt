TITLE: Rockstar’s Limited GTA 6 PS5 Pro Skips Fans for Influencers and Streamers
OUTLET: freeyork

Rockstar Games has released a limited-edition gta 6 ps5 pro styled after Vice City. The console is going to influencers, streamers, and the Miami HEAT NBA team. Most of the players who have spent hours in the game’s open world will not get one.
Fans who hunt for easter eggs and hidden locations have little reason to expect a unit. The consoles are being handed out as promotional items, not sold.
Who gets the consoles
The Vice City-themed consoles and controllers have already reached the Miami HEAT NBA team. The remaining units go to 100 recipients.
Fans would be a better choice. Giving some units to dedicated players would keep the excitement going. Instead, many recipients already own multiple PS5 consoles, and some may stop playing once the launch buzz fades.
Limited-run gaming hardware has a long history of selling out among collectors, and retro-styled pads show how themed designs can build fan loyalty. 8BitDo’s Limited Edition Tomb Raider Controllers shows how a franchise anniversary can turn a controller into a collectible.
What the console looks like
The hardware matches a standard PS5 Pro. Only the exterior changes. Dark tones cover the casing, and palm tree motifs run across the outer shell.
The design borrows from the game’s neon palette. Magenta and turquoise lighting reflect the look of Vice City’s in-game ambiance.
Inside the accessories
Two customized DualSense controllers complete the set. The touchpad carries VI branding. A carry case holds the console, both controllers, and the game case.
Why a retail version matters
Rockstar Games and Sony should offer a limited edition ps5 pro model to general buyers. The design is distinctive, and it suits players who want to spend long sessions exploring the GTA 6 world.
Until a sale is announced, this gta 6 console remains a promotional item for insiders. The fans who know the map best will have to watch from the outside.
