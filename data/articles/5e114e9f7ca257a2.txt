TITLE: PlayStation Portal can cloud stream GTA 6 without PS5 console, but Sony blocks PCs
OUTLET: Notebookcheck

Another reasonably affordable way to play GTA 6 has arrived soon after its release date. Sony has made the Rockstar title cloud streaming compatible on the PlayStation Portal. PS Plus Premium subscribers don’t need to own a pricey PS5 console. Unfortunately, the recent shutdown of a Windows app has blocked access on PCs.
What's needed to stream
Social media posters noticed the company added a new tag to the PlayStation Store page. To play the GTA 6 PS5 version on the gaming handheld, Premium tier members must still purchase the game. However, they can do so through the marketplace’s mobile app or website.
Previously, PlayStation Portal loyalists more commonly used Remote Play. This method relies on having a PS5 console at home. Starting in 2024, Sony launched cloud streaming for select games in the PS Plus Premium library. Notably, this differs from the streaming feature on other tiers, which doesn’t require digital licenses for titles.
Ahead of the GTA 6 release date, a Verge report claimed Xbox would hold exclusive cloud streaming rights. That turned out to be false, but it is available on the Xbox Series X|S and even the aging Xbox One. It’s unclear when Microsoft or its rival may expand access to a wider range of devices.
Sony's shift away from PCs
Until recently, PCs, the Steam Deck, and various gaming handhelds could connect to Sony’s cloud servers. Sadly, the company discontinued its Windows app, and third-party alternatives are no longer options either. Asobi mimicked PlayStation Portal functionality and offered ports on multiple platforms. Its developer is currently searching for a workaround.
Some gamers are now pinning their hopes on an unofficial GTA 6 PC port. Cyberleek leaked an Xbox build that several groups are adapting for Windows. Whether they can solve compatibility issues before Take-Two or Rockstar lawyers file DMCA notices is questionable.
For now, PlayStation Portal owners benefit the most from the new cloud streaming option. Boosted by a growing PS Plus Premium catalog, the $249.99 system has become less dependent on $600+ PS5 consoles.
