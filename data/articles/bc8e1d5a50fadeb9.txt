TITLE: Take-Two’s Rockstar Games announces Grand Theft Auto VI now available
OUTLET: Yahoo

Rockstar Games, a publishing label of Take-Two Interactive Software (TTWO), announces that Grand Theft Auto VI is now available for the PlayStation 5 (SONY) computer entertainment systems and the Xbox Series X S (MSFT) games and entertainment system. "Grand Theft Auto VI represents everything we have been working towards since we first started making games," says Sam Houser, President and Founder of Rockstar Games. "Whether you can trace your journey to this game through the entire series or are experiencing Grand Theft Auto for the first time, we hope that we have created an experience that exceeds your expectations for what's possible from this medium."
