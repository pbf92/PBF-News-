TITLE: Sea of Remnants: Set Sail for the Dawnbreaker Closed Beta on XBOX Series X
OUTLET: Xbox Wire

Captains, your next adventure is on the horizon! We’re bringing Sea of Remnants to XBOX Series X for the Dawnbreaker Closed Beta Test to XBOX Insiders, beginning December 3. Gather your crew, prepare your ship, and get an early taste of a world filled with strange sea creatures, unlikely companions, and mysteries waiting beyond the next island.
Developed by Joker Studio, Sea of Remnants is a free-to-play turn-based roguelike adventure that combines exploration, strategic turn-based battles, and a distinctive puppetfolk world. Here’s what awaits on your first voyage and how to join us.
Find Your Crew, Chart Your Course
You begin as a sailor with lost memories, setting out alongside the mysterious R.S to discover what lies beyond the horizon. As Captain, you’ll recruit puppetfolk companions, assemble a combat team of up to four, and prepare a ship ready to brave the open sea.
Every voyage is an opportunity to strengthen your crew. Gather equipment, Sea Shanties, and Enhancement Dice, experiment with combinations, and discover new ways to turn a difficult battle in your favor. Between expeditions, return to Orbtopia, the bustling pirate city at the heart of your adventure. Explore the world of Sea of Remnants.
Discover What’s New
The Dawnbreaker CBT opens up the White Sea and Bridge Sea, with new Journey and Roaming quests to discover throughout both regions. A broader crew roster gives you more room to experiment with combat strategies, whether you prefer focused attacks or clearing the field with area damage.
You’ll also have two ships to try: the Drift Bottle and the Fireworks Wanderer, each with a different naval combat style. When you’re ready for a tougher challenge, take your favorite team into Peril Theatre and put your tactics to work. See the Dawnbreaker announcement.
Captain quests span the prologue through Chapter 3. For the first time, the test also opens the Sea of Remnants area and its core mechanics, giving Captains who complete a weekly Voyage a chance to reunite with a Black Spot crew member. Progression improvements and Quick Outfit make it easier to prepare your team and try new builds. Read Dev Talk Vol. 2.
Take a Piece of Dawnbreaker into Launch
Your first voyage will leave you with something to remember it by. Captains who participate in the Dawnbreaker CBT will receive an exclusive commemorative reward that carries over to the global launch of Sea of Remnants.
While your beta progress will be reset after the test, this reward will be yours to enjoy when the full adventure begins. Come aboard early, help shape the game, and mark the beginning of your journey!
How to Join
There are two ways to get access:
- Join when the test opens. A limited number of direct registration spots will be available on launch day, first come, first served, subject to server capacity.
- Enter the additional access draw. Pre-register for the game or sign up for the CBT, and create a NetEase Passport account. Selected players will receive an email with access results and download instructions.
Start at the official Dawnbreaker recruitment page. Signing up for the draw does not guarantee access.
Use the same email address for your NetEase account and game pre-registration so that access can be granted correctly. Watch for platform-specific download instructions as pre-download rolls out. This test supports XBOX Series X; XBOX Series S support is planned for global launch. Details in Dev Talk Vol. 2.
How to Download on XBOX Series X
Once pre-download is available on December 1, follow these steps to get ready:
- On your XBOX Series X console, sign in and launch the XBOX Insider Hub app (or install the XBOX Insider Hub from the Store first if necessary)
- Navigate to Previews > Sea of Remnants
- Select Join
- Wait for the registration to complete and be directed to the Store to install Sea of Remnants
Places are limited and available through launch-day registration or the additional access draw.
Mark Your Calendar
- Pre-download begins: December 1, 2026, at 4:00 AM PST / 7:00 AM EST.
- CBT begins: December 3, 2026, at 4:00 PM PST / 7:00 PM EST.
- CBT ends: December 18, 2026, at 4:00 AM PST / 7:00 AM EST.
The test is free to participate in and welcomes players worldwide. Test progress will be wiped when it ends. You can share progress across supported platforms during the beta by signing in with the same NetEase Passport account. Full schedule and recruitment FAQ.
Help Shape the Next Voyage
Participants will receive an exclusive commemorative reward that carries over to global launch. During this test, multiplayer is limited to selected casual minigames with players on the same server; full open-world multiplayer adventures will be introduced gradually after launch. Learn more in Dev Talk Vol. 2.
This is still a game in development, and we want to hear about your time aboard. Join the official Discord to share your experiences, report issues, and tell us what you’d like to see improved.
We’ll see you in Orbtopia, Captains!
