TITLE: VNLOK steps up action against illegal gambling ads on Meta
OUTLET: Gaming Intelligence

The Dutch online gaming association has followed its complaint to the European Commission with a lawsuit against Meta in the Netherlands
Meta is facing a lawsuit from licensed online gambling operators in the Netherlands over illegal gambling advertising on its platforms.
The lawsuit has been filed in Amsterdam by the Dutch online gambling industry association VNLOK, which argues that the parent company of Facebook and Instagram is failing to effectively combat illegal gambling advertising on its platforms.
The legal action in the Netherlands comes after VNLOK asked the European Commission to investigate Meta for alleged violations of the European Digital Services Act.
“The complaint to the European Commission and the lawsuit are two separate steps to compel Meta to take action,” VNLOK said in a statement Friday. “The issue is also of importance to other European countries, where consumers and especially minors can come into contact with illegal gambling advertisements via Meta’s platforms.”
Research commissioned by VNLOK shows that the vast majority of gambling ads on Facebook and Instagram are for gambling operators that are not licensed in the Netherlands, with the share of illegal gambling ads rising from 95.5 per cent in October 2025 to 96 per cent in May 2026 and 98.8 per cent in June.
VNLOK chairman Björn Fuchs said: “There are no suspicions here, but three investigations showing the same thing over a period of eight months. Moreover, two MEPs already asked the Commission questions about illegal gambling advertising on meta-platforms in November 2025.
“If 99 per cent of gambling advertising on a platform is illegal, then that is not an isolated incident but a systemic failure. Brussels has intervened regarding online platforms before. We ask the Commission to do the same now.”
