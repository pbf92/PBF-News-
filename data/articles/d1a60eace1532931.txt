Lionsgate Studios Corp. operates as a standalone, pure-play content company focused on creating, producing, and distributing premium entertainment across multiple formats and platforms. It operates through the following segments: Motion Picture and Television Production. The Motion Picture segment includes the development and production of feature films, acquisition of North American and worldwide distribution rights, North American theatrical, home entertainment, and television distribution of produced and acquired films, as well as the worldwide licensing of distribution rights. The Television Production segment covers the development, production, and worldwide distribution of television content, including series, television movies, mini-series, and non-fiction programming. The company was founded on January 13, 2024 and is headquartered in Vancouver, Canada.
