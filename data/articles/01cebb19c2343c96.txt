Applied Digital Stock Is Trending Thursday: What's Going On?
Applied Digital Corp. APLD shares are trending on Thursday after Anthropic’s IPO priced above the range pitched to investors.
- Applied Digital shares are trending higher. What’s pushing APLD stock higher?
Anthropic IPO Prices Above Range
Anthropic PBC’s ANT Nasdaq debut under the ticker “ANT” took place Thursday. The offering priced at $83 per share, and the stock opened for trading at $111.50.
Anthropic and its seven co-founders raised $107.3 billion through an upsized IPO and private placement, Bloomberg said Thursday. The original offering had been expected to raise roughly $64 billion at the top of its marketed range.
The price tag has skeptics, though. Some question whether Anthropic can expand fast enough, some worry about how much capital it will keep absorbing, and others doubt the business can earn solid returns over time.
Strong demand and pricing above the marketed range may ease investor concerns about financing AI expansion and the demand outlook for AI cloud services.
Anthropic sold 956.4 million shares in the IPO, while its co-founders sold 23.6 million. The company pays for the computing power used to build and run Claude.
Applied Digital shares could move Anthropic’s debut as investors use the listing to gauge demand for AI data center capacity. Anthropic is one of the largest buyers of computing power in the industry.
APLD Shares Are Rising
