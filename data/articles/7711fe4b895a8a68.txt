TITLE: The Claude payday: How Anthropic’s IPO could make thousands of staff millionaires
OUTLET: The Times of India

Anthropic launched its IPO this week, bringing the company’s market value to nearly $1.3 trillion by the end of its first day of trading. Shares of the Claude maker closed at $97.17 on first day (November 19), about 17% above the $83 offering price. The Nasdaq debut also gave employees a public market price for the stock awards they received as part of their pay.
At the $83 offering price, CNBC estimated that employee equity was worth about $232 billion on paper, or roughly $44 million per employee across a workforce of 5,300. Early hires who accumulated more shares stand to gain more. Investors told CNBC that at least 400 early employees could each hold equity worth $100 million or more at that price. The exact number of staff whose holdings are worth at least $1 million remains unclear.
How Anthropic employee stock awards gained value
Anthropic’s prospectus listed outstanding options covering about 1.77 billion shares, with a weighted-average exercise price of $2.91. An option gives its holder the right to buy a share at a preset price. At the $83 offering price, an option with that average exercise price was worth about $80 per share before taxes. The filing also listed more than 1 billion shares tied to restricted stock units that had not yet met service-based vesting conditions at the time of the listing.
Vesting schedules determine when employees gain rights to their stock awards, and lockup agreements can delay when they can sell. How much employees ultimately receive will depend on how many shares they can sell, the price at the time and the taxes they owe.
Where the wealth could go
Redfin estimated that the combined post-IPO equity wealth of current and former Anthropic employees would be enough to buy 9% of San Francisco’s homes. CNBC reported that brokers expect some employees to borrow against their shares to buy property.
Some of the equity could also go to charity. Under Anthropic’s donation matching programme, employees hired before May 17, 2024, can pledge up to half of their vested shares for a three-to-one company match. Employees hired on or after that date can pledge up to a quarter of their vested shares for a one-to-one match.
