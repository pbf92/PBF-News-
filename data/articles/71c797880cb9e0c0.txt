TITLE: Pochettino wraps US reign with Haiti win, heads to Manchester
OUTLET: Dawn

LOS ANGELES: Mauricio Pochettino’s reign as United States coach ended with a simple 2-0 win over Haiti on Tuesday, sent off in style by a Christian Pulisic bicycle kick as he heads to his new role at Manchester United.
Teenager Julian Hall also added his fifth goal from six games to secure an 8-0 aggregate victory in the CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final second leg in Cincinnati.
Set to fly to England just hours after the final whistle, Pochettino exchanged bear hugs, jokes and smiles with players and staff on the pitch after the game.
“We wanted to finish in a good way… Of course it’s tough, because it’s emotional. Because I really love these guys, and I love the players, I love the staff,” said Pochettino.
Admitting he had shed tears moments before his final US press conference, Pochettino added: “The connection with the fans really touched me in the end. I am so grateful. Always, I’m going to be so grateful about the way that everyone treats us in this country.”
Pochettino, who steered the US to the last 16 of a home World Cup this summer, picked a strong lineup for his swansong, and his favoured stars appeared determined to wave their coach off with a win, beginning with high intensity.
Pulisic gave the US the lead in the 25th minute as Weston McKennie crossed from the right and the AC Milan forward, running away from goal, improvised a bicycle kick over his left shoulder that bounced in off the far post.
Hall doubled the lead in the 43rd minute. McKennie again provided the assist, chipping the ball over the Haitian backline.
More testing challenges lie ahead for Pochettino, with United languishing in 18th place in the Premier League and due to face bitter rivals Liverpool on Sunday.
Pochettino is expected to head directly to United’s Carrington training facility on arrival.
“We know that the situation is not a situation that deserves to be a club like Manchester United,” Pochettino told TNT Sports.
Teased by a presenter about Manchester City’s recent dominance, Pochettino jokingly responded: “No way. You will see. Give me time. Give me time, and that is going to change.”
