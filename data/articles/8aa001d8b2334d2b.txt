Shopify Stocks Jump 6.05% as Record Online Holiday Spending Kicks Off
Shopify SHOP shares jumped 6.05% to $141.47 at 10:35 a.m. ET on Friday, November 27, as early Black Friday data pointed to strong online spending. The Canada-based company sells storefront, checkout and payments software to merchants, and TradingKey linked the move to early holiday merchandise-volume momentum, Shop Pay adoption and demand for its AI merchant tools.
Adobe data cited by ROIC shows U.S. consumers spent $7.1 billion online on Thanksgiving Day, up 10.4%, with Black Friday projected at $12.9 billion. In Toronto, Shopify gained 5.6% and led Canadian tech higher, The Globe and Mail reported. The risks have not gone away: TradingKey flagged conservative fourth-quarter gross-margin guidance, European GMV growth slowing to 33% from above 40%, and a forward P/E above 65 times, while Cantor Fitzgerald cut its target to $150.
The GuruFocus chart shows a GF Score of 87/100, with a strong balance sheet and momentum but profitability as the softer spot. The GF Value of $166.40 implies a 15.0% discount, which GuruFocus calls modestly undervalued, yet at more than 65 times forward earnings that gap closes only if holiday volume turns into margin. The 6.05% jump also put Shopify's $182 billion market value back in play for momentum buyers. The next test is Cyber Monday, then the fourth-quarter report, where gross margin will show whether the holiday rush paid off.
