TITLE: Man City player ratings vs Napoli with four 4/10s but Jeremy Doku impresses
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Man City player ratings vs Napoli with four 4/10s but Jeremy Doku impresses
Manchester City were poor again as Napoli twice took the lead at the Etihad before Rayan Cherki rescued a point at the Etihad
Manchester City twice came from behind to claim a Champions League draw with Napoli. Enzo Maresca made five changes from the team that drew with Fulham at the weekend but it was another uninspiring performance.
City dominated possession but were unable to break their Italian opponents down and Rasmus Hojlund fired past Gianluigi Donnarumma to give the visitors a second-half lead. Ayyoub Bouaddi equalised shortly after but Noa Lang restored the lead for Napoli before Rayan Cherki scored late on to spare City blushes.
The point moves City closer towards qualifying for the last-16 but is less than what was expected at home to a struggling Napoli. They now go to Arsenal in the Premier League on Sunday looking for their first win in five league games.
Here are the player ratings from the Manchester Evening News.
Donnarumma: Made a very good save in the first half and was let down by teammates for the goals. 6
Nunes: Needed to recover well just before the Napoli goal after being caught out of position, and was nowhere for the second goal. 4
Guehi: Lucky just before the first goal, at fault for the first and not much better for the second - so fragile at the minute. 4
Gvardiol: Looked confident early on and played some good balls forward but could do nothing to stop City being played through. 5
O'Reilly: Still hasn't found his rhythm going forward and he was caught out horribly for the second goal. 4
Bouaddi: Gave Anderson a pass whenever he needed it and took his goal well in what was a respectable effort. 6
Anderson: One of City's better players and tried to push forward but was unable to make the difference. 6
Fernandez: One corner found Gvardiol in a good spot but otherwise he didn't offer enough (again) and was hooked early. 5
Cherki: City's biggest threat going forward. Was culpable for the Napoli opener but rescued a point with his goal. 6
Allan: His biggest threat remains the surprise aspect of his game, with defenders unsure of what he is going to do. The end product wasn't there though. 5
Haaland: Arguably should have had a penalty in the first half but otherwise just stood about looking less than pleased. 4
