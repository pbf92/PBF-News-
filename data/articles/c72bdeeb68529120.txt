TITLE: Data, Stats and Facts from NVIDIA Earnings for NASDAQ:NVDA by ClearStreet
OUTLET: TradingView

NVIDIA reported $115.8B in revenue for Q3 FY2027, the quarter ended October 25, 2026. The release presents that total through more than one classification. Read the table labels before comparing the rows: the same sales can be grouped in different ways, and some rows are subtotals of others.
📌 One total, two reporting views
NVIDIA’s Q3 FY2027 CFO commentary has a “Revenue by Reportable Segments” table and a separate “Revenue by Market Platform” table. The first lists $106.0B for Compute & Networking and $9.8B for Graphics. The second lists $107.6B for Data Center and $8.2B for Edge Computing. Each table sums to the same $115.8B company total. They are two classifications of the same revenue; adding rows across both tables would count the sales twice.
The market-platform table then shows detail within Data Center: $60.1B from Hyperscale and $47.5B from AI Clouds, Industrial, & Enterprise. Those two rows sum to the $107.6B Data Center subtotal. Edge Computing supplies the remaining $8.2B of total revenue. Keep the subtotal and its component rows at separate levels when reading or illustrating the table.
📌 A percentage needs a denominator
For Q3 FY2027, Data Center’s share of total revenue is $107.6B divided by $115.8B, or about 92.9%. Edge Computing’s share is $8.2B divided by $115.8B, or about 7.1%. These percentages describe revenue mix. They do not allocate profit to either platform.
The same fiscal-quarter comparison gives a longer view. Data Center represented about 80.1% of total revenue in Q3 FY2024, 87.7% in Q3 FY2025, 89.8% in Q3 FY2026, and 92.9% in Q3 FY2027. Each percentage is Data Center revenue divided by total company revenue for that fiscal Q3. Data Center revenue rose from $14.514B to $107.6B across those periods, while total revenue rose from $18.120B to $115.844B. The share increased in each of the four fiscal-Q3 observations. This records a change in reported mix; the percentages alone do not identify its causes or establish a future mix.
📌 Growth rate and dollars added answer different questions
The Q3 FY2027 commentary gives comparable prior-year figures for the two disclosed Data Center categories. Hyperscale increased from $35.1B to $60.1B: a 71% year-over-year increase and $25.0B more revenue. AI Clouds, Industrial, & Enterprise increased from $16.1B to $47.5B: a 195% increase and $31.4B more revenue. Together, those dollar increases equal the $56.4B increase in Data Center revenue, from $51.2B to $107.6B.
The percentage-growth formula is (current value − prior value) ÷ prior value. A smaller starting value can produce a higher percentage rate. In these figures, the category with 195% growth added more dollars year over year, while Hyperscale remained the larger current category at $60.1B versus $47.5B. Percentage growth, dollars added, and current revenue are three different comparisons.
There is a classification detail in the historical records. NVIDIA’s contemporaneous Q3 FY2026 commentary showed the $51.2B Data Center subtotal with $43.0B of Compute and $8.2B of Networking beneath it. The later Q3 FY2027 commentary presents Q3 FY2026 comparison values using Hyperscale at $35.1B and AI Clouds, Industrial, & Enterprise at $16.1B. Both pairs sum to the same Data Center subtotal, but they are different breakdowns. For the 71% and 195% growth rates, compare the matched rows in the Q3 FY2027 commentary rather than matching differently labeled rows across releases.
📌 Revenue, net income, EPS, and market capitalization use different bases
Q3 FY2027 revenue was $115.844B and GAAP net income was $65.287B. Dividing net income by revenue gives an approximate 56.4% company-wide net-income-to-revenue ratio. It is not a Data Center profit margin: the cited market-platform table reports revenue by platform, not net income or margin by platform.
GAAP diluted EPS was $2.70. The release lists 24.186B weighted-average diluted shares for the quarter; $65.287B divided by 24.186B is approximately $2.70 per diluted share. EPS therefore uses a weighted-average share count over the reporting period.
Market capitalization uses a different basis: share price at a stated time multiplied by common shares outstanding. NVIDIA’s latest filed count available for this draft was 24.1B shares outstanding as of August 21, 2026, in its Q2 FY2027 Form 10-Q. That point-in-time share count is different in definition and date from the 24.186B weighted-average diluted shares used for Q3 EPS. A dated market-cap figure should state both the price timestamp and the share-count date.
The Q3 release date was November 17, 2026. Fiscal Q3 here means NVIDIA’s fiscal quarter, not a quarter ending on the same calendar date each year.
