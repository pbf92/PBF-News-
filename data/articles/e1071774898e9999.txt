YZY: Team allocation unlocks 29.17 million tokens - 12 Jul 2027
YZY unlocks 29.17 million YZY tokens for the team on 12 Jul 2027. The scheduled release is part of a monthly team allocation and equals 9.76% of the token's circulating supply.
The unlock increases the supply available to team holders. A release of this size creates dilution for existing holders and potential selling pressure if the team sells some or all of the newly unlocked tokens.
