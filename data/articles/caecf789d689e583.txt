Nasdaq 100 teeters near Friday's close as tech selloff deepens
The Nasdaq 100 NDX is approaching a key level from last week as renewed selling in technology stocks deepens the market's retreat.
The index was down 4.18% at 25,117.24 on Wednesday, just above its Friday close of 25,029. The Nasdaq Composite (COMP) also slid 4%.
Wall Street opened sharply lower as investors assessed the latest GDP and personal consumption expenditures reports, with Nasdaq leading the declines.
Technology stocks have come under renewed selling pressure this week, with investors pulling back after recent gains. The Nasdaq Composite fell 2.2% on Tuesday, extending the tech-led weakness into Wednesday as the sector remained the biggest drag on broader markets.
The Nasdaq 100 has now fallen 20% from its peak, putting the index on track to enter bear-market territory.
