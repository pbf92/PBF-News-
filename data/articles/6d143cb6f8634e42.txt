OUTLET: TradingView

Tyler Technologies Chief Administrative Officer Sold Shares Worth Over $1.3M
Abigail Marshall Diaz-Pedrosa, Chief Administrative Officer, sold 3,107 shares of Tyler Technologies common stock on November 24, 2026, at $420 per share for a total of $1,304,940. After the sale, she directly owned 4,437 shares and indirectly owned 400 shares through a trust for which family members are beneficiaries and she is a co-trustee, with shared voting and dispositive power.
