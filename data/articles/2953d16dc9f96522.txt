TITLE: Stock Market Today, Nov. 17: Nvidia Slides Ahead of Earnings but Soars in After Hours Trading
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

On Nov. 17, Nvidia (NASDAQ:NVDA), a semiconductor industry leader in AI accelerators, graphics processing units (GPUs), and networking hardware, closed at $155.46, down 0.44%. The stock continued to reel from the fallout of its CEO Jensen Huang's resignation on Nov. 12, as investors positioned ahead of the company's after-the-close fiscal third-quarter earnings report. Data center sales and post-Huang guidance represent key factors.
Trading volume reached 345.4 million shares, coming in about 127% above its three-month average of 152.0 million shares. Nvidia IPO'd in 1999 and has grown 378,915% since going public.
How the markets moved today
The S&P 500 (SNPINDEX:^GSPC) fell 1.03% to 7,341, while the Nasdaq Composite (NASDAQINDEX:^IXIC) declined 0.74% to 24,328. Among semiconductor chip stocks, Advanced Micro Devices (NASDAQ:AMD) closed at $503.41, down 0.17%, while Broadcom (NASDAQ:AVGO) closed at $341.60, up 2.46%, showing mixed AI-chip sentiment.
What this means for investors
On Tuesday, Nov. 17, ahead of Nvidia's fiscal third-quarter earnings announcement, shares closed down as investors anxiously awaited insight into the health of the artificial intelligence market through the company's Q3 performance.
As the leader in AI semiconductor chips and a partner to businesses across the AI industry, Nvidia is seen as a bellwether for the sector. The resignation of its CEO was a blow to the company, and led to a stock sell-off. That changed after Nvidia's CFO and interim CEO Colette Kress revealed record fiscal Q3 revenue that blew past Wall Street expectations. As a result, shares rose 1.3% in early after-hours trading.
The semiconductor giant reported a 103% year-over-year increase in sales to $115.8 billion, while net income also grew over 100% to $64.9 billion. Its data center revenue rose 110% year over year and 21% from the previous quarter to $107.6 billion, demonstrating demand for its chips remains strong.
The icing on the cake was Nvidia's fiscal Q4 revenue forecast of approximately $130 billion. This was ahead of Wall Street expectations, and bodes well for the health of the AI sector.
