TITLE: Five Things We Learned From Liverpool 4-0 Manchester United
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Liverpool return to action with a statement victory over a poor Manchester United side, winning 4-0, with several players having their best showings not only this season, but in their LFC career so far.
Here are five things we learned from this comprehensive win over the Red Devils.
Dominik Szoboszlai Is A World Class Free Kick Taker
The game started full of action and drama from minute one, with the first major flashpoint being the Leny Yoro red card.
That sending off resulted in a free kick right on the edge of the box that Dominik Szoboszlai dispatched with power and accuracy, past a helpless Senne Lammens in the United goal.
And we are getting to a stage now that when Liverpool gets a free kick that is remotely a shooting opportunity, with Szoboszlai in the team, the odds of it going in are double or even triple, which is a very useful asset to have.
Florian Wirtz's Best Game In Red
Fresh off a successful international break with Germany, Florian Wirtz came back to Merseyside with a confidence that we had yet to see in his first season and a bit at the club.
The goal he scored was a sensational piece of skill from the playmaker, who managed to spin, connect with Rio Ngumoha's cross into the box on the volley and use his weaker foot to redirect the ball into the back of the net.
And the assist he played to Victor Munoz late in the second half looked effortless and is exactly the type of line-breaking pass that LFC fans expected when the club spent so much on Wirtz last summer.
He will now hopefully build upon this performance at Anfield, and we could be looking back at this game as the coming-of-age fixture for Liverpool's number seven.
The Midfield Still Has Work To Do
While the individual displays from Szoboszlai and Alexis Mac Allister were good, there were still a few occasions where United would win the ball back and be able to breeze through the midfield with plenty of space for the away side to operate in.
Now, while the red card meant Liverpool were pushing up the pitch and focusing heavily on attacking, this isn't the first time that opposition teams have been able to glide through the Reds' double pivot and going forward, it will need to be something Andoni Iraola tries to address.
Rio Ngumoha Was Electric
While Florian Wirtz was given Player of the Match, Rio Ngumoha was incredible, having beaten his full-back Diogo Dalot almost every single time.
He was instrumental in the creation of the second goal and managed to get on the scoresheet himself as well.
Alexander Isak's Best Game For Liverpool?
While the Reds striker didn't get himself a goal or an assist in this game, his overall game and link-up play with his fellow attackers was the best it has ever been at the Merseyside club.
He will no doubt be disappointed at not scoring, despite the number of chances he had with Iraola, in particular, happy with his ability to work with the rest of the team, helping to create chances for others to score goals.
