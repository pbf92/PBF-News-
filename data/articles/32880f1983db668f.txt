TITLE: How To Find The GTA VI Scarface Easter Egg & Unlock A Special Weapon
OUTLET: RockstarINTEL

Grand Theft Auto VI is filled with small details and references to other famous movies and games. One of these is a pretty interesting Easter egg inspired by the classic movie Scarface. However, finding it isn’t as simple as walking into a building.
The encounter also rewards players with a special weapon called the Little Friend W-10 SMG. If you want to find it yourself, here’s everything you need to do.
How to Find the Scarface Easter Egg in GTA VI
Before getting started, make sure you visit the location between 12 PM and 2 PM in-game. Arriving too early and waiting around can prevent the encounter from triggering. It’s best to head to the hotel entrance after noon.
Here is how to trigger the Easter egg and get your hands on the “Little Friend W-10”.
Step 1 – Find the Hotel
First, head to Ocean Beach and look for the building right next to the Cortez Hotel. This is where the Scarface Easter egg takes place. Once you arrive, make your way to the front entrance.
Step 2 – Try Opening the First Door
Take the front stairs and go all the way up. At the top, you will find the first apartment door shown in the screenshot.
Try opening it with Jason or Lucia. The door won’t open, but this interaction is important for triggering the encounter.
Step 3 – Take the Back Stairs
After trying the locked door, don’t go back down using the front stairs. Instead, take the stairs at the back of the building and make your way down to the alley.
From there, head back towards the front of the hotel and onto the street.
Step 4 – Watch the Encounter
Once you return to the front, the Easter egg should finally trigger. You will see a car leaving the area while a man is left lying on the road.
This is the encounter that references one of Scarface’s most memorable scenes.
Step 5 – Get the Little Friend W-10
After the encounter, head back upstairs to the same apartment. The previously locked door should now be open. Inside, you can pick up the Little Friend W-10 SMG, which is waiting on the floor near the entrance.
Don’t forget to check the bathroom as well. There is a particularly gruesome detail inside, referencing the famous chainsaw scene from Scarface.
Bonus – Police Arrive at the Scene
If you stay around for a little longer, you can also see police officers and ambulances arriving at the location. It’s another small detail that makes the encounter feel even more complete. Just make sure you grab your new weapon before leaving!
