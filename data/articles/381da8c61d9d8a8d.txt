TITLE: Next Week on XBOX: New Games for November 23 to 27
OUTLET: Xbox Wire

Welcome to Next Week on XBOX! In this weekly feature we cover all the games coming soon to XBOX Series X|S, XBOX One, XBOX on PC, and Game Pass! Get more details on these upcoming games below and click their profiles for further info (release dates subject to change). Let’s jump in!
Jaden Industries: Cozy Automation Puzzler – November 23
Smart Delivery / XBOX Play Anywhere
Jaden Industries is a relaxing factory-building puzzle game about managing compact crafting chains across floating islands. As an expedition engineer, you’ll explore a peaceful Archipelago, configure production flows, refine resources, and achieve perfect efficiency. Build. Optimize. Relax.
The Spotter: Dig or Die – November 23
XBOX Play Anywhere
You’re a Spotter, the unlucky soul stationed at the worst gas station in Nevada. Your mission: hold the line against mutant hordes spilling from the ruins of Las Veganos, while awaiting a message from the President himself. But when a colossal sandstorm wrecks the base and ruins your only receiver, you lose your one chance of contact. Now everything hinges on repairing it in time. Dig by day, fight by night, and reclaim the signal before the world’s last hope is lost. Descend into a mine to scavenge for resources, discover roguelike artifacts that rewrite the rules, and turn your rusty junkyard into an impenetrable fortress in this unique hybrid of digging simulator and Tower Defense.
Grab your friends. Lift them over your head. Throw them off a pizza rooftop. Bungle Brawl is a wobbly physics party brawler for 1–4 players on one screen, with bots to fill every match. Everyone is the same jelly body in a different costume. Be a Banana, a Shark, a Hot Dog, a Ghost or a Nightstand. Punch, kick and grab. Knock a fighter dizzy, carry them to the edge while the crowd chants, and let go.
Master the art of auctions and score big against others for treasures in Storage Hunter Simulator! Stay ahead of the competition in the high-risk, high-reward world of storage auctions. Showcase your skills, win intense bidding wars and sell your hard-earned valuables to customers. Getting the best deal and maximizing your earnings is key to staying in business and building a solid reputation.
A deceptively simple puzzle game where symmetry is your greatest challenge. Control two cats at the same time, navigating complex levels where every move is mirrored. It seems easy… until your mind is put to the test. Can you master the reflection?
The forest has been invaded by slime creatures, and now it’s up to Bear to help the villagers reclaim their home! Get ready for an adventure filled with puzzles, sticky monsters, and slime explosions. Explore 40 puzzle levels where every move matters. Push slime balls through the environment, plan your moves carefully, and find the right way to eliminate all the Slime Monsters.
Welcome to a sinful night with the galaxy’s craziest aliens! Not every alien visits Earth to witness the indomitable human spirit. Some just want to chill on the Moon’s surface and play games. Born from the legendary internet meme, this isn’t just your average “friendslop” party game. Alien humor is… how should we put it… a bit of a brainrot. In this first-person, four-player, online multiplayer space adventure, every alien at the table must do whatever it takes to win in a variety of tabletop games. You’ll craft sneaky strategies, tell outrageous lies, pull hidden weapons, and literally blow up the heads of your so-called “friends.”
Calculus of Rot – November 26
Smart Delivery
The world is dead. Mutants roam the ruins, and every step forward could be your last. Explore dark, infected zones hidden beneath the fog of war. Reveal new paths, discover useful items, and carefully decide which enemies to confront. Every encounter costs resources, and every mistake brings you closer to death. Study enemy strength, plan your route, and use your limited health and attacks wisely. Some monsters are worth the risk. Others should be avoided at all costs. As you progress, stronger abominations begin to appear, turning each map into a deadly tactical puzzle where survival depends on smart decisions rather than luck.
Full Metal Sergeant 2 – November 26
Smart Delivery / XBOX Play Anywhere
If you die on the battlefield, it’s not my fault. You think boot camp’s hard? Try surviving my camp. In Full Metal Sergeant 2 you’re a hellish instructor. Your mission is to transform raw recruits into specialized war machines. Mold their minds and bodies, instill discipline, and hone their combat skills.
Gravity SWITCH – November 26
Smart Delivery / XBOX Play Anywhere
Slide, dodge, and survive! A retro-inspired arcade puzzle game where every move propels you until you literally hit a wall. Collect every dot across 50 hand-crafted stages, dodge deadly hazards, and unlock the warp crystal to escape. Features a dozen unique chiptune tracks! Step into a high-stakes sliding maze where stopping isn’t an option. In this fast-paced arcade puzzler, once you push in a direction, you slide until a wall stops you. Plan your routes, collect all the dots, and activate the warp crystal to reach the next level – if you can survive the traps in your way.
Machine Party – November 26
XBOX Play Anywhere
Welcome to Machine Party. In this series of dangerous, and some would say highly illegal, party games you will have to use every trick in the book to survive. Play through classic party game formats and new twists on the genre for sessions of fun, screaming, and horror.
MANIC MINER – November 26
Smart Delivery / XBOX Play Anywhere
The legendary platforming classic is back! Experience a milestone in video game history in a completely reimagined version! Authorized by original developer Matthew Smith, this reboot of the 1983 classic combines the distinctive and challenging gameplay of MANIC MINER with modern graphics, improved controls, newly arranged music, and extensive historical bonus content.
Marupoyo was born so round that he cannot walk… he can only roll! Embark on a colorful physics-based platforming adventure and help this little chick find his lost siblings. Tilt the world, control his momentum, and roll through environments filled with obstacles, secrets, and challenges. Easy to learn, fun from the very first roll, and deep enough for players determined to master every level.
River Strike – November 26
Smart Delivery
Take control of legendary combat helicopters and battle your way upstream through enemy territory. Destroy bridges, warships, submarines, tanks and helicopters while completing 24 action-packed missions. Unlock new helicopters, upgrade their firepower and take on increasingly dangerous enemies in this modern low-poly tribute to the classic arcade shooters of the 80s and 90s.
Security 51 – November 26
Smart Delivery / XBOX Play Anywhere
Security 51 is an atmospheric security guard simulator inspired by Papers, Please, and SCP paranoia. You’re on duty in the underground bunker of Area 51, where the most dangerous part begins at the elevator: the shaft descends for miles down to unknown levels. Whether it’s laboratories, anomalies, inhumane experiments, or something alien—you’re not supposed to know. Your task is simple: let in those who have access and stop those who don’t… even if everything looks “right” on the surface.
Welcome to The Penrose. Service is impeccable and the rooms are immaculate. You’re its most valued guest, but you can’t remember checking in. The Spectrum Retreat: Redux is a first-person puzzle thriller created by Dan Smith, Winner of the 2016 BAFTA Young Game Designer Award.
Arkheron (Game Preview) – November 27
XBOX Play Anywhere
The horn sounds. The Tower calls. How will you choose to fight? Arkheron is a fast-paced, clip-worthy team PvP game where the items you equip dynamically change how you play. Grab your friends, loot Relics that change your build mid-fight, and climb a haunted Tower floor by floor. If you’ve played a shooter or a MOBA, your aim and your instincts already work here. The builds, the counters, the hundred ways a fight can turn all boils down to your adaptability and skill.
Bunker Tidy Up – November 27
Smart Delivery / XBOX Play Anywhere
Bunker Tidy Up is a relaxing game about organizing a bunker. Arrange 17,437 supplies on shelves, categorize resources, and relax! Bunker Tidy Up is a meditative game about organizing a space without rushing or pressure. No timers, no panic — just you, the shelves, and thousands of items that need to find their place.
Feral Road – November 27
Smart Delivery
The world is over. The roads are crawling with monsters, and your only way forward is to keep moving. Choose from four unique vehicles and fight your way through dangerous post-apocalyptic stages, each filled with enemies that behave and attack in different ways. To survive, you’ll need more than just firepower, you’ll need the right strategy.
Luminova – November 27
Smart Delivery / XBOX Play Anywhere
In a clean, geometric 2.5D world, timing and precision are your only allies. Take control of a continuously moving white cube in this exciting auto-runner platformer. With direct, intuitive controls — where any button performs a jump — your rhythm dictates your survival. To change movement direction, use the stage walls as springboards, bouncing off at just the right moment to reverse course and reach new platforms.
My Lovey Dovey Demon – November 27
Smart Delivery
A cute love story between a young man and an affectionate, adoring, utterly insatiable demon girl set in the Japanese countryside.
The Last Cat in the Universe – November 27
Smart Delivery
Scavenge the remains of a dying space station to build a cozy home for the last cat in the universe. A short, narrative-driven incremental game about finding peace amidst the darkness. You are an AI on a decaying space station. Humanity is gone. All that remains is dust, silence, and a small, frightened creature. Your only instruction: Take care of it.
