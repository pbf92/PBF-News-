Opendoor Technologies Establishes $300 Million At-the-Market Offering
Less than 1 min read
Opendoor Technologies entered into an agreement to offer and sell up to $300 million of common stock through an at-the-market offering.
Key Highlights:
- The company may sell shares from time to time at its sole discretion; it is not obligated to issue or sell any shares.
- Virtu Americas will act as sales agent and may receive commissions of up to 2.0% of the gross sales price per share.
- Opendoor said the offering is intended to complement existing capital resources and provide flexibility to access equity markets opportunistically.
Original SEC Filing: Opendoor Technologies Inc. [ OPEN ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
