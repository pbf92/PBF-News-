TITLE: Grand Theft Auto VI become a Spotify top 10 Artist whilst dominating the charts
OUTLET: RockstarINTEL

Grand Theft Auto VI has become one of Spotify top 10 Artists in its weekly charts. Whilst the GTA6 Score has dominated to become the most played song.
Every Friday, streaming Platform Spotify release the weekly stats from the previous week. Top Artists of the week, Top Songs Of The week and Top Album Of The Week. And lets just say Rockstar Games have dominated the charts with Grand Theft Auto VI.
Top Song
First off, in the weekly most played songs GLOBALLY, the Original Score for Grand Theft Auto VI known as ‘Flower In A Hurricane’ produced by French Electric Duo known as JUSTICE has dominated the charts to hit the Number 1 spot with 33,742,904 streams. It beat ‘Stop the Wedding!’ by nearly 2.5 Million Streams.
Last week, BbY WOW by KAROL G was previously at the top spot, this song is now number 3. Flower In A Hurricane was the Number 18th spot on the global streams just last week. Shooting up due to the release of Grand Theft Auto VI. To 10 places this week are:
- Flower In A Hurricane – Justice
- Stop The Wedding! – Ashe
- BbY WOW – KAROL G
- Housewife – Tate McRae
- Last Christmas – Wham
- All I Want For Christmas Is You – Mariah Carey
- The Cure – Olivia Rodrigo
- Suzuki (From GTAVI: the Album – Fuerza Regida, Grand Theft Auto VI
- Nicole Kidman – ADÉLA
- Babydoll – Jamie Miller
Top Album
Also announced today by Spotify in the weekly updates. Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (Deluxe Edition) has taken the Top Spot for the most played Albums during last week. It was also classed as the ‘Highest New Album Entry,’ knocking Olivia Rodrigo ‘you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love‘ from the Top Spot after Many weeks. Top 10 most played Albums right now are:
- Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (Deluxe Edition) – Grand Theft Auto VI
- you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love – Olivia Rodrigo

- The Great Divide: The Last Of The Bugs – Noah Kahan
- The Art of Loving – Olivia Dean
- Visitor (Deluxe) – SIENNA SPIRO
- Un Verano Sin Ti – Bad Bunny
- NO ME ARREPIENTO DE SENTIR TANTO – KAROL G
Weekly Top Artists
In Spotify’s weekly Top Artists globally, it has been revealed that Grand Theft Auto VI has entered the top 10 most listened Artists of the week by taking the 9th position. Last week, they was 94th, up 85 places making them the highest gainer of the week
Justice is the highest new entry, taking position 134 after previously not being in the charts. The Top 10 current most played artists are:

This is an incredible achievement by everybody at Rockstar Games and who has had some input of the GTA6 Album and really does show what Gaming does for the Music Industry, especially a game as huge as Grand Theft Auto VI, with the game now selling over 53 Million copies in around 1 week since release.
