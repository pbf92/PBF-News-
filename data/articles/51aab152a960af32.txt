TITLE: Rockstar Defends GTA 6's Most Controversial Feature
OUTLET: OpenCritic

Rockstar Games has publicly defended Grand Theft Auto 6's Criminal Profile mechanic, suggesting it gives players "other options" in how they approach the game. The Criminal Profile system is one of the big new mechanics in GTA 6, but it hasn't been overly popular with everyone.
