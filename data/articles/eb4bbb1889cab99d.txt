TITLE: LingSante-Live vs Gemini 3.8 Live: The Clinic Is the Moat
OUTLET: OrcaRouter

Gemini 3.8 Live already does most of what LingSante-Live was announced to do. It hears, it sees a camera feed, it answers in native audio, and it has been generally available since 15 September 2026 at a price anyone can read. So the interesting question is not whether Google's live model can look at a medicine box — it can — but what Ant Group thinks it has that a general live model does not. The answer, from the material Ant published on 23 November 2026, is a clinical workflow and a background reasoning tier sized for it. The answer it has not given is any number that would let you check.
That makes this the most honest matchup in the set. Against Eleven Realtime Max and Fish Audio Live 3, LingSante-Live's camera is a category difference. Against Gemini 3.8 Live, it is a feature both have, and the comparison drops to the part Ant has not documented.
Gemini already does the loop
The loop Ant demonstrated on stage is: watch the frame, notice that the label is unreadable, ask the user to move closer, keep the conversation going while they do, then read the ingredients. Gemini 3.8 Live supports image and video input over a WebSocket session with audio output, at 131,072 input tokens and 65,536 output, across 97-plus languages with mid-conversation switching. There is no published demo of it asking someone to reposition a medicine box, but nothing in its capability set prevents it, and the prompt to do so is a paragraph of instructions.
What Gemini 3.8 Live does not ship is the clinical layer: the medication cross-reference, the ingestion-risk advice, the structured follow-up that assumes a patient record exists somewhere. That layer is a product, not a modality, and it is where the comparison actually lives.
One architecture, two model ids
Here is the sharpest structural difference, and it is easy to miss. Gemini's background reasoning is a separate model. Google shipped Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking alongside the standard Live model on the same day, with low, medium and high thinking levels and non-blocking tool calls. Choosing between them is a deployment decision: you pick the fast id or the thinking id, or you route between them.
LingSante-Live describes a single architecture with two tiers inside it — the foreground stays responsive, the background agent does the analysis, and the result is folded into the next turn automatically. Ant is not asking you to choose a model id; the split is internal.
• Foreground/background — one architecture with an internal background tier vs two model ids you select between.
• Tool use — Ant describes background planning; Gemini Extended Thinking exposes non-blocking function calling with a blocking mode that auto-cancels.
• Continuity — Ant's design assumes the background result re-enters the same conversation; with two Gemini ids, the handoff is code you write.
The difference is real but smaller than it sounds. A router with a rule — fast model for the turn, thinking model for the analysis, results merged back — reproduces Ant's description closely, and it is a well-trodden pattern rather than a research problem. What Ant has that a router does not is the clinical content inside the background agent.
Where the medical product starts
Ant's background agent is not a generic reasoner. The family it belongs to includes LingSante-Pulse for risk across more than 150 conditions and LingSante-MedGuard for safety, compliance and bioethics. In the demo, LingSante-Live gave immediate advice on accidental ingestion and eye exposure to a topical medicine, based on the ingredients it had just read from the box. That is a narrow, high-stakes task with a defined correct answer, and it is the kind of thing a general live model will attempt politely and sometimes wrongly.
This is the substance of the moat claim, and it is worth stating precisely rather than dismissively. A general model can read the label. Knowing which of four ingredients makes an ingestion call urgent, and phrasing that in a way that does not constitute a diagnosis, is domain work. Ant has 20 million daily consultations to learn from and a safety model in the family. Google has neither, and would have to buy or build them.
Against that, Gemini has a distribution advantage Ant cannot match outside China: an API, a free tier, enterprise terms and a global developer base. LingSante-Live has an app.
Published numbers versus none
Gemini 3.8 Live is one of the most measured live models available. On Artificial Analysis' Speech Agent Arena it sits fourth overall with an Elo of 1,083 and a 93.2% task success rate, behind three systems that are either cheaper or narrowly better on preference; its own Extended Thinking sibling scores lower on preference but higher on agentic tasks, which is the usual trade. Time-to-first-audio is reported around 1.2 seconds in third-party comparisons — slower than Eleven Realtime Max's 570 ms, and a reminder that "sub-second" in marketing is not the same as sub-second in a p95.
LingSante-Live has none of this. No latency, no task success, no preference score, no parameter count, no context length, no language list. The only quantitative facts attached to it are about its siblings: LingSante-3.1-flash at 560 billion total parameters and 25 billion active with a one-million-token context, LingSante-3.1-air-VL at 124 billion and 5.5 billion active. Neither number describes the agent a patient talks to.
There is a specific asymmetry worth naming. Gemini's weaknesses are published — the standard Live model is weaker on agentic tool use than its thinking sibling, and its first-audio latency trails the fastest speech vendors. Ant's weaknesses are unpublished, which is not the same as absent. A reader should weight an undocumented system as unknown rather than as good.
What parity would cost you to build
Suppose you wanted Gemini 3.8 Live to behave like LingSante-Live. The bill has three lines, and none of them is the model.
• The realtime layer — audio in at $3.00 per million tokens and audio out at $12.00 per million, which Google normalises to roughly $0.005 and $0.018 per minute. For a ten-minute consultation that is cents, not dollars.
• The reasoning tier — a separate model for the background work. Gemini 3.8 Flash is on our catalogue at $0.75 per million input and $3.75 per million output with a 1,048,576-token context, passed through at provider list price with no markup, and routable alongside other vendors behind one key with automatic failover. The Live variant is not on our catalogue, so the foreground in this design is still a direct Google integration.
• The clinical layer — the ingestion rules, the interaction checks, the refusal policy, the safety guardrails. This is the expensive line, it is not purchasable, and it is the actual product.
That third line is why "just use Gemini" is a weaker answer than it first appears, and why "Ant built a better model" is probably the wrong description of what Ant did. Ant built a workflow and put a model inside it.
The question a buyer should ask
If you are a health system or a telehealth company, the choice is not between these two models. It is between integrating Gemini 3.8 Live plus your own clinical layer, or waiting for Ant to offer LingSante-Live to third parties, which it has not done and may never do.
The first path is buildable now. The foreground is a documented live API with published pricing and a measured arena record; the background is a text model of your choosing; the clinical content is your own regulatory problem, which it would have been anyway. The second path has no date, no terms and no price, and the only evidence it works at scale is that Ant's own app uses it.
There is a version of this comparison in which LingSante-Live wins clearly: a hospital in China, deploying on-premises, wanting a Chinese-language video consultation with medical safety built in, and able to get commercial terms from Ant directly. That is a real customer and Ant is clearly building for them — the company has said the family's models are intended for local institutional deployment. For everyone else, the honest summary is that Gemini 3.8 Live is the model and LingSante-Live is the demonstration of what a clinic built around a live model looks like.
