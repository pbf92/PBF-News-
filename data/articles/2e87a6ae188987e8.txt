TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino Delivers Farewell Message to USMNT in Video Ahead of Manchester United Move
OUTLET: Bleacher Report

Mauricio Pochettino expressed gratitude for his tenure coaching the United States men's national team as he prepares to join Manchester United.
"Thank you so much from the bottom of my heart for all the fans, for all the people that was supporting us in the last two years," Pochettino said in a video shared Tuesday by the USMNT. "Special, great memories of the World Cup and everything that we lived together. Always in my heart, always one of you. All the best and I wish everything good for the future."
His contract with the club lasts until the summer of 2029, according to David Ornstein and Adam Crafton of The Athletic.
Pochettino will continue coaching the USMNT during the squad's Concacaf Nations League quarterfinal match against Haiti on Tuesday before he's set to join Manchester United for a clash with Premier League rival Liverpool on Sunday.
The 54-year-old was hired as coach of the U.S. national team in Sept. 2024. The team posted a 22-12-2 record after Pochettino was brought in.
During the 2026 World Cup, the USMNT won two of its three group stage games before defeating Bosnia and Herzegovina 2-0 in the round of 32.
While the United States was ultimately eliminated from the tournament with a 4-1 loss to Belgium in the round of 16, the team was still able to earn its first knockout round victory since 2002.
Tuesday's matchup between the USMNT and Haiti begins at 7 p.m. ET.
