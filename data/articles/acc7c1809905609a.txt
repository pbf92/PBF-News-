Mauricio Pochettino’s secret Tottenham contract clause as he joins Man Utd
Mauricio Pochettino has been appointed Manchester United manager and started work on Wednesday ahead of his Premier League return but details of a Tottenham clause have emerged
Tottenham were one of three clubs Mauricio Pochettino included in an exit clause in his United States contract, according to reports. The Argentine coach oversaw his final match in charge of the US against Haiti before officially starting work at Manchester United on Wednesday.
The ex-Spurs boss' love for the Lilywhite has emerged once again, however, with a secret clause in his contract allowing him to depart for Tottenham, United or Real Madrid, per the Telegraph. Pochettino is said to have insisted on the clause before signing a new four-year contract in August.
Scarcely three months later, Pochettino has landed himself back in club football, looking to turn around the Red Devils fortunes. It is another Premier League adventure that might land like a dagger in the heart of Spurs fans following his fantastic five-year spell in North London.

Pochettino has repeatedly admitted his interest in a return to Spurs, who he formed into a Champions League constant and title contender.
"When I left the club I always remember one interview I said I would like one day to come back to Tottenham," the 54-year-old told Sky Sports last year.
"I am in the USA, so I am not going to talk about that now - but what I said then I still, after six years or five years, feel in my heart. Yes, I would like one day to come back."
Asked if he would have still been interested in Spurs if they'd have been relegated last season, Pochettino replied: “Yes, of course. Tottenham? Yes, of course.
"It’s the only club that always I am open to help. Whatever division. Another club, no. But Tottenham, yes.”
Speaking in September, Pochettino revealed he intended to return to club football and the Premier League at the end of his USA contract in 2030.
"I have the ambition," he said. "My dream now is to be in the USA for four years, but after that, why not? We are still young, and I think coming back is always my goal; to win the Premier League, to be in the Premier League and to build a project with the possibility of winning.
"I love the football here. I think to have the chance again is one of my wishes and I hope that happens."
But having last managed Chelsea in the Premier League for just a single season, Pochettino has found another route back into the division before Spurs came calling. Whether he can fulfil his ambitions with Manchester United remains to be seen.
Pochettino's first challenge will be to lift United from the relegation zone. They currently sit on eight points from 10 matches in 18th position, a single point ahead of bottom-place Crystal Palace.
