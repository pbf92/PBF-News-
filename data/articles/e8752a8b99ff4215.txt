Their most recent league outing brought no relief. Birmingham fell 1-0 to Aston Villa on November 15, a result that deepened their position at the foot of the table.
Manchester United arrive in far better shape. Eva Olid's side sit fourth in the WSL and have won three of their last five matches, including a 2-0 league win over Crystal Palace and a 2-1 WSL Cup victory against Everton.
United also claimed a 1-0 win over Aston Villa in the WSL earlier this month, a result that underlines their form heading into this fixture.
The head-to-head record strongly favours the visitors, and United's recent consistency makes them clear favourites on paper.
