TITLE: Ice Cream Simulator: Why We Gave Our Customers Infinite Patience
OUTLET: Xbox Wire

In Ice Cream Simulator, you can leave a customer waiting, go away for an hour, and come back to find them still standing there, ready to order.
They have nowhere more important to be. They have chosen ice cream.
We respect that.
Their infinite patience is one of our most deliberate design decisions. We wanted to capture the pleasure of running your own ice cream shop while giving you time to enjoy it. Real life already supplies plenty of deadlines. We didn’t feel the need to put another one on a cone.
It Started with a Scoop
Our inspiration was simple: we love ice cream.
We also hadn’t seen many games explore running an ice cream shop in the way we imagined. We wanted to create something cozy and hands-on, where you could turn your love of dessert into a business of your own.
The design challenge was giving that simple idea enough depth to keep you interested, while protecting the relaxed atmosphere that made us want to build it.
Hold the Stress
We built the shop around a daily rhythm. During the day, you serve ice cream with different toppings and sauces, along with milkshakes. At night, you prepare for the days ahead.
That rhythm gives you both sides of the experience: the satisfaction of serving an order and the preparation that makes tomorrow’s orders possible.
But serving customers can easily become a game about speed. We wanted you to settle into the routine, so we gave customers unlimited patience. Take your time with an order. Step away if you need to. Your customer will remain impressively committed to dessert.
For us, this was a way to put the emphasis on decisions about the business. You have time to think about what you are building, which leads to the question at the heart of the game: what kind of shop do you want to run?
Three Customers, Three Priorities
We designed three customer groups to give that question a meaningful answer.
Elite customers want the best ice cream and don’t care about the price. They are here for quality, and your ingredient budget is about to hear about it.
Office customers look for a balance between price and quality. They appreciate good ice cream, but they would also appreciate the numbers making sense.
Casual customers care most about affordability. Premium ingredients are not the selling point for them. A reasonably priced ice cream is.
Those differences are why we wanted you to choose between cheaper and more premium ingredients. Keeping costs low can support a shop aimed at casual customers. Spending more on quality can support one designed to attract elite customers.
We wanted both approaches to have a purpose. Buying the most expensive ingredients should be a decision that fits your business, rather than an automatic answer to every problem.
Your Shop, Your Style
Once you start thinking about your audience, the walls become surprisingly relevant.
Our attractiveness attribute connects the appearance of your shop with the customers it attracts. Renovating the floors, walls, and ceiling becomes part of your business strategy. This means staring at flooring options counts as a business meeting. You’re the owner. You make the rules.
With renovation choices ranging up to premium options, you can shape the surroundings alongside the menu. We wanted decorating to feel connected to the shop you are building and the people you hope will visit.
Seating adds another small but meaningful interaction: customers can sit, enjoy their ice cream, and leave a tip. Seeing someone spend time in a space you have put together helps make it feel like a little shop with a life of its own.
A Change in the Weather
We wanted variety to come from the world around the shop, too.
There are four weather conditions, changing every two in-game days. Each affects the visuals and the number of customers visiting, bringing a different atmosphere and pace to familiar surroundings. Apparently, our customers can wait forever, but they still have opinions about the weather.
These choices all grew from the same goal: creating a cozy business that gives you room to make it your own. Your ingredients, prices, and surroundings help define the shop you want to run. Our love of ice cream started the project. The interesting part was turning that love into decisions players could enjoy making for themselves.
We can’t wait to see what kind of shop you create in Ice Cream Simulator. Whether you’re serving affordable scoops or building a premium ice cream destination, we hope you enjoy making it your own.
See you behind the counter. Take your time—we’ve already told the customers.
