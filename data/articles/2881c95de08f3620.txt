TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino makes Man United demand after Sporting CP draw
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United Draw at Sporting Leaves Mauricio Pochettino Demanding More Focus
Mauricio Pochettino does not need long to identify the problem. Manchester United can play well enough in spells, press with intent and show a clearer structure, then throw it away with needless mistakes. That was the story again in Lisbon, where a 2-2 draw against Sporting extended the messy start to his time at Old Trafford.
There were signs of improvement, that much is fair. United looked sharper, more aggressive out of possession and more coherent in the second half. But football punishes carelessness, and United were careless. They conceded two first-half penalties, one of them rescued by a save, then handed Sporting a late equaliser when Senne Lammens spilled the ball. Youri Tielemans then made things worse in stoppage time with two bookings and a red card. It was avoidable, all of it.

Pochettino frustrated by Manchester United errors
The manager was direct afterwards. Asked what United must do to stop these mistakes, he said: "With this type of situation, in different clubs, we need to be better. We need to concentrate more, focus more, try to recover better, all the efforts." That is not tactical theory, it is basic competence. At this level, if your concentration goes, points go with it.
Sporting draw shows progress and familiar flaws
Even so, Pochettino saw enough to take into the next game. "We start to fix. With a performance like today it's the best medicine to prepare the next game.
"We showed the way we want to play. We've only been working together one week."
That is the balance here. United were better, but better is not enough if you keep sabotaging yourself. The pressing improved, the energy improved, the shape looked more deliberate. The sloppiness remained.
Brentford test comes quickly for United
Now attention turns to Brentford, with little time for real coaching in between. Pochettino made that clear too: "The priority is next one. We need to believe more and more and more. Second half we recovered a lot of balls in the opposition half. That's the way we want to play.
"We need to go to Old Trafford and show the commitment and play the way we want to play today."
For Manchester United, the message is simple. The ideas may be taking shape, but focus has to follow. Without it, progress means very little.
