TITLE: 'Don't treat AI as a trusted oracle' says YouVersion founder
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

The founder of the YouVersion Bible app has expressed concern over the accuracy of artificial intelligence (AI) when it comes to spiritual guidance.
Bobby Gruenewald told Christianity Today that people believe AI is smarter than humans and regard it as a trusted “oracle”. But he said people “lose touch with the fact that it could be greatly wrong, or it might be right, or it might be somewhere in between those two”.
He said he and his YouVision colleagues had abandoned plans to develop a prototype of the Bible app which incorporated a chatbot, because of concerns that it would be regarded as a “spiritual authority” with users seeking its guidance rather than the word of God.
He said there was a particular concern over issues such as helping people struggling with suicidal thoughts in which people should be directed towards human rather than artificial support.
He added that in many cases YouVersion is “the app of last resort for some people in whatever circumstances, and sometimes very desperate situations, that they’re in” so it was important to get the theology right.
Gruenewald also expressed his fear of misquoting the Bible, because if “all the work and care that goes into translating the Bible correctly just erodes because the translations become modified in a subtle way over a generation, then I feel like we’ve failed in our responsibility to steward this text. So I feel like it’s a big responsibility for us to try to challenge that”.
Despite his concerns, Gruenewald said he was not “anti AI”, but keen to encourage people to “make sure they understand what the tool is and what the right job is for the tool”.
YouVersion has been downloaded more than a million times.
