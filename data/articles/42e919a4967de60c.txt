TITLE: AppLovin vs. Reddit: Which Technology Stock Is a Better Buy for 2027?
OUTLET: The Globe and Mail

Key Points
- AppLovin maintains a highly profitable ad-tech ecosystem with a net margin of approximately 66%.
- Reddit leverages massive user engagement and AI content licensing to drive revenue growth of 62%.
- Which of these high-growth technology platforms is the better addition to your portfolio for 2027?
The digital advertising landscape is shifting as data-driven platforms fight for marketing budgets. Choosing between AppLovin(NASDAQ:APP) and Reddit(NYSE:RDDT) requires weighing a seasoned software powerhouse against a rapidly growing social platform.
AppLovin specializes in ad-tech software that helps developers grow their apps, while Reddit operates a massive network of user-generated communities. Both companies are successfully capitalizing on specialized data to attract advertisers, but they operate with different business models and cost structures.
The case for AppLovin
AppLovin provides a software-based marketing platform and advertising solutions for consumer brands and gaming apps. The company notes that some of its customers are also competitors, such as Meta Platforms, Alphabet, Amazon(NASDAQ:AMZN), and Unity Software(NYSE:U). Following the June 2025 divestiture of its Apps business, the company focuses entirely on its high-margin software platform.
In the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached nearly $7.6 billion, representing growth of approximately 46.5% compared to the prior period. Net income was roughly $5.0 billion during this time frame, supported by a strong net margin of 65.9%. This performance highlights the company's ability to scale its ad-tech infrastructure efficiently while maintaining significant profitability.
As of its December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was 1.7x, which compares total debt to shareholder equity. The current ratio was 3.3x, a measure of the ability to pay short-term bills using current assets. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow, which is cash from operations minus capital expenditures, was nearly $3.9 billion.
The case for Reddit
Reddit is establishing itself as a unique player among communication stocks by leveraging its vast array of user-generated communities. The company generates roughly 94% of its revenue from third-party advertisers, though it is expanding into content licensing for AI model training. While it serves a global audience, Reddit faces some customer concentration, with its top ten advertisers accounting for approximately 21% of total revenue.
In the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached nearly $3.1 billion, fueled by growth of approximately 62.2%. Net income was close to $991.7 million, marking a significant transition to profitability compared to previous years. The platform continues to focus on increasing user engagement and expanding its data-licensing partnerships to diversify its income streams.
As of its December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was 0.0x, while the current ratio was 11.6x, measuring the company's ability to cover short-term debts with current assets. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow was close to $684.2 million. Note that stock-based compensation represented roughly 49.7% of operating cash flow, which inflates reported cash generation since SBC is a non-cash expense added back in the cash flow statement.
Risk profile comparison
AppLovin faces significant risks related to its reliance on third-party platforms like Apple and Alphabet, which can alter access to user data through privacy changes. The company also deals with intense competition from large technology firms, including Amazon and Unity Software, in the mobile app ecosystem. Furthermore, revenue is heavily concentrated in the gaming sector, leaving the business vulnerable to shifts in that specific market.
Reddit relies heavily on advertising revenue, making the company vulnerable to volatility in marketing budgets and user engagement levels. The platform depends on volunteer moderators to maintain its communities and relies on cloud service providers like Amazon and Alphabet for its core infrastructure. Changes in search engine algorithms or a decline in user growth could significantly impact its site traffic and overall business results.
Valuation comparison
Reddit appears to trade at a more attractive valuation based on its Forward P/E and P/S ratio, which measures market capitalization against sales over the past twelve months.
Valuation metrics include sourcing from Financial Modeling Prep (FMP) and may differ from other data providers.
Which stock would I buy for 2027?
I'd go with AppLovin. It has built a leading, highly profitable ad business in mobile games, and it is just getting started in online shopping. Advertisers pay it for results like installs and purchases, so it grows when its customers do. Last quarter, sales jumped 55% year over year, and management guided for another strong quarter, exceeding analyst expectations.
The stock has been volatile this year as the company faced short-seller attacks and investor lawsuits it disputes. But the latest quarter beat the company's own targets by a wide margin, and the stock is still down for the year, which gives long-term investors a lower entry point.
To give Reddit its due, it is very profitable and its sales are still growing fast. But Google's AI search is sending it fewer visitors, its U.S. users fell last quarter, and it warned of more pressure ahead, which sent the stock plunging. AppLovin faces no such traffic problem, since its ads run inside other companies' mobile apps, and advertisers are spending more with it. With fast growth, big profits, and a whole new market opening up in online shopping, AppLovin is my pick for 2027.
