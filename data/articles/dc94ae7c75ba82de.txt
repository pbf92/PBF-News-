TITLE: Man United suffer major security breach as Pochettino’s first training session leaks online ahead of Liverpool clash
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United have suffered an embarrassing security breach after 12 minutes of footage showing Mauricio Pochettino's first training session as head coach appeared online.
The video was captured by a drone hovering over Man United's Carrington training ground on Thursday.
It shows the Argentine tactician working with his players and coaching staff ahead of Sunday's Premier League clash against Liverpool at Anfield.
Footage was uploaded to YouTube by an account called CC Overview, claiming the drone was operating legally under Civil Aviation Authority rules because the airspace above Carrington was unrestricted.
Per the Daily Mail, the Red Devils paid £10 million to secure the 54-year-old and his backroom staff from the United States Soccer Federation, where he had been managing the national team.
The leaked footage is unlikely to hand Liverpool a tactical advantage, although it will raise questions about security at the training complex.
Man United introduced signs banning drones near the facility six years ago because aerial footage could expose tactical preparations.
The video was reportedly recorded around 30 minutes into the session.
It shows Pochettino overseeing drills alongside his trusted assistants, including Jesus Perez, Miguel D'Agostino, Toni Jimenez, Sebastiano Pochettino and Silvia Tuya.
Pochettino arrived in Manchester on Wednesday after taking charge of his final game with the United States against Haiti in Cincinnati.
Man United arranged a private jet to bring him and members of his staff back to England.
The former Tottenham Hotspur and Chelsea manager also addressed the club's internal media team on Thursday, outlining his plans to improve a struggling squad.
"The culture is the most important," Pochettino said. "Create the habits, the principles, and I think that is what we want to create."
With Liverpool next on the agenda, Pochettino has little time to settle into his new surroundings before facing one of United's fiercest rivals.
The leaked footage is an unexpected glimpse into his early work at Carrington, but his immediate priority will be turning his methods into results on the pitch.
