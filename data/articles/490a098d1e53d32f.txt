5 Best Side Activities In GTA 6, Ranked
Contains spoilers for "GTA 6"
The main story might be the overall focus of "Grand Theft Auto 6," but it's the side content that makes the game truly shine. With the map of Leonida being so large, Rockstar Games had a brilliant opportunity to design a host of side activities that allow players to ignore the story almost entirely. You can spend dozens of hours in "GTA 6" just doing side activities and discovering secrets on the map.
While you'll have to engage with the story at some point, partaking in certain side content can be extremely beneficial to your playthrough. Whether it's because you can make some extra money, upgrade Jason or Lucia, or uncover more of the map, side activities are worth doing in "GTA 6." However, some of these activities are better than the others, and we're today to rank the five best side activities that you can do in "GTA 6."
5. Racing Time Trials
Vehicle races have always been a part of the fabric of "Grand Theft Auto," but "GTA 6" takes the racing activity to an entirely new level. Not only can you find street races to participate in, as well as an entire side questline around them, but there are also racing time trials in a variety of locations. You can participate in Drone Time Trials, which let you control a drone as you race through checkpoints, or race down Mount Kalaga in a mountain bike to beat the course time.
In every single region of the map, you'll find some sort of race or time trial to compete in, and all of them are extremely well-done and enjoyable. While you still might be partial to the classic street races, you can also check out the Port Gellhorn Raceway for some stock car racing, which is some of the best racing content in "GTA" history. You can also make some money from races, but nothing special.
4. Mud Club
We recently called Kelly County the best region in the entirety of Leonida, and a major reason for that is the Mud Club. You learn about this location from Wyman at the Starlet Motel in Kelly County, and it's found just south of Mount Kalaga and Manifest Canyon. This entire place is dedicated to one thing: getting dirty.
The Mud Club features an assortment of races, challenges, and time trials. It all takes place in dirt and mud, so you'll have to use vehicles suited for the environment. Once you're here, you can take part in the stunt jump challenge (which is something right out of "MX vs. ATV"), a hill climb time trial, drag strip race, or a human race where you and NPCs run through water and mud to see who gets to the finish line first. The entire Mud Club is an absolute blast to explore and play through, and there's even a side questline that comes up after you visit it for the first time. This isn't a place where you need money or where you'll make any, but it's "GTA 6" at its purest form of enjoyment.
3. Underwater Treasure Hunts
Now let's start getting into side activities that you can profit off of in "GTA 6." One of the first profitable side activities you can do is diving underwater searching for buried treasure, which you'll learn about through Kenton in the main story. While this seems tedious at first, the entire treasure hunting experience is unique and can make you a significant amount of money, especially during the early game.
When you visit an underwater treasure site, you can equip scuba gear and a metal detector. Then, dive underwater and use the detector to find roughly five or six pieces of treasure on the ocean floor. Once you're done, return to Kenton at his shop and collect your reward. You won't earn extreme amounts of money, but it's certainly enough to warrant doing the jobs. There's also something cool about the underwater parts of "GTA 6," as there's nothing else like them in the game.
2. Vehicle Contracts
Far and away one of the most best money-making side activities in "GTA 6" is selling vehicles to certain NPCs in Leonida. Specifically, there are NPCs named Moses and Randy, both of whom you'll meet through the main story. These characters will give you locations of vehicles that they want stolen and brought back to them in exchange for cold, hard cash.
Not only can you make tens of thousands of dollars per vehicle you bring in, but this is also one of the only chances you get to drive some of Leonida's most luxurious cars. From Pegassis to Grottis to Kamachos, vehicle contracts require you to steal some truly incredible vehicles. The money you receive for these contracts is more important, but how can you not have fun driving supercars around the map in "GTA 6?"
1. Bank Robberies
And finally, the best side activity in "GTA 6" is, you guessed it, robbing banks. Once you've progressed far enough in the story, you'll be able to rob much larger banks by just using Jason, Lucia, and some tools you've picked up along the way. There's no need to plan a heist or get special gear, as you can be in and out of a large bank in two minutes if you do things correctly.
Bank robberies have become a staple of "Grand Theft Auto," and there's no limit to how many financial establishments you can rob in "GTA 6." This is also the best money-maker in the game, as your potential score from robbing banks is anywhere from $50K to $150K, depending on what you steal. This is also a great way to have Jason and Lucia interact with each other in side content, so it's a win-win across the board.
