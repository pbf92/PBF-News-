TITLE: Trump Says Wednesday’s Sharp Wall Street Selloff Briefly Worried Him
OUTLET: TradingView

Trump Says Wednesday’s Sharp Wall Street Selloff Briefly Worried Him
According to ChainCatcher, U.S. President Donald Trump said Wednesday’s sharp stock-market decline briefly worried him, though major indexes recovered most of their losses. He told reporters that seeing some substantial declines the previous day had concerned him, but stocks had risen again by the close.
The Nasdaq fell more than 4% at one point Wednesday before rebounding to finish slightly lower. Trump also said markets reacted positively to Jensen Huang resuming as Nvidia’s CEO, adding that the market felt good about it.
