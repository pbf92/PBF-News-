TITLE: Man taken to hospital after being hit by Bee Network bus in Little Hulton
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Man taken to hospital after being hit by Bee Network bus in Little Hulton
People were seen trying to lift the bus off the victim, according to eye witness reports
A man has been taken to hospital after being hit by a Bee Network bus in Little Hulton this afternoon. Emergency services descended on Cleggs Lane at around 1pm today (November 23).
At least four police cars are pictured as well as an ambulance at the scene this afternoon. There are also reports of an air ambulance in attendance following the collision.
The Manchester Evening News understands that a man was hit by a bus on Cleggs Lane at some point are around 1pm this afternoon.
The pedestrian was taken to hospital as a precaution and no life-threatening or life-changing injuries have been reported as a result of the incident.
Eyewitness reports from the scene describe a man 'trapped under a bus' with other people trying to lift the bus off him 'to help him breath' while waiting for emergency services to arrive.
Transport for Greater Manchester confirmed that a Bee Network bus was involved in the incident.
