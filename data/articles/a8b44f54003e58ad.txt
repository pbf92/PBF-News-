TITLE: GTA 6 hair and beauty salons: Every hairstyle, nails and makeup option for Jason and Lucia
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

GTA 6 hair and beauty salons will give options for Jason and Lucia to change various aspects of their appearance.
Access to hair and beauty salons will allow both characters to customise their hairstyles, with Jason also having access to beard options while Lucia is able to change her makeup and nails. They combine nicely with the various tattoo options available in the game, giving you a high degree of customisation.
We’ll go over every hair and beauty salon available in Grand Theft Auto VI, as well as every option available to you as both Jason and Lucia.

How to change your hairstyle in GTA 6
To change your hairstyle in GTA 6, you need to visit one of 18 hair salons located across Leonida. Each one will offer a standard range of cuts, as well as some signature and creative ones that are only available at specific salons.
Advert
Salon types range from hair salons, to barbers, and unisex cuts, each one offering different types of services. Some also include nail tech and makeup services for Lucia.
You can also make a few changes to your hairstyle by interacting with a mirror in any of the player homes that you live in throughout the game’s story mode, though these are much more basic options compared to what you can get in a salon.
When you can access hair and beauty salons
You will be able to access hair and beauty salons as Jason in GTA VI after completing the game’s first main mission, Gone Fishing, where you gain access to the open world as Jason and can start exploring.
Although Chapter 1 is largely set in and around Key Lento and Mariana, all of the hair and beauty salons located around the map are available to you should you decide to venture outside of this territory.
As for Lucia, you won’t be able to access hair and beauty salons until you reach Chapter 2 and can access the open world free roam.
