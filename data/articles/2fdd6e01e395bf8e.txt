TITLE: Complete List of The Game Awards 2026 Nominees Revealed, With GTA 6 Up For 8 Of Them
OUTLET: wolfsgamingblog.com

The nominees for The Game Awards 2026 have been revealed ahead of the show on December 10. And the obvious bit of news here is that GTA 6 is up for 8 awards, despite not being out yet. Hell, the reviews aren’t even live yet. But that’s because GTA 6 has been in the hands of the people who vote in The Game Awards for a little while now. It just looks and feels a bit odd for the rest of us.
GTA 6 is up for main Game of the Year category, alongside 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
With 8 nominations, GTA 6 is topping the list, with Control: Resonant and Resident Evil: Requiem tied for 2nd place with 6 each. Phantom Blade Zero is in 3rd with 5 nominations.
It’s also worth noting that for the first time in 12 years, there isn’t a PlayStation first-party game in the Game of the Year battle. In fact, with 3 wins, Sony is actually the publisher with the most Game of the Year awards from Geoff Keighley’s show.
