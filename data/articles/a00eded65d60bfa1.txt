TITLE: GTA 6 Players Figure Out A Way To Remove Its Framerate Cap, But 60FPS Ain’t Happening Anytime Soon
OUTLET: Kotaku

A new glitch on Xbox lets players play Grand Theft Auto 6 without a framerate cap. It’s a neat trick that, in some circumstances, does allow the game to run at nearly 60FPS. However, this odd glitch really shows how GTA 6 is unlikely to get an actual performance mode anytime soon.
Grand Theft Auto 6 targets 30FPS on PlayStation 5 and Xbox. This performance limitation upset some players, especially those hoping the game would hit higher numbers on PS5 Pro. And in some areas of the game, it struggles to maintain that level of performance due to the immense amount of simulation happening under the hood and all the incredible visual effects running at all times. Rockstar has also explained that current-gen hardware can’t support higher framerates. That hasn’t stopped fans from trying, and a new Xbox bug has some claiming a 60FPS mode is here. But it’s more complicated than that.
As explained in a new video from Digital Foundry, this Xbox glitch, which was discovered by Reddit user Meowwie64 and involves tweaking some refresh settings while booting up GTA 6 and a second game, is fun to play around with, but isn’t a hidden high-performance mode.
After extensive testing, DF determined that playing GTA 6 with this glitch in effect resulted in an average framerate of 36FPS. That is higher than normal, but it doesn’t equate to a smoother experience. There are moments when you can get the game running at nearly 60FPS, but to do so you have to go out in the middle of the ocean, away from all the simulation and action, and look at the sky. And even then, GTA 6 can’t hit a locked 60FPS using this newly discovered glitch.
It also seems like this bug causes some weird issues with gameplay. When the framerate goes up, the game appears to speed up alongside it. This is likely a result of the bug and not a sign that GTA 6′s action is tied to the framerate. The one positive, as noted by Digital Foundry, is that input lag is reduced somewhat when using this mode. But keep in mind that as the framerate goes up, so does that game speed issue, making it harder to control the action while driving or shooting.
Some online have tried to claim this is some secret 60FPS mode that Rockstar has hidden in the game for… some reason. Or that Rockstar lied when it told Kotaku these consoles, even the PS5 Pro, can’t handle a 40 or 60FPS mode. But the truth is that this is just a weird bug that might get patched in the near future by Xbox or Rockstar.
All this bug really shows is that Rockstar was telling the truth. These consoles are being pushed to their limits by Grand Theft Auto 6‘s massive size, advanced visuals, and detailed simulation. If you want 60FPS, you’ll have to wait for the PC port or the inevitable PS6 and Project Helix versions.
