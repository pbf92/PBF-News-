Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia chief — and center of the stock market
After 11 days, and a stock market meltdown, Huang has been reinstated as head of the chipmaker. But what does it mean for one person to be the center of the stock market?
Jensen Huang was reinstated as CEO of Nvidia on Monday, just two week after he was ousted following multiple extramarital affairs — including one with an alleged Chinese intelligence asset.
The news roiled the stock market. Nvidia stock tumbled 40% between initial reports of the Chinese spy plot in late October and last Friday. Last week, the Nasdaq and Dow saw their worst weekly decline in over six years.
The Trump administration pressed Nvidia’s board to reinstate Huang, assuring them that the White House did not believe Huang posed a national security risk.
Those efforts were successful. His reinstatement as CEO triggered a market rally; the Nasdaq closed Monday up 6.5%.
To understand why an individual executive is so critical to a company’s public image — and why the federal government got involved — “Marketplace” host Amy Scott spoke to “Marketplace Tech” host Meghan McCarty Carino, who has been following the Nvidia turmoil closely.
