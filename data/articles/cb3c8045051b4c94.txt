TITLE: Bruno Fernandes speaks out on Man Utd future, contract rejections, and Mauricio Pochettino
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Bruno Fernandes has confirmed that Manchester United have presented him with a new contract offer — but revealed that talks are currently on pause. Fernandes, 32, is entering the final six months of his existing deal and has reportedly rejected two proposals to remain at Old Trafford.
The United captain's future hangs in the balance following a turbulent start to the season and a managerial change. Mauricio Pochettino has taken over from Michael Carrick, whom Fernandes maintains he had no falling out with, despite being dropped from the starting lineup last month.
Pochettino's appointment has thrust the Portuguese midfielder's future back into the spotlight, as United may look to cash in during the January transfer window to avoid losing him for free next summer, according to The Mirror. Fernandes insists he would not obstruct the club's plans if they chose to move him on, while dismissing suggestions that he is demanding higher wages or an extended contract.
"No, there's no negotiations ongoing," Fernandes told Sky Sports. "But I've seen people saying that he's asked for more money, he's asked for more years.
"If I wanted more money, I wouldn't be here. I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United. I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester."
"Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here. The thing is, I've always showed how much I care about the club and how much I want to win at this club.
"And I always said, in the moment the club thinks it's time for me to move on, I will move on. I'm not going to be the player that I've seen many in this time at Manchester United.
"They're going to stay here just by staying, just finish my contract and then we will see. In the moment the club says, 'Bruno, it's time to part ways', I'm going to be the first one walking out the door.

"Yeah, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay. And as you said, there are no negotiations going on.
"We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side because I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games."
Fernandes insists his desire remains to claim both the Premier League and UEFA Champions League titles with United, yet acknowledges that their immediate objective is to secure a top-four finish. The midfielder is eager to work alongside Pochettino, who made an unsuccessful attempt to bring him to Tottenham Hotspur back in 2019.
"He's someone that likes to bring a very good energy to the team, someone very positive, very strong, strict, said it straight away that he won't allow us to not give him what he demands from the team, from each player," Fernandes, speaking ahead of Sunday's 4-0 loss to Liverpool, added.
"He wants the club to succeed so much, he wants to win games, and that's pretty clear and obvious by the way he and his staff speak and want us to improve."
