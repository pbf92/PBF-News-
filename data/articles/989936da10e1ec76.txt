TITLE: 'GTA 6': How to listen to the radio outside of a vehicle
OUTLET: Mashable

One of the most important aspects of any Grand Theft Auto game is the soundtrack, and for the first time in the series, GTA 6 doesn't require players to be in a car or near a radio to listen to it.
Rockstar Games went above and beyond licensing dozens of existing bangers across almost any genre you can imagine for GTA 6's in-game radio stations, and that's before you even get to the fantastic original score from Justice. However, one small limitation of previous games in the series was that you couldn't really enjoy all of that music while walking around.
Thankfully, Rockstar Games took a cue from how people listen to music in 2026. Here is how to listen to music in GTA 6 without driving a car.

How to listen to music outside of a car in GTA 6
In real life, most people have a pair of headphones or earbuds handy so they can listen to music on their phones. GTA 6 joins some other open-world action games like Watch Dogs 2 in allowing players to use virtual headphones to jam out to some tunes from a smart device while simply walking around the world. The best part is that you can do this from a very early point in the game, though it will require a little bit of in-game money.
Protagonist Jason Duval is perhaps the only guy in Leonida who doesn't own headphones of any kind at the start of GTA 6, so you'll have to go buy some. This can be done at any electronics store in Vice City and the surrounding areas, and you'll know a store sells headphones if the store's icon on the in-game map is a pair of headphones. Convenient!
Unless you really branch out right away and decide to explore the whole map before getting on with the story, the easiest way to get headphones at the start is to drive up north from Jason's neighborhood to the town of Hamlet. Some of the early main story missions will take you there, anyway. What you're looking for is a store called Zeke's Electronics on the north side of Hamlet.
Simply go in and you'll be able to buy either over-ear headphones or earbuds next to the cash register. Over-ear cans will run you about $200, while earbuds cost $119. I can't tell if there's a meaningful difference between the two aside from looks, so just go with whatever you like more.
Once you have your headphones, simply hold left on the D-pad to listen to the radio while outside of a car. I recommend the Dirty South Classics station, personally, but your mileage may vary.
