TITLE: SoftBank Credit Risk Hits Four-Year High Amid $64.6 Billion OpenAI Bet
OUTLET: TradingView

SoftBank Credit Risk Hits Four-Year High Amid $64.6 Billion OpenAI Bet
SoftBank Group (SFTBY, Financials) saw its credit risk rise to about four-year highs as investors assessed growing borrowing rates and the possible returns on its artificial intelligence projects.
The five-year credit default swap spread for the Japanese investment company rose to 534.6 basis points, the largest since October 2022, according to Reuters.
Meanwhile, the market-implied chance of default rose to 34.8%, indicating that investors in the credit markets are becoming more worried.
The disparities are rising as investors mull SoftBank's $64.6 billion bet on OpenAI and the funding needs of its broader artificial intelligence ambition.
SoftBank's borrowing costs soared to 461.9 billion yen, or about $2.9 billion, in the July-September quarter, 2.6 times the year-ago level.
During the same period, the company raised about 9 trillion yen.
S&P Global Ratings noted this month that key credit metrics for SoftBank are approaching the tolerance limitations attached to its current grade.
The company's loan-to-value ratio stood above 30%, creeping toward the 35% that may see the rating agency downgrade the company.
Higher financing costs could make it harder for SoftBank to fund further technological projects and preserve its credit profile.
Investors will be looking at the valuation of OpenAI, SoftBank's borrowing needs and possible credit-rating actions.
