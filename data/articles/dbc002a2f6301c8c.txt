Jeremy Gillis Announces Acquisition Of Securities Of Sendero Resources Corp.
(TheNewswire)
Toronto, Ontario – November 26, 2026 – TheNewswire – Jeremy Gillis (the “Acquiror”), Interim Chief Executive Officer of Sendero Resources Corp. SEND (“Sendero”), announces that 1302580 B.C. Ltd., a company controlled by the Acquiror (the “Joint Actor”), acquired 40,000 units (the “Units”) of Sendero pursuant to its non-brokered private placement completed on November 24, 2026, at a price of $0.53 per Unit for aggregate consideration of $21,200.
Each Unit consists of one common share of Sendero (a “Common Share”) and one common share purchase warrant (a “Warrant”). Each Warrant entitles the holder to acquire one additional Common Share at a price of $0.70 until November 24, 2029.
As a result of acquisitions made since the Acquiror's previous early warning report dated January 24, 2025, including purchases of Common Shares in the open market, grants of stock options and the acquisition of Units by the Joint Actor, the Acquiror is updating his early warning disclosure.
Immediately prior to the November 24, 2026 acquisition, the Acquiror beneficially owned or exercised control or direction over an aggregate of 1,978,676 Common Shares, 1,500,000 Warrants and 830,000 immediately exercisable stock options, representing approximately 7.30% of Sendero's issued and outstanding Common Shares on an undiluted basis and 14.64% on a partially diluted basis.
Following the acquisition, the Acquiror beneficially owns or exercises control or direction over an aggregate of 2,018,676 Common Shares, 1,540,000 Warrants and 830,000 immediately exercisable stock options, representing approximately 6.56% of Sendero's issued and outstanding Common Shares on an undiluted basis and 13.23% on a partially diluted basis. These holdings include securities held directly by the Acquiror and indirectly through 1302580 B.C. Ltd. and 11661728 Canada Inc.
The securities were acquired for investment purposes. The Acquiror may, depending on market conditions and other relevant circumstances, acquire additional securities of Sendero or dispose of securities held by him from time to time.
An updated early warning report will be filed under Sendero's profile on SEDAR+ at www.sedarplus.ca. A copy may also be obtained by contacting the individual identified below.
