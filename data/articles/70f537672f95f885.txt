TITLE: I'm playing GTA 6 like it's The Sims and I couldn't be happier with that
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Grand Theft Auto VI is out now in the wild, and some players have found themselves disappointed in the game's early chapters.
Although you have plenty of opportunities to steal cars, shoot at police, and rob grocery stores pretty much from the get-go, GTA VI's opening few hours where I get to chill in my coast-side pad, drink some beers and work out is probably the most fun I've had so far.
As the game gets review bombed on social media for this supposed "boring" filler content, I can honestly say I've been having a blast playing it like it's a very expensive version of The Sims.

Lucia is still in prison, I've got Brian and Cal pestering me to come see them, and the Kingfishers are trying to murder me at any opportunity they get. Despite the sheer stress of all this that Jason, and therefore me, is under, I've been able to fully renovate the house, work on my gym routine, and have enough time to go for a walk or a drive around Leonida Keys.
GTA 6 allows me to live out a peaceful day exploring Leonida and relaxing with a bunch of hobbies
My days are pretty simple. As Jason, I wake up and go to the kitchen to pour myself a cup of coffee. After watching a little TV and finishing the coffee, I put the mug in the sink and feed Dolores, Jason's pet snake that he keeps in the living room. I'm not much of a snake guy, but after feeding her for the past several days, we've come to form a strong bond with each other.
Following this, I take Jason out onto the balcony where he keeps a lot of his gym gear. I start my workout with some kettlebell exercises, followed by some deadlifts and some squats to increase my strength. Then I practice my swings at a punching bag I've set up downstairs, which boosts my cardio.
I round that off with a quick shower and then pick out my outfit for the day. Now the real fun can begin.
Going out on the boat off to the shores just outside the keys, I find a nice spot to go fishing and spend the rest of my morning make a couple of catches, which I can later sell to make my money. On some days, I trade fishing for birdwatching, where I take a trip up to Grassrivers, park up, and try to study some of the wildlife that's located in the area.
The afternoon is more "me time". I take Jason into the town and buy him a few clothes, pick up some food and drinks at the grocery store, and go for a little walk around, meeting and greeting with the various locals and tourists.
As the evening comes, I drive back home and relax with a beer in front of the couch, doomscrolling on my phone and answering any messages that are pinged my way.
Honestly, I'm having so much fun playing GTA VI as a highly expensive spiritual successor to The Sims that I'm more than happy to pace myself in the main story.
These life sim elements are something that previous Grand Theft Auto games did not feature, and it's only really since Red Dead Redemption 2 that Rockstar Games has started to emphasise the elements of its in-game worlds that make you feel like an inhabitant of that world.
Sorry, Lucia, but you're going to have to wait for me a little while longer while I'm out here living my best life. I'm sure there's an excellent 200 hours of story to come, and I'll get to it probably in the new year.
