TITLE: Roblox Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $4.6M
OUTLET: TradingView

Roblox Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $4.6M
Mark Reinstra, Roblox’s Chief Legal Officer and Corporate Secretary, sold 17,310 shares at a weighted average price of $47.11 per share for a total of $815,559. The sales covered tax withholding obligations under a mandatory sell-to-cover arrangement. After the transactions, he directly owned 364,244 shares and may be deemed to beneficially own additional shares through trusts.
Matthew D. Kaufman, Roblox’s Chief Safety Officer, sold 14,390 shares at a weighted average price of $47.10 per share for a total of $677,751. The sales covered statutory tax withholding obligations under a mandatory sell-to-cover arrangement. After the transactions, he directly owned 320,670 shares.
David Baszucki, a 10% owner and Roblox’s President and CEO, sold 50,724 shares at a weighted average price of $47.13 per share for a total of $2,390,412. The sales covered statutory tax withholding obligations under a mandatory sell-to-cover arrangement. After the transactions, he directly owned 748,922 shares and held 3,493,765 shares through The Freedom Revocable Trust.
Naveen K. Chopra, Roblox’s Chief Financial Officer, sold 16,901 shares at a weighted average price of $47.11 per share for a total of $796,262. The sales covered statutory tax withholding obligations under a mandatory sell-to-cover arrangement. After the transactions, he directly owned 346,348 shares.
