TITLE: GTA 6 devs say home decorating and crafting got cut—mini golf can stay
OUTLET: Digg

Home decorating and item crafting were among the features abandoned during GTA 6’s development, according to Dexerto’s November 25 report on a PC Games interview. Rockstar North development manager Rob Nelson and design producer Scott Mitchell also discussed cuts including crawling, tennis, and cinema and theater visits. There goes the interior-design side hustle. Source
Dexerto reports that tennis was planned as a returning activity from GTA 5, but its implementation didn’t meet Rockstar’s standards. Mini golf made it into the finished game. Source
Cinema and theater visits reportedly went for a different reason: Rockstar felt they weren’t interactive enough. Sitting down and watching something is rather central to the proposition, but that apparently wasn’t a strong enough pitch. Source
Dexerto says some of the discarded mechanics didn’t fit the game’s story or gameplay, but doesn’t specify which ones. It also doesn’t say how far decorating, crafting and crawling progressed before being abandoned. Source
