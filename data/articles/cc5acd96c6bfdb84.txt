Who makes the list of the Top 10 European Strikers of the Century? In this Euro Leagues special, John Bennett is joined by Julien Laurens, Guillem Balague and Rafa Honigstein to dissect the list, as voted for by users on the BBC Sport website. This is Part One, where the team count down from spots 10 to 6, discussing players’ achievements, accolades, and their career ups and downs. Part Two will be available on Friday morning at 6am, where we count down from spots 5 to 1 and discover who was voted the Best European Striker of the Century.
