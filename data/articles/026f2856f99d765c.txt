TITLE: Bob Hoskins, founder of OneHope dies at 90
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

Bob Hoskins, the founder of the global ministry OneHope, has died at the age of 90.
The evangelist who had a vision and plan to reach the world’s children and youth with the Bible passed away at his home in Florida on 14 November after a brief battle with cancer.
Born in Oklahoma, Bobby D. Hoskins travelled the US as a child evangelist. His unwavering belief in the power of God’s Word to transform lives led him to serve as a missionary across Africa, Asia, the Middle East, Europe, and South America. He later founded OneHope, extending his missionary calling across more than five decades of global ministry.
That lifelong commitment to sharing Scripture took shape through several pivotal moments. In 1980, he became the president of Life Publishers, the Assemblies of God’s international literature ministry, growing it into a multilingual publishing house that produced Bibles and resources in Spanish, Portuguese, and French. Through this work, he observed how Scripture and media could advance the Gospel.
In 1987, a powerful conviction from God set Hoskins on a new mission: to bring the Bible’s message of hope and love to every child and youth in the world. The following year, an extraordinary opportunity arose when El Salvador’s Minister of Education requested Bibles for the nation’s nearly one million schoolchildren. Hoskins recognized the need to present Scripture in a format children could easily understand and engage with, so he developed the Book of Hope, a magazine-style booklet designed to bring the Bible’s message to young readers. The first Book of Hope reached nearly a million children in El Salvador, marking the beginning of OneHope, then known as Book of Hope International. It soon grew into a global ministry.
Stephan Tchividjian, Chairman of the OneHope Board of Directors, said: “Bob Hoskins’s legacy is both profound and lasting. He fully embraced the Lord’s vision to reach every child in every generation with the message of Jesus through His Word, drawing from his deep dependence on and personal understanding of Scripture. As a selfless leader, Bob focused on serving the global Church, inviting others to join the mission, and consistently modelling faithfulness, courage, and joy. While we may never fully know the impact of his obedience, his example compels the OneHope community to carry this God-given vision forward until every child has the opportunity to hear about Jesus”.
Today, more than 2 billion children and youth around the world have received one of Hoskins’s Scripture-based children’s publications.
Hoskins also wrote many books chronicling his own missionary and ministry work.
