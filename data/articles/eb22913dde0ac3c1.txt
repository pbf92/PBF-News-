TITLE: Arteta sends message to Pochettino and reveals what new United coach told him
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Mauricio Pochettino has returned to the Premier League dugout after being appointed Manchester United manager during the international break. The Argentine is familiar with English football, having previously managed several clubs in the country, and will now have the opportunity to take charge of one of the biggest teams in the league.
Pochettino enjoyed a lengthy spell at Tottenham, sandwiched between managerial appointments at Southampton and Chelsea. His experience in England means he understands the demands of the Premier League and what is required to compete at the highest level.
The opportunity to manage Manchester United was therefore difficult to turn down, particularly given the club's ambition and resources. However, he faces a significant challenge as he attempts to improve a team that has struggled to maintain consistency over recent seasons.
United enjoyed a positive finish to last season but have made a disappointing start to the current campaign, leading to Michael Carrick's dismissal. Pochettino now has the responsibility of getting the club back on track and delivering the results expected by its supporters.
Arteta speaks to Pochettino after United appointment
As Arsenal prepare to face Newcastle United, Mikel Arteta was asked about Pochettino's appointment and whether he had spoken to his fellow manager.
The Arsenal boss confirmed that he had been in contact with the Argentine and revealed that his former Premier League rival was excited about the opportunity ahead of him.
Speaking via Football365, Arteta shared his message to the new Manchester United manager.
"I spoke to him. He seems delighted, really happy, really motivated what he has ahead of him. I wish him the best of luck."
Pochettino faces a major challenge at Old Trafford
Pochettino will be returning to a Premier League that has changed considerably since his previous spell in English football. Nevertheless, his experience and understanding of the competition could prove valuable as he begins his new role.
United will expect him to make an immediate impact, although rebuilding consistency and improving the team's performances will require considerable work.
The Argentine has an important opportunity to establish himself at Old Trafford, and the club will be hoping that the support available to him helps deliver the improvements they desperately need.
