'Beautiful' boy, 3, who died after police called to nursery in Middleton is named as inquest scheduled
A police investigation is underway following the 'beautiful' boy's death after police were called to Cosy Toes
The three-year-old boy who died after police were called to a nursery in Middleton has been named as Thiago Wall.
Thiago tragically died after being rushed to hospital from the Cosy Toes nursery on Mainway East. Emergency crews were called to the nursery on November 2.
Pictures from the scene showed a large response with three police vehicles, an ambulance and rapid response paramedic car in attendance. The air ambulance was also pictured landing nearby.

Sadly, Thiago died in hospital on November 5. His family are being supported by specialist officers. An inquest opening has been scheduled at Rochdale Coroners' Court for November 25.
The Cosy Toes nursery has been closed following his death. Greater Manchester Police confirmed an investigation has been launched.
Representatives for the nursery described Thiago as a 'wonderful and beautiful child'. A spokesperson for the nursery said: "We are all heartbroken to hear of the tragic news of the child’s passing.
"He was a wonderful and beautiful child, and our thoughts and prayers are all with his family at this terrible time. We will of course give our fullest possible assistance in every way possible to the enquiries that must rightly follow this tragic event."
Nursery shut for six weeks
Ofsted has suspended the nursery's registration in the wake of the incident, meaning the nursery will be closed for six weeks whilst enquiries are carried out. An Ofsted spokesperson said: "Our thoughts are with the family of the young boy and everyone affected by this deeply distressing incident.
"We have worked closely with the police, and we have suspended the nursery's registration. We will continue to support the police and other agencies in their enquiries. We are unable to comment further while the police investigation is ongoing."
A GMP spokesperson said no arrests have been made at this stage. "As the circumstances leading up to the boy’s death occurred in a care setting, and in line with usual procedure, a police investigation has been opened", the force spokesperson added.
Detective Chief Inspector Claire Brennan said: "This is an incredibly tragic development in a deeply upsetting incident and our thoughts remain with the boy's family. We are in the very early stages of our involvement and are carefully considering events and the evidence available to us.
