TITLE: GTA 6 Secretly Hides Stephen King’s Terrifying Horror Icon in Surprise Crossover
OUTLET: PlayStation LifeStyle

Is GTA 6 a horror game? Not at all. Did that stop it from crossing over with one of Stephen King‘s most horrifying characters? No.
Among the many things players can do in GTA 6, one Stephen King Easter egg seems pulled straight from one of his novels. It not only has the creep factor it deserves, but it can also almost trick the player into thinking the game has switched genres.
Players discover the Easter egg when they come across an elevator with a telltale red balloon tied to it. Once inside the lift, the player is greeted with more balloons. Lights flicker as the elevator begins to move, then the entire scene goes dark. When the lights come on, a clown wearing a mask similar to Pennywise’s attacks the player. However, the lights flicker again, and the apparition is gone, leaving Jason or Lucia to wonder if he/she had imagined the ordeal.
Pennywise’s GTA 6 appearance is only part of Rockstar’s latest surprises
Pennywise is just one of the Easter eggs that exist in the game world for players to discover. The setting is peppered with interactive and non-interactive references to several pieces of real-world media. With television stations you can flip through and apps on in-game cellphones dedicated to doomscrolling, some references to real-world media also made their way into the game.
The Skunk Ape, aka Bigfoot, is one of the game’s most legendary Easter eggs. It makes a return in GTA 6. Along with the cryptid, UFOs also return, and players can even witness an alien abduction. If players interact with the woman, they themselves wake up in the game three days later, rather confused.
The game includes Easter eggs to classic media, such as the Fast & Furious franchise, Seinfeld, and even more recent releases like Clair Obscur: Expedition 33. The game also includes parodies of properties like the X-Men, with fully realized, albeit shallow, storylines.
