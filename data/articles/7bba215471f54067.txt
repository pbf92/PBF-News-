TITLE: Alan Shearer Predicts Man Utd's New Premier League Finish Under Mauricio Pochettino
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

What is Mauricio Pochettino's best Man Utd starting XI?
Manchester United are set to face Liverpool at Anfield this weekend in their first game under new head coach Mauricio Pochettino.
The Argentine has replaced Michael Carrick in the Old Trafford hotseat after the United legend was sacked for a poor start to the season.
The Reds are currently in the relegation zone in the Premier League and Pochettino will be tasked with improving results and rising the team up the table.
After returning to the Champions League this year, United will be desperate to remain in European competition and finish in the qualification spots.
Whether or not Pochettino will be able to guide United back into those positions remains to be seen.
Shearer predicts United's European fate
United finished 15th a couple of seasons ago but were never actually in danger of relegation due to the performances of those below them in the table.
However, as Spurs shown last season, relegation is not far away from any team if you are underperforming as badly as United have been this season.
It would be a genuine concern if performances did not improve but Pochettino is expected to bring some extra life to Old Trafford and turn things around.
Alan Shearer does not believe United will be in danger of finishing at the bottom of the table and believes a top-half finish would be a 'success' for the Reds.
However, the Premier League's all-time top scorer does not see United finishing in the European spots this term.
"Success will depend on Pochettino seeing what he needs to get into the squad and what he needs to get out. He will assess the squad this season, and if he can get them into the top half, that would be a success," Shearer told Betfair.
"They are too good to be in a relegation battle, and I do expect them to climb away from danger. I wouldn't put a lot of money on that, though. I expect Man Utd to climb to mid-table, maybe just above. They've got too much ability to be in serious danger of going down."
What can Pochettino do to fix United in the short term?
Confidence is everything in football. We saw how United's fortunes turned under Carrick last term, partly because players were playing with confidence.
That is Pochettino's first priority at United. He will get players running but it is crucial that they are platformed to play to their best abilities.
While United are in the relegation zone, they have quality within the squad. The defence holds major question marks but the midfield and frontline can cause damage to opposition teams.
The Reds would be disappointed with a midtable finish in the Premier League after finishing third last term. The January window will perhaps suggest how strongly those in charge want to push for European football again.
