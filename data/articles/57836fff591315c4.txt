TITLE: EFL Trophy holders Luton face Palace U21s in last 32
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
Holders Luton Town will host Crystal Palace Under-21s in the last 32 of the Jersey Mike's Trophy.
The Hatters, who beat Stockport County 3-1 in last season's EFL Trophy final at Wembley, won their group in the opening stages of the competition, coming through their three matches unbeaten.
Three-time winners Peterborough United have been drawn away at Plymouth Argyle with Posh hoping to win the trophy for a record fourth time.
Wycombe Wanderers have been handed a potentially attractive tie at home to Arsenal's U21s while Leicester City visit Barnet.
In the northern section, last year's runners-up Stockport will face League Two strugglers Port Vale while Wigan Athletic and Doncaster Rovers, who have both won the competition twice will meet at The Brick Community Stadium.
Managerless Blackpool, who have also won the trophy on two previous occasions have been drawn away at Notts County.
