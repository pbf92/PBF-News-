TITLE: GTA 6’s 7 Game Awards Nominations Have Fans Making the Same Joke
OUTLET: PlayStation LifeStyle

Grand Theft Auto VI has officially made history before even hitting store shelves. It has earned seven nominations at The Game Awards 2026, including a spot in the coveted Game of the Year category. However, the recognition has prompted fans to make the same joke that’s been circulating for years: GTA 6 could win awards before most players even get a chance to play it.
According to Kotaku, the upcoming Rockstar Games release received nominations for Game of the Year, Best Game Direction, Best Action/Adventure Game, Best Narrative, Best Audio Design, Best Score and Music, and Best Art Direction. Manni Perez also earned a Best Performance nomination for her work in the game.
The nominations dropped just two days ahead of GTA 6’s scheduled November 19, 2026, release date, prompting swift reactions from the gaming community.
@Axialmatt commented, “We really got GTA 6 as Game of the Year before GTA 6.”
@grandtheftfishy wrote, “We really got GTA 6 GOTY nomination before GTA 6.”
@Halcyon_Alkimos added, “We really got GTA 6 nominations before GTA 6.”
That timing has made its awards recognition a talking point, with the game potentially earning major honors before its public launch. The Game Awards 2026 ceremony is scheduled for December 10, with the winners set to be announced during Geoff Keighley’s annual show.
What makes GTA 6 one of the most anticipated games in history
The excitement surrounding GTA 6 comes after more than 13 years without a new mainline entry in the franchise. Grand Theft Auto 5 has sold more than 230 million copies, while the series as a whole has surpassed 470 million units sold since 1997.
The scale of that success has raised expectations for the next installment to unprecedented levels. Circana analyst Mat Piscatella told CBC News that 29% of U.S. gamers who own a PC or console intend to purchase GTA 6. He described the level of purchase interest as unusually high.
The game also promises a new setting and two playable protagonists, Jason and Lucia, whose relationship and criminal ambitions unfold in Leonida, Rockstar’s fictional version of Florida. However, the studio has kept many details about the story and gameplay under wraps.
