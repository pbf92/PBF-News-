TITLE: GTA 6's dynamic, persistent weather exposed in new first look from the Weather Channel
OUTLET: TweakTown

GTA 6's marketing team strikes again as The Weather Channel appropriately shows off the game's tropical storms, sea fog, and murky swamps.

Rockstar's new crime caper is everywhere you look these days--even The Weather Channel is getting in on the fun, debuting new looks at GTA 6's robust climate systems ahead of the big Thursday launch. There was even a quote from Rockstar's VP of art Owen Shepard about GTA 6's weather:
"Grand Theft Auto VI takes place in Leonida - a fictional world inspired by modern-day Florida, a landscape with one of the most unpredictable weather systems in the US. To match the fidelity of all aspects of what is the deepest, most immersive take on the Grand Theft Auto series yet, we had to completely reimagine our in-game weather systems to feel more localized, persistent, and above all dynamic. Weather can range with everything from clear blue skies to towering vertiginous cumulonimbus clouds, grounding players in a world through weather we hope Florida locals - and new visitors to Leonida - can appreciate."
GTA 6's weather systems are among the most interesting things about the game, and the locale itself; Florida is fraught with turbulent weather ranging from hurricanes to even snow. Despite Rockstar's massive marketing push, specifics on the weather still remain unclear, which is by design, as Rockstar doesn't want to spoil everything.
A handful of new screenshots show various weather effects across Leonida's multi-faceted biomes, ranging from cloaking fog clouds on the sea coast to the misty fields of Gambit Bay.
