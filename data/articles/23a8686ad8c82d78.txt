TITLE: 'Just play Maguire!' - Pochettino 'didn't help his cause'
OUTLET: AOL.com

Why did Mauricio Pochettino drop Harry Maguire for Matthijs de Ligt in his first competitive match in charge?
That's the question former Premier League players Chris Sutton and Conor Coady have been debating on BBC Radio 5 Live's Monday Night Club after Liverpool thrashed Manchester United 4-0 at Anfield.
"Maybe Pochettino wanted to rest Maguire for the midweek Champions League game, but I think that is highly unlikely," said Sutton. "In fairness to him, who do you play? That's the issue - but it did seem a bit of a stretch to put De Ligt in.
"I just wonder in all of this how Maguire would've felt. He must be angry about the decision. De Ligt could barely move and he looked like RoboCop just before Leny Yoro received his red card.
"The sending off actually gave Manchester United a bit of a way out of this performance. It is hard to really lay the blame on Pochettino, but my feeling is they could well have been thrashed 11v11.

"Manchester United are in a mess."
Coady added: "It couldn't have started any worse for Pochettino, could it? The one thing he didn't need as a new manager going into a struggling club was that. It was the worst possible start you could imagine.
"I don't think he helped himself by playing De Ligt, who has played one game in the past year, though. That didn't help his cause.
"There is this thing at Manchester United where managers come in and want to be the one who makes a change - but just play Maguire! No matter what you think of him, whether you think he isn't ready or he isn't right, he has still played a lot of minutes so you have to play him in a game as significant as Liverpool away."
