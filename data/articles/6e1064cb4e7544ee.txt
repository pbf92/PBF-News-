TITLE: Mauricico Pochettino tells Pep Guardiola to take US coaching job
OUTLET: The Telegraph

Mauricio Pochettino has urged Pep Guardiola to succeed him as head coach of the United States.
Pochettino will take charge of his final game with the US, against Haiti on Tuesday at the TQL Stadium in Cincinnati, before flying to England to begin work as Manchester United’s new head coach.
Guardiola is thought to be US Soccer’s dream target to replace Pochettino and the Argentine was clear about what he would advise the former Manchester City and Barcelona manager to do if offered the post.
“Take it,” Pochettino told reporters in Cincinnati. “If someone asked, like Pep… take it. Because it’s a perfect moment to arrive at the federation. Everything is on the right path, in the right direction. Of course, take it. Of course, you are going to find really good players, good people – the players can fit his style.”
Guardiola is taking time out after leaving City at the end of last season and Telegraph Sport reported on Sunday that the Catalan was expected to resist any attempts by the US to hire him.
