TITLE: Prediction: This Will Be Anthropic’s Stock Price by the End of 2026
OUTLET: The Motley Fool

Anthropic’s (ANT +10.98%) first two days as a publicly traded company were already days for the history books. It started off trading at about $110 per share before closing at $97. That's still a double-digit percentage gain from its list price, so it was a great day. Then it was caught up in a rout of AI stocks and, on Nov. 24, fell below its IPO price.
With a tumultuous start, Anthropic is an intriguing stock, but is it a buy? Here's where I think Anthropic could end the year priced, and investors may want to pay attention to get maximum returns.
Image source: Getty Images.
Anthropic is the culmination of AI investing
There are multiple ways to engage in the AI investing trend. You could invest in a hardware supplier like Nvidia (NVDA +0.80%) or Broadcom (AVGO +3.82%), or in a utility company benefiting from increased electricity usage. There are also construction companies and AI hyperscalers that are putting up hundreds of billions of dollars' worth of data center infrastructure.
However, all these styles of investments have one thing in common: None of them are 100% pure-play AI investments. These companies all have other business units that aren't exposed to AI. There are very few AI-focused stocks on the market, as the entry costs to get into the AI game are quite high.
However, all that changed when Anthropic went public. Anthropic is one of the two primary AI labs (the other being OpenAI). So, with it going public first, it is drawing a lot of investor interest. Anthropic is the poster child for AI investing, as all of its business comes from the usage of its Claude family of AI models. The more AI is used, the better Anthropic does. So, if the market is bullish on AI, Anthropic will do great. If it's bearish, the market will suffer.
Right now, it's pretty clear that the market has an overall bearish stance on the AI build-out. However, that's mostly the market making its opinion on massive infrastructure spending known. There isn't much that will stop the AI hyperscalers from investing in data centers, as they see a massive opportunity.
Anthropic is a bit different from most AI companies, as it doesn't own any computing assets. Instead, it chooses to rent them. So, the risk exposure from overbuilding is practically nothing, since it only rents computing power rather than owning it. That should help it be analyzed from a pure AI usage standpoint by the market, but if market sentiment is going one way, nothing will be able to save Anthropic from further downturn.
So, what's in store for the rest of 2026?
Anthropic's performance depends on AI recovery
The reality is that the AI sector is being harmed by one company: Nvidia. The resignation and return of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang have shaken the AI industry, but aside from Nvidia, there really aren't many domino effects. Anthropic will be just fine.
I think the market will come to this realization over the next few weeks, and Anthropic stock will be bought up in December so that portfolio managers can show their clients they bought the hot new AI stock. This could contribute to Anthropic rising in a Santa Claus rally – the name of the rally that occurs during December due to heavy buying.
As a result, I wouldn't be surprised if Anthropic's stock returned to the $ 110-per-share level it opened at on Nov. 19.
On the flip side, if the market says bearish on AI, there could be more downside from here. However, I think the odds of that are lower, so I'm confident in saying Anthropic has ample upside from here.
