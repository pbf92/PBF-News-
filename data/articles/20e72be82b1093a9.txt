OUTLET: TradingView

First Citizens Bancshares Director Sold Shares Worth Over $520K
Director Ellen R. Alemany sold 30,000 shares of Class C Preferred Stock on November 19, 2026, at a weighted average price of $17.34 per share, for a total of $520,200. She directly owns 2,930 shares of Class A Common and 300 Depositary Shares, and no longer directly owns Class C Preferred Stock. She indirectly owns 11,795 shares of Class A Common: 4,559 through the Family GST Exempt Trust, 4,660 through the Family Non-GST Exempt Trust and 2,576 through the Alemany September 2025 GRAT. The Alemany August 2024 GRAT no longer holds any shares.
