TITLE: 18th, injuries and five left-backs: the problems waiting for Pochettino
OUTLET: Dave.Sport

Mauricio Pochettino started work at Manchester United on Wednesday with four days to prepare for Liverpool at Anfield. He inherits a side in the relegation zone, without a league win in eight games and with three players expected to miss his first match.
Pochettino flew from Cincinnati to Manchester after the USA beat Haiti 2-0 on Tuesday to complete an 8-0 aggregate win in the Concacaf Nations League quarter-finals. Asked about Manchester City by former City player DaMarcus Beasley on TNT Sports US after the game, he replied: “You will see. Give me time.”

Pochettino Man Utd: 18th in the table
United have eight points from 10 games: one win, five draws and four defeats. They have not been this low after 10 matches of a top-flight season since 1986-87.
Michael Carrick’s final game left Pochettino with a point that had looked beyond United. Aston Villa led 4-2 going into stoppage time at Old Trafford before Joshua Zirkzee scored and Patrick Dorgu headed in a 96th-minute equaliser. It was United’s fifth draw of the league campaign.
Who could miss the Liverpool game?
Benjamin Šeško (shin), Carlos Baleba (hamstring) and Manuel Ugarte are expected to miss the trip. Ugarte is recovering from knee surgery after damaging ligaments while playing for Uruguay at the World Cup. Bryan Mbeumo started up front against Villa in Šeško’s absence.
Marcus Rashford (hip) and Luke Shaw (muscle) are targeting Sunday. Lisandro Martínez is available after serving his one-match suspension against Villa.
Dorgu had returned from a hamstring injury to make the bench against Villa. Denmark sent him back to United after he failed medical tests on international duty. Harry Maguire came off at half-time against Villa with a back spasm. Our United injury update before Liverpool has the latest on each player.
Five left-backs and three January priorities
United focused their summer recruitment on midfield, signing Youri Tielemans, Andrey Santos and Baleba. They did not recruit a left-back and have already used five players in the position. With Shaw and Dorgu both working towards a return, Pochettino’s first team sheet could depend on who gets through training this week.
Sky Sports News reports that United want a centre-back, a left-back and a forward in January. Everton’s Jarrad Branthwaite and Fulham’s Calvin Bassey are among the centre-backs under consideration. United could also revisit a move for Barcelona left-back Alejandro Balde if a deal suits all parties. Our January transfer update sets out the targets.
Barcelona sporting director Deco and his colleague Bojan Krkić said the club was not considering selling Balde when asked about his future, according to Barça Blaugranes.
Who has joined Pochettino at Carrington?
United confirmed on Wednesday that Jesús Pérez, Miguel D’Agostino, Toni Jiménez, analyst Alec Scott and Sebastiano Pochettino had joined the head coach. Pérez, Jiménez and Sebastiano arrived early to prepare for the handover while Pochettino finished his USA duties.
Jonny Evans, Steve Holland, Jonathan Woodgate, Travis Binnion and Craig Mawson left alongside Carrick. Our guide to Pochettino’s staff explains who will lead the coaching, goalkeeping and sports science work.
Anfield first
Liverpool are fourth on 18 points. They have won four and drawn one away from home, taking 13 points from a possible 15. At Anfield, one win, two draws and two defeats have brought five points from five games. They failed to score at home against Fulham, Manchester City and Arsenal.
