TITLE: Rockstar reports $3.79B in GTA 6 launch sales
OUTLET: The Rundown AI

Rockstar Games says Grand Theft Auto VI generated an estimated $3.79 billion in worldwide retail sales on its first day. The game arrived on November 19, 13 years after GTA V.
The company announced that figure at 2:20 p.m. EST on November 20, after The Rundown’s coverage earlier that day. Take-Two, Rockstar’s parent company, is counting on the release to help meet its fiscal 2027 forecast.
Inside Leonida
Rockstar released GTA VI on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Players switch between Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, an outlaw couple, across Leonida, Rockstar’s version of Florida. Vice City sits at its center, surrounded by beaches, swamps and small towns.
Rockstar’s launch announcement quotes Bloomberg’s assessment, “Grand Theft Auto VI Is a Masterpiece.” The company also reported a 99 Metacritic score in its later sales announcement.
Getting ready to play
The game’s size makes preloading useful for players who want to start at release. Network operator DE-CIX estimates that a roughly 210GB download takes more than nine hours on a 50 Mbps connection under ideal conditions.
Its Frankfurt exchange reached a traffic peak of 22.30 terabits per second on November 10, during the preload period. DE-CIX compared that with about 13 complete game downloads per second. The comparison describes total exchange traffic, with GTA VI’s share unclear.
Why it matters
Thirteen years after GTA V, Rockstar has another instant blockbuster. For Take-Two, the opportunity stretches from launch sales to the possibility of earning money from the game for years.
Take-Two reiterated guidance of $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion in fiscal 2027 net bookings on November 9. The forecast covers the whole company for the year ending March 31, 2027. Net bookings measure products and services sold, including digital sales and physical copies supplied into retail channels.
Retail sales and net bookings use different definitions. Investors will need Take-Two’s financial results to see how the launch translates into bookings and how much it contributes to that annual target. The retail total alone also says little about the game’s profit.
GTA V and GTA Online offer a concrete precedent for lasting sales. Rockstar reports nearly 235 million GTA V units sold-in, meaning copies supplied into sales channels. Take-Two also documented a GTA Online update on July 14, 2026, showing continued support for the older game.
Across Take-Two, recurrent consumer spending represented 81% of bookings in its fiscal second quarter. That spending fell 9% from a year earlier.
If GTA VI follows GTA Online’s path, updates could give players reasons to return and Take-Two years of spending beyond the first purchase. That would require ongoing support from Rockstar. The game’s online plans, the pace of updates and players’ willingness to keep returning will help determine whether that opportunity lasts.
