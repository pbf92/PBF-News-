TITLE: The opposition lowdown: Manchester United
OUTLET: Liverpool FC

New man in the dugout
United travel to Anfield with a new head coach in place following the November international break.
Mauricio Pochettino will take charge of his first game having replaced Michael Carrick.
Pochettino returns to the Premier League with a wealth of experience coaching in the division and across Europe and international football.
He departed his role with the USA national team to officially take up position at Old Trafford earlier this week.
Pochettino began his managerial career at Espanyol in Spain, before a move to England with Southampton and then Tottenham Hotspur.
He notably faced the Reds in the Champions League final in 2019 when Jürgen Klopp's charges clinched the trophy for a sixth time in the club's history by winning 2-0 in Madrid.
A move to Paris Saint-Germain followed - where he lifted the Ligue 1 title in 2021-22 - ahead of a spell at Chelsea back in England.
Switching to international football, he led the USA during the past summer as co-hosts of the World Cup.
Season so far
United sit 18th in the Premier League standings after the opening 10 games of the season.
They have won one, drawn five and lost four to begin the 2026-27 campaign. In their last fixture they played out a topsy-turvy 4-4 draw with Aston Villa, where they scored twice in added time at Old Trafford to secure a point.
In the Champions League, they have won two of their first four fixtures of the competition.
Key players
In the Premier League, Bruno Fernandes has been the Reds Devils' standout performer so far this season.
The midfielder has scored four goals and recorded two assists in his nine league appearances to date. Bryan Mbeumo has also netted on four occasions for the visitors.
Team news
Lisandro Martinez is available for United after serving a one-match suspension.
Patrick Dorgu and Harry Maguire have returned to training and could be in contention for involvement.
Carlos Baleba, Marcus Rashford, Benjamin Sesko, Luke Shaw and Manuel Ugarte have been sidelined recently with injuries, however.
Goalkeeper Karl Darlow was also absent for Wales' second game of the November international window due to an injury.
What they said
Pochettino
"We need to be ready to compete [on] Sunday. Now it's not time to think about rest, it's not time to think about sleep. It's about being ready and to prepare everything to be sure that we are going to compete in the best condition at Anfield."
Stats
- Pochettino is the first-ever United boss to see his first league game with the club come against Liverpool
- After winning this fixture last season, United are aiming for successive league wins at Anfield for the first time since January 2016 under Louis van Gaal
- The last 11 Premier League meetings between Liverpool and United have seen 44 goals scored, with both sides netting in four of the previous five encounters
