TITLE: Winklevoss Twins, Gemini Sued Over Sports Prediction Markets
OUTLET: Bloomberg Law News

Gemini Space Station Inc. and its billionaire brother cofounders drew a nationwide proposed class action on behalf of users who say they’ve lost money on its sports prediction markets — swelling the ranks of litigation over the industry that states, tribes, users, lawmakers, and others say operates like unlicensed sportsbooks.
New York-based Gemini, its entities, and Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss are disguising sports betting as financial trading in violation of state gambling laws, bettor Rayath Chowdhury told the US District Court for the Southern District of New York.
