TITLE: Fans complaining it's too hard to be a jerk in Grand Theft Auto VI
OUTLET: A.V. Club

Fans complaining it's too hard to be a jerk in Grand Theft Auto VI
Updated mechanics in the video game sequel apparently make getting away with it a bit less breezy.
Screenshot: IGN/YouTube
As Rockstar’s latest blockbuster entry in the Grand Theft Auto franchise continues to take in all the money, everywhere, the expected outcry over the game’s content has erupted from the usual sources. Fox News has broken glass on its “what is the world coming to?” video game hysteria, with commenters lamenting the game’s violence and sexual content, while adding in some Dr. Oz-style clichés about gamers living in their parents’ basements instead of doing their part for all-American productivity. Meanwhile, the right-wing bro-fringe has chimed in with their own predictable online yammer about how the game’s central romantic relationship is too even-handedly non-toxic, even petulantly review-bombing the game in their impotent foot-stamping against its perceived “wokeness.”
