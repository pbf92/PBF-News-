TITLE: Players have found a simple way to fix a weather bug in GTA 6 on the PlayStation 5 console.
OUTLET: GameGPU

PlayStation 5 users have discovered an effective fix for a common technical issue in Grand Theft Auto 6, which caused the weather in the game's open world to completely stop changing, with no rain or storms. Despite the release of Rockstar Games' first post-release patch, designed to improve weather variety, many players continued to encounter endless sunny weather for dozens of hours. It turns out that to properly apply the update's changes, it's necessary to force-clear the console's system cache through the Safe Mode menu, after which the weather cycle returns to normal.
The entire procedure takes about two minutes, doesn't affect installed games or save files, and is performed in several sequential steps. First, completely power off the PlayStation 5, then press and hold the power button on the console for about seven seconds until the second beep sounds to enter Safe Mode. After connecting the DualSense controller via USB, select the sixth menu item, "Clear cache and rebuild database," then click "Clear system software cache" and confirm the system reboot.
Players who have used this method report the immediate appearance of thunderstorms, clouds, and precipitation immediately after loading a save, even if they haven't seen a single storm in 50 or more hours of play. In some cases, users also recommend rebuilding the database in the same menu if simply clearing the cache doesn't produce an immediate effect. Experts note that the game's weather system remains dynamic, so after clearing the cache, weather conditions may change gradually as you explore the state of Leonidas.
