TITLE: Grand Theft Auto 6 Players Point Out Major Flaw with Jason and Lucia
OUTLET: OpenCritic

This article contains spoilers for GTA 6.GTA 6 players have noticed a big flaw with Jason and Lucia's relationship throughout the game. The concern focuses on a gameplay issue in GTA 6, rather than a narrative one, but it's worth raising nonetheless.
Jason and Lucia's narrative lives up to the promise of the "Bonnie and Clyde" storyline that has been promoted by Rockstar over the last few years. The GTA 6 reviews have largely praised Rockstar's depiction of the couple, their personalities throughout the game the perfect foil for one another. However, there is one area where gamers are hoping for improvements in the future.
Grand Theft Auto 6 players discover that Rockstar Games has put in a clever way to troll players who don't pay attention to what's in front of them.
A Reddit thread started by user kingoflint282 has raised the issue that Jason and Lucia's relationship in GTA 6 declines far too quickly when playing solo in free roam. They claim they spent about a day in-game apart before they were told their relationship level had declined, and most in the thread agreed that it was far too aggressive an approach from the game....
