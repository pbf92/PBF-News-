Travis Scott Has Another Nike x GTA 6 Clothing Capsule to Go Alongside His Air Force 1 Collab
Published
Quick Facts
- Travis Scott has another capsule of GTA 6 merch releasing
- This time, he's teaming with Nike to release apparel that matches his upcoming AF1 GTA 6 collab
- Expect a release on Nike SNKRS on November 21 and a drop on Scott's website on November 23

Everyone is trying to get in on the Grand Theft Auto 6 hype, with companies and brands across all industries making reference to the game's release on November 19. But a select few have the privilege of actually working with Rockstar Games on officially licensed gear for GTA 6, like Travis Scott. La Flame is not only appearing on the in-game soundtrack, creating original music for GTA, and headlining the GTA 6 Music Festival, but he’s also bringing his fashion prowess into the mix. Scott has plans for a jersey collab with the Miami Heat, Nike, and Rockstar, an apparel capsule with the three, and even a pack of Air Force 1 Lows with Nike and Rockstar. Adding to the massive Travis Scott x GTA 6 collection is an apparel pack to match the AF1s.
Travis Scott’s Nike x GTA 6 Clothing Collection
Unlike the Miami Heat clothing collab collection, this new collection of GTA apparel leans more into the criminal aspects of the Grand Theft Auto series. Highlighting this is the white “WANTED” T-Shirt, featuring “WANTED” in black text with pink trim and mini Rockstar, GTA 6, and Cactus Jack logos. There’s also a black six-star T-shirt, with the six wanted stars in pink on the chest, and a camo T-shirt with six stars in black.
Alongside the T-shirts are hoodies, with one featuring a large “VI” sprayed on the chest and a Rockstar logo on the back. Pink and white money bands, which are identical to the ones included on the Black and White colorways of Travis’ upcoming GTA 6 Air Force 1, are added to the hood and the right arm. Another hoodie features upside-down Nike Air logos with a six-star wanted level in matching grey.
Travis Scott’s Nike x GTA 6 Clothing Collection Release Info
According to the Cactus Jack Instagram account, Travis Scott’s GTA 6 Nike apparel collection will be released via Nike SNKRS on November 21, 2026 at 10 AM ET. A release via cactusjack.com will take place on November 23, with “additional exclusive styles” made available on that day.
