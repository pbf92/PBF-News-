OUTLET: NBC Sports

By the time you read these quotes, Mauricio Pochettino will probably already be in the air, heading to Manchester tonight, to take over United tomorrow, but not before coaching the USMNT to one last victory, 2-0 (8-0 aggregate) victory over Haiti in the CONCACAF Nations League.

Below is the latest Pochettino reaction, speaking on Tuesday after his final game in charge of Team USA, 26 months after taking the job and just three months after re-signing on a four year contract through the 2030 World Cup. Instead, Pochettino officially starts as Man United’s new boss on Wednesday.
What did Mauricio Pochettino say after final USMNT game before taking over Manchester United?
On becoming Manchester United manager: “We are excited. I think the new challenge is a big challenge, but at the same time it’s adrenaline you want to feel. We are going to have a different job, but I think it’s important that we have this experience [today with USMNT]. We need to bring our common sense for the way that we are going to approach things. We know the situation is not a situation that a club like Manchester United deserves to be. And for the fanbase, everything is amazing after we signed. In America, every 10 people that I cross in the street is a Manchester United fan. It’s amazing.”
On leaving the USMNT: “My feelings are, difficult to find my feelings. … I think it was our dream two years ago, to play in this [amazing] way. … I think the identity of the team, the way the play, they really believe that now we are playing how we want to play. That is my best present that I can receive from the players. … I’m going. So happy about that, but so sad because I leave a group of amazing players, amazing people, all the staff, the people here.”
