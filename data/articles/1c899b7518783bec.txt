Who Is Brian Heder In GTA 6? Jason's Connection Explained
"Grand Theft Auto VI" is not just the gaming event of the century thus far, it's perhaps the biggest shared cultural event of any kind in recent human history. It's been 13 years since the release of "GTA V," and Rockstar has finally delivered the much-anticipated follow-up. That means meeting a whole new assortment of characters across the sprawling map of Leonida and its various locations.
The main game itself features a big lineup of core characters who impact the story. Players primarily switch between Jason and Lucia in "GTA 6," as they're the protagonists of the game. When an easy score goes wrong, Jason and Lucia find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in the U.S., all in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida.
One of the characters at the center of all of this drama is a man by the name of Brian Heder. Voiced by Stephen Root, the beloved character actor was the first voice actor confirmed for "GTA 6." As for Brian himself? First and foremost, he's a drug runner, and the various quotes that were attributed to him ahead of the game's release include, "I hauled so much grass in that plane, I could make the state of Leonida levitate."
So, yeah, Heder means business. But how does he connect to the game's main narrative? Basically, it's important to note that he has a crucial connection to Jason, and it's something that, in turn, impacts Lucia.
Brian Heder is an old-school drug runner connected to Jason Duval
Jason Duval has a checkered past in "GTA 6" (to say the least), much like his lover and partner in crime, Lucia Caminos. That's more or less how Jason is connected to Brian Heder. Per the Rockstar website:
"Brian's a classic drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys. Still moving product through his boat yard with his third wife, Lori, Brian's been around long enough to let others do his dirty work...Brian's letting Jason live rent-free at one of his properties — so long as he helps with local shakedowns, and stops by for Lori's sangria once in a while."
In other words, Jason is indebted to Brian because he's, more or less, his landlord. Needless to say, then, the aforementioned score gone wrong doesn't sit well with Brian, an old-school drug boss who lets others "do his dirty work." But because he's old school, he's not afraid of getting his hands dirty, either.
Rockstar has two more telling quotes attributed to Brian as well, including "Nothing better than a Mudslide at sunset" and "Looks like a Leonida beach bum — moves like a great white shark." To be sure, you can glean a lot about his personality just from those lines alone.
"Grand Theft Auto VI" is now available on PlayStation and Xbox consoles. The PC release will come at a later date.
