Insider trades: Apple, Meta, Coca-Cola among notable names
Insider trades were seen across a slew of major companies this week, including at Big Tech firms like Apple and Meta. The following transactions took place between November 16 and November 20.
- Apple AAPL director Arthur Levinson sold 90,000 shares at about $352-$353 each for a total value of $31.77M. The transaction represented about 2.40% of his holdings, leaving him with 3.67M shares.
- Meta Platforms META Chief Financial Officer Susan Li sold 2,127 shares at $641.69 each for $1.36M. The sale accounted for about 8.68% of her indirect holdings, leaving her with 22,382 shares.
- Coca-Cola KO Executive Vice President Manuel Arroyo sold 57,581 shares at $99.40 each for $5.72M. The transaction represented about 36.65% of his direct holdings, leaving him with 99,514 shares.
- Merck MRK EVP and President of Merck Animal Health Richard DeLuca sold 58,185 shares at $148.01 each for around $8.61M. The sale accounted for about 33.74% of his holdings, leaving him with 114,291 shares.
- Duke Energy DUK EVP and Chief Power Grid Operations Officer Scott Batson sold 15,126 shares at $129.25 each for $1.96M. The transaction represented about 45.16% of his direct holdings, leaving him with 18,365 shares.
- Boeing BA director Akhil Johri purchased 3,000 shares at $166.69 each for $500,067, increasing his direct holdings to 3,150 shares.
- Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company TSM VP Lee Lee-Chung purchased 5,000 shares at $60.57 each for $302,850, increasing his holdings to 318,514 shares. Meanwhile, director Ursula Burns purchased 1,375 shares at $363.67 each for $500,039, increasing her holdings to 4,992 shares.
- DraftKings DKNG director Matthew Kalish purchased 55,000 shares at $18.22 each for $1M, increasing his holdings to 5.69M shares.
- 3D Systems DDD director Jim Kever purchased 400,000 shares at $1.43 each for $572,000, increasing his holdings to 1.16M shares.
- Corteva CTVA CEO Luther Kissam IV purchased 32,292 shares at $9.30 each for $300,312, increasing his holdings to 185,379 shares.
