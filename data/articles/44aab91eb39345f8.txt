TITLE: Anthropic IPO Moves Closer as Nasdaq Certification Arrives Ahead of November 19 Debut
OUTLET: Coinpaper

Key takeaways
- Nasdaq certifies Anthropic's proposed ANT stock listing
- Claude developer targets November 19 trading debut
- Nvidia and Google support massive AI offering
Anthropic's anticipated initial public offering has moved closer after Nasdaq submitted a formal listing certification to the Securities and Exchange Commission on November 18, marking another procedural milestone before the Claude developer's planned stock market debut.
The company is targeting November 19 for its first trading session under the ticker ANT, with a preliminary offering price of $70–$78 per share.
However, strong investor demand has raised expectations that the final price could exceed the indicated range.
Nasdaq Certification Brings Anthropic Closer to Trading
According to the November 18 SEC filing, Nasdaq submitted its formal certification concerning Anthropic's registration of securities for exchange listing.
The document follows Anthropic's November 17 amended prospectus, which confirms that its Class A common shares have been approved for listing on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under ANT.
The company plans to offer 825 million shares, including newly issued stock and shares sold by existing investors. At the preliminary $70–$78 price range, the offering would represent approximately $57.8 billion to $64.4 billion in gross share sales.
That would place the transaction among the largest public offerings ever, with Anthropic's targeted valuation approaching $1.4 trillion. The deal's scale reflects the broader IPO fundraising boom, even as technology stocks face renewed valuation pressure.
Importantly, Nasdaq certification does not establish the final offering price or guarantee that trading will begin on the scheduled date.
Nvidia and Google Commit Billions to Anthropic IPO
Anthropic's public offering is accompanied by a separate $26 billion private placement involving several major technology companies, including Nvidia, Google, Microsoft, AMD, Broadcom and Micron.
Nvidia and Google have each committed approximately $10 billion, making their combined investment a significant component of the financing package. These commitments have also attracted attention amid Nvidia's recent stock rally.
The arrangement highlights an increasingly interconnected AI industry, where infrastructure providers are also financing companies that purchase their computing products and services.
Anthropic's growth has been substantial, although its reliance on major technology partners presents additional considerations. Earlier financial disclosures showed that Amazon and Google's cloud platforms accounted for a significant share of its sales distribution.
These relationships could become increasingly important as investors assess whether Anthropic's revenue growth can justify its considerable infrastructure spending.
What Happens When Anthropic Stock Begins Trading?
Anthropic's Nasdaq debut could attract substantial institutional and retail activity, particularly given growing investor interest in artificial intelligence companies.
Asset managers have already started preparing specialized investment products, including a proposed 2X leveraged Anthropic ETF designed to amplify the stock's daily performance.
Potential index inclusion could eventually create another source of demand, although eligibility and timing will depend on index-provider requirements rather than the IPO alone.
Investors will also need to consider Anthropic's governance structure. Its prospectus gives a special founder-controlled share class 50.1% of aggregate voting power on most shareholder matters, allowing founders to retain considerable influence after the listing.
Meanwhile, the company's enormous AI infrastructure commitments remain central to questions about future profitability and valuation.
