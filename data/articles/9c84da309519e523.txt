Rockstar Games Justifies One of GTA 6’s Most Controversial Decisions and Explains Why It Removed a Feature Fans Loved
Scott Butchard, the studio’s senior director of design, said the feature “didn’t fit the story”
WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW:
- Rockstar Games removed the mission replay feature from GTA 6 to emphasize the importance of players’ decisions.
- The game introduces a criminal profile system that influences how the world reacts to the protagonists.
- The story and missions in Grand Theft Auto 6 can change considerably depending on players’ actions.
GTA 6 is one of the most important games in history, and many people claim it’s perfect in every way. Although its quality is undeniable, certain decisions have divided fans. Furthermore, the absence of features from previous installments has frustrated some players.
In this regard, one of Rockstar Games’ top executives has finally explained why the studio decided to remove one of the best features from Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2. While the explanation may not satisfy every fan, it makes sense when considering the kind of experience the new installment in the franchise offers.
Rockstar Games Explains Why It Removed the Mission Replay Feature From Grand Theft Auto 6
Every 3D game in the series has been known for its enormous map filled with possibilities. While simply exploring the open world is already entertaining, missions are also one of the pillars of the experience. The earlier games only allowed players to experience those levels once, but that changed with GTA V.
In the 2013 game, Rockstar Games introduced a feature that lets players replay all main and side missions. It even includes a medal system to evaluate their performance. Red Dead Redemption 2 has a similar mechanic, allowing players to revisit previous levels and relive specific moments from the campaign.
However, GTA 6 doesn’t have that system. This means players must restart the game from the beginning to revisit a previous mission or take advantage of the save file system to create checkpoints at strategic moments.
The absence of a mission replay feature sparked controversy among fans, especially considering that Grand Theft Auto 6 features some of the most spectacular and memorable missions in the entire franchise. A developer has now revealed why the team decided to remove this feature from GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2.
In a recent interview with Kotaku, Scott Butchard, Rockstar Games’ senior director of design, argued that the feature “didn’t fit” the story. According to him, allowing players to return to previous missions would diminish the importance of their decisions and could feel out of place.
The Rockstar Games creative emphasized that the feature is absent from the current version of Grand Theft Auto 6, although he didn’t discuss the possibility of adding it later through an update.
“We certainly considered the idea of replaying missions early in development, but we don’t currently offer that. As the game progressed, it felt like it didn’t fit the story we were trying to tell and seemed somewhat strange and disconnected. Being outside the game, so to speak, and replaying missions diminished the importance of the decisions you had made,” Scott Butchard explained.
Unlike Grand Theft Auto V, players cannot replay main campaign missions in GTA 6.
Rockstar Games Explains Why Grand Theft Auto 6 Rewards Empathy
To a certain extent, it’s understandable why the developers decided to discard that system. Unlike GTA V, the story and missions in Grand Theft Auto 6 can change considerably depending on the decisions players make at certain moments.
Without going into too much detail, some characters can survive until the end of the game if players make certain choices. The relationship between Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval also develops differently depending on the player’s behavior. There are plenty of possibilities, as some dialogue options and cutscenes only appear under specific circumstances.
Therefore, it’s easy to assume that implementing a mission replay system in GTA 6 would be more complicated than it was in Grand Theft Auto 5 or even Red Dead Redemption 2. We must also consider that the new installment introduces a “criminal profile” system that changes how the world reacts to the main characters.
In another interview with Eurogamer, Rockstar Games co-founder Rob Nelson discussed how this mechanic rewards empathy, because “being a demigod who rampages through the world” is only fun for so long.
“The idea naturally emerged that we should give players options to interact with the world beyond violence, beyond simply ‘pointing a gun at something,’ because that’s fun (...). But you can still unleash your fury and basically destroy your criminal profile until it breaks, at which point you enter an irredeemable state.”
The criminal profile is one of Grand Theft Auto 6’s most controversial new mechanics.
