TITLE: Fresh faces lead new 2028 presidential primary poll post-midterms
OUTLET: AOL.co.uk

The 2028 White House race is already heating up, with two potential frontrunners from both the Republicans and Democrats rising to the top of a new Cygnal poll.
The poll, released over the weekend, showed two new potential frontrunners, with former Transportation Secretary Pete Buttigieg and Secretary of State Marco Rubio leading among their respective parties.
According to the latest poll, the Democratic nomination race could be a barnburner, with Buttigieg leading New York Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez by less than one percentage point.
Following them, former Vice President Kamala Harris was third, with California Gov. Gavin Newsom in fourth, about half a percentage point behind the 2024 Presidential nominee.
Among Republicans, Rubio leads Vice President JD Vance by more than three percentage points, with Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis in a distant third, followed by Texas Sen. Ted Cruz with only about 2% support, according to the poll.
The Cygnal poll, conducted Nov. 4-5, surveyed 3,000 midterm voters with a 1.79% margin of error.
A poll potpourri
Cygnal’s latest poll comes as a variety of surveys released over the last week showed fluctuating leaders in both parties.
In a Nov. 12 poll released by the Economist/YouGov, Harris and Vance led their respective primaries.
A Reuters/Ipsos poll released the day before showed that 29% of Democrats thought Buttigieg and Sen. Jon Ossoff, D-Georgia, would be "excellent" presidential candidates. Forty-five percent of GOP voters gave Rubio an "excellent" rating; 43% said the same of Vance.
A pre-midterms poll from AtlasIntel showed a tight Democratic primary with Ossoff and Ocasio-Cortez tied at 18%, followed closely by Buttigieg (16%) and Harris (13%).
AtlasIntel’s poll found a two-horse race among GOP primary voters with Rubio leading Vance by 13 percentage points.
