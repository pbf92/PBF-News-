TITLE: Manchester United Trigger Option to Keep Bruno Fernandes Until 2028
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United have activated the one-year option in captain Bruno Fernandes' contract, extending his stay at Old Trafford until the summer of 2028.
The move, confirmed by club sources on Friday, comes at a moment of acute vulnerability for the club and a deliberate pause in longer-term talks.
Fernandes' previous deal was set to expire next summer. Without the option being exercised, overseas clubs would have been free to open pre-contract negotiations with him from January 1. By triggering the clause, United have protected the asset, preserved the possibility of a transfer fee, and removed an immediate external distraction. Sources indicate both sides view the step as mutually sensible while the team's results remain the clear priority.
The 32-year-old has already turned down two formal offers for a new long-term contract. In an interview with Sky Sports earlier this month, he was explicit: the club has shown it wants him to stay, but "because of the context of the team and the club, I just put that to one side… The most important thing now is that the club gets back to winning games." He has also stressed he will not run down a deal if United decide his future lies elsewhere.
That context is stark. United sit 19th in the Premier League after 11 matches (one win, five draws, five defeats), their worst start to a top-flight season in decades. New head coach Mauricio Pochettino is still searching for his first league win. Fernandes himself has been a pale imitation of the player who broke the Premier League assists record and swept the individual awards last season while United finished third. He has nonetheless contributed goals and assists this campaign, including a brace in Wednesday's chaotic 2-2 Champions League draw at Sporting Lisbon.
Complicating matters further, Fernandes is a doubt for Saturday's home match against Brentford. He missed Friday's training session with a virus contracted in Portugal and is being assessed carefully. Pochettino said the club will take no risks.
Since his £47 million arrival from Sporting in January 2020, Fernandes has made 343 appearances and scored 115 goals for United. The one-year extension buys both parties time. It does not resolve the longer-term question. Talks on a fresh deal remain on hold until results improve, and a summer exit in 2027 cannot yet be ruled out. For now, United have simply ensured their captain cannot walk away for free while the rebuild continues.
