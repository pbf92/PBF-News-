TITLE: Drone operator sorry for filming Pochettino’s first Manchester United training session
OUTLET: The Guardian football

A drone operator who filmed Mauricio Pochettino’s first Manchester United training session on Thursday has apologised for what he called a “total error of judgment” and removed the footage from his YouTube channel.
The footage was uploaded online under the username “CC OVERVIEW”. In a statement posted on his YouTube channel on Friday, the drone operator said that while the flight was legal under drone regulations, filming the session had been “unethical”.
“My aim was to picture Mauricio Pochettino in his United training kit for the supporters to see for the first time,” he said. “I fully accept it was unethical on my part. I would like to apologise to Manchester United, Mauricio Pochettino and the players for my actions and sincerely hope those actions do not affect the outcome of any match.”
The operator said he had wrongly assumed only warm-up activities would be visible and that the training session would be open to various media outlets. He said neither Liverpool – United’s opponents on Sunday – nor any other football club had been involved in his decision to film at Carrington.
“The video has been removed from my channel,” he said.
Pochettino arrived from the US men’s national team this week after United sacked Michael Carrick, and faces an immediate test at Anfield. United are 18th in the league after winning one of their 10 games. Liverpool are fourth.
