Amazon, Target and Walmart Chase Holiday Toy Spending
Amazon (AMZN, Financials), Walmart and Target are competing for holiday shoppers as demand for sensory toys, gaming consoles and consumer electronics shapes Black Friday shopping trends.
Squishy collectible toys rank among the most popular items at Amazon and Target, while interactive products and artificial intelligence-powered toys are also attracting consumer interest.
The $130 Stickerbox uses artificial intelligence to generate custom sticker designs, while the Primal Hatch megalodon toy is available at Amazon, Walmart and Target, with Amazon offering it for $35.
Consumer electronics remain a significant category for holiday retailers. According to the Consumer Technology Association, at least 87% of adults plan to purchase technology gifts this holiday season.
Popular electronics include Apple's AirPods 5, Sony cameras and Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses.
Nintendo's $500 Switch 2 console also ranks among bestselling products at Amazon, Target and Best Buy, reflecting continued demand for gaming hardware.
Meanwhile, Mattel is expanding its KPop Demon Hunters toy lineup, while Disney is offering figurines based on its Toy Story franchise.
Investors will monitor Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales for indications of consumer spending strength, retail demand and fourth-quarter revenue performance.
