TITLE: Haaland considers Man City exit - Friday's gossip
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
Erling Haaland considers Manchester City exit, Liverpool explore Jamal Musiala deal and Manchester United eye move for Maxi Araujo.
Manchester City and Norway forward Erling Haaland, 26, is considering an exit in the summer, with Barcelona among his preferred destinations. (El Nacional - in Spanish), external
Liverpool are prepared to explore a deal for Bayern Munich midfielder Jamal Musiala, 23, if the German international can prove his fitness by the end of the season. (Football Insider), external
Manchester United are eyeing a move for Sporting defender Maxi Araujo, 26, and the Uruguay international is said to be keen to join the club in January. (Sport Witness), external
Atletico Madrid are confident of keeping Argentina striker Julian Alvarez, 26, amid interest from Paris St-Germain and Arsenal. (Teamtalk), external
Chelsea are considering re-signing former German international centre-back Antonio Rudiger, 33, from Real Madrid in January. (Football Insider), external
Leeds, Brentford and Crystal Palace are all tracking Leicester City's 18-year-old English attacker Bismark Owusu. (Teamtalk), external
