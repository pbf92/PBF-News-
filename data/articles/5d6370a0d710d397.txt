Nasdaq 100 Futures Surge 6% as Nvidia Reinstates Jensen Huang, Sparking Wall Street Rebound: Dow Jones, S&P, Wall Street
US stock index futures rallied sharply on Monday after Nvidia (NASDAQ:NVDA) announced the reinstatement of Jensen Huang as chief executive officer, providing a significant boost to technology shares following one of Wall Street's steepest weekly declines in recent years.
At 09:01 ET (14:01 GMT), Nasdaq 100 futures jumped 6%, while S&P 500 futures advanced 3.8%. Dow Jones Industrial Average futures gained 2.3%, indicating a broad recovery ahead of the opening bell.
The rally followed a difficult week for US equities, during which the S&P 500, Dow Jones Industrial Average and Nasdaq Composite all entered correction territory, defined as a decline of at least 10% from recent closing highs.
Investors had already been anticipating a rebound following Friday's heavy selling, but Nvidia's announcement accelerated gains in futures markets.
The recovery comes amid continued concerns about artificial intelligence investment, technology sector valuations and the outlook for corporate earnings.
Nvidia Leadership Announcement Drives Technology Futures Higher
Nvidia's decision to reinstate Jensen Huang as chief executive provided an immediate catalyst for the technology sector's recovery.
The semiconductor company had faced uncertainty following Huang's unexpected departure earlier in November, alongside reports of government investigations and the possibility of shareholder litigation.
The leadership disruption added pressure to Nvidia shares at a time when investors were already reassessing the valuations of companies benefiting from the artificial intelligence investment boom.
Huang's return helped improve sentiment towards the chipmaker and the broader technology sector.
Nasdaq 100 futures rose 6%, significantly outperforming the S&P 500 and Dow futures.
The strength of the Nasdaq 100 reflected renewed investor interest in technology companies following the previous week's sharp declines.
Nevertheless, the sustainability of the rebound remains uncertain as markets continue to evaluate the economic and financial implications of substantial AI infrastructure spending.
S&P 500 and Nasdaq Record Heavy Losses as Wall Street Enters Correction
The futures rally followed a particularly difficult trading session on Friday.
The S&P 500 declined 3.7% to close at 6,910.39 points, while the technology-heavy Nasdaq Composite dropped 5.3% to 22,617.73 points.
The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 1.9%, ending the session at 48,147.33 points.
These declines confirmed that the S&P 500 and Dow had joined the Nasdaq Composite in correction territory.
