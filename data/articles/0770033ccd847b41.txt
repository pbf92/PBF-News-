IM Cannabis to consolidate share for Nasdaq compliance
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- IM Cannabis () said on Wednesday that its board has approved a 3:1 share consolidation of its issued and outstanding common shares, no par value per share.
- The primary purpose of the consolidation is to increase the per-share trading price of the common shares to support the company's efforts to maintain compliance with Nasdaq's US$1.00 minimum bid price requirement for continued listing, the company added.
- The company anticipates the common shares to begin trading on the Nasdaq on a post-consolidation basis at the opening of trading on November 23, 2026.
- Following the consolidation, the issued and outstanding common shares are expected to be reduced from 1,844,162 to nearly 614,721 shares.
