TITLE: Grand Theft Auto 6 Might Have Confirmed Rockstar's Next Game
OUTLET: OpenCritic

A Grand Theft Auto 6 leak from 2023 has resurfaced online, with many fans now convinced that it reveals Rockstar Games' next title, which is reportedly scheduled to release around 2030. Speculation about the company's next AAA release has continued since the day it officially confirmed Grand Theft Auto 6, with guesses ranging from Red Dead Redemption 3 to a revival of Bully.
The following paragraph may contain minor spoilers for GTA 6's story.
The reason people are returning to the 2023 leak is that they have now realized it accurately revealed several details about GTA 6 before they were officially confirmed. For example, it said that Jason and Lucia have a baby in the game who is taken away because of their criminal records. Another detail it revealed was that GTA 6's romance is not forced on players, meaning they can treat the co-protagonists as partners in crime and nothing more. And fans now believe its predictions about Rockstar's next game could also be accurate.
Some fans say their prayers have been answered after learning that Grand Theft Auto 6 fixes one of GTA 5's biggest and most frustrating issues.
