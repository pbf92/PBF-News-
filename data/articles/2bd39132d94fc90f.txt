TITLE: Outrage as Stockport graveyard decimated by callous thieves who make off with £20k of urns and plaques
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Outrage as Stockport graveyard decimated by callous thieves who make off with £20k of urns and plaques
Police are appealing for information
A Stockport church has been left devastated after almost 200 memorial urns and plaques were stolen from its graveyard. Nearly 100 cast iron memorial plaques and 80 granite and stone flower urns were removed from the graveyard of St Mark's Church on Redhouse Lane in Bredbury this weekend.
The missing and damaged plaques were discovered by a volunteer at the church at around 5.30am on Sunday morning (November 23). Photos from the churchyard show dozens of missing plaques from a memorial area of the church yard, with some remaining plaques chipped and broken.

The Stockport branch of Greater Manchester Police is now appealing for information following the theft, as they estimate the total value of the stolen property to be more than £20,000, alongside its 'substantial' sentimental value.
The force said that 94 memorial plaques and 80 memorial urns had been removed and stolen from graves throughout the church yard, with an estimated value of £8k and £12k respectively.
"At this stage, the total estimated value of property stolen is in excess of £20,000," GMP said in a Facebook post.
"It is anticipated that this figure may increase as further families visit the churchyard and report additional missing items.
"In addition to their monetary value, the plaques hold significant sentimental value to the families of the deceased persons commemorated by them.

"CCTV enquiries are ongoing to establish any persons or vehicles seen in the area during the relevant time period.
"Anyone with information is asked to contact us on 101 or via Live Chat on our website, quoting CRI/06JJ/0022334/26."
In a post on Facebook, the church entreated the public to share the appeal, saying: "We were devasted when we turned up this morning to find nearly 100 plaques and countless flower urns with memories of loved ones had been stolen.
"We are so sad about this awful crime. So many families and individuals in our community are affected by this.
"Coming up to Christmas so many people visit the graveyard in memory of their loved ones. So many people in our community will be affected by this awful crime."
