TITLE: Manchester United reach Bruno Fernandes contract decision with talks on hold
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United have activated the option to extend Bruno Fernandes' contract by one year.
Fernandes had been set to become a free agent at the end of the season, but United held the option to prolong his deal by 12 months, which they have now triggered.
The United captain has rejected two new contract proposals from the club, but both parties are now reportedly in agreement on resuming negotiations down the line, with an emphasis on improving United's current standing.
Club officials believe activating their one-year option makes sense given that European clubs could approach him regarding a pre-contract agreement in January.
Addressing his future last week, Fernandes stated that his priority was helping United climb the Premier League table, according to the Manchester Evening News.
"For us to start winning games, and then we can start speaking about contract. That's not important now, it's irrelevant," he said of potentially signing a new deal.
"Obviously I know it's my future and I shouldn't probably look into that way because I haven't got much left in my contract, but I'm not really worried about that. I'm more worried about getting results, trying to bring the club back in a position that we can be proud of, and we're not there at the moment.

"We're going to have time to speak about that. As I said, we had conversations. The conversations stopped in the moment that we thought that was not as important at the moment because results and performances have not been at the level we should be. So we're going to have time to think about that."
Fernandes shattered the Premier League assist record last season and claimed the Sir Matt Busby Player of the Year award for an unprecedented fifth time, yet his performances have declined during this campaign.
The Portugal international was also relegated to the bench against AFC Bournemouth last month following his public criticism of Michael Carrick's tactical approach after United's heavy defeat to Como in the UEFA Champions League.
He was reinstated to the lineup by Carrick for the subsequent fixture and has featured in the starting eleven for both of Mauricio Pochettino's matches at the helm so far.
