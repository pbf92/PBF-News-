Custom Health Holdings Inc. (CHLT) Amends Note at 17% Interest, Up to US$15.0M
- Custom Health has engaged Yorkville Ives & Co., LLC ("Yorkville Ives"), a U.S.-based registered broker-dealer and member FINRA and SIPC, as financial advisor to support the Company's U.S. capital markets strategy.
Custom Health engaged Yorkville Ives for a 12-month U.S. capital-markets advisory term and amended its note to up to US$15.0M, with 17% annual interest payable monthly in cash. Q3 2026 revenue was US$14.4M, up 220% year over year.
