Mauricio Pochettino takes Manchester United to Sporting Lisbon in the UEFA Champions League on Wednesday as he searches for some positive vibes.
United were hammered 4-0 at bitter rivals Liverpool in Pochettino's first game in charge. Not helped at all by Leny Yoro's early red card, which has now been overturned, it was still a woeful display from United as they sit in the Premier League's relegation zone after 11 games of the season. But they've fared much better in the Champions League so far this season with two wins from four games and will fancy their chances of at least grabbing a point at Sporting.

As for the Portuguese giants, they are way off the pace domestically with rivals FC Porto (who they play this weekend) top of the table and nine points ahead of them. But after arriving as manager a few weeks ago Paulo Fonseca has won a couple of games and Sporting have won three of their four Champions League games so far. They will be scrappy and tough to break down and the home crowd will create quite the atmosphere.

Sporting Lisbon team news
Colombian striker Luis Suarez is Sporting's main threat in attack and is in great form as he's scored 13 goals in 15 games so far this season. Italian international Issa Doumbia has been excellent in midfield and Sporting will aim to score early and make United as uncomfortable as possible.
Manchester United team news
Luke Shaw is back in training for United which is a huge boost for Pochettino. Matheus Cunha is suspended after his red card in the win against Roma, while Marcus Rashford, Benjamin Sesko, Manuel Ugarte and Carlos Baleba are all out injured. It seems likely Pochettino will rotate a few youngsters into the lineup as Shea Lacey and Ayden Heaven could feature. Joshua Zirkzee, Mason Mount, Noussair Mazraoui Andrey Santos and Harry Maguire will all likely start as Poch will perhaps prioritize Saturday's huge Premier League clash with Brentford.
