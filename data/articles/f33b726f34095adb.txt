TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino interview slip-up after 4-0 Man Utd defeat suggests he hasn’t left the US behind
OUTLET: HITC

Mauricio Pochettino’s first post-match interview as Manchester United manager included an amusing slip-up that suggested his time in charge of the United States men’s national team is still fresh in his mind. The Argentine was speaking after a humiliating 4-0 defeat to Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday, the worst first game by a United manager in 100 years.
Discussing the result, Pochettino briefly reverted to American terminology before correcting himself. “Everyone who loves soccer — sorry, football,” he said, seemingly forgetting that the sport is almost universally called football in England.
Pochettino spent two years in charge of the USMNT after replacing Gregg Berhalter following the 2024 Copa America. He led the country to the round of 16 at the 2026 World Cup on home soil, but their elimination against Belgium meant he ultimately fell short of expectations.
The 54-year-old had signed a contract until 2030 in August, committing himself to the next World Cup, but agreed to leave just three months later. He finished his time with victories over Haiti, winning 6-0 and 2-0 in his final week, before signing a two-and-a-half-year deal to replace Michael Carrick at Old Trafford.
Pochettino’s first afternoon in charge could hardly have gone worse. The Liverpool defeat was a stark reminder of the scale of the rebuilding job ahead, and Pochettino may one day regret his decision to leave the USMNT.
