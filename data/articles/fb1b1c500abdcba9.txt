TITLE: 5-minute Holiday Gift Guide: MerchantFloor Launches AI Gift Guide Tool for Shopify Merchants
OUTLET: markets.businessinsider.com

Berkeley, CA, Nov. 25, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Imagine how many users will search Google or ChatGPT for “gift ideas for...” over the next few weeks. Store owners want that traffic, but their product and category pages are not optimized for those keywords. They need a gift guide tailored to those searches.
Pick the gift guide prompt template
MerchantFloor, which offers AI content tools for Shopify merchants, today announced the Gift Guide template in its Blog Writer app, making that possible.
A store owner types in “moms who love pearls,” pastes a link to a Shopify category or collection page, and gets back a finished AI-generated holiday gift guide with images and inline links all set up in less than five minutes.
Holiday shoppers look for gift ideas by person: gifts for mom, for a coworker, or for someone who loves pearls. But writing a guide manually means picking products, writing copy for each one, uploading images, adding links, and formatting the post, all of which is hard to fit in during the busiest weeks of the year. The new template does that work and saves the result to the store’s blog for review and easy publishing.
How it works
“When I ran a store, every November I knew a gift guide was worth writing, but I never had the time. Most store owners are the photographer, the copywriter, and the customer service team all at once,” said Ash Moosa, founder of MerchantFloor. “Now, with our Blog Writer app, you tell it who the gift is for, point it at a collection, and you get a fleshed-out gift guide that you can publish.”
Pricing
New accounts start with 25 free credits. Credit packs start at $20. The template is part of MerchantFloor’s suite of tools for Shopify merchants, which also includes an AI photo studio and a product description generator.
