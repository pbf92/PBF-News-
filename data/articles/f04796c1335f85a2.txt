TITLE: Which Players Should Manchester United Sign In January 2027?
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Mauricio Pochettino hasn't even walked through the door at Old Trafford as Manchester United's new manager and speculation is already swirling over potential targets for the January transfer window. Most agree the 54-year-old will need reinforcements to reshape United in his own image.
Top of the shopping list must surely be a new centre forward. Benjamin Sesko's injury has left United short of a player in this position for the first three months of the season and so Pochettino will require a new attacking focal point as quickly as possible. His new club will be aware of this.
Folarin Balogun could be a candidate to make the move to Old Trafford. The American forward is someone Pochettino coached during his time as US men's national team head coach and is likely to be available in January after coming extremely close to leaving Monaco for Everton in the summer.
Eli Junior Kroupi would be a statement signing. The young Frenchman is considered one of the brightest attacking prospects in the Premier League and would cost a significant fee to prise from Bournemouth in January. If Manchester United wants to improve under Pochettino, though, he will need new tools.
Left back cover must be signed too. Lewis Hall was mentioned as a target in the summer and could be on United's radar once more with the 22-year-old possibly an upgrade on Luke Shaw whose injury troubles have lingered over the course of his career. Calvin Bassey is also someone who could be a January target.
Pochettino will be limited by what Manchester United can do in the January window. The former Chelsea, Paris Saint-Germain, Tottenham Hotspur and USA manager has inherited a mess of a situation and a squad that doesn't appear to be suited to play the style of soccer favoured by the new man in charge at Old Trafford.
The youth academy could be a place Pochettino turns to as Manchester United's new manager. The Argentine has a good track record of developing young players and United boasts a youth academy that has produced countless stars of the sport. Pochettino could find some new talent there.
Despite having coached at the elite level over a number of years, the Manchester United job could be the most challenging of Pochettino's career to date. The struggling Premier League side is in a troubled position right now and is counting on Pochettino to turn things around. To do that he needs a better squad.
