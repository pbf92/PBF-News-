NextCure Received Notice of Noncompliance
On November 16, 2026, NextCure received notice from Nasdaq that its stockholders’ equity of $5.503 million was below the $10 million minimum required by Listing Rule 5450(b)(1)(A), and it did not meet alternative compliance standards. The company has until January 4, 2027, to submit a plan to regain compliance, but expects its proposed merger with Avere Therapeutics to close before then and potentially resolve the issue; trading continues on Nasdaq in the meantime.
Original SEC Filing: NextCure, Inc. [ NXTC ] - 8-K - Nov. 20, 2026
