TITLE: GTA 6 release sparks ‘GTA Flu’ as gamers plan days off
OUTLET: The American Bazaar

The highly anticipated launch of “Grand Theft Auto VI” (GTA 6) is prompting gamers across the United States to rearrange work schedules, request paid time off and, in some cases, consider calling in sick to experience the blockbuster video game.
The game, developed by Rockstar Games, is scheduled to launch at midnight on Thursday, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Its release has generated excitement among gaming enthusiasts, with some planning extended breaks to spend time exploring the game’s fictional world.

According to ABC News, the phenomenon has gained an informal nickname on social media: “GTA flu,” referring to employees and students planning to miss work or classes around the launch.
The anticipation comes 13 years after the release of “Grand Theft Auto V” in 2013, making the latest instalment one of the most eagerly awaited releases in the gaming industry.
Joost van Dreunen, an associate adjunct professor at New York University’s Stern School of Business, told ABC News that GTA 6 could represent the biggest launch in gaming history and potentially in the broader entertainment industry. “Once midnight hits, it’s going to be a frenzy,” van Dreunen said.
Gamers schedule extended breaks
The enthusiasm surrounding the release has prompted some players to plan several consecutive days away from work.
Content creator and gamer Bud “Gata Bud” Burruss told ABC News that he had arranged nine days off, combining leave taken before Thanksgiving with the three nonholiday working days during Thanksgiving week, Thanksgiving Day and Black Friday. Other gamers have also shared plans to take time off or adjust their schedules to play the game immediately after its release.
Brodie Shea, a New York University student and gamer, said the game’s detailed environments and non-player characters were among the features driving excitement.
Miami, which inspired the fictional Vice City setting, is also joining the celebrations. Miami Heat temporarily rebranded as the Vice City Heat for its Wednesday game against the Milwaukee Bucks, featuring custom uniforms and a GTA-themed basketball court as part of an event called “A Night in Vice City.”
GTA 6 launch creates workplace buzz
The launch has also drawn attention to how major entertainment releases can influence workplace attendance and employee leave patterns.

However, claims circulating online that 1.5 million Americans would skip work and cost the U.S. economy $1 billion in lost productivity have not been substantiated by reliable research, according to a November 18 fact-check by PolitiFact.
The report noted that available surveys showed interest in taking time off but did not establish those viral estimates as accurate. Sony has also reminded PlayStation 5 users to download a required software update before the launch so they can access the game at midnight.
With the release arriving after more than a decade of anticipation, GTA 6 is generating excitement beyond the gaming community, turning its launch into a wider cultural event.
