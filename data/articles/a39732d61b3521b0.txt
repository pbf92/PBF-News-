Grand Theft Auto 6 is absolutely jam-packed with features, mini-games, activities, and collectibles. It can take hundreds of hours to see and do everything, so let’s see if two of the series’ most popular activities, darts and bowling, are somewhere in Vice City.
Take out all the main story missions in GTA 6, and you still have a ridiculously deep array of entertainment. Backyard Wrestling, Illegal Operations, and even meditating. But Rockstar wouldn’t cut two iconic games, right?

Can You Play Darts in GTA 6?
You can’t play Darts in GTA 6 as Rockstar Games has opted not to include the mini-game this time around.
We all played a decent amount of Darts in GTA 4 and GTA 5, but that won’t be the case in GTA 6—for now. Darts is not one of GTA 6’s activities, so you’ll need to throw some real-life arrows for your fix.

Can You Play Bowling in GTA 6?
Another recurring mini-game in the Grand Theft Auto franchise has been bowling, but bowling has been omitted from GTA 6 as well.
You almost can’t complain about GTA 6 lacking features when it has so much. But you’d think Darts and Bowling would be dead-certs, especially with the game’s various bars and entertainment facilities.

There’s a reason for this, though, and here’s what Rob Nelson, Rockstar North co-studio head, told IGN:
“Darts was a contentious one. Even we… you hear they have unlimited money or unlimited time, even we do not and so sometimes you just have to admit we are not going to be able to make this to the level we feel we need to. A lot of work [is needed] to get it tuned right. Darts is one we just didn’t get to.”
