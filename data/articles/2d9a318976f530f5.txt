TITLE: Rockstar Games' next game revealed as GTA 6 ending confirms long-standing theory
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Rockstar Games is back, baby.
After an agonising 13 years, Grand Theft Auto VI has revitalised the best series to grace our screens.
Roaring to success, GTA VI has smashed all kinds of records, teeing itself up to be the greatest single piece of entertainment of all time.
But it's not just a standalone project. Already, Rockstar Games has confirmed that Red Dead Redemption 2 played a major part in making GTA VI's prologue, as the influences of their entire portfolio are felt in this amalgamation of greatness.

Yet with some fans already powering through the main storyline, the ending might have just given a major clue about the studio's next project.
And it could mean a beloved detective series is finally making a comeback.
GTA 6 ending gives old LA Noire 2 leak a fresh lease of life
Way back in 2023, a post shared on GTAForums by a user named 'Shahanshah' relayed information from an anonymous 4chan source.
Among the many claims made about GTA VI was an intriguing suggestion that Rockstar was working on a sequel to LA Noire. At the time, the rumour was largely met with scepticism.
However, following GTA VI's release, players have revisited the original post and noticed that several of its story details appear to have been accurate.
That includes information surrounding Jason and Lucia's relationship, the game's Criminal Profile system and, most notably, details about its ending.
Naturally, this has led to renewed speculation about whether the LA Noire 2 claims could also be legitimate.
As highlighted by a post on the GamingLeaksAndRumours subreddit, the original source reportedly got enough GTA VI details right to make the detective sequel rumour worth another look.

According to the original leak, a potential LA Noire sequel would move away from 1940s Los Angeles and take players to San Francisco.
The story would reportedly revolve around the infamous Zodiac Killer, whose identity remains one of America's most enduring criminal mysteries.
The post also suggested that the game was already in the very early stages of development, meaning that it could be on its way following the release of GTA Online.
However, as we know all too well, Rockstar doesn't do anything by halves, and the development cycle could take a few years.
In fact, the leaker reportedly suggested that a 2030 release was a possibility.
Fans should take the resurfaced information with a healthy pinch of salt. The fact that the original source appears to have correctly predicted elements of GTA VI is certainly intriguing, but it doesn't automatically mean every other claim was accurate.
Still, after more than 15 years without a new entry, the prospect of returning to LA Noire is enough to get plenty of detective fans excited.
