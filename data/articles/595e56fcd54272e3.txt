Rockstar Games teams up with Discord for exclusive GTA 6 rewards, including themes, icons, and more
GTA 6 is just around the corner, with the highly anticipated game scheduled to launch on November 19. Fans have been waiting years to return to Vice City, and with two days remaining until release, excitement is at an all-time high. To celebrate the upcoming launch, Rockstar Games has teamed up with Discord to offer players exclusive GTA 6-themed customization options, including a new color theme, app icon, and display name style.

Discord announced the collaboration through an official blog post, confirming that the new customization options are available starting November 16. The best part is that you don’t need to purchase GTA 6 or have an active Discord Nitro subscription to try them out.

Discord and GTA 6 collaboration
The main highlight is the Vice City color theme, which changes Discord’s appearance to match the style of Grand Theft Auto VI. Players can also apply a special Vice City app icon and customize how their display name appears on their profile. All three options are available on both desktop and mobile, meaning you can give Discord a GTA 6-inspired look regardless of which device you’re using.
However, these rewards won’t be available forever. According to Discord, the Vice City theme will remain available to everyone until December 13. Those subscribed to Discord Nitro, excluding Nitro Basic, will have extended access until February 20.
As for how to activate the rewards, Discord has made the process pretty simple. Between November 16 and November 22, players can visit the Quest Home section and apply the new theme directly.
After November 22, the customization options will still be accessible through Discord’s settings. Desktop users can find the Vice City color theme and app icon under User Settings > Appearance, while mobile users can access them through Settings > App Settings. The new display name style will appear when customizing your profile.
And that’s not all, as Discord is also offering another reward through a separate GTA 6 promotion. The Grand Theft Auto VI: Pre-Load Quest allows eligible players to earn Discord Orbs by completing a video-based Quest. These Orbs can be used for rewards within Discord, giving players another reason to participate in the promotion. However, this particular Quest is available for an even shorter period, as it is scheduled to end on November 18, just one day before GTA 6 launches.
