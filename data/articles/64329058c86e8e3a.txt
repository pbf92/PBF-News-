TITLE: GTA VI: 5 Small Details That Make a Big Difference
OUTLET: Only Comic Universe

GTA VI has been out for a little while now, and it’s been one hell of an experience. Everywhere I turn, whether it’s on social media, my PlayStation friends list or even at my barber’s, it’s seemingly all anyone can still talk about.
We’ve seen secrets come to light, discussions about the best cars to get early, the best weapons, the perfect setups for robberies and so much more. In this article, though, I want to focus on five small things I’ve personally discovered during my 40-plus hours with GTA 6 that have completely blown me away.
1. Talking to NPCs From Inside Your Car
I spotted this one early on when you speak to Shirley. I realised I didn’t have to exit my vehicle in order to speak to her. It’s a very small detail, but I found it much more realistic. It always bothered me that you had to get out of your car just to have a chat with someone, whereas this feels far more natural. It’s such a simple addition, but it makes those everyday interactions feel more believable.
2. Starting Your Car With an Ignition Key
I’m over 40 hours into the game, and I still love this. I like the fact that when you enter a vehicle, the engine doesn’t automatically start. Instead, you have to press the trigger again to turn it on. It adds another element of immersion, giving you a greater sense of control over the vehicle itself.
I do wish we had the option to do it the other way around, though. Being able to turn the engine off while remaining in the car would be a fantastic addition. It’s a minor criticism, but given how much control Rockstar has introduced elsewhere, it feels like something that would fit perfectly.
3. Manual Indicators
We haven’t had proper working indicators (turn signals for our American readers) like this since The Getaway, all the way back in 2002. It always bothered me that one of the most basic functions of a vehicle wasn’t something you could manually control, or at least have the option to.
In GTA VI, you can activate your left or right indicator using the D-pad, and they’ll even switch themselves off shortly after you’ve completed your turn. We’ve all been guilty of driving like an ordinary civilian in GTA every now and again, and adding indicators just helps that immersion a little more… until you get impatient, that is.
4. Refuelling Your Vehicle
I’ve seen mixed reactions to this feature, but thankfully, it’s not something you need to do frequently. I’ve only been forced to refuel twice in over 40 hours of gameplay, so it certainly hasn’t become an inconvenience.
It’s another element of realism added to the game, and something I always wanted as a kid growing up with GTA, even as far back as San Andreas. I appreciate that not everybody wants to worry about petrol in a video game, but personally, I love having that extra interaction. It makes vehicles feel more like something you actually own and maintain rather than just another way of getting from A to B.
5. Carrying Two Consumables at Once
Being able to carry a burger and a drink, switch between them and even hand them to your partner. What an addition! I honestly can’t wait to see how this translates to GTA Online.
You can even carry two bags, and the animations work so fluidly. I find it incredibly satisfying that you can literally order a burger and a drink, have them both in your hands and switch between them naturally. It’s something so simple, but once again, it adds another small level of realism to the game.
What impresses me most is how natural these interactions feel. They don’t come across as features that have been added simply for the sake of it. They’re little things you might do without even thinking about them, and that’s exactly why I appreciate them so much.
Final Thoughts
Ultimately, I appreciate these little details won’t mean much to everybody, but they’re exactly what makes GTA VI so special to me. Rockstar has always been fantastic at creating worlds that feel alive, and it’s these small additions that make the experience that little bit more immersive.
I’m over 40 hours into the game now, and I’m still discovering things that impress me. I genuinely can’t wait to see what else I’ve missed because, knowing Rockstar, there’s probably a ridiculous amount I’ve yet to discover.
