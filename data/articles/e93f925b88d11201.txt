TITLE: Liverpool 4-0 Man Utd: What Pochettino said
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Man Utd boss Mauricio Pochettino, speaking to Match of the Day: "We need to adapt. It's true that it's a difficult decision that we maybe don't agree with, but we cannot talk too much about the game.
"11 v 10 from the beginning of the game. In the way we approached the game, and we wanted to play, it's normal to affect all the preparation.
"It's so painful, we feel pain for that defeat, but we need to keep the head up and translate that anger to an energy to start to work from tomorrow for the next game."
On how his team responded after going 2-0 down: "I told the players [it was a] completely unfair situation, but when a situation is like this, to turn a negative to positive, we need to make double effort.
"More concentration, more focus, care more about everything. We have players and, in a way, after the 12th minute when we conceded and started to play with 10 men, the players were committed and they tried everything.
"We made effort, we ran, we tried. A very difficult team, a team that found the goal when there was the red card. Every day we will find the possibility to improve."
On whether he agreed with Leny Yoro's red card: "Today we have the technology, and it's only to the fans and everyone that loves football.
"You need to see one minute before the action of Jacquet on Mbuemo and you go to the next minute and see the action from Yoro and Isak. It's difficult to accept that because it was a foul under my view and for me was a penalty. Look how everything changes if that action was a red card and a foul. I think we need to review. When I played football, that situation is normal. The action against Mbuemo, if that [Yoro] was a red card then that was a penalty."
Man Utd captain Bruno Fernandes, speaking to Match of the Day: "Difficult game. Coming to our rivals and losing is always bad. After 15 minutes, the picture of the game changes completely, and it makes it very difficult for us.
"Everyone tried very hard. The result won't show that and will hide a lot of good things that the team did. We still created a lot of chances to try and do something in the game, even with one player less.
"Everyone tried a lot, ran a lot. Unfortunately, we were not able to avoid two more goals in the second half, but we need to clear now and go to the next game in the Champions League and try to get the best out of that game."
On Leny Yoro's red card: "Today it's one that needs to be looked at. We have far too many meetings with the referees for this to happen to us again. The last one against Chelsea you can give it as a red card and it was probably a second yellow also.
"Today, you can't give a red card. It's very clear they show us if the player is not in control of the ball, it's never a red card. Isak kicks the ball forward, the ball goes into the hands of Senne. Isak has got no control of the ball, he has no chance to get on the ball. He puts his leg across to try to get some advantage from Leny."
Did you know?
- Mauricio Pochettino suffered the joint-heaviest defeat by a Manchester United manager in his first game (0-4), along with a defeat by the same scoreline for Lal Hilditch in October 1926 (0-4 v Bolton Wanderers in the top-flight).
- Manchester United boss Pochettino was the first manager in Premier League history to see his team concede twice and receive a red card in the first half of his first match in charge of a club.
