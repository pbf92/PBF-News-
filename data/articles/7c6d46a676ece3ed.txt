TITLE: Why isn’t Marcus Rashford playing for Manchester United against Sporting in the Champions League?
OUTLET: Diario AS

Manchester United and Mauricio Pochettino can use all the help they can get at the moment, but they won’t be getting any from Marcus Rashford against Sporting in the Champions League on Wednesday, with the attacker missing another game due to injury.
What injury does Marcus Rashford have?
Rashford’s last appearance for the Red Devils came on October 13, when he played 66 minutes of the 2-0 loss to Atlético Madrid, the game that arguably sparked the crisis the team now finds itself in.

The 29-year-old was on the bench for United’s next game, the 1-1 draw away to Leeds, but was an unused substitute. Since then, he has missed six games, including defeats to Como, Bournemouth, Chelsea and Liverpool, a draw with Aston Villa and a victory over Roma, because of thigh and hip injuries that have been bothering him for some time.
When will Rashford be fit to return?
The good news is that Rashford has resumed training, which suggests his return will come sooner rather than later. There was some talk that he could feature in some capacity against Sporting, but he ultimately didn’t make the trip to Portugal.
United next face Brentford in the Premier League on Saturday, although it is too soon to know whether the forward will be involved.
When did Rashford last score for Manchester United?
The former Barcelona loanee has made eight appearances this season after returning to Old Trafford, six of which have been starts. However, having also had a temporary spell at Villa, he is still waiting to score his first United goal since December 2024.
