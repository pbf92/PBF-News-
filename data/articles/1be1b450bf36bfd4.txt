TITLE: Confirmed Man Utd v Brentford Premier League team news: Mamadou Sangaré returns to Bees' starting XI
OUTLET: Brentford FC

Brentford head coach Keith Andrews has made one change to the starting XI for this afternoon's Premier League game against Manchester United (3pm kick-off GMT).
The Bees face the Red Devils at Old Trafford in what is Mauricio Pochettino's first home match in charge.
Mamadou Sangaré replaces Yehor Yarmoliuk, who is not in the matchday squad, in the west Londoners' midfield, in what is the only change from Monday night's 4-0 victory against Everton.
Josh Dasilva is included in a first-team matchday squad for the first time in 2026/27, having featured for Brentford B in a 2-1 victory against Hull City last weekend.
Brentford (4-3-3): Kelleher; Kayode, Ajer, van den Berg, Lewis-Potter; Janelt, Sangaré, Damsgaard; Ouattara, Wilson, Anthony
Subs: Valdimarsson, Fredrick, Schuster, Diouf, Jensen, Carvalho, Dasilva, Nunes, Schade
