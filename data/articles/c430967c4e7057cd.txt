TITLE: Cochrane Pipe Band raises funds for Wayfinders Wellness Society
OUTLET: Cochrane Eagle

The Cochrane Pipe Band is bringing the community together for an afternoon of music and culture, with all ticket proceeds supporting an organization that helps first responders, military personnel and veterans living with post-traumatic stress injuries.
On Saturday, Nov. 28, the band will host its second annual community concert, this year raising funds for Wayfinders Wellness Society.
“About a third of our members are either currently serving or former members of the military and 1st responders,” said band manager Chris McKiernan. “We thought this was a very appropriate society for us to sort of donate to and benefit from this concert.”
Wayfinders is a non-profit society dedicated to empowering first responders, military personnel, veterans and their families through peer-led support for those living with post-traumatic stress injuries.
All ticket proceeds will go toward the society’s general operations.
The year-end concert began as a way to give band members another opportunity to perform.
“That's really the last sort of formal event we have until the Burns Night event the following year, in January,” he said.
The concert also gives the band an opportunity to introduce new music ahead of its annual Robbie Burns Night performance.
“It keeps us on our toes. It helps us learn new music. And much of the music we'll be playing at this concert is new in our repertoire,” McKiernan said.
The afternoon will feature performances by the Cochrane Pipe Band, Alberta Rocky Mountain Highland Dancers and Gardner School of Dance.
“We've got adults and little children dancing, poetry reading and we also have a brother and sister piper and drummer duo performing,” McKiernan said.
The Cochrane Pipe Band community concert takes place at the Cochrane RancheHouse on Saturday, Nov. 28, from 3:30 to 5 pm.
