TITLE: GTA VI Shatters Records with Historic 1st-Day Sales After Highly Anticipated Launch
OUTLET: Bleacher Report

Grand Theft Auto VI made entertainment history with Thursday's release of the series' first installment in 13 years.
On Friday, Rockstar Games announced GTA VI earned a company-estimated $3.79 billion in retail sales through Thursday to mark "a new record for entertainment across any medium."
There had already been 32.5 million units sold prior to the game's Thursday release, according to Rockstar Games.

Thursday's launch crushed the initial retail sales earned by GTA V, which made around $800 million in the first day after the series' last installment was released in 2013.
GTA V ultimately sold almost 235 million units, while the series as a whole has sold around 480 million units, according to Rockstar Games.
Rockstar Games invested what CNBC estimates is more than $1 billion into creating the open-world representation of fictional Miami, which one of the game's creators previously said took him 80 hours to play through.
Circana's Mat Piscatella reported 61 percent of U.S. PlayStation 5 users and 56 percent of Xbox players around the world played GTA VI on the game's launch date.
GTA VI U.S. players averaged 513 minutes on PS5 the first day the game was available, while global players averaged 494 minutes on Xbox, according to Piscatella.
The anticipation over the latest game in the series built amid a series of delays following the release of the first GTA VI trailer in December 2023. The trailer stated the game would be dropped at some point in 2025.
Rockstar Games announced in May 2025 the launch would be delayed to May 2026, then updated that date six months later to November 2026.
Athletes around the sports world celebrated the Thursday release of the game, which features a cameo from former Miami Dolphins wide receiver Tyreek Hill as well as a nod to Las Vegas Raiders quarterback and Miami native Fernando Mendoza.
