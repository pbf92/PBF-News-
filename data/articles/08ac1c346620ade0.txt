Ellsworth Growth & Income Fund 10% Owner Purchased Shares Worth Over $542K
Saba Capital Management, L.P., a 10% owner, reported purchasing 50,036 shares of Ellsworth Growth & Income Fund common stock indirectly on Nov. 20, 2026, at $10.84 per share, for a total of $542,390. The securities are held by funds and/or accounts for which Saba Capital Management, L.P. serves as investment manager. Following the transaction, Saba Capital Management, L.P. indirectly owned 1,916,586 shares. Boaz R. Weinstein is the managing member of Saba Capital Management GP, LLC, the general partner of Saba Capital Management, L.P.
