TITLE: Chelsea Are Set To Rival Manchester United For This Barcelona Star: Good Fit For Alonso?
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

In a recent report, Sport revealed that Chelsea are set to rival Manchester United for Barcelona star Fermin Lopez. It has been mentioned that the Blues are eyeing a move to bring the Spanish playmaker to Stamford Bridge next year.
Lopez's Impressive Numbers In La Liga
Lopez has enjoyed a good run of form at the Spanish club in this campaign so far as he has been responsible for putting in a series of impressive displays for them in the final third. The Spain international has scored seven goals and grabbed five assists in 11 appearances for Barcelona this season across all competitions.
The 23-year-old is currently one of the most talented playmakers in La Liga. Hence, it is no wonder that the Blues are showing interest in recruiting him next year.
Lopez's current contract at the Nou Camp will run out in the summer of 2031, so Chelsea would have to launch a convincing offer if they want to sign him.

Will Lopez Be A Good Fit For Chelsea Boss Xabi Alonso?
Lopez can shoot the ball with power and precision from long range and has the eye to play some decisive passes in and around the opponent's penalty area. He is a good dribbler with the ball at his feet and can chip in by scoring and creating a handful of important goals for his team.
The Spanish talent is primarily an attacking midfielder but can also operate as a box-to-box midfielder or as a left-sided wide player if needed. However, there are some concerns on whether he can make a smooth transition to life in the Premier League if the Blues manage to get a deal over the line for him in 2027.
We can expect Lopez to bring more quality and depth to Chelsea head coach Xabi Alonso's frontline. He has what it takes to fight for a regular first-team spot at Stamford Bridge in the coming years.
At 23, Lopez has his peak years ahead of him, which makes him a great fit for the West London club's needs in the long run. He will also help the Blues compete across all fronts in the future. Thus, Alonso should focus on bringing him on board soon.
