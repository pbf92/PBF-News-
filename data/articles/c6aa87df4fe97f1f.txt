TITLE: The Best Black Friday Deals on 4K UHD and Blu-ray Movies Are Already Live
OUTLET: VGC / IGN

If you want to save money on 4K UHD and Blu-ray movies, there’s no longer any need to wait for actual Black Friday. While that date lands on November 27 this year, the early Black Friday deals on 4K and Blu-ray movies have already begun in earnest. Tons of 4K movies, including brand-new releases, are on sale for $12.99 or lower right now. Let’s have a look at the best deals that are already available.
Amazon: 4K UHD Movies for $12.99
Both Amazon and Gruv are running $12.99 sales on 4K Blu-rays. These include a wide array of movies, from new releases like the horror hit Obsession and Steven Spielberg’s Disclosure Day to classics like Interview with the Vampire and Ben-Hur. A Charlie Brown Christmas is even on the list, and in 4K, no less. This is a cheap way to add a bunch of great movies to your collection.
Gruv: 4K UHD Movies for $12.99
Criterion Sale: Up to 50% Off
A couple times a year, the good people at The Criterion Collection run a sale offering up to 50% off pretty much their entire collection of new and lovingly restored classic movies. The current offerings on sale have some of the best movies ever made, ranging from the entire Kubrick collection to KPop Demon Hunters. There’s a Godzilla collection. There’s The Princess Bride. There’s No Country for Old Men and there’s Wall-E. If you can’t find a dozen movies worth buying in this sale, I don’t even know what to tell you.
Gruv's 3 for $33 Sale
Getting 4K movies for $12.99 apiece is a killer deal. But if you can find three movies in this list that you want to own, you’ll get them for $11 apiece, which is even better. Tons of great horror movies are here, like The Conjuring and Sinners, along with PTA’s Phantom Thread, Clint Eastwood’s Unforgiven, and a bunch more.
