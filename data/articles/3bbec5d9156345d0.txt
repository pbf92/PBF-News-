Liverpool vs Manchester United – Premier League Preview
Date: Sunday, 22nd November 2026
Venue: Anfield
Kick-off: 16:30 GMT
After another exhausting international break, Liverpool return to Premier League action with one of the biggest fixtures in world football as Manchester United travel to Anfield.
Plenty has happened during the international period. Dominik Szoboszlai has returned following a spell playing with his knee strapped, raising further questions about how long he has been carrying an injury. Encouragingly, both Giovanni Leoni and Conor Bradley have returned to first-team training, with each hoping to make themselves available for selection by December.
For Andoni Iraola, the immediate priority is getting his squad ready for a demanding encounter against a Manchester United side now under new management.
Mauricio Pochettino has been appointed as United's new manager, and the prospect of a new-manager bounce makes this already enormous fixture even more interesting. The Argentine will be desperate to make an early statement, while Liverpool will be determined to protect their home advantage and deliver a performance worthy of the occasion.
Liverpool Need to Set the Tempo
Liverpool have shown encouraging signs under Iraola, but consistency remains the challenge. The recent victories and defeats have demonstrated both the attacking potential within this squad and the work still required to establish the manager's preferred system.
Against Manchester United, the Reds must be aggressive from the opening whistle. Anfield needs to be a difficult place for the visitors, and Liverpool should look to press high, win the midfield battles and prevent United from settling into their new manager's approach.
The midfield partnership will be particularly important. Alexis Mac Allister and Trey Nyoni offer a combination of experience, composure and energy, with the young Englishman continuing to make a strong case for regular involvement.
There will also be considerable attention on Szoboszlai's fitness. His recent knee-strapped appearances suggest that Liverpool must manage his workload carefully, particularly with important fixtures ahead.

The Final Word
Liverpool needs to approach this game with conviction.
Manchester United will arrive with renewed optimism under Pochettino, and the visitors will see this as an opportunity to make an early statement under their new manager. Liverpool cannot afford to give them the encouragement of an easy start.
