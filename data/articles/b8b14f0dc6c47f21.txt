Boeing Stock Slumps 25% -- Another Director Sees an 'Opportunity to Buy'
Boeing BA director Akhil Johri bought 3,000 shares for about $500,067 on Nov. 17, adding to a series of board-level purchases as the aircraft maker's stock trades near its 52-week low.
Johri paid an average of $166.689 a share, lifting his direct holdings to 3,150 shares. His purchase marks the third reported director buy in less than three weeks, following transactions by Mortimer Buckley and Steven Mollenkopf totaling roughly $1.51 million.
Those earlier buyers were reported to be sitting about 5% to 6% below their entry prices. Boeing shares have fallen roughly 25% to 26% in recent months and recently traded around $161.75 to $163, putting Johri's purchase price modestly above that range.
The company's operating figures offer a mixed backdrop. Boeing delivered 49 aircraft in October, down from 57 in September, but booked 165 gross orders, its strongest October total since 1997. A 140-jet 737 MAX order from aircraft lessor Avolon accounted for most of the new business. Production constraints remain a challenge for cash flow and margins despite the stronger order tally.
