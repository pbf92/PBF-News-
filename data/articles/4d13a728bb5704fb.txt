TITLE: GTA 6 Star Reveals the Story Behind Her Nearly 10-Year Journey With Lucia
OUTLET: Yahoo Tech

Nearly a decade of work went into bringing Grand Theft Auto VI protagonist Lucia Caminos to life. Now, Manni L. Perez is finally sharing the story behind her long journey. The actress has also thanked fans for their overwhelming support.
In a post shared on November 26, Perez thanked fans for their positive response to Rockstar Games' latest release. She wrote, "Life has been such a whirlwind these past couple of days, and I just want to take a moment to say thank you. Thank you to all the new followers, to all the beautiful messages, and the positive reception the game and my character have received."
She then revealed, "I've dedicated almost a decade to bringing Lucia to life. She's been such a significant part of mine, and I am beyond grateful she now gets to be a small part of yours." Perez ended her message by wishing everyone a happy holiday season.

GTA 6 brings a new female protagonist to the forefront
GTA 6 marks a significant change for Rockstar Games by making Lucia Caminos one of the main protagonists. Lucia shares the spotlight with fellow protagonist Jason Duval, whose personal history, ambitions, and decisions are central to the crime-focused narrative.
According to her official character description, Lucia is sharp, cautious, and practical. Her background has shaped her life. Her father taught her to fight, and she eventually moved from Liberty City in search of a better future. She eventually spent time in Leonida Penitentiary after standing up for her family.
She then developed a close connection to Jason, a former army operative who became involved in the drug trade. Together, they navigate the criminal underworld of Vice City and the wider Leonida region. Their relationship is central to the narrative, allowing players to pursue a romantic relationship or keep things platonic.
