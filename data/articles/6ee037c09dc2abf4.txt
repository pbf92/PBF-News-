TITLE: Rockstar Takes Legal Action Against Popular GTA 6 PC Port
OUTLET: 80 Level

The Leonida project's Discord server has been taken down.
Rockstar
It's been almost a week since GTA 6 launched, and the game has already made history as the biggest entertainment launch ever. For now, it's only available on consoles: Rockstar still hasn't announced when PC players can explore the massive world of Leonida, packed with secrets. Of course, some fans weren't willing to wait and took matters into their own hands by working on unofficial GTA 6 PC ports.
Apparently, the leaders behind Leonida, the biggest of these projects, which was attempting to recompile the game's leaked Xbox build for PC, have decided to call it quits following legal action from Rockstar Games. PC Gamer reports that NotRexed, who led the Leonida project, confirmed that Rockstar took down the Discord server and shared an image of the copyright infringement notice they received:
"Last post from me. I wanna clear things up. Rockstar taking my server down was only part of the reason. Like I've said before, I'm a 15 year old kid. Managing school and sports is hard enough without having middle aged men yell and complain to you on Discord. Goodbye."
PC Gamer
All posts from their X/Twitter account have been deleted, but that doesn't necessarily mean attempts to bring GTA 6 to PC are over. Still, the original Leonida project never got very far, so be careful with any so-called "early GTA 6 PC builds" popping up on your feed this week. Chances are, they're malware in disguise, since there is currently no playable GTA 6 PC version available to download.
Grand Theft Auto 5 arrived on PC 19 months after its initial console release. Rockstar still lists Grand Theft Auto 6 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S only.
