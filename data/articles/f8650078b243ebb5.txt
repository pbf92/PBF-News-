TITLE: 10 Most Useful GTA 6 Controls the Game Doesn't Tell You About
OUTLET: Comic Book Resources

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI has quickly become the best-selling game of the year, to no one's surprise. After all, people have been waiting for their next game since Red Dead Redemption 2 released in 2018. Already, people are diving into the game and finding all manner of fun secrets, as they're immersed in one of the greatest open-world experiences of all time.
GTA 6 spends a good part of its first chapter teaching players the basics of playing the game. However, they've left a ton of other things for players to discover on their own, including within the controls. Some of these controls and options will create an entirely different experience for people enjoying this epic classic.
Hiding From the Cops Inside a Car Keeps You Out of Trouble
By this point, everyone's aware of just how hard it can be to get away from cops in GTA VI. However, there are some options that make sneaking away a bit easier. It's not always necessary to create a giant chase scene to escape, and it's probably better for some players if they don't.
By parking the car in place and pressing either Circle or B, the player can have their character duck down in the car. This way, if the police don't know what the player looks like, they won't actively look for their car. This makes things that much easier if players can escape the cops and duck off in an alley for a bit.
Leaving the Car Running Is Essential to Fast Getaways in GTA 6
When people get out of cars in modern open-world games, the car naturally cuts off. However, this can be a big problem in a game like Grand Theft Auto, where escaping from the law is a major part of the game. Yet there's an easy solution for this most people don't know about.
When getting out of the war, instead of pressing triangle to exit, double-tap triangle instead. This will leave the car running even after Jason or Lucia get out. That makes it that much easier to make a quick getaway after robbing a store or a bank.
Kicking While Riding On a Bike Isn't Just Practical, It's Fun
Even the nicest people don't pick up something like Grand Theft Auto with the intent of being perfect citizens. Sometimes it's just fun to cause as much chaos as possible. One way to get away with doing that is kicking people while riding a bike.
It's possible to kick someone while on the bike by just getting close to them and pressing Square or X while riding a bike. With this, players can attack people while on the roads for a good laugh, but there are other purposes, too. While racing other cyclists, a quick way to stop them is by just knocking them off their bikes.
Activating Turn Signals Causes The NPCs to React to the Player's Driving
Compared to previous Grand Theft Auto games, Grand Theft Auto 6 features more simulation features than ever. While players are driving, they can now activate their turn signals. To signal left or right, players can press left or right on the directional pad before making a turn.
While some games might just have this as a fun side feature, this actually has a purpose in GTA 6. While driving the speed limit, activating turn signals in the right spots allows the NPCs to react to the player's actions. They'll actually allow players to merge and make turns without causing any accidents, which is pretty useful for players who've stolen a car and want to get it to Moses without causing too much damage to the vehicle.
Maintaining the Speed Limit Isn't Necessary In GTA 6, but Still Good to Know
Obviously, most players are going to want to get in a car and hit the accelerator to push a car as fast as they can. For many, getting to the next mission is the most important thing, after all. However, there are ways to simply obey the speed limit while playing as well.
On the PS5 in particular, players can jump into the settings and go to Controller, and scroll to the DualSense Wireless Controller Features. From there, simply make sure the Adaptive Triggers Resistance notch is on. After that, players can hold R2 down until they feel a click, which is the main point of resistance. From that point on, while driving, Jason or Lucia will automatically travel the speed limit.
Using Two Cars At Once Makes for Easier Getaways
One of the biggest selling points of Grand Theft Auto VI is rarely having to do missions alone. Jason and Lucia can team up to do bank heists or stick up stores whenever they want. Normally, the character not being controlled will support the player by aiming guns at enemies, but there are other options.
For instance, it's possible to take two cars to any given mission. Players simply press right on the D-Pad, then press X on PS5 or A on Xbox to tell the partner character to do the driving. From there, players can just get into a car on their own, then press right again to bring up the menu and ask them to follow you. They'll stay close behind you, and it's easy to switch cars after the mission to keep the cops from figuring out what vehicle Jason and Lucia are in.
Get a Beached Jetski Back Into the Water Or Turn a Flipped Car Over
One of the complaints about Grand Theft Auto 6 is that players can't flip cars over if they get flipped like they did in older games. It's true, just rocking the control stick won't flip a car over. However, there are options available to flip cars and more when those situations pop up.
When a car is flipped over, simply walk to the side of the car and press L3 or click the left stick on Xbox, which will start to push the car. Eventually, a prompt will appear offering to press a button to push it back over. Likewise, if a player accidentally drives a jetski or boat onto the beach, the same option is available to get the vehicle back out onto the waters. That said, there is a caveat: if the character isn't strong enough, they'll exhaust their stamina too soon, so players better keep lifting.
Activating GTA 6's Auto-Drive Shows Off the Environment
One thing's for sure about Grand Theft Auto VI: it's the prettiest game ever released. Of course, players are often too busy riding around to pay attention to just how gorgeous the world is. However, not only is there a way to enjoy the sights, but there's a way to avoid having to drive as well.
After setting up a waypoint to go to a new destination, players can press down on the D-Pad to bring up a menu. From there, they can press Options on PS5 or Menu on Xbox to get the cinematic camera. From there, players can press circle or B to start auto-driving, with the ability to switch between speeds as well. From there, the game will auto-drive players to their next goal, allowing players to take in the sights and obey the traffic laws.
Advanced Combat Controls Like Lift Slam & Triangle Armbar Ramp Up Fights
While playing Grand Theft Auto VI, it's expected that Jason and Lucia will eventually get into some physical conflicts. Most players will simply mash the attack button to finish off the random fights on the streets of Vice City. However, that can get pretty boring, so fortunately, Rockstar programmed plenty of contextual combat actions. Sure, it's not like players are enjoying a great fighting game, but it'll still spice things up.
For one, if players want to clothesline someone, they can just sprint towards an NPC with L3 or the Left Stick, then press Square on PS5 or X on Xbox. For another option, if they want to hit an NPC with a German Suplex, they can hold the right trigger, push the left stick diagonally down, and mash square or X to pull off a wrestling classic.

They can also hit someone with a Rock Bottom by locking on to a target with the left trigger while standing in front of them, then hold the right trigger to grapple them. From there, they only have to tilt the left stick and press Square or X to pick them up and slam them. There are a ton of other options available, so there's plenty of room to experiment.
Activate Simplified Mini-Games To Speed Things Up
Grand Theft Auto VI is a game players can spend countless hours in already. Between the main narrative, plus all the side content, it can almost be overwhelming as a game. Knowing that, sometimes it might be nice to just push through some of the game's incredible sim elements just to get to the next thing.
One way to do that is activating the simplified mini-games mode. Players can go into settings, go over to gameplay, and scroll down until they see Simplified Mini-Games and turn it on. Doing this will make mini-games like hot-wiring cars and lifting weights much easier.
