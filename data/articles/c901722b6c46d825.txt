TITLE: When Do You Leave The Motel in GTA 6? Chapter Three Apartment, Explained
OUTLET: Insider Gaming

Grand Theft Auto 6 follows the same operating model as Red Dead Redemption 2. For each new chapter, you’ll move to a new location. In Chapter One, you’ll be at Jason’s safehouse in Key Lento. In Chapter Two, you relocate to a motel in Kelly County, near Port Gellhorn. But in Chapter Three, things change dramatically, and you head into Vice City.
This is when you land in your first ‘apartment’ in Leonida, even if it’s a run-down loaner that leaves a lot to be desired. In this guide, I’ll help you figure out when you can leave the motel in GTA 6, because even though Linda is a charming individual, it’s hardly a place to call home for too long.
When Do You Get A New Apartment in GTA 6?
You’ll get your new apartment in GTA 6, located square in the middle of Vice City, at the end of Chapter Two and the beginning of Chapter Three. Once you’ve completed the mission ‘Checking Out’, you’ll head to Vice City to connect with Dre’Quan, who will subsequently introduce you to Boobie Ike, whom you might recognize from the earlier marketing materials.
Boobie has an apartment that you can stay in until you’re on your feet again, but it’s not exactly a luxurious spot.
In terms of the overall story percentage, you’ll make your way to the new apartment in Vice City when you’re around 39% complete. There’s still plenty of life left in the game at this point.
Can You Buy Property in GTA 6?
By this point, you might have noticed that, even though there’s a real estate app in GTA 6, you can’t buy property such as apartments and homes. You can buy garages all over Leonida, and more open up as you explore the story, but that’s about it… For now.
There’s an XBOX Achievement and PlayStation Trophy tied to purchasing all the garages across the map, so make sure you save a little money to do just that. They’re not cheap!
