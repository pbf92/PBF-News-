Cyberpunk 2 director calls GTA 6 a “creative miracle” and says his own sequel will have a different flavour
GTA 6 has impressed the creative director of Cyberpunk 2, and he is not being subtle about it. Igor Sarzyński called Rockstar’s game a “technological and creative miracle” while praising its scale, density and attention to detail.
He also joked that his own game was cancelled after playing it. To be clear, that was a joke, not a cancellation announcement. In a follow-up, Sarzyński said his team is making something of its own with a different flavour.
The comments offer an interesting glimpse of how another open-world developer sees a game that has dominated the conversation since its launch. They do not reveal a release date or finished features for Cyberpunk 2.
What the Cyberpunk director said about GTA 6
In a Bluesky post on November 21, Sarzyński described Rockstar’s game as “probably the single greatest artistic achievement in history” and pointed to its scale, density, coherence and thoughtfulness.
He then wrote that Cyberpunk 2 was cancelled and joked about sweeping leaves in his garden. That playful line should not be mistaken for an update on the game’s production status.
In a later post, he put the comparison in context. “GTA is impressive in a way all great art is,” he wrote, adding that it expands what is possible and calling it a captivating vision.
He is not the only person at CD Projekt to praise Rockstar’s release. As we reported separately, joint CEO Michał Nowakowski also congratulated the studio and called the game a masterpiece.
Different worlds do not have to make the same game
Sarzyński’s follow-up makes the distinction worth remembering. His comment about a different flavour is a broad statement of intent, not a list of mechanics or a promise about the final sequel.
GTA 6 and Cyberpunk 2077 both give players large worlds to explore, but their settings and stories are quite different. It is reasonable to be curious about what developers learn from Rockstar’s work without assuming the next Cyberpunk will copy it.
Our featured image and garage screenshot come from Cyberpunk 2077, the existing game. They are not screenshots of the unreleased sequel. The other images are official GTA 6 material from Rockstar.
GTA 6 is available on consoles
If you want to see the game Sarzyński was discussing, Amazon lists GTA 6 for PlayStation 5. Check the listing before ordering: this version is a code in a box, not a game disc, and it is separate from Cyberpunk 2.
For the sequel, the useful takeaway is more modest: its director is enthusiastic about Rockstar’s achievement and says his team is pursuing its own direction. We will have to wait for CD Projekt RED to share more about the game itself.
