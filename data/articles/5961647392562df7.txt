Titan FX Introduces Titan Options, a New Short-Duration Trading Product
Port Vila, Vanuatu, November 27th, 2026, FinanceWire
Titan Options is Titan FX's planned short-duration options product. The product structure is different from CFDs, but the strategic purpose and fit for traders is clear: it gives traders another way to express a market view on FX, gold, and other instruments of their choosing.
Since 2014, Titan FX has provided active traders with high-leverage CFD trading through MT4 and MT5 across hundreds of instruments. The opportunity to add Titan Options came from strong client demand and wanting to strengthen the Titan product ecosystem.
Titan Options complements the existing FX offering. It is designed for a similar profile of active trader but provides a simpler and shorter-term way to take a view on market direction, with the maximum risk, potential payout and timeframe all known before the trade is placed.
The product offers:
- Turbo durations ranging from 10 to 55 seconds;
- High/Low durations ranging from one minute to 14 hours;
- A maximum loss limited to the amount invested;
- A predetermined payout when the prediction is correct; and
- A clearly defined outcome and timeframe before the trade is placed.
Titan FX will continue investing and improving its core CFD offering as well as explore other exciting initiatives that benefit the trading community.
