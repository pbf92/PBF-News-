TITLE: Perplexity pitches Computer for Meta ads, Klaviyo email marketing and Shopify stores
OUTLET: Digg

Perplexity pitched its Computer tool for e-commerce operations on Nov. 3, 2026, claiming sellers can run Meta ads, Klaviyo email marketing and Shopify stores directly inside Computer. The company also claimed it can automate “every part” of an e-commerce business.
On its e-commerce page, Perplexity describes a workflow across Shopify, Klaviyo and ad accounts: find what's selling, build a promotion and run it. A short description accompanying the page also advertises landing pages and budgets, alongside ads, email and store operations. Those are advertised functions, not demonstrated results.
In a quote-post of the announcement, AravSrinivas claimed many Shopify store owners were already automating operations with Computer. He cited competitive analysis, audits, web apps, Klaviyo campaigns and Instagram ad spend, but gave no usage figures. Another account, derSemih, said they would try reports and audits before putting ad budgets on autopilot.
