Nucor upgraded to Outperform at Morgan Stanley on cash flow outlook
Nucor NUE edged up 0.5% in Tuesday's trading as Morgan Stanley upgraded shares to Outperform from Equal Weight with a $310 price target, up from $300 previously, as a strong balance sheet combined with declining capex and volume growth from recent investments support expanding free cash flow and higher returns.
The bank raised its near-term steel price forecasts as tight supply sustains hot-rolled coil above $1,100/st H1 2027, and analyst Carlos De Alba said he continues to expect profitability across domestic steel producers to peak in early 2027 before moderating as prices decline over the remainder of the year.
De Alba said he remains selective on steel equities as a result and favors "defensive quality," prompting an upgrade to Nucor NUE as the company approaches the end of its latest capitalspending cycle, with recent investments beginning to contribute more meaningfully to earnings in the coming quarters.
The analyst forecast capex declining from $3.4B in 2025 and $2.5B in 2026 to an estimated $1.5B annually in 2027-28, supporting a meaningful free cash flow inflection—with an ~11% FCF yield in the next two years—and greater flexibility for shareholder returns.
