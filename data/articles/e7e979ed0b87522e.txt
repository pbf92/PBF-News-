“A masterpiece”: Cyberpunk’s co-CEO congratulates Rockstar on GTA 6
CD Projekt joint CEO Michał Nowakowski has congratulated Rockstar on Grand Theft Auto VI, calling it a masterpiece while saying he plans to spend the coming weeks and months exploring it.
His November 19 message praised the people who made the game and acknowledged how difficult an achievement like that can be. It is a warm launch-day response from the company behind Cyberpunk 2077 and The Witcher.
Nowakowski's comment is a congratulatory message, not a detailed review. He did not say he had completed the game; in fact, he wrote that he intended to explore it over a longer period.
From Night City to Vice City
The CD Projekt executive shared a launch-day greeting from the official Cyberpunk 2077 account. That message played on the names of the two games' cities and congratulated Rockstar.
Other CD Projekt developers joined in. Paweł Sasko, associate game director on the next Cyberpunk, praised an early look at the game, while The Witcher 4 director Sebastian Kalemba said he was looking forward to spending a weekend with it.
It is easy to see why people connect the games: both Cyberpunk 2077 and GTA VI ask players to spend time in a detailed fictional setting. They are still different games, with different stories and design goals, so a launch-day compliment is best read as appreciation between developers rather than a judgment about which one is better.
A major launch for Rockstar
GTA VI launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19. The following day, Take-Two estimated $3.79 billion in first-day retail sales. That figure is the publisher's sales estimate, not a count of players.
Rockstar's story follows Jason and Lucia in Leonida, a fictional state that includes Vice City. The publisher describes them as a couple drawn into a conspiracy after a job goes wrong. Nowakowski did not single out a particular mission or character in his message.
