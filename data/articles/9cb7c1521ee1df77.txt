TITLE: Steven Gerrard claims Mauricio Pochettino 'disrespected' Man United star against Liverpool
OUTLET: Yahoo News Singapore

Steven Gerrard has blasted Mauricio Pochettino over his 'disrespectful' call to drop Harry Maguire against Liverpool. The newly appointed Manchester United manager raised eyebrows by handing Matthijs de Ligt just his second start since November 2025.
De Ligt — who had been sidelined for almost a year due to a serious back injury — struggled from the outset, and Maguire was quickly thrown into the defensive line following Leny Yoro's early red card, with United already trailing 2-0 when he entered the fray in the 22nd minute. Having departed the U.S. Men's National Team job on Tuesday, Pochettino had only a matter of hours to familiarize himself with his squad ahead of the trip to Anfield.
The Red Devils boss went with De Ligt and watched his team suffer a humiliating 4-0 defeat at the hands of their fiercest rivals. Gerrard was left bewildered by the selection, siding with former teammate Jamie Carragher, who also took aim at Pochettino for fielding the Dutchman ahead of Maguire, via The Mirror.
"I thought he got it wrong," Gerrard told the Overlap Daily. "I thought he got it wrong in the back line. I think to start someone (De Ligt) in a game of that magnitude, it's the biggest game in the country. All eyes are on it all over the world.
"To play someone that hadn't played since last November. You can understand the manager wanting to come in and put his stamp on it and change a few things that had been going on previously, but Harry Maguire's their best defender at the moment. Forget the youngsters that are maybe not ready at the moment. Matthijs de Ligt's been out for a year.
"Harry Maguire's their best defender at the moment. He should have started the game. And he made a difference when he came on. And he was one who was putting his body on the line. He made a great block, I think it was around the 38th, 39th minute, which stopped a certain goal.
"And I've watched Manchester United a lot this season, and he's one who's having a go. He's one who is rolling his sleeves up and having a go. Okay, he hasn't been perfect, but to not play him and put De Ligt in before him, I thought it was a bit disrespectful."

United supporters are pressing club executives to bring in a new center-back during January, but Gerrard has cautioned them about the difficulties of the winter transfer window.
"Who's going to let world-class center-halves go in January?" the Liverpool legend asked. "Unless someone's prepared to pay astronomical money. That's what's going to have to happen."
