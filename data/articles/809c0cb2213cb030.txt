Top Down Analysis: Why an "87.7% Win Rate" Can Still Lose Money
An "87.7% win rate" sounds like the holy grail. I tested a simple top-down strategy on 13 months of gold. The win rate reached 77.5%, and it still lost money. Here's why, and how to use top-down analysis the right way.
◆ THE PROBLEM WITH WIN RATE
Win rate shows how often you win, not how much. If your stop is $10 and your target is $2, you need to win 83% of the time just to break even.
Key idea: a high win rate is easy to fake. Just shrink the target.
◆ TOP-DOWN IN 3 STEPS
D1, direction: price above the 50 EMA, buy only. Below, sell only.
H4, zone: wait for price to pull back to the 20 EMA.
H1, trigger: enter when a candle closes back across its 20 EMA.
Stop: just past the low of the last 6 hourly candles (the high for sells).
◆ WHAT THE TEST SHOWED
Same entries, same stops, only the target changed. Gold, November 2025 to November 2026:
Target 0.2R: 77.5% wins, lost money
Target 1R: 46.1% wins, lost money
Target 3R: 35.7% wins, made money
Higher win rate, worse result. Spread and fees are not included, so the small profit at 3R is not a guarantee.
◆ HOW TO USE IT
Trade only in the D1 direction.
Let price come to your H4 zone. Don't chase.
Enter on an H1 close, not a touch.
Never set a target smaller than your stop.
◆ CONCLUSION
Don't ask "how often does it win?" Ask "how much does each win pay?"
