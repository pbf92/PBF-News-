Granite Ridge Resources: Grey Rock Plans to Distribute 14 Million Shares
Less than 1 min read
Granite Ridge Resources announced that Grey Rock affiliates plan to distribute 14 million shares to fund limited partners on or about November 24, 2026.
Key Highlights:
- Grey Rock affiliates intend to distribute 14 million Granite Ridge shares in kind under an effective resale registration statement.
- After the distribution, Grey Rock and its affiliates are expected to own approximately 30% of Granite Ridge’s outstanding common stock.
- The distribution is not underwritten; Granite Ridge will issue no shares, receive no proceeds, and its share count will remain unchanged.
- The distribution will not change Granite Ridge’s Master Services Agreement or opportunity-sharing and Operated Partnerships arrangements with Grey Rock.
Original SEC Filing: Granite Ridge Resources, Inc. [ GRNT ] - 8-K - Nov. 19, 2026
