TITLE: Napoli make initial contact for Manchester United’s Joshua Zirkzee
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Joshua Zirkzee's career has been faltering at Manchester United, where he continues to fall short of game time. A move back to the Serie A has previously never come to fruition but that can possibly change in January.
In the summer, Fiorentina and Juventus were keen on the Dutchman. While the Bianconeri could never make a formal approach, La Viola could never seek acceptance from the player for a transfer.
Zirkzee started for Manchester United against Sporting this week but that may not change his future. Radio Kiss Kiss Napoli – via AreaNapoli, now report that Napoli are keen on Zirkzee, as they look to add an extra striker to their squad in January.
Lorenzo Lucca has disappointed and this leaves them short in the attacking positions.
As a result, sporting director Giovanni Manna has already opened discussions with Manchester United over a deal. Contact has also been made with Zirkzee's entourage, with Corriere dello Sport indicating that Napoli's aim is to iron out a loan deal.
The Red Devils' openness remains to be seen but it is clear that the player retains several fans in the Serie A.
