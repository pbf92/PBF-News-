Spire downgraded at Jefferies ahead of rate case outcomes
Spire SR slipped 1.6% in Thursday's trading as Jefferies downgraded the natural gas utility to Hold from Buy with an unchanged $94 price target, saying recent wins leave a more balanced risk/reward while remaining constructive on the company's longer-term earnings profile.
Spire SR shares have moved from local distribution company laggard earlier this year to the sector's strongest performer over the past three months, leaving fewer near-term catalysts to drive the stock higher, Jefferies analyst Julien Dumoulin-Smith said.
The company's portfolio has transitioned toward a cleaner regulated gas utility mix, with Tennessee providing a constructive growth jurisdiction and Missouri Gas adding contracted pipeline earnings, Dumoulin-Smith said, forecasting a 7.3% FY 2028-31 earnings per share compound annual growth rate, modestly above the 7% guidance midpoint, with the Missouri recovery supporting the FY 2028 step-up.
The Missouri rate case remains the largest FY 2028 swing factor, according to the analyst, who modeled $111M, or ~65%, of the $171M of Spire's SR net new request, supporting a 9.8% earned return on equity.
