TITLE: Drone Operator Films Manchester United's 1st Training Session Under Mauricio Pochettino
OUTLET: Bleacher Report

Mauricio Pochettino's first training session at Manchester United turned into an unexpectedly public affair.
According to Simon Stone of BBC Sport, "footage was captured by a drone flown next to the club's Carrington training ground" from Thursday's session. Twelve minutes of that footage was then uploaded onto YouTube, showing Poch working with Harry Maguire, Kobbie Mainoo, Amad Diallo, Matheus Cunha, Bryan Mbeumo, Ayden Heaven, Andrey Santos, Joshua Zirkzee and Harry Amass.
Two other groups of players were working separately during the training session.
The drone operator said he was flying over "unrestricted airspace" and "remained in line of sight at all times, it didn't fly over anyone or even near anyone so was a perfectly legal flight going by the CAA rules that regulates drone usage. The drone is correctly labelled, and I hold all the relevant licenses, public liability and insurance required."
It remains to be seen if Manchester United share the drone operator's assessment of the situation, though the team's media channel also filmed the session, per Simon.
