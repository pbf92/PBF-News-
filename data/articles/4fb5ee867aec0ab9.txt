TITLE: What Are the Most Popular Thanksgiving Dishes? Turkey, Potatoes and Stuffing Lead
OUTLET: Mitchell Now

MITCHELL, SD (Mitchell Now) — Turkey, mashed potatoes, stuffing, bread or rolls, and ham ranked as the five most popular Thanksgiving dishes in a national YouGov survey comparing holiday foods.
With Thanksgiving arriving Thursday, Nov. 26, Mitchell families planning their menus can look to national polling for a snapshot of America’s holiday favorites.

The percentages show how often each food won its comparisons, rather than how many Americans selected it as their single favorite.
What Is The Most Popular Thanksgiving Side Dish?
Mashed potatoes ranked as the top Thanksgiving side in that survey, edging stuffing by one percentage point. Among Midwestern respondents, mashed potatoes tied turkey, with both winning 85% of their matchups.
What Do Americans Eat For Thanksgiving Dinner?
A separate YouGov survey conducted Nov. 14-17, 2025, found 69% of Americans expected to eat turkey. Stuffing and mashed potatoes followed at 61% each, ahead of pie at 60%, bread or rolls at 58% and gravy at 56%.
That survey included 1,120 U.S. adult citizens, with an approximate margin of error of 4 percentage points. Both surveys reflect national findings, rather than a ranking specific to Mitchell or South Dakota.
