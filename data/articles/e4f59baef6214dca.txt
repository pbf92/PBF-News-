TITLE: GTA 6 gets its first 'Mount Chilliad'-esque mystery and I'm already losing sleep over it
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Grand Theft Auto VI is officially out in the wild, and after rave reviews it’s safe to say Rockstar Games delivered exactly what we wanted.
GAMINGbible gave GTA VI a glowing 10/10 review and even got a few words from Rockstar’s co-lead Rob Nelson to tell us more about its open-world design, and what was learned from Red Dead Redemption 2.
Rockstar has crafted one of the most realistic video game worlds we’ve ever seen, but that’s not to say the more goofy elements from Grand Theft Auto V aren’t still weaved into its code.
During my 70+ hours with the game I stumbled upon a dark mystery that needs to be solved…before I go insane thinking about it.
GTA 6 is hiding a dark secret in Kalaga National Park
Leaving the hustle and bustle of Vice City behind, some of my exploration in GTA VI took me out into the wilderness.
Advert
To tell the truth, when I initially saw the dense foliage and mountains I worried Rockstar Games had repeated the sins of the past. GTA V’s Blaine County was pretty plain and boring, but Mount Chiliad always stood out through more than just its size.
While there are no mysteries to GTA VI’s mountains (that I know of) the area surrounding them is rife with secrets.
Near the gold mines, you’ll find some man-made tunnels and caves lying dormant. They’re not much to look at, but one in particular felt very Until Dawn-coded.
You can find the cave on the map point below, and when you reach it, walk all the way down to the end of the tunnel till you reach the bend. Make sure the flashlight is equipped as it gets very dark in there.
On the wall you’ll spot a trailer of blood leading upwards to an inaccessible platform. Far above it is an opening that leads to the surface, and while you find it above it’s not a safe way down. If you drop through the hole you won’t survive the fall.
Since discovering this though I’ve been desperate to find out what’s up there, and what could have left that blood trail.
My first thought was some sort of bear or other wild animal, but knowing Rockstar’s love of the occult it wouldn’t surprise me to learn skinwalkers could be found in the game.
I’ve tried driving a pick-up truck down the tunnel to see if I could climb atop and scale my way up from there, but even that’s not enough. I’ll pile up as many vehciles as I have to though, and the only reason I haven’t done it already is because everytime I go to get said vehicles something else distracts me.
Even more magical is knowing this is just one of many secrets found in GTA VI. We’re going ot be scouring Leonida for months.
