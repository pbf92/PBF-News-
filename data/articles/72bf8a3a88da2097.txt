TITLE: Rockstar Publishes Full GTA 6 Credits — But Beware, They Contain Major Spoilers
OUTLET: IGN

With GTA 6 now launched across the world, Rockstar has published the game's full credits online — with some major spoilers hidden in plain view.
Thousands of developers are named on Rockstar's GTA 6 credits page, which also includes a list of the dozens of cast members who portray characters in the game. We reckon around 6,000 names are on the list.
As revealed earlier this week when her Game Awards nomination was announced, Manni L. Perez is the actress behind main character Lucia Caminos. And as confirmed today via our first photo of the game's stars together in real life, Lucia's partner-in-crime Jason Duval is played by actor Dylan Rourke.
Other major characters include Donnie played by True Detective's Brad Carter, Cal played by former Saturday Night Live cast member Bobby Moynihan, Brian Heder played by King of the Hill voice actor Stephen Root, Lori Heder played by prolific voice actress Orlagh Cassidy and Kenton Rees played by Trinidadian singer and songwriter Machel Montano.
Scroll further down the cast list and players will likely spot something that we're going to absolutely label as a spoiler — it's your choice whether you want to read more right now.
Celebrity names in the cast list include a string of celebrity radio hosts, including "Weird Al" Yankovic as DJ Al, Brigitte Nielsen as Madame Brigitte, Puerto Rican rapper and Super Bowl LX halftime show star Bad Bunny as El Martillo, British acting legend Jeremy Irons as DJ Dicky, Gloria and Emilio Estefan as themselves, The Big Bang Theory's Brian Posehn as Big Fan Brian, and the legendary Keith Richards as DJ Keith.
Development-wise, as you'd expect, Rockstar Games founder and executive producer Sam Houser leads the credits list, alongside head of publishing Jennifer Kolbe, plus co-studio heads Rob Nelson and Aaron Garbut. If you're searching for them, the 34 staff fired by Rockstar last year in controversial circumstances are all credited.
There's no sign of Sam Houser's brother and Rockstar co-founder Dan Houser, who left Rockstar in 2020 — despite GTA 6's early development already being underway by this point. GTA 6 is the franchise's first major 3D release not to include his name in the credits. (And no, there's no sign of Leslie Benzies' name either, though he departed several years prior, back in 2016.)
Clearly, GTA 6 was an enormous development effort across Rockstar Games and its parent company, Take-Two, which has yet to announce early sales numbers for the game. We don't know how much GTA 6 cost to make, but we're looking at the most expensive video game ever made, with a budget potentially over the $1 billion mark. Based on preorder estimates, Take-Two will have made its money back on preorders alone.
