TITLE: Disney execs asked for 'gay turtles' in Noah's Ark, claims ex-animator
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

A former Disney animator has claimed that studio executives asked him to include homosexual animal couples in a biblical retelling of Noah’s Ark.
Davy Liu, who worked as a layout artist in the 1990s on projects including Aladdin and Beauty and the Beast, said he left after believing the studio’s direction was clashing with his Christian beliefs.
Speaking on the Transforming Communities with Caleb Altmeyer podcast, released on 11 November, Lui said his initial encouragement for how the biblical account of Moses influenced the Lion King was soon dashed by “woke agendas”.
Liu left Disney to work on Christian-themed projects, going on to pitch a story of Noah’s Ark from the animals’ perspective called The Giant Leaf.
He said: “When I pitched it on Disney’s top floor, to the executives, they wanted two gay turtles going into the ark. I said, ‘No can do’... Those are the battles that I had working for Disney.”
“God says, if I save you from burning hell and if your life is just a reflection of one man’s praise, of a statue, you know, I'd rather you bring one child to heaven than a billion Oscars,” Liu added.
