TITLE: Rio Ngumoha makes Liverpool history with remarkable display against Manchester United
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

The potential of Rio Ngumoha has been clear from his first opportunities in a Liverpool shirt. Now, it appears, the shirt is his to lose.
Even among a collection of forward talents signed for fees upwards of £100m, Ngumoha looks at home. Against Manchester United on Sunday, he was arguably the shining light.
The 18-year-old is the youngest player since Opta records began to have at least four shots, create four chances and complete four take-ons in a singlePremier League game.
The Liverpool winger hit all three marks in Sunday's 4-0 win over 10-man Manchester United at Anfield, aged 18 years and 85 days.
The only other teenager to manage it was Raheem Sterling against Everton in September 2014, at 19 years and 293 days, sinceOpta first collected the data 20 seasons ago.
Ngumoha started in place of the injured Cody Gakpo. He beat Diogo Dalot on the outside to cross for Florian Wirtz's goal, then finished Bradley Barcola's square ball past Senne Lammens in front of the Kop. That goal saw him become the youngest ever Liverpool goalscorer against Manchester United, and the youngest scorer in the fixture during thePremier League era.
Ngumoha's dribbling numbers rival Jeremy Doku
Ngumoha attempted 11 dribbles in his 78 minutes on the pitch. Only Theo Walcott (December 2006) and Sterling (September 2012) have attempted 10 or more in a Premier League match at a younger age.
Just three players have tried more dribbles in a league game this season, and two of those performances belonged to Manchester City's Jeremy Doku, with 20 against Aston Villa and 15 against Brighton.
His four chances created were matched only by Wirtz, who carved out 10 opportunities as Liverpool ruthlessly punished 10-man Manchester United, who had Leny Yoro sent off early on. Ngumoha also had 80 touches, 20 of them in the United penalty area, with only Dominik Szoboszlai (109) and Milos Kerkez (83) seeing more of the ball, both over the full 90 minutes.
He is also the fourth-youngest player to score and assist in the same Premier League match, after Owen, Alan Smith and Evan Ferguson.
