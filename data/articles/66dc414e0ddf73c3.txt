TITLE: GTA 6's huge success is bad news for anyone hoping to buy a PS5 this Black Friday
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

After years of anticipation, GTA 6 is finally here, and to the surprise of absolutely no one, Rockstar Games' latest blockbuster has been a monumental success. However, that success could come at a cost for PlayStation fans hoping to bag a bargain this Black Friday.
Whether you've been holding off upgrading from a PS4 or you're looking to trade in your launch PS5 for a Slim or PS5 Pro, Black Friday and the build-up to Christmas would seemingly be the perfect time to take the plunge. Unfortunately, the overwhelming popularity of Grand Theft Auto VI may have thrown a spanner in the works.
From stock shortages following its launch in 2020 to rising console prices, getting your hands on a PlayStation 5 hasn't always been easy, and prospective buyers might once again struggle to find a console at its recommended retail price, let alone a discounted one.
GTA 6 causes PS5 stock shortages ahead of Black Friday
As reported by Notebookcheck, PS5 stock shortages have hit several regions following the launch of GTA VI, including the US, UK, parts of Europe and Asia.
In the US, even Sony's own PlayStation Direct store has struggled to meet demand, as has Amazon. Over in Singapore, retailers have reportedly been inundated with calls from Rockstar Games fans desperate to get their hands on a console.
Advert
It's a similar story on this side of the pond, with What Hi-Fi? reporting that Currys and John Lewis were out of stock on November 25, with limited availability at Argos and Amazon via console bundles. Meanwhile, some third-party sellers have listed the PS5 Digital Edition and PS5 Pro for as much as £899 and £1,475, respectively.
When I originally reported on GameStop selling pre-owned PS5 Pro consoles in October, the US retailer was charging a whopping $1,400. At the time of writing, that price appears to have increased by another $100.
With prices such as these, you'd be forgiven for drawing comparisons with scalpers, who are often criticised for selling sought-after hardware at inflated prices.
Of course, GTA VI isn't entirely to blame. Hardware supply issues, coupled with the increased demand that traditionally comes with Black Friday and Christmas, certainly haven't helped matters.
If you're still looking to get your hands on a console, it might be worth keeping an eye on bundles such as the PS5 Digital Edition with GTA VI at EE.
However, if you're already fortunate enough to own a PS5, there are still some decent bargains to be had in Currys' PlayStation Black Friday sale, including a DualSense wireless controller for just £43, saving £16.99. We've also rounded up some of the best deals on PlayStation games and accessories.
GTA 6's record-breaking launch proves it's a PS5 console seller
As we reported earlier this week, GTA VI reportedly sold 32.5 million copies across PS5 and Xbox Series X/S before it was even released, with estimated revenue reaching an eye-watering $3.79 billion by the end of launch day.
Sony has since confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI is the biggest video game launch in PlayStation history, and when you take into account some of the fantastic games released across five generations of PlayStation consoles, that's some achievement.
According to data shared on the PlayStation Blog, between 19 and 22 November 2026:
- More than 39 million unique PS5 players experienced Jason and Lucia's story.
- GTA VI set a new PlayStation record for concurrent players.
- PS5 players clocked up a combined 683 million hours exploring Leonida, averaging 17.4 hours per player.
- GTA VI was the first game played on 70% of newly connected PS5 consoles.
Sony also recorded its biggest single-day surge in newly connected PS5 consoles since 2020, outside of the end-of-year holiday periods.
If ever there was an example of a video game being a console seller, this is surely it.
How to play GTA 6 without buying a PS5
Thankfully, if you're desperate to explore the streets of Vice City but are struggling to get your hands on a PS5, there are potentially cheaper alternatives.
As we reported earlier this week, you can now play GTA VI on the PlayStation Portal without even owning a PS5. All you'll need is Sony's handheld, a PlayStation Plus Premium subscription, an eligible digital copy of the game and a reliable internet connection, provided cloud streaming is supported in your region.
Alternatively, if you're not too fussed about which platform you play GTA VI on, the Xbox Series S could be a more affordable option. What's more, even Xbox One owners can enjoy Rockstar Games' latest blockbuster via Xbox Cloud Gaming with an eligible Game Pass subscription and a digital copy of the game.
Hopefully, PS5 stock levels will improve ahead of Christmas. However, as tempting as it might be to splash the cash, I'd recommend exercising a little patience rather than paying hundreds of pounds or dollars over the odds for a new console.
After all, Vice City isn't going anywhere.
