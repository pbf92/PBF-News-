TITLE: The 10 highest-grossing video games of all time GTA 6 will inevitably overtake
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

GTA 6 is expected to become one of the biggest entertainment launches of all time. Still, even Rockstar Games' highly anticipated sequel faces some serious competition if it's to become the highest-grossing video game ever. After all, some of the industry's biggest games have already generated billions of dollars in revenue.
Not only does Grand Theft Auto VI have its predecessor, 2013's GTA V, to contend with, but also gaming giants such as Fortnite, Minecraft and Candy Crush Saga. However, when it comes to the games that have made the most money, there may be a few lesser-known titles that will surprise you.
GTA VI is scheduled to launch worldwide on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, with expectations of record-breaking sales and revenue for Rockstar Games and its parent company, Take-Two Interactive. But how will it compare to the highest-grossing video games of all time, and could it eventually take the top spot? Let's take a look.
What counts as the highest-grossing video game?
Before we get into the nitty-gritty details, it's worth explaining how we've ranked the highest-grossing games of all time. We're looking at the reported or estimated lifetime revenue of individual games, including full-game sales, DLC, expansions, micro-transactions, and subscriptions directly linked to a game.
We're not including merchandise, hardware sales, movie or TV adaptations, or revenue generated by an entire franchise. It's also worth remembering that the highest-grossing game isn't necessarily the best-selling, with revenue coming from more than just copies sold.

Finally, these figures represent revenue, not profit, and haven't been adjusted for inflation. Where official figures aren't available, we've used estimates from reputable industry analysts.
You might be wondering why Minecraft, the best-selling video game of all time with over 425 million copies sold, isn't included in this list. While Microsoft's sandbox phenomenon has undoubtedly generated billions in revenue, its total lifetime earnings from game sales, subscriptions, and in-game purchases haven't been publicly confirmed. As such, rather than include an unreliable estimate, we've left Minecraft out of the rankings.
The 10 highest-grossing video games of all time
Dungeon Fighter Online – over $22 billion reported
Developer: Neople
Publisher: Nexon
Released: 2005
Platform: PC (free-to-play)
This side-scrolling action RPG might not be as familiar to Western gamers as Fortnite or GTA V, but that hasn't stopped Dungeon Fighter Online from becoming one of the biggest money-makers in gaming history.
Originally released in South Korea in 2005, this dungeon-crawling RPG has all the ingredients that fans of the genre appreciate, including fast-paced action, character progression and multiplayer gameplay. Being a free-to-play game, it also includes optional in-game purchases, such as items and cosmetics.
According to Nexon's official investor website, the Dungeon&Fighter franchise has generated more than $22 billion in lifetime gross revenue, with over 850 million registered users.
The original PC game remains active to this day, continuing to generate revenue. However, it's worth noting that the $22 billion figure covers the wider franchise and cannot be attributed entirely to the original game.
Fortnite – over $20 billion reported
Developer/publisher: Epic Games
Released: 2017
Platforms: PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Switch and mobile (free-to-play)
Very few games have made an impact quite like Fortnite, both culturally and financially, since its arrival in 2017. It's fair to say that Epic Games' battle royale has become a global phenomenon, attracting millions of players and establishing itself as one of the most recognisable games in the world.
On its own, Fortnite is fun to play without spending a penny, but with regular seasonal events, new content, appealing cosmetic collaborations, emotes and more, players have plenty of reasons to spend money on V-Bucks.
In November 2023, a biography of former Epic Games executive Donald Mustard claimed that Fortnite had generated more than $20 billion in revenue.
However, it's worth keeping in mind that Epic Games hasn't publicly released a complete, audited lifetime revenue total. Nevertheless, Fortnite remains hugely popular, with regular updates and collaborations helping to maintain its success.
Candy Crush Saga – $20 billion reported
Developer/publisher: King
Released: 2012
Platforms: Mobile, browser and Windows (free-to-play)
Despite its continued success since launching in 2012, King's Candy Crush Saga is sometimes overlooked by traditional gaming audiences, despite being one of the most recognisable mobile games ever.
Candy Crush Saga is also one of the most accessible games you could play, not only because of its availability on mobile devices, but also because its simple gameplay appeals to people who might not even consider themselves gamers.
Players can spend real money on extra moves and boosters, especially when they're just a few moves away from overcoming a particularly tricky puzzle.
In September 2023, King announced that the Candy Crush franchise had reached $20 billion in lifetime revenue, with Candy Crush Saga playing a major role in that success.
However, the important distinction is that this figure covers the wider franchise rather than the original game alone. Nevertheless, Candy Crush Saga remains commercially active, with new levels and updates continuing to attract players.
Honor of Kings – nearly $17.2 billion estimated
Developer: TiMi Studio Group
Publisher: Tencent/Level Infinite
Released: 2015
Platforms: iOS and Android (free-to-play)
Honor of Kings is another free-to-play game that hasn't enjoyed quite the same level of recognition in the West as it has in its home country of China.
Developed by TiMi Studio Group, Honor of Kings is a hugely successful multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game.
Originally released in 2015, it sees teams of five players compete in fast-paced battles, similar to those found in League of Legends. Much of its revenue comes from optional in-game purchases, including character skins and seasonal content.
According to Sensor Tower, Honor of Kings had generated approximately $17.2 billion in estimated lifetime mobile revenue by August 2024, establishing it as one of the highest-earning mobile games ever.
While the game has expanded into other regions, its success in China remains a major reason for its extraordinary revenue. Honor of Kings continues to receive updates and remains commercially active.
CrossFire – over $13.5 billion reported
Developer/publisher: Smilegate Entertainment
Released: 2007
Platform: Primarily PC (free-to-play)
CrossFire might not have the same level of recognition in the West as a military first-person shooter such as Call of Duty, but since its launch in 2007, it has become one of the most commercially successful games in the genre.
Over the years, CrossFire's tactical gameplay and competitive multiplayer modes, which pit opposing teams against one another, have been key ingredients in its success.
Its free-to-play business model has also helped attract a massive audience, particularly in Asian markets, while optional in-game purchases have generated substantial revenue.
According to Smilegate's official company fact sheet, CrossFire has accumulated more than $13.5 billion in revenue and attracted over 1.1 billion registered users.
However, Smilegate doesn't clearly separate the original PC game's earnings from those of the wider franchise in its published figures.
The original game remains active, with the franchise continuing to expand through other releases.
Monster Strike – $11.4 billion estimated
Developer/publisher: Mixi
Released: 2013
Platforms: iOS and Android (free-to-play)
Monster Strike is yet another game that has possibly flown under many Western gamers' radars.
Developed by Japanese studio Mixi, this free-to-play mobile game combines monster collecting with physics-based battles. While it might appeal to fans of Pokémon, its gameplay is quite different, with players launching their monsters around the screen to defeat enemies.
Most of its success has come from Japan, but that hasn't stopped Monster Strike from generating extraordinary amounts of money.
According to Sensor Tower data reported by PocketGamer.biz in April 2025, Monster Strike had generated approximately $11.4 billion in gross mobile revenue.
It previously had an English-language version, which closed in 2017. However, thanks to regular events, updates and optional in-game purchases, Monster Strike continues to enjoy commercial success in Japan.
Clash of Clans – over $10 billion reported
Developer/publisher: Supercell
Released: 2012
Platforms: iOS and Android (free-to-play)
Even if you're not much of a mobile gamer, just like Candy Crush Saga, there's a good chance you've heard of Clash of Clans, one of the most successful strategy games on the platform.
Developed by Supercell, Clash of Clans tasks players with building villages, upgrading defences and attacking rival settlements. Players can enjoy the game on their own or join clans to compete against others around the world.
Its addictive gameplay loop, regular updates and events have helped keep loyal players coming back for more. Similar to Fortnite, Clash of Clans is also no stranger to celebrity collaborations, including appearances involving WWE Superstar Cody Rhodes.
In September 2023, Supercell CEO Ilkka Paananen confirmed that Clash of Clans had generated more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. More than a decade after its launch, Clash of Clans remains commercially active, with optional in-game purchases continuing to support its ongoing development.
Pokémon GO – approximately $9.1 billion estimated
Original developer: Niantic
Current operator: Scopely
Released: 2016
Platforms: iOS and Android (free-to-play)
While the likes of Candy Crush Saga appeal to players who might not consider themselves gamers, Pokémon GO has also managed to attract people who aren't necessarily fans of the long-running Pokémon franchise.
Originally released by Niantic in 2016, Pokémon GO encourages players to explore the real world using their mobile devices to catch fan-favourite creatures such as Pikachu and Charmander, battle at Gyms and take part in raids against powerful Pokémon, including Mewtwo.
Pokémon GO is also one of the most sociable games you could play, especially when a major community event is taking place.
While it's free-to-play, Pokémon GO offers plenty of optional in-game purchases, including PokéCoins and event tickets.
According to AppMagic estimates reported by PocketGamer.biz, Pokémon GO had generated approximately $9.1 billion in gross player spending across the App Store and Google Play by July 2026.
That figure doesn't include revenue from its official web store or other payment channels, meaning total consumer spending could be higher.
The game remains active under Scopely, which acquired Niantic's games business in 2025.
League of Legends – lifetime revenue not officially disclosed
Developer/publisher: Riot Games
Released: 2009
Platforms: PC and Mac (free-to-play)
We mentioned League of Legends earlier, and for good reason, because this 2009 free-to-play MOBA has become a huge hit, especially on the esports scene.
Developed by Riot Games, League of Legends pits two teams of five players against one another, with the ultimate goal of destroying the opposing team's base.
You can easily enjoy League of Legends without spending a penny, but with an enormous selection of optional cosmetics and other purchases, even relatively small amounts of spending can add up when millions of players are involved.
Riot Games hasn't published a verified lifetime revenue figure for the original game. However, industry analyst SuperData estimated that League of Legends generated $2.1 billion in 2017 and a further $1.75 billion in 2020.
