TITLE: Anthropic IPO — Maker of Claude Breaks Records With Massive Nasdaq Debut
OUTLET: The Korea Daily

The day is finally here. Anthropic, the AI startup behind Claude, is making its official Nasdaq debut at 11:30 PM tonight. Overwhelmed by surging investor demand for its shares, the initial target price range of $70 to $78 was completely bypassed as the final IPO price was locked in at $83 per share, shattering the ceiling of its original expectations.
This massive public offering is set to raise approximately $107.3 billion, equivalent to about 143 trillion won. This easily surpasses the previous record held by SpaceX ($86.2 billion), making it the largest IPO in history. The final remaining milestone is watching where the opening trade price settles.
In major initial public offerings, matching buy and sell orders takes time before the initial transaction price is finalized. Key points to watch include whether the opening price will exceed the offering price and if it can maintain those gains until the closing bell. The stock prices of major tech giants intertwined with Anthropic, such as Nvidia, Amazon, and Google, are also expected to fluctuate. Furthermore, this event sets a brand new valuation benchmark for OpenAI, which has yet to go public.
Through this IPO, Anthropic is slated to secure around $105 billion (140 trillion won) in capital. The company announced that these substantial funds will be directed toward building out the computing infrastructure required to power its advanced AI models and securing top-tier talent. Co-founders will also amass immense wealth alongside the public offering. Seven co-founders, including siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei, plan to sell approximately 2.6 trillion won worth of their existing shares during the offering. Even after the listing, they will maintain roughly 3% stakes each and secure 50.1% of the general shareholder voting rights through a single Class F special share held by a jointly established entity.
Global Money Club, an American stock investment media outlet created through a joint venture between Bloomberg and JoongAng Ilbo, has prepared a dedicated ‘Anthropic Listing Live’ page to aggregate all related updates. Global Money Club has published roughly 300 reports on Anthropic over the past year, closely tracking the company from the moment Claude shook the AI industry until the countdown to its market debut.
The dedicated coverage page features a variety of content, including the ‘IPO Unboxing’ series by reporters Yong-hwan Jung and Hwan-hee Eo—who meticulously analyzed over 300 pages of the S-1 registration statement for nearly a month—alongside a ‘Listing Timeline’ tracking key milestones from the Claude shock in the spring to its Nasdaq debut week, and a ‘Bloomberg Breaking News’ tab delivering real-time financial updates translated into Korean. Witness this historic Anthropic listing tonight alongside Global Money Club.
