Route1 announces C$350,000 private placement offering
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- Route1 () said on Tuesday that it launched a non-brokered private placement of units at a price of C$0.09 per unit for gross proceeds of up to C$350,000.
- Each unit consists of one common share in the capital of the company and one common share purchase warrant.
- Each warrant entitles the holder to purchase one common share at a price of C$0.13 for a period of 12 months following the issue date of the units.
- Route1 intends to put the net proceeds from the offering and the previously announced patent sale toward customer deployments in parking management and public safety.
- The offering is expected to close in multiple tranches, with the first tranche anticipated to close on or about December 4, 2026.
