TITLE: Turmeric supplements may offer little relief for knee arthritis, research finds
OUTLET: Southend Echo

Turmeric supplements may provide little or no pain relief for people with knee arthritis, researchers have said.
A Cochrane review examined nine studies involving 1,245 people with knee osteoarthritis, comparing supplements with placebos and painkillers.
Combined results found turmeric was no more effective than placebo or anti-inflammatory drugs, but some studies had limitations and the evidence remains uncertain.
The trials lasted between four weeks and three months, with participants aged 52 to 62 on average.
