TITLE: More than half of Germans oppose sending additional weapons to Ukraine — poll
OUTLET: The New Voice of Ukraine

A majority of Germans (53%) now oppose further weapons deliveries to Ukraine, DW reported, citing an annual survey by the Körber Foundation on Nov. 24.
The study found that 53% of German respondents favor ending military assistance to Kyiv, while only 46% support continuing it, down from 59% a year earlier.
Supporters of the far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) party are the most strongly opposed to weapons deliveries, with 88% of respondents in this group holding that view. Jonathan Lehrer, a researcher who conducted the study, linked the sentiment to the party’s growing popularity in state elections, where it has campaigned heavily on criticism of aid to Ukraine.
The survey also revealed a broader preference among Germans for a more restrained foreign policy: 48% of respondents favor reducing Berlin’s international engagement, while 77% believe the government should prioritize diplomacy over military measures.
Study author Florian Bigge described the decline in support as "significant," noting that figures on this issue had typically fluctuated by only 2 to 3 percentage points annually.
Additionally, 83% of Germans believe their country should remain neutral in the rivalry between the United States and China, while only 15% are willing to side with Washington.
According to Bigge, these figures reflect Germans' desire for security and reluctance to become involved in other countries' conflicts. Neutrality is supported by 92% of respondents in eastern Germany and 81% in western Germany.
Meanwhile, 82% of respondents continue to view Russia as a military threat. However, opinions are divided over tough retaliatory measures by Berlin, such as the closure of the Russian consulate in Bonn following an attempted drone attack on Leipzig Airport. Some 51% of Germans approve of such measures, while 46% favor a more cautious approach.
A YouGov poll conducted on Nov. 21 found that nearly half of German respondents (49%) favor reducing or completely ending financial, military and humanitarian assistance to Ukraine. Specifically, 25% believe Berlin should end its support for Kyiv altogether, while another 24% favor reducing it.
According to an October survey by the Forschungsgruppe Wahlen research group, 30% of Germans supported increasing military assistance to Ukraine from European countries, while 32% favored reducing it.
