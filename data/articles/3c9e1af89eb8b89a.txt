Wall Street Analysts Favor Devon Energy, Energy Transfer and ConocoPhillips
According to CNBC, analysts at Goldman Sachs, TD Cowen and Wells Fargo reiterated buy ratings on Devon Energy, Energy Transfer and ConocoPhillips, respectively. Goldman Sachs analyst Neil Mehta set a $59 price target on Devon, which declared a 32-cent-per-share dividend for the fourth quarter, payable December 30. Mehta said the company expects more than $1 billion in share buybacks in Q4 2026, compared with $414 million in the third quarter, and about $6.3 billion in excess free cash flow in 2027.
TD Cowen analyst Jason Gabelman set a $25 price target on Energy Transfer. The company reported third-quarter net income per common unit rose about 18% year over year to 33 cents and a Q3 2026 cash distribution of 34.25 cents per common unit, up more than 3%. It also announced a $1.6 billion pipeline project expected to enter service in 2030.
Wells Fargo analyst Sam Margolin lowered his price target on ConocoPhillips to $185 from $189 while reiterating a buy rating. ConocoPhillips raised its quarterly dividend 11% to 93 cents per share, effective with the fourth-quarter dividend payable on December 1, and reported Q3 2026 adjusted earnings per share of $3.27.
