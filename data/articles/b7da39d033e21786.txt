TITLE: Goodbye, Lucia: GTA 6 Fans' Official New Obsession Is Mandy
OUTLET: Polygon.com

Less than 24 hours ago, social media posts about Grand Theft Auto 6 were flooded with thirsty images of Lucia Caminos, the first female protagonist in Rockstar's open-world series. Now that the game is playable in some regions of the world, much of that enthusiasm has transferred to an entirely new character who hasn't appeared in any of the game's screenshots or marketing. Meet Mandy, GTA 6's new it girl.
Who is Mandy in GTA 6?
Mandy is a character who appears shortly after the game's first chapter, during the Pipework Mission. Brian, Jason's landlord, tasks the player with collecting rent from Mandy. Once the player arrives at Mandy's house, she refuses to pay up until Jason helps her out with a plumbing problem.
If this sounds like a thinly-veiled premise for a porno flick, that's on purpose. Mandy starts flirting with Jason almost immediately, which sets the tone for the rest of the encounter. Mandy coyly leads Jason through her home as she discusses her issues as Brian's tenant. Somehow, she ends up making small talk while lying provocatively in bed. Jason doesn't take the bait — he immediately asks Mandy where she keeps her plunger. Mandy lets Jason get to work, but not before calling him a stud.
Mandy briefly disappears behind her bathroom door, where she can be seen applying makeup.Jason then uses the plunger—presumably the same one used on Mandy's toilet—on her sink. The sink starts overflowing, so Jason takes a look under the cabinet to fix it. As Jason starts wrestling with a wrench, Mandy reappears wearing nothing but a bikini.
This is when Mandy reveals she's been digitally stalking Jason and looking up everything from his music preferences to where he works out. Jason tries to call her out on it, but Mandy doesn't stop flirting.
"I just wanna know why I have to keep breaking appliances to get you to come see me," Mandy says. As Jason tries to leave, Mandy gets more aggressive.
"Why don't you like me? Who are you with?" Mandy asks.
At this point in the game, Lucia is still in prison. This may be at the heart of why early GTA 6 players have become fixated on Mandy: the game aggressively presents her as a potential love interest before Lucia fully comes into the picture. Since the game's release, Google searches for Mandy have exploded in volume. In major GTA 6 communities, such as r/gta6unmoderated, many of the top threads at the moment center on the bombacious brunette. The jokes also write themselves.
"Couldn’t believe I was laying pipe 20 minutes in," one GTA 6 fan wrote.
"TL has been nothing but yam shots of this Mandy girl," another GTA 6 player said.
While sharing a picture of Mandy, one fan opined in all caps that "MONEY $100 WELL SPENT." Incidentally, most of the letters in that sparse, ungrammatical message can be written with half of a keyboard.
Posts about Mandy have become so pervasive that some fans are already sick of seeing them — and are asking the so-called "gooners" to take their camera zooms elsewhere.
Jason and Lucia's relationship is the core of GTA 6, and many of the game's mechanics are built around strengthening their bond. The couple can hold hands, go on dates, and even text each other between missions. Rockstar says that players can choose whether they want to keep that spark alive throughout the adventure. This early in the game, though, the story ensures Jason stays faithful to Lucia, who is in prison.
