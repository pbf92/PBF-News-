iBio Disclosed Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard
On November 19, 2026, Nasdaq notified iBio that its stock had closed below the $1.00 minimum bid price for 30 consecutive business days, violating Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2). The company has until May 18, 2027, to regain compliance, including by maintaining a bid price of at least $1.00 for 10 consecutive business days; trading continues on Nasdaq with no immediate effect, and iBio may consider a reverse stock split.
Original SEC Filing: iBio, Inc. [ IBIO ] - 8-K - Nov. 20, 2026
