Scilex Holding Received Notice of Noncompliance
On November 17, 2026, Nasdaq notified Scilex Holding that its market value of listed securities had remained below the $35 million minimum under Listing Rule 5550(b)(2), and that it did not meet the alternative equity or net-income standards. Trading continues without immediate effect, and the company has until May 17, 2027, to regain compliance by meeting the threshold for at least 10 consecutive business days; if it does not, Nasdaq may issue a delisting notice that the company can appeal.
Original SEC Filing: Scilex Holding Co [ SCLX ] - 8-K - Nov. 20, 2026
