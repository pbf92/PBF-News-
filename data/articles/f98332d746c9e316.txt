Pochettino Gives Zirkzee Chance to Revive Man United Career
Manchester United striker Joshua Zirkzee could receive an extended opportunity to establish himself under manager Mauricio Pochettino, with injuries to key attacking players creating openings in the first team following the club's 4-0 defeat against Liverpool on November 22, 2026.
The Dutch forward has struggled for regular playing time this season, making 11 appearances exclusively as a substitute. However, former United defender Phil Bardsley believes the 25-year-old possesses the qualities needed to lead the attack.
According to statistics published by The Peoples Person, Zirkzee has accumulated 237 minutes across all competitions, scoring two goals and recording no assists. He has completed 62 of his 90 passes, achieving 68.9% passing accuracy.
Bardsley believes injuries elsewhere in the attacking department could provide Zirkzee with an opportunity to become more influential under the new manager.
"He's not had the easiest time since he's been at the club, but I just feel like there might be a chance for Zirkzee to come into the fold, with Cunha being out," Phil Bardsley said in comments published by Manchester United's official media.
The former defender also highlighted Zirkzee's contribution during his appearance against Liverpool. He believes consistent playing time could help the striker improve his fitness and establish a stronger connection with his teammates.
"I just feel like when he came on the other day [at Anfield], he made a bit of a difference. He held the ball, he tried to take us up the pitch when he could. If he could get a run of games, and get a feel of it, and get his fitness levels up, he could make a real impact to this team," Bardsley added.
He also identified the Dutchman's physical qualities and attacking potential as important attributes for Pochettino's system.
"He could come in and make the difference. He's got a lot to add to his game, but I think he's certainly got the qualities to lead the line," Bardsley said.
However, Zirkzee's performance against Liverpool also highlighted concerns about his effectiveness as a central striker. Football FanCast reported that he replaced Matheus Cunha at halftime and struggled to provide the ball retention United needed.
During 45 minutes at Anfield, Zirkzee completed 67% of his passes, won four of nine duels and lost possession 12 times. He recorded 32 touches, created one chance and attempted one shot off target.
United's attacking problems have been compounded by Benjamin Sesko's injury, which is expected to keep him unavailable until January or potentially longer. Bryan Mbeumo has consequently been used as a central striker, scoring four goals in 11 Premier League appearances this season, according to Football FanCast.
Despite the criticism, Zirkzee's recent goals against Como 1907 and Aston Villa have provided evidence of his scoring ability. His limited opportunities, however, remain a significant obstacle to establishing himself as a regular starter.
Manchester United are also considering attacking reinforcements for the January transfer window. Victor Osimhen has attracted attention after scoring 10 goals in 11 appearances this season, although his potential salary represents a substantial financial consideration.
Another candidate is Folarin Balogun, who previously impressed while representing the United States under Pochettino. His familiarity with the manager could make him an alternative option for strengthening the forward line.
Ivan Toney has also emerged as a potential target. The former Brentford striker has scored 14 goals in 10 Saudi Pro League appearances this season, attracting interest from Manchester United, Tottenham Hotspur and Arsenal.
According to TEAMtalk, representatives of all three clubs have held discussions with intermediaries about Toney's availability. The England international is also understood to be particularly interested in a potential move to Old Trafford as he considers leaving Saudi Arabia in 2027.
Meanwhile, Zirkzee's immediate prospects depend on the availability of United's injured attackers and the opportunities Pochettino provides during the upcoming fixtures.
