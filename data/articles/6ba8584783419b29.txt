Cap: Supply unlock releases up to 90.16 million CAP - Nov 2027
Cap has scheduled a supply unlock for November 2027 that will release up to 90.16 million CAP. The tokens in the unlock are set aside for community members, investors and the team.
Recipients can sell their tokens once they are unlocked. If Cap releases the full amount, tokens equal to 1.97% of the circulating supply will become available for sale.
