Topstep Challenges and Friendsgiving FAQs
What are Topstep Challenges?
Topstep Challenges are a way for traders to test their skills by reaching a Profit Target in a Challenge Round, then repeating it in a Payout Round to earn a payout.
What are the Topstep Challenge rules?
The Topstep Challenge rules require you to reach your Profit Target in the Challenge Round without breaking any trading rules, then repeat that in the Payout Round. Each Topstep trading challenge lists its own targets and limits.
How does the Friendsgiving referral code work?
The Friendsgiving referral code works by giving your friend 50% off their first eligible Topstep purchase, including a Topstep Challenge. When they use your code, you earn 50% off your next eligible purchase.
Are Trading Challenges a part of Topstep Labs?
Topstep Challenges began in Topstep Labs but are now a permanent part of Topstep. Traders can take a Topstep trading challenge whenever they’re ready, without waiting for a limited Labs drop.
