TITLE: England defender Carter joins Bay FC after Gotham exit
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
England defender Jess Carter has completed a move to Bay FC after leaving Gotham FC as a free agent.
The 29-year-old has signed a contract that will keep her with Bay FC - a club based in the San Francisco Bay Area - until 2028, with the option for an additional year.
Carter joined New York-side Gotham in July 2024 after a six-year spell with Chelsea that saw her win the Women's Super League on five occasions.
"Coming to Bay FC is a new challenge, and that's something I'm excited about," said Carter.
"I've been fortunate to be part of some successful teams, and I know how much work goes into winning. I'm looking forward to getting to know this group, earning the trust of my team-mates and doing my part to help us move forward."
Carter played in all 30 of Gotham's matches in 2026 as the club won the NWSL Shield - given to the league's regular-season champions.
She also helped Gotham win the NWSL Championship in 2025, when they defeated Washington Spirit in the final.
"Jess has been a big part of what we've accomplished as a club, and we're very thankful for the contributions she's made during her time here," said Yael Averbuch West, the president of football operations for Gotham.
Carter, who has 56 senior England caps, was part of the squad that won the 2022 European Championships, while she appeared in all six games as the Lionesses defended their title last summer.
"Jess has consistently demonstrated her quality in championship-winning environments at both club and international level," said Bay FC's chief football officer Paul Green, who worked with Carter at Chelsea.
"I've seen her development and the professionalism she brings every day. She will bring valuable experience to Bay FC, and we're delighted to welcome her to the club."
The 2027 NWSL regular season kicks off in February.
