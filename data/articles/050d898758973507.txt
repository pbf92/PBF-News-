TITLE: Rockstar Games has released a bug fix update for GTA VI.
OUTLET: GameGPU

Rockstar Games has released a new technical update for the crime action game Grand Theft Auto VI, addressing a number of critical software bugs that users encountered immediately after the game's official release. According to published information, the download size is 600 megabytes for PlayStation 5 owners and 1,2 gigabytes for Xbox Series X users. The primary goal of this patch is to improve the overall stability of the software code and eliminate crashes that could cause the application to unexpectedly close during gameplay.
In addition to fixing critical crashes, the developers made a number of important improvements to key game mechanics and progression systems. Specifically, they fixed a glitch that prevented the auto-save feature from working correctly and recording player progress, and fixed errors with incoming phone calls, including story-related calls necessary for progressing through the main campaign and unlocking new missions. Additionally, the studio's specialists addressed a flaw in the environment simulation system that prevented dynamic weather conditions from changing over long periods of time.
The final part of the official changelog addresses local gameplay issues that interfered with interaction with certain elements of the game world. The developers fixed a bug that prevented players from lowering their current wanted level after committing offenses. The update also restored the proper functionality of the deadlift barbell in gyms and fixed an AI bug that prevented teammates from handing over loot bags to the protagonist, which were necessary for subsequent sale to fences.
