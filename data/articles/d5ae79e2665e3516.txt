Patrick Michael George Reports 29.9% Stake in Versa Bancorp
Patrick Michael George reported beneficial ownership of a 29.9% stake in Versa Bancorp, including shares he holds directly and shares held by GBH Inc. The stake arose from VersaBank’s November 13, 2026 reorganization, which exchanged the investors’ VersaBank shares for Versa Bancorp shares on a one-for-one basis. George controls GBH, making him the beneficial owner of a combined 9,738,047 shares.
Investor Intent
George and GBH say they hold the shares for investment. GBH may sell part of its stake in a secondary portion of a future Versa Bancorp equity offering, but the filing describes no current plan to do so.
Investor's Background
GBH Inc. is a private Ontario holding company that holds investments, including Versa Bancorp shares. Patrick Michael George serves as GBH’s sole director and president. George’s principal occupation is president of GA Masonry in Breslau, Ontario.
Original SEC Filing: Versa Bancorp [ VRSA ] - SCHEDULE 13D - Nov. 20, 2026
