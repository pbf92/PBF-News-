TITLE: 'GTA 6': Fox News Slams Fans, Clavicular & Manosphere Bros Hate Lucia
OUTLET: Variety

“Grand Theft Auto VI” is poised to break sales records and could even impact the larger economy as scores of fans take the day off work to cause digital mayhem. But some right-wing pundits aren’t thrilled with all of the attention the video game is getting.
Fox News had a roundtable discussion on the Nov. 19 episode of “Outnumbered” about the game and adults who take the day off to play it, and clips of the discussion posted on X by journalist Acyn Torabi have gained attention.
“Outnumbered” co-host Harris Faulkner criticized fans taking paid time off as shirking their civic duties, saying, “If this generation wants to preach about affordability, but they’re willing to cost billions of dollars in revenue loss on a day like today to go play a game, that probably tells you everything you need to know … Get yourself to work.”
Fox News contributor Paul Mauro agreed, saying, “You’re taking off of work, you’re abdicating your responsibilities to whoever it is in your life so you can do this nonsense? I mean, c’mon, grow up.”
Mauro was also upset with the game’s violence and how he thinks it could impact children, saying, “This is almost the equivalent of giving [a kid] a gun at an age when they don’t understand the responsibility.”
Meanwhile, Manosphere influencers are frustrated that the game encourages players to treat main character Jason’s girlfriend, Lucia, with respect.
Clavicular took to X on Nov. 18 to write, “‘GTA 6’ is insanely cucked, half of the missions are jestermaxing and simping for girls.”
Myron Gaines wrote on X on Nov. 19, “Despite the game getting solid first-day reviews, this is yet another example of how political correctness, inclusivity and feminism has destroyed a legendary game series … The main character gets cucked by his gf the whole time …”
Zack Hoyt, better known online as Asmongold, complained during a stream of the game that “I had to watch a stupid TV show with her, rub her feet and listen to her friends’ stories about Snapchat … Do I have go to the corner store and buy her some tampons too?”
Despite chatter from commentators, “GTA 6” is breaking internet usage records and is already one of the best-reviewed games of all time.
