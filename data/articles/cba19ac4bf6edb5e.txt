TITLE: Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei Unwinds in Southern Italy Days After Record IPO
OUTLET: Briefs Finance

Arrival and local coverage
People familiar with the situation said Amodei's jet touched down in Brindisi on Italy's Adriatic coast around midday Friday, with a stay of a few days anticipated. Regional broadcaster Antenna Sud filmed him at the airport, and Bloomberg News confirmed his arrival. Anthropic declined to comment.
The IPO in focus
Anthropic, maker of the Claude chatbot, hit Wall Street on Thursday after a record-breaking raise of $107.3 billion. The stock finished its first session 17% above the IPO price, then surrendered most of that pop on Friday, a move that lined up with growing caution around the AI trade.
Place and personal context
Brindisi sits in Puglia, the heel of Italy's boot, and Amodei has said he and his wife sometimes spend time in Italy, where they have a horse. "I'll just look at our horse and I'll be like, Calypso, who's our horse, she doesn't know about any of this," he previously told Bloomberg's Emily Chang on The Circuit. "She's just a happy horse."
