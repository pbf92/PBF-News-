TITLE: GTA 6 Sparks Controversy with 'Anti-Furry Law' Scene
OUTLET: PinkNews

Rockstar Games has made it ‘illegal’ to be a furry in GTA 6
Players exploring Grand Theft Auto VI have been sharing clips of a satirical in-game storyline in Vice City and the fictional state of Leonida that frames “furry activity” as illegal, including a police standoff with a fox-suited character.
In one widely shared encounter, armed officers surround a character dressed as an anthropomorphic animal, turning what looks like a random street scene into a grim reveal about Leonida’s supposed “law”.
The gag is reinforced through in-universe TV-style public messages. One televised address tells citizens: “Furry activity of any kind is illegal in the state of Leonida, and all citizens are required to report any suspicious activity.”
It continues: “Please act accordingly if you hear an ‘animal’ say anything like: Uwu, Rawr xD, Or hewwo.”
The same message escalates again, claiming: “Licensed hunters are authorised to shoot any furry on sight. This message is paid for by the Anti-Furry Association.”
What is the GTA 6 ‘furry ban’ players are seeing?
Players have also spotted in-game TV ads urging them to report furries to police, suggesting the Leonida “ban” is threaded through the game’s background media as well as street-level encounters.
In a review for CNet, David Lumb wrote that players might come across a standoff between “a man in a fox suit” and police, and said that both wearing a fur suit and expressing your “fursona” is illegal in Leonida. He added that players can choose to intervene, writing that he “broke through the police blockade to liberate” the man in the fox suit, who then linked up with his “fellow furries and spirited them away to a compound”.
The furry fandom is an international community built around anthropomorphic animal art, characters, roleplay and conventions. Many people create a “fursona”, a personalised character used in online and community spaces, and the fandom includes a significant LGBTQ+ presence in many corners of the community, even though it is not inherently an LGBTQ+ identity.
Could the Leonida ‘furry law’ lead to more GTA 6 missions?
The storyline may go further than a one-off gag. Those who have already played GTA 6 say the script claims that banning furries in Leonida prompts a secret resistance group to form, called The Furverts.
In 2025, the second GTA 6 trailer also sparked viral reactions, with viewers fixating on the game’s new protagonist alongside the usual chaos of corrupt cops, car chases and gunfights.
