Meta Platforms is Cheap at 18.78X P/E: Buy, Sell or Hold the Stock?
Meta Platforms META shares are cheap, as suggested by the Value Score of B. In terms of the forward 12-month price/earnings (P/E), META is trading at 18.78X, lower than the Zacks Internet Software industry’s 26.90X. The social-networking giant is trading at a discount compared with Amazon’s AMZN 23.39X and Microsoft’s MSFT 24.20X. However, Meta Platforms’ shares are trading at a premium compared with Alphabet’s GOOGL 18.30X.
META Stock’s Valuation
So, is Meta stock a buy, sell or hold at this level? Let’s find out.
META Suffers From Rising AI Spend
Meta shares have lost 5.1% year to date (YTD), underperforming the broader Zacks Computer & Technology sector’s appreciation of 6.9%, Microsoft’s rise of 7.2%, and Amazon’s return of 11.7%. However, META shares have outperformed Alphabet shares, which have dropped 9.3% over the same time frame.
META Stock’s Price Performance
Meta’s aggressive investments in AI infrastructure represent a significant near-term challenge. The company expects 2026 capital expenditures, including finance lease principal payments, of $135-$145 billion, reflecting substantial spending on data centers, servers and networking infrastructure. Capital expenditures reached $34.91 billion in the third quarter compared with $19.37 billion in the year-ago period.
Despite generating $32.79 billion in operating cash flow, Meta reported negative free cash flow of $2.12 billion during the quarter. The company has $454.52 billion in non-cancelable contractual commitments and approximately $355.73 billion in leases that had not yet commenced. These commitments relate primarily to cloud capacity, computing infrastructure, data centers and energy requirements.
Meta’s accelerating operating expenses are creating pressure on profitability despite strong revenue growth. In the third quarter of 2026, total costs and expenses surged 68% year over year to $51.61 billion, significantly outpacing revenue growth of 26%. GAAP operating income declined 37% year over year to $12.99 billion, while operating margin contracted to 20% from 40% in the year-ago quarter. Although the results included a $9.89 billion legal settlement charge, increasing infrastructure expenses, third-party cloud spending, depreciation and AI token costs remain underlying concerns. Meta’s continued investments in advanced AI models and computing infrastructure are expected to limit margin expansion and pressure earnings growth, making its discounted valuation less compelling in the near term.
Moreover, Meta continues to face significant regulatory and legal challenges related to privacy, competition, content moderation and youth safety. In August 2026, the company reached an agreement with 52 U.S. attorneys general to resolve litigation related to teen mental health and social media, providing for payments of up to approximately $18.59 billion.
Meanwhile, Reality Labs remains a drag on profitability. The segment reported an operating loss of $4.13 billion in the third quarter, despite continued growth in AI glasses revenues. Meta expects Reality Labs operating losses of $18-$19 billion in 2026, followed by approximately $15 billion in 2027. Although the anticipated reduction is encouraging, persistent losses and uncertain returns from immersive technologies could continue weighing on consolidated profitability.
2026 Earnings Estimate Revisions Negative for META
The Zacks Consensus Estimate for the 2026 earnings is pegged at $29.29 per share, down 5% over the past 30 days, suggesting a 24.7% rise from the figure reported in 2025.

META’s AI Integration Boosts User & Advertiser Engagements
Meta continues to benefit from robust digital advertising demand, improving AI-powered recommendation systems and growing engagement across Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. Third-quarter 2026 advertising revenues increased 26% year over year to $63.06 billion, supported by a 10% increase in ad impressions and a 14% rise in average price per ad. Family daily active people increased 3% to 3.64 billion, reflecting the strength of its global platform.
Meta’s AI-powered advertising systems, including GEM, Andromeda and Meta Generative Recommender, are improving targeting precision and conversion rates. The expansion of generative AI advertising tools and automated campaign optimization capabilities is helping advertisers improve returns on spending. Nearly 10 million advertisers used at least one of Meta’s generative AI creative tools during the quarter. Continued improvements in engagement, advertising efficiency and monetization should support revenue growth and reinforce Meta’s competitive position.
Meta’s investments in its Muse AI ecosystem, including Muse personal agents, Muse Wonder and enterprise AI services, are creating opportunities beyond traditional advertising. More than 10 million people in the United States and Canada adopted Muse personal agents within approximately six weeks of launch, with roughly 25% of registered users engaging daily. Shopping has emerged as a major use case, highlighting opportunities in agent-driven commerce.
Meta plans to monetize Muse through subscriptions, commerce transaction fees, enterprise services and additional business capabilities. The company also sees opportunities to leverage its existing ecosystem of approximately 500 million self-identified small businesses using its family of apps. The company’s Meta Enterprise Platform, Muse Code and AI models could generate additional revenue streams as adoption expands. These initiatives strengthen Meta’s long-term growth potential, although monetization remains at an early stage.
Conclusion
Meta’s discounted valuation, strong digital advertising business and expanding AI capabilities support its long-term growth prospects. Improving ad targeting, rising user engagement and emerging monetization opportunities from the Muse AI ecosystem remain key positives. However, aggressive AI infrastructure spending, rising operating expenses, persistent Reality Labs losses and regulatory uncertainties could continue to pressure profitability and cash flow. Moreover, downward earnings estimate revisions suggest limited near-term upside potential. Although META’s attractive valuation offers some downside protection, investors should remain cautious until AI investments translate into sustainable earnings growth and improved margins. Hence, holding META stock appears prudent at current levels.
