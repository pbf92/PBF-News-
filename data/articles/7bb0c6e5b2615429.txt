TITLE: Antonee Robinson on Fulham's development, Pochettino at Man United, Guardiola for USMNT and trophy dreams
OUTLET: NBC Sports

LONDON — Fulham and USMNT star Antonee Robinson was relaxed and reflective as he took some time to chat with NBC Sports at Fulham’s Motspur Park training base ahead of the busy festive period coming up in the Premier League.
The marauding left back praised the way new manager Alvaro Arbeloa has tweaked Fulham’s style of play and explained how they’re creating more chances and have clicked in recent weeks.
Their impressive draw at Manchester City last weekend stretched their unbeaten run to five league games and Fulham are extremely fun to watch.
Robinson praises Arbeloa, explains how his role has changed
“The form has really picked up. The first few games with a new manager and new players it’s difficult to have everything figured out straight away,” Robinson explained. “There were times when even if we were playing good we were getting picked out for little mistakes. The team has adapted and the manager has done really well picking out problems and seeing where we need to improve. He said from the start he liked a lot of the things we were seeing in the games, we just needed to tidy up on a few things.”
“For me personally I feel like I’ve been a lot higher on the pitch,” Robinson continued. “He picks one full back to be high and wide and then the other side it is the winger and with Sander [Berge] and Shea [Charles] playing together and being a bit more solid in the midfield has helped us a lot. Like I said, the fact there was a lot of underlying things at the start of the season where even if we weren’t winning games, he was still really happy with us and told us to ‘keep building the belief, keep building our identity’ and it’s a long season...
“The fact we’ve kept to his ideals and carried on pushing through and now we are starting to reap the benefits of that, it instils confidence in us as a team.”
Robinson on Pochettino: “He is such a personable guy, a great manager”
On the international stage Robinson reflected on a positive 2026 World Cup for the USMNT, as he was a key part of the team which inspired so many new fans as they reached the last 16.
He hopes they can kick on and succeed at the Gold Cup this summer, then hopefully the 2028 Copa America being held in the U.S. before taking a big step forward at the 2030 World Cup.
Robinson, 29, also spoke of his belief that former USMNT boss Mauricio Pochettino will turn things around at Manchester United after leaving the U.S. side unexpectedly.
“It was a great experience,” Robinson said when asked about playing for Pochettino. “He is such a personable guy, a great manager and fills the team with a lot of confidence and belief and the kind of passion he has for the game I feel like he really instilled that in our team. That translated into the World Cup and us playing pretty well. You can understand why when Man United came along he felt like it was time to change project. That is up to him. I think everyone wishes him all the best and know that despite the bad start they’ve just had they know the quality he’s got and what he can do to turn things around. Hopefully things go well for him.”
With Man United linked with a new left back in January, it’s no surprise Robinson’s name has come up in transfer reports considering he has just over 18 months left on his Fulham contract and played so well under Pochettino for the USMNT.
Time will tell how that all plays out when it comes to Robinson’s future at club level.
Pep Guardiola next for USMNT head coach? “I think it’s an attractive job” - Robinson
When asked about who U.S. Soccer should be looking at to replace Pochettino, Robinson admitted that a manager of the ilk of Pep Guardiola (heavily linked with replacing Pochettino) makes sense given the direction the USMNT are heading in.
“Hopefully someone who can carry on the good exciting football that we’ve done and someone who gets results and someone who can figure out how we win any game we are faced with and can deliver results,” Robinson said of what a new USMNT manager should have.
“You’d put him [Guardiola] and Poch and other people like that in the same category of managers. I’m not necessarily sure who would be the best fit. It would be interesting for Pep as that would be his first international managerial stint, it is different going from club to international and you have less control than you would at a club. It’s going to be interesting and hopefully someone of a Pep caliber would definitely be interested because I think it’s an attractive job. I feel like we’ve got very good players so they’re taking on a good team.”
Reflective Robinson eager to win more silverware to put extra gloss on a superb career
Off the pitch Robinson is an impressive character. From card tricks to playing the piano to acrobatic back flips when he scores goals, USMNT and Fulham legend Tim Ream once described Robinson as having ‘more talent in his pinky finger than I’ve got in my whole body!’ So has Robinson got any new skills?
“I’m putting all of my energy into getting better at golf, because I’m crap at that!” Robinson laughed. Given his talent for just about anything, he’ll likely be on the PGA Tour soon.
As for his main targets and goals for the rest of his career, Robinson was reflective on just how far he’s come from a youngster at Everton going out on loan to Bolton Wanderers and Wigan Athletic to becoming one of the most consistent left backs in the Premier League over the last five years.
“When I look back to when I was younger and before I made my way in the pro game, I don’t think if you’d have told me I would have played in two World Cups, played in 100 or so Premier League games, played for my national team so many times, that I would have believed it. So I think it’s about taking pride in what I’ve already achieved and enjoying that.”
He also revealed winning silverware at Fulham, and some more with the USMNT, is a huge aim.
“And at the same time when I finish I want to play as many games as I can at the highest level,” Robinson continued. “Try and win trophies which I feel like at some point Fulham is overdue one. The past few years we’ve had a good few cup runs that have fell short when they shouldn’t have.
“So hopefully this season the FA Cup comes along. Playing at the top level for as long as I can, hopefully win some trophies, playing with the national team to the best I can and hopefully win some more trophies with them too.”
