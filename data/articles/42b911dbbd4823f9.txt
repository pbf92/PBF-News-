TITLE: Miami Heat players receive GTA VI care packages from Rockstar Games
OUTLET: ESPN

Players around the NBA are gearing up for "Grand Theft Auto VI," but some are going to be particularly ready for the game's highly anticipated Thursday release: Miami Heat players.
The ties between Miami and the Grand Theft Auto series date back to 2002 with "Grand Theft Auto: Vice City." The fourth game in the series, it is set in a fictional city based on Miami. GTA VI returns to Vice City.
Unsurprisingly for a team that has been meticulously embracing Vice City branding for years, the Heat are rolling out an impressive promotion ahead of GTA VI going live Thursday. Wednesday will be "A Night in Vice City" at the Kaseya Center, featuring a new court design, concessions and more.
But the promotional street runs both ways -- and Miami players are reaping the benefits.
A number of players, including Davion Mitchell, Nick Richards, Tim Hardaway Jr. and more posted about receiving a game-themed care package from Rockstar Games, PlayStation and luggage company Rimowa for GTA VI's release. It included custom controllers, consoles and, of course, a copy of the game. The care package came in a fitting color scheme -- Miami's Vice City pink and black alternate branding.
Mitchell, who posted about the gift on his Instagram story, added a humorous caption:
"I'm about to deactivate my account because I'm about to lock in," he wrote, adding saluting and laughing emojis. "It's up!!!"
The Heat will face the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday night (7 p.m. EST, ESPN).
