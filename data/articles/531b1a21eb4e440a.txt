TITLE: Anthropic: AI company behind Claude chatbot value rises above $2 trillion in stock market offering
OUTLET: Channel 4

Five years ago Anthropic – the AI company founded by brother and sister Dario and Daniela Amodei – didn’t even exist.
On Thursday its value briefly rose above $2 trillion, when it was launched on the Nasdaq in New York.
It is the biggest public offering ever, the first of an AI giant, and it comes with a 94-page health warning from the company itself, that includes warnings like “our products could help terrorists develop biological weapons” or “even trigger the extinction of the human race”.
It is believed between 400 and 1,000 early hires at Anthropic are now worth at least $100 million.
