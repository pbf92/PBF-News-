PJT Partners CEO Elects to Exchange 36,000 Units
Less than 1 min read
PJT Partners CEO Paul J. Taubman elected to exchange 36,000 Partnership Units in the current quarterly window.
Key Highlights:
- The exchange will be settled after PJT Partners’ fourth-quarter 2026 earnings release in February 2027, in cash or Class A common stock at the Board’s election.
- Taubman intends to elect to exchange up to 36,000 Units in each of the next three quarterly exchange windows.
- If he exchanges the full amount in all three subsequent periods, the Units exchanged would represent approximately 2.5% of his total economic ownership.
