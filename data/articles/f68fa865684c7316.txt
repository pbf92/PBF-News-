TITLE: Pochettino confirms Fernandes captain decision as Man Utd reign begins
OUTLET: London Evening Standard

The Red Devils’ new head coach addressed the media for the first time since his appointment at Old Trafford, as he takes over the reins from Michael Carrick.
A big issue that hangs over the club is the future of captain Fernandes, who has reportedly rejected two offers of a new contract this term.
The Portuguese’s current deal in the north west expires at the end of the current campaign, and come January, he will be able to sign a pre-contract agreement with a foreign club that would see him depart for free.
The No8’s discipline was called into question as well before the international break, when Fernandes called out his former manager’s tactics in the 4-1 demolition by Como in the Champions League.
It led to the Portugal international being benched for the 3-0 home defeat by Bournemouth, but he was immediately reinstated for the 2-0 loss at Chelsea after clear-the-air talks with Carrick.
Now, Pochettino has had his say on the attacking midfielder.
“I think the commitment of all the players is amazing from day one,” the former Tottenham and Chelsea head coach said.
“Bruno is our captain and he's a very important player, but all the player must feel they are important.
“We chatted yesterday and spent three hours chatting in my office with all the players. I missed some injured players, but in the next few days, I will talk to everyone.”
The 54-year-old added that there “would be time” to discuss Fernandes’ contract, but refused to be drawn on a straighter answer regarding the skipper’s future.
