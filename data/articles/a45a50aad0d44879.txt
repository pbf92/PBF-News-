Air Industries Group Sets Dec. 3 Special Meeting on Tenax Merger
Less than 1 min read
Air Industries Group reminded stockholders that its special meeting to vote on the proposed Tenax merger is scheduled for December 3.
Key Highlights:
- The meeting was postponed from November 18 to December 3, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time.
- Stockholders will vote on the merger-related proposals described in the definitive proxy statement/prospectus.
- Stockholders of record as of October 13, 2026, may vote in person or by internet, telephone or mail.
- Internet and telephone voting for holders of record closes at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on December 2.
