TITLE: Anthropic Expands Research Hub in Zurich
OUTLET: Briefs Finance

Anthropic PBC plans to upsize its Zurich footprint, taking about 2,500 square meters (26,910 square feet) in the landmark Fraumünsterpost property.
From Switzerland, the Claude maker will run research and help train its AI models, aiming to plug into Zurich's AI talent and nearby healthcare giants.
After a record-breaking IPO this month, the company stayed silent due to a quiet period, and its shares were off 4.6% in Wednesday premarket as the sector slid in the wake of it raising $119.5 billion last week.
What happened
Anthropic, the company behind the Claude chatbot, is moving into a larger office in central Zurich. The lease covers about 2,500 square meters in the Fraumünsterpost building overlooking the Limmat river, according to someone familiar with the deal who asked not to be identified. From its Swiss base, the firm plans to conduct research and contribute to training its AI models. It was not clear how many hires are planned locally.
Swiss newspaper Tages-Anzeiger reported the plans earlier Wednesday. Anthropic offered no comment because it is in a quiet period following its record-setting initial public offering this month.
Why Zurich
The draw is a mix of deep technical talent and proximity to heavyweight clients. Switzerland is home to UBS Group AG, Roche Holding AG and Novartis AG, and healthcare has been a focus for Anthropic. Novartis Chief Executive Officer Vas Narasimhan sits on the company's board, and in September Anthropic said it had teamed up with Danish pharmaceutical company Novo Nordisk A/S to use AI to speed up drug development. Novo Nordisk also has a sizable footprint in Zurich.
Local talent and peers
Global AI players already cluster in the city, including Google, Meta Platforms Inc. and Nvidia Corp. In May, Bloomberg reported Google's Zurich workforce exceeds 5,000 employees representing more than 85 nationalities. ETH Zurich, long considered a top engineering institution, placed third on Times Higher Education's computer science ranking this year, coming in ahead of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. According to the ETH Venture Report 2025, the university has generated a greater number of new ventures in recent years, and starting in 2025 it began separating spinoffs from startups in its tallies.

What this means for your portfolio
In the week after it raised $119.5 billion, Anthropic's stock has been swept up in the broader AI slump, showing a 4.6% drop in Wednesday premarket and sitting well under the IPO price. The Zurich expansion lands during a quiet period in which the company is not commenting publicly, but the signal is clear for investors: it is investing to capture European talent and customers while markets reprice AI risk in the short term.
