TITLE: GTA 6 will be released on PC in March 2027, along with the launch of GTA 6 Online.
OUTLET: GameGPU

A leak has surfaced online stating that GTA 6 Online will officially launch in March 2027 simultaneously on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC. The information was discovered two months before Rockstar Games' supposed announcement during an analysis of Take-Two Interactive's financial reports for the 2027 fiscal year using the Fable 5.2 tool. The analysis revealed an anomaly in the company's net bookings forecast of $8 billion, which experts attribute to the launch of a major online revenue source before March 31, 2027. According to the leak, GTA 6's total development budget was $2,1 billion, and Rockstar will spend an additional $780 million on its infrastructure and content before the multiplayer launch. If GTA 5 Online maintains its revenue figures of $8,6 billion, with $540 million spent on updates, the new online version could generate up to $12 billion over the next decade.
According to published materials, with 9,69 million pre-orders, the game's multiplayer mode will offer a single, permanent staff, Leonidas, spanning Vice City, Port Gellhorn, and Ambrosia. Players will enjoy an updated character creator powered by new technology, co-op heists, street races, contact missions, and a business system. Special attention will be given to official role-playing servers created with the participation of the FiveM team, which Rockstar acquired in 2023. However, funds and progress from GTA 5 Online will not carry over, so all players will start from scratch.
Despite the massive investment, a number of technical and commercial issues remain surrounding the upcoming release. The main risks are identified as server stability at launch, with a significantly larger audience compared to the 2013 launch of GTA 5 Online, as well as technical issues with the base game, including map crashes and critical mission errors. Furthermore, according to insiders, the console version did not launch with a 60 frames per second performance mode. The multiplayer distribution model also remains unclear: it is not yet known whether GTA 6 Online will be included in the price of the base game or sold separately.
