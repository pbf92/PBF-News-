Major study reveals how often Brits are having sex a month
- Research published in The Lancet shows adults under 45 in Britain now have sex twice a month on average, down from four times monthly in 1990-91.
- The decline is largely driven by younger people, with one in three 16 to 24-year-olds having never had sex, compared to one in five between 1990 and 2010.
- Study authors suggested multiple complex factors could be driving the trend, including digital sexual behaviour, housing and economic pressures, and rising mental health conditions.
- The proportion of women without children who state they definitely do not want them has doubled over the past decade, from 8 per cent to 17 per cent.
- Reported same-sex sexual experiences doubled among women to 14 per cent and tripled among men to 12 per cent over the last ten years.
