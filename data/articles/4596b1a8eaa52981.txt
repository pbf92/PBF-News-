TITLE: 'It Would Be Absolutely Incredible' — Hideki Kamiya Wants to Make a New Castlevania Game With Konami
OUTLET: VGC / IGN

Hideki Kamiya, the mind behind Devil May Cry, Bayonetta, and Okami, wants to make a new Castlevania game, saying his team at CLOVERS could make something "absolutely incredible" alongside Konami.
Hideki Kamiya is back in the saddle at CLOVERS, his independent game studio made up of former CLOVER staff who are currently hard at work on the Okami sequel. He made the company’s independent status extremely clear in his latest YouTube video, reminding excited fans to stop asking him about Capcom-owned franchises (or even sequels to games like God Hand, since he had no part in its development).
Given CLOVERS’ status as an independent company that’s already working with a major developer in Capcom, Kamiya floated ideas for future collaborations with other big developers once the Okami sequel is out in the world. One of those ideas was a partnership with Konami for a Castlevania game, which he promised would be “incredible,” if it actually ends up happening.
“Since we’re independent and don’t have any outside funding, we want to stay on good terms — or rather, collaborate — with various companies and keep doing interesting things,” he explained. “Of course, I want to keep building a good relationship with Capcom, and naturally, I’d like to be on good terms with Nintendo, too.
“...Maybe I could make a new Getsufumaden with Konami. Oh, and Castlevania. If we made it, it would be absolutely incredible, so please, let us take the job. I am a bit busy right now, though,” he laughed.
A Castlevania game from a mind like Kamiya’s would definitely be something special. Would it be a 3D action game like Bayonetta, or more of a 2D side-scroller in the vein of Viewtiful Joe? The possibilities are endless, and given that Konami is already on a mission to revitalize its classic IPs with new games, the possibility of a team-up with CLOVERS on a Castlevania title isn’t totally out of the question.
It's also worth noting that the Castlevania franchise enjoyed a new installment in Belmont's Curse late last month. Alongside its animated adaptations on Netflix, Konami's classic vampire-slaying game series is getting a second wind, and Kamiya would like to be part of that, though whether or not this actually happens remains to be seen.
Kamiya also brought up ideas for other potential games that CLOVERS might make in collaboration with big publishers in the future, though how serious he was about these is up to interpretation. He playfully mentioned making a Star Fox sequel with Koei Tecmo or a King’s Knight sequel with Square Enix, and even mentioned a Kinnikuman M.U.S.C.L.E.: Tag Team Match game with Bandai Namco. Of course, these are all just goofy ideas he likely said in jest, but it does seem like he’s eager to make more games with major companies once Okami is out the door.
The Okami sequel was first announced at the 2024 Game Awards. It’s only been two years since then, so it’s unlikely that we’ll hear anything else from CLOVERS about the game’s development process anytime soon.
