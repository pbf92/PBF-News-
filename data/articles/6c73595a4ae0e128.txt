TITLE: All Red Dead Redemption references in GTA 6
OUTLET: Rock Paper Shotgun

In many ways, GTA 6 veers closer to Red Dead Redemption than previous Grand Theft Auto games, especially with regard to its Criminal Profile system and vast open world. But Leonida also contains many tangible Easter Eggs referencing Rockstar's western saga. These range from the hat of Arthur Morgan, Red Dead Redemption 2's protagonist, to references to New Austin, the Red Dead series' Texas analogue.
In this guide, we'll explain all of the Red Dead Redemption references in GTA 6, starting with Arthur Morgan's hat before delving into more obscure Easter Eggs.
On this page:
Arthur Morgan's hat
Arthur Morgan's hat can be purchased for $35 in a yard sale located in southwest Port Gellhorn, not far from Moses' Garage.
To get there easily, go to Moses' Garage and go south. Look out on the left side of the road until you spot balloons; that's where the yard sale is. Once you get there, head to the back to find the Genuine Cowboy Hat sitting on a guitar case. Yeah, Rockstar doesn't flat-out call this Arthur Morgan's hat persay, but the design is exactly the same.
You can find this hat from the very beginning of the game, but since it's located in the northwest section of Leonida, most players will probably find it in Chapter 2, which begins with Lucia and Jason hiding out in Port Gellhorn.
Arthur Morgan doll
To find a tiny Arthur Morgan doll, head to the Frontier Adventure Golf course in Leonard County and look for a small chihuahua wandering the area.
You don't need to actually pay to play mini-golf to find this dog, who wears the doll on its back and is formally known as the Ride 'Em Chihuahua. Pretty cute.
The Cowboy's Prayer is to Die Young
Go to Brian's Boat Works and Marina and examine the book by the mirror, which is located to the side of his desk.
It's entitled The Cowboy's Prayer is to Die Young, written by some fellow named Elijah G. Rubens, and contains several references to Red Dead Redemption 2 - including what seems to be a retelling of the mission "The Noblest of Men, and a Woman", which saw Arthur Morgan duelling Flaco Hernández near a snowy cabin. In the book, bounty hunter Rust McCreary is also tracking Flaco, and finds him by the cabin already dead.
New Austin license plates
This one's tricky to nail down, since it involves simply observing license plates as you explore. But if you do so, you'll eventually find plates for New Austin, Red Dead Redemption's Texas equivalent.
I'm actually still searching for this plate in the wild myself, but others have already posted it online. Other plates in the game that I have spotted include ones for Liberty City, San Andreas, Gloriana, and Alderney - all GTA locales based on the real-world US locations of New York, Los Angeles, the state of Georgia, and the state of New Jersey.
Duke Arms Company
Head to any of Leonida's many Ammu-Nation stores and zoom in on some of the boxes under the counters to find labels for the Duke Arms Company.
The Duke Arms Company, established in 1853 (note the Roman numerals on the boxes), is a weapons manufacturer in Red Dead Online, and their trusty Navy Revolver can be purchased from any gunsmith in that game.
Rad Dads Redemption Tour
Last but not least is this fun flyer which can be found in Key Lento's Three Sheets Liquor Store, which you'll visit several times over the course of the beginning missions. Take a look at the flyers on the wall in the store to see this little notice about the so-called Rad Dads Redemption tour.
That wraps up this guide on all Red Dead Redemption references in GTA 6, as well as how to get Arthur Morgan's hat.
