TITLE: How to Hunt Animals and Where to Sell Them in GTA 6
OUTLET: GameTyrant

Leonida's wilderness is packed with wildlife, and hunting the most prized animals and selling them is one of the best ways to make some extra cash in Grand Theft Auto VI. However, this isn't a task that can be done from the beginning of the story. If you start shooting deer and rabbits early on, you will quickly realize that you cannot sell those carcasses to anyone just yet, no matter how clean your kill is.
How to unlock hunting
You need to already be in Chapter 2 and playing as Jason for this side activity to unlock, which is when the Killtelligence Provisions meat-processing business is introduced. Run by Jason's old army friend Bret, the contact teaches you how to track, hunt, and process wild animals before becoming a permanent drop-off point for your future hauls.
Bret will begin texting Jason with marked locations containing hunting contracts for extra cash later on as well. Because of their past connection, this is an activity that only Jason can participate in after unlocking it.
How to hunt for most profit
While it's possible to sell almost any wild animal you take down to Killtelligence, it's important to get clean kills to turn a good profit.
Once you've reached a location with plenty of wild animals, activate Focus Mode by clicking the L3 + R3 (PS5) / LS + RS (Xbox) sticks simultaneously. This will highlight tracks of animals that have passed through that area recently, letting you check what made those prints. Once a target is identified, follow that trail until you find the animal. One tactic you can follow here is to approach the target from the highground.
When you're in range, it's a good idea to identify and study the animal you're hunting with binoculars or a rifle scope first. The bottom right information screen lists the best weapon for getting a clean kill on the chosen target, which maximizes profits.
The goal here is to get the kill with a single bullet, as extra shots reduce the value of the carcass. Entering Focus Mode again while aiming at the animal with a scoped weapon will slow down time and highlight red spots on the head and the heart, showing exactly where you must shoot for claiming that clean kill. Once the job is done, stow the animal in the back of a vehicle and drive out to Killtelligence Provisions to collect your payout. Be sure to drive out with a vehicle with lots of trunk space if you’re planning on bringing back more than a few carcasses.
Keep in mind that if the animal you just hunted is larger than a deer, Bret will come and do the hard work of picking up the carcass himself once you send him the location.
