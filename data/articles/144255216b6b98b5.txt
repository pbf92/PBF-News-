TITLE: GTA 6 earns Game of the Year nomination before release
OUTLET: ABC7 New York

One of the year's most anticipated video games has earned one of the industry's biggest honors before players have even gotten their hands on it.
Grand Theft Auto VI has been nominated for Game of the Year at the 2026 Game Awards despite not being scheduled for release until Nov. 19, just one day before the awards eligibility window closes.
The nomination puts Rockstar Games' blockbuster sequel in contention for gaming's top prize alongside 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
"GTA 6" was one of the most-nominated games overall, earning recognition in several major categories. In addition to Game of the Year, it received nominations for Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, Best Art Direction, Best Score & Music, Best Audio Design and Best Action/Adventure Game.
The game also received a Best Performance nomination for actor Manni L. Perez.
The nominations were announced Tuesday ahead of the annual awards show, which will stream live Dec. 10 from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles.
Nominees for most categories are selected by an international jury made up of more than 100 media and influencer outlets focused on video game criticism and coverage. Winners are determined through a combination of jury voting, which accounts for 90% of the final vote, and fan voting, which accounts for the remaining 10%.
The Game Awards describe the event as a celebration of the video game industry featuring awards, world premieres, musical performances and new game announcements.
Fan voting remains open through Dec. 9, with winners set to be announced during the Dec. 10 ceremony.
