TITLE: GTA 6 Online release date odds: Kalshi traders see a mid-2027 launch
OUTLET: Kalshi News

Players are exploring the world of Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S consoles after Rockstar Games released its newest title earlier today. And according to early reports, users can do more than create mayhem in Vice City. They can play basketball, go fishing, go on dates, shoot pool, and even participate in backyard wrestling.
The only thing they cannot do yet is play the game online with other players. However, Kalshi traders are already forecasting when the game's online multiplayer mode will launch.
Kalshi’s GTA VI Online release date market shows a 70% chance that Take-Two Interactive and Rockstar will launch it before July 2027, a 60% chance it will launch before May 2027, and a 26% chance it will launch before February 2027.

When will GTA VI Online launch?
Rockstar Games announced in June 2026 that GTA VI would be a single-player experience at its launch.
Polygon reported that Rockstar’s announcement made no mention of when an online version might launch for the new game. An online multiplayer mode is widely expected, given the financial success of GTA Online.
“The popular online mode is a cash cow for Rockstar and publisher Take-Two Interactive, and is regularly updated with new content for its rabid fan base,” Polygon wrote.
Bloomberg reported in September 2026 that Twitch Chief Executive Officer Dan Clancy expects Rockstar to launch an online mode for GTA VI in 2027.
“We spent a lot of time talking with Rockstar about their plans, and also helping them think about how they can engage our creators,” Clancy told Bloomberg. “You are going to see this big bump with GTA VI, but then an even bigger one next year when they launch multiplayer.”
The last version of GTA Online generated significant revenue for Rockstar and Take-Two. GameGPU reported that GTA Online’s lifetime revenue surpassed $5 billion for the companies.
“There’s an argument to be made that GTA Online is the more important product, and not launching the games simultaneously gives the developers the chance to make sure both games meet Rockstar’s high-quality standards,” PC Gamer wrote.
PCMag also argues that Rockstar may be taking its time to perfect the next iteration of GTA Online to make sure it runs properly at launch.
“GTA Online had an infamously rough launch before it became what it is today, so it makes sense that Rockstar would want to get it right this time,” PC Gamer wrote. “The game is reportedly the most preordered title in history, so the developers can afford to wait.”
GTA VI sales forecast for 2026
Kalshi traders are also forecasting how many units the game will sell by the end of 2026.
Traders price a 55% chance that Take-Two will report at least 33 million GTA VI units sold before Jan. 1, 2027, a 44% chance it will report at least 36 million, and a 40% chance it will report at least 39 million.
