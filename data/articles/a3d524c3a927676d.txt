TITLE: Trump's net approval hits a new low. One-quarter of his 2024 voters don't approve of his job handling
OUTLET: YouGov

The share of registered voters who approve of how Donald Trump is handling his job reached a new low in this week's Economist / YouGov Poll, continuing a long-term downward trend. Only 35% strongly or somewhat approve of Trump's job performance, the lowest share in either of Trump's two terms. 63% disapprove, the highest for either of Trump's terms.
Trump's net job approval of -28 is also his lowest yet, and compares with a net approval of -8 at this point in Trump's first term.
Trump began his second term with approval from 93% of his 2024 voters. Only 73% of this group now approves of Trump's job handling, matching his low from earlier this month. The share of those who didn't vote in 2024 who approve has fallen to 22% from a range of 30% to 40% in the first year of his second term.
Essentially no Kamala Harris voters (2%) approve of Trump's job handling.
