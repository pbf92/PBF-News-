TITLE: GTA 6 is already playable in your browser, but only if you're extremely patient
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Even after all the safeguards and console updates, Grand Theft Auto VI is still playable on PC months (or years) before an official port.
GTA VI launched last week exclusively for the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. It's without a doubt one of the best games ever made.
But, as per usual, PC players are having to wait their turn as Rockstar Games always releases the console version first and the PC version later.
It’s understandable. Console players make up the majority of Grand Theft Auto players, but in 2026, we’d have really expected a multi-platform launch.

Worry not though PC owners. You can in fact play GTA VI on your rig, but you will have to get in line…
How to play GTA 6 in your browser
Over on X, a user by the name of germanfndezz has done something incredible. They’ve made their PlayStation 5 console playable in a web browser: “My PS5 does nothing most of the day, so I put it online. You get in line and when it's your turn you have the controller.”
It’s completely free, and while its popularity has unfortunately led to a temporary takedown at the time of writing, it’s expected to be back up and running soon.
The only catch is you need to queue for a go on the console, and the queue can get really, really long.
It’s also not considered piracy like other free web browser ports. You don’t need a ROM of GTA VI to play, and the copy being played on the console was purchased legally. This is basically the digital equivalent of passing your friend the PlayStation 5 controller when they’re at your house.
The experience isn’t seamless, of course. Because you’re essentially streaming someone’s console footage to your device, you can expect some input delay and stuttering issues, but it’s a hilarious and oddly wholesome way to play the game.
As we said before though, the popularity of the project has unfortunately taken it offline for a while, and germanfndezz has warned that he’ll never ask for money or funding to bring it back, so beware of impersonators. They’ve encouraged players to join their Discord though for updates on the project.
It's possible that other PlayStation 5 owners will see this and get inspired to do the same. Now we’ll be honest and say we don’t know how one would set something like this up, but it’s still really cool.
It’s probably also the only way you’ll play the game on PC for quite a while… Rockstar Games has made no mention of the PC port just yet, but dataminers and leaks believe it’ll release early next year.
Whether the next iteration of GTA Online drops at the same time, or later in 2027, remains to be seen.
