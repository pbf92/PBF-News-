Utz Brands Announced Transfer or Voluntary Withdrawal of Listing
On November 20, 2026, following the merger, Utz Brands notified the NYSE that it had closed the transaction and requested suspension of trading and filing of a Form 25 to delist and deregister its Class A common stock. Trading was suspended before the market opened that day; the delisting and deregistration will become effective 10 days after Form 25 is filed, and the company intends to file Form 15 to terminate registration and suspend reporting obligations.
Original SEC Filing: Utz Brands, Inc. [ UTZ ] - 8-K - Nov. 20, 2026
