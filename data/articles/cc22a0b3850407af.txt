TITLE: How to Survive Vice City: 14 GTA 6 Tips to Help You Build Your Criminal Empire
OUTLET: PCMag

Every time I fire up Grand Theft Auto VI, I do something new. I go to the mall, I dive into the ocean to escape cops, I slap a guy who wanted to go back into his burning car for more insurance money. The beauty of the open-world video game, very much pioneered by Grand Theft Auto, is the freedom to go wherever you want and do whatever you want. We can all finally play GTA 6, and it’s not long before you have free rein over the state of Leonida, whether you’re hijacking yachts off the coast or robbing banks in the boondocks.
All that freedom can be overwhelming, though, especially in a game as huge as this. GTA 6 has so many emergent outlaw opportunities, and increased mechanical complexity, that it’s the franchise’s best take on simply being an interactive crime simulator. Even separate from the action-packed, shoot ‘em up main story, the game encourages you to make cold and cunning decisions to chart your own nefarious path. Once you immerse yourself properly, it’s immensely entertaining, but it can be a lot to take in at first.
Need some suggestions on how to kick off your crime spree right? I’ve played the whole game, and these are my top tips.
1. Different Cars, Different Rules
As the title suggests, you’ll be stealing a lot of cars in Grand Theft Auto VI, whether you’re selling them for profit or simply escaping the law. This entry in the series gives you several unexpected factors to contend with when jacking vehicles. Some cars can be manually refueled at a gas station while others require an electric charge. Some cars you can just smash the window, while others must be unlocked via phone hack. The best cars, the sports cars outside the nightclubs, even force you to maintain a certain speed or the cops will start tracking you. So before you steal a car, know what you’re getting yourself into.
2. Mind Your Appearance
GTA 6 has countless shops where you can buy new clothes and change your hair. Choosing the game's Ultimate Edition over the Standard Edition will grant you access to even more customization options. But adopting new styles isn’t just about aesthetics. Commit a large enough crime and cops will broadcast your appearance all over the surrounding area, making it much more difficult for you to evade them. Changing up your look helps you become anonymous faster. Just make sure to hide your face with a mask and not carry your guns out in the open.
3. Save Your Money
Cash rules everything around you in GTA 6. Often in the story, you’ll need to earn a certain amount just to proceed, usually thousands of dollars. You soon gain the ability to keep your money in the bank, with higher limits the more you save, and you’ll want to do that. Banked funds may not be as immediately accessible as cash in your pocket, but that money is also much safer locked away. It will also earn interest, and you’ll lose far less of it if you die or get busted. You can even use your phone to find nearby ATMs for a quick deposit. Criminal mayhem is no excuse for ignoring fiscal responsibility.
4. Stay in Shape
Harkening back to Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, the playable characters in GTA 6 can become more or less healthy depending on their lifestyle. Lift weights to get stronger or get your cardio in for better stamina. Don’t fill up on junk food, but also don’t starve yourself and remember to get some rest. That’s just a good life tip, in a video game or otherwise.
5. Be Romantic
While past GTA games exclusively starred chaotic criminal men, GTA 6 pushes things forward by starring a chaotic criminal couple. Even better, the level of romance between Lucia and Jason is entirely up to you. Want to strengthen their bond? Try holding hands or going out to bars. Want to turn these lovers into enemies? Get too handsy at the strip club or abandon your partner during a shootout with the police. Maybe I’m a softie, but I recommend being romantic whenever you can. The world of Vice City is already harsh enough; who needs relationship tension on top of all that?
6. Aim Carefully
You’ll frequently have to gun down a lot of guys in GTA 6. If you aren’t careful, though, you’ll quickly get shot to death yourself, even with the best weapons. My advice is to stick to cover and advance cautiously but steadily so you aren’t pinned down and overwhelmed. Similar to Max Payne and Red Dead Redemption, you can briefly slow down time to focus and precisely hit enemy and vehicle weak spots. Don’t be afraid to take advantage of that system to proactively thin out the herd. The right drug store might even sell you something to temporarily boost your adrenaline and focus in the middle of a fight, so look out for that, too.
7. Pick Your Targets
Not only can you enter dozens of locations throughout Leonida, but chances are you can exploit them for cash. Each opportunity for crime comes with its own considerations. Robbing a local pawn shop or strip mall restaurant may be quick and easy, but the payout is so low it may not be worth the effort. Hitting a big bank may set you up for life, but between the high security inside and the cops on your tail once you exit the building, you’re in for a fight. You could even scope out where other criminals are hiding and destroy their drug labs or steal from their warehouses, all without the authorities ever caring. But these gangs are heavily armed, so don’t bring a knife to these gunfights. Our advice is keep an open mind and be aware of the potential creative crimes to commit all around you. It’ll make for a more varied, and profitable, illicit adventure.
8. Plan Your Exit Strategy
There’s a lot you can steal in GTA 6, from marked bills to illegal narcotics to luxury jewels. But none of it is worth anything if you can’t hold onto it. So before you start a crime, think about your exit strategy. Maybe park a getaway car at a convenient location so you don’t waste time stealing a new one before driving off. Consider which highways will get you out of the search zone the fastest. Choose who you’ll sell the stolen goods to, weighing factors like what price they’ll pay and how soon you can reach them. Basically, steal smarter.
9. Be Able to Pivot
GTA 6 gives you the option to go quiet before things get loud. You don’t have to shoot everyone, just threaten civilians and employees until they give you the access code for a safe. As you progress, you also gain more tools that make stealth more viable, like silently unlocking doors or nabbing bills without the dye pack exploding. However, be prepared to pivot if everything blows up. Be willing to use a bomb on the safe if you can’t get the code in time, or maybe place a propane tank next to it and blast it from a distance to cause an explosion. Shoot the guards before they shoot you back. How you handle situations affects your reputation and criminal profile. Being flexible, and not panicking, will help you handle any situation.
10. Know When to Cut Your Losses
If you plan your heists well, you’ll have a good amount of time to score some loot and escape before cops show up, especially if you bring along your armed partner to provide more defense. But you don’t have infinite time, so don’t get greedy. It’s better to leave with something than get arrested trying to get everything and winding up with nothing.
11. Enjoy the Side Activities
GTA 6 is a huge game with a lot to do, and it wants you to see it all. So take the time to actually enjoy it. Treat this fake Florida like the virtual vacation it is. Spend some time doing side missions like hunting, fishing, or searching for sunken treasure. Find a nice spot to meditate or hop on a plane and drop off drugs by literally flying under the radar. Simply exploring the world with no goal at all may reward you with anything from a secret sniper rifle on top of a casino to just a cool selfie photo opp on the beach. GTA 6 isn’t just about a life of crime, it’s about life itself.
12. Buy Headphones
GTA 6 is so packed with detail that it kept finding ways to consistently surprise me. One of the joys of Grand Theft Auto has always been listening to the carefully curated radio stations as you cruise to your next objective. When I drove to the electronics store, I thought the headphones I bought were simply a cosmetic item. I was surprised to discover that wearing them lets you keep listening to the excellent tunes even while just taking a stroll. The vibes are immersive and immaculate. I highly recommend doing this just for how it improves your mood, and puts you in the perfect headspace to plan your next big score.
13. Ignore Obstacles
True criminals don’t care about arbitrary social rules telling them where they can and can’t go. Not every building in GTA 6 has an interior to enter, but if you use your imagination, you can reach most places in the world. You see that mountain? You can go there and climb it. You see that airport? Outrun enough security guards, and you can hijack that airplane. If you don’t mind the chaos, Vice City has even more to show you if you explore without limits. So hop on that cruise ship, and don’t let anything get in your way.
14. Cheat Codes? Why Not
Like classic GTA games, GTA 6 lets you enter cheat codes to make your antics even wackier. There are dozens of codes to uncover, from putting more cash in your pocket to making the cops more bloodthirsty. However, you have to unlock these cheat codes as you play by fulfilling different requirements. Also, you can’t even use cheat codes until after you beat the game. So play GTA 6 the normal way first, then get weird with it.
