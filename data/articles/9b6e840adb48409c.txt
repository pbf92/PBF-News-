TITLE: GTA 6 players urged to avoid major glitch which softlocks your save
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Some Grand Theft Auto VI players are reporting that they're experiencing a soft-lock during Chapter 3 and have urged players to avoid a certain side mission that causes this glitch.
Although GTA VI has launched polished and relatively bug-free, some glitches have persisted and are being slowly patched by Rockstar.
Some, unfortunately, remain in the game and have yet to be fixed in an update.

One glitch that has caught a lot of players is a soft-lock that can occur at any point from Chapter 3, and it's to do with completing a certain side mission at a certain point in the game. We'll keep this as spoiler-free as possible, but bear in mind this will include some information on Chapter 3.

GTA 6's Missing Persons side mission may cause your game to soft-lock
At the beginning of Chapter 3, you're locked into playing as Jason for a bit and can't switch to Lucia. At this point, the game directs you to go meet Boobie in Vice City, but you're still able to free roam and complete side activities.
While players are reporting that most of the side content is safe to do as Jason at this point, the one that you should avoid doing is the Missing Persons side quest in Kelly County, which forces you to switch to Lucia. However, when you finish the mission, you remain locked as Lucia and can't switch back to Jason, effectively soft-locking you as there's no way to continue the story.
Players started reporting the glitch over the weekend, with many urging others to stay away from the Missing Persons questline at any point where you can't switch freely between Jason and Lucia.
We can also confirm this glitch hit a GAMINGbible colleague, who is now unable to continue their progress until Rockstar Games issues a fix.
As for when this could be fixed, GTA VI got a new patch today, numbered 01.002.002, though players continue to report that the soft-lock still remains in-game. It's possible that with enough reports, Rockstar will be able to issue a fix for this soon.
As always, make sure to keep multiple saves to avoid any other glitches or soft-locks that could occur during your playthrough.
Generally speaking, it's best to rotate between 3-5 different saves, making a new manual save every hour of gameplay, to avoid any major upsets that impede your progress. That way, if you run into a major glitch that ruins your playthrough, you can hop back a save or two. You'll lose up to a few hours of progress, but at least you'll be able to continue playing.
You also can't rely on the autosave feature, which seems to only occur after every major story mission and doesn't apply to anything you do while playing free-roam.
