Mineral Road Discovery announces C$2.25M private placement
- Mineral Road Discovery (CRSTF) announced a private placement of up to 15M units at C$0.15 per unit, targeting gross proceeds of ~C$2.25M.
- Each unit will consist of one common share and half of one common share purchase warrant.
- Each whole warrant will be exercisable at C$0.25 per share for a period of 12 months from issuance.
- Net proceeds will fund exploration, technical and development activities at the company's Bergslagen Tungsten Project, along with general working capital and corporate expenses.
- Registered finders will receive a cash commission or units equivalent to 5% of the units sold through their participation.
