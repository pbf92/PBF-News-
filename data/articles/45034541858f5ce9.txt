IP Strategy Holdings Received Notice of Noncompliance
On November 24, 2026, Nasdaq notified the company that it is not in compliance with Listing Rule 5250(c)(1) because it has not filed its Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The notice has no immediate effect on the stock's listing; the company has until January 23, 2027, to submit a compliance plan, and Nasdaq may allow until May 24, 2027, to regain compliance if it accepts the plan.
Original SEC Filing: IP STRATEGY HOLDINGS, INC. [ IPST ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
