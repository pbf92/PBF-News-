TITLE: My boss canceled our 1:1 so I could keep playing ‘GTA’
OUTLET: NBC News

Within the first 20 minutes of playing “GTA 6,” I’ve been Tased, driven a boat, ransacked a remote drug dealer’s compound, stolen their cash and made my getaway in a plane.
“Drive” by The Cars plays through the speakers as I get behind the wheel for the first time. That first ride in a new “GTA” still hits different. This time, it’s down a rainy road, lit by the glow of warm lights. It’s nostalgic and new at the same time.
Yes, my boss knows this is happening. And no, I do not have my driver’s license in real life.
But given that “Grand Theft Auto” is such a cultural phenomenon, I was given the green light to chronicle my adventures behind the virtual wheel.
For the uninitiated, “GTA” is one of the biggest entertainment franchises in history. Fans of the property have been waiting 13 years for Rockstar Games, the game’s publisher, to release its new installment. The adventure series almost always involves vehicle driving (and sometimes robbery), heist missions and exploring fictional cities that are modeled after real-world places.
To prepare for the game, I clocked over 700 hours in “Red Dead Redemption 2,” Rockstar’s previous major release. The new installment of “GTA VI” clearly carries shades of everything that made “RDR2” mesmerizing and addicting. From the reactive nonplayable characters (NPCs) — characters players don’t even control and only sometimes interact with — to the lifelike graphics.
“GTA VI,” however, feels even more alive and almost overstimulating. There’s so much happening at once, both to you as a player and the world around. You can also, for the first time, play as both the male and female leads. Jason Duval (a male) and Lucia Caminos (a female), the protagonists, are a couple that find themselves “in the middle of a criminal conspiracy” across the fictional state of Leonida (inspired by Florida).
Here’s a play-by-play of the chaos that ensued in my first few hours of gaming.
Hour 1
It’s chore time! This game is low-key turning into a drywall installation simulator.
I just fed my snake, and I am hanging drywall in my bedroom. The snake’s name is Dolores BTW.
And now I am painting. I got to choose between three colors: a blue, green and peach. I went with peach.
I am thinking about stealing a boat. Do I steal a boat?
Wait. The boat has scuba gear on it. Are we going scuba diving?
We are going scuba diving.
OK, stole the boat. We’re going offshore to get to deeper water to dive!
UHHH, SOMETHING JUST EXPLODED IN THE WATER.
OH, THERE IS A BOAT ON FIRE AND A BODY FLOATING IN THE WATER.
I’ve encountered a shark.
Oh, he got me. But I SURVIVED. First near-death experience, check.
Hour 2
There is also a tattoo parlor in Vice City. I do not have a lot of funds, so we gotta go cheap.
I am plunging some girl’s sink.
My man at the auto shop just fixed the engine of my car and is throwing in a respray for free. What color should I go with?
Update: I went with hot pink.
Oops. I already messed up the brand-new paint job on a drive less than a mile later.
I also hit a guy in an electric wheelchair, caused three cars to spin out, and now I’m being dragged down the street by a car towing a boat.
An inevitability in this game: I’m being apprehended by law enforcement.
Hour 3
I realized I need to buy this gun to do the next mission, so I am taking $500 out of my house stash (I now have $1,980 stashed away).
I think we need to start making some money. I am going to have to try and steal another car because I don’t feel like spending money on getting my car out of the pound.
Welp. I’ve been ambushed and am now fistfighting three men I allegedly wronged in the game intro.
“Everybody Have Fun Tonight” by Wang Chung is blasting as I knock them out, loot them and made at least $200.
Now I am stealing their truck.
First mission, going to a grow house. “HOT TO GO!” is now on the radio.
I need to keep babygirl Jason hydrated. But I realized I have been driving the wrong way for a mile.
Just made $150 stealing a car … that rate seems low?
I’m off to pick out a new outfit for Jason.
First real mission complete. I am sweaty and anxious.
Something I didn’t expect to be doing today: chasing an iguana.
Back on the more typical “GTA” grind: I stole a beat-up car next to a “vagrant camp” that seemed out of the cops’ sight.
Now I am going to see about getting my hot pink babygirl car out of the pound.
Oh, my God, I forgot to take off my gun before I walked into the impound. I have been arrested again. I lost $650.
So far, my main takeaway from “GTA 6”: Every time you are trying to make a decision, something else happens that makes you want to do another thing.
Stole a little white vespa. Now I am taking pictures in the police department parking lot to see how much it costs to get my babygirl pink car back.
Update: I GOT MY CAR BACK! It only took breaking into the impound lot...
