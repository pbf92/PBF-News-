EyePoint Faces Securities Class Action Over DURAVYU Statements
Less than 1 min read
EyePoint said it will vigorously defend against a securities class action alleging misleading statements and omissions about DURAVYU.
Key Highlights:
- The lawsuit was filed November 17, 2026, in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of New York against EyePoint and its executive officers.
- The proposed class covers people who acquired EyePoint securities from May 27, 2025, through August 14, 2026.
- The complaint alleges violations of federal securities laws related to statements and omissions concerning DURAVYU.
- The plaintiff seeks unspecified damages, fees and costs; EyePoint believes the allegations lack merit.
