TITLE: GTA 6: Meet Lexie Shoaibi, the Actress Behind Mandy
OUTLET: LAmag

When logging onto the new Grand Theft Auto VI, one of the very first characters to interact with is a woman named Mandy. Playing as Jason Duval, the gamer is tasked with collecting Mandy’s rent for his boss, meeting her at her house. Mandy welcomes the player inside, claiming to need help with her sink. The mood quickly shifts when her plea for help turns into a seduction attempt.
Jason refuses to play along with Mandy’s advances. Still in love with another character from the game, Lucia, he rejects her over-the-top flirtatious pursuit. She takes it personally, revealing her dark, sharper edge that could mean deadly consequences for Jason. A suspicious truck lurks outside her house, and Jason removes what might be a human bone from her sink — all signs leading to Mandy being a kind of serial killer.
The voice behind the real Mandy is actor Lexie Shoaibi, who said the instant virality has felt like “living in a dream.”
Though the character was modeled after Miami-based influencer Marissa DuBois, Shoaibi gained internet traction after the game’s release. She auditioned for the role in 2023, but was unaware it was for GTA 6.
“I think it was called something like ‘Project Bubblegum.’ They sent me some sides to read, and I did it like any other commercial audition,” Shoaibi told Vulture.
She didn’t realize her part made it into the storyline until GTA’s release day. Mandy became an overnight sensation across gaming communities.

“I think because I have some experience with online attention as a content creator, I’ve developed a pretty effective lens for seeing more good than bad,” Shoaibi said. “There are weirdos out there, and they’re going to try to bring you down, so it’s important to stay around the people and the places that aren’t doing that.”
The actress recently joined Hasan Piker’s livestream, drawing immediate online criticism. The chatter doesn’t bother Shoaibi.
“A lot of navigating is just scrolling along,” she said. “People feel a type of way about Hasan Piker, and now some people feel that way about me.”
Still curious to know what was in Mandy’s sink? Unfortunately, it’ll remain a mystery.
“When we were recording, it wasn’t specifically clarified or intended for me to base my acting around,” she told Vulture. “It was just some weirdness in the sink. That’s what I got.”
