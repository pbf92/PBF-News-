TITLE: OpenAI’s GPT-6.2 Astra finds optimizations that rival models cannot
OUTLET: Crypto Briefing

The new flagship model posts benchmark gains over its predecessor while cutting standard API prices by 20%
OpenAI’s newest flagship model is earning a reputation as the engineer who finds the slow query everyone else walked past. A post on X said that GPT-6.2 Astra finds performance optimizations that other models cannot.
That is a bold claim about a model barely out of the box. The benchmark numbers OpenAI attached to the release, however, give it some cover.
Two models, two jobs
OpenAI unveiled GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol on November 17, 2026. Both are available through the OpenAI API, ChatGPT Work and Codex.
The pair splits the workload. Astra targets complex professional tasks, while Sol is built for everyday applications.
Astra is pitched at long-horizon work, the kind of multi-step problem that takes hours rather than seconds. Its focus spans science, law, finance and coding.
Both models carry a context window of 1,050,000 tokens.
Both also run exclusively on OpenAI’s hosted infrastructure. Companies that want to deploy them on their own servers are out of luck.
The benchmark scorecard
On the Agents’ Last Exam, Astra scores 5.2 points higher than GPT-6 Astra. It does so while costing less than one-third as much as its predecessor on that test.

The more pointed comparison is with a competitor. Astra comes in 5.5 points ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 on the same exam, at around 5% of the cost.
The gains extend beyond a single test. Both new models posted a +9 point improvement on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 compared with GPT-6 Astra.
They also logged a +6.2 point increase on OpenAI’s internal professional tests. On those tests, the models used 22% fewer output tokens to get there.
Cheaper by design
OpenAI paired the performance bump with a price cut. Standard API pricing for GPT-6.2 Astra is 20% lower than GPT-6 Astra.
The new rates are $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. Cached input costs $0.40 per million tokens.
A fast release cadence
GPT-6.2 Astra follows GPT-6 Astra, which launched approximately in September 2026. That puts roughly two months between a flagship and its upgrade.
What it means for buyers and rivals
The research accompanying the launch suggests that cheaper high-end capability may push businesses to adopt AI at a faster rate.
There are trade-offs for customers too. The hosted-only setup means organizations with strict data residency or on-premises requirements cannot run these models inside their own walls.
The claim from X about unique optimization skills will be tested the way these claims usually are: by developers throwing their slowest code at the model and seeing what comes back.
