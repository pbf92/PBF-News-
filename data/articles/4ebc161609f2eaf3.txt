OUTLET: TradingView

Modexus Solutions announces $500M share repurchase program
Modexus Solutions MOD announced Wednesday that its board of directors has authorized a share repurchase program of up to $500M of the company's outstanding common stock.
Shares of MOD slipped nearly 6.8% in pre-market.
The company said that the authorization is effective immediately and does not expire.
This share repurchase authorization reflects our strong conviction in Modexus Solutions' next phase of compounding growth and our commitment to delivering on our long-term financial targets," said Modexus Solutions President and Chief Executive Officer, Neil D. Brinker. "Having transformed our business into a focused leader in mission-critical thermal management, we are capitalizing on secular tailwinds across our data centers and commercial HVAC end markets. Guided by our 80/20 culture and disciplined capital allocation methodology, we have the balance sheet strength and financial flexibility to invest in high-return organic capacity expansion and strategic M&A while returning capital to our shareholders."
