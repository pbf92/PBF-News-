TITLE: Metroid Dread Has Been Sold Out for Ages, and Is Now in Stock at a Steep Discount
OUTLET: VGC / IGN

After physical copies of Metroid Dread have become impossible to find at a normal price, Nintendo has now taken the sting out of defaulting to the digital version — now only £33.29 on the Nintendo Store (normally £49.99) in a huge Black Friday deal at 33% off.
That new price is for either a direct download or having a code emailed over to you, but you’ll still also get a physical A2 double-sided Metroid Dread poster sent to you, as well, as a free bonus item.
Save On A Poster Bundle Of Metroid Dread
For extra context, this is not only the lowest price ever seen for this version of Metroid Dread (confirmed by my own price-tracking methods), but is now the best way to grab the Samus’ earlier game to catch up before Metroid Ravenous comes out in January 2027.
The upcoming sequel is actually the reason why brand-new physical copies of Metroid Dread are impossible to find. After being announced in September 2026 as a sequel to Dread, and surprisingly receiving massive levels of positive fan anticipation, many Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 owners keen for Metroid Ravenous flocked to buy Metroid Dread — presumably to check out the 2021 side-scroller and see what the fuss is about, enough that any available cartridge copies are gone from regular retailers.
Essentially, the sequel ended up looking so good, the physical versions of the original at MSRP are now completely sold out.
What’s more, with Metroid Dread out of print (Nintendo never seemed to produce any more after its initial launch run), any physical copies left available to buy are either £40-50 for used/cartridge-only without-a-box copies on eBay and other reselling sites, or £60+ for brand new and sealed — like this £61.98 copy at Amazon UK via a third-party seller.
So, if you were keen on trying out Metroid Dread before Ravenous, but wanted to spend as little as possible, this £16.70 price drop offer is your best option right now before the sequel lands on January 28, 2027 on Nintendo Switch 2. You have until December 2 to decide before Nintendo’s Black Friday deal for this one runs out.
