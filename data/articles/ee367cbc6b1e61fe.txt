JJ Gabriel set for immediate Real Madrid setback as ex-Man United wonderkid put in his place
JJ Gabriel is set for a humble start to life at Real Madrid after leaving Manchester United
JJ Gabriel is set for an understated start to life at Real Madrid as Los Blancos look to avoid repeating previous mistakes. The 16-year-old confirmed his departure from Manchester United by posting a farewell message to the club on social media.
He also appeared in a glitzy video announcing he had signed with super-agent Ali Barat. While Gabriel is training in Madrid, he must bide his time for now, as Castilla (Real's B team) and the C team cannot sign players until January.
In the meantime, he is expected to play for the under-18s. When he does sign, he won't train with the first team initially, which may come as a blow to Gabriel and his entourage, led by his father Joe O'Cearuill.
Instead, the intention is for him to train and play for the B team. For Real, that is a departure from the club's handling of Martin Odegaard more than a decade ago.
Real signed the Norwegian as a 15-year-old, but their treatment of the youngster was highly questionable. Odegaard wasn't wanted by then-manager Carlo Ancelotti, who branded the signing a "PR exercise" by club president Florentino Perez. The now-Arsenal captain was also given little game time by Zinedine Zidane.
Odegaard was given a lucrative contract and trained with the first team, only to play with the B team. Spanish football expert Guillem Balague believes Los Blancos have learned lessons from that episode.

He told the BBC: "Madrid learned from the Martin Odegaard period that training with the first team while playing for the B side is a bad idea. So the plan is a spell in the B side, and when he is ready, Gabriel jumps straight into the first team."
Gabriel's perceived lack of progress at United was believed to be a factor in his father urging him to leave. That was despite the fact that he was on course to become the club's youngest-ever player against Brighton in the Carabao Cup.
Balague added that Los Blancos may not be pleased that their pursuit of Gabriel has become so public. He said: "Where Madrid are less happy is with how things have unfolded.
"Madrid wanted to set the timing themselves and be discreet, because they do not want problems with Manchester United and do not want to upset anyone. At Real Madrid, nobody is bigger than the club.
"The idea was to keep everything quiet until January and announce it then. But with Gabriel already training in Madrid, the secret was never going to last."
