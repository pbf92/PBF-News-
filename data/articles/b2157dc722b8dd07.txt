TITLE: Grand Theft Auto 6 is The Biggest Physical Game Launch in the UK Since 2019
OUTLET: GamingBolt

All the protests against Rockstar’s decision to not include a disc with Grand Theft Auto 6’s physical edition seem to have had little effect, at least in the UK. Nielsen IQ recently revealed (via The Game Business) that Rockstar’s title had the biggest physical game launch this year in the region, earning £32 million in revenue within three days.
But the ridiculous part is that it beat FIFA 20’s record, set in 2019, making it the biggest UK physical launch in over seven years (and the seventeenth biggest since 1995). And once again, that’s despite only featuring a download code in the box.
Much like in the United States, the PS5 and Xbox Series X/S saw tremendous sales increases – the former by 46 percent and the latter by a whopping 75 percent. Of course, the rest of Europe recorded some impressive “physical” sales. France sold over one million in the first three days while Spain recorded its biggest physical game launch ever.
The crazy part is that the open-world masterpiece, which became the highest-rated game of all time on Metacritic, has only been out for a week. It already has over 39 million unique players on PS5 and that momentum isn’t stopping anytime soon.
