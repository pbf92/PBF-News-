TITLE: Manchester United boss sends JJ Gabriel message after abrupt exit announcement
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United chief executive Omar Berrada insists the club "did everything" within their power to retain JJ Gabriel.
The 16-year-old prodigy announced his exit from United via social media on Tuesday ahead of his "next chapter". While Real Madrid went unmentioned, Gabriel is set to join the Spanish giants in the January transfer window, having gone AWOL from United.
Gabriel has not featured for United since early September, and the club has received no communication from him, his family, or his representatives since that time. The club has been taken aback by his conduct, blindsided by his self-proclaimed departure while he remains registered with the club, and is reserving the right to pursue legal action against Real.
United have yet to officially comment on Gabriel's situation; however, club chief Berrada gave a rare interview in which he was questioned about the forward, who was recently crowned the best player in the UK academy system by The Mirror.
"We did everything. Absolutely everything," Berrada told Sky Sports. "Let's not forget with JJ, he's a very young boy with a lot of media attention around him. Our primary concern is his well-being.
"Credit to the academy and people that have been here for many years, they helped him in his development. We still think this is the best place for him to continue his career and to grow.
"This club has a very rich history and tradition of developing young players and giving them the opportunity, as we're seeing this season with Shea Lacey. Obviously, Kobbie Mainoo has been doing well for us for a few years now - we're not afraid of giving young players opportunities.
"Obviously, him and his family have taken the decision to leave. We can only wish him the best. We want players that want to be here, that feel being at Manchester United is a privilege."

According to reports emerging from Spain, Gabriel has enlisted agent Ali Barat and has been presented with a three-year deal worth in excess of £850,000 (over $1.1 million) per season by Real.
In a polished video released on Tuesday, he stated: "Dear Manchester United, I arrived in 2020 as a 10-year-old boy with big dreams. Here I grew as a player and as a person. From scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award, you gave me moments I'll always treasure.
"To every coach, every member of staff, to my teammates and everyone who supported me, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow. As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything, JJ."
Although Gabriel is firmly committed to joining José Mourinho's club, who intend to place him within their Castilla reserve setup, the transfer remains unfinalized, with United prepared to pursue Real for additional compensation.
