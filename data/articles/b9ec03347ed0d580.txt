TITLE: Archbishop Moth taken to hospital ahead of Ann Widdecombe memorial service
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

Archbishop of Westminster, Most Rev Richard Moth, has been taken to hospital unwell.
He was due to lead a memorial service for Ann Widdecombe at Westminster Cathedral on Thursday afternoon. Auxiliary Bishop of Westminster, Rt Rev James Curry, will take his place.
A statement from the Diocese said: “Archbishop Richard Moth felt unwell and was taken to hospital on Wednesday. He is currently undergoing preliminary tests at the hospital.
“While awaiting the results of these tests, he is in good spirits and is looking forward to returning to Archbishop’s House.
“The Archbishop’s diary appointments are currently suspended. We pray for his full recovery.”
Political figures, including Reform UK leader Nigel Farage and Conservative chairman Kevin Hollinrake, are set to gather at Westminster Cathedral for the service at 2pm. The Cathedral’s choir will sing and eulogies will be given.
Widdecombe, a devout Catholic, died in July having been attacked at her home on Dartmoor, Devon. Joshua Kerry, 28 from Rotherham, has been charged with murder.
At a Requiem Mass in August, the former Conservative minister and later Reform party spokeswoman was remembered for “fearlessly” upholding her Christian convictions.
