TITLE: 'I love the owners' - Kevin De Bruyne has his say on Man City guilty verdict
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Kevin De Bruyne said that nothing will stop his love for Manchester City or his memories of winning trophies at the club. The club have been found guilty of breaking Premier League financial rules across nearly a decade that threatens to cast a cloud over everything they won during De Bruyne's nine years at the club.
De Bruyne signed for City from Wolfsburg in 2015 and became one of the greatest players ever seen at the club before an emotional departure in 2025. He won six Premier League titles, five Carabao Cups, three FA Cups and the Champions League, Super Cup and Club World Cup during his time in Manchester; he was also named Premier League Player of the Season twice.
City insist on their innocence and have appealed the decision, but even if it is upheld and should titles be stripped it will not change how the Belgian feels about the club and the people who brought him here. While acknowledging that others may have other opinions, De Bruyne believes that everything he won with City he won fairly.
"It still does not change. My football has not changed. Of course, in life there are rules, but everybody plays on the limit, I would say, and for me, I played in between the white lines, and I competed with other players, and I had many experiences with my teams, with the players," he said.
"That's the thing that I value most, and even later, I think in my career, the most important thing is the relationships between everybody that I had and how you manage these things, and I've given my all, and that's not going to change in my mind, I don't think a lot of people will change in their mind how they view me as a player, or how we played, but everybody has his own opinion, I guess, and other people may be disgraced a bit, but I can't change anything about that
"My feelings have never been different. Honestly, the way that I feel is just the way that I've been treated by this club, what I've done with this club for me personally, and how we've worked together as a group," he said. "Of course, it's a lot of things that's been going on, but I cannot change anything about that. I’m proud of what I've done as a football player, and honestly, this is my club,
"I love the owners, I've talked about a lot of stuff outside football, I've even spoken to Khaldoon [al Mubarak, chairman] about how to buy a club and everything, so there's a lot of things that go around after this, so I have nothing but affection for this club and that will never change. Even now, the people who treated me for 10 years, except for my family and everything, that's for me the most important thing."
The 35-year-old is playing at the Etihad for the second time since leaving but will hope he can have a better experience on Tuesday night. Last year he was substituted in the first half as Antonio Conte shuffled his team around after a Napoli red card, and watched from the sidelines as City won 2-0.
