TITLE: Innovid Receives Top Ranking in Current Offering Category in Creative Advertising Technologies Evaluation by Independent Research Firm
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Innovid named one of two Leaders and received highest possible scores in 13 evaluation criteria
NEW YORK, November 17, 2026--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Innovid, the leading omnichannel advertising platform, today announced that it has been named a Leader in The Forrester Wave™: Creative Advertising Technologies, Q4 2026. Innovid received the top ranking in the Current Offering category, with the highest possible scores in 13 evaluation criteria, including generative and agentic AI, ad serving, cross-channel sequencing, and desktop and mobile video.
The recognition follows Innovid's merger with Flashtalking under Mediaocean, bringing together complementary omnichannel capabilities across creative, delivery, measurement, and optimization. With NIVO AI at the core, Innovid is advancing its vision for a more connected, intelligent advertising ecosystem, where AI agents and orchestration help marketers turn insights into action across the campaign lifecycle.
In Forrester's evaluation, Innovid received the highest score in the Current Offering category with the highest possible scores (5 out of 5) in the following criteria: Generative and Agentic AI, Vision, Innovation, Partner Ecosystem, Adoption, Ad Serving, Decisioning, Cross-Channel Sequencing, Tagging, Identity, Desktop and Mobile Video, TV (OTT, CTV, and Linear TV), and Other Paid Formats (Out-of-Home, Audio, Native).
The report describes Innovid as having "a bold vision to become the independent operating system for omnichannel orchestration substantiated by its ability to sync creative, delivery, measurement, and optimization." The report also states that "Innovid's partner ecosystem excels due to deep integrations with DSPs, social media platforms, publishers, and measurement providers, while adoption of Innovid's AI brain – NIVO – spans expansive use cases."
"We believe this recognition reflects the progress we've made in bringing together the technology, intelligence, and infrastructure advertisers need to navigate a complex ecosystem and drive growth," said Zvika Netter, Innovid Co-Founder & CEO and Mediaocean President. "Our focus is on connecting fragmented processes, from creative development and delivery to measurement and optimization. With NIVO, we're taking that further, enabling orchestration of AI agents across these capabilities and helping marketers move from insights to action with speed and impact."
The evaluation also recognized Innovid's capabilities across video and TV, including its purpose-built CTV tools, broad tagging support, and unified linear and CTV workflows. The report also notes that customers praise Innovid's combination of dynamic creative and omnichannel activation, with responsive service and a roadmap accelerated by the Flashtalking merger.
