What to Look for in an End-of-Day Drawdown Plan
Lots of firms offer end-of-day drawdown. Before choosing a plan with end-of-day drawdown, it's worth checking these things first:
● Do payouts change with the plan? Picking a drawdown style shouldn't mean waiting on winning days or a payout window to get paid. On TPT's EOD plan true daily payouts start on day one.
● Is there a consistency rule after you're funded? A funded consistency rule can hold up a payout even when you're profitable. On TPT’s EOD plan there’s no funded consistency rule once you pass the test.
● Is it a profit cap or a withdrawal limit? A daily profit cap limits how much you can make in a day. A withdrawal limit keeps you from getting profits you’ve earned. They're not the same thing. On TPT’s EOD plan there’s no withdrawal limit so you can take your profits as soon as you earn them.
● What happens when you hit the daily loss limit? On TPT’s EOD plan if you hit your daily loss limit at or above your starting balance, it’s a soft breach. Trading pauses for the day and you can pick back up the next trading day.
