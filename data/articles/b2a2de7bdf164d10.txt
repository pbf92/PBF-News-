Who Is Lisa Cook?
Lisa D. Cook is a member of the Federal Reserve Board of Governors. She took office on 23 May 2022. She was reappointed in September 2023 for a term that ends on 31 January 2038, per her Fed biography.
She is an economist with a PhD from the University of California, Berkeley. She taught at Michigan State University.
On 29 June 2026 the US Supreme Court let Cook stay on the Board while she challenges the president's attempt to remove her, Bloomberg Law reported. In October 2026 the White House set up a committee to review allegations against her, and Cook denies them, PBS NewsHour reported. On 16 November her lawyer said she would not take part in the White House hearing, Yahoo Finance reported.
Why Cook's October Fed Dissent Matters
A dissent is a vote against the majority decision of the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC). On 28 October the FOMC kept rates at 3.75%-4.00%. Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack "preferred to raise the target range" by a quarter point, per the FOMC statement.
It was Cook's first dissent since she joined the Board in 2022, Fortune reported. Governors vote at every meeting, while most Reserve Bank presidents vote in rotation, per the Fed's FOMC page. A governor's dissent can point to a wider debate inside the Committee.
