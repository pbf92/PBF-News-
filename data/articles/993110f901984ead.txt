TITLE: Manchester United consider legal action as Real Madrid close in on JJ Gabriel
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Real Madrid had a memorable 2026 summer transfer window, as they brought in up to six new players to bolster their team. Now, a few months after the end of that transfer window, the Merengues are seemingly keeping up their high standards in the market.
This is clear from their pursuit of the English wonderkid JJ Gabriel. The young forward recently announced his desire to leave Manchester United, and several top European clubs were interested in him, including the likes of FC Barcelona, Bayern Munich, and Paris Saint-Germain.
However, as things stand, Los Blancos have won the race for his signature, and reports suggest that only official confirmation from the club remains before the transfer can be finalized.
While this is delightful news for Real Madrid, Manchester United are understandably not very happy with the situation. In fact, MARCA highlights that the English outlet The Times has reported that the Red Devils are investigating whether a club made illegal contact with him before he turned 16 years of age and considering legal action if they find this to be the matter.
It remains to be seen how this story will unfold, but United's frustration is understandable. They are set to get only €300,000 for one of the brightest talents in European football, and are very unhappy with the player and his family. Gabriel had spent the last six seasons with the Premier League side, and had not yet made his official first-team debut, even though he played a few minutes in a friendly game last summer.
Meanwhile, reports from Spain suggest that JJ Gabriel's move to Real Madrid is in its very final stages. In fact, he visited Valdebebas on Wednesday, which is a clear signal of what his next destination is going to be. Meanwhile, Manchester United would be considering all available options to ensure they get as much as possible from his departure.
