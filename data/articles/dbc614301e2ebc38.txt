TITLE: How To Play GTA 6 In First Person Mode Via A Recently Found Glitch
OUTLET: HotHardware

How To Play GTA 6 In First Person Mode Via A Recently Found Glitch
A duo by the name oneboyler and OrangeGuy shared the glitch in action on YouTube. Even though the developers aren’t offering it as a feature it works well as is and lets players soak in the gorgeous scenery of GTA 6. We can imagine that some fans will use this first-person mode to make some fun content.
To activate this glitch the camera binding needs to be modified first. On the PS5 hit the “Start” button, go to “Settings,” select “Controller,” and set the Touch Pad Button to “Camera 2/2.” On the Xbox Series consoles tap the “Menu” button, choose “Settings,” select “Controller,” and set the “View Button” to “Camera 2/2.”
Once you’re done with that head back to the open world and jump into any vehicle. Tap “up” on the D-pad so your character whips out their phone, then put it away by either tapping “Circle” on the PS5 or the “B” button on the Xbox Series consoles. Do this a few times until the first-person mode kicks in.
Because it’s a glitch not everything works perfectly, and if you want to go back into the default third-person mode you’ll need to reload your save. Therefore, we don’t recommend doing any story or side quests using this mode unless you’re prepared to lose that progress. Those who are more patient can wait for this mode to officially be added to the game, just like it was for GTA 5.
