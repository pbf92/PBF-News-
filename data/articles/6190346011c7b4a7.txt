Palatin Technologies Disclosed Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard
On November 24, 2026, Nasdaq notified Palatin Technologies that its stockholders' equity of $2,026,079 fell below the $2.5 million minimum required by Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(b)(1), and that it does not meet either alternative continued-listing standard. The notice has no immediate effect on trading; the company has until January 8, 2027, to submit a plan to regain compliance, and Nasdaq may grant up to 180 days to demonstrate compliance if it accepts the plan.
Original SEC Filing: PALATIN TECHNOLOGIES INC [ PTN ] - 8-K - Nov. 25, 2026
