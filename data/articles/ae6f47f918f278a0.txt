Agilent Technologies Inc. (A) Opens Its First Experience Center in Thailand
Agilent Technologies and Chulalongkorn University opened an experience center in Bangkok for research, method development, demonstrations, training and internships. It is Agilent's first collaboration of its kind in Thailand.
