TITLE: Woman, 40, arrested after gun found in Moston raid
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Woman, 40, arrested after gun found in Moston raid
She has been arrested on suspicion of possession of a firearm
A woman has been arrested after a gun was found during a police raid in Moston. Officers executed a warrant at a home on Mortlake Drive on Thursday evening (November 26).
Police searched the property and found a Glock firearm with bullets. A 40-year-old woman was arrested on suspicion of possession of a firearm. She is currently in custody for questioning.
The raid was part of a Greater Manchester Police operation aimed at tackling serious organised crime and the removal of firearms from communities across Greater Manchester.

Detective Inspector Rick Castley from GMP's Serious Organised Crime Group said: "This warrant and the recovery of a loaded firearm demonstrates the proactive work being carried out by our officers every day to target those involved in serious organised crime and to prevent dangerous weapons from being used on our streets.
“The possession of firearms presents a significant risk to the public, and removing a weapon such as this from circulation has undoubtedly made our communities safer.
"We remain committed to identifying, locating and arresting those suspected of involvement in organised criminality, and we will continue to pursue every available line of enquiry to bring offenders to justice.
“Our message is clear: we will not tolerate the possession or use of firearms in Greater Manchester. We work closely with local communities and partner agencies, and information provided by the public continues to play a vital role in helping us disrupt criminal activity and protect residents.
“Anyone with concerns about organised crime, weapon possession or suspicious activity should contact us via 101, or via our Live Chat on www.gmp.police.uk. Always dial 999 in an emergency.”
