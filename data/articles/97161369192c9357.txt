TITLE: Poundland falls into administration with around 11,000 jobs at risk
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Poundland falls into administration with around 11,000 jobs at risk
The company has officially appointed administrators
After weeks of trying to secure a takeover deal, discount retailer Poundland has fallen into administration, putting around 11,000 jobs at risk.
The chain, which runs roughly 600 stores across the UK, has held urgent talks over the past month to seek a buyer but has entered insolvency after failing to strike a deal before a key deadline late on Wednesday.
AlixPartners confirmed that experts from the corporate finance firm have been hired as administrators for Poundland.

The retailer will continue to trade from its stores as the administrators continue to seek a sale of the company and its assets.
Poundland’s ownership had received a number of offers, including from Irish real estate firm Martin Property Group, investment firm Xyra Holdings and German investment firm Zachert Private Equity but failed to secure a deal.
Gordon Brothers has faced mounting political pressure to protect the chain’s future and MPs have accused it of deliberately cutting off funds and pushing the retailer to the brink of insolvency.
Poundland’s owner Gordon Brothers said: “First and foremost, our thoughts are with Poundland colleagues. We recognise how difficult the continued uncertainty is for them. Efforts to preserve as many jobs and stores as possible will continue, led by the administrators.”
The firm insisted it had “explored every avenue to secure a sale of the whole company”, but said the chain had “faced several years of instability”.
It said: “Supported by Gordon Brothers, the management team and colleagues at Poundland worked hard over the past 12 months to improve the underlying performance and achieve £100 million of cost savings.
“Despite a reduced cost base, looking forward, the business is still vulnerable to inflationary pressures or any downturn in sales and margin. We recognise and we are thankful for all the hard work and effort by management and colleagues throughout.”
In an "urgent update" shared on its website, Poundland wrote: "Poundland Limited (the Company) has entered administration on 25 November 2026 (the Administration), and Clare Kennedy, Daniel Imison and Kevin Wall (the Joint Administrators), all of AlixPartners UK LLP, were appointed as Joint Administrators.
"The Joint Administrators are licensed insolvency practitioners, licensed in the UK by the Insolvency Practitioners Association. Insolvency practitioners are bound by the Insolvency Code of Ethics when carrying out all professional work relating to an insolvency appointment.
"The affairs, business and property of the Company are being managed by the Administrators, who act as agents of the Company without personal liability. The Company, including Dealz in the Republic of Ireland, will continue to trade in the near term and most stores will remain up until further notice."
In an update regarding refunds and exchanges, the company wrote: "We no longer offer refunds or exchanges for items purchased in-store if you simply change your mind. This applies to purchases made on or after 26 November 2026."
