Jaron Schneider reviewed the Pulse Explore gaming earbuds and gave it high marks as a gaming earbud, but docked some points for leaving out features that are found in other non-gaming earbuds, like ANC and transparency mode. But that's the point. These earbuds are designed from the ground up for gaming.
