TITLE: ‘GTA 6’ makes history with estimated $2.36 billion launch
OUTLET: TheGrio

“Grand Theft Auto 6” is already generating billions of dollars in estimated sales following its November 19 release, positioning the latest installment in Rockstar Games’ blockbuster franchise among the biggest entertainment launches in history.
Analytics firm Sensor Tower estimates that pre-orders and first-day sales exceeded 24.5 million copies, generating approximately $2.36 billion. The figures have not been confirmed by Rockstar Games or its parent company, Take-Two Interactive, which described demand for the game as “unprecedented.” The release follows a 13-year wait since Grand Theft Auto 5 debuted in 2013.
The game’s launch triggered significant increases in downloads and online activity across multiple markets, according to a report by BBC. Internet infrastructure company Cloudflare reported that PlayStation 5 downloads in the United Kingdom peaked at 8.6 times normal levels around launch. New Zealand, where the game became available first, recorded a 14.8-fold increase approximately four hours earlier.
Rockstar’s online services also experienced substantial traffic surges, reaching 11.2 times normal levels in Australia and 9.3 times normal levels on the U.S. East Coast. In the United Kingdom, interest reached seven times normal levels, compared with 5.3 times in France and Germany. Virgin Media O2 also reported that the launch broke its evening internet traffic record as players downloaded the game’s massive 212GB installation file.
Sensor Tower estimates that approximately three-quarters of the game’s sales have been on PlayStation 5, with the remainder on Xbox. Gaming research firm Niko Partners projects that GTA 6 could generate more than $3 billion in worldwide sales during 2026. The online component could provide another significant revenue stream, following the success of GTA 5 Online.
The latest installment introduces players to Lucia and her partner Jason, two small-time criminals navigating Leonida, a fictional state inspired by Florida. The franchise, which has become one of gaming’s most commercially successful properties, is known for combining open-world gameplay with satire of American culture and the pursuit of wealth.
