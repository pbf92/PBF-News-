TITLE: A list of AMD graphics cards for the unofficial launch of GTA 6 on PC has been published.
OUTLET: GameGPU

A preliminary list of compatible graphics cards has been published for the unofficial port of Grand Theft Auto 6 to computers as part of Project VIctory. AMD RadeonThe list was prepared by the developer Marvis and included 16 models of three generations.
