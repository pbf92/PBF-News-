TITLE: Nioh 3 Is Coming to XBOX in 2027
OUTLET: Xbox Wire

Summary
- Team NINJA and Koei Tecmo are bringing the Nioh series to XBOX for the first time ever with Nioh 3 and Nioh 3: Complete Edition coming to XBOX Series X|S on January 28, 2027.
- The Dark Samurai Action RPG explores a variety of locations and time periods of feudal Japan in an epic story that blends fantasy and history.
- Nioh 3 is Team NINJA at its peak with the kind of aggressive, mastery-focused combat that built their reputation with other action series, including NINJA GAIDEN, plus the ability to seamlessly switch between Nioh’s classic Samurai and brand-new Ninja Combat styles.
- As the culmination of Team NINJA’s years of experimentation and refinement of the Nioh series, and following the incredible response to Ninja Gaiden 4, the studio felt that it was finally time to bring Nioh to XBOX.
2025 marked the “Year of the Ninja” when the legendary Team NINJA development team released both Ninja Gaiden 2 Blackand Ninja Gaiden 4. Well, 2027 is poised to be the “Return of the Ninja” as for the first time ever, the Nioh series is making its debut on XBOX with Nioh 3 and Nioh 3: Complete Edition coming to XBOX Series X|S.The Complete Edition includes the full game and the two DLC expansions, which brings new story content, new bosses, new weapons, new abilities, endgame content, and more. For XBOX players, this is the perfect entry point into the series as it features the pinnacle of combat refinement you’ve come to know and expect from Team NINJA, as well as an open-field format that lets you explore and take on challenges at your own pace. No prior series knowledge is needed to understand the epic story that takes place across multiple eras of feudal Japan!
If you aren’t familiar with Nioh 3, it’s Team NINJA’s take on the Age of the Samurai in Japan, as an open-field Dark Samurai Action RPG where you’re caught in a nefarious coup started right before you are appointed Shogun – the ruler of Japan. You play the role of Tokugawa Takechiyo, and just before taking the throne, your younger brother succumbs to his hatred and jealousy, and leads an army of Yokai (demonic spirits) to overthrow the natural order and take over Japan. Takechiyo survives thanks to the intervention of the Guardian Spirit Kusanagi, who lets you travel through time and experience battlefields across different eras and key locations.
In order to deal with the hordes of demonic Yokai, human rebels, and giant, grotesque bosses standing in your way of getting revenge on your younger brother and becoming the true Shogun of Japan, Takechiyo has some excellent tutelage in both Samurai and Ninja combat. In a series-first, you can switch seamlessly between the Samurai combat style and the new Ninja combat style: Samurai combat relies on specific weapon stances, which holds the keys to unlocking devastating combos, whereas Ninja combat relies on faster, fluid movements, aerial attacks, and elusiveness to chain together relentless attacks.
Takechiyo, as one would expect of a Shogun-to-be, can wield a series-high amount of weapons – from a variety of swords, bows, gunpowder weapons, axes, spears, fist weapons, and more. Weapons and armor provide increases for a wide variety of combat stats – with plenty of drops to sort through, finding the right weapon with the perfect stats to complement your play style is a key element of the Nioh series. Players also have access to Transformed Arts, Ninjutsu, Onmyo Magic and creatures summoned through Summoning Seals for you to perfect the ultimate combat style.
Team NINJA is in peak form with the combat of Nioh 3, and XBOX players getting to experience it for the first time is a key reason why 2027 marks the Return of the Ninja. After years of the development team experimenting and tweaking the series formula for Nioh 3, and following the incredible Xbox fan reactions to Ninja Gaiden 4 on XBOX, the team felt it was the perfect moment to release the series on XBOX – just in time for the series’ 10th anniversary. The Nioh 3: Complete Edition gives XBOX players access to the base game, but also the two DLC expansions, Hell Rising and Bloody Insurrection. Both DLC expansions add to the game’s story with new Eras and locations to explore, adding new equipment, combat abilities, skills, and much more. The DLC also adds several end-game level modes, such as the Battlefield of Carnage, which challenges you to take on 100 floors of battlefields overrun with enemies and bosses.
Nioh 3 and Nioh 3: Complete Edition will launch for the first time on XBOX Series X|S beginning January 28, 2027 – don’t miss out on the epic battle!
