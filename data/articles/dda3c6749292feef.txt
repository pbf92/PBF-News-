SPACE ID: Unlocks 58.21 million ID - 22 Mar 2027
SPACE ID has scheduled a supply unlock for 22 Mar 2027. The release covers 58.21 million ID, equal to 3.74% of the circulating supply. The tokens are allocated to the foundation, investors and team.
The full amount unlocks in a single day, giving all three groups the option to sell tokens that were previously locked. If recipients put a significant share of those tokens up for sale, the added supply could put pressure on the price of ID.
