TITLE: GTA 6 gets surprise PlayStation Plus perk that lets you play without a PS5
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

PlayStation and Rockstar Games have enjoyed a long-standing relationship, and the launch of GTA 6 is no exception. Sony's “plays best on PS5” slogan has been front and centre of its marketing campaign, and now PlayStation Plus subscribers have another way to enjoy Rockstar's latest blockbuster.
Just days after the launch of GTA VI, a game that's already sparked quite the debate among gamers, PlayStation Plus Premium subscribers can now stream the game directly to their PS5 or PlayStation Portal. What's more, if you own Sony's handheld, you don't even need a PS5 to enjoy your adventures in Vice City and beyond.
Of course, you'll still need to purchase GTA VI, as it's not included in the PS Plus library. However, for those who haven't yet made the jump to the current console generation, this could be a cheaper way to play.
GTA 6 gets a welcome PlayStation Plus Premium benefit
As confirmed by Push Square, cloud streaming for GTA VI wasn't available on PlayStation at launch, but the feature has since been successfully tested. I've also checked my PS5 with a PS Plus Essential subscription and, as expected, the cloud streaming option is absent.
Unlike Remote Play, which relies on connecting to your own console, cloud streaming allows you to play compatible games directly from Sony's servers without having to download them. All you need is a compatible device, a PS Plus Premium subscription, a digital copy of the game and a reliable internet connection.

Sony's “plays best on PS5” campaign began on June 24 2026, raising questions as to why its console would be the better platform to play GTA VI over Xbox Series X/S. Would it have exclusive graphics and performance features or perhaps even exclusive DLC?
While no PlayStation-exclusive DLC has been confirmed, GTA VI takes advantage of the DualSense controller's haptic feedback and adaptive triggers, as well as the PS5's 3D audio technology and high-speed SSD. Now, PS Plus Premium subscribers have another perk to enjoy.
GTA 6 can also be played on Xbox One
It's not just PlayStation gamers who can benefit from cloud streaming, either. While Sony has only just enabled the feature, Microsoft has supported cloud streaming for GTA VI since launch, meaning that even those who still own an Xbox One can enjoy Rockstar's latest blockbuster without upgrading their console.
What's more, Microsoft published a handy guide yesterday explaining how to get up and running with cloud streaming and Remote Play.
It's worth keeping in mind that GTA VI uses Xbox Series S graphics settings when played via Xbox Cloud Gaming, and certain Game Pass subscriptions have monthly cloud playtime allowances.

Can you play GTA 6 on PC without a console?
Unfortunately, it's not quite such good news for PC gamers who are still waiting for a port of GTA VI. While both PlayStation and Xbox support Remote Play on PC, you'll still need a current-gen console to stream the game.
While cloud streaming might not be everyone's cup of tea, especially if you don't have a reliable internet connection, it's great to see more ways to enjoy GTA VI without having to spend hundreds of pounds or dollars on a new console.
Fingers crossed, PC gamers won't have to wait too much longer to join in on the fun. In the meantime, while Sony might insist that GTA VI “plays best on PS5”, you don't necessarily need to own one to enjoy Rockstar's latest blockbuster.
Grand Theft Auto VI is out now on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
