TITLE: The Kids, Aunties, and Uncles Building Websites
OUTLET: Christianity Today

Artist and author Sho Baraka recently traveled to East Palo Alto, California, to visit StreetCode Academy, a nonprofit that aims to provide low-income communities with the tech skills they need to make a living in Silicon Valley, or pursue entrepreneurship and innovation.
In this short documentary from CT’s Big Tent Initiative, Baraka interviews StreetCode cofounder and CEO Olatunde Sobomehin and others about East Palo Alto, Silicon Valley’s digital divide, and how faith shapes the organization’s mission.
