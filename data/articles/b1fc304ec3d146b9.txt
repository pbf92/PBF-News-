TITLE: Frontier Nuclear receives Nasdaq non-compliance notice over late annual filing
OUTLET: TradingView

Frontier Nuclear receives Nasdaq non-compliance notice over late annual filing
- Frontier Nuclear and Minerals received a Nasdaq notice on November 18, 2026, for failing to timely file its FY2026 Annual Report.
- Nasdaq has given Frontier until January 18, 2027, to submit a plan to regain compliance.
- The company expects to file its annual report before the deadline and will submit a compliance plan if required.
- If Nasdaq accepts the plan, Frontier could receive an extension of up to 180 days, through May 17, 2027, to regain compliance.
