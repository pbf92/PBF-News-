TITLE: US political coalition calls for US-China trade to be tied to religious freedom
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

A former US Senator has backed a new political coalition which is calling for religious freedom to be a condition of strong trade ties between the United States of America and China.
Former Republican US Senator and Governor of Kansas Sam Brownback, who served as an ambassador-at-large for international religious freedom in President Trump's first term, supported calls to revoke permanent normal trade relations between the two countries as he announced the China Freedom Alliance.
The group has proposed a return to annual congressional reviews, that would judge whether there have been improvements in human rights and religious freedoms in the last year, in order to continue trading.
Permanent normal trade relations (PNTR) between the two countries came into effect in 2000 - a decision which Brownback regrets supporting.
The Kansas politician called it “the worst vote [he] cast while [he] was in the United States Senate.”
Brownback said that 14 groups have joined the coalition, which is increasingly concerned with cases of Christians being imprisoned, and the scale of the controversial "sinicization" policy.
"Sinicization" began as a historic phenomenon of Chinese cultural exchange, but is perceived today as the process of forcing religious organisations to follow the doctrine of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP).
Rev Dr. Bob Fu, president of Christian human rights organisation ChinaAid, believes that persecution of believers is intensifying in China.
Fu highlighted the imprisonment of Pastor John Cao, a US resident with advanced prostate cancer, who has completed a seven year sentence but has been barred from returning to his family.
He also spoke about 69-year-old Pastor Gao Quanfu, of Light of Zion Church in Xi’an, Shaanxi Province, who is facing more than ten years on fraud charges over church offerings, with his trial ending without an outcome this month.
“Trade must serve freedom—not finance tyranny. These are not isolated incidents,” Fu said. “They reveal a deliberate campaign to crush religious freedom.”
The Committee for Freedom in Hong Kong Foundation also allege that government influence over religious organisations is leading to oppression.
Hong Kong, is a Special Administrative Region (SAR) of the People's Republic of China, which held autonomy over lawmaking until growing influence on public policy by Beijing.
The 2020 National Security Law extended penalties of up to life in prison for subversion and secession among other crimes.
Frances Hui, Policy and Advocacy Manager, cited the imprisonment of British-Hong Konger businessman Jimmy Lai and pro-democracy campaigner Joshua Wong, both of whom are Christians.
In light of these cases of alleged Christian persecution, Brownback suggested Helsinki Accords-style negotiations.
The Helsinki Accords were signed in 1975 by countries on either side of the Iron Curtain during the Cold War, committing to human rights, equal opportunity for all and self-determination of peoples.
The comments were made before President Trump travelled to Shenzhen for historic three-way talks with President Xi Jinping of China and Russian President Vladimir Putin.
Xi and Trump were seen holding hands at the summit, as the three leaders released a symbolic "peace declaration" - details of which are unknown.
