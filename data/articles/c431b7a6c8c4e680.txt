Prop firms recorded $1.97 billion in payouts since October 2023
Tracked prop firm payouts are nearing $2 billion since October 2023 across 45 firms, with more than $115 million in November 2026 alone, based on Payout Junction’s blockchain-matched data. These figures verify that payments occurred, not that average traders are profitable after fees, resets and subscriptions. Evaluation rules, drawdown mechanics and payout eligibility determine whether a profitable trading record converts into a withdrawal. Advertised account size is a weak comparator: compare loss allowances, trailing versus static drawdowns, profit targets, buffers, caps and consistency rules. The industry is expanding into crypto and tokenized stocks, but most programs start with simulated accounts and payouts are not guaranteed.

How To Compare Accounts: Key Numbers
- Target-to-loss ratio: Profit target divided by loss allowance indicates difficulty relative to risk room. Larger advertised balances can mask tighter allowances.
- Static vs trailing drawdown: Trailing thresholds can rise with unrealized gains, reducing cushion for later losses, especially with intraday trailing logic.
- Payout eligibility: Consistency rules, buffers and caps can delay or limit withdrawals even after profits.
- Prohibited strategies: Copy trading, news trading, automation and hedging rules differ by firm and phase.
- Total cost: Beyond challenge fees, include subscriptions, resets, funded activation, data and platform fees, plus withdrawal charges.
Legitimacy And Risk
Retail prop programs sell evaluations and simulated funded accounts with contractual payouts for eligible performance. Belgium’s FSMA warns about repeated-challenge costs and program risks. Review the operating entity, governing law, dispute venue and rule-change provisions. High payout counts do not guarantee future payments or firm solvency.
Final Verdict
Approaching $2 billion in tracked payouts signals industry scale, not net trader profits. The most suitable firm is the one whose evaluation and funded-stage rules match the trader’s strategy and risk control. Calculate real loss room, target-to-loss ratios, trailing effects, payout buffers and total attempt costs before purchase. These metrics are more predictive than advertised account size or headline profit splits.
