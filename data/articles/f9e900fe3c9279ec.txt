Apple Stock Is Trending Today: What's Going On?
Apple Inc AAPL shares volatile Wednesday. Another bout of AI selling, hot inflation data, bets on Fed tightening and proposed tariffs pressure growth stocks heading into the long holiday weekend.
- Apple stock is showing upward bias. What’s the outlook for AAPL shares?
Apple in Focus as AI Sell-Off Resumes
Nvidia Corp‘s NVDA leadership news set off a rally in communication services stocks earlier this week, but that rally was short-lived. Nvidia is off more than 8%, after a 5.9% fall Tuesday and a 31.5% leap Monday.
Apple is volatile alongside the broader tech group as AI uncertainty lingers, and investors may be trimming risk ahead of the long weekend. Although tech stocks extended their slide Wednesday morning, the broader market was bouncing midday at last check.
Apple stock turned positive for the session in early afternoon trading. Investors have treated Apple as a safer tech play during the AI sell-off because it isn’t spending heavily on data centers.
Inflation, Fed and Tariffs Stay in the Spotlight
Excluding food and energy, October core PCE inflation ran at 3.3% annually, beating the 3.2% forecast and reaching a level last seen in November 2023. Higher borrowing costs can hit stocks priced on future profits.
Traders expect a Fed rate hike in December, with CME FedWatch data cited by Benzinga assigning it a 99.8% probability. At the last reading, the 10-year yield sat at 4.85%.
Trade policy is the third drag. The U.S. Trade Representative’s office floated new duties on 14 trading partners, including Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, the EU and China, with China set to take an additional 20% on top. Higher import duties could raise costs for tech companies like Apple.
Apple Stock Remains In Uptrend
Technically, Apple appears to be pausing inside a larger uptrend. After topping out at $317.40 on June 8, the shares fell to $273.75 by June 25 and then rallied to fresh highs. They remain above their 20-, 50- and 200-day averages, with the 50-day above the 200-day.
Wednesday’s momentum is mixed. MACD is under its signal line but above zero, and RSI at 55.5 is neutral, which fits a pause more than a deep sell-off.
A close above $360.83, the 52-week high and a key resistance level, would open the door for further upside. A close below the 20-day average at $348.55 would likely drive selling pressure.
