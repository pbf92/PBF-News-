TITLE: Claude maker Anthropic prices record $81.3 billion IPO ahead of Nasdaq debut
OUTLET: EdTech Innovation Hub

Anthropic set its IPO price at $83 per share, above its initial range, in an offering expected to bring the company $79.4 billion before fees
Anthropic has priced the largest initial public offering on record, with 980 million shares on offer at $83 each. The maker of Claude is expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19 under the ticker symbol
The deal totals $81.34 billion at the offer price, surpassing the roughly $75 billion SpaceX raised in June.
Anthropic increased both the price and the number of shares
Anthropic initially marketed 825 million shares at $70 to $78 each. The final offering adds 155 million shares and is priced $5 above the top of that range.
The company is selling about 956.4 million shares, accounting for $79.38 billion in gross proceeds. Existing stockholders are selling the remaining 23.6 million shares, worth about $1.96 billion at the offer price. Those proceeds go to the selling stockholders.
The offering is expected to close on November 20, subject to customary closing conditions. Underwriters have 30 days to buy up to 147 million additional shares.
The IPO follows an expansion of Claude’s education work
The pricing comes less than a week after Anthropic extended free access to Claude for Teachers through July 30, 2028. The offer covers verified U.S. K-12 educators and qualifying schools and districts. Anthropic also plans to study how the tool affects educator wellbeing and teaching practice in Detroit Public Schools Community District.
Anthropic’s education work also includes a four-year, $200 million commitment with the Gates Foundation, announced in May. The partnership covers several areas, including K-12 education, global health and economic mobility, and combines grants, Claude usage credits and technical support.
