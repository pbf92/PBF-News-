Stock of the Day: How Low Will Micron Go?
Micron Technology, Inc. MU fell almost 14% yesterday. There was no company specific news. Analysts attribute the move to the broad selling in the semiconductor industry.
There is a good chance the shares may drop even lower. This why Micron is the Stock of the Day.
If a stock is in a downtrend, it is because there is more supply than demand. There are more shares to be sold than there are to be bought.
To gain the attention of buyers, sellers must offer their shares at a discount to the last price. This creates a downtrend.
Support is a price or level where there are large amounts of buy orders. Downtrends end when they reach support.
One of the main reasons why there is support at certain prices is because of remorseful sellers. These are people who regret selling after the price moves higher.
Many of them vow to buy their shares back if they can eventually get them for the same price they were sold for. If the shares return to this level they enter buy orders and if there are enough of them, it will create support.
As you can see on the chart, there was support for Micron around $653 in May. When the shares returned to this level in November, remorseful sellers placing buy orders created support.
When Micron rallied from $463 to $653 in April and May, the move was so rapid that the shares spent little time at the levels in between. There were even some ‘gaps’, which means there wasn’t even any trading at some of these prices.
The stock closed at one price and opened at a higher price. There was no trading in these gaps.
Because of this, there were not a lot of people who sold who will be trying to buy back on the way down. This means there may not be many buy orders between current levels and $463.
This means there’s a chance Micron reaches $463. There is also a chance it does so quickly. There may be support at this level because as you can see on the chart, it was a resistance level earlier this year.
