Most people struggle to name more than 20 or 30 bird species. So, of course, Rockstar has gone overboard and thrown in 62 types of birds into GTA 6. They will add to the game’s incredible density, but make game completion that much harder.
It would be very unRockstarlike to skimp on the details and granularity of GTA 6‘s world. It’s no surprise to hear then that Grand Theft Auto 6 has more bird types littered throughout the game than America has states—much more.
