Passage Bio Received Notice of Noncompliance
On November 12, 2026, Passage Bio received notice that it did not meet Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(b)(1), which requires at least $2.5 million in stockholders' equity; it reported equity of $50,000 and did not meet the rule's alternative requirements. The notice has no immediate effect on trading. The company expects its pending merger with Remix Therapeutics to restore compliance; following closing, the shares are expected to trade under the symbol RMTX.
Original SEC Filing: Passage BIO, Inc. [ PASG ] - 8-K - Nov. 18, 2026
