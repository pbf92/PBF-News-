TITLE: Theorem Launches Founding Partner Program for Shopify Brands
OUTLET: MarTech Cube

Brands selected for the 90-day program will receive free access to Upward to improve and close their storefront conversion gap
Theorem, a digital marketing solutions provider, today announced the launch of its Founding Partner Program for its Upward for D2C solution. During this free, 90-day program, retailers that use Shopify as their ecommerce platform will have the opportunity to gain early access to Upward for D2C and work closely with Theorem to identify their storefront conversion gaps, activate existing video content and improve shopping experiences. Eligible Shopify D2C brands are encouraged to apply for the program now.
The Storefront Conversion Gap
Today’s shoppers discover and explore products through new and existing avenues such as video, creators, customer content, reviews and social proof. By the time they reach a brand’s storefront, they likely already have a strong purchase intent.
However, the stark contrast of going from a dynamic discovery to a static shopping page can cause shoppers to lose their momentum before making a purchase. This storefront conversion gap is a common challenge for many retailers and can be difficult to close for three reasons:
- Limited visibility into friction – While brands can see that they’re getting traffic and clicks, they lack visibility into exactly where shoppers lose confidence and drop off.
- Disconnected shopping experiences – Social platforms can create excitement around a product, but that momentum can fade when shoppers land on a static product page.
- Perceived complexity – Brands want to invest in video commerce, but the perceived cost, complexity and measurement challenges make them hesitant.
The Founding Partner Program is designed to give brands a structured, customized way to identify those gaps, test solutions and measure what works.
“With more than 20 years in AdTech, MarTech, and digital marketing, Theorem has brought that experience to the development of Upward for D2C, creating a solution that helps brands transform their storefronts into a growth engines,” said Jay Kulkarni, founder and CEO of Theorem. “Through our Founding Partner Program, selected brands will gain visibility into shoppers drop off and turn existing content into shoppable video experiences that close conversion gaps. A 30-day customer pilot achieved a 15% conversion rate using Upward and helped earn the2026 MarTech Breakthrough Award for the Overall Conversion Solution of the Year. We build on conversion gain to drive higher average order value and stronger customer retention, with measurable results at every stage.”
