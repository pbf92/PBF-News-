TITLE: Petition against 'anti-Christian' Netflix series passes 130,000 signatures
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

More than 130,000 people have called on Netflix to axe a new series, believing it is blasphemous and anti-Christian.
The Body, an eight-part drama for young adults, has been described by its creator Quinn Shephard as a “raunchy teen psychodrama with a dash of Catholic horror”.
It features schoolgirls from a Catholic high school who perform a mock satanic ritual, offering a classmate as a virgin sacrifice. When she falls into a coma, the pupils believe they see visions and hysteria spreads.
A petition hosted by CitizenGo, set up before the show was released on 11 November, said the streaming giant is sending “something into Christian homes that no parent would ever invite in”.
It states: “On All Saints' Day, Netflix promoted it by dressing its characters as mock saints. One of them wore a crown of thorns.
“The show's own tagline promises that the girls ‘pray hard and sin harder’. You spend years teaching your daughter to pray. Netflix wants to teach her that prayer leads to the Devil.”
“Ask yourself this. Would Netflix ever turn Muslim prayer, Hindu worship or even Wiccan ritual into a sex-and-Satan horror story for teenagers? Of course not. Netflix does this to Christians because it believes we will stay quiet,” it adds.
The series has been in the streaming giant’s top ten list of English shows, with more than five million views.
Premier Christian News has contacted Netflix for comment.
