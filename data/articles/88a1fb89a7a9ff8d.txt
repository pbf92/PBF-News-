Alphabet Stocks Move Lower as Anthropic IPO Filing Shows $121.3 Billion Google Commitment
Alphabet GOOGL shares decreased 0.62% to $281.97 at 9:55 a.m. ET in regular trading on November 17, as Anthropic's IPO paperwork showed at least $121.3 billion of compute committed to Google from April 2026 to July 2033. That makes Google the largest disclosed supplier, ahead of Amazon's $110 billion floor and Microsoft's $47.2 billion to $49.2 billion. Anthropic prices its Nasdaq listing on November 18.
The Google commitment applies regardless of actual utilization, which gives Google Cloud unusually firm visibility. Google also released Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12, and on Zapier's AutomationBench it scored 51.1% at about $0.82 per task. Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 scored 51.4% at its max setting, at about $4.64 per task.
Spread evenly over roughly 7.25 years, $121.3 billion works out to about $16.7 billion a year of contracted demand. That is a solid floor for Google Cloud, but it concentrates exposure in one fast-spending customer whose models compete directly with Gemini. GuruFocus gives Alphabet a GF Score of 98/100, reflecting top-ranked profitability and a sturdy balance sheet. Its GF Value of $275.95 sits about 2% below the share price, a fairly valued reading, so investors are not getting the Anthropic contract at a discount. The next test is whether Google Cloud backlog in the next quarterly report reflects the commitment.
