TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino's 1st Man United training session filmed by drone operator
OUTLET: ESPN

Video footage of Mauricio Pochettino's first training session as Manchester United head coach has appeared on YouTube after it was filmed by a drone operator.
Around 12 minutes of the session was posted online on Thursday -- Pochettino's first full day in the job after arriving in Manchester on Wednesday. United have declined to comment.

The footage shows Pochettino -- who signed a two-and-a-half year deal to take over from Michael Carrick last week -- working with a group of players while another two groups work separately under the supervision of his assistant, Jesus Perez.
Harry Maguire, Kobbie Mainoo, Amad Diallo, Matheus Cunha and Bryan Mbeumo were among the players shown working with Pochettinho.
Perez, along with staff members Toni Jimenez and Sebastiano Pochettino, arrived at Carrington earlier in the week after being released from their commitments with the USMNT ahead of Pochettino's final game in charge against Haiti on Tuesday.
Pochettino travelled directly from Cincinnati to Manchester immediately after his final match with the USMNT and arrived at Carrington on Wednesday afternoon. His first game as United head coach is against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.
