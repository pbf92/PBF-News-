TITLE: The 9 best things to do in the first 10 hours playing GTA 6
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

There’s a lot to do in GTA VI and once you finally step foot into the game’s beefy open-world it’s easy to become overwhelmed by choices.
My first 10 hours with the game consisted of story missions, side-activities and generally just soaking in the vibes of Leonida.
But, after nearly 90 hours with the game by this point I’ve got a lot of wisdom to impart, so below are XX things to see and do once you’ve beaten GTA VI’s speedy prologue.
Fix Jason’s house
When you first get control of Jason you’ll notice his home is in disarray. Fortunately, the game lets you fix his place up in a way that’s reminiscent of the infamous house-building segment in Red Dead Redemption 2.
You can replace the stairs, mend the roof, paint the walls and more, and there’s an added incentive of Lucia being very pleased with you for going the extra mile.
Raid the military base (unsuccessfully)
Rockstar Games knew exactly what it was doing when it put GTA VI’s military base just down the road from the game’s opening area.
Advert
It’s an instant temptation as you can see jets flying overhead as you approach. Be warned though, stealing a jet or tank is nowhere near as simple as it was in GTA V, and you’re probably going to die – a lot.
Head to Vice City – and the strip club
While the game encourages you to stick around Key Lento for the first chapter, you’re not forced to.
The map is completely open by the time Jason is free to explore, which means you can immediately head to Vice City and visit the NINE1NINE strip club. Or check out the other tourist spots I guess…
Get hench, swole and/or shredded
GTA VI brings back body composition and Jason has a pretty sweet weight-lifting set-up on his porch.
Once free roam unlocks though you can visit any gym, pay for a membership and sculpt your physique to your liking. Or, you can skip the gym entirely, eat your feelings and watch your protagonists get doughy.
Go fishing
I’ve sunk (haha, get it?) a shameful amount of hours into the fishing so far, but it’s just so much fun.
Jason’s friend Cal challenges you to catch bigger fish than him, and will send you all across Leonida’s waters to do so. You’re free to fish wherever you like though.
Invest in your BuckMe account
You unlock this just after Jason gets reunited with Lucia, and it’s an integral part of the game. You’re given several saving milestones to hit, and I’d encourage you to start saving early if you can.
Save your cash for yourself of course, but when you’re done raiding Kingfisher outposts or selling cars, be sure to put a little aside to get into the habit.
Search for Mr Mangrove
In true Rockstar Games fashion GTA VI has some supernatural secrets stashed away in Leonida, and one can be found near Pelican Valley, which isn’t far from the game’s opening area.
You’ll know him when you see him, and once you do it kicks off one of the game’s best side-missions…
Build Jason and Lucia’s relationship
One of the most-rewarding parts of GTA VI is the blossoming relationship between Jason and Lucia, and the player has near-complete control over their affections.
Holding hands, sharing a smooch or even treating each other to ice cream all contribute to the way they feel about each other. It’s genuinely lovely.
Continue the main story!
As tempting as it can be to jump straight into free-roam, let me assure you that the story mode is 100% worth it.
It’ll also unlock new opportunities as you progress, so while you’re free to explore at your leisure you should try getting to at least Chapter 3 before committing hours, and hours and hours to it. You’ll have way more options by then.
