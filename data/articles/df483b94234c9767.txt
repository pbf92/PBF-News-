TITLE: This is the man tasked with waking football’s ‘sleeping giant’. It won’t be easy
OUTLET: Nine.com.au

In his first public interview after joining Manchester United, Mauricio Pochettino gave a glimpse of how he’s going to go about the enormous task of awakening the sleeping giant the Red Devils have become.
“It’s about to start to act and to change things, and to try and affect in a good way all the areas, to try to really believe that we can recover that feeling about to win,“he said.
The enormity of the task which lays ahead of Pochettino at Old Trafford is one the Argentinian is aware of, but insists he has the capabilities to turn the current situation around.
United currently sits in the Premier League relegation zone with one win from their first ten matches.
Pochettino though is confident he will be able to transform the situation immediately, starting with United’s away trip to Anfield to face Liverpool on Monday morning (AEDT).
“We are going to go there, to Anfield, with the idea to win and be brave and to beManchester United,” he said.
United’s much-publicised issues are both on and off the field. There’s disharmony and a growing tension between the club’s leadership, players and supporters.
A club right now United in name only, Pochettino must end the divisions if they are to pull themselves from their current predicament.
Critics will point to the apparent lack of success Pochettino had during his managerial career, but his stints both at Southampton and Tottenham Hotspur showcased a manager capable of drawing the best from the tools available to him to compete in the Premier League.
Pochettino’s first task will be to address the defensive solidity of the squad. This season, United has leaked goals for fun both domestically and in Europe. The lack of organisation under Michael Carrick was of particular concern with teams punishing United for their defensive naivety.
How Pochettino addresses this will be of particular interest. A mix of injuries and form has meant the club has struggled to settle on a consistent back four this season.
His midfield and forward mix will also be of huge significance as they look for a more ruthless edge up front.
Pochettino’s options will be limited by the squad which he has acquired, but his ability to inject confidence and belief within a group of players which has lost its way will be key in his formative weeks.
His relationship with senior members of the squad – including Bruno Fernandes and Lisandro Martinez, who have both been vocal in their criticism of the football club – will also play a significant role in whether United can move up the table.
Pochettino has said all the right things coming into Manchester United, but greater figures have come before and swiftly left what has now become a poisoned chalice at Old Trafford.
Whether Pochettino is the right man to finely steer United in the right direction, time will only tell.
But one thing is for sure, it makes for intriguing viewing.
