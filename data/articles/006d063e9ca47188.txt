TITLE: GTA 6 is the UK's biggest physical launch since 2019, despite having no actual game disc
OUTLET: TweakTown

According to NielsenIQ and data reported across multiple outlets, GTA 6 generated an estimated £32 million in UK physical sales during its first three days of launch. That figure makes it the biggest physical game launch in the country since FIFA 20 in September 2019, and the 17th largest physical game release since 1995.

For context, the entire boxed software category that week hit roughly £43 million, which was a 409% jump from the previous week and the strongest physical sales period since Christmas 2018. GTA 6 alone made up about 74 percent of that total, with 88 percent of its own sales coming on the first day.
The wider retail picture was just as shocking. Overall UK games retail revenue (software, hardware and accessories combined) reached £142 million, almost double the prior week. Console hardware rose 38 percent, with PS5 sales climbing 46 percent and Xbox Series X/S rising a sharp 75 percent.
The same surge appeared across Europe. France moved more than a million physical copies in the first three days, a figure not far from what GTA 5 managed there more than a decade ago. Spain also recorded its biggest physical retail launch on record.

It is worth noting the irony. Collectors and traditionalists complained heavily when Rockstar confirmed the physical edition would contain only a download code, not a physical disc. Independent retailers worried the format would lower interest. Yet the figures show that for a title of this level, the plastic case, the artwork, and the simple act of walking out of a store with something tangible still matter to most.
