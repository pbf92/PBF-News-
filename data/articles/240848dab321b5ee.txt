Is GTA 6 available on PC?
- No, GTA 6 is not available on PC.
GTA 6 is only available on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S natively. Xbox One players can play GTA 6 via Cloud Gaming.
Has Rockstar confirmed a PC release date?
- A PC version of GTA 6 has not been announced by Rockstar.

What has Rockstar said about a PC version?
- Rockstar hasn’t mentioned a PC version of the game.
Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick mentioned in an interview that the games come to console first because that’s where GTA’s “core audience” is.
Former Rockstar developers have shared their opinion on why the PC version of Rockstar games are usually released later.
In an interview with the Kiwi Talkz podcast, John Ricchio – who was a producer on Red Dead Redemption, Max Payne 3 and Grand Theft Auto 5 – was asked about whether making GTA 5 for Xbox 360 and PS3 hardware led to Rockstar being limited in what it could do with the game.
Ricchio replied that Rockstar’s policy is to concentrate on the console versions first, because then the hardware’s limitations are known to the developers, and then worry about making a PC version later – as opposed to making the PC version first then trying to figure out how to get it working on potentially less powerful hardware.
