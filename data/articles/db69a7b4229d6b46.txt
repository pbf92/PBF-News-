TITLE: Ambarella Reports $115.2 Million Revenue, $12.7 Million GAAP Net Loss
OUTLET: TradingView

Ambarella Reports $115.2 Million Revenue, $12.7 Million GAAP Net Loss
Ambarella reported third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $115.2 million, up 6.2% year over year, and a GAAP net loss of $12.7 million. Non-GAAP net income was $12.7 million, or $0.28 per diluted share. The company expects fourth-quarter revenue of $70 million to $75 million.
Financial Highlights
- Revenue was $115.2 million, compared with $108.5 million in the prior-year quarter; fourth-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue guidance is $70 million to $75 million.
- GAAP gross profit was $66.5 million, with a gross margin of 57.8%; non-GAAP gross margin was 59.5%.
- Loss from operations was $14.5 million, compared with a loss of $16.2 million in the same quarter last year.
- GAAP net loss was $12.7 million, or $0.29 per diluted share; non-GAAP net income was $12.7 million, or $0.28 per diluted share.
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable debt securities totaled $282.8 million at quarter-end.
Business Highlights
- Ambarella reported record edge AI revenue in the quarter.
- Consumer IoT revenue came in below the company’s expectations.
- Memory price increases and supply scarcity intensified, prompting many customers to reduce their outlooks.
- The company plans to invest in new products, expand its Partner program, enhance the Cooper Development Platform and pursue new applications; management expects fiscal 2028 to be a growth year.
