VisionWave Raises About $2 Million in Registered Offering With Resettable Warrants
VisionWave closed a registered direct offering that generated approximately $2 million in gross proceeds, with investors purchasing common shares or pre-funded warrants paired with common warrants. The securities purchase agreement allows purchasers to buy up to an additional $2 million, while the warrants include a price reset subject to a $0.0475 floor. Aegis Capital acted as exclusive placement agent, and a separate waiver letter with YA II directs a substantial portion of offering and ATM proceeds toward covered debt.

- Agreement type: Securities purchase agreement for a registered direct offering
- Counterparty: Participating investors

- Duration / Termination: Offering closed Nov 27 2026; warrant term of five years
- Reason: Raise capital through a registered securities offering
