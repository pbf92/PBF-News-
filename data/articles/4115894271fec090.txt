Costco Stock Gains Following October Sales Growth
Earlier this month, Costco reported sales of $24.50 billion for the four-week retail month ended Nov. 1, representing a 12.6% increase from $21.75 billion a year earlier.
Sales for the first nine weeks of fiscal 2026 reached $54.52 billion, up 12.8% from $48.33 billion in the comparable year-ago period.
Comparable sales, which measure performance at warehouses operating for at least a year, increased 11.1% companywide in October and 11.3% over the nine-week period.
U.S. warehouses led growth, with comparable sales rising 12.6% in October and 12.5% over the nine weeks. Canada recorded gains of 4.9% and 5.7%, respectively, while other international markets grew 9.4% and 10.2%.
Costco Announces $30 Special Dividend and $4 Billion Buyback
Earlier this month, Costco announced plans to return approximately $13.3 billion to shareholders through a special cash dividend of $30 per share.
The payout is double the $15 special dividend distributed in January 2024, which totaled approximately $6.7 billion.
The company’s board also reauthorized a stock repurchase program of up to $4 billion, with the new authorization running through November 2030.
