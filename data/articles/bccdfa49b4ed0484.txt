GoodRx Received Notice of Noncompliance
On November 17, 2026, GoodRx received notice from Nasdaq that it is not in compliance with Listing Rule 5250(c)(1) because it delayed filing its Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The company has until January 19, 2027, to submit a plan to regain compliance; if Nasdaq accepts the plan, it may allow until May 17, 2027. The notice has no immediate effect on the stock's Nasdaq listing.
Original SEC Filing: GoodRx Holdings, Inc. [ GDRX ] - 8-K - Nov. 23, 2026
