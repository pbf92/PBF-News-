Barclays, Citadel and BNY Put Anthropic’s Claude to Work
Anthropic raised $107.3 billion in the biggest initial public offering in history, PYMNTS reported Thursday (Nov. 19). The company disclosed in its IPO prospectus that it had more than 11,900 customers with $100,000 or more of revenue in the 12 months through Sept. 30. More than 1,900 of them generated $1 million or more, and about 250 generated $10 million or more.
