TITLE: Manchester United aim to avoid unwanted Champions League record against Sporting
OUTLET: Diario AS

After enduring a nightmare debut as Manchester United boss at the weekend, Mauricio Pochettino has vowed to deliver results befitting the club’s stature, declaring that the Red Devils will “turn around this situation”.
If the Argentine is to follow through on that pledge, he’ll need to sort out a dreadful defense that has now shipped 34 goals in all competitions this season. That’s more than any other club in Europe’s top five leagues.
Sunday’s 4-0 defeat to Liverpool not only left United mired in the Premier League relegation zone, but took the team’s goals-against tally in the English top flight to 23 this term. The Mancunians have also conceded three in the EFL Cup, and a further eight in the Champions League.
Record for ropey Red Devils defense?
Three days on from their Anfield annihilation, United now return to European duty with a visit Sporting CP today. And unless they can start to shore up their leaky backline in Portugal, the three-time continental champions could make an unwanted piece of history at the Estádio José Alvalade.
Having conceded at least twice in each of their last three Champions League outings - against Atlético Madrid, Como and Roma - United would break a club record by stretching that run this evening. According to the soccer stats specialists Opta, United have never previously conceded multiple goals in four straight matches in the competition.
Speaking ahead of today’s matchday-five league phase game in Lisbon, though, Pochettino rejected any suggestion that he has take over a team in a state of fragility.
“We’re a strong team”
“We’re not fragile - we’re a strong team,” he told reporters. “We showed that last season, [when we were] capable of finishing third [in the Premier League]. Now we’re playing in the Champions League because of that performance. I think things sometimes happen in football for different reasons […]. Sometimes you need to identify why - and we’re already working on that.”
The former U.S. national-team boss added: “I think we’re going to turn around this situation and start to perform at the level that […] this club deserves.”
Man United’s UCL results this season:
- Nov. 3: Manchester United 3-2 Roma
- Oct. 21: Como 4-1 Manchester United
- Oct. 13: Atlético Madrid 2-0 Manchester United
- Sep. 10: Manchester United 4-0 Sabah FK
In his prematch press conference yesterday, Pochettino also railed at “unfair situations” during Sunday’s thrashing by Liverpool - notably Leny Yoro’s early red card, which has since been rescinded on appeal.
The 54-year-old also pointed to an apparent Bradley Barcola infringement that could have led Dominik Szoboszlai’s free-kick opener for Liverpool to be disallowed.
“I need to defend my players and my club, and that is to denounce and to say we were right and the decision wasn’t good,” Pochettino said. “And now the three points, where are they?”
However, while Sunday’s loss on Merseyside left United languishing in 19th place in the league, they head to the Portuguese capital in a significantly less catastrophic position in the Champions League.
Having come back from two down to beat Roma 3-2 on matchday four, United have six points from a possible 12, placing them comfortably inside the top 24 ahead of this midweek’s games. Now, victory over Sporting would lift United to a points total that might be enough already for knockout-stage qualification. Last season, two teams advanced from the league phase with nine points.
Given that United’s win over Roma was the team’s only victory in the last 12, though, they are hardly overwhelmingly favorites to beat Sporting. Per Oddschecker, the bookmakers have the visitors at +160 for the win, only marginally in front of their hosts, who are priced at +170. What’s more, Opta favors Sporting, who go into today’s matchup on a five-game winning sequence.
Opta’s ‘supercomputer’ gives Paulo Fonseca’s men a 38.4% chance of securing all three points, just ahead of United on 36.4%.
