TeraWulf Stock Plunges: What's Happening?
TeraWulf Inc. WULF shares are hitting a new 52-week low Tuesday as the broader AI industry trades lower. Here’s what you should know.
- TeraWulf stock is among today’s weakest performers. Why is WULF stock falling?
TeraWulf Falls with AI Trade
TeraWulf is moving toward AI infrastructure, which ties its shares to demand for computing power. That corner of the market has been volatile. AI stocks leaped Monday after Nvidia put Jensen Huang back as CEO, following a stretch in which AI stocks had been hammered.
Tuesday looks different. AI-linked stocks are pulling back, and TeraWulf’s drop may reflect investors giving back part of Monday’s gains.
Nvidia sits at the center of the AI buildout, and doubts over who would lead it had weighed on AI sentiment before Monday. A sharp one-day rebound on that news may have left the group exposed to a pullback.
TeraWulf Chart Shows Heavy Selling
TeraWulf touched $9.25 on Tuesday, a new 52-week low. Before the session, the 52-week range ran from $9.51 to $29.84, and $9.51 was the Nov. 5 low.
The stock remains in a long downtrend and trades beneath its downward-sloping 200-day moving average. It peaked at $29.84 on June 22, bottomed at $9.51 on Nov. 5, bounced to $13.81 on Nov. 12 and has since slid to around $9.62.
Tuesday began with a gap lower at $10.58, beneath the prior $10.91 close, and the stock never got back to that close. Heavy volume is accompanying the decline, which points to strong selling.
Reclaiming $10.91 on a closing basis would erase Tuesday’s drop. A move back above $9.51, the former swing low and support area, would show the stock stabilizing after breaking through it.
