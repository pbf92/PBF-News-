TITLE: Bitcoin Won't Be the Best Way to Get Rich in the Next Bull Run, Expert Claims
OUTLET: TradingView

Bitcoin Won't Be the Best Way to Get Rich in the Next Bull Run, Expert Claims
Bitcoin BTCUSD could reach $250,000 and still trail smaller crypto assets in the next bull run, according to Ran Neuner, founder of Crypto Banter.
His pick for where the biggest gains could come from: trading platforms that use the fees they earn to buy back their own tokens.
"The most important investments in this cycle are the trading venues," Neuner said on the When Shift Happens podcast in an interview published Thursday.
His reasoning: Bitcoin has already proved people want to hold it. The newer opportunity is in apps people pay to use each time they trade.
Where Neuner’s 17x Forecast Comes From
Neuner based his forecast on a chart comparing the combined market value of crypto assets outside the top 10 with Bitcoin’s market value.
He said the ratio rose about 16-fold during the 2017 bull run and just over sixfold in 2021.
For Bitcoin, Neuner used $86,500, its price at the time of recording, as his starting point. A move to $250,000 would roughly triple it.
For smaller tokens, a return to the top of the chart’s historical range would lift the ratio by about 5.7 times, by his estimate. Paired with a threefold rise in Bitcoin, that implies roughly 17 times the starting dollar value for the group.
That last figure is a forecast for the group’s market value, not the return on a typical coin. New tokens can enter the group, and existing projects can issue more coins.
Why Trading Fees Matter More Than Hype
Neuner’s clearest example was Hyperliquid.
He pointed to two features working together: fees fund token buybacks, while developers who lock up HYPE to launch certain markets leave fewer tokens available to trade.
In his view, that gives holders a stake in the platform’s growth without relying solely on the next buyer’s enthusiasm.
Neuner’s own positioning was more cautious than his forecast.
He said he took profits after Bitcoin rose from $60,000 to $86,500 and held cash for another drop.
