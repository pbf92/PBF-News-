TITLE: How to Get Ready for November 19
OUTLET: Xbox Wire

November 19, 2026, has long been circled on your calendar. Birthdays, anniversaries, and holidays have come and gone, but this day belongs to you and your XBOX console. We can hardly believe it’s almost here, either. Once midnight strikes, you want to be locked in the action, so to help you prepare, we created a checklist to ensure distractions are put to bed so you can settle in for a season of gaming like nothing you’ve ever experienced before.
Make Some Hard (Drive) Choices
2026 has been full of some absolute bangers: Gears of War E-Day, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, Forza Horizon 6, Halo: Campaign Evolved, Minecraft Dungeons II, Diablo 4: Lord of Hatred, Control Resonant, Mixtape, Pragmata, The Blood of Dawnwalker, Star Wars: Galactic Racer, 007 First Light, Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve… seriously, all these games came out this year. That also means you might have a hard drive that is nearing capacity. And you will be faced with some hard choices to free up approximately 212GB of space on your XBOX Series X|S before November 19.
You could always upgrade the capacity of your console to keep all your games at the ready, using the storage expansion slot on the back of the console. Otherwise, you’ll have a hard choice to make which we do not envy. But know that you’re in good company – we had to find a way to make room too.
Connect to the Cloud
XBOX Cloud Gaming means that you don’t need to own an XBOX Series X|S console to experience the latest games (or use up that hard drive space) – you can stream directly from the cloud to your XBOX One console. Game Pass subscribers receive an allotment of hours per month as part of their plans, but you can also purchase blocks of time. Certain upcoming major releases have been confirmed as cloud-ready on day one, so even if you don’t own a current gen console, your latest obsession isn’t out of reach.
Ready Your Console
You never want to settle in for a long gaming session only to immediately get hit with an update download. If you haven’t used your XBOX Series X|S or XBOX One in a minute, be sure to power up and connect to the internet beforehand and make sure you’re all set with the latest system updates. You can also install any upcoming releases you may have pre-ordered, while you’re at it.
Call in Sick
If you’re feeling a gaming flu coming on (and there’s a lot of that going around right now), then take a sick day, rather than risk infecting your coworkers or classmates. Sometimes you just need to listen to your body’s needs, relax, and let the fever run its course. Miami’s own DJ Khaled, aka Dr. XBOX will write you a personalized note to help explain the situation. If this thing is really serious, you may even need the whole week to recover – it’s the responsible thing to do.
We all have responsibilities. Some of them come with four legs and a suspect bladder. Don’t let Rufus ruin your gaming fun. Get ahold of a local dog walker to take them outside so you can enjoy the big day uninterrupted. Or drop them off at doggie daycare for a day (or week) to get them out of your hair.
Reconsider Placement of Your XBOX
Is your XBOX in an area of the house that is easily accessible to younger players? Consider the steps you need to take to get it out of younger sightlines, like running an extension cable out to the shed in the backyard or huddling up in your at-home office. Even if you do find yourself bouncing between rooms, your save files reside in the Cloud, allowing you to pick up your session wherever you last plugged your console in.
Update Your Playlist
We’re always on the hunt for new tunes and one of the things we’re most looking forward to on November 19 is discovering a whole bunch of new tracks that are sure to make our listening playlist pop throughout the year. You can get started here on Spotify.
Create a New XBOX Wireless Controller to Commemorate the Day
XBOX Design Lab lets you create your own next-gen XBOX Wireless Controller. Choose from a combination of colors, patterns, textured grips, and more. You could even pick up some specially themed versions of controllers styled in hot pink and pastels – this one is particularly striking.
Charge Your Controller
Speaking of controllers, if you expect long gaming sessions in your near future, make sure that it’s plugged in so it has a full charge when you need it, or stock up on AA batteries. Maybe even get a backup controller ready so you can hot-swap them in and minimize interruptions.
Prepare for a Tale of Two Tragic Lovers in Crime
Looking for something to add to your queue? Check out the 1967 classic, “Bonnie and Clyde,” featuring Warren Beatty and Faye Dunaway to get you in the mood for an epic story about two tragic lovers who fall into a life of crime. We’ll have to wait and see if the outcome is any different this time around starting on November 19.
