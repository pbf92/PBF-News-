GTA 6's First-Day Sales Numbers Are Official, And They're Absolutely Historic
We all expected "Grand Theft Auto 6" to be the biggest thing ever, but now we have the numbers to back that up. It's truly gigantic, and this is just the tip of the iceberg.
Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive Software have officially announced that the first day of sales for "Grand Theft Auto 6" reached $3.79 billion. That sets a new record for entertainment across any medium, becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history. Coupled with the Metacritic score that makes "GTA 6" one of the highest-rated video games of all time, it's safe to say that this is not only meeting but exceeding what were sky-high expectations.
"GTA 6" sold 32.5 million units pre-release, a new record across all platforms, per Rockstar. That's a dizzying number, especially when we consider that the highest-selling "Call of Duty" game of all time sold just over 30 million units in total. And that's one of gaming's marquee franchises. This is on another level, selling more units pre-release than the biggest "COD" game did in its lifetime. Sam Houser, President and Founder of Rockstar Games, had this to say about it:
"It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI. We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn't be more excited to share this moment with you."
Grand Theft Auto VI is already a smash hit, but it's just getting started
Just how big are we talking here? To put this into a broader context, "Avengers: Endgame" is the highest-grossing movie of all time with $2.94 billion, per Box Office Mojo. That was likely as big as movies are ever going to get, and those total sales, at least in terms of tickets sold in movie theaters, didn't come close to what "Grand Theft Auto 6" did in a single day.
The lifetime sales for "GTA 5" were revealed in 2020, and at that time, they totaled more than 130 million copies. Of note? Around 40 million copies were sold between 2018 and 2020. The game was released in 2013, so it had a very long lifespan and generated billions for Rockstar for years. That means "GTA 6" is just getting started. Lord knows what these numbers are going to look like in seven years, or even a month from now.
The first-day sales were always going to be huge, as this is the single most-anticipated video game in history. What's going to help in the longer term is the rapturous reception the game has received. SVG gave "Grand Theft Auto 6" a perfect 10/10 review, calling it "an unbelievable achievement in every sense." We're far from alone in that praise, which should help motivate some people who were on the fence to get in on the action.
Quite a few hit video games sold millions of copies despite bad reviews. Praise isn't a prerequisite for success. In this case, though, it's going to help push the numbers through the roof in the weeks and months to come. The only question now is just how high "GTA 6" can fly.
"Grand Theft Auto VI" is available now for PlayStation and Xbox consoles.
