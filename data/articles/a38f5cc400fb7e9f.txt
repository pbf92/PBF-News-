Understanding Non-Repainting Signals in TradingView Indicators
Traders comparing TradingView indicators keep running into four problems:
- Repainting signals: TradingView's Pine Script documentation estimates more than 95% of indicators repaint in some form, making past signals look better than live results.
- Indicator overload: Stacking tools often creates conflicting signals and slower decisions.
- Free plan limits: TradingView's free Basic plan allows only two indicators per chart.
- Unproven scripts: Many buy and sell indicators show arrows but no hit rate.
KIRIN was built to solve all four.
How KIRIN Generates Buy and Sell Signals on TradingView
As a confluence indicator, KIRIN gives each layer a veto:
- Trend Cloud: An ATR-based band sets direction: blue below price allows only buys, red above allows only sells.
- Trend Candle: Colors each candle against a price channel, so pullbacks stand out.
- Twin Range Filter (TRF): Blends fast and slow range filters and turns only when price breaks its normal range.
- Bar-close lock: Rules are checked on closed candles, so confirmed signals never move.
An A+ signal needs a pullback candle to arm the setup, then a genuine bullish or bearish close with TRF agreeing. A B+ signal needs price to dip into the cloud zone, reject past its outer rail, then confirm. Near-misses are discarded, not chased, and a cloud flip resets every setup.
Multiple Indicators in One: Trade Levels, Dashboards and Alerts
One KIRIN script replaces several tools:
- Entry and exit levels: A stop behind the 20-bar swing with a 5-pip buffer (or 2x ATR), and targets at 0.5R, 1R, and 2R.
- Backtest dashboard: TP hit rate for the last 100 signals versus all signals, plus A+/B+ counts and long/short split.
- Multi-timeframe dashboard: Trend, strength, and ranging status from 1-minute to weekly charts.
- TradingView alerts: Nine bar-close alert conditions, delivered to the TradingView app or to Discord and Telegram webhooks.
- Any market: Forex, gold (XAUUSD), indices, crypto, and stocks.
- Free plan friendly: Signals and TP/SL tags work on TradingView's free tier; alerts need a paid plan.
