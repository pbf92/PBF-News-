TITLE: 9 GTA 6 easter eggs we've spotted that you might have missed
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Grand Theft Auto VI players across the world are no doubt causing all sorts of havoc in Vice City and across the wider state of Leonida. However, even in the short time since its release, players have already uncovered some of the secrets Rockstar Games has hidden throughout its world.
After all, we all know that Rockstar loves a good Easter egg, and its latest entry in the Grand Theft Auto series is no exception. Whether it's a cheeky reference to a previous game, such as the ongoing Mount Chiliad mystery, a nod to a popular TV show or just a bizarre interaction that leaves you scratching your head, GTA VI has you covered.
Thankfully, the GTA VI community and we at GAMINGbible have encountered some fun secrets and bizarre interactions, and we've rounded up nine of our early favourites, including a dog riding a jet ski. With that in mind, here are some of the GTA VI Easter eggs and encounters you might have missed.
GTA 6 takes Easter eggs rather literally
Climbing one of Leonida's enormous communications towers takes around seven minutes, but those who make it to the top will discover a bird's nest containing several colourful Easter eggs, quite literally. Better still, throwing one of the eggs from the tower triggers a hilarious secret cutscene involving an unsuspecting driver, which also appears to be a cheeky nod to a similar Easter egg hidden in 2002's GTA: Vice City.
Dexter Morgan's apartment is hiding in Vice City
Fans of the hit TV series Dexter might want to check out this Easter egg. As shared by Reddit user y_oake23, players can visit a building in Vice City that looks remarkably similar to Dexter Morgan's apartment from the popular crime drama. Considering Dexter is set in Miami, it's a fitting little nod from Rockstar Games. Unfortunately, you can't actually enter the apartment, but it's still a nice discovery for fans of the show.
A talking parrot leads players to hidden cash
This is one encounter that I've experienced myself, and it seems I'm not alone. As shared by Reddit user imachoculatedonnut, visiting a liquor store in the Keys leads to a bizarre encounter involving several angry men and a bunch of talking parrots. Not only do the parrots cheer you on during the fight, but one even reveals the location of some hidden cash near a generator. Only Rockstar could make something so ridiculous seem perfectly normal.
GTA 5's Mount Chiliad mystery makes a comeback
Another Easter egg that I've experienced myself was also shared by Reddit user Tr1angleChoke. During an early mission involving Donny and Brian, two NPCs sitting outside a bar can be overheard discussing the infamous Mount Chiliad mystery from GTA V. It's a nice little nod to the 2013 game and its mysterious mural featuring a UFO, jetpack and egg, which had fans scratching their heads for years.
A dog riding a jet ski might be GTA 6's best encounter
This discovery might just be my favourite, because who doesn't love a dog riding a jet ski? As shared by Reddit user penoloxai, players who find themselves stranded at sea can be rescued by a rather talented pooch wearing goggles and a life jacket. Better still, as we previously reported, this adorable encounter appears to have been inspired by a real-life golden retriever spotted riding a jet ski in Florida in 2025.
A creepy roadside encounter takes a supernatural turn
Rockstar Games has never been shy when it comes to including creepy encounters in its games, and GTA VI is no exception. As shared by Reddit user peoples888, during Chapter 2, they discovered an abandoned car with its doors open and the alarm sounding. After investigating the nearby woods, things took a rather sinister turn, with a mysterious body and what appeared to be an alien abduction. I'll certainly be keeping an eye out for this one.

A mysterious statue could be GTA 6's next big puzzle
Could this discovery be GTA VI's answer to the infamous Mount Chiliad mystery? As shared by Reddit user akspree, a mysterious statue near Manifest Canyon features four horses with unusual symbols. Approaching it apparently causes your controller to vibrate and triggers strange beeping noises. One horse even has a traffic cone on its head, possibly referencing Glasgow's famous Duke of Wellington statue. Whether there's a bigger mystery to solve remains to be seen.
An apparently invincible NPC gives players a fright
This encounter is a rather creepy one, and I'm not entirely sure what to make of it. As shared by Reddit user seepa-man, while exploring GTA VI at night, they aimed a sniper rifle at an unsuspecting NPC, only for the character to apparently become invincible and suddenly teleport behind them. Whether this is a deliberately hidden Easter egg or simply a bizarre glitch is anyone's guess, but it's certainly enough to frighten you.
Bigfoot might be hiding in GTA 6's wilderness
Finally, what would a Grand Theft Auto game be without a good old-fashioned Bigfoot mystery? Rockstar Games has been teasing fans with the legendary creature since GTA: San Andreas, and it seems GTA VI might be continuing that tradition. As shared by Reddit users NorthAmericanVex and Lil-Ingy-Fock, Bigfoot has reportedly been spotted roaming the woods near Cottonmouth Lake, with one player claiming to have chased it towards a strange, foul-smelling effigy. It is possible to come face to face with the infamous beast, but we'll let you uncover that story for yourselves.
Of course, these discoveries are likely just the tip of the iceberg when it comes to the secrets GTA VI has hidden away. Knowing Rockstar Games, I wouldn't be surprised if we're still uncovering mysteries for years to come. Who knows, perhaps we'll still be talking about them 13 years from now.
Grand Theft Auto VI is out now on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S
