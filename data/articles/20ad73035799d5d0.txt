Midnight: Private Collateral Vault launches - Q1 2027
Midnight plans to release its Private Collateral Vault in Q1 2027. Traders will be able to deposit assets once and use that deposit as margin on compatible blockchains.
Traders will not need to bridge the deposited assets to use them as margin. They can also use that margin without making their data public. That gives traders who use compatible blockchains a reason to bring collateral to the network behind NIGHT.
