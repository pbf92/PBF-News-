TITLE: GTA 6 crashes on PS5 with error CE-108255-1, leaving players stuck between Sony and Rockstar support
OUTLET: Notebookcheck

Some PlayStation 5 owners who waited over a decade for Grand Theft Auto VI now cannot get past its opening minutes. Since the game launched on November 19, players on the base PS5 and PS5 Slim, along with some PS5 Pro owners, have reported repeated crashes with error code CE-108255-1, and neither Sony nor Rockstar has acknowledged the problem.
Crashes strike at launch, in cutscenes, and during early missions
Players on r/PS5HelpSupport and other forums say GTA VI crashes at launch, on loading screens, during the opening cutscene, and at specific points in early story missions. Each time, the game closes to the PS5 home screen and displays the same error. "Legit cannot get past opening cut scene tried so many different things," wrote one poster, who added that skipping the cutscene only led to the game freezing moments later.
Most reports come from owners of the base PS5 and PS5 Slim, but the problem does not appear to stop there. Gizmodo notes that launch-era PS5 models seem especially common among those affected, while also pointing to reports from Slim and PS5 Pro owners.
Sony's official guidance stops at "Try updating your game"
Error code CE-108255-1 isn't specific to GTA VI. Sony's support page describes it only as "An error occurred with the game/application," which confirms that the game crashed without explaining why. The page's sole recommendation is "Try updating your game," and if that fails, it sends players to PlayStation Repairs.
Rockstar has given players even less to work with. Its Grand Theft Auto VI error troubleshooting article, last updated on November 18, simply links to Sony's and Microsoft's error-code pages. The studio has not released a patch addressing CE-108255-1, nor has it commented on the reports.
Players caught in a support loop as hardware and software theories clash
Players, meanwhile, say they are being bounced between the two companies. One shared Rockstar's reply to their error report: "For technical support for your PlayStation 5 console, please contact PlayStation support." Others say their support tickets were closed and marked as "logged." Standard fixes have not reliably helped either, with another player writing, "Cleared cache, rebuilt database, reinstalled, updated, restored license and it does the same thing."
Opinion on the cause remains divided. One player said a Sony support agent quoted $230 before tax for a repair, while another agent said it was definitely a software issue. A commenter who switched to a replacement PS5 Slim reported no crashes during the first five hours of play, which some see as a sign of faulty hardware. Gizmodo has floated the PS5's liquid metal thermal interface, which can dry out over time, as one possible culprit, although the outlet also notes that other games reportedly run fine on affected consoles.
Until Sony or Rockstar names a cause, affected PS5 owners are left waiting for a statement and a patch, or paying for a repair that may not solve the problem.
