TITLE: Stock market jumps after reinstatement of Jensen Huang as Nvidia CEO
OUTLET: CBS News

Stocks soared on Monday after Nvidia announced it as president and CEO, erasing losses from a major on Friday that plunged the S&P 500 and Dow into correction territory.
The Nasdaq Composite jumped 6.2% in midday trading, regaining ground after the technology-heavy index fell 1,261 points, or 5.3%, on Nov. 20, its second-worst day of 2026. The S&P 500 climbed 4.5%, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 2.8%.
Huang resigned less than two weeks ago amid scandal over an affair with a woman U.S. intelligence agencies suspected of spying for China. On Monday, the board said it believes Huang is the "right person to lead Nvidia" and that it has "every confidence" in him as he resumes his leadership role at the company. Huang's reinstatement is "effective immediately," the board said.
"The company reported fantastic numbers last week, but the uncertainty of their leadership was like a huge overhang," eToro investment analyst Bret Kenwell told CBS News.
"Now that uncertainty suddenly is not only removed, but it's replaced with the very familiar leadership of Jensen," he added.
Nvidia shares surged more than 30% on Monday after the opening bell, quickly adding $1 trillion to its market value. Shares of CoreWeave, a cloud computing company and Nvidia customer, shot up 25%. The PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index, which tracks 30 of the largest companies involved in the design, distribution, manufacture and sale of chips, rose 14%.
"I believe the White House leaned hard on the board, and the message is crystal clear. America needs Jensen Huang leading Nvidia and, therefore, the wider AI industry," Nigel Green, CEO of investment advisory firm deVere Group, said in an email. "His return hands him more control than ever. He's got the backing of the board, the U.S. president and the market, and that's a phenomenal platform to take Nvidia to another level."
Not out of the woods
Despite Monday's market exuberance, some Wall Street analysts said investors remain on edge amid concerns that the artificial intelligence sector is unraveling.
"Stocks in the U.S. are staging a large rebound rally so far this morning, although, just to put things into context, the S&P futures aren't even reclaiming their loss from Friday, much less the entire week," Vital Knowledge analyst Adam Crisafulli said in a note to investors on Monday before Nvidia announced it was reinstating Huang.
"Sentiment remains pretty bleak — most people are looking at this [Monday] uptick as nothing more than technical in nature, a partial reversal of extremely oversold conditions," he added.
