TITLE: Voting intention, 22-23 November 2026: Lab 25%, Con 22%, Ref 22%, LD 12%, Grn 10%
OUTLET: YouGov

Latest YouGov Westminster voting intention figures
YouGov’s latest voting intention poll for The Times and Sky News shows a three point lead for Labour, on 25% of the vote, within the margin of error of the 27% recorded in last week's poll. This is ahead of the Conservatives, who are on a 22% share, within the margin of error of the 23% recorded in last week’s poll, and Reform UK, who are also on 22% of the vote, relative to 21% previously.
The Lib Dems and Greens are likewise within the margin of error of our previous figures, on 12% and 10% of the vote respectively.
- Labour: 25% (-2 from 15-16 Nov)
- Conservative: 22% (-1)
- Reform UK: 22% (+1)
- Lib Dem: 12% (+1)
- Green: 10% (+1)
- Restore Britain: 4% (+1)
- SNP: 2% (-1)
- Plaid Cymru: 1% (=)
- Others: 2% (=)
Overall, our latest figures reaffirm the substantial shift in voting intention polls seen in recent months. Comparing the average of our tracker over the last month with the last month prior to the Makerfield by-election in June, shows Labour up seven points and the Conservatives up three, while the Greens are down four points, Reform UK are down three, and the Lib Dems are down one.
