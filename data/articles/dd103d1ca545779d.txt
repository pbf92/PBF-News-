TITLE: Regeneron Pharmaceuticals Director Sold Shares Worth Over $1.9M
OUTLET: TradingView

Regeneron Pharmaceuticals Director Sold Shares Worth Over $1.9M
George L. Sing, a director, sold 1,000 shares of Regeneron Pharmaceuticals common stock on November 16, 2026, at a weighted average price of $960.26 per share, and 1,000 shares on November 17, 2026, in three transactions at a weighted average price of $957.69 per share. The total sale amount was $1,917,945. Following the transactions, Sing directly owned 23,147 shares and indirectly owned 750 shares by his spouse, 400 shares by his spouse and custodial son, and 1,000 shares through a trust for his son.
