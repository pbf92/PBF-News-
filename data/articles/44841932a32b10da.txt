TITLE: Remziye Zaim receives JAMA Editorial Fellowship
OUTLET: University of Toronto

Remziye Zaim, an interdisciplinary researcher and research fellow at the University of Toronto's Collaborative Centre for Climate, Health and Sustainable Care, has been awarded a 2026-2027 JAMA Editorial Fellowship.
The fellowship offers physicians and health researchers the opportunity to work with the editorial team at JAMA, a prestigious medical journal published by the American Medical Association. Over 10 months, editorial fellows receive mentorship from a JAMA editor, review research manuscripts and join weekly manuscript and content discussions examining research and its relevance to clinical care.
Zaim will contribute to the JAMA+ AI team, which brings together research, commentary and multimedia content on AI and digital medicine.
A member of the Temerty Centre for AI Research and Education in Medicine (T-CAIREM), Zaim's research examines how health systems can implement and oversee AI responsibly. Her work connects health technology assessment with regulatory science, public policy and bioethics, including questions about who benefits from AI and how institutions remain accountable for its use.
At U of T, Zaim, who is supported by CIFAR’s AI for Health Solution Networks program, has worked on developing governance frameworks for AI tools intended for disease prediction and prevention in public health.
“I’m delighted to represent the University of Toronto in these JAMA programs," said Zaim. "My research examines how health systems can implement and oversee AI responsibly, and careful evaluation of the evidence is central to that work.”
