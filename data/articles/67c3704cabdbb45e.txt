TITLE: How to increase your Bond in GTA 6 (Jason & Lucia Romance)
OUTLET: Rock Paper Shotgun

After Jason and Lucia are reunited in Chapter 1 of GTA 6, you'll be introduced to the Bond system. In essence, it's a secret meter that dictates how loved up Jason and Lucia are, and while that may seem like it only has minor consequences, in actuality, the outcome of their entire story will rest on how high this bar is and the decisions you make that affect it.
Like all great relationships, you have to work to make sure these two are keeping their bond as strong as possible. We've decided to play cupid and make a quick breakdown of how the Bond system works, how to increase it, and what actions will affect it.
On this page:
What is Bond in GTA 6?
Bond is a stat in GTA 6 that will increase and decrease based on how Jason and Lucia interact with each other. Thoughtful, caring and loving actions performed by either character will increase the couple's Bond, while being avoidant, uncommunicative and cold will decrease it. These actions can be anything from reminiscing over old photos to dancing in a club, but you'll know when your Bond has grown thanks to a small heart icon with a plus beside it appearing on screen.
To give you an example of actions that will boost Bond, around midway through Chapter 1, Jason learns that Lucia is being released from prison. Going out of your way to celebrate her return by buying her flowers, getting the house stocked with groceries, or purchasing some jewellery will boost the couple's Bond.
Certain choices in story missions will also impact Bond, such as opting to volunteer Jason's stashed money to start a bank account in Chapter 1. These will usually occur during cutscenes, with the player being prompted to press a button to make a decision. If the other character appreciates the decision you made, your Bond will increase.
Why should you boost Bond in GTA 6?
Bond may seem like a bit of a nothing stat early into GTA 6, but it's arguably one of the most important stats in the game, as it affects key story moments. The biggest of these is the ending of the game, with the finale of Lucia and Jason's story being impacted by several factors, with one of the biggest being how deep the Bond between the pair is.
It's another example of GTA 6 largely giving you a lot of control of how you want Jason and Lucia's character arcs to unfold. So if you want them to be deeply in love, go out of your way to make each character take care of the other, or do things to deepen their relationship. If you want everything to be strictly business, ignore them and firmly drive a wedge between them.
How to boost Bond quick in GTA 6
There's no way to reliably farm Bond, per se, considering GTA 6 doesn't have any easily repeatable actions that consistently boost the stat. However, there are definitely recurring actions that you can perform to ensure you get quick, consistent Bond boosts.
In Chapter 1, Lucia frequently asks Jason to give her a ride to community service or pick her up after she's done. Making sure Jason always offers a ride when requested will provide an easy Bond boost that you can capitalise on. Another definitely worth watching out for is when either character is leaning against a bannister. You can aim at them and press the social interaction prompt to give them a quick cuddle, which boosts your Bond too.
In general, make sure to fulfil your partner's requests and be there when they need you, and your Bond should grow quickly.
