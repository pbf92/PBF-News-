TITLE: GTA 6’s Worst Feature Needs to Be Patched ASAP
OUTLET: Polygon.com

After years of waiting, Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here. And somehow, miraculously, it's living up to the impossible level of hype surrounding it. Rockstar's game is a staggering technological and artistic achievement in countless ways. The world of Leonida is vast and intricate, and the emergent gameplay enabled by hundreds of unique NPCs is truly astonishing. But there's one thing about GTA 6 I find absolutely infuriating so far, and it's extremely minor: depositing and withdrawing money from Jason's stash box and ATMs is a total pain in the ass.
In the early hours of the game, Jason doesn't have a bank account. (He later tells Lucia he doesn't want to be "in the system.") So while he's making a living doing odd jobs and waiting for Lucia to be released from prison, he's keeping money under the floorboards in his bedroom. In order to select the amount you want to deposit or withdraw from the stash box, you press left or right on the d-pad. It takes forever for the numbers to tick up or down — until suddenly it doesn't, and you've whizzed far past the figure you wanted to input. The sensitivity of the counter is totally out of whack, going from a trickle to a fire hose instantaneously. I figured I was missing something — surely it should not be this difficult to wrangle the stash box?
At first, I generously assumed this was some kind of artistic flourish. Jason isn't counting the cash he places in the stash by hand every time; he's just eyeballing it. Then, about halfway through Chapter 1, you get access to the BuckMe app, essentially a bank account for people with terrible credit like Jason and Lucia. In addition to keeping cash at home, you can now deposit your ill-gotten gains at ATMs throughout Leonida. (Having a BuckMe account also allows you to make more expensive purchases at the gun store.) I assumed my days of wrangling that horrible slider were over. Surely the ATM would feature a numerical keypad, and I could just enter the amount I wanted to deposit or withdraw. After all, a similar interface appears elsewhere in the game, for door codes and lockboxes. Nope! For no discernible reason, ATMs also use the same clunky, left-to-right scrolling interface as the stash box.
Perhaps the intent here is to encourage you to put all your money in your BuckMe and Family Savings account as soon as the option becomes available. But I enjoy having a few hundred dollars of "fuck around money" for buying snacks or car repairs. Yes, you can use the app for most stuff, but I really hate the idea of having to schlep back to the house or find an ATM to get cash. I can't imagine this would be difficult for Rockstar to fix, and I would even tolerate it for the stash box if I must. But there is literally no excuse for an ATM to work this way in a video game in 2026.
I'm not the only one who has beef with this mechanic. Plenty of folks on Reddit have noticed it too. "Ok I’m not the only one I was hoping there was a way to input the exact numbers and I haven’t found it yet but guess not," wrote one user. "Little bit more, little bit more, little bit more, ok and stop.... Fuck, way too much," wrote another.
The horrible banking interface does not appear to have slowed GTA 6's ascent to cultural dominance. The game sold more than 32 million copies during the run-up to release and has already made $3.79 billion in revenue, according to Rockstar Games. GTA 6 is available now on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.
