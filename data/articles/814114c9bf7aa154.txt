Walrus: Token unlock releases up to 53.54 million WAL - Mar 2027
Walrus plans to unlock up to 53.54 million WAL in March 2027, according to the circulation plan the project filed with Upbit. That amount is equal to 1.89% of the circulating supply.
The unlock makes previously locked WAL available for holders to trade. If holders offer these tokens for sale, the extra WAL available to buyers could add sell pressure.
