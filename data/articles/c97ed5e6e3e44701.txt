TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino shares message to fans
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

New Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino shared a message with fans on his first day at the club.
The message reads:
Hi Reds, it's Mauricio Pochettino,
I am looking forward to meeting you guys. I hear so many good things about you. I hope we bring all the happiness working together and connecting together. This is going to be a special season to support and to make our players fight on the field and to get the results that we deserve.
This message is about being united, like the name Manchester United. We all need to work together. I hope to bring the happy moments that this club deserves and create the unity that every team needs so that the players can be at their best on the pitch.
The Reds began training with the new manager on Thursday, but his staff took over at Carrington on Monday while Poch oversaw his final game in charge of the United States men's national team.
