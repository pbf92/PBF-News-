ChainOpera AI: Unlocks at least 11.25 million COAI - Dec 2026
ChainOpera AI is scheduled to unlock at least 11.25 million COAI in December 2026. The tokens are allocated to advisors, investors and the team, and represent 5.98% of the circulating supply.
The unlock makes that allocation available to its recipients. Selling by those holders could bring additional COAI onto the market and add selling pressure as the released tokens become available for trading.
