Micropolis receives extension from NYSE American to file delayed annual report
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- Micropolis AI Robotics announced on Monday that on November 11, it received a letter from NYSE Regulation accepting the company's request for an extension to regain compliance with the continued listing standards of NYSE American.
- NYSE Regulation granted the company a plan period through December 13, 2026, to file its annual report on Form 20-F for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, and any subsequently delayed filings with the SEC.
- During the plan period, NYSE Regulation staff will periodically review the company's adherence to the milestones in its plan. Exchange staff will initiate delisting proceedings as appropriate if the company does not make progress consistent with the plan during the plan period.
- The same applies if the company does not file the 2025 Form 20-F and any subsequent delayed filings by May 15, 2027, the end of the maximum 12-month cure period. The company may appeal a staff delisting determination in accordance with Section 1010 and Part 12 of the Company Guide.
- However, the letter has no immediate impact on the listing of the company's ordinary shares. The shares will continue to be listed and traded on NYSE American during the plan period, subject to the company's adherence to its plan and its continued compliance with the other listing requirements of NYSE American.
- MCRP shares up 6% premarket.
