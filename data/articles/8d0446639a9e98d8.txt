TITLE: PlayStation and Xbox sales more than double as GTA 6 gives consoles a much-needed boost
OUTLET: TweakTown

Just last month, we reported that both Sony and Xbox suffered terrible hardware sales, with Xbox particularly hitting an all-time low. Of course, GTA 6 was always meant to move more consoles for both gaming companies, and it has done exactly that.

According to Mat Piscatella, industry advisor at Circana, in the week ending on November 14, Xbox saw a 2.3x increase in unit sales, while PS5 hardware saw a 2.4x increase compared to the same week last year.

While GTA 6 is driving console sales, it isn't the only system seller this month, as Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake might be the bigger deal for Nintendo fans. As Mat Piscatella reports, "Switch 2 hardware unit sales increased by more than 40% compared to the same week a year ago."
This goes to show that all people care about is high-quality games. Many argue that the concept of a "system seller" no longer exists, but the way Nintendo and Rockstar have shaken up console sales says otherwise. Sony, Microsoft, and Nintendo have all had their missteps this generation, whether it's inflated prices, the culling of physical games, or a focus on live-service titles.
Complaints regarding all those decisions are valid, but at the end of the day, the mainstream gamer wants to experience an incredible game. To that end, 2026 has had no shortage of such titles, with games like Control Resonant and Phantom Blade 0 also hitting the Steam bestseller charts.
Gaming is certainly more expensive now than it ever was before. The "RAM apocalypse" certainly isn't doing us any favors either. Even so, people are still willing to buy consoles and upgrade their PCs if budget allows, certainly when the right games are around the corner.
