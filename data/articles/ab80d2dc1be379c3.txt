TITLE: Liverpool 4-0 Manchester United: New Manager Faceplant
OUTLET: The Busby Babe

The Mauricio Pochettino era begins already on fire at Anfield as Liverpool take advantage of a 10-man United to the tune of four goals to nil.
Following an international break filled with turmoil, a match at Anfield was already a tall enough order. The opening minutes of the match proved that United and its new boss would not find joy.
Alexander Isak received a long ball up the right wing in the eighth minute as Matthjis de Ligt opted to hope for an offside flag instead of playing defense. Isak raced up the pitch but took a heavy touch at the top of the box as Yoro closed him down. Yoro ran into the back of Isak and it was ruled a denial of a goal-scoring opportunity and was confirmed by VAR.
Dominik Szoboszlai took the subsequent free kick from the top of the box, hitting a curling shot low to the left that beat Senne Lammens to the back of the net.
Like so many other matches this season, United conceded a second, just minutes after the first. Rio Ngumoha, who put together an AND 1 Mixtape against Diogo Dalot, carried the ball up the left side toward the backline, barely shadowed by Dalot. At the last second, Ngumoha chipped a cross to an unmarked Florian Wirtz who knocked the ball in with a spinning flourish.
Pochettino replaced Bryan Mbeumo with Harry Maguire in the 22nd minute to move to a five-man backline which slightly stabilized the flow of the match, relative to the fact that the hosts had a man advantage.
Ngumoha, who became the youngest-ever player to register a goal contribution against Manchester United, raised the ante as the youngest-ever to score against Manchester United in the 66th minute. Bradley Barcola had plenty of space to carry the ball up the right side, pulling away from a trailing Fernandes, as De Ligt struggle to close down the space. Barcola then squared a ball to Ngumoha who was unguarded and able to easily one-time the ball by Lammens.
A reckless pass in United’s defensive end from De Ligt to Joshua Zirkzee saw the Dutchman take a heavy touch that was picked up by Liverpool. Wirtz picked out Victor Muñoz who timed his run perfectly and was one-on-one with Lammens. Muñoz then had no problem shaking the back of the net with his shot.
Mercifully, only two minutes of stoppage followed, and United were then left to reflect on their place at 19th in the table and sporting the worst defense in Europe. The broadcast often displayed Omar Berrada and Jason Wilcox with sullen faces. Hopefully, the people above them could see a correlation between them and what happened on the pitch.
The matches continue to come thick and fast for Pochettino as United travel to Lisbon to face Sporting in the Champions League on Wednesday, Nov. 25. Difficult to imagine things get better for United with Sporting winning three of four in the competition.
