Police comb Oldham's Alexandra Park for clues after double stabbing as update issued
Two people were stabbed in Alexandra Park, Oldham, on Saturday night
Teams of police officers were combing a park in Oldham today after two teenagers were stabbed and taken to hospital in a Saturday night attack.
Images from the scene on Sunday in Alexandra Park show officers searching through undergrowth and wooded areas as a cordon remained in place.
Tactical Aid Unit officers were pictured in and around the park in the Glodwick area of the town as investigations continue.

The cordon, meanwhile, forced the cancellation on Sunday of the weekly junior Parkrun event for youngsters, with the course inaccessible as a result.
Emergency services descended on Alexandra Park at around 5pm on Saturday.
The two injured men are both 18 and were found close to the entrance of the park. They were taken to hospital with injuries consistent with a stabbing. Their injuries, however, aren't believed to be life-changing or life-threatening, police said.
In an update on Sunday, Greater Manchester Police said no arrests have been made and no update on their conditions in hospital was available.

A spokesperson said a scene would remain in place at the park.
GMP are appealing for any witnesses who were in and around the 'Lions Den' area of the park to come forward with information.
Detective Inspector Kelly Paterson of Oldham CID said: “Thankfully the two men that have been injured are not believed to have life-threatening injuries at this time however knife crime has absolutely no place in our communities and on our streets.
"We are currently appealing for any witnesses who may have been in and around the 'Lions Den' area of the park and may hold vital information for our investigation.
"Communities may be concerned by this incident however I’d like to reassure you that our officers will maintain a robust presence in and around the park as we carry out our enquiries.
"There will be a scene in place until at least tomorrow as our enquiries are ongoing, and I’d urge people to stay away from the scene to let our officers carry out their work.
"If you have any information regarding this incident, please come forward. You can report this via 101 or our LiveChat quoting log 2243 of 21.11.26. Alternatively, you can give information anonymously via CrimeStoppers on 0800 555 111."
