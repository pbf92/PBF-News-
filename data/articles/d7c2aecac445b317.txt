TITLE: RancheView School and No Stone Left Alone honours Cochrane veterans
OUTLET: Cochrane Eagle

Grade five and six students from RancheView School's Remembrance Day Club partnered with the No Stone Left Alone program to hold a special ceremony honouring Cochrane veterans at the Field of Honour in the Cochrane Community Cemetery on Nov. 16.
Students said the ceremony was a special opportunity to appreciate and honour veterans who fought to keep our country safe, according to the Rocky View Schools (RVS) release.
