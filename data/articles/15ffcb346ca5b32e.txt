TITLE: GTA 6 locks in-game content behind subscription as players brand it 'unacceptable'
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Grand Theft Auto VI is officially out, as Rockstar Games’ highly anticipated follow up to GTA V is in the midst of rolling out worldwide.
It’s officially the joint highest-rated game of all-time, landing a score of 99 on aggregate website Metacritic, with GAMINGbible awarding the game a perfect score of 10 in our GTA VI review.
With those in New Zealand - or pretending to be in New Zealand - already hands-on with the game, new details about GTA VI are emerging, including an unfortunate new controversy.
GTA VI, as it turns out, includes in-game content that’s featured behind the paywall that is GTA+, Rockstar Games’ subscription service that’s predominantly used for GTA Online.
Advert
A screenshot of the GTA+ benefits was taken by Tex2 and shared to Reddit by user volstic where fans branded the move “unacceptable”.
GTA 6 is locking vehicles, a garage, and discounts behind GTA+
“GTA+ Members get optional in-game benefits in Grand Theft Auto VI, including the high-peformance ‘90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy widebody sports car and a garage in the Crosstown district,” began a message in the screenshot.
“Both are available to claim in Chapter 3, while subscribed, along with access to a suite of bonuses for Jason and Lucia across Leonida.”
As for what those other bonuses are, GTA+ subscribers should expect a 10 percent discount at “select businesses across Leonida”, a 10 percent increase on eligible vehicle sales, and transit service bonuses.
As for the latter, that entails the following: “Get upgrades from Scooter Bros Premium vehicle care, premium membership on the RydeMe rideshare app, free scooter, bike, and Seashark rentals, plus free rides on Leonida public transportation.”
This isn’t great to see for a single-player game. Paywalls in multiplayer and live-service titles are perhaps expected, but they don’t belong in single-player games.
You have to remember that this isn’t GTA VI’s only paywall either, as similar types of in-game content are locked behind the Ultimate Edition of the game.
We’re used to deluxe or premium editions boasting unique skins or cosmetics, but exclusive in-game features is a much bigger shake-up for the industry than many are perhaps acknowledging.
Copies of GTA VI do come with one free month of GTA+ but these benefits are only available with an active subscription so it’s not as if you can “claim” the content before your free month runs out.
You’re also going to want to cancel before that month ends, otherwise you could risk being charged as the subscription is recurrant.

Needless to say, fans aren’t happy at this display of greed from Rockstar.
“This is just unacceptable… on many levels. If they’re putting paywalls in single player, you can IMAGINE what they’re planning for online,” said Reddit user Tombz31.
“Now that is scummy,” added another user.
Willal212 commented: “Profound game. Truly can't wait to play. [Redacted] this tho and everything it stands for.”
It puts a slight dampner on launch day, albeit one that most players are probably going to overlook.
