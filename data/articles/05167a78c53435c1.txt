TITLE: Applied Optoelectronics Falls After $550 Million Share Sale Plan
OUTLET: TradingView

Applied Optoelectronics Falls After $550 Million Share Sale Plan
Applied Optoelectronics Inc. AAOI, which makes optical transceivers and lasers for data centers and cable networks, fell 9.29% in premarket after it disclosed an agreement to sell up to $550 million of its common stock from time to time. Raymond James & Associates and Needham & Company will act as sales agents for a 2% commission.
The program follows a sharp step-up in the business. Third-quarter revenue more than doubled to $269.9 million, with data center sales of $155.6 million, up from $43.9 million a year earlier, and the company guided fourth-quarter revenue to $385 million to $420 million. It also returned to GAAP profitability, with net income of $6.3 million, or $0.07 a share.
The company is spending heavily on capacity. It expects to produce more than 500,000 800G and 1.6T units a month by the end of 2026 and more than 1 million by the end of 2027, though its 2027 U.S. expansion is taking longer than planned because of delays in securing electrical power. It had $621.5 million in cash at the end of September.
