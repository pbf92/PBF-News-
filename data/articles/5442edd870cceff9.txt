First Citizens BancShares, Inc. (FCNCA) to Acquire United Fidelity Bank in FDIC Deal
First Citizens Bank agreed to acquire substantially all of United Fidelity Bank's loans and certain assets, and assume specified deposits and liabilities. Based on Sept. 30 balances, these include $5.9B in assets, $4.5B in deposits and $3.1B in loans; estimates remain subject to adjustment.
