TITLE: Man United's Yoro gets suspension lifted for 'wrongful' red card. Pochettino angry with VAR
OUTLET: The Killeen Daily Herald

MANCHESTER, England (AP) — Manchester United defender Leny Yoro has had his one-game ban for a red card against Liverpool lifted after it was ruled a “wrongful dismissal,” the English Football Association said on Tuesday.
Yoro was sent off after eight minutes and new United coach Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge soon turned sour in a 4-0 loss on Sunday.
“Leny Yoro's one-match suspension has been removed following a successful claim of wrongful dismissal,” the FA said.
Yoro was ruled to have denied an obvious goal-scoring opportunity when he fouled Alexander Isak on the edge of the area, but it wasn't clear Isak had full control of the ball. Liverpool was awarded a free kick and scored its opening goal from it.
Pochettino disputed the challenge was worthy of a red card. He'll now have the 21-year-old French defender available to face Brentford on Saturday. The loss to Liverpool left United in deep trouble at 19th in the Premier League.
Pochettino said he was “very disappointed” about the mistake.
“My question always was the same — we can accept the referee can make a mistake because of the emotion of the game, sometimes the view or the position can affect that. But if we have VAR, what were they doing?” he said Tuesday at a news conference ahead of United's Champions League match at Sporting Lisbon.
“Now, are we going to repeat the game? Are we going to have the possibility to play with similar tools — 11 v 11? We have an unbelievable car, but we need to have the level to drive, we need to drive in the best way. We love the technology, yes, and we accept the technology is there, but what are they doing? But it’s not only this mistake, it affects everything."
United captain Bruno Fernandes told the BBC after the game that it was “never a red card" and criticized the lengthy video review process.
“To make a mistake like this can change a game," Fernandes said. "But obviously, then, it’s not because of them that we concede the goal from the free kick, but it changes completely the flow of the game.”
