TITLE: Rockstar says GTA 6 accessibility is considered from the outset
OUTLET: Mezha.net

Rockstar says GTA 6 accessibility is considered from the outset
Photo source: TechRadar
Players will be able to choose options such as larger text, a more minimal screen experience and adjusted sprint controls, associate UI director Sam Grosset said.
Sam Grosset, associate UI director for UI/UX design on Grand Theft Auto 6, said Rockstar Games aimed to make the game's accessibility features help create an inclusive and immersive experience. “We create inclusive, intuitive, and immersive experiences for every player,” Grosset said. “Accessibility is considered from the outset, and designing for diverse needs enables us to create better games for everyone.”
This is reported by TechRadar.
Vice President of Art: 2D/UI Design Stuart Petri spoke about modernizing the game's UI and HUD “while retaining the heritage that makes it unmistakably GTA.” Grosset said the team wanted every player to have options for how they play.
“Whether it’s selecting a larger font size, opting for a more minimal screen experience, or making a controls tweak to sprinting... the choices are open to all,” Grosset said. He added that the UI should support players without interrupting gameplay or breaking immersion, unless necessary.
Grosset also discussed the phone feature, saying the mobile interface being held in hand was “a big shift.” He said it supports the feeling of playing as Jason or Lucia and helps keep players embedded in the game world.
“The UI should be complementary to the game and appropriate for each scenario,” Grosset said. He added that the team designs flexible systems to handle the scale and complexity of the world and evolve with players’ needs, and to keep raising the bar through testing and iteration.
