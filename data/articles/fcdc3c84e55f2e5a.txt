Jefferies thinks Ralph Lauren is poised for more gains
Jefferies is positive on Ralph Lauren RL after meeting with senior management and visiting flagship New York City stores. Analyst Blake Anderson and his team walked away with even greater confidence in Ralph Lauren's RL long-term brand elevation opportunity, best-in-class merchandising, and highly diversified and stable growth for a fashion brand.
Anderson noted that while the apparel giant's European business is likely still challenged near-term, the medium-term growth profile is intact. Meanwhile, the North American business is seen as resetting for long-term expansion.
Crucially, Jefferies thinks Ralph Lauren's RL China guidance still appears conservative. "China has been a standout, growing from 3% of sales just a few years ago to now 10%, with recent growth of 40%+ despite macro and traditional luxury challenges. Store growth is helping, but LFL remains strong too, driven by brand elevation, digital marketing, and, in our view, favorable fashion trends," highlighted Anderson. "While guidance for China implies a slowdown, we see this outlook as conservative, and management remains upbeat on durability with multiple drivers still ahead," he added.
Looking ahead, Jefferies sees multi-year upside to Street estimates and durable growth for Ralph Lauren RL, which warrants the premium multiple. Jefferies' price target of $440 represents more than 20% upside.
Shares of Ralph Lauren RL are up more than 5% in afternoon trading.
