Propanc Biopharma Arranges $3 Million Convertible Note Financing for $2.1 Million
Propanc Biopharma agreed to issue convertible notes with $3 million in aggregate principal and warrants for a total subscription amount of $2.1 million, including $2 million in cash from Eleven 11 Management and $100,000 in credits from Hexstone Capital. At the initial November 20 closing, the company issued notes totaling $2.22 million in principal and warrants for 1,268,573 shares; an additional $780,000 in subscriptions is planned for a second closing subject to conditions. The notes carry 14% annual interest, mature August 20, 2027, and have an initial conversion price of $1.75 per share. Hexstone also waived certain existing restrictions and consented to the transactions.
