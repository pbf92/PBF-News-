TITLE: GTA 6 makes $3.79bn on day one, the biggest entertainment launch ever
OUTLET: Inven Global

Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) made $3.79bn in day-one retail sales, according to Rockstar Games and its parent company Take-Two Interactive.
Take-Two announced the figure in a release on November 20th, a day after the game launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The company called it "a new record for entertainment across any medium" and "the biggest entertainment launch in history." According to the release, the number is based on company estimates of worldwide sales through the end of November 19th.
The release also said GTA 6 sold 32.5 million units before launch, which Take-Two described as a record across all platforms. It pointed to the game's Metacritic score of 99 as well, tying the highest score ever recorded on the site.
What Rockstar said
Sam Houser, President and Founder of Rockstar Games, thanked players in the announcement. "It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI," Houser said. "We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn't be more excited to share this moment with you."
GTA 6 is set in Vice City and the wider state of Leonida. It follows Jason and Lucia, a pair of criminals who end up caught in a conspiracy after a simple job goes wrong. The game is sold through the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Games Store and retailers.
GTA 6 and the series so far
Rockstar was founded by Houser in 1998 and helped popularise the open-world genre with Grand Theft Auto III in 2001. According to the release, GTA 5 has sold-in nearly 235 million units on its own, and the Grand Theft Auto series as a whole has sold nearly 480 million.
The launch had been a big part of Take-Two's plans for the year. When it reported its second-quarter results, the company projected that net bookings would roughly double in the following quarter on the back of GTA 6. Inven Global's own review of GTA 6 gave the game a perfect 10.
