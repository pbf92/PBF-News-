Dow, S&P 500, Nasdaq Futures Mixed Ahead Of Much-Anticipated Nvidia Earnings, Anthropic IPO: SLS, BABA, JPM, OMER In Focus
- Nvidia is set to report Q3 results on Tuesday amid its fresh leadership turmoil.
- On the geopolitical front, U.S. Vice President JD Vance vowed that the conflict with Iran would not become a prolonged "forever war" lasting two years.
- Walt Disney, Home Depot and Klarna are among other companies slated to post their quarterly earnings on Tuesday.
U.S. stock futures traded mixed overnight late Monday as markets gear up for Nvidia Corp.'s (NVDA) third-quarter earnings on Tuesday and Anthropic's much-anticipated public listing on Wednesday.
As of 10:37 p.m. EST, Dow futures declined 0.26%, while the S&P 500 futures gained 0.12% and Nasdaq-100 futures climbed 0.67%.
On Monday, the three U.S. benchmark indexes closed lower amid a tech rout. The Nasdaq Composite led the decline, shedding more than 600 points to close 2.49% lower. The S&P 500 lost 1.92% and the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 1.41%.
Key US Market Drivers
On Monday, markets took a beating as investors sold off Nvidia ahead of its Q3 results. The stock plunged more than 4% at close, dragging down other AI stocks and chipmakers.
Nvidia's results will need to prove that demand for its AI chips remains strong and that its growth outlook can sustain despite the recent leadership upheaval.
New U.S. restrictions on China's access to advanced AI chips and offshore computing power added to concerns over Nvidia's leadership crisis and AI financing risks.
Advanced Micro Devices Inc. (AMD) fell more than 5% at close, while Micron Technology Inc. (MU) tumbled more than 7%. Broadcom Inc. (AVGO), Intel Corp. (INTC) and Marvell Technology Inc. (MRVL) also posted significant losses.
The VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) and iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) also slumped about 5% each on Monday, while the Roundhill Magnificent Seven ETF (MAGS) fell 2.51%.
Charlie Bilello, Chief Market Strategist at Creative Planning, highlighted the sharp slide in NVDA stock following CEO Jensen Huang's infidelity reports. In a post on X, he said, "Oct 6, 2026: Nvidia was up 28% YTD with a $5.8 trillion market cap, the world's largest. Today: After a 35% drawdown, Nvidia is now down 16% YTD with a market cap of $3.8 trillion. And Apple has reclaimed the world's top spot. $2 trillion erased in the last six weeks."
Meanwhile, Hideyuki Ishiguro, chief strategist at Nomura Asset Management, told Bloomberg that "Tighter restrictions could raise concerns about slowing sales growth and may weigh on earnings in the short term" for Asian chip-related firms.
