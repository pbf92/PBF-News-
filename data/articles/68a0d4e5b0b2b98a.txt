SoFi Stock Hits 52-Week Low as Rate Fears Build
Financial stocks stumbled Wednesday as investors braced for higher interest rates. With the 10-year Treasury yield sitting near 5.10%, traders worried that borrowing will get more expensive, and doubts about private credit exposure, along with swings in tech and AI shares, kept the mood cautious.
SoFi has now lost ground for eight straight sessions. The stock touched $13.57 Thursday, undercutting the $13.86 floor it set Oct. 15 and sitting far below its 52-week peak of $30.42.
Wednesday’s Fed minutes sharpened the focus on rates. At the Oct. 27-28 gathering, policymakers kept their target at 3.75% to 4%, yet nearly every official saw more hikes coming. The next Federal Reserve policy meeting is scheduled for Dec. 8–9.
Because rising rates make borrowing more expensive, they are the main worry for financial firms, and SoFi, as a financial services stock, may feel the strain.
SoFi Stock Technical Read: Oversold Near Multi-Month Lows
SoFi is trading at multi-month lows and has lost ground eight sessions in a row. The shares sit below their 20-day, 50-day and 200-day averages, with the 50-day trailing the 200-day, which is itself sloping down.
The stock reached $19.74 on July 10, fell to $14.88 on July 29, bounced to $19.50 on Aug. 28 before dropping below $14.
At 12.8% below its 20-day average of $15.88, the shares look stretched, which could limit further losses. A close back over $13.86 would undo the breakdown, and reclaiming $14.88, the July 29 swing low, would signal a stronger recovery.
