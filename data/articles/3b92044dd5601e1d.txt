TITLE: “It’s a potential” McKennie responds to Manchester United rumours
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Weston McKennie has been linked with a potential move to Manchester United, with the Juventus midfielder reportedly among the American players Mauricio Pochettino would like to bring to Old Trafford.
Pochettino is the latest manager to take charge of the Red Devils and is expected to receive financial backing to strengthen his squad during the January transfer window. Given his previous involvement with the United States national team, it is perhaps unsurprising that several American players have been linked with a move to United.
The Argentine manager had been in charge of the national team until the current international break and is expected to assume his club responsibilities afterwards. His immediate priority will be to improve United's performances and help the club move away from the threat of relegation.
It has been a difficult campaign for United, but the club believes Pochettino's extensive Premier League experience will help him address their problems. The manager will therefore be expected to identify suitable reinforcements who can improve the squad.
McKennie and Balogun linked with United
Several reports have suggested that McKennie and Folarin Balogun are among the American players Pochettino would like to recruit. Both have attracted attention because of their performances, and their potential arrivals would give the manager familiar options as he looks to reshape his squad.
McKennie remains an important player for Juventus, where he continues to contribute to the team's performances. However, Juve could consider allowing him to leave if they receive an attractive offer during the transfer window.
The midfielder has also acknowledged that Manchester United could become a destination for American players currently attracting transfer interest.
(Photo by Simone Arveda/Getty Images)
McKennie comments on potential transfers
Speaking via Football Italia, McKennie discussed the possibility of American players moving to the Premier League giants and suggested that several could be approaching important career decisions.
"It's a potential, we have a lot of guys on our team who are due to make moves within the next year.
"So it's inevitable, but you never know what's going on in coaches' minds. But you can see guys from here being talked about going to Manchester United."
His comments suggest that he is aware of the growing transfer speculation surrounding American players. Whether Juventus would sanction his departure remains to be seen, but Manchester United could explore the opportunity if Pochettino decides to make him a priority.
