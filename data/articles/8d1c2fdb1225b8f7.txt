Shoals Technologies cut at UBS on uncertainty around Array merger
Shoals Technologies SHLS sank 10.8% in Tuesday's trading as UBS downgraded the designer and manufacturer of electrical balance of systems solutions to Neutral from Buy with a $9 price target, cut from $12, saying its planned merger with Array Technologies ARRY creates some uncertainty around integration but is an overall strategic positive.
UBS analyst Jon Windham said he would not consider the Shoals-Array SHLS ARRY combination a finished product and expects further mergers and acquisitions into tangential product categories likely after the closing.
Windham raised his cumulative 2026-28 adjusted EBITDA estimates by 5% to reflect Shoals' SHLS Q3 result and higher gross margins, driven by consolidation of manufacturing activities at the company's new facility.
