TITLE: Liverpool mull Quansah return - Thursday's gossip
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
Liverpool consider return for Jarell Quansah, Tottenham scout Vangelis Pavlidis and Paris St-Germain target Estevao Willian.
Liverpool have not ruled out triggering their buy-back option for Bayer Leverkusen's Jarell Quansah, 23, which would mean a fee of just over £50m would secure the England defender in 2027. (Teamtalk), external
Tottenham Hotspur have sent scouts to watch Greece striker Vangelis Pavlidis play for Benfica and are monitoring the 28-year-old's situation. (Record - in Portuguese), external
Chelsea and Brazil forward Estevao Willian, 19, is a target for Paris St-Germain, who have lost a number of attackers to injury. (CaughtOffside, external)
Arsenal have added Club Brugge and Portugal winger Carlos Forbs, 22, to their list of January targets. (Football Insider, external)
Fikayo Tomori, 28, could depart AC Milan for Atletico Madrid, with the England centre-back entering the last six months of his contract. (Fichajes - in Spanish, external)
Everton will not move for Cagliari and Senegal forward Paul Mendy in January, but they retain a long-term interest in the 19-year-old. (Football Insider, external)
Monza striker Gustavo Varela, 21, has attracted interest from Premier League clubs, but Napoli, Juventus, AC Milan, and Inter Milan are also all monitoring the Portugal youth international. (Gazzetta dello Sport), external
Arsenal goalkeeper Kepa Arrizabalaga has not received an offer from Valencia despite a report linking the 32-year-old with a move back to his native country. (AS - in Spanish), external
Bayern Munich stand a good chance of France winger Michael Olise, 24, signing a contract extension, with his current deal running out in 2029. (Foot Mercato - in French), external
Premier League clubs are interested in Fenerbahce left-back Archie Brown, 24, with the Englishman's release clause valued at around £19m. (Florian Plettenberg), external
