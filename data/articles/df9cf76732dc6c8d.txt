Liverpool vs Man Utd prediction
Manchester United start life under new boss Mauricio Pochettino with a trip to Anfield on Sunday to take on rivals Liverpool in the Premier League (4:30pm, Sky Sports Main Event).
The former Tottenham Hotspur and Chelsea boss replaced Michael Carrick earlier this week and officially took charge of the first-team on Wednesday after leaving his role as USA national team boss.
He has had just three days of training with his new charges, but they will be hoping a change in manager can kick-start a change in fortunes.
It's hard to see too much changing, after all Pochettino still has the same defence, who have conceded 30 goals in their 15 games so far, to choose from.
All the talk over the past few weeks has centred around the future of Carrick and, in some ways, that has helped take the pressure off the underperforming players, but now they will have nowhere to hide.
United go into the game in the bottom three with just eight points and one win on the board; their opponents, on the other hand, sit in fourth place, 10 points clear of their rivals and are odds-on with UK betting sites to get the better of Poch in his first game as United boss.
Liverpool vs Man Utd betting preview: Can Pochettino have an immediate impact?
The new United boss could go a long way to announcing himself at United if he can get a win at Anfield, but despite the Red Devils winning there last season, the task couldn't be much tougher.
The 2-1 win last October, thanks to goals from Bryan Mbeumo and Harry Maguire, was their first on Merseyside since January 2016, ending a run of 11 games without a win at Anfield in all competitions.
The Reds have already lost twice at home this season, going down to Manchester City back in October and the champions Arsenal earlier this month.
Their only other defeat came away at Fenerbahce, just days after that Arsenal defeat, but they bounced back with a 3-1 win at Crystal Palace before the international break with Alexis Mac Allister, Florian Wirtz and Alexander Isak all on the scoresheet.
Despite all the optimism surrounding United, football betting sites have priced up Liverpool as strong favourites at a best price of 10/13, while you can get 16/5 on a United win.
Both teams have scored in five of the last six meetings between these two and we've had over 2.5 goals in 12 of the last 13.
In the league, United have scored 14 but conceded 19 and they've not kept a single clean sheet. Liverpool have also conceded way more than they would have liked, with 10 in their 10 games so far, and they've scored 14.
