TITLE: “GTA VI” release draws anticipation after years of delays
OUTLET: The American River Current

After multiple development delays and nearly 13 years since the release of “Grand Theft Auto V”, Rockstar Games is set to release “Grand Theft Auto VI” on Nov. 19 for PlayStation 5 and the Xbox Series X/S.
Like previous games in the series, “GTA VI” takes place in a fictional state and a fictional setting. This game is set in the state of Leonida, which includes Vice City.
Confirmed by Rockstar Games through indepth looks and trailers, Vice City is a Miami Florida-inspired city where neon lights shine miles of Atlantic Ocean beaches and high-rise apartments. Other destinations include Watson Bay, inspired by Cedar Key, Florida and Leonida Keys, inspired by the Florida Everglades, which are also represented in the game as Grass Rivers.
As for the protagonists, there are two this time instead of the three featured in “GTA V.” The game features two protagonists: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. They are in a relationship throughout the game causing whatever mayhem the player needs to cause through the game’s story. It will have a Bonnie and Clyde like narrative.
Jason and Lucia find themselves on the wrong side of the law, and rightfully so. Thinking that they both build a better life, they return to crime through a heist that eventually gets botched. That single botched job blows up, entangling Jason and Lucia in a large-scale criminal conspiracy stretching across Leonida. Jason & Lucia now have to pull off even more heists, scope out security systems and dodge law enforcement because they cannot go back to a normal life.
Some landmarks shown in Rockstar’s trailers include an airport, a football stadium, an unnamed amphitheater and a zoo. Many buildings are bound to be explored, and the places mentioned will also hopefully be explorable.
The game is also expected to feature over 170 variations of wildlife, according to GameInformer magazine. Wildlife will include flamingos and alligators, along with a wider variety of exploreable buildings, such as malls, hotels, pawn shops, skyscrapers and nightclubs.
Vehicle interactions may also change in “GTA VI”. Unlike previous games, in which players steal cars by breaking a window, the upcoming installment may introduce different methods depending on the vehicle. Players may use lockpicking tools on older vehicles and more advanced equipment for newer vehicles. This has been confirmed by GamesRadar and IGN.
The game also introduces fueling up vehicles and charging electric vehicles, confirmed by Rockstar co‑studio head Rob Nelson and other developers in Sept. 2026. For both gas and electric vehicles, refueling and recharging time will only take about 10 seconds no matter if it’s a truck or sports car. Gas and charging stations will also double as repair points for your vehicle.
Another rumored feature in correlation includes varying fuel prices and the possibility that players’ action could influence those prices, although Rockstar has not confirmed these mechanics.
Vehicle damage is also expected to be more detailed compared to “GTA V” potentially including lasting damage and slow-motion effects during major collisions. This has been shown in Rockstar’s extended look special that was shown on Netflix on Aug. 27.
“GTA VI” is expected to run at 30 frames per second at 1440p on all systems. This has been confirmed by IGN and FandomWire with no confirmed 60 frames per second at launch. The PS5 Pro will have some enhancements confirmed by PlayStation themselves. These enhancements will be Ray-Traced shadows, Spectral Super Resolution, and DualSense haptic feedback.
Players can reportedly preload ”GTA VI” on Nov 10, ahead of its scheduled Nov. 19 release date. The download is expected to require approximately 212 gigabytes of available storage space. This has been confirmed by an announcement article on Rockstar’s website.
“GTA VI” is available as a digital game only with one technically physical edition. The technical physical edition will only contain a download code inside the box, no disc. The standard edition starts at $69.99, while the ultimate edition will be priced at $99.99. The ultimate edition will include 3 exclusive vehicles, additional clothing options, and personalized weapon variants for Jason and Lucia. The game being digital instead of having a disc was a decision made by Rockstar back in June of this year.
“GTA VI” is planning to be released on a PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S gaming console. A PC release date has not been confirmed by Rockstar, but multiple industry reports published by Forbes and Gamebeat report that a PC version is targeting release for Q1 of 2027 between January and March.
Fans are also anticipating an online multiplayer mode for “GTA VI,” although Rockstar has not announced an official release date. According to a Forbes article cited by the author, speculation about multiplayer features has emerged reported data-mining findings. The game is expected to launch with a single-player game.
With a new setting, two protagonists and years of the release benign delay “GTA VI” has high expectations to satisfy. Whether this game lives up to its reputation will be clear once players have the chance to experience playing the game.
