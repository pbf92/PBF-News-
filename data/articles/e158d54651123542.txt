Argan upgraded at Piper Sandler on share price decline
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- Piper Sandler upgraded Argan to Neutral From Underweight, citing a significant decline in its share price.
- The stock is down ~17% over the last month and off ~58% over the last six months.
- The firm upped its price target by a dollar to $274 (~1% upside based on Nov. 24 close).
- Analyst Dimple Gosai said that the current share price "now implies ~2.0GW/yr of new [Combined Cycle Gas Turbine] awards, in line with the ~2.1GW/yr in our model and AGX's win pace since late 2024."
- She added that the company has two upcoming opportunities. The first, CPV Shay, is a 2.1GW/year energy center in West Virginia that Gosai believes Argan has the advantage over privately held Kiewet "since CPV is a repeat AGX customer and Shay uses the same 1x1 design AGX is building at Basin Ranch."
- The other is Canadys Station, a 2.2GW facility in South Carolina that is being transformed from a decommissioned coal site.
