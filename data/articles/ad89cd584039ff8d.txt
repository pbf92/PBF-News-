TITLE: GTA 6 Lets Players Revisit the Original Vice City, Here’s How
OUTLET: PlayStation LifeStyle

Fans have found a surprising trick that lets them visit the original Vice City from Rockstar Games’ 2002 title in GTA 6. The open-world game takes players back to the fictional coastal city inspired by Miami, now expanded with new locations. The new Vice City includes many easter eggs, including one that lets players use a portal to enter a space based on the PS2 game, complete with era-appropriate animations, character modes, and more.
To enter the original Vice City, players need to head to the radio tower in Key Lento. After entering it and climbing to the top floor, players need to flip certain switches. From there, players need to visit the various LPL power stations to find letters that make up the phrase, “Life is passing me by.” Fans then need to press a specific set of buttons on the map screen and visit Reactor 2 at the T.H. Power Station. Once there, a portal will open at a specific time in the evening, letting players enter the old Vice City. YouTuber Memio found the Easter egg and detailed the steps to enter the old game online.
GTA 6 continues to give longtime fans reasons to look closer
Entering Vice City is just one of the many Easter eggs in the game. Among them are parodies of DC characters, Dexter, and even in-game video games. These secrets make up a large chunk of the game’s 200-hour playtime for those hunting the Platinum trophy.
Besides Easter eggs, players have also found an absurdly expensive trick to play it at 60 FPS on consoles. Despite the game’s absence of a first-person view, players have also found a way to enter it.
