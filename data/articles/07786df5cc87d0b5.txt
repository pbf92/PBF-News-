Disney Stock Hits 52-Week High: What's Happening?
The Walt Disney Co. DIS shares are rising to a fresh 52-week high Wednesday. The company is seemingly building on last week’s earnings report as ESPN gets ready for the Super Bowl in February.
- Walt Disney stock is approaching key resistance levels. Why are DIS shares at highs?
Disney Hits 52-Week High
The stock hit $125.17 on Wednesday, a new peak for the past 52 weeks, and it trades near $125.04. Coming into the session, the 52-week range ran from $92.19 to $123.37.
The chart backs the breakout. Disney is above its 20-day, 50-day and 200-day averages, and its 50-day average is higher than the 200-day. MACD reads 3.04, above both its 2.80 signal line and zero, which points to supportive momentum.
One caution: the shares are approximately 5.9% over their 20-day average of $118.06. A close under the former high of $123.37 would erase the breakout, and staying over the 20-day line would preserve the current pace.
Disney Sports Profit Jumps on NFL Deal
The rise is building on last week’s earnings. Sports operating income jumped 53% in the quarter, and Disney expects the game and the College Football Playoff calendar to lift Sports results in the fiscal second quarter.
CEO Josh D’Amaro said “live sports remain among the most powerful ways to bring fans together.” Disney credited stronger ad pricing, more affiliate revenue and its NFL equity stake, and said “Monday Night Football” had its best-ever Week 1 and Week 2 audiences.
For the entire fiscal year, Sports revenue grew 5% to $18.6 billion and operating income rose 7% to $3.1 billion.
ESPN will produce its first Super Bowl when Super Bowl LXI kicks off Feb. 14 at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. ESPN and ABC will broadcast it, and Disney+ will stream it for U.S. subscribers.
DIS Shares Are Moving Higher
