TITLE: Rockstar Games and Discord have blocked Leonida's GTA VI porting project.
OUTLET: GameGPU

Leonida, an unofficial project aimed at recompiling and porting a leaked build of Grand Theft Auto VI for Xbox to PC, has finally ceased operations. The creators of the initiative reported receiving official letters from Discord administrators and Rockstar Games representatives. The developers are currently uploading their personal data and have completely ceased public activity, thanking the community for their support.
At the time of the ban, the project's main Discord server had over 160 members, with approximately 120 joining in less than 12 hours amid news of initial technical successes. Following the deletion of the main platform, the backup server created by the authors was also blocked. The project's GitHub repository was also deleted, and the alternative Git hosting service to which the team attempted to migrate their work stopped responding. The lead developer, known by the nickname NotRexed, announced his departure from the project, citing copyright interference and circumstances beyond his control.
Shortly before the shutdown, enthusiasts, including those using AI tools to reverse engineer leaked files from the CyberLeek group, managed to achieve the first noticeable results. Project participants reported that the game had begun to run on Windows and reached the main menu, albeit accompanied by a black screen and audio interruptions. Despite Leonid's demise, reports continue to appear online of other closed groups of enthusiasts still trying to get the unofficial port up and running.
