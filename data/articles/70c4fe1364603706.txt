TITLE: Watch: Bruno Fernandes tees up bizarre Manchester United goal with ‘assist of the season’
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Bruno Fernandes has been lauded for the 'assist of the season' after he appeared to inadvertently create the divot which helped him equalise for Manchester United on Wednesday night.
United trailed 2-0 at half-time as they visited Sporting in the Champions League, and who else but their captain should grab the match by the scruff of the neck.
After the break, he dispatched a well-taken penalty before equalising with one of the stranger goals scored in the Champions League.
He struck a low effort from about 25 yards and his shot appeared to be right down the throat of goalkeeper Kique Pereira.
Until, that is, the ball caught a divot on the edge of the six-yard box and popped up, looping over the head of the helpless Pereira and into the net to take the score to 2-2.
And new footage which emerged post-match appears to show that he created that divot himself, moments before benefiting from its presence to score.

TNT Sports has released a clip which shows Fernandes pressuring Pereira moments before his goal went in, and, after the goalkeeper clears his lines, the United captain appears to cut the turf as he turns to get back onside.
He shoots roughly 20 seconds later, with a marker clearly showing the ball crossing the part of the turf he disturbed.
Analysing the snippet, Ex-Man United star Owen Hargreaves, working as a pundit, joked: "This is the assist of the season! The best assist you've never seen.
"The maddest game ever, and this sums it up. I tell you what, with that bounce, the goalkeeper has absolutely no chance. Unbelievable."
