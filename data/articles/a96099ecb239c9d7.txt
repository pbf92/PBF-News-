RaveDAO: 21.38 million RAVE unlock - Aug 2027
RaveDAO is scheduled to unlock 21.38 million RAVE in August 2027. The release includes allocations for the community, ecosystem, foundation, investors, and team.
The tokens in the release equal 7.78% of the circulating supply. Once those tokens unlock, holders who receive them will have access to their released allocations and can sell more RAVE than they could before the release.
