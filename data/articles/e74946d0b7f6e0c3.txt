BioLineRx (BLRX) Sells 14.4% APHEXDA Royalty Interest for $5.6M
BioLineRx agreed to sell its 14.4% royalty interest in future APHEXDA net sales to Soleus for a one-time $5.6M payment. The transaction is expected to close on or about November 30, 2026.
