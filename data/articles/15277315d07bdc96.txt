TITLE: Drone operator films Pochettino’s first Man United training session, footage posted online
OUTLET: Oskaloosa Herald

MANCHESTER, England (AP) — Mauricio Pochettino’s first training session as Manchester United coach came under closer scrutiny than expected when a drone operator filmed it on Thursday.
About 12 minutes of the session was uploaded onto a YouTube account called CC OVERVIEW with a man, who also appeared to be controlling the drone hovering around the Carrington training ground, providing his own live commentary.
