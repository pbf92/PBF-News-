China Risun Group Limited Starts No. 4 Furnace at Anode Project
China Risun said Xiangfu New Energy's No. 4 graphitization furnace unit commenced operation on Nov. 16, expanding graphitization capacity. The project is designed for 200,000 tonnes per annum; no output or revenue figures were provided.
