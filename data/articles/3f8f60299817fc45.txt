We pulled primary sources on November 28, 2026:
Privacy Center consumer path
Anthropic's article How do I change my model improvement privacy settings applies to Claude Free, Pro, Max, including Claude Code on those plans. Steps:
- Open Settings from your account menu.
- Choose Privacy or visit claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls.
- Toggle Help Improve our AI models.
The article says off means new and stored chats skip future training runs, but data already in completed training stays used. Safety classifiers may still use flagged chats.
Privacy policy training section
The Anthropic privacy policy states consumer inputs and outputs train models unless you opt out through account settings, with exceptions for safety review and explicit feedback. Commercial Claude for Work and API products are out of scope for that consumer toggle; Anthropic says it does not train on those inputs/outputs by default.
Commercial vs consumer split
The Privacy Center separates articles:
- Consumer training choice: model improvement toggle.
- Commercial products: Is my data used for model training? — default no training on work and API data unless you report bugs or allow use.
If your HN screenshot shows no toggle, first check whether the account is Team, Enterprise, or Education. Absence may match documentation, not a outage.
What is verified vs still open
Until Anthropic posts a status or changelog entry, teams should treat missing-toggle reports as incidents to log, not as proof policy changed.
