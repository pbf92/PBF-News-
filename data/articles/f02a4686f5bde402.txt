“The relationship is yours to nurture or yours to lose”: Rockstar on GTA 6
Grand Theft Auto VI will let players shape the relationship between its two main characters, Jason and Lucia. Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson says whether that bond remains romantic is in the player’s hands.
The detail comes from The Guardian’s visit to Rockstar, where the newspaper saw the game being played ahead of its November 19 launch. Nelson described the relationship as something players can nurture or lose.
That gives the story a connection to the time you spend with both characters. The Guardian reports that, while some parts of the game follow one perspective, players can switch between Jason and Lucia for much of the experience.
Jason and Lucia can spend time together in Vice City
When the characters are apart, The Guardian reports that players can have them call or message each other. When they are together, the newspaper says they can visit a bar or gym, as well as go on less peaceful outings.
“The relationship is yours to nurture or yours to lose,” Nelson told the newspaper. He also said its romantic direction is up to the player. Rockstar has not provided a full account of every possible outcome, so it would be premature to map out all the choices.
The Guardian’s report describes a more personal focus within the familiar open-world setting. It is still a Grand Theft Auto game, but the reporter saw character interactions woven into everyday moments as well as criminal activity.
The opening jobs will not make every target available
The newspaper reports that Jason and Lucia can initially hotwire lower-end vehicles and pick basic locks, with more valuable targets opening up as they develop.
Nelson linked that approach to the pair’s circumstances in the story. The Guardian describes players looking for ways into locations and considering security before attempting some jobs, rather than treating every target as immediately accessible.
The Guardian’s observations come from a preview arranged by Rockstar. The finished game may have details and options that were not shown during the visit.
The game arrives on consoles on November 19
Rockstar has announced GTA VI for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S. Amazon lists a PS5 edition as a code in a box, rather than a game disc. Amazon says the code is for US and Canadian PlayStation Network accounts. Check delivery and account compatibility if you order it.
