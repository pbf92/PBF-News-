TITLE: 'GTA 6' on Xbox Series S: How well it actually runs
OUTLET: Mashable

Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here.
Reading through Reddit, there is no shortage of concerns about how well Xbox Series S will or won’t run the game. As the release neared, fans seemed confident that Rockstar Games would ensure the game was playable, but opinions varied widely on what graphical sacrifices would be required for it to run, including reduced crowd density and scaled-back visuals.
How much time did I spend sweating the idea of crowd density? Too much. Did I ever think about it or care about it until Redditors said it’ll likely be less on the Series S? Not at all.

And yet, while it filled me with regret for not buying a PS5 last Black Friday before the price hikes arrived, it did not stop me from buying GTA 6 on Xbox Series S, debating whether or not to resist the pull off the New Zealand timezone trick to play it early, and preparing to explore Leonida.
And now, having played GTA 6 for a few hours, I can tell you everything you need to know about how it runs and how it feels.
How GTA 6 runs on Xbox Series S
I won’t keep you waiting: GTA 6 runs smooth as butter on Xbox Series S. There were no stutters, no lag or other slowdowns that pulled me out of the gameplay.
During a prologue that introduced me to protagonists Jason and Lucia, I quickly experienced a variety of locations in daytime and nighttime, as well as rainy weather. After that, I was able to play around Leonida and check out all the basic things that might catch your attention as you play: how the weather looks, flying down the road in a car through busy streets, random tasks around Jason's home.
In addition to moving quickly through various areas, I also took a moment to slow down. I watched a sunset by the water, went for a swim surrounded by fish, and observed how the environment affected Jason. Nothing slowed down, glitched out, or made me think anything other than I was playing a AAA game.
GTA 6 on Xbox Series S versus other consoles
What I can’t tell you right now is how much better GTA 6 is or isn’t on PS5 or any of the higher-end consoles. We’re in the process of comparing the Xbox Series S version to the PS5 Pro version as we speak.
Rockstar has explicitly said the game will run best on PS5 consoles, with faster load times and technology that may improve resolution. The PS5 Pro version will also include ray tracing, making the lighting even more eye-catching. But both games will still be locked at 30 frames per second, according to Rockstar. The game developer did not say if any other sacrifices had to be made to get it to run on the Xbox Series S.
Regardless, what I can tell you is there is a good game waiting for you to play on Xbox Series S. If you don’t have the funds to upgrade your console, do not let comparison be the thief of joy.
GTA 6 is a generational moment in gaming and a cultural moment much larger than any game launch I’ve ever seen. The game is fun, the music is a vibe, and the world is immersive without feeling like a chore. I can't imagine waiting to play this if they have the means to do so right now.
