TITLE: Stock Market Week Ahead Brings Inflation Data After Nasdaq Falls 10%
OUTLET: JournalArta

The stock market’s week ahead brings US inflation data and earnings from chipmakers and retailers after the Nasdaq fell 10%. Nvidia shares dropped 23% for the week following its earnings report, contributing to a broad selloff that also hit the Dow Jones Industrial Average and the S&P 500.
It was a difficult week for stocks. The S&P 500 fell 8.6%, while the Dow lost 8%. The Nasdaq and Dow had their worst week since March 2020.
Nvidia’s weekly decline followed sexual and alleged espionage scandals involving its former chief executive. The company’s shares have lost nearly 40% in November.
The Nasdaq is more than 18% below its October 6 high. The S&P 500 ended the week 11.9% below its high, while the Dow closed 12% below its August high.
Some stocks held up better. Walmart barely felt the market’s downturn, while Tyson Foods and Tractor Supply Hardware rallied. Building maintenance, general retail and restaurants were among the best-performing industry groups in the month through the end of the week.
Inflation data leads economic calendar
The personal consumption expenditures price index, the Federal Reserve’s primary inflation gauge, is due in the coming week. Wall Street expects the core index to rise 0.29%, based on previously reported consumer and producer price data.
The October personal income and outlays report and monthly durable goods orders are also due. Investors will also see any revisions to the government’s initial estimate of 3.4% growth in third-quarter gross domestic product.
The Federal Reserve’s Beige Book, an anecdotal report on economic conditions, is on the calendar. S&P Global will release preliminary US activity gauges for November, and the Conference Board will publish its consumer confidence index.
Chipmakers and retailers report earnings
Analog Devices, Semtech and Ambarella are scheduled to report earnings during the holiday week. Analysts expect adjusted earnings at Analog Devices to rise 72% on a 41% sales gain in its fiscal fourth quarter.
For Semtech’s third quarter, analysts forecast earnings growth of 135% and a 56% increase in sales. They expect Ambarella’s earnings to rise 15% on a 10% sales gain. Semtech shares have dropped 31% in November.
Retailers scheduled to report include Abercrombie & Fitch, Dollar Tree, Kohl’s, Burlington Stores and Best Buy. Analysts expect earnings to rise 251% at Dollar Tree and 129% at Kohl’s.
Dell Technologies is also due to report third-quarter results. Analysts expect adjusted earnings of $6.62 a share, up 156% year over year, on sales of $49.32 billion, up 83%.
Analysts expect sales of data center servers and storage equipment to drive Dell’s results, offset by slower personal computer sales. Hewlett Packard Enterprise and HP are also scheduled to report fiscal fourth-quarter results.
Deere is due to report earnings, with analyst consensus pointing to its first earnings gain since the fourth quarter of 2023. Deere shares are up 30% in 2026.
