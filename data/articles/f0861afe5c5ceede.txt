Less than 1 min read
Morningstar reported Q3 2026 adjusted operating margin of 28.3%, up 3.9 percentage points year over year, and outlined business trends in investor Q&A.

- Adjusted operating income rose 26.1% to $189.9 million; severance costs of $6.9 million were included in adjusted results.
