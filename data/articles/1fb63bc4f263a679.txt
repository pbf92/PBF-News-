TITLE: Meta Platforms, Inc. (META): Strong Advertising Growth Supports Its Long-Term Investment Case
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

We just published our list, Ken Fisher Portfolio Review: 10 Highest-Conviction Technology Stocks for Long-Term Growth, and Meta Platforms, Inc. ranked 9th on this list.
Ken Fisher, founder and former CEO of Fisher Asset Management, has historically maintained a constructive, growth-oriented view of technology stocks. His investment philosophy centers around companies with strong long-term growth potential and competitive advantages.
Technology has been an important part of his investment thesis, particularly large-cap companies with exposure to software, semiconductors, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence. Additionally, Fisher Asset Management favors technology companies with strong intellectual property, established ecosystems, and pricing power that can sustain growth over time.
Ken Fisher has historically maintained an overly optimistic outlook on global equities. Early in the year, he argued that stocks were on course to deliver another strong year but slightly lower than in 2025. Take a look at the 12 best NASDAQ growth stocks to buy.
According to Fisher, global stocks have largely performed as he expected, though the first half of the year was stronger than anticipated. While he did not forecast the conflict in Iran and rate hikes, these events are unlikely to disrupt his full-year outlook.
      "Now I want to take you back to something I said earlier and try to put that into perspective. The tech world in aggregate, on average, is doing about 4% better year- to-date than the US as a whole, which includes the tech part as its biggest weight," Fisher said.
