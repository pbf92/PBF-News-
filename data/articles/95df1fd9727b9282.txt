TITLE: Sporting Lisbon v Manchester United: Live stream, Time & How to watch
OUTLET: The Busby Babe

Manchester United return to UEFA Champions League action on Wednesday night, as we travel to Lisbon to take on Portuguese giants Sporting.
The Reds came from two goals down to defeat Roma last time out in this competition and our new head coach Mauricio Pochettino will be keen to get a maiden victory under his belt, following the 4-0 defeat at Anfield on Sunday against Liverpool.
Sporting currently sit third in Liga Portugal, and they’ve made a positive start to their European campaign too, winning three and losing just one of their league-phase matches so far.
TEAM NEWS
MANCHESTER UNITED: Matheus Cunha is banned after his red card against Roma. Lisandro Martinez returned from domestic suspension by featuring against Liverpool, and Matthijs de Ligt made his first start in the Premier League since November 2025 at Anfield, so both could be involved again on Wednesday.
Leny Yoro is available for Wednesday and for the weekend after his red card was rescinded.
Luke Shaw, Carlos Baleba, Manuel Ugarte, Marcus Rashford and Benjamin Šeško have all been sidelined for the Reds.
SPORTING: Zeno Debast is a doubt for the hosts, having not featured since September for Paulo Fonseca’s side. Fonseca made a raft of changes for Saturday’s 4-0 cup thrashing of fourth-tier UD Serra, with only Sergi Altimira, Nestory Irankunda and Luis Guilherme retaining their places in the side.
Luis Suarez (not that one) is the one to watch for Sporting - he’s netted nine times in the league, and on four occasions in the Champions League.
SPORTING’S FORM
The Lions have lost just twice all season across all competitions, and both defeats have come at home - against Academico Viseu in the top-flight and once in the Champions League against LASK.
The 21-time Portuguese champions head into Wednesday’s contest in excellent form, having won their last five matches across all competitions and conceding just one goal. That run includes a 1-0 victory over Shakhtar Donetsk which moved Sporting into 10th place in the league phase.
They are, however, nine points off the pace set by FC Porto, who have remarkably won all 11 of their fixtures so far.
PREVIOUS MEETINGS
The Reds have only crossed paths with Sporting on two occasions in the UEFA Champions League. Both came in the group stages of the 2007-08 campaign, which would eventually result in United lifting the trophy in Moscow.
We’ve won both meetings with Sporting, claiming a 1-0 victory away in Portugal courtesy of a Cristiano Ronaldo strike, before beating them 2-1 at Old Trafford with Ronaldo on the scoresheet once again against his former club, along with Carlos Tevez.
We also played the Portuguese side in the 1964 European Cup Winners’ Cup, with Sporting prevailing 6-4 on aggregate.
Champions League Schedule
It’s an evening kickoff in the UK, which means a 3:00 p.m. kickoff on the East Coast of the U.S.
Champions League Channel
The match is on Prime Video in the UK. Those Stateside can watch on Paramount+. In Canada, it’s on DAZN and Fubo.
