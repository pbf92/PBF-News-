TITLE: Panini Turns ‘GTA VI's’ In-Game Collectibles Into a Real Sports Trading Card Collection
OUTLET: Hypebeast

Panini America and Rockstar Games are releasing the ‘GTA VI’ Sports Trading Card Collection, their first trading card tie-in for the game
The officially licensed set turns the collectible cards found inside ‘GTA VI’ into physical cards
Parallels range from Special Silver, Pink, Teal and Light Blue up to numbered Legend Bronze, Silver and 1-of-1 Gold cards
Panini America and Rockstar Games are launching the GTA VI Sports Trading Card Collection, a licensed set that takes the card collectibles players chase in Grand Theft Auto VI and puts them into physical packs. It’s the first trading card partnership built around the game, so collectors can open real packs of the same cards that show up in-game.
Each box holds four packs and is built to give a predictable spread of base hits. On average, a box yields two Rookies, one Retro and one Illustrated card, plus four Special Silvers, so every rip covers the set’s main card styles. With the Rookies, Retros and Illustrated cards working as distinct subsets, collectors have a clear checklist to work through before the parallel hunt begins.
The parallels are where the chase gets serious. Beyond Special Silver, the set comes in Pink, Teal and Light Blue versions for collectors building rainbows. The numbered tiers then narrow quickly: Legend Bronze is limited to 49 copies, Silver drops to 25, and the Gold parallels are true 1-of-1s, making them the hardest cards in the set to pull.
The GTA VI Sports Trading Card Collection from Panini America and Rockstar Games is set to release in December 2026.
