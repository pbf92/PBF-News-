TITLE: PlayStation Network Problems: Is GTA 6 Causing An Outage?
OUTLET: ScreenRant

Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to make a huge splash at midnight, and it looks like a side effect of the game's massive popularity may have already struck. Some PlayStation 5 owners are currently struggling to access online services, which can be very inconvenient timing for those attempting to preload the game the day before release.
At the moment, PlayStation's official status checker claims that all services are up and running, with a green checkmark denoting standard operation on everything from account management to the PlayStation Store. It did, however, acknowledge an outage earlier in the afternoon. Based on player reports, it seems like problems may not entirely be resolved, even if the worst has already passed.
Downdetector currently shows a massive number of PlayStation Network problem reports, with a spike appearing around 1 PM EST today, dropping back down a bit, and then skyrocketing to another peak around 3 PM. At its highest point (2:51 PM), Downdetector notes 1787 reports of PlayStation Network problems, with error messages and codes varying for different users.
For those experiencing issues, the best solution is likely to wait, although that may not seem like a great answer right ahead of GTA 6's release. There's also a valid concern that the service could drop again around midnight, with plenty of players who didn't bother to pre-order or pre-load potentially attempting to download GTA 6 at the same time. Some fans may have also waited to see reviews before purchasing, and orders placed in the wake of glowing critical acclaim for the game might be part of what's causing trouble for PlayStation servers at the moment.
In the event of connection issues, checking back on a frequent (perhaps hourly) basis might be the optimal approach for the time being, as long as your schedule and location permits it. It's also possible to redeem a code through the web store or app without using the console itself, if you want to go ahead and take care of that while servers are struggling.
Physical game aficionados may point to this apparent outage as another side effect of the move away from physical games, as the lack of any Grand Theft Auto 6 discs means that every player is required to download the game in its entirety from the PlayStation or Xbox digital storefronts. The code-in-box approach for physical copies also came under fire recently due to the potential for theft, with reports suggesting that some copies have come without functional codes after thieves took advantage of a flashlight method to read codes without opening cases.
Hopefully, the worst of the storm has passed, and access to PlayStation Store functionality will stabilize as the day goes on. If there's one thing to be thankful for, it's that GTA 6's online component isn't releasing just yet, giving online servers one less cause of stress. When Grand Theft Auto 6's multiplayer does arrive (potentially in 2026), fans might want to prepare themselves for rougher seas ahead.
