TITLE: Nvidia sinks 14% as Anthropic’s IPO glow fades
OUTLET: Digg

TLDR
Nvidia fell 14% on November 20 despite raising its 2027 revenue outlook. Anthropic gave back most of its debut gains, dropping 8.5% to $88.91—still above its $83 IPO price. The Nasdaq Composite lost 5.3% and the S&P 500 fell 3.7%.
Nvidia fell 14% on Friday, November 20, 2026, despite a strong earnings report and a higher 2027 revenue outlook, according to Axios. Anthropic dropped 8.5% in its first full day of trading after Thursday’s debut. Upbeat guidance and IPO fanfare were having a very bad day. Source
The Nasdaq Composite fell 5.3%, while the S&P 500 closed down 3.7%, Axios reported. Source
Axios put Anthropic shares at $88.91, still above the $83 price of its initial public offering but far below Thursday’s first-day high of $115.10. An offering-price buyer was still ahead; someone who bought near Thursday’s peak was in the red. Source
According to Axios, Nvidia had lost more than $2.5 trillion in market capitalization since its October 6 all-time high. That’s the cumulative loss from the peak, not Friday’s bill alone. Source
“Right now the market is selling first and asking questions later,” Michael Monaghan, a partner at Founder ETFs, wrote, according to Axios. Source
