TITLE: 'GTA VI' Players Discover a 'Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles' Easter Egg
OUTLET: Complex

Key Takeaways
- DiscussingFilm claimed on Nov. 24 that players found a Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Easter egg in GTA VI, but shared neither its location nor details about the reference.
- The X post drew more than 30,000 likes and 1,700 reposts within hours, though Rockstar has not confirmed the discovery.
- The report comes less than a week after the game’s Nov. 19 launch, as players explore Vice City and the Florida-inspired state of Leonida.
Less than a week after the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, players are already uncovering some of the pop culture references scattered throughout Rockstar Games' latest blockbuster, including an apparent nod to the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles franchise.
In the video shown, at the end of a storm drain, the player can find four turtles next to some pizza boxes.
The discovery comes just days after Grand Theft Auto VI officially arrived on Nov. 19, giving players their first opportunity to fully explore Rockstar's fictional state of Leonida and its reimagined version of Vice City, which draws inspiration from Miami.
The reported Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles reference would bring together two of entertainment's most recognizable franchises. Since debuting as a comic book in 1984, the adventures of Leonardo, Donatello, Raphael and Michelangelo have expanded into animated television shows, live-action films, video games and countless other pop culture ventures.
Meanwhile, Rockstar has developed a reputation for incorporating hidden jokes, cultural references and elaborate Easter eggs throughout the Grand Theft Auto series, encouraging players to explore areas beyond the games' main storylines.
With GTA VI still in its first week of release, the reported discovery could be among the earliest of numerous hidden references players uncover throughout Leonida.
