TITLE: Rockstar Officially Comments on GTA 6's Difficulty
OUTLET: OpenCritic

One of the biggest challenges facing Rockstar Games with GTA 6 was figuring out how much freedom to give players without making its missions feel either frustrating or completely effortless, but Rockstar believes it found a comfortable "middle." The Grand Theft Auto series has experimented with different approaches over the years, and the gap between GTA 4 and GTA 5 is particularly noticeable. One leaned into harder encounters, while the other made it easier for players to get through its missions. With GTA 6, Rockstar wanted to find a middle ground that could accommodate a much wider range of players.
That is easier said than done, especially for a game with the specific mission structure and open-world freedom of GTA 6. Some players enjoy having to carefully plan their approach, while others are more interested in seeing the story unfold without repeatedly getting stuck on the same mission. Rockstar also has to account for people who have spent years playing Grand Theft Auto and newcomers who might be experiencing the series for the first time.
