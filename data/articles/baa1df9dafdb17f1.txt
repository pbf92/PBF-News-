Big banks, crypto stocks, neoclouds among losers: week's financials wrap
Wall Street’s major averages finished the week deep in the red as the S&P 500 entered correction territory, all while investors digested Anthropic’s landmark IPO and closely monitored Nvidia’s latest earnings report.
Large U.S. bank stock prices formed the 'death cross' bearish technical chart pattern this week, with the KBW Bank Index's BBKX 50-day moving average falling below its 200-day moving average.
The week saw JPMorgan Chase JPM face a "technical issue" that had caused customers' transactions to take longer to process on Monday. Other bank stocks, such as Bank of America BAC, US Bank USB, and Wells Fargo WFC, were also impacted.
Meanwhile, bitcoin BTCUSD sank as Wall Street’s enthusiasm on technology and artificial intelligence fizzled out, accompanying a selloff that dipped the cryptocurrency below $58K.
Among megacap financial stocks, Mitsubishi UFJ Financial MUFG led the weekly losers, shedding 14.43% of its value to close the week at $18.68.
The Japanese banking giant had reported its first-half financial results last week, revising its full fiscal year earnings target higher to ¥2,800B from ¥2,700B. The stock's 14-day RSI fell to 26.32 on Friday, indicating heavy selling pressure.
"Downside risk remains elevated — I see no convincing technical or fundamental catalyst for a reversal yet," said Anton Kharitonov, expert at Traders Union.
Goldman Sachs GS (-13.73% W/W to $790.69) and Morgan Stanley MS (-13.10% W/W to $165.67) were the next major losers in the category, despite leading Anthropic's blockbuster IPO this week.
Among largecap stocks, Pershing Square PS led the losers, retreating 24.83% from the previous week to $33.61. Bill Ackman's hedge fund disclosed notable shifts in its portfolio this week for the quarter ended September 30.
Crypto stocks Circle Internet CRCL (-23.62% W/W to $47.51) and Robinhood Markets HOOD (-20.15% W/W to $62.17) were among notable decliners amid the crypto rout.
For midcap stocks, StoneX SNEX was the top loser, retreating 36.02% to $36.63. The company's fiscal Q4 earnings failed to meet Wall Street expectations. While operating revenue grew from a year ago but fell from fiscal Q3, its expenses rose both from the prior quarter and a year ago.
IREN IREN lost 34.95% of its value to close the week at $11.82. Neocloud stocks dropped after Nvidia reversed its post-earnings gains. Nvidia's neocloud commitments total $32B through 2032.
Strive ASST (-32.07% W/W to $14.51), Abacus Global ABX (-30.19% W/W to $6.15), and MARA Holdings MARA (-27.25% W/W to $5.09) were the top smallcap decliners.
