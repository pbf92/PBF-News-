OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Forgotten Man United star called up to first-team training by Mauricio Pochettino
Mauricio Pochettino will make his home debut from the Old Trafford dugout when Manchester United play Brentford this weekend
Mauricio Pochettino invited forgotten striker Enzo Kana-Biyik to train with the Manchester United first team ahead of Saturday's Premier League clash with Brentford.
The highly-rated French forward was joined by Jack and Tyler Fletcher, Jim Thwaites and Jaydan Kamason as Pochettino got a good look at the youngsters in his squad as he prepares to make his Old Trafford debut.
Kana-Biyik joined United on a free transfer from Le Havre in the summer of 2025 but was immediately sent out on loan to Lausanne-Sport in Switzerland to aid his development.
The 19-year-old striker netted just once in 26 appearances before returning to Manchester, where he has played four times for the U21s so far this season. He now has his eye on a first-team call-up.
Pochettino could be without Bruno Fernandes for the clash with the Bees, with the club captain suffering from a virus. Should he fail a late fitness test, the midfielder will miss the fixture alongside Benjamin Sesko, Marcus Rashford, Carlos Baleba and Manuel Ugarte.
"Yes, we don't know," Pochettino said of Fernandes in his pre-match press conference on Friday. "He's affected by a virus from Portugal. He finished the game a little bit with a problem, and today, he cannot be part of the training session.
"We'll see. We are going to see during the day and tomorrow, how he wakes up, and we'll see. We're not going to take any risks, because you know when this type of situation happens, you don't feel [you can]. We are going to pay attention but, still, we decide nothing."
United go into the game in the relegation zone, with Pochettino yet to taste victory in his opening two fixtures in all competitions. Brentford, by contrast, are defying odds once again, and battered Everton 4-0 last time out.
But Bees boss Keith Andrews isn't getting carried away ahead of a trip to the Theatre of Dreams. "It's Old Trafford, it's Manchester United, it's Mauricio Pochettino," he said. "He's an incredibly impressive manager with what he's achieved.
"So, we have to expect Manchester United to be at their absolute best. If we compete to our levels, we're confident with what we can do. I expect a really tough game."
