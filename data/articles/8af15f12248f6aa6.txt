TITLE: 'Welsh Elvis' hospital chaplain to raise money for churches in concert
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

A Welsh hospital chaplain will today raise money for churches by performing Elvis Presley’s famous tunes.
Known as the "Welsh Elvis", Rev Wynne Roberts, from Bangor, will take to the stage at Groeslon Village Hall on Friday to sing the greatest hits of “The King of Rock and Roll".
His “Elvis’ Winter Wonderland” show will raise money to pay for heating and upkeep for six churches in the Beuno Sant Uwch Gwyrfai Ministry Area, near Caernarfon in Gwynedd, North Wales.
The churches, which belong to the Church in Wales, are facing increased costs to heat and repair their buildings.
Rev Wynne said: “These churches are an important part of their communities, so if Elvis can help keep the heating on this winter, I'm very happy to do my bit.”
Revd Nia Catrin Williams, Ministry Area Leader, added: “We want everyone who comes to celebrate Christmas with us to find a warm church and a warm welcome.
“In January we’ll start taking turns to host Sunday worship. That will help us spend less on heating and give us a chance to get to know people from the other churches.
“For some of us, the chance to sit and have a chat on a Sunday is as much a part of the week as the service itself."
Rev Wynne is a hospital chaplain manager for hospitals in North Wales, such as Ysbyty Gwynedd in Bangor and Ysbyty Glan Clwyd in Bodelwyddan.
When his late mother was in a nursing home, the former RAF serviceman began his tribute act.
He was awarded the British Empire Medal in 2019 for raising more than £250,000 for dementia charities.
