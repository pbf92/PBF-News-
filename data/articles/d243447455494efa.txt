TITLE: Sporting 2-2 Man Utd: Manchester United fan views
OUTLET: BBC

- Published
We asked for your thoughts after Wednesday's Champions League game between Sporting and Manchester United.
Here are some of your comments:
John: Disaster. This team is worse than Tottenham last season. Don't think this manager is the answer to the problem - he has never won anything and this team look deflated.
Mike: Potentially the best performance of the season but silly mistakes and the most awful ref of all time cost us. Good tactics - compact and very good counter-pressing, plus the midfield didn't look as exposed as usual.
David: The referee should never be allowed to referee another game ever again. As simple as that. What an absolute shambles. United should have came off that pitch with three points.
Carl: A battling performance from the Reds and a baffling permance by the officials. Still too prone to unforced errors and until these are eradicated, we will continue to struggle. Karl Darlow did well for Leeds United last season so maybe it is time to give Lammens a rest.
Christine: United pressed high and were effective in transitions. The defence, midfield and forwards all went together and played with a higher intensity. Lammens had a howler but he will come through this. United were robbed but there is a lot more to be happy about.
