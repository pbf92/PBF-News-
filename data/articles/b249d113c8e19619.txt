Nucor raised to Buy at UBS on coming 'cash flow inflection'
Nucor NUE gained 0.6% in Monday's trading as UBS upgraded the steel producer to Buy from Neutral with a $307 price target, raised from $256, forecasting 8%-11% free cash flow yields in 2027-30, providing capacity for higher cash returns as capital expenditures moderate.
UBS analyst Andrew Jones raised his hot-rolled coil steel price forecasts for 2027, 2028, and 2029 to $1,250/st, $1,050/st, and $950/st, respectively, and increased his EBITDA outlook for 2027, 2028, and 2029 by 29%, 15%, and 12%, respectively, reflecting higher steel industry costs and continued U.S. Section 232 trade protections.
Jones pointed to a 32% quarterly increase in earnings before tax from Nucor's NUE steel products segment, alongside expanding order backlogs that provide visibility into demand through mid-2027.
The analyst said he expects the U.S. sheet steel market to remain tight in the near term, with lead times of ~9.5 weeks, although he thinks prices will eventually moderate as imports increase and Nucor NUE ramps up production at its ~3M-ton West Virginia mill.
