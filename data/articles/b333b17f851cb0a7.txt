TITLE: Arsenal and PSG keen on Nusa - Tuesday's gossip
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
Arsenal and PSG are both looking at a move for RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa, Xabi Alonso has Chelsea hierarchy backing and Liverpool open contract talks with keeper Alisson Becker.
Arsenal have spoken to representatives of Norway winger Antonio Nusa with RB Leipzig looking for 80m euros (£67m) for the 21-year-old. (Teamtalk), external
However, Paris St-Germain, who have had France winger Desire Doue, 21, and 25-year-old Georgia forward Khvicha Kvaratskhelia ruled out with long-term injuries, are also keen on Nusa. (AS - in Spanish), external
Liverpool, PSG, Real Madrid, and Barcelona would all be interested if Manchester City are open to selling Norway forward Erling Haaland, 26, next summer. (Fichajes - in Spanish) , external
Tottenham's wonderkid forward Luca Williams-Barnett, 18, is on the brink of signing a new long-term contract with the club, which will disappoint Chelsea and Borussia Dortmund. (Telegraph - subscription required), external
Manchester United will target a new striker in the January transfer window, with new coach Mauricio Pochettino keen for a more reliable option than injury-prone Slovenia forward Benjamin Sesko, 23. (Times), external
Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso retains the backing of the club's hierarchy, despite the Stamford Bridge side's struggles. (Telegraph - subscription required), external
Liverpool have opened contract extension talks with 34-year-old Brazil keeper Alisson, who wants a new deal running until 2029. (Nicolo Schira), external
Brighton and Liverpool are among a number of clubs keen on Borussia Monchengladbach's 17-year-old German attacking midfielder Wael Mohya. (Teamtalk), external
Crystal Palace's new manager Thomas Frank is set to decide whether the club sign former player and 34-year-old Ivory Coast winger Wilfried Zaha, who is a free agent and has been training with the Eagles. (Football Insider), external
France midfielder Adrien Rabiot, 31, is close to signing a new contract with AC Milan in a deal which is expected to run until 2030. (Calciomercato - in Italian), external
