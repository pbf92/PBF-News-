INVESQUE ANNOUNCES SHAREHOLDER APPROVAL OF GO-PRIVATE TRANSACTION
TORONTO, Nov. 16, 2026 /CNW/ -- Invesque Inc. (IVQ) (IVQ.U) (the "Company" or "Invesque") is pleased to announce that, at a special meeting of holders ("Shareholders") of common shares of the Company (the "Shares") held earlier today (the "Meeting"), Shareholders approved the previously announced plan of arrangement (the "Arrangement") involving the Company and IVQ Acquisition Inc. (formerly known as 1610354 B.C. Ltd.) (the "Purchaser"), a special purpose entity managed by Magnetar Financial LLC ("Magnetar").
Under the Arrangement, the Purchaser will acquire all of the issued and outstanding Shares, other than substantially all of the Shares held by funds managed by Magnetar, for cash consideration of US$0.101 per Share, less any applicable withholdings, after which the Purchaser and Invesque will amalgamate. Further information concerning the Arrangement is contained in the Company's management information circular dated October 16, 2026 (the "Circular"), which is available under Invesque's issuer profile on SEDAR+ at www.sedarplus.ca and on the Company's website at www.invesque.com.
Meeting Results
The Arrangement required approval by: (i) at least two-thirds of the votes cast on the special resolution approving the Arrangement (the "Arrangement Resolution") by Shareholders present in person or represented by proxy at the Meeting; and (ii) not less than a simple majority of the votes cast on the Arrangement Resolution by Shareholders present in person or represented by proxy at the Meeting, excluding the votes attached to Shares required to be excluded under Multilateral Instrument 61-101 – Protection of Minority Security Holders in Special Transactions ("MI 61-101").

Court Approval and Closing
Invesque intends to seek a final order of the Supreme Court of British Columbia approving the Arrangement (the "Final Order") at a hearing expected to be held on November 19, 2026 at 9:45 a.m. (Vancouver time). Subject to receipt of the Final Order and the satisfaction or waiver of the remaining conditions to completion contained in the arrangement agreement dated September 30, 2026 between the Company and the Purchaser, the Arrangement is expected to be completed on or about November 24, 2026.
Following completion of the Arrangement, the Shares will be delisted from the Toronto Stock Exchange, and the Company is expected to apply to cease to be a reporting issuer under applicable Canadian securities laws.
About Invesque Inc.
Invesque is a North American health care real estate company. Over the past several years, Invesque has been liquidating assets and retiring debt with the goal of returning capital to shareholders. Invesque currently has four senior housing properties and a portfolio of loans receivable. Three of the four assets are currently subject to purchase and sale agreements with targeted closing dates prior to December 31, 2026. These sale transactions remain subject to standard due diligence and closing conditions.
