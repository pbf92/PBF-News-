TITLE: OpenAI Brings GPT-6.2 Sol to ChatGPT's Chat Tab
OUTLET: MacRumors

A version of GPT–6.2 Sol made for the Chat tab is rolling out to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise tiers, OpenAI said today. OpenAI says GPT–6.2 Sol has been tweaked for everyday conversation and can more easily handle complex requests involving detailed instructions or locating information online.
Compared with GPT–6, GPT–6.2 Sol does a better job detecting fine details in photos, like symbols on a sign or text on a label. It has improved at choosing options online based on the user's priorities, and it is smarter about when to offer a follow-up after a conversation, such as an alert about a price drop.
OpenAI says the model scores higher in teen safety evaluations, with gains in areas like emotional reliance, disordered eating, dangerous activities, and age-restricted goods and services. The model meets OpenAI's safety policies in challenging conversations 91 to 94 percent of the time, up from 77 to 80 percent for GPT–6.
GPT–6.2 for chat comes a week after it launched for Work and Codex, and those models will stay the same. GPT–6.2 is not available for Free and Go plans.
