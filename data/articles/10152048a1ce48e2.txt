TITLE: GTA 6 Is Now Out in the US and Canada – PS5 Download Size and Game Sharing Explained
OUTLET: VICE

After 13 years of waiting, GTA 6 is finally out in the US and Canada on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Here’s what you need to know before jumping into Leonida, including when the game unlocked, how long it takes to download, and how PS5 game sharing works.
What Time Does GTA 6 Come Out in California?
GTA 6 unlocked in California at 9 PM PT on Wednesday, November 18 and is playable right now. If you bought and preloaded the digital version on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, you can jump in as soon as the game unlocks and any pending updates finish installing.

How Big Is the GTA 6 PS5 Download?
The GTA 6 PS5 download size is around 210 GB. if you’re downloading Grand Theft Auto VI. According to Rockstar’s website the preload can require up to 212 GB of storage space depending on your device. If you’re close to that limit, you can see how much room you have by going to Settings > Storage on your PS5.
What PS5 Update Is Required to Play GTA 6?
The latest reported GTA 6 launch patch on PS5 is version 01.002.001, which is around 602.5 MB. It started rolling out on November 16, so your PS5 may have already installed it if you preloaded the game and have automatic updates enabled.
If you want to check manually, highlight GTA 6 on your PS5 home screen, press the Options button, and select Check for Update.
How Long Does It Take to Download GTA 6?
A 212 GB GTA 6 download takes about 4 hours and 43 minutes at 100 Mbps, assuming your console can sustain that speed the whole time. At 300 Mbps, the same download takes around 1 hour and 34 minutes.

Can You Game Share GTA 6 on PS5?
Yes, you can game share GTA 6 on PS5. PlayStation 5 supports sharing eligible digital purchases through Console Sharing and Offline Play. To try it with GTA 6, sign in with the account that bought the game on the PS5 you want to share it on.
Go to Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Console Sharing and Offline Play, then select Enable. Download the game through the account that owns it, then switch back to the profile you want to play on. Sony allows each account to enable sharing on one PS5 at a time.
