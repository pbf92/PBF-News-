GoPlus Security: Unlocks at Least 99.78 Million GPS - 16 Jan 2027
GoPlus Security has scheduled a GPS token unlock for 16 Jan 2027. The release covers tokens allocated to advisors and investors. At least 99.78 million GPS is due to be released as part of the event.
The scheduled release is equal to 1.63% of the circulating supply. Advisors and investors who receive the newly unlocked GPS will be able to sell those tokens.
