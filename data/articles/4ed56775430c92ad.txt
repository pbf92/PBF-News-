TITLE: Jason Duval's in-game Leonida driver's licence puts his birthday at 1 May 1996, making him 30 at the start of GTA 6, and Lucia Caminos turned 26 three days before the story opens on 23 November 2026
OUTLET: Make Tech Easier

Jason Duval is 30 at the start of Grand Theft Auto VI and Lucia Caminos is 26, according to the in-game driver’s licenses that players have found for the two leads, as VICE reads them. Jason’s Leonida license gives his birth date as 1 May 1996. Lucia’s Liberty State license gives hers as 20 November 2000. By VICE’s reading of the dates, that means she turned 26 three days before the story opens.
The figures come from VICE’s breakdown of the characters’ ages, published on 23 November 2026. Rockstar Games had released the game on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S four days earlier, on 19 November 2026. Beebom credits the license images to Rockstar Games via a post on X.
What the driver’s licenses show
Beebom says the characters’ profiles list their height, weight and in-game stats but not their ages. The Big Lead says players spotted the licenses in the game and that the documents then appeared online. VICE says players uncovered them, and it reports the birth dates and ages from the documents.
Each license lists a birth date and a height. VICE reports that Jason’s license lists his height as 6 feet 3 inches and gives Key Lento as his address. VICE says that makes him four years older than Lucia when we first meet them in Chapter 1. Lucia’s license lists her height as 5 feet 3 inches.
The Big Lead reproduced the details from both documents. It reports that Jason’s license records his date of birth as 05/01/1996 in the American month-first format. Lucia’s records 11/20/2000, along with brown eyes and a weight of 155 pounds. The Big Lead also lists Jason’s sex as male.
VICE calls the address a small detail and says the licenses give a look at where both characters lived before the events of the story. It notes that Jason has a Leonida license while Lucia’s was issued in Liberty State.
The two leads hold licenses from different states. According to VICE, Jason’s was issued in Leonida and Lucia’s in Liberty State. Licenses for two supporting characters also list birth dates and heights. They are Lucia’s best friend Andy Lee-Scott and Donald “Donnie” Carter. Beebom gives the same birth dates for both. VICE does not name the state that issued either document. Andy is the youngest of the four at 25 at the start of the game, and Donnie is the oldest at 48.
How Jason’s license compares with the police scanner
Jason’s license disagrees with other parts of the game. Beebom reports that the license lists him at 6ft 3in, while the police descriptions and his in-game character profile give 6ft 1in. The Big Lead noticed the same gap. It wrote that police describing Jason over the scanner estimate his height “to be two inches shorter.”
The scanner also gives a different estimate of his age. According to The Big Lead, police describe Jason as being in his mid-thirties, although “assuming the game takes place in 2026, he’d actually only be 30 years old.” The same outlet reports that the scanner places Lucia in her mid-twenties.
Why Lucia’s birthday dates the opening
Lucia’s birth date falls close to the start of the story. VICE writes that Lucia “only just turned 26” when she appears at the beginning of the game. In VICE’s reading, she celebrated her birthday on 20 November 2026, three days before the events at the start of the game. Counting three days on from that birthday puts the opening on 23 November 2026, four days after the game’s release date.
“If the story had started just four days earlier, she still would’ve been 25,” VICE notes. That date would fall one day before her birthday.
