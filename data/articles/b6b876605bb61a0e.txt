TITLE: GTA 6 First Post-Launch Release Officially Confirmed For December 2026
OUTLET: ScreenRant

There was so much anticipation ahead of the release of GTA VI that it actually feels a little weird that its now here. Ever since its arrival on November 19, players have been exploring Vice City and everything Rockstar has been cooking up over all these years, and aside from a few folks online determined to stand in the way of a good time, most players seem to be loving it.
That theory is further borne out by the fact that the game is already proving to be one of the most enormous commercial successes in history, blowing past all sorts of records and selling more than 40 million copies at last count. All of this is to say that the game is immensely popular, and there are inevitably going to be a bunch of additional releases designed to take advantage of that fact.
Well, as it happens, the first of those releases has been confirmed, and it will almost certainly come as a pleasant surprise for most fans.
On November 24, trading card company Panini, who are most well-known worldwide for their football stickers, confirmed that they are set to release the GTA VI: Sports Trading Card Collection in conjunction with Rockstar. The cards in the collection will be recognizable to anyone who is playing the game, as they feature real-world versions of the sports cards you can find while playing.
In sharing the news, Panini released an official statement, saying:
                        "The ground-breaking video game is now bringing the trading card elements found in Grand Theft Auto VI to life for the first time with officially licensed trading cards from Panini America to be released in December 2026.
"The GTA VI: Sports Trading Card Collection features real-world versions of in-game card collectibles found throughout the world of Grand Theft Auto VI. Grand Theft Auto fans will find on average two Rookies, one Retro, one Illustrated and four Special Silvers in each four-pack box. The GTAVI: Sports Trading Card Collection will also feature rare-numbered cards and team color parallels. Look for special Silver, Pink, Teal, and Light Blue parallels in addition to Legend Bronze (numbered to 49), Silver (numbered to 25), and 1-of-1 Gold parallels."
Though these kinds of releases that are dropped adjacent to video games are usually just a bit of fun, it's easy to see a world in which the cards players pull could actually have plenty of value given GTA VI's ridiculous popularity. In particular, those 1-of-1 cards seem like they could easily be highly desirable for a lot of fans, so it could even end up being quite a worthwhile investment if you do manage to pick some up.
It's worth noting that these are trading cards in the traditional sense, rather than ones where you can actually use them as part of a game, so that's also worth factoring in before buying. One thing is for sure, though, this will almost certainly be the first of many licensed GTA releases over the next year or so, so it's worth keeping an eye out to see what's coming next.
