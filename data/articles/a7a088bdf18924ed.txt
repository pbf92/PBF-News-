TradingKey - Anthropic (ANT) recently disclosed that following an overhaul of its backend systems, the median wait time for Claude Code users from submitting a request to seeing a response begin to appear decreased by about 20%.
The resources required by the company to process each request have also fallen significantly, with central processing unit (CPU) usage dropping by about 70%; across all live systems, memory usage per request decreased by more than 80%.
This overhaul addresses a practical issue brought about by rapid business growth. From early 2024 to the fall of 2026, request volume on Anthropic's own platform surged to approximately 1,800 times its original level, which also includes calls from the company's own products. Whenever a user asks Claude a question, the backend must inspect the request, deliver the content to the model, and return the answer. Now that these steps consume fewer resources, the same fleet of servers is expected to handle more requests, easing the company's pressure to expand capacity as business grows.
Simply put, even if business expansion continues to drive up total expenditures, as long as the cost of services grows at a slower pace than the corresponding revenue, Anthropic will be able to retain more gross profit for every dollar of revenue it generates.
In addition, Situational Awareness, a fund managed by Leopold Aschenbrenner, also offered a clear assessment of Anthropic. In an investor letter, Aschenbrenner stated that the current market presents some of the highest potential-return opportunities since the fund's inception. He believes superintelligent AI could reshape the industry landscape, and he is therefore bearish on the long-term competitive advantages of existing leaders such as Nvidia, AMD, Microsoft, and Apple.
Anthropic was the fund's largest private holding prior to its listing. Aschenbrenner stated that even if building the portfolio from scratch at the IPO offering price of $83 per share, he would still make Anthropic his top holding, with a weighting significantly higher than other positions. For investors, upcoming earnings reports provide an entry point to evaluate this bet: as paid Claude usage grows, whether revenue can consistently outpace the cost of services, allowing the measured efficiency gains to ultimately flow through to gross margins.
