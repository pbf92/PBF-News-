TITLE: Cochrane utility bills could rise nearly $14 per month in 2027
OUTLET: Cochrane Eagle

Cochrane residents could see their monthly utility bills increase in 2027 under proposed rate changes presented to council during budget deliberations.
At its Nov. 12 budget deliberation session, Cochrane Town Council received the findings of a comprehensive utility rate review, outlining proposed changes to water, wastewater and stormwater rates for 2027 and beyond.
The proposed rates would represent an increase of approximately 14 per cent, raising an average monthly bill from $102.78 to $116.98.
“Water, wastewater and stormwater services are essential to our community, and maintaining these systems requires thoughtful, long-term financial planning,” said Shawn Tooth, the Town’s director of utilities.
“We recognize that increasing utility costs place additional pressure on households. This review helps us better understand the true cost of delivering these services, plan for future infrastructure needs and establish rates that are fair and financially sustainable.”

The review also projects smaller increases of 5 per cent in 2028 and 4 per cent in 2029. The proposed adjustments are intended to support reliable utility services, build financial reserves for future infrastructure replacement and reduce the risk of larger rate increases over time.
The waste, recycling and organics collection charge and the Eco Centre management fee are proposed to remain unchanged in 2027 at $26.65 and $5 per month, respectively.
The proposed rates are not final and will be considered alongside other budget priorities before council makes its decision.
Council is scheduled to consider adoption of the 2027 budget on Dec. 7. Residents can learn more about the proposed budget and utility rates at letstalkcochrane.ca/2027budget.
