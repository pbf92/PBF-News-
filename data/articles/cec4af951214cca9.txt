TITLE: GTA 6's Map Has a Useful Feature I've Only Just Noticed
OUTLET: Push Square

I’ve tallied over 300 hours in GTA 6 so far, and today I learned something new.
Forgive me if you already knew it, but I figured I’d share anyway as it never occurred to me in all my sessions so far.
One of the big things about this new game is that you can go inside a lot of buildings. Each is uniquely furnished, and it’s all pretty unprecedented for an open world game, to be honest.
However, not all houses can be entered, obviously.
For example, this house in Key Lento with the gnomes outside – I can’t go in:
Well, today I learned that the map actually acknowledges this.
There are light grey buildings and dark grey buildings, as shown in screenshot below:
So let’s go to that dark grey building next door and see if we can go inside, shall we? It turns out we can:
And it seems like the family living here are big sports fans:
What’s the learning, then? Well, it’s simple: light grey buildings on the map can’t be entered and dark grey buildings can. I literally had no idea about this until a few hours ago.
