TITLE: Husband who drugged and raped his wife was 'looking for some extra fun and games', jury hears
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

A husband who drugged and raped his wife said he was 'looking for some extra fun and games', a jury heard.
Jurors at the trial of nine men who are accused of committing sexual offences against his wife heard that the husband posted online to say he was ‘looking to try new stuff’. As the trial at Minshull Street Crown Court in Manchester entered its fourth week, one defendant changed some of his pleas to guilty, while remaining on trial for one allegation.
Robert Stewart admitted one count of conspiracy to administer a substance to the wife with intent, and two counts of rape committed against her on September 21, 2025. He continues to deny conspiring to rape her and remains on trial.

Another man, former pub landlord Tommy Golden, 59, of HMP Risley, appeared in a seperate hearing at Manchester Magistrates Court on Monday, and pleaded guilty to sexually assaulting the wife after attending her and her husband’s home on June 29, 2025. His case was sent to Minshull Street Crown Court for sentencing, which will take place at a later date.
The husband - a man in his 60s from Stockport - has admitted multiple offences, including rape and administering substances with intent to stupefy his wife so other men could engage in sexual activity with her.
The Manchester Evening News and other media organisations are not naming the man in order to protect his victim's anonymity. The nine men on trial deny the charges they face. Seven of the nine defendants have been described as 'contact offenders', who jurors heard visited the couple's home expecting her to be 'wasted' and 'out of it'.
It is alleged the 'contact offenders' are taxi driver Philip Wild, 59; recruitment agency worker Mohammed Sabir, 28; building supplies employee Jordan Wallace, 31; industrial cleaning firm worker Alan Keelan, 42; retiree Robert Stewart, 70; sheet metal worker Richard Townsend, 38; and senior paramedic Jonathan Kirk, 43.
The Crown alleges 'non-contact' offenders, who allegedly 'hid behind a screen' as they conspired to administer substances to the wife, are kitchen firm sales rep David Graves, 60; and self-employed mechanic Daniel Rayner, 42. Four other men - Keith Fotheringham, Karl Lindsay, Sean Peers and Graham Brougham - pleaded guilty at earlier hearings to various offences linked to the case.
Jurors heard about messages sent between the husband and an unnamed third party named in court as 'A' on an app named Session. On May 10, 2025, the husband said: "Hi. Hopefully this leads to you having some fun with [the wife].
The husband then sent two images of his wife, one of her clothed in an 'outdoor location', and a second with her on a bed, naked. "Drunk recently," the husband said. A said: "Wow I would love to slip those knickers to the side and slip my c*** inside as she is passed out." The husband said: "Hopefully we will be able to."
Then on May 12 the husband told A: "I have been looking for someone local for a while who I can trust." The husband added that he wanted to 'hopefully have them get their hands on [his wife] for real'. A said: "I 100pc want to meet. When can I have her.”
The husband said: "We have to plan it properly." Jurors were told about a chat between the husband and a second individual, named in court as 'PE', on September 8, 2025. PE asked the husband: "How's you and the f*** toy."
"She is well and totally unaware," the husband said. Jurors heard that the husband sent PE images of Mr Kirk's attendance at the house. Asking PE if he had saved the images of his wife, the husband said: "Please be discreet.”
"I have been collecting her for years and have never shared," PE said. "How many men have raped it now," PE said. "Just two," the husband said. "Lucky buggers," PE said.

The profile said 'looking for some extra fun and games'. It continued: "Want to see what I have been missing to see if it is as much fun as they say it is. I'm also looking to try new stuff. I want to try it all. Married guy looking for fun. Wife is hot and cold these days so will rarely be joining me.
"Wanting to try new and varied things. My ideal would be to find a genuine friendly couple for me to join them from time to time for some satisfying fun."
Jurors heard that Mr Sabir's Fabswingers account left a 'review' on the husband's account which said: "It was my first experience f****** someone's wife in front of them so thanks for that. Hope to meet again soon."
