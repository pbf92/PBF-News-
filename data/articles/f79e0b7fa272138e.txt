TITLE: As GTA VI Dominates the Sales Charts, Which Classic Grand Theft Auto Entry Is Your Favourite?
OUTLET: Time Extension

Grand Theft Auto VI had the biggest "entertainment launch" in history, recording 32.5 million sales and raking in an ungodly $3.79 billion USD on its first day of release.
It was an almost ordained turnout for the long-in-development sequel, success all but guaranteed regardless of controversies that might have - should have - threatened its launch. Such concerns likely never troubled developer Rockstar Games, because the continued success of GTA 6 is practically as inevitable as Thanos.
Though the long-running series had a more inauspicious debut, with DMA Design's original Grand Theft Auto being more a controversial curiosity than it was a critical success, the open-world crime sim has only gone from strength to strength. The true watershed moment came with the 2001 PS2 release of Grand Theft Auto III, one of the most influential and stylistically important titles of the 21st century.
The shift to 3D gameplay opened up a whole new world not only for GTA, but for gaming in general. While "sandbox" gaming certainly wasn't invented by GTA III, the smash-hit sequel definitively redrew the blueprint, leading to a slew of franchises that would adapt its third-person, open-world adventure for their own million-dollar series. Assassin's Creed, Far Cry, Like a Dragon, Cyberpunk 2077, Watch Dogs, Saints Row... even fantasy franchises The Witcher, Dark Souls, and Elden Ring, all pay a varying quantity of tribute to GTA's '00s redesign.
But while subsequent releases have seen a steady increase in financial success, each entry's style, setting, stories, features, and locations offer their own appeal. While some love the satirical nature of Grand Theft Auto V and its trio of mismatched protagonists, others think the series is yet to top Grand Theft Auto IV's grittier tale of immigrant Niko Bellic. Some players, myself included, have never left the vulgar, neon-washed shores of Grand Theft Auto: Vice City, while many GTA die-hards will forever raise a cup of hot coffee to the '90s vibes of Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas.
Wherever you fall on Rockstar's magnum opus, we'd like you to share with us your personal favourite Grand Theft Auto entry. Given the distinct nature of each release's characters, stories, and missions, we've also included expansions Grand Theft Auto IV: The Lost and Damned and Grand Theft Auto: The Ballad of Gay Tony, along with handheld offerings such as Grand Theft Auto: Chinatown Wars.
