TITLE: Pinterest open ad inventory to Microsoft Advertising, extends reach across 661 million users
OUTLET: Storyboard18

Pinterest has opened its advertising inventory to Microsoft Advertising through a custom integration, enabling eligible advertisers to extend their campaigns across the visual discovery platform without adopting new buying tools or changing their existing campaign workflows.
The integration will allow Microsoft Advertising campaigns to access Pinterest’s open-auction inventory across its home feed, search results and related Pins. The initial rollout will cover North America, Australia and New Zealand, with India excluded from the first phase.
The partnership brings Pinterest’s visual discovery inventory into Microsoft Advertising’s existing programmatic ecosystem, allowing advertisers to reach audiences across search-led and visual discovery environments through a common buying interface. Campaigns will continue to use familiar Microsoft Advertising tools and measurement workflows.
The development comes as Pinterest looks to expand monetisation opportunities across its growing user base and advertising inventory, while programmatic buying and automated campaign optimisation become increasingly important to its advertising business.
Contextual bidding connects search intent with visual discovery
Under the integration, Microsoft Advertising campaigns will access Pinterest inventory through a custom contextual bidding model. The model uses Pinterest search queries, contextual signals and first-party intent data to identify relevant advertising placements across the platform.
The system is powered by Pinterest Intelligence, the company's AI-driven understanding of user interests. It combines hundreds of billions of connections with visual-understanding technology and behavioural signals to determine the relevance of content and advertising placements.
Campaign delivery will operate through programmatic infrastructure, with demand extending beyond Microsoft's search business. Advertisers across Microsoft's media properties, including Bing, MSN, Xbox, Microsoft Casual Games and Microsoft Outlook, will also be able to contribute to the demand ecosystem.
For advertisers, the integration provides access to Pinterest's inventory through existing Microsoft buying infrastructure rather than requiring them to establish separate campaign operations on the platform. The arrangement also brings visual discovery placements into campaigns that may otherwise be concentrated across search and Microsoft's owned media properties.
Jason Fairchild, GM of Programmatic and Affiliate at Pinterest, said, "Now, Microsoft Advertising customers don't have to choose between streamlined buying and the environments they want to be in most."
Jason McKay, General Manager, Microsoft Advertising Global Partnerships, said eligible customers could "extend campaigns into Pinterest inventory using familiar buying and measurement workflows".
661 million monthly users and 80 billion searches underpin the opportunity
Pinterest's audience scale and search activity are central to the advertising opportunity. The platform reaches 661 million monthly active users globally and handles approximately 80 billion searches every month, according to the company. More than half of these searches have commercial intent, creating opportunities for advertisers to reach consumers during product discovery and consideration.
More than 96% of text searches on Pinterest are unbranded, indicating that users are often exploring categories, ideas and products without necessarily having settled on a particular brand. This provides advertisers an opportunity to enter the consumer journey before a purchase decision is made.
The integration also gives Microsoft advertisers access to a predominantly mobile audience engaging with visual content and discovery-led searches. By combining Microsoft's existing advertiser relationships and buying infrastructure with Pinterest's first-party intent signals, the partnership expands the range of environments available through eligible campaigns.
India, however, is not included in the initial rollout. Advertisers in the country using Microsoft Advertising will have to wait for further expansion of the integration before they can access Pinterest's inventory through this arrangement.
The partnership builds on Pinterest's broader programmatic advertising expansion, following the introduction of programmatic deals across Asia-Pacific and the Americas. The latest integration adds another route for external advertising demand to access its inventory.
Pinterest's advertising expansion also comes against the backdrop of its efforts to improve monetisation as advertising impressions grow faster than pricing. In the third quarter of 2026, the company reported a 17% year-on-year increase in advertising impressions, while advertising pricing declined 4%.
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Pinterest reported revenue of $1.174 billion, up 12% from $1.049 billion in the corresponding period last year. The company's advertising business continued to receive demand from consideration-led and performance-driven cost-per-click campaigns, followed by awareness advertising. Retail remained a key advertising contributor, while financial services, healthcare and travel were among the smaller but faster-growing verticals.
The company's latest financial performance indicates the importance of expanding advertiser demand and improving campaign returns as it scales its advertising inventory. The Microsoft integration adds another distribution channel for that inventory, with the potential to bring additional programmatic demand into Pinterest's advertising ecosystem.
