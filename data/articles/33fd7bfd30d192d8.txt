Sonic: Sonic burns remaining airdrop allocation - 15 Dec 2026
Sonic has scheduled a burn of the remaining airdrop allocation for 15 Dec 2026. The burn covers 91.7 million S, the full balance still set aside for distribution to users through the airdrop.
Destroying those tokens will cut the total supply of S by about 2%. Once the burn is complete, those tokens will no longer be able to enter circulation.
