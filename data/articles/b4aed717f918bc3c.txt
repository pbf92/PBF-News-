JJ Gabriel sparks concern in Spain with ex-Man United star on verge of joining new club
JJ Gabriel looks set to join Real Madrid after controversially announcing his departure from Manchester United.
JJ Gabriel has raised eyebrows in Spain after announcing his departure from Manchester United. The 16-year-old, who’s expected to join Real Madrid, confirmed his decision to leave in a video on social media earlier this week, where he thanked staff and teammates for their support.
The Manchester Evening News understands that higher-ups at Old Trafford were disappointed to be caught off guard by the Instagram post. The Reds had made multiple attempts to contact Gabriel and his camp beforehand to check on his welfare.
In fact, United have been unable to establish contact with the teenager’s family since September, when they received a request on his behalf to leave. The Reds are understood to be ready to take legal action, should evidence of inducement from Los Blancos come to light.
Gabriel, who has not played a competitive game for the first team, remains registered to United, yet he’s been training with an individual coach; first in Castelldefels, Barcelona, and now in the Spanish capital in anticipation of a transfer to Real Madrid.

The saga has raised eyebrows in Spain, with one journalist describing his latest post on social media as ‘unbelievable’. During an episode of the sports radio show El Partidazo de Cope, Juanma Castano said: "I say this with the slight fear that this kid might go on to become the greatest player of all time.
"But it’s just unbelievable. This lad hasn’t played a single minute for Manchester United. Yet he’s said goodbye as if he were a United legend.
"[You’d expect it from] someone leaving the club, the sort of player whose departure would have people in Manchester in tears. [But he is] a 16-year-old kid who hasn’t even made his debut. He’s a prospect."
Manolo Lama echoed the sentiment. He replied: "We in the media are largely to blame for this. Every week I see Real Madrid players, kids of 14 or 15, some of whom make it and others don't, but we usually try to give them much more value than they deserve.
"Take any newspaper. Every day you find the diamond from who-knows-where, the pearl from who-knows-what."
On El Chiringuito TV, one of the most famous late-night football talk shows in Spain, they joked that Gabriel celebrated his birthday with a half-and-half Barcelona-Real Madrid cake, having been linked to both La Liga clubs.
As for Marca, a daily sports newspaper and digital news outlet, they took a little jab at United fans before revelling in the prospect of such a controversial transfer.
They wrote: "In England, they're tearing their hair out over the young striker's departure, and given the circumstances, they're trying to cash in on what's considered the best prospect to emerge in the country in recent years.
"The presence of Gabriel, who has been training alone in Spain for several weeks awaiting confirmation of his signing for Real Madrid, has aroused great excitement at the club's training ground, where they are well aware of the media frenzy surrounding both the player's departure from Manchester United and his arrival at the club."
