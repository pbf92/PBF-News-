TITLE: A new Sony patent reveals a graphics toggle button on the PS6's DualSense 2 controller.
OUTLET: GameGPU

Sony Interactive Entertainment has published a new patent application revealing additional functionality for the upcoming DualSense 2 game controller for the PlayStation 6 console. Document numbered US 2026/0364447 A1 describes the addition of a dedicated button on the controller's body, designed for instantly switching graphics modes during gameplay. This innovation will eliminate the need for users to pause the game and access system settings to adjust the balance between visual quality and frame rate.
The proposed concept is based on the technology of adaptive visual quality (Adaptive Visual Quality), which can dynamically scale multiple rendering parameters in real time. Unlike traditional dynamic resolution, Sony's system analyzes the pixel differences between adjacent frames and, if necessary, smoothly adjusts texture resolution, filtering, shader levels, and anti-aliasing. In quiet scenes, the image remains as sharp as possible, and when the load increases sharply in fast-paced battles, detail is seamlessly reduced to maintain stable performance, with the transition smoothed out over several dozen frames without any sudden jumps.
In addition to a hardware graphics mode switch, earlier patents from the company also indicated that the next-generation gamepad would feature a built-in touchscreen and an improved haptic feedback system. Combined with PSSR neural network scaling technology, the new adaptive graphics control system is designed to help the PlayStation 6 achieve its stated benchmark of 4K at 120 frames per second. However, Sony has not yet officially announced the console itself or the DualSense 2 controller, so the features described in the patent remain at the engineering concept stage.
