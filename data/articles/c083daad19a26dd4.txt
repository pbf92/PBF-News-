TITLE: Newcastle chiefs issue not for sale stance on Lewis Hall following Manchester United interest
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Newcastle United have made clear that Lewis Hall is not for sale this January, amidst expected interest from Manchester United.
Hall is well liked at Old Trafford, and a move was considered in the summer, but nothing materialised.
The young left back has since signed a new contract at Newcastle, and now Toon chiefs have issued a hands-off stance.
One source described Hall as "a top young player", before indicating that he is someone Matthias Jaissle wants to build his team around.
Given Hall's performances for both Newcastle and England this season, it is obvious why Manchester United will maintain an interest in him. Most recently, he put in a star display for the Three Lions as they defeated world champions Spain in Madrid.
Hayters TV spoke to Hall following England's win over Croatia last week, with Hall adamant that "there's still heavy competition for the left back spot" under Thomas Tuchel.
He did, however, hope that the way he played in that match (and likely the Spain clash too) will have helped his case.
On Newcastle, Hall admitted that it has been "a weird season so far", with the Magpies struggling to find consistent form.
Hall has been one of their standout performers, and despite the struggles, was confident that Newcastle's young squad will only "get better and better".
If his personal form continues, it is likely that the Red Devils will try to test the waters in January, but Newcastle have today drawn their line in the sand, that Hall is not for sale.
