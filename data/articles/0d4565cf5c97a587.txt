TITLE: Bird-watching in 'GTA 6' has the seal of approval from America's top birders
OUTLET: Business Insider

"Grand Theft Auto 6" players can steal cars, run drugs, and evade the police. One player had another priority: finding every bird.
Birding expert Nicholas Lund tracked down all 60 species listed in the game's bird catalog, searching wetlands, beaches, forests, a farm, and a zoo across Rockstar Games' fictionalized Florida.
His verdict? "GTA 6" offers what feels like "the most accurate version of modern birding ever put to screen," he wrote in "Audubon," the magazine of the National Audubon Society.
Named for renowned 19th-century ornithologist John James Audubon, the society is the premier birding organization in the US and has a history spanning over 120 years.
The game's wildlife has attracted attention from birders as well as GTA fans. Ahead of its release, a post in Reddit's r/birding community drew excited replies from players who planned to catalog birds between missions.
"I think the crime sprees are going to have to wait," the original poster wrote. Another responded: "I have found my people. Hopefully there’s an achievement for finding them all."
In "GTA 6", players can use binoculars to "study" birds and add them to a progress menu. Lund wrote in "Audubon" that he approached the search much as he would in real life, looking in different habitats. This led to finding gulls and pelicans near the water, wading birds in wetlands, and smaller birds farther north.
He found 50 species during roughly eight hours of play after launch. The following morning, he completed the list by spotting an American Barn Owl on a water tank.
Rockstar advertised 62 bird species before launch. Lund said the bird progress screen listed 60, with several others appearing elsewhere in the game.
He noticed inaccuracies, including the appearance of endangered Whooping Cranes more often than they would in the real world. But he praised the familiar landscapes and the traffic, noise, and litter that accompanied the birdsong.
The hunt is one of several ways players are spending time in the game's expansive world. Some players have chosen to do virtual home repairs or even doomscroll. Sony said players logged 683 million hours on PlayStation 5 in the game's first four days.
After completing his list, Lund returned to a more familiar GTA pursuit: He drove his car off a cliff into the ocean.
