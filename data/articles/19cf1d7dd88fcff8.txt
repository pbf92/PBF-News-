TITLE: Amazon blocks third-party Victoria's Secret sellers from November 26
OUTLET: PPC Land

Amazon will remove Victoria's Secret listings held by third-party sellers on Amazon.com on November 26, 2026, leaving the brand's products available only from sellers that hold the Brand Representative role for Victoria's Secret. The terms are set out in a notice signed by Amazon Services, which Ephraim Rosenberg, founder of ASGTG, who works on marketplace compliance and seller advocacy, posted on LinkedIn today.
In Short
Amazon is pulling every Victoria's Secret listing that belongs to an independent seller on Amazon.com on November 26, 2026, and only accounts holding the brand's Brand Representative role will keep selling it. The cutoff falls on Thanksgiving Day, one day before Black Friday, and it applies a No Wholesale Distribution rule that sellers first reported receiving in October to a named household brand. Affected sellers have until the cutoff to sell their remaining units or delete empty listings, and Amazon says the removals will not count against their Account Health Rating.
What the notice says
The notice carries a blunt headline: "Victoria's Secret listings to be removed 2026-11-26 from Amazon.com." Beneath it, a second line spells out the scope: "Starting 2026-11-26, Victoria's Secret is no longer eligible for third party sellers to list on Amazon.com."
The body is short. "Your Victoria's Secret listings on Amazon.com will be removed on 2026-11-26," it reads. "These products will continue to be available in the Amazon store, but only from sellers with the Brand Representative role for Victoria's Secret."
Amazon gives one reason. "We are taking this action because Victoria's Secret distributes products only through direct-to-consumer channels and does not sell through wholesalers, distributors, or third-party resellers," according to the notice. The phrase direct-to-consumer does a lot of work here. It is the whole of Amazon's stated rationale for removing every seller outside the brand's own accounts, regardless of where those sellers bought their stock.
The version Rosenberg shared shows no send date. Only the removal date is fixed. The notice also points sellers to a Seller Central help page titled "Resolve No Wholesale Distribution (NWD) errors 5465 and 6045," a page PPC Land could not find in any public version. Amazon's public guide to listing errors, published on February 19, 2025, explains error 5461, which signals that a seller lacks permission to create new product pages for a brand, but does not cover 5465 or 6045.
How the removal works, according to the notice
According to Amazon Services, the process has five parts.
- Removal date. Victoria's Secret listings held by sellers without the Brand Representative role are removed on November 26, 2026 (written as 2026-11-26 throughout the notice).
- Continued availability. The products stay in the Amazon store, offered only by sellers holding the Brand Representative role for Victoria's Secret.
- Steps before the deadline. Sellers with existing Victoria's Secret inventory are told to "take one or more of the following steps before 2026-11-26": sell the remaining inventory, or delete Victoria's Secret SKUs that have no inventory.
- No action. If a seller does nothing, the listings are removed "starting 2026-11-26."
- Account records. "SKUs removed due to NWD restrictions do not impact your Account Health Rating," the notice states. "They will appear in Manage Products and Account Health pages, until deleted." Questions go to Amazon's Seller Assistant or to Selling Partner Support.
Two options are listed, not three. The notice does not mention removal orders, and it says nothing about what happens to Victoria's Secret units still sitting in Fulfillment by Amazon warehouses after the cutoff. For a seller with stock in transit or unsold on November 26, that silence is the open question.
Who keeps selling: the Brand Representative role
The notice turns on a single label. Amazon's Brand Registry splits its roles into two families, according to a guide Amazon published on April 23, 2025: protection roles, which govern a brand's rights and enforcement tools, and selling roles, which govern a seller's relationship with a brand. Amazon's FAQ on the guide says a selling account can hold only one of the two selling roles.
Source: Amazon, "Guide to Brand Registry roles," April 23, 2025. ChannelX reported on August 7, 2025 that brand administrators could also assign selling roles to Vendor Central accounts.
Read against those definitions, the notice's wording is narrow. It names only the Brand Representative role, which Amazon reserves for sellers employed by the brand itself. A seller holding the Reseller role, the label Amazon built for authorized outside sellers, is not named among those who keep selling. The practical effect is that Victoria's Secret's own selling operation becomes the only route for the brand on Amazon.com.
According to ChannelX, the Brand Representative role carries A+ Content, Amazon Brand Analytics, Amazon Vine, Brand Stores, Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, Subscribe and Save and virtual bundles, among other tools. The Reseller role carries a smaller set, including A+ Content, Brand Stores, product videos and the sponsored ad formats.
The role has been picking up weight all year. In February, PPC Land reported that Amazon would restrict manufacturer barcodes for FBA to Brand Representative accounts from March 31, 2026, forcing everyone else, authorized resellers included, onto Amazon's own FNSKU labels. In September, Amazon set January 11, 2027 as the date from which most bundles must be packaged by the original manufacturer or brand, with noncompliant listings deactivated.
An October precedent
Victoria's Secret is not the first brand to receive this treatment. In mid-October, sellers began posting about NWD notices on Amazon's Seller Forums and in trade publications. According to EcomCrew, which published its account on October 18, 2026, sellers reported receiving the notices on October 14, with listing restrictions threatened later that month. Forum threads cited a removal date of October 27, 2026.
Those earlier notices circulated mostly without brand names. The wording sellers posted followed the same template as the Victoria's Secret notice, keeping products available only to sellers with the Brand Representative role. According to AMZ123, a Chinese-language seller publication that compiled reports on October 20, the October notices listed three options before the deadline: sell existing stock, create removal orders for unsold stock, or delete SKUs with no stock. The Victoria's Secret notice drops the removal order line.
The October round also caught brand owners. EcomCrew reported that some trademark holders with completed Brand Registry enrollment received the same warning, apparently because their selling accounts did not hold the Brand Representative role. On the Seller Forums, a moderator account named Roberto_Amazon told sellers that accounts with an active Brand Representative role were not affected by the NWD removal date, and another moderator, Christine_Amazon, said one brand owner's case had been escalated internally. No resolution was posted in the threads.
What Victoria's Secret itself says about its channels
Amazon's stated reason, that Victoria's Secret "does not sell through wholesalers, distributors, or third-party resellers," is broader than the way the company describes its own business.
Victoria's Secret first opened on Amazon in April 2022 with a storefront for Victoria's Secret Beauty and PINK Beauty. According to Retail Dive, which reported the launch on April 29, 2022, about 120 products went on sale, eligible for Prime delivery. eMarketer described it as the first time the brand had sold through any third-party retailer. In June 2023, according to FashionUnited, Victoria's Secret & Co. added lingerie, swimwear and loungewear for Victoria's Secret and PINK to Amazon Fashion, the first time those categories were sold through a retail partner in North America.
The company's annual report for the fiscal year ended January 31, 2026 lists its North American channels as stores, digital websites, mobile applications and third-party digital marketplaces, and says the company uses leading digital marketplaces to extend its global reach. Outside North America, according to the same filing, more than 560 partner stores in about 70 countries operate under franchise, license and wholesale arrangements, alongside wholesale travel retail operations. At January 31, 2026, the filing counts 562 international partner-operated stores, up from 505 a year earlier.
Source: Victoria's Secret & Co. Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended January 31, 2026. The total is a PPC Land calculation.
None of this contradicts Amazon's action on Amazon.com. The company's wholesale and franchise arrangements sit outside North America, and its own marketplace selling is precisely the channel the notice preserves. But the notice's sentence about wholesalers is a general claim, and the company's filing describes a business that uses wholesale partners in dozens of countries.
Rosenberg's reading
Rosenberg's post, presented here as his own view, summarizes the notice and adds a short assessment. "It appears Victoria's Secret was already fairly restricted, but this makes the policy more explicit," he wrote. He closed with a question to other sellers: "Is anyone currently selling this on Amazon?"
His summary of the obligations was close to Amazon's text. "Affected sellers are being advised to sell through remaining inventory before the deadline," he wrote, adding that "Amazon says these removals will not impact Account Health Ratings."
Rosenberg has been tracking this ground for months. In May, he was among the sellers who questioned whether Amazon was indirectly enforcing brands' private distribution systems through gating, letters of authorization and approval restrictions, in PPC Land's coverage of Amazon's seller approval rules and the debate over distribution control. Earlier this month, he was the person who made public Amazon's FBA lead time estimates, under which a five-day shipment can take 12 days at peak.
The timing: Thanksgiving, inside Black Friday Week
November 26, 2026 is a Thursday, and it is Thanksgiving Day in the United States. The removal therefore lands one day before Black Friday and in the middle of Amazon's Black Friday Week, which runs from November 19 to November 30, with Cyber Monday on the final day.
The calendar is unforgiving for resellers. Amazon's inbound cutoffs for Black Friday and Cyber Monday stock fell in October, according to the holiday 2026 seller terms Amazon set in July: October 14 for Amazon Warehousing and Distribution, October 21 for FBA shipments with minimal splits and October 28 for Amazon-optimized splits. A seller that sent Victoria's Secret stock to Amazon for the holidays has had it in the network for weeks. Counted from today, when the notice became public, the window to sell through it is three days, a PPC Land calculation. When individual sellers received the email is not known.
Why this matters for brands, sellers and advertisers
For brand owners, the notice shows a lever that works differently from the approval and gating systems PPC Land has covered this year. Under gating, a reseller can still qualify by producing invoices or a letter of authorization. Under the No Wholesale Distribution template, Amazon removes the listings outright and names the role that survives. A brand that distributes only through its own channels, or that Amazon classifies that way, ends up with detail pages on which other sellers' offers no longer appear.
