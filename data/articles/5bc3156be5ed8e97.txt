TITLE: When is the Euro 2028 qualifying draw?
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
The Euro 2028 qualifying draw will take place on Sunday, 6 December at 17:00 GMT at the International Convention Centre in Belfast.
It will form the twelve groups that make up the qualifying group stage; six containing four teams and six containing five teams.
All 54 Uefa member associations will enter qualifying at the group stage.
Despite hosting the 2028 tournament, the UK home nations and the Republic of Ireland must also participate in qualifying rounds.
How does the draw work?
The 54 teams have been divided into five pots based on their interim overall ranking in the 2026-27 Nations League following the conclusion of the league phase.
Pots one to four contain 12 teams, and pot five contains six teams.
The teams will then be drawn into the 12 qualifying groups, with the four host teams, as well as Northern Ireland, drawn into different groups.
The four winners of the Nations League quarter-finals must be drawn into a group containing four teams.
Twenty teams, made up of 12 group winners and eight best runners-up, will advance directly to the tournament proper. The eight highest-ranking runners-up will be based on the overall qualifying rankings.
If host nations fail to win or are runners-up in their group, there will be two spaces in the tournament proper reserved for the best-ranked nations at the end of the group stage, regardless of how many other host nation teams have qualified.
As a result, the final number of teams in the tournament proper will be between 20 and 22 nations, depending on the performance of the host nations.
How can teams qualify for Euro 2028 via the Nations League?
There are also qualifying routes available through the 2026-27 Nations League.
Nations League final rankings will also determine the seeding of nations for Euro 2028 qualification.
You can read more on how the process works here.
What are the qualifying pots?
- Pot One: England, Portugal, France, Germany, Spain, Belgium, Netherlands, Norway, Greece, Italy, Denmark, Croatia
- Pot Two: Wales, Serbia, Turkey, Czech Republic, Switzerland, Sweden, Austria, Northern Ireland, Slovenia, Bosnia-Herzegovina, Hungary, Republic of Ireland
- Pot Three: Ukraine, Scotland, Kosovo, Poland, Israel, Romania, Georgia, North Macedonia, Montenegro, Albania, Iceland, Slovakia
- Pot Four: Estonia, Belarus, Faroe Islands, Cyprus, Finland, Moldova, Armenia, Bulgaria, Kazakhstan, Luxembourg, Latvia, San Marino
- Pot Five: Malta, Azerbaijan, Lithuania, Andorra, Gibraltar, Liechtenstein
When are the Euro 2028 qualifying fixtures?
- Matchday one: 26 March 2027
- Matchday two: 29 March 2027
- Matchday three: 11-12 June 2027
- Matchday four: 14-15 June 2027
- Matchday five: 23-25 September 2027
- Matchday six: 26-28 September 2027
- Matchday seven: 30 September-2 October 2027
- Matchday eight: 3-5 October 2027
- Matchday nine: 11-13 November 2027
- Matchday 10: 14-16 November 2027
- Play-offs: 23-28 March 2028
Where will the host nations play if they qualify?
If the host nations and/or Northern Ireland qualify for the tournament proper, they will have dedicated slots at stadiums:
- England (Group B): Etihad Stadium (one match), Wembley Stadium (two matches)
- Northern Ireland (Group D): Tottenham Hotspur Stadium (one match), Villa Park (one match), Wembley Stadium (one match)
- Republic of Ireland (Group E): Dublin Arena (three matches)
- Scotland (Group F): Hampden Park (three matches)
- Wales (Group A): National Stadium of Wales (three matches)
If the hosts and/or Northern Ireland qualify via the play-offs, they will still be assigned to their designated group, but will not play all their group matches in the locations mentioned above.
