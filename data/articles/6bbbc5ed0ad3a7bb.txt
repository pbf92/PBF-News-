TITLE: GTA 6 PC Version Gets Official Release Date Update
OUTLET: ScreenRant

Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here, and for the most part, it seems to be delivering on all the fronts that people were hoping for over these last few years. It's about as immersive an experience as any developer has managed to create for players, and it's visually stunning, with an attention to detail that would basically be impossible for any other game to replicate given the budget.
All of that said, there is a big portion of gamers out there who aren't able to get in on the fun for the time being, as GTA VI is currently only available on Xbox and PlayStation. A PC release is an inevitability, but Rockstar has remained relatively tight-lipped about what exactly their plans are in that regard up to this point.
Now, a new update has emerged, and it's very good news for those who were hoping to play the game on PC before long.
Over on Reddit, one user shared a post from GamesBeat citing inside sources that explicitly state that Rockstar is planning to release the PC version of GTA VI in the first three months of 2027. That's considerably sooner than many industry experts were predicting, though GTA V's PC release was pushed several times, so don't be surprised if that happens again.
Underneath the post, the community was keen to share their thoughts on the PC release, and it's safe to say that there is a fair amount of skepticism that they'll be able to pull it off so quickly.
"This feels like a too good to be true scenario. Rockstar is famous for delaying their PC releases. If this said Q1 2028 I'd believe it," one comment read.
"I think it would make sense considering the circumstances: Low availability of consoles for the foreseeable future and the rapid progress of PS5 emulation etc. at the same time could pose a real threat. Due to the console life cycles, this time, they would probably have to delay the PC version for too long for a combined release with a new console generation," another added.
"It doesn't make sense from a financial standpoint. If any announcement were made, they would wait AFTER the sales for the console release so they can have people double dip and buy their game twice. A Q1 release date would be too soon and a lot of PC players would just wait... thus, RS would be losing a lot of sales. It's a very weak rumor," a third said.
Whatever launch plan Rockstar does decide to go with for the PC release of GTA VI, it's difficult to see how it won't be an enormous commercial success on the platform regardless. The console figures are already proving to be astronomical at this early stage, and there are considerably more PC players worldwide who will all have the opportunity to play the game. Add whatever they've got planned for GTA Online into the mix, and the developer likely won't be particularly worried about their capital moving forward.
