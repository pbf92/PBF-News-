TITLE: Man Utd to resurrect Hall interest - Tuesday's gossip
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
Manchester United may struggle to prise defender Lewis Hall away from Newcastle, Arsenal midfielder Martin Zubimendi is pondering his future, Chelsea eye double swoop for Barcelona duo.
Newcastle will not entertain bids for Lewis Hall, 22, in January with Manchester United potentially resurrecting their interest in the England left-back. (Telegraph - subscription required), external
Arsenal midfielder Martin Zubimendi is open to leaving the Gunners because of a lack of first-team opportunities with Atletico Madrid and Chelsea interested in the 27-year-old Spaniard. (AS - in Spanish), external
Chelsea have set their sights on a January double swoop for Barcelona's Spain midfielder Fermin Lopez, 23, and France defender Jules Kounde, 28, with a £150m offer to tempt the Catalan club into selling. (El Nacional - in Catalan), external
Crystal Palace's Senegal winger Ismaila Sarr, 28, is keeping his options open before the January transfer window after little progress was made on talks over a new contract at Selhurst Park. (Football Insider), external
Real Madrid have been keeping a close eye on Brighton this season, with Croatia defender Luka Vuskovic, 19, and Spanish midfielder Chema Andres, 21, among the players to have caught their attention. (Teamtalk), external
Bayern Munich goalkeeping prodigy Leonard Prescott, 17, looks increasingly likely to leave the Bundesliga club after contract talks stalled with Chelsea, Arsenal, Brighton and Aston Villa among those interested in the New York-born Germany youth international. (Sky Sports Germany - in German), external
Leeds have asked Stuttgart about the possibility of a January deal for 22-year-old Morocco midfielder Bilal El Khannouss. (Teamtalk), external
Barcelona remain interested in Manchester City's Croatia defender Josko Gvardiol, 24, but do not think a deal will be possible. (AS - in Spanish), external
Newcastle are preparing to compete with Liverpool and several other Premier League clubs for the signature of Lens' 21-year-old French defender Ismaelo Ganiou. (Football Insider), external
Mauricio Pochettino wasn't the only man contacted by Manchester United to replace Michael Carrick as manager, with former France boss Didier Deschamps also spoken to. (Talksport), external
Como boss Cesc Fabregas was also discussed by Manchester United's hierarchy but they did not get as far as formally approaching the former Arsenal midfielder. (The Athletic - subscription required), external
Monaco's 25-year-old American striker Folarin Balogun, whose deadline-day move to Everton collapsed, is in talks with AC Milan and Juventus over a January deal. (Calciomercato - in Italian), external
Tottenham are prepared to let Richarlison leave for between £10m-£15m during the January transfer window, with Trabzonspor interested in signing the 29-year-old Brazilian forward. (Teamtalk), external
