SA analyst: Wall Street’s dramatic rebound masks mounting market risks
Wall Street staged a dramatic late-session recovery Wednesday after heavy early losses, though persistent volatility across artificial intelligence stocks and renewed inflation concerns kept investors on edge.
Seeking Alpha analyst Daniel Jones described the November 25 trading session as “a wild day,” noting that the Dow Jones Industrial Average DJI had fallen roughly 1,100 points, or -2.2%, while the Nasdaq Composite COMP plunged as much as -4.6% and the S&P 500 (SP500) dropped -3.2%.
The indexes sharply pared those declines in afternoon trading. The Dow closed +0.3%, the S&P 500 ended -0.2%, and the Nasdaq Composite COMP concluded -0.6%.
Jones said the rebound was difficult to reconcile with ongoing weakness in AI shares, including Nvidia, which had fallen -5.3% Wednesday and stood roughly 40% below its 52-week high. He also warned that mounting AI debt could threaten the sector.
He also pointed to October inflation of 3.8%, with core prices up 3.3%, exceeding forecasts, alongside newly announced targeted tariffs affecting more than a dozen countries.
