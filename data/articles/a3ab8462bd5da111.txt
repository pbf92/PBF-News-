TITLE: GTA VI Nominated For Game of the Year, Has Most Nominations
OUTLET: RockstarINTEL

GTA VI has been nominated for Game of the Year at The Game Awards for this year. This is an incredible moment given the game has not even been reviewed or released yet. Reviews are expected to drop tomorrow alongside the game unlocking in New Zealand.
The fact that it has received the most nominations also shows that the game is clearly going to get rave reviews from critics. Plus, we now have confirmation Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos in the game.
On Tuesday, The Game Awards and Geoff Keighley held a livestream revealing the nominees across more than 30 categories. GTA VI has been nominated in multiple categories. It is up for winning:
- Game of the Year
- Best Art Direction
- Best Narrative
- Best Audio Design
- Best Score & Music
- Best Performance (Manni L. Perez)
- Best Action/Adventure Game
- Best Game Direction
- Game of the Year
NoPixel V x GTA Online was also nominated for Best User Creation; a new category.
Grand Theft Auto VI won “Most Anticipated Game” at The Game Awards for the past two consecutive years. This is due to the game being delayed multiple times. Also, GTA Online was nominated for the Player’s Voice award before. Plus, GTA V won Game of the Year and Best Soundtrack in 2013 during the previous iteration of The Game Awards.
Over thirty categories are part of this year’s awards show which takes place on Thursday, December 10th. It will be live on the official YouTube and Twitch channels from 4:30pm PT, 7:30pm ET, and Dec 11th 12:30am GMT. Fans have until 6pm PT on December 9th to vote which can be done here. Both the fan vote and a jury vote are combined to find the winner.
The jury is made up of over a hundred ” jury of over 100 leading media and influencer outlets“. These include websites such as Game Informer, IGN, Variety, Wired, Eurogamer and more spanning countries worldwide.
Rockstar Games are actually on the company’s advisory board. It is made up of various members who “help guide and advance the mission of the game awards”. They are not part of the nomination or voting process however.
