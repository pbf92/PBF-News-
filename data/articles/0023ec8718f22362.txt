TITLE: Sporting vs Manchester United: Bruno Fernandes Returns to Lisbon
OUTLET: beIN SPORTS

Sporting vs Manchester United: Date, Time and Everything You Need to Know About the Champions League Clash
- Bruno Fernandes returns to the stadium where he starred before joining Manchester United, now under Mauricio Pochettino and facing a Sporting side in good European form.
Sporting CP will host Manchester United on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, at 3:00 PM ET at the Estádio José Alvalade in Lisbon, Portugal, on Matchday 5 of the UEFA Champions League league phase. Polish referee Szymon Marciniak will officiate the match.
The two clubs will meet for the first time since the 2007-2008 season, when Manchester United won both group-stage encounters thanks to decisive goals from Cristiano Ronaldo.
How Sporting Comes Into the Match
Sporting enters Matchday 5 in 10th place in the Champions League standings with nine points, having recorded three wins, no draws and one defeat. The Portuguese side defeated Galatasaray and Lens, lost to LASK and recorded a 1-0 victory over Shakhtar Donetsk in its most recent European outing.
Paulo Fonseca has won his first two matches in charge of Sporting since replacing Rui Borges in early November. The Lisbon side is coming off a 4-0 victory over União da Serra in the Portuguese Cup, while Zeno Debast and Nuno Santos are injury doubts for the clash against Manchester United.
How Manchester United Comes Into the Match
The Red Devils enter Matchday 5 in 17th place in the Champions League standings with six points, following two wins, no draws and two defeats. In their most recent European outing, they came back from two goals down to defeat Roma 3-2 at Old Trafford.
Mauricio Pochettino began his tenure as Manchester United manager with a 4-0 defeat to Liverpool, leaving the team second from bottom in the Premier League. Matheus Cunha will miss Pochettino's European debut through suspension following his red card against Roma, although Luke Shaw has returned to training.
Players to Watch
Luis Suárez
The Colombian striker continues to showcase his goalscoring ability both in Liga Portugal and in Europe. Luis Suárez has scored four goals in four Champions League appearances this season and, with nine goals since joining Sporting, has already become the club's all-time leading scorer in the competition proper. His current form poses a serious threat to Manchester United's defense.
Bruno Fernandes
The match will have special significance for Bruno Fernandes, who made 137 appearances and scored 64 goals for Sporting before joining Manchester United in January 2020. The Red Devils captain will be crucial in creating attacking opportunities as he looks to secure a victory at the stadium where he established himself as one of Portuguese soccer's biggest stars.
Final Assessment
Sporting enters the match in better form and with the backing of its home crowd. The Portuguese side has won six of its last eight Champions League home matches and has scored in 22 consecutive group-stage or league-phase matches, a record for a Portuguese club.
Manchester United, meanwhile, needs to address its defensive problems and end a six-match winless run away from home in the Champions League. A victory in Lisbon would bring the Red Devils level on points with Sporting and significantly improve their qualification prospects.
Three Keys to Sporting vs Manchester United
- The managerial matchup: Paulo Fonseca and Mauricio Pochettino will make their Champions League debuts with their respective clubs.
- The Colombian threat: Luis Suárez will look to exploit Manchester United's defensive problems and add to his impressive goalscoring record.
- A special homecoming: Bruno Fernandes returns to the José Alvalade to face the club that propelled him to the elite of European soccer.
