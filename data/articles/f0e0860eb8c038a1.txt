TITLE: 'You could hear the building moving, and you realised it was going to go'
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

'You could hear the building moving, and you realised it was going to go'
'We got out, and ten seconds later, it collapsed'
The neighbours of a collapsed building in Bolton have told of the 'scary' moment the building began to collapse around ten seconds after they evacuated.
Greater Manchester Fire and Rescue Service were called to the scene on Queensgate off Chorley New Road in the town on yesterday afternoon (November 18) following reports of a collapsed building.
Photos from the scene showed the front wall of a terraced property buckling forward, with the first floor above largely having given way.
Firefighters evacuated residents from the building as a precaution, GMFRS said, but no-one was trapped inside and no injuries were reported.
Neighbouring salon Classique Hair Design is currently still standing, but owners Trisha Sharples and Angela Runciman have not been able to return to their business since the collapse happened.
"We are a bit stuck in limbo," Trisha told the M.E.N. "It is still not safe enough for a building inspector to go in and examine it.
"We have two tenants above the salon that have essentially been evicted from their flats. Two young lads who have got nothing but their phones on them, not even their work uniforms, and have had to go and stay with family.
"We had to knock on and get them out, and we've spent all day rearranging appointments for the rest of the week. A customer had to go home with her hair still wet."
Trisha said that building work had been going on at the house since Monday (November 17). "The builders came in at about 3pm and told us to come and look at the wall where there were cracks starting to form.

"Just as we came out, the cracks got bigger, and you could hear the building moving, and you realised it was going to go.
"It was a close shave too. We got out and ten seconds later it collapsed, there was glass and bricks and dust. It was scary, we thought the whole salon was going to go down."
Trisha said building control officers from Bolton Council were at the scene yesterday evening 'removing pieces' from the house in apparent efforts to secure the area.
"We've been here for 33 years," Trisha said. "I want the tenants to be back in their homes and get the salon back open. Four of us work here and we're out of work for the time being."
A spokesperson for Bolton Council said: "Building Control have had no prior involvement with the property, had not received any applications for building works at the property, nor had any record of concerns being raised about works at the site before the collapse.
“Following the incident, Building Control attended the property to oversee the emergency works that were carried out to make the area safe . We continue to work with the owners of the affected properties on the next steps.
“We have been informed that Greater Manchester Fire and Rescue Service evacuated the affected properties before Building Control arrived on site.”
