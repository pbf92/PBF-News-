TITLE: GTA 6 has 10 tattoo parlours you don't need the Ultimate Edition to visit
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

One of the fan-favourite features of the Grand Theft Auto series is making its characters your own. From changing Tommy Vercetti's outfits in GTA Vice City to Rockstar Games blowing character customisation wide open in GTA San Andreas, it's a feature that has evolved over the years and has arguably reached its peak in GTA 6.
Whether it's keeping Jason and Lucia in shape, dressing them in the finest clothes money can buy, giving them a questionable haircut or covering them in tattoos, there's plenty of customisation on offer. Sure, it might cost a pretty penny, but it's all money well spent, right?
There are 11 tattoo parlours scattered throughout the fictional state of Leonida, each specialising in different styles of ink. While one is exclusive to owners of the GTA VI Ultimate Edition, thankfully, the other 10 are accessible regardless of which edition you own. So, here's where to find every tattoo parlour in Grand Theft Auto VI and what kind of ink you can get at each location.
All GTA 6 tattoo parlour locations and tattoo styles
Depending on what kind of ink you're after, it's worth exploring Leonida rather than sticking to your nearest tattoo parlour. Whether you're a fan of Japanese anime, prefer something more traditional or simply fancy some lettering, there's plenty to choose from.

Below are all 10 tattoo parlours in GTA VI that you can visit without owning the Ultimate Edition, where to find them and the styles of ink they offer.
Mariana County
- The Inked Mermaid (Key Lento): American Traditional – classic designs with bold outlines and vibrant colours.
Vice-Dale County
- Layin’ Ink (Hamlet): Black & Gray – ideal for those who prefer monochrome tattoos with detailed shading.
- Dominion Ink (Downtown Vice City): Anime – perfect for fans of Japanese animation.
- Hyper Ink (Little Cuba): Illustrative – artistic designs inspired by drawings and illustrations.
- Cross My Heart Tattoos (Crosstown): New School – bold, colourful and often exaggerated designs.
- Inkomplete Tattoo Co. (Rockridge): Old School – classic tattoo designs with bold outlines.
- Twa Ponya Tattoo (La Perle): Neo-Traditional – a modern twist on traditional tattoo artwork.
- Black Spot Tattoo (Ocean Beach): Geometric & Blackwork – intricate patterns, shapes and bold black ink.
Kelly County
- Blood Sugar Tattoo (Ambrosia): Lettering – tattoos that focus on words and stylised typography.
- Freedom Tattoo (Port Gellhorn): Stick & Poke – designs inspired by traditional hand-poked tattoos.
If you're looking to get inked early in your GTA VI adventure, The Inked Mermaid in Key Lento is one of the first tattoo parlours you can visit after completing the prologue.

How to get tattoos and unlock more designs in GTA 6
Thankfully, getting some fresh ink in GTA VI is a fairly straightforward process, but depending on how extravagant you want your tattoos to be, you might need a healthy bank balance.
Here are some useful tips when visiting tattoo parlours in GTA VI:
- Finding tattoo parlours: Open the in-game map, select the Style category (indicated by a T-shirt icon) and select Tattoos. Tattoo parlours are represented by orange heart icons, which appear as you uncover more of Leonida. You can also select a parlour from the list to set a waypoint.
- Getting a tattoo: Enter a tattoo parlour, interact with the staff and select the Tattoos & Piercings option.
- Tattoo placement: There are various areas of the body to customise, including the head, neck, torso, back, arms and legs.
- Previewing tattoos: You can see what a tattoo looks like on your character before parting with your hard-earned cash.
- Tattoo variations: Some designs are available in outline, shaded and coloured variations, with prices differing depending on your choice.
- Tattoo prices: Tattoo prices: According to GTA Playbook, tattoos at The Inked Mermaid can cost as little as $53, while an elaborate Battle Royale back tattoo will set you back a whopping $8,120. However, some designs are considerably more expensive, such as the Bubblegum Power back tattoo for Jason at Dominion Ink, costing an eye-watering $13,500.
- Bank account required: Purchases exceeding $1,000 generally require a bank account, so having plenty of cash in your pocket might not be enough for some of the more expensive tattoos.
- Removing tattoos: If you regret your choice of ink, you can pay to have it removed. One removal fee documented at The Inked Mermaid was $250.
- Piercings: Tattoo parlours also offer eyebrow piercings, nose rings, earrings and more, with prices starting at $50 before discounts at The Inked Mermaid.
- Grillz: If you fancy giving your character some extra bling, select tattoo parlours, including Dominion Ink, Hyper Ink and Black Spot Tattoo, also offer grillz.
What's more, not every tattoo design in GTA VI is available from the get-go. By scanning tattoo magazines scattered throughout Leonida using the WAiNK app on your in-game smartphone, you can unlock additional designs.
You can keep track of your discoveries by heading to Progress, Collections and then Discovered Styles.

GTA 6 Ultimate Edition has an exclusive tattoo parlour
If you own the Ultimate Edition of GTA VI, you'll have access to an additional tattoo parlour called Electric Fang Tattoo, located in the Stockyard neighbourhood of Vice City.
Available from Chapter One, this tattoo parlour offers more than 50 exclusive designs for Jason and Lucia, created by the real-world, Brooklyn-based artist collective FAILE. What's more, as part of the 'Vice City Style' bonus included with the Ultimate Edition, additional tattoo designs become available as you progress into Chapter 4.
However, if you're a fan of New School tattoos and don't fancy paying extra for the Ultimate Edition, Cross My Heart Tattoos in Crosstown also offers that style of ink, albeit with different designs.
It might be tempting to pick up the GTA VI Ultimate Edition for all the extra content, especially if you're someone who likes to have access to everything. While it's a shame that cosmetic content is locked behind a paywall, I suppose it would be worse if main story missions were exclusive to more expensive editions of the game.
Still, if, like me, you picked up the Standard Edition of GTA VI, the Ultimate Edition Upgrade is available to purchase separately on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, should you fancy some of those additional goodies.
