DarkHorse Technologies Inc. (DRK) Names Business Development Head, Grants 350K RSUs
DarkHorse Technologies Inc. appointed Soren “Anders” Larson as Head of Business Development, effective November 1, 2026. The board approved a one-time inducement award of 350K restricted stock units, vesting in four equal six-month installments over two years, subject to continued employment.
