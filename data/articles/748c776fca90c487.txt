TITLE: Anthropic prepares Google Drive file browser for Claude
OUTLET: TestingCatalog AI News

Anthropic is developing a new Google Drive integration that will let users browse and manage their files directly in Claude. The upcoming interface will display Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides, allowing users to search and filter their files without leaving Claude or switching between applications.
In its current implementation, the functionality appears as a separate item in Claude's navigation sidebar. However, Anthropic commonly uses this approach during development, and the final implementation could become part of its updated Projects experience. Users could potentially manage their Google Drive files within individual projects, move files between folders, and add relevant documents directly to the project context.
Anthropic already supports Google Drive through its connectors, alongside native creation and editing capabilities for Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides. The upcoming interface would complement these capabilities by giving users a dedicated file-management experience rather than relying primarily on conversational commands or file-selection menus.
Interestingly, the implementation looks very similar to how Google Drive management currently works within ChatGPT Spaces, where OpenAI already offers comparable functionality. Anthropic appears to be moving in the same direction, gradually positioning Claude as a comprehensive workspace rather than just a conversational AI assistant.
The final placement of the feature, its file management capabilities, and its release timeline remain unclear. However, given the ongoing development of Claude Projects, we may hear more about this functionality soon.
