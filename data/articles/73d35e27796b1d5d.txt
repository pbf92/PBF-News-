TITLE: Manchester United legend Rio Ferdinand sends clear message to Bayern over Musiala
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Rio Ferdinand has criticised Bayern Munich over the way the club has handled the ongoing situation surrounding Jamal Musiala.
The former Manchester United defender shared his thoughts in a post on X, questioning Bayern's decision to discuss the matter publicly. Ferdinand believes the club should address any concerns directly with the midfielder and his representatives rather than through the media.
"Bayern Munich are better than this… if they want to negotiate more reasonable terms for the club going forward due to the health issues Jamal has, then surely speak to him and his team rather than to the media" , Ferdinand wrote.
The 48-year-old also expressed sympathy for Musiala, suggesting that the public debate is making an already difficult situation even harder for the Germany international.
Ferdinand went on to highlight the reputation Bayern have built over the years, arguing that the club's recent conduct does not match the standards he has traditionally associated with them.
"This situation must be hard enough to deal with for Jamal without the media and club officials talking about it publicly. Bayern Munich are one of the clubs who from afar have always carried themselves with a class and standard that most have admired. Sadly, this just doesn't feel like Bayern Munich in this situation" , he concluded.
Ferdinand's comments add to the criticism Bayern have faced following public statements from several senior figures at the club. Lothar Matthäus has also expressed his disappointment, urging everyone involved to resolve the matter through direct conversations rather than a public exchange.
The situation continues to attract attention as Bayern and Musiala navigate a difficult period.
