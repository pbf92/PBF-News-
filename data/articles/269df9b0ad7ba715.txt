TITLE: Stifel Holds $415 Target on Palo Alto as Peers Go Higher
OUTLET: TradingView

Stifel Holds $415 Target on Palo Alto as Peers Go Higher
Stifel reiterated a Buy rating and $415 price target on Palo Alto Networks Inc. PANW after fiscal first-quarter results showed next-generation security ARR up 66%, product revenue up 37% and total revenue up 39%. Palo Alto shares opened up 4.67%.
The firm noted that Palo Alto guided second-quarter results above expectations and raised its full-year next-generation security ARR and revenue outlook by more than the first-quarter beat. Palo Alto also plans to buy Applied Compute for $4.5 billion, which it expects to add $1 billion in annual recurring revenue by the end of fiscal 2029, with capex of under $3 billion through fiscal 2030. As a result, it lowered its fiscal 2028 adjusted free cash flow margin target, excluding data center capex, to 38% or more from above 40%.
Other brokers raised their targets after the results, with Rosenblatt moving to $460, Mizuho to $440 and DA Davidson to $430. Cantor Fitzgerald reiterated an Overweight rating and a $425 target, and Wedbush kept an Outperform rating and a $400 target.
