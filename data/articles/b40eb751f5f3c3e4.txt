TITLE: World Cup winner Mata retires after previous stints with Man United, Chelsea
OUTLET: TSN

MELBOURNE, Australia (AP) — Juan Mata has announced his retirement from soccer following a career that included World Cup and UEFA Champions League titles.
The 38-year-old Spaniard, who had stints at Manchester United and Chelsea in the Premier League, had not recommitted to the A-League’s Melbourne Victory for the current season and on Wednesday confirmed the end of his career.
“Sometimes, to love ... is to let go. Thanks football,” he posted on social media.
His 2025-26 A-League season saw him win the Australian league’s Johnny Warren Medal for best player.
He scored five goals and produced 13 assists in that season before committing to take an ownership interest in the club and as chair of the Victory’s football committee. He also played for the A-League’s Western Sydney Wanderers.
Mata won the World Cup with Spain in South Africa in 2010, beating the Netherlands 1-0 in extra time. He scored in the final at the Euros in 2012 when Spain defeated Italy 4-0 in the final.
He finished with 41 international appearances and 10 goals.
With Chelsea in 2012 he won the Champions League in his first season. Three years later Mata joined Manchester United, winning the FA Cup and the Europa League.
Leaving Britain in 2022, Mata won league titles in Turkey with Galatasaray and in Japan with Vissel Kobe before finishing his career in Australia.
