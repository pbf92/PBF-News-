TITLE: GTA 6 Tops PlayStation Store Charts in Every Country But One
OUTLET: OpenCritic

Grand Theft Auto 6 currently dominates PlayStation Store sales charts in 46 countries, including the United States, but has lost the race to Apex Legends in Japan. According to Sensor Tower, Grand Theft Auto 6 has sold nearly 15 million copies across PS5 and Xbox Series X/S, generating about $1.5 billion ahead of its November 19 release. However, Japanese players seem to have other priorities at the moment. One reason Apex Legends is currently more popular than GTA 6 in Japan could be its November 10 update, The Godzilla Event.
Until December 1, players can compete on a map featuring the beloved fictional monster, which has likely helped the game top Japan's PlayStation charts. Godzilla is a major part of modern Japanese pop culture and remains incredibly popular in the country. That said, another factor might explain why GTA 6 sales aren't as strong in Japan as they are elsewhere.
A new report reveals previous unreported GTA 6 details, including the game's story length, chapter structure, and achievement tidbits.
