TITLE: 23 Best "GTA 6" Easter Eggs Found So Far
OUTLET: Yahoo News Singapore

After 13 years of waiting for Grand Theft Auto VI, players have finally been let loose in Leonida. As is natural, some have abandoned the main storyline in favor of investigating suspicious noises and hunting down mythical creatures.
Can you blame them? Rockstar has a long history of hiding secrets and callbacks to its previous games, and GTA VI is already proving to be a goldmine for Easter egg hunters.
Here are 23 of the coolest ones players have discovered so far:
1. Arthur Morgan's cowboy hat from Red Dead Redemption 2.
PC Gamer's Christopher Livingston documented the location of a familiar-looking cowboy hat players can buy for $5 at a yard sale.

2. Tony the talking parrot from GTA: San Andreas.
Polygon reports that the parrot says "generator" to reveal a hidden cash stash.
3. The "ASSMAN" license plate from Seinfeld.
Kramer was accidentally assigned the vanity plate in the episode "The Fusilli Jerry."
4. A conspiracy theorist who knows he's living inside a video game.
A house teased in GTA: San Andreas five years ago contains a garage filled with notes from a conspiracy theorist who noticed NPCs were acting weird and the world around him behaved like a simulation.
5. A jet-skiing dog (dubbed "Cool Dog") who helps you out when you wander too far from shore — a reference to a real-life dog named Loosy.
6. Players can witness a UFO abducting a woman, which can also lead to a close encounter for Jason or Lucia.
7. They are later able to rescue an alien from an Area-51-type location.

8. One of the franchise's longest-running bits is back in the form of Bigfoot (or the "Skunk Ape"). According to X users, there's a secret mission where you can hunt him down and kill him...and then you turn into a frog?
9. A Love Fist sticker from the original GTA: Vice City.
Players have reported spotting the fictional rock band's logo on a door at the Starlet Motel.
10. A replica of Dexter Morgan's Miami apartment has a "kill kit" containing a syringe, knife, tape, and a coil of rope hidden in a toolbox on the roof.
11. Michael De Santa's stolen yacht from GTA V can be spotted outside Jason's home in GTA VI. Redditor u/rahiou discovered that the boat's GPS screen displays GTA 5's Los Santos map rather than GTA 6's Leonida map, all but confirming it to be Michael's long-lost boat.
12. A reference to a GTA V yoga instructor.
A copy of Body Copy magazine reportedly contains an article involving Fabien LaRouche, Michael's yoga instructor from GTA 5, who has a new job at a retirement community.

13. There's also a ringtone called "Wind Chimes" buried in the game's phone settings — the same one Michael De Santa used in GTA V.
14. A Red Hulk-like character found in a side mission.
Kotaku asked Rockstar about the encounter, and a developer discussed the studio's fondness for including "weird, zany creatures."

15. Speaking of zany creatures, a giant mutant enemy crab ambushes players at a research facility — a reference some have taken as a nod to the E3 2006 "giant enemy crabs" meme from Genji 2.
Per Polygon: "Back in 2006, during Sony's E3 presentation, one of the developers of Genji 2 explained that the game's stages were based on actual battles that took place in ancient Japan, with hundreds of warriors on either side."
16. An X-Men parody comic called RX-Men, featuring medicine-themed superheroes and a villain named Dr. Placebo.
17. Another Red Dead Redemption reference is hidden in a readable Western at Jacob's Tavern, mentioning a New Austin cowboy.
18. A tribute to Tom Petty in the form of a "Petty Forever" sticker on a store door. Petty reportedly has seven songs on the franchise's returning classic rock station, V-Rock.

19. A satanic ritual summons demons. Placing an unconscious NPC atop a bloody pentagram in an abandoned building in Capri reportedly triggers a creepy supernatural encounter.
20. Brian O'Conner's green Mitsubishi Eclipse from The Fast and the Furious. It's a near-identical replica driven by Paul Walker's character in the original 2001 movie — and players can break into the garage it's hidden in to steal and drive it.
21. People are still talking about Mount Chiliad. Two patrons in a bar discuss a secret bunker containing jetpacks under a mountain, referencing an enduring GTA V mystery that players believe could kick off a puzzling new chapter.
22. The "We got ___ before GTA 6" meme referenced in a post on the game's fictional social media platform, Snapmatic.
A post on the platform complains, "We really got barnyard blue games before RAC7" — a nod to the meme, with "Race and Chase 7" substituting for GTA 7, according to Kotaku.
23. And of course, Rockstar couldn't resist taking the "Easter egg" literally. As discovered by X user @xGarbett, making a 7-ish-minute climb to the top of a towering radio antenna in Vice City results in a bird's nest containing actual painted Easter eggs.
Players can throw it off the tower, triggering a cut scene where it falls on the windshield of a man driving below.
And to think...they've barely scratched the surface.
