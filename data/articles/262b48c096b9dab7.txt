TITLE: Pochettino reveals what he will bring in first message to Man Utd fans
OUTLET: London Evening Standard

The Argentine arrived at his new club on Wednesday, touching down in Manchester immediately after overseeing his final game in charge of the United States.
He led the USMNT to a 2-0 victory over Haiti on Tuesday in Cincinnati, before boarding a flight to England with his trusted assistant Miguel D’Agostino, who will work with him at Old Trafford.
The 54-year-old began work at Carrington on Wednesday, meeting with the players and club hierarchy, as he was pictured with director of football Omar Berrada.
In his first message to United fans, posted to the club’s social media channels, he said: “I am looking forward to meeting you guys.
“I have heard so many good things about you. I hope we bring all the happiness, working together and connect[ion], so that it is going to be a special season, and to make our players fight on the field and get the results we deserve.”
Pochettino’s first game in charge of United will be a tricky one, as he takes his new side to Anfield to face Liverpool on Sunday.
Pochettino has plenty of Premier League experience in his managerial career, having overseen Southampton, Totttenham and Chelsea before spells at Paris Saint-Germain and in the United States.
