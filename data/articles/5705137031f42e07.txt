Everforth ECS secures $163M contract to support DARPA research
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- Everforth said its Everforth ECS unit has been awarded a task order valued at approximately $163M to provide scientific, engineering, and program support to the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency's (DARPA) Multi X Office (MXO).
- The contract includes a one-year base period and four one-year options, consolidating two existing DARPA support task orders.
- Everforth ECS will provide technical analysis, program management, source selection, independent assessments, and scientific expertise across MXO's research programs.
