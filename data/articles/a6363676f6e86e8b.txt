TITLE: Meta Ad Account Fraud Drains Nearly Rs 60 Lakh From Kolkata Family
OUTLET: urbanacres.in

A Kolkata family lost nearly Rs 60 lakh after suspected cybercriminals hijacked a Meta advertising account and used a linked bank account to fund repeated promotions for an unrelated Vietnamese Instagram page, according to a police complaint and the First Information Report. The case has exposed how compromised digital advertising credentials can turn a payment-linked account into a channel for large-scale automated billing.
Urbana Complex resident, aged 33, reported the matter to police after the family noticed a substantial depletion in a private bank savings account. The account belonged to the complainant’s sister and had been added as the payment method for a Meta advertising account. Police registered a case at Anandapur police station on November 14 after the Cyber Cell’s East Division forwarded an E-Zero FIR.
The case was registered under Sections 318(4), 319(2) and 308(2) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, relating to cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property, cheating by personation, and extortion. The reported unauthorised deductions occurred mainly between October 7 and October 12, although the complaint said billing activity extended across nearly a month.
According to the complaint, an audit of the Meta advertising dashboard showed that approximately Rs 54 lakh had been billed to promote content from an unknown Vietnamese profile. Additional withdrawals took the total reported loss to about Rs 60 lakh. The campaigns were unrelated to the account holder’s business and were allegedly launched after unauthorised access to the advertising account.
The reported method differed from a single unauthorised withdrawal. Investigators said the suspected perpetrators initiated repeated automated billing transactions, allowing the deductions to accumulate without immediately triggering the account holders’ attention. The family said the amount exceeded its total advertising expenditure over the preceding three years.
After detecting the breach, the family blocked the linked payment card, submitted chargeback and dispute requests to the bank, and contacted Meta’s support division for an account audit and refund. Complaints were also filed on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, with the divisional cyber cell and with senior officers at Kolkata Police headquarters in Lalbazar.
Investigating officers are examining digital access logs, IP trails, Meta billing records and banking transaction trails. They are also trying to identify the ultimate beneficiaries behind the Vietnamese page and determine how the payment gateway was compromised. Police said the account was under investigation to establish whether unaccounted money had been routed through it, while an investigating officer said the account holder would also come under the agency’s scrutiny as part of the inquiry.
