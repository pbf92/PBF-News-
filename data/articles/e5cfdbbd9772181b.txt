TITLE: ‘Are We Going Too Crazy?’ Here’s Why Rockstar Put A Red Hulk In GTA 6
OUTLET: Kotaku

Yeah, there’s a Red Hulk in GTA VI.
Fans have found this big, silly guy and started posting about him, which prompted me to ask my reviewer colleague what could possibly be happening in this clip:

According to Zack Zwiezen, this guy is the result of a quest to help a farmer grow some extremely large strawberries. In that quest, he ends up using some uh, questionable chemicals, and the end result is what happens above. So yeah, in that clip, Jason does already know the guy. He’s not just hugging a strange murderous monster.
Heads up, I’m gonna show you an up-close photo of the guy post-transformation. Here he is:
When Zack asked Rockstar’s Rob Nelson about this, he said that adding weird, zany creatures like this guy to the game was very much “a feel thing,” and that he would periodically wonder, “Are we going too crazy?” when such ideas came up.
However, Nelson added that they’re always getting pitched so many different ideas from team members, and that they always encourage people to try stuff out. If it’s fun or funny enough, they keep it in. “We love that stuff,” he said.
Red Hulk isn’t the only strange, mythical beast in GTA VI. People have also found Bigfoot already and seen UFOs, and there’s also a freaky giant crab monster that I still don’t have any context for.
“We love sort of playing with players,” Nelson said. “You never know whether it’s real paranormal stuff or it’s phony paranormal stuff. Is it really Bigfoot? Is it a guy wearing a Bigfoot mask? We just love it.”
