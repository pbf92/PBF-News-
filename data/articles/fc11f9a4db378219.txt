TITLE: Pochettino on Yoro red, mentality and Shaw
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United boss Mauricio Pochettino has been speaking to the media before Wednesday's Champions League game against Sporting at Estadio Jose Alvalade (kick-off 20:00 GMT).
Here are the key lines from his news conference:
- On Leny Yoro's sending off at Liverpool and the decision being overturned, Pochettino said: "Very disappointed because I think this type of thing never can happen. My question always was the same. OK, we can accept the referee can make a mistake because of the emotional relationship with the game - sometimes of the view, the position, obviously it can be affected by that. But if we have VAR, what were they doing?"
- He added: "Now [we] move on. Now we are thinking [about] tomorrow. After the game, we all were very disappointed. But what I felt in the dressing room is a group of players that really care, that really want to perform, that are going to do everything for the club and to change that dynamic."
- The United boss was asked about Luke Shaw and whether he can have an impact: "All the players need to feel that they can be an impact for the team, and that they can perform. After that, it's up to us to give the opportunity to every single player, in the moment, that we believe they deserve to be involved in the game."
- Pochettino added: "Of course, I know Luke from the past in our time in Southampton, but we all evolve. We are not the same people now than more than 10 years ago. But, always, it's good to see him. He had a small issue, but now he needs to recover his best form. It's good to have him because of his experience with the squad, but we'll see if he can be involved tomorrow or not, from the beginning or during the game."
- On whether there is fragility around the club he needs to solidify: "What I found is the situation is fragile in the environment, or the people want [it] to look like we're fragile. I don't say we are fragile. I think we are strong, we have great mentality, great mindset."
