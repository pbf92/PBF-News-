TITLE: Achievement Unlocked: An Interview with the First XBOX Player to Reach 10 Million Gamerscore
OUTLET: Xbox Wire

On September 9, 2026, at 3:35pm Pacific Time, SiegfriedX unlocked a 110-point XBOX Achievement that pushed his Gamerscore to a historic 10 million. With that, he became the first XBOX player in the world to reach the milestone – just in time for XBOX’s 25th anniversary celebrations.
His journey began on XBOX 360, and it took 20 years and more than 6,400 games to get there. Along the way, he has racked up some remarkable accomplishments:
What Is Gamerscore?
Gamerscore is an individual scoring system introduced with the launch of XBOX 360 in 2005. It represents the achievement points a player has earned across XBOX games.
“Since 2020, so for the past six years, I’ve unlocked at least one achievement every day,” he told XBOX Wire Brazil in an exclusive interview.
SiegfriedX is the XBOX profile of Brazilian player Marcello Malta, a 42-year-old systems analyst whose biggest hobby is hunting achievements. “I like picking up a game and unlocking every achievement, even if the last one is worth just 10 points and takes 500 hours to get. I’m still going to try,” he says.
With a milestone like that, we wanted to hear more about his passion for achievements, how it all started, and what keeps him chasing that next unlock. Here’s what he had to say.
Achievements and Gamerscore were introduced with XBOX 360. What first got you interested in achievements?
Siegfried: I was hooked on achievements right away. I didn’t even know the system existed, and I don’t think it was something people really talked about at the time. It was just a feature built into the console. You certainly didn’t buy an XBOX knowing it was there, let alone knowing it would eventually influence the entire industry and inspire similar systems elsewhere.
I remember playing and suddenly seeing that notification pop up on the screen. I thought, “What is this?” So, I looked it up, found a couple of achievement websites, started learning how the system worked, and that was it. I was hooked.
Back then, achievements really encouraged you to experience everything a game had to offer: finish the campaign, beat it on the highest difficulty, find all the collectibles, and complete specific challenges along the way. To me, they felt like the developer’s vision for how they wanted you to experience the game. Almost like they were saying, “If you’ve done all of this, you’ve seen my entire game, from beginning to end, and everything I created for you.”
What Are Achievements?
Achievements are specific goals and objectives players can complete in a game to earn Gamerscore points. Examples: finishing it on hard difficulty, collecting every collectible, or defeating a set number of opponents in multiplayer.
Each game released on XBOX includes 1,000G worth of achievements to unlock. That total can increase with additional content and expansions.
What motivates you most when it comes to chasing an achievement?
Siegfried: I first fell in love with games through Nintendo, and their games have always had that focus on collecting things and completing everything. I think that mindset stuck with me from the very beginning. achievements became an extension of that, another way of going after everything a game has to offer.
I’ve never been interested in earning points just for the sake of earning points. When I start a game, I want to unlock every achievement, even if the last one is worth just 10 points and takes 500 hours to get. I’m still going to try. I might give up or not make it in the end, but I always start with the same goal: to get everything, no matter how much time or effort it takes.
You recently reached 10 million Gamerscore. How did you plan the achievement that would take you to that milestone?
Siegfried: I wanted to hit 10 million with a game that meant something to me, something I really loved and that could make the milestone feel even more special. So I decided to do it by completing the full 1,000G in Final Fantasy VII Rebirth.
Final Fantasy is a franchise I love, so it felt like the perfect choice. The final achievement I unlocked was for beating the game on its hardest difficulty. That completed the full achievement list and brought me to exactly 10 million Gamerscore.
Was being the first player to reach 10 million Gamerscore always a goal for you?
Siegfried: I never really had a specific goal in mind. Because of the way I play, always trying to complete everything, I naturally ended up second or third in the world for Gamerscore, with someone else usually ahead of me.
Over time, though, people started dropping out, either because they got tired of chasing achievements or simply decided they didn’t want to keep going. That left me at 9 million Gamerscore with a real shot at becoming the first person to reach 10 million. That’s when I really started pushing for it.
When XBOX 360 launched, the idea of anyone ever reaching 10 million Gamerscore was almost unthinkable. But I don’t have another milestone in mind now. For me, the goal has always been to play, have fun, and do what I enjoy.
“When I start a game, I see it through to the end, at least when it comes to the original achievement list. I’ve been doing that for more than 10 years.”
Looking back on your achievement journey, which one was the hardest to unlock?
Siegfried: For me difficulty comes down on how much time something takes. Some achievements can be incredibly time-consuming, and Monster Hunter: World is a great example.
Getting every gold crown in Monster Hunter: World for the Giant Crown Master achievement was one of the toughest things I’ve ever done. It can be really frustrating to play for one, two, or even three days without making any progress because so much of it comes down to luck and getting the right monsters appear. It makes you want to cry and give up. [Laughs]
What Are Mythic Achievements?
A Mythic Achievement is awarded when a player unlocks all of a game’s original achievements. Achievements added later through DLC or updates are not required, and once Mythic status has been earned, it remains permanently.
What did you have to do in the game to unlock that achievement?
Siegfried: You have to hunt both the smallest and largest monster of every species. The smallest earns you a miniature gold crown, while the largest earns you a giant gold crown.
How long did it take to complete every achievement and reach Mythic?
Siegfried: The base game, Monster Hunter: World, has 1,000G, and its expansion, Monster Hunter World: Iceborne, adds another 1,000G and is almost like a full game in itself. All told, it took me around 600 hours to unlock every achievement. It’s easily the game I’ve spent the most time completing.
Now I’m working on the new Gears, and that one is definitely a challenge…
Yes! It has some very difficult achievements, including more than 25,000 eliminations in Versus and 50,000 in Horde.
Siegfried: Those are the only two I still need to unlock to earn Mythic. They’re both a real challenge, and I know they’re going to take a long time. But I’m definitely not giving up.
On the other end of the spectrum, which achievement did you enjoy earning the most?
Siegfried: Completing Diablo II: Resurrected. I played the original throughout my teenage years. I was obsessed with Diablo and spent years playing it.

So getting a remake of one of my favorite games, having it turn out so well, and seeing achievements that really reflected the experience of longtime players made completing it especially meaningful.
One of those, for example, was reaching level 99 with a Hardcore character. That’s probably the achievement I enjoyed earning the most. I started playing at launch in 2021, and I didn’t unlock that final achievement until March 2023. It took me two years!
Do you only stop playing a game after earning its full 1,000G/Mythic?
Siegfried: It’s been a very long time since I played something without completing the full 1,000G. I don’t think I’ve done that since the launch of XBOX One in 2013. There are a few extremely long and difficult DLC packs that I never went back to finish, but normally, when I start a game, I see it through to the end, at least for the original achievement list. I’ve been doing that for more than 10 years.
Mythic Achievements are one of the new features XBOX has introduced to celebrate its 25th anniversary. What did you think of them?
Siegfried: I think Mythic Achievements were implemented really well. Even little details like the badge design and the sound that plays when you unlock one… Microsoft always does a great job with that kind of thing. Rare Achievements were already great, and Mythic Achievements made the experience even better.
What advice would you give someone who wants to earn more achievements?
Siegfried: More than anything, games are supposed to be fun. If you focus too much on climbing the rankings and push yourself too hard, you can end up taking the fun out of it and burning yourself out.
It’s all about finding the right balance. Spend some time playing something you really enjoy, then switch things up and play something just for the achievements or take on a tougher challenge. Going back and forth between the two is key. That’s what I’ve always done.
