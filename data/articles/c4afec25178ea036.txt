TITLE: Manchester United ready to take legal action following JJ Gabriel update
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United are willing to pursue legal action regarding JJ Gabriel's move to Real Madrid if proof of inducement surfaces.
Agency Epic Sports formally confirmed on Monday that they represent Gabriel as his new agents. A day later, the youngster published a farewell video to express gratitude to United for supporting him on his path.
United were dissatisfied that the farewell video was shared without communication from Gabriel's representatives. The club have made numerous attempts to verify the teenager's well-being since they received a departure request in September, but have been unable to establish contact with his family.
United are now ready to pursue legal action should proof of inducement from Madrid surface. There are stringent regulations surrounding signing Under-18 players, and United are willing to contest through every legal avenue available, according to the Manchester Evening News.
United have received no communication from any governing bodies regarding the de-registration of Gabriel, who remains registered with the club.
There have been worries at United about the guidance Gabriel has received. The 16-year-old was on track to become the club's youngest-ever debutant, but was counseled to depart United by not attending training.
United would welcome the chance to ensure that Gabriel has had input in a life-altering decision, with concerns that he has been inadequately advised by his father throughout the process.
In Gabriel's farewell video, he said: "Dear Manchester United, I arrived in 2020 as a 10-year-old boy with big dreams. Here I grew as a player and a person. From scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award.

"You gave me moments I will always treasure. To every coach and every member of staff, to my teammates and everyone who supported me, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow.
"As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything, JJ."
Michael Carrick handed Gabriel his preseason senior debut against Atlético Madrid in Stockholm, but the youngster did not feature in any other friendly throughout the remainder of the summer. United had planned to hand Gabriel his competitive debut against Brighton and Hove Albion in the Carabao Cup in September; however, his representatives requested a transfer ahead of that fixture.
Following his transfer request, Gabriel initially trained in Castelldefels, Barcelona, with a personal coach, but has since relocated to Madrid ahead of his anticipated move to the Bernabeu.
