TITLE: Futu Trust Launches Digital Asset Family Trust Supporting BTC and ETH
OUTLET: TradingView

Futu Trust Launches Digital Asset Family Trust Supporting BTC and ETH
Futu Trust, a wholly owned trust institution under Futu Holdings, has launched a digital asset family trust. According to Odaily, the trust can include accepted digital assets such as BTC and ETH alongside Hong Kong and U.S. stocks, ETFs and other traditional financial assets within one trust structure. It can also coordinate with Hong Kong-licensed securities firms, banks and licensed virtual asset trading platforms to plan asset holding, custody arrangements, day-to-day management and intergenerational transfers. The service is aimed mainly at Hong Kong tax-resident families and family offices, with a minimum combined trust-asset threshold equivalent to $3 million.
