TITLE: Manchester United ace Watson signs new deal
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United midfielder Emma Watson has signed a new long-term contract with the club until summer 2031.
WSL Full-Time were informed of the news via an official media update from The Red Devils this afternoon.
The 20-year-old joined Manchester United from Scottish Women's Premier League (SWPL) club Rangers in summer 2023. Watson's progress with The Red Devils was initially halted by an Anterior Cruciate Ligament (ACL) injury before she made a full recovery. The midfielder has made five appearances for Manchester United to date, scoring two goals.
The player has also gained some experience in loan spells with Everton, Crystal Palace and Tigres UANL in the last couple of years.
After signing a new deal with Manchester United, Watson said "I'm really proud to extend my time with Manchester United. I've learned so much during my time here and I'm grateful for the belief and support that the club has shown in me.
"I feel like I'm continuing to grow as a player under Eva [Olid] and her coaching staff and I'm excited to see how much further I can develop. We have a great group of players and staff, with some real momentum building, so I'm looking forward to being part of what we can achieve together."
Watson will be hoping to cement her place in the Manchester United side in the next four-and-a-half years.
