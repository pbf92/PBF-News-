TITLE: HGV wedged beneath Bromley Cross railway bridge
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

HGV wedged beneath Bromley Cross railway bridge
The incident affected train services over the bridge this afternoon
A HGV lorry was wedged beneath a railway bridge in Bolton this afternoon (November 23), causing disruption to rail services throughout the day.
The lorry was pictured trying to fit under the railway bridge, at the junction of Turton Road and Darwen Road in Bromley Cross, with its top left corner stuck against the structure as emergency services attended the scene.
The incident took place around 2pm this afternoon, with the HGV being removed by around 2.45pm. Emergency services, Network Rail and railway officers attended the scene after the vehicle struck the bridge.
Traffic was reported to be building up in the area as the lorry was blocking the road beneath the bridge, with pedestrians photographed apprehensively making their way past the HGV.
Only minor damage appears to have taken place to the lorry on its roof and there have been no reported injuries from the incident.
Following the incident the bridge was inspected and trains started running again after getting the all clear from the relevant authorities.
A Network Rail spokesperson said: "Following a report of a bridge strike, a recovery vehicle and inspection team were sent to Bromley Cross railway bridge.
"The vehicle was removed and the bridge was thoroughly inspected. Train services have now resumed."
