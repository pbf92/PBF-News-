TITLE: GTA 6 Includes A Mario And Luigi Easter Egg
OUTLET: Kotaku

You are likely aware that Rockstar’s recently released Grand Theft Auto 6 is a big game. But what you might not understand is just how much of this massive open world is filled with secrets, Easter eggs, and other oddities just waiting to be discovered. This includes a well-hidden block that you can break to reveal two hats that look a lot like what Mario and Luigi might wear.
As discovered and shared by Twitter user xGarbett, if you head to Lummox County, the region of GTA 6 located north of Vice City, and start exploring the woods in the southwest portion of Moreau, you’ll be able to find the dilapidated brick and stone ruins of a long-destroyed building. Explore these ruins, and near the top of them is a partially invisible block floating in the air above one of the tallest bits of the out-of-the-way ruin. Jump into that block, just like a famous plumber might do in a Nintendo game, and out will pop two hats for Jason and Lucia.

I went there on my own to see if this was all accurate, and yes, I can confirm that this works. I didn’t see the box at first, but after I got up there just like xGarbett did, the hidden cube became visible.
However, try as I might, I could not get Jason to break the block by jumping into it. Instead, I just pulled out my gun and shot it, and the hats popped out. I imagine Mario would love to have an SMG on him for times like this.
Anyway, I grabbed both, called Lucia, and headed to a clothing shop where we got dressed up in the sunlight with our new, legally distinct hats.
GTA 6 appears to be filled with secrets and little Easter eggs like this. For example, if you’re wondering how I got the shirt you see Jason wearing in the screenshot I shared above which was once worn by Tommy Vercetti, you might want to read this post about the wildest hidden secret people have found in GTA 6 so far: The ability to travel back in time to Vice City on the PS2.
