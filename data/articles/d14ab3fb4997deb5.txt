TITLE: The chilling confession of a killer who murdered his wife and hid her body under the bed
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

The chilling confession of a killer who murdered his wife and hid her body under the bed
Kyle Howard pleaded guilty to the murder of Keeley Aspinall on Monday, November 16
A murderer who stabbed his wife to death then hid her body under the bed made a chilling confession to medics, telling them: "There is no point being here, I’ve done it, she’s dead, she’s been dead for two days."
Kyle Howard, 36, called the police on May 25 this year to report that he had killed his wife, Keeley Aspinall, at their home on Tweedale Street, in Rochdale.
He told cops he had stabbed Ms Aspinall to death two days earlier, and was reporting his crime because her body was 'beginning to smell', Greater Manchester Police said.

Officers arrived at their home and found the 44-year-old deceased and concealed underneath a bed. Howard was arrested on suspicion of murder.
He later told attending paramedics: "There is no point being here, I’ve done it, she’s dead, she’s been dead for two days."
Following his arrest, Howard spent several days in hospital receiving treatment for an infection to his hand before being discharged back into police custody on June 1. He was charged with Ms Aspinall's murder the following day.
Howard, of Tweedale Street, previously pleaded not guilty to murder, but today (November 16), on the first day of the trial at Manchester Crown Court, he entered a guilty plea. He will be sentenced on Wednesday, November 18.

Detective Inspector Neil Higginson, the senior investigating officer, said: “Keeley’s family have been left devastated by her death and continue to come to terms with the loss of a loved one in the most tragic of circumstances.
“Our thoughts remain with them as they navigate the profound impact this has had on their lives.
"Kyle Howard violently took the life of a woman who should have been safe in her own home. He then concealed Keeley’s body beneath a bed and left her there for two days before contacting emergency services.
"His actions showed a shocking level of cruelty and complete disregard for Keeley.
"Today’s guilty plea means Howard has finally accepted responsibility for his actions and has spared Keeley’s loved ones the added trauma of having to sit through a lengthy trial.
"While no result can ever ease the grief felt by Keeley’s family and friends, I hope this outcome provides them with some comfort in knowing the man responsible for her death will now face the consequences of his actions when he is sentenced later this week.”
