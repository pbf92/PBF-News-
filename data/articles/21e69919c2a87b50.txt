TITLE: Szymon Marciniak facing suspension after Man United decision condemned as 'catastrophic' by ex-refs chief
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United fans were left raging by the refereeing performance against Sporting CP on Wednesday.
Mauricio Pochettino continues to rage at Samuel Barrott's decisions at Anfield over the weekend, with Leny Yoro's red card being overturned.
That one decision changed the course of the game, and while Liverpool were always favourites to win, 4-0 was an extremely harsh result.
Man United did end their Champions League clash with 10 men on 25 November, and once again, this can be put down to the referee.
Szymon Marciniak made an abundance of critical errors at the Estadio Jose Alvalade, forcing the Red Devils to settle for a point away from home.
But when it comes to referees making mistakes, there is a frustrating lack of action taken to make sure it doesn't happen again.
What did Szymon Marciniak get wrong?
So what did Marciniak get wrong? Well, firstly, Youri Tielemans' second yellow card may have been a bookable offence, but his first was so unfair.
Amad Diallo had scored to equalise, only for his goal to be ruled out for a foul in the build-up. However, replays showed that the goal should have stood.
Players should never be able to talk back to the referee, but when the error is bad enough for ex-PGMOL Keith Hackett to describe it to Football Insider as "catastrophic", Tielemans was understandably angry.

This came after Sporting were awarded an extremely soft penalty. Geny Catamo made a meal out of a kick to the shin by Lisandro Martinez, and the referee bought it.
Man United must push for UEFA punishments
There was absolutely no consistency between decisions throughout the game, and it was clear to see why fans, players and manager were all seething.
Roberto Rosetti, head of UEFA referees, must take serious action after this performance. But if it's anything like the Premier League, nothing will happen.
Sure, referees are human and mistakes are just part of the sport. But when a player makes an error, he might be docked wages and suspended, like a red card.
Discipline is also an important part of football, and unless Rosetti is going to show some here, his referees will continue to make poor errors in judgement.
