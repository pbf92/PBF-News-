TITLE: 'GTA 6' is out now and officially ready to play: Everything you need to know about the Rockstar game launch including reviews, pricing, story mode and more

More than 13 years after GTA 5 launched on consoles, Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here — and millions of people are reportedly taking time off work to play it. GTA 6, which appears to be much more detailed and immersive than GTA 5, launched at midnight local time worldwide and has already earned rave reviews from critics.
It's likely the biggest game launch of the decade. In fact, it's already estimated to have brought in more than $1.6 billion in preorders across PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series consoles. That's a lot of sales, which means you're about to see GTA 6 everywhere.
The game lets players freely explore the state of Leonida — essentially a Florida analog — including the legendary Vice City. It even features fully functional bus and train schedules, which is a level of detail that may contribute to speculation that it'll be the most expensive game ever made.
If you're thinking about joining in on the frenzy, we've rounded up everything you need to know to get started.
When is GTA 6 available?
Grand Theft Auto 6 is available now after officially launching on Nov. 19, 2026, placing it squarely in the busy holiday shopping season. The game has been delayed a couple of times in the past, but Rockstar kept its promise with the final release date.
The initial launch features only the single-player story mode, so don't expect to jump into online multiplayer right away.
What platforms is GTA 6 available on?
At launch, GTA 6 is only available for current-generation game consoles. That means you can only play it on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X or Xbox Series S. While we've seen some other modern games also launch on PlayStation 4 and Xbox One, GTA 6 skipped them altogether due to the high technical demands of the game's world and systems.
How much does GTA 6 cost?
GTA 6 is a little more expensive than normal new game releases, coming in at $80 for the standard edition. For diehard fans, there's also the Ultimate Edition and a physical Collector's bundle that, weirdly, doesn't include the game. Here's how it breaks down.
- Standard Edition ($79.99): Includes the complete base game with the full single-player campaign. Available both digitally and in physical stores.
- Ultimate Edition ($99.99): Exclusive to digital platforms. Includes the full game, alongside bonus cosmetics, in-game vehicles and property vouchers for online play.
- Collector's bundle ($399.99): Officially called "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection," this bundle doesn't include a game code. However, it packs a lot of physical collectibles that GTA fans will enjoy, including a Macca the Gator figure, Oakley sunglasses, a snapback hat and more.
There's an important caveat, however, if you're planning to buy a physical copy in stores. The game box doesn't include a disc. Instead, you'll get a digital code that you can redeem on your console's store.
What are early GTA 6 reviews saying?
While it's still early, reviews for GTA 6 are stellar so far. At the time of writing, the game has an aggregate score of 99 out of 100 on Metacritic from 150 critic reviews. Reviewers called it the "best game Rockstar has made to date," and said it "reaches new heights of immersion." If that's not a ringing endorsement, we don't know what is.
How long does the GTA 6 story take to complete?
According to gaming outlet IGN, GTA 6's story takes roughly 73 hours to beat. However, there are potentially hundreds of hours of activities to eat up your time in the game.
Are there any pre-order bonuses or collabs?
Yes! If you pre-ordered GTA 6 before its November 19 launch, you could get the Vintage Vice City pack. It includes various retro-inspired Vice City cosmetics, along with an exclusive car, the '55 Vapid Stanier. It also includes one free month of GTA+.
How big is GTA 6?
GTA 6 requires up to 212GB of free space, depending on which console you're playing on. That makes it double the file size of Rockstar's last game, Red Dead Redemption 2. For reference, the base PlayStation 5 comes with a 825GB storage drive, of which only around 670GB is usable after accounting for the console's system software.
GTA 6 is the biggest GTA game yet, encompassing the entire state of Leonida. It's also a fully modern game with high-end assets, physics and more. If you're planning to buy it, start considering what you might want to delete to make space for it now.
Can you play GTA 6 on PC?
Not at launch. In typical Rockstar fashion, a PC version of GTA 6 is expected to arrive later, though the developer hasn't given an exact release date. However, rumors suggest it could be in the first three months of 2027. If you're planning to wait for a PC launch, expect some pretty heavy system requirements when it lands.
Can you play it online?
GTA Online has been one of the biggest success stories from GTA 5, and Rockstar will push that into the GTA 6 era as well. But not at launch. Like the PC launch, GTA 6 online doesn't have a release date yet, but it's widely expected to arrive sometime in 2027.
What should parents know?
Like past GTA games, GTA 6 is intended for adults. The game is rated M for Mature (17+) by the Entertainment Software Rating Board (ESRB), which is responsible for rating games in the U.S. "Grand Theft Auto VI is rated M for Mature 17+ by the ESRB with Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, and Use of Drugs and Alcohol. Also includes In-Game Purchases," the organization says.
If you're planning to purchase the game for yourself and are concerned about your child gaining access to it, both Xbox Series X/S and PlayStation 5 have parental controls that may help. Each console lets you do things like block games by age rating, require authorization for in-game purchases and restrict online voice and text chat interactions. You can see how to use these settings at the online parental control help pages for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, respectively.
