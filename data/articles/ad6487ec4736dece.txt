Why Fastly shares are trading lower today
What happened?
Shares of edge cloud platform Fastly FSLY fell 10.1% in the afternoon session after AI-linked shares continued to pull back. Anthropic slid more than 10% following its debut, failing to revive confidence in the sector's spending boom.
Investors had looked to Anthropic's record $81.3 billion initial public offering and Nvidia's quarterly earnings beat under interim CEO Colette Kress to steady sentiment following Jensen Huang's resignation, CNBC reported. Instead, Anthropic's retreat toward its $83 offer price echoed Seaport Research warnings, covered by CNBC, that its valuation demands much faster revenue growth.
Separately, Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook warned against delaying rate hikes ahead of an expected December increase. Compounding the concerns, President Donald Trump threatened penalties on overseas U.S. data-center builds as new Commerce Department export-licensing rules weighed on chipmakers.
What is the market telling us
Fastly’s shares are extremely volatile and have had 84 moves greater than 5% over the last year. But moves this big are rare even for Fastly and indicate this news significantly impacted the market’s perception of the business.
Fastly is up 45.6% since the beginning of the year, but at $14.84 per share, it is still trading 55.7% below its 52-week high of $33.50 from April 2026. Despite the year-to-date gain, investors who bought $1,000 worth of Fastly’s shares 5 years ago would now be looking at only $359.77.
