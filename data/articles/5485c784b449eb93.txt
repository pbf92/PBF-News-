TITLE: Manchester United vs Sporting CP: Live updates from Champions League clash
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Live Updates
Manchester United will look to win their first away game of this season's Champions League when they take on Sporting CP on Wednesday.
Unlike their Premier League form, the Red Devils have won twice in Europe, including a stunning come-from-behind win against AS Roma on the previous matchday.
Mauricio Pochettino makes four changes from Sunday's humbling at Anfield, with one of them enforced due to Matheus Cunha's red card against the Serie A side.
Non-performers dropped
Matthijs de Ligt, who was abysmal against Liverpool, gets dropped, alongside the struggling Diogo Dalot. Harry Maguire and Noussair Mazraoui have been picked instead.
Up front, Amad makes a return to the starting XI, with Joshua Zirkzee handed a rare start. The Dutchman has impressed during his cameos and he has the chance to make a real statement at the Estádio José Alvalade.
Bruno Fernandes, formerly of Sporting CP, returns to his former home for the first time since departing for Old Trafford.
Can Mauricio Pochettino win his first European tie since taking over? Stay tuned to find out.
