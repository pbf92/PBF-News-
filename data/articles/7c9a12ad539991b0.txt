Xbox Alligator Controller Sent Ahead of GTA 6 With a Hidden Ultimate Edition Code
Xbox has started sending out an alligator-themed controller ahead of Grand Theft Auto 6’s launch, and the go-bag it comes in hides a code for the game’s Ultimate Edition. DayOne‘s Jon Clarke showed off one of the kits on 16 November. It came in an Xbox-branded duffel bag, handed over by a courier who said only “Lucia sent me” and then walked off.
GTA 6 releases on Thursday 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Anyone who has already bought it can preload now.
The Xbox Alligator Controller and What Else Is in the Bag
The Xbox alligator controller is a black pad covered in alligator scales.
The Xbox button sits inside a section shaped like a gator’s eye, teeth wrap around the side and claw accents finish the design. It sits in its own case inside the duffel bag, alongside:
- stacks of Xbox-themed novelty US dollar bills
- a Maglite torch with UV functionality
- a lockbox, plus a key on an alligator keyring
- a 12-month Xbox Game Pass Ultimate code
- a code for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4
- a note signed by The Xbox Team
The GTA 6 Code Hidden in the Cash
The note calls the bag a loot drop from a partner in crime. It also carries a tip: “Don’t take the money at face value.” It tells recipients to look closely, because secrets may come to light. Clarke did, and found a code on the cash that redeems the Ultimate Edition of GTA 6. The UV torch in the same bag looks like the tool meant for the job, which makes the novelty cash a small scavenger hunt instead of just set dressing.
Marketing GTA 6 Without Naming It
Clarke pointed out that Xbox has been somewhat restricted from marketing GTA 6 directly, and the whole kit is built around that limit. The note never names the game. It points recipients to Game Pass titles like Gears of War: E-Day, Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater, Age of Empires and Forza Horizon 6, and to “another upcoming release”. Every GTA 6 reference comes in sideways: the courier’s line about Lucia, one of the game’s two lead characters, the alligator styling, and a code you only find by inspecting the cash.
The go-bag also ties into a bigger Xbox stunt: a taxidermy alligator with an Xbox Series X built inside it. That one is also linked to GTA 6’s arrival without naming the game, and the alligator console is up for grabs as a prize. For a platform holder that can’t put the biggest release of the year front and centre, it’s a smart workaround, and it leaves Game Pass and Call of Duty doing the visible selling.
Xbox One Cloud Access and the 30fps Console Lock
According to Clarke, Xbox has secured cloud rights for GTA 6. That would let players still on Xbox One hardware play it through the cloud, as long as they’ve bought the game. He also wrote that every console version runs at a locked 30fps. The PS5 Pro gets some enhancements, but not a higher frame rate.
If the cloud deal works as described, Xbox One owners won’t need to upgrade to a Series X|S to play GTA 6, even though the game itself only launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
