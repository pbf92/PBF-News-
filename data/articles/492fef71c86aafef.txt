TITLE: Rockstar Defends GTA 6's Most Polarizing New Feature After Backlash
OUTLET: Comic Book Resources

The arrival of Grand Theft Auto 6 came with many major changes for the video game franchise, with one new addition becoming particularly polarizing. While it's led to some division from players, it's a mechanic that Rockstar stands by.
In GTA 6, there's a new "Criminal Profile" system that essentially serves as a morality meter. It works similar to the Honor system in Red Dead Redemption 2, with Jason and Lucia each having an individual wanted rating based on their behavior. This was designed to encourage more empathy with players, with much longer-lasting consequences for playing like a cold-blooded, murderous maniac. Addressing the mechanic in a Video Games Chronicle interview, GTA 6 producer Rob Nelson defended the move.
Grand Theft Auto 6 Measures Player Morality
Nelson explained that when showing footage of the game to the press earlier on in development, the on-screen icon for the Criminal Profile system was observed. He was asked if this was a sign that morality was being added as a mechanic, which is a big decision, given that this was never present in any previous GTA game. While Nelson admitted that being questioned about this concerned him at first, Rockstar ultimately felt that it adds something new to the GTA experience.
“I thought, ‘Oh sh*t, we’re in trouble,’ but I said, ‘It doesn’t change everything, it changes what GTA can be,’" Nelson noted. "So for us, I think it opens a new range of choices and decisions for the player to make. You can still do whatever you wanted to do before, but now you have other options.”
It's not always possible to please 100% of any fandom. Nelson realized that there were inevitably going to be some players who "reject this new stuff we're offering." While Nelson understands this, he stressed how it was also necessary to encourage more empathy when playing as the Jason and Lucia characters, who are designed to be more morally gray than the leads in Grand Theft Auto 5.
Jason and Lucia Were Designed Differently
“I hope it changes the way players who want to engage with it play the game," he said. "In GTA 5 you started with a bank robbery in North Yankton, and you’re murdering like 40 cops off the rip, and you’re setting these guys up as what they are. We were conscious of that [with GTA 6]. Lucia takes the bullets out of the gun at the start of the game. She goes in, and it’s not like you can kill a bunch of people. So we wanted to start them off with a bit less baggage. They’re trying to make a go of this semi-legitimate life."
Nelson continued, “When you pick up with Jason, he’s still a criminal; he’s still getting in trouble with Donnie and all that, but he’s sort of been living this relatively criminal-adjacent existence.”
There are six Criminal Profile levels, with the worst tier, "Irredeemable," adding the most challenge to the gameplay. There are alternate endings, with the "best" ending reserved for the most honorable playthroughs. So, while players still have the freedom to go violently ballistic if they choose, the story and casual gameplay both greatly benefit from playing with more honor.
Grand Theft Auto 6 is available on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
