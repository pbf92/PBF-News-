TITLE: 'We need Him': Stephen 'Wonderboy' Thompson spreads the Gospel during UFC retirement speech
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

Stephen “Wonderboy” Thompson took the opportunity to spread the gospel after his final mixed martial arts fight in the UFC.
The former kickboxing world champion and welterweight UFC veteran of 14 years ended his career in style against Charles Radtke with a stunning spinning wheel kick at UFC 334 at Madison Square Garden.
Before the Karate specialist laid down his gloves onto the mat of the octagon, the symbol of a fighter retiring, he used the opportunity to urge fans to follow God.
Speaking minutes after the fight to fight caller and podcaster Joe Rogan, he told everyone that he loves them and who loves them.
Thompson said: “I told myself I wasn’t going to cry, but I’m crying. Even if I don’t dance with you, or train with you, or laugh with you or cry with you, I love you.
“Most importantly, our Lord and Saviour loves you too, so do your best to follow him.”
During the post-fight press conference after earning the fight of the night honour, he tearfully spoke about why he thought the world needed Jesus.
“Wonderboy” added: “Well, look at the world today, and look where it’s going. You know, I know what the Lord has done for me and who I am.”
Fighting tears, he continued: “We need Him. Everybody needs Him.”
Last year, Irish UFC superstar Conor McGregor announced that he had given his life to Christ, saying that he lives “by God’s word”.
He also posted on X in 2025 that he had an encounter with God during psychoactive treatment.
