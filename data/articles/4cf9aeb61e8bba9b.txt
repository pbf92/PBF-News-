Adani Energy Solutions Ltd. engages in establishing, commissioning, setting up, operating, and maintaining electric power transmission systems. It operates through the following segments: Transmission, Distribution Business, Trading, and Others. The Transmission segment manages the transmission line for power transmission. The Distribution Business refers to the transmission and distribution of power business for Mumbai City and Mundra Distribution. The Trading segment offers trading activity of goods and services. The Others segment includes smart metering business and EPC activities. The company was founded by Gautambahi Shantilal Adani on December 9, 2013 and is headquartered in Ahmedabad, India.
