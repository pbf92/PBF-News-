TITLE: InMobi launches AI platform to automate digital advertising for SMBs
OUTLET: Indian Television Dot Com

MUMBAI: Small businesses may soon need fewer hands on deck to run their advertising campaigns, as InMobi brings seven AI agents to do the heavy lifting.
The company has launched InMobi Accelerate, an agentic AI-powered, self-serve advertising platform designed to help small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) and agencies plan, execute and measure campaigns across multiple digital platforms through a single dashboard.
The launch comes as advertisers increasingly spread their budgets across different platforms but struggle to understand how those investments perform collectively. A campaign may generate clicks on one platform, conversions on another and sales elsewhere, leaving businesses with a fragmented picture of what their advertising spend is actually delivering.
InMobi’s platform aims to address that problem by bringing campaign planning, creative development, execution and optimisation into one workflow, using seven specialised AI agents to manage different stages of the process.
The scale of the challenge is reflected in research conducted by NewtonX for InMobi among 800 US-based SMB advertisers and agencies in May 2026. The study found that 93 per cent of respondents could not confidently measure performance across the platforms they used. More than 70 per cent ran campaigns on three or more platforms, while only 4 per cent could launch a campaign on the same day they decided to advertise.
InMobi Accelerate divides campaign management into seven functions: business analysis, audience strategy, media planning, copywriting, creative design, campaign launch and optimisation.
Businesses begin by sharing their website and campaign objective. The platform then analyses the business, identifies potential target audiences, develops a media plan and prepares campaign creatives and budgets for approval.
The idea is to reduce the number of separate specialists and manual steps required to take an advertising campaign from brief to execution. For smaller businesses without dedicated marketing teams, that could mean gaining access to capabilities that would otherwise require multiple agencies or specialised hires.
For agencies, the proposition is slightly different. Automating repetitive campaign tasks could allow teams to manage more clients without increasing operational resources at the same pace.
“AI agents change the economics of expertise. A growing business should be able to run a disciplined advertising programme without hiring seven specialists first, and an agency should be able to take on more clients without adding repetitive work at the same rate,” said Rohit Dosi, VP and GM of InMobi Accelerate.
The platform also allows advertisers to interact with the system through plain-English instructions. They can ask it to shift budgets between channels, revise creative assets or make campaign changes without navigating multiple advertising dashboards.
Measurement is another major part of the offering. InMobi says Accelerate uses its first-party pixel and universal conversion tracking to deduplicate conversion events across connected advertising platforms.
This is intended to give businesses a more consolidated view of campaign results and reduce the risk of counting the same conversion multiple times across channels.
The platform also calculates what InMobi calls True ROAS (return on ad spend), providing advertisers with a cross-channel view of the returns generated by their campaigns.
The distinction matters for businesses running advertising across several platforms. Without a consolidated measurement system, each platform may report conversions against its own attribution model, making it difficult for advertisers to understand the overall contribution of their media investments.
InMobi is also connecting Accelerate to Mobius, its unified intelligence system for agentic advertising decision-making. Mobius uses campaign history and performance signals to forecast advertising options and recommend or execute changes based on an advertiser’s objectives.
According to InMobi, Mobius processes more than 25 trillion predictions and evaluates over 200 billion advertising opportunities on an average day. These figures highlight the scale of the underlying intelligence system that the company is bringing into its new self-serve platform.
At launch, InMobi Accelerate supports Google Ads, Meta Ads, Microsoft Advertising, TikTok Ads and ChatGPT Ads.
The company plans to add integrations with Glance, its AI-powered personal shopping platform, as well as other major advertising platforms.
Advertisers retain control over campaign approvals. They can review and approve media plans, creative assets and budgets before campaigns go live, rather than handing over unrestricted control to the AI system.
They can also authorise continuous optimisation within predefined parameters. Approval workflows and role-based access controls are designed to ensure that campaign changes remain within the limits set by advertisers.
This combination of automation and human approval is central to the platform’s proposition: AI handles the operational workload, while businesses retain oversight of budgets, campaign direction and the changes being made.
InMobi introduced Accelerate at Glance Forward in New York on 18 November, positioning the platform as a response to the growing complexity of digital advertising for smaller businesses and agencies.
The bigger question is whether agentic AI can make multi-platform advertising genuinely easier to manage, rather than simply adding another layer of technology. With advertisers already struggling to measure results across channels, the value of Accelerate will ultimately depend on how effectively it can turn fragmented campaign data into clearer decisions and measurable returns.
