GTA 6 Was the Biggest Launch in PlayStation History With Over 39 Million Unique Players - Sales
by William D'Angelo , posted 2 hours ago / 492 Views
Sony Interactive Entertainment announced Grand Theft Auto VI set the record for the biggest launch in PlayStation history with over 39 million unique players.
"The launch of GTAVI marks an extraordinary moment for gaming and entertainment, becoming the biggest game launch in PlayStation history, across three decades and five console generations," said Sony Interactive Entertainment content communications manager Tim Turi.
"More than 39 million unique players have experienced Jason and Lucia’s story in Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5. The PlayStation community came together in a big way to experience Grand Theft Auto VI during its launch and opening weekend."
Through November 22, players on the PS5 logged 683 million hours of gameplay in Grand Theft Auto VI with an average of 17.4 hours per player.
The game helped drive "the largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day since the console’s launch, outside of end-of-year holidays. Since its launch, GTAVI was the first game played on 70% of new PS5 consoles."
Grand Theft Auto VI released for the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19.
