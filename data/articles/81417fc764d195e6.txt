TITLE: Cochrane invites residents to 42nd annual Light Up
OUTLET: Cochrane Eagle

With the Cochrane Light Up set to take place this Saturday, November 28, the Town of Cochrane invites all residents to attend as the event will feature something for everyone.
The Town of Cochrane reminds the community about the annual tradition by highlighting key features of the event.
“Christmas is one of our community’s favourite times of the year, and Cochrane Light Up is such a special tradition for our community,” said Mayor Morgan Nagel.
“I want this to be a true Hallmark holiday experience for Cochrane – a place where families, friends and neighbours can come together, make memories and celebrate the magic of the season.”
Aside from the aforementioned Hallmark movie vibes, the Light Up will also feature the following:
- Horse-drawn wagon rides
- Expanded kid-friendly activities and entertainment
- Additional food options
Back by popular demand are the fireworks display, and the outdoor holiday movie which was introduced in last year’s celebration.
“We heard from residents last year that they wanted fireworks back, and we're excited to deliver on that feedback,” Nagel added. “From the fireworks to the outdoor holiday movie, firepits and festive atmosphere throughout our downtown, there's something for everyone.”
As the event typically occurs during cold weather, attendees are encouraged to wear layers for the evening. Additional warming areas have been introduced with the traditional firepits for the Light Up.
Hot chocolate and other warm holiday beverages will also help warm people during the event and residents are encouraged to bring reusable mugs to help with the event’s sustainability efforts.
Those considering to attend the event are also encouraged to take advantage of free transit for the day, All COLT fixed-route and on-request trips free for November 28.
With the official Light Up ceremony taking place at approximately 5:15 p.m, patrons are called to gather at The Station or in Historic Downtown. Once lit, lights will continue to shine until early January 2027.
The Light Up is considered by many in town as the official kick-off to the holiday season, now on its 42ndyear, the Town of Cochrane thanks sponsors, Winducks Cochrane, Our Electrician and First Light Automation and Big Bang Fireworks Inc. among many others.
“Cochrane Light Up is a Town-supported, community-led celebration,” the Town said. “The event is made possible through the generous support of local businesses, community organizations, volunteers and the many residents who come together to make this annual tradition so special.”
Festivities will run from 5 to 8 pm. Visit cochrane.ca/lightup for more details.
