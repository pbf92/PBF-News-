TITLE: Pochettino Says Man Utd Were Punished as Two Goals Were Ruled Out
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

- Manchester United drew 2-2 at Sporting in the Champions League on Wednesday night after Amad Diallo's first-half goal and a late third were both ruled out, per BBC Sport.
- Youri Tielemans was sent off for a second booking in added time and will miss the home game against RB Leipzig on 8 December.
- Former United winger Ashley Young called referee Szymon Marciniak's decision making a disgrace, and Pochettino nodded when asked about the call on Amad's goal.
Young and Hargreaves Turn on Marciniak as Pochettino Counts the Cost in Lisbon
Ashley Young, speaking on the BBC after Manchester United's 2-2 Champions League draw at Sporting on Wednesday night, gave his verdict on referee Szymon Marciniak: "The referee's performance was just simply wow. The decisions that he gave." Marciniak, who took charge of the 2022 World Cup final, ruled out two United goals and booked Youri Tielemans, who was later sent off in added time for a second yellow. Mauricio Pochettino, two games into his time as United manager, answered a question about the first of those calls with a nod.
The Goal That Never Counted
The first flashpoint came before half-time. Marciniak blew his whistle before Amad scored what would have been United's equaliser, so VAR had no chance to assess whether Tielemans had fouled Goncalo Inacio in the build-up. The referee judged that he had and booked the midfielder. Replays showed Tielemans got the ball first and made minimal contact with his follow-through.
"On first viewing I thought his foot must have hit him on the follow through but when you look at it again, Tielemans actually tries to take his foot away. He doesn't touch him because he knows the midfielder is coming towards him."
"He tried to pull his foot out of the way. It is one of the worst decisions I've seen with a referee giving that decision and even VAR not saying to him to go back and review it because it's not a foul."
Had Tielemans not been booked in the first half, the second yellow in added time would not have sent him off. United insiders felt it was clearly not a foul and that Amad's goal should have stood.
A Late Winner Taken Away
Then came the third goal. Sporting keeper Kaique punched the ball into his own net at a corner, and United thought they were 3-2 ahead. VAR sent Marciniak to the pitchside monitor, and he disallowed it for a Patrick Dorgu foul on the keeper. United insiders felt any contact was marginal.
"Youri Tielemans getting booked to eventually getting sent off later because you booked him when it should never have been a booking and it should have been an equalising goal. The third [Man Utd] goal that should have stood because he's saying Patrick Dorgu fouled the keeper."
"The keeper comes out and flaps at it before he is even touched. How that has gone to VAR is beyond me. His decision making today is nothing more than a disgrace."
Owen Hargreaves, another former United player, joined in on TNT Sports. "The amount of things he got wrong today was astonishing." He added: "People don't come to the game to hear you blow your whistle."
Pochettino Chooses His Words
Pochettino was more guarded. In an earlier interview with TNT Sports he said: "It's difficult to find a game where you get punished so much in one game. We were punished a lot in key actions."
In the news conference he was asked whether the call on Amad's goal and Tielemans' booking was harsh. He stayed tight-lipped and nodded.
"It's difficult to answer because I think everyone knows it's impossible to understand why that happened on the field," Pochettino said, before adding sarcastically: "I think it's a good welcome for us after seven days! Today it's going to be my first week in charge."
"Through our experience, if we go back and remember circumstances in different clubs, things like this you will suffer because it's the circumstance that always is against us."
"I think we dominated and we deserved to win the game, but we didn't for different circumstances," he said. "All the teams make mistakes, but for these mistakes the punishment (for us) is too big in every single mistake. Many other clubs make mistakes or tilt in a different circumstance, in a more positive way. But that is in the way we are. We need to take responsibility."
A Week of Refereeing Rows
Three days earlier, Pochettino had been furious about Yoro's red card at Anfield, a dismissal the FA have now overturned.
The 4-0 defeat at Liverpool was his first game in charge. Across his first two games the 54-year-old, who was United States coach until last week, has seen two red cards, scored two goals and conceded six.
United have now had four players sent off in their past five games. They have won once in their past 13 matches and are still to win away from home in any competition this season.
What Comes Next
In the Champions League table United are 19th, two points in front of Roma, who sit just below the top 24, the places that guarantee passage to the next phase. Their next match is at home to RB Leipzig on 8 December, and Tielemans is suspended for it. Pochettino still has no win from his first two games in charge.
