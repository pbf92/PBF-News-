TITLE: GTA 6 — Grand Theft Auto 6 Breaks Records With $3.79B Opening Day
OUTLET: The Korea Daily

Rockstar Games’ GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto 6) has shattered historical video game launch records by generating approximately 5 trillion won ($3.79 billion) in sales on its very first day. This massive opening is roughly 4.7 times the record set by its predecessor, GTA 5, when it was released back in 2013.
Rockstar Games and its parent company, Take-Two Interactive, announced on the 20th (local time) that worldwide retail sales for GTA 6 reached $3.79 billion on launch day, based on company estimates up to the sales cutoff on the 19th. In comparison, GTA 5 recorded about $800 million on its opening day.
Pre-order sales prior to the official launch also reached an astounding 32.5 million copies. The company explained that this represents an all-time record for pre-orders across all platforms.
A 13-Year Wait Ends in Miami
GTA 6 arrives as the long-awaited sequel to GTA 5, ending a 13-year hiatus for the franchise. Set in the fictional state of Leonida and Vice City, which is inspired by Miami, Florida, the game follows the intertwined criminal lives of two protagonists, Jason and Lucia.
Critical acclaim has matched the commercial success. According to company announcements, GTA 6 scored an exceptional 99 out of 100 on the game review aggregation site Metacritic immediately following its release.
Platforms and Future Outlook
GTA 6 has initially launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, while a specific release date for the PC version has not yet been announced.
Anticipation for PC and Online Modes
Attention is now shifting toward the release timeline for the PC version and the online multiplayer mode. Reuters reported that some industry analysts expect a PC release in 2027. The multi-player online mode is widely regarded as a core revenue driver that will continue generating sustained sales long after the initial game purchase.
