TITLE: Bruce Arena rips U.S. Soccer as Pochettino exit exposes another coaching circus
OUTLET: Waking The Red

Former United States men’s national team head coach Bruce Arena has never been shy to call it as he sees it. 
Arena launched a scathing assessment of U.S. Soccer’s search for Mauricio Pochettino’s successor, questioning whether the federation has a clear idea of what it wants from the next person in charge.
Arena, who currently manages the San Jose Earthquakes, did not mince words when asked about the process following Pochettino’s departure for Manchester United.
“In my view, they don’t have a clue what they are doing,” Arena said Wednesday, according to ESPN.
The outspoken comments come at an awkward time for U.S. Soccer, which is once again faced with finding a new leader for its men’s national team after investing heavily in Pochettino and committing to a longer-term partnership with the Argentine.
Pochettino’s departure was confirmed on Nov. 13, with the former Tottenham, Chelsea and Paris Saint-Germain manager accepting the Manchester United job. He remained in charge for the Americans’ two Concacaf Nations League quarter-final matches against Haiti before leaving for England.
