AI Stocks are on Sale: A Generational Buying Opportunity
What if you could buy Amazon at $6.00 a share? Or Booking at $1.80?
It may seem like a dream to think of buying these two successful, well-known companies at such low share prices. But in 2001, you could.
Amazon DID sell at $6.00 a share in 2001. Booking, then known as Priceline, was $1.80, or split adjusted, $10.35 in 2001.
Yes, it was after the dotcom bubble had burst and had turned into a bust. By 2001, investors had run for the hills and had dumped their Internet stocks.
But if you were an investor willing to jump in and take a chance, with a multi-year time horizon, it became the buying opportunity of a lifetime.
How Do You Know When It’s a Buying Opportunity and Not a Trap?
After the Internet boom, there were plenty of companies that went on sale. Many of those were dotcoms like Webvan or Pets.com, which couldn’t hold on for a turnaround and ended up bankrupt.
But Amazon and Booking, both also dot-coms, did not fail.
Was their survival just luck or something else?
The 2 Keys to Finding Generational Buying Opportunities
What if an investor could look at certain fundamentals to determine if the company was a buying opportunity and could avoid the Webvans and find the Amazons?
There’s no sure thing in investing. There’s also no secret screen you can run that will give you only the Amazons during a market sell-off.
But you can stack the odds in your favor by looking at a few key fundamentals.

1) Is the company profitable?
This may seem basic but whether a company has profits, and therefore has earnings, is a key indicator of survival when times get tough.
In 2000, Booking actually lost $0.05 a share, but by 2001, it made $0.01 and never saw negative earnings again. It has grown the earnings every year since. In 2025, Booking made $9.14 per share. 2) Are earnings growing?
In 2022, technology and growth stocks plunged after the Federal Reserve sharply raised interest rates to cool the economy and inflation. This created a generational buying opportunity in many technology stocks like Shopify, which fell 86% from its highs in 2021.
In 2021, Shopify made $0.36 per share. But after the Federal Reserve raised rates, Shopify’s business took a hit and earnings went negative. In 2022, Shopify lost $0.39.
Still, the company didn’t stay down for very long. By 2023, it had turned the earnings around and saw a gain of $0.27. It has grown its earnings by the double digits or better every year since.
This was a buying opportunity in Shopify. Shares are up 368% from the October 2022 lows. Are AI Stocks a Generational Buying Opportunity?
Despite all the worrying, in November 2026, the AI Revolution doesn’t appear to be slowing down. But the stocks have retreated from their highs earlier this year, some dramatically so.
Is this another generational buying opportunity like buying Amazon and Booking in 2001 or Shopify in 2022, or a trap.
CoreWeave has been one of the AI Revolution companies that has sold off on worries about financing of the AI buildout. Shares have plunged 55% in the last three months.
A quick look at its earnings should tell the tale.
CoreWeave saw negative earnings of $1.52 per share in 2025 and the loss is expected to get bigger in 2026, at negative $3.79. Even into 2027, analysts still expect another year of negative earnings of $2.04.
It’s going the wrong way on earnings as well. They are sliding instead of growing. This is a red flag.
But what about those AI Revolution companies that are still expecting earnings growth?
Comfort Systems USA is one of those. It cools the data centers. Shares of Comfort Systems are down 39% in the last three months.
The company recently posted yet another earnings beat and raised its guidance. Earnings are expected to be up 74.1% to $50.29 in 2026 and analysts forecast another 32.2% earnings growth to $66.47 in 2027.
There are no negative earnings here. And the earnings growth outlook is still robust.
Use the Earnings Outlook as a Guide
Comfort Systems is a Zacks Rank #2 (Buy) stock as the analysts have been raising their earnings estimates in the last month. CoreWeave is a Zacks Rank #3 (Hold).
Remember, the Zacks Rank is a short-term recommendation of 1 to 3 months but investors can use it to find top stocks.
Future earnings are not guaranteed and the analysts will revise their estimates. But investors should still be monitoring the AI stock earnings to see if the slowdown everyone fears is real, and to find those companies that are true buying opportunities.
Because some of the AI Revolution companies will be the next Amazon, Booking, or Shopify.
