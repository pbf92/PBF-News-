TITLE: CEI Christmas market Cochrane set for Dec. 5 to Dec. 6
OUTLET: Cochrane Eagle

The Cochrane Ecological Institute (CEI) is hosting a Christmas tree sale and craft market on Dec. 5 and Dec. 6 at the CEI & Cochrane Wildlife Reserve Society.
According to CEI, the event will run from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. featuring freshly cut Christmas trees for the cost of a $40 donation to CEI. The craft market will feature local vendors.
"100% of all proceeds goes towards supporting wildlife rehabilitation at release Cochrane Ecological Institute," said CEI.
For more information, please visit the CEI website.
