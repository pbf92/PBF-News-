TITLE: 28% of GTA 6 buildings can be entered according to player findings
OUTLET: TweakTown

Ambitious gamers have apparently counted up every enterable building in GTA 6's in-game world, finding that around 28% of the houses, stores, hospitals and more can be explored.

GTA 6 has more buildings than any other game in the series with more than 5,500 various edifices, businesses, and facilities across Leonida. According to data from the GTA community, 1,563 of Leonida's structural locales can be interacted with in a meaningful way.
That's about 28% of the total buildings found in the game that can be entered, ranging from fast food restaurants all the way to high-scale hotels. On the map, these areas are marked dark blue; the light blue sections can't be entered.
This is a significant leap over past GTA games. Only 1% of the buildings in GTA 3 could be entered or interacted with, and GTA 4 pushed this up to about 40-50 buildings, depending on if mission interiors are counted. Finally, GTA 5 pushed this count to over 100...meaning GTA 6 has roughly 14x to 15x more explorable interiors than its predecessor.
A sizable portion of these areas contain unique encounters with NPCs (and sometimes even giant crabs). There are tons of easter eggs strewn throughout the world--Mikey, Don, Raph and Leo say hi--and it feels like a big interactive scavenger hunt.
