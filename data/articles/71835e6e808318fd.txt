Central Bank Decisions in December 2026: Dates, Times and What Markets Expect
Eight central banks announce rate decisions between Wednesday 9 and Friday 18 December 2026. The Fed goes first on 9 December at 20:00 CET. The Bank of Japan closes the run on 18 December. Most of these banks have raised rates this year, and markets price further hikes at several of them.
This page lists every date and time in Central European Time. It also shows the current rate and the latest sourced market expectation for each bank. Where a time or a figure has not been published yet, it is marked as to be confirmed.

Central bank decisions in December 2026: the short answer
- The Fed decides on Wednesday 9 December at 20:00 CET, with the press conference at 20:30 CET.
- The Bank of Canada announces its decision earlier that day, at 15:45 CET.
- The SNB publishes its decision on Thursday 10 December at 09:30 CET.
- The Riksbank follows on Wednesday 16 December at 09:30 CET.
- Thursday 17 December brings Norges Bank at 10:00 CET and the Bank of England at 13:00 CET.
- The ECB decides on the same Thursday at 14:15 CET, with Christine Lagarde's press conference at 14:45 CET.
- The Bank of Japan releases its statement on Friday 18 December, at a time still to be confirmed.
The wave started before December. On 26 November the Bank of Korea raised its rate to 3.25%, its third hike of 2026, as Korea JoongAng Daily reported.

Fed on 9 December
The FOMC meets on 8-9 December and publishes new economic projections. The statement comes at 2:00 p.m. ET, which is 20:00 CET. The press conference follows at 20:30 CET, according to the Fed's December calendar. Kevin Warsh led the last press conference on 28 October, when the target range was held at 3.75-4.00%, as the transcript shows.
Futures pricing points to a 25 bp hike as the base case. CME FedWatch showed about 95% for 25 bp and about 5% for 50 bp on 18 November, Regards of Wall Street reported. A sourced reading for 25 November is still to be confirmed.
Governor Waller argued for a larger step. "You have to go 50 in December", he told CNBC on 18 November, as Stocktwits reported.

Bank of Canada on the same day
The Bank of Canada announces at 15:45 CET on 9 December. It raised its rate to 2.50% on 28 October and said further increases could be needed, according to its press release.
SNB on 10 December: zero rate and the franc
The SNB releases its monetary policy assessment at 09:30 CET and holds a news conference at 10:00 CET, per its event schedule. The policy rate has been 0% since June 2025. The latest hold came on 24 September, when the SNB said it remained willing to act in the currency market as necessary, as its assessment states.
Swiss inflation rose to 1.5% in October, the highest in nearly three years. Economists have raised the odds of a December hike, investinglive reported, but a consensus figure is still to be confirmed.
The franc is the other side of the story. Safe-haven demand lifted the franc in October, and SNB officials have said they are prepared to act in currency markets if needed, Swissinfo reported. The euro reference rate for the Swiss franc stood at 0.9085 francs per euro on 25 November, according to the ECB.
Riksbank on 16 December
The Riksbank publishes its decision at 09:30 CET on 16 December. Its rate is 2.00% after a hike on 4 November, per the November decision.
ECB and Bank of England on 17 December
The ECB Governing Council meets on 16-17 December. The decision is due at 14:15 CET and the press conference at 14:45 CET. The ECB deposit rate is 2.50%, with the main refinancing rate at 2.65%, per the 29 October decision.
Economists agree on the size of the move. All 71 economists in a Reuters poll expect a 25 bp hike to 2.75%, and 46 of them see that as the peak, FXStreet reported. Bundesbank President Nagel said a December hike is "very likely to be necessary", investinglive reported.
The Bank of England is the meeting where markets and economists disagree most this December.
The Bank of England announces at 12:00 UK time, which is 13:00 CET. It raised Bank Rate to 4% on 5 November in an 8-1 vote, with one member voting for 50 bp, per the November minutes. After October inflation came in at 3.6%, markets put about 80% on a December hike, down from about 90%, Devdiscourse reported. A Reuters poll had 64 of 65 economists expecting a hold, Mortgage Introducer reported.
Norges Bank on 17 December
Norges Bank decides at 10:00 CET and publishes a new Monetary Policy Report. Its rate is 4.50%, and in November it said it was prepared to raise it if needed, per its press release.
Bonus date: the European Council
EU leaders meet on Thursday and Friday, 17-18 December. They are expected to approve Lagarde's successor there, Eunews reported. Lagarde steps down on 7 February 2027, according to the Banque de France. Our overview of the race covers the candidates.
Bank of Japan on 18 December: a third hike this year?
The Bank of Japan rate decision comes after a two-day meeting on 17-18 December. The BoJ lists the release time as undecided, so the CET time is still to be confirmed. The CET time will be added to this page once the BoJ confirms it.
The rate is 1.25% after a hold on 30 October. In a Reuters poll, 61 of 71 economists expect 1.50% in December, investinglive reported. That would be the first year with three hikes in 37 years. OIS pricing stood at about 80% on 26 November, up from about 70% earlier that week. On the same day, board member Tamura said the rate should reach a neutral level "soon", Trading and Investment News reported.
Governor Ueda speaks in Nagoya on Monday 30 November at 02:05 CET, per the BoJ release schedule. Markets may read such a speech for hints about the December decision. USD/JPY was close to the 160 area on 26 November, according to Trading and Investment News. Our USD/JPY analysis looks at that level in detail.
