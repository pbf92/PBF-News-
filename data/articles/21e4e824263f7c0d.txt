Range Impact 3Q 2026: Revenue $620.5K, EPS ($0.03) — 10-Q Summary
Range Impact's revenue rose 9% year over year, while its net loss widened, driven mainly by a $1.8M credit loss on a note receivable and higher non-cash depreciation, amortization and accretion expenses. The company attributed the revenue increase primarily to $20,833 from a lease development term extension.

Business Highlights
- Revenue mix: Coal royalties accounted for most revenue; the company also recognized $20,833 from a lease development term extension.
- Operating costs: Operating loss widened primarily because of higher non-cash depreciation, amortization and accretion, largely tied to sites added in December 2025.
- Project development: A 50%-owned joint venture plans a power generation and data center facility at the Fola mine site.
- Consulting agreements: Agreements for a Premier-Cambrian mine reclamation and repurposing project were terminated September 30, 2026, after the project was deemed no longer feasible.
