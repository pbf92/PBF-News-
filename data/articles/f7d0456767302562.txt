TITLE: GTA 6 PS5 Update 1.002.002 Rolling Out Now
OUTLET: PlayStation LifeStyle

Rockstar Games is rolling out a new Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) PS5 update in phases. Dated November 23, 2026, update 1.002.002 varies in size according to platform. On the PS5, the patch weighs roughly 600 MB. Xbox players are reporting a larger update, weighing around 1.2-1.6 GB.
What does GTA 6 PS5 update 1.002.002 do?
Rockstar Games hasn’t released any patch notes for today’s update, but some players have confirmed that GTA 6’s money glitch still works for them, so that seems safe.
Unfortunately, unlike the PS4, PS5 game updates don’t come with patch notes, so we’ll have to wait for the developer to release information. We’ll update this article with the patch notes when we have them.
In the meantime, we’ve seen some reports of GTA 6 gameplay feeling smoother and some notable visual improvements after applying the patch. However, this could be a placebo effect.
Since the update is being rolled out across all platforms, I don’t think it has anything to do with QSSR on the base PS5 either.
If you don’t have the update yet, don’t fret. The patch is being staggered by region. I can confirm that GTA 6 update 1.002.002 isn’t live in the U.K. yet.
