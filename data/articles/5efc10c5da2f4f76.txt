TITLE: Can Onto Innovation Sustain Its Growth Momentum in 2027?
OUTLET: TradingView

Can Onto Innovation Sustain Its Growth Momentum in 2027?
Onto Innovation ONTO is entering 2027 with strong growth momentum, powered by increasing investments in AI infrastructure, advanced semiconductor manufacturing, HBM production, next-generation logic and memory technologies, and advanced packaging technologies. Revenue reached $410 million in the third quarter, up 19% sequentially and nearly 90% year over year. Power revenue doubled, driven by silicon photonics and power components.
For the fourth quarter of 2026, management projects revenue of $430-$450 million, implying more than 7% sequential growth at the midpoint. That midpoint would bring fiscal 2026 revenue to approximately $1.49 billion, representing growth above 45%. Non-GAAP gross margin is guided to 57.7-58.3%, with a non-GAAP operating margin of 33.5-34.5%. Management expects sequential revenue growth through at least the first half of 2027. Gross margins are expected to exit 2026 above 58%, with quarterly increases of around 50 basis points projected for 2027.
Management expects panel revenue to double in 2026, while existing backlog supports growth exceeding 40% in 2027, with orders extending into 2028. Overall, advanced-packaging revenue is expected to increase more than 80% in 2026. It anticipates further growth exceeding 35% in 2027 as customers increase spending on advanced interconnects and packaging process control.
A key indicator supporting Onto Innovation's 2027 outlook is its rapidly expanding backlog. At the end of the third quarter, its backlog exceeded $1.3 billion, representing growth of 20% during the quarter. Customers are placing orders earlier to secure equipment for multiyear expansion plans, with some commitments extending into 2028, particularly in emerging packaging applications. Management indicated that this backlog reflects customer investment schedules rather than longer equipment delivery lead times. Multi-year customer investment plans could support a more durable revenue trajectory. There is high confidence in the logic and DRAM markets, with more than 35% growth forecasted in 2027.
Intensifying Competition Challenges ONTO’s Growth Prospects
KLA Corporation KLAC benefits from AI infrastructure spending, which broadens demand for process control across leading-edge logic, memory and advanced packaging. It raised its calendar 2026 wafer equipment market outlook, including advanced packaging, to the mid-$150 billion range and expects the market to exceed $200 billion in calendar 2027. KLA anticipates second-half calendar 2026 revenues to grow approximately 25% over the first half, supported by improving supply capacity, expanding backlog and sustained AI-related investments. For the fiscal second quarter, KLA expects revenues of $4.7 billion (+/-$200 million). Non-GAAP earnings are projected at $1.44 per share (+/-11 cents).
Applied Materials AMAT benefits from AI-led investment in leading-edge foundry-logic, DRAM and advanced packaging, where rising process complexity supports demand for its equipment. It expects a strong 2027, with wafer fab equipment demand projected to grow over 30%, driven by AI-related markets. For the fiscal first quarter, revenue guidance is $11.1 billion, up 58%, with a focus on growth in DRAM, logic and packaging markets. Customers plan to ramp capacity steadily through 2027 and beyond. Applied expects mid-teens growth of about 15% for its MSS segment driven by increased installed base and tool price hikes. AGS operating margins are projected to grow into the high 30s-low 40s%, contributing to long-term margin targets.
ONTO Price Performance, Valuation and Estimates
ONTO’s shares have lost 20% in the past six months compared with the Zacks Nanotechnology industry’s decline of 21.7%.
On a forward price-to-earnings basis, ONTO’s shares trade at 17.08X, higher than the industry’s 3.31X.
The Zacks Consensus Estimate for ONTO’s earnings has moved up for both 2026 and 2027 over the past 60 days.
