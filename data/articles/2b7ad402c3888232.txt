TITLE: The Nasdaq surges 6% and the S&P 500 rebounds 5% as Nvidia stock soars on Jensen Huang's return
OUTLET: Quartz

Nvidia $NVDA reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, a move that lifted U.S. stocks in Monday morning trading. The Nasdaq $NDAQ Composite climbed 6%, while the S&P 500 rose almost 5% and the Dow Jones Industrial Average was up 1,445 points, a 3% increase.
Nvidia stock, which had been hammered in recent weeks, rose a whopping 35% in Monday morning trading after the announcement of Huang's return, adding more than $1 trillion in market capitalization compared to Friday's close.
President Donald Trump praised Nvidia's decision, calling Huang a "Great Patriot and National Treasure" in a social media post on Monday.
"The Country needs Jensen's leadership at Nvidia in order to ensure the Success and Prosperity of not only the Company, but the American Economy," Earlier on Monday, Trump had written in another message that he wanted the board to bring Huang back, which gave the stock an initial lift.
Other AI-related stocks climbed with Nvidia. Micron $MU Technology rose 14%, CoreWeave 29% and Advanced Micro Devices 9%. Alphabet $GOOGL gained 4%, while Microsoft $MSFT and Meta $META Platforms each added 3%.
"Jensen Huang's return restores and rebuilds confidence in the AI ecosystem: its major suppliers, its core building blocks, and its demand outlook," said Hendi Susanto, portfolio manager and research analyst at Gabelli Funds, according to CNBC.
Huang's return came less than two weeks after he resigned following revelations of multiple affairs, including with a suspected Chinese spy.
The rebound Monday follows a steep selloff last week. All three major averages ended Friday in a correction, having fallen 10% from their recent peaks, and the Nasdaq was below where it started the year. The Nasdaq-100 index finished Friday nearly 20% below its most recent peak, edging toward bear-market territory.
The Nasdaq and Dow each posted their steepest weekly drop in over six years last week, while the S&P 500 saw its biggest since April 2025, according to CNBC. Anthropic's debut on Thursday did not stop the slide.
U.S. markets will stay shut Thursday in observance of Thanksgiving. Friday's session is abbreviated and will end at 1 p.m. ET.
