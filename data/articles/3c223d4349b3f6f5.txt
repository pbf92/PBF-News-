TITLE: GTA 6 Has “Deep Backstories for Almost Everything,” Says Rockstar
OUTLET: GamingBolt

If it’s not the handcrafted NPCs, each with their own unique clothing styles and body types, it’s their moods and personalities that truly make Leonida feel alive in Grand Theft Auto 6. Naturally, Rockstar Games went the extra mile, creating “deep backstories for almost everything” to ensure the world felt alive.
As Rockstar’s open world senior design director Steven Messinger revealed to IGN, “A big part of it was establishing deep backstories for almost everything, even if they were never meant to be fully explained to the player, and then really sticking to those backstories. For example, our gang compounds have a complex drug network we’ve fully mapped out: where the drugs come from, who brings them in and how, how they’re distributed to each compound, what happens at each stop, and how they eventually reach the street-level buyer.”
Just to note: This isn’t lore players could miss, as Messinger says they’ll only “pick up on bits and pieces of this.” However, “having that structure in the background as something we consistently refer to helps everything feel more real, like this big machine is always running even when players aren’t there.”
Of course, those research trips to Florida certainly helped, as the development team sought to recreate the “little stories” happening everywhere. “We had a lot of fun trying to capture the raw energy and vibrant chaos that Florida is known for. By immersing ourselves in a number of these locations on reference trips, spending time with locals, experiencing as much as possible firsthand, we gathered a direct sense of what would make Leonida feel truly alive.
“Everywhere you look, little stories constantly unfold. It could be as simple as what someone’s wearing, how fit they are, how they walk and talk, who they’re with, what tunes they’re blasting as they cruise down the street. We tried to match that in-game as much as possible, hopefully resulting in something that feels full of life,” states John Macpherson, senior design director of world and population.
Perhaps most impressive of all when designing its NPCs is that they’re on the same level of quality as Jason and Lucia. As senior technical art director Valmor Garcia notes, this is a massive step up from Grand Theft Auto 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, and considering how detailed the latter’s NPCs could be with their unique stories, that’s saying something.
“We evolved from monolithic NPCs built to visual standards that were lower than the main cast in GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, to fully fleshed-out characters with singular awareness and visual identities. They are driven by dynamic moods, personalities, unique anatomy, facial features, and dialogue that are visually on par with our protagonists, Jason and Lucia.”
