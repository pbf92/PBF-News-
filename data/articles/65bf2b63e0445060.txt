TITLE: Grand Theft Auto 6 features a hyper-realistic and localized dynamic weather system
OUTLET: WDRB

LOUISVILLE, Ky. (WDRB+WAVE) —
Grand Theft Auto 6 (GTA VI) is set to be released this Thursday, November 19th, and is expected to be one of if not the biggest video game release of all time. Perhaps you’ve heard of it. So, why is a meteorologist (Bryce) writing about it?
Well, besides liking to play video games myself, there is actually a very cool addition to this video game that has to do with the weather!
Image: Rockstar Games
GTA 6 New Weather System
Grand Theft Auto 6 will feature a hyper-realistic, re-engineered, and localized dynamic weather system! In particular, this system is designed to simulate Florida’s tropical climate. Why Florida? Well this version of GTA is based in the fictional state of Leonida. That is basically the games fictional counterpart to the state of Florida. Much of the game is based specifically in “Vice City”, or in real life...Miami!
We all know what kind of weather Florida has, right? It’s very tropical, there’s hurricanes, a chance of *scattered* storms and downpours almost every afternoon in the summer months; well, GTA 6 developer Rockstar Games has made this games weather system to reflect that.
Image: Rockstar Games
Localized Weather
In past GTA games, and many video games that are open world and outdoors, the weather is pretty uniform across the entire area/map. If it’s raining, it’s raining everywhere, if it’s sunny, it’s sunny everywhere. Well we know that Florida isn’t always like that.
Individual cloudscapes, or cloud formations, will move independently across the state of “Leonida”. This means that where your character is standing on the map could be bright and sunny, but out in the distance you may see a downpour or a thunderstorm occurring on a different part of the map simultaneously.
Speaking of thunderstorms, Rockstar’s vice president Art for Lighting and Rendering, Owen Shepherd, even was quoted in an interview with The Game Informer saying that “vertiginous cumulonimbus clouds with sporadic lightning” are in the game to reflect the summertime storms that often happen in what we know as Florida.
Image: Rockstar Games
Tropical Weather
Florida also gets a lot of tropical weather as well, including hurricanes. While not confirmed specifically, Shepherd did say that the game would introduce severe atmospheric changes, bringing “heavy clouds, strong winds, dense atmospherics, and a lot of rain.”
It’s not just the rain either, the wind will also be very intricate in how it affects certain things and how it moves. The topography and buildings in the city will affect wind patterns just like in real life. Even the boats on the water will be rocked heavily during stronger wind gusts.
Image: Rockstar Games
Even flying planes or helicopters into severe weather and storms causes turbulence that makes them harder to fly, just like why pilots try to avoid them if possible in real life. It’s not just your character either, even the NPC’s will seek shelter in downpours and worsening weather.
