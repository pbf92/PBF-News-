QuantumScape extends losses to seventh straight session
QuantumScape QS widened its losing streak to the seventh straight session on Wednesday after registering steep declines in recent trading. The shares ended 2.74% lower at $3.19.
Between November 10 and November 17, shares of the lithium metal battery maker plunged more than 26% compared to a 2.4% fall in the broader markets.
On a year-to-date basis, QuantumScape has tumbled nearly 69% compared to a 7% rise in the S&P500 index.
In October, the company reported a narrower third-quarter loss of $0.14 per share. QuantumScape ended the quarter with $819.8M in liquidity and reaffirmed its full-year adjusted EBITDA loss guidance of $250M to $275M and capital expenditure outlook of $27M to $37M.
As per Seeking Alpha’s quant rating, QS has a Hold rating with a score of 2.50 out of 5. The firm has been rated an F for profitability and momentum, while it has been graded a C for valuation.
Seeking Alpha analysts have assigned a Hold rating for QS with one Hold and one Sell call. Meanwhile, Wall Street analysts have seven Hold calls and 2 Strong Sell ratings for the stock.
