TITLE: Claude Maker Anthropic Opens 34% Above IPO Price on Nasdaq
OUTLET: 80 Level

At the opening price, Anthropic's valuation exceeded $2 trillion.
Mijansk786 / Shutterstock.com
Anthropic, the maker of the Claude chatbot, began trading its shares on the Nasdaq on November 19, 2026. The stock opened at $111.50, well above the $83 IPO price.
According to a TechCrunch report, the IPO price, set the day before, was already above the $70–$78 range the company had projected. At the opening price, Anthropic's valuation reached more than $2 trillion, which put it ahead of Meta, Tesla, and Walmart and behind only Apple, Amazon, Alphabet, Nvidia, and Microsoft.
The price kept moving after the open. Trading under the ticker ANT began at 11:50 a.m. New York time, and by 1:55 p.m., shares were at $95.69, according to Al Jazeera. Superpower Daily reported that the decline coincided with a broader market drop, with the Nasdaq and the S&P 500 each down 1.5% at midday and the Dow Jones Industrial Average down 1.8%. The same report quotes IPOX associate Lukas Muehlbauer, who said some buyers may not want to miss a rare chance to own a leading AI company despite uncertain long-term profitability. Analyst Dan Ives called the IPO a near-term win for technology that should help restore confidence in AI growth.
As of writing, the price is about $89:
In October, Anthropic filed a registration statement on Form S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission for a proposed IPO of its Class A shares, which it planned to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. The company has also been expanding into new areas; for instance, it set up a wet lab for biology research.
