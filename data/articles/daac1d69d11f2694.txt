TITLE: Grand Theft Auto VI sets $3.79 billion first-day sales record
OUTLET: The American Bazaar

“Grand Theft Auto VI” generated an estimated $3.79 billion in worldwide retail sales on its first day, setting a new record for an entertainment launch, Rockstar Games announced on Nov. 20.
The latest instalment in the popular video game franchise launched on Nov. 19, drawing a strong commercial response after years of anticipation, Rockstar said the first-day sales figure was based on its worldwide estimates through the end of launch day.
The company also reported that the game had sold 32.5 million units before its official release, setting a new prerelease sales record across platforms.
“We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you,” Sam Houser, president and founder of Rockstar Games, said in the announcement.

The figures mark a substantial opening for a franchise whose previous instalment, “Grand Theft Auto V,” has remained a major commercial success since its 2013 release.
The predecessor generated $1 billion in retail sales within its first three days, according to Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar’s parent company.
“GTA V” has since sold more than 230 million copies worldwide, while the Grand Theft Auto franchise has recorded more than 470 million units in cumulative sales, according to the publisher.
Market research firm Sensor Tower released a separate estimate earlier Nov. 20, projecting that “GTA VI” had generated approximately $2.36 billion in revenue from about 24.6 million units sold.
That estimate differs from Rockstar’s subsequently announced retail sales figure and is based on a separate methodology.
The new game takes players back to Vice City, Rockstar’s fictional version of Miami, within the fictional state of Leonida.

The release marks the first new mainline entry in the series in 13 years as its arrival followed an extended promotional campaign and sustained interest from players, with PlayStation announcing earlier in November that the title had become the most-preordered game in the history of its digital storefront.
