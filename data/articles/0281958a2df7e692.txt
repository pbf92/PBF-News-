American Salars Announces $210,000 Debt Settlement
(TheNewswire)
VANCOUVER, BC, NOVEMBER 27th, 2026 – TheNewswire - AMERICAN SALARS LITHIUM INC. ("AMERICAN SALARS" OR THE "COMPANY") (CSE: USLI, OTC: USLIF, FWB: Z3P, WKN: A3E2NY) announces that it has agreed to settle $210,000 in outstanding debt by issuing units of the Company to six creditors, including certain members of management (the “Debt Settlement”).
The units will be issued at a deemed price of $0.165 per unit, with each unit consisting of one common share of the Company and one common share purchase warrant. Each warrant will entitle the holder to acquire one additional common share at an exercise price of $0.21 per share for a period of 36 months from the date of issuance.
The Debt Settlement is intended to preserve the Company's cash resources while satisfying certain outstanding obligations through the issuance of equity securities.
All securities issued pursuant to the debt settlement will be subject to a hold period of four months and one day as required under applicable securities legislation.
ABOUT AMERICAN SALARS
American Salars Lithium is an exploration company focused on exploring and developing high-value battery metals projects to meet the demands of the advancing electric vehicle market.
