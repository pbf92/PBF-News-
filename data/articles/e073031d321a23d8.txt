Manchester United's woeful start to the 2026/27 season has some former Red Devil pundits losing their marbles on the air in England.
11 matches into the season, the giant Premier League continues to flounder near the bottom of the table, sitting 19th with just eight points and one lone victory on the year so far. Michael Carrick paid for the club's struggles with his job as manager, replaced by former USMNT boss Mauricio Pochettino after the international break.
Pochettino's debut went horribly, with United falling down to 10 men in the opening stage of their rivalry match at Anfield on Sunday, beaten by Liverpool 4-0 while playing a man down nearly the whole way.
The latest dismal result had Sky Sports pundit Gary Neville apoplectic of the team's defensive performance, and his reaction was one for the ages.

Gary Neville slaps himself in angry Man United tirade
Following the defeat to Liverpool on Sunday, former Manchester United star and current pundit Gary Neville had an animated rant about the team's state of play that caught the attention of fans for a number of reasons.
First was the content of his tirade, pointing a harsh finger at Man United's defense for their lackadaisical attitude.
"I never see any of them on their toes, alert, looking like they've got something fixed in here," Neville said on Sky Sports before pointing to his head. "I never see any of them look like that. They look like that," he said, donning an inattentive look.
Then, came the shocking twist to Neville's rant. "What's going on? Wake up!" he shouted, before slapping himself in the face. He shouted the line again and slapped himself again, which sent co-host Jamie Carragher into a fit of laughter. Neville then finished by saying "you play for Man United!"
Man United have chance for holiday season turnaround
There's still an opportunity for the Red Devils to turn things around before the Christmas season truly arrives.
Manchester United have a pretty soft schedule through the early portion of December. They host Brentford to close out November, and then visit a struggling Newcastle, host newly-promoted Coventry City, and visit a relegation-threatened Crystal Palace.
Then, they visit Arsenal in a test of United's potential progression under Mauricio Pochettino by that point, before games against Nottingham Forest and Sunderland to close out 2026.
If Pochettino can have a real influence on this squad, it should show up in this period.
