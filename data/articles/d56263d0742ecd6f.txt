TITLE: Rubrik, Inc. Class A (RBRK) Receives a Buy from CIBC
OUTLET: The Globe and Mail

CIBC analyst Todd Coupland reiterated a Buy rating on Rubrik, Inc. Class A today and set a price target of $120.00.
According to TipRanks, Coupland is a 4-star analyst with an average return of 9.0% and a 45.63% success rate. Coupland covers the Technology sector, focusing on stocks such as Celestica, Shopify, and BlackBerry.
Rubrik, Inc. Class A has an analyst consensus of Strong Buy, with a price target consensus of $126.65.
Based on Rubrik, Inc. Class A’s latest earnings release for the quarter ending July 31, the company reported a quarterly revenue of $427.26 million and a GAAP net loss of $61.78 million. In comparison, last year the company earned a revenue of $309.86 million and had a GAAP net loss of $95.93 million
