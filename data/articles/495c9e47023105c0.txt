Osimhen is on Manchester United's radar! A definitive decision from Galatasaray
25.11.2026 23:51
Galatasaray's star player Victor Osimhen has risen to the top of Manchester United's transfer list with his impressive performance, but the yellow-red management has absolutely rejected the English club's mid-season transfer offer.
Victor Osimhen, who is spending his third season in a Galatasaray shirt, has climbed to the top of European giants' transfer lists with his impressive performances.
POCHETTINO INSISTENT, MANAGEMENT DETERMINED
At Manchester United, who appointed Mauricio Pochettino to the managerial seat and lost 4-0 to Liverpool in the Argentine coach's first match, striker reinforcement has become a priority. After Pochettino requested that the forward line be definitively strengthened in January, the English giant put the 27-year-old Nigerian star at the top of its list.
GALATASARAY DETERMINED NOT TO SELL
According to the news in TEAMtalk, although Manchester United made attempts to add Osimhen to its squad during the winter transfer window, the Galatasaray management is absolutely not warm to parting with its star striker mid-season. The English representative, who has not given up on the transfer, is expected to knock on the door of the yellow-reds again in the summer transfer window.
