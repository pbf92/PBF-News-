TITLE: Three Points: Liverpool vs. Manchester United
OUTLET: The Busby Babe

Three points of emphasis for Manchester United’s next match against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.
Energy and Intensity
If there’s one thing Mauricio Pochettino’s sides are known for, it’s their energy and intensity. These qualities have always been a hallmark of the Premier League, but it bears reminding that Pochettino’s sides have often topped metrics like distance covered in the most intense league in the world. Now, these metrics in isolation aren’t very useful, but they give us an idea of what to expect from United in the coming months. Liverpool certainly won’t lack this on Sunday, so it’ll be interesting to see whether United can match them on day one under the new boss.
The Defensive Line
Mauricio Pochettino has had to adapt his methods over the years, but there’s no doubt he’ll try to get United to close down sides quickly, which means defending a bit higher than United have in recent years. One of the big questions for him is whether he starts going about things his way from day one against Liverpool at Anfield. This is a risky strategy for this game because the players are low on confidence and are 18th in the table. Things could get bad pretty quickly if the players aren’t equipped to play in his image against one of the better sides in the league, who also happen to be United’s arch rivals.
Another worry is that picking players who are more suited to this approach haven’t been the most reliable. United’s young defenders Leny Yoro and Ayden Heaven have had a tough start to the year for different reasons. Heaven has looked isolated on many occasions this season, which has led to a drop in confidence, whereas Yoro looks like a more improved player this season but still can’t complete 90 minutes on most days without committing an error that leads to a goal. Finding a way without dropping points will not be easy.
Senne Lamens
The young keeper was one of the bright spots of last season, and was considered by many not just as one of United’s most consistent performers but one of the best signings of the Premier League last season. He has been very busy in goal this season and has made some top saves in many games, but he has also made some blunders that ultimately cost Michael Carrick his job. This isn’t great considering reliability was one of his standout qualities last season. In fact, even Sir Alex heaped praise on him, which isn’t something United’s legendary manager has done too often since retirement. He needs to get back to his best and will certainly be tested against Liverpool.
