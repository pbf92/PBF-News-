TITLE: GTA 6 Studio's Next Game Is A Sequel We've Waited All Been Begging For
OUTLET: ScreenRant

Rockstar has broken numerous records with Grand Theft Auto 6, making it the biggest entertainment release of all time. It's a remarkable feat, one that will undoubtedly go unmatched by traditional game development for potentially decades. Naturally, the important thing to do now is to relish this once-in-a-blue-moon achievement, to immerse ourselves within Leonida, enjoy the shock and awe of its ridiculously detailed world, and not busy ourselves too much with thoughts of Rockstar's next game.
However, if, for whatever reason, you're already contemplating the future of GTA 7 while playing Grand Theft Auto 6, or indeed what game Rockstar could be producing next, then you're in luck. After mere days of being available, leakers have already seemingly confirmed what GTA 6's successor will be. The good news is that it looks set to be a sequel to an iconic Rockstar game that never quite got the appreciation it deserved.
With GTA 6's record-breaking success ostensibly setting Rockstar up financially for the next few years, it is no surprise that the studio is taking a somewhat big risk on its next game. At least, that appears to be the case according to a now three-year-old leak that has begun to resurface. Back in 2023, a comprehensive leak was posted to the GTA Forums website that broke down not just GTA 6 details, but also Rockstar's lineup of upcoming games. Crucially, it is claimed that the studio's next game will be a sequel to L.A. Noire.
The reason this leak is being given any degree of credence all these years later is that it got several key details about the game correct, including details surrounding GTA 6's multiple endings. While there are inaccurate details sprinkled throughout the leak, one could easily brush this off as being gameplay features or mechanics that were altered or removed altogether between the leak's release and GTA 6's launch day. If one were to take it at face value, it would mean that we're getting an L.A. Noire sequel in 2030.
That is both surprising and unsurprising. On the one hand, Rockstar didn't develop the original L.A. Noire. The now-defunct Australian studio, Team Bondi, did most of the development work, with Rockstar publishing it. Rockstar retains the IP, meaning that it could be producing its own sequel or spin-off, but without any of the original creative leads, it may take a very different direction. There are also many other Rockstar titles that fans have been clamoring to see get a sequel, most notably Bully. It was meant to get a sequel at one point, but unfortunately that project was canceled.
On the other hand, with the advent of new motion capture technology, the revolutionary mechanics in the original L.A. Noire would be more achievable than ever before. A sequel would allow Rockstar to deliver a game between GTA 6 and potentially Red Dead Redemption 3 while finally delivering the definitive version of L.A. Noire. This leak should still be taken with a grain of salt, as there's every chance it's out of date or altogether incorrect. However, should it transpire to be true, it would be very exciting to get an L.A. Noire sequel after GTA 6.
