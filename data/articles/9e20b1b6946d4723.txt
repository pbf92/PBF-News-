Auddia announces reverse stock split to regain Nasdaq compliance
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- Auddia announced a reverse stock split of its common stock as the company seeks to increase its share price and regain compliance with Nasdaq's minimum bid price requirement.
- The company said the reverse stock split is intended to lift its share price above Nasdaq's $1.00 minimum bid price threshold for continued listing.
- The stock price fell about 3% on Thursday pre-market trade.
