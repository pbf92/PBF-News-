Driven Brands Received Notice of Noncompliance
On November 20, 2026, Driven Brands received notice from Nasdaq that it is not in compliance with Listing Rule 5250(c)(1) because it has delayed filing its Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 26, 2026. The notice has no immediate effect on trading; the company has until January 19, 2027, to submit a compliance plan, and Nasdaq may allow until May 10, 2027, to regain compliance.
Original SEC Filing: Driven Brands Holdings Inc. [ DRVN ] - 8-K - Nov. 23, 2026
