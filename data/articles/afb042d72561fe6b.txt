TITLE: Shaw set for Man Utd return at Sporting
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United defender Luke Shaw could be in line for a comeback in Wednesday's Champions League encounter with Sporting.
Ever-present in the Premier League last season, Shaw has missed eight games through injury since the draw with Tottenham on 10 October.
However, the 31-year-old was present for Tuesday's open section of training at Carrington before the trip to Lisbon.
Shaw's return would be a major boost for new boss Mauricio Pochettino, who worked with the England international at the start of his career with Southampton.
Marcus Rashford was still absent, so with fellow forward Matheus Cunha suspended following his red card in the dramatic 3-2 win over Roma three weeks ago, Joshua Zirkzee might be given a rare start in the number nine role.
Pochettino began the 15-minute section that was opened to the media by getting his squad together in a huddle for a short chat.
The Argentine is due to speak to the media at the Jose Alvalade Stadium at 18:15 GMT, where he will be accompanied by skipper Bruno Fernandes, who is facing Sporting for the first time since his £47m move to United in January 2020.
