FS Bancorp Authorizes Up to $15 Million Share Repurchase Program
Less than 1 min read
FS Bancorp authorized a share repurchase program of up to $15 million of its common stock over a 12-month period.
Key Highlights:
- The program runs through November 24, 2027, and permits repurchases in open-market or privately negotiated transactions.
- Repurchases may begin no sooner than the second trading day after the public announcement.
- Management will determine the timing and amount of purchases based on factors including market conditions and alternative uses for capital.
- The program does not obligate FS Bancorp to buy any particular number of shares and may be suspended, terminated, or modified at any time.
- Approximately $5,000 remains authorized under the repurchase plan announced August 24, 2026.
Original SEC Filing: FS Bancorp, Inc. [ FSBW ] - 8-K - Nov. 24, 2026
