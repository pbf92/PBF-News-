TITLE: How long is GTA 6? Everything we know about the main campaign length
OUTLET: Diario AS

One of the biggest questions surrounding Grand Theft Auto VI is how long it will take to complete its story. Rockstar Games has yet to reveal an official estimate for the game’s main campaign, but a recent statement from one of its developers offers an interesting look at the scale of the upcoming adventure.
How long does it take to beat GTA 6?
As of November 2026, Rockstar has not confirmed how many hours players will need to complete the main campaign of Grand Theft Auto VI. The closest reference comes from Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North, who discussed the game’s development in an interview with The New York Times. Nelson revealed that he completed a playthrough of GTA 6 in February 2026, which took him approximately 80 hours. The experience included the main story alongside some optional objectives with narrative consequences.
However, there’s an important distinction: the 80-hour figure does not represent the time required to finish the main campaign alone, nor does it establish how long a typical player will spend in Leonida. Instead, it provides an indication of the amount of content Rockstar is building into its next open-world adventure. The final playtime will depend on how players approach the experience, including how much time they spend exploring, completing optional activities, and interacting with the world beyond the main missions.
How long are previous Grand Theft Auto and Red Dead Redemption games?
To put GTA 6’s reported playtime into perspective, it is worth looking at how long it takes to complete some of Rockstar’s previous releases. According to HowLongToBeat, which collects completion times submitted by players, the main campaigns of the most recent mainline Grand Theft Auto games have generally taken between 15 and 32 hours.
