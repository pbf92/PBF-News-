TITLE: Liverpool v Manchester United: 13 stats to know ahead of Anfield clash
OUTLET: Liverpool FC

Andoni Iraola's side return to action following the November international break with a fixture against the Red Devils at Anfield.
Check out key facts and figures about the encounter below…
The last 11 Premier League meetings between Liverpool and United have seen 44 goals scored, with both sides netting in four of the previous five clashes.
Cody Gakpo has scored more Premier League goals versus United than he has against any other opponent (five), netting in each of his last three appearances.
This is the latest into a league season (matchday 11) that the clubs have met with one of them in the relegation zone since 1972-73, when bottom side United beat league leaders Liverpool 2-0 at Old Trafford in November.
This will be Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge of United – the first-ever manager to see his first league match with the club come against Liverpool.
Just one of United's last nine managers (including caretakers) has lost his first Premier League game of a spell in charge of the club (won four, drawn four).
Virgil van Dijk is set to start a 50th consecutive Premier League game for the Reds, which would be the fourth time he has had a run of 50+ unbroken starts in the competition – the joint-most by an outfield player, along with Sylvain Distin.
Aged 35 years and 137 days, Van Dijk would also become the oldest outfield player to start 50+ consecutive Premier League games for a single club, overtaking Kevin Davies for Bolton Wanderers (67 in a row aged 34 years and 175 days).
United are yet to win a Premier League away game this season (drawn three, lost two) and could lose successively on the road for the first time since September 2025.
After winning this fixture last season, United are aiming for successive league wins at Anfield for the first time since January 2016 under Louis van Gaal.
Pochettino has won just one of his eight previous Premier League matches against Liverpool at Anfield (drawn two, lost five), coming in his first visit with Southampton in September 2013 (1-0).
In the Premier League, Pochettino has lost more games to the Reds than any other side (eight), with his lowest points per game also coming against them (0.88 – minimum five matches).
Milos Kerkez could make his 50th start for Liverpool across all competitions.
After 218 previous meetings between the clubs, the cumulative goals tally stands at 294 for the Reds and 296 for United.
