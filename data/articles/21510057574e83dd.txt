TITLE: InMobi Announced the Launch of InMobi Accelerate
OUTLET: MarTech Cube

Seven specialized AI agents plan, build, launch and optimize omnichannel campaigns across major ad platforms including Google Ads, Meta Ads, Microsoft Advertising, TikTok Ads and ChatGPT Ads
InMobi, a global technology company shaping the future of agentic commerce and advertising, today launched InMobi Accelerate, a self-serve agentic AI advertising platform that plans, builds, launches and measures campaigns across major advertising platforms from one dashboard.
Accelerate is built for small and medium-sized businesses that want to grow without standing up a large in-house marketing function, and for the agencies that serve them. It runs on seven specialized AI agents that work together across a campaign, each taking on a distinct responsibility.
The agents are a business analyst, audience strategist, media planner, copywriter, creative designer, launch engineer and optimizer. A business can begin with its website and a campaign objective. From there the system analyzes the company, develops audiences and a media plan, creates campaign assets and assembles budgets for review.
“AI agents change the economics of expertise,” said Rohit Dosi, VP and GM of InMobi Accelerate. “A growing business should be able to run a disciplined advertising program without hiring seven specialists first, and an agency should be able to take on more clients without adding repetitive work at the same rate. Accelerate puts that capability in the hands of the people running the campaigns.”
Accelerate arrives as the day-to-day demands of performance advertising outgrow what most small and medium-sized businesses can staff. Running one program now means working across several platforms at once, each with its own interface, bidding logic and reporting. The harder question is what all that spending actually produced. Boards and clients want to see the return across every channel together, and that view has to be assembled from reports each platform writes for itself.
Research conducted by NewtonX for InMobi among 800 U.S. SMB advertisers and agencies in May 2026 found that 93 percent cannot confidently measure performance across the platforms they use. More than 70 percent run campaigns on three or more platforms, and only 4 percent can get a campaign live the same day they decide to run it.
One View of Performance Across Platforms
Each advertising platform reports on the activity inside its own environment, which is what it is built to do. Assembling those reports into a single picture falls to the advertiser or their agency, and it is slow work that is difficult to get right. Accelerate uses InMobi’s first-party pixel and universal conversion tracking to deduplicate conversion events across connected platforms and produce one cross-channel view. InMobi calls that measure True ROAS (return on ad spend).
Those results feed Mobius, InMobi’s unified intelligence system for agentic advertising decisioning, which forecasts advertising options against the outcome an advertiser wants and uses campaign history and performance signals to recommend or execute changes within the parameters an advertiser has set. Mobius processes more than 25 trillion predictions and evaluates more than 200 billion advertising opportunities on an average day, and each campaign informs the planning of the next one.
Advertisers decide how much of this runs on its own. Every plan, creative asset and budget is presented for approval before anything goes live. Campaigns can then be adjusted through plain-English instructions, so a marketer can shift budget between channels or revise creative without rebuilding the campaign in separate platform interfaces. Advertisers who want continuous optimization can authorize it within parameters they set, while rules, approvals and role-based access keep every change inside those limits.
Accelerate is live at launch across Google Ads, Meta Ads, Microsoft Advertising, TikTok Ads, ChatGPT Ads and more, with additional integrations including Glance, the AI-powered personal shopping platform backed by InMobi, and other major platforms planned. InMobi introduced the platform today at Glance Forward in New York, a gathering of leaders across technology, commerce and advertising examining how AI is changing the way consumers find and buy products.
