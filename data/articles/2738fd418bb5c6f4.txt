TITLE: GTA 6 Marks Biggest Launch in PlayStation History
OUTLET: Inven Global

Rockstar Games' GTA 6 has set the record for the biggest launch in PlayStation history. Just four days after launch, over 39 million players played the game on PS5, accumulating a total playtime of 683 million hours.
Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) announced GTA 6's launch milestones via the official PlayStation Blog on November 24 KST. Based on internal data collected from November 19 to 22, this marks the single largest game launch across PlayStation's 30-year history spanning five console generations.
According to SIE, GTA 6 broke the all-time record for concurrent players on PlayStation during its launch window. The figure surpassed previous records across all titles, including multiplayer, free-to-play, and single-player games. However, specific concurrent player numbers were not disclosed.
The game also set impressive engagement benchmarks. Total cumulative playtime among PS5 users reached 683 million hours, with players averaging 17.4 hours of playtime per person. This indicates that players spent a substantial amount of time exploring the State of Leonida during the first four days following launch.
The launch of GTA 6 also drove a surge in new PS5 user onboarding. SIE revealed that, excluding holiday periods, the launch saw the highest single-day count of newly connected consoles since the PS5 launched. Furthermore, 70% of newly connected PS5 consoles had GTA 6 as the very first game launched.
Alongside these milestones, SIE highlighted its long-standing partnership with Rockstar Games. Their collaboration dates back to GTA 3 on PlayStation 2. To celebrate the launch of GTA 6, two limited-edition DualSense wireless controllers inspired by Vice City were unveiled. Additionally, "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival" was held over the weekend in Miami, USA, to mark the game's launch.
SIE stated, "The launch of GTA 6 created a truly special moment across gaming and entertainment," expressing gratitude to Rockstar Games for bringing the game to life and to PlayStation players worldwide.
Released on November 19, GTA 6 is Rockstar Games' open-world action-adventure title. Set in Vice City and the broader State of Leonida—inspired by the state of Florida—the game follows the story of Jason and Lucia.
Meanwhile, Take-Two Interactive announced in an official press release on November 20 that GTA 6 generated $3.79 billion (approx. 5.306 trillion won) in worldwide retail sales on its first day. First-day sales figures represent Take-Two's internal estimates through the end of November 19. The company also disclosed that pre-launch sales reached 32.5 million units across all platforms, emphasizing that GTA 6 has become the largest entertainment launch in history across any medium.
