SmartKem slips 10%, announces 1-for-5 reverse stock split
Less than 1 min read
- SmartKem SMTK board of directors approved a 1-for-5 reverse stock split of its common stock as the company seeks to support its continued Nasdaq listing.
- The reverse stock split will become effective on November 20, 2026, at 5 p.m. ET, with shares beginning split-adjusted trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market at the opening of trading on November 23 under the existing ticker symbol SMTK.
- The stock price slipped about 10% on Thursday pre-market trade.
