TITLE: Manchester United pursue cut-price deal for Moroccan full-back El Hilali
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Mauricio Pochettino has placed Espanyol defender Omar El Hilali on his Manchester United shortlist for the January window.
The Morocco international's deal is due to expire in the summer with Espanyol set to consider offers. According to El Nacional Catalonia, a fee of just €10m could be enough to sign the 23-year-old in the winter window.
United are in the market for defensive reinforcements and El Hilali's availability and versatility appeal. The defender has featured at right-back and centre-back for the Spanish side, each of which have been problem positions for the Red Devils.
Neither Diogo Dalot or Noussair Mazraoui have convinced at right-back, while United have doubts over several defensive options. Harry Maguire, Lisandro Martinez and Luke Shaw are all out of contract in the summer, while Ayden Heaven is reportedly considering a move in search of first-team football. As a result, Pochettino is weighing up a raid on his former side for El Hilali.
El Hilali was born in Spain and has played his entire career with Espanyol, making 135 appearances. The defender opted to represent Morocco at international level and has won one senior cap.
