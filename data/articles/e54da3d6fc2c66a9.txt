OUTLET: TradingView

Danantara Investment Management Takes 17.2% Stake in HighPeak Energy
PT Danantara Energy International, backed by its parent PT Danantara Investment Management, acquired preferred shares convertible into a 17.2% stake in HighPeak Energy, Inc. The purchase closed on November 20, 2026, for $250 million. At closing, HighPeak added two directors, including Manish Madhukar Chiddarwar, whom DEI designated.
Investor Intent
DEI and DIM say they will review their investment on an ongoing basis and could discuss strategic alternatives with HighPeak, including a merger, asset sale, joint venture, financing or governance changes. They describe any such discussions as preliminary and exploratory, not a settled proposal.
Investor's Background
PT Danantara Investment Management is an Indonesian investment entity and serves as the ultimate parent of PT Danantara Energy International. The two companies say they primarily invest in securities and other assets. DIM supplied working capital to DEI, which used the funds for HighPeak's $250 million preferred-stock investment.
