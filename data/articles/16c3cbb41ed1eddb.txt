TITLE: Is Virgil van Dijk playing for Liverpool vs. Manchester United?
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Liverpool will have its captain available for Sunday's Premier League match against Manchester United at Anfield. Virgil van Dijk is fit to face the Red Devils after a fitness issue forced him out of the Netherlands squad during the November international break.
The 35-year-old center back has been a constant for Andoni Iraola's side, playing all 900 minutes across 10 Premier League matches this season. A statement from the Dutch national team said he "did not return fit" from the Reds' 3-1 win at Crystal Palace on Nov. 8. That kept him out of the Nations League matches against Greece and Germany.
Iraola spoke to the media Friday and delivered good news on his skipper, along with fellow Dutch internationals Ryan Gravenberch and Jeremie Frimpong.
"I think if everything goes to plan, they should all be available for the game," the Liverpool boss said in his pre-match press conference. He closed the injury update by saying Van Dijk "also is fine to go."
Having Van Dijk back matters at a ground where Liverpool hasn't been at its best. The Reds have won just one of five league matches at Anfield this season, a 2-0 victory over Brighton on Oct. 25. On the road, they've taken four wins and a draw.
Iraola pointed to that split himself while talking about Sunday's opponent.
"We want to improve our form at home. We've been very good away, but not good at home, and the first chance we have is against United on Sunday," he said.
Liverpool sits fourth in the table, three points behind third-place Manchester City. United comes to Anfield in 18th place with former U.S. men's national team head coach Mauricio Pochettino making his debut. A healthy Van Dijk gives the Reds a better shot at spoiling Pochettino's first afternoon in charge.
