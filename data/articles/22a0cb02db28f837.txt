Advance Monthly Sales for Retail and Food Services
CB26-181
Advance Estimates of U.S. Retail and Food Services
Advance estimates of U.S. retail and food services sales for October 2026, adjusted for seasonal variation and holiday and trading-day differences, but not for price changes, were $748.2 billion, up 0.2 percent (±0.4 percent)* from the previous month, and up 6.8 percent (±0.5 percent) from October 2025. Total sales for the August 2026 through October 2026 period were up 6.4 percent (±0.5 percent) from the same period a year ago. The August 2026 to September 2026 percent change was unrevised from up 0.8 percent (±0.2 percent).
