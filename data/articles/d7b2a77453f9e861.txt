TITLE: Shopify Rebels Win the ChalkedCast Rank $10k Rocket League Event
OUTLET: Happy Gamer

Who took the money? Shopify Rebels did. On Tuesday, November 24, the official Rocket League Esports account announced that the org won the ChalkedCast Rank $10k.
“The @ChalkedCast Rank $10k winners are… ✨ @ShopifyRebels ✨” – @RLEsports
That’s the result. Shopify Rebels walk away as champions.
$10,000 Was on the Line
The event carried a $10,000 prize pool. That’s real money. It’s also real pressure.
A payout like that is lowkey a big deal for any org outside the top circuit. One bad series and you’re out. One good run and you’re cashing a check.
The event is run with ChalkedCast, and Rocket League Esports put the win on its own account. That’s a decent stamp of approval for a creator-linked event.
What a Win Like This Means for Shopify Rebels
A title is a title. Shopify Rebels now have one more result on their record, and that actually matters when you’re building a name.
Events like this give teams a place to play for prize money. They don’t need to sit inside the main RLCS schedule. If you’re a Rocket League fan, they’re also extra matches to watch.
The game itself has been around since July 6, 2015. Psyonix built it, and you can play it on PC, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Mac. It’s still pulling in a ten-grand tournament over a decade later.
The tweet itself is just a winner reveal. So if you want the bracket, the finals and the matchups, you’ll have to dig through the ChalkedCast side of things yourself.
