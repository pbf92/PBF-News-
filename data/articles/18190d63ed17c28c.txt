Meta Launches Holiday Ad Push for Smart Glasses
The "Stay in the Moment" campaign, developed by Mother LA, runs across social and out-of-home channels this holiday season.
Updated on Nov. 20, 2026 in Advertising

Meta has launched a holiday campaign, "Stay in the Moment," promoting its smart glasses across social and out-of-home channels. The effort, developed by Mother LA, centers on a minute-long hero film titled "The Play.
Why it matters
The campaign targets a persuadable audience open to AI glasses but not yet convinced of their value, positioning the devices as sentimental holiday gifts. It follows a 2025 holiday campaign spotlighting Messenger, reflecting Meta's continued push to define its consumer hardware story.
EssilorLuxottica sold more than 7 million Meta AI smart glasses across its Ray-Ban and Oakley ranges in 2025, up from roughly 2 million across 2023 and 2024 combined. Citi projects AI-powered glasses sales could reach $40 billion by 2030, though the basis for that forecast was not detailed.
The players
Meta
Meta is the technology company behind Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Messenger, and it has expanded into AI-powered consumer hardware such as smart glasses.
Mother LA
Mother LA is a Los Angeles-based advertising agency that developed the campaign for Meta.
Kylie Jenner
Kylie Jenner is a media personality and entrepreneur whose June collaboration with Meta to promote the Starfire glasses drew public backlash.
The details
The campaign features a minute-long hero film, "The Play," directed by Tom Noakes of Prettybird, about a young girl who freezes during her school holiday play. It also includes a series of 15-second shorts showing holiday activities, with the hero film showing her spotting her mother through Meta Glasses with no screen between them.
Timeline
- June: Meta collaborated with Kylie Jenner to promote the Starfire glasses, a partnership that drew backlash.
- 2025: EssilorLuxottica sold more than 7 million Meta AI smart glasses.
- 2026: Meta's Super Bowl commercial featured its smart glasses.
- Holiday season 2026: The "Stay in the Moment" campaign runs across social and out-of-home channels.
Market Landscape
Meta's holiday push follows its 2026 Super Bowl commercial featuring the glasses and its Kylie Jenner collaboration, building a sustained advertising effort around wearable AI hardware. The campaign positions Meta against rivals in the AI wearables market as the category's sales climb past the millions-per-year level.
For shoppers weighing a holiday gift, the campaign signals that Meta's Ray-Ban and Oakley smart glasses are being marketed as a mainstream purchase this season. Buyers should compare current retail pricing and product options before committing to the device.
The takeaway
Brands are increasingly using emotional storytelling to make high-tech wearables feel personal rather than purely functional. For consumers, the key question is whether the glasses deliver everyday value beyond the sentimental pitch.
