TITLE: Pochettino has under £50m and only two signings to make in January
OUTLET: Tu Futbolero

Back to the board
Manchester United will have less than £50million for the January window, according to Daily Mail, and Mauricio Pochettino is expected to make only two new signings, one of them possibly a loan. The club sit second from bottom in the Premier League after losing 4-0 at Anfield in his first game in charge, and the league's new financial rules tie their hands, although a gamble is not ruled out if relegation becomes a genuine threat.
Nathan Collins of Brentford is a strong candidate in central defence, ahead of Everton's Jarrad Branthwaite, valued at over £70million. At left-back, Barcelona's Alejandro Balde could return as an option. Up front, Folarin Balogun, Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Ivan Toney have all been linked, although Toney's wages of around £500,000 a week put him out of reach.
Leny Yoro was sent off in the eighth minute at Anfield, and the club have appealed to the FA to overturn his one-match ban so he can play Brentford on Saturday.
