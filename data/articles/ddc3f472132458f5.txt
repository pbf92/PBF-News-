TITLE: Grand Theft Auto VI reaches more than 39m unique PS5 players, SIE says
OUTLET: Inven Global

Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) says Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto VI is the biggest game launch in PlayStation history, with more than 39 million unique players on PlayStation 5 (PS5). The figures were shared in a PlayStation Blog post published on November 23rd.
According to the post, written by SIE Content Communications Manager Tim Turi, the launch is the biggest across three decades and five console generations of PlayStation. SIE said players have experienced the story of Jason and Lucia in the game, and that across PS5 they logged a combined 683 million hours, an average of 17.4 hours per player during the opening days.
SIE also said Grand Theft Auto VI set a new record for the highest number of concurrent players for any game on PlayStation, surpassing previous records across multiplayer, free-to-play and single-player titles. The post did not give a concurrent player figure.
New PS5 consoles
The company said the launch window brought the largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day since the console's launch, outside of end-of-year holidays. Since launch, Grand Theft Auto VI has been the first game played on 70% of new PS5 consoles, according to SIE.
All figures are based on SIE internal data collected from November 19th to November 22nd 2026, the post says. They have not been independently verified.
Partnership with Rockstar Games
SIE congratulated Rockstar Games and noted a partnership that stretches back to Grand Theft Auto III on PlayStation 2. The post also mentions two limited-edition DualSense wireless controllers inspired by Vice City, and live events including Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival in Miami last weekend.
