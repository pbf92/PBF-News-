TITLE: Bruno Fernandes Man Utd captaincy decision made after private talks with Mauricio Pochettino
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Mauricio Pochettino has confirmed that Bruno Fernandes will retain the Manchester United captaincy, despite ongoing uncertainty surrounding his long-term future at the club.
Fernandes has recently turned down two contract renewal offers from United, and the departure of Michael Carrick following a dismal start to the season has thrown his plans into further doubt. The 32-year-old was dropped after openly criticizing the tactics in the heavy defeat to Como and has made no secret of his frustration throughout the campaign.
His deal is set to expire at the end of the season, though United do hold the option to extend it by 12 months. He turned down a lucrative move to Al-Hilal in the summer of 2025 and has also attracted interest from Fenerbahçe.
Fernandes has served as club captain since July 2023 and boasts an impressive record of 113 goals and 111 assists in 341 appearances since his £47 million ($60 million) switch from Sporting Lisbon in January 2020. Pochettino has held talks with the Portugal international and is firmly backing him.
"Bruno is our captain and I think he's a very important player, but all the players need to feel that they are important, from the top to the bottom, or from the bottom to the top," he said in his first press conference on Friday ahead of a trip to Anfield, via The Mirror.
"But Bruno is our captain, and he's a very important player. I think we will have time after to talk about the things that you are asking me."
He added: "We spoke yesterday, I spent three hours chatting with all the players individually yesterday. I think I missed some injured players but in the next few days we will talk with everyone.
"It was very nice contact. It is important to listen to the players and what they need to say about areas on and off the field."

United's hierarchy is believed to be unconcerned about Fernandes' future, despite him rejecting two contract proposals. He will don the captain's armband when United pursue their second league victory of the season against Liverpool on Sunday.
Pochettino has taken charge of a struggling side sitting 18th in the Premier League, having secured just three wins across all competitions this season. Like the overwhelming majority of his teammates, Fernandes has not performed to his peak this season, yet still boasts six goals and three assists in 14 appearances.
United's new manager is looking to foster unity and establish a fresh culture at the club, but has already expressed his desire for greater involvement in decision-making going forward. "I was in all my career a coach, head coach, manager. It does not change too much," he said.
"I know as head coach I am the guy who tomorrow will decide the squad for Sunday, pick the 11, make the changes. I know in the future I would like to be involved in all the main decisions that affect the squad. At the end of the day, Manchester United is a football club."
