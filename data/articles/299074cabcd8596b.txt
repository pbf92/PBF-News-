What's Going On With IES Holdings Stock on Friday?
IES Holdings, Inc. IESC shares are trading lower on Friday and remain near their 52-week low, with trading volume roughly twice the average.
IES Holdings is a U.S. specialty contractor and infrastructure company that designs, builds, and maintains electrical, technology, mechanical, and structural systems across residential, commercial, industrial, and data center markets.
On Friday, shares of data center infrastructure-related companies are trading lower amid a broader AI selloff as investors take profits following Anthropic’s post-IPO performance.
Sentiment is also pressured by rising financing costs, debt-heavy expansion plans, and President Trump’s opposition to building data centers outside the U.S., raising uncertainty around global expansion and future AI infrastructure spending.
Earnings Snapshot
IES Holdings reported adjusted EPS of $4.07, compared with the approximately $2.81 consensus estimate. Revenue increased 55% year over year to $1.39 billion, exceeding expectations of $1.148 billion.
The company reported a backlog of approximately $4.96 billion, up from $2.37 billion a year earlier. This figure excludes an additional $2.6 billion in backlog contributed by the DBM Global acquisition.
As of Sept. 30, 2026, remaining performance obligations totaled approximately $3.48 billion.
DBM Global Acquisition And Financing
IES completed its acquisition of DBM Global on Oct. 5, 2026, adding a Structural business and $2.6 billion in additional backlog, which was not included in the Sept. 30 backlog figure.
To finance the transaction, IES expanded its credit facility from $300 million to $700 million, borrowed $525 million and subsequently repaid $125 million.
Cash Position And Capital Allocation
IES ended fiscal 2026 with $155.1 million in cash, $288.9 million in marketable securities and no debt.
Following the fiscal year-end, the company repurchased 350,000 shares at an average price of $248 to offset dilution from shares issued for the DBM Global acquisition.
On Nov. 19, the board approved an additional $350 million for the share repurchase program.
Management anticipates continued strong organic growth in fiscal 2027 across its Communications, Commercial & Industrial, and Infrastructure Solutions segments.
