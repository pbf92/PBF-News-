The Travis Scott x Rockstar x Nike Air Force 1 Low GTA 6 Pack Raffle is Live
Published
Quick Facts
- The raffle for Travis Scott's three GTA 6 Nike Air Force 1 Lows is live now
- Scott's website is the only place to get the multi-color pair, which is limited to just 600 pairs

Hopefully you were able to sink some hours into Grand Theft Auto 6 over the weekend, as social media feeds, television broadcasts, and conversations with strangers have been revolving around the largest entertainment launch in human history. In its first day alone, the game netted nearly $4 billion in sales, shattering the previous first-day sales record set by GTA 5 at $800 million.
And on top of the video game itself, GTA has been taking over the real world in Miami, including a Miami Heat game, its own music festival, and a pop-up vault selling Travis Scott apparel and Air Force 1s. If you missed out on the Travis Scott x Rockstar x Nike Air Force 1 Low GTA 6 collection, which dropped over the weekend, Scott’s website is raffling pairs right now.
Where to Buy the Travis Scott x Nike Air Force 1 Low GTA 6 Pack
Travis Scott’s website is hosting a raffle for all three pairs of his Rockstar Games x Nike Air Force 1 Low GTA 6 pack, including the ultra-rare friends and family colorway. This multi-color rendition is limited to just 600 units and is only available through Scott’s website.
The other two colorways keep it simple with an all-white patent leather color and a triple-black pair featuring debossed map layouts. As is the case with previous Travis Scott raffles, you’ll only have a few hours (at most) to enter the release.
We first saw the pairs load up on Travis’ website on GTA launch day, November 19, alongside apparel items, and since then, multiple apparel drops have hit the site. However, the shoes were labeled “Soon” until the raffle finally went live at 5:00 PM EST on November 24, 2026. In order to enter the raffle, you must play a mini-game and collect six pairs of the AF1s. From there, you can toggle a checkbox to enter for all three sneakers.
The Sole Retriever mobile app will be keeping track of the raffle in real-time. We’ll alert you as soon as it closes and when winners are being selected.
