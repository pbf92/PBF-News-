TITLE: HIPAG releases interim considerations to help healthcare facilities respond to chlorhexidine shortages
OUTLET: EurekAlert!

The Healthcare Infection Prevention Advisory Group (HIPAG), a national expert advisory initiative established by the Association for Professionals in Infection Control and Epidemiology (APIC) and the Society for Healthcare Epidemiology of America (SHEA), has released Interim Considerations for Chlorhexidine (CHG) Shortages to support healthcare facilities responding to ongoing supply disruptions.
The resource addresses the shortage of 2% chlorhexidine gluconate (CHG) bathing cloths, commonly known as CHG wipes, and the resulting increased demand for alternative CHG formulations. Because CHG products play an important role in infection prevention practices across healthcare settings, supply limitations require careful consideration to maintain patient safety.
HIPAG’s interim considerations provide a framework to help healthcare facilities assess available supplies, implement conservation strategies, prioritize use based on clinical evidence and patient risk, and evaluate potential alternatives. The resource emphasizes maintaining established infection prevention practices, following manufacturers’ instructions for use, and involving multidisciplinary teams in decisions about temporary changes to clinical protocols.
These considerations are intended to support local decision-making and do not establish new clinical recommendations or endorse specific replacement products as equivalent to CHG.
This is an evolving resource. HIPAG will continue to assess available evidence and supply conditions and update the considerations as additional information becomes available, including further information on conservation strategies and prioritization of patient populations.

About APIC
The Association for Professionals in Infection Control and Epidemiology (APIC) is the leading professional association for infection preventionists with a mission to create a safer world through the prevention of infection.
About SHEA
The Society for Healthcare Epidemiology of America (SHEA) is a professional society representing physicians and other healthcare professionals in the fields of healthcare epidemiology, infection prevention, and antimicrobial stewardship.
About the HIPAG The Healthcare Infection Prevention Advisory Group (HIPAG) is a national expert advisory initiative established in 2026 by APIC and SHEA. Bringing together multidisciplinary experts from across the healthcare continuum, HIPAG provides timely, evidence-informed expertise on infection prevention and control, emerging infectious threats, and antimicrobial resistance. The group works to identify gaps, review and contextualize evolving evidence, and support coordinated approaches to infection prevention that protect patients and healthcare personnel across healthcare settings.
