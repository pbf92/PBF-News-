TITLE: GTA 6 pre-order bonus is a 'trash game' disgruntled gamers don't want to play
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

GTA 6 launches this week on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, but fans are collectively cringing at a laughable ‘freebie’ announced for later this month.
As everyone who pre-ordered GTA VI will know, aside from the full game, you also get a free one-month membership to GTA+.
Rockstar Games’ subscription service is primarily used to unlock bonuses in GTA Online, but the studio is yet to confirm what kind of functionality it’ll have with GTA VI or the unannounced GTA 6 Online.
Active memberships also grant subscribers a collection of Rockstar games at no additional cost. You can play titles like Red Dead Redemption, L.A Noire or Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition for a slice of nostalgia.
Advert
Late this month, a bonus game will join the lineup: NBA 2K27 Standard Edition. Gamers have been relentlessly mocking the announcement ever since.

GTA 6 fans are saying NBAnything but this
NBA 2K27’s inclusion was shared to X by @TexFunz2, and the comments were rife with insults and memes aimed at the online basketball game.
“These losers trying to boost copies so bad y'all are finished, we dont want this”, read one of the comments.
Other examples included: “2K is begging y'all to play this trash game” and “gun to my head, and I'm still not downloading this”
Despite it being a freebie, some gamers even claimed they’d pay extra just to keep it off the service.
“Is there an even higher tier that doesnt come with 2K?” read one such comment, and another said, “Can I pay extra to not have this included?”
Some even made memes of Peter Griffin from the animated sit-com Family Guy, saying “If that even touches GTA+, I will kill you.”
The NBA 2K series has been slated for years now. Due to its annual releases, the previous instalment quickly becomes obsolete and many even have their servers shut down. It’s safe to say no one wants to buy a game they won’t be able to play a year later, and including it for free with a subscription doesn’t make it any better.
Others also pointed out how ridiculous it is that NBA 2K27 is getting added to GTA+ in the same month as GTA VI. You know, the biggest game of the decade.
“Asking people to make space on their console a few days before GTA 6 launch to play a sports game instead” pointed out another comment.
“I'll be playing GTA 6; why would I want that POS.”
Clearly, this wasn’t the reaction Take-Two Interactive was expecting, which makes you wonder what the intended reaction was envisioned to be.
Anyway, if you want to play NBA 2K27 with your GTA+ subscription, it’ll be available from November 27 to January 15 2027 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S users.
