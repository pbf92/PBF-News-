TITLE: Walt Disney Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $40M
OUTLET: TradingView

Walt Disney Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $40M
Paul M. Roeder, a Senior EVP and Chief Comm Officer, sold 4,854 Walt Disney common shares on Nov. 18, 2026, at $123.16 per share for a total of $597,819. Following the sale, he no longer directly owned Walt Disney common stock.
Robert A. Iger, a director, sold 321,694 Walt Disney common shares directly on Nov. 18, 2026, at a weighted average price of $122.82 per share for a total of $39,510,457. Following the sale, he directly owned 232,662 shares and indirectly owned 156 shares held by his spouse and 21,206 shares in the Walt Disney Stock Fund through his 401(k) plan.
