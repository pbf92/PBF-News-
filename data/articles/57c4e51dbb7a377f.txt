Why Is AMBA Stock Crashing 37% Overnight Today?
- Ambarella’s Q3 revenue rose 6.2% to $115.2 million but missed estimates.
- The company’s Q4 revenue outlook of $70 million - $75 million is below Street view.
- CEO Fermi Wang said rising memory prices and limited supplies are affecting customers across markets.
Ambarella Inc. (AMBA) stock plunged 37% overnight after the chipmaker delivered weaker-than-expected third-quarter (Q3) earnings and warned that spiraling memory supply conditions could hurt sales across its business segments.
The company also forecast a weak Q4 as rising component costs prompt customers to rethink or delay orders. Ambarella stock is also headed for its worst monthly performance since January 2022.
Ambarella’s Revenue Misses Estimates As Margins Come Under Pressure
For Q3 fiscal 2027, Ambarella’s revenue increased 6.2% year-on-year to $115.2 million but missed the analysts’ consensus estimate of $119.80, according to Fiscal.ai. Profit remained under pressure as well. Gross margin slipped 180 basis points to 57.8%.
The company posted a net loss of $12.7 million, equivalent to $0.29 per diluted share, also below the $0.31 estimate. For Q4, Ambarella sees revenue between $70 million and $75 million, below the Street view of $112.62.
Ambarella Warns Data Center Demand Tightens Memory Supply
During the Q3 earnings call, CEO Fermi Wang said Ambarella's enterprise Internet of Things (IoT) and automotive divisions performed roughly in line with internal projections. However, its consumer IoT operations fell short, with sales declining despite Q3 typically benefiting from seasonal demand.
“In recent weeks, despite our efforts to help our customers mitigate the memory-related challenges, it became apparent the memory induced weakness we began to see in the consumer market in Q3 was spreading into all of our markets to various degrees.”
Wang also pointed to a broader shift in semiconductor manufacturing capacity toward data center applications. The change has made it harder for customers to secure memory components, even as Ambarella works to address supply-related problems.
“The unprecedented rise in memory prices as well as the scarcity of memory supply is causing our customers to different degrees to cancel push out or delay new order,” said Wang.
