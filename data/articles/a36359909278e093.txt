TITLE: LibertyStream files for proposed U.S. IPO, seeks Nasdaq listing
OUTLET: TradingView

LibertyStream files for proposed U.S. IPO, seeks Nasdaq listing
- LibertyStream Infrastructure Partners filed a Form S-1 registration statement with the SEC for a proposed U.S. initial public offering.
- The company has applied to list its common stock on Nasdaq under the proposed ticker symbol LSTM.
- The number of shares to be offered and the IPO price range have not yet been determined.
- Following the IPO, LibertyStream expects to cease trading on the OTCQB and is considering delisting from the TSXV.
