TITLE: Manchester United outcast deserves his first start of the season
OUTLET: Yahoo

Manchester United didn't produce many moments of quality against Liverpool at the weekend, so Joshua Zirkzee's pass to send Bruno Fernandes through on goal sticks out in the mind.
At that stage, Liverpool were winning 4-0 with a few minutes remaining, meaning the goal would have merely been a consolation, but Fernandes couldn't convert after a beautiful pass from his teammate.
There have been green shoots from Zirkzee when he's come off the bench over the last few weeks, and perhaps it's time to give him his first start of the season.
Everyone knows the story with Zirkzee at this point. He is clearly not a striker or best suited to playing in the Premier League, but in his defence, he has impacted games when he's been given the chance this term.
The Dutchman scored United's only goal against Como in the Champions League last month and scored against Aston Villa earlier this month, making it 4-3 to give hope that a late draw could be salvaged.
Zirkzee has been limited to late cameos from the bench when games have appeared to be over, but he deserves to start against Sporting Lisbon on recent form.

Matheus Cunha began the season up front before it was decided that Bryan Mbeumo was the best option there. Although Mbeumo initially looked better than Cunha, his performances have now dropped off, and it seems he would also benefit from a return to his natural position.
Mbeumo is most effective playing on the right wing. And if Mauricio Pochettino wants to play him there, he could move Cunha back up front, but Marcus Rashford isn't fit to come into the team on the left side.
Amad is obviously an option to play up front, although he hasn't scored for United since finding the net against Bournemouth in December 2025, so it would be logical to give Zirkzee another chance this week.
There is enough evidence to show that starting Zirkzee up front is a bad idea. He was awful when he played in that position against Sunderland in May, but he is arguably currently the best option for that role.
It would be worth Pochettino giving him opportunities to start against Sporting and Brentford this week. Mbeumo is screaming out to play on the right wing again, so there is ultimately not much for Pochettino to lose.
Zirkzee is at least in form judging from his substitute appearances, and he will feel he has a point to prove after being overlooked for a start all season. It would also be a chance for him to put himself in the shop window.
The 25-year-old has entertained the idea of leaving in search of regular minutes over the last two transfer windows, but the message from United has been that they need him as a contributing member of the squad.
Zirkzee's long-term future is clearly away from Manchester, and he will inevitably enjoy success when he moves to another league, where his skillset is better suited, but he could be important at United in the short term.
The only reason Pochettino might be against starting Zirkzee is that he is not a forward who can press ferociously and set the tempo from the front, two traits the United boss needs from his attacking players.
But what Zirkzee could offer up front in the next week could outweigh those negatives.
