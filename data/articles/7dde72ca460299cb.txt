TITLE: U.S. Soccer executive opens up on search for Mauricio Pochettino replacement
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

With just one international friendly to go this calendar year, and no competitive matches until the CONCACAF Nations League semifinal in March 2027, U.S. Soccer has plenty of time to work with in order to find Mauricio Pochettino's heir apparent.
The Argentine recently stepped down after just over two years in charge, during which time he led the USMNT to the Gold Cup final and the FIFA World Cup Round of 16, concluding his tenure with six consecutive wins and an 8-0 aggregate Nations League quarterfinal win against Haiti.
Now, the search for his replacement is well underway, with U.S. Soccer Federation chief operating officer Dan Helfrich telling The Athletic that they were already well prepared for such an eventuality.
He explained that when Pochettino informed them of his desire to leave, there was no sense of "chaos or panic, because you feel prepared," with the USSF having long prepared for such eventualities in "recurring meetings" where possible successors have been established.
Not looking to make a hire by a certain date, Helfrich explained that chief executive officer JT Batson will be in charge of the process, with USWNT head coach Emma Hayes and assistant sporting director Oguchi Onyewu among those who will weigh in.
Helfrich said that there will be regular talks amongst the group as the process moves forward, with others both within and outside of the USSF as well as within and outside the nation set to be involved.
He explained, "We collectively have a set of relationships and a network that allows us to tap into perspectives about the role, perspectives about the state of football, perspectives about, certainly, potential candidates."
When it comes to the style of coach the USSF is on the lookout for, Helfrich said, "Our guide for the types of managers that we would consider is not based on the level of fame or celebrity.

"But it is based on a very high standard of excellence, and ambition, and talent, and experience, and belief."
The process of finding such an individual is already underway, with interested candidates and/or their representatives having already spoken to U.S. Soccer.
Helfrich added that they would be looking for "a very high standard of excellence, and ambition, and talent, and experience, and belief," going on to say, "There's managers that have that set of characteristics in a variety of places, with a variety of experience."
What's more, while they'll be looking to replace one of the bigger names in global soccer management in Pochettino, that's not to say his replacement must possess an equally notable profile.
"What matters is a manager who is going to propel us to our ambitions, and who's going to do that in a way that generates buy-in and enthusiasm and excitement from our players, from our staff, from our external stakeholders, from our fans," he said. "And that is something that happens over time."
In the meantime, it remains to be seen whether or not a replacement will have been found by the time the players return to camp for their friendly against Jamaica on December 13, before disbanding once more until the Nations League semi-final against Mexico on March 25.
