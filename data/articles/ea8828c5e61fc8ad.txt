TITLE: Grand Theft Auto 6 is Trolling Players in the Best Way Possible
OUTLET: OpenCritic

Grand Theft Auto 6 players claimed to be convinced that Rockstar Games painted a fake tunnel in Vice City just to trick them into crashing head-on in a manner reminiscent of a Looney Tunes cartoon. Some GTA 6 players took to social media to share clips of the painted tunnel gag alongside some hilarious reactions to it.
GTA 6 is the biggest entertainment launch of all time, having made over $3 billion in revenue within a day of the game's release. Additionally, over 32.5 million pre-orders of GTA 6 were sold worldwide. On November 23, PlayStation revealed that over 39 million unique PS5 players experienced the game on Sony's current-gen console. PlayStation also revealed that 70% of newly-connected PS5 systems that were sold and set up from November 19 to 22 had GTA 6 being the first game that was played on those consoles.
A member of GTA 6's voice cast reveals a hilarious 'argument' that broke out after their spouse discovered their vocal contributions in-game.
