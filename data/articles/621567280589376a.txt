TITLE: Rio Ngumoha Reflects on Star Turn vs. Manchester United
OUTLET: The Liverpool Offside

At just 18 years of age, Rio Ngumoha has already established himself as a key player in Andoni Iraola’s Liverpool side, and his well taken goal and assist in Sunday’s 4-0 demolition of Manchester United on Sunday really served to drive that point home.
Beyond the those moments, though—and the goal in particular was quite a notable moment for the youngster, unseating Michael Owen and making him the new youngest ever goal scorer in this fixture—it was clear Liverpool were looking to build their attacks through his side.
It was also clear that United were worried enough about his threat that they regularly setting up to double mark him. Even then it rarely worked out well for them.
“Like I always say it’s about the belief from the coaches, the gaffer, I can’t thank them more, really,” the young, rising star winger said after the match. “Just to play in a game like Liverpool versus United you know it’s a big, big game, the biggest game in England.
“And to score? It’s good to get off the mark in the season as well and especially in a big game like this. So right now I’m just really glad, really happy.”
A knock for Cody Gakpo helped to ensure Ngumoha got the start against United, but he’s been a danger man any time he’s played for the Reds this season to the point that no one watching on went into the game worried about his place in Liverpool’s attack.
“I just want to help the team as much as I can really and get the three points,” he added. “Me playing a big part in that—I just want to thank fans, my teammates, and coaches for believing in me. It was a great day.”
