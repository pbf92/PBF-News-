GTA 6 Copies Sold—Lifetime Sales, Explained
As of November 20, 2026, GTA 6 has sold 32.5 million copies and counting, including pre-sales and post-launch units.
Here’s what Take-Two Interactive‘s GTA 6 press release had to say:
‘Achieving a near perfect score of 99 on Metacritic and resounding critical acclaim, Grand Theft Auto VI sold 32.5 million units pre-release, a new record across all platforms.’

Just before GTA 6’s launch, Take-Two revealed GTA 5 sales had reached almost 235 million copies since launch. The sixth main game has a long way to go to catch up to GTA 5, but it’s off to an incredible start.
In fact, by launch day alone, GTA 6 is in the top 30-40 best-selling games of all time. It’s breaking records, and expect these figures to keep rising.
How Much Money Has GTA 6 Made?
GTA 6 made just under $4 billion through the end of November 19, 2026.
Take-Two also addressed the game’s initial revenue:
‘NEW YORK–(BUSINESS WIRE)–Nov. 20, 2026– Rockstar Games®, a label of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTWO), is proud to announce that Grand Theft Auto VI day one retail sales* reached $3.79 billion, setting a new record for entertainment across any medium, and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history.’
This means it’s already made nearly a billion dollars more than the biggest box office movie in history—Avengers: Endgame—and is well on its way to becoming one of the most profitable (maybe the most) pieces of multimedia we’ve ever seen.
