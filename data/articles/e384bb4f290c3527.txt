TITLE: GTA 6 Players Have Found a Surprising Way to Play in First Person Despite Rockstar’s Plans
OUTLET: PlayStation LifeStyle

GTA 6 gives players plenty of ways to explore Vice City, but one familiar camera option is missing. Although the game lacks a dedicated first-person mode, players have discovered a glitch that lets them experience much of the game from a first-person perspective.
The workaround requires careful timing and a change to the controller settings. Players must first open Settings, select Controller, and change the Touch Pad Button on PlayStation 5 or View Button on Xbox from Map to Camera. Next, they need to sit in a vehicle, open the in-game phone, and close it while quickly pressing the camera button several times.
If executed correctly, the phone disappears while the first-person view remains active. Players can then leave the vehicle and explore on foot, drive, and use weapons from this perspective. However, the glitch does have some visual inconsistencies. Some visual issues remain, and players must reload their save to return to the regular third-person view.
Why GTA 6 fans keep demanding an official first-person mode
Rockstar has included first-person gameplay in its previous major releases, making its absence as a dedicated feature in GTA 6 more noticeable. Grand Theft Auto V introduced first-person mode with its PlayStation 4 and Xbox One versions in November 2014, more than a year after its original release. The feature later came to PC, giving players a new way to explore Los Santos and Blaine County. Rockstar also added new animations, a first-person cover system, and updated aiming controls.
Red Dead Redemption 2 also featured first-person gameplay when it launched in October 2018. Players could switch camera views while exploring the world as Arthur Morgan, as well as during combat and other activities. This established first-person gameplay as a recurring feature in Rockstar’s major releases.
The GTA 6 glitch highlights why players want the feature officially added to the game. Its first-person view works across several activities, despite some visual issues. It also allows players to experience the game’s detailed interiors and environments from a first-person perspective. For fans familiar with Rockstar’s previous games, an official version would be a welcome addition.
