TITLE: Wall Street Futures Stabilise Following Nvidia Rebound as Fed Policy and Technology Earnings Remain in Focus: Dow Jones, S&P, Nasdaq
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance UK

US equity futures fluctuated around unchanged levels on Tuesday after a substantial recovery in global stock markets following the reinstatement of Jensen Huang as chief executive of Nvidia (NASDAQ:NVDA).
Investors were also assessing the outlook for US monetary policy, quarterly results from Hewlett Packard Enterprise and expectations of a global oil supply surplus.
Nvidia Leadership Announcement Drives Global Equity Recovery
Nvidia shares fell more than 3% in Tuesday's premarket session after advancing more than 30% on Monday, an increase that added over US$1 trillion to the semiconductor manufacturer's market value.
The recovery followed the announcement that Huang would return as chief executive, having resigned approximately two weeks earlier.
The development supported widespread gains across Wall Street, particularly among technology companies exposed to artificial intelligence investment.
The Nasdaq Composite finished Monday's session 6.51% higher, while the S&P 500 gained 4.73% and the Dow Jones Industrial Average advanced 3.01%.
The session represented the strongest performance for US equity markets since April 2025.
"Jensen Huang's return as Nvidia CEO lifted stocks on Monday and cleared the negative sentiment, opening room for a near-term rebound," analysts at BCA Research said in a research note.
Taiwan and South Korea Lead Asian Market Gains
Asian semiconductor-related markets recorded substantial increases following Monday's recovery in US technology stocks.
Taiwanese equities advanced 8.99%, while South Korea's KOSPI index gained 6.32%, reflecting renewed investor interest in companies supplying components for artificial intelligence infrastructure.
Nvidia's expansion under Huang has positioned the company as a major supplier of advanced processors for AI datacentres, alongside its established gaming semiconductor operations.
The company has also supported investment across the AI industry through commercial and financing arrangements, some involving its own customers.
Despite the recovery in technology shares, uncertainty remains regarding the returns on AI infrastructure expenditure, the industry's reliance on debt financing and the future pace of US datacentre development.
Huang is expected to appear before US congressional committees, where Nvidia's business relationships with China and adherence to export restrictions could be examined.
Federal Reserve Rate Expectations Remain Elevated Ahead of December Meeting
Financial markets continue to anticipate a further increase in US interest rates at the Federal Reserve's December policy meeting.
