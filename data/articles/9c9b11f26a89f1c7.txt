TITLE: Here’s How To Get A Banshee In GTA 6 And Unlock A GTA 3 -Inspired Trophy
OUTLET: Kotaku

Obviously, you need to steal cars in Grand Theft Auto 6; it’s in the name of the game. But you might as well get something out of it beyond just new wheels and some money, so how about scoring a trophy along the way?
The Bravado Banshee is a nice ride that you get a mission to steal in GTA 6’s third chapter, via a character named Randy. You could luck out and find a Banshee on the street, but if you want the Capital Offense trophy, you can kill two birds with one stone by hitting up the Sunrise Premium Autos showroom in Southside. You’ll find it between Vice City International Airport and Peacock Bay, next to an Illegals Good Store.
Note that you can’t do this before you do Randy’s mission “Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs.” Why? The Bravado Banshee, like a lot of expensive cars in the game, requires a key cloner, and Randy is the person who gives you one as part of that mission. After he’s added the app to your phone, you can head to Sunrise Premium Autos and walk back to the showroom where a shiny blue Bravado Banshee is just sitting there.
Clone the key via the same method you would use to slim-jim a car door, and then step on in. The next part is how you get the Capital Offense trophy: Drive the car straight through the glass window and then gun it before the police get there. The trophy should pop immediately.
The trophy itself is a reference to the easiest and most reliable way to get a Banshee in Grand Theft Auto 3, where you would just visit a car showroom in the south part of the map, sit in a Banshee in the showroom, and then drive it through the glass window. The similarity to that setup here in GTA 6 seems to be a pretty direct reference to the now-25-year-old PS2 title.
You can either give the Banshee to Randy for that mission or get it repainted and registered for yourself. And you can return later to this same spot and get another Banshee! Regardless of what you choose, you’re a bit richer and a bit further along in your progress toward a platinum trophy or a mythic achievement. Not bad for an afternoon’s work.
