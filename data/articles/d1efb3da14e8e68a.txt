TITLE: Rockstar Boss Says GTA 6 Was Made to Show What Games Are "Capable Of"
OUTLET: 80 Level

Rockstar Games President Sam Houser issued a rare GTA 6 launch statement, saying the game was created to exceed expectations and demonstrate what the medium can achieve.
It's the day before Grand Theft Auto 6 officially releases, and Rockstar Games President Dan Houser issued a rare statement on the impending release. Just today, official reviews started to publish from across the industry, and with over 140 critic scores in, GTA 6 is officially the highest-rated game in history.
That's right, it's currently sitting atop the rankings, even above the original 1998 release of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the Nintendo 64.
The statement from Houser was provided directly to GamesRadar+ as GTA 6 began unlocking around the world in various regions. Houser rarely speaks publicly, making the statement particularly notable after Rockstar allowed trailers, development features, and the game itself to handle most of its marketing.
"Rockstar Games was founded on the belief that games could achieve the same distinction as all other forms of storytelling, become essential to modern culture, and to be thought of as stories and worlds that live on with us.
This week, Grand Theft Auto returns to Leonida and modern-day Vice City. While things have changed and time has passed, one aspect holds true: We have tried to create an experience that will exceed your expectations while showing what this medium is capable of, and why we believe games are so special.
Whether you can trace your journey to this game through the entire series or are experiencing Grand Theft Auto for the first time, this is a narrative set in a world of possibilities – and it is up to you as the player to shape how it ultimately feels, and concludes. We hope it feels both familiar and entirely new, and above all, we hope you have fun. We look forward to many more years of sharing these experiences with you."
- Dan Houser, President of Rockstar Games in a statement to GamesRadar+
With GTA 6, the studio has attempted to create an experience that exceeds players’ expectations while demonstrating what video games are capable of achieving. Houser said Rockstar wants the new installment to feel “both familiar and entirely new” to longtime players and those discovering Grand Theft Auto for the first time.
That balance has defined much of GTA 6’s development. The game returns to Vice City but dramatically expands its scale through the surrounding state of Leonida, an enormous NPC population, highly reactive systemic interactions, detailed interiors, and the evolving relationship between Jason and Lucia.
As shown during the game’s official gameplay reveal, players can switch between both protagonists, explore crowded environments, interact with Leonida’s residents, and approach criminal activities in different ways. Rockstar reportedly created approximately 600,000 NPC animations to help make the population more varied and responsive.
Houser also emphasized the player’s role in determining how GTA 6’s story ultimately feels and concludes. That wording reflects Rockstar’s recent comments about consequences and relationships, reportedly as a way to build empathy with players.
The statement concludes with Rockstar looking forward to “many more years” of sharing these experiences with players. That could simply acknowledge the expected longevity of GTA 6, particularly after GTA 5 remained active for more than a decade. Rockstar has not yet formally announced a successor to GTA Online, however, so the comment should not be treated as confirmation of its multiplayer plans.
Grand Theft Auto VI launches on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
