TITLE: POCHETTINO’S DEBUT: You can watch it on USA Network, Telemundo on Sunday
OUTLET: Front Row Soccer

Overview:
Fans wanting to watch Mauricio Pochettino's debut as Manchester United head coach on Sunday, can do so.
Mauricio Pochettino will direct Manchester United in the Premier League for the first time on Sunday. (Jon van Woerden Photo)
STAMFORD, Conn. – Fans wanting to watch Mauricio Pochettino’s debut as Manchester United head coach on Sunday, can do so.
United’s game at fourth-place Liverpool will be available on the USA Network and Telemundo on Sunday, Nov. 22 at 11:30 a.m. ET.
The game is one of several across NBC Sports this weekend.
Pochettino ended a two-year tenure as U.S. men’s national team coach, with a 2-0 victory over Haiti in the second leg of the Concacaf Nations League quarterfinals in Cincinnati on Tuesday night. The Americans won the aggregate-goals series, 8-0.
ManU hired the 54-year-old Pochettino after it fired Michael Carrick last week.
Pochettino, whose first day as United coach was Wednesday, and his team will have their hands full with Liverpool.
Liverpool defeated Crystal Palace 3-1 on Sunday, Nov. 8, with unanswered goals from Alexis Mac Allister, Florian Wirtz, and Alexander Isak. Wirtz’s goal was his first league goal of the season. With the win, Liverpool are fourth in the Premier League, just three points behind third-place Manchester City.
In its last match, Manchester United drew 4-4 with Aston Villa after Joshua Zirkzee and Patrick Dorgu scored a pair of second-half stoppage-time goals to level the match.
Manchester United “did the double” against Liverpool last season, winning 2-1 on Oct. 19, 2025, at Anfield, and 3-2 on May 3, 2026, at Old Trafford. In 68 Premier League matchups, United leads the series, 31-17-20. NBC Sports will have a pitch-side desk at Anfield on Sunday, with Jon Champion hosting alongside Lee Dixon and Graeme Le Saux. They will also call the match live and deliver pregame pitch-side hits and post-match interviews with players and managers.
This weekend’s coverage begins Saturday, Nov. 21, with Premier League Mornings on USA Network at 7 a.m. ET before Manchester City’s match against Fulham at 7:30 a.m. ET (USA Network and Universo). At 10 a.m. ET, NBC Sports presents five matches live: Spurs-Ipswich Town (USA Network, Universo), Bournemouth-Nottingham Forest (Peacock), Aston Villa-Sunderland (Peacock), Chelsea-Leeds United (Peacock, NBCSN), and Coventry City-Crystal Palace (Peacock).
Peacock’s Multiview feature returns with coverage of the four matches available on Peacock during Saturday’s 10 a.m. ET multi-match window. The Goal Rush whip-around show will also stream live on Peacock on Saturday at 10 a.m. ET, providing viewers with the most compelling action in real time and on replay so they won’t miss a single goal or key play.
First-place Arsenal visits Newcastle United at 12:30 p.m. ET on USA Network and Universo with Peter Drury and Dixon on the call from St. James’ Park. Goal Zone closes Saturday’s slate after the match at 2:30 p.m. ET on USA Network.
Coverage continues Sunday with Premier League Mornings at 8 a.m. ET on USA Network, followed by Hull City-Brighton at 9 a.m. ET (USA Network, Universo).
Then at 11:30 a.m. ET, fourth-place Liverpool host Manchester United on USA Network and Telemundo. Sunday’s coverage concludes with Goal Zone at 1:30 p.m. ET on USA Network
USA Network closes out the matchweek Monday, Nov. 23, with Premier League Live at 2 p.m. ET, ahead of Brentford-Everton at 3 p.m. ET. Goal Zone follows the match at 5 p.m. ET.
Rebecca Lowe hosts Saturday’s and Sunday’s Premier League Mornings and Goal Zone shows alongside former Premier League players Tim Howard and Robbie Mustoe. On Monday, former Premier League player Danny Higginbotham joins Lowe and Mustoe in the studio for Premier League Live and Goal Zone.
