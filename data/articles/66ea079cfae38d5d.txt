Corn Futures Slipping Back Lower on Friday Morning
Corn price action is down 1 to 2 cents so far on Friday morning. Futures posted gains of 1 ½ to 4 ½ cents across most contracts on Thursday, holding onto early gains into the close. Open interest was down 1,338 contracts on Thursday, mainly in the in the December (-49,026), as much of the rest of the board say rising OI. December options expire today. The CmdtyView national average Cash Corn price was up 4 1/4 cents at $4.07 1/4.
Export Sales data was out on Thursday, with a total of 1.588 MMT of 2026/27 corn sold in the week of 11/12. That was down 44.9% from the previous week’s marketing year high and 33.3% below the same week last year. New crop business was tallied at 41,911 MT. Mexico was the top buyer of 926,300 MT, with South Korea buying 197,800 MT.
