TITLE: GTA 6 players have discovered iconic cars from famous films and TV series.
OUTLET: GameGPU

In Grand Theft Auto 6 on PlayStation 5, players continue to discover cars based on famous films and television series. Rockstar Games has placed fictional models throughout the open worlds of Leonidas and Vice City, their appearances reminiscent of iconic vehicles from pop culture. Among the discovered Easter eggs are a car from Ghostbusters, Brian O'Conner's green coupe, and Dominic Toretto's muscle car from the Fast and Furious franchise. These cars have been given their own in-game names, but their designs make their original prototypes recognizable.
Other finds reference American film and television from the 1970s and 1980s. Specifically, Bo the Bandit's red muscle car from "Smokey and the Bandit" was discovered in-game, as well as a red Ferrari sports car with a distinctive stripe reminiscent of the car from "Miami Vice." Of particular note is a green Maibatsu Penumbra FF—an in-game replica of the Mitsubishi Eclipse from the first "Fast and Furious" film. The car is located in a locked garage of a mansion on Vespucci Island. The player can enter, retrieve the car, and register it as their personal vehicle.
Automotive references have long been a part of the Grand Theft Auto series, but in the sixth game, they are enhanced with unique liveries, body parts, and custom license plates featuring quotes from the original sources. Once registered in the player's garage, these cars retain their unique features. As players explore Leonida, they continue to uncover new secrets, so the list of discovered cars may expand. All of the above findings are for the PlayStation 5 version of GTA 6; the game's official release date is November 19, 2026.
