TITLE: Cochrane Remembrance Tree Ceremony honours loved ones who have passed away
OUTLET: Cochrane Eagle

The Cochrane and Area Hospice Society is inviting residents to honour the memory of loved ones at its annual Remembrance Tree Ceremony this month.
On Saturday, Nov. 28, community members will gather at St. Peter’s Lutheran Church to remember those who have passed away and add coloured lights to a special tree on the church grounds.
Hospice Society president Julie Handrahan said the ceremony provides an opportunity for people to recognize the lasting place their loved ones hold in their lives.
“It's a large part of honouring that loved one and letting their light shine so that they know that their loved one is still very important, even though they have passed on,” she said.
Throughout December, the tree will be illuminated with white lights, which participants can replace with a coloured bulb in memory of someone they have lost.
“It could be a red light, yellow light, green light, blue light,” Handrahan said. “It’s in memory of those that we've lost, whether it's been this year or previous years. It's just that acknowledgement that their light still shines with us.”
Following the ceremony, participants are invited inside the church to visit over coffee and snacks.
Handrahan said the society is grateful for the church’s support in making the ceremony possible.
“We're a non-denominational organization, but they are very open to helping us provide this for our community and we're ever so grateful to the folks there,” she said.
The Remembrance Tree Ceremony has become a longstanding tradition for the Hospice Society, typically drawing about 30 participants each year.
Handrahan said there is always room for more community members to take part.
“Just come. We start at noon with registration, we’ll get you sorted, and we have lights available,” she said.
The ceremony takes place Saturday, Nov. 28, at St. Peter’s Lutheran Church. Registration begins at noon.
For more information, email info@cochranehospicesociety.ca.
