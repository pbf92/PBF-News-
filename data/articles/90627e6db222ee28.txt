OUTLET: Cantech Letter

Meta, Microsoft or Apple? Buy all of them, fund manager says
The recent selloff in technology stocks has created buying opportunities in Meta Platforms, Microsoft and Alphabet, according to Harris Douglas Asset Management portfolio manager Paul Harris, who believes their established businesses and financial strength position them to benefit from continued artificial intelligence investment.
Speaking on BNN Bloomberg’s Market Call on Nov. 13, Harris named the three technology companies as his top picks, arguing that concerns about AI infrastructure spending have pushed their valuations below levels justified by their growth prospects.
Harris said Meta Platforms (Meta Platforms Stock Quote, Chart, News, Analysts, Financials NASDAQ:META) remains attractive because of the strength of its advertising business and its ability to use AI to improve advertising performance. “We’ve owned Meta for a while, so we like it because there’s a couple of things,” Harris said. “One, when you look at their actual advertising business, it’s really doing very well.”
The fund manager said the company’s focus on consumer applications gives it opportunities to generate returns from AI without competing directly across every segment of the market.
Harris also cited Meta’s gross margins of approximately 82% and its valuation relative to the broader market. “I think that this is a unique opportunity where you’re buying the stock at below the market multiple,” he said. “And I think you have a lot of opportunity for growth over the next several years.”
Although he remains concerned about the amount of debt and capital spending associated with AI infrastructure, Harris believes established technology companies could benefit if additional data centre development faces regulatory restrictions. He argued that companies with existing infrastructure would have an advantage over newer competitors, potentially strengthening their pricing power.
Harris also selected Microsoft (Microsoft Stock Quote, Chart, News, Analysts, Financials NASDAQ:MSFT), citing its cloud computing operations, established software business and financial capacity to support AI expansion.
The investor thinks concerns that AI applications could replace traditional productivity software have contributed to pressure on software valuations, but Microsoft’s large customer base remains an important advantage. The company’s Azure cloud business continues to grow rapidly, while Outlook, Microsoft 365 and Copilot provide opportunities to incorporate AI into applications already used by businesses.
“Microsoft has some very unique businesses,” Harris said. “One is that they have the cloud business, which continues to grow.”
Harris argued that most corporate customers are looking for practical AI applications rather than the most advanced technology available.
He thinks Microsoft’s existing relationships with large organizations could help it retain customers even when competing AI models offer some performance advantages. “So I think there’s a lot of, you know, in big companies, are just not going to take a big chance on technology that, you know, proliferating that through their whole businesses,” he said.
The investor also believes Microsoft’s financial resources and investments in AI companies, including Anthropic, provide additional growth opportunities. “I think they’ll do well, and it’s not as expensive as it was a few years ago,” Harris said. “So I think you’re buying it at a good multiple here at these levels.”
Harris’s third selection was Alphabet (Alphabet Stock Quote, Chart, News, Analysts, Financials NASDAQ:GOOGL), which he believes remains undervalued despite its established advertising, cloud computing and autonomous vehicle businesses. He said investors have repeatedly underestimated Google’s ability to compete in AI, while overlooking the continued growth of its core operations.
“The reality is that people constantly think that this company is nowhere in AI, and then they show you that they are,” Harris said.
Alphabet’s advertising business, YouTube and cloud computing operations provide several sources of revenue, while its Waymo autonomous vehicle subsidiary offers exposure to the developing robotaxi market. Harris said YouTube is one of the world’s leading streaming platforms and competes directly with Netflix for viewers. He also considers Waymo an important competitive advantage in autonomous transportation.
“So they’ve got a lot of really good businesses, and I think you’re buying it very cheap,” he said.
Harris estimates Alphabet is trading at approximately 16 times earnings, which he considers attractive given its diversified operations and growth potential.
