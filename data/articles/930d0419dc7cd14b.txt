Zeely – AI Advertising Platform, Video Ad Creation & Meta Campaign Management
Zeely is an AI advertising platform from Zeely, Inc., a company with Ukrainian roots. It helps small businesses and marketing teams create image and video ads, launch Facebook and Instagram campaigns, and track performance. Users add product details manually or import them from a public product link, then select visual references and generate content. Video tools include AI avatars, editable scripts, voice settings and captions. Brand DNA guides content around a business's identity, while campaign dashboards support reviewing results. The website lists Starter, Plus and Growth subscriptions with credit allowances. Current pricing is listed on the official website; advertising spend is separate.
