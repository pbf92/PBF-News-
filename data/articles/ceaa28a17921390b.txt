bet365, DraftKings Top US YouGov Poll on Satisfaction, Awareness
bet365 and DraftKings have come out on top in respective YouGov polls gauging “customer satisfaction” and “brand consideration,” the company's report suggests.
In its “Moneyline: U.S. Online Sports Betting and Prediction Market Rankings 2026” polls, YouGov gauged opinions from over 12,000 homegrown gamblers. And the results make interesting reading.
The key point is that the stats cover regulated markets in the U.S. Therefore, there is no data on offshore brands. However, there are enough legal online gambling brands to compile a Top 10 based on the “most considered sports betting” brands and the top sites for customer satisfaction.
YouGov Rankings: Key Findings
- DraftKings is the “most considered” betting brand among U.S. adults
- bet365 scored highest for customer satisfaction, beating DraftKings by 26 points
- DraftKings also tops table for satisfaction among Millennials & Gen Xers
- bet365 still wins out for Gen Zs
DraftKings Tops ‘Most Considered’ Brand List
Among U.S. adults, the YouGov poll suggests, DraftKings came out on top with 8.9% “consideration” in 2026.
In this poll, “consideration” means a customer would consider trying the brand the next time they make a bet.
Close behind at 7.8% was FanDuel, with U.K. betting giant bet365 coming in third at 5.6%. The only prediction market to make the Top 5 was Kalshi, scoring a 4.6% consideration percentage.
The YouGov BrandIndex stats covered a year of data, from Nov. 1, 2025, to Oct. 31, 2026.
YouGov’s Most Considered Betting Brands
- DraftKings: 8.9%
- FanDuel: 7.8%
- Bet365: 5.6%
- Kalshi: 4.6%
- BetMGM: 4.1%
bet365 Shines for Customer Satisfaction
YouGov also ranked online betting brands based on customer satisfaction. Current and former customers were asked how satisfied they were with a range of gambling brands in the U.S.
Out top by some margin was bet365 Sportsbook, which operates in several regulated U.S. states. It recorded an 81.9% customer satisfaction rating, ahead of next-best DraftKings in second. BetMGM was close behind in third at 54.7%.
Customer Satisfaction: Best in Show
- Bet365: 81.9%
- DraftKings: 55.3%
- BetMGM: 54.7%
- FanDuel: 51.4%
- PrizePicks: 51.2%
Millennials Love DraftKings
YouGov also assessed generational splits among U.S. gamblers by brand trust.
According to the findings, DraftKings had the biggest sway among Millennials, with a 13.9% consideration rating. Millennials also considered FanDuel and bet365 above other operators.
For Gen Xers, when not fretting about retirement or mortgage rates, DraftKings also got top marks. 10.3% of Gen X gamblers favored DraftKings, with FanDuel (9.0%) coming second.
Gen Z players, the youngest group of legal U.S. gamblers, won out with a 13.3% consideration rate. That’s astonishing, given how much longer bet365 has been operating than newer rivals like DraftKings and FanDuel.
The latest polls had Kalshi at 6.9% to 7.7% for consideration among Gen Z players and Millennials. Only 4.4% of older Gen X gamblers gave Kalshi the thumbs-up.
Kalshi on the Up?
With prediction markets growing hugely in 2026, it will be interesting to see next year’s YouGov poll.
Kalshi has grown exponentially this year, with both its valuation and average volumes rising. Media buzz has also grown as sports bettors find new ways to trade on NFL, NHL, and other markets.
DraftKings Poll Comes Amidst Jump in Net Losses
The good polling on DraftKings' awareness comes amid a surprising jump in net losses for Q3, according to the company’s latest report.
DraftKings shares slumped nearly 22% after the company revealed a net loss of $468.7 million. This is due in part to the company making — or trying to make — inroads into the competitive prediction market industry.
