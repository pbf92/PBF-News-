TITLE: Snapchat Opens Its AI Creator Network to Brands in Beta
OUTLET: https://www.thekeyword.co/

Snapchat has opened the Snap Creator Network, an AI tool that finds creators for brands, in open beta. An advertiser describes the creator or content it wants, by audience, category or campaign goal, and the tool returns the creators it judges the best fit.
Snap announced the beta on 18 November in a Snapchat for Business post, alongside two other creator updates: Visited By on Snap Map and a Creator Agency Partner Program. The post gives no date for a full launch, and it publishes no pricing or eligibility rules for the beta.
From a June Promise to an Open Beta
Snap first described the tool in June, when it opened its ad platform to third-party AI agents. It said then that the Snap Creator Network would launch later in 2026. It called the tool a first step toward a more agentic creator marketplace, where AI turns an advertiser's intent into a campaign that is ready to launch.
The open beta arrives after most of Snap's rivals shipped their own versions. TikTok added Creator AI Search to TikTok One, matching creators to campaigns on goals and past partnership data. X launched Creator Connect, built on xAI, and LinkedIn opened a Creator Marketplace for brands. Each platform now wants to own the step where a brand finds its creator, not just the ad that follows.
What Brands Can Do in the Snap Creator Network
Search is only the first step. From the same place, a brand can review how a creator's content has performed, save creators to shortlists and message them to set up a partnership.
A brand can also boost a creator's organic content. That turns a Snap the creator has already posted into paid reach, without a new piece of content being made. Pinterest made the same move in October, opening creators' published Pins to brand promotion. Snap's developer documentation adds a condition the announcement does not. Boosting an organic post is a brand-level partnership, and it requires the creator to have posted and tagged the brand, with the resulting ad carrying a "Paid Partnership with [Brand]" label. The permission therefore has to be in place before the content goes up, rather than applied to any public Snap a brand later picks out.
Snap says the tool matches on audience fit rather than follower count. The post does not explain how the tool decides fit, or which performance measures a brand sees before it reaches out. Snap's help centre answers part of that. Creators enter the pool through Brand Partnerships settings, which require a Public Profile, an age of 18 or over, more than 5,000 followers, more than 3 Spotlights or 20 Public Stories in the last 28 days, and more than 10,000 views in the same period — so the set of creators a brand can be matched with has a follower floor regardless of what the matching weighs. Those settings are opt-in, and Snap says each creator controls what is visible to brands, including their Public Profile analytics, which means the performance history a shortlist shows differs from creator to creator. Snap says approved brands may then message a creator in Chat.
Snap's own screenshot shows the starting point: a chat box asking "What do you want to work on today?" Its suggested prompts include "Beauty creators with over 500K followers", so follower count remains a filter a brand can ask for.
The Evidence Snap Offers
Snap backs the launch with research it commissioned from eye square. In a US survey of 1,579 people, 70% of Snapchatters said they enjoy seeing the everyday lives of their favourite creators on Snapchat, and 56% said they trust those creators' recommendations. The figures come from a 2026 study called The New Era of Creators, and Snap has not published the question wording.
The post also points to creator campaigns for Sour Patch Kids and Steve Madden. Both ran in 2025, before the Snap Creator Network existed, so they show what creator ads delivered for two brands rather than what the new tool adds. Snap puts numbers on each, and sources them differently. The Sour Patch Kids campaign, which combined creator content with Snap Ads and Story Ads in an AR activation, is credited with 2.3x ROAS from a Nielsen Catalina Solutions sales-lift study running 7 April to 16 July 2025, and a 6-point lift in overall ad awareness from a Kantar brand-lift study over the same window. Steve Madden, working with the agency January Digital, is credited with reaching 2.2 million Snapchatters at a 44% lower CPM and $7 incremental ROAS — which Snap's footnote attributes to Steve Madden's own internal data rather than to a third-party study.
Snap says it will keep investing in its creator ecosystem next year. For now, advertisers trying the beta are testing a matching model whose workings Snap has not described.
Recap
What is the Snap Creator Network?
The Snap Creator Network is an AI-powered platform where brands find and work with Snapchat creators. It entered open beta on 18 November 2026.
How does the Snap Creator Network work?
An advertiser describes the creator or content it wants by audience, category or campaign goal, and the tool surfaces matches. The brand can then review creator performance, shortlist creators, message them and boost their organic content.
What does the Snap Creator Network change for advertisers?
Creator discovery, outreach and paid boosting now sit inside Snapchat's own tools. Snap says matches are made on audience fit rather than follower count, though it has not explained how fit is judged.
