SA analyst: Volatility could fade, but markets must stabilize first
Wall Street endured a steep selloff Friday, with technology stocks leading a broad market retreat that left major indexes facing one of their worst weekly performances in quite some time.
The Dow Jones Industrial Average DJI fell -1.9%, while the S&P 500 (SP500) dropped -3.6% and the Nasdaq Composite COMP plunged -5.2%.
Seeking Alpha analyst Alex King of Cestrian Capital Research acknowledged the difficult stretch for bullish investors but suggested several factors could help markets stabilize in the coming week.
King pointed to the possibility that the CBOE Volatility Index’s recent spike could quickly reverse, while declining Treasury yields may signal improving conditions for equities. He also cited monthly options expiration as a potential contributor to Friday’s market turbulence.
“This last week has tested the patience, and likely the resources, of the most zoomed-out bulls,” King stated. However, he cautioned against assuming the worst is over, emphasizing that market price action remains the most important signal.
“But as always the only thing that matters is price; and price has yet to stabilize in the indices,” he added.
