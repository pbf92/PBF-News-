TITLE: Raheem Sterling discovers sentence after pleading guilty to dangerous driving
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Raheem Sterling avoids jail after pleading guilty to dangerous driving
Raheem Sterling received the verdict following a court appearance this morning
Former Manchester City star Raheem Sterling has been handed a suspended 12-month prison sentence for dangerous driving. He has also been disqualified from driving for two years and ordered to carry out 100 hours of unpaid work.
For the offence of failing to provide a specimen, Sterling received a two-month prison sentence, suspended for 18 months, to run concurrently. He was also ordered to pay a victim surcharge of £187 and prosecution costs of £85.
Dressed in a black top and dark trousers, Sterling arrived at the Hampshire court on Wednesday morning. He has been subject to an interim driving ban and unconditional bail since entering a guilty plea in September.
Sterling admitted to driving his Lamborghini Urus dangerously along the M25, M3, A327 and Minley Road prior to crashing into a metal gate on the morning of May 28. The 31-year-old also admitted to possessing six nitrous oxide canisters for wrongful inhalation, a class C drug and failing to provide a specimen.
Police body-worn footage of the footballer's arrest at 9.15am shows him in the crashed car with two canisters on his lap as the officer tells him: "You can put that down mate."
Defending Sterling, Jason Bartfeld KC said he has tarnished his own reputation, but he is more concerned by "the unfortunate side consequences it's had for his family, particularly his children who have had to deal with a certain degree of unpleasantness".
He said to Sterling's credit, he has recognised various "unhealthy coping systems" which led to this offence and has sought to address them in the "most positive and effective way with some success".

He said: "He is absolutely determined he will never appear before a criminal court again. I think that as an aspiration is mirrored by the assessment of the writer of the pre-sentence report, and it is very much his intention to repay his debt to society whichever this court feels is appropriate."
The lawyer added Sterling wants to put this behind him, move forward and "resume his role as very much a role model" for those who have followed his career and engaged with him and his organisations.
Before delivering her verdict, District Judge Kirsty Allman said: "Mr Sterling, you appear before the court for sentence in respect of three offences you pleaded guilty to. You are 31 years old and a man of previous good character. I accept your character has been positively exemplary."
During the September hearing at Basingstoke Magistrates' Court, prosecutor Simon Jones said a number of calls were made between 7.45am and 8.52am about a black sports car and the manner it was being driven.
Witness Christopher Jennings called 999 at 7.49am after driving alongside Sterling's car on the M25, and "he was shocked by what he saw", Mr Jones said, and made clear "he sighted the defendant inhaling from the balloon".
Fiona Jennings – driving on the southbound M3 at around 8.45am – said she saw a black Lamborghini and described the driving as "absolutely horrendous" and "swerving in and out of lanes", the court heard.
"She had genuine concerns the driver might kill someone," Mr Jones told the court. "She looked across, initially thought an air bag was in play, then realised in fact it was a large white inflated balloon and the neck of balloon was in mouth of the driver."
Dashcam footage released by the Crown Prosecution Service shows a black Lamborghini switching lanes multiple times before hitting a concrete barrier on the right side and continuing on the road. Sterling, who played for City between 2015 and 2022, has been a free agent since leaving Dutch side Feyenoord at the end of last season.
