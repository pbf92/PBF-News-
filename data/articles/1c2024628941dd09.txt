TITLE: Shopify Stock Is Down 16% in the Past Month: Should You Buy the Dip?
OUTLET: Yahoo! Finance Canada

Written by Puja Tayal at The Motley Fool Canada
Shopify (TSX: SHOP) stock fell 16% in the past month since October 15, 2026. The broader sell-off came after October 28, 2026, when the Bank of Canada hiked the interest rate by 25 basis points.
The US Fed also hiked interest rates on September 16, but Shopify stock rallied 40% after that. And if we look at Shopify's exposure, it earns 63% of its revenue from the United States of America. This is because the overall sentiment remained positive for tech stocks in September.
Behind Shopify stock's 16% dip in the past month
The October hit came as the capital markets had already started booking profits on artificial intelligence (AI) stocks. Look at the October 7 to November 3 stock price momentum of any AI stock, and you will see a correction. Meta announced plans to raise equity capital to fund expansion. Google's third-quarter earnings showed how its AI efforts reduced AdSense revenue. All this slowed tech momentum, and Bank of Canada's rate hike added fuel to the fire.
Is there more downside for Shopify stock?
The market expects the US Fed to announce another rate hike on December 16. Moreover, Nvidia's stock price fallout saw many investors lose money. Then came the US midterm elections and US President Donald Trump's push to use his executive powers more aggressively. All this has made investors weary. Until the Fed meeting, markets might remain bearish towards anything that appears overvalued.
After the 16% dip in the past month, Shopify stock is trading at 13.5x its sales per share and 59x its forward earnings per share. These valuations are closer to its March 2025 valuations when the stock fell to seasonal lows. The technical trading indicator also shows Shopify stock moving towards the oversold category with a Relative Strength Index of 35.
While the dip looks lucrative, investors could hold off buying Shopify stock for another month and adopt a wait-and-watch approach till there is some clarity. Even if Shopify stock appears reasonably valued, the macro environment could influence the stock market and trigger a bigger dip.
Should you buy the November dip?
The current market is not on a normal course. A tighter monetary policy could affect the stock market. While the scenario may not be as severe as 2022, you cannot rule out the possibility of an AI bubble burst.
The tech sector is currently on a cliffhanger. AI investments could face stagnation amidst the Nvidia scandal. The involvement of government and geopolitical tensions in AI chips could create a supply shock. Amidst all this, Shopify could be a silver lining with its limited capital expenditure and steady growth that does not depend on AI.
