OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Bookings for viral 'AI slop’ Christmas markets cancelled as organisers issue apology
The event was described as an 'absolute shambles'
A company behind a viral Christmas market which was slammed for its 'AI slop' posters that were drastically different to what the occasion was like has cancelled all bookings and apologised. Kernow Christmas Markets was advertised with the use of generative AI ahead of its launch on Friday (November 20).
The promotional material featured images that showed a Christmas themed wonderland with a red carpet and garland-lined stalls.
Traders had paid £300 each to man a stall at the fete, however, during the premiere, both stallholders and visitors voiced their displeasure at the virtually empty hall, a handful of black gazebos, an undecorated Christmas tree and random 'festive' buckets they encountered.

The event was to be held in conjunction with Cornwall's Winter Wonderland. Unfortunately, that also saw its fair share of troubles with delays and an unfinished bar and ice rink.
The bar and ice rink are now understood to be much improved and running, reports Cornwall Live.
The contrast between the AI generated posters and the lonely looking tables under black gazebos and 'festive' buckets was so astonishing that the publication's story went viral around the UK and in other parts of the world with people describing it as an "absolute shambles".
The story was picked up by national newspapers, appeared on ITV This Morning, hit Tik Tok in New York and TV news in America and was shared countless times on Facebook and social media.
Now family business Rowlands Attractions, known for the funfairs they hold around the country in spring and summer, has come out to explain itself. It created the marketing posters and dealt with the traders' fees, of which it kept half, on behalf of Pool Market.
In a long post on the Kernow Christmas Market Facebook page, which is meant to be its last word on the matter "human to human", the company said it had tried to "do a good deed and bring a little festive magic into Pool Market and for those who live locally in Cornwall".
To the traders the business said: "We understand the pressure and stress this must cause for all of you and we are very open to discussing options and supporting where we can. We apologise for the inconvenience caused and none of this takes away the greatness of your products and the effort you put into your trade."
It said: "We advertise it, book the stalls and they do the rest - such as signage, decorating and greeting the traders and helping them with queries. Yes the funds were paid to us, then they were going to be split with Pool Market.
"We are gutted, just as much as you all that things didn't go as planned. The criticism we have faced and the words that have been seen on social media and across the news make us look at the world slightly differently. Kindness and compassion goes out of the window so easily to knock something that people don’t care to understand the full story of.
"As a result of this, we are going to cancel and refund all current and future bookings and Kernow Christmas Market will take no further bookings for 2026. Any current bookings will be fully refunded and if any traders wish to continue to trade they must book direct with Pool Market."
Rowlands added: "Our focus is and will always remain on providing the very best experience for those who visit us. At our festive Winter Wonderland. Where you are greeted by festive huts, catering court, red carpets, large ice skating rink, Kernow lodge, live Music performances, character meet and greets, Santas grotto and funfair.
"Entry is free, kindness is free, and we will always be grateful for those who continue to recognise the efforts, cost and dedication that goes on behind the scenes. We see you, we hear you and we appreciate you."
It is understood some traders are being given pitches at the Winter Wonderland event to help support them after the fiasco indoors.
Pool Market has been unavailable for comment.
