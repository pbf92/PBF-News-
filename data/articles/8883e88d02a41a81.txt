TITLE: New Residential Sales Press Release
OUTLET: Census.gov

New Home Sales
Sales of new single-family houses in October 2026 were at a seasonally-adjusted annual rate of 701,000, according to estimates released jointly today by the U.S. Census Bureau and the Department of Housing and Urban Development.  This is 14.2 percent (±22.6 percent)* above the September 2026 rate of 614,000, and is 7.5 percent (±23.0 percent)* above the October 2025 rate of 652,000.
For Sale Inventory and Months’ Supply
The seasonally-adjusted estimate of new houses for sale at the end of October 2026 was 470,000.  This is 1.5 percent (±1.4 percent) below the September 2026 estimate of 477,000, and is 4.1 percent (±4.1 percent) below the October 2025 estimate of 490,000. 
This represents a supply of 8.0 months at the current sales rate.  The months' supply is 14.0 percent (±21.7 percent)* below the September 2026 estimate of 9.3 months, and is 11.1 percent (±20.6 percent)* below the October 2025 estimate of 9.0 months.
Sales Price
The median sales price of new houses sold in October 2026 was $391,300.  This is 0.3 percent (±10.9 percent)* below the September 2026 price of $392,600, and is 3.2 percent (±9.2 percent)* below the October 2025 price of $404,200.  The average sales price of new houses sold in October 2026 was $459,900.  This is 2.2 percent (±9.3 percent)* below the September 2026 price of $470,200, and is 12.1 percent (±8.1 percent) below the October 2025 price of $523,400.
