Eightco Holdings Disclosed Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard
On November 20, 2026, Nasdaq notified Eightco Holdings that its stock had closed below the $1.00 minimum bid price for 30 consecutive business days, failing Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2). The company has until May 19, 2027, to regain compliance; its stock will continue trading on Nasdaq under the symbol ORBS during the compliance period.
Original SEC Filing: Eightco Holdings Inc. [ ORBS ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
