TITLE: Pochettino’s first Manchester United headache before blockbuster debut against Liverpool: EPL Injury Update
OUTLET: football360.com.au

Mauricio Pochettino already has one injury concern as he prepares for the start of his Manchester United tenure.
Pochettino has replaced Michael Carrick at embattled Premier League giants United, who languish in the relegation zone heading into this weekend’s blockbuster fixture against rivals Liverpool.
Patrick Dorgu, who has been hampered by injuries this season, withdrew from Denmark’s squad during the international break and is a player to watch as former Tottenham, Chelsea and Paris Saint-Germain boss Pochettino gears up for life in Manchester following the end of his United States tenure.
“Dorgu arrived and trained with the rest of the national team on Wednesday,” Denmark head coach Brian Riemer told Tipsbladet recently. “He’s still on his way back. The fact that he got those minutes against Aston Villa and did a fantastic job should be taken with a grain of salt.
“There are still some lingering issues with the major muscle injury he suffered. We need to make sure it doesn’t flare up again. We’re optimistic, and we also believe things will go well.
“But he’s a player we need to be careful with. As for what that means in terms of matches and playing time, we’ll have to wait and see.”
Ahead of the Premier League’s return following the international break, Football360 looks at some of the key updates for SuperCoach EPL managers across Australia.
