TITLE: Rockstar Developers Detail the 'GTA VI' Features That Got Cut
OUTLET: Hypebeast

Rockstar North developers revealed in an interview with German outlet 'PC Games' that several planned 'GTA VI' features were scrapped during development Tennis, cinema and theater visits, crawling, home decorating and crafting were all considered before being dropped The reasons ranged from quality concerns to features that didn't fit the game's story or felt too passive
Grand Theft Auto VI arrived with a long list of activities across Leonida, but Rockstar has now confirmed that several more were dropped before launch. In an interview with PC Games during the review period, Rockstar North development manager Rob Nelson and design producer Scott Mitchell went through the mechanics and pastimes that were planned for Jason and Lucia and didn’t make the final game.
The biggest cut is tennis. The sport was a playable side activity in GTA V and was planned for its sequel, but according to the developers, Rockstar was never happy enough with how it played to ship it. Mini golf, on the other hand, made it into the final game, so Leonida still has at least one leisure sport.
Movie and theater outings were removed for a different reason. A cinema visit or a night at the theater was considered as a way to fill downtime, but Rockstar decided these were too passive. In a game built around player control, sitting and watching apparently didn’t give players enough to do.
A third group of cuts dealt with how the two protagonists move through and live in their world. The team looked at letting Jason and Lucia crawl along the ground, decorate their homes and craft items. The developers said some of these ideas were dropped because they clashed with the story or the core gameplay loop, although the interview doesn’t say which reason applied to which feature or how far each one got before it was cut.
What did make it in reflects where Rockstar spent its effort. Nelson said the studio is proudest of the relationship between Lucia and Jason, and that the slower pacing of Red Dead Redemption 2 shaped the quieter, character-focused moments between missions. That focus seems to have paid off: GTA VI holds a Metascore of 99, matching The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the top score in the aggregator’s history.
