TITLE: Endava Becomes Anthropic Select Partner to Accelerate AI-Native Engineering and Business AI Value
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Achieving Select Partner status strengthens Endava's AI-native transformation capabilities and expands choice for enterprise clients in moving from AI experimentation to measurable business outcomes.
LONDON, November 25, 2026--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Endava (NYSE: DAVA), the technology-driven business transformation group whose AI-native approach combines cutting-edge technology with deep industry expertise, today announced it has achieved Anthropic Select Partner status. This status recognises Endava's Claude-certified expertise, enterprise production deployments and demonstrated customer outcomes.
We believe this milestone strengthens Endava's position as an AI-native transformation partner, enhancing its ability to help enterprises modernise complex technology environments, securely deploy intelligent AI agents, and unlock measurable business value with and from AI.
While many organisations have successfully experimented with AI, turning that investment into scalable, production-ready solutions remains a significant challenge. Endava is addressing this by combining Anthropic's Claude technology with deep industry expertise, enterprise engineering and Dava.Flow™, Endava's AI-native engagement lifecycle, designed to support greater speed, governance, transparency and accountability across the software delivery lifecycle.
As part of its approach Endava helps its clients to use Claude alongside their existing applications, data and workflows, connecting the technology with existing systems and applying industry-specific business rules and controls.
Endava is focusing its expanded Anthropic collaboration on three strategic areas of enterprise transformation:
- AI-Native Software Engineering: Enhancing the modernisation of complex enterprise applications and transforming software development through Claude Code. By embedding AI throughout the engineering lifecycle, Endava aims to improve developer productivity, reduce technical debt and accelerate the delivery of business-critical applications.
- Enterprise Agentic AI: Moving beyond isolated AI use cases to design, deploy and securely manage intelligent agents that operate across enterprise applications, data and workflows. Combining Claude's reasoning capabilities with Endava's industry knowledge, integration expertise and governance aims to enable organisations to automate complex processes, improve decision-making and unlock new levels of operational efficiency.
- AI Transformation Across Hyperscalers: Supporting enterprises to adopt and scale Claude within their existing cloud environments, including AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure. Endava's multi-cloud engineering capabilities are designed to help clients integrate AI with their existing technology investments, with the aim of providing greater flexibility, scalability and choice while reducing transformation complexity.
