Anthropic IPO: 5 Big Ways It Could Boost NVIDIA Stock
NVIDIA Corporation NVDA is facing pressure amid a recent share price correction. Against this backdrop, Anthropic’s initial public offering (IPO) could provide the much-needed boost to the artificial intelligence (AI) stock. Here’re five ways the listing could benefit the chipmaker –
Anthropic’s IPO Puts AI Investor Confidence to the Test
The company behind the AI chatbot Claude — Anthropic — is expected to launch its IPO on Nov. 19, subject to regulatory clearance. The company is expected to list its Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol “ANT”. The company already started its IPO roadshow on Nov. 10 and has proposed selling shares at $70–$78 each, with total shares offered at 825 million. This would potentially value the total share offering at $57.75 billion to $64.35 billion.
So, what does the IPO mean for Anthropic? The company is allowing ordinary investors to buy its shares and, in the process, raise a huge sum of money to expand its AI infrastructure and develop Claude to compete with archrival OpenAI. It also gives Anthropic greater access to public-market funding to support its path to future profitability. Anthropic may have reported revenues of $33.2 billion in the nine months ended September 2026 but recorded an operating loss of $1.26 billion, indicating the high costs it is bearing to grow its AI business.
Nonetheless, Anthropic’s IPO could significantly impact the broader AI trade, which is currently facing bouts of volatility due to concerns over lofty valuations, boardroom controversies, and growing doubts over the sustainability of massive AI infrastructure spending. A strong listing could instill faith in the AI trade and boost chipmakers, data center operators and cloud providers. A weaker one may deepen concerns that the AI trade may be fading with time.
How a Strong Anthropic IPO Could Boost NVIDIA’s AI Business
Leadership uncertainty has weighed on NVIDIA as its shares recently experienced a sharp stock correction. However, a strong Anthropic IPO outcome could provide a boost to NVIDIA in many ways. Of course, the IPO proceeds will go to Anthropic rather than NVIDIA. However, a successful IPO could help Anthropic raise additional capital to expand its AI infrastructure, potentially driving greater demand for NVIDIA’s cutting-edge graphics processing units and various other data center hardware.
A positive Anthropic IPO outcome could also reassure investors that demand for AI products remains strong and that customers are willing to pay for these technologies. Sustained AI spending would support NVIDIA’s data center business, which continues to be its primary growth engine. Moreover, a positive Anthropic IPO could revive investors’ appetite for AI-related stocks, including NVIDIA.
NVIDIA, anyhow, has strong business ties with Anthropic. The chipmaker has committed to investing up to $10 billion in Anthropic for computing infrastructure development. Meanwhile, Anthropic has committed to purchasing $30 billion of Microsoft Azure computing capacity powered by NVIDIA’s state-of-the-art chips. A successful IPO could strengthen this relationship and provide more opportunities for additional NVIDIA chip sales.
More broadly, a strong Anthropic listing could encourage further investment across the entire AI ecosystem, benefiting companies involved in chip manufacturing, data centers, and cloud computing. Being a leader in supplying AI computing infrastructure, NVIDIA stands to gain from this renewed investment.
Can Anthropic’s IPO Revive NVIDIA Shares Amid Market Uncertainty?
With NVIDIA’s share price facing considerable pressure, Anthropic’s IPO could act as the much-needed catalyst for a recovery. A positive IPO debut could revive confidence in AI spending, strengthen future demand for chips, and help NVIDIA regain investor favor. But the chipmaker’s potential recovery also hinges on its fiscal third-quarter earnings results, scheduled for release on Nov. 17 (read more: Boardroom Drama Ahead of NVIDIA's Q3 Earnings: Buy or Sell the Stock?).
