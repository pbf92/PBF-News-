TITLE: Crooks turn woman’s Meta ad account into money-minting tool, siphon Rs 60 lakh
OUTLET: The Times of India

Kolkata: A suspected international cyber-fraud syndicate linked to Vietnam siphoned nearly Rs 60 lakh from a woman’s bank account by hijacking a linked Meta advertising account to promote an unrelated Vietnamese Instagram page.
The fraudsters executed an ad-account hijacking modus operandi. After gaining unauthorised access to a Meta advertising account linked to the victim’s private bank savings account, they bypassed standard security checks to run high-budget promotional campaigns for a third-party Vietnamese Instagram Reel entirely unconnected to the account holder’s business.
Instead of draining the account in a single heist, the perpetrators triggered a barrage of repeated automated billing transactions — exceeding the user’s cumulative ad spend over the preceding three years — without alerting the account holders until nearly Rs 60 lakh had been wiped out.
Based on an E-Zero FIR forwarded by Cyber Cell (East Division) and a written complaint by a 33-year-old resident of Urbana Complex, Anandapur police registered a case on Nov 14 under Sections 318(4) (cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property), 319(2) (cheating by personation), and 308(2) (extortion) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS).
The police and cyber experts claimed that the account is already under investigation to ascertain if unaccounted money had been parked here. 
“We believe the account was compromised and its holder also will come under the investigating agency’s scanner,” said an investigating officer.
According to the FIR, the unauthorised deductions occurred primarily between Oct 7 and Oct 12, though cumulative billing spanned nearly a month. The complainant stated that his sister’s savings account at a private bank was used as the payment method for his Meta ad account. Upon noticing the massive depletion of funds, he audited the advertising dashboard and discovered that approximately Rs 54 lakh alone had been billed toward boosting content from an unknown Vietnamese profile, besides withdrawals, pushing total losses to roughly Rs 60 lakh.
Immediately upon detecting the breach on Oct 7, the family blocked the linked payment card, lodged chargeback and dispute requests with the bank, and escalated the matter to Meta’s support division seeking an audit and refund. Parallel complaints were also filed on the National Cyber Crime reporting portal, with the divisional cyber cell, and with senior officers at Kolkata Police headquarters in Lalbazar, who directed the complainant to the local police station.
Investigating officers said they were examining digital access logs, IP trails, Meta billing records, and banking transaction trails to trace the ultimate beneficiaries behind the Vietnamese page and establish how the payment gateway was compromised.
