Caribou Biosciences Disclosed Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard
On November 20, 2026, Caribou Biosciences received a Nasdaq deficiency notice because its common stock had closed below the $1.00 minimum bid price for 30 consecutive business days, under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5450(a)(1). The company has until May 19, 2027, to regain compliance, generally by maintaining a closing bid price of at least $1.00 for 10 consecutive business days; it may qualify for an additional 180-day period if it meets other requirements.
Original SEC Filing: Caribou Biosciences, Inc. [ CRBU ] - 8-K - Nov. 25, 2026
