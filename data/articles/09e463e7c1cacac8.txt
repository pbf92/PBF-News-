TITLE: Is ‘GTA 6’ out? How people are playing the new game already, plus what the verdict is so far.
OUTLET: Yahoo Tech

It's finally here: The long-awaited Grand Theft Auto VI drops at midnight local time, so players in New Zealand are already back in Vice City. So far, the reaction is overwhelmingly positive: as of 8:30 a.m. EST, GTA 6 became Metacritic's highest-rated video game in history, based on 167 reviews and counting. Digital marketing firm Sensor Tower estimates that GTA 6 pre-orders have already topped $1.65 billion in sales.
If you absolutely can't wait until midnight in your time zone, you can try to set your console's region to New Zealand, but be warned the process is much simpler on an Xbox than it is on a PlayStation5. And some PS5 users have reported their accounts getting banned when they tried this workaround. Since it may cost slightly more to buy the game due to currency conversions, PS5 users around the globe might just want to sit tight until midnight in their home time zone.
If you don't want to be spoiled, consider getting offline for most of today. Due to the staggered rollout, more regions will get access to GTA 6 throughout the day on Nov. 18. Here's when different areas hit midnight on Nov. 19, 2026:
- New Zealand: 6:00 AM EST (Nov 18) / 03:00 UTC (Nov 18)
- Australia Eastern: 8:00 AM EST (Nov 18) / 05:00 UTC (Nov 18)
- Japan: 10:00 AM EST (Nov 18) / 15:00 UTC (Nov 18)
- Europe: 6:00 PM EST (Nov 18) / 23:00 UTC (Nov 18)
- UK (UTC): 7:00 PM EST (Nov 18) / 00:00 UTC (Nov 19)
- US Eastern: 12:00 AM EST (Nov 19) / 05:00 UTC (Nov 19)
- US Central: 1:00 AM EST (Nov 19) / 06:00 UTC (Nov 19)
- US Mountain: 2:00 AM EST (Nov 19) / 07:00 UTC (Nov 19)
- US Pacific: 3:00 AM EST (Nov 19) / 08:00 UTC (Nov 19)
- Alaska: 4:00 AM EST (Nov 19) / 09:00 UTC (Nov 19)
- Hawaii: 5:00 AM EST (Nov 19) / 10:00 UTC (Nov 19)
So far, users who have access to the game are impressed with the high quality of the graphics across the game and how smooth the driving feels, both on the road and on the beach, and how realistic other aspects are like water. So far there haven't been any widespread reports of glitches, but we'll keep an eye out and report back if we see them.
At launch the game only features single-player mode but you can still show off to your friends online.
The standard version of GTA 6 costs $79.99 for PS5 and Xbox, while the "ultimate" version is priced at $99.99. If you haven't cleared space on your Xbox or PlayStation5 to pre-load it, there's still time — but don't wait until the last minute. It takes up a whopping 212GB depending on the device, so you'll likely need to clear some space and wait a bit for the download to finish.
For PS5 users, you'll need to take an extra step before you can play. On Wednesday, Sony issued a system software update for PS5 that it says is required to play GTA 6. "Players should download this update prior to midnight on November 19 so that they're ready to play on launch day," the company said in a post on X.
And if you're one of the many gamers planning to take the day off from work or school on Friday to play GTA 6, Xbox has a doctor's note from DJ Khaled (aka Dr. Xbox) that may or may not be the excuse you need (or maybe just use your PTO like you normally would).
