TITLE: PS5’s are apparently going up in literal flames thanks to GTA 6
OUTLET: Yahoo Tech

Running a game as demanding as GTA 6 isn't easy, especially not for a six-year-old console using hardware even older than its release date. How Rockstar managed to cram 30 FPS into this little white box is genuinely a wonder to me, but it seems not every console can withstand the pressure.
As spotted by Insider Gaming, users online have begun reporting consistent crashing while playing GTA 6 on PS5, which in some cases was followed by their console going up in literal flames. On a few of the bricked consoles, it appears their chips and parts actually, genuinely got fried by the game's immense requirements.
Make sure you go and check out our sister site, GTA 6 Bible, for all your GTA 6 needs.
"One PS5 on our bench logged 100.9°C on the APU and 15 thermal shutdowns," technician modyfikator89 wrote on X. The system was built by AMD, so having it run a little hot is, I guess, to be expected, but exceeding 100 degrees Celsius (~213.6 Fahrenheit) is extreme even for Team Red, especially since the PS5 does not come equipped with a state-of-the-art cooling system like your usual high-end PC.
Whether this only affects Sony's consoles is unknown. Even if it is, it's quite inconsistent, since most seem to be handling the game just fine. I, for one, haven't crashed a single time on my base PS5, nor have I had any thermal or framerate issues past the usual and expected drops in high-intensity situations or areas. The game does seem to be much more stable on XBOX, though, with the Series S reporting higher average FPS and better frame times and stability than the base PS5.
What I can't help thinking about now is how GTA 6 will affect PCs once it launches on the platform. Nvidia's 12VHPWR cables have a knack for melting down when overloaded, and I think if there is any game out there that can melt a 5080, it's Rockstar's smash hit.
