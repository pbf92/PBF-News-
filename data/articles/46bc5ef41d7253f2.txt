Corn Ticking Back Higher on Thursday Morning
Corn price action is starting Thursday with fractional to 1 ¼ cent gains so far. Futures posted losses of 4 ½ to 11 ¼ cents across the board on Wednesday, pressured by some outside markets. New lows for the recent move were made, but not quite to contracts lows from June. Open interest suggested new selling, up 14,963 contracts on Wednesday. The CmdtyView national average Cash Corn price was down 11 3/4 cents at $4.03 1/4.
The outside markets played a factor on Wednesday, with the US dollar index rallying to a 19-month high at 103.725. Crude oil is also down $3.06 to add some pressure.

Export Sales data will be published this morning, with traders looking for a range of 1.2 to 2 MMT in corn sales for 2026/27 in the week of 11/12. New crop business is seen between 30,000 and 100,000 MT.
Wednesday’s weekly EIA report showed ethanol production slipping 4,000 barrels per day in the week of November 13 to 1.126 million bpd. Stocks were building on that week, up 563,000 barrels to 24.908 million barrels. Ethanol exports slipped 7,000 bpd to 133,000 bpd, with refiner inputs of ethanol improving 6,000 bpd to 894 bpd.
