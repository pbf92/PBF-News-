TG Offtakes Corp. Offers $5.75 Per Share for Up to 4.3M Dynacor Shares
TG Offtakes Corp. offered $5.75 cash per share for up to 4,289,754 Dynacor Group Inc. shares, approximately 7.9% above the 30-trading-day VWAP and 6.1% above the Nov. 23 close. The offer expires Dec. 22, 2026, and is not a formal takeover bid.
