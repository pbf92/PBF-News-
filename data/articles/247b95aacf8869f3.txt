TITLE: Conor McGregor celebrates GTA 6 launch by giving all 400 of his employees the day off
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Former UFC champion Conor McGregor claims to have 400 employees scattered around the globe.
Wherever they are and whatever they do, tomorrow will be a day of rest. That's because Rockstar Games is releasing Grand Theft Auto (GTA) 6, the latest installment in the record-breaking video game franchise. It's expected to generate anywhere between $3-5 billion dollars following Thursday's launch.
So yeah, it's a pretty big deal.
"In honor of GTA launching tomorrow, I announce to my entire workforce team, 400 strong across countries and continents, you have your entire day off tomorrow!" McGregor wrote on social media. "Enjoy the game! Your pal, the boss, Conor McGregor Snr."
Unpaid, of course.
