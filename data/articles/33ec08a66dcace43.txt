DocGo to Sell Cardiac RMS Assets to EAG Holdings for Up to $25 Million
DocGo entered into an asset purchase agreement with EAG Holdings to sell substantially all of Cardiac RMS's assets and transfer certain liabilities. The purchase price is $25 million, including $20 million in cash at closing, subject to adjustment, and up to $5 million in earnout payments tied to retained revenue. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including third-party consents and buyer financing, and may be terminated if it has not closed by April 30, 2027, subject to possible extension.
Agreement details:
- Agreement type: Asset Purchase Agreement
- Counterparty: EAG Holdings
- Signed / Effective: Nov 20 2026 / same
- Duration / Termination: Until closing; termination date of Apr 30 2027, subject to possible extension
- Reason: Sell substantially all of Cardiac RMS's assets
Original SEC Filing: DocGo Inc. [ DCGO ] - 8-K - Nov. 24, 2026
