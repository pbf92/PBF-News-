TITLE: How to Find Your Next Winning Ad
OUTLET: Marketing Brew

Your best ad could already be running. What you need now is the next ten versions of it, on brand and live across Meta, Google and TikTok, with trackable results in one place. For most teams, finding the next winner comes down to how many ads they can make.
Angela Wu, Enterprise Growth & Deployment at Runway, and Scott Schmidt, Director of Growth Marketing at Runway, will explore this full workflow live. They’ll connect ad accounts in Runway Ads, upload an ad that’s already working, get on-brand variants, and publish them without leaving Runway. They’ll also share how Runway runs its own paid program on the same system, which took the team from 77 to roughly 900 ads a week and cut cost per subscriber by 41%.
