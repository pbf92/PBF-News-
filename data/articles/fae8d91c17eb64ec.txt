TITLE: Leo Messi's gesture with Pochettino that ‘boosted’ his career as a coach
OUTLET: MARCA

Modern footballis full of locker-room stories that rarely come to light, where the biggest stars influence the fate of their own coaches. On this occasion, a striking episode starringLionel Messi ended up opening the doors to international success for one of the most sought-after managers today.
The anecdote, recently revealed on talkSPORT's "Kick Off" program by former striker Charlie Austin, exposes how a simple courtesy from the Argentine captain toward Mauricio Pochettino generated deep immediate respect in English football. When the strategist took on the challenge of managing in the Premier League, many doubted his ability due to his limited experience in Europe, but one detail completely changed the squad's perception.
Far from being a mere coincidence, the implicit backing of the global superstar served as a powerful credential that ultimately led the ‘Saints’ dressing room to believe in the tactical idea of their new manager. Below, we tell you all the details of this story that brings together the paths of two of the most important figures in Argentine football.
A landing surrounded by doubts in England
When Mauricio Pochettino was announced in January of 2013 as Southampton's new manager, replacing Nigel Adkins, the news caused a strong divide among the fans and the British press. On the one hand, the board was backing an international and innovative profile, but on the other, the Argentine coach was a complete unknown to the wider Premier League public.
The pressure on his shoulders was monumental from day one, mainly because his predecessor had done a fantastic job by achieving two consecutive promotions. The players, accustomed to a very particular kind of leadership, looked with suspicion at the arrival of a foreign coaching staff that would impose an extremely demanding work methodology, based on intensity and physical wear.
To win over the dressing room, Pochettino needed more than his tactical board and his motivational speeches in English, a language he was still refining at the time. He needed validation in front of a group of professional footballers who measured their coach’s authority based on his résumé and the respect he commanded on the biggest international stages.
The weight of global admiration
The key to acceptance did not take long to arrive thanks to the echo of the great European nights and the prestige that the coach had already accumulated in select elite football circles. According to testimonies gathered by media outlets such as the newspaper The Sun, several Southampton players began to look at their boss differently when they remembered his time and his previous reputation in Spanish football.
The impact of figures of the stature of Lionel Messi proved decisive in an indirect way in building that aura of absolute respect. The fact that the Rosario native and other leading figures in the beautiful game acknowledged Pochettino’s work ethic and tactical rigor laid a solid foundation of credibility within the English club.
That initial rapport showed that the former center-back's leadership was not based on imposition, but on a deep understanding of the game and the stars who play it. The Southampton players themselves admitted that seeing the way the elite world valued their coach completely changed the team's dynamic within a few weeks.
Toward the global elite and definitive recognition
That firm step through the south of England became the perfect springboard for Pochettino to propel his career to the summit of European football. After establishing Southampton in the Premier League with an attractive and bold approach, he would make the leap to Tottenham, where he led the team to play in memorable continental finals.
Later, life would cross his path again with Lionel Messi himself, this time directly when both coincided on the bench and on the pitch at Paris Saint-Germain. Managing a dressing room full of stars in France consolidated his tactical maturity, allowing him to lead national teams and, more recently, take charge of the bench of a historic giant like Manchester United.
History shows that, in top-level football, the small details and the backing of the giants make the difference. The nod and the stature that surrounded Pochettino’s figure in his early years in England ended up forging a career of resounding success that today keeps him on the global front page.
