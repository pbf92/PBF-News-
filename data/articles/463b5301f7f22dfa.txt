How to Short Anthropic (ANT): Leverage Shares by Themes Explains ANDD and ANSS Inverse Anthropic ETFs, Short Selling and Put Options
With Anthropic now trading on Nasdaq (ticker: ANT), Leverage Shares by Themes outlines three ways investors can take a bearish view on Anthropic stock, including planned 2X Short (ANDD) and 1X Short (ANSS) Anthropic ETFs.
GREENWICH, Conn., Nov. 22, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Leverage Shares by Themes today published a guide for investors exploring how to short Anthropic stock following the company's highly anticipated initial public offering. Anthropic, PBC priced its IPO at $83.00 per share on November 18, 2026, raising approximately $81.3 billion through the offering of 980 million Class A shares. The stock began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT on November 19, opening at $111.50 per share.
With Anthropic now publicly traded, investors can evaluate different approaches to gaining inverse exposure to its stock, including traditional short selling, put options and the planned Leverage Shares 2X Short Anthropic Daily ETF (ANDD) and Leverage Shares 1X Short Anthropic Daily ETF (ANSS)
How Can You Short Anthropic Stock?Now that ANT is trading, some investors may want to express a bearish view. There are three common ways to short Anthropic stock: sell borrowed shares in a margin account, buy put options once options on ANT are listed, or buy an inverse ETF designed to move opposite to the stock on a given day.
Short Selling Anthropic StockShort selling means borrowing ANT shares through a brokerage, selling them and buying them back later. It needs a margin account, and newly listed stocks can be hard or expensive to borrow. Losses have no upper limit because there is no limit to how far a share price can rise, and a rising price can trigger margin calls.
Buying Put Options on ANTA put option gives its buyer the right to sell ANT at a set price before a set date. Listed options on ANT will not be available on its first trading day: exchange rules allow options on a large new listing to begin trading no earlier than the second business day after the IPO day. The most a put buyer can lose is the premium paid, and options lose value as their expiration date approaches.
Inverse Anthropic ETFs: ANDD and ANSSThe Leverage Shares 2X Short Anthropic Daily ETF (ANDD) will seek -200% (-2X), and the Leverage Shares 1X Short Anthropic Daily ETF (ANSS) will seek -100% (-1X), of the daily performance of Anthropic common stock, before fees and expenses. Once launched, they will trade like any ETF on Cboe, with no margin account and no share borrowing needed, and an investor cannot lose more than the amount invested. An investor could lose the full amount invested in ANDD if Anthropic's stock price rises by more than 50% in a single trading day.
ANUU, ANDD and ANSS are not yet trading. Their first trading date on Cboe is November 23, 2026.
What Is the Difference Between ANDD and ANSS?ANDD seeks twice the inverse of Anthropic's daily move; ANSS seeks the inverse without additional leverage. If ANT falls 5% in a day, ANDD seeks a 10% gain and ANSS a 5% gain for that day, before fees and expenses. If ANT rises 5% in a day, ANDD seeks a 10% loss and ANSS a 5% loss.
"Shorting a stock on the day it lists is harder than most people expect, because borrowing can be scarce and options are not there yet," said Paul Marino, Chief Revenue Officer, Themes ETFs. "ANDD and ANSS are built to give active traders a daily inverse view on Anthropic in a standard ETF wrapper, and we want them to understand exactly how the daily reset works before they use either one."
Frequently Asked Questions
Can You Short Anthropic on Its First Trading Day?Short selling ANT on November 19 depends on whether your brokerage can locate shares to borrow. Listed options on ANT and the ANDD and ANSS ETFs will not be available that day.
Is There an Inverse ETF for Anthropic?Leverage Shares by Themes has two: ANDD, which will seek -2X the daily performance of Anthropic stock, and ANSS, which will seek -1X. Both seek their objectives for a single day only.
Can You Lose More Than You Invest When Shorting Anthropic?With short selling, yes, because losses grow as long as the price rises. With a put option the loss is limited to the premium paid. With ANDD or ANSS the loss is limited to the amount invested, which can be lost in full.
