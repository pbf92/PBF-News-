TITLE: Anthropic’s IPO is imminent – should you invest?
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Anthropic, one of the world's leading developers of artificial intelligence (AI) models, is just hours away from its stock market debut.
The company, which creates the Claude family of large language models, is expected to start trading on the Nasdaq on 19 November.
Anthropic's IPO follows SpaceX's IPO in June, which saw Elon Musk's space exploration and AI company post the largest stock market debut in history, raising over $75 billion at a valuation of $1.77 trillion.
Anthropic's IPO is expected to be as significant and possibly bigger, with the firm reportedly anticipating it could raise as much as $90 billion at a valuation of around $1.4 trillion, although this is less than touted in October.
The Claude developer reported revenue of around $16.3 billion in the first half of 2026, but has seen a net loss of $81 billion in the first six months of this year. Spending shows no sign of slowing down: according to its IPO prospectus, the company has purchase commitments of almost $900 billion payable by 2045, with around $70 billion of that due within 12 months of September 2026.
However, wider scepticism over whether AI firms can offer a return on the massive amounts of capital expenditure invested in the technology's underlying infrastructure has come to the fore in recent weeks.
Trillions of dollars has been wiped off the combined market valuations of tech companies such as Nvidia and Google parent Alphabet. The Nasdaq 100 index, which is composed heavily of tech stocks, fell by over 13% between 6 October and 17 November.
The possibility that US Democrat politicians could seek to slow the buildout of AI following their success in the US midterms has also rattled the market.
Susannah Streeter, chief investment strategist at wealth manager Wealth Club, said: "Anthropic is spying an opportunity to get its IPO over the line. However, the tech rout has come at a big cost for the company. Gone are the lofty ambitions of trying to float at a $2 trillion valuation.
"Anthropic is having to deal with the stark reality of a much more sceptical investor base."
When will Anthropic's IPO take place?
Anthropic's shares are expected to start trading on 19 November.
The AI developer filed its IPO paperwork on 26 October and will list on the Nasdaq, under the ticker symbol 'ANT'.
It is not expected to pay dividends initially and the founders will hold just over 50% of the voting power.
