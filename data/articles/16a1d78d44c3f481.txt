Phio Pharmaceuticals Disclosed Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard
On November 16, 2026, Phio Pharmaceuticals received notice from Nasdaq that its common stock had closed below the $1.00 minimum bid price for 30 consecutive business days, violating Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2). The notice does not immediately affect trading, and the company has until May 17, 2027, to regain compliance by maintaining a closing bid price of at least $1.00 for 10 consecutive business days.
Original SEC Filing: Phio Pharmaceuticals Corp. [ PHIO ] - 8-K - Nov. 20, 2026
