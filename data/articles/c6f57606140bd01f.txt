CROSS TIMBERS ROYALTY TRUST DECLARES NOVEMBER CASH DISTRIBUTION
DALLAS, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Argent Trust Company, as Trustee of the Cross Timbers Royalty Trust (the "Trust") (CRT), today declared a cash distribution to the holders of its units of beneficial interest of $0.118138 per unit, payable on December 14, 2026, to unitholders of record on November 30, 2026. The following table shows underlying oil and gas sales and average prices attributable to the current month and prior month distributions.
Prior Month Distribution
XTO Energy has advised the Trustee that sales volumes for May 2026 through July 2026 were received in the prior month from Scout Energy Management LLC, the new operator of the Hewitt Unit, which is one of the properties underlying the Oklahoma Working Interest net profits interests.
Excess Costs
XTO Energy has advised the Trustee that excess costs increased by $141,000 on properties underlying the Texas Working Interest net profits interests. However, these excess costs did not reduce net proceeds from the remaining conveyances. Underlying cumulative excess costs remaining on the Texas Working Interest net profits interests total $6,697,000, including accrued interest of $1,836,000.
