TITLE: GTA 6 Dominates The Game Awards 2026 Even Before Release
OUTLET: Only Comic Universe

GTA 6 is basically one day away from release, and yet it has already begun to dominate the Game Awards. The game has become the most-nominated game at the Game Awards 2026. The Award ceremony takes place on 10 December 2026.
All 8 nominations are;
- Game of the Year
- Best Game Direction
- Best Narrative
- Best Art Direction
- Best Score and Music
- Best Audio Design
- Best Performance (Manni L. Perez as Lucia)
- Best Action/Adventure Game
