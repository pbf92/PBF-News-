SA Asks: What's the best-positioned chip stock right now?
In the wake of a sharp sell-off in AI-related names, what's the best-positioned chip stock right now for investors?
Seeking Alpha analysts Julian Lin and Mike Zaccardi weigh in with their picks.
Julian Lin: Nvidia NVDA may indeed fit the bill for one of the more contrarian picks among higher-quality names. But Micron MU might stand out despite the higher volatility. The company benefits regardless of whether NVDA cedes market share and looks poised to sustain tremendous profits for at least the next several years.
(Julian Lin leads the SA investing group Best of Breed Growth Stocks.)
Mike Zaccardi: While Nvidia's NVDA valuation remains compelling, I think Advanced Micro Devices AMD offers a more favorable risk/reward play into year-end and early 2027. Shares took a hit last week, partly due to the broad chip selloff and partly due to a short report from Hunterbrook.
As I detailed last week, there are some technical levels to watch, along with its low valuation. Investors also should be on guard for possible regulatory risk next year as the new Congress takes control of Capitol Hill. While I don't expect dramatic shifts, policy headwinds could persist next year and beyond.
