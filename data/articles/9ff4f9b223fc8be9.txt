Nature's Sunshine Extends Bank of America Credit Line Availability Through 2031
Nature's Sunshine entered into an amended and restated loan agreement with Bank of America, extending the availability period for its revolving line of credit through July 1, 2031. The agreement carries forward outstanding debt and existing lender protections, adds a change-of-control event of default, and permits capped investments in and loans to excluded subsidiaries. The amendment updates the company's existing financing arrangements and extends access to revolving credit.
Agreement details:
- Agreement type: Amended and restated revolving loan agreement
- Counterparty: Bank of America
- Signed / Effective: Nov 18 2026 / Nov 18 2026
- Duration / Termination: Revolving credit availability through Jul 01 2031
- Reason: Extend revolving credit availability and update financing terms
