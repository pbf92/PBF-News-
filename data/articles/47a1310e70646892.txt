TITLE: Andrews delivers positive injury update ahead of Man Utd trip
OUTLET: Brentford FC

Brentford head coach Keith Andrews hopes to have Josh Dasilva and Antoni Milambo back in a matchday squad before Christmas after both players came through 45 minutes for the B team this week.
Aaron Hickey (hamstring), Nathan Collins (knee), Kaye Furo (metatarsal) and Rico Henry (hip and quad) remain sidelined, while Igor Thiago will serve the second game of his three-match suspension when Brentford face Manchester United at Old Trafford on Saturday (3pm kick-off GMT).
Asked whether Dasilva and Milambo could feature in a matchday squad before Christmas, Andrews said: “Yeah, that would certainly be the plan. We have a few games before then. They played 45 minutes the other night in the B-team game. They both played well and came through the game well.
“They’re building the fitness level that’s needed to come into the first-team space, but they’re training with us pretty consistently. They’ve been in and out to be ready for the B-team game.
“They’ll continue that over the next week or two and then hopefully they can gatecrash the first-team squad.”
“They’re building the fitness level that’s needed to come into the first-team space"
Saturday’s fixture is Mauricio Pochettino’s first home match in charge of Manchester United, and Andrews expects the Bees to face a side at their best under a manager he has huge respect for.
“It’s Old Trafford, it’s Manchester United, it’s Mauricio Pochettino,” said Andrews. “He’s an incredibly impressive manager with what he’s achieved.
“So, we have to expect Manchester United to be at their absolute best. If we compete to our levels, we’re confident with what we can do. I expect a really tough game.”
