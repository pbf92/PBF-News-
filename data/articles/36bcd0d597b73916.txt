TITLE: Drone footage of Pochettino’s Man Utd training session posted online
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Footage from Mauricio Pochettino's first training session as Manchester United manager has appeared online after being captured by a drone operator.
A 12-minute video of the session was posted onto YouTube after being captured at United's Carrington training ground.
Pochettino and his United players can be seen in the video, with the squad having been divided into three groups.
On the post, the drone operator wrote: "For the people asking about legality. It is unrestricted airspace, meaning anyone can fly over it.
"The drone remained in line of sight at all times, it didn't fly over anyone or even near anyone so was a perfectly legal flight going by the CAA rules that regulates drone usage.
"The drone is correctly labelled, and I hold all the relevant licences, public liability and insurance required. As for leaking team news/tactics, it was half an hour into the manager's first training session, there are many media there so certainly won't be the only footage of training released."
Manchester United have declined to comment.
Pochettino was taking his first training session after being appointed as United's new head coach last week, succeeding Michael Carrick who was sacked with the club in the relegation zone during this international window.
Pochettino arrived in Manchester on Wednesday, having taken charge of the United States for the final time in their 2-0 win over Haiti, which finished in the early hours of Wednesday morning UK time.
His first match in charge will be a visit to old rivals Liverpool on Sunday afternoon.
