TITLE: Upstart Chief Executive Officer Purchased Shares Worth Over $860K
OUTLET: TradingView

Upstart Chief Executive Officer Purchased Shares Worth Over $860K
Paul Gu, Chief Executive Officer, reported purchasing 50,000 shares through The Gu Qiao Family Trust at a weighted average price of $17.2013 per share, for a total purchase amount of $860,065. Following the transaction, Gu directly owned 1,102,616 shares and indirectly owned 394,930 shares: 120,000 through The Gu Qiao Family Trust, 44,930 through The Paul Xinquan Gu 2021 Gifting Trust, and 230,000 through JECCO, LLC.
