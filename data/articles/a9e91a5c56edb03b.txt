TITLE: Best-of lists climb to 65% of AI Black Friday toy links, iPullRank finds
OUTLET: PPC Land

iPullRank, an AI search and content marketing agency, on November 24, 2026, published a study of 416 shopping prompts tracked every day for nearly two years across up to eight AI platforms. It found that Black Friday changes which web pages AI answers link to far more than which brands they name. During Black Friday week 2025, publishers' best-of lists rose from 46% to 65% of the links in answers to toy questions and from 62% to 78% in answers about superstores, across ChatGPT and Google's three AI products. Brand websites did not gain, according to the study.
In Short
A search marketing agency checked, every day for nearly two years, which websites ChatGPT and Google's AI tools linked to when people asked shopping questions about toys, big-box stores and shoes. In the last 10 days before Black Friday 2025, the AI answers leaned heavily on deal roundups that publishers had written that season, while brand websites got no extra links and the brands named in the answers barely changed. For companies selling over Black Friday week, the findings point to publishers' lists and ChatGPT's choice of store as the places where AI answers changed, although the study cannot show any effect on clicks or sales.
Two years of shopping questions
The study, titled "How Black Friday Changes AI Shopping Answers," carries the byline of Talya Camacho, a business intelligence analyst at iPullRank, and lists two co-authors: Michael Tandoh, an SEO engineer, and Cate Dombrowski, an associate business intelligence analyst. The data comes from Profound, a commercial platform that tracks how brands appear in AI answers. iPullRank set up three research workspaces in Profound, one per product category, and ran the same prompts daily from November 14, 2024, to November 10, 2026.
The aim, according to the study, was to see "what changes in AI answers around Black Friday (the pages cited, the brands named, or the products shown)," whether every AI model changes in the same way and at the same time, and how 2024, 2025 and 2026 compare. That puts it in the growing field of AI visibility measurement, where agencies and software companies count how often a brand or a web page appears inside answers from ChatGPT, Gemini and similar tools.
Each category contained a small set of Black Friday prompts, such as "Best Black Friday toy deals for toddlers," and a larger set of ordinary shopping prompts that do not mention Black Friday. The Black Friday prompts have run unchanged every day since November 2024. iPullRank added 84 ordinary prompts in early 2025, four in Toys and 80 in Superstores.
The resulting dataset holds 13.3 million rows of brand visibility data and 13.7 million rows of citation data, along with the web searches ChatGPT ran in the background and, from October 7, 2025, the product cards ChatGPT displayed, including the store and the price on each. The team also pulled archived copies of the 400 most-cited Black Friday web addresses from the Wayback Machine, the Internet Archive's record of old web pages, and scraped 549 product pages.
The study uses four terms throughout. A citation is "a page an AI model links to as a source in its answer." A best-of list is "a publisher's list or deals roundup, such as 'best Black Friday toy deals.'" Product tiles are "the product cards ChatGPT shows in an answer, each with a product, a store, and a price." Ordinary prompts are "shopping prompts in the same category that don't mention Black Friday."
Not every AI platform was tracked for the full period, so each comparison uses only the models with data on both sides of it.
When the study speaks of "ChatGPT and Google's three AI models," it means ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode and Gemini combined.
How the ordinary-week test works
AI answers drift from week to week for reasons unrelated to shopping. A new model version, a change in how a chatbot searches the web, or a reshuffle of its sources can move every answer at once. iPullRank built its method around separating those background swings from anything Black Friday itself causes. According to the study, each result goes through the following steps.
- Define the periods. Black Friday week runs from the Monday before Black Friday through Cyber Monday: November 24 to December 1, 2025, and November 25 to December 2, 2024. The lead-in is November 1 to 23, 2025. The 2024 lead-in is shorter, because the data starts on November 14 that year.
- Measure the change on Black Friday prompts. For each model and category, the team records how a figure, such as the share of links going to best-of lists, moved from the lead-in to Black Friday week.
- Subtract the change on ordinary prompts. The study defines the Black Friday effect as "the change on Black Friday prompts minus the change on ordinary prompts over the same days." That removes shifts that hit every prompt.
- Repeat the calculation on ordinary weeks. The same calculation runs on every ordinary week between January 8 and October 31, which gives 30 to 80 comparison weeks depending on the model and category.
- Rank Black Friday week against those weeks. If the Black Friday change is larger than the change in at least 95% of ordinary weeks, the study calls it a real Black Friday effect. Between 90% and 95% is borderline. Under 90% counts as ordinary weekly change. Put plainly, a Black Friday result has to beat at least 19 out of every 20 ordinary weeks.
- Smooth the daily lines. Day-by-day results use a seven-day average for each model.
- Rerun ChatGPT on a clean window. ChatGPT "behaved differently from October 18 to November 14, 2025," according to the study, so key results were rerun on a "clean" lead-in from November 15 to 23, 2025, that avoids those days.
Links were sorted by rules applied to the domain and the web address. Best-of lists are publisher pages whose address "reads like a list or deals page ('best,' 'top 10,' 'deals,' 'gift guide')." Brand pages are the brands' own sites. Retailers include marketplaces such as walmart.com and target.com.
Best-of lists take over the final 10 days
Best-of lists were already the largest single source of links on Black Friday prompts before the week began. Timing matters as much as size. Best-of lists sat near their October level until mid-November, then rose quickly into Black Friday week and peaked in early December. On ChatGPT, they accounted for 43% of Black Friday links on November 14, 62% a week later and 75% on Black Friday itself. Google AI Overviews followed almost the same path, from 54% on November 14 to 72% by Black Friday. Perplexity rose more slowly. Gemini and Google AI Mode showed no clear rise.
The jump was specific to Black Friday questions. On ordinary prompts over the same two weeks, the best-of share on ChatGPT moved only from 16% to 21%, and on Google AI Overviews from 17% to 18%. iPullRank flags two exceptions: on Perplexity, and on ChatGPT in Toys, the best-of share also rose on ordinary prompts at the same time, so those increases are not specific to Black Friday.
Tested one model at a time against ordinary weeks, the rise was clearest on Google AI Overviews, where best-of lists gained 18 to 27 points in all three categories, and on Perplexity, where they gained 18 to 20 points in Toys and Footwear as retailer sites lost share. ChatGPT showed a clear gain only in Superstores, where best-of lists went from 68% to 94% of links. Gemini showed no clear gain in best-of lists, although retailer sites lost 20 points in Toys. Google AI Mode showed no real change in either.

The study's own summary says best-of lists "grew to 65% to 94% of Black Friday citations in Toys and Superstores." That range joins two different measures: 65% is the combined four-model figure for Toys, while 94% is ChatGPT alone in Superstores.
Across all three categories, the most-cited lists were that year's editions. Rank & Style's Black Friday shoe deals page drew 481 citations during Black Friday week and The Bump's best Black Friday toy deals page drew 290, according to the study.
The pages behind those numbers are the kind of listicles that PPC Land has tracked through a long dispute over self-promotion. In October, Lily Ray reported that Google AI Overviews cited 38% fewer self-ranking vendor listicles than in June. iPullRank's category is different: it covers publisher roundups and puts a brand's own pages in a separate bucket.
2026 so far: ChatGPT and Google part ways
Comparing the same dates in both years, November 1 to 10, the models are moving in opposite directions this season.
ChatGPT's turn toward best-of lists started earlier this year, "at least so far this season," according to the study. In 2025, best-of lists held near 38% of its Black Friday links until mid-November. In 2026, the share was already 50% in late October. Retailers lost share on ChatGPT in all three categories.
Google AI Overviews went the other way. In the November 1 to 10 window, its mix of Black Friday links shifted from 48% best-of lists, 8% retailers and 40% other sources in 2025, with brand websites a sliver too small to label, to 26% best-of lists, 22% brand websites, 17% retailers and 35% other sources in 2026. ChatGPT's mix moved from 38% best-of lists, 19% brand websites, 27% retailers and 16% other sources to 66%, 11%, 9% and 14%.
Those 2026 numbers come from a single 10-day window before the peak. They also predate a Google ranking change that landed squarely in the shopping run-up: Google started its November 2026 core update on November 10, the same day iPullRank's data ends, with a rollout that may take up to two weeks.
This year's pages arrive on a different clock
Alongside the rise in best-of lists, AI answers switch to the current year's Black Friday pages, and that switch starts earlier. The clearest signal is the year in a page's address, as in ".../black-friday-deals-2025." In October 2025, about 10% or less of Black Friday links went to pages with 2025 in the address. By Black Friday week the share was 57% in Toys, 58% in Superstores and 41% in Footwear, across ChatGPT and Google's three AI models.
The change builds over two to three weeks, starting around the second week of November, and peaks after Black Friday. On ChatGPT, current-year pages made up 6% of Black Friday links on November 1, 20% on November 14, 53% on Black Friday and 70% on December 9. In the study's summary table, the November 1 figure is described as the start of November 2025.
Each model keeps its own calendar, according to the study:
- ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews start latest and climb fastest. In the last week before Black Friday alone, ChatGPT gained 19 points and Google AI Overviews gained 23.
- Gemini changes earliest. It was already at 50% by November 14 and then stayed flat.
- Google AI Mode rises slowly and keeps rising into late December.
- Perplexity peaks before Black Friday, at 25% on November 20, and then moves away from current-year pages, down to 2% by December 15.
iPullRank calls this the strongest effect in the study. On ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, the jump into Black Friday week was larger than in every ordinary week, in all three categories. On ChatGPT, the biggest change in any ordinary week was 16 to 18 points, against 23 to 40 points in Black Friday week. Google AI Mode's jump was larger than in almost every ordinary week. Gemini showed no jump that week because it had already switched. In 2024, the pattern was clear only in Footwear. "This is a 2025 result and not yet an every-year one," the study states, adding that start dates are approximate.
