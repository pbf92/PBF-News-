Traeger CFO Joey Hord to step down
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- Grill maker Traeger announced Wednesday Joey Hord will step down as executive vice president and chief financial officer, or CFO, effective Feb. 1, 2027.
- "I look forward to supporting a smooth transition over the coming months," said Hord.
- "We have a strong finance team in place, and our strategy and priorities are unchanged. We look forward to welcoming a new CFO as we continue to execute on our plan," said Jeremy Andrus, Traeger’s CEO.
- The company has commenced its search for a new CFO and may appoint an interim CFO if a successor is not in place by Feb. 1, 2027.
- In addition, Traeger reiterated its full-year fiscal 2026 guidance, as previously provided on November 4, 2026.
