SpaceX Gets a Fresh Vote of Confidence From Cathie Wood
ARK Invest, led by Cathie Wood, acquired 37,788 shares of SpaceX SPCX on Wednesday through its ARK Innovation ETF (ARKK).
The transaction was valued at about $4.8 million using a reference price of $127.44.
The purchase adds to the fund's exposure to the space company, although the disclosed data do not show how large the position was before the trade. The $4.8 million figure is an estimate based on the stated reference price, not a confirmed execution value.
The purchase was made through ARKK, so the reported transaction reflects the fund's portfolio activity rather than a personal share purchase by Wood. ARK's trade details provide the number of shares and the fund used for the purchase, but do not identify the timing of individual executions or the average price paid.
The trade was part of a wider round of portfolio changes on Wednesday, which included purchases of other assets and reductions in several existing holdings. TipRanks did not provide ARK's total SpaceX holdings after the transaction or the stake's share of the ETF's assets.
