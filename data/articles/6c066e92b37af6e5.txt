TITLE: GTA 6’s Lucia Writer Quit X. The Abuse Then Hit Another Woman With Her Name
OUTLET: MotorBiscuit

Alexa D’Agostino, a Rockstar writer who talked about writing GTA 6’s Lucia, has deleted her X account after a wave of online harassment.
Video Games Chronicle reports that people who dislike Lucia’s inclusion in the game targeted D’Agostino with “a wave of online harassment and misinformation.” She also has thank-you credits on GTA 5, GTA Online and Red Dead Online.
Critics Blamed Her for Lucia
Many of the posts blamed D’Agostino for perceived faults in GTA 6, mainly Lucia being a playable lead. A thread on X also pointed to her support for Pride and her posts critical of President Donald Trump.
Critics also claimed she once ran a blog encouraging women to manipulate men. VGC says the post they pointed to was advice for women on meeting their boyfriend’s friends.
Much of the anger focused on what she said about Lucia in a LOVE Magazine interview before the game launched.
“When it came to writing Lucia, I thought of the women I know. My mother was an immigrant who grew up in Newark, New Jersey, close to Lucia’s hometown of Liberty City. I thought about the way she raised me: rowdy, kind, but not to take any shit,” she said.
She added that she related a lot to the character. “When you are focusing on writing a fully realised person with their own wants, you’re going to get something perfectly imperfect-with clichés, but also undeniably real. That’s how you get a woman that can stand tall next to the likes of Arthur Morgan and Niko Bellic.”
The Abuse Moved to Someone Else
VGC says that line in particular was used against her, and that “many of the posts about her are misogynistic and racist in nature.”
Leaving X didn’t end it. “After D’Agostino deleted her X account, much of the vitriol was instead sent to another woman of the same name,” VGC reports.
Neither D’Agostino nor Rockstar has commented, according to VGC.
Lucia shares GTA 6’s story with Jason in the game’s return to Vice City, and fans have spent the weeks since launch picking apart its world, cars included. We spotted plenty of real models when Rockstar showed its extended look at GTA VI.
