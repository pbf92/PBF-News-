Reaction to dreadful refereeing display vs Sporting tells Manchester United everything
Man United were on the end of some awful refereeing decisions in Wednesday night's draw with Sporting Lisbon in the Champions League.
And so we wait for an update on whether Manchester United will complain to UEFA about referee Szymon Marciniak's performance on Wednesday night.
If you hadn't watched United's draw with Sporting Lisbon, you'd likely roll your eyes at the news Mauricio Pochettino was unhappy with the referee's display in a game where United made it nine away matches without a win this season. Anyone who did watch the game will know the United boss has every right to feel a sense of injustice.
"I think your first question is difficult to answer because I think everyone knows all that was... was impossible. It's difficult to understand why that happened on the field," was Pochettino's measured reply to a question about Amad's disallowed goal on Wednesday night.
The Ivory Coast winger looked to have levelled the scores at the second time of asking, having seen his initial shot saved by Kaique Pereira, only for Marciniak to award a free-kick for a foul that never was by Youri Tielemans in the build-up. To make matters worse, Tielemans was booked for the 'foul' and later sent off for a second yellow card.
Further controversy was to come, with Sporting goalkeeper Pereira punching the ball into his own net to seemingly hand United a late lead, only for Marciniak to rule the goal out for a push by Patrick Dorgu. Again, there didn't look to be a foul.
