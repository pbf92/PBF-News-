TITLE: Indi Gregory: Parents meet Pope Leo to present book honouring her legacy
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

The parents of Indi Gregory, who died after a legal battle over the withdrawal of her life-saving treatment, have presented Pope Leo with a book honoring their daughter’s legacy.
Eight-month old Indi suffered from an “irreversible” mitochondrial disease. Courts sided with the NHS in the belief it was in her “best interests” to die. Her parents wished to transfer her to a Vatican-owned children’s hospital which was ready to provide treatment, with the Italian government also granting her citizenship.
Indi died on 13 November 2023 after her life support was turned off, having been taken from Queen’s Medical Centre in Nottingham to a hospice under security escort.
Dean Gregory and Claire Staniforth gifted Pope Leo a pre-publication copy of a new book telling the story of the fight for their daughter’s life, during a visit to the Vatican.
The case raised fundamental questions about parental responsibility, freedom of medical choice and the role of courts in preventing access to treatment abroad, according to the Christian Legal Centre which supported their case.
Speaking to The Mirror about the meeting, Dean said: “To stand before the Pope, holding Indi’s story in my hands, and personally present him with a copy was an emotional and unforgettable moment.
“The Vatican played such an important part in Indi’s journey, and this book is also a tribute to those who stood beside our family when we needed them most. Indi may have only spent a short time on this earth, but her story and legacy will live on forever.”
The late Pope Francis offered prayers and support for the family during the court challenge, sending a message of condolence after Indi died calling her a “precious child of God”.
Dean spent 18 months writing the book, which is set to be published in Italy by Catholic publisher San Paolo Edizion next year.
