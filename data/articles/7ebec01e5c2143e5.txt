KAITO: Up to 17.6 million tokens unlock - Jun 2027
KAITO has a token unlock scheduled for June 2027. The unlock covers up to 17.6 million tokens, equal to 7.29% of circulating supply, across allocations for the community, ecosystem, foundation, investors, liquidity and team.
The unlock gives recipients the option to sell tokens that were previously locked. If recipients sell part of their allocations, those sales can put downward pressure on the token's price.
