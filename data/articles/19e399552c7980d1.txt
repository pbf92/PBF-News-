TITLE: Zimbabwean preacher Uebert Angel arrested over alleged sexual offences
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

A controversial British Zimbabwean preacher has been arrested after being accused of indecent assault and sodomy.
48-year-old Uebert Angel founded the charismatic church Spirit Embassy which has branches in the UK and South Africa, as well as in Zimbabwe. The self-styled prophet is also close to the government of Zimbabwe having been appointed as President Mnangagwa’s special presidential envoy and ambassador-at-large to Europe and the Americas.
According to local media reports, he was arrested during a church service in Harare, Zimbabwe on 22 November.
No stranger to controversy, Angel, whose real name is Uebert Mudzanire, was accused of using his diplomatic status for money laundering in 2023. Two years earlier, a number of women accused him of sexual impropriety. He has denied the allegations.
In a post on Facebook, Malawian televangelist Shepherd Bushiri from the Jesus Nation Church wrote that he had been a "spiritual son" of Angel's for nearly two decades and described him as a "father and mentor".
He called on the authorities in Zimbabwe to handle the matter "transparently, impartially and free from political pressure".
