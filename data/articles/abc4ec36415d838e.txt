TITLE: Anthropic IPO: $83 Share Price and Year-End Valuation Odds
OUTLET: Time Magazine

The startup preparing for a blockbuster initial public offering refers to its employees as "Ants," a nickname it says "captures the collaborative, ego-light culture of the team."
That 5,300-member colony generated more than $6.6 million in revenue per Ant over the 12 months through September. Its next hill: Wall Street.
Anthropic will begin trading on the Nasdaq Thursday under the ticker ANT. The company behind Claude generated $33.2 billion in revenue through the first nine months of 2026, up from $2.5 billion during the same period last year. Customers contributing at least $100,000 in trailing annual revenue for Anthropic climbed from 950 to 11,900 year-over-year through September, with more than half the Fortune 500 companies among those customers. But as more businesses rely on Claude, Anthropic faces a growing computing bill—one that outpaced sales growth last quarter.
Computing and infrastructure expenses reached $9.9 billion in the third quarter, up 77% from the second quarter. Revenue grew 47% over the same period. That comes as some investors question the payoff of the AI spending boom. Nearly two weeks before Jensen Huang stepped down as Nvidia CEO, Apple overtook the chipmaker as the world's most valuable company, with Bloomberg citing the "perceived safety of the iPhone maker amid concerns about artificial intelligence spending." Anthropic's route to Wall Street has been clouded by uncertainty. Its buyers just gave it a $1.5 trillion vote of confidence.
The New York Times reported Wednesday that Anthropic has set its initial share price at $83 per share, above the $70 to $78 range it had pitched to investors and implying a $1.5 trillion fully diluted valuation.
The next test comes at Thursday's closing bell. Polymarket traders widely expect Anthropic to close at or below a $1.5 trillion market cap on its first day on the stock market—and lookahead lines give the Claude maker roughly the same chance of falling to an $800 billion valuation as climbing to $3 trillion by the end of the year.
Anthropic Valuation Predictions
Polymarket's year-end valuation contracts track whether Anthropic reaches each threshold at any point through December 31.
Traders give Anthropic an 8% chance of reaching $3 trillion and a 9% chance of falling to $800 billion by the end of the year. Both outcomes are considered long shots, but the similar probabilities reflect the broader turbulence surrounding AI stocks, which shed more than $6 trillion in market value between September 30 and November 6, according to MSCI. One percentage point separates two outcomes that are $2.2 trillion apart—in a market that will be settled in just 29 trading sessions.
Traders give Anthropic an 82% chance of ending Thursday with a valuation at or below $1.5 trillion, even after the company priced its IPO above its initial range.
The combined probability of the <$1.25 trillion and $1.25-$1.5 trillion contracts on Polymarket has remained steady throughout Wednesday, but traders did revise expectations up following Anthropic's share price announcement. In the hour after the $83 IPO price was reported, Anthropic's probability of closing Thursday below a $1.25 trillion valuation fell from 38% to 31%.
Polymarket has generated more than $30 million in trading volume in markets tied to Anthropic's IPO and valuation. The bulls see a path to $3 trillion. The bears see one to $800 billion. The Ants arrive Thursday.
