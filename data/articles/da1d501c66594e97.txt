TITLE: GTA 6 Free Printable Map Available Now, Get Yours Here
OUTLET: PlayStation LifeStyle

Rockstar Games has made GTA 6 Leonida map available for free just in time for the weekend. Both digital and physical copies of the map are available for everyone, including a printable map via Rockstar’s official website.
Rockstar Games offers several ways to get GTA 6 map for free
The quickest and easiest way to get a physical copy of Leonida’s map is to go over to this webpage and download the printable version. A printable copy of the game’s poster is also available. A physical copy of the poster (double-sided) is included in retail boxes of GTA 6.
If you don’t want to download a digital map or have to print one yourself, then there are two other ways to get one.
Rockstar Games will be selling physical copies of Leonida’s map for just $0.99 via its website. Alternatively, if you purchase anything from the developer’s online store, you’ll get a physical copy of the map for free as a gift.
Last but not least, Rockstar Games partnered with IGN to release an official GTA 6 walkthrough and guide alongside an interactive map of Leonida. Simply head over to IGN’s dedicated GTA 6 guide page to access both.
