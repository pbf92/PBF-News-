TITLE: Meta expands Instagram ad business with Muse
OUTLET: 디지털투데이

Meta is expanding its Instagram advertising business by pushing an ad-free personal AI agent called Muse, CNBC reported on Nov. 24, citing multiple people familiar with the matter.
Meta is encouraging Instagram users to organise saved posts in Muse, the report said.
A source said about 20 percent of Instagram daily users save at least 1 post a day. Many of those saved posts are shoppable products or ads.
A senior Meta executive said, "Muse does not serve ads, but it still has significant value for the advertising business because it increases advertiser conversion rates." The executive said Muse reduces friction in the shopping process and helps users find products again later. "Advertisers get a higher return on investment (ROI) on what they already spend," the executive said, adding, "All because Muse helps with shopping."
Information about what users buy through the agent is a useful asset for making Instagram ads more personalised. If users complete purchases through Muse, it could become a new source of revenue for Meta, CNBC reported.
Meta is expected to eventually introduce ads in Muse. Sources said Meta is not under financial pressure to rush monetisation and tends to monetise after securing a large enough user base. They said placing ads in a Muse feed that is closer to a newsfeed, rather than on screens answering specific questions, would be a natural testing ground.
