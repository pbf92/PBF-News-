OUTLET: TradingView

Whats's Going On With Abercrombie & Fitch Shares On Wednesday?
Abercrombie & Fitch Co. ANF shares were down almost 2% in premarket trading Wednesday after the company reported record third-quarter results Tuesday and raised its fiscal 2026 outlook.
Following the results, BTIG and Needham raised their price forecasts.
BTIG analyst Janine Stichter maintained a Buy rating on Abercrombie & Fitch and raised her price forecast to $205 from $175, citing higher revenue estimates and continued potential for the company to outperform its guidance.
The analyst highlighted solid execution across both Abercrombie & Fitch and Hollister, with brands delivering growth across regions and multiple product categories.
The Abercrombie brand returned to positive comparable sales growth, while Hollister continued to build sequential momentum, with easier year-over-year comparisons ahead, Stichter said.
Disciplined inventory management and flexible replenishment helped support higher average unit retail (AUR) prices while limiting promotional activity despite a competitive environment.
Although still relatively small, newer sales channels and product categories are showing early promise and could become meaningful growth drivers as they become a greater internal focus, Stichter said.
She also expressed greater confidence in sustaining operating margins at low-double-digit levels or higher, citing tight financial controls and several years of fixed-cost reductions. Continued share repurchases provide additional support for EPS growth.
Outlook
The company increased its fiscal 2026 GAAP EPS forecast to $13.70-$13.90 from $13.10-$13.60, above the analyst estimate of $13.45.
Full-year sales are now expected to reach approximately $5.582 billion, compared with the previous forecast of $5.529 billion and the analyst estimate of $5.53 billion.
For the fourth quarter, Abercrombie expects GAAP EPS of $4.40-$4.60 and sales of $1.786 billion, compared with analyst estimates of $4.84 and $1.783 billion, respectively.
Fourth-quarter freight costs are expected to create approximately 100 basis points more pressure than anticipated in August. Despite that pressure, the company still expects modest year-over-year gross margin improvement.
Store Expansion and Productivity
Abercrombie plans to open 50 stores and remodel or right-size 80 locations in FY26, while closing approximately 20 stores. The company has opened 23 Hollister stores and remodeled or right-sized 48 Hollister locations so far.
New stores are generating roughly twice the productivity of locations being closed, with targeted payback periods of less than two years and four-wall operating margins above 20%. The company has also brought elements of its updated A&F store format to 15 stores in major markets.
Long-Term Growth Strategy
Abercrombie aims to sustain gross margins in the 60% range, maintain double-digit operating margins and generate strong cash flow, with operating income and EPS growing faster than sales.
Growth opportunities include store expansion, new product categories, additional channels, partnerships and international markets.
Management expects strong double-digit operating margins in 2027 but has not issued formal guidance. Freight costs could remain a headwind, while incentive compensation is expected to normalize following a strong second half of fiscal 2026.
