TITLE: Cochrane residents hear about Age-Friendly action plan update
OUTLET: Cochrane Eagle

Cochrane residents aged 50 and older heard an update about the Age-Friendly Cochrane action plan and attended a resource fair at the Cochrane RancheHouse on Nov. 24.
Cochrane social planning and inclusion advisor, Chantal Barber, explained that the Age-Friendly Cochrane action plan was officially endorsed by council in 2025 which established a 54 page document focusing on achieving eight actions across five years to improve Cochrane for seniors.
Action one is broken into two objectives of improving housing accessibility and outdoor space design, action two focuses on transportation, action three on improving age-friendly communication, action four on lifelong learning, action five on inclusive living spaces, action six on community health services, action seven on health information, and action eight on safety and inclusion.
"Some of these will be a journey, not a destination, while others will be completed and reassessed at the end of the five year plan," said Barber.
Barber said that the domains can overlap to impact one another such as non-accessible transportation limiting community involvement, or housing can overlap with inclusive living spaces.
Action one, action three, action four, action seven, and action eight are already underway with an importance on improving communication.
"We recognize that communications connect every single action on this plan and it is one of the key actions that can allow for the success of the other," said Barber.
Cochrane housing strategist Gina van den Burg highlighted that action one is underway exploring a partnership with University of Calgary students who will help develop age-friendly housing design.
Once the partnership is confirmed, a housing task-force will be established to officially advise age-friendly housing guidelines in early 2027, and they hope to adopt the guidelines by fall 2027.
"We've heard about the challenges of finding suitable housing, and there needs to be change," said van den Burg.
