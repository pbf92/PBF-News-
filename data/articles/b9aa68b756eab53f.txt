TITLE: GTA 6 Nominated for Game of the Year Before PS5 Reviews Are Released
OUTLET: Push Square

We got GTA 6 nominated for Game of the Year at The Game Awards before GTA 6.
The Game Awards 2026 has just announced the nominees across all its categories, and sticking out like a sore thumb is GTA 6.
It's the odd one out, because it's the only game shortlisted that has yet to release. Despite the fact it isn't out yet — and even that reviews have yet to land — Rockstar's hotly anticipated game is nominated across numerous categories.
In fact, it's the most-nominated game at this year's awards, with a total of eight nods if you include Manni L. Perez's nomination for Best Performance. It's up for the following trophies:
- Best Performance (Manni L. Perez as Lucia)
- Best Action/Adventure
- Best Art Direction
- Best Audio Design
- Best Game Direction
- Best Narrative
- Best Score and Music
- Game of the Year
Yes, on top of all its other nominations, GTA 6 is in contention for Game of the Year.
There has been much debate online about whether the game would be considered for The Game Awards at all, given how close its launch is to the event itself.
Many believed it would not receive any nominations, because the nominee announcement was set to occur before GTA 6 launches.
However, the voting jury — which comprises hundreds of gaming media outlets from around the world — has deemed the game worthy of recognition in eight categories, more than any other 2026 title.
What's clear is that many jury members must have played the game, but more interesting is that they clearly hold it in high regard if it's been shortlisted for so many awards.
This has been quite revealing, then; it's a big hint at what critics think of the game, and we now also know (officially) who is playing the role of Lucia Caminos, one of the two lead characters.
