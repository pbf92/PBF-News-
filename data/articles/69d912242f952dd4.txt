TITLE: Essential GTA 6 Chapter 1 advice before starting ‘point of no return’ Tourist Trap mission
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

I’m well into the end-game content of GTA 6 now after wrapping up the main story and a large chunk of side-missions. But after 100 hours I’ve only got one piece of advice for those still in Chapter 1.
And before we continue, this is a promise that no spoilers lie ahead in this article when it comes to the main storyline around Jason and Lucia.
For GAMINGbible's GTA 6 review, I played through the game at a moderate pace, with a 60 / 40 split of main story content and side-missions / exploration.
In the end it took me around 70 hours to see the end credits and since being dropped back into the open-world I’ve been busy looking for anything I’d missed or left unfinished.

After seeing everyone else play the game, though, I feel somewhat empty inside, because in my playthrough the best part of the game practically passed in the blink of an eye. We're talking about Chapter 1.
Saviour GTA 6 Chapter 1, make sure you can return
In hindsight I’d have liked to have spent a bit longer in Chapter 1. That’s not to say I rushed through it though.
While Jason is free to explore Leonida in its entirety before Lucia even becomes a playable character, I never left Key Lento unless the story missions explicitly told me to.
There’s just enough to do in terms of side-content, but what I’m really missing right now is the roleplaying aspect.
It was something Rockstar co-lead Rob Nelson spoke about in my interview with him, but GTA 6 leans heavily into the idea of Jason and Lucia being your characters.
Jason actually lives what I’d consider to be my ideal lifestyle in Chapter 1, minus the drug-running, obviously.
He’s got a good house, workout equipment on his porch, a boat for fishing and friends he both works and spends time with at the bar nearby. He gets along with his neighbours and is generally free to live how he chooses due to his boss owning the house.
When Lucia re-enters his life it becomes even more ideal. The pair redecorate the house and turn it into a home. While many players have called these segments boring I thought they were lovely.
Unfortunately, I’m locked out of that part of the game due to my save files. The earliest I can go back is Chapter 2.
You can still return to Key Lento once Chapter 1 has ended, but by that point the story has moved on briskly and it’s no longer the same. What I will say, though, is it you left anything valuable at Jason’s house it’ll still be there when you return later on – including any money you left in your stash.
I’ll see Chapter 1 again someday as I plan on replaying GTA 6 eventually, but it’s going to be a while. Until I’ve seen and done everything of note I’ll be sticking to my current save.
For players who are still finding their feet in the game’s first chapter though, you’ve still got time. It’s not too late to make a save file so you can return to this slice-of-life portion of the game later, and I can guarantee you’ll want to.
