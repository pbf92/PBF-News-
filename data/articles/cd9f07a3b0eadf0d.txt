GTA 6 Update 01.002.002 Download Size
The latest Grand Theft Auto 6 update has a different download size depending on which platform you’re playing on.
If you’re playing on PlayStation 5, the update comes in at just over half a gigabyte. However, those playing on Xbox Series X|S will have a considerably larger download to deal with. Here are the exact download sizes for GTA 6 Update 01.002.002 on each platform:
- PlayStation 5: 603.8 MB
- Xbox Series X|S: 1.6 GB
Thankfully, neither download is particularly demanding, so you shouldn’t need to start clearing out your console’s storage to make room. The relatively small file sizes should also mean you won’t be waiting around for too long before jumping back into Vice City.
