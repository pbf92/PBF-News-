TITLE: Tennis was planned for GTA 6 and cut, two Rockstar North developers told PC Games, while crawling, furnishing homes and crafting were considered at length and rejected and cinema and theatre trips were dropped as not interactive enough
OUTLET: Ma…

Tennis was planned for GTA 6 and then cut because Rockstar was not entirely happy with how it was implemented, according to a PC Games report based on an interview with two Rockstar North developers. The developers were Rob Nelson, head of development at Rockstar North, and Scott Mitchell, design producer at Rockstar North.
Michael Grünwald wrote the PC Games report, whose English-language page is dated 26 November 2026. PC Games says it had 30 minutes for the interview, which covered the game’s features and missions. The report paraphrases the developers’ answers and prints no direct quotes from either one.
What PC Games reports
PC Games spoke to Nelson and Mitchell about the game’s features while it was reviewing the game. The outlet reported that GTA 6 had arrived after years of waiting and had a Metascore of 99. That score ties it with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time as the highest-rated game of all time, according to PC Games.
The outlet noted that GTA 6 has no proper tennis minigame. When it asked the two developers about this, it learned that tennis had been planned. PC Games reports that Rockstar was not entirely happy with the implementation, so the activity did not make it into the finished game. PC Games does not say what Rockstar found lacking in the implementation or how far work on tennis had progressed before it was dropped.
What was rejected and why
PC Games says players were supposed to be able to crawl along the ground with the game’s two protagonists, furnish their homes and craft items. These features were considered at length and ultimately rejected, the outlet reports.
The reason PC Games gives is that some of this would not have suited the story and gameplay, so it was scrapped. The report does not say which of the three ideas fell under that reasoning, or whether any of them was dropped for a different reason.
Trips to the cinema and the theater were also dropped. According to PC Games, Rockstar did not consider them interactive enough. The outlet gives no further detail about how those trips would have worked in the game.
GamesRadar, in a report by Anna Koselke published on 26 November 2026, covers several of the same cuts, including movie-theater visits. Its headline uses GamesRadar’s own phrase, “Sims 4-style”, to describe the house decorating. GamesRadar also points out that tennis is available in GTA 5.
What stayed in the game
Not every side activity was removed. Minigolf did make it into GTA 6, according to both PC Games and GamesRadar. PC Games sets minigolf against tennis as an example of an activity that reached the finished game while tennis did not.
PC Games also reports that Nelson said the relationship between Lucia and Jason, the two protagonists, is what Rockstar is particularly proud of. The outlet adds that Rockstar took cues from Red Dead Redemption 2 on letting a story breathe. PC Games presents both points in its own words.
GamesRadar cites Sony as saying GTA 6 is “the biggest launch in PlayStation history”, with “more than 39 million unique players” on PS5 in the first four days after launch. Make Tech Easier has previously reported on a Rockstar boss’s 80-hour playthrough of the game.
An earlier comment on darts
Nelson has spoken about leaving out activities before. In a separate report by Jordan Middler of VGC on 19 November 2026, Nelson said darts was one the team “just didn’t get to” and concluded, “It’s better not to do it than to do it and have it not be good enough.” VGC reports that darts and bowling are not playable in GTA 6.
