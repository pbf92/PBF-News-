Joby Aviation stock trades up, here is why
What happened?
Shares of Joby Aviation jumped 7.3% in the afternoon session after broader U.S. equities surged as Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, reviving the artificial intelligence trade after all three major averages ended the previous week in correction territory.
CNBC reported that the S&P 500 soared 5.1%, the Nasdaq Composite soared 6.9% and the Dow Jones Industrial Average climbed 1,595 points, or 3.3%, while Nvidia soared 31% in a turnaround for the technology sector. TipRanks reported that electric air taxi stocks, including Joby Aviation, Archer Aviation and Vertical Aerospace, bounced back in premarket trading following a three-month decline as the broader technology rebound prompted investors to revisit hard-hit growth stocks.
What is the market telling us
Joby Aviation’s shares are extremely volatile and have had 47 moves greater than 5% over the last year. In that context, today’s move indicates the market considers this news meaningful but not something that would fundamentally change its perception of the business.
Joby Aviation is down 72.8% since the beginning of the year, and at $3.91 per share, it is trading 76% below its 52-week high of $16.30 from January 2026. Investors who bought $1,000 worth of Joby Aviation’s shares 5 years ago would now be looking at only $493.06.
