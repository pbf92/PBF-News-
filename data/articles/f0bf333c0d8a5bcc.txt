TITLE: GTA 6 First Update 1.002.002 Released on November 23
OUTLET: MP1st

The first game update for Grand Theft Auto 6 has been released today, November 23. If you can’t see it, it’s most likely because how Rockstar Games is handling patches, which is a rolling update worldwide.
For now, the first 33% of players will be able to download GTA 6 update 1.002.002, and it clocks in at a modest 600 MB. Don’t expect any new content from this, as this should deal with some priority bugs.
While this patch squashes bugs, don’t expect everything to be sorted out in one update.
GTA 6 Receives First Update on November 23
As expected, Rockstar Games has not released the official changelog just yet, though that should change within the day.
For now, here are the known issues players are dealing with.
Game crashes and stability
- Game crashes to the PS5 home screen, sometimes with error code CE-108255-1.
- Repeated crashes during the opening prologue and cutscenes.
- Xbox consoles reportedly shutting down unexpectedly while playing.
- Random freezing during gameplay, sometimes requiring a restart.
- Crash frequency reportedly increases for some players after playing for a period of time.
Gameplay and mission bugs
- Missions becoming stuck because an NPC fails to appear or behaves incorrectly.
- Mission objectives failing to register, preventing progression.
- Players falling through the map or terrain.
- Characters becoming stuck in animations or unable to perform certain actions.
- NPCs clipping through characters, furniture, or other objects.
- Objects, vehicles, and characters spawning or behaving incorrectly.
- Softlocks requiring players to restart a mission or reload a save.
Graphics and visual issues
- Missing textures or textures failing to load properly.
- Blurry graphics, particularly at a distance on some Xbox Series S reports.
- Flickering lights and graphical artifacts.
- Green, pink, cyan, or other unusual color tints covering the screen.
- Black flashing artifacts, especially around water and reflective surfaces.
- Water rendering incorrectly, including distorted or stretched visual effects.
- Characters and environmental objects clipping through geometry.
Audio issues
- Crackling or distorted audio on Xbox Series S.
- Audio cutting out intermittently.
- Sound issues occurring alongside controller vibration problems.
- Audio glitches during certain opening sequences.
Save and progression problems
- Lost progress following crashes or unexpected console shutdowns.
- Save progress reportedly failing to retain some recent gameplay.
- Players having to replay sections after crashes or mission softlocks.
Once the official patch notes are available, we’ll update the article. Hopefully, this sorts out the PS5 error code that makes the game crash.
