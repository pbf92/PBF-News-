TITLE: Lithium Suicide Review; Restraint Chairs for Teens; YouTube's $3B Addiction Deal
OUTLET: MedPage Today

An umbrella review and meta-analysis found greater uncertainty around lithium's role in suicide prevention than earlier studies suggested. (American Journal of Psychiatry)
Former heavyweight boxing champion Tyson Fury revealed he was diagnosed with schizophrenia and bipolar disorder. (NBC News)
Intramuscular olanzapine for acute behavioral disturbances did not sedate a greater proportion of children and adolescents after 1 hour compared with intramuscular droperidol in the randomized PEAChY-M trial. (JAMA)
The New York Times discussed how the "Big Five" personality traits may be linked to mental health and genetic influences across various disorders.
Plans to introduce restraint chairs for teens at a Connecticut psychiatric hospital raised concern over their effect on young patients. (NBC Connecticut)
Intolerance of uncertainty increased among U.S. college students from 1992 to 2023, with a steeper rise after 2012, and targeting it may help curb anxiety prevalence in young adults, data from a meta-analysis suggested. (JAMA Psychiatry)
YouTube reached a tentative, roughly $3 billion settlement with U.S. school districts over student addiction claims, a deal that includes new product safeguards for teens. (New York Post)
Marsha Linehan, PhD -- the psychologist who developed dialectical behavior therapy for borderline personality disorder, a condition she later revealed she lived with as well -- died at age 83 from complications of pneumonia. (New York Times)
A Swedish cohort study found that a celiac disease diagnosis was associated with an increased risk for eating disorders. (JAMA Network Open)
Johnson & Johnson said it filed a supplemental new drug application with the FDA for lumateperone (Caplyta) to treat manic or mixed episodes in adults with bipolar I disorder, marking its fifth potential indication.
A growing number of National Hockey League goaltenders are opening up about mental health struggles. (New York Times)
