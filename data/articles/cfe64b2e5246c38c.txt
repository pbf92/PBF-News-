TITLE: Liverpool vs Manchester United player ratings as Wirtz, Ngumoha tear apart 10-man Red Devils
OUTLET: NBC Sports

It wasn’t a terribly simple day to give our player ratings but it sure felt easy after an early red card gave Manchester United almost 75 minutes of playing down a man at their longtime rivals Liverpool.
MORE — Recap, video highlights | Pochettino reaction
Anfield eventually saw their hosts with a 4-0 win on goals from Dominik Szoboszlai, Rio Ngumoha, Florian Wirtz, and Victor Munoz.
Wirtz and Ngumoha were especially venomous in their attack with the latter delivering eye-popping chance creation numbers en route to his goal and an assist.
So who shined, and who didn’t?
Liverpool vs Manchester United player ratings
Alisson Becker: 6.5 — Made his save.
Milos Kerkez: 7 — Led the game in sprints.
Virgil van Dijk: 7 — Wasn’t tested a ton but controlled his area especially in the air. Missed a header to make it 4-0 off a brilliant Wirtz cross.
Jeremy Jacquet: 7 — A little erratic with his long passing but made a few notable interventions in the rare instances where Man United hinted at threatening to threaten.
Ronald Araujo (Off 85'): 7.5 — Quiet, tidy, strong. No need for heroics today.
Alexis Mac Allister (Off 72'): 7 — Sent a game-high 14 passes into the final third.
Dominik Szoboszlai: 9 — His heat map in the United half was basically the Sun. Led the Reds in recoveries and is quite frankly one of the most valuable players to his club (and country) in the world. One of the best free kick takers in the game, approaching Messi levels of confidence with shorter-range efforts.
Florian Wirtz: 10 — Sensational technique to direct Ngumoha’s cross under the bar. Had seven created chances by the 72nd minute. Created 10 in total. TEN! Covered a game-high distance. Just sensational stuff.
Bradley Barcola (Off 73'): 8.5 — Was ready to go from the jump, and his electric pace to set up Ngumoha’s goal was something else.
Rio Ngumoha (Off 72'): 9.5 — Slick spun cross to assist Wirtz’s goal, and buried his chance in the 66th minute off Barcola’s blazing run. Nearly churned out two xG+xA in a spectacular performance. Was leading the game in touches when he subbed out, an unruly stat for a left wing.
Alexander Isak (Off 85'): 5 — Hit a sixth-minute chance at Lammens, then drew Yoro’s red card with a fall after his heavy touch. Otherwise pretty poor but his cleverness did change the game. One of three Liverpool players an xG+xA <1 and the only one without a goal or an assist.

Manchester United player ratings at Liverpool
Senne Lammens: 7.5 — Posted seven saves and the only knock on him might be the organization of his wall and defenders on Szoboszlai’s free kick... but was that on him or Bruno Fernandes? Stayed strong to save Alexander Isak on sixth-minute break.
Diogo Dalot: 5 — Was good with the ball but struggled in the duel and in defense.
Leny Yoro: INC or 5 — Honestly, not even sure it was a foul as Isak slows his sprint after a heavy touch to draw contact outside of the box, taking DOGSO out of the question. VAR might’ve been limited by the application of laws. If you think it’s a clear red card, I’m just not sure what to say.
Matthis De Ligt: 4 — I have been a huge advocate to see De Ligt over Harry Maguire and goodness, gracious it makes sense now. Rusty to the nth degree. Absolutely comical awareness on the play that led to Yoro’s red card. Worse on a play that should’ve been a 64th-minute Isak goal.
Lisandro Martinez: 4.5 — Contributing almost nothing defensively aside from a heroic sliding tackle that did not make up for a series of errors. Just three defensive contributions over 90 minutes on a day when it was difficult not to contribute defensively to the team.
Kobbie Mainoo (Off 88'): 6.5 — Made 15 defensive contributions as mop-up duty was a big part of his work in front of the backs.
Youri Tielemans (Off 65'): 7 — Rang up 12 defensive contributions and was good with the ball. The midfield wasn’t great, but the back line helped almost nothing.
Bruno Fernandes: 6.5 — Ran, challenged, and won almost every one of his duels.
Patrick Dorgu (Off 65'): 6.5 — Far from the problem. Zipped up and down his flank and won a good number of his duels.
Bryan Mbeumo (Off 22'): 6 — Removed after the red card following a decent-enough start that included a dangerous run into the Liverpool box
Matheus Cunha (Off 46'): 5 — Barely saw the ball but won 75% of his duels before giving way to Zirkzee. Is his place in jeopardy?
