TITLE: GTA 6 is Officially the Biggest Entertainment Launch in History
OUTLET: 80 Level

Rockstar has confirmed that GTA 6 generated nearly $4 billion in estimated retail sales during its first 24 hours and is now the biggest entertainment launch in history across all industries.
Grand Theft Auto VI has officially delivered the biggest entertainment launch in history, generating an estimated $3.79 billion in worldwide retail sales during its first 24 hours.
Rockstar Games announced that GTA 6 also sold 32.5 million units before its November 19 release, establishing a new pre-release record across all platforms. The $3.79 billion figure is based on the company’s estimates for sales through the end of the game’s first full day of availability.
Those numbers are considerably higher than even the already historic third-party estimates published before launch. Sensor Tower previously estimated that GTA 6 had generated approximately $1.84 billion from 19 million copies by release day, as covered in our earlier GTA 6 sales report. Rockstar’s official announcement confirms that the game’s actual commercial performance was operating on an entirely different scale.
“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI. We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world, and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”
- Sam Houser, president and founder of Rockstar Games
Bloomberg’s Jason Schreier offered a particularly striking comparison: Avengers: Endgame, currently the highest-grossing film in history, earned approximately $2.94 billion throughout its entire worldwide theatrical run. GTA 6 surpassed that total by around $850 million in a single day.
Pre-sales alone have already placed GTA 6 among the 30 bestselling video games ever released. That comparison is especially remarkable because the 32.5 million figure does not include purchases made following the game’s launch. It's likely much higher already.
GTA 6 has also dramatically exceeded the record established by its predecessor. GTA 5 generated approximately $800 million during its first day in 2013 and reached $1 billion after three days. GTA 6 earned nearly five times as much during its opening 24 hours. GTA 5 has since sold more than 230 million copies, meaning its sequel still has a long way to go before matching its lifetime total.
The extraordinary commercial launch follows similarly historic critical reception. GTA 6 currently holds a 99 Metascore, making it the highest-rated game in Metacritic history. Reviewers have praised the density and reactivity of Leonida, the relationship between Jason and Lucia, and the remarkable collection of new technology and simulation systems Rockstar developed for the game.
However, given the volatile state of the game industry, developers at Rockstar are still worried about the security of their jobs despite this success.
Before launch, even aggressive analyst projections suggested that GTA 6 might generate more than $1 billion through pre-orders. The actual result is now officially the largest opening-day commercial performance achieved by any entertainment release, regardless of medium.
GTA 6 is available for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
