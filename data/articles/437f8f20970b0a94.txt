Zentek (ZEN) Engages Micon to Lead Albany Graphite Pre-Feasibility Study
Zentek engaged Micon International Limited to lead a pre-feasibility study for its 100%-owned Albany Graphite Project. The company will report results when the study is complete; no cost or completion date was provided.
