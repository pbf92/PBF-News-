TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino delivered farewell message to fans ahead of final USMNT match
OUTLET: Yahoo

When the U.S. Soccer Federation hired former Spurs/PSG/Chelsea manager Mauricio Pochettino as USMNT head coach in 2024, it marked an ambitious move that ultimately ended in the Round of 16 of the World Cup. But the momentum and vision were clear, which led to Pochettino re-signing for the 2030 World Cup cycle.
That second cycle at the helm lasted three months.
And now, Pochettino is officially saying goodbye.
Just hours before the USMNT was set to face Haiti in the second leg of the CONCACAF Nations League quarterfinal (U.S. leads 6-0 on aggregate), the USMNT's social media accounts shared a farewell message from the soon-to-be Manchester United head coach.
Pochettino said:
"Thank you so much from the bottom of my heart, for all the fans, for the people that was supporting us in the last two years. Special. Great memories of the World Cup and everything that we lived together. Always in my heart. Always one of you. All the best, and I wish everything good for the future."
Pochettino will leave Cincinnati for Manchester immediately after the match. U.S. Soccer has yet to hire a permanent replacement, but it is expected to target six-time Premier League champion Pep Guardiola.
