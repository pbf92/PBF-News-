Gift Nifty signals weak opening for domestic markets
Indian stock markets are likely to open on a weak note on Wednesday amid mixed global cues. Analysts expect the market to remain in consolidation mode after a sharp recovery from recent lows. As the result season has almost comes to end, the focus has now shifted to global and domestic micro economic factors.
Hariselvan Radhakrishnan, Founder & CEO of HST Wealth, said Wall Street ended lower as investors pared exposure to richly valued AI-linked stocks. However, Nvidia’s better-than-expected results, announced after the close, could help stabilise sentiment towards technology stocks. “Any improvement in global tech sentiment may offer some support to market mood, although it does little to alter the earnings outlook for Indian IT companies, which remains dependent on client spending, deal conversion and margin resilience,” he said.
Meanwhile, Gift Nifty at 25,260 signals a gap down opening of about 120 points for Nifty.
The near-term bias therefore remains constructive, underpinned by softer crude prices, persistent foreign inflows and firmer global markets. However, the strength of institutional participation and developments on the geopolitical front will remain critical to sustaining the positive momentum, said Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money.
According to Osho Krishan, Chief Manager - Technical & Derivative Research, Angel One, the Bank Nifty scaled a fresh lifetime high, providing a meaningful impetus to the overall market sentiment. “Furthermore, broader market participation remained encouraging, reflected in a favourable advance-decline ratio. With sectoral rotation becoming increasingly evident, market participants should maintain a stock-centric approach and prioritise thematic outperformers rather than chasing broad-based exposure,” he said adding while the underlying bias remains constructive, disciplined position sizing and stringent risk management are imperative, particularly as the indices approach elevated technical thresholds.
Technically, the market is in consolidation mode, said an expert.
Pabitro Mukherjee, Deputy Vice President-Research, Bajaj Broking , said: Going ahead, a move above Tuesday’s high of 25,501 will open upside towards 25,800 levels in the coming sessions, while failure to do so will lead to extended consolidation in the range of 25,500-25,000 in the coming sessions. “We expect the index to extend its last four sessions consolidation and trade in the broad range of 24,800–25,500 amid stock-specific action. Following a sharp 13 per cent rally of over 3,000 points in just 12 sessions, some consolidation is warranted. This phase is likely to make the overall trend healthier by cooling off the overbought conditions in daily oscillators.”
On the derivatives front (23 Nov expiry), the highest put OI stayed at 25,000 (97.9 lakh), keeping it the key support, while 26,000 still holds the highest call OI (1.36 Cr), said Neel Parekh, Derivatives Research Analyst, SAMCO Securities. “Near the money, put writers added 31.7 lakh while call writers covered 97.4 lakh, a bullish mix of put writing and short covering; the biggest put addition was at the in-the-money 25,500 put, above the current level, a sign of strong conviction among put writers. PCR (all expiries) rose to 1.06 from 1.03 and India VIX eased 1.0 per cent to 15.52, both supportive of the bulls,” he added.
Global cues are broadly favourable with Asian equities trading higher in early deal on Wednesday. Japan’s Nikkei 225 has advanced more than 0.50 per cent, while South Korea’s KOSPI has gained over 2 per cent, signalling an improvement in regional risk appetite even as Wall Street ended sharply lower last night.
