Anthropic prices IPO at $83, valuing Claude maker at $1.5 trillion
The final price came in above the expected range, giving the AI company a market debut among the largest in history. Anthropic is set to raise about $66.5 billion from the offering, with a separate $26 billion private placement taking the combined funding to roughly $94.5 billion.
Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83 per share on Wednesday, above the previously announced range of $70 to $78, setting the stage for one of the largest stock market debuts in history and valuing the maker of Claude at approximately $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis.
The company, founded in 2021 by former OpenAI employees Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei and several other researchers, is expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker symbol ANT. The final price was disclosed to CNBC by a person familiar with the matter.
At $83 a share, the original offering of 825 million shares would generate approximately $68.5 billion in gross proceeds. Anthropic is selling about 801.4 million shares, while existing shareholders, including its seven co-founders, are selling another 23.6 million. The company would receive approximately $66.5 billion from its portion of the offering, before fees and expenses.
The public offering is accompanied by a separate $26 billion private placement of non-voting shares to seven technology companies: AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC. Together, the public offering and private placement represent approximately $94.5 billion in gross funding, before fees and excluding any additional shares sold through the underwriters' option. The underwriters have the right to purchase another 123.75 million shares from Anthropic, which could add more than $10 billion at the IPO price.
The pricing marks a significant increase from Anthropic's $965 billion valuation following its May funding round. It also puts the company ahead of the $1.25 trillion valuation recently attributed to rival OpenAI, although that figure is based on private-market reporting rather than a public listing.
The offering comes amid extraordinary growth in Anthropic's business. The company reported revenue of $33.2 billion in the first nine months of 2026, compared with $2.5 billion in the same period a year earlier, an increase of more than 1,200%. Third-quarter revenue alone reached $17 billion. Anthropic said its annualized revenue run rate had climbed to $81 billion by the end of October, up $10 billion in a single month.
The growth reflects the rapid adoption of Claude among businesses and software developers, particularly for coding and other enterprise applications. The number of customers generating at least $100,000 in annual revenue for Anthropic rose to more than 11,900 by the end of September, from more than 950 a year earlier. Those customers accounted for approximately 74% of the company's revenue in the first nine months of 2026.
Anthropic's expansion has also required enormous investment in computing capacity. Its prospectus disclosed approximately $900.9 billion in aggregate commitments for compute and infrastructure, including purchase commitments and leases. About $451.6 billion is expected to be incurred before the end of 2030, with much of the remainder extending through 2045. The company had nearly $132 billion in cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments as of September 30. 
Anthropic reported an operating loss of $1.3 billion in the first nine months of 2026, although its adjusted operating income was $4.1 billion. Its reported net loss reached $104 billion, but that figure was heavily affected by non-cash accounting charges, including a $94.3 billion loss related to changes in the fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities and other instruments. The company said the increase reflected the rise in its valuation.
