TITLE: Luciano Spalletti plays down Weston McKennie-Manchester United rumours
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Across the week, Manchester United have emerged as a possible destination for Juventus' Weston McKennie. The player was also asked about it during the international break and he appeared to hint towards an interest in the move, but later tracked back on those, suggesting he wasn't translated properly.
But with club football back, McKennie is back in the Juventus camp.
Manager Luciano Spalletti was asked about the speculation around Manchester United and McKennie. He shut down the talk, saying that everything is fine and he has spoken to the American too.
Spalletti said, via Corriere dello Sport: "It didn't seem to me like he had hinted at the possibility of leaving. I have a very good relationship with him, as with everyone else in the dressing room.
Further, Spalletti revealed that everything is fin with the player and his situation at Juventus.
"As soon as he returned, we discussed the matter, and everything was absolutely calm."
Convenient links floated
It never felt like there was anything to the rumours around McKennie and Manchester United. There were floated simply because of Mauricio Pochettino's appointment at Old Trafford and the Argentine's connection with the versatile midfielder. There have barely been suggestions of any contact and the story was turned into something more than it ever was initially.
