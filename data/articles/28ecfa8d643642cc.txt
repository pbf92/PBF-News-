On Nov. 19, 2026, GTA 6 was released after a wait of almost 13 years. After a decade-long wait, fans were finally able to return to the long-awaited franchise. However, despite the game's popularity and hype, UFC's Sean Strickland is not a fan of it. Despite Rockstar Games allowing players to return to Vice City, Strickland wants youngsters to go outside and train.
Strickland, 35, sent out a strict message about GTA 6 on its release day and targeted young people aged 18 to their early 20s. In a blunt message, Strickland blasted the fans who are spending their time in the virtual world instead of going out and training.
"I don't understand these young men, I'm talking like 18 to early 20s. Like, I get it, dude, you guys want to play GTA. When I was a kid, I wanted to play GTA too. But my main drive was training and getting p****. You want to call it a relationship, whatever you want to call it, I don't care.
