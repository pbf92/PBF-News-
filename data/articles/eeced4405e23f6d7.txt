Kent A. Steinwert Discloses Investment at Farmers & Merchants Bancorp with 5.39% Stake
Kent A. Steinwert, Farmers & Merchants Bancorp’s president, CEO and board chair, disclosed beneficial ownership of 5.39% of the company’s common stock. In a Schedule 13D filed Nov. 18, 2026, he reported 37,580 shares, using the 697,388 shares outstanding as of Oct. 31. Although he reported no current proposal for a transaction or other major change, he regularly considers strategic options in his leadership role.
Investor Intent
Steinwert says he holds shares he purchased or otherwise acquired outside company compensation for investment purposes. In his leadership roles, he regularly explores potential actions that could benefit Farmers & Merchants Bancorp, including mergers, acquisitions, reorganizations and changes to the company’s business, management or shares. He says he has no current plan or proposal for those changes.
Investor's Background
Kent A. Steinwert has worked at Farmers & Merchants Bancorp for 29 years. He serves as the company’s president and chief executive officer, as well as chairman of its board. He is a U.S. citizen and says he acquired shares through the company’s compensation and retirement plans.
