TITLE: Meta integrates Muse personal AI into Instagram
OUTLET: Techloy

Meta expands AI agent Muse features to Instagram
Meta has rolled out an update for its personal AI agent, Muse, within Instagram. Users in the US, Canada, Mexico, Japan, and Singapore can now use Muse to organise saved posts into collections or travel itineraries. The tool also creates grocery lists for recipes and allows users to chat in DMs to learn about posts or catch up on missed content. Conversations appear as side chats in the Muse app.
This rollout follows recent reports that the AI agent had issues reading encrypted messages and making unauthorized purchases.
The expansion makes the AI agent more accessible to users without requiring them to switch between apps.
