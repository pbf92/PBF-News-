Who Is Raul Bautista In GTA 6? The Bank Robber Explained
After well over a decade of waiting, "Grand Theft Auto VI" is finally upon us, and it's already generated more than $1 billion in pre-orders. Rockstar Games will surely be happy, as this is expected to be one of if not the single most expensive game ever made. What's really crazy is that, ahead of the release, there's still so much we don't know about "GTA VI." We do, however, have an idea of who the main characters and their supporting cast will be.
The game's story focuses largely on playable characters Jason and Lucia, who have the deck stacked against them. When an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida. We know quite a bit about Jason Duval so far, as well as Lucia Caminos. But what about the supporting cast?
One of the core characters who has shown up in the trailers and in the marketing is a man by the name of Raul Bautista. He's a thief who has a fair amount of experience and is something of a mentor to Jason and Lucia as they undertake their crime-filled life in Leonida. While it still hasn't been fully confirmed who is playing Raul as of this writing, you can see what the rumored cast of "GTA 6" looks like in real life.
The bits we see of Raul in the trailers and in official screenshots make him seem like a no-nonsense guy. Long-haired, well-dressed, and sporting a grizzled look, he appears to mean business. He's armed to the teeth in a lot of the shots, and seems more than capable of handling himself if s*** goes wrong.
Raul Bautista is a mentor to Jason and Lucia in Grand Theft Auto VI
Raul Bautista also seems like the sort of guy one wouldn't want to double cross. Rockstar is keeping things close to the chest but some quotes on the game's official website are attributed to him, including, "Life is full of surprises, my friend. I think we'd all be wise to remember that." Ominous.
Rockstar also has a couple of minor quotes attributed to Raul, including "Experience counts" and "A professional adapts." To go along with that, they have a bit of descriptive text for the character, which reads as follows:
"Confidence, charm, and cunning — Raul's a seasoned bank robber always on the hunt for talent ready to take the risks that bring the biggest rewards ... Raul's recklessness raises the stakes with every score. Sooner or later, his crew will have to double down or pull their chips from the table."
So there we have it! That's Raul in a nutshell. He's undoubtedly going to be a huge part of the main story but "GTA 6" also has more than 25 confirmed side activities players can indulge in.
