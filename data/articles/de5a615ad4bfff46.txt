Malibu Boats Agrees in Principle to $300,000 SEC Penalty
Less than 1 min read
Malibu Boats reached an agreement in principle to pay a $300,000 civil penalty to resolve an SEC investigation.
Key Highlights:
- The investigation concerns statements about channel inventory in fiscal 2023 and the first quarter of fiscal 2024.
- It also concerns alleged failure to disclose a deteriorating relationship with Tommy’s Boats from December 2023 or January 2024 until April 2024.
- The proposed resolution includes a cease-and-desist order and requires definitive documentation and SEC approval.
- Malibu Boats does not expect the resolution to materially affect its financial position.
Original SEC Filing: MALIBU BOATS, INC. [ MBUU ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
