Some Fed officials argued for a hike in October: FOMC minutes
Some Federal Reserve officials argued for a 25-basis-point rate hike at the Federal Open Market Committee meeting on Oct. 27-28, according to minutes released on Wednesday.
The policymakers, though, voted unanimously to keep its policy rate at 3.75%-4.00% after hiking it by 25 basis points in September, their first rate increase in three years. Still, the sentiment to boost rates to bring down inflation is building.
A couple of participants said a rate hike of more than 25 bps "could warrant consideration at the next meeting if incoming inflation data were unfavorable."
