GoodRx receives notice of non-compliance from Nasdaq
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- GoodRx Holdings () on Monday received a notice from Nasdaq indicating that, as a result of the company’s delay in filing its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended September 30, 2026, the company is not in compliance with listing rules.
- Nasdaq listing rules require listed companies to timely file all required periodic financial reports with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.
- The notice provides that the company has until January 19, 2027, to submit to Nasdaq a plan to regain compliance.
- If Nasdaq accepts such a plan, it may grant an extension of up to 180 calendar days, or until May 17, 2027, to regain compliance.
- The notice has no immediate effect on the listing of the company’s Class A common stock on Nasdaq.
