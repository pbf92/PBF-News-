GTA 6 Remote Play Explained: PlayStation And Xbox Support
It's been 13 years since "GTA 5" debuted and anticipation is high. Sadly, the build-up to launch day hasn't been without a few disappointments. For one thing, Rockstar Games announced that the "GTA 6" "physical edition" wouldn't actually include a disc, and the $100 "Ultimate Edition" appears to be an excuse for the company to charge more money by including everything you'd expect from a full game. What's more, the download size of "GTA 6" could be a major problem for some gamers. At 212GB, the game is massive, which means many will be frantically backing up their saved data to make room for Rockstar's colossal sandbox crime sim.
With that gargantuan download size and a price tag bordering on prohibitive for many gamers in 2026, many might be considering other options. Thankfully, Rockstar has confirmed that "GTA 6" will be available to stream via XBox Cloud Gaming, which means Microsoft adherents will be able to stream the game via their Xbox Series X|S consoles and even their old Xbox One consoles. PlayStation users, however, have no choice but to free up space on their hard drives — at least for now. Fortunately, with Remote Play, the game's portability is more flexible than its hard drive space.
GTA 6 will work with Remote Play on both PS5 and XBox consoles
At the same time Rockstar Games officially confirmed the 30 frames per second (FPS) limit for "Grand Theft Auto 6," the developer also confirmed the game will "fully support" Remote Play on both PlayStation and Xbox consoles. Rockstar didn't provide any caveats, so that should mean PlayStation and Xbox owners can stream the game from their consoles to their phones, PC, or, in the case of the PS5, their PlayStation Portal handheld.
PlayStation users will have to download the PS Remote Play app on their smart devices to stream "GTA 6" from their console. As long as you have a remote handy, you'll then be able to stream the game from your console to your device. Similarly, Xbox users can stream the game from their consoles to their mobile devices by grabbing a controller and heading to xbox.com/remoteplay on their chosen device.
It should be noted that neither of these options are the same as cloud gaming. Remote Play only allows players to stream the game from their console to their other devices. For now, PlayStation owners are out of luck when it comes to cloud gaming with "GTA 6," as Microsoft seems to have acquired the exclusive rights to stream the game via cloud and ease that hard drive crunch — at least at launch.
Only XBox owners will be able to stream GTA 6 via the cloud
"GTA 6" will be available to stream via XBox Cloud Gaming, but that doesn't mean you'll be able to stream the game to your PCs or smart TVs via the cloud. Rockstar made it clear that Xbox Cloud Gaming for "GTA 6" will only be available on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles. So, while you can stream the game locally from your console to other devices, there's no streaming the game from Microsoft via the cloud on anything other than your Xbox consoles. What's more, the cloud version uses XBOX Series S graphics settings, so don't expect the highest quality experience.
Microsoft confirmed that "GTA 6" will go live on the cloud gaming service at 1am ET on November 19, 2026, which means those planning on accessing the game this way won't have to wait any longer than other players. Meanwhile, Xbox One owners will have a way of enjoying the big day without shelling out hundreds of dollars for new generation consoles.
Those interested in this option would typically need a Game Pass subscription to use the Cloud Gaming service. But Microsoft notes that "players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime to stream supported games without a Game Pass subscription." It's also worth noting, as Rockstar does in its post confirming the Remote Play options, that connection speeds and other "technical factors" could negatively impact the streaming experience. That's the exchange you make for hard drive space.
Sadly, no such cloud gaming option exists for PlayStation owners. "GTA 6" will not be available via the PlayStation Plus Premium cloud service at launch, though there's nothing to say that won't change eventually.
