TITLE: GTA 6 Fans Split Over Whether They Take the Day Off Work to Play the Game
OUTLET: IGN

IGN has conducted a poll on if our readers would be taking time off to play GTA 6, and nearly half of them said yes.
We are officially less than a week away from the release of GTA 6, which means fans are preparing their launch plans as we speak. Economists have estimated that the U.S. economy will incur a $1 billion loss in productivity when the game launches on Thursday due to people calling out sick. We asked IGN readers if they will be part of that drop in productivity, and it seems like a significant amount of them will be.
The poll had three options, two of which allowed fans to choose whether or not they'd be using PTO or calling in sick on the day of release. The third choice were for users who plan to go into work on November 19. At the time of writing, over 39,000 users have voted, and 46.5% of them stated that they would be taking the day off to play GTA 6 using either PTO or by calling in sick at launch, while 53.3% stated that they wouldn't be taking time off.
The full break down for each poll option is as follows:
Are IGN Readers Taking Time Off to Play GTA 6?
- Yes, I am requesting/have already requested PTO - 38.3%
- Yes, but I am calling sick on the day of release - 8.2%
- No - 53.3%
GTA 6 is expected to have one of the largest entertainment launches of all time and will likely shatter GTA 5's records. Rockstar set new records in 2013 for having the fastest entertainment product to hit $1 billion, with $815 million of that coming in the first day. Given GTA 6 will actually cost more than GTA 5 ($80 for the standard version, $100 for the ultimate edition), it wouldn't be surprising if Rockstar blows way past that. The game has also managed to earn some early praise, as GTA 6 is nominated for eight awards at The Game Awards, including the highly sought-after Game of the Year trophy.
To make sure fans are aware of the cultural juggernaut's looming release, Rockstar has been turning Miami into Vice City in real life. The city is currently plastered with in-universe ads for GTA brands, with GTA-branded cars driving all over the place, a Vice City blimp flying over it, and the Miami Heat's arena has even been decked out with Vice City signage. Rockstar also paid $975,000 to install a neon pink sign that reads "Welcome to Vice City." Tomorrow, Rockstar will sponsor a regular season Miami Heat game and turn the team into the Vice City Heat with new jerseys and a never-before-seen court.
The game is less than 24 hours from beginning its regional roll out, starting with New Zealand. With all of that said, if you're one of the people skipping work or school play, make sure you pre-load the game so you don't waste a second of your precious time off downloading the game. And while you wait, be sure to check out our interviews with various content creators to see how they're preparing for this gargantuan launch, as well as community members who are preparing to say goodbye to GTA 5.
