Almond-Stuffed Laughs
Enjoy a bit of Roman humor this holiday season
The ability to laugh and find humor in most any situation is something that has connected people across time, including the Romans, who often played practical jokes on each other. This Test Kitchen recipe, perhaps the simplest yet from ancient Rome, was intended to trick guests and invite a laugh. Try it out this holiday season to see if you can get a laugh out of family and friends, just as the Romans did thousands of years ago.
At the risk of ruining the joke: Ancient Romans did not have the luxury of buying pitted dates as we do today. Therefore, whenever eating dates, they were careful not to bite down too hard, as date stones (which are hard like peach pits) could really hurt—or ruin—your teeth. Imagine, then, a Roman who bit into a date expecting to find a hard stone, only to realize the stone had been replaced with a tasty almond—how surprising and delicious, and no doubt cause for a good laugh!
This recipe, adapted from the first-century CE Roman gourmand Apicius,[1] thankfully does not require any tricky or challenging ingredients. If you’d like to be truly authentic, you could buy dates and pit them yourself, but if you’d like to save time (and sanity), just use pitted dates.
[1] Patrick Faas, Around the Roman Table: Food and Feasting in Ancient Rome (Chicago: Univ. of Chicago Press, 1994), p. 240.
Ancient Recipe
“Take one kind of date or another and remove the stone. Stuff in a nut, or pine nuts or ground pepper. Salt on the outside, bake in boiled honey and serve.”
BAR’s Recipe
Makes 4 servings
- 12 dates
- 12 raw unsalted almonds
- 3 tbsp honey
Instructions
- Remove stones from dates; if using pitted dates, simply count out dates and set aside.
- Place enough water into a pot to cover almonds and bring to a rolling boil. Place almonds into the water and boil for 60 seconds.
- Remove almonds immediately and place into a bowl of ice water to cool.
- While stuffing the dates, bring honey to a boil and cook for about 5 minutes until it reduces to a syrup-like consistency.
- Once almonds are cool, peel off the brown skin and stuff one almond into each date.
- Plate the almond stuffed dates and drizzle the honey on top. Serve immediately.
