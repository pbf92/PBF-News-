TITLE: Rockstar Developer Explains How Players Should Experience GTA VI
OUTLET: RockstarINTEL

According to a recent report by Kotaku, Rockstar Games wants Grand Theft Auto VI players to experience the world of Leonida without constantly worrying about completing tasks or reaching 100% completion. According to the game’s Vice President of Design, Stephen Donkin, players should take their time and discover things naturally instead of treating the game like a checklist.
In a set of development notes shared with the press, Donkin explained that there is so much to do and discover in Leonida that he hopes players will eventually stop thinking about tasks, completion percentages and online guides.
He also explained that exploring the world at your own pace can make the experience feel almost infinite. While Leonida feels incredibly large, Donkin believes that individual encounters still feel carefully handcrafted, with a human touch behind them. 
Donkin’s first Rockstar Games credit was as a QA tester on Grand Theft Auto V. His comments also give an insight into how the studio approaches world design and player exploration.
GTA VI Also Has Quiet and Relaxing Moments
Senior Design Director Ghyan Koehne also shared his thoughts about the different experiences found throughout GTA VI. According to him, the game features plenty of high-energy moments, action, thrills and humor. However, he also mentioned quieter and more meditative experiences that offer a completely different feeling.
When asked to describe the feeling Rockstar wants players to experience with a single word, Koehne chose “wonder.” He explained that the studio wants players to constantly experience this feeling and go through a mixture of emotions throughout the game.
Donkin also shared what he hopes players feel while exploring Leonida. He explained that players who let themselves become immersed in the world and simply follow their curiosity can experience Leonida in the same way he did during development.
These comments offer an interesting perspective on how Rockstar approaches open-world games. Instead of focusing entirely on completing objectives and finding everything through online guides, the studio encourages players to explore at their own pace.
With GTA VI having been out for a week now, players have already started exploring Leonida and discovering its many details. As Donkin explained, sometimes the best way to experience the game is simply to follow your curiosity.
