TITLE: USMNT stars already linked to Manchester United before Mauricio Pochettino heads a single match
OUTLET: New York Post

CINCINNATI, Ohio — Once upon a time, Fulham was lovingly nicknamed “Fulhamerica,” due to the stable of U.S. internationals playing for the West London club. Soon, Manchester United might need a similar moniker, should Mauricio Pochettino look to bring in some familiar faces.
TeamTalk reported earlier this week that United are watching fullback Alex Freeman and that Folarin Balogun’s representatives have told the club that Pochettino makes a move there more appealing, with Balogun having said last week that he’ll look to leave AS Monaco in January. Weston McKennie was added to the list Monday morning in a report from the Italian paper La Gazzetta dello Sport, despite having a contract with Juventus that runs through the 2029-30 season.
Those links, McKennie said Monday, are “inevitable.”
“I think, obviously, [Pochettino’s] time here, he’s obviously grown to like this team and seen the quality that this team has to offer,” McKennie said ahead of the manager’s farewell match Tuesday against Haiti. “I think it’s a potential. I think we have a lot of guys on our team that are due to probably make moves soon, or within the next year.
“And so I think it’s kind of inevitable, but at the end of the day, you never know what’s going on in a coach’s mind and what players he’s looking for. I think you could potentially see some of the guys from here being talked to go to Man U.”
Balogun and Freeman, notably, play positions that United is in dire need of strengthening. Benjamin Šeško’s injury has left them without a natural No. 9, while Luke Shaw and Diogo Dalot have struggled massively at either fullback position, without any depth behind them.
Balogun nearly ended up at Everton on deadline day, but left their training ground without signing a contract after his medical evaluation went awry.
“Good players, always, have dreams to reach the last level, and I think United is one of the teams that is on the last level and it’s a dream for many players,” Pochettino said. “But now I think they need to focus on today, on tomorrow’s game, on their clubs.
“I know really well, for more than two years, every single player. I think all our players are unbelievable talents and of course in the future, when that happens, of course we are going to pay attention, in case, to make some decision to pay attention to players we know really well.”
Pochettino said his top assistant Jesús Pérez, along with goalkeeping coach Tony Jiménez and fitness coach Sebastiano Pochettino, flew to Manchester to get a head start at United after Saturday’s match.
That, he said, had been the plan when terms were agreed between United and the U.S. Soccer Federation.
Pochettino and the rest of the assistants he’s taking — coach Miguel D’Agostino, analyst Alex Scott and fitness coach Silvia Tuyá Viñas — will fly from Cincinnati to Manchester overnight after Tuesday’s match on a jet chartered by United, landing in time for training Wednesday morning.
