TITLE: Dispersal order extended in Oldham town centre following anti-social behaviour
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Dispersal order extended in Oldham town centre following anti-social behaviour
Police continue their efforts to tackle anti-social behaviour
Another dispersal order has been placed in Oldham town centre. The Section 35 order comes after several reports of anti-social behaviour amongst youths in the area.
Union Street has been spotlighted as a hot spot for trouble and now police have been handed enhanced powers to remove individuals involved in, or suspected of being involved in anti-social behaviour from the area.

Residents have complained about a number of ongoing incidents. The order will run from 4:42pm today until 4:42am tomorrow for areas within the outlined image on the map.
Police officers will continue to patrol the area and locals have been encouraged to speak to them with any queries. This marks the second dispersal order placed in the town centre in as many days.
A 12-hour dispersal order was imposed for the town centre yesterday (November 27).
Anyone with information or concerns is asked to contact police via 101 or through the Live Chat service on our website.
Alternatively, you can contact the independent charity Crimestoppers, anonymously, on 0800 555 111.
