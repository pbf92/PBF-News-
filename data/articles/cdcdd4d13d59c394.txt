TITLE: 'GTA 6' players can't stop doomscrolling in the game: 'Art really does imitate life'
OUTLET: Business Insider

Gosh, stealing cars is super tiring, ain't it? Maybe you should take a moment and just doomscroll to relax.
Rockstar Games released "Grand Theft Auto 6" on Thursday, the first full reboot of its "GTA" crime epic franchise in 13 years. In the video game, players can open the Instagram-like Snapmatic on their iFruit phones and scroll through a raunchier version of the internet humans know all too well.
"He's got to doomscroll before he goes to sleep," Andre Rebelo-Soares, the YouTuber and Twitch streamer known as TypicalGamer, said during his first livestream of the game. "I mean, amen."
For about 10 minutes, the streamer scrolled through animated posts of buff men lifting weights, sexual-innuendo memes, and bikini-clad women on Vice City beaches.
"Yo, doomscrolling is kind of great in 'GTA 6,' not going to lie."
The "GTA" franchise has always connected jokes to the absurdities of everyday American life — even when its characters perform tasks (stealing cars, robbing banks, murder) that feel entirely foreign. Previous versions skewered topics like political corruption and policing.
The game's predecessor, "GTA 5," also had a smartphone that largely served in-game functions, like calling the police or connecting with other characters. In that game, Snapmatic let players take and share photos — but didn't have a social media-like feed.
In "GTA 6," the focus is on the tech industry and its time-sucking products.
"Not me accidentally doomscrolling the day away while playing GTA 6," another player posted on X. "Art really does imitate life."
"GTA 6" has had a historic opening. Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar's corporate owner, reported $3.79 billion in sales for the game on the first day of sales. That's a record for any entertainment release, movie or video game. Reviewers have also called the game a "masterpiece."
The release has reverberated through the global economy, with an influx of staffers requesting PTO or calling out sick to play the game. Some streamers, including Rebelo-Soares, flew to New Zealand to play the game early.
