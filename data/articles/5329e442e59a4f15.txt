TITLE: Emma Hayes on role in USMNT coaching search after Mauricio Pochettino exit
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

U.S. women's national team head coach Emma Hayes said she will support U.S. Soccer "through a technical lens" as it searches for a new U.S. men's national team head coach.
Mauricio Pochettino led the USMNT for the final time on Tuesday, Nov. 17, coaching the team to a 2-0 win over Haiti in the Concacaf Nations League quarterfinal second leg.
The Argentine has now departed to take over Manchester United, leaving the USMNT with a head coaching vacancy for the third time since the end of the 2022 World Cup.
Hayes said U.S. Soccer CEO J.T. Batson asked her to lend a hand in the search for Pochettino's replacement, and she was happy to answer the call.
"My role is merely to support [the search] through a technical lens, knowing I do this job, albeit with the women's team, and that I've coached for a number of years and can offer some of that insight into the application of what to look for and why," Hayes said on a call Wednesday, Nov. 18 after naming her Concacaf W Championship roster.
"I feel very embedded in what we're doing with the federation, so it doesn't feel unusual to me. Like I've said many times, let's build off the good work that Mauricio has done. I think that's the important part, and let's get it right."
One person who won't be involved in the search for Pochettino's successor? Pochettino himself.
The incoming Manchester United boss said after Tuesday's game he would not be assisting Batson and U.S. Soccer president Cindy Parlow Cone in their hiring process.
"It's not my job, and for sure they have the capacity to find the right people and the right profile," Pochettino said at his postgame press conference. "I don't know how to measure my colleagues and I think it's not my job."
He added with a laugh: "Two years ago, they signed Emma Hayes and then Mauricio Pochettino, so I think they have good eyes."
