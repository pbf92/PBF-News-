TITLE: Huge Man Utd injury blow as new Baleba injury timeline confirmed
OUTLET: London Evening Standard

Baleba was injured during United’s Champions League clash against Roma on November 3, pulling up as he made a run into the penalty area before being forced off in tears.
The Cameroon international has not played since, with former manager Michael Carrick initially confirming he would be sidelined for several weeks.
Pochettino has now confirmed Baleba faces up to three months out, with the timeframe dating back to the injury against Roma. The setback is expected to rule him out throughout the festive period and potentially until the end of January.
“Baleba, the moment was Roma, [when he got] injured, and the medical staff were talking about 10 to 12 weeks,” Pochettino told a press conference on Friday.
Baleba is set to miss United’s upcoming fixtures against Brentford, Newcastle, Coventry and RB Leipzig, as well as league matches against Crystal Palace, Arsenal, Nottingham Forest, Sunderland and Brighton.

He is also expected to be unavailable for Man Utd’s FA Cup third-round tie in January and their Premier League meeting with Newcastle on 7 January.
The midfielder could remain sidelined for further fixtures against Aston Villa, Bayern Munich, Liverpool and Villarreal later in January, depending on his recovery.
Man Utd have eight matches remaining in 2026, beginning with Saturday’s home fixture against Brentford, as Pochettino looks to navigate a busy schedule without the injured midfielder.
