Bitcoin plunges 6%, dips under $58K
Bitcoin BTCUSD sank more than 5.9% on Friday as Wall Street’s enthusiasm on technology and artificial intelligence fizzled out, accompanying a selloff that dipped the cryptocurrency below $58,000.
Bitcoin BTCUSD is down more than 34% year-to-date and down almost 29% this month alone. The crypto has also lost more than 40% from its high point in a year.
Moreover, its decline has followed the tech trade selloff, suffering alongside the Nasdaq, which is down more than 5%, and the S&P 500 (SP500), down more than 3.5%. South Korea’s Kospi index also fell more than 5%.
Within the ETFs, BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust IBIT lost $541.1M on Thursday, marking its biggest one-day outflow on record.
