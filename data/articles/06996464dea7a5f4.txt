TITLE: RDDT vs. GOOGL: Which Digital Advertising Stock Has an Edge Right Now?
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Reddit RDDT and Alphabet GOOGL are major players in the digital advertising space. While Reddit is an emerging social media platform gaining traction with community-driven advertising, Alphabet remains the dominant force in global search and digital ad markets.
Reddit or Alphabet — Which of these Digital Advertising stocks has the greater upside potential? Let's find out.  
The Case for RDDT Stock
Reddit is benefiting from its expanding advertising portfolio, growing advertiser adoption, and increasing demand for performance-driven marketing solutions. The company's investments in artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, and advertising automation are strengthening monetization. In third-quarter 2026, advertising revenues surged 54% year over year to $847 million, driven by higher pricing and impressions. Click volume increased more than 50%, while conversion volume more than tripled, reflecting improving advertising effectiveness.
Reddit's AI-powered advertising solution, Reddit Max, is emerging as a key growth driver. The platform entered public beta in the third quarter, with advertiser adoption more than tripling sequentially. Enhanced machine-learning models, real-time signals and improved targeting are helping advertisers improve campaign performance. The company also expanded shopping capabilities to support up to 20 million products. Shopping and app-install advertising objectives each registered more than 95% year-over-year revenue growth in the third quarter of 2026, highlighting strong demand for lower-funnel solutions.
Reddit is also expanding its advertising opportunities through video, business-to-business (B2B) marketing and strategic partnerships. Video advertising revenues more than doubled year over year, supported by the introduction of a 15-second engaged video-view objective. Enhanced lead-generation tools and in-market audience targeting are strengthening Reddit's appeal among B2B advertisers.
Reddit's expanding advertising ecosystem, supported by integrations with Shopify, HubSpot, Smartly, Innovid and Skai, is improving accessibility for advertisers. Revenues from its Ads API ecosystem increased more than 45% sequentially in the third quarter of 2026. Improvements in personalized feeds, video experiences and community engagement are further strengthening user retention. These initiatives are expected to support sustainable advertising growth and higher average revenue per user.  
The Case for GOOGL Stock
Alphabet is benefiting from strong advertising demand across Google Search, YouTube and the broader advertising ecosystem. In the third quarter of 2026, Alphabet's total advertising revenues increased 11% year over year, reflecting growth across all major verticals. Google Search and other advertising revenues rose 12% to $63.3 billion, driven primarily by strength in retail and financial services. YouTube advertising revenues increased 9% to $11.2 billion, supported by robust brand advertising demand and growing connected TV engagement. Living-room viewing contributed more than one-third of YouTube's advertising revenues, highlighting its expanding appeal among advertisers.
Alphabet's AI-powered advertising capabilities are further strengthening its competitive position. The integration of Gemini models across Google's advertising infrastructure is improving ad targeting, personalization and conversion predictions. AI Max and Performance Max continue to witness strong adoption, helping advertisers optimize campaigns and improve returns on advertising spending. During the third quarter, Gemini models helped advertisers generate more than 1.5 billion campaign assets, representing more than sevenfold year-over-year growth.
Growing adoption of AI Overviews and AI Mode is creating additional advertising growth opportunities. Alphabet is expanding advertisements across these AI-powered search experiences, including international markets, while introducing multiturn advertising formats in the United States. Higher search engagement and stronger commercial intent are expected to support long-term monetization.
However, Alphabet is facing headwinds from rising AI infrastructure investments, higher depreciation expenses and increasing data center operating costs, which are pressuring free cash flow. Slowing Search advertising growth, lower monetization of AI Mode queries, foreign exchange headwinds and potential near-term Google Cloud margin pressure from third-party compute capacity are additional concerns.
