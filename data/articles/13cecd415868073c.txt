TITLE: Rockstar Leaves Sex Workers Out of Grand Theft Auto for First Time in 25 Years
OUTLET: Consequence of Sound

For the first time in 25 years, players won’t be able to hire a prostitute in a new Grand Theft Auto game. In a recent interview with Shacknews, Rockstar Games revealed that the feature is missing from GTA 6 because it wanted to focus on Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos’ Bonnie and Clyde story.
“GTA VI is a game about a couple in a bind, lovers on the run,” explained a Rockstar representative. “While we have taken a lot forward from previous GTA titles, everything we have implemented in VI has been done to support the spirit of the story we wanted to tell, and the gameplay systems we wanted to expand upon — things like [a] wanted system, the radio, robberies, and car theft. Many aspects of previous titles are not present in GTA VI, but the elements we have we believe are all necessary and complementary to the feeling and motivations we wanted to emphasize as you play through Jason and Lucia’s story.”
It’s worth noting that while sex workers are off the table in GTA 6, strip clubs are alive and well. Tied for the highest-rated game of all time, Rockstar’s latest is currently rolling out globally for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
