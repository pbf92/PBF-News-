The Miami Heat Unveil Its Vice City-Inspired Court for GTA 6
Published
Quick Facts
- Ahead of the Miami Heat's A Night in Vice City GTA 6 promotion on November 18, the team has unveiled its Vice City court
- In addition to the court, the team will wear special jerseys and sell collab Cactus Jack apparel
- Check below for images of the court

Tomorrow night, the Miami Heat are taking a trip to Vice City as part of the NBA team’s partnership with Rockstar Games in promotion of Grand Theft Auto 6. So far, we’ve seen the Travis Scott-designed jerseys, lifestyle apparel capsule, and three Nike Air Force 1s, but the Heat have kept the one-night-only court design under wraps until now. Just one day before the Miami Heat take on the visiting Milwaukee Bucks for “A Night in Vice City,” we finally have a look at the Vice City-inspired court.
Miami Heat’s Vice City Court
The Heat don’t go full NBA Cup and completely cover the court in colorful neon pastels. Instead, the GTA 6-inspired court features the stylized Vice City logo first premiered in 2002’s Grand Theft Auto: Vice City in pink at center court. From the three-point line up to the paint, the court turns bright pink and features subtle palm tree graphics. As for the paint itself, a bright blue hue is used, with a small pink “Vice City” logo below the basket.
An “I LOVE VICE CITY” graphic is applied on both ends of the court, while GTA graphics are displayed across the jumbotron and screens inside the arena. The team is also set to adopt a bright pink Heat logo in place of its standard red design. Players are also expected to get in on the action, with Giannis Antetokounmpo previewing a GTA 6-inspired Nike Freak 8 PE that he'll likely wear on November 18.
A Night in Vice City Drops
While those at home will be able to enjoy the court, those in attendance on November 18 can cop some exclusive merch. The Miami Heat will be selling a special Vice City Swingman jersey, designed in collaboration with Rockstar Games, Nike, and Travis Scott, starting at 5:00 PM ET when doors open to the arena. The arena will also have the Cactus Jack Nike Miami Heat GTA 6 lifestyle collection, featuring hats, T-shirts, hoodies, and more, with two styles set to be exclusive to the arena. Finally, you’ll be able to enter giveaways for exclusive NBA 2K27 merch at the pop-up 2K Mart inside the Kaseya Center.
Shortly after the game ends, Grand Theft Auto 6 will finally be available to play in the United States. The game is being released at midnight local time across the globe, so the Miami Heat, Milwaukee Bucks, and fans can leave the arena with another experience to look forward to. We’ll be on the ground in the arena during A Night in Vice City, so stay tuned for updates on our socials and via the Sole Retriever mobile app.
