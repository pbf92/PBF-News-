TITLE: Felix Nmecha defends new 'Jesus Loves You' celebration amid fan boos
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

Felix Nmecha has told football fans “Jesus loves them” in a new goal celebration, as Borussia Dortmund club executives were booed for defending his Christian expression on the pitch.
The midfielder has been at the centre of criticism from Dortmund supporters over the ‘Crowns Down’ celebration, created by players part of the King’s Return movement at the World Cup.
It is affiliated with football ministry Ballers in God, which has attracted criticism after German broadcaster ARD published a story about its members' conservative Christian views on issues such as abortion and transgenderism. A club supporters group accused Nmecha of promoting intolerance and “deny[ing] people their dignity”.
Nmecha responded: “My message has never been about promoting intolerance, discrimination, or hate in any form. As Christians, our message is one of love and respect for everyone, regardless of where they come from, what they believe, or what their worldview may be.”
After scoring an 80th minute winner against Borussia Monchengladbach on Saturday, Nmecha signed “Jesus loves you” towards the crowd. He told Sky Sports: “I wanted to show people today whether they are for me or against me, Jesus loves them.”
Dortmund’s managing director, Carsten Cramer, backed Nmecha when the player was present at a supporters meeting but was booed and whistled at by the crowd, Welt reported on Sunday.
Cramer said “let’s just forget about it” as he advocated for reconciliation between fanbase and player, having previously said he understood fan criticism of the 26 year-old.
Football teams in Germany are required to have a majority of their voting rights held by their fans, meaning supporters' associations have significant sway on the club's direction.
German national team manager Jurgen Klopp, himself a Christian, said earlier this month ahead of international fixtures that Nmecha would not perform the‘Crowns Down’ celebration.
