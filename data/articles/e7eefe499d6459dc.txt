After today's close, NVIDIA (NASDAQ:NVDA) reports earnings, coming in at that moment down 26% over the past month. That decline, plus leadership turmoil that Barron's linked to Jensen Huang's exit, has made tech the market's weak spot. Strong morning reports from Home Depot (NYSE:HD) and Disney (NYSE:DIS) are competing with that drag before the open.
Pre-Market Snapshot Before the Open
MarketWatch reported futures tipping toward gains for the Nasdaq and S&P 500 while the Dow dips. The VIX sits in its normal range after falling 6 points in a week.
Tech Selloff Meets a Tougher Bar for Nvidia
Technology is the weakest sector, down 3% in the latest pre-market data. Consumer staples and utilities are the only gainers. Money is moving toward defensive sectors while traders wait on one report.
Analysts expect Nvidia to post $2.4712 in EPS on roughly $108.7 billion in revenue. That is just above the company's own guidance of $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%. Nvidia is the largest weight in both the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100, so its reaction will move both headline numbers.
