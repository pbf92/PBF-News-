TITLE: Manchester United win appeal over Yoro’s red card despite Webb backing decision
OUTLET: The Guardian football

Manchester United have successfully appealed against Leny Yoro’s red card in their 4-0 defeat to Liverpool on the day referees’ chief Howard Webb gave his backing to the original decision. The defender was dismissed for denying an obvious goalscoring opportunity (Dogso) after eight minutes, but this was found to be incorrect.
The Frenchman will no longer be required to serve a one-match suspension after a ruling by an independent panel. He fouled Alexander Isak when the Liverpool forward was through on goal in the early stages at Anfield but Mauricio Pochettino, United’s new head coach, insisted the striker would not have reached the ball, and therefore was not a sending-off offence. The referee Sam Barrott made a different assessment, for which the video assistant referee did not seek a review, a judgment supported by Pro Ref’s chief refereeing officer, Webb.
“The reason we’re talking about it is the fourth consideration [in Dogso decisions], that’s control or likelihood of keeping or gaining control of the ball,” Webb told Mic’d Up, which was recorded before the confirmation of the card being annulled.
“Isak’s got control of the ball, he’s got possession, he’s got the touch, he knocks it past Yoro. He’s moving at pace at that moment, and a foul comes in and knocks him down. There’s a question mark, I guess, around whether or not he would get on to that ball before Lammens. Now, in the moment, the referee looked at it and felt he would. The VAR checks to see whether that decision made on the field was clearly and obviously incorrect, and formed the opinion that it wasn’t.
“I think by and large, most people would see that as a correct decision, with Isak moving quickly on towards that ball. The alternative view is that Lammens might get there first, but I think it’s a little bit debatable. [It’s] certainly not clearly wrong.”
Pochettino expressed his opinion on Sunday: “I don’t agree because the ball wasn’t in Isak’s control. Leny was running with him and when Isak saw the control wasn’t good and the ball was going to the keeper it is normal you delay, you stop running, and you wait to feel the touch and you go down. It is never a foul.”
Dominik Szoboszlai scored the resulting free-kick, setting Liverpool on their way to victory, to make it a difficult first match in charge for Pochettino. Yoro will now be available to face Brentford on Saturday in the Premier League.
