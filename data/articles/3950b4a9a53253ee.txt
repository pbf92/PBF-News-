TITLE: All the ‘GTA 6’ Movie and TV References: From ‘Jaws’ to ‘Die Hard,’ Rockstar Pays Tribute to Hollywood Classics
OUTLET: Variety

SPOILER ALERT: This article contains spoilers for Rockstar Games’ “Grand Theft Auto 6.“
“Grand Theft Auto” has been referenced and parodied in movies and TV shows since the popular Rockstar Games video game franchise first debuted in 1997.
While “GTA” has always been good at turning the tables and using its satirical nature to poke fun at pop culture and Americana classics, the game’s developers packed even more Hollywood references into “GTA 6” than any previous game in the franchise.
See below for all the movie and TV show references we’ve discovered in “GTA 6” so far. Variety will add more to this list as we play through the rest of the game, which was released Nov. 19 — with a Day One haul of $3.8 billion in sales — and has a reported play-through time of approximately 80 hours to complete its main storyline.
Pulp Fiction
A low-angle view of Jason and Lucia grabbing weapons out of their car pays homage to a classic scene of Vincent Vega (John Travolta) and Jules Winnfield (Samuel L. Jackson) from “Pulp Fiction.” In another scene, Jason and Lucia go about robbing a diner in a similar manner to the film’s iconic couple “Honey Bunny” and “Pumpkin.”
Fast & Furious
A car inspired by Brian O’Conner’s (Paul Walker) Mitsubishi Eclipse can be found in a garage in the game.
Jaws
When Jason completes a mission to save his friend Kenton, a boat charter and dive shop owner, the two are left doggy-paddling back to shore on the back of some debris. Combined with some exchanged dialogue, the scene is pretty much a shot-for-shot homage to the end of “Jaws,” when Brody and Hooper are swimming back to shore after killing the great white shark and destroying their boat in the process.
Die Hard
Lucia is seen crawling through an air vent in an office building complaining about the situation when her and Jason are stuck saving everyone from terrorists in a direct reference to the same situation Bruce Willis’ John McClane found himself in.
Marvel
If you choose to help out a farmer with cropdusting his strawberry plants, beware that the pesticide used will turn him into a raging Hulk-esque creature. You can then try to soothe the “big guy,” just like Scarlett Johansson’s Black Widow does for Mark Ruffalo’s Hulk in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
Rocky/Million Dollar Baby
One mission requires Lucia to become a body builder and a training montage ensues that is reminiscent of classics like “Rocky” and “Million Dollar Baby.”
Saw
A few unfortunate choices can lead you to be randomly kidnapped and wake up with a collar around your neck in a nondescript room. Your kidnapper will then come on a TV screen — like Jigsaw from the “Saw” horror movie series — and task you with surviving a “hunt” on his private, deserted island.
Dexter
Players have discovered that a replica of Dexter Morgan’s (Michael C. Hall) apartment from the Showtime series “Dexter” can be found in the game, along with a few other locations from the series about the fictional Miami serial killer.
Scarface
Entering one specific crime scene location will give you a practically perfect recreation of the infamous chainsaw moment from the 1983 classic.
