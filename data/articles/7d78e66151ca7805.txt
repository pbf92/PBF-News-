American Resources gets Nasdaq delisting notice
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- American Resources () said on Tuesday that it received a delisting notice from the Nasdaq stock exchange stating that it has not regained compliance with the exchange's listing rules.
- The determination follows the company's failure to file its quarterly reports on Form 10-Q for the periods ended March 31 and June 30, 2026, by the November 16, 2026, expiration of its previously granted extension.
- In addition, the company has not yet filed its quarterly report on form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
- The company said that it intends to appeal Nasdaq's determination to a Nasdaq Hearings Panel by the November 25, 2026, deadline and request a stay of any suspension pending the hearing process.
- The company added that it continues to work closely with its accounting, audit and other external service providers to complete its outstanding SEC filings, including the accounting associated with the deconsolidation of former subsidiaries and presentation of discontinued operations.
