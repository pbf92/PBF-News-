TITLE: Pornhub’s traffic plummeted by 15.5 per cent following the release of GTA VI
OUTLET: UkrMedia News

The release of Grand Theft Auto VI had an impact not only on the gaming industry, but also on traffic to other popular online platforms. Just one hour after the game’s release on 19 November 2026, traffic on Pornhub in the US fell by 15.5 per cent. At the same time, internet service providers recorded record network loads due to the mass downloading of the new Rockstar Games title.
- Traffic to Pornhub in the US fell by 15.5 per cent during the first hour following the release of GTA VI.
- The game was officially released on 19 November 2026.
- Internet service providers have recorded record levels of network traffic.
- The release of GTA VI has had an impact on how the online audience’s attention is divided.
Traffic to Pornhub plummeted following the launch of GTA 6
According to Mashable, citing internal statistics from Pornhub, two hours before the release of GTA VI, traffic on the platform in the US was 16.5 per cent higher than the average daily figures for that time of day. However, at midnight, when the game became available on the US East Coast, traffic fell 7.5 per cent below normal levels, and by 1 am the drop had reached 15.5 per cent.
The huge buzz surrounding the game also led to the rapid growth in PS5 sales and a shortage of consoles in major US retail chains.
At the same time, the number of search queries related to the Grand Theft Auto series on the platform itself rose by 16,196 per cent on 19 November. Since 18 November, the service has recorded around 15 million such queries.
GTA VI has set a record for internet traffic
According to DE-CIX, an international internet exchange point operator, data transfer speeds across its global network reached a record 33.03 Tbit/s in the run-up to the game’s release. Overall traffic levels during the launch of GTA VI were 25 per cent higher than during a typical week in early 2026.
The American telecoms giant Comcast also reported that, at peak times, GTA VI accounted for up to 24 per cent of all traffic on its network.
The release of the new Grand Theft Auto game even had an impact on sports broadcasts. The National Football League match between the Indianapolis Colts and the Houston Texans, which took place on the day of the game’s release, attracted 12.23 million viewers — the smallest audience of any Thursday match this season. However, a direct causal link between these figures and the game’s release has not been proven.
