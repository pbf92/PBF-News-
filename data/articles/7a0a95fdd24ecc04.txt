TITLE: Where Can I Play GTA 6? Here’s What Rockstar’s New Game Is Like
OUTLET: Revista Merca2.0

GTA 6 is finally here! Its release marks the return of one of Rockstar Games’ most popular franchises. The latest installment features a new story, characters, and settings that expand the series’ universe. If you’re ready to dive into this new adventure, here’s where you can play it.

Where Can I Play GTA 6?
GTA 6 is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Its availability on other platforms will depend on future announcements from Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive.
What Is GTA 6 Like?
Grand Theft Auto VI is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Rockstar Games. The story takes place in Leonida, a fictional state inspired by Florida, and brings players back to Vice City, a city based on Miami. The plot follows Jason and Lucia, a couple who become involved in a criminal conspiracy after a seemingly straightforward heist goes wrong.
The game features an open world filled with urban environments, coastal areas, and various locations for players to explore. The story centers on its two protagonists and their relationship as they try to make their way in a crime-filled world. GTA 6 offers a single-player experience and represents another evolution of the popular franchise.
How Much Does GTA 6 Cost?
The official prices are $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. Prices may vary depending on the retailer and region.

How Much Does the GTA 6 Collector’s Edition Cost?
The GTA 6 Collector’s Edition is priced at $399.99. The package is aimed at fans looking for physical merchandise related to Grand Theft Auto VI, so the price covers only the collection of items included. The video game is sold separately and is not part of the collection.
What Is the GTA Video Game Series Like?
Grand Theft Auto is an open-world video game franchise developed by Rockstar Games. Its titles combine exploration, driving, combat, and side activities across large environments that players can freely explore.
The series is known for featuring stories centered on characters involved in various conflicts within fictional cities inspired by real locations in the United States. Players can travel on foot, drive different vehicles, interact with non-player characters, and complete missions that are part of the main campaign.
GTA also includes numerous activities outside the main missions, allowing players to explore the map and take part in different situations throughout the game world. Driving plays a major role, as do combat and interactions with other characters.
With GTA 6, Rockstar Games is preparing another installment built around this formula, once again set in a vast open world and featuring a story focused on new protagonists.
