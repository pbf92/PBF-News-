Bonk to Raise About $100,000 in Registered Stock Offering to Former Chairman
Bonk entered into a Securities Purchase Agreement with former Executive Chairman John Miklos Gulyas for a registered direct offering of 274,243 common shares at $0.36464 per share. The offering is expected to generate approximately $100,000 in gross proceeds before expenses, with closing expected on or about November 30, 2026, subject to customary conditions. Bonk plans to use the net proceeds for general corporate purposes, including working capital, capital expenditures and potential acquisitions.
Agreement details:
- Agreement type: Securities Purchase Agreement for a registered direct offering of common stock
- Counterparty: John Miklos Gulyas
- Signed / Effective: Nov 20 2026 / same
- Duration / Termination: Through closing
- Reason: Fund general corporate purposes, including working capital and potential acquisitions
Original SEC Filing: BONK, INC. [ BNKK ] - 8-K - Nov. 25, 2026
