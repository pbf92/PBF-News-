TITLE: Grand Theft Auto VI: Collectors Box. Whats inside?
OUTLET: RockstarINTEL

Its been a few days since Grand Theft Auto VI released worldwide, and one thing we can say is, that its been a huge success. breaking records left right and centre. As we all know, a few months ago, Rockstar Games announced a Collectors Box, costing a controversial £350 plus shipping.
Our collectors box arrived a couple of days ago, but we’ve been so indulged in playing GTA6 we didn’t have time to unbox it, until today, one thing we can say is this box is a beast. Its gigantic. In this article, we unbox the Collectors edition, and show you close up what you get for £350.
Below you can find pictures of the box, and pictures of each item that you get included. The box has 11 items included in it:
- Macca™ the Gator Figure
- Oakley® Frogskins™ Sunglasses
- Leonida Keys Crossbody Bag
- New Era® 9FORTY Snapback Hat
- Macca™ the Gator Magnetic Mirror
- Chunkee the Manatee Shot Glass
- Vice City™ Collectible Swizzle Spoon
- Vice City™ Razor Blade Keychain
- Leonida Keys Enamel Pin Set in Metal Tin
- Leonida Keys Sticker Pack in Stash Bag
- Double-Sided Souvenir Poster with Map of Leonida
The Box
The box itself is gigantic, a plain black glossy box that has two logos on it, the VI logo and Vice City Leonida Logo. The box has a sleeve which has the Vice City Leonida Artwork on the front, then 3 of the artworks on each other side.
When you open the box, its has 3 layers to it. The top which has the Macca the Gator Statue, Enamel Pin set, Oakley Sunglasses and the Vice City Shot glass.
The second tray down has the Vice City Leonida New Era snapback the and Fanny pack. Then the very bottom tray has the Mirror, collectors spoon, razer keychain, map and stickers.
Macca The Gator Figure
Vice City Shot Glass & Enamel Pin Set
The Shot Glass is just a simple Glass Shot Glass, nothing that great about it. The Enamel Pin Badges, come in a metal tray which is quite neat and there is 9 different pin badges to use.
Oakley Sunglasses
The Oakley Sunglasses are probably the best thing in this box. These alone are worth around £150+ without any of the GTA 6 / Rockstar Branding. The left arm features the Rockstar Games Logo, whilst the right arm features the VI logo. The arms also feature pal tree leaves type of detail.
The middle Tray
The middle tray on the collectors box features two items, the Vice City Leonida New Era Snapback and the Leonida Keys Crossbody Bag.
Bottom Tray
In the bottom tray of the Collectors Box we have some cool items, the Magnetic Mirror, Collectibles Spoons, Razor Blade Keychain (Definitely a drugs kit) The double sided posted with a map of Leonida and a pack of stickers.
