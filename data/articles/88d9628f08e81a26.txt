TITLE: GTA 6 Prologue explained: Lucia’s son Tommy and how the mission ends
OUTLET: eGamers.io

Spoiler note: this page covers the end of the Prologue and what becomes of Lucia’s son.
Lucia’s son Tommy is a baby. During the Prologue she leaves him at a coffee shop, goes off to rob a Payday Loan store and ends up arrested. PowerPyx and GTABase both report that the coffee shop had already flagged him as abandoned, and he is taken into child welfare. The mission steps themselves are in our Prologue walkthrough.
Who is Tommy in GTA 6?
He is Lucia’s son, still a baby. Tommy explains Lucia’s actions in the Prologue, and he is the reason for what she wants inside Hamlet Correctional: release, and a reunion with him.
His name returns later in Chapter 1. Both guides say the optional errand Buy Lucia a Necklace has Jason order a custom gold chain with Tommy’s name engraved on it.
Why does Lucia go back to the coffee shop?
You open the Prologue in prison. Lucia chats with her cellmates, then a guard called CO Wilson escorts her to the counselor’s office. GTABase gives the counselor’s name as Stefanie. Her questions set off a flashback, and from there you play out the crime.
The flashback opens with Lucia walking out of a coffee shop and choosing to hit a Payday Loan store for fast money. You point your gun at the teller, threaten him and grab the cash he leaves on the counter. When a customer seizes Lucia, a quick time event lets you break free. For the stores you can rob in free roam, see our Payday Loan robberies guide.
Only at the end do you learn why she heads back to the coffee shop: the baby was still there. By then, staff had reported the child as abandoned.
Can you escape the police in the Prologue?
No. Once the robbery is done, a flashing blue and red circle on the minimap marks where the police are searching. GTABase describes the officers as relentless and says they carry stun guns. Both guides agree that the chase is scripted: get caught or keep running, and either way the screen fades to black and drops you back in the counselor’s office. GTABase’s advice is not to spend time hunting for an escape route.
Can you avoid the arrest at the coffee shop?
No. When the chase ends, Lucia makes her way back to the coffee shop, which is marked on the minimap and compass. Officers arrest her as she gets near, and PowerPyx points out there is no way around it. In the closing cutscene Tommy goes into child welfare and Lucia is taken into custody.

What happens right after the Prologue?
Next up is Gone Fishing, in which Jason must save Donnie from the Kingfishers. GTABase says that when the mission wraps up, Lucia calls Jason to tell him she is being released from prison early. Her release is covered in our Coming Home walkthrough.

FAQ
Who is Tommy in GTA 6?
Tommy is Lucia’s son, a baby. PowerPyx and GTABase say the Prologue reveals she left him at a coffee shop before the Payday Loan robbery that got her arrested.
What happens to Tommy after Lucia is arrested?
He is placed in child welfare. Both guides say the coffee shop staff had already reported him as abandoned, and Lucia’s aim becomes getting out of prison so she can be with him again.
Can you escape the police in the GTA 6 Prologue?
No. The chase is scripted, and the game returns you to the counselor’s office by itself, so there is no escape route to look for.
Can you avoid being arrested at the coffee shop?
No. According to PowerPyx, Lucia is arrested automatically once she gets close, and there is no way to avoid it.
Does the Prologue have any choices?
Neither guide lists any. The only interactions are threatening the teller and a quick time event to break free from a customer.
Does Tommy appear again later?
Yes, by name. Both guides report that the optional necklace errand in Chapter 1 has the chain engraved with Tommy.
