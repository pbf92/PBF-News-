TITLE: GTA 6 might take over 50 hours to complete, and that’s barely scratching the surface
OUTLET: Digital Trends

The clock is ticking, and we’re inching closer to the release date of Grand Theft Auto 6. Releasing on November 19th, GTA 6 has everyone holding their breath in anticipation. While the lack of a proper physical release is a bummer, that’s not stopping people from pre-ordering it to ensure they can pre-download and play from the get-go.
How long will it take to beat the single-player campaign of GTA 6?
In case your curiosity was piqued regarding the length of the full campaign, it has been revealed by the prolific leaker and video game journalist Thomas Henderson. According to the report by Insider Gaming, GTA 6’s main single-player campaign will last you a whopping 50 hours on average, spanning 100 missions with 5 chapters in total.
Taking different playstyles into consideration, of course your playtime will differ from others. Even Rockstar’s own Rob Nelson, head of development and co-studio head, had mentioned to The New York Times in an interview that he had reached the 80-hour mark during his play-through back in February.
How long will it take to complete the entirety of GTA 6?
For the completionists among us, aiming for that Platinum trophy on PlayStation or the Mythic Achievement on Xbox side of things, GTA 6 will take around 200 hours of play-time.
Without sounding like a broken record, of course things will vary in accordance to the randomness of a plethora of variables in the game.
Mind you, these numbers are just for the solo campaign experience and in no shape or form takes the upcoming behemoth; GTA Online into consideration. Not everyone is into the multiplayer aspect of GTA Online, but it’s hard to not ignore it being the major cash cow for Rockstar. So much so that they cancelled single-player DLCs planned for GTA 5 in favour of GTA Online.
Priced at $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Deluxe Edition; rest assured, you’ll be relishing the gameplay for hours and hours to come.
