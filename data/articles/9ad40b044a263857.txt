TITLE: GTA 6's headphone feature was originally planned for GTA: Vice City back in 2002, former Rockstar developer reveals
OUTLET: TweakTown

GTA 6 is full of small touches that make the world feel real. You can push your car when it runs out of gas, and you can open the door for Lucia. One of those touches is the headphones mechanic. Music used to live in your car radio and stop the moment you got out, but now you can put in a pair of headphones and take it with you.

Turns out, that feature could have been part of GTA more than two decades ago. Former Rockstar tech lead Obbe Vermeij says the studio planned a headphones feature for Vice City in 2002. He posted on X that it "didn't happen for some technical reason I can't remember," and attached the pickup icon the team made for it.
Vermeij's post was a reply to a viral post praising the headphones in GTA 6 and how much they add to immersion. He worked at Rockstar from 1995 to 2009, so he was there for Vice City. He doesn't remember why it got cut, so nothing is confirmed, though fans are guessing. He also clarified that it was meant to be a portable radio, rather than a Walkman-style player that would let you pick songs. That's worth noting, since GTA 6 doesn't let you pick your own tracks either.
In GTA 6, you can put in earbuds or headphones and keep listening to the radio or podcasts when you step out of the car, and the game plays nicely with combat and exploration.
