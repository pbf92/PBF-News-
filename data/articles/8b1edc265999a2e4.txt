TITLE: 'Things are going backwards, not forwards'. EU envoy responds to persecution report
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

A European special envoy says a report into persecuted Christians should prompt urgent action on religious freedom.
Aid to the Church in Need's (ACN) Persecuted and Forgotten? report examined 18 countries and found persecution had worsened in ten.
Speaking at the Irish launch, Mairead McGuinness promised to raise her voice in the halls of power wherever she can.
“This topic is one where the voices need to be lifted,” she said.
“So that the issue of freedom of religion and belief is registered here in Europe and people understand that it’s a big problem, and people do everything they can to defend that right.”
McGinness is the EU Special Envoy for the Promotion of Freedom of Religion and Belief Outside the European Union.
The report cited cases of persecution in Africa, the Middle East and Asia.
"The scale and severity of Christian persecution today amounts to a human rights catastrophe, one that is worsening in many cases," the authors of the report write.
"That this should come as a surprise to so many reflects the prevailing view in the West which presumes that most Christians are to be found in the northern hemisphere."
"[But] most Christians live in the less wealthy global south and are scattered widely in cultures increasingly hostile to them."
McGuinness thanked ACN for making the plight of persecuted Christians known.
“That’s my job. To promote this basic human right anywhere and everywhere I can. To listen to the voices and horrible experiences of the many around the world who are not free,” she said in an interview at the launch.
“And to hope that someday we can make a real difference.”
