Dow Plunges 700 Points as S&P 500 and Nasdaq Fall Ahead of Nvidia Earnings
Wall Street stocks came under pressure Tuesday as another wave of selling in semiconductor shares dragged major indexes lower, with investors awaiting Nvidia's NVDA quarterly results.
The Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped about 730 points, or 1.4%, while the S&P 500 fell 1.6% and the Nasdaq Composite lost 1.7%.
Nvidia shares declined more than 3%, extending a recent downturn and adding pressure across the chip sector. Micron Technology MU led semiconductor losses, falling roughly 6%.
The weakness comes as investors reassess the outlook for AI-related stocks ahead of Nvidia's earnings report. The chipmaker's shares have fallen sharply this month, adding to uncertainty around the broader semiconductor trade.
Economic data offered a mixed backdrop. U.S. retail sales increased 0.2% in October to $748.2 billion, matching expectations. Sales excluding automobiles and gasoline rose 0.6%, ahead of the 0.4% forecast, according to the Commerce Department.
