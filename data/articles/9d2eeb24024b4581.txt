TITLE: Schmeichel warns Manchester United face relegation as Pochettino acknowledges reality
OUTLET: Chosunbiz

"Manchester United are a team that would not look out of place being relegated right now."
Peter Schmeichel, Manchester United's legendary goalkeeper, criticized his former team's shocking slump and raised the possibility of relegation. New manager Mauricio Pochettino also acknowledged the situation.
British outlet Football365 reported on the 23rd (Korea time), "Peter Schmeichel warned Manchester United about relegation after its 0-4 loss to Liverpool. Pochettino also gave his response."
Manchester United suffered a crushing 0-4 defeat to Liverpool in the 12th round of the 2026-27 English Premier League season on the 22nd.
It was Pochettino's first match in charge of Manchester United. Appointed on the 13th to replace Michael Carrick, Pochettino faced the club's biggest rival in his first game, but the result was disastrous.
Things went wrong from the start. In the eighth minute, Leny Yoro was sent off for bringing down Alexander Isak as he charged toward goal. Down a man, Manchester United conceded the opening goal on a free kick from Dominik Szoboszlai in the 10th minute.
Just three minutes later, the team conceded another goal to Florian Wirtz. Manchester United went into halftime trailing 0-2, then fell apart in the second half, conceding in quick succession to Rio Ngumoha and Victor Muñoz.
Liverpool created several more chances to score. Manchester United could consider itself fortunate to have lost by only four goals.
The defeat left Manchester United in 19th place in the Premier League. The team is trying to climb out of the relegation zone, but its first match after changing managers ended in a heavy defeat.
After the match, Schmeichel gave a blunt assessment of Manchester United's situation on Viaplay.
He said, "Realistically, wouldn't it be acceptable if Manchester United finished the season 10th or 12th? I know it sounds ridiculous even to say that. But we're in the relegation zone right now."
He added, "Manchester United really looks like a team that could be relegated right now. If so, the goal now has to be avoiding relegation."
Schmeichel stressed that what happens next matters more than the team's current position.
"We've all played in the Premier League. It's November, and December is just around the corner. March is when it really becomes a problem," he said, warning, "If performances like this continue, there will be a serious problem."
Schmeichel still had faith in Pochettino. "At least I believe Pochettino can fix these problems. But with the players he has now, he won't be able to build a team capable of winning the title," he said.
Pochettino also did not deny the reality. Asked after the game about the relegation battle, he replied, "The relegation battle? Isn't that our reality right now? We're actually in that position."
He added emphatically, "We have to win games."
He expressed a similar view at the official news conference. "There are still many games left to play. We have to accept reality clearly and take responsibility," Pochettino said.
He added, "Everyone has to pull together. It's a disappointing result. What happened just eight minutes into the game completely wrecked the plan we had prepared," explaining that Yoro's early dismissal changed the course of the match.
Manchester United have changed managers several times since Alex Ferguson retired, but have struggled to find stability.
The club appointed Pochettino this time to turn things around, but it was hit with a shocking 0-4 defeat in his first match.
Manchester United, once a dominant force in the Premier League, now find themselves focused not on the title race but on escaping the relegation zone. Schmeichel, a club legend, has publicly warned of the possibility of relegation, and the current manager has acknowledged that it is a reality.
