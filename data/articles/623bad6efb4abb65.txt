TITLE: ‘GTA 6’ Lucia actor just made her first statement since the game’s launch
OUTLET: NME

Manni L Perez, the actor behind Grand Theft Auto 6 protagonist Lucia Caminos, has spoken publicly about the role for the first time.
In the months leading up to Grand Theft Auto 6’s release, Perez had been rumoured to be the actor behind Lucia. She worked with Rockstar Games on the 2019 ‘Diamond And Casino & Resort’ update for Grand Theft Auto Online, voicing an unnamed blackjack dealer, and in 2025, she said she was working on something “big” but couldn’t reveal any more about it due to “non-disclosure agreements”.
Her role as Lucia was confirmed when she was nominated in the Best Performance category at The Game Awards 2026, the day before Grand Theft Auto 6 launched. Yesterday (November 26) she took to Instagram to reflect on the response to the game.
“Life has been such a whirlwind these past couple of days and I just want to take a moment to say thank you,” she wrote. “Thank you to all the new followers, to all the beautiful messages, and the positive reception the game and my character have received. I’ve dedicated almost a decade bringing Lucia to life. She’s been such a significant part of mine and I am beyond grateful she now gets to be a small part of yours.”
Meanwhile Jason actor Dylan Rourke wrote: “Years witnessing the hardest working and most skilled artisans game making has to offer. We are truly honored to collaborate on this worldwide testament to human creativity, ingenuity, and sheer willpower. So much learned and so much to learn, keep fighting for what you love, stay humble.”
Speaking to Love Magazine (via Insider Gaming) earlier this year, Grand Theft Auto 6 writer Alexa D’Agostino said the goal was to create a “full realised person” with Lucia, who is the first fully-voiced female protagonist in GTA history. “That’s how you get a woman that can stand tall next to the likes of Arthur Morgan [from Red Dead Redemption 2] and [Grand Theft Auto 4’s] Niko Bellic.”
“When it came to writing Lucia, I thought of the women I know,” she added. “My mother was an immigrant who grew up in Newark, New Jersey, close to Lucia’s hometown of Liberty City. I thought about the way she raised me: rowdy, kind, but not to take any shit. I related a lot to Lucia, and writing her came very naturally to me. There are many badass women like her, just as there are many men like Jason. We just focused on capturing that.”
According to the latest reports, Grand Theft Auto 6 sold more than 44.9million copies in its first four days.
