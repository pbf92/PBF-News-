TITLE: Is AppLovin (APP) Poised To Reshape Digital Advertising With A Major Acquisition?
OUTLET: Simply Wall St

AppLovin (NasdaqGS:APP) is in talks to acquire Paris-based adtech peer Criteo in a potential sector reshaping deal.
The discussions center on combining AppLovin’s app-focused advertising tools with Criteo’s broader retail media and web retargeting platform.
Any transaction would join two established digital advertising players with global client bases and distinct data and bidding technologies.
There is more to AppLovin than the potential Criteo acquisition that just hit the headlines. We have also spotted 1 warning sign worth knowing about at AppLovin.

AppLovin runs an artificial intelligence powered advertising platform that helps businesses place and optimize campaigns across mobile apps and other digital channels, so any move it makes to broaden that toolkit can influence how marketers allocate budgets across the wider media industry.
How a Criteo deal would test the AppLovin Narrative
The investment story around AppLovin hinges on whether its AI-powered ad engine can keep turning heavy compute spending and new channels into durable, high-return ad performance, even as legal and competitive pressures build. Talks to buy Criteo plug directly into that bet because they touch both reach and regulatory complexity.
"The main thing that has to go right is that AppLovin continues to turn heavy AI and compute investment into reliable model improvements that sustain strong ROAS for both gaming and consumer advertisers while managing rising legal and competitive pressures..."
If AppLovin folds Criteo’s retail media and web retargeting into its AXON stack, the thesis about broader advertiser adoption across gaming and consumer gets a real-world test. It would push the business from a mobile and CTV focused specialist closer to Meta or Alphabet territory. That could strengthen its data asset but also raise the stakes on execution and integration.
At the same time, absorbing Criteo’s footprint could sharpen existing Narrative risks around privacy rules, lawsuits over AI disclosures, and rivalry with other adtech platforms. The opportunity is a wider funnel of mid-market and ecommerce clients feeding AXON. The challenge is whether added complexity and regulation erode the high-margin profile analysts currently build into their models.
