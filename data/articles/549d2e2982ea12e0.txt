RaveDAO: Supply Unlock Releases 21.38 Million RAVE - May 2027
RaveDAO plans to release 21.38 million RAVE in its May 2027 supply unlock. The release equals 7.78% of the circulating supply and covers tokens allocated to the community, the ecosystem, the foundation, investors and the team.
Once the release takes effect, holders who receive tokens will be able to sell RAVE that was previously locked. That increases the amount those holders have available to sell.
