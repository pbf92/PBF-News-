TITLE: ‘Red Dead Redemption’ Hollywood adaptation on the table, reports claim
OUTLET: NME

Rockstar Games is “more open” to teaming up with someone to make a Red Dead Redemption adaptation, claims a new report.
In 2024, Rockstar co-founder Dan Houser claimed the reason why no Grand Theft Auto or Red Dead Redemption adaptation had been made was because the studio wouldn’t have full creative control. “The risk never made sense. In those days, the perception was that games made poor-quality movies,” he told The Ankler. “It’s a different time now,” he added, with celebrated adaptations of The Last Of Us and Fallout.
According to a new report from The Ankler, Rockstar is now “more open” to bringing Red Dead Redemption to film or television, but it’ll be an expensive project. “At one point, they were throwing around a purchase price of more than $100 million minimum for licensing rights,” an agent who works with writers in the video game space told the outlet.
And if any potential studios are looking for an actor to take on the role of Red Dead Redemption protagonist Arthur Morgan, Jeffrey Dean Morgan has said he’d do it in a “heartbeat”.
The same report claims that Rockstar is still not interested in an adaptation of Grand Theft Auto “despite years of incoming calls from entertainment companies eager to adapt the blockbuster 30-year franchise.”
Grand Theft Auto 6 was released last week (November 19) and racked up sales of more than $3.8billion in its first 24 hours, making it the biggest entertainment launch in history.
In a rare public statement, Rockstar Games’ boss Sam Houser said the game developers “tried to create an experience that will exceed your expectations while showing what this medium is capable of, and why we believe games are so special.”
“Rockstar Games was founded on the belief that games could achieve the same distinction as all other forms of storytelling, become essential to modern culture, and to be thought of as stories and worlds that live on with all of us,” he continued. “Whether you can trace your journey to this game through the entire series or are experiencing Grand Theft Auto for the first time, this is a narrative set in a world full of possibilities. We hope it felt both familiar and entirely new, and above all, we hope that you had fun.”
While many fans are expecting the game studio to return to Red Dead Redemption for their next project, one report suggests they might be working on a sequel to 2017 crime thriller L.A. Noire. Whatever does come next, Rockstar have promised it won’t take the eight years it took between Red Dead Redemption 2 and Grand Theft Auto 6.
