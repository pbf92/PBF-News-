TITLE: Missouri City dad finds missing download code in sealed GTA 6 copy, warns other buyers
OUTLET: Click2Houston

MISSOURI CITY, Texas – A Missouri City father is warning other Grand Theft Auto 6 buyers to open their physical copies right away, after discovering the download code inside his sealed game was missing.
Jamar Walker ordered GTA 6 from a Walmart in Missouri City as an early Christmas gift for himself and his son to share. He specifically wanted a physical copy of the long-awaited game, which he purchased for about $86.
“I could have gotten it online on the PS5 website, but I actually wanted a physical, just something showing that it was a GTA 6,” Walker said.
When the sealed copy arrived at his home and he opened it, the download code was simply not there, only a blank gray area where the code should have been.
“I don’t see it. So I’m trying to scratch the little area where the code is supposed to be at and nothing’s coming. I’m like, ‘I think I got scammed,’” Walker said.
Walker turned to social media and quickly discovered he was not alone. TikTok videos began circulating from other buyers who had the same experience, some furious, some confused.
“Sony and Rockstar Games, count your days. You will not believe this, they sold me a game with no code in it,” one TikTok user said.
Others noted the problem was not limited to PlayStation.
“The no-code issue with physical GTA 6 copies isn’t limited to PlayStation. Xbox users are now reporting the same problem,” another TikTok video stated.
The issue was not limited to Walmart either. Some buyers reported purchasing codeless copies from Best Buy and being denied reimbursement.
Walker drove back to Walmart, where he spent about an hour at customer service before getting his money back. He later downloaded the game directly through PlayStation 5 and says that is the safer option for now.
“If you got a copy and you have it opening or you’re trying to save it or wait, I would say open it now and make sure that code is in there so the same thing doesn’t happen to you,” Walker said.
It remains unclear how or why some physical GTA 6 copies are missing their download codes. KPRC 2 News reached out to Rockstar Games with questions and is awaiting a response.
