TITLE: "I would not be surprised if Anthropic's market cap reached $5 trillion in the not-too-distant future"
OUTLET: CTech

"I would not be surprised if Anthropic's market cap reached $5 trillion in the not-too-distant future"
Notable Capital invested in Anthropic at a $61.5 billion valuation in March 2025, before the launch of Claude. Managing partner Oren Yunger says the firm's stake, initially worth tens of millions of dollars, is now valued at approximately $1 billion, and that he has no intention of selling.
Among the Israelis benefiting from Anthropic's rise is Oren Yunger, a managing partner at San Francisco-based U.S. venture capital firm Notable Capital. The firm invested in Anthropic's Series E round in March 2025, when the AI company raised $3.5 billion at a post-money valuation of $61.5 billion. Notable is estimated to have invested tens of millions of dollars, a stake that is now valued at approximately $1 billion. Recent market turbulence has not shaken Yunger's conviction in the company.
"At the time, the company had an annual revenue run rate of approximately $900 million, with projections to reach $12 billion by 2027. In reality, by the end of October 2026, its annual revenue run rate had reached approximately $81 billion," Yunger said. "When we invested in Anthropic, OpenAI was the market leader. The investment took place before the launch of Claude and Claude Cowork."
Why did you decide to invest at that point?
"We looked at Anthropic's Sonnet 3.5 model. An upgraded version was launched in October 2024, featuring significant improvements, particularly in coding capabilities. Coding was already its strong suit, and we saw the impact this had on the market. Many developers chose to use it independently. We viewed this as an indication that enterprise companies would also adopt Anthropic's models, and that the company could potentially become a global leader in the field.
"We had previously invested in companies that grew through a bottom-up approach, such as HashiCorp, Slack, Vercel and Neon. When we analyzed Anthropic's activity, we recognized that it was following a similar path."
What were the main risks you identified when evaluating the investment?
"It was a challenging round. It took place against the backdrop of the launch of China's open-source model DeepSeek-R1 in January 2025. At the time, there was growing market discussion suggesting that models would become significantly cheaper and that the costs of using them would plummet. However, we believed Anthropic's models would remain highly significant. We thought that if developers adopted its models, that adoption could continue to grow through its own momentum.
"We have an investment memo from that period projecting that the company would reach $12 billion in revenue by 2027, based on estimates provided by Anthropic. Even a prophet could not have imagined that the company's future would turn out to be this bright."
How do you view the company's future?
"Leading publicly traded software companies, particularly those with high growth rates, command premium valuations in the capital markets, with revenue multiples reaching 20 to 40 times or even higher. For instance, CrowdStrike, Palantir and Cloudflare have a combined market capitalization of approximately $728 billion, against combined annual revenue of about $15.4 billion, implying a weighted revenue multiple of roughly 47 times.
"Given such multiples, I would not be surprised to see Anthropic's market capitalization reach $4 trillion to $5 trillion in the not-too-distant future. We are not selling our shares at this time and remain very bullish on the stock. We are witnessing a truly exciting development that has surpassed all our forecasts."
