TITLE: The Shrek Toaster Is the Only Black Friday Deal You Need
OUTLET: VGC / IGN

Do you ever throw some bread in the toaster and think to yourself, "Wow, this toast would taste better with Shrek’s face on it?"
Well, Amazon’s Black Friday deals can make your questionable culinary dream a reality. Uncanny Brands produces a line of retro TV-themed toasters that burn images straight onto your bread. For the season of deals, Amazon gave most of these quirky (and, somehow, officially licensed) toasters a 15% discount, bringing them to their lowest-ever prices.
What Is Going on With These Toasters
Amazon’s discount knocks around $7 off the price of each toaster in these lightning deals, which will disappear once they’re fully claimed. For whatever reason, that seems to be happening surprisingly fast.
The list of themed toasters is about as weird as the concept itself. We’ve got Shrek, of course, but you can also sear into your bread images of Bluey, Sonic, Snoopy, and, um, Bob Ross? Uncanny Valley also produces a Grinch toaster, but that one isn't getting a discount. Stingy guy, I guess.
According to the official listing, the two-slot toaster features an adjustable thermostat with defrost, quick stop, and reheat functions. Each toaster is emblazoned with an image, dedicated to whatever weird property it prints on bread, in a light-up frame.
I’m going to be honest, I can’t vouch for the quality of the Shrek toaster myself. I use the cheapest possible toaster option from Amazon and have been pretty satisfied with it. Still, maybe I’m not quite as satisfied as users of the Shrek-ster:
"I love this toaster," says the most-upvoted review. "I like that it lights up when you are making toast." Very insightful, I know.
The most legit-looking reviews note that you have to do a little experimentation to find the right settings for maximum image effect, and sometimes that means straight-up burning the bread. Apparently you might also notice a weird smell the first time you use it. Yum!
We're Keeping Track of Normal Black Friday Deals, Too
We’re in the thick of Black Friday season. For the Shrek-maxxers, you can probably find the movie in Amazon’s DVD and Blu-ray deals, while Dreamworks home Peacock is one of the many services offering a Black Friday streaming discount. Even LEGO Shrek is on sale in the mess of LEGO deals.
