TITLE: Federal Reserve Board issues enforcement action with former employee of First Security Bancorp
OUTLET: Federal Reserve

November 19, 2026
Federal Reserve Board issues enforcement action with former employee of First Security Bancorp
For release at 11:00 a.m. EST
The Federal Reserve Board on Thursday announced the execution of the enforcement action listed below:
Consent Cease-and-Desist Order against Rush F. Harding III
Former employee of First Security Bancorp
Breach of fiduciary duty and violation of Regulation W
