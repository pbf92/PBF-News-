TITLE: I Didn’t Expect GTA 6 To Be The Game That Tries To De-Toxify Toxic Masculinity
OUTLET: Kotaku

GTA 6 just might cure the male loneliness epidemic. Wait, why are you laughing? I’m being dead serious. Much has been made about how a generation of young men have been getting their talking points and relationship advice from misogynist incel-fluencers—the Andrew Tates and Asmongolds of the world. And though there are a growing crop of dudes offering healthier alternatives, GTA 6 might prove a useful tool toward that goal.
There are moments early in the game where the player, as Jason, is prompted to do something nice for Lucia like buying her flowers or a piece of jewelry. These are optional quests designed for little more than reinforcing the relationship the two have. It’s this element, the game’s continued emphasis on the building and maintenance of the relationship between Jason and Lucia and how it’s a core part of the game, that has the potential to change hearts and minds.
This is such a simple clip, but in it you can see the game influence a pattern of thinking that many, many women wish their partners could have in real life. “What if this isn’t her favorite color?” the streamer asks as Jason paints a wall in his house. “Do we even know what her favorite color is?” That right there, the mere consideration of another person’s thoughts or feelings or preferences, is such a simple component of maintaining a healthy relationship that so many people unconsciously get wrong, especially younger folks.
I understand it is incredibly simplistic and absurd to think a video game of all things—and GTA of all video games!!—could be the thing to detoxify masculinity. I know. But aren’t positive role models who exhibit the behavior we want to see one of our tools for combating those social ills? GTA 6 has gamified the action of being a good partner and because it is just so big, and in the hands of so many young people, there is a chance that for some of them, this could rub off in real life. Combine that with the growing isolation, diminishment, and imprisonment(!) of reactionary chuds, and Rockstar could do some good work here.
