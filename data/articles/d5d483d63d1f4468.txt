Boxlight Disclosed Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard
On November 18, 2026, Nasdaq notified Boxlight that it failed to meet Listing Rule 5550(b)(1), which requires at least $2.5 million in stockholders' equity; its latest reported equity was $1.917 million, and it did not meet either alternative listing standard. Boxlight intends to appeal by the November 25 deadline; a timely hearing request will stay suspension, while trading could be suspended November 30 if it does not appeal. Its proposed compliance plan includes anticipated proceeds from a $2 million financing tranche and an equity line of credit of up to $15 million, subject to conditions.
Original SEC Filing: Boxlight Corp [ BOXL ] - 8-K - Nov. 20, 2026
