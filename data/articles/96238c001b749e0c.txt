Suncrete Director Purchased Shares Worth Over $731K
David Rees-Jones, a director, reported purchasing 100,000 shares of Suncrete Class A Common Stock through the David G Rees-Jones 2012 Family Trust on November 17, 2026, at a weighted average price of $6.20 per share, and 16,470 shares through the trust on November 18, 2026, at a weighted average price of $6.75 per share. The total purchase amount was $731,173. Rees-Jones is the trust’s co-trustee and sole beneficiary and disclaims beneficial ownership except to the extent of his pecuniary interest. Following the transactions, the trust held 116,470 shares.
