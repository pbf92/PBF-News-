TITLE: Anthropic rolls out “Install as app” on Mac and PC for Claude’s mini-app Artifacts.
OUTLET: The Verge

Similar to browser-based web apps, Anthropic says individual app installation for Claude’s interactive Artifacts is now rolling out in beta on the desktop.
The installed apps have their own toolbar icons and can be set up to either work on default permissions just like they would in a browser tab, with app permissions to support sending notifications, intercepting shortcuts, and opening over other programs, or they can be given full computer access.
