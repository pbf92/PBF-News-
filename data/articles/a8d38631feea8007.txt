Authorities complete 2026 review of Payments Memorandum of Understanding
The Bank of England (the Bank), Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) and Payment Systems Regulator (PSR) have completed their annual review of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on payments.
The Authorities have considered the effectiveness and efficiency of their coordination under the MoU. This includes feedback from industry and regulated firms to determine whether the MoU’s principles of coordination and cooperation continue to be met in practice.
The review found that cooperation and coordination remain effective, with respondents welcoming tangible progress in how the Authorities work together. Examples of this include collaborating on a range of developments, including the future regime for dual regulated stablecoin issuers and other innovations in payments, including the work of the Payments Vision Delivery Committee.
Following the review, the Authorities have concluded that no changes to the MoU are needed at this time.
The Authorities have provided further updates to the information detailing each Authority’s regulatory remit and will continue working together to identify areas where greater coordination would benefit the industry.
This will help firms navigate the regulatory landscape and support a more coordinated and effective approach to payments regulation. This approach is further embedded through the Government’s decision to consolidate the PSR into the FCA, meaning greater coordination, clearer responsibilities and a simpler experience for firms.
