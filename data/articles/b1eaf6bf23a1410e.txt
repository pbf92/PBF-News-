TITLE: UFO location and alien encounter walkthrough in GTA 6
OUTLET: Polygon.com

The Grand Theft Auto 6 UFO alien abduction encounter is the beginning of a very peculiar optional mission, which will eventually have you gallivanting all of Leonida in search of a mysterious building. It all starts when you investigate an abandoned car at the side of the road, but this can happen in multiple different locations, so you need to know where to go, and where to find the building shown to you in a dream after that.
Here's a complete walkthrough for the GTA 6 UFO encounter, including where to find the abandoned car, how to get through the dream sequence, and where to find the mysterious building near the radio tower. Note that we're keeping this guide as spoiler-free as possible.
Where to find the UFO alien abduction location in GTA 6
Marked on the map above with star icons are two locations you can find the GTA 6 UFO encounter, although reports online state that it can be found elsewhere around Kelly County too. It can only happen at night, after around 10 p.m. or so.
When you approach each of these locations, you're looking for a red abandoned car, with both front doors and trunk open, and its headlights flashing. Get out of your car and investigate to find a trail of clothes leading away from the vehicle. Equip your flashlight because it'll be quite dark, and eventually you'll see a bright white light in the distance. Go towards this and voila! You've encountered your first UFO.
After you walk towards the light, a cutscene will play out. You're then tasked with retrieving the abandoned car, but you'll pass out before you get there. Spooky.
How to start the UFO dream sequence in GTA 6
The next part of the UFO encounter comes when you go to sleep. Your character will have a dream where they're exploring the inside of a UFO. For the most part, this is fairly linear, so we'll leave you to discover it yourself, but there are a few things to note:
- Don't worry if you crack any of the eggs, as you can try this section over and over.
- You'll be shown a four-digit code, represented by horizontal lines above a keypad. Remember your code, because you'll need it later. (We would provide you with the answer here, but the code differs from player to player.)
- On the control panel, you have four buttons to press (numbered one through four from left to right), and your goal is to make them all turn green. Press them in the following order: 3-2-4-4-3-2-2-1.
- When you're presented with three doors, go through the leftmost door first, collect the item, then go through the door on the right. Do not enter the middle door, as it will kill you.
After that, the dream will end. Your next task will be to find the mysterious building depicted on planet Earth in the planetarium room.
Where to find the mysterious UFO building location in GTA 6
Even though the first UFO encounter happens in Port Gellhorn, the UFO dream house location is down south near Key Lento, where GTA 6 began. Specifically, it's directly north of Gambit Bay and south of Hamlet, a stone's throw northeast of the airstrip that is running north-to-south and is visible on the map.
If you approach by road, you'll see a brown sign for the Grassrivers Pelican Valley recreation area. Take a left and go through to Southpoint airfield, ignoring the signs for the restricted area. The gate is locked, but you can get through by running around to the left and jumping over part of the chainlink fence that doesn't have any barbed wire at the top. Go around to the other side of the building to find the front entrance.
From here, you're back reliving the dream you encountered, except everything is somewhat decrepit. It will all look familiar to you, which means you'll need the same code you entered in the dream, when you reach that point. When you eventually insert the item into the machine, aka at the point the dream ended, pick up the thing that gets ejected from the machine and get the heck outta there.
