TITLE: The weather system in GTA 6 is the most advanced in Rockstar's history.
OUTLET: GameGPU

Rockstar Games, in collaboration with The Weather Channel, unveiled a series of new footage from Grand Theft Auto 6 for PlayStation 5, showcasing the updated weather system. The developers confirmed they have abandoned the fixed weather presets of previous games in favor of a fully dynamic and localized atmospheric simulation. In the state of Leonidas, weather conditions now generate independently in different parts of the map, allowing players to observe an approaching storm front from afar while still being in a sunny area.
The new images demonstrate a wide range of climate effects, including clear skies, towering cumulonimbus clouds, dense sea fog, high humidity with low-lying haze, tropical storms, and heavy downpours with gusty winds. According to the studio, this system is the most advanced in the history of their projects. After the rain, puddles and wet surfaces dry at different rates, and in marshy areas like Pelican Valley, storms significantly reduce water clarity, making fishing and hunting difficult.
Weather conditions directly affect the world's physics, NPC behavior, and vehicle control. Strong winds and rough seas alter boat behavior, forcing players to take the coastline's terrain into account to protect themselves from the waves, while flying planes or helicopters through storms results in severe turbulence and minor vehicle damage. During heavy rain, visibility on roads significantly decreases, prompting the use of reflectors on highways and pedestrians seeking shelter from the elements.
