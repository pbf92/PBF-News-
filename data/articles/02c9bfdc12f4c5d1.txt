Mauricio Pochettino delivers defiant message after disastrous start to Man United reign
- Mauricio Pochettino insists Manchester United remain a “strong team” capable of reversing their current slump, despite suffering a disastrous 4-0 defeat to Liverpool in his first game in charge.
- The heavy defeat leaves Manchester United second from bottom in the Premier League after 11 fixtures, ahead of only Crystal Palace, as they prepare for their upcoming Champions League match against Sporting in Lisbon.
- Pochettino highlighted United's third-place finish last season as proof of the squad's underlying quality, expressing confidence that they will build a strong identity and turn their fortunes around.
- Captain Bruno Fernandes has put contract extension talks on hold, insisting that restoring the team to winning ways is far more important than discussing his personal future at the club.
- Fernandes, who is into the final year of his current deal, remains focused on helping Manchester United climb the table and achieving further success in this season's Champions League.
