TITLE: Nasdaq Resumes Selloff as Nvidia, Anthropic Shares Slump
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Tech stocks are stumbling again, as fears around the AI trade creep back in a day after Jensen Huang's return at Nvidia sparked the best day for U.S. stocks since April 2025.
The Nasdaq fell more than 1%; Anthropic shares slid about 8%, dropping below their IPO price, and Nvidia is also giving up a chunk of yesterday's record gains.

Huang's return could support the AI trade, for instance as a signal of the Trump administration's conviction that Nvidia's chip sales are crucial for the U.S. economy. But it doesn't resolve the big uncertainties: whether the pace of data-center spending can be maintained, and whether the investment will pay off.
The reinstated Nvidia chief on Tuesday told staff that he is in therapy and apologized for violating the company's code of conduct.
Aside from the AI trade, today brings a busy slate of earnings from Dell, Analog Devices, Autodesk and others, and economic data releases including home-price and consumer-confidence readings.
