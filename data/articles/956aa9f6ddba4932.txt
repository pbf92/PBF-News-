Missed GTA 6’s Vintage Vice City bonus? Rockstar is now selling the pack separately for $4.99
If you missed the early-purchase window for GTA 6’s Vintage Vice City Pack, you can now buy it separately. The add-on is listed for $4.99 on both the US PlayStation Store and Xbox Store, and it brings a classic car, a garage and retro looks for Jason and Lucia.
Rockstar originally offered the pack with purchases made before November 20. So, before paying again, I recommend checking your game library and add-ons if you bought early: you may already have it.
Both the PlayStation listing and the Xbox listing describe this as additional content. You need GTA 6 itself to use it; the $4.99 purchase is not a copy of the game.
The pack includes a classic car and a garage
The centrepiece is a two-tone ’55 Vapid Stanier, along with the Shore Court garage near Ocean Beach. The store descriptions say the garage includes a weapon locker and a secure place to deposit stolen goods for fencing.
There are also period outfits and hairstyles for both protagonists: a pastel linen suit for Jason and a red sequin dress for Lucia. A tropical weapon pattern takes inspiration from the palm-tree shirt worn by Tommy Vercetti in the original GTA: Vice City.
Those items do not necessarily appear the moment you start a new save. Rockstar says they become available as Jason and Lucia’s story progresses, so check your progress and the game’s menus before assuming something is missing.
Check your existing purchase before buying twice
The standalone price is useful if you bought the game after the bonus window or missed the original promotion. It is less useful if the pack is already tied to your account. Look at your installed add-ons or your platform’s library first, particularly if someone else helped set up your copy.
The store pages list the pack’s car, garage, appearance items and weapon pattern. They do not list the separate GTA+ trial that accompanied eligible digital early purchases, so do not assume the $4.99 add-on includes a subscription.
Prices and availability can vary by country. The figures above are from the US stores, and the pack needs a compatible copy of GTA 6 on the corresponding platform.
If you are still looking for the base game on PS5, Amazon lists a boxed download-code edition. There is no disc in that box, and the code requires a PlayStation account registered in the US or Canada.
