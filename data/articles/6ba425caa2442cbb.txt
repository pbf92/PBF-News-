TITLE: Second Tommy Robinson 'put Christ back into Christmas' carols announced
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

A second ‘Unite the Kingdom’ Christmas carols event has been announced by Tommy Robinson.
The controversial activist has encouraged supporters to “put Christ back into Christmas” at the event on 12 December in London, at a venue yet to be confirmed.
Around 1,000 people attended the first carols event last December. It came as a follow-up to the Unite the Kingdom rally in September 2025, organised by the former English Defence League leader, which drew 150,000 attendees according to some estimates.
A line-up of speakers includes Rikki Doolan, the minister who told Premier Christian News in 2025 that he led Robinson to Christ while the activist was in prison. Others include Merlin Kraft of BibleLadUK, who has organised mass baptisms in the sea, and Laurence Fox, actor and leader of the right-wing Reclaim Party.
Robinson was found guilty of harassing journalists, by a jury at Southwark Crown Court last month. He is back in court on 16 December for sentencing; the judge stating that all options were being considered, including a custodial sentence. Robinson has described the carols as “my farewell event” in response.
A number of church leaders challenged the use of “put Christ back into Christmas” as a slogan last year, following on from criticism of Christian symbols being used during the Unite the Kingdom rally.
Christian group Better Story will be gathering on the steps of St Martin-in-the-Fields by Trafalgar Square on the morning of Robinson’s event, “to remember that Christmas is an act of divine solidarity with the stranger and the outcast.”
