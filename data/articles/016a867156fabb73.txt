Net Power Extends Deadline to Defer $3.5 Million Saulsbury Payment to Dec. 4
Net Power's subsidiary entered into an amendment with Saulsbury extending the deadline to execute proposed EPC changes and a limited notice to proceed, and make related payments, from Nov. 20 to Dec. 4, 2026. Saulsbury confirmed that the $3.5 million termination payment remains deferred and has not been demanded. The extension gives the parties more time to negotiate project terms; Net Power has not entered into a binding power offtake agreement with the prospective customer.
Agreement details:
- Agreement type: Amendment to agreement extending the FNTP date
- Counterparty: Saulsbury Industries
- Signed / Effective: Nov 22 2026 / Oct 15 2026
- Duration / Termination: Through Dec 04 2026
- Reason: Allow more time to finalize EPC changes and limited notice-to-proceed payments
Original SEC Filing: Net Power Inc. [ NPWR ] - 8-K - Nov. 23, 2026
