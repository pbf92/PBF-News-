TITLE: Full list of Poundland stores in Greater Manchester as retailer falls into administration
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Full list of Poundland stores in Greater Manchester as retailer falls into administration
There are a total of 14 Poundland stores across the region
High street chain Poundland has fallen into administration, putting around 11,000 jobs at risk across the UK.
The retailer has been holding talks over the past month to find a buyer, but was unable to reach any deal by today's deadline (Wednesday, November 25).
AlixPartners has been appointed to handle Poundland’s administration, and the retailer will continue to trade from its stores as the administrators seek a sale of the company and its assets.
A spokesperson for AlixPartners said: “We appreciate this is a deeply concerning time for all involved – customers, suppliers and, in particular, Poundland colleagues.
“Our priority now is to work with all stakeholders, including representatives from government and USDAW, as we identify the best possible route forward.

“We would encourage anybody interested in acquiring the business in part or as a whole, as well as UK retail operators with open employment opportunities, to contact the administrators.”
Poundland’s owner Gordon Brothers said: “First and foremost, our thoughts are with Poundland colleagues. We recognise how difficult the continued uncertainty is for them.
“Efforts to preserve as many jobs and stores as possible will continue, led by the administrators.”
The firm insisted it had “explored every avenue to secure a sale of the whole company”, but said the chain had “faced several years of instability”.
It said: “Supported by Gordon Brothers, the management team and colleagues at Poundland worked hard over the past 12 months to improve the underlying performance and achieve £100 million of cost savings.

“Despite a reduced cost base, looking forward, the business is still vulnerable to inflationary pressures or any downturn in sales and margin.
“We recognise and we are thankful for all the hard work and effort by management and colleagues throughout.”
No final decision has yet been made on stores, which will remain open for the time being. In total, there are 14 Poundland stores across Greater Manchester according to their website, as of Wednesday afternoon.
