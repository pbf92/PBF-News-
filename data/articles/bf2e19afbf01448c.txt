TITLE: GTA 6 players can now claim a free gift as Rockstar celebrates monumental launch
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Grand Theft Auto VI has completed its worldwide rollout, with millions of people across the globe now exploring the full breadth of Leonida.
While we’re yet to get official numbers, it’s safe to say that Rockstar Games’ latest release has broken about every record going, with Sensor Tower predicting first day sales of more than 24.5 million units, which is around $2.36 billion in revenue.
This launch has been a long time coming for us fans, and being hands-on with the game can feel surreal at times; if you want to make the most of every opportunity to celebrate GTA VI’s arrival, though, you can also claim a small gift.
As of November 19, you can now download GTA VI’s cover art as a printable poster from Rockstar Games’ website, or you can claim a map of Leonida as a free digital download.
Advert
These free launch gifts aren’t exactly going to blow your socks off, but I’m sure there are crafty folks out there that’ll put them to good use.
Heck, you could simply set the Leonida map as your desktop background and then when you’re working, you can plan your next excursion.
Again, you can download / print these both from Rockstar’s own website, or there is a way you can actually secure an officially printed version of the poster, but you will have to splash a tiny bit of cash.
If you want a double-sided poster featuring GTA VI’s cover art, you can now also order one of these from the Rockstar Games store for a measly $0.99, although there’s probably a delivery charge on top of that.
Still, considering how expensive some of GTA VI’s merch is, this could be a nice cost-effective memento to mark your time in Leonida.

GTA 6 is the joint highest-rated game of all-time, securing a GOTY nomination pre-launch
GTA VI is flying. It was always clear that it was a hit with critics given that GTA VI secured a GOTY nomination at this year’s The Game Awards ahead of its review embargo dropping.
That review embargo then dropped several days later to reveal a Metascore of 99, placing GTA VI as the joint highest-rated game ever, alongside 1998’s original The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
Here at GAMINGbible in our GTA VI review, we awarded the game a score of 10, writing, “My 70-plus hours with GTA VI are far too memorable to summarise succinctly, but to attempt to put it as briefly as possible, they were sublime.”
It continued: “So much has changed since I enjoyed simpler times in Los Santos - and at first, I thought the extent of GTA VI's changes had completely missed the mark, but just because something is different, that doesn’t mean it’s bad - and Rockstar quickly proved that it knew exactly what it was doing. GTA VI cherry-picks some of the best qualities from previous Grand Theft Auto entries and the eternal masterpiece Red Dead Redemption 2 to create a truly impactful video game.”

Despite GTA VI only having been out for a day or so, there’s been no shortage of talking points when it comes to what’s in the game.
The removal of prostitutes, a jet-skiing dog, a GTA+ subscription paywall, and a hilarious hidden Easter egg cutscene have all dominated said early chatter.
And with the weekend practically upon us, the next few days are only going to dredge up new discoveries thick and fast.
