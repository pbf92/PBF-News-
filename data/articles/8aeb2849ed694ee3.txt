TITLE: All 6 GTA 6 Endings, Ranked
OUTLET: Comic Book Resources

Grand Theft Auto VI has been a smash hit for Rockstar Games and could arguably be the title of the year. While there are debates about whether it's the best of the series, what is clear is that players will have to give up a lot of time to be able to complete the title. To 100% finish the game and all of its side challenges, players have to indulge over 200 hours into the experience. To be able to complete the narrative and some of the side quests, they are looking at, at least, 100 hours.
Those are some brutal numbers, but it shows just how much attention to detail has been put into Grand Theft Auto 6. What's interesting is that players are still finding new endings to the game, as they make alternative choices. Those finales can essentially be narrowed down into six ending types at the time of writing. However, with new aspects of the game always being discovered, players shouldn't be shocked if there are additional options available when they get to the ending themselves. The fun is trying to walk a path that no one else has gone down before.
The Solo Variants Offer Some Hope
Because there are so many paths that players can take, there are a couple of alternative options that all end with the same solo story. That either Lucia or Jason survive and must raise their son by themselves. In these scenarios, one of them will be killed, based on the deals that have been struck, the allies and enemies made and ultimately, how well the player navigates this complex criminal underworld. It's a bleak scenario, but one that offers some hope.
At the very least, one of the characters gets somewhat of a happy ending, living to see another day and ensuring that their son can be raised in a quieter neighborhood without the sins of the past there to haunt them. It's an ending that can also vary, based on whether the player is arrested instead. Rather than one of Lucia or Jason being killed, they may also be taken into custody, which offers additional support for a future scenario.
These solutions are the least cinematic though and don't offer the excitement of some of the other options. It feels disappointing to actually get arrested in the end, while creating a situation where one of the characters is left alone and doesn't provide the feel-good factor players are looking for
Swimming to Safety Ends In Disaster
There are some explosive, cinematic endings that lead to absolute disaster for Lucia and Jason, and while they may be heartbreaking, they are rather thrilling. For the 'sleeping with the fishes' style ending, players need to be in a clash with the police. They will find themselves trying to leave the scene armed. If they do, both Lucia and Jason will be shot, meaning that custody of their child is given to family and players would have failed to find a happy ending.
Equally, if players attempt to swim away through the body of water, police will continue to fire on them until they are taken out. Again, the custody of the child is passed on to family and neither Lucia nor Jason survive the encounter. Not even through some surprise reveal or fake-out, that's shown after the credits roll. That's it for the dynamic duo. It might have been a fun run, but they go down in flames and gunfire.
It's those late choices, especially on the bridge, that will influence how this plays out, accompanied by reputation. Nonetheless, this is still a disappointing end to the game and one that does not showcase the best of GTA VI and the characterization it offers. For those who want a slightly more niche or nuanced character ending, this is not the path to take. This is, however, a great ode to Bonnie and Clyde and a fitting end for these troublemakers.
The Heroic 'No Surrender' Finishes In Flames
Of course, there is another route to go. One that doesn't mean players are trying to run away. Rather, they face down the enemy head-on and deal with the consequences. This is the no-surrender path and requires players to begin a firefight with their assailants. When cornered by the police at the roadblock on the bridge, Lucia and Jason attempt to shoot their way out of the situation, refusing to escape or sacrifice just one of their party.
What ensues is a pretty brutal finale for the GTA series, where both Lucia and Jason are actually killed in action by the cops. There haven't really been any versions of this play out so far where the characters end up getting arrested. Indeed, it seems violent actions get a violent end and, for players who have created a dangerous reputation for themselves throughout the title, it's no surprise that this is the story's conclusion that they must face.
This is a more exciting finale that gives players a little more agency. They don't just have to give up or give in and can go out the way they might want. However, it still doesn't provide any hope for the future.
The Ultimate Sacrifice Could Occur
For those that want a heroic ending for Jason and still want a protagonist to survive the ordeal, there is the self-sacrifice play to consider. When players are faced with their demise, Jason can trick Lucia into believing that he will jump into the water with her. She jumps, assuming that he is to follow, only for him to stay behind. In this instance, a couple of things could occur, with multiple endings having taken place thus far.
Either Jason is arrested, and the chase is at an end, with the character supporting Lucia from afar, or the ultimate tragedy occurs. Jason is shot down at that moment, after fighting back. Lucia, confused by what has taken place, manages to escape to safety, with Jason causing enough of a distraction. She is able to raise their child, while Jason is removed from the board completely. It's a bold narrative swing, but it's one that paints Jason as a redeemable hero.
Grand Theft Auto 6's self-sacrifice ending is not the most heartwarming and nor does it satisfy those looking for a happy ending. But it is a great, explosive conclusion that lives up to the ambition and the stakes of the title. It's also one that narratively pays off the relationship between Jason and Lucia, making it all the more worthwhile. It's a sensible option for those who want Lucia to fight on another day.
The Swamp Betrayal Takes Place If a Deal Is Struck
Players will be aware that they can make deals in GTA 6 that could change the way they play the game. Striking a deal would seemingly have a great outcome for the player, if there were no betrayals to endure. Sadly, no one can be trusted, and for the swamp scene in particular, no foolproof agreement is enough for Lucia and Jason to get out alive.
For those players who decide to take Officer Méndez's deal, they will find themselves in the swamp, where they are executed. This quiet ending is one that is genuinely dramatic and offers a shocking twist on what players might have been expected. It's not the all-guns-blazing finale that some might be looking for, but it is a great way to bring this story to a close, while offering an insight into the true corruption taking place in the game.
Players who come across this particular ending may feel a sense of satisfaction in seeing such a big story moment play out. It's definitely one that does not reward the risks that the player took in taking the deal, but it is one that ties up a couple of loose threads along the way. Lucia and Jason's son is given to family for custody, allowing the child to have a fresh start though.
The Fresh Start Is GTA 6's True Ending
The truth is, most players want Lucia and Jason to survive, so there's only one conclusion everyone is searching for. That's the option where both of them manage to escape justice and are able to start a fresh life with their son. It's the one true ending, and while it is hard to get there, it's entirely plausible. Many players have already found this solution.
There are a couple of ways to do this in GTA 6, but it often involves one of the characters ending up in prison before they are eventually reunited. A short-term sacrifice can therefore end up with long-term benefits. A good relationship status and low criminal profile will definitely help to make this storyline a reality. Players will also have saved money, up to $2 million, that they can use to fund this new life.
This is definitely the best ending of the game, and one that pays off all the time spent strengthening the relationship between Jason and Lucia. It offers a future for the couple and could even point towards potential sequels down the line. While not everyone will want a happy ending, preferring to see their heroes go out in a blaze of glory, this is the best option for those who have put so much work into nurturing the characters and don't want to see it all thrown away.
