TITLE: Securities and Exchange Commission Proposes Direct Control of Audit Trail
OUTLET: TradingView

The SEC proposed rescinding Rule 613 and requiring SROs and broker-dealers to report substantially the same CAT data to the Commission or its designee. The proposal would use congressional appropriations for CAT costs; comments are open for 60 days after Federal Register publication.
