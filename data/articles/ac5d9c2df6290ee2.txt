TITLE: All the GTA 6 aircraft we've spotted so far
OUTLET: Aerospace Global News

Rockstar Games has finally released Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) after 13 years of development, returning players to Vice City and the fictional state of Leonida. While most will be busy exploring the streets, aviation enthusiasts have another reason to look around: the aircraft.
GTA 6 draws on an eclectic mix of real-world designs. There are Canadian bush planes, Second World War trainers, aerobatic machines and modern helicopters. Rockstar has taken liberties with the details, but several of the inspirations are hard to miss.
The GTA Base vehicle database lists 19 aircraft, including nine aeroplanes, an airship, and nine helicopters.
The Mammoth Dodo (DHC-2 Beaver): A Canadian bush-flying legend
The Mammoth Dodo is inspired by the de Havilland Canada DHC-2 Beaver. This remarkable aircraft has spent nearly eight decades serving Canada’s most remote communities.
First flown on 16 August 1947, the Beaver was designed to operate in places where airports are scarce. It can be fitted with wheels, floats or skis, carrying passengers and supplies into areas that would otherwise be hard to reach.
Its high wing provides useful clearance, with sturdy landing gear and short-field performance.
De Havilland Canada built 1,692 examples of the aircraft, and it’s a familiar sight in bush aviation today.
For plane spotters exploring Leonida, finding Rockstar’s version of this aviation workhorse should be a treat.
The Western Company Duster (Boeing-Stearman Model 75): From trainer to crop duster to movie star
The Western Company Duster, with two stacked wings, open cockpits, a fixed undercarriage and a radial engine, is most likely inspired by the Boeing-Stearman Model 75 Kaydet, which trained thousands of US military pilots during the Second World War.
The Stearman was built to withstand the knocks of elementary flying instruction. Its fabric-covered wings and welded-steel fuselage were straightforward to maintain, although that did not make it effortless to fly. Boeing records 8,584 Kaydets built between 1936 and 1944.
Surplus aircraft found an unlikely second career after the war. Agricultural operators adapted Stearmans for spraying and crop dusting, taking advantage of their rugged construction and low-speed characteristics. Film fans may also recognise the Stearman as the crop-dusting biplane that terrorises Cary Grant in Alfred Hitchcock’s 1959 thriller North by Northwest.
The Mallard (Extra EA-300): Built to fly upside down
GTA Base associates the Mallard with the German-built Extra EA-300, an aerobatic aircraft designed for pilots who enjoy a thrill ride.
The Extra 300’s compact airframe, powerful piston engine and aerobatic wing allow it to perform demanding rolls, loops and inverted manoeuvres.
It’s named after competition pilot Walter Extra, who began developing his own aircraft in the 1980s. The original EA-300 received German type certification in May 1990.
Whether Rockstar’s Mallard matches the real aircraft’s extraordinary manoeuvrability is an open question. Aerospace Global News has not independently checked its in-game flight characteristics. But do let us know if you fly it.
The Buckingham Volatus (Airbus H160): A quieter kind of helicopter
GTA Base says the Buckingham Volatus is inspired by the Airbus H160, whose curved Blue Edge main rotor blades and enclosed Fenestron tail rotor make it easy to recognise.
Airbus developed the swept rotor blades to reduce blade-vortex interaction noise. The shrouded tail rotor also contributes to acoustic performance. The twin-engine H160 was designed for passenger transport, emergency services and offshore operations.
EASA certified the H160 in 2020, and the FAA issued its own certification in 2023.
For now, GTA Base’s Volatus only appears on promotional artwork; it’s unclear where, or even whether, players can fly the helicopter in the released game.
What other aircraft can plane spotters find in GTA VI?
The GTA VI fleet includes designs that can be identified as the Boeing 747, Cessna 172, Citation X, Learjet 45 and de Havilland Canada DHC-6 Twin Otter. Helicopter lookalikes include the Bell 206 LongRanger, MD Helicopters Little Bird, and the Airbus H145.
Some names will be familiar to players of earlier Grand Theft Auto titles. Others have been documented for GTA VI through trailers and promotional material.

Is there a Gee Bee racer in GTA VI?
One aircraft we were hoping to spot is the Gee Bee R-1 Super Sportster, the extraordinary American racing aircraft of the 1930s. Its barrel-shaped body and stubby wings have an almost cartoonish, kawaii charm, although its performance was anything but cute.
The Granville Brothers Aircraft company developed the R-1 in Springfield, Massachusetts, wrapping a short fuselage around a powerful Pratt & Whitney radial engine, with the cockpit set back near the tail.
Jimmy Doolittle won the 1932 Thompson Trophy race in the aircraft, averaging roughly 252 mph (406 km/h), and set a landplane speed record of 296.287 mph (477 km/h) over a measured course.
The Gee Bee could be hard to handle, and its racing history included several serious accidents. Still, it’s an icon of aviation racing, and we would have thought a game that involves chases might have found a spot for it.
