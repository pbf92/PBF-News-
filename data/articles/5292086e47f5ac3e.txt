Luke Shaw has handed Mauricio Pochettino a boost by returning to full training as the Manchester United manager looks to fix his side’s leaky defence.
Sunday’s 4-0 defeat at Liverpool means United have now conceded more goals in all competition, 34, than any other team from Europe’s top five leagues.
Shaw has featured just five times for United this season and his appearance in the 1-1 draw against Tottenham last month is his only match since the start of October.
United failed to sign a back-up left back for Shaw, 31, in the summer and Michael Carrick was dismissed before the international break following a dismal run of form.
Right back Diogo Dalot has struggled when used on the left and Pochettino played Lisandro Martinez there at Anfield, before Leny Yoro’s early red card.
Pochettino was Shaw’s manager when he broke through as a teeanger at Southampton and they have been reunited at Old Trafford 13 years later.
He would be in contention to feature for United as the return to Champions League action in Lisbon against Sporting on Wednesday.
Pochettino will give an update at his pre-match press conference on Tuesday evening, but United remain without Benjamin Sesko and Carlos Baleba.
