TITLE: Fofana has apologised for interview - Alonso
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso says defender Wesley Fofana has apologised for a controversial interview he gave on YouTube.
Fofana has been criticised for comments he made in the interview with YouTuber Zach Nani in which he appeared to joke about his penalty miss in a Carabao Cup shootout defeat at Liverpool.
He did not have permission to hold the two-hour interview, which was aired after Chelsea's humiliating 4-1 defeat at home to rivals Leeds on Saturday, but Alonso said the matter was now "closed".
"It was unfortunate. He has apologised. He knows it was not right, the moment, it was not right, the content," said the Blues boss, who was speaking before Friday's Premier League match at Nottingham Forest.
"We are here to take things seriously. He's said it will not be repeated. We don't want these kind of things and they won't be repeated."
Alonso refused to confirm whether Fofana had been punished by the club, but said "we have addressed that with him."
Fofana's comments followed criticism from fans of his performances this season, including in last weekend's humiliation at the hands of historic rivals Leeds.
But interviewer Nani defended Fofana against the backlash, saying: "People can clearly see he wasn't happy. He admits he didn't play well and says he needs to improve."
The interviewer also defended Fofana's explanation for putting himself forward to take a penalty against Liverpool, saying the defender believed it was his responsibility as one of the senior players on the pitch.
The 25-year-old France defender has often struggled for form and fitness since completing a £70m move from Leicester City in August 2022.
He has made 81 appearances for the club in four years, but has played nine times under new manager Alonso this season.
In that time Chelsea have struggled with defensive frailty, conceding 20 goals in their 11 Premier League games.
They are eighth in the table after falling to a third defeat in their last four league matches.
