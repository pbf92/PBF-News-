Bloom Energy upgraded at BMO as order momentum builds
Bloom Energy BE wobbled between small gains and losses in Thursday's trading as BMO Capital upgraded shares to Outperform from Market Perform with a $230 price target, following recent meetings with CFO Simon Edwards and investor relations VP Michael Tierney.
BMO analyst Ameet Thakkar said he had previously been among the more cautious voices on the company, questioning large-scale proof-of-concept, the durability of a higher-cost generation solution once turbine and grid availability improve, and reliance on U.S. data center demand.
"Those debates remain, but the setup has changed," Thakkar wrote, noting the shares have pulled back and large projects including Nebius are increasingly providing the execution evidence the analyst had sought, and he now believes the risk-reward for investors is more attractive.
Thakkar said BMO's meetings with the Bloom BE executives "provided greater comfort around contractual protections, milestone-based customer payments, project finance structures, manufacturing scalability, and continued cost-down opportunities."
