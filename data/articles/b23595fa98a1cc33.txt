Congressman Discloses First Public Stock Trades, and It’s All Sales
The trading activity of members of Congress continues to attract investors’ attention. One member’s recently disclosed trades were all sales.
Congressman Discloses Stock Trades
Rep. Robert (Bob) Onder (R-Mo.) recently disclosed stock trades for the first time as a congressman, according to data from the Benzinga Government Trades page.
Here are the trades, which were all made on Oct. 29:
- Sold $1,000 to $15,000 in Ameriprise Financial AMP shares
- Sold $1,000 to $15,000 in Comcast CMCSA shares
- Sold $1,000 to $15,000 in Walt Disney Company DIS shares
- Sold $15,000 to $50,000 in Duke Energy DUK shares
- Sold $1,000 to $15,000 in GE HealthCare GEHC shares
- Sold $1,000 to $15,000 in HP Inc. HPQ shares
- Sold $15,000 to $50,000 in Mondelez International MDLZ shares
- Sold $1,000 to $15,000 in Medtronic PLC MDT shares
- Sold $15,000 to $50,000 in PepsiCo PEP shares
- Sold $15,000 to $50,000 in Procter & Gamble PG shares
- Sold $1,000 to $15,000 in U.S. Bancorp USB shares
- Sold $1,000 to $15,000 in Verizon VZ shares
Onder’s Net Worth and Investing History
Onder took office in the House of Representatives in January 2025. He won reelection in the 2026 midterms and will continue serving in Congress.
While this is his first public disclosure of stock trading, a previous financial statement showed millions of dollars invested in ETFs and hundreds of thousands of dollars invested in individual stocks, according to data from Quiver Quantitative.
Most of the stock positions previously disclosed by Onder were in the $1,000 to $15,000 and $15,000 to $50,000 ranges. The largest individual stock holding disclosed previously by Onder was Microsoft Corporation MSFT with $200,000 to $500,000 in shares owned.
