TITLE: PlayStation Plus drops 4 new games today, including a gloriously chaotic GTA 6 alternative
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

PlayStation Plus has dropped four more games today, following the arrival of the likes of Death Stranding 2: On the Beach earlier this month. Among the new additions is a gloriously chaotic action RPG that might just tempt you away from the streets of Vice City. So, if you're looking for a GTA VI alternative this Thanksgiving weekend, you're in luck.
Announced earlier this month on the PlayStation Blog, the latest batch of PS Plus Extra and Premium games includes an over-the-top pirate adventure, a brutal supernatural FPS and something for those who enjoy a little competitive sport.
PlayStation Plus adds four new games for PS5 and PS4 today
The four games joining the PS Plus Game Catalogue today, 24 November, have a combined retail value of $209.96/£194.96 when purchased at full price on the PlayStation Store. However, Extra and Premium subscribers can enjoy all four at no additional cost.
- Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii (PS5, PS4) – $59.99/£54.99 – Metacritic: 79/100.
- Trepang2 (PS5) – $29.99/£29.99 – Metacritic: 74/100.
- MotoGP 26 (PS5) – $59.99/£59.99 – Metacritic: 75/100.
- Cricket 26 – The Official Game of the Ashes (PS5, PS4) – $59.99/£49.99 – Metacritic user score: 8.2/10.
GTA 6 fans should give Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii a go
Developed by Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio and published by SEGA, Yakuza series fan-favourite Goro Majima gets to be the leading man once more in this absolutely bonkers spin-off that's still closely linked to the mainline series.
Set six months after the events of Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth, Majima washes up on the shores of a tropical island with no memory of who he is or how he got there. So, naturally, he reinvents himself as a pirate captain, making a name for himself on the open seas of modern-day Hawaii.

The game features fantastical melee and naval combat, with plenty of wacky side missions to complement its equally ridiculous main story. It's honestly one of the most fun games in the Yakuza/Like a Dragon series, and that's saying something. It’s also a great game for those with no prior knowledge of the series. In fact, it might make you a new fan.
I scored Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii an excellent 8/10 in my review for GAMINGbible, and one of the highlights for me was its naval combat. Not only can you board rival ships for all-out brawls, but you can also kit out your own vessel with some ridiculous weaponry. After all, as I said in my review, "what pirate ship is complete without ye olde laser cannons and flamethrowers?"
Granted, Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii and GTA VI are two very different games, but if causing absolute chaos in a gorgeous tropical setting sounds like your cup of tea, then you're in for a treat.

What else is new on PlayStation Plus today?
If swashbuckling adventures aren't your thing, then one of these three games might take your fancy instead.
- Trepang2: A brutal first-person shooter featuring supernatural abilities, intense gunfights and stealth mechanics. If you're a fan of the F.E.A.R. series, this might be right up your street.
- MotoGP 26: Take to the track in the official game of the 2026 MotoGP championship, with rider ratings influenced by real-world performances.
- Cricket 26: Cricket fans can enjoy an in-depth Ashes experience, take charge of a team in the management career mode or get stuck into the expanded Pro Team mode.
Unlike the monthly PS Plus Essential games, which you can return to with an active subscription once claimed, you'll only be able to play these four titles for as long as they remain in the Game Catalogue and you have an active Extra or Premium subscription.
What's more, if you've not checked them out already, this month's PS Plus offerings also include the aforementioned Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, as well as Atomfall and Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake. So, there's certainly no shortage of games to keep you busy this Thanksgiving weekend.
