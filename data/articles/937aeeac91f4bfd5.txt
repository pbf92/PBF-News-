TITLE: 'GTA VI' Has an NPC Who Thinks He's Trapped in a Video Game: 'Why Can't I Leave???'
OUTLET: Complex

Key Takeaways
- GTA VI players found a bungalow in Belville, Leonida, that matches the mysterious UFO photo Rockstar Games teased in 2021’s GTA: San Andreas – The Definitive Edition.
- The garage’s conspiracy board declares “IT’S A SIMULATION,” suggesting its non-player character resident knows they’re trapped inside a game.
- Players have also uncovered Easter eggs like a a UFO encounter near Port Gellhorn, a Skunk Ape side quest, and a giant mutant crab in Gullet Marsh.
Grand Theft Auto VI has a non-player character who seems aware that they’re trapped inside a video game, and Rockstar Games subtly showed the character’s house in an earlier release.
Following the game’s Thursday (Nov. 19) release, players stumbled upon the cream-colored bungalow from a 2021 photo in GTA: San Andreas - The Definitive Edition. The home is located in Belville, Leonida, and has a for-sale sign out front, hinting toward the character preparing to escape.
When players enter the bungalow garage, they find a board with "IT'S A SIMULATION" scrawled in big red letters. Next to it, a message reading "Identical People" shows a tally of 140, along with an urgent plea reading, "Why can't I leave Leonida???" and the line, "There's nothing behind closed doors." The NPC also notices that there’s no children in the GTA universe.
Clippings on the board show the NPC’s heightened paranoia about their environment.
"We were never alive. Simulants rise" and "America's most abducted man,” are among the messages. Other clues read, "Who is L, who is J?"—which possibly represents game protagonists Jason and Lucia—and "Number 6 - what does it mean?"
The Definitive Edition game swapped a new picture onto a wall of UFO-themed bar Lil' Probe'Inn: a tiny UFO floating above a cream bungalow with two palms standing in the yard. The house didn't appear in any location from earlier GTA games, according to LevelUp.
Players have been uncovering Easter eggs throughout GTA VI. Other sightings include a UFO encounter near Port Gellhorn connected to a "The Truth Is Out There" trophy, a Skunk Ape side quest, and a giant attacking mutant crab in Gullet Marsh.
Celebrities have also made cameos in GTA VI as NPCs and voice actors, with the lineup including Bad Bunny, Sexyy Red, Kodak Black, Denzel Curry, and Bobby Moynihan.
