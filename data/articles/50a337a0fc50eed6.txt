5 Best GTA 6 Weapons, Ranked
As arguably the most anticipated video game in history, "Grand Theft Auto 6" had some high expectations to live up to. The game more than delivers on its promise, and tens of millions of players are now driving through the streets and boating through the swamps of Leonida. Rockstar has given gamers its biggest map to date, and everywhere you look in "GTA 6" there's another excuse to draw your weapon.
The gunplay in "GTA 6" isn't dramatically different than it was in previous GTA games. Fans who were hoping for a first-person mode in "GTA 6" are disappointed, but Rockstar has absolutely fine-tuned its third-person combat formula. The ability to use a character's focus to slow time and see lethal and non-lethal weak points on targets makes shooting feels more precise and impactful than it ever has in the series. On top of that, "GTA 6" has a mind-boggling amount of guns, each with its own unique feel and use case.
Most of the weapons in the game can be further customized at gun stores, though some legendary variants are locked-in with special cosmetics and powerful stats. Hunting for weapons will take you to some of the best locations in Leonida, but if you don't have an explorer's heart, we've traveled the state for you. These aren't necessarily the biggest, flashiest weapons in "GTA 6," but they're the best and most reliable firearms to see you through every trial Jason and Lucia face.
5. Deever Tech GLR40
Some of the best guns in "GTA 6" are incredibly practical weapons that you'll use in almost every mission or robbery. The Deever Tech GLR40 is not like that at all. This legendary grenade launcher is essentially a panic button that you'll only pull out when a job goes south in the worst possible way. That said, when you're in a bad spot, surrounded by armored enemies or vehicles, nothing can clear a fresh path for you quite as smoothly as the GLR40.
The two eye-catching stats on the GLR40 are power and accuracy. This gun can blow just about anything to smithereens, and as long as you're within a functional range, you can be pretty sure that your shots are going to hit their mark. As a legendary weapon, the GLR40 can't be modded, but that hardly matters when its base stats are so good and when it's so easy to obtain. Stop by the Sunrise Motel in Tequestra to scoop up yours: The GLR40 will be sitting on top of some metal boxes in one of the single-car garages. Take it and head into your next large-scale assault with nothing but explosive confidence.
4. Hillinger M306 TMR
Machine guns are great for criminals with a spray and pray philosophy, but a real professional needs a tactical rifle designed for high accuracy and maximum lethality. "GTA 6" has plenty of high-grade rifles on offer, but the Hillinger M306 TMR is an exceptional choice if you can get your hands on it.
The Hillinger is a semi-automatic rifle with a 20-round magazine, and some of the highest accuracy and range stats of any weapon in the game. That's just the base model. Because it isn't a legendary weapon variant, the Hillinger can be fully customized and modded out until it becomes the single strongest weapon in your arsenal.
Despite its deadly accuracy and supremely useful range, the Hillinger probably won't end up in the trunk of every "GTA 6" player. The weapon is one of the few in the game locked behind a challenge. Before you can purchase the Hillinger for yourself, you have to earn the gold prize at every shooting range challenge. Most players are going to be so busy getting through every mission in "GTA 6" that they'll overlook the Hillinger, but this gun is more than worth the effort it takes to unlock.
3. Unpacked Goodwin Comshot 12
Sometimes the one thing you need in "GTA 6" combat is more damage. On that front, the Unpacked Goodwin Comshot 12 has you covered. It's a longarm, so that does mean players are somewhat restricted when it comes to carrying the gun openly. However, the Goodwin's major selling point makes up for that small inconvenience. The gun has a 7.7 damage rating, making it one of the hardest hitting weapons in the game. It doesn't matter if the enemies you're fighting are fully armored. As long as you get close before pulling the trigger, the Goodwin will see you through.
Players can pick up a generic version of the gun from a store, but the Unpacked legendary variant has a few advantages over the base model. The Unpacked Goodwin comes with a pre-attached scope that boosts the weapon's range while slightly reducing its handling — a tradeoff that's more than worthwhile because sometimes a few extra feet of range can make all the difference. The Unpacked variant can't be modded, but to make up for that, the gun can be picked up for free from a house in Key Lento right at the beginning of the game.
2. Scrapper VS5C
"GTA 6" masterfully ramps up the stakes and difficulty of missions as the game progresses. After making it through the shocking Chapter 1 ending, you'll definitely be wanting something more than a basic pistol to get you through your next crime wave. Luckily, Chapter 2 is the ideal point to retrieve one of the most reliable SMGs in all of "GTA 6." During that segment of the game, players are given the option to clear out the La Cascara Motel as a side activity. The Scrapper VS5C is on the 4th floor of the motel, which players can reach by climbing cabinets on the 3rd floor or by bringing explosives and blasting open a locked door near the top of the building.
The Scrapper VS5C is a legendary longarm SMG that can help you clear out warehouses full of enemies. The gun comes with a silencer already installed, so unlike many longarms in the game, the Scrapper can be perfect for quick and stealthy work. It is still an SMG, so don't expect to be silently headshotting enemies from a block away. However, thanks to the Scrapper's high handling, it doubles as a great backup option for when your stealthy plan falls apart and you find yourself surrounded by angry gang members.
1. Silenced Duke '22
There is no shortage of great weapons in "GTA 6," and you can get one of the most useful weapons in the entire game in Chapter 1. The Duke 1922 is a relatively inexpensive heavy pistol that you can buy from gun stores in the Keys in the early part of the game. By default, the Duke deals out a fairly significant amount of damage, and the gun has decent range and handling stats.
It's a reliable enough weapon on its own, but adding a silencer to the Duke takes it to a whole new level.The noise reduction from the silencer will stop your shots from alerting the police and let you take out nearby cameras without attracting attention. To be fair, any silenced pistol will quickly become a workhorse gun and your go–to robbery tool, but suppressed shots from the Duke still pack a serious punch.
With good aim, you can expect to be one-shotting unarmored enemies with no problem. You might need a little more firepower to break into the Mile Zero military base, but the Duke with a silencer will get you through most robberies and gang shootouts. Because of that, it could easily carry you through most of the game's story.
