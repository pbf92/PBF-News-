TITLE: Match Preview: Sporting Lisbon vs Manchester United
OUTLET: The Busby Babe

Fresh from another humbling Premier League setback, Manchester United will attempt to return to winning ways when they travel to the Estadio Jose Alvalade to face Sporting Lisbon in the Champions League on Wednesday night.
Three points and seven places separate these two teams in the 36-team League Phase standings, with Sporting occupying 10th place as the Red Devils sit down in 17th spot at the halfway stage.
While Pochettino has acknowledged the “reality” that his team are in a relegation battle, he hopes that his players can now translate their anger from another “painful” setback into energy to help improve their fortunes as quickly as possible.
Sorting out an alarmingly porous backline will be one of the Argentine’s top priorities, as Man United have now conceded a whopping 34 goals in 16 games across all competitions this season, more than any other team in Europe’s top-five leagues.
A quick fix in Portugal is far from guaranteed, though, as the Red Devils have won only one of their last 11 Champions League away matches (D3 L7) – 2-0 at Villarreal in 2021-22 – and have already lost two of their first four League Phase games this term (W2), including a heavy 4-1 away loss to Como.
However, Man United did secure a 3-2 comeback victory over Roma on matchday four and have also prevailed in three of their previous four encounters with Sporting Lisbon (L1), including two group-stage wins in their triumphant 2007-08 UCL campaign (1-0 away, 2-1 at home) – Cristiano Ronaldo netted the winning goal in both matches.
Team News:
For Man United, ex-Sporting midfielder Manuel Ugarte (knee), Carlos Baleba (hamstring) and Benjamin Sesko (calf) all remain out injured, while there is uncertainty over the availability of Marcus Rashford (hip), Luke Shaw (muscle) and backup goalkeeper Karl Darlow (knee).
After scoring the match-winning goals against Roma last time out, Matheus Cunha was then sent off for two bookable offences and is now serving a one-match ban.
Matthijs de Ligt endured a difficult afternoon at Anfield on his first PL start in 357 days. Pochettino could therefore swap him for Harry Maguire and potentially stick with a three-man defence including Yoro and Lisandro Martinez, with Diogo Dalot and Patrick Dorgu operating as wing-backs.
In the absence of Cunha, Pochettino could recall Amad Diallo or hand a rare start to striker Joshua Zirkzee, which would allow Bryan Mbeumo to move over to the right side of attack.
Captain Bruno Fernandes is expected to continue in midfield and face his former club Sporting for the first time since leaving to join Man United in January 2020. The Portuguese scored 64 goals in 137 appearances for Sporting, while he has contributed to a total of 21 goals (nine goals, 12 assists) in 29 UCL career appearances.
