TITLE: MNC: Leeds dreaming of Europe and when's the right time to start a young player?
OUTLET: BBC Sport

Episode details
Available for 29 days
Mark Chapman is joined by Conor Coady, Chris Sutton and Rory Smith. After Leeds dismantled Chelsea at Stamford Bridge, the panel discuss their chances of making Europe and how Dominic Calvert-Lewin has rebuilt his career. On the other side, another setback for Xabi Alonso's Chelsea - can he find the right formula at Stamford Bridge? Manchester United continue to make headlines for all the wrong reasons after a nightmare first game in charge for Mauricio Pochettino - will they be ruthless enough to shift the deadwood? And with Rio Ngumoah and Luca Williams-Barnett starring over the weekend, when's the right time to bring teenagers into first-team football?
