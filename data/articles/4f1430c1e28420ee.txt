ChainOpera AI: Unlocks at Least 11.25 Million COAI - Jan 2027
ChainOpera AI has scheduled a COAI supply unlock for January 2027. The release is for advisors, investors and the team. It covers at least 11.25 million COAI. That minimum amount is equal to 5.98% of the circulating supply.
The unlock makes the released tokens available for sale. Each recipient can decide whether to sell any of the COAI they receive.
