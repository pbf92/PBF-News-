TITLE: GTA 6's Day 1 copies sold have already beaten the Nintendo 64's lifetime sales
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Rockstar Games has revealed the pre-sales numbers that Grand Theft Auto VI generated prior to its November 19 launch.
Unsurprisingly, the release of Grand Theft Auto VI has become the biggest entertainment launch in history.
Rockstar's games have always sold exceedingly well, but combined with the 99 Metacritic score and 13 years of hype, the latest release has solidified itself as the most successful video game launch in history, and it'll probably never be topped by anything else.
As revealed by Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar's parent company, Grand Theft Auto VI has sold 32.5 million units pre-release across PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.

In terms of total revenue this generated, sales are estimated to have hit $3.79 billion, which has already recuperated costs for the game. These numbers are based on company estimates through to the end of the day of November 19th worldwide.
“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” said Rockstar Games president and co-founder Sam Houser. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”

How do GTA VI sales compare to the rest of the industry?
To put this into perspective, the number comes exceedingly close to the lifetime sales of the Nintendo 64, which has shifted 32.93 million units since 1996 according to the latest data from Nintendo.
It's also more than other consoles that were considered a success such as the Sega Mega Drive (30.75 million), the original Xbox (24 million), and the Nintendo GameCube (21.74 million).
In addition to that, GTA VI has already become the 30th best-selling game of all time in its first day alone.
For context, it took Elden Ring three years to hit the same number of sales. It's also beaten games like It Takes Two, Call of Duty Black Ops Cold War, and Super Mario Odyssey which were all resounding successes at the time they released.
Comparing this to other Rockstar games, GTA V sold around 11 million copies in the first day of release, while Red Dead Redemption 2 shifted 17 million copies within its first week. In total, GTA V has sold 235 million sales across its lifetime, coming in third place only behind Minecraft (425 million), and Tetris (520 million).
Meanwhile, Red Dead Redemption 2 is the fourth best-selling game of all time, with total lifetime copies sold reaching 89 million.
As for where other Grand Theft Auto games land on the list of best-selling video games, San Andreas sold 27.5 million copies across its lifetime, while Vice City sold 17.5 million units and GTA III sold 14.5 million units.
Analysts are confident that GTA VI will eventually beat GTA V as Rockstar Games' most successful game of all time, though this depends on the developer's implementation of Grand Theft Auto Online, which is where the bulk of the company's revenue comes from.
The game's online mode has been delayed, as it had with GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 before that, so that the company could focus on shipping the story mode.
