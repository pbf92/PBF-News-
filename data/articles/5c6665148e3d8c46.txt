TITLE: Anthropic’s Claude nears 350 million weekly website visits ahead of IPO
OUTLET: Crypto Briefing

Similarweb data shows Claude's traffic climbing fast, though it still trails ChatGPT and Gemini by a wide margin
Anthropic’s Claude chatbot reached nearly 350 million weekly website visits in the run-up to the company’s initial public offering.
The numbers behind the surge
According to Similarweb data, Claude drew approximately 1.3 billion web visits in October 2026. That was a 23.8% increase from the prior month.
The year-over-year comparison is more dramatic. Traffic rose 536% from the same month a year earlier.
Breaking the monthly figure down, October’s peak worked out to an estimated 325 million weekly visits. That lines up closely with the roughly 350 million weekly figure reported separately.
The gap between the two numbers likely comes down to timing and methodology. Traffic estimates are modeled rather than counted at the source, so small differences between snapshots are normal.
Still third in a three-horse race
For all the growth, Claude remains the third-most visited AI chatbot site, per Similarweb.

OpenAI’s ChatGPT recorded 6 billion visits in October 2026. Google’s Gemini logged 2.6 billion.
So Claude’s 1.3 billion monthly visits are roughly half of Gemini’s and a bit over a fifth of ChatGPT’s.
Following the IPO, Claude also climbed to No. 25 on Similarweb’s global ranking of most-visited websites.
The IPO backdrop
Anthropic filed its S-1 registration statement with the SEC on October 26, 2026.
According to the filing as described in the research, Anthropic reported $16.3 billion in revenue for the first half of 2026. That represented a 1,300% increase over the same period a year earlier. The company also reported a net loss of $80.7 billion.
The IPO was priced at $83 per share, raising $81.3 billion and valuing Anthropic at around $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis.
The October traffic spike also overlapped with the S-1 filing.
