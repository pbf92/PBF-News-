TITLE: Enphase Energy Director Sold Shares Worth Over $3.5M
OUTLET: TradingView

Enphase Energy Director Sold Shares Worth Over $3.5M
Thurman J. Rodgers, a director, sold 171,400 shares of Enphase Energy common stock through the Rodgers Massey Revocable Living Trust on November 24, 2026, at a weighted average price of $20.4393 per share, for a total of $3,503,296. The sale provided liquidity for capital commitments related to private portfolio funds. After the sale, Rodgers directly owned 4,464 shares and indirectly owned 223,719 shares: 130,319 through the Rodgers Massey Revocable Living Trust, 4,100 through the Valeta Massey 2012 Irrevocable Trust, 4,100 through the TJ Rodgers 2012 Irrevocable Trust, and 85,200 through a charitable trust.
