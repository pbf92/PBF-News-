TITLE: Radio advertising grows marginally in Jan-Sept 2026; Maruti Suzuki leads advertiser rankings: TAM AdEx
OUTLET: Storyboard18

Special Coverage

Radio advertising in India recorded a marginal decline of 0.7% in ad volumes during January-September 2026 compared with the corresponding period last year, according to a report by TAM AdEx. However, volumes grew 3% compared with January-September 2024, indicating a largely stable performance for the medium.
Maruti Suzuki India emerged as the leading advertiser during the period, while Vimal Pan Masala topped the brand rankings. More than 10,700 brands advertised on radio during the nine-month period.
The report, which tracks advertising trends across more than 120 radio stations in association with RCS India, analyses ad volumes, leading sectors, advertisers, brands, geographical contribution and preferred advertising time bands.
Services sector accounts for 31% of radio ad volumes
Banking, finance and investment and the automobile sectors followed, each contributing 11%, while personal accessories accounted for 10% and education contributed 8%.
Retail and food and beverages each held a 7% share. The top 10 sectors together accounted for 92% of radio advertising volumes.
Education moved up to the fifth position in the sector rankings, with an 8% share.
Real estate, jewellery and automobile categories lead
Properties and real estate retained the top position among advertising categories, contributing 18% of total ad volumes during the period.
Retail outlets-jewellers and cars followed, each accounting for 9% of volumes, while hospitals and clinics contributed 8%. Life insurance accounted for 4%, followed by multiple courses at 3%.
The top 10 categories together contributed 59% of radio advertising volumes. Schools entered the top 10 list during January-September 2026 compared with the year-ago period.
In terms of growth, properties and real estate recorded a 14% increase in ad volumes, while life insurance grew 86%. Corporate NBFCs registered 2.1 times growth, while digestives grew 2.6 times.
Anywhere banking recorded the highest growth among the top 10 growing categories, at 7.3 times.
Maruti Suzuki tops advertiser list; Vimal Pan Masala leads brands
Maruti Suzuki India retained its position as the largest radio advertiser during January-September 2026, followed by LIC of India and SBS Biotech.
Muthoot Financial Enterprises, Vishnu Packaging, SBI, Hyundai Motor India, Kedia Pavitra, Anand Jewels and Reliance Retail also featured among the top 10 advertisers.
The top 10 advertisers together accounted for 18% of total radio ad volumes, with seven advertisers common to the list compared with the corresponding period last year.
At the brand level, Vimal Pan Masala moved up from the second position in January-September 2025 to become the leading brand in 2026. Muthoot Financial Enterprises ranked second, followed by Maruti Suzuki Arena and SBI.
Three of the top 10 brands belonged to Maruti Suzuki India, including Maruti Suzuki Arena, Maruti Suzuki E Vitara and Maruti Suzuki Grand Vitara.
Gujarat and Jaipur lead regional radio advertising
Gujarat retained its position as the largest state contributor to radio advertising, accounting for 17% of total ad volumes. Maharashtra followed with a 15% share.
The top five states together contributed 61% of radio advertising volumes during January-September 2026.
At the city level, Jaipur retained its top position with a 9% share, followed by Nagpur and Indore. New Delhi, Surat, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Ahmedabad, Vadodara and Lucknow completed the top 10 list.
The top 10 cities together accounted for 62% of radio advertising volumes.
Evening remains the preferred time band for advertisers
Evening emerged as the most preferred time band for radio advertising, followed by morning and afternoon. Evening and morning slots together accounted for 69% of total ad volumes.
In terms of commercial duration, advertisements lasting 20-40 seconds remained the most preferred format during January-September 2026, as they were in the year-ago period.
Together, commercials of 20-40 seconds and those shorter than 20 seconds accounted for 92% of radio advertising volumes.
