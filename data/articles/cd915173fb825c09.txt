TITLE: ‘GTA VI’ Sales Near $4B In First Day Of Launch; Yes, You Read That Correctly, Nearly $4B
OUTLET: Deadline

Less than 48 hours after Grand Theft Auto VI went live, Rockstar Games has released sales figures for the long anticipated and heavily hyped misadventures out of the seedy fictional state of Leonida.
Based on data from November 19, GTA VI has hit an astonishing $3.79 billion in day one retail sales.
Including the massive pre-sales the download only open sandbox video game has had over the past few weeks, that translates into “32.5 million units pre-release, a new record across all platforms,” says the Take-Two Interactive subsidiary.
By a wide wide margin, like a Vice City car chase, that makes GTV VI the most successful entertainment debut in media history.

That thrashes the game’s own record of earlier this week and the previous record 800 million holder of 2013’s GTA V. Set up against the likes of 2019’s Avengers: Endgame or Christopher Nolan’s acclaimed The Odyssey from earlier this year, GTA VI has made more cash already than either of those big screen blockbusters have made over their entire release.
Putting pedal to the metal towards $4 billion, today’s sales figure will almost be certainly eclipsed when the single-player GTA VI lands on the multiplayer GTA Online platform in the next several months. Which means the jaw dropping debut is truly just the beginning …there may be many more adult snow days to come, if you know what I mean?
Add to that future shock, unlike most media properties (we see you $56.99 a share Netflix) in Friday’s hard hit and volatile market, Take-Two’s stock is on the uptick today, hitting $211.25 so far.

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” Rockstar prez and founder Sam Houser said Friday of the game that pushed internet traffic up double digits the past few days. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”
This very lucrative moment comes after a decade of GTA VI development at an estimated $1 billion cost — an investment that is clearly paying off. Available on Playstation and Xbox, the single-player but multi-level GTA VI is a massive 212GB, which has led to fans downloading the game as early as November 10 to be ready for launch.
At the same time as being a commercial and critical success (GTA VI has been near universally praised by critics), the game has proven to be culturally dominate the past week. Such a height is almost unknown in the fractured and wide focused media environment of 2026. Which has seen the White House, the President of Brazil, a legion of YouTubers and Twitch posters, the NBA, and many many more hook their social media wagons to GTA VI.
