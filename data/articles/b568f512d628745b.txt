Amazon Stocks Climb 2.96% as Thanksgiving Online Spending Hits $7.1 Billion
Amazon.com AMZN shares climbed 2.96% to $251.66 at 10:35 a.m. ET on Friday, November 27, as early holiday data showed U.S. consumers spending more online than expected. Shoppers spent $7.1 billion online on Thanksgiving Day, up 10.4% from a year earlier and ahead of Adobe's $6.9 billion forecast.
Adobe expects Black Friday online sales of $12.9 billion, up 9.2%, and $15.1 billion on Cyber Monday, with the full Cyber Week reaching $47.5 billion. Spending from November 1 to 22 already totaled $82.5 billion, up 9.1%, running ahead of Adobe's full-season forecast of 6.7% growth to $275.1 billion. CNBC's Investing Club flagged the Thanksgiving figure before the open, citing Reuters.
The GuruFocus chart shows a GF Score of 94/100, with strength across profitability, balance sheet and momentum. The GF Value of $260.58 implies only a 3.4% discount, which GuruFocus calls fairly valued, so much of the holiday optimism already sits in the price. The more useful signal is the spread: season-to-date growth of 9.1% is running about 2.4 points above Adobe's forecast, and if that holds, retail could add upside to fourth-quarter estimates. The next test is Cyber Monday, where Adobe expects $15.1 billion in online spending.
