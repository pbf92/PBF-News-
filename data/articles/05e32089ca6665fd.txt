TITLE: Manchester United and Liverpool monitor Atalanta star Giorgio Scalvini
OUTLET: Liverpool FC

The 22-year-old has attracted considerable attention across Europe, and his recent return to top form has strengthened his reputation as one of the most promising young defenders in Italian football.
According to a TEAMtalk report, Manchester United and Liverpool are both assessing their central defensive options, with Scalvini emerging as a potential long-term addition. Chelsea are also monitoring his situation, while Newcastle United have maintained their interest in the defender for several years.
