TITLE: GTA 6 proves in our review: it is the long-awaited masterpiece
OUTLET: PCWorld

At a glance
Expert's Rating
Pros
- Experiencing this world in all its depth is simply wow
- Detail far beyond that of Red Dead Redemption 2
- Stellar character design (hair, skin, clothing, etc.)
- Brilliantly implemented weather effects, calculated for every NPC
- Very comprehensive driving and damage models for cars

Cons
- The story takes a little too long to pick up pace

Our Verdict
GTA 6 for Sony PlayStation and Microsoft Xbox is a revolutionary experience. Rockstar Games had an endless supply of money and time at its disposal, and you can feel it at every turn: Whether it’s the incredibly detailed interiors of hotels, the thousands of animations that bring the most lifelike NPCs of all time to life, or how tropical storms transform the entire atmosphere and rain showers soak through our clothes. This is a world we want to spend time in, but one that also tells us an Oscar-worthy story with characters that stick in your memory. Rockstar also squeezes every last drop out of the older consoles, though these do noticeably hold GTA 6 back. It’s close to perfection: On the PC, it’ll secure its well-deserved award as the best game of all time.

Lombank, 2 pm: We’re about to experience the first 6-star chase in GTA 6 history, and wow, it really blew us away. At first, the plan for this massive bank heist seemed to be working: We’re standing on the roof and the trap snaps shut. Using a remote trigger, we set off the C4 pack attached to an SUV parked outside the bank. The bulletproof glass shatters, columns of flame shoot out of the vehicle, immediately blowing up two other parked cars. It’s complete chaos; security storm onto the street with M4 assault rifles at the ready. And they don’t even notice that Jason and Lucia are already inside.
by Benjamin Kratsch
We snuck in through the delivery entrance, took out a couple of security guards, and threatened a bank employee until he gave us the code for the security room. There, on a display, is today’s code for the vault. We typed it in, stuffed the money into black bags — we felt like we were in Michael Mann’s HEAT. And just like in HEAT, everything now goes pear-shaped.
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
SWAT special forces are closing in from all directions; we pull out the grenade launcher and turn the first SWAT truck into a ball of flame even as it approaches. We mow down three or four more units from the safe cover of the bank. We’ve got to get out somehow, so a plan is quickly hatched. We fight our way through to the bank’s underground car park and grab a cash-in-transit van because it’s heavily armoured. AI-Jason drives, while we blast away with the grenade launcher as Lucia.
by Benjamin Kratsch
From level 5 onwards, NOOSE Special Forces arrive — NSA elite units in heavily armoured Humvees with machine-gunners in rotating turrets. They turn our cash-in-transit van into Swiss cheese, so it’s back to the stronghold — the bank offers the most protection. But not against the U.S. military, who are activated at 6 stars from Miles Zero Air Base. Striker wheeled armoured vehicles now roll in and open fire on the building. Super Stallion transport helicopters drop marines onto the roof. And if we try to escape in a helicopter, they’ll shoot the fighter jets out of the sky.
The ultimate open world
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
Rockstar Games’ vision and mission are clear: Their Miami isn’t just a location for missions; it’s a living, breathing ecosystem. For the first time in a video game, a city is truly a city. We discover, for example, a 5-star hotel, walk past the lobby and into a restaurant. Every table there is taken, candles are burning everywhere, and people are chatting. Young couples on a date. A child with their parents who refuses to eat unless the family dog is allowed to sit at the table.
The waiting staff have to put up with comments like, “Bloody hell, I didn’t mean to buy the whole place!” This restaurant has a kitchen where every single ingredient is prepared to perfection — whether it’s fish, chicken, or leeks. We can watch dozens of chefs at work, practising their craft. Staff haul crates of fresh produce in — if we follow them, we discover a sort of underground car park with lorries delivering fresh ingredients.
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
These include giant strawberries, which are the highlight of the desserts. We eavesdrop on conversations, can follow the delivery van in a car, and then discover a completely mad organic farmer who’s experimenting with dangerous substances to grow these XXL strawberries. But we can also head out of the hotel, take the lift up — there’s an infinity pool where hundreds of guests are enjoying themselves. There are pool bars, jacuzzis, everything your heart desires. 
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
Some are normal, others a bit quirky — like an elderly lady desperately trying to persuade her dog to go for a swim — but the little dog prefers to stay safely at the edge of the pool. This love of scale is evident everywhere: There are huge shopping centers with countless shops, both small and large. Every single pair of trainers, every suit, every silk blouse is rendered in full detail. And everywhere you look, there are shop assistants whose quality is only slightly behind that of the story characters. All these shopping arcades are also teeming with NPCs who behave realistically.
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
Try on some shoes, buy some sports caps for the next match of the Vice City Manatees (GTA’s version of the Miami Dolphins, who of course also have their own stadium, fan shops, etc.). There are 120 bars and no two are alike — from the high-end lounge with white marble and a mahogany bar, where elegant ladies and gentlemen in evening wear sip champagne, to the run-down dive bar, where the wooden tables are completely scratched and the beer flows freely. 
What a billion dollars buys
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
6,000 of the world’s best game designers had a budget of a good billion U.S. dollars at their disposal to build the perfect video game world. They succeeded, and they put an incredible amount of work into it. There are dedicated mini-engines for every occasion: Every NPC emerging from the sea has wet, animated hair. If a lady flips her hair back, it splashes slightly. Sand sticks to wet feet. When we go into a hot club, a specially patented sweat engine calculates how many beads of sweat form on Lucia’s forehead.
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
A fluid engine calculates how the sweat then runs from her forehead, down her nose, along her cheeks, into her cleavage or over her navel. If we shoot up spirits at a bar, the whisky bottles spill out. If bullets fly through a supermarket, packets of crisps are torn to shreds and all manner of cola and Fanta spurt out. When tropical storms roll in, the wind is calculated for every single object in the street — whether it’s small chairs being blown over outside a restaurant, or a parasol billowing in the wind.
When it rains, it’s not just Lucia and Jason who get wet; this is calculated for every single NPC. GTA 6 distinguishes between whether a thin T-shirt gets soaked, or a thick jumper. Or whether someone is wearing a raincoat or a leather jacket, off which the raindrops roll. Rockstar Games is mad in the best possible way, because they calculate all these nuances for every single NPC when it comes to weather conditions. Take, for example, long hair blowing in the wind. Or the “soaked poodle” look after heavy rain, whereby an engine then calculates how long it takes for it to dry in the Miami sun. 
GTA 6 is crying out for a PC edition
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
Yes, even on consoles that are now seven years old, such as the Xbox Series X or PS5 Pro, GTA 6 looks excellent and renders its technical features — such as ray-traced global illumination — in a truly atmospheric way. Rockstar is a master at playing with light and shadow; the blazing sun, for example, makes the chrome on a Ferrari really gleam. It’s also impressive how high-quality the reflections in glass doors are, or how every single item in a supermarket casts its own individual shadow.
However, GTA 6 clearly belongs on the PC; or rather, it requires the PS6 and Xbox 720 for console gamers, as Rockstar is forced to rely heavily on workarounds that do limit the visual experience. For instance, on the road, only the vehicles directly in front of us are rendered at maximum quality. Those driving past are rendered with lower-resolution textures, which look washed out on closer inspection. On the Xbox Series X and PS5, GTA 6 runs at 1440p resolution; on the PS5 Pro, the textures are significantly sharper. Furthermore, the open-world epic struggles to maintain its 30 FPS, particularly at 5 to 6 stars with hundreds of police units and explosions everywhere.
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
Please don’t get me wrong: GTA 6 is a delight because Rockstar knows its craft. Take, for example, the attention to detail in the design of huge yachts, which feature proper interiors — bars, bedrooms, fully functional Michelin-starred kitchens with chefs, and so on. Or how boats of different sizes displace the water in their own unique ways. How fantastic volumetric smoke looks after explosions. 
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
It’s also striking, however, that flames, for example, die out very quickly — to save on performance. Just imagine GTA 6 with the graphics of Cyberpunk 2077 — with ray tracing cranked right up, or even path tracing. And with native 4K textures at 120 FPS — and we’re talking about the best game of all time. According to the industry magazine GamesBeat, the PC version of GTA 6 is even set to arrive very soon, namely in March 2027.
Fast & Furious meets Red Dead Redemption 2
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
In terms of the depth of its storytelling, GTA 6 is much more like Red Dead Redemption 2 than GTA 5. It is more nuanced, more emotional, and operates more in the gray area. GTA 5 was, for the most part, just completely mad, with a very unsympathetic cast. We really grow to love Lucia and Jason, and they’re joined by a brilliant ensemble of actors and actresses, almost all of whom leave a lasting impression.
However, we also believe that the story will be the biggest point of contention surrounding GTA 6, because it’s very mature. It’s very much in the vein of The Sopranos, True Romance, The Departed, and Peaky Blinders. There’s no end to the comedy and the quirky moments, but these are found much more in the side quests, which are also, at times, brilliantly staged. GTA 6 essentially channels its humor into really big, and at times lengthy, side stories, which then reach their own climaxes.
by Benjamin Kratsch // PC WELT
Rockstar has asked us not to go into too much detail about the story, and we agree: You don’t spoil a Rockstar epic. It’s a good 70-hour ride that often feels like James Bond — for instance, when we have to creatively infiltrate heavily guarded military bases. But it also likes to really kick into high gear and then directly channel The Fast & the Furious. For instance, when we rip a safe out of a villa using a steel chain and then use it as a crash ball against the drug lord’s army.
