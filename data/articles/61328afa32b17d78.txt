TITLE: GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox as Rockstar posts the full map
OUTLET: GamesHub

Our team consists of a rotating staff of games enthusiasts who love journalism and journalists who love video games.
Grand Theft Auto VI launched worldwide on Thursday, 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no PC version on release day.
Rockstar Games marked launch day by posting a high-resolution version of the game’s full map online, covering Vice City and the wider fictional state of Leonida. The studio released the map because the in-game version keeps much of itself hidden: a fog-of-war system leaves areas obscured until the player travels through them, and even fully cleared, the map cannot be viewed in one piece on screen at once.
How big is the map?
Rockstar said before release that Leonida is bigger than GTA 5’s map, bigger than Red Dead Redemption 2’s, and bigger than both of those put together, and PC Gamer writer Chris Livingston, who reported spending about 100 hours with the game, backed the scale up: “Rockstar said GTA 6’s map is twice as big as GTA 5’s map, but it feels twice as big as “twice as big” should feel. It’s enormous.”
The game follows new protagonists Jason and Lucia through Vice City, where an apparently straightforward job goes wrong and pulls the pair into a larger criminal conspiracy, as Rockstar’s launch trailer set up in the lead-in to release. Early players are also combing the state for optional missions, including a Skunk Ape creature hunt and other side quests buried in radio bulletins and notice boards.
What launch day players have found
Not every copy of the game is the same. Kotaku reports that the Japanese version of GTA 6 is censored: the build is over 1 GB smaller than the global version, all nudity is absent, shower and beach scenes are altered, and blood is reduced. Rockstar gave no confirmation of any changes before release, so Japanese players only learned of the differences once they began playing. The Japanese release also carries just English and Japanese subtitles with the English voice track, and physical copies’ digital codes must be redeemed within 170 days of release under local laws.
Grand Theft Auto VI is out now worldwide on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and no PC release date has been announced.
