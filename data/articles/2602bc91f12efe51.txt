TITLE: Markets News, Nov. 24, 2026: S&P 500, Nasdaq Fall After Massive Rally as Nvidia Leads AI Sell-Off; Anthropic Drops Below IPO Price
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

The Nasdaq Composite and S&P 500 closed lower Tuesday as tech stocks tumbled after posting blockbuster gains to start the week.
The tech-heavy Nasdaq and benchmark S&P 500 fell 1.5% and 0.5%, respectively, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average added 0.9%, or more than 400 points. Stocks were coming off a furious rally on Monday, when the S&P 500 rose nearly 5% and the Nasdaq gained more than 6% as investors responded to the news that Jensen Huang had been reinstated as Nvidia's CEO. The major indexes recouped a big chunk of the losses they posted last week, when the AI trade that has propelled the market to record highs in recent years melted down.
Shares of Nvidia (NVDA), the chipmaker at the center of the AI boom, fell 6% today, after soaring 31% yesterday and adding more than $900 billion in market value in the process. The stock remains down 35% from the record closing high it hit on Oct. 6.
Other chip stocks also fell sharply today. Shares of Analog Devices (ADI) plunged 17% to lead S&P 500 decliners after the company reported earnings late Monday. Memory chip makers Micron (MU) and SK Hynix (SKHY) both dropped roughly 10%, while Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), Intel (INTC) and Marvell Technology (MRVL) each fell more than 8%, and Broadcom (AVGO) declined 4%. The iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) ended the day down 8%, while the Roundhill Memory ETF (DRAM) lost 9%.
Shares of Dell Technologies (DELL), a major server maker and Nvidia partner, declined 11% today after soaring 20% yesterday, as investors prepared for the company's earnings release after today's closing bell. Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) shares plunged 16%, even as the company, another key player in the AI buildout, reported better-than-expected results and boosted its guidance.
Anthropic (ANT) shares fell 6.5% to just under $82, trading below the $83 level that the company priced its record-setting IPO last week. Other mega-cap stocks were mostly lower on Tuesday, led by a near-6% decline for SpaceX (SPCX), though Apple (AAPL) added more than 1%.
Outside the tech space, shares of drugmaker Insmed (INSM) surged following news the company is being acquired by France's Sanofi (SNY) in a transaction valued at $41 billion. Insmed shares were up 14%, while Sanofi's U.S.-traded stock climbed 10%.
Nike (NKE) and biotech giant Amgen (AMGN) both gained more than 5% today to pace gainers on the Dow.
Meanwhile, bitcoin is losing ground after bouncing back yesterday from several weeks of losses. The cryptocurrency was trading at $59,500 recently, down from an overnight high above $64,000.
