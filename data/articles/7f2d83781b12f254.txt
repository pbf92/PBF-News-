TITLE: Grand Theft Auto 6 smashes estimates with $3.79 billion in 24 hours
OUTLET: The Game Business

Grand Theft Auto 6 has made $3.79 billion in its first day, nearly five times bigger than Grand Theft Auto 5.
The crime epic from Rockstar Games had analyst expectations of between $2 billion and $3 billion over the first weekend. It has comfortably beat that in a single day.
The game’s pre-orders alone exceeded 32.5 million units and it is the biggest entertainment launch of all time. The figures are internal estimates from GTA parent company Take-Two.
By comparison, GTA 5 generated $800 million in one day, and $1 billion over the first weekend.
The result follows record-breaking critical scores for GTA 6, with a Metacritic rating of 99. It’s the highest score in the modern industry, and the joint highest of all time.
“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto 6,” said Rockstar president and founder Sam Houser.
“We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”
