TITLE: Pochettino on Fernandes' illness, being 'good but not nice' and leadership
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United boss Mauricio Pochettino has been speaking to the media before Saturday's Premier League game against Brentford at Old Trafford (kick-off 15:00 GMT).
Here are the key lines from his news conference:
- Pochettino revealed he is unsure if captain Bruno Fernandes will be available for selection on Saturday, adding: "He's [been] affected by a virus from Portugal [after Wednesday's Champions League draw with Sporting]. He finished the game with a little bit of a problem and today he cannot be a part of the training session. We are going to assess during the day and tomorrow, how he wakes up, and see."
- He also provided an update on the recoveries of Marcus Rashford's and Carlos Baleba: "Today [Friday], Marcus started an individual session on the field. I think he is starting to feel better day by day and we'll see. Baleba was injured against Roma, the medical staff were talking about 10, 12 weeks."
- When asked about the "noticeable" difference in his team's aggression and organisation against Sporting, the United boss responded: "In only a few training sessions, [we have been working on] the intensity and organisation. Of course we cannot be perfect in three days, or two weeks, but yes, we are thinking long-term - today we need to win, but it's [also] working on the progression we need."
- He said the players are "very open" to buying into his ideas, adding: "We need to improve of course, but like all styles, you need time to settle all the principles."
- Pochettino wanted to make it clear that he is "good" but he is not "nice", explaining: "Sometimes we confuse two words to [have] a similar meaning. We have to be good but it is a different thing. I am not nice, I try to be good, be careful because we know we need to push, be strong and build something to improve everything. This is two different things that mean complete [opposites]."
- The Argentinian said he is trying to put the players in "uncomfortable" situations: "If we allow them to be in a comfortable zone, it's like us - you have everything, it's difficult to go out in the rain or the cold. That is us from day one, to push them to be better every day."
- When asked which players are "leaders" as he tries to implement his ways, he responded: "At the moment, we are, I am. We need to be careful of the demands to the players, and give them the most freedom and time to care about [themselves] and the team. It's not the responsibility on the players to lead the organisation of the club. Be careful with that."
