OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Manchester United criticise ProRef over Leny Yoro comments as Pochettino claims new Liverpool error
Man Utd have won an appeal against Leny Yoro's red card at Anfield but the head of the referees' body has stood by the decision.
Mauricio Pochettino slammed the failure of the VAR to overturn Leny Yoro's red card at Liverpool and said Manchester United had been robbed of the opportunity to compete with their rivals on equal terms.
Pochettino's anger at the officials - and in particular VAR John Brooks - came after Manchester United officials launched a scathing private attack on Howard Webb and ProRef, who backed the decision to dismiss Yoro despite the club winning an appeal against the red card.
Yoro was sent off eight minutes into the game at Anfield after bringing down Alexander Isak as he raced through on goal. Referee Sam Barrott branded an immediate red card, and United believe the conversation with the VAR official offered 'no meaningful challenge' to the decision.
ProRef chief Howard Webb has since said on the Mic'd Up show that the decision was "certainly not wrong" and praised the referee for being decisive. Webb's comments have angered United officials after they won an appeal against the sending-off.
An Old Trafford source said the interpretation of ProRef was 'totally wrong' and had been compounded by Webb's failure to recognise the error, even after an FA disciplinary panel had overturned the decision.

United insist they have the data to show that, even if Isak had maintained his top speed, he still wouldn't have reached the ball before Lammens, which means Yoro was not denying a goalscoring opportunity.
A club source said they will now raise the matter with the Premier League and ProRef, while Pochettino sounded furious about the incident two days on as he spoke to the media ahead of United's Champions League tie with Sporting in Portugal.
"Very disappointed because I think this type of thing can never happen," said the United head coach. "My question always was the same. Okay, we can accept the referee can make a mistake because of the emotional relationship with the game from the referee, but if we have VAR, what were they doing?
"Because now are we going to repeat the game? Are we going to have the possibility to play with the similar tools, 11 vs 11? No, the problem is when we were there, and in the moment that I think we need the time to review the situation."
Pochettino also claimed Dominik Szoboszlai's free-kick straight after the red card should have been ruled out, with Bradley Barcola in contact with the United wall, something that he said Bruno Fernandes raised with Barrott at the time.
"I think we didn't pay attention, but Bruno was aware, that is why he's the captain and because he's intelligent and perceives and feels what is going on on the field," said Pochettino.
"No one talked about that the goal wasn't legal. Should disallow the goal because Barcola was touching our wall. Eze, last season against Palace, when Eze scored, [it was disallowed]. Why didn't they check the VAR, check the goal? That must be disallowed.
"[I] need to defend my players and my club. Where are the three points? We need to think a little more deep... in the way that we use the technology.
"Now we need to move on. I am so proud of the players and the way they managed all these unfair situations that happened on the field, and our focus now is to improve, to be better and to start winning games, and tomorrow we have a great opportunity here."
