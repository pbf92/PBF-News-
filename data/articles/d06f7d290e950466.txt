TITLE: Roy Keane blasts Gary Neville in furious rant after Manchester United dismantled by Liverpool
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

A furious Roy Keane unleashed his frustration after Manchester United were hammered 4-0 by Liverpool, maintaining that the performance was every bit as dismal as the scoreline indicated, despite more measured assessments from Gary Neville and Jamie Carragher following the final whistle.
The Red Devils were thoroughly dismantled at Anfield. An early red card for Leny Yoro made an already daunting challenge even harder, and goals from Dominik Szoboszlai and Florian Wirtz left United two goals down just five minutes after Yoro's dismissal.
Rio Ngumoha added a third before substitute Víctor Muñoz rounded off another humiliating matchday for United, who were competing in their first game under Mauricio Pochettino.
Following the full-time whistle, Neville and Carragher offered their takes on the result, but Keane's assessment was considerably harsher.
Speaking from the stadium, Neville said, via The Mirror: "I'm just smiling at a small group of Manchester United fans behind the goal, who are singing 'Don't Worry' - the Bob Marley song. It's making me smile because they deserve so much better, those fans. They really do. 'Every little thing is going to be alright' - it will one day."
Carragher went even further, suggesting United "did okay" given that they faced Liverpool for the majority of the game with a man down. "They [Liverpool] were fantastic in parts of the game," said the former Liverpool defender.
"United did okay considering the situation they found themselves in, but Liverpool were absolutely devastating at the start of the game. We've questioned the attacking players, but they looked absolutely electric."
Keane, however, wasn't buying it and told Sky Sports: "If I hear that one more time from the lads in the commentary that 4-0 is not too bad when you're down to 10 men...has a team ever got a result with 10 men before?

"It can be done. I thought United were desperate. I thought they were so bad and everyone and Nev, who played for Man United saying 4-0 ain't bad."
The former Manchester United captain continued: "[The red] doesn't help but you're telling me they couldn't have done better? They lost 4-0 with 10 men, that's not too bad? Has a team ever got a result with 10 men? It can be done. At least show a bit more fight, a bit more quality; I can't believe what I'm hearing from people. You played for the club, saying 4-0 isn't too bad. Wow. Fans are applauding. Applauding what? Performing rubbish.
"Liverpool were excellent, but how easy did United make it for them? I can't believe what I'm watching sometimes.
"This team two years ago finished 15th. The last year or two, we're talking. We're blaming the board, blaming ownership, blaming managers.
"When do the players take responsibility for some decision-making? Liverpool probably should have scored eight, probably eight or nine, unbelievable chances they had. And everyone's coming off the pitch saying 4 is not too bad? I feel like swearing."
