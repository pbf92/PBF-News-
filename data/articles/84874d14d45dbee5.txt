TITLE: Anthropic adds beta feature for installing Claude artifacts as desktop apps
OUTLET: Mezha.net

Anthropic adds beta feature for installing Claude artifacts as desktop apps
Photo source: 9to5Mac
Claude artifacts installed as apps get their own icon and window, with users able to control whether they can interact with the computer.
Anthropic is rolling out “Install as app” in beta in Claude for Mac and Windows. The feature lets users create, run, and share desktop apps using Claude artifacts.
This is reported by 9to5Mac.
Starting today, you can use Claude artifacts to create, run, and share desktop apps. The “Install as app” feature is rolling out in beta in Claude for Mac and Windows.
- Anthropic
When an artifact is installed as a Mac or Windows app, it gets its own icon and opens in its own window instead of inside Claude. You can share it with others, and it needs your permission before it can interact with your computer. You choose how much to allow.
- Anthropic
Anthropic says apps made this way can benefit from Claude connectors. Claude usage is tied to desktop apps made with artifacts. The apps can send notifications, add items to the menu bar, and have their own permissions.
This is the second Claude update this week made specifically for builders.
