OUTLET: TradingView

Jackson Acquisition II Extends Business Combination Deadline by up to 12 Months
Jackson Acquisition II entered into an amended trust agreement with Continental Stock Transfer & Trust Company, allowing its board to extend the deadline for completing an initial business combination by up to 12 one-month periods, through December 11, 2027, without another shareholder vote. For each extension, the sponsor or its affiliates or designees will deposit the lesser of $250,000 or $0.02 per outstanding public share into the trust account in exchange for a non-interest-bearing, unsecured promissory note. The amendment gives the company additional time to complete a business combination.
Agreement details:
- Agreement type: Amended and restated investment management trust agreement
- Counterparty: Continental Stock Transfer & Trust Company
- Signed / Effective: Nov 24 2026 / Nov 24 2026
- Duration / Termination: Up to 12 monthly extensions, through Dec 11 2027
- Reason: Provide additional time to complete an initial business combination
Original SEC Filing: Jackson Acquisition Co II [ JACS ] - 8-K - Nov. 25, 2026
