TITLE: GTA 6 Lucia Actress Officially Revealed During 2026 Game Awards Nominations
OUTLET: Insider Gaming

The actress behind Lucia Caminos, one of the two protagonists at the centre of Grand Theft Auto 6, has been revealed. With the game’s worldwide launch now just around the corner, we’ve finally got confirmation of one of the two performers.
Manni L. Perez takes on the role of Lucia, with her identity being confirmed during The 2026 Game Awards Nominees. She’d long been suspected to be taking on the role, but that rumor has now officially been put to rest just one day ahead of release.

Jason & Lucia Finally Revealed
Thanks to The Game Awards 2026, we now know for certain that Manni L. Perez brings Lucia Caminos to life.
Manni L. Perez had long been rumored for the role, and at one point, she even hosted a livestream where viewers repeatedly asked about her connection to GTA 6, but she refused to acknowledge either the game or her character; we can only imagine how strict Rockstar’s NDAs have been.
Still, it’s great to finally put a name to one of the two characters who are already incredibly recognisable, though we’re still waiting on an official reveal for her partner in crime, Jason Duval. The pair will undoubtedly become two of the most iconic protagonists in the franchise, with Rockstar confident that Lucia can “stand tall” with the likes of Niko Bellic and Arthur Morgan.
Many were hoping for an official reveal by Rockstar, especially with the game just a day away now. However, Manni L. Perez’s nomination for Best Performance seems to have beaten Rockstar to the punch.
A complete voice cast list is yet to be revealed, but with GTA 6 now just a day away, we shouldn’t have to wait much longer to discover everyone involved. The in-game credits will eventually provide those answers, although you’ll have to put in a fair amount of time before reaching them, particularly if you’re hoping to earn that elusive Platinum trophy.
