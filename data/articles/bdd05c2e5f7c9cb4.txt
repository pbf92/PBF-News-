TITLE: The man who slipped through cops’ fingers but was caught 7 months later
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

He slipped through cops’ fingers during a dramatic Moss Side police chase. 7 months later they caught him at a fast food restaurant
Marcel Toby has been charged
A man who tried to evade police has been handed a custodial sentence after he was found to be in possession of £34,000 worth of drugs.
The man, Marcel Toby, 34, made himself know after speeding away from officers who were out on patrol in Moss Side on March 6.
He sped off and ran a red light before hitting a member of public who was driving a van, GMP said. Thankfully, the innocent party wasn't injured.
Toby's vehicle then skidded to a halt and the driver was seen 'rummaging' around the driver's area before leaving the car. He took a few steps away from the vehicle, and then returned to grab something and finally made off on foot.
Officers found nothing in an area search but found a large quantity of class A substances from the driver side rear passenger seat in a plastic bag during a preliminary search of the vehicle.
The drugs seized had a street value of £34,000 from pre court valuation, GMP said.
Toby, of Littler Avenue, Manchester, left his mobile phone in the vehicle and was identified by local officers at the time of the incident. He then went on the run from police for 7 months - before being captured out of force at a fast-food restaurant.
Following a section 18 search of Toby’s new property, a large amount of Cannabis was found along with items used in drug supply.
Multiple high value items were also found in the property, designer clothes, bags, a Rolex watch, and a custom-made ring. These items were all seized under POCA. Valuations from Rolex confirmed a value of £40,000 - £50,000 and Custom Jewellers who made the ring valued at £3500.

Toby was charged with driving a motor vehicle dangerously; possession with intent to supply a controlled drug – Class A – Heroin; possession with intent to supply a controlled drug – Class A – Crack Cocaine; possession with intent to supply a controlled drug – Class A – Cocaine; and acquire/Use/Possess criminal property.
Toby has given a plea of guilty to all charges and has received a custodial sentence of 56 months along with a driving ban for 40 months.
PS Gibb, Moss Side Neighbourhood Sergeant, said: "This conviction and 56-month custodial sentence sends a clear message that drug dealing will not be tolerated in our community.
"By removing an individual responsible for supplying illegal drugs, we have disrupted criminal activity, reduced the harm caused by drug misuse, and helped make the area safer for residents, businesses, and young people.
"This result demonstrates our commitment to protecting vulnerable people and improving the quality of life for everyone who lives and works in the community."
