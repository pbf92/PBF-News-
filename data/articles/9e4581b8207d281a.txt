Anthropic shares fall over 6% in US premarket trading after Nasdaq debut
Claude developer gives back part of its first-day gains as Nvidia and AMD extend declines amid concerns over AI spending and US export restrictions
Mucahithan Avcioglu
20 November 2026•Update: 20 November 2026
ISTANBUL
Shares of US artificial intelligence company Anthropic fell more than 6% in premarket trading on Friday, giving back part of their Nasdaq debut gains as chipmakers Nvidia and AMD also declined.
Anthropic, the developer of AI chatbot Claude, traded at $91.04 as of 1050GMT, down $6.13, or 6.31%, from Thursday's closing price.
Despite the decline, shares remained approximately 9.7% above their initial public offering (IPO) price of $83.
Anthropic closed its first Nasdaq trading session on Thursday at $97.17, gaining 17.07% from its offering price.
The company sold 980 million shares in the IPO, raising $81.34 billion.
Nvidia, AMD extend losses amid AI concerns
Other major technology stocks also declined in premarket trading Friday.
Nvidia shares fell 2.67%, while AMD dropped 5.43% as of 1000GMT.
The declines came amid investor concerns about how quickly technology companies could generate returns on their AI investments.
Uncertainty over tighter US export controls and their potential impact on chip sales to China also weighed on sentiment.
AMD had already lost more than 7% during Thursday's trading session after short seller Hunterbrook Media raised concerns about the company's business relationships in China.
Hunterbrook alleged that AMD firmware had been found at entities subject to US trade restrictions.
AMD disputed the report's characterization of its business relationships and said it complies with US export controls.
Separately, documents released by the US House Foreign Affairs Committee showed that the Commerce Department had approved sales of more than 879,000 AI chips to Chinese companies.
