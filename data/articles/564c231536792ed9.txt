TITLE: Wondering why your friends are MIA? ‘Grand Theft Auto VI’ might be to blame
OUTLET: NBC News

Between spikes in vacation day requests and college professors reportedly granting extensions to their students, the Thursday release of Grand Theft Auto VI could bring America (or the world) to a halt.
GTA VI, which will be available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, is the first release in the Grand Theft Auto series since 2013.
Rockstar Games, the publisher behind the company, reportedly spent more than $1 billion to simply make the game. That’s more than the rumored budgets of the upcoming “Avengers” and “Dune” movies combined.
As of Wednesday, the game has notched a whopping 99/100 on Metacritic, which distills reviews from around the Internet. That ties it with Legend of Zelda Ocarina of Time for highest rated game of all time.
Here’s everything you need to know about why GTA VI is shaping up to be one of the biggest cultural events of the year.
The game made $1.5 billion in prelaunch sales
GTA VI is the eighth entry in the main Grand Theft Auto series, which doesn’t include spinoffs and expansion packs. The series began with Grand Theft Auto in 1997, but entered a new era in 2001 with the release of Grand Theft Auto III, the first in the series to feature a three-dimensional world.
Since then, Rockstar Games released Grand Theft Auto: Vice City in 2002, Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas in 2004, Grand Theft Auto IV in 2008 and Grand Theft Auto V in 2013.
The 13-year gap between V and VI is the longest wait between games in the main series.
In 2025, more than a full year before the release of the game, the second trailer for GTA VI racked up more than 475 million views, Rockstar told The Hollywood Reporter at the time.
The company ramped up its promotion this year by partnering with Netflix to release a 27-minute video of gameplay on the streaming service. The extended look drew 31.1 million viewers in one weekend. That’s more than the 2026 average viewership of “Sunday Night Football.”
As of Tuesday, the game, which costs $79.99 for the standard edition, has sold nearly 16 million copies, earning Rockstar more than $1.5 billion already, according to SensorTower, an analytics company that tracks market performance in the gaming industry.
Why do fans care so much? “It’s a really good question,” said Bart Spiegel, the global media, entertainment, gaming and sports leader at PricewaterhouseCoopers, one of the world‘s “Big Four” professional service firms. “The easiest way to explain is it’s been 13 years in the making, and they spent over a billion dollars. So it’s really about the level of effort that’s gone into this game, and just everybody waiting for it.”
Even before its release, GTA’s latest installment was recognized as a nominee in the game of the year category for the upcoming Game Awards, which are being held in December. Nominees were chosen by a global jury of more than 100 media publications and creators.
Geoff Keighley, the CEO of The Game Awards, was not surprised that the Rockstar game made the cut, despite not being released to the general public yet.
“What makes the game so unique is its scale and ambition, and the freedom it gives players to explore a simulated virtual world,” he wrote in an email to NBC News.
People are already canceling their plans to play
To Keighley’s point, what’s allowed for the Grand Theft Auto series to endure is the freedom and playable hours it affords consumers.
Completing the game’s missions will likely take around 50 hours, according to the publication Insider Gaming. But completing every single side task to pursue “platinum” status could push playtime up to 200 hours.
And that doesn’t include time players can spend in the game’s world doing a variety of activities of their choosing, which include but are not limited to: working out, gambling, scuba diving, petting animals, going to a strip club, hijacking cars, jetskiing, robbing banks and even watching television.
For the first time in the game’s history, players will be allowed to toggle between the perspectives of Jason (a male) and Lucia (a female), who exhibit a Bonnie-and-Clyde dynamic as the player follows their story.
Simply put, playing through GTA VI can be a weekslong commitment if someone wants it to be. And that is almost definitely selling it short.
Miami leans into its alter ego: Vice City
The story of GTA VI takes place in the fictional Vice City, seemingly the most thinly veiled representation of Miami. Other locations on the game’s map are inspired by the Everglades and the Florida Keys.
The game’s first trailer, which was released in 2023 and set to Tom Petty’s “Love is a Long Road,” contained several references to incidents from Florida.
The city of Miami has not shied away from the game, accepting an advertisement package from Rockstar for $3.9 million.
“With millions of people around the world waiting for this release, we have an opportunity to make sure Miami-Dade benefits from that attention,” Miami-Dade County Mayor Daniella Levine Cava said in October. “These agreements allow us to turn that excitement into additional revenue for our community.”
Among those participating are the NBA’s Miami Heat, who will play on a special, Vice City-themed court at their home arena Wednesday. The Heat also partnered with Rockstar on a merch drop, and the roof of the three-time NBA champion’s arena is emblazoned with the words, “Welcome to Vice City.”
Rockstar employees told Dazed magazine that it took years of on-the-ground exploration in Florida to shape the game’s world.
A team of almost 5,000 people worked on the game, according to The Guardian, with a lot of attention being put into the details.
Rockstar even built a studio dedicated entirely to the look and behavior of nonplayable characters (NPCs) — those are characters players don’t even control and only sometimes interact with. You can even buy the game just to look at birds, of which there more than 62 species.
“We will meet with ex-criminals, law enforcement, club promoters, gun dealers and anyone notorious or influential who’ll take our calls,” Rupert Humphries, Rockstar’s senior vice president of narrative, said. “We hang out, get a feeling for the place, listen to the stories, cross-reference that with books and journalism, and try to get to the heart of which characters will bring the city we are creating to life.”
The developers said year that this GTA VI world will be “more grounded and reactive than before,” meaning nonplayer characters will react appropriately to players who walk down the street with rifles.
GTA VI will also feature changes in the protagonists’ physical condition akin to the system in Rockstar’s acclaimed Red Dead Redemption 2. That means, for example, Jason and Lucia can gain or lose weight, build muscle and incur physical markers of exhaustion.
‘One of the biggest entertainment events of the decade’
GTA VI is expected to not only cross cultural lines, but also generational ones.
“The average gamer age is like high 30s to low 40s or something like that. Don’t think it’s just 18-year-old, 19-year-old, or teenage boys that are going to be playing,” Spiegel said. “You’re going to see this across all demographics.”
His own son was only 6 when the last GTA came out. Now that he’s 19, Spiegel said, he’s as excited to play as his dad is.
The gaming industry, which has in recent years been hit with massive layoffs, also has high hopes for the game. GTA VI leads the pack of Game Awards nominees, racking eight total nods.
“I expect [GTA VI] to be one of the biggest entertainment events of the decade,” Keighley said, noting the game’s “reach goes well beyond those holding the controller.”
“People will encounter it through livestreams, clips, music and conversations with friends,” he said. “That’s what makes this such a significant moment: a game can become the thing everyone is talking about.”
