TITLE: OpenAI countersues Apple for allegedly stealing trade secrets
OUTLET: Washington Examiner

OpenAI is accusing Apple of stealing trade secrets, escalating a legal battle between the technology giants over competing efforts to develop artificial intelligence-powered devices.
In a countersuit filed Friday in federal court in the Northern District of California, OpenAI alleged a former employee who briefly worked for the ChatGPT maker took dozens of confidential documents containing trade secrets and information about unreleased products when he returned to Apple late last year.
