(UPD) Update 01.002.002 for GTA VI is currenty being rolled out
, by Thibaut
Update from November 23, 2026, at 2:53 p.m.
This new update for GTA VI includes fixes for game crashes, as well as issues with the weather not changing for extended periods and problems with automatic saves. Bugs that prevented players from seeing their wanted level decrease, caused phone calls to fail to trigger properly, or prevented them from lifting the deadlift bar at the gym have been fixed too.
Original article
Rockstar Games has released a new patch for GTA VI. It's the free update 01.002.002, whose existence was revealed by PlayStation Game Size on X.
The update is 603 MB. We don’t know what it contains yet. It likely includes bug fixes and additional optimizations. Note that 33% of players can download it right now, and 66% will be able to get it in three hours. Everyone should have it within six hours.
