TITLE: Raheem Sterling defends JJ Gabriel as Manchester United debate legal action
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Raheem Sterling has come to the aid of JJ Gabriel, calling on Jamie Redknapp and Tim Sherwood to 'cut him some slack'. The 16-year-old has faced intense criticism from several quarters for making such a public spectacle of his exit, despite never featuring in a competitive fixture for Manchester United.
Gabriel, who arrived at the Reds in 2020 as a 10-year-old, recently confirmed his departure through a heartfelt Instagram video, expressing gratitude to coaches and teammates for their guidance. Notably, the announcement came just one day after Epic Sports Agency was revealed as the youngster's new representation.
While his destination wasn't disclosed, he's anticipated to sign with Real Madrid. On an episode of 'The Overlap Daily', Redknapp and Sherwood weighed in on the teenager's social media activity.
The former Liverpool midfielder opened, via the Manchester Evening News: "He's 16 years of age, he left Manchester United, and he looks set to be joining Real Madrid. And what he's done this week is put out two videos."
The former Tottenham Hotspur manager interrupted and said: "Yeah, I've got a problem with that. I just think it's unnecessary scrutiny, what he's bringing upon himself or his management.
"I've never heard of the agency before, but I have now. Perhaps that's what they wanted, you know, to put the agency out there.
"But first and foremost, they have to look after the player. I think he's going to get enough scrutiny as his career goes on."
Redknapp said: "He's an academy kid, Tim. He's had zero appearances for Manchester United, and he's saying a goodbye video."

Sherwood replied: "Who's he saying thank you to? The youth coaches who brought him through. I think the thank you would have been maybe stick around and play in the first team. That's what they would have enjoyed."
Redknapp had the final say, stating: "In his defense, this is what every young kid does now. When they deregister from a club [or] not played a game, Tim, It's like a bit of a running joke in a WhatsApp group.
"When a player that hasn't played a first team game starts saying thank you... if you liked it that much and you're thanking them, why don't you just stay?
"But you're leaving one club because you think you've got something better. But he is not the first to do this.
"Because everyone now will be looking at it going, 'Oh, he's trying to do something'. This is the norm. This is what probably every young academy kid thinks is what you have to do now, saying thank you to my coaches, thank you to Man United, and I'm not comfortable with it either, Tim."
A clip of the exchange between the two Sky Sports analysts was posted to Instagram, amassing nearly 7,000 likes within hours. Among approximately 250 comments, Sterling jumped to Gabriel's defense, urging Redknapp and Sherwood to 'cut him some slack'.
The former Manchester City, Liverpool and Chelsea winger, currently without a club, wrote: "Just wish him the best on his journey, Ultimately it's his career cut him some slack, JJ all the best mate."
His comment attracted more likes than any other response beneath the post.
Meanwhile, United have been unable to establish contact with Gabriel's family, according to the Manchester Evening News. The teenager has been working with an individual coach in Spain, initially in Castelldefels, Barcelona, before relocating to Madrid ahead of a potential move to Los Blancos.
As things stand, it remains unclear whether Gabriel will definitively join José Mourinho's side. However, United are reportedly prepared to pursue legal action should evidence of any illegal approach from Real Madrid come to light.
