STERIS Raises $1.5 Billion Through Senior Notes to Fund Xodus Acquisition
STERIS, through its subsidiary STERIS Irish FinCo, completed a $1.5 billion senior notes offering, consisting of $750 million of 5.650% notes due 2033 and $750 million of 5.875% notes due 2036. The notes are guaranteed by STERIS and two subsidiaries, and proceeds are intended to help fund the acquisition of Xodus Medical, related expenses, and general corporate needs.
Agreement details:
- Agreement type: Senior notes issuance
- Counterparty: Note investors
- Signed / Effective: Nov 24 2026 / Nov 24 2026
- Duration / Termination: Maturing Dec 15 2033 and Dec 15 2036
- Reason: Fund the Xodus acquisition and support general corporate needs
Original SEC Filing: STERIS plc [ STE ] - 8-K - Nov. 24, 2026
