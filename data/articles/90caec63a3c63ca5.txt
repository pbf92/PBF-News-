TITLE: eBay sellers are chasing Anthropic IPO hype by hawking $1,800 hoodies and $100 tote bags
OUTLET: Business Insider

Anthropic's massively hyped IPO didn't save the AI trade, but some eBay merchants are hoping for big gains in other items tied to the AI lab.
Anthropic's historic IPO came and went on Thursday, with the stock popping on day one but falling sharply on day two. The millionaire-minting event may be helping clothing resellers cash out, and eBay pages are awash in clothing and other items with the Claude maker's branding.
An eBay search for "Anthropic" reveals a wide variety of items and some high price tags. The company doesn't sell merchandise but many sellers claim to have procured official items.
"This is an extremely rare Anthropic hoodie only issued to employees at the company and it is never sold anywhere else," wrote one seller. "Anthropic does not directly sell any merchandise. So this is an extremely rare hoodie only available to employees and swag collectors."
The hoodie has a starting bid set of $1,350, or a "buy now" price of $1,800 — and that's not the most expensive item. Another seller is offering a baseball that they say was signed by Dario Amodei and later authenticated for $2,099, or best offer.
The seller's page says that Amodei signed it during a July 21 event at the White House, though they offered no additional context about why the tech CEO was asked to sign the ball.
Other sellers are offering Claude API keys, which can allow users to connect the chatbot to other programs. These keys would be delivered virtually. Anthropic has warned that buying these codes from third party vendors poses security risks.
Most of the Anthropic merch going on eBay seem to be more fashion than tech. While there isn't quite as much variety as there was following the SpaceX IPO, Anthropic fans still have plenty to choose from
Here's a run down of a few items that stand out.
- An Anthropic Claude tote bag with pin and stickers: $100.
- One beige Claude t-shirt with the bot's name and logo: $100.
- Black Anthropic "Keep Thinking" baseball cap: $90
- A two-inch tall figurine with Claude's logo - $85
A look at eBay's sold items reveals that buyers have been snapping up the "thinking caps" that have become a hot commodity among tech enthusiasts. Multiple $90 hats were sold in the week leading up to the IPO for as much as $125.
Someone snapped up a set of two Claude pins for $65, while a buyer purchased a Claude's Constitution by Anthropic's in-house philosopher Amanda Askell, described by the seller as a first print and limited edition, for $500.
They may have gotten a better deal, though, than the person who paid $820 for a sealed copy of Dario Amodei's Machines of Loving Grace, an essay that can be read for free on the CEO's webpage.
