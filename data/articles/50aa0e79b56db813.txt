OUTLET: TradingView

Markel Group boosts share repurchase authorization by $2.5B
- Markel Group announced on Wednesday that its board approved a $2.5B increase to its existing share repurchase authorization.
- The company may repurchase shares through open-market or privately negotiated transactions.
- The authorization has no expiration date, and the timing and amount of repurchases will depend on market conditions and capital needs.
