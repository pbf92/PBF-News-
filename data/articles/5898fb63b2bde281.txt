Shopify, NXP Semiconductors among technology stocks to join Russell 3000 index; One Stop Systems, Veritone to exit
Less than 1 min read
 FTSE Russell has released a preliminary list of companies set to join or leave the Russell 3000 Index as part of its 2026 annual reconstitution.   The technology companies that are set to join the index include Shopify SHOP, NXP Semiconductors NXPI, Firy FIRY, Netskope NTSK, and Quantum Corp QMCO, among others.   The telecommunications companies that are slated to leave the index include One Stop Systems OSS, Veritone VERI, Atomera ATOM, Soluna Holdings SLNH, and Intellicheck IDN.   The newly reconstituted indexes take effect after the U.S. market close on December 11.
