Claude maker Anthropic prices record $81.34B IPO at $83 per share
Offering surpasses SpaceX's $75B initial sale, values AI company at about $1.5T on fully diluted basis
Mucahithan Avcioglu
19 November 2026•Update: 19 November 2026
ISTANBUL
Anthropic, the US artificial intelligence company behind Claude, priced its initial public offering at $83 per share Wednesday, putting the 980 million-share sale at $81.34 billion and making it the world's largest IPO at initial pricing.
The price, above the company's initial $70-$78 range, values Anthropic at approximately $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis. Its shares are expected to begin trading Thursday on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT.
The offering surpasses the $75 billion base IPO priced by SpaceX in June. SpaceX's completed offering subsequently reached approximately $86 billion following the exercise of its underwriters' option to purchase additional shares.
Anthropic's offering comprises 956,351,354 shares sold by the company, generating approximately $79.38 billion before fees, and 23,648,646 shares sold by existing shareholders for about $1.96 billion.
Anthropic will not receive proceeds from shares sold by existing shareholders.
The company has also granted underwriters a 30-day option to purchase another 147 million shares, worth approximately $12.2 billion at the offering price. If fully exercised, the total public offering would reach $93.54 billion.
Seven technology companies have separately agreed to invest a combined $26 billion in Anthropic through a private placement at the IPO price, according to its earlier prospectus. The investors are AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron Technology, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners.
Together, the shares sold by Anthropic in the public offering and the separate private placement would generate approximately $105.38 billion for the company before fees, excluding any additional shares sold to underwriters.
The IPO is expected to close Friday, Nov. 20, subject to customary closing conditions.
Revenue rises 13-fold as computing commitments approach $900B
Founded in 2021, San Francisco-based Anthropic began selling access to Claude in early 2023. It earns revenue from subscriptions and customers who pay according to how much they use its models.
In the first nine months of 2026, usage-based revenue rose by $24.2 billion from a year earlier, while subscription revenue increased by $6.5 billion.
Revenue reached about $17 billion in the third quarter alone, taking the nine-month total to $33.2 billion, roughly 13 times the level a year earlier. The company's five largest customers accounted for about a quarter of nine-month revenue, though no single customer contributed more than 10%.
Anthropic reported a $104 billion net loss for the nine months. Of that, $94.3 billion reflected changes in the value of financing agreements, mainly convertible notes that became more valuable as the company's own valuation rose.
The company also disclosed approximately $895.4 billion in commitments for computing capacity and infrastructure, including data centers. About $70 billion was due within 12 months.
