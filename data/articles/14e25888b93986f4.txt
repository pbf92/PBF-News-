Altimeter's Brad Gerstner to invest $5B in Anthropic IPO - CNBC
Altimeter Capital plans to invest $5 billion in the Anthropic ANT IPO late this week, Altimeter's Brad Gerstner said earlier.
“I think this is a cornerstone compounder for a long period of time,” Gerstner said in a CNBC interview earlier on Monday, saying his investment would be five times the amount he’s ever put into an IPO. “It’s where the trade is going. I think they are going to benefit the most and so we want to continue to be a major shareholder of Anthropic."
Anthropic ANT is scheduled to price its shares on Wednesday and begin trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on Thursday, and is targeting a valuation as high as $1.4 trillion
Anthropic's ANT IPO has drawn about six times the orders for the shares available, Bloomberg reported last week.
