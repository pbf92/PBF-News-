TITLE: GTA 6 removes controversial franchise feature as players declare it 'makes no sense', but it does
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

GTA 6 is finally here and as expected, it’s the highest-rated game of 2026, but it’s already run into its first controversy.
When reviews went live on November 18, all you could see were 10/10 scores. Our own GTA VI review was written by yours truly, and I’ve got nothing but praise for what Rockstar Games has accomplished, especially after speaking with the studio’s co-lead Rob Nelson.
Now that GTA VI is in the hands of gamers across the world though, we’ve been tuning into the discourse to learn what they love and what they hate. While the praise continues to pour in, one aspect of the game hasn’t gone down well with the more – shall we say – ‘down bad’ players?
Advert
GTA VI has removed prostitution, which has been a controversial staple of Grand Theft Auto games for decades now.
In an interview with Shacknews (via Vice), Rockstar confirmed the decision wasn’t made lightly, but ultimately made sense due to the game’s story. The new protagonists Jason and Lucia can be as close or as cold as the player would like, but they’re ultimately stuck together, so Rockstar wanted the ‘extracurricular’ activities to reflect that.
“GTAVI is a game about a couple in a bind, lovers on the run. While we have taken a lot forward from previous GTA titles, everything we have implemented in VI has been done to support the spirit of the story we wanted to tell,” read a statement from Rockstar.
GTA 6 players are up in arms over the lack of prostitutes
Over on the GTA VI subreddit, fans are challenging Rockstar’s decision to remove this feature, with u/Mr_Revan22 believing it “makes no sense at all”.
They went on to say: “It’s been an iconic feature in the franchise for a long time, but they suddenly decide to scrap it because Jason has to stay loyal to Lucia. Did we just forget that Michael was able to pick up hookers and cheat on his wife in GTA 5?”
While I can see their point, let’s not forget that Michael De Santa was not a good man. He had a history of cheating on Amanda long before the events of GTA V, but immediately chose violence when Amanda cheated on him with her tennis instructor.
Being able to hire a prostitute may have been a truism in previous Grand Theft Auto games, but anyone who says it’s their favourite part of the game is lying. I played well over 70 hours for my review, and I didn’t even notice it was missing.
Rockstar’s reasoning behind this removal makes perfect sense, as Jason and Lucia are a monogamous couple. It could have very easily given players the option of an open relationship, but when you play the story, it’s understandable how that could get messy very quickly.
On the bright side, you can still take the duo on a romantic date to their local strip club. That should count for something, right?
