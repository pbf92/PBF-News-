TITLE: Microsoft Unveils Biggest Bidding Upgrade In A Decade 11/18/2026
OUTLET: MediaPost

Microsoft Advertising on Wednesday began rolling out what the company calls its biggest upgrade to automated bidding models in a decade -- promising faster optimization, more consistent performance
and better budget pacing.
The company outlined the rollout in a blog post on Wednesday, adding that internal global data tracked the redesign as powering 10% more conversion value per click
for advertisers globally using value-based bidding strategies. The measurement for the bidding models took place in August 2026.
This is the first phase of the upgrade, and more advertisers
will see the technology over time. Additional bidding improvements are planned for 2027.
These upgraded bidding models aim to deliver three major improvements such as consistent performance in
campaigns that meet cost per acquisition (CPA) and return on ad spend (ROAS) targets more consistently without sacrificing conversion volume or value.
Because of the way Microsoft engineers
designed the changes, bidding systems should respond nearly twice as quickly when advertisers change their CPA or ROAS targets, providing rapid optimization.

Budgets are intended to pace more
evenly throughout the day, so campaigns can reach full budget use more rapidly to gain more predictable delivery of campaigns.
Microsoft also shared data around how the upgraded bidding models
impacted campaigns. The company said that when advertisers use "Maximize Conversion Value" or "Target ROAS," these improvements have delivered on average 10% more conversion value per click at a
similar ROAS. 
Each bid depends on a prediction of the likelihood that a click will convert into a sale, and the projected value of that purchase.
To sharpen those estimates, the
company has overhauled its core prediction model, integrating new user signals alongside a new method for valuing each conversion.
The prediction model was rebuilt with new user signals and
what Microsoft called "a better way of valuing each conversion." Following the change, Microsoft said, advertisers have been experiencing stronger results.
Internal data suggests that
advertisers that switched their Search campaigns from Target CPA to Target ROAS saw 8% greater conversion value on average, while maintaining a similar return on ad spend following the
switch. The strongest improvements came when advertisers optimized using actual transaction values like purchase revenue. This test was run globally between August and
September in 2026.
Microsoft also reported an additional improvement, with 18% more conversions. Advertisers that are switching from "Enhanced CPC" (eCPC) to Maximize
Conversions with a CPA target saw an average 18% increase in conversions.
Microsoft did not include information in the post on the number of advertisers or campaigns included in the
analysis, sample sizes used to calculate the averages, specific industries or advertiser categories that benefited most, distribution of results across individual campaigns. and
if smaller advertisers experienced similar improvements to larger accounts
Microsoft's latest automated bidding upgrade signals a continued push toward autonomous-driven campaign
management, with a greater emphasis on conversion value, faster optimization and more predictable budget delivery.
While the reported performance gains are based on Microsoft's internal data
and may vary across advertisers, the changes highlight the growing importance of accurate conversion tracking and value-based bidding.
For advertisers, the biggest opportunity may be in moving
beyond simply generating conversions and focusing on actual revenue, bidding improvements and the business value that those conversions deliver.
