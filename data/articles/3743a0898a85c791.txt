TITLE: Rob Nelson explains why Tennis & Theatres was removed from GTA 6
OUTLET: RockstarINTEL

In a recent Interview, Rob Nelson the Head Of Development at Rockstar North explained to PC Games why certain activities such as playing Tennis & going to the Cinema didn’t make it to the final version GTA6.
Its been a week since Grand Theft Auto VI released, and we’ve been absolutely loving it, we cant put down the controller, there has been so much to do and see its almost impossible to get bored of this game. One thing people have noticed though is that certain popular activities in past GTA Games haven’t made it into GTA6. From visiting Cinemas and Theatres, playing Tennis and Darts and even doing Taxi Missions which have been in every Grand Theft Auto game since GTA III back in 2001.
PC Games had an interview with Rob Nelson who explained why some of these activities haven’t made it into the final version of Grand Theft Auto VI
Tennis Removed
When PCGames asked Rob Nelson and Scott Mitchell about cut content, they asked why Tennis wasnt in the game, which was a popular minigame in Grand Theft Auto V (Personally my favourite sport minigame) and they confirmed Tennis was actually planned to be in GTA6 but they weren’t satisfied with the implementation. So the Minigame got removed and scarpped.
Cinema & Theatres
Another feature of being able to visit Cinemas and Theatres was also removed from the final version of GTA6, another feature that’s been in past GTAS and even in the Red Dead Series. Personally, this one doesn’t really bother me as i never used them but i do understand why so many people are annoyed by this one.
Rob Nelson and Scott Mitchell said in the interview that Rockstar Games deemed Cinema & Theatre visits not interactive enough. Which i dont personally think that’s a good enough reason as sometimes its good to not do much interaction.
They also confirmed other features were considered and didn’t end up making it to the game, one feature was for Jason and Lucia to crawl across the ground, Craft Items and Furnish their homes. These were scrapped due to not fitting the story.
Rest of the Interview
They asked Rob Nelson which feature in GTA6 is Rockstar Games most proud of, and the answer was ‘the dynamic of Jason and Lucia’s relationship.’ A lot of passion went into creating the characters.
