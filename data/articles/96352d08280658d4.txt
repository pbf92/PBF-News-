GTA 6's Most Unrealistic Detail Has Nothing To Do With The Game's Graphics
The world that Rockstar Games has built in "Grand Theft Auto 6" is one of the most realistic we've ever seen in a video game. From the police AI that chases you down with smart tactics as part of the game's Wanted system to the NPCs that uniquely respond to your actions in-game, nearly all of the moments you spend in "GTA 6" feel more life-like than any other game out there. Of course, we're still talking about a video game, so it's reasonable to expect that "GTA 6" will still be unrealistic in more ways than one.
Indeed, the protagonists in "GTA 6" can get shot multiple times and walk away without a scratch, while smoking cigarettes improves your stats rather than harming them. So, again, not every action you take is rooted in realism. However, there's one particularly underrated aspect of "GTA 6" that comes off feeling more unrealistic than anything else, and it concerns the items you can purchase across the state of Leonida.
Clothing and groceries are unrealistically priced in GTA 6
In the current real-life economic climate, it seems like everything has gotten more and more expensive lately. Whether you're going out to eat, buying clothes at a retail store, or doing something as monotonous as purchasing eggs or milk, it feels as though you're spending more than you used to five years ago. However, while the real world's prices have steadily been rising, it looks like Rockstar Games wants "GTA 6" to be an escape from unreasonable receipts.
Specifically, when you go out to buy clothes or food items in "GTA 6," most of what you're purchasing seems far less expensive than you'd normally expect. As an example, a beer at the Rusty Anchor in Key Lento can cost as low as $4, which is unheard of in most real-world bars nowadays. A higher-quality beer or cocktail will cost slightly more, but still not as much as you might think.
Another example involves your trips to a nearby grocery store, which you have to take a few times throughout the game. Drinks, fruit, and items like protein bars are all priced reasonably, and you can exit the store with multiple bags of groceries for well under $50.
Naturally, this is still a video game, and Rockstar knows that it needs to price items lower in "GTA 6" so players will be more willing to buy them. With the rest of the game being so realistic, though, it can throw you off when you walk into a regular clothing store and spend $100 buying a full outfit. Yes, hats, shirts, pants, shoes, and accessories in most stores mostly cost around $20-30, allowing you to fill up your wardrobe with dozens of clothes before you even make it out of Key Lento.
Some stores still gauge you on prices in GTA 6
Of course, not everything is going to be so cheap in "GTA 6," especially as you start to discover some of the richer areas in Leonida. And while you can buy a ton of items for cheap in areas like Key Lento, Port Gellhorn, and Ambrosia, the same can't be said for locations such as Capri and Vice Beach.
When you visit those locations, you'll find that every single purchasable item has been jacked up in price. Vehicle garages cost double or even triple what they do in other spots, while gyms cost more in membership purely for their location. As for clothes? Forget about spending on a budget. Some high-class retail stores offer single clothing items that run into the thousands of dollars, and that's just for a hat or an accessory.
So, while Rockstar definitely made some stores offer unrealistic prices for clothes, food, and other items, don't expect that same unrealistic treatment all across Leonida in "GTA 6." In some spots, you can expect to be utterly shocked at the receipt that comes your way following a lengthy shopping spree, just like in the real world.
