Raheem Sterling delivers JJ Gabriel truth bomb as Man United consider legal action
Raheem Sterling has had his say on JJ Gabriel and his decision to leave Manchester United.
Raheem Sterling has leapt to the defence of JJ Gabriel, urging Jamie Redknapp and Tim Sherwood to ‘cut him some slack’. The 16-year-old has been heavily scrutinised by many for making a song and dance about his departure, having never made a competitive appearance for Manchester United.
Gabriel, who joined the Reds in 2020 at the age of 10, recently announced his exit in an emotional video on Instagram, thanking coaches and teammates for their support. Interestingly, the post was uploaded just a day after Epic Sports Agency were named as the teenager’s new representatives.
Although there was no mention of his next club, he’s expected to join Real Madrid. During an episode of The Overlap Daily, Redknapp and Sherwood discussed the teenager’s post on social media.
The former Liverpool midfielder began: "He's 16 years of age, he left Manchester United, and he looks set to be joining Real Madrid. And what he's done this week is put out two videos."

The former Tottenham Hotspur boss interjected and said: "Yeah, I've got a problem with that. I just think it's unnecessary scrutiny, what he's bringing upon himself or his management.
"I've never heard of the agency before, but I have now. Perhaps that's what they wanted, you know, to put the agency out there.
"But first and foremost, they have to look after the player. I think he's going to get enough scrutiny as his career goes on."
Redknapp said: "He's an academy kid, Tim. He's had zero appearances for Manchester United, and he's saying a goodbye video."
Sherwood replied: "Who's he saying thank you to? The youth coaches who brought him through. I think the thank you would have been maybe stick around and play in the first team. That's what they would have enjoyed."
Redknapp had the last word and said: "In his defence, this is what every young kid does now. When they deregister from a club [or] not played a game, Tim, It's like a bit of a running joke in a WhatsApp group.
"When a player that hasn't played a first team game starts saying thank you… if you liked it that much and you're thanking them, why don't you just stay?
"But you're leaving one club because you think you've got something better. But he is not the first to do this.
"Because everyone now will be looking at it going, 'Oh, he's trying to do something'. This is the norm. This is what probably every young academy kid thinks is what you have to do now, saying thank you to my coaches, thank you to Man United, and I'm not comfortable with it either, Tim."
The exchange between the two Sky Sports pundits was clipped up and uploaded to Instagram; within hours, it had garnered nearly 7,000 likes. Among the 250 comments or so, Sterling leapt to Gabriel’s defence, urging Redknapp and Sherwood to 'cut him some slack'.
The former Manchester City, Liverpool and Chelsea winger, who’s currently a free agent, wrote: "Just wish him the best on his journey, Ultimately it’s his career cut him some slack, JJ all the best mate."
The comment chalked up more likes than any other under the post.
In the meantime, United have been unable to establish contact with Gabriel’s family, the Manchester Evening News understands. The teenager has been working with an individual coach in Spain, first in Castelldefels, Barcelona, and now in Madrid ahead of a transfer to Los Blancos.
As it stands, it remains to be seen whether Gabriel will definitely join Jose Mourinho’s side. But the Reds are understood to be ready to take legal action, should evidence of tapping up from Real Madrid emerge.
