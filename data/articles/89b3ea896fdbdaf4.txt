TITLE: Man United have had 'handshake' for 'two-year' Mauricio Pochettino extension - club adviser
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United have pencilled in a "two-year" extension for new manager Mauricio Pochettino if the early stages of his reign go to plan.
That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Red Devils may have already had a "handshake" with the Argentine over a planned longer-term stay.
This comes amid the news that the 54-year-old has only signed an initial two-and-a-half-year contract to become United's new manager.
However, Wyness believes that United will already be planning an extension to that deal this time next year, if Pochettino performs well.
The Manchester giants have had a horrific record with managers in recent years, but have high hopes that Pochettino could finally steady the ship.
They have chased him for several years now, and the timing has finally aligned for him to take charge at Old Trafford.
Mauricio Pochettino may be on rolling contract
Everton's former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Pochettino may effectively be on a rolling contract at Old Trafford.
He expects the new boss' future to be reviewed again in 12 months' time, before the club choose whether to make a further commitment to him.
Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness tried to explain United's thinking behind the short contract.
"I think their thinking is that we're going to give him the first year, see how he gets on, and then we'll extend at that stage," he said.
"There will probably have been a handshake right now.
"I would think that if he does well and they're happy after the end of this season, another two-year extension could go on to that contract.
"So that's how I think United are going to play it."

Football Insider revealed earlier this week that Monaco are ready to sell striker Folarin Balogun to United in January.
Pochettino is a "big fan" of the 25-year-old, and is keen to add him to his options at the top of the pitch this winter.
Sources say that the door is open for United to bring Balogun in, with all parties open to the idea of a move.
Monaco are searching for a big sale to improve their financial situation, and they are ready to offload Balogun once again.
