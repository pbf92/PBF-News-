TITLE: What Mikel Arteta really thinks about Mauricio Pochettino taking the Man Utd job
OUTLET: Football London

Mikel Arteta has revealed that Mauricio Pochettino is “really motivated” to succeed with Manchester United.
Having played together at Paris Saint-Germain, they then locked heads as coaches while the Argentine took charge of Chelsea. And Arteta has admitted to phoning his former team-mate to congratulate him on his new job.
Pochettino’s return completes the quartet of coaches, including David Moyes, Andoni Iraola and Xabi Alonso, that the Spaniard has such affinity for.
“Yeah, when I look at it, it's quite crazy because four people that have been really important in my life and I know them for so many years are managing now four top clubs in the Premier League,” he said.
“That’s the beauty of it. I spoke to him; he seems delighted, really happy, really motivated about what is going ahead of him. So yeah, wishing him the best of luck.”

Pochettino’s training session was disturbed after a drone was used to film at Carrington with the footage posted online before the operator then removed the video from YouTube and apologised.
Arsenal’s League Cup defeat was delayed for eight minutes after a drone hovered over Highbury stadium, and with this new incident, Arteta admitted that he doesn’t yet know how this modern issue could be prevented.
“Well, very delicate, obviously,” he said. “We had an episode as well against Fleetwood; you see what is happening around the world as well.
“So yeah, I don't know what the solution is. Obviously, the technology is growing, the ability to get into systems is growing at a really rapid rate.
“We need to be very aware of that, but I cannot tell you what the solution is.”
Pressed on whether he was alluding the potential threat of hacking, the Arsenal manager was clearly adamant that it was a threat being addressed by the champions.
“I don’t know, they are looking after that constantly just in case it happens, but obviously what happened yesterday, it's a very serious matter.”
