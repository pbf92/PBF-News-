TD Cowen Backs Walmart Despite a Soft Fourth-Quarter Guide
TD Cowen reiterated a Buy rating and $125 price target on Walmart Inc. WMT after third-quarter adjusted EPS of $0.65 beat estimates by a cent and U.S. comparable sales growth of 2.9% matched expectations. Walmart shares were down 1.85% as of 9:30 a.m. ET.
Excluding health and wellness, U.S. comparable sales rose 3.6%, up 0.2 percentage point from the second quarter, driven by mid-single-digit grocery growth on market share gains. General merchandise sales beat expectations by 0.3 point and helped the quarter's operating margin come in slightly ahead.
Walmart guided fourth-quarter EPS to $0.73 to $0.78, compared with the consensus estimate of $0.78, even as it raised its full-year adjusted EPS outlook to $2.84 to $2.89. Truist also reiterated a Buy rating and a $114 target after the results.
