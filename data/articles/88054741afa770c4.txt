TITLE: Fernandes puts Manchester United results ahead of contract talks
OUTLET: Chosunbiz

Bruno Fernandes (32, Manchester United) has twice rejected the club's contract extension offers and said restoring the team's form takes priority over extending his deal.
British newspaper The Sun reported on the 25th (Korea time) that "Fernandes is focused on making Manchester United a winning team again rather than discussing a new contract."
According to the newspaper, Fernandes rejected both contract extension offers from Manchester United. His current contract has about seven months left, but the club's senior executives remain optimistic that he will extend it.
Manchester United is expected to exercise the one-year extension option in his contract and keep Fernandes through 2028.
Fernandes attended an official news conference with coach Mauricio Pochettino ahead of Manchester United's UEFA Champions League match against Sporting CP. He was asked about his future.
Asked what it would take to restart contract talks, Fernandes replied, "We need to start winning games first. Then we can talk about the contract."
However, he was not making the team's results a condition for signing a new contract. The Sun reported that Fernandes explained privately that he did not mean he would decide whether to stay based on the team's results; rather, he wanted to focus on improving performances right away.
"It's not an important issue right now. It's unrelated. Of course, it concerns my future, and with not much time left on my contract, maybe I shouldn't think about it this way. Still, I'm not too worried," he said.
"What worries me more is getting results. It's important to get the club back to a position we can be proud of. We're not there right now," he stressed.
"There's plenty of time to talk. We've already held talks, but I decided the contract wasn't that important when we weren't getting the results and performances we expected. So we stopped the discussions. We'll have time to think about it again in the future," Fernandes added.
Fernandes' own situation is not ideal either.
He has been criticized for a sharp drop in form this season. After Manchester United suffered a 4-1 loss to Como last month, he questioned former coach Michael Carrick's tactics. Four days later, he was left out of the starting lineup for the home game against Bournemouth.
Manchester United lost 3-0 to Bournemouth that day. Some fans both jeered and cheered Fernandes as he came out to warm up.
Fernandes has played in all four games since the Bournemouth match. He has also kept the captain's armband after Carrick left and Pochettino took over.
Pochettino had also wanted to sign Fernandes in 2019, when he was coaching Tottenham Hotspur.
Fernandes did not shy away from taking responsibility for his poor form. "When I get home, I think about myself. I don't think about other things I can't control. I only think about how I can improve my performances and what I need to change to help my teammates and the club," he said.
"Thinking about other problems within the club that aren't my responsibility doesn't help anyone. What matters is improving my performances and becoming a better player. I still have time to learn and improve. And I need to spend time with my children," he said with a laugh.
Fernandes left Sporting and joined Manchester United in January 2020. He has been a key player for the team for a long time, but his only trophies have been the League Cup and the English Football Association (FA) Cup.
He has lost two Europa League finals and finished runner-up in one FA Cup final. He has yet to win the Premier League or Champions League.
The Champions League, in particular, has eluded him.
Manchester United were eliminated in the Champions League group stage in 2020 and 2023. The only Champions League knockout game Fernandes has played in a United shirt was the home leg of the round of 16 against Atletico Madrid in March 2022.
Fernandes has long said that winning the Premier League and Champions League is his goal. Eighteen months ago, he turned down a lucrative offer from Saudi Arabia's Al Nassr and stayed at Manchester United.
As he approaches his mid-30s, he still believes he has plenty of time to challenge for the top honors.
"Every player wants to play in the biggest competitions and the most important games. That's not always possible. Even I had no chance to play in the Champions League for the first few years of my career. Back then, it felt like a very distant dream," he recalled.
"I'm proud of what I've achieved so far, even though I've played in the Champions League knockout stage only once. I still have time. I believe I have many more years ahead of me as a player. I'll get more opportunities, and maybe this season will be one of them," he said confidently.
"My goals haven't changed. It doesn't matter whether I have a lot of time or a little. I'll do my best to go as far as possible in every competition I take part in," he stressed.
Fernandes also spoke about Cristiano Ronaldo, who recently announced his retirement from Portugal's national team.
Ronaldo announced his retirement from the national team early this month after a dispute with Portugal coach Jorge Jesus. His mother, Dolores, later accused Jesus of "deceiving" her son.
"That may be how Ronaldo felt. I think everyone on the national team has always tried to show him the utmost respect," Fernandes said.
"Ronaldo is the biggest role model for players across generations on the national team. As national team players, I think we've always done everything we can to show respect not only for Ronaldo the player but also for Ronaldo the person," he added.
