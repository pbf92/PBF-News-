TITLE: Study finds 619 million people have low back pain worldwide
OUTLET: qz.com

A 2023 study in The Lancet Rheumatology put the number of people with low back pain at 619 million, a 60% increase from 1990, according to The New York Times.
The study's authors project that the total will reach 843 million by 2050, which would exceed the population of Europe, The Times reported.
Low back pain is the leading cause of disability worldwide, with only migraines coming close, according to The Times. The Spine Journal made that statement in the opening line of an article titled "Development of an Internationally Agreed National Minimum Dataset for Low Back Pain: A Modified Delphi Study." The article focused largely on efforts to standardize diagnostic procedures across the field.
