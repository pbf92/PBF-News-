SunPower Received Notice of Noncompliance
On November 23, 2026, SunPower received notice from Nasdaq that it was not complying with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5250(c)(1) because it had delayed filing its quarterly report for the quarter ended September 27, 2026. The company has until January 22, 2027, to submit a plan to regain compliance; if Nasdaq accepts the plan, it may grant up to 180 additional calendar days. The notice has no immediate effect on the listing or trading of SunPower's common stock or warrants.
Original SEC Filing: SunPower Inc. [ SPWR ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
