TITLE: Bruno Fernandes proud of Manchester United despite 4-0 defeat at Liverpool
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Bruno Fernandes has said he is proud of Manchester United despite the club's 4-0 loss at Liverpool.
Manchester United endured a nightmare afternoon at Anfield as Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge ended in defeat. The Red Devils had Leny Yoro sent off inside the opening minutes, with Liverpool racing into a 2-0 lead through Dominik Szoboszlai and Florian Wirtz.
Pochettino tweaked his team in a bid to find some defensive cover, but Liverpool added further goals after the break. Rio Ngumoha became the youngest player to score in aPremier League fixture between the teams, before Victor Munoz completed the rout.
The defeat leaves Manchester United mired in thePremier League relegation places, after one win in 11 games this season. Their 34 goals conceded is also a high among teams in Europe's top five leagues this season.
Despite the scoreline, Fernandes said he was proud of the performance from his side amid adversity.
"Difficult game. Coming to our rivals and losing is always bad. After 15 minutes the picture of the game changes completely and it makes it very difficult for us," Fernandes toldBBC Match of the Day.
"Everyone tried very hard. The result won't show that and will hide a lot of good things that the team did. We still created a lot of chances to try and do something in the game even with one player less.
"Everyone tried a lot, ran a lot, unfortunately we were not able to avoid two more goals in the second half, but we need to clear now and go to the next game in the Champions League and try to get the best out of that game.
Fernandes explains why he is proud of Manchester United despite Liverpool defeat
"The team tried very hard. We were very brave. In some moments we lost the ball but we were trying to play, we were trying to do something in the game, we were not afraid.
"There were some moments we maybe even played a little too much and we ended up conceding goals but I would rather that and see the confidence of my team, to replicate the ideas of what we want to do than just defend and be afraid of anything that we can do.
"You want to get results, but today it was not possible for us. There were a lot of things we can look at and I saw a lot of character from the team. The result won't show that but I am very proud of everyone in the dressing room."
