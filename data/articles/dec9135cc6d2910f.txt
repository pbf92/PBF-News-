TITLE: Customers report major outages with online banking in US
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Customers report major outages with online banking in US
Users have reported difficulty accessing the online accounts of major US banks
Major US banks, including Chase Bank, have been hit by widespread outages, according to customer reports.
Users of banks including Bank of America, Chase, J.P. Morgan, U.S. Bank, Wells Fargo and Zelle are reporting trouble accessing online accounts, the Express reports.
A spokesperson for Chase Bank said on Monday (November 16) its services are experiencing a technical bug issue, not a cyber issue.

“Due to a technical issue, some transactions are taking longer than usual to complete, but customers still have access to their accounts and funds,” a Chase spokesperson said in a statement. “We are working to resolve this as quickly as possible.”
Some users are also reporting an error message on the mobile app, the BBC reports. The message says: "Some services aren't available. We're working on them. Please try back later.".
Around 4,700 people reported outages with Chase's online banking at around 7pm (UK time), according to the website Downdetector. The website also shows 80 reports of Chase outages in the UK at around 8pm.
A spokesman from U.S. Bank said its systems are not directly impacted, the BBC reports. It added "clients attempting to send or receive Zelle payments with another financial institution may experience transaction delays."
However, a spokesperson for the digital payment app Zelle told the BBC that its service was "operating as it should".
It comes after a global Microsoft outage left hundreds of thousands of users worldwide without access to emails and Teams last week.
The issue began late on Thursday afternoon (November 12) and saw users unable to access many of its most popular services, with thousands worldwide reporting they are unable to send and receive emails through Outlook.
Teams was also affected for many, with the tech giant’s Copilot AI assistant and other Microsoft 365 services also disrupted.
