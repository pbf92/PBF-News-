Legality dispute and privacy
The drone operator argued that the flight complied with Civil Aviation Authority rules, saying, "The drone remained in line of sight at all times, it didn't fly over anyone or even near anyone" and that the drone was correctly labelled with relevant licences, public liability and insurance. Civil Aviation Authority guidance warned, "If you use these devices where people can expect privacy, such as inside your home or garden, you're likely to be breaking data protection laws." Manchester United fans received a view of the session as Pochettino and his players were split into three groups, with Bruno Fernandes shown playing rondos on an adjoining pitch.
