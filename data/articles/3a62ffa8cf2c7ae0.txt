TITLE: GTA 6 Players Discover Fan-Favorite Character Is Actually This Beloved Netflix Show Star
OUTLET: PlayStation LifeStyle

Players have discovered that one of GTA 6‘s colorful characters is played by a fan-favorite actor from one of Netflix’s biggest hits. Those who play Rockstar Games’ latest title will meet Vicky in chapter 2, who serves as a bartender and dancer working at Delights Cabaret in Port Gellhorn. Players have discovered that Vicky is played by Taryn Manning, who played Pennsatucky in Orange Is the New Black.
Vicky has a supporting role in the game, appearing in the second chapter to help Lucia plan a heist. Manning has won several SAG awards as part of the ensemble performance on Orange Is the New Black. Besides that, she has also appeared in Jack and Jill vs. the World, 8 Mile, Hawaii Five-0, Sons of Anarchy, and more. GTA 6 marks her first performance in a video game.
Vicki is just one of the many colorful characters populating GTA 6’s world
GTA 6 features one of the most notable casts in video games, including actors, musicians, and comedians. The game features several supporting characters played by celebrities with fans all over the world. Most notably, GTA 6’s in-game radio features Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Denzel Curry, and more. Fans also think that one of its radio stations features comedian Stavros Halkias. It also seems to include a surprising connection to The Weeknd.
Despite its universal acclaim, some fans are raising concerns about GTA 6’s poor performance on PlayStation 5. Players have also found a way to enter the original Vice City from the 2002 game in its massive open world. Ahead of its launch last week, actress Manni L. Perez was nominated for best performance at The Game Awards for voicing Lucia. The game’s second lead, Jason, is portrayed by Dylan Rourke.
