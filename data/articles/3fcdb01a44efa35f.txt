TITLE: GTA 6 Pegassi Toros location
OUTLET: Eurogamer.net

The Pegassi Toros is one of the cars required for Randy's Wishlist in Chapter 3 of GTA 6. You'll need this to hit 100% completion as well, but you might also just be curious to net a new car or make some money. Whatever it is, we'll share the Pegassi Toros location we've found so far.
Note that this page does contain spoilers.
For more help with your time as Lucia and Jason in Leonida, take a look at our GTA 6 walkthrough.
GTA 6 Pegassi Toros location
Getting your hands on a Pegassi Toros for Randy early on can be hard since he doesn't provide any clues on where to find one. Whilst you might get lucky and come across it randomly, we've found one of the locations worth keeping an eye out for.
You have a chance to find the Pegassi Toros at Sunrise Premium Autos in Southside, but keep in mind it is an RNG spawn.
For example, I found it alongside the Bravado Banshee and, after stealing that car and returning, the Toros had de-spawned. Not just that, but stock rotates over time naturally, so it's definitely worth checking this area a few times to see if it's there for you, even if you don't steal anything.
If you do find it in this showroom though, all you need to do is break into the car, and drive out the nearby window. Doing this for the first time even nets you the 'Capital Offence' trophy/achievement. Once it's yours, simply evade the cops and drive it back to Randy - or keep it for yourself, so long as you've unlocked the ability to register cars with Advanced trackers.
