OUTLET: Instant Gaming News

Rockstar is trying to have the unofficial PC port for GTA VI taken down
, by Thibaut
It was to be expected, but Take-Two Interactive and Rockstar Games didn’t sit idly by regarding the unofficial PC port of GTA VI. In fact, the recompilation project is now in the crosshairs of both companies.
On X, user NotRexed, who describes himself as just a “15-year-old kid” behind the port, explained that Rockstar took his Discord server offline. The GitHub repository has also been deleted. The young user also stated that he was leaving the project.
However, the GTA VI PC port (known as Leonida) is likely not dead yet. The current devs, fowntain and thankful Aakash, reportedly said on a new Discord channel and on Telegram that they are going forward with development.
