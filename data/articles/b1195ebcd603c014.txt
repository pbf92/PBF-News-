TITLE: World Vision to host global prayer event for children facing poverty and conflict
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

International Christian charity World Vision has invited people to join an online prayer gathering for children across the globe as it marks World Children’s Day on 20 November.
The livestream, beginning at 1pm, will bring together supporters and special guests to pray for children facing conflict, extreme poverty, climate disasters and a lack of access to clean water and education.
The event comes amid warnings about the scale of challenges facing children globally, with around one in five still living in extreme poverty.
Elijah Kirby, director of ministry development at World Vision UK, said many supporters felt overwhelmed by the scale of the problems facing children.
"Too many children are growing up in conflict zones, living in extreme poverty, facing threats of child labour and early marriage, or living in areas being hit by repeated climate shocks. Charities like World Vision are achieving amazing impact in so many communities we work with, and our supporters are enabling huge practical support, but it is also a fundamental part of our make-up to bring the ultimate response tool – the power of prayer.".
The gathering will focus on children living in conflict zones, those affected by climate emergencies and families struggling to access basic necessities.
Among those highlighted is Halima, a child in Sudan whose life has been affected by war. She lives in an area of Eastern Jebel Marra where around 40,000 displaced people have settled, with many facing shortages of food, water and shelter. The livestream will also include prayers for 11-year-old Amina in Kenya, who spends hours each day collecting water for her family.
World Vision is holding the prayer event as the United Nations climate conference COP31 draws to a close. It wants to highlight the impact of climate change on children and vulnerable communities.The charity said climate shocks and debt pressures are making it harder for some countries to invest in children's futures and prepare for further emergencies.
World Children's Day is observed annually on 20 November, marking the anniversary of the UN's adoption of the Declaration of the Rights of the Child.
Registration for the free online prayer gathering is available through Eventbrite. Participants can register to receive the livestream link.
