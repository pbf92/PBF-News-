TITLE: OpenAI offers ChatGPT Business credits to Lenovo PC users with preinstalled app
OUTLET: Crypto Briefing

Eligible Lenovo owners can unlock $100 in workspace credits, but only if ChatGPT shipped on the machine from the factory
OpenAI is paying businesses to open an app that is already on their laptops. Owners of eligible Lenovo Windows PCs with ChatGPT preinstalled can now earn $100 in ChatGPT Business credits by signing in through that desktop app.
OpenAI’s help center lays out the offer. The terms differ depending on whether a company already pays for ChatGPT Business or is starting from scratch.
How the Lenovo offer works
There are two paths. The first is for organizations that already run a ChatGPT Business workspace.
Those users open the preinstalled ChatGPT desktop app on their Lenovo machine and log in to their existing workspace. An eligible sign-in adds $100 in total to the workspace balance.
Note the word “total.” The workspace gets one flat credit, and individual employees signing in do not add to it.
The second path is for new customers. Anyone who creates a new Business workspace through the eligible app can earn $100 for each seat purchased within the first 90 days.
There is a catch. The credits must be used within 60 days of being issued.

The fine print on eligibility
The promotion applies only to Lenovo devices that came with ChatGPT preinstalled. Installing the app yourself on a Lenovo machine does not count, and other installation methods don’t either.
OpenAI’s guidance tells users who are unsure about their device to check with Lenovo support. Workspaces also have to meet specific eligibility rules tied to the Lenovo installation.
The two companies have not disclosed a formal alliance, a joint press release or revenue-sharing terms. The program looks like a direct promotion from OpenAI to users, not a broad corporate partnership.
What this means for OpenAI and Lenovo
For OpenAI, the promotion is a way to win workplace customers. The research findings describe it as an effort to boost ChatGPT Business adoption and build loyalty among new customers in a competitive AI market.
The per-seat credit for new workspaces shows what OpenAI wants most. It rewards buyers who add more seats early, instead of starting with one or two to test the product.
The 90-day window for seat purchases and the 60-day expiry on credits push the same way. Both shorten the time companies have to decide, so they commit and start using the product quickly instead of letting it become a forgotten line item.
For businesses considering the offer, the practical questions are simple. Does the device actually qualify, and can the team realistically use the credits inside 60 days?
Companies already paying for ChatGPT Business get a modest bonus for a few seconds of effort. New customers planning a team rollout have more reason to look closely, since they get a credit for every seat bought in the first 90 days.
