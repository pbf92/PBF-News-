TITLE: Can You Replay GTA 6 Story Missions? Rockstar Explains Why Not
OUTLET: Kotaku

Story missions in Grand Theft Auto 6 are one and done. This means no going back and experiencing some of its biggest set pieces and most memorable moments, and no second chances to complete them perfectly. Rockstar Games has a good reason for dropping the feature, but the decision will probably still leave some players disappointed.
“Mission replay was certainly something we looked at early on in development, and we do not currently support it,” senior design director Scott Butchard told Kotaku in an email prior to the game’s launch. “As the game came together it felt at odds with the story we were trying to tell and felt a bit alien and disconnected. Sitting outside of the game as it were and replaying missions really diminished the choices you had made.”
Mission replay wasn’t always available in Grand Theft Auto. Earlier entries didn’t have it either. Rockstar added the option starting with the Ballad of Gay Tony expansion pack for GTA 4. Replayable story missions returned in GTA 5. A medal system showed how well players had completed secondary objectives, with many going back until they’d achieved a mission’s highest rating.
GTA 6 is a different kind of GTA game, though. It has a lot of Red Dead Redemption 2‘s DNA in it, with an emphasis on immersive storytelling and realistically simulating elements of an open-world experience. At times, story missions in GTA 6 feel more like quests in an RPG like The Witcher 3 where getting a perfect outcome is less important than how the outcome you do get tells the larger story of a player’s time in the world.
It doesn’t sound like Rockstar has any plans to add a mission replay feature down the road, but once the game comes to PC I’m sure one will get modded in anyway.
