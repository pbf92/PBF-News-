TITLE: Pochettino ‘proud’ and encouraged by draw with Sporting
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Mauricio Pochettino says Manchester United showed the style and character he wants to establish during their 2-2 Champions League draw with Sporting CP.
United trailed at half-time despite Pochettino feeling his side had controlled much of the opening period, but he was particularly encouraged by their response after the break, as Bruno Fernandes scored twice before a late Senne Lammens error cost his team victory.
"The first half I think we controlled the game, I think we dominated," he said.
"We were disappointed to finish the first half 1-0 down. We controlled it but didn't receive enough in the opposition half, in the last third.
"But the second half I think we started the way we want to play. That is the style, the character. That is the way we want to establish and feeling this way we can perform."
Pochettino has only been working with the squad for a week but believes the performance offered an early indication of the identity he wants to create at Old Trafford.
"I'm so proud of my players," he said.
"I think it was a very tough game. I liked the team during 90 minutes, in the way that we showed personality and character.
"It's difficult to find a game where we are punished more in one game. We were punished a lot in different actions that were key, I think every one.
"But watch the game, I saw all the action and only what I can say is I feel proud of all the players.
"Today I think we showed how we want to play. It's only one week working together and I feel so proud. I told them after the game."
