ANT: Anthropic Heads for IPO Pricing with Demand at Six Times Supply
- Anthropic to price its IPO
- Shares set for Thursday debut
- IPO six times oversubscribed at $1.4T
Claude maker is expected to price its Nasdaq listing Wednesday. A separate $26 billion private placement brings some of the biggest names in AI into the deal.
📋 What’s on offer and when
- Anthropic is expected to price its IPO Wednesday, November 18, with Nasdaq trading expected to begin Thursday under ANT.
- As of Tuesday morning, the latest public terms call for 825 million shares at $70 to $78 each, putting the base offering at up to $64.35 billion before the underwriters’ option.
- Anthropic would receive about $62.5 billion before fees at the top of the range. The company is selling about 801.4 million shares, while existing holders are selling the rest. The upper end implies a valuation of about $1 trillion based on outstanding shares, or up to $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis.
- Early orders ran well ahead of the shares available. Reuters reported that demand reached about six times the offering by the end of last Thursday, citing a person familiar with the deal.
- That figure captures an early stage of the roadshow. Wednesday’s final price and share count will show how the banks responded.
💵 Big Tech commits another $26 billion
- Seven strategic investors have agreed to buy $26 billion of Class N shares in a separate private placement at the final IPO price. The group includes Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC Partners.
- Public shareholders will buy Class A stock while founders retain voting control. The prospectus says the Class F shares held through the founders’ entity will carry 50.1% of voting power on matters submitted to shareholders generally. That structure gives founders control over most director elections after the listing.
- Claude’s growth gives investors a starting point for the valuation. Anthropic reported $16.96 billion in third-quarter revenue, up 47% from the prior quarter. Its annualized revenue run rate reached about $81 billion at the end of October.
📉 The IPO meets a punishing tech market
- The Nasdaq fell 2.5% Monday` and closed in correction territory, more than 10% below its October closing high.
- Nvidia reports Tuesday after the bell, a day before Anthropic is expected to price. Nvidia’s outlook could shift sentiment around AI spending just as Anthropic’s bankers settle the terms.
- The offer price and Thursday’s opening trade answer different questions. Pricing shows what institutions agreed to pay for their allocations.
- The open shows what buyers will pay once shares start changing hands on Nasdaq. A strong opening can fade, so the first close will give traders another measure of demand.
