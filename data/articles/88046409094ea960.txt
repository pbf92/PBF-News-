Stock Market Today: Jensen’s Return to Nvidia Is Causing Index Indigestion

NVIDIA (NASDAQ:NVDA | NVDA Price Prediction) is giving back part of Monday’s rally, and the Nasdaq-100 is falling with it. As of 11:12 a.m. ET, the chipmaker was down about 8% at roughly $151. Criticism from both political parties over Jensen Huang’s return as CEO has passed Monday’s relief. The S&P 500 is down about 1%, but the Dow is flat. That gap tells you money is rotating out of tech while staying in stocks.
Tech Drags the Nasdaq-100 While the Dow Holds Flat
Huang’s Return Turns From Relief Into a Political Fight
Nvidia’s board reinstated Huang as president and CEO effective immediately, and the news set off a broad rally across Wall Street on Monday. Nvidia closed at $165. By Tuesday, the mood had changed. Republicans and Democrats are objecting to President Trump’s role in bringing Huang back, and Huang is scheduled to face Congress next week. Niall Hearty, a regulatory lawyer at Weightmans, said putting the stock price first risks backfiring. He said the move puts “short-term stock market valuation above serious national security concerns” while the SEC, FBI, and Justice Department are still looking into it.
The money pulled out of tech is moving into defensive sectors. As of 11 a.m., technology was down 4%, while health care and consumer staples were each up about 2%. Defensive sectors like these sell products people buy in any economy. Bonds are giving some relief. The 10-year Treasury yield closed Monday at 4.92%, down from 5.15% earlier this month. Yields eased further after New York Fed President John Williams warned about inflation.
What This Selloff Means for Your Index Funds
Monday’s rally didn’t change the bigger picture. Nvidia is still down 28% over the past month, and QQQ is down 10% over the same period. QQQ is still up 3% for the year. A big share of the money in an S&P 500 or Nasdaq-100 fund sits in a handful of chip and AI stocks (we rounded up seven AI infrastructure names outside the chipmakers in a free report here). A flat Dow on a day like this shows how much of the market’s swings come from those few names. Check how much tech you actually own across all your funds.
A Treasury Auction and Huang’s Hearing Could Set Tech’s Next Move
Investors are waiting on today’s 5-year Treasury auction. Weak demand could push yields higher again, which would put more pressure on tech valuations. Next comes Huang’s appearance before Congress. It will show whether the governance questions around Nvidia are fading or getting bigger.
