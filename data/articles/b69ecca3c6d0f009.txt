TITLE: Grand Theft Auto 6 becomes biggest entertainment launch in history
OUTLET: IBC.org

The release of Grand Theft Auto 6 (GTA 6) broke retail sales records on its first full day (November 19) of availability, hitting $3.8bn (£2.9bn).
Take-Two, the parent company of Rockstar Games, said the figures marked a record for "entertainment across any medium". The company pre-sold 32.5m copies of the game.
By comparison, the highest-grossing film of 2026 so far – Spider-Man: Brand New Day – hit $2.5bn (£1.8bn) over its entire run in cinemas.
The video game sales record was previously held by GTA6’s predecessor Grand Theft Auto V, set 13 years ago. This earlier game $1.15bn (£1.2bn) in five days.
The latest in Rockstar’s crime simulator franchise, GTA6 follows a pair of small-time criminals, Lucia and her partner Jason.
It is estimated to have cost Take-Two more than $1bn to develop, making it the most expensive video game in history.
Despite a 13-year wait and reported delays, GTA 6 has been hailed by some reviewers as one of the most impressive gaming experiences ever.
The game was launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. The industry is now waiting to hear from Take-Two about its plans to roll out a PC and an online version of GTA 6.
Some industry analysts are expecting the PC launch and online mode in 2027.
“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” said Sam Houser, President, Founder, Rockstar Games. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world, and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”
