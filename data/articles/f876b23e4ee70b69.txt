TITLE: Football gossip: Paz, Amad, Balde, Pavlidis, McKennie, Nusa, Schade
OUTLET: BBC

- Published
Manchester United pursuing Nico Paz, Besiktas plot move for Amad Diallo, Barcelona to rebuff offers for Alejandro Balde, plus more.
Mauricio Pochettino's arrival as Manchester United manager could help their pursuit of Nico Paz, 22, although Real Madrid retain an 80m euro (£67.6m) option to re-sign the Argentina midfielder from Como in 2027. (Sun), external
Besiktas are plotting a £37.5m January offer for Manchester United's Amad Diallo, 24, after adding the Ivory Coast winger to their list of targets. (NTV Spor - in Turkish), external
Liverpool and Manchester United are interested in Barcelona defender Alejandro Balde, 23, but the La Liga side do not want to sell the Spain left-back. (Teamtalk), external
Benfica and Greece striker Vangelis Pavlidis is one of four players Pochettino wants to add to his Manchester United squad, but the 27-year-old is also wanted by Tottenham and Barcelona. (Caught Offside), external
Juventus midfielder Weston McKennie, 28, is unlikely to move to the Premier League despite recent comments suggesting American players could follow their former international boss Pochettino to Manchester United. (Sky Sports), external
Arsenal are continuing to track RB Leipzig and Norway winger Antonio Nusa, 21, and Brentford and Germany forward Kevin Schade, 24, as they assess potential additions to their squad. (Sky Sports), external
Barcelona have renewed their pursuit of Brighton & Hove Albion and Croatia centre-back Luka Vuskovic, 19. (Teamtalk), external
Middlesbrough are confident England Under-21 international Will Lankshear, 21, will snub Premier League interest to stay with them. (Sun), external
Former Arsenal defender Rob Holding, 31, is available as a free agent and is attracting interest from Sheffield United, LAFC and Houston Dynamos. (Sky Sports), external
Arsenal are monitoring Club Brugge and Portugal winger Carlos Forbs, 22, who is valued at about £20m. (Fichajes - in Spanish), external
