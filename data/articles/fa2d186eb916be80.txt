TITLE: How to make climate anxiety your superpower
OUTLET: Nature News

In this episode:
Late last week the UN’s climate conference, COP31, failed to reach a consensus on its headline message. That news came on the heels of the UN acknowledging that the 1.5 ˚C warming limit set out in Paris will be breached. So, it’s easy to feel anxious in a world where it seems that not enough is being done about climate change. But is it possible to overcome such anxiety or even use it?
In the final episode of Cooked? I open up about my own climate anxiety and discover that there are still things that I can do to battle climate change. And how those small individual actions could potentially lead to more systemic change.
