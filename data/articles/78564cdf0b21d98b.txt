TITLE: Lenny Kravitz Brings Bawma, the Pirate King of Aleph in 007 First Light, to HITMAN World of Assassination
OUTLET: Xbox Wire

Summary
- Grammy Award winner Lenny Kravitz makes his World of Assassination debut as Bawma, the Pirate King of Aleph from 007 First Light, in The Mutiny, a new Elusive Target available starting today through December 24.
- For once, the client briefs Agent 47 personally, as Bawma guides him across Ambrose Island to deal with three rogue lieutenants, and a set of Voodoo Dolls offers one of the most creative ways to take them down.
- The mission is free for new players via the Free Demo and for all HITMAN World of Assassination players, and launches alongside the new Rockstar Pack.
Agent 47 has a new client, and he isn’t usually the type to stay in the shadows. HITMAN World of Assassination’s latest Elusive Target, The Mutiny, stars Lenny Kravitz as Bawma, the magnetic and unpredictable Pirate King of Aleph, first brought to life in 007 First Light. Kravitz lends both his likeness and his voice to the character, who crosses over into the World of Assassination for the first time.
The Mutiny Elusive Target mission is available globally on Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, ROG Xbox Ally X, ROG Xbox Ally, and PC starting Thursday, November 26, through Thursday, December 24, 2026.
From the opening briefing, it’s clear this assignment is unusual. Diana never lets a client brief 47 directly, but Bawma has paid the Agency generously and insists on directing the operation himself. Little is known about the man behind the alias: intelligence suggests he was raised as a child soldier before building Aleph, a sprawling black-market hub in the Western Hemisphere that he calls his kingdom.
Now he’s hiding out on Ambrose Island, and 47’s objective is simple: find Bawma, make contact, and await further instructions.
Once 47 tracks Bawma down through the island’s ruins and jungles, it soon becomes clear that something far more valuable is at stake. Three of Bawma’s rogue lieutenants stand in the way, and none of them will part with what they’re holding easily.
Bawma stays in 47’s ear throughout the mission, and with its bright, upbeat tone and easygoing holiday feel, The Mutiny is one of the most laid-back contracts in the World of Assassination. On Ambrose Island, though, nothing is ever straightforward, and fortune favors the bold.
Bold players will want to pay a visit to Madame Serafine. All three targets can be eliminated with a Voodoo Doll: gather the ingredients, add them to the Ritual Bowl, and pick up the doll Madame Serafine creates.
Then “kill” the doll to take out its target, whether that means stabbing it with a knife, throwing it in water, shooting it or blowing it up, hitting it with a thrown sharp object, or exposing it to fire or electricity.
As with all Elusive Targets, The Mutiny will be removed from the game when it expires on December 24. However, players who own HITMAN World of Assassination or the Ambrose Island location will keep access to The Retaliator, the Arcade version of the mission, as well as its Special Assignment, completely for free.
Players who complete The Mutiny with an IOI Account while it’s live will also unlock the Rock Royalty Duck, a regal rubber duck in flashy sunglasses that detonates when it detects nearby motion.
Launching alongside The Mutiny, IO Interactive is also releasing the Rockstar Pack, a collection of outfits, weapons, gear, and Freelancer Safehouse cosmetics inspired by the bold style and electric energy of rock ’n’ roll icons. The pack is available for $5.99/€5.99/£4.99 and includes:
