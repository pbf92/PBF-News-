Falcon Finance: Schedules Unlock of About 203 Million FF - Jun 2027
Falcon Finance has scheduled a token unlock for June 2027. The project is set to release about 203.06 million FF, an amount equal to 5.73% of the circulating supply. The release covers tokens allocated to the ecosystem, the foundation, investors and the team.
The unlock makes these tokens available for sale. If recipients sell any of their newly unlocked FF, those sales could add to selling pressure on the token.
