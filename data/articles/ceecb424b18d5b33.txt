TITLE: Bruno Fernandes misses Manchester United training as Mauricio Pochettino provides update
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United may be forced to line up without Bruno Fernandes against Brentford on Saturday after Mauricio Pochettino revealed the reason behind his absence from Friday's training session.
Fernandes was notably absent from United's final training session on Friday at Carrington. The United captain netted both goals in the chaotic 2-2 draw against Sporting CP in the UEFA Champions League on Wednesday but is now a significant doubt for the Brentford clash.
"We don't know, he been affected by a virus from Portugal," Pochettino said in his pre-match press conference when asked if Fernandes was available, via The Mirror. "He finished the game with a little bit of a problem and today he cannot be a part of the training session.
"We'll see. We're going to assess during the day and tomorrow. He'll wake up and see. We're not going to take any risks because we know that when these types of situations happen you don't feel your best. We're going to pay attention but still we haven't decided."
Pochettino is still searching for his first victory as United manager following the draw in Lisbon and the 4-0 hammering by Liverpool last weekend at Anfield. He will additionally be without midfielder Carlos Baleba and forward Marcus Rashford, with both players sidelined by their respective injuries.
Baleba, a £70 million ($95 million) summer acquisition from Brighton and Hove Albion, remains unavailable due to the hamstring injury he picked up against AS Roma. Pochettino confirmed the midfielder is expected to remain sidelined for 10 to 12 weeks in total.
Rashford has been sidelined since the 2-0 defeat against Atlético Madrid on October 13, but made his return to the training pitch on Friday. "Marcus today he did an individual session on the field," Pochettino confirmed.
