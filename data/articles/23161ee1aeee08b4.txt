TITLE: Liverpool receive triple injury boost ahead of Manchester United visit
OUTLET: Yahoo

Virgil van Dijk, Ryan Gravenberch and Jeremie Frimpong all participated in training with their Liverpool teammates on Thursday as Andoni Iraola ramped up preparations for Sunday's home game against Mauricio Pochettino's Manchester United.
Giovanni Leoni and Conor Bradley, though, were not present with the squad as their rehabilitation from long-term knee problems continues. Both individuals have taken part in sessions at the AXA Training Centre this week and featured in Wednesday's action at the club's practice facility.
Nevertheless, the duo are being carefully monitored as part of gradual reintegration into full first-team training, and their non-participation was scheduled, according to the Liverpool Echo.
Van Dijk missed the international break with the Netherlands with what was characterized as a minor issue, but the club captain was alongside his teammates on Thursday and is set to feature in the starting lineup against United.
Gravenberch was able to participate in training on Thursday after being sidelined for the previous three matches with a heel concern.
The former Bayern Munich midfielder was unavailable for the fixtures with Fenerbahçe and Crystal Palace and remained on the bench for the 1-0 defeat to Arsenal before those matches.
Frimpong was also given the green light for a return, having been absent for the past month with a muscle setback, which he sustained just minutes into his second-half appearance in the 2-1 victory at Brentford.
Bradley has been unavailable since sustaining a knee injury against Arsenal in the scoreless stalemate at the Emirates back in January of this year.

Leoni, meanwhile, has been sidelined for the last 14 months following his own serious and complex knee injury, sustained late in a Carabao Cup victory over Southampton.
The defensive pair will not be cleared to feature in Sunday's clash against United as the club looks to gradually reintegrate both players back into action.
Their returns are understandably being regarded as enormously encouraging developments, though there will be no expectation of an immediate recall to the starting lineup given the extended nature of their absences.
Leoni has made just one appearance for the club since his August 2025 transfer from Parma. The Italian teenager impressed in a 2-1 victory over Southampton at Anfield in the League Cup before suffering the devastating setback late in the match.
Bradley's recovery, meanwhile, has played a significant role in Liverpool being forced to field as many as eight other players at right-back since the Northern Ireland international's injury 10 months ago.
Midfielders Dominik Szoboszlai, Curtis Jones, Wataru Endo and Gravenberch have all been utilized in that position this calendar year, while Joe Gomez and Victor Muñoz have also featured in the role under Iraola.
Ronald Araújo has started the last eight Premier League matches there, with Frimpong also called upon at various points. Szoboszlai also took part in training alongside his teammates, sporting strapping around his right knee, mirroring the precaution he took during Hungary's international break commitments.
Iraola is set to face the press on Friday, where he is expected to provide an update on both Leoni and Bradley, two players who have yet to feature under the Basque manager.
