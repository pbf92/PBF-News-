TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino's Manchester United staff arrive early ahead Liverpool trip
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Mauricio Pochettino's coaching staff have already touched down in Manchester as the club looks to salvage what has been a nightmare start to the Premier League campaign — and that's before the Argentine manager himself has even set foot at Old Trafford, with his arrival expected later this week.
The 54-year-old is fulfilling his obligations with the United States Men's National Team, steering them through a CONCACAF quarter-final double-header before embarking on the next phase of his managerial career at the Theatre of Dreams. The Stars and Stripes enjoyed a 6-0 demolition of their Caribbean neighbors on Saturday in Miami, leaving David Badia's Haiti with a near-impossible task when the two sides meet again in Tuesday's second leg in Cincinnati.
Consequently, Pochettino won't set foot on British soil until that tie is wrapped up. Nevertheless, given the size of the aggregate lead, he has given the green light for his backroom team to head to Greater Manchester ahead of his own arrival.
Jesús Pérez, Toni Jiménez, and Sebastian Pochettino — the manager's own son — landed in the northwest on Saturday. Miguel D'Adostino has stayed behind in the United States with Pochettino but is also expected to make the trip over when the head coach officially takes charge at United on November 18.
The timing underscores just how steep a challenge awaits Pochettino if he is serious about turning things around at Old Trafford. At the time of writing, United sit 18th in the Premier League table with just eight points, having claimed only one win from their opening 10 fixtures, reports the Manchester Evening News.
It is United's worst start to a season in four decades, underscoring the club's desperate predicament at present. A statement from the club last week confirming Pochettino's appointment read: "Mauricio Pochettino has agreed to become the Manchester United men's head coach from 18 November.
"The club has reached an agreement with the US Soccer Federation for Mauricio and his staff to be released after the conclusion of their responsibilities with the US Men's National Team. The Argentinian is renowned for his high-intensity, attacking style of football, his dedication to building strong, cohesive teams and his outstanding record of developing individual players.
"Pochettino has consistently performed at the highest level of football across multiple leagues. He began his coaching career with Espanyol in 2009 before joining Southampton and achieving a club-record Premier League points tally in 2014.

"During five full seasons at Tottenham Hotspur, Pochettino established the club as regular UEFA Champions League contenders, securing qualification in four consecutive seasons and leading the club to its first final in the competition in 2019.
"In 2021, he joined Paris Saint-Germain, where he won the Ligue 1 title, the Coupe de France and the Trophée des Champions before joining Chelsea in 2023. Most recently, he led the US Men's National Team to the Round of 16 at the FIFA World Cup."
Pochettino faces an immediate baptism of fire as United boss, making the short trip to Anfield to take on Liverpool on Sunday, November 2. Should he officially take the reins at Old Trafford on Wednesday, he will have just three days to prepare his side for the clash.
Just 72 hours later, United hit the road again — this time traveling to Sporting CP in the UEFA Champions League, desperate to turn around an equally disappointing European campaign. The club holds a record of two wins and two defeats in the 2026/27 continental competition, having overcome Sabah and Roma while falling short against Atlético Madrid and Como.
Those results leave United sitting 17th in the UCL standings, three points behind the automatic knockout qualification places. A home encounter with Brentford rounds out November's schedule, before attention turns to a hectic festive period that could prove pivotal to their season.
