TITLE: A battered stock market looks for the bottom: What to watch this week
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

It'd be impossible to top last week's action. Anthropic (ANT) went public, marking the first pure-play generative AI lab to list on the public market in the US. GTA VI was released to near-unanimous adoration and record sales. President Trump, Chinese leader Xi Jinping, and Russian President Vladimir Putin held their first trilateral talks.
And the stock market got crushed, with the S&P 500 (^GSPC) losing over 8%.
To put it mildly, it was a big one. Luckily for investors, the upcoming holiday-shortened Thanksgiving stretch should be a bit tamer with no key standout events. (Though, in this market, don't ever discount the possibility of a dark horse event.)
Key calendar things to circle
A relatively modest earnings calendar will be helmed by IT leader HPE (HPE) and computer giant Dell Technologies (DELL) on Monday and Tuesday, respectively, complemented by a smattering of retailers, including Dollar Tree (DLTR), Best Buy (BBY), Burlington Stores (BURL), and Dick's Sporting Goods (DKS).
Medical device maker Agilent Technologies (A) and software names Workday (WDAY) and Zoom Communications (ZM) will also share their quarterly reports.
The headline event on the economic data front will be Wednesday's monthly Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) report, the core reading on which is the Federal Reserve's preferred measure of inflation — a key data read ahead of the Fed's December meeting. Investors will also get broader reads on the economy with S&P Global's activity PMIs and the Chicago Fed's national activity index on Monday, followed by a consumer sentiment vibe check brought to you by the Conference Board on Tuesday.
Anthropic's real test begins as it starts its post-IPO journey
Anthropic finally went public on Thursday as the first public pure-play generative AI lab. The reception has been thus far — mixed, to say the least.
Anthropic made a record debut on Wall Street Thursday, with shares opening at $111.50 a little before noon ET, a 34% premium to the $83 IPO price. But the AI company's stock couldn't hold on to its early gains. The stock closed at $97.17, up 17% on the day, giving Anthropic a market capitalization of about $1.28 trillion, or roughly $1.75 trillion on a fully diluted basis.
Shares of the model builder dropped around 10% on Friday as investors booked profits after a frenetic first day of trading. Not helping sentiment: The broader market plunged on Anthropic's well-received IPO day and remains under pressure today, a sign of a test ahead of investor appetite.
