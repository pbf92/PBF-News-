TITLE: Spectrum is giving US customers free gigabit internet upgrades so they can download GTA 6 faster
OUTLET: Gamereactor UK

Grand Theft Auto VI will need up to 212GB of console storage when it launches on 19 November 2026, and one American broadband provider has built a marketing campaign around that number: Spectrum is giving eligible customers a free speed upgrade, called the 'Gig Speed Lift', so the huge download takes less than half an hour.
Charter Communications, Spectrum's parent company, announced the promotion on 4 November, timed to GTA 6's pre-load period, which Rockstar opened on 10 November on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Qualifying customers' speeds are boosted at no extra cost until 30 November, after which they return to their normal plans.
How fast is fast enough?
Spectrum claims its 1 Gig service can deliver the full game in under 30 minutes, and the company puts it in marketing language: "With Spectrum Internet Gig, customers can start playing Grand Theft Auto VI in less than 30 minutes, so game day is for cruising, not downloading."
That estimate assumes ideal conditions: pulling 212GB at a steady 1Gbps works out at roughly 28 minutes with no overhead at all. Real-world speeds rarely hold at that level for half an hour straight, so players should expect somewhat longer, and Rockstar advises making "sure you've got ample space to begin pre-loading as soon as possible". Storage matters too, since the install size may need temporary free space beyond the headline figure.
The offer is aimed squarely at the game's launch, but Charter frames it as a broader customer appreciation initiative around Thanksgiving, Black Friday and Cyber Monday, and it is not limited to GTA 6 players.
A European footnote
The 'Gig Speed Lift' is a US-only promotion, so European players downloading GTA 6 will have to rely on their own broadband. The game itself launches worldwide on 19 November for $79.99 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with physical copies containing just a download code rather than a disc.
