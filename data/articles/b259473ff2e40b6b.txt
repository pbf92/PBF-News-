Beneficient Seeks HCLP Debt Cancellation and Recovery of $211 Million
Less than 1 min read
Beneficient filed a complaint seeking to invalidate HCLP debt documents, cancel the purported obligations and recover approximately $211 million previously paid.
Key Highlights:
- The Delaware Court of Chancery approved Beneficient’s intervention in the existing HCLP litigation; that ruling did not decide the merits.
- The complaint seeks to invalidate HCLP credit agreements, cancel the claimed debt and establish that HCLP has no valid right to related collateral.
- Beneficient alleges that former CEO Brad Heppner fabricated the purported debt and concealed his control of HCLP.
- If successful, the action could leave Beneficient with an unleveraged balance sheet; the company cautioned that there is no assurance it will obtain the relief sought.
- The lawsuit advances Beneficient’s strategy to eliminate the purported debt and separate from Heppner and affiliated entities.
Original SEC Filing: Beneficient [ BENF ] - 8-K - Nov. 24, 2026
