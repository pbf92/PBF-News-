TITLE: GTA 6 Launch Figures Expected to Rise When PC Version Arrives
OUTLET: A 90s Kid

Grand Theft Auto 6’s launch-window sales figures are expected to climb further when the game becomes available on PC, Polygon reports. That expectation follows an already enormous opening: Alinea Analytics reported 44.9 million copies sold and $4 billion in gross revenue by the end of launch weekend, as relayed by Polygon.
The distinction matters: those are reported launch-weekend results, while the PC contribution is a forecast of further growth. For players waiting on that version, the PC release is the next availability step discussed in the report — not part of the tally already on the board.
A launch measured against entertainment giants
Polygon also reports that Rockstar Games called GTA 6 the biggest entertainment launch of all time, and that the game recorded the biggest launch in PlayStation history. Those are two different benchmarks: one places the opening against entertainment more broadly, while the other puts it within PlayStation’s own history.
For a sense of scale, Polygon compares the game with Taylor Swift’s Eras Tour, which played nearly 150 shows for millions of fans and earned $2 billion in ticket sales. It is a striking comparison, though game gross revenue and concert ticket sales measure different businesses — not interchangeable measures of audience size or profit.
The useful takeaway is the scale rather than a pop-culture victory lap. GTA 6’s reported opening is already being compared with an entire concert tour; the anticipated PC contribution concerns how much further its commercial run can grow.
