OUTLET: TradingView

Anthropic Posts Claude’s VP ≠ VNP Proof, With No Human Verification
According to ChainCatcher, Anthropic added a `vp-vnp` directory to its GitHub repository `formal-math`, publishing a paper titled “VP ≠ VNP over every field” and a Lean 4 formalization attributed to Claude (Anthropic). The work claims to prove that VP is a strict subset of VNP over any field, addressing Valiant’s conjecture in algebraic complexity theory.
The paper says an internal research Claude model discovered the result. It claims superpolynomial circuit-size lower bounds for computing the Hamiltonian cycle polynomial over any field and the permanent when the field’s characteristic is not 2. Anthropic said the result does not resolve P vs. NP.
Anthropic said AI models wrote both the paper and formal proof, and no human mathematician has checked the proof. Lean can verify proof steps, but no machine can confirm that the formalized statement fully matches the claimed conclusion. Leslie Valiant proposed the conjecture in 1979; it is considered an algebraic analogue of P vs. NP.
