Mauricio Pochettino's very first day on the job at Manchester United has already been met with a crisis as the club was victim of an embarrassing security breach.
The Argentine has only just arrived as new United manager, flying into Manchester on Wednesday morning, having departed straight after his final game with the U.S. national team on Tuesday night.
He has only a few days to prepare for his debut as Red Devils' boss, a difficult challenge on the road at Anfield.
Now, it seems that much of the work he did in his first training session might have to be scrapped as his session was leaked publicly and available to the rest of the world.

Individual films Man United training session with drone
An unknown individual managed to film 12 minutes of Pochettino's first training session at the club's Carrington training ground, which is closed to media and the public.
The individual flew a drone high above the training ground and filmed Pochettino running drills and giving tactical instructions to his new Red Devils squad, an event which is usually highly confidential and secretive as to avoid giving opponents clear insight into how teams will line up or approach matches.
The video, uploaded to YouTube with the pilot of the drone also narrating the footage, shows various selections of players broken off into position groups training on their own before coming together to take tactical instructions from Pochettino.
It appears that the pilot uses a drone with impressive zoom capabilities, and does not seem to cross over the Carrington training ground itself to capture the footage. Early in the video, the narrator states he "does not want to be a nuisance" so he decides to "make full use of the zoom."
He later claims "as you can see I'm absolutely miles away," but then pilots the drone along the edge of Carrington's grounds and says "we can get quite a bit closer yet without being a nuisance." The narrator is clearly wary of going too close to the training ground, as about two-thirds through the uploaded video he declares "let's see how much closer I dare go," before flying lower and zooming in on Pochettino leading a huddle with Harry Maguire, Amad Diallo, Ayden Heaven, and others.
As of this publish, Man United had refused to comment on the incident, according to the BBC.
Are drones banned at Man United training ground?
According to the Daily Mail, Man United put up signs around Carrington banning the use of drones in and around the club without the proper permission.
United regularly use drones to film their training sessions, as many clubs do around the world, both for internal purposes and social media use, but outside spying is largely frowned upon.
"For the people asking about legality, it is unrestricted airspace, meaning anyone can fly over it," the account claimed in the YouTube comment section of the video.
"The drone remained in line of sight at all times, it didn't fly over anyone or even near anyone so was a perfectly legal flight going by the CAA rules that regulates drone usage.
"The drone is correctly labelled, and I hold all the relevant licences, public liability and insurance required. As for leaking team news/tactics, it was half an hour into the managers first training session, there are many media there so certainly won't be the only footage of training released."
