Manchester United extend Bruno Fernandes' contract until 2028
Premier League club activates one-year extension clause as Portuguese captain puts talks over new long-term deal on hold
Halis Sünnetci
27 November 2026•Update: 27 November 2026
İSTANBUL
Manchester United have activated a one-year extension clause in captain Bruno Fernandes' contract, keeping the Portuguese midfielder at the Premier League club until June 2028, British media reported Friday.
The 32-year-old's previous contract was set to expire in June 2027, but United exercised their option to extend it by another 12 months, according to Sky Sports.
The decision came after Fernandes reportedly rejected two offers for a new long-term contract, with negotiations between the player and the club continuing since the summer.
Both sides have agreed to revisit negotiations in the future, with their immediate focus on improving United's results, the report said.
Fernandes confirmed in an interview with Sky Sports that the club had offered him a new contract but said negotiations were on hold because of the team's struggles.
"I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games," he said.
Fernandes joined Manchester United from Portuguese club Sporting CP in January 2020 and has served as club captain since 2023.
The Portuguese international has scored 115 goals in 343 appearances for United across all competitions.
