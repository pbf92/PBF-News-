TITLE: Rockstar explains why GTA 6 currently has no mission replay
OUTLET: Mezha.net

Rockstar explains why GTA 6 currently has no mission replay
Photo source: IGN
The studio says replaying missions from outside the game would diminish the impact of players’ choices in its story.
Rockstar Games considered adding a mission replay feature to GTA 6 early in development but does not currently support it, senior design director Scott Butchard told Kotaku. GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 both let players replay missions.
This is reported by IGN.
Mission replay was certainly something we looked at early on in development, and we do not currently support it. As the game came together it felt at odds with the story we were trying to tell and felt a bit alien and disconnected. Sitting outside of the game as it were and replaying missions really diminished the choices you had made.
- Scott Butchard
The missing feature has frustrated some fans. The source says finishing GTA 6 can take 50 - 100 hours, meaning players may be reluctant to restart the whole game just to revisit a favorite mission. Butchard said the game does not “currently support” mission replay; Rockstar has not confirmed whether it might be added in an update.
Players’ choices can change missions and the story: some characters can be killed or spared, objectives can be completed in different ways, and Jason and Lucia’s relationship can vary. Those choices can also lead to different dialogue and cutscenes. Red Dead Redemption 2 also had choices that affected Arthur’s character, though the source describes its variations as less nuanced. The source notes that modders may add mission replay if a PC version is released.
