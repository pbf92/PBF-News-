TITLE: Rockstar Games website 'seized by authorities' after players scan in-game GTA 6 QR code
OUTLET: TweakTown

Rockstar Games has hidden working QR codes throughout Grand Theft Auto 6, and players scanning them with their real phones are landing on a parody "website seized" notice hosted on Rockstar's own site.

So, where do the GTA 6 QR codes actually go? Rockstar's rsg.ms/qr1 short link, which fan tracking site Peekhole.de lists as a QR destination, redirects to a page on rockstargames.com stating the site was seized "by the Federal Investigation Bureau," GTA's version of the FBI, under a seizure warrant citing "47 U.S.C. § 11.25(A)," alongside a Vice City Police Department badge.
The page then jokes that "due to lack of federal funding, the FIB is selling ad space on seized IP addresses," and seemingly these ads and sponsors change per player or between visits as one player's version cited organ trafficking and a "Dark Web Dating" ad, while the version I loaded cited wire fraud and an ad for a pet delivery service offering black rhinos.
Notably, Peekhole.de says the seized pages tie into Peekhole, GTA 6's parody of creator subscription sites, whose QR stickers show up on cars around Leonida. Movistar eSports' post closes with actually fantastic information, and a general rule of thumb, "Never scan codes you don't recognize."
