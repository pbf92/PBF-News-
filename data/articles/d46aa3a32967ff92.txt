TITLE: OpenAI’s GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol take first and third on DesignArena’s game dev leaderboard
OUTLET: Crypto Briefing

Human voters ranked OpenAI's new models above rivals including Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 and Meta's Muse Wonder
OpenAI’s newest models are building games that people actually prefer. GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol have claimed first and third place on DesignArena’s game development leaderboard.
Astra holds the top spot with an Elo rating of 1497, as of November 21, 2026. Sol sits in third with a score of 1430.
How the models stacked up
The GPT-6.2 series launched on or around November 17, 2026. That means both models climbed the board in roughly four days.
DesignArena does not grade models on a fixed exam. It shows people outputs from two models side by side and asks them to pick the better one.
Those pairwise human votes feed an Elo score. The platform has evaluated 142+ models this way.
The gap between Astra and Sol is 67 Elo points. That suggests voters noticed a real quality difference between OpenAI’s two offerings, even as both beat most of the field.
The pair finished ahead of several heavyweight competitors. Those include Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5.5 and Meta’s Muse Wonder.

Game generation was not the only arena where the models showed up. Astra and Sol also ranked #3 and #7, respectively, on Code Arena’s WebDev leaderboard shortly after launch.
Notice the ordering stays the same across both boards. Astra leads Sol in game development and in web development.
Sol’s real pitch is the price tag
Sol reportedly delivers output quality comparable to earlier models at approximately 4x lower cost.
Sol has also shown a 40% improvement in generation speed in certain evaluations.
OpenAI’s models also improved on previous GPT-6 performance in areas such as visual reasoning and coding efficiency.
Why human-voted benchmarks matter
DesignArena’s approach leans on real-world preference instead of traditional benchmarks. The question is simple: which result would a person rather have?
With pairwise voting across a large pool of models, the Elo system is designed to smooth out individual quirks over time.
