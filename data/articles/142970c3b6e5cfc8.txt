TITLE: GTA 6's Car Physics Were Tuned by a Gran Turismo Pro
OUTLET: Push Square

Rockstar hired a former Gran Turismo pro to help master the car handling in GTA 6.
Lewis Bentley, a 2018 GT World Series Finalist, says he spent the last three-and-a-half years with the studio’s physics team, working on how the cars feel to drive.
In a now-deleted YouTube video – watched by GT Planet – he says he helped “put the auto in Grand Theft Auto”. He’s credited as a developer on the Physics Development Group, and he said in his video that the entire team was “amazing”.
The Edinburgh-based dev is no stranger to real-life racing, as it fielded its own winning team in the 24 Hours of Nürburgring event four years ago. Ben Lyons, who made up part of the squad, was the Technical Director in charge of physics on the title.
I personally really love the way cars feel in the game.
Each type has its own distinctive handling model, and you can really feel the difference when you go to a Mod Shop and tune your car to taste.
It doesn’t have the distinctive heavy handling of GTA 4, but it’s also not quite as arcadey as GTA 5 either – I’d say it sits somewhere comfortably between the two, which is a good place to be.
I love all the extra work that’s been put into the DualSense’s haptic feedback, too; the way you can physically feel the change of grip between the different road surfaces – it’s impressive stuff.
I just wish Rockstar had put a little more energy into the races themselves, because they do rely on aggressive rubber banding, which is pretty frustrating. Considering how meticulously the rest of the game is crafted, it would have been nice if a bit more thought went into this aspect.
All in all, though, I’m not surprised to learn GTA 6 had real-life race car drivers and pro Gran Turismo players on its team. You spend a good chunk of your time in Leonida driving, and I think by and large Rockstar really nailed this important aspect of the game.
