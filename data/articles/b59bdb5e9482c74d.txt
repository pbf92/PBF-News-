TITLE: GTA 6 Online Release Date: No Multiplayer at Launch
OUTLET: ThePCEnthusiast

Grand Theft Auto VI is almost here, but players hoping to explore Vice City with friends will have to wait a little longer.
After years of waiting, we’re finally just days away from getting our hands on Grand Theft Auto VI. Preloads are live, Vice City is waiting, and Jason and Lucia’s story begins on November 19, 2026. However, anyone hoping to jump straight into a new GTA Online experience with friends is going to be disappointed.
Rockstar Games is launching GTA 6 as a single-player experience on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The developer hasn’t announced when online multiplayer will arrive, although comments from Twitch’s CEO suggest we could be waiting until 2027.
There are also some interesting clues buried in GTA VI’s PlayStation data, giving us an early glimpse of what Rockstar may be preparing for multiplayer.
GTA 6 Launches November 19 Without Online Multiplayer
Rockstar confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI would be a single-player experience when it opened preorders in June. Sony’s official PlayStation listing makes this equally clear, with the game’s multiplayer FAQ stating:
Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience.
The PlayStation Store currently lists GTA VI as supporting one player, and Rockstar hasn’t announced any multiplayer modes for launch.
Instead, players will be stepping into the shoes of Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, whose story unfolds across Vice City and the sprawling fictional state of Leonida.
There’s certainly plenty to be excited about. Rockstar has spent years building its next open-world adventure, and judging by the trailers and gameplay footage released so far, exploring the new Vice City should be quite an experience.
But for those of us who have spent countless hours pulling off heists, racing through Los Santos, or simply causing chaos with friends in GTA Online, the wait isn’t quite over.
And that brings us to the big question – when will the next GTA Online arrive?

GTA 6 Online Could Launch in 2027, According to Twitch’s CEO
One of the strongest indications of a possible multiplayer release window came from Twitch CEO Dan Clancy.
During a September interview with Bloomberg, Clancy discussed GTA VI’s anticipated impact on game streaming and revealed that Twitch had been talking with Rockstar about its plans and how to engage content creators.
He expected GTA VI to deliver a significant increase in Twitch viewership at launch, followed by an even bigger surge “next year when they launch multiplayer.”
Since the interview took place in September 2026, Clancy’s remarks point toward a possible GTA 6 Online release sometime in 2027. That’s certainly interesting, especially coming from someone who indicated he had been discussing Rockstar’s plans directly with the developer.
However, Clancy was sharing his expectations, not announcing an official release date on Rockstar’s behalf. We still don’t know whether the new multiplayer experience will be a separate download, part of the existing game, or even what Rockstar intends to call it.

GTA 6’s PS5 Data Already Contains Multiplayer References
While Rockstar remains quiet about its online plans, some interesting details have surfaced from GTA VI’s PlayStation data.
On November 9, PlayStation database tracker PlayStation Game Size reported discovering three activity-related entries associated with the game:
- joinSession
- launchActivity
- launchMultiplayerActivity
These entries are associated with PlayStation’s activity system, which allows players to launch specific game activities or join multiplayer sessions directly through the console interface.
The launchMultiplayerActivity reference is particularly intriguing because it suggests that GTA VI has provisions for multiplayer-related functionality.
However, these are system-level references, not evidence of a finished or playable online mode. They also reveal nothing about its features or release schedule.
Still, it’s encouraging to see signs of multiplayer integration in the game’s data, even if Rockstar isn’t ready to show us what comes next.layer integration, but neither overrides Rockstar’s official single-player launch plans.
When Will Rockstar Announce GTA 6 Online?
With GTA VI’s launch almost upon us, Rockstar’s immediate focus is getting Jason and Lucia’s adventure into players’ hands.
A 2027 multiplayer release remains a possibility, and the PlayStation database discoveries suggest that some multiplayer-related support is already being prepared. But there is still no officially confirmed GTA 6 Online release date.
For now, we’ll finally get to explore Rockstar’s new Vice City and everything Leonida has to offer. The prospect of eventually returning with friends is exciting, too, especially after all these years playing GTA V Online.
We’ll just have to wait for Rockstar to reveal what it has planned.
