TITLE: Yes, You Can Invest in Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6). Here's How.
OUTLET: The Motley Fool

The release of the Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) video game is finally upon us. No new version of this extremely popular interactive digital entertainment has come out since the release of GTA 5 in 2013, and since the release of the first iteration of the game in 1997, it has ranked as one of the best-selling gaming franchises of all time.
Not surprisingly, the anticipation has drawn the interest of gamers and investors alike. Fortunately, the GTA 6 release presents an opportunity for shareholders; here's how investors can capitalize.
Image source: Getty Images.
Who owns GTA 6?
The most straightforward way to invest in this game is to buy Take-Two Interactive (TTWO +6.22%) stock. Take-Two is the parent company of Rockstar Games, the studio that developed the game. The game's launch brings to mind multiple takeaways for long-term investors, raised just after the company released its latest earnings report. Take-Two stock rose by 6.6% in the trading session that closed hours before the game's release. However, the stock has dropped over the last year largely due to the company's past delays in releasing the game.
In the first half of fiscal 2027 (ended Sept. 30), revenue of $3 billion fell by 8% from year-ago levels. Analysts blamed weaker in-game spending on NBA 2K and the anticipation of GTA 6 as reasons for the decline.
Also, the company reported an $88 million loss during the same period, though analysts forecast a return to profitability in upcoming quarters. For now, Take-Two's price-to-sales (P/S) ratio is 6.3. That is slightly above the five-year average of 5.3 and suggests investors will pay a modest premium by buying the stock now.
ETF investment options
Amid that high valuation, some investors might not be comfortable owning Take-Two stock. Others might see the GTA 6 release as an opportunity for gaming in general. Such investors may choose to invest through an exchange-traded fund (ETF), with multiple opportunities available.
The ETF with the highest exposure to Take-Two is the VanEck Digital Native Economy ETF (GENZ +0.77%). Approximately 8.25% of the fund's holdings are in Take-Two. Unfortunately, this comes with a 0.51% expense ratio, just above the industry average of 0.48%. Moreover, it lost 26% of its value over the last five years, further dampening its appeal.
The one with the best five-year performance is the VanEck Video Gaming and eSports ETF (ESPO +1.55%), which returned about 35% over that time. Its exposure to Take-Two is slightly less, just 6.05%. Still, the higher return probably makes the 0.55% expense ratio more palatable.
Another fund to consider is the State Street Communication Services Select SPDR ETF (XLC +0.30%). It returned 31% over the last five years, and its 0.08% expense ratio makes it cheap to own. Still, Take-Two makes up just 4.75% of the fund's holdings, and most of the stocks are not in gaming.
Investing in GTA 6
As one of the best-selling gaming franchises of all time, the GTA 6 release looks to be huge, and investors have many options to capitalize on the opportunity.
Investing in the launch of GTA 6 involves holding a position in Rockstar Games' parent company, Take-Two, either directly or through an ETF. Whatever direction investors take, the revival of one of gaming's most popular franchises is an opportunity that does not come along often.
Knowing that, investors probably have good reason to at least consider investments tied to the communication stock and, possibly, the gaming industry in general.
