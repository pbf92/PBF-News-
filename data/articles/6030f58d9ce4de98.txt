USA Rare Earth Director Purchased Shares Worth Over $2.9M
Michael Blitzer, a director of USA Rare Earth, reported purchasing 100,000 shares directly on November 17, 2026, at $6.7155 per share and 250,000 shares through Tristram Ave LLC that day at $6.6439 per share. He reported purchasing another 100,000 shares through Tristram Ave LLC on November 18 at $6.6412 per share. The total purchase amount was $2,996,645. Following the transactions, Blitzer directly owned 1,855,537 shares and indirectly owned 350,000 shares through Tristram Ave LLC. Blitzer disclaimed beneficial ownership of the shares held by Tristram Ave LLC except to the extent of his pecuniary interest.
