TITLE: Manchester United still smarting from missing out on Harry Kane
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Quinton Fortune believes Manchester United made a massive mistake by passing on Harry Kane, and he isn't holding back on how much the England captain could have changed things at Old Trafford.
Asked whether missing out on Kane was a major transfer blunder, the former United midfielder didn't hesitate.
"A hundred and twenty million percent," Fortune told Mr Gamble.
Fortune argued that Sir Alex Ferguson would have gotten the deal done immediately and never let Kane leave English football if he were still in charge.
"Sir Alex would've got the deal done straight away. There wouldn't be any doubt that he wouldn't leave England to go abroad if Sir Alex was there, my goodness," Fortune said.
He also praised Kane's all-around game — dropping into midfield, creating chances, and finishing clinically — even saying United should target him later in his career despite his age.
"When he leaves Bayern, I'll take him at 36. No, no, seriously, I'll take him at 36," Fortune said.
Comparing Kane's efficiency to Erling Haaland, Fortune pointed out how few openings the striker needs to hurt opponents.
"He's ruthless. He does not give you two or three chances, he scores two out of three," Fortune noted.
It's tough to argue with the assessment. Kane has thrived as one of Europe's most complete forwards at Bayern Munich, and United could still use a striker of his quality. Missing out on him continues to sting for United supporters.
