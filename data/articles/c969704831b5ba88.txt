TITLE: Manchester United target defender for January window but manager refuses to sell
OUTLET: Read Man Utd

Manchester United have leaked goals this season and Mauricio Pochettino is now set to get some names in January to address that.
United are reportedly considering several options at centre-back, with Calvin Bassey high on their shortlist. The 26-year-old has impressed since joining Fulham from Ajax in July 2023 for €22.5 million and has become an important figure at Craven Cottage. He currently captains Alvaro Arbeloa’s side.
The Nigerian defender has emerged as a potential target for Mauricio Pochettino as Manchester United look to address their defensive problems ahead of the second half of the season.
However, United have suffered a potential transfer setback after Fulham head coach Alvaro Arbeloa made it clear that Calvin Bassey will not be allowed to leave during the January transfer window.
Alvaro Arbeloa has also confirmed that discussions are already taking place over a new contract.
“I am so happy with Calvin and obviously he is not on sale. No chance that he can leave this club in January. He is happy here, he is one of our captains and one of our leaders,” Arbeloa said about Bassey during his press conference.
Fulham working to secure Bassey’s future
Arbeloa believes the interest from other clubs reflects Bassey’s performances over time and is keen to keep him in his squad beyond the current season.
“You can see how he is playing and it’s normal that so many clubs are interested in him. It’s good for him and for us because it means he is performing really well.”
The Fulham manager also confirmed that the club are working on an improved contract for the Nigerian international.
“It’s very important for me as a coach, for the club and we are working on a new contract. I hope he can sign as soon as possible.”
Bassey’s current agreement runs until 2027, although Fulham have the option to extend it for another year. Amid the interest, the club is keen to reach fresh terms before his contract situation becomes a bigger issue.
Pochettino wants to strengthen his options, with the club sitting 19th in the Premier League table after 11 matches. United have a little budget for January and big names aren’t cheap to sign in that budget. Their interest in Bassey is understandable but given Arbeloa’s comment, United need a solid offer to get him in January.
But unless Fulham change their position or contract negotiations break down, United may have to look elsewhere for defensive reinforcements.
