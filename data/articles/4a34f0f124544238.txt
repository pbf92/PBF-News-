Midnight: Real-world asset issuance opens to institutions - Q1 2027
Midnight plans to add real-world asset issuance in Q1 2027. The release will let institutions issue tokens backed by investment funds, credit products and real estate on the network.
Institutions will also be able to shield the tokens they issue, allowing them to use private tokens for these assets. The release gives NIGHT exposure to a wider range of institutional asset activity.
