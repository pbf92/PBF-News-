TITLE: Manni L. Perez, the actress who plays Lucía in GTA 6: “I’ve dedicated almost a decade bringing Lucia to life”
OUTLET: Diario AS

Development of GTA VI began after the release of the fifth installment, a lengthy process that has spanned more than ten years. The new video game from Rockstar Games went on sale on December 19, but Manni L. Perez, the actress who plays Lucía, has spent nearly as long developing the character.
“Life has been such a whirlwind these past couple of days and I just want to take a moment to say thank you,” the actress wrote in an Instagram Story. “Thank you to all the new followers, to all the beautiful messages, and the positive reception the game and my character have received.”
A whole decade with the character
It’s been a long journey, as Perez herself explained: “I’ve dedicated almost a decade bringing Lucia to life. She’s been such a significant part of mine and I am beyond grateful she now gets to be a small part of yours. Happy Holiday’s y’all”
In 2023, when the first trailer for GTA VI was released, some people figured out that Manni L. Perez was the actress behind the character Lucía, although this wasn’t officially revealed until recently. As with other projects, actors sign confidentiality agreements, so they can’t announce that they’re part of a project until the company gives them permission or officially reveals it—as was the case here.
GTA VI is now available on PS5, Xbox Series X, and Xbox Series S.
