TITLE: 'GTA VI' Cactus Jack x Rockstar Games x Nike Air Force 1
OUTLET: Yahoo

As confirmed by Travis Scott earlier this month, his Cactus Jack label is working with Rockstar Games and Nike on a three-way Air Force 1 collab inspired by the highly-anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI video game.
Nike and Cactus Jack just confirmed that the aforementioned Air Force 1 release will be accompanied by matching apparel. Both the "GTA VI" Air Force 1 Lows and the apparel range will feature designs inspired by the game, featuring the "Five Star" and camo graphics throughout, GTA VI branding, and elastic money bands. The "GTA VI" Cactus Jack x Nike Air Force 1 Lows have been unveiled in the model's classic "Triple White" and "Triple Black" color schemes as well as a more limited purple and camo makeup teased by Travis earlier this month.
Ahead of the release in November, here's everything you need to know about the "GTA VI" Cactus Jack x Rockstar Games x Nike Air Force 1 collection.

What's the pricing for the "GTA VI" Cactus Jack x Rockstar Games x Nike collection?
The "GTA VI" Cactus Jack x Rockstar Games x Nike Air Force 1s are currently rumored to retail for $130, but prices have yet to be officially announced by the collaborators.
