TITLE: GTA 6: What’s included in the Ultimate Edition and how it differs from the Standard Edition
OUTLET: Diario AS

GTA 6 is now on sale. In fact, you can purchase the game directly from the PS Store or Microsoft Store to start preloading it ahead of its official release on December 19, although you can also buy the physical version for PS5 and Xbox Series, which includes a download code and a poster. In any case, both options are the standard version of Grand Theft Auto VI, though there’s also the so-called Ultimate Edition—a separately purchasable bundle that includes a number of extras not found in the base version of the game.
What does the Ultimate Edition of GTA 6 offer?
As we mentioned, GTA 6 is available in two versions, both in digital format: the Standard Edition and the Ultimate Edition. The Standard Edition is priced at $79.99 and includes the base game Grand Theft Auto VI, the Vintage Vice City Pack, and one month of GTA+. On the other hand, there’s the Premium or Ultimate Edition of GTA 6, which includes everything from the Standard Edition plus the so-called Ultimate Edition Upgrade, for $99.99. If you already own the base game, you can also purchase the Ultimate Edition for $20 starting November 19.
This Ultimate Edition includes a number of extras not found in the Standard Edition of GTA 6: “Welcome to Leonida, the state where anything goes. Seize everything this massive world has to offer with the Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition, an exclusive collection of premium vehicles, weapons, apparel, and action around every corner,” say the developers. “The bonus content in the Ultimate Edition is threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia’s story, with new items uncovered behind each chapter,” add representatives from Rockstar Games.
As Rockstar notes, the bonus content in the Ultimate Edition will be unlocked as you progress through the story. Here are the main bonus features:
- ‘95 Grotti Cheetah
- Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers
- Personalized Weapon Variants
- Vice City Styles
- Jason’s Safehouse Vehicles
- Ganado Retro Build
- Rideout Customs Mod Shop
- Sara’s Unisex Salon
- Shitzu Squalo
- Stock 305 Clothing Store
- ‘67 Vapid Dominator Buggy and Garage
- Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor
- One-Eyed Willie’s Mod Shop
- Goodtime Gear
- PTT Youngin$ Compound
- Classic Car Collection
Bonuses for Pre-ordering the Vice City Vintage Pack
- ‘55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and Garage
- Outfits and Hairstyles
- Exclusive Weapon Pattern
As we can see, this is a fairly generous selection of bonus content not included in the base edition of GTA 6: from exclusive vehicles to businesses and stores, as well as cosmetic items, modifiers, unique weapons, and more.
