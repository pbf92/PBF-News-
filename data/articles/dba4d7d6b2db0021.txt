TITLE: SNDL launches renewed C$100M share buyback program
OUTLET: TradingView

SNDL launches renewed C$100M share buyback program
- SNDL SNDL stock price surged about 5% on Monday pre-market hours as it renewed share repurchase program, authorizing the repurchase of up to C$100M of common shares between November 23, 2026, and November 22, 2027.
- The program allows SNDL to repurchase up to ~23.57M shares, representing 10% of its public float.
- Under its previous program, which ended November 20, 2026, SNDL repurchased 24.49M shares, representing 10% of its public float and 100% of the shares eligible for repurchase.
