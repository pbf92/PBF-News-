TITLE: Anthropic shares set at $US83 as blockbuster float prepares for launch
OUTLET: AFR

Anthropic is expected to be valued at more than $2.2 trillion when it debuts on the Nasdaq this week, with the five-year-old company behind the popular Claude artificial intelligence platform pricing shares at $US83.
The price has risen higher than initially expected when the company filed its prospectus last week, with plans to raise a potential $US90.4 billion, including a $26 billion private placement. Its 825 million shares were initially listed in the prospectus with a price at between $US70 and $US78 each, and a company valuation of about $US1.4 trillion ($2.08 trillion).
