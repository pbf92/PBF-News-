Non-Invasive Monitoring Systems Extends Gravitics Merger Deadline and Related Note Maturities
Non-Invasive Monitoring Systems and Gravitics extended the outside closing and termination dates for their proposed merger to December 31, 2026, from November 30. The company also extended the maturity dates of four $75,000 affiliate promissory notes to December 31. Separately, it agreed to pay Defender Opportunity a $25,000 fee to extend a convertible note's merger-related conversion deadline to the same date.
Agreement 1: Non-Invasive Monitoring Systems Extends Gravitics Merger Deadline to Dec. 31
- Agreement type: Sixth amendment to merger agreement
- Counterparty: Gravitics
- Signed / Effective: Nov 23 2026 / Nov 23 2026
- Duration / Termination: Outside closing and termination dates extended to Dec 31 2026
- Reason: Extend the deadline to complete the merger
Agreement 2: Non-Invasive Monitoring Systems Extends Frost Gamma Note Maturity to Dec. 31
