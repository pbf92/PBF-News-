TITLE: Resident Evil Requiem, Silent Hill: Townfall and More Horror Games See Big Discounts for Black Friday
OUTLET: VGC / IGN

As a big fan of horror games, I've been so happy to see how stacked this year's Black Friday sale is with spooky releases. That includes Resident Evil Requiem and Silent Hill: Townfall on PS5 down to $30 a piece, Silent Hill 2 on sale for $20, and (my favorite finds so far) Resident Evil 2, Resident Evil 3, and Resident Evil 7 down to just $13.
As a newer game, a $30 price tag at Walmart for Resident Evil Requiem has been a highlight, though the discount only applies to the PS5 version. If that sells out, the good news is it's available across all platforms for just $40 at Amazon. Silent Hill: Townfall is also worth a spin. I had an amazing time playing immersing myself in the eerie town of St. Amelia and unraveling main character Simon Ordell's story along the way.
Some of the best Black Friday horror game deals have been on older Resident Evil releases. RE2, RE3, and RE7 have dropped to just $13 at Amazon, while RE4 is available for $14. Even Resident Evil Village is on sale right now for just $18. All five come out to $70.55 together, or about the cost of Resident Evil Requiem at its usual full price. That's a lot of Resident Evil for not a lot of money.
