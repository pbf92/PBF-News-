TITLE: Elohim / Creator God
OUTLET: Christianity Today

My husband and I recently road-tripped through Zion National Park in Utah, and it was absolutely breathtaking. The beautiful canyons and massive rock formations were so overwhelming that I could hardly process it all. I took a few photos through the car window, hoping to remember the experience, but they could not capture the magnitude of what my senses were taking in.
After we left the park, I eventually dozed off, leaving my husband to drive in silence. About an hour later, the crunch of gravel under the tires woke me. We’d arrived at another geological site, but this time, we had to leave the car and hike to an overlook. Shaking off my sleepiness, I pulled on my coat and sluggishly stepped onto the path.
As we crested the hill, Bryce Canyon came into view. I stood wide-eyed and suddenly very awake. Thousands of fiery orange, deep red, and white stone spires, or “hoodoos,” sprawled out below me. The sunlit, spire-filled canyon was surrounded by plunging rock cliffs and dark green forest. I was speechless, and so were the other onlookers. The atmosphere was somber and reverent; we whispered and moved slowly, with quiet intentionality, across the overlook.
Were we all feeling the same thing? I was in awe and so thankful to experience this incredible work of art, a truly unexpected gift.
Over the next few months, I did some research. I discovered that a series of spectacular events combined to form the beauty of Bryce Canyon. Years of various rock sediments piled up to produce vibrant colors and striped patterns. Tectonic plate movement forced the earth upward by 9,000 feet, creating the massive plateau and plunging canyon. Finally, continuous weathering from rain, wind, and frost carved the remaining rock pillars into intricate fins and iconic hoodoos. And to think, the masterpiece I witnessed was set in motion by a single breath (Gen. 1–2), carried along by the Creator’s masterful design, and gifted to me on the day I stood at its rim as a reflection of his glory.
Imagine God creating the heavens and earth. He suspended the sun, moon, and stars in every galaxy—not one existing before and none of them alike. Land and sea emerged from the void. God set light apart from darkness. Seasons from timelessness. Fruit and vegetation from barrenness. All creatures great and small from lifelessness. Everything was perfectly designed and effortlessly crafted by Elohim, our Mighty Creator.
Now, imagine Elohim creating you and me. Ephesians 2:10 (NLT) calls us God’s “masterpiece”—beautifully designed by his hand, just like Bryce Canyon. While that truth alone is awe-inspiring, God’s intention in our conception goes even deeper. Unlike the rest of creation, we are made in his image (Gen. 1:26), designed to mirror the glory of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and ordained to walk in the good works he prepared for us. Breathed into existence out of divine relationship and love, we are made and empowered through Jesus to show His love to the world.
Like walking right into the surprise beauty of Bryce Canyon, I hope we can step into a hurting world this Advent and surprise someone with the magnificent gift of Jesus’ love.
Kelli Petrik is currently the Director of Women’s Ministry at The Vail Church in Avon, Colorado. She and her husband of 38 years have raised 5 children and have followed the Lord in ministry all over the world.
