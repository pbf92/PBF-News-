Zscaler soars on report CrowdStrike in talks to purchase
Zscaler ZS jumped 18% on a report that CrowdStrike CRWD is in talks to acquire the cybersecurity company. CrowdStrike fell 11%.
The two companies are discussing a potential deal that could come together soon, according to a Bloomberg report on Friday, which cited people familiar with the matter.
Deliberations are ongoing, and there’s no certainty a deal will happen, Bloomberg reported. Crowdstrike CRWD has a market cap of $250 billion, while Zscaler ZS is valued at $34 billion.
