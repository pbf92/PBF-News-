TITLE: The Real-World Planes GTA VI's Aircraft Are Based On
OUTLET: Simple Flying

After around 13 years of waiting, Grand Theft Auto fans have finally been treated to the next installment in the thrilling franchise. Even non-gamers will find something to enjoy. Rockstar Games doesn't give any of the vehicles in the game real-world names or state that they are based on real aircraft. However, eagle-eyed avgeeks have already spotted clear similarities.
The game also features no car brands despite similarities between in-game and real cars. The reasoning is likely that Rockstar Games would have to spend a huge amount on licensing deals and ensure all design elements in the game mirror real life. Some brands may also have decided to keep away from a game franchise that has frequently been controversial due to its violence, in-game crime, and more.
GTA VI Has 23 Named Aircraft
GTA Base has listed 23 different fixed-wing aircraft that players can use in-game. They include a mixture of planes from general, commercial, private, and military aviation. This is a similar type of vehicle in the game, as there are supercars, sports cars, and vans, and far more than there are bicycles, trucks, industrial, and ground military vehicles.

Planes without clear real-world connections include some from the in-game brand Lapwing, including the Stolen Valor and the 300-H. GTA Base also says that the Decker Crake, Buckingham Cellerica Shamal, and Bottcher O15A are original.
Grand Theft Auto VI also has 19 helicopters, many of which have clear connections to real-world examples. GTA Base says that the military helicopter, the Buckingham Valkyrie, has connections with the Bell UH-1Y Venom and the UH-1 Iroquois Huey. In civil aviation, helicopters with real-world parallels include the Buckingham Supervolito (Eurocopter EC145) and Swift (AgustaWestland AW109). The game also has emergency helicopters. For example, the Police Maverick is akin to the Eurocopter AS350B.
The Lapwing Mammatus Represents A Commercial Giant
Grand Theft Auto VI includes a handful of light aircraft from the world of general aviation, including two variants of the Mammatus. This aircraft is based on the 172 Skyhawk, with the Cessna light aircraft being by far the most commercially successful of all the real-life equivalents to planes in the Rockstar title. At least 44,000 of these planes have been sold, and no plane has had as many examples built.
The Skyhawk was first introduced in the mid-1950s and developed from the Cessna 170. The main innovation was the inclusion of tricycle landing gear. The light aircraft has a crew of one, with space for three passengers and 120 lb (54 kg) of baggage. The 172R variant is powered by a single Lycoming IO-360-L2A four-cylinder, horizontally opposed aircraft engine, which produces 160 hp (120 kW) to turn the two-bladed metal propeller.

Following its successful commercial run, the Skyhawk was developed into the Cessna 175 Skylark. This aircraft was more powerful. Yet, it was less commercially successful, with 2,108 built. Production of this plane stopped in 1962, while the Skyhawk has remained in production since resuming after a hiatus from 1986 to 1996.
The Main Fighter Jet Takes Inspiration From The F-16
The Hauner P-996 Lazer is the primary fighter jet in Grand Theft Auto VI. It bears clear similarities to the F-16 in performance and agility. The Lazer also shares the Fighting Falcon's many hardpoints for missiles and other ordnance. However, it is not a perfect replica, with tail fins on either side of the single engine rather than a single fin mounted directly above it.
The twin tail fins are far from the only point where the Lazer diverges from typical F-16 expectations. Its armament includes a twin cannon, which is not something that is seen on the F-16 or most modern fighter jets. The F-16 has a single M61A1 Vulcan six-barrel Gatling cannon.
This aircraft also takes off conventionally in both GTA V and GTA VI, while it had vertical takeoff and landing capabilities in the beta version of GTA V. The F-16 is a multirole fighter, initially manufactured by General Dynamics and now by Lockheed Martin.
A range of other manufacturers, such as Fokker and KAI, have also been licensed to build the aircraft at various points in its history. Over 4,600 of the aircraft have been built during its almost 50 years of service. It is flown by air forces worldwide, serving the United States, Belgium, Pakistan, Oman, Romania, and more.
The Superjumbo Isn't Dead In Leonida
You will struggle to see 747s or Airbus A380s in Florida, apart from at Miami International Airport (MIA). On the whole, it is a state that has witnessed the death of the quadjet, despite being among the world's most popular tourist destinations. However, in Leonida, the Florida-based setting for GTA VI, you will be able to see and fly the Bottcher O20s, the in-game equivalent of the 747.
Taking off with the Queen of the Skies will be on the to-do list of many avgeeks booting up GTA VI for the first time. Gamers can steal the jet by heading to Vice City International Airport (VCIA) in Vice City, which is based on Miami. You can steal the jet by breaking into the airport and climbing the boarding stairs onto the jet.
The police will be after you immediately, but you'll earn the Catch Me If You Can trophy if you successfully escape the airport. Speculation is ongoing about whether the Bottcher O20 is a replica of the 747-400 or 747-8, with the jet sharing characteristics of both aircraft. The Dreamliner had failed to hold short of the taxiway as it had been instructed. Luckily, the four-engined jet came to a stop before disaster could strike.
The Lufthansa crew told the air traffic control tower that they would file a report because "that was very close to an accident in the making." Miami's tower replied that the 787 had "made every wrong turn possible." The 747 ultimately departed safely from MIA, but was delayed by an hour.
There Are Also Cars In GTA VI That Mirror Real Life
Avgeeks will find lots to get their teeth into when they play the new Grand Theft Auto, which could be akin to a flight simulator if played in a certain way. However, petrolheads will find even more to enjoy. Of the hundreds of vehicles in the world of GTA VI, the majority of them are road vehicles. Many of these cars and trucks have close real-world equivalents.
The fastest cars are in the supercar category. An example is the Progen Seaspray Emerus, which has a speed rating of 8.9 out of 10 and is based on the McLaren Senna. Things get a little slower, but more stylish with the sports cars. One of the most eye-catching is the Declasse Endurance Mamba GT, which is based on the Shelby Daytona Coupe.
Rockstar Games also pays homage to classic American muscle with an impressive selection of muscle cars. The Invetero Spiderweb Coquette is close to the Chevrolet Corvette C2. Some in-game muscle cars are based on modern vehicles, like the Declasse Twinstripe Vigero, which resembles the Chevrolet Camaro Gen VI.
GTA VI includes 77 different types of motorbikes, so bike enthusiasts also aren't left out. The Nagasaki in-game manufacturer makes sports bikes based on various Suzuki and Ducati models, while Ubermacht makes bikes based on BMW models.
