TITLE: Magic: The Gathering Play Booster Boxes Are up to 50% Off at Amazon for Black Friday
OUTLET: VGC / IGN

Magic’s busy year might have you wondering how in the multiverse, you can keep up with it all, but thankfully, Amazon has some handy Black Friday deals to help fill in the gaps in your collection.
Across its catalog, there are savings to be found on Booster Boxes from this year, as well as a product I’d highly recommend: The Starter Collection from Foundations. Here’s what’s on offer, and how much you can save.
Before We Begin
Sadly, the following are all Play Booster boxes. That means they’re ideal for building a collection without spending a ton of money, but they do have fewer chances at rare cards than the much pricier Collector Boosters. Plus, those are also harder to find.
The Best Amazon Magic: The Gathering Deals This Black Friday
Kicking us off is the oldest set of the Play Booster Boxes on offer, albeit not by much. Marvel’s Super Heroes was a huge step up from the Spider-Man set, and while your interest will naturally vary depending on how much you want the likes of Iron Man and Captain America cards in your Magic: The Gathering collection, we can’t argue with a good deal.
And, that’s exactly what Amazon is offering, with a $104.90 Play Booster Box down from $147.95 - at least according to Amazon. In reality, the box started off at $209.70, the standard MSRP for a Universes Beyond box.
That brings the price-per-pack from $6.99, all the way down to $3.50 - a 50% price cut.
Next up, The Hobbit brought us back to Middle-Earth after our (excellent) first time visiting with The Lord of the Rings set, and you can save big on a Booster Box.
Again, Amazon says it’s a 12% discount but that doesn’t take into account the MSRP of $209.70. So you’re actually saving a bunch more than that when paying $142.08.
In terms of per-pack, you’re paying $4.74 for each, but this set has the added advantage of a Box Topper card.
Finally for Play Booster Boxes, Reality Fracture is discounted. It’s an awesome set (one that maybe deserved more chance to shine…) and includes Mirrorverse versions of classic Magic: The Gathering spells, characters, and creatures.
It’s now $125.08, reduced from $139.65, meaning each pack costs you $4.17.
One More Thing
Also reduced in the Black Friday sale is the Magic: The Gathering Foundations Starter Collection.
This is a box of more than 350 cards, including a bunch of staples for building your own decks, and is an ideal purchase for anyone new to the game who wants an instant collection.
It’s usually $59.99, but with a 25% saving, it’s down to $44.95 - and I can wholeheartedly recommend it.
