OneMain Wins UDFI and FDIC Approval to Establish OneMain Bank
Less than 1 min read
OneMain received conditional UDFI and FDIC approval to establish OneMain Bank, a Utah-chartered industrial bank expected to open in approximately one year.
Key Highlights:
- UDFI and FDIC approved OneMain’s applications to establish the bank and insure its deposits, subject to customary conditions.
- When it opens, OneMain Bank plans to offer personal loans, auto finance and credit cards to customers nationwide.
- The bank will operate independently with its own board, management, governance, policies and controls, while OneMain retains its non-bank status.
- Lee Carter, who has more than 35 years of banking experience, was selected to lead OneMain Bank as president.
- The bank must complete its organization plan and meet applicable regulatory requirements before opening.
Original SEC Filing: OneMain Holdings, Inc. [ OMF ] - 8-K - Nov. 17, 2026
