Invesque Inc. (IVQ) Shareholders Approve $0.101-Per-Share Go-Private Deal
Invesque shareholders approved the go-private arrangement, under which the purchaser will pay US$0.101 per share, less applicable withholdings. Court approval and remaining conditions are pending; closing is expected on or about November 24, 2026.

Context by AI Analyst
This is a late-stage step in Invesque's previously announced go-private transaction, following shareholder approval. A final court order and satisfaction or waiver of remaining conditions are needed before the expected closing and delisting.
