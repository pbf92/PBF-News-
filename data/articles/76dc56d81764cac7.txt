HYLQ Strategy Announces Proposed Name Change and Appointment of Director
Toronto, Ontario--(Newsfile Corp. - November 23, 2026) - HYLQ Strategy Corp. (formerly, Tony G Co-Investment Holdings Ltd.) HYLQ (the "Company" or "HYLQ Strategy") is pleased to announce that it has filed articles of amendment to change its corporate name, effective December 1, 2026, from "HYLQ Strategy Corp." to "Tony G Corp." (the "Name Change"). In connection with the Name Change, the Company's trading symbol on the Canadian Securities Exchange will be changed to "TONY". The Company expects its issued and outstanding common shares will commence trading under the new name and ticker symbol at market opening on December 1, 2026.
A new CUSIP number has been obtained to replace the previous CUSIP number. No action will be required by existing shareholders with respect to the Name Change. Share certificates representing common shares of the Company will not be affected and will not need to be exchanged.
In addition, the Company is pleased to announce that effective December 1, 2026, Mr. Antanas (Tony) Guoga has been appointed a director and the Chairman of the Company. Mr. Guoga is a prominent Lithuanian-Australian entrepreneur, poker player and former politician. In business, Tony founded PokerNews, which has become one of the largest poker media outlets globally, and launched TonyBet, an international betting platform that was acquired by Betsson. He also established the Blockchain Centre Vilnius, a hub for blockchain startups, and is a director and advisor to numerous crypto-related businesses. His academic background includes a degree in Accounting and Finance from RMIT University in Australia.
About the Company
HYLQ Strategy Corp. HYLQ is a Canadian investment company focused on identifying and capitalizing on high-growth opportunities across emerging technologies and innovative industries. The Company seeks to create long-term shareholder value through disciplined capital allocation, strategic investments, and active treasury management. HYLQ evaluates opportunities across digital assets, artificial intelligence, robotics, aerospace, energy infrastructure, and other transformative sectors.
