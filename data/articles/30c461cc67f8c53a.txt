TITLE: GTA 7 is already being teased by Rockstar Games in GTA 6 - I'm convinced
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Now that GTA VI is out many gamers have one question on their minds; when’s GTA VII coming out?
We’re kidding of course. The leaks may have already revealed Rockstar’s next game, and no it’s not Red Dead Redemption 3 either.
The question does remain though; where do we go from here? How on Earth could Rockstar ever top GTA VI with another game? Personally, I think I’ve found the answer – it won’t.

I think those rumours that GTA VI will be an ever-evolving world were right on the money, because after putting it well over 100 hours by now there seems to be a staggering amount of content left inaccessible.
GTA 6 is going to be Rockstar’s personal infinite money glitch for years to come
While exploring Leonida I’ve stumbled upon plenty of buildings that look like they have something in them, but can’t be accessed by normal means.
The best example I can give is the casinos. Two have been found in the game so far and while they’re inaccessible they don’t look that way on the game’s map.
You see, any building on your map that’s opaque cannot be entered, but any that are translucent usually can.
The casino just North of Vice City is one such translucent building, so I’m adamant we’ll be able to enter it one day.
When is the next question though. Everyone is expecting GTA 6 Online to be the next big thing and add a bunch of new content to the game, but I personally think a lot of it will bleed into the story mode too.

The single-player even has a Store in the pause menu. Right now it only has contents from the Ultimate Edition, but I’ll bet money that it’ll be getting additional microtransactions sooner or later.
Whatever gets introduced will likely pair with new things to do in Leonida, like the casinos opening up as new recreational activities or heist locations.
Sure, Rockstar could put all of its eggs into one basket and focus primarily on the new Online mode, but it’ll retain far more players if it appeals to both camps.
GTA VI is comparable to Red Dead Redemption 2 in terms of open-world design and appeal. People are going to be exploring for months before they even consider starting a new save file, so what better way to keep them playing than additional content, and maybe a story expansion or two like the old days.
Before launch everyone assumed Liberty City would be a secret location in the game, so how cool would it be to make it one via some flashback missions starring Lucia? If Rockstar played its cards right GTA VI could easily be the only Grand Theft Auto game we'd need for decades. GTA VI, VII and even VIII all rolled into one.
Of course on day there will inevitably be an official GTA VII. The series is far too profitable to stop now, but with Rockstar's current business model the pressure is all the way off for another instalment.
Both GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 really suffered from a lack of post-launch single-player content and I’m praying Rockstar has learned from its mistakes, especially when there’ll be thousands if not millions of players uninterested in taking their gameplay experience online.
