TITLE: Hiker airlifted off moors after 'serious fall' on hills above Dovestones
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Hiker airlifted off moors after 'serious fall' on hills above Dovestones
The coastguard helicopter winched the walker to a waiting ambulance
A walker was airlifted from the moors after a 'serious fall' on the hills above Dovestones reservoir. A major rescue operation was launched after the hiker was hurt in the fall in Charnel Clough, a small, rocky gully near the reservoir in Saddleworth.
Firefighters and more than 20 volunteers from Oldham Mountain Rescue Team were called to the remote scene last Sunday morning.
The walker, whose age or gender hasn't been revealed, was winched from the clough by the coastguard helicopter and taken to a nearby ambulance before being driven to hospital.

In a statement on Facebook OMRT said: "Our team medic assessed the casualty, administered pain relief, and in the challenging rocky location ensured full spinal support with our volunteers working as a team to carefully package into a vacuum mattress before being securely strapped into our specialist rescue stretcher.
"Given the steep and challenging terrain of the clough, along with the nature of the injuries sustained, the assistance of HM Coastguard helicopter was requested.
"The crew skillfully winched the casualty from the clough and flew them down for a safe transfer into a waiting road ambulance for onward hospital care."
The rescue operation took four hours. OMRT added: "We wish the walker a full and speedy recovery."
