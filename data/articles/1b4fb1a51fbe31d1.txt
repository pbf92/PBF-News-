TITLE: Who was 'a consistent threat'? Newcastle v Arsenal player ratings
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
Arsenal and Newcastle played out an entertaining 2-2 draw at St James' Park on Saturday.
The Gunners took the lead through Eberechi Eze in the first half before Harvey Barnes equalised after the break.
The hosts went ahead when Malick Thiaw bundled in from a corner before Noni Madueke brought the Gunners level with an excellent strike, and that is how it finished.
BBC Sport's Alex Howell has rated the players - and you can have your say too.
Newcastle United
Lukas Hornicek: His kicking was slightly erratic. Nearly got caught on the ball by Kai Havertz after taking too long to release it. Could have done better with the second Arsenal goal. 5
Amar Dedic: Booked after pulling back Eberechi Eze and had a tough evening against the England forward. 5
Malick Thiaw: Scored the goal to put Newcastle in front, which capped an excellent performance in central defence. 8
Dan Burn: Some crucial clearances in the first half and played a big part as Newcastle took the lead. 7
Lewis Hall: A big error for the opening goal when he failed to deal with a clipped ball over the top. The left back redeemed himself with a crucial block to deny Noni Madueke and provided some excellent deliveries into the Arsenal box. 7
Sean Steur: Showed quality on the ball and a calmness you do not often see in an 18-year-old, but taken off at half time. 6
Lewis Miley: Quality on the ball and worked well with Steur. Was the deepest of the Newcastle midfield in the second half. 7
Jacob Murphy: An early shot and tried to catch David Raya off his line, but also taken off at the break after a quiet first half. 5
Joe Willock: An all-action performance and his physicality was a big reason why Newcastle improved in the second half. 7
Harvey Barnes: An excellent finish to bring Newcastle level and was always direct. 8
William Osula: Isolated as he led the Newcastle attack but did very well to assist his side's opening goal. 7
Subs:
Tino Livramento (on 46, Dedic): Made a huge impact after coming on at half-time. 7
Anthony Elanga (on 46, Murphy): His pace meant Newcastle always had an option in behind. 6
Jacob Ramsey (on 46, Steur): Added drive to the midfield. 7
Sven Botman (on 92, Hall)
Subs not used: Pope, Schar, Gonzalez, Wissa, Fernandez-Pardo.
Arsenal
David Raya: Was taken off with what looked like a serious shoulder problem after conceding twice. 5
Jurrien Timber: Arsenal used the Dutchman's aerial ability to get up the pitch quickly. Picked up some dangerous positions when the Gunners had the ball. 6
Ezri Konsa: The centre-back looked to be cruising before Arsenal conceded twice in the second half. 6
Gabriel Magalhaes: Got caught up in a battle with Osula as Arsenal conceded a second to go behind. 6
Riccardo Calafiori: A quieter attacking display than usual from the marauding left-back. 6
Declan Rice: Started the game very well but could not stop the Newcastle turnaround. 7
Bruno Guimaraes: Had to deal with a hostile reception whenever he was on the ball. Should have scored in the first half with a great chance and then taken off. 5
Martin Odegaard: An uncharacteristic mistake to gift Newcastle a way back into the match, but then made amends by setting up Madueke. 6
Eberechi Eze: Scored the opening goal of the game with a powerful finish. Looked sharp and provided an outlet. 7
Noni Madueke: Lots of positive moments from the winger and scored a great goal to bring Arsenal level. A consistent threat. 8
Kai Havertz: Plenty of smart play and almost nicked a goal when he closed down Hornicek. Provided a great pass for Eze's goal. 7
Subs:
Kepa Arrizabalaga (on 70, Raya): Made a couple of smart stops. 6
Myles Lewis-Skelly (on 70, Guimaraes): A solid performance from the bench. 7
Christos Tzolis (on 70, Eze): Worked hard after coming on. 6
Merino (on 94, Odegaard)
Gyokeres (on 94, Havertz)
Subs not used: White, Mosquera, Saliba, Hincapie.
