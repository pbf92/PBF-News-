TITLE: New Residential Construction Press Release
OUTLET: Census.gov

Building Permits
Privately-owned housing units authorized by building permits in October were at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,295,000.  This is 7.4 percent below the revised September rate of 1,398,000 and is 8.7 percent below the October 2025 rate of 1,418,000.  Single-family authorizations in October were at a rate of 772,000; this is 8.7 percent below the revised September figure of 846,000.  Authorizations of units in buildings with five units or more were at a rate of 475,000 in October.
Housing Starts
Privately-owned housing starts in October were at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,309,000.  This is 9.9 percent (±16.8 percent)* above the revised September estimate of 1,191,000 and is 2.8 percent (±16.5 percent)* above the October 2025 rate of 1,273,000.  Single-family housing starts in October were at a rate of 864,000; this is 4.0 percent (±9.0 percent)* above the revised September figure of 831,000. The October rate for units in buildings with five units or more was 433,000. 
Housing Completions
Privately-owned housing completions in October were at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,272,000.  This is 6.5 percent (±13.2 percent)* above the revised September estimate of 1,194,000, but is 10.4 percent (±11.6 percent)* below the October 2025 rate of 1,419,000.  Single-family housing completions in October were at a rate of 900,000; this is 6.3 percent (±13.3 percent)* above the revised September rate of 847,000. The October rate for units in buildings with five units or more was 362,000.
