TITLE: Stock Market Today: Nasdaq, S&P 500 Futures Rise as Nvidia Shares Jump After Strong Earnings Report; Anthropic IPO on Deck
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Major stock indexes turned in a mixed performance Wednesday despite a strong earnings report from AI bellwether Nvidia, as investors prepared for Anthropic's highly anticipated IPO.
The Dow Jones Industrial Average shed 1.1%, or about 560 points, adding to the previous session's 850-point drop. The benchmark S&P 500 fell 0.2% today, extending its losing streak to five consecutive sessions, while the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite closed 0.4% higher to snap its four-day skid. U.S. stocks have been under pressure recently amid mounting concerns about the sustainability of the AI-related gains that have unpinned the market's record performance in recent years.
Some of those concerns appeared to ease following a blockbuster earnings report last night from Nvidia (NVDA), the chipmaker at the forefront of the AI boom. Nvidia shares jumped in early trading today but ended the session down nearly 3%. The stock has been hammered lately as investors weigh how the swift downfall of former CEO Jensen Huang could affect the company and the broader AI buildout.
Trading in other chip stocks was also volatile today. Shares of Nvidia rivals Broadcom (AVGO) and Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) jumped a respective 4% and 5%, while Marvell Technology (MRVL) declined 2%, and Intel (INTC) and Micron (MU) both fell 1%. The iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) bounced between gains and losses throughout the session, before ending the day slightly higher.
Cybersecurity stocks were the big gainers on the Nasdaq today amid mounting fears about cyber threats. Shares of Palo Alto Networks (PANW) soared 10%, CrowdStrike (CRWD) climbed 8%, and Fortinet (FTNT) added 6%.
The AI trade could get a boost with the IPO of the company behind the Claude chatbot. Anthropic priced its shares today, with trading set to start tomorrow on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol "ANT". Anthropic will likely have a market value of about $1.5 trillion when it starts trading, making it one of the largest companies in the world by market capitalization.
UnitedHealth (UNH) was the biggest decliner in the Dow today, with shares falling 10% amid rising concerns about the regulatory scrutiny health insurers could face. Shares of Humana (HUM) were down 13%, pacing decliners in the S&P 500. Mining stocks Freeport-McMoRan (FCX) and Newmont (NEM) each fell more than 6% as precious metals prices declined.
Meanwhile, shares of several retailers were on the move as a busy week of earnings reports from the sector continues. Shares of TJX Companies (TJX) jumped 8%, while Target (TGT) gained 4% and Lowe's (LOW) tacked on 1%, after the retailers reported results that topped Wall Street expectations. The big report of the week comes tomorrow, when Walmart (WMT) is scheduled to release its results.
