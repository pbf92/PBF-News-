Pochettino sets the tone
Mauricio Pochettino used his first interview as Manchester United head coach to say, "To create the culture is the most important [thing]," as he outlined priorities for a club he described as needing to be close to winning every single game.
Pochettino, who completed his stint in charge of the United States on Tuesday, met key staff on Wednesday, took training for the first time on Thursday, and held his first wider media conference at United's Carrington training ground on Friday.
Pochettino faces an immediate test at Anfield on Sunday, with United 18th in the Premier League and, by 17:00 GMT on Saturday, potentially bottom.
Pochettino also framed the task as changing the mindset around pressure, saying, "Pressure needs to bring energy. Responsibility needs to bring energy because if not, it is too heavy to put all that pressure and responsibility on your back."
Defence and midfield questions
Pochettino’s first interview highlighted defensive issues, with the Carrick regime having felt Leny Yoro fit their needs despite the 21-year-old being prone to mistakes and also being needed at right-back. Diogo Dalot, who was consoled by Joshua Zirkzee when he was replaced against Villa, has been described as having one of the most committed and professional approaches to the game, while Pochettino arrived in England on Wednesday after overseeing the USA beating Haiti in his final game in charge.
Pochettino’s first challenge will be at Anfield on Sunday, when 18th-placed United take on Liverpool, and it quoted DaMarcus Beasley telling him, "Manchester’s blue and always will be blue." The New York Times said Pochettino told United’s in-house media that "The best squad that we can have are the players that we have," as he aimed to create an environment for players to "translate all their talent on the field."
What comes next at Old Trafford
United’s next phase under Pochettino includes January planning, with Goal reporting that Manchester United officials are ready to strengthen the left-back position, central defense, and attack this winter after acknowledging a lack of squad depth following the summer transfer window. Defensive recruitment will focus on left-footed players, with Lisandro Martinez entering the final year of his contract despite a 12-month extension option, and it also named Fulham defender Bassey, Everton’s Branthwaite, and Antonee Robinson as targets.
Pochettino accepted a packed schedule that starts with a trip to Anfield to face Liverpool in the Premier League on Sunday, with United’s league position leaving them facing a fast turnaround. Pochettino flew from Cincinnati to Manchester overnight after overseeing the USA beating Haiti in his final game in charge, and it added that conversations had been held with various departments within the club as he sought to learn about the squad’s health.
