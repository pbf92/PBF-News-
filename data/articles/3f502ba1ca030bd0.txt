BGSF Received Notice of Noncompliance
On November 17, 2026, BGSF received notice from the NYSE that it was not meeting Section 802.01B, which requires average market capitalization and stockholders' equity of at least $50 million; its figures were approximately $45.0 million and $46.4 million, respectively. BGSF plans to submit a compliance plan within 45 calendar days, seeking to regain compliance within 18 months. Its stock will continue trading on the NYSE during the cure period, subject to NYSE review and acceptance of the plan.
Original SEC Filing: BGSF, INC. [ BGSF ] - 8-K - Nov. 23, 2026
