Criteo jumps on report of takeover interest from AppLovin, others
Criteo ADRs CRTO rose 1.8% on a report that AppLovin APP is among companies in talks to buy the Paris-based adtech firm.
A transaction involving Criteo (CTRO) could come before the end of the year, although no deal is guaranteed and the company could decide to remain independent, according to a Reuters report on Tuesday, which cited people familiar with the matter. Details of the AppLovin talks, including a potential sale price, weren't known.
The news comes after Bloomberg and Reuters reported in July that Vista Equity Partners and Quinti Capital have teamed up on a takeover bid for the French advertising-technology company. Criteo ADRs jumped 21% on July 6 on the report.
Criteo declined to comment to Reuters. AppLovin, Vista Equity and Quinti did not respond to requests for comment.
