TITLE: Who Is Tobi Lütke? Shopify’s BFCM Globe Goes Live Today
OUTLET: The Profile | Polina Pompliano

Updated November 27, 2026 (ET).
Photo: Benjamin Forrest, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0. Tobi Lütke at a Shopify+ event in New York, October 3, 2019. The Commons filename spells his surname as Lütki.
On November 27, 2026, Tobi Lütke, the co-founder and chief executive of Shopify, wrote that the company’s BFCM Live globe was live for Black Friday. “It’s Black Friday (GMT) which means the BFCM globe is live. More data this year and a new game. We put the globe in the middle of a skate park. Play with your friends. It uses WebGPU with HDR, so the arcs really shine,” he said, linking to bfcm.shop. In a follow-up post the same day, he added that the game is multiplayer. The post went up at 12:58 a.m. UTC on November 27, as Friday began in Greenwich Mean Time, which was still Thursday evening in Eastern Time.
Tobi Lütke: Quick facts
- Full name: Tobias Lütke, also known as Tobi Lütke, as used in Shopify’s April 21, 2026, management information circular and on the company’s board of directors page.
- Age: Shopify’s 2026 circular listed him as 45.
- Known for: Co-founding Shopify in September 2004 and serving as its chief executive since April 2008. He was the company’s chief technology officer from 2004 until he became CEO, and he is chair of its board. He also serves on the board of Coinbase Global, Inc.
- Education: In a 2013 essay, he wrote that he left school at 16 and began an apprenticeship as a computer programmer, or Fachinformatiker, at BOG Koblenz, a Siemens subsidiary, under Germany’s dual-education system. He wrote that the resulting vocational qualification is not recognized in North America and that he is “technically a high school dropout.”
- Latest news: On November 27, 2026, he announced that Shopify’s BFCM Live globe was live at bfcm.shop, this year placed in a multiplayer skate-park game.
What is Tobi Lütke known for?
Lütke is originally from Germany and has described Koblenz as his hometown. In a 2017 fireside-chat transcript published by the Ottawa Business Journal, he said he moved to Ottawa in 2002, and that he used his programming background and his knowledge of snowboarding to build an online snowboard store after he found existing e-commerce software “absolutely horrendous.” He said he launched the store, which a later Shopify blog post calls Snowdevil, in November 2004. Shopify’s filings say he co-founded the company in September 2004, served as chief technology officer until April 2008, and has been chief executive and a director since. The filings also say he worked on the core team of the Ruby on Rails framework and created open-source libraries including Active Merchant.
Shopify went public on May 21, 2015, opening for trading on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker SHOP, NYSE Group said. The company announced the closing of the offering on May 27, 2015, and also listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange under the ticker SH. As of April 21, 2026, Shopify’s circular identified Lütke as the only person who, to the company’s knowledge, beneficially owned or exercised control or direction over more than 5% of any class of its shares. The same circular describes a Founder Share designed to give him, his immediate family, and affiliates at least 40% of the company’s voting power, and not more than 49.9%.
What is Shopify’s BFCM Live globe this year?
Black Friday through Cyber Monday is a major stretch of the retail calendar for Shopify’s merchants. In his November 27 post, Lütke said the globe included more data than last year and a new game, that the globe had been placed in the middle of a skate park, and that the presentation uses WebGPU with HDR so that the arcs “really shine.” He invited people to play with their friends at bfcm.shop and, in a follow-up, said the game is multiplayer. Shopify replied, “Meet you at the skate park.” This page does not independently describe the exhibit’s on-screen features beyond what he posted.

Frequently asked questions about Tobi Lütke
Who is Tobi Lütke?
Tobias Lütke is the co-founder, chief executive, and board chair of Shopify, the commerce platform. Shopify’s filings say he co-founded the company in September 2004, was its chief technology officer until April 2008, and has been CEO since. Shopify’s 2026 circular listed him as 45. He also serves on the board of Coinbase.
What is the BFCM Live globe?
It is Shopify’s Black Friday–Cyber Monday live globe of shopping activity, published this year at bfcm.shop. On November 27, 2026, Lütke said the globe was live, that it included more data and a new game, and that the company had put the globe in the middle of a skate park. He said the game is multiplayer and uses WebGPU with HDR.
When did he start Shopify?
Shopify’s official filings date the co-founding to September 2004. Lütke has said he launched his online snowboard store, later referred to as Snowdevil, in November 2004, after building his own software to run it. He became chief executive in April 2008, and the company went public in May 2015.
