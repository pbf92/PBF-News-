TITLE: USMNT advances past Haiti 2-0 in Mauricio Pochettino's farewell
OUTLET: NBC 5 Chicago

Mauricio Pochettino's farewell ends on a high note.
The U.S. men's national team advanced past Haiti 2-0 in the second leg of the CONCACAF Nations League quarterfinals on Tuesday.
After a strong 6-0 first-leg victory on Saturday, the U.S. didn't need to exert too much energy into what was a formality game.
Christian Pulisic started the scoring in the 25th minute. Weston McKennie clipped a ball toward the left side of the box, where Pulisic adjusted his body and scored a stunning overhead kick.
It was Pulisic's third goal of the two-legged tie, as he recorded a two-minute second-half brace in Saturday's rout that included a penalty conversion.
Then a few minutes just before the first half ended, McKennie came into midfield and played a terrific overhead ball toward young striker Julian Hall.

Hall, 18 years old and a New York Red Bulls product, brought down the ball with incoming defenders before clipping it past Haitian goalie Alexandre Pierre.
It marked Hall's fifth goal in six U.S. games. He's scored in each of his four starts, too, as he makes serious noise for more starting minutes despite his age.
The second half didn't feature too many moments, though the U.S. should've had a third goal early on when defender Auston Trusty's effort was cleared off the line.
Upon some replay angles, it appeared Trusty's effort just barely crossed the goal line. But the referee waved on.
It was a routine finish to the match from there, with Pochettino also giving debut senior caps to midfielder Santiago Castaneda and defender Reed Baker-Whiting.
The U.S. will play in the semifinals in March 2027. Its next game will be a friendly vs. Jamaica in December, but it'll feature mostly MLS players due to falling outside a FIFA-recognized international break.
It's unclear when the U.S. will hire Pochettino's replacement, but that process should be underway.
