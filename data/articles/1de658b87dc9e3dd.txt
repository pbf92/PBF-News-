PB Fintech, Turtlemint gain 5% after IRDAI sets up expert committee to review distribution reforms
Shares of insurance companies, along with PB Fintech and Turtlemint, are trading with gains of over 5% on Wednesday, November 25, after the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) constituted a six-member expert committee to examine feedback received on its proposed reforms to insurance distribution and commission structures.
The regulator's consultation paper on the proposed changes received approximately 2.18 lakh responses covering 32 questions. The committee will review the feedback and assess the potential impact of the proposed measures on policyholders, insurers and distribution channels.
The panel will be chaired by Indu Bhishan, founding CEO of Ayushman Bharat. Its other members include Suresh Goyal, Director General of the National Council of Applied Economic Research (NCAER); AV Girija Kumar, former chairman and managing director of Oriental Insurance; Praveen Vashishta, former CEO and chairman of Howden Insurance Brokers India; MK Sharma, former managing director and CEO of SBI Life; and Ayyaj Tamboli, executive director at IRDAI.
What will the IRDAI committee examine?
The committee has been tasked with reviewing the proposed reforms and recommending additional measures or modifications to strike a balance between the interests of policyholders, insurance companies and distributors.
It will also assess the potential impact of the proposed changes on employment across the insurance sector and the earnings of individual agents and other distribution intermediaries. The panel will suggest suitable transition mechanisms, including phased implementation or glide paths, to address concerns raised by smaller insurers and distributors.
Another area of focus will be determining appropriate parameters for fixing commission limits on low-premium insurance policies.
The committee will also examine whether additional measures are needed to improve insurance penetration in smaller towns, rural regions and underserved areas.
Further, it will assess whether large commercial insurance customers could be better served through a gradual transition towards fee-based services.
The expert committee has been asked to submit its recommendations by December 31, 2026.
Morgan Stanley's view on insurance sector
Global brokerage Morgan Stanley has maintained an 'Attractive' view on the Indian insurance industry following the regulatory development.
The brokerage noted that IRDAI had received around 2.2 lakh responses to its consultation paper on distribution reforms, with the newly constituted committee expected to review the feedback and submit its recommendations by December 31, 2026.
Morgan Stanley expects the review to consider the implications for employment, agent income and potential mitigation measures. It also sees the possibility of some relaxation in the proposed norms, including phased implementation timelines for smaller distributors.
The brokerage believes insurers are relatively better positioned than distributors to navigate the proposed changes and remains positive on life insurance companies.
Insurance stocks, along with PB Fintech and Turtlemint, will therefore remain on investors' radar as the regulatory review progresses.
