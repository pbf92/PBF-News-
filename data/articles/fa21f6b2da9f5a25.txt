TITLE: Bradford councillor stabbed door-knocking as man held for attempted murder
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Bradford councillor stabbed door-knocking as man held for attempted murder
Bradford councillor Ibrar Ali was stabbed while out canvassing and has been named in Parliament, as West Yorkshire Police arrest a man on suspicion of attempted murder.
An independent councillor who was stabbed while canvassing in Bradford has been named in Parliament.
Ibrar Ali was attacked at around 11am on Thursday while out meeting constituents, and a man has since been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder.
Local government minister Baroness Taylor of Stevenage named Mr Ali in the House of Lords, saying: "I am afraid I have to mention a very tragic incident that's occurred in Bradford... where an independent councillor who was out canvassing has been stabbed.
"I would want to send the wishes, that I'm sure noble Lords would agree, of the whole House to councillor Ibrar Ali for a speedy recovery, and our thoughts are with all of those in Bradford who are affected by this awful incident."
Officers were called to Manse Street in the city, where the 52 year old Bradford councillor had been assaulted, West Yorkshire Police confirmed.
The councillor was subsequently taken to hospital with serious injuries.
A police spokesman stated: "A man aged 32 has been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder."
Chris Herd, the Lord Mayor of Bradford, said: "Sadly, a Bradford councillor was seriously assaulted this morning. He is currently receiving treatment in hospital and we wish him a full and speedy recovery.
"Our thoughts are with him, his family and his friends at this difficult time.
"It is deeply distressing that this incident has occurred. Councillors work tirelessly on behalf of residents across the district and should be able to carry out their duties safely.
"We will continue to work closely with our colleagues at West Yorkshire Police as they investigate this matter and seek to bring those responsible to justice and would like to thank those who provided aid and support at the time."
Officers continue to maintain a presence in Manse Street, with additional units stationed in the neighbouring Leeds Road and Thornbury Avenue areas, while detectives carry out their investigation.
