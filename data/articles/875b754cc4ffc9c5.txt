TITLE: DoorDash Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $6.9M
OUTLET: TradingView

DoorDash Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $6.9M
DoorDash President and COO Prabir Adarkar, a company officer, sold 16,913 shares on November 20, 2026, in seven transactions at a weighted average price of $157.25 per share, for a total of $2,659,606. After the transactions, he directly owned 904,560 shares of Class A common stock, some represented by restricted stock units.
DoorDash International COO Keith Yandell sold 7,863 shares on November 20, 2026, in seven transactions at a weighted average price of $157.25 per share, for a total of $1,236,478. After the transactions, he directly owned 110,642 shares.
DoorDash Chief Financial Officer Ravi Inukonda sold 19,261 shares directly on November 20, 2026, in seven transactions at a weighted average price of $157.25 per share, for a total of $3,028,840. After the transactions, he directly owned 198,650 shares and held 89,507 shares indirectly through the RK Trust.
