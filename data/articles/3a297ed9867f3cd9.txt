Vivos Therapeutics Received Notice of Delisting
On November 20, 2026, Nasdaq determined to delist Vivos Therapeutics' common stock after its closing bid price was $0.10 or less for 10 consecutive trading days, triggering Nasdaq Listing Rule 5810(c)(3)(A)(iii). Trading was scheduled to be suspended November 27, 2026; the company timely requested a hearing, but the request does not stay the suspension, and the company plans to seek OTC quotation while pursuing compliance.
Original SEC Filing: Vivos Therapeutics, Inc. [ VVOS ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
