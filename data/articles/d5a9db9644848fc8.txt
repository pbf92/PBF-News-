Phillips 66 shares uptick after eight-session losing streak
Phillips 66 PSX shares snapped eight straight sessions of losses, as the stock was up 1.78% at $240.29 on Friday.
The integrated downstream energy company fell about 9.4% in the preceding six sessions. PSX is down 11% over the past one month, while the stock has risen over 88% so far this year, compared to a 5% rise in the broader S&P 500 Index (SP500).
"PSX's stock appears optically cheap on trailing multiples, but the current price ($260) already embeds optimistic assumptions about sustainable refining margins and Midstream growth. The upside depends on PSX sustaining higher normalized refining margins or Midstream outperformance; downside risk emerges if margins revert or infrastructure returns disappoint," pointed out a recent Seeking Alpha analysis.
Looking at Seeking Alpha's Quant Rating, PSX has a Strong Buy rating with a score of 4.97 out of 5. The company received an A in the prospect of profitability, while it got an A+ in the growth factor.
Turning to the Wall Street community, eleven analysts gave PSX a Buy and above rating. Eight analysts have given the stock a Hold recommendation, while one recommended Sell.
