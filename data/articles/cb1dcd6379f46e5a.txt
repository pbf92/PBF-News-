Ondo: Unlocks About 1.94 Billion ONDO - Jan 2027
Ondo is scheduled to unlock about 1.94 billion ONDO in January 2027. The unlock covers tokens allocated to the ecosystem, investors and the team. The amount equals 39.83% of the circulating supply.
Once the unlock takes effect, tokens in those allocations will be available for transfer. The unlock does not sell the tokens. It increases the amount of ONDO that holders can sell.
