TITLE: Nasdaq drops 3%, as Wall Street tumbles on renewed selling pressures
OUTLET: TradingView

Wall Street trades mixed as it pares earlier losses
U.S. equities traded mixed on Wednesday and bounced off their lows, with the Nasdaq having fallen as much as -4% earlier in the day.
The blue-chip Dow DJI was last +0.5%, the benchmark S&P 500 (SP500) was last -0.2%, and the tech-focused Nasdaq Composite COMP was last -0.7%.
From a sector-by-sector basis, seven of the 11 segments were in positive trading territory, with materials leading the way higher, while info tech has been the weakest area.
"We are not out of the woods with the downside risk from the AI trade, as the Jensen Huang stock put is fading. Investors are still nervous and none of the fundamental questions about AI's return on investment from the heavy capex spending have been resolved even with the unusual return of Jensen as CEO of Nvidia. We expect the downside risk in stocks to continue with the S&P 500 dipping close to 6,500 at year-end," Chris Brigati of SWBC stated.
U.S. Q3 GDP growth revised higher in the second estimate. The U.S. jobless claims fell in the past week. The core PCE price index rose 3.3% Y/Y, slightly hotter than expected. The PCE Price Index came in at +0.3% M/M vs. +0.3% consensus and +0.5% prior.
U.S. Treasury yields declined across the curve on Wednesday. The U.S. 2-year Treasury yield US02Y dropped 5 basis points to 4.63%, the U.S. 10-year Treasury yield US10Y shifted down 9 basis points to 4.83%, and the U.S. 30-year Treasury yield US30Y moved down 9 basis points to 5.22%.
As for other stocks that were on the move, shares of HP Inc.
