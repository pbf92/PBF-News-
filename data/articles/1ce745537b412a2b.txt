TITLE: Kinetik jumps on report Targa Resources in talks to purchase
OUTLET: TradingView

Kinetik jumps on report Targa Resources in talks to purchase
Kinetik Holdings KNTK soared 8% in after-hours trading on a report that Targa Resources TRGP is in talks to purchase the pipeline company that's partly owned by Blackstone BX. Targa Resources fell 3%.
A deal could happen as soon as next week, according to a Bloomberg report on Wednesday, which cited people familiar with the matter. No final decision has been made, and Kinetik could remain a standalone, or another bidder could emerge.Kinetik declined to comment to Bloomberg. Blackstone and Targa didn’t immediately respond to requests for comment.
Kinetik has a market value of about $8.9 billion, while Targa is worth $61.5 billion.
Bloomberg reported in September that Kinetik KNTK was in the early stages of exploring options, including a potential sale.
In February, the Financial Times reported that Kinetik KNTK was considering a sale following an approach from Western Midstream Partners WES.
