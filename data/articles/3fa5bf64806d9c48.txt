Abercrombie & Fitch Posts Record Q3 Sales of $1.44 Billion, Raises Outlook
Abercrombie & Fitch reported record third-quarter net sales of $1.44 billion, up 11% year over year, with comparable sales rising 6%. Operating income was $223 million, and diluted earnings per share increased to $3.82 from $2.36. The company raised its fiscal 2026 sales and earnings outlook.
Financial Highlights
- Net sales were $1.435 billion, up 11% year over year; comparable sales increased 6%.
- Operating income was $223 million, compared with $155 million a year earlier. Operating margin was 15.5%, versus 12.0%.
- Net income attributable to Abercrombie & Fitch was $164.4 million, compared with $113.0 million in the prior-year quarter.
- Adjusted non-GAAP diluted EPS on a constant-currency basis was $3.82, compared with $2.43 a year earlier. GAAP diluted EPS was $3.82, versus $2.36.
- Fiscal 2026 outlook calls for net sales growth of around 6% and diluted EPS of $13.70 to $13.90. Fourth-quarter diluted EPS is expected to range from $4.40 to $4.60.
Business Highlights
- Growth spanned all reported regions: Americas sales rose 11%, EMEA sales increased 12%, and APAC sales grew 24%.
- Both brand families achieved their best-ever third-quarter sales: Abercrombie brands grew 12%, while Hollister grew 11%.
- Management cited disciplined inventory management, higher average unit retail, and lower-than-expected promotions during the quarter.
- Fiscal 2026 real estate plans include approximately 50 store openings, 20 closures, and 80 remodels and right-sizes.
