Solv Protocol: Scheduled Unlock Releases 159.32 Million SOLV - Jul 2027
Solv Protocol has scheduled a 159.32 million SOLV unlock for July 2027. The tokens are set to go to ecosystem, investor, and team wallets. The amount equals 2.70% of the circulating supply.
The release lifts the transfer restriction on these allocations. Investors and team members will be able to sell tokens from their unlocked allocations, increasing the amount of insider-held SOLV that can reach the market.
