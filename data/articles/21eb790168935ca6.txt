Life has been difficult in the early going for Mauricio Pochettino at Manchester United, but the Argentine boss will largely have to make do with the squad on hand for the rest of the season.
Pochettino admitted that the Red Devils are indeed stuck amongst the relegation mix as they sit 19th in the Premier League table after a 4-0 drubbing by Liverpool in the Argentine's managerial debut.
Given United's situation, spending in the winter transfer window to bolster the squad and fit Pochettino's style would be a reasonable expectation, but it's not necessarily a given as the club assesses its financial situation.
According to one report, United will not have unlimited resources to boost the team with outside reinforcements as the 2026/27 season reaches the halfway point.

What we know about Man United's winter transfer capabilities
While Mauricio Pochettino may wish to overhaul the Man United squad in January, that may not be remotely possible.
There are needs all over the pitch, but according to a report by Chris Wheeler of the Daily Mail, Man United will have "well under" £50 million ($65.6m) in transfer funds to work with this winter.
Fixing the back line will likely take more than that on its own, with a reported price tag for coveted Everton defender Jarrad Branthwaite more than that on his own.
What are the positions of need at Manchester United?
There are a significant number of positions that need patching up in the Man United squad.
First and foremost, defenders are needed. The club did not replace Luke Shaw in the summer, and now a left-back is high up on the priority list.
Meanwhile, a new striker is needed after Benjamin Sesko picked up a leg injury that will keep him out for significant time, and Matheus Cunha not a natural No. 9. Pochettino is familiar with Monaco striker Folarin Balogun from his time with the U.S. national team, and he could be an option as the French club is wishing to sell after a failed move to Everton in the summer.
Carlos Baleba and Manuel Ugarte are also dealing with injuries, and so another midfielder could be in order if they can find one that fits the budget and would be a genuine improvement.
