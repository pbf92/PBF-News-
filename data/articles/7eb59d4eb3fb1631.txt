Six Flags Director Purchased Shares Worth Over $4.2M
Director Rehan Jaffer reported purchasing 175,000 shares of Six Flags common stock on Nov. 19, 2026, at $11.94 per share; 50,000 shares on Nov. 20 at $11.05 per share; and 150,000 shares on Nov. 23 at $10.96 per share. The purchases, totaling $4,286,000, were made by funds owned and managed by H Partners Management, LLC. As founder and managing member of H Partners Management, Jaffer may be deemed to have voting and dispositive power over the funds’ shares. He disclaimed beneficial ownership except to the extent of his pecuniary interest. Following the transactions, he indirectly beneficially owned 8,100,000 shares.
