PharmaCyte Biotech Sets 49.99% Q/C Ownership Cap and Extends Preferred Stock Maturity
PharmaCyte Biotech amended its securities arrangements with Q/C Technologies, setting a 49.99% beneficial ownership limit on conversions of its Q/C preferred stock and exercises of certain warrants. Separately, PharmaCyte had consented to extend the maturity of Q/C's Series H preferred stock to September 2, 2027. Together, the amendments establish conversion and exercise limits while extending the Series H stock's maturity.
Agreement 1: PharmaCyte Biotech Raises Q/C Ownership Conversion Cap to 49.99%
- Agreement type: Omnibus amendment to preferred stock designations and warrants
- Counterparty: Q/C Technologies
- Signed / Effective: Nov 20 2026 / Nov 20 2026
- Duration / Termination: At will
- Reason: Set the beneficial ownership limit for preferred stock conversions and warrant exercises
Agreement 2: PharmaCyte Biotech Extends Q/C Series H Preferred Stock Maturity to September 2027
- Agreement type: Amendment extending Series H Convertible Preferred Stock maturity
- Counterparty: Q/C Technologies
- Signed / Effective: Nov 15 2026 / Nov 20 2026
- Duration / Termination: Until Sep 02 2027
- Reason: Extend the Series H preferred stock maturity by six months
Original SEC Filing: PharmaCyte Biotech, Inc. [ PMCB ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
