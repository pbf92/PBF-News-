TITLE: GTA 6 First Major Update Officially Released
OUTLET: ScreenRant

A huge number of gamers have spent the weekend diving into the delights of GTA 6 after a 13-year wait, and now Rockstar Games has published the first official post-launch update for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S consoles, bringing the game up to version 01.002.002.
The good news is this update won't take you too long to install. It's a tiny 603.8MB on PlayStation 5, and 1.6GB on Xbox Series consoles. The only major caveat is that it doesn't seem to be available for everyone yet. For whatever reason, Rockstar Games has opted to roll the update out in waves, at least on PlayStation 5. Rockstar dataminer Tex2 reports that the update is live right now for jusy 33% of GTA 6 owners on PS5. The rest of the game's substantial playerbase will receive the update in a few hours, and then everyone else should have it by the end of the day.
Apparently, this is to negate potential server overloads. This makes sense, given more than half of PlayStation 5 players have GTA 6, according to the most recent data. Rockstar Games has yet to release any official patch notes for the update, so I can't actually tell you what it does at this point, but I'll update the article as soon as we know more.
In our 10/10 review of GTA 6, we wrote: "In the end, the world and the characters of Jason and Lucia really help cement GTA 6 as one of the best games in the series, and one of the most interesting games of the year. The entire cast is up to the task (even random NPCs that give you mundane requests like fetching rum from a local liquor store), and the greater Vice City area is one of the most beautifully realistic zones ever put on screen. Even if you don't normally dig GTA, you might want to consider giving GTA 6 a shot. There's a lot here for people who didn't enjoy the games before, and getting around is more convenient than ever."
We certainly haven't been alone in our effusive praise for the new game. Over on Metacritic, GTA 6 is now tied as the highest-rated game of all time with a score of 99, something no game has achieved since 1998's The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
Unfortunately, gamers seem to be slightly less taken with the game. The Metacritic user score for GTA 6 is currently 5.6, largely due to multiple 0 scores from gamers apparently frustrated at the concept of treating your in-game girlfriend Lucia with love and respect. Other 0 scores appear to be from those frustrated the game didn't launch on PC, although according to reports the anticipated PC port is set to arrive sooner rather than later.
