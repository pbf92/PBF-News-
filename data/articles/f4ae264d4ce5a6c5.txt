TITLE: GTA VI Sells 24.58 Million Copies and $2.36 Billion by Launch Day, the Biggest Game Launch Ever
OUTLET: Startup Fortune

Thirteen years after Grand Theft Auto V, Rockstar's next game has arrived and the early numbers are unlike anything the games industry has seen. By launch day it had already sold more copies than its predecessor managed in its first 24 hours, twice over.
- Sensor Tower's tracker puts GTA VI sales, net of returns, far above any game launch on record
- It has sold more than double the copies of GTA V's first 24 hours
- About three quarters of sales are on PlayStation and the rest on Xbox
- These are third-party estimates, not figures from Rockstar or Take-Two
Grand Theft Auto VI went live on Thursday 19 November, after two delays and a 13-year wait since GTA V in 2013. Sensor Tower's sales tracker, in its final pre-order estimate up to the end of 18 November GMT, puts units sold at 24.58 million and revenue at $2.36 billion, both net of returns, across PlayStation and Xbox.
Bigger than anything before it
The record it is chasing belongs to GTA V. According to Guinness World Records, that game sold 11.21 million copies and earned $815.7 million in its first 24 hours, which is still listed as the highest revenue for a video game in a day. On Sensor Tower's numbers, GTA VI has sold more than twice as many copies and brought in nearly three times as much money. Before GTA V, the day-one record belonged to Call of Duty: Black Ops II at $500 million.
It is not only a gaming story. Deadline noted that the estimate is already larger than the whole box office of one of this year's biggest films, The Odyssey, which made $1.805 billion worldwide. Only one film this year has earned more than GTA VI has so far, according to Forbes.
How the numbers climbed
The tracker moved quickly in the final days. Reuters reported about 17.1 million units and an estimated $1.65 billion at noon on Wednesday 18 November. By launch day, Deadline reported that revenue had risen by about $710 million in that stretch, as fans rushed to download the 212GB game. Sensor Tower's Karl Kontus said total pre-order revenue exceeded $2 billion through launch day, which made GTA VI the best-selling game of 2026 on pre-orders alone.
Rockstar's parent company guided to $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion in fiscal 2027 net bookings, and Bank of America says GTA VI must deliver about $1.9 billion of that. The standard edition costs $79.99, and PS5 owners need a firmware update to play. - Take-Two shares jump after GTA VI reviews - GTA VI scores 99 on Metacritic
The split is lopsided. About 75% of sales are on PlayStation, and the rest on Xbox. The game comes in two price tiers, $80 for the standard edition and $100 for the ultimate edition.
A caution on the figures
These are estimates from a market research firm, not sales reported by the publisher. Sensor Tower tracks digital sales on the two consoles, so physical copies are not in the count, and the number of individual buyers is unknown. It also matters that so many copies were pre-ordered: some analysts note that launch day may therefore take more of the total than it did for GTA V, so sales could slow faster from here.
Why it matters
A game that took more than a decade to make has turned into the biggest entertainment launch on record by these estimates. For the industry, it shows what a fully anticipated sequel can do, and how much money still sits in a single, well-built, patient franchise. Rockstar waited 13 years. The market clearly waited with it.
