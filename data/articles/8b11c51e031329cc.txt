TITLE: Matos accepts Swansea fans' anger over shock exit
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
Vitor Matos admits he knows why Swansea City fans will be angry following his sudden departure from the club.
The Portuguese head coach quit the Championship side this week for the top job at French Ligue 1 club RC Strasbourg.
His move took Swansea by complete surprise, just a week before marking his first year in charge with the club sixth in the table and having signed a new contract until 2030 in July.
Speaking at his first press conference at Strasbourg, Matos admitted he does "not expect any goodwill" from the Welsh club's supporters.
He said: "One situation I need to address is Swansea. It was a really hard and tough decision to leave the club mainly because it was a place from the first day, that treated me with all respect and care – me and my family.
"So it was a really tough decision to leave and that's why I needed to thank everyone there.
"Saying that, I really don't expect any good will from the Swansea supporters after my decision. I understand they are angry with that, but it's a club that I love that I worked every day to make better, me and my staff."
Swansea are expecting a compensation package of about £5m and have appointed former players Ashley Williams, Leon Britton, Joe Allen and Kris O'Leary to take temporary charge for Saturday's league fixture against West Bromwich Albion.
"To the staff and to the players I have no doubt they will succeed in the future," said Matos.
"I have no doubt that the manager will go into the office and make the club move forward and fulfil the dreams of the club."
He added: "I'm really proud to be Racing's next manager. This is a wonderful club that deserves all thee respect of everyone."
