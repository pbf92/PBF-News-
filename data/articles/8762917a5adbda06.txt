TAG Immobilien AG (TEG) to Repurchase Up to 150,000 Shares
TAG Immobilien AG will repurchase up to 150,000 shares, approximately 0.08% of share capital, for employee participation and long-term Executive Board compensation. The buyback is capped at €1.9M and runs from 23 November to 31 December 2026; no prior-period comparison was provided.
