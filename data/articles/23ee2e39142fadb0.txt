MFB Bancorp Offers Up to 1,035,000 Shares at $10 Each
MFB Bancorp commenced an offering of up to 1.035M shares at $10.00 each, subject to adjustment up to 1.19025M shares. Completion requires approvals and sale of at least 765,000 shares at the minimum offering range; no prior-period comparison was provided.
