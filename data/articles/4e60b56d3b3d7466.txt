RaveDAO: Unlocks 21.38 Million RAVE - Sep 2027
RaveDAO has scheduled an unlock of 21.38 million RAVE for September 2027. The tokens in the release equal 7.78% of the circulating supply. The unlock covers allocations for the community, the ecosystem, the foundation, investors, and the team.
Recipients gain access to their previously locked tokens when the release takes place. That increases the amount of RAVE they can sell.
