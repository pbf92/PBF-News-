Nasdaq Edges Up in Choppy Open as Wall Street Awaits Nvidia Earnings
The Nasdaq Composite opened slightly higher on Tuesday while the broader market slipped, with investors positioning ahead of Nvidia's earnings report for signals on where the artificial-intelligence trade goes next.
The tech-heavy index gained 18.3 points, or 0.07%, to 24528.057 at the opening bell. The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 221.2 points, or 0.43%, to 51386.98, and the S&P 500 shed 5.6 points, or 0.08%, to 7411.02. Trading was volatile from the start.
Nvidia's results are the main event for a market that has leaned heavily on AI-linked names. The company's shares had already come under pressure a day earlier, dropping more than 2% to just under $160, with its market value falling below $4 trillion from $4.5 trillion at the start of last week.
That slide followed a spying scandal that led to the departure of the chipmaker's chief executive, a development that has added uncertainty to the AI narrative just as quarterly results arrive.
