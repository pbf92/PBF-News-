TITLE: Fernando Mendoza gets cheeky GTA 6 nod from protagonist Lucia
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Grand Theft Auto 6 has finally arrived.
Fans have been waiting years for the latest installment of the legendary GTA franchise, which finally released with GTA 6 on Thursday.
Grand Theft Auto 6's primary setting is Vice City, which is Rockstar Games' modern-day take on Miami.
But it was Las Vegas Raiders starting quarterback Fernando Mendoza who is getting love from the video game.
Many who started playing GTA 6 on Thursday noticed an early scene in the game where the two protagonists, Jason and Lucia, were sitting on the couch eating ice cream together.
Lucia was wearing a black and silver football jersey with No. 15 on the front.
Rockstar largely avoids real-world brand licenses in Grand Theft Auto games so that they don't have to pay for the rights. But almost everything in the game is a parody of a real-life brand.
This would seem to be the case with this jersey, as it doesn't officially have a Raiders logo. But the black and silver, combined with Mendoza's No. 15, makes it clear who this jersey is supposed to represent.
Not to mention that Mendoza is from Miami, Florida, which makes this in-game connection make perfect sense.
