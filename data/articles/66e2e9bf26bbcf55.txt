TITLE: Snapchat Launches a Two-Tier Program for Creator Agencies
OUTLET: https://www.thekeyword.co/

Snapchat has launched a Creator Agency Partner Program for agencies that run creator campaigns on its platform. The program gives them training, support and access to incentives for Creator Ads, Snap's ads built around creator content.
It is live in the US, the UK and the Middle East and North Africa (MENA), with more regions to follow. Agencies can apply to join now. Snap announced the program on 18 November in a Snapchat for Business post, alongside the open beta of its Snap Creator Network and the global rollout of Visited By on Snap Map.
Snap says the goal is for agencies to become experts in its creator ecosystem and help clients build effective creator campaigns.
Two Tiers, Split by Experience
The Standard tier is for agencies building their Snapchat creator practice. It comes with tools, training and support to start driving measurable results for clients.
The Strategic tier is for agencies already running Creator Ads at a higher level. Snap judges that level on three measures: spend, advertiser scale and team certification. Strategic partners get deeper support, stronger creator-facing opportunities and a closer partnership with Snap.
Snap has not published a threshold for any of the three measures, so an agency cannot tell from the announcement which tier it would qualify for. The post also names "incentive access" without saying what the incentives are. It sets no application deadline and does not say how many agencies Snap will admit to either tier.
What the Sign-Up Page Adds
The program's sign-up page fills in some of that. It lists structured training, certifications, playbooks and early access to product updates. It also lists partner badges, co-marketing, case studies, exclusive events, media coupons and a direct line to Snapchat's product teams.
The application form asks each agency for its accounts active on Snapchat, the services it offers and the industries it serves. It also asks for estimated monthly client media budgets and a quarterly Snap budget, which suggests spend will weigh on placement.
Built on Snap's Existing Agency Program
The creator program follows a model Snap already uses. Its Agency Partner Program has two tiers of its own, an Agency Partner badge and a Strategic Agency Partner badge. That program launched in the US and the UK, with Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates (UAE) to follow. The creator program makes MENA a launch market from the start.
Agencies that meet that program's requirements are listed in the Snapchat Partner Directory, where businesses look for Snap Ads experts. They also get a dedicated partner representative, training through an Agency Partner Hub, and special offers including client coupons and early tests of new ad products.
The new program applies that structure to creator work, as platforms compete for the agencies that place creator budgets. TikTok pitches Creator AI Search in TikTok One to brands and agencies, and YouTube has moved its Creator Partnerships platform into Google Ads and Display and Video 360 (DV360).
Snap now pairs the agency program with its own matching tool. An agency that joins gets training on Creator Ads while the Snap Creator Network handles discovery inside Snapchat.
