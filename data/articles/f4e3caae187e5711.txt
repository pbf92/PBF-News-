TITLE: Manchester United hire coach who helped Arsenal become powerhouse
OUTLET: talkSPORT

Former Arsenal fitness lead Sam Wilson has reportedly joined Manchester United's coaching staff.
Wilson left the Gunners after 12 years in the summer, with his final season ending in Premier League glory.
At the time of his departure, the BBC reported that the move was the coach’s own decision.
The fitness coach had the option to remain with Arsenal, but he chose to leave for a more senior role in Europe.
That role turned out to be the Head of Performance position at Serie A side Parma, but it didn’t last very long.
Wilson left the Italian side in October, but he has now made a return to English football with Manchester United.
United hire Premier League-winning coach
While Mauricio Pochettino has been the talk of the town following his appointment at Old Trafford, Wilson is said to have been hired before the Argentine took the reigns last week.
According to The Sun, the former Arsenal man has joined United as a fitness coach for the first team.
Wilson was on the bench at Anfield for Sunday’s 4-0 defeat to Liverpool, which was Pochettino’s first game in charge.
His arrival isn't expected to affect United's pursuit of former US national team fitness coach Silvia Tuya, amid reports she is set to join Pochettino's coaching team.
Tuya arrived in Manchester on the same jet as the Argentine, but was not named in his official staff on Wednesday and United sources have said a decision still needed to be made about whether she joins.
After Pochettino's first game ended in a crushing defeat, he will be looking to Wilson to oversee an improvement in off the ball statistics.
United's Premier League running stats came under scrutiny in the start to this season before Michael Carrick's season.
They rank second from the bottom for total distance covered in the top-flight, with an average of 111.5 km per match.
Improving this will no doubt be a priority under Pochettino, who is also set to crack down on the players' fitness levels by introducing frequent body-fat tests.
Wilson certainly has the experience to oversee this, having been a popular figure at Arsenal after joining the club in 2014.
After starting out as a strength and conditioning coach for the Under-18s, he worked his up to earn his break with the first-team as part of Freddie Ljungberg’s interim coaching staff in November 2019.
Mikel Arteta then kept Wilson as part of his backroom team and appointed him Arsenal's lead physical performance coach in 2022.
This proved a popular appointment, with Wilson's time in the role coinciding with the Gunners' rise up the table.
After three consecutive second-place finishes between 2022 and 2025, his final year at the Emirates Stadium saw the club finally end their 22-year wait for Premier League glory.
Wilson then opted for a fresh start after this, as he opted to leave Arsenal in the summer.
He joined Arteta’s former assistant, Carlos Cuesta, at Parma, following his appointment in June 2025, where he was handed the role of their head of performance.
Cuesta was sacked in September, however, and Wilson left the Italian side the following month.
