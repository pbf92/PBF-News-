TITLE: Contemporary management of Crohn’s disease: optimizing outcomes
OUTLET: Nature

Strategy studies published in 2026 demonstrated that, with already approved medications or established surgical approaches, better outcomes for patients with Crohn’s disease can be achieved.
Key advances
- The PROFILE trial’s 5-year follow-up supports early top-down anti-TNF combination therapy in newly diagnosed Crohn’s disease, with fewer abdominal surgeries, hospitalizations and B2–B3 complications than a step-up strategy3.
- The 10-year follow-up in the LIR!C trial demonstrates significantly higher therapy-free remission after ileocaecal resection than after infliximab in selected patients with limited ileal Crohn’s disease, supporting shared medical–surgical decision-making5.
- In the REScUE study7, 4-weekly ustekinumab was not superior to 8-weekly dosing after intravenous reinduction; objective reassessment within 24 weeks can help to identify patients who should switch treatment rather than continue ineffective intensification.
