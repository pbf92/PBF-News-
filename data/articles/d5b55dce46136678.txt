What factors are used to calculate daily and weekly USD/MAD price predictions?
Daily and weekly USD/MAD price predictions use different historical data windows depending on the forecast horizon. Daily forecasts analyze USD/MAD price data from the previous 7 days, while weekly forecasts use data from the previous 4 weeks. The models combine this data with technical indicators, statistical forecasting models, historical price patterns, and trend analysis.
How often is the USD/MAD forecast updated?
Daily USD/MAD price predictions are recalculated approximately every hour using the latest available market data. Weekly USD/MAD forecasts are reviewed and recalculated daily or sooner if significant macroeconomic or market developments affect the underlying models.
Can the USD/MAD forecast guarantee future prices?
No. The USD/MAD forecast represents the most probable price scenarios based on historical data and current market information. Actual exchange rates may differ due to unexpected macroeconomic developments, central bank decisions, geopolitical events, or changes in market sentiment.
