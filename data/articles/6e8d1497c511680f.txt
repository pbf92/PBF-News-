TITLE: Pope Francis wrote of seeing the Devil 3 times in private diary
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

Pope Francis claimed to have had visions of the Devil three times in his life, according to extracts from his private diary published on Tuesday.
The Private Diary of Pope Francis covers the pope’s inner thoughts during his from 1992 to 2024.
The pope wrote of his 2018 encounter of the devil during a trip to Chile amid the clerical abuse scandal.
“Just as I can’t forget his eyes from the previous two, in this case, I can’t forget the powerful raspiness of his voice,” he wrote. “The Lord granted me an opportunity to leave that meeting, and he thus freed me.”
Pope Francis also wrote about a “spiritual crisis” he suffered during two years of his pontificate, in 2021 and 2022.
“That crisis prepared him to find true inner peace, to fulfil the highest purpose of his life," said longtime friend Cardinal Victor Manuel Fernández, who edited the diary.
He said the pope had showed him the diary and three other documents while he was alive. “I remember him saying: ‘Whatever may serve for edification’” Fernández recalled.
“In other words, he was asking me, when the time came, to see what in them might help edify other people.”
Francis led the Catholic Church for twelve years until his death in 2025, aged 88.
