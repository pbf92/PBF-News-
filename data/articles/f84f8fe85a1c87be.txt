Pulmatrix Received Notice of Noncompliance
On November 20, 2026, Pulmatrix received notice from Nasdaq that it no longer meets the $2.5 million stockholders’ equity requirement under Listing Rule 5550(b)(1) and does not meet the alternative standards. It has until January 4, 2027, to submit a compliance plan; if accepted, Nasdaq may grant up to 180 days from the notice date to regain compliance. Its shares will continue trading on Nasdaq for now.
Original SEC Filing: Pulmatrix, Inc. [ PULM ] - 8-K - Nov. 24, 2026
