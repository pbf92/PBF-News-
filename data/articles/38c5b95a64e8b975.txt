TITLE: US Stock Futures Rebound After AI Sell-Off as Nvidia Investigation and Fed Outlook Remain in Focus: Dow Jones, S&P, Nasdaq, Wall Street
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

US stock futures rallied sharply on Monday as investors attempted to recover from last week's technology-led sell-off, while concerns surrounding Nvidia (NASDAQ:NVDA), tighter semiconductor export restrictions and the Federal Reserve's interest rate outlook continued to influence market sentiment.
The recovery comes ahead of a holiday-shortened trading week, with investors assessing the implications of the recent correction in artificial intelligence-related stocks and renewed scrutiny of Nvidia's business dealings with China.
US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has called for answers regarding Nvidia's semiconductor sales to China, while market strategist Ed Yardeni has lowered his year-end forecast for the S&P 500 for the second time in approximately two months.
Corporate earnings, including results from Hewlett Packard Enterprise, and developments in Middle Eastern energy markets are also expected to attract investor attention.
Nasdaq Futures Jump 2.9% Following Technology Sell-Off
US equity futures advanced strongly during early Monday trading, suggesting that Wall Street could recover some of the losses recorded during the previous week.
At 01:20 Eastern Time, or 06:20 GMT, Nasdaq 100 futures had climbed 740 points, equivalent to 2.9%.
S&P 500 futures increased by 143 points, or 2.1%, while Dow Jones futures gained 680 points, representing a rise of 1.4%.
The rebound followed a particularly difficult session on Friday, when the Nasdaq Composite plunged more than 5%, completing its worst week since the COVID-19 market turmoil of 2020.
The decline reflected mounting concerns about the valuations of artificial intelligence companies and the potential returns from substantial investments in AI infrastructure.
Investors have become increasingly cautious about the scale of spending required to develop artificial intelligence systems, particularly where expansion has been supported by borrowing.
Questions surrounding the profitability of these investments have contributed to a reassessment of technology stocks that previously benefited from strong enthusiasm for AI.
Nvidia Leadership Crisis Adds to Semiconductor Sector Uncertainty
Nvidia has become a central focus of the recent market volatility following the unexpected departure of former Chief Executive Jensen Huang.
The company's latest earnings results were not sufficient to restore confidence, with investors increasingly concerned about leadership uncertainty and the potential consequences of tighter restrictions on advanced semiconductor exports.
