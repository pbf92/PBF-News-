Falcon Finance: Supply unlock releases about 203 million FF - May 2027
Falcon Finance has scheduled a supply unlock of about 203.06 million FF for May 2027. The amount is equal to 5.73% of the circulating supply. The unlock covers allocations for the ecosystem, the foundation, investors, and the team.
Once the tokens are unlocked, holders can sell them on the market. Sales from these allocations could add to the selling pressure on FF.
