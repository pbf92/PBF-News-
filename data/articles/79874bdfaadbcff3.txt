Midland States Bancorp to Sell Six Branches and Combine Two Others
Less than 1 min read
Midland States Bancorp announced plans to sell six branches and combine two others as part of a strategic network optimization.
Key Highlights:
- Three buyers agreed to acquire six branches, including approximately $156.3 million in deposits and $1.5 million in loans as of September 30, 2026.
- The sales are expected to close by the end of the first quarter of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions.
- Two additional branches are planned to combine with nearby locations during the first quarter of 2027.
- Midland expects a one-time pre-tax gain from the sales, partially offset by combination costs; it expects to operate 45 branches afterward.
Original SEC Filing: Midland States Bancorp, Inc. [ MSBI ] - 8-K - Nov. 19, 2026
