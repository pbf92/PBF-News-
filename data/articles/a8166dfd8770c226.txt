TITLE: Bombay HC Denies Blink Digital’s Plea To Restore Meta Ad Accounts
OUTLET: BW Marketing World

The Bombay High Court has declined to grant immediate restoration of four business manager accounts and associated advertising assets belonging to Blink Digital India , which were disabled by Meta in August 2026. However, the court has allowed the agency to approach Meta again for reinstatement, subject to assurances of compliance with the platform’s policies.
Meta has been directed to consider Blink Digital’s fresh request and communicate its decision within two weeks of receiving it.
In an order pronounced on 20 November, Justice Sandeep V Marne held that Blink Digital had not established grounds for an interim mandatory injunction directing restoration of its accounts. The court noted prima facie material indicating policy-violating advertisements and business practices, which Meta was entitled to take into consideration while initiating enforcement action.
Court Directs Meta To Preserve Account Data
The court also directed Meta to preserve all data, records, content, logs and communications associated with Blink Digital’s Business Manager accounts, advertising accounts, Facebook page and Instagram account while the agency’s reinstatement request remains pending.
The data preservation direction will cease to operate if Blink Digital fails to submit its request within four weeks or if Meta rejects the reinstatement plea.
The order was passed in Interim Application (L) No. 31469 of 2026, filed by Blink Digital in its commercial suit against Facebook India Online Services and Meta Platforms Inc.
Blink Digital was represented by senior advocate Kunal Dwarkadas, along with counsel from Tuli & Co. Meta Platforms Inc was represented by senior advocate Janak Dwarkadas and a team from Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas, while Facebook India was represented by advocate Varun Pathak, also from Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas.
The ruling leaves Blink Digital without immediate access to its disabled advertising assets but provides a fresh avenue for reinstatement, subject to Meta’s review and the agency’s compliance assurances.
