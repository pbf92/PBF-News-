The man who left a woman feeling 'ashamed and damaged'
'The whole thing made me feel like I was nothing'
Police praised a woman's 'immense courage' as the man who sexually abused her as a child was jailed. Ian Garner 'deliberately exploited' a vulnerable youngster and 'manipulated her trust', Greater Manchester Police said the 57-year-old started a prison sentence.
Garner, of Caittlin Way, Oldham, was found guilty of 13 charges following a trial, including sexual assault; rape; and causing a child to engage in sexual activity.
He was jailed for 15 years at Manchester Crown Court earlier this week. In a powerful victim impact statement submitted to the court, the woman told of the lasting effect the abuse has had on her life.

"The whole thing made me feel like I was nothing, my romantic relationships have suffered," she said. "If my current partner comes to hug me it freaks me out and I often don't want him near me.
"If someone touches me without me knowing it scares me. I find it hard to trust men in general. I struggle to sleep and I often wake up panicking and fighting out.
"I feel like I have lost so much of my life, and I feel ashamed and that I am damaged. I had to grow up a lot earlier. He manipulated me. I didn't feel like I was good enough for anyone and I still don't."

Detective Sergeant Kate Dawson, who led the investigation, said: "This sentence is a result of the survivor's immense courage in coming forward and speaking about the abuse she suffered as a child.
"Garner deliberately exploited a vulnerable child, manipulating her trust and abusing his position over a number of years. The impact of his offending has been profound and long-lasting, affecting the survivor well into adulthood.
"I hope this outcome gives her some measure of justice and helps her as she continues to move forward with her life. I would also like to thank her for the bravery she has shown throughout our investigation and the court process.
"This case demonstrates that no matter how much time has passed, we will listen to victims, thoroughly investigate allegations of child sexual abuse, and do everything we can to bring offenders before the courts."
