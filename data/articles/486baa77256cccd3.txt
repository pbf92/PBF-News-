TITLE: New boss Pochettino arrives at Man Utd training ground
OUTLET: Sport Nation

Mauricio Pochettino is "not wasting time" after he began work as Manchester United head coach on Wednesday following an overnight flight from the United States.
The Argentinian officially took charge after completing his final duties as United States coach in Cincinnati the day before.
Pochettino, who is understood to have signed a deal until the summer of 2029, flew straight to Manchester and was whisked to the club's training ground at Carrington to meet United staff and start preparations for Sunday's Premier League match against Liverpool.
"I have plenty of energy," Pochettino told United's website after his whirlwind few days. "It's a privilege to be head coach of Manchester United. And we are not wasting time. We need to be ready to compete on Sunday.
"Now it's not time to think about rest, it's not time to think about sleep. It's about being ready and to prepare everything to be sure that we are going to compete in the best condition at Anfield."
His appointment was announced last Friday, just over 24 hours after United had confirmed the sacking of Michael Carrick following the Red Devils' worst start to a campaign since 1986.
Mauricio Pochettino after leading the US to a 2-0 win over Haiti in his final match in charge | Photo: AP 
On Tuesday night, Pochettino asked fans to "give him time" to make United the city's dominant club again after his last match in charge of the United States.
Told by former Manchester City loanee DaMarcus Beasley "Manchester is blue, it will always be blue" in the TNT Sports US studio after the second leg of the Nations League tie against Haiti, Pochettino responded: "You will see. Give me time.
"The new challenge is a big challenge, but at the same time it's adrenalin you want to feel.
"We need to add our common sense … we know the situation is not a situation that deserves to be a club like Manchester United, the fan base is amazing."
Pochettino then gave a very short interview to United club staff after arriving at Carrington.
"Very excited to be here," he said.
"It's an honour. It's a privilege to be in this amazing club. Yes, looking forward to start work."
