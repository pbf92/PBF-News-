Micron Stocks Jump 6.63% as 75% of 2027 Capacity Sells Out
Micron Technology MU shares jumped 6.63% to $752.01 at 9:45 a.m. ET on November 19 in regular trading, as investors reacted to management's November 18 statement that 75% of 2027 production capacity is sold out. Anthropic's final prospectus also confirmed that Micron will buy 12,048,192 Class N shares for about $1.0 billion at the $83 IPO price.
Investing.com reported that Micron has secured $32 billion in multiyear supply agreements and that the shares had fallen roughly 40% from their June peak before this move. The Anthropic stake is modest next to Micron's $851 billion market value, but it places the memory maker inside a customer base that has committed about $900.9 billion to compute and leases.
Selling out three-quarters of 2027 capacity a year early shifts the debate from volume to price, and contract terms will decide margins. GuruFocus gives Micron a GF Score of 96/100, pairing high profitability and financial strength with middling momentum after the slide. Shares trade about 12% below the GF Value of $852.43, a modestly undervalued reading that fits a cyclical name recovering from a drawdown. The next test is Micron's next quarterly report and any detail on 2027 pricing.
