TITLE: Steam's 2026 Black Friday Sale Brings First-Time Discounts to New PC Games
OUTLET: VGC / IGN

Steam is getting in on the Black Friday deals, offering thousands of discounted PC games, more than a few of which just released this year. Some, like The Blood of Dawnwalker and Gears of War: E-Day, are seeing their first price cuts ever on Valve's digital games platform. Feel free to peruse the full Steam sale yourself to see what sticks out.
In addition to the games above this sale also covers two great 2026 Star Wars games: Star Wars: Zero Company and Star Wars: Galactic Racer. It also includes Silent Hill: Townfall and Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival, two terrifying and fun first-person horror games. Of those two, Townfall might be the best, at least if you agree with Tristan Ogilvie, who's IGN review gave it an eight and called it "a welcome shot in the arm" for the Silent Hill series.
For something older, I'd recommend Avowed if you enjoy open-world first-person RPGs, as well as The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered, a beautiful update of the 2015 classic. For more of a retro feel, Shinobi: Art of Vengeance is a great reinvention of the series and is also on sale.
