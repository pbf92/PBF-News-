TITLE: New GTA 6 Screenshots Showcase Intense Weather Systems
OUTLET: Insider Gaming

In a series of new GTA 6 screenshots shared by an unlikely source – The Weather Channel – we get a look at six weather systems in the upcoming open-world title. We’re mere hours (technically) away from the release of GTA 6, which drops November 19 worldwide, ending years of painstaking waiting and hoping.
The images The Weather Channel shared on social media were mocked up as though the profile was literally giving readers a breakdown of the weather elements in Leonida, the in-game world of GTA 6. You can check them out below.
GTA 6’s Weather Looks Gnarly
In a string of posts on social media, The Weather Channel published six never-before-seen screenshots of Grand Theft Auto 6, focusing on the game’s weather mechanics.
We saw heavy rain (borderline torrential) in an urban setting, creating incredible reflections on the asphalt, combined with the awesome impact of the droplets. There was a landscape shot from ‘Pelican Valley’, showing a storm approaching over the swamp. The Vice City skyline appeared in another shot, drenched in sea fog, ‘coming in off the ocean’.
The fourth image was of a storm by a bar called the Rusty Anchor, lighting up the volumetric cloud with insane depth and granularity. The fifth shot is somewhat reminiscent of a Red Dead landscape, showing how humidity in Gambit Bay created a low-lying sheet of mist.
Finally, a windy, rainy setting over Key Lento, drenching roads and cars in a shot that could have been ripped straight from reality.
