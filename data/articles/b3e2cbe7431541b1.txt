TITLE: What is Claude? Everything you need to know about Anthropic's AI powerhouse
OUTLET: Tom's Guide

Claude, the AI chatbot developed by Anthropic, has grown from a relatively unknown ChatGPT rival into one of the biggest names in artificial intelligence. Known for its focus on safety, strong writing capabilities and impressive coding skills, Claude has become a serious competitor to ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok.
Anthropic is also making headlines beyond its chatbot. The company's highly anticipated initial public offering (IPO) on November 19, 2026, marks a major milestone for the AI industry and puts a new spotlight on Claude, the technology at the center of its business. With investors betting heavily on the future of artificial intelligence, Anthropic's stock market debut raises new questions about how the company will compete with OpenAI and Google.
Meanwhile, Claude has evolved into much more than a chatbot. Anthropic has expanded its AI assistant with tools that can write and debug code, analyze documents, conduct research and interact with apps and computers on your behalf. Its Claude Code and Cowork tools are part of a broader push to make Claude useful for everything from everyday tasks to complex professional workflows.
Anthropic's latest models, including Claude Opus 5.5, Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, are also pushing the boundaries of what AI can accomplish, particularly in coding, reasoning and research.
But what exactly is Claude? How does it compare to ChatGPT, what makes it different, and is it worth using? Here's everything you need to know about Anthropic's AI assistant as the company enters a new chapter.
What is Claude?
Claude is a conversational AI model (yet, less chatty than ChatGPT) built by Anthropic, a company founded by former OpenAI researchers with a strong focus on AI alignment and safety.
Named after Claude Shannon (aka the father of information theory), this chatbot is designed to be:

How does Claude work?
At its core, Claude is a large language model (LLM) trained on massive datasets. But what sets it apart is the "Constitutional AI" system — a novel approach that guides Claude's behavior based on a written set of ethical principles, rather than human thumbs-up/down during fine-tuning.
Claude runs on the latest version of Anthropic's model family (currently Claude 4.5 Sonnet), and it's packed with standout features:
- Constitutional AI framework. Instead of relying on hidden human feedback, Claude evaluates its own responses against a "constitution" — a list of rules like Don't generate harmful advice or Explain reasoning clearly. Almost as built-in AI values.
- Massive context window. Claude can handle up to 200,000 tokens of input — roughly 150,000 words — meaning it can analyze full books, codebases, or long email threads without losing context.
- Extended Thinking Mode. Claude 3.7 Sonnet introduces a hybrid reasoning model that allows users to choose between rapid responses and more thoughtful, step-by-step reasoning. This model integrates both capabilities into a single framework, eliminating the need for multiple models. Users can control how long the model "thinks" about a question, balancing speed and accuracy based on their needs.
- Artifact creation. Claude can create standalone artifacts for code, documents, diagrams and other structured content during conversations. This makes it easier to collaborate on creative writing, programming projects or technical documentation without cluttering the chat interface. These Artifacts can be published and shared with other users or kept private.
Claude Code
Claude Code is Anthropic's AI-powered coding assistant designed to help developers and everyday users build software, fix bugs and automate technical tasks. Unlike the standard Claude chatbot, which primarily responds to prompts, Claude Code can work directly with files, run commands and make changes to an entire project.
First introduced in February 2025, Claude Code has become one of Anthropic's most important products and a major competitor to OpenAI's Codex and other AI coding tools.
You don't have to be an experienced programmer to use it, either. People can describe what they want to build in plain English, and Claude Code can help turn those instructions into working software. For example, you could ask it to create a website, build a simple app, organize a database or troubleshoot a program that isn't working.
How it works: Claude Code can access project files, understand how different parts of a program fit together and carry out multi-step coding tasks. It works through the terminal, desktop app, supported coding environments and the web, with cloud sessions that can continue running even after you close your computer.
Claude Code can:
- Write and edit code: Build websites, apps and software features from natural-language instructions.
- Find and fix bugs: Identify errors, suggest solutions and test changes.
- Work across entire projects: Read multiple files, understand existing code and make coordinated updates.
- Automate repetitive tasks: Handle testing, code reviews, documentation and other development work.
- Use your browser and computer: Interact with supported apps and websites to test software and complete tasks, with appropriate permissions.
- Work more independently: Break larger assignments into smaller tasks and use specialized AI agents to tackle different parts of a project.
Claude Code is available through eligible paid Claude subscriptions, including Pro and Max, with additional options for teams and developers using Anthropic's API. Usage limits and costs depend on the plan and how extensively you use it.
Although Claude Code can complete substantial portions of a development project, its output still needs human review, particularly when modifying important files, handling sensitive data or deploying software.
Claude Cowork
Claude Cowork takes Claude beyond simple chatbot conversations, turning it into an AI assistant that can complete tasks on your behalf. Built on the same technology as Claude Code, Cowork can organize files, create documents, analyze information, manage projects and work across connected apps with less hands-on guidance.
Originally launched as a research preview for Claude Max subscribers on macOS in January 2026, Cowork is now available across paid Claude plans, including Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise. It works on Mac, Windows and Linux (beta), as well as through the web and mobile apps.
How it works: Instead of simply answering questions, Cowork can plan and carry out multi-step tasks. You can give Claude access to specific files and folders, connect services such as Gmail, Google Drive and Slack, or let it interact with your computer using its computer-use capabilities.
Cowork can:
- Read, create, edit, rename, and organize files
- Turn screenshots into spreadsheets
- Compile notes into reports
- Reorganize downloads or documents automatically
Claude plans tasks, executes them step-by-step, and keeps you informed as it works—similar to how Claude Code operates, but designed for non-technical workflows.
What's different about Claude Cowork is that it gives users more agency: Claude can plan and complete multi-step tasks independently, without repeated context-giving or format conversion. Users can also queue tasks and give feedback while Claude keeps working.
Power features:
- Works with existing connectors to pull in external information
- New built-in skills for documents, presentations, and files
- Can pair with Claude in Chrome to handle browser-based tasks
Context window: Up to 1 million tokens
One of Claude’s standout features is its massive context window. Most users get around 200,000 tokens by default — that's equivalent to about 500 pages of text — but in certain enterprise or specialized use cases, Claude can handle up to 1 million tokens. This is especially useful for summarizing research papers, analyzing long transcripts or comparing entire books.
Now that Claude includes vision capabilities, this huge context window becomes even more powerful. Claude can analyze images, graphs, screenshots and charts, making it an excellent assistant for tasks like data visualization, UI/UX feedback, and even document layout review.
Which Claude model is right for you?
Anthropic’s Claude family has become one of the most talked-about alternatives to ChatGPT and Gemini.
Whether you're looking for a fast, lightweight assistant or a model that can deeply analyze documents, code or images, there's a Claude model that fits the bill.
Here’s a breakdown of the Claude 3 series, including the latest Claude 3.7 Sonnet, to help you decide which one best suits your needs.
Claude Sonnet 4.6
Claude Sonnet 4.6 sits in the middle of Anthropic’s model lineup, designed to deliver strong reasoning, emotional intelligence and reliability at speeds and costs suitable for everyday use.
While Opus focuses on frontier-level complexity, Sonnet 4.6 is optimized for real-world decision support, communication and structured problem solving — the kinds of tasks people actually face daily.
It combines thoughtful reasoning with consistent tone control and practical judgment, making it especially effective for collaborative work, planning and situations that require nuance rather than raw computational depth.
What Claude Sonnet 4.6 excels at:
- Practical reasoning & decision support. Strong at evaluating tradeoffs, constraints and real-world feasibility.
- Emotional intelligence & tone adaptation. Produces responses that feel human, tactful and socially aware.
- Structured problem solving. Breaks down complex situations into clear, actionable steps.
- Reliability & safety alignment. Emphasizes careful, non-harmful outputs and consistent behavior.
- Long-form writing & communication. Particularly strong at clear messaging, summaries and persuasive writing.
- Collaborative workflows. Functions well as a thinking partner for planning, strategy and everyday decisions.
- Efficiency & responsiveness. Faster and more cost-efficient than frontier models while maintaining strong reasoning quality.
Claude Opus 4.6
Claude Opus 4.6 is Anthropic’s flagship frontier model, designed for complex reasoning, long-context analysis and agent-level workflows. Positioned as the company’s most intelligent system, it emphasizes careful planning, sustained problem solving and reliability in real-world tasks rather than quick conversational responses.
With major upgrades in adaptive reasoning, large-scale context handling and autonomous workflow coordination, Opus 4.6 is built to function less like a chatbot and more like a high-level cognitive engine for professional, technical and enterprise use.
What Claude Opus 4.6 excels at:
- Deep reasoning & planning. Designed for complex, multi-step problem solving and long-horizon thinking.
- Agentic workflows & automation. Supports coordinated “agent teams” and multi-step task execution.
- Massive context understanding. Up to ~1M-token context enables analysis of entire codebases, contracts or research corpora.
- Adaptive thinking & effort control. Dynamically decides how much reasoning a task requires.
- Coding & technical workflows. Industry-leading performance in software engineering and complex technical tasks.
- Long-workflow coherence & self-correction. Improved planning persistence and error detection during extended tasks.
