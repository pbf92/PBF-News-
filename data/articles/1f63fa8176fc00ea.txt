TITLE: Rockstar Games Announces 'Grand Theft Auto VI' Updates, Including a Fix for Weather Issue
OUTLET: Complex

As Grand Theft Auto VI’s record-setting ubiquitousness continues, Rockstar Games is letting fans know that a number of updates have been pushed out for PlayStation and Xbox players.
Monday (Nov. 23), Rockstar published a list of launch updates, including a fix for an issue that caused weather to not change for “extended periods.” The weather issue is one that’s received considerable attention on social media, specifically with regards to rain, but players have since reported that the issue does indeed appear to have been fixed.
Other problems given attention in this wave of updates include issues that caused the game to crash, autosaving hiccups, phone calls triggering incorrectly, and more. See the full list here.
These updates arrive the same day as the surprise-dropped deluxe edition of GTA VI: The Album, featuring Travis Scott and Slayyyter.
