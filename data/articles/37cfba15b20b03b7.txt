Flexible Solutions International Q3 Sales Rise 35%; Net Loss Narrows
Less than 1 min read
Flexible Solutions International reported third-quarter 2026 sales up 35% year over year and a narrower net loss.
Key Highlights:
- Q3 sales rose to $14.3 million from $10.6 million a year earlier, with revenue from a large food-grade contract contributing to the increase.
- The net loss narrowed to $226,513, or $0.02 per share, from $503,358, or $0.04 per share, in Q3 2025.
- Management expects a return to profitability in Q4 2026, followed by rapidly increasing profits in 2027.
- The company warned that a food-product customer may not be honoring contract terms, potentially leading to arbitration and reduced or no further revenue.
