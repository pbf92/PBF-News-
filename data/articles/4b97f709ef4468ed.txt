Manchester United Extend Bruno Fernandes Contract to 2028
Manchester United have secured captain Bruno Fernandes' future until summer 2028 after activating a 12-month extension option on his contract on Friday, November 27, 2026. The decision prevents other European clubs from approaching the midfielder over a potential free transfer next summer while allowing both parties to postpone longer-term negotiations.
Fernandes' previous agreement was due to expire at the end of the current season. Manchester United had been discussing a new contract with the Portuguese international since the summer, but negotiations have not produced a permanent new agreement.
According to Sky Sports News, both sides consider it appropriate to resume discussions at a later stage, with their immediate attention directed toward improving the club's current performance.
Activating the existing clause also prevents clubs outside the Premier League from approaching Fernandes in January over a pre-contract agreement for a move next summer. The midfielder has attracted interest during previous summer transfer windows, particularly from Saudi Arabia.
Fernandes also missed Manchester United's training session on Friday because of illness, putting his availability for Saturday's match against Brentford in doubt. The Portuguese midfielder was understood to have contracted a virus while in Portugal.
Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino addressed the captain's condition ahead of the Brentford fixture.
"We don't know. He got a virus in Portugal. He finished the game with a problem and today he cannot be part of the training session.
We'll see. We are going to see during the day, and [Saturday], how he wakes up. We are not going to take any risks," said Mauricio Pochettino, Manchester United manager.
Sky Sports News journalist Danyal Khan explained the implications of activating the extension clause, highlighting its importance in protecting the club's position during the next transfer window.
"What triggering this option does is reduce the chance to zero that any club outside of the Premier League can approach Fernandes from Europe's top leagues around a potential move next summer for free. So this move protects both the club and the player from that situation. We know he's been the subject of interest in the last few summers, especially from Saudi Arabia. I think negotiations will continue in the future over Fernandes' long-term future, but for now he will remain at the club until the summer of 2028," said Danyal Khan, Sky Sports News journalist.
Fernandes previously confirmed that Manchester United had offered him a new contract. However, he chose to put negotiations on hold because he believes the club should prioritize returning to winning form.
The midfielder also rejected two contract proposals. The initial offer arrived during the summer, while his most recent agreement with Manchester United was signed in August 2024.
Explaining his position on the negotiations, Fernandes dismissed suggestions that financial demands were behind his decision to delay discussions.
"I've seen people saying that he's asked for more money, he's asked for more years. If I wanted more money, I wouldn't be here. I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United.
I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester. Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here. The thing is, I've always showed how much I care about the club and how much I want to win at this club.
And I always said, in the moment the club thinks it's time for me to move on, I will move on. I'm not going to be the player that I've seen many in this time at Manchester United. They're going to stay here just by staying, just finish my contract and then we will see.
In the moment the club says, 'Bruno, it's time to part ways', I'm going to be the first one walking out the door. Yeah, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay. And there are no negotiations going on.
We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract. But about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to one side because I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games. My aim will always be to win the Premier League and the Champions League within the club I am," said Bruno Fernandes, Manchester United captain.
Manchester United chief executive Omar Berrada also acknowledged the club's ongoing discussions with its captain. He praised Fernandes' contribution and emphasized the importance of maintaining a positive relationship with the midfielder.
"Listen, I've said before, we have a tremendous amount of respect for Bruno. We're very fortunate to have him as a captain. What he's done for this team over the last six, seven years is absolutely incredible.
Last year he won, I think, all the individual awards you can win. And we're very proud to have him as captain. It's true that we're having conversations about his new contract, but I prefer to keep that private.
I think it's something that needs to be discussed behind the scenes between him and the club," said Omar Berrada, Manchester United chief executive.
Fernandes will therefore remain under contract at Manchester United until summer 2028, while discussions about his longer-term future remain on hold. His immediate availability for the Brentford match depends on his recovery from illness.
