American Resources Received Notice of Delisting
On November 18, 2026, Nasdaq notified American Resources that its common stock could be suspended from trading on November 30 and removed from listing because the company had not filed its March and June 2026 quarterly reports by the extended deadline; the missing September 2026 report was an additional basis for delisting. The company filed an appeal and requested a stay pending a hearing; the appeal request temporarily stays suspension for 15 days, and the hearing panel is expected to decide on an extension within that period.
Original SEC Filing: American Resources Corp [ AREC ] - 8-K - Nov. 24, 2026
