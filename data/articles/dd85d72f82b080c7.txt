TITLE: Site lets gamers play GTA 6 for free, as alternative to PS5 or Xbox cloud gaming
OUTLET: Notebookcheck

The $80 GTA 6 price is only part of the cost of playing the Rockstar release. Fans typically must buy an increasingly expensive Xbox or PS5 console. While cloud gaming options exist, they also require purchasing the title or a subscription. A new streaming service is completely free, but with serious limitations.
How the site works
PlayMyPS5 utilizes a web-based interface and relies on PS Remote Play from a PlayStation console. Gamers don’t need a gaming system, a PSN account, a monthly subscription, or a copy of GTA 6 PS5. Interested parties enter the site’s address, select a nickname, and wait their turn.
The service supports controller, keyboard, mouse, and touchscreen inputs catering to mobile devices. Aside from higher latency and a lower resolution, the experience resembles playing on a local machine. The major drawback is that users have 5 minutes at a time to cruise around Leonida.
When their turn comes, players have 20 seconds to respond to a prompt. If they go AFK for 30 seconds or longer, they forfeit the turn to the next name in the queue. When idle, fans can join a live chat hosted on YouTube, Twitch, and Kick. Since co-op action with Jason and Lucia isn’t available and GTA 6 Online could be months away, it’s the closest alternative to multiplayer gameplay.
PlayMyPS5 aims to grow by allowing supporters to register additional consoles for PS Remote Play. However, the site recommends creating a separate PSN account. Sony advises against commercial or public sharing of gaming systems through third parties.
Streaming the latest GTA
For gamers who find 5 minutes not long enough, other reasonably affordable solutions exist. Still, none are free like PlayMyPS5, and all require paying the $80-or-more list price of the GTA 6 PS5 or Xbox version.
The most recent option targets the PlayStation Portal handheld. PS Plus Premium subscribers can try the cloud gaming feature for the Rockstar release. Other platforms are limited after Sony delisted its PC app, but Portal owners don’t need a costly console.
Xbox Cloud Gaming also streams the title on the Series X, S, and One via its new free but time-limited Game Pass tier. Regardless, players must again obtain the game’s digital license.
