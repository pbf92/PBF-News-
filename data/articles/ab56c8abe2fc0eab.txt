TITLE: Grand Theft Auto VI's Record-Changing Opening Day Gross Officially Revealed
OUTLET: MovieWeb

Rockstar Games is a video game publisher that has long prided itself on the detail, immersion, and innovation featured in its video games. The action-adventure genre is where they shine the most, and that has never been more evident than in the Grand Theft Auto and Red Dead series of video games. The last game released by the publisher was Red Dead Redemption II, which launched on October 26, 2018. Development of that game took eight years, though work on the next entry in the Grand Theft Auto series would make that look short in comparison.
The announcement trailer for Grand Theft Auto VI dropped on Rockstar's YouTube page 2 years ago, though development for the game has gone on for much longer than that. A big part of the reason why there was so much work to be done was because of how successful Grand Theft Auto V managed to be across three different console generations. The live service that accompanied the game, Grand Theft Auto Online, which also launched in 2013, is still incredibly popular to this day. Overall, the combined sales and revenue from the main game and its online mode earned Rockstar $10 billion in worldwide revenue, making it one of the most financially successful entertainment products of all time.
It has been 13 years since the last Grand Theft Auto Game came out on video game consoles, and the excitement for the latest entry, Grand Theft Auto VI, has been palpable for several years now. Rockstar and Take-Two took their time in developing this latest video game, which officially launched on November 19, 2026. Funnily enough, it took little to no time for the fruits of their labor to show: it is now being reported that Grand Theft Auto VI has earned $3.79 billion on its first day of release, making the game the biggest entertainment launch of all time. The companies behind the production of the game had a lot to live up to with the unprecedented success of the last entry, and it seems like this newest entry didn't take long to catch up to its predecessor.
Well-known video game reporter Jason Schreier provided some insight in his breaking news tweet on X, saying that Grand Theft Auto VI sold $3.79 billion in 24 hours, "making it the biggest entertainment launch of all time," while also adding a specific number of units, stating that "pre-sales of the game reached 32.5 million units." Given that the game was available to purchase at both $80 and $100 respectively, it's no wonder that Rockstar was able to have such a successful launch day. These numbers don't even take into account how many more copies will be sold throughout the holiday season as well. Overall, it feels like Grand Theft Auto VI is primed for even more success.
Given how much the video game has captured the pop culture zeitgeist, a lot of the conversation surrounding Grand Theft Auto is about how video games in general are not given the same level of status or recognition as other mediums of entertainment such as movies or television. When comparing the game's first-day sales to the total box office of one of the most successful films of the year, it's clear that video games are not only seen as an investment because of how many hours of entertainment they can provide when compared to a film or show, but also because the stories and characters in them are just as compelling, and give players an active role to play in the narrative.
