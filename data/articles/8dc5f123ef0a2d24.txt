Alset Announces 1-for-5 Reverse Stock Split Effective November 23
Less than 1 min read
Alset will implement a 1-for-5 reverse stock split effective November 23, 2026, at 8:00 a.m.
Key Highlights:
- At the effective time, every five outstanding common shares will be combined into one share.
- The common stock’s par value will remain $0.001 per share after the split.
- No fractional shares will be issued; holders entitled to fractions will receive cash based on the prior trading day’s closing price.
- The common stock’s CUSIP number will change to 02115D307.
Original SEC Filing: Alset Inc. [ AEI ] - 8-K - Nov. 18, 2026
