TITLE: Why Jensen Huang's Nvidia Return Could Hurt Anthropic Stock
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia sent its shares soaring roughly 30% on Monday. But Anthropic, whose Claude AI models face growing competition from Nvidia, missed the rally.
The Nasdaq jumped around 6%, while Anthropic (ANT) slipped 3%. The stock has also fallen from its $97.17 first-day closing price.
So why could Huang's comeback be bad news for Anthropic investors?
Why Jensen Huang is Bad News for Anthropic
Anthropic makes money by charging customers to use Claude. Businesses pay for access but cannot download its models and run them independently.
Nvidia takes a different approach.
Its Nemotron AI models are free to download and can run on a company's own computers.
That could save businesses money on AI subscriptions, although running these models still requires computing resources.
If more companies choose cheaper alternatives, Anthropic could lose customers or face pressure to lower prices.
Nvidia has also backed open-model developers, including Reflection AI, Thinking Machines Lab, and Poolside.
CNBC's David Faber warned Monday that Huang's return could hurt Anthropic because of his support for open-source AI.
The disagreement goes back months.
In July, Huang urged Washington against "premature restrictions" on open models. Anthropic refused to sign his letter.
CEO Dario Amodei later insisted that his company "has never advocated for a ban on open-weights models."
Nvidia Still Has $10 Billion Riding on Anthropic
Despite their rivalry, Nvidia invested approximately $10 billion alongside Anthropic's IPO.
Anthropic also relies on Nvidia's chips, although it is diversifying across other suppliers.
During IPO week, Amodei described Nvidia as a "long and productive partnership."
Meanwhile, Nvidia's comeback lifted crypto stocks. Strive gained 15.8%, Strategy rose 12.1%, and Coinbase climbed 7%.
      "Glad the Board acted before we needed to," said Strive CEO Matt Cole, whose clients hold nearly $100 million in Nvidia shares.
What Should Anthropic Investors Watch?
With Morgan Stanley's IPO price support over, investors should watch the $83 offering price.
Longer term, the bigger question is whether OpenAI models start taking paying customers away from Claude.
For now, Huang's return increases competitive pressure on Anthropic. Whether that translates into weaker growth remains uncertain.
