Does Anthropic Offer a Claude Student Discount in 2026?
No. As of November 2026, Anthropic does not run a public student discount program for Claude Pro. The pricing page at claude.com/pricing lists the individual plans (Free, Pro, Max, Ultra), the Team plan, the Enterprise plan, and an Education plan that only institutions can buy. There is no “Student” tier and no .edu price. Anthropic’s help center says its coupons and promotions are generally available only to users trying a paid plan for the first time, and that App Store subscribers cannot use them at all. None of them is tied to student status.
What exists instead is a set of institution-level and program-level access routes that put Claude in the hands of students without changing the consumer pricing. The most important one is Claude for Education, a comprehensive university-wide plan Anthropic launched on April 2, 2025. If your school has signed an agreement, you sign in to claude.ai with your university email and land in your university-sponsored account. If your school has not signed one, no individual workaround inside Anthropic exists, and that is the gap most “Claude student discount” search results misrepresent.

5 Legitimate Ways Students Can Use Claude for Less in 2026
Below are the only routes that actually move you to free or discounted Claude in 2026. None of them involve a code, a coupon, or a third-party “verification” site. Each one has a real eligibility test, and we list it next to the price and the access level you get.
1. Claude for Education: Free Pro Through Your University
Cost: $0 to the student. Eligibility: students, faculty, and staff at a partner institution. Anthropic’s flagship student route launched on April 2, 2025 and signs entire campuses, not individuals. When your university joins, you sign in with your university email and get a university-sponsored Claude account. Anthropic’s help center lists what that account includes: the newest Claude models, Projects, uploads of hundreds of pages, more messages per day than individual plans, and priority access at busy times. Claude for Education also launched Learning mode, which works inside Projects and guides students with Socratic questions instead of handing over answers. Your university can switch features on or off, so check its IT guidelines.
Anthropic named Northeastern University, the London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE), and Champlain College as campus-wide partners at launch in April 2025, then added the University of San Francisco School of Law and Northumbria University in July 2025. Its education page shows a handful of partner logos rather than a full list, among them Dartmouth, Syracuse University, the University of Pittsburgh, and the University of Virginia, so the fastest way to check eligibility is to open claude.ai and sign in with your university email. If you land in a personal account, ask your IT department whether your school has a Claude agreement. Anthropic also added integrations with Canvas, Panopto, and Wiley in 2025, which is worth flagging to your university IT team if they are evaluating the plan.
Teaching rather than studying? Anthropic runs a separate free plan for teachers. Claude for Teachers is free for verified US K-12 educators, schools, and districts through July 30, 2028, a date Anthropic extended on November 13, 2026. It covers teachers only, not students or university staff. Our Claude for Teachers guide walks through verification.
2. Claude Campus Program: A $3,600 Stipend for Student Leaders
Cost: $0, and leaders are paid. Eligibility: students aged 18+ with work authorization in their country of study. The Campus Program now runs three tracks for a full school year, September 2026 to June 2027: Claude Builder Clubs for undergraduates, who run workshops, hackathons and demo nights; Claude Campus Conversations for master’s students, who host discussion groups on AI and society; and Claude Science workshops for PhD students and postdocs. Every selected leader receives a $3,600 cash stipend. Students anywhere in the world can apply, though the master’s and PhD tracks may pick from a smaller pool of schools during their pilot. Anthropic’s campus page does not promise participants free Claude Pro, so treat any blog that says it does as unconfirmed.
The catch is timing. Applications ran from September 1 to September 12, 2026, and the program is now closed for Fall 2026. Past ambassadors have to reapply each year, and Anthropic has not announced the next window. If you want the perks of an established Builder Club without applying yourself, see whether your campus already has one and join as a member: club leaders share resources such as API credits with members, though Anthropic publishes no fixed amount per member.
3. GitHub Student Developer Pack: Free Copilot With Claude (Auto Mode Only)
Cost: $0. Eligibility: verified students with a school email through the GitHub Student Developer Pack. GitHub gives verified students free access to GitHub Copilot, which routes prompts through Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google models. This is the closest thing to a “free Claude” path for student coders, but the model picker changed on March 12, 2026. GitHub announced that “some premium models, including GPT-5.3-Codex, GPT-5.4, and Claude Opus and Sonnet models, will no longer be available for self-selection” under the new GitHub Copilot Student plan.
The workaround is auto model selection, where Copilot picks the model for you and can still route requests to Sonnet or Opus. GitHub confirms it: “through Auto mode, you’ll continue to have access to a powerful set of models from providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google.” Since June 24, 2026 that is the only option on the student plan, so no Claude model is pickable by hand any more. The sign-up pause that blocked new applicants in April ended on June 17, 2026, when GitHub reopened Student, Pro, Pro+ and Max, so the route is open again to anyone who can verify. See the GitHub Copilot Student plan guide for the full eligibility breakdown.
