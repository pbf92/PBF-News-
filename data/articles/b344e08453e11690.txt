TITLE: Eagle winter dining cards return with 11 participating restaurants
OUTLET: Cochrane Eagle

A Cochrane Eagle tradition continues in a new home as the newspaper’s winter dining cards go on sale Nov. 26, offering residents savings at 11 local restaurants.
The Eagle invites residents to visit its new location inside the RE/MAX West office at 110 Second Ave. W. to purchase a card and save up to $180.
Participating restaurants include:
- The Canadian Brewhouse
- Cabeza Grande
- Flames of India (formerly Gate of India)
- Mama Mia Italian Kitchen
- The Boot
- Boston Pizza
- Half Hitch Brewing Company
- Bison Food & Drink
- The Venue
- Lion Head Pub
- Texas Gate Bar & Grill
The offer allows cardholders to purchase one entrée and receive a second entrée free, up to a maximum value of $15.
The card costs $35 plus GST and is valid from Jan. 1 to June 30, 2027, at participating Cochrane locations only. Cards are non-refundable.
Debit and credit payments are accepted; cash is not.
