TITLE: GTA 6's story seemingly confirms leak revealing Rockstar's next game
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

GTA 6 finally being out in the wild has inadvertently added credibility to leaks released prior to the game’s launch. Some of these leaks revealed Rockstar’s next planned game.
Before you jump to any conclusions, no, it’s not GTA 7 or Red Dead Redemption 3 – it might be better…
There are no spoilers for GTA 6 below
Back in 2023, an anonymous leaker took to gtaForums to drop fresh intel on GTA VI, and what would come after the game had launched.
Several story details were disclosed, such as Jason and Lucia’s past as well as some of the game’s multiple ending outcomes.

Having reviewed and beaten the game, I’m shocked by how accurate these leaks were, especially considering they’re from 2023.
Because the anonymous user got so much right, it makes what they had to say next all the more interesting; Rockstar Games’ next title will be L.A. Noire 2.
Obviously we need to take this with a pinch of salt as Rockstar hasn’t officially announced plans for its next title, but the leaker got so much right about GTA VI it’s a hard rumour to ignore.
The L.A. Noire sequel will apparently be set in San Francisco, so expect a different title if it does get revealed, and it will follow a new detective tracking down the Zodiac Killer. Again, take this with a pinch of salt as in the end it could be a fictional killer that’s simply inspired by the real-world events.
Additional info from the leaks says it’ll bring back the face-tracking technology from the previous game, but with vast improvements to make it “the best-looking game ever made.”
When the leak was first published, it said the game was in early development and would target a 2030 launch window, but that probably didn’t factor in the several delays GTA VI received prior to launch.

An L.A. Noire sequel is the palate cleanser we need after GTA 6
While Rockstar Games fans would have probably expected another Red Dead Redemption game, or God forbid GTA 7, after the release of GTA VI, an L.A. Noire sequel is a much better idea.
GTA VI has been years in the making and whatever open-world Rockstar crafts next is going to take just as long, if not longer, as we move onto next-gen consoles like Xbox Project Helix and the PlayStation 6.
Something like L.A. Noire or even the long-awaited sequel to Bully is exactly the type of game we need next. It’d be an excellent bridge between the massive, open-world titles, in addition to just being games we all want to see.
Again, we need to take the leaks with a pinch of salt, as while they were on the money regarding GTA VI that doesn’t mean they’re correct about Rockstar’s next game. Fingers crossed, though…
