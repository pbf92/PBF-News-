TITLE: All safe codes in GTA 6 and where to find them
OUTLET: Rock Paper Shotgun

Regardless of whether you're exploring criminal compounds or strange research outposts in GTA 6, you'll frequently come across locked safes full of cash, valuables and illegal goods to fence off. Annoyingly, the residents of Vice City are damn good at keeping their valuables secure, and these safes are tricky to get into unless you use explosives, which bring unwanted attention.
To counter that, you'll need to find safe codes, which you'll discover by exploring. They're well worth the effort though, as they let you crack open safes without making a sound. Below, we've covered where to find all the safe codes in GTA 6, so you can bypass every safe door and loot all the valuables inside.
All safes and safe code in GTA 6
Below, we've listed every safe in GTA 6 and the code required to open it. If you want to know how to find the codes for these safes, we've gone into more detail further down the page. Click each safe you want a more detailed explanation for, and you'll skip right there.
Here's every safe and safe code in GTA 6:

Kingpin Bowling safe
Safe code
0300
Safe location
The safe is found while completing the Kingpin Bowling alley Illegal Operation in Port Gellhorn. Track down the storage room, and look in the office adjacent to it. You'll find the safe in the back left-hand corner.
How to get the code
Found while completing the Brooks and Boatworks Illegal Operation in Port Gellhorn. You'll find an office on the right-hand side of the compound, near the boat in front of the warehouse. On the wall inside is a map, with multiple safe codes printed on it. The Kingpin Bowling Alley code is included.
Brooks Boatworks safe
Safe code
6823
Safe location
The safe is found while completing the Brooks and Boatworks Illegal Operation in Port Gellhorn. It's located in the room up the stairs to your left when entering the front doors of the Brooks and Boatworks warehouse. It's to the left of the pool table, near a white plastic chair.
How to get the code
Found while completing the Brooks and Boatworks Illegal Operation in Port Gellhorn. You'll find an office on the right-hand side of the compound, near the boat in front of the warehouse. On the wall inside is a map, with multiple safe codes printed on it. The Brooks and Boatworks safe code is included.
La Cascara Motel safe
Safe code
6969
Safe location
The safe is found while completing the La Cascara Illegal Operation in Port Gellhorn. It's locked in a small cell in the centre of the motel's top floor.
How to get the code
Found while completing the Brooks and Boatworks Illegal Operation in Port Gellhorn. You'll find an office on the right-hand side of the compound, near the boat in front of the warehouse. On the wall inside is a map, with multiple safe codes printed on it. The La Cascara Motel safe code is included.
Pelican Valley Outpost safe
Safe code
0072
Safe location
Found in the Pelican Valley Outpost in Pelican Valley. Upon entering the house in the centre of the outpost, you'll find the safe directly to your left.
How to get the code
While exploring the house in the centre of the Pelican Valley Outpost, view the engravings in the floor in the bedroom. You'll see that "CLEAVES" is scratched into the floor. Once you've viewed it, head to the computer in the living room and choose CLEAVES when asked for a password. It'll reveal a clue, claiming that the code is "double success."
After a nasty surprise upon exiting the PC screen, head to the shed near the main house, and you'll find a smashed crab tank inside. Near the tank is a list of crab statistics. One of the crabs in the list is highlighted, with a note claiming 00.36 was a "success". Double 00.36 and you'll have your code.
