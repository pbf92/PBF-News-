Fed Rate Decision December 2026: When It Lands and Whether the Fed Hikes 25 or 50 bp
The December Fed meeting runs on 8 and 9 December 2026. The Federal Reserve announces its rate decision on Wednesday 9 December at 2:00 pm ET, which is 19:00 in London. Chair Kevin Warsh holds his press conference at 2:30 pm ET, according to FedRateCalc.
Markets see a hike as close to certain, so the question is its size. CME FedWatch readings from 17 to 19 November put a 25 basis point (bp) hike at 90% to 97% and a 50 bp hike at 3% to 10%.

25 or 50 Basis Points? What Fed Officials Are Saying
The Case for 50 bp
Governor Christopher Waller leads it. On 10 November he said 50 bp should be considered if core inflation ran near 0.3% again, per StockAnalysis. October core CPI rose 0.3%, above the 0.2% forecast, per StockAnalysis.
On 18 November he went further in a CNBC interview. "If you're not going to signal this very clearly to markets you're going in January, my view is then you have to go 50 in December," Waller said, per Yahoo Finance.
The Case for 25 bp
New York Fed President John Williams spoke on 17 November. He said the situation does not call for "dramatic action", per Seoul Economic Daily. He also said the AI boom could push the Fed to raise rates further next year, per Axios.
On Friday 20 November, Waller is scheduled to give welcoming remarks at 08:25 ET. Cook speaks at 12:15 ET, as listed in our Lisa Cook speech preview. This preview was written on 19 November, before both appearances.

What Markets Are Pricing
The Fed rate hike odds for December come from fed funds futures, which the CME FedWatch tool turns into probabilities. They move with every speech and data release.
Source: CME FedWatch as reported by Kitco, Seoul Economic Daily, Investing.com and Trading Economics. Market pricing reflects expectations at the time of each reading and can change quickly. Forecasts are not a reliable indicator of future performance.
Market pricing points to a 25 bp hike, though pricing can change quickly. Markets may react more to the signal about January and to the dot plot than to the hike itself.
Futures also price more hikes after December. On 18 November markets had about 62 bp of hikes priced over the next three meetings, per investingLive.

How Markets Could React to 25 or 50 bp
Higher rates have often lifted the dollar and short-term yields. They can weigh on gold and rate-sensitive stocks. The size of any move tends to depend on the surprise against pricing.
