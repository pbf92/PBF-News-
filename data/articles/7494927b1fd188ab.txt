TITLE: GTA 6 release date causes mass PS5 console shortage before Black Friday sales
OUTLET: Notebookcheck

Analysts anticipated the GTA 6 release date would massively boost hardware sales. Limited or nonexistent PS5 console stock supports those predictions. Any shopper hoping for a Black Friday deal on a PlayStation system will likely come home disappointed.
A scalpers delight
Not only are discounts unavailable, but some retailers have listed the PS5 Slim Digital and Disc models above MSRP. The inflated prices largely come from third-party sellers on storefronts like Walmart. These businesses take advantage of popular sold-out targets, particularly amid the holiday rush.
Amazon and Sony’s PlayStation Direct are also out of stock the week after the November 19th GTA 6 release date. The shortage extends to other parts of the world, including the UK and Asia. As units vanished, Channel NewsAsia reported that calls from desperate Rockstar fans overwhelmed stores in Singapore.
Ahead of the game’s debut, Circana’s Mat Piscatella estimated 250,000–800,000 additional Xbox and PS5 console sales during the holiday quarter. Black Friday deal hunting and the usual amplified demand in November and December are additional factors.
The Grand Theft Auto effect
Celebrating the impact of GTA 6, Sony offered data suggesting the forecast was conservative. Jason and Lucia’s crime story attracted the most concurrent players for any PlayStation title ever. The blog also claimed it saw the “largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day since the console’s launch, outside of end-of-year holidays.”
The Rockstar project was the first game loaded on 70% of those new systems. Many buyers settled for a base model, since the PS5 Pro has been scarce for months. Dwindling memory and storage supply led GameStop to list the flagship machine for hundreds above its standard retail price.
It’s unclear whether GTA 6 will keep encouraging adopters well into next year. Gamers who prefer multiplayer may be waiting for a GTA Online sequel. Some rumors identify the 1st quarter of 2027 as a likely release window, while other reports hint at a later arrival. With PS5 console sales declining before Rockstar hype peaked, Sony is hoping for a steady stream of fresh content.
