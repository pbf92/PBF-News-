TITLE: GTA 6 cheat codes: how to unlock and activate them
OUTLET: Sortir à Paris

Money, weapons, vehicles or retro filters: GTA 6 has 61 cheat codes hidden away. The trick is knowing how to access them. Here’s everything you need to know.
Unlimited money, a tank in the middle of Vice City, infinite ammo... Ever since GTA III, cheat codes have been part of the series’ DNA, and Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) is no exception. But Rockstar Games has changed the rules of the game. According to Dexerto’s tally, the game has 61 codes, available on PS5 and Xbox Series, affecting money, weapons, vehicles, the weather and visual filters.
Unlike in previous installments, you can’t use them from the start of the adventure: you first have to complete Jason and Lucia’s main story, then unlock each code one by one. Here’s how to do it, step by step.
How to unlock cheat codes in GTA 6?
Forget HESOYAM in San Andreas or the phone numbers in GTA V, which you could enter from the very first hour of gameplay. Here, the first mandatory step is to complete the main story. And it’s a long one: according to PC Gamer, cited by Selectra, the cheat menu stays locked until all 91 story missions are complete. Once the credits roll, head to the Progress menu and select the Cheats tab. As our colleagues at Journal du Geek explain, this is where all the cheats and their unlock conditions are displayed. Any you haven’t unlocked yet will be marked as unavailable, and their input sequence will remain hidden.
Second step: meet the requirement for each code. Throw 100 grenades, catch 100 fish, steal 100 police cars... There’s something for everyone. The good news, according to Dexerto, is that these requirements can be met before finishing the story, and objectives completed during the main storyline count too. So if you’ve already explored a lot of Leonida, some of the list will already be unlocked.
Some cheat codes require nothing more than having completed the game, starting with one that gives you $250,000. A nice nod, considering GTA V never offered a money cheat.
How to activate a cheat code on PS5 and Xbox Series consoles?
Once the code has been unlocked, using it is simple. On PS5, according to our colleagues at Journal du Geek, open the phone with the up arrow, hold down L1 and R1, then enter the sequence using the directional pad. On Xbox Series, the same steps logically involve LB and RB. All the combinations use only the arrow keys, so they’re identical on both consoles. Note that the codes only work in story mode, not online.
Before you get started, a word of advice: save your game. Activating a code disables saving and prevents you from earning trophies and achievements for the rest of your current session. If you’re going for the platinum trophy, it’s best to keep a separate “clean” save. To return to a game without cheats, quit without saving and reload your latest save. There may be other ways to disable a code; we’ll update this article as soon as they’re confirmed.

What are the screen filters in GTA 6?
The big new feature: 16 filters that transform Vice City into a film noir, an old VHS tape or a retro game. Several unlock as you play with your partner.
Golden Hour (golden light, cinematic format): spend 4 hours holding hands with your partner.
Powdered Sugar (a white, sparkling world): scan all the space debris with WAiNK.
Pinhole (pinhole effect): dismantle 25 gang operations with your partner.
Neon Jungle (neon reflections): discover all 16 Girlypop vehicles.
Yesteryear (black-and-white CRT TV): discover the Ridgway.
