Rockstar is sending exclusive GTA VI-themed PS5 Pros to content creators
, by Thibaut
Although GTA VI has been out for about a week and has sold around 45 million copies, Rockstar Games is still going strong with its promotional campaign by thanking some of its fans.
During a YouTube livestream, Davy Jones unveiled an exclusive PS5 Pro inspired by GTA VI. The studio has given away 100 of them to influencers, celebrities, and other content creators.
This console features a black design with palm trees and the game’s logo. The console’s usual black stripes are colored here. Of course, this PS5 Pro is not available for purchase.
