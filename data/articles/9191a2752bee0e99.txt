TITLE: Rockstar Games Employees Voting to Unionize Amid ‘GTA VI’ Release
OUTLET: Yahoo

After spending more than a decade working on Grand Theft Auto VI, Rockstar Games employees are now in the process of voting on whether to form a union.
In an op-ed for The Guardian, Rockstar Games animation programmer Nestor Uriarte shared that the unionization ballot is open until Nov. 26.
"As someone who cares deeply about our team, cares about what we do, and wants us to be able to keep delivering world-class games into the future, I'm voting yes," Uriarte wrote.
Uriarte explained that while gamers are eagerly counting down the weeks and days leading up to the release of a highly-anticipated title, like GTA VI, the employees working on these games are "expected to do unhealthy amounts of overtime" to ensure that the deadline is reached.

According to Kotaku, Rockstar Games employees experienced what was referred to as a "culture of crunch" in the final year of development on Red Dead Redemption 2. Since there was no plan on pushing back the release date for a third time, these workers were left with no other choice but to commit additional nights and dedicate their weekends in order to cross the finish line.
"With a strong union we could make sure there is no return to this system," Uriarte wrote. "We have already seen the introduction of some overtime pay, and now we want to keep on pushing to ensure that every hour worked in our industry is an hour paid."
Uriarte mentioned that Rockstar Games employees have received no indication that layoffs are on the horizon, but that does not mean they are not aware of what's happening in their industry. BioWare, the studio behind Mass Effect, was shut down by Electronic Arts after 30 years last month. Ubisoft is currently going through mass layoffs again.
"It says a lot about the industry that even my colleagues and I, working on the biggest game of all time, are worried about getting laid off," Uriarte wrote. "But the truth is that workers across the sector are helpless to resist the waves of redundancies crashing around us, until we get organised."
According to GameSpot, GTA VI has already earned about $2.6 billion in revenue through pre-orders and physical copies.
