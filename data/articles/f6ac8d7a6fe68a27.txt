OpenGradient: Unlocks 15.04 Million OPG - Oct 2027
OpenGradient has scheduled an unlock of 15.04 million OPG for October 2027. The release equals 6.29% of OPG's circulating supply and covers tokens allocated to the ecosystem, the foundation, investors and the team.
The unlock increases the amount of OPG available for sale by giving recipients access to their released tokens. That includes OPG held in allocations for investors and team members.
