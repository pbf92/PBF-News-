TITLE: Situation is fragile – the team is not, says Man United boss Mauricio Pochettino
OUTLET: The Irish News

Mauricio Pochettino admitted Manchester United’s “situation is fragile” but argued he has inherited a “strong team”.
Pochettino’s reign as United head coach got off to a nightmare start with a 4-0 defeat at rivals Liverpool on Sunday, leaving them second bottom of the Premier League after 11 matches, above only Crystal Palace.
Sporting boss Paulo Fonseca refused to accept there was a fragility to United ahead of their Champions League showdown in Lisbon on Wednesday night and Pochettino pointed to their top-flight finish last season under Michael Carrick as a reason to believe they can start to turn the tide.
“The situation is fragile,” Pochettino said. “We are not fragile. We are a strong team, they showed that last season when they finished third and now we are playing in the Champions League.
“Sometimes things happen in football for different reasons. We need to identify why and already we are working on it. I think we are going to turn round the situation.
“We need to go game-by-game. In the end, being at Manchester United is about winning every single game, one is not more important than another.
“We are Manchester United and we need to believe we have the capacity to turn that situation. We are building our identity and that is the most important thing.”
