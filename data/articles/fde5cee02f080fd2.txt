OUTLET: TradingView

TBV Fund I Completes First Close With $30 Million Target
TB Ventures said its closed-end venture fund, TBV Fund I, has completed its first close, with a target size of $30 million. According to Odaily, the fund was co-founded by Tobias Bauer and Brent Fulfer and combines early-stage equity investments with rule-based token purchases. It plans to allocate 60% of its capital to tokens with on-chain revenue and buyback or fee-sharing mechanisms, and the remaining 40% to seed and Series A equity investments in stablecoins, AI agents and infrastructure, and tokenized real-world assets. Initial equity investments will range from $250,000 to $500,000, with follow-on investments of up to $1 million.
