Wayne Rooney's family life with three footballer sons as tears shed after one released from club
Wayne Rooney has briefly said to goodbye to his loving family after signing up to I'm a Celebrity
Wayne Rooney has broken new ground by following wife Coleen into the I'm a Celebrity jungle. Two years after his childhood sweetheart finished runner-up to Danny Jones, the Manchester United legend is the headline act as the 2026 series gets underway in Australia.
The line-up includes actress Joanna Page, model Leomie Anderson, Kaiser Chiefs frontman Ricky Wilson, Paralympian and TV presenter Ade Adepitan, YouTuber and content creator Sharky, TV presenter AJ Odudu and comedian Jack Skipper. They are joined by TV personality and radio DJ Chloe Burrows, former Coronation Street star Claire Sweeney and broadcaster Mariella Frostrup.
But the eyes of football fans will be on how Rooney, who has reputedly been paid £800,000 by ITV, fares. While his football heroics with United and England are well known, here's the lowdown on Rooney's life away from the game…
Net worth
Rooney has amassed an estimated net worth of around £126m. At one point in his career, he was earning in the region of £20m per year.
Aside from his earnings from playing and management, he's had lucrative endorsement deals with brands like Nike, EA Sports, Ford and Coca-Cola. High-profile broadcasting and punditry contracts have cemented his status as one of British sport's wealthiest figures.

Childhood sweetheart
Rooney's personal life is anchored by his enduring marriage to Coleen. The couple and their four children live in a £20m mansion near Knutsford in Cheshire.
The pair first crossed paths at age 12 in the Liverpool suburb of Croxteth. A persistent young Wayne famously waited outside Coleen's all-girls school and even bribed her brothers with football boots to get a date, eventually winning her over at age 16 after she finished her GCSEs.
Married in Portofino, Italy, in 2008, the couple have weathered intense media scrutiny and well-publicised personal storms to build a solid-looking partnership. He recently said his marriage is in a much better place after he gave up alcohol and shed three stone.
Footballer sons
The apples haven't fallen far from the tree when it comes to their sons. The eldest, Kai, 16, is rapidly following in his father's footsteps as a bright prospect in United's youth set-up.
Klay, 13, is also in the United youth system. Breaking from the family mould, Kit, 10, has taken a break from football to focus on darts, according to Coleen.
Youngest son Cass, eight, was on the books of Everton, only to suffer the pain of being let go by his dad's boyhood club. On breaking the news to him, Coleen said: "I was like, 'Cass, I've had a call and you're not going to be going into the next [year at Everton]' and he threw his head into his hands and he just burst out crying.
"But an hour later, he forgot about it, he was fine. He still plays for his grassroots team."
Wayne has stressed that he doesn't expect any favours from his former clubs regarding his sons' footballing dreams, insisting he wants them to progress on merit.
