TITLE: Startups Raise $515 Million to Build AI Agents Their Own Internet
OUTLET: PYMNTS.com

Startups Raise $515 Million to Build AI Agents Their Own Internet
The internet was built for people. Its search engines, browsers and phone lines assume a person is on the other end, reading a screen at human speed. Artificial intelligence (AI) agents are now the web’s newest users, and they search, read and act faster and in far greater volume than a person can. A parallel internet is being built for them.
