BigBear.ai Warrants Expire Dec. 7, 2026; Last Trading Day Is Dec. 3
Less than 1 min read
BigBear.ai announced its warrants will expire on Dec. 7, 2026, with public warrant trading ending three days earlier.
Key Highlights:
- Public and private warrants, each exercisable for one common share at $11.50, expire at 5:00 p.m. New York City time on Dec. 7, 2026.
- About 12.3 million public warrants and 0.1 million private warrants were outstanding as of Sept. 30, 2026; unexercised warrants will become worthless.
- Public warrants will stop trading on the NYSE before market open Dec. 4, making Dec. 3 the last trading day.
- The NYSE intends to file a Form 25 to delist and deregister the public warrants; BigBear.ai common stock will continue trading under BBAI.
Original SEC Filing: BigBear.ai Holdings, Inc. [ BBAI ] - 8-K - Nov. 25, 2026
