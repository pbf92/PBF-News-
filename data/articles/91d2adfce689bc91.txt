TITLE: GetHookd Expands Meta Ad Research With AI-Agent Access For Jewelry Brands
OUTLET: FinancialContent

GetHookd has added AI-agent access to its Meta ad research platform, giving jewelry brands a conversational way to research live ads alongside the existing manual search.
-- GetHookd has announced that jewelry brands and other advertisers can now connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, or Perplexity to its library of live Meta ads. Marketers can research active Facebook and Instagram campaigns in plain English, with each answer returned alongside the source ad so users can check it themselves.
The announcement comes as social advertising remains an important channel for jewelry businesses. Jewel360’s 2026 State of the Jewelry Industry Report, based on a survey of 117 independent jewelers, found that 74% ranked Facebook among their most effective marketing channels, and 59% named it among the platforms driving customer demand. The same report found that 90% of jewelers say social media shapes what customers ask for when they walk in, while 94% still sell almost entirely in person and 61% say their digital presence is critical to driving foot traffic. GetHookd says the expanded access is intended to help jewelry advertisers research creative activity more quickly before deciding what to develop and test.
GetHookd’s library contains more than 22 million live Facebook and Instagram ads and is updated daily. Within the jewelry category, marketers can study how products are presented, which formats remain active, and how competing brands frame gifting, occasions, and collections.
GetHookd’s jewelry advertising guide illustrates the kind of creative variety marketers can study. It breaks down Meta ads from Tiffany & Co., Pandora, Mejuri, Kay Jewelers, and Brilliant Earth, which use approaches ranging from heritage storytelling and licensed collaborations to creator-led content, price-anchored gifting, and interactive buying journeys. According to the guide, the strongest jewelry ads share three traits: visuals that communicate quality and wearability, copy tied to a real emotion or occasion, and a clear next step that removes friction from the purchase decision. The guide also covers four ad formats for jewelry brands: single image, video, carousel, and collection ads.
The AI-agent access lets marketers query the library through tools they already use. The manual interface remains available on any plan for teams that prefer to search and filter ads themselves.
Beyond individual ads, GetHookd offers Brand Spy, which notifies users when a competitor launches and shows the launch date and the number of live ads. Its Shops tool shows a Shopify store’s monthly visitors, best sellers, and the Meta ads it runs. Concepts identified through the platform can be turned into new static ads with Clone Ads, which builds on a creative already running in the market.
“AI can accelerate creative production, but speed alone does not tell a marketing team what is worth making,” said Alex Fedotoff, CEO of GetHookd.
About GetHookd
GetHookd is a Miami-based advertising intelligence platform founded by CEO Alex Fedotoff, serving ecommerce brands, media buyers, and agencies researching and developing Meta ad creative.
