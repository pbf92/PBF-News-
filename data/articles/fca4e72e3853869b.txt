TITLE: 'Read a book': Chris Russo admonishes people playing GTA VI all weekend
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

The Grand Theft Auto VI launch is so massive that some people think even the NFL blinked because of its launch.
Sure enough, the highly anticipated video game smashed records, reaching $3.79 billion in sales on launch day, including 32.5 million units sold pre-release, according to Rockstar Games.
To put that in perspective, Avengers: Endgame made around 2.94 billion during its entire theatrical run.
Given the immense hype and expected sales GTA VI had heading into this weekend, some have speculated that professional sports leagues took that into account when scheduling games, including the mighty NFL, which almost never plays second fiddle to anything.
ESPN and SiriusXM host Chris Russo doesn't seem to buy that conspiracy theory, even though this weekend's NFL slate is a bit weak. However, the topic gave him the opportunity to launch into a classic Mad Dog rant about something he's only vaguely familiar with.

"I know there's a lot of theorists out there," Russo said on Friday's Mad Dog Unleashed. "They put out that video game which, you know, I guess, what, 9 billion people are going to play? Read a book. I mean, geez.
"I mean, 9 billion people are going to play that Grand Theft Auto, including my son. I mean, are going to play that all weekend, just sit there… brainless and play that dopey video game all weekend? And that's why the NFL put on a lousy schedule: because they knew there was a video game coming out, so they figured they'd give you 6 teams off and give you a bad schedule? I don't think that's the reason, but I know there's a lot of folks out there who think that could have something to do with it.
"The NFL is smart with that, and I guess this video was going to be released on this date, announced a long time ago. I don't know that. I don't know enough about that. And this is the kind of thing that would affect viewership, because if you don't want to watch—and I wouldn't blame you—Arizona getting stomped on in Kansas City, you play a video game all day.
"Sit in your pajamas, sit on your couch, don't move all day, have a pepperoni pie followed by a meatball hero, and go out there and play that dopey video game."
Now that he'd gotten that out of his system, Russo offered an all-time boomer admission: that he thought Grand Theft Auto was a movie.
"I thought that was a movie, that video game… I had no idea," he said. "Coming out November 20th, I thought that was a big movie. I didn't know that it was a video game."
Mad Dog then claims he's never played a video game, right before explaining all the times he used to play video games.
"I'm proud of myself. I have never played a video game," said Russo. "I mean, I swear to God, I used to go out there and play Pac-Man and, you know, those little games where you shoot the little things in the sky with the— I forget which game that is, with the little pinball machine thing (we think he means Asteroids). But I had a video game. I've never played solitaire on a stupid phone, nothing."
Finally, Russo made one of his patented outdated references to bring everything home.
"So I'll go read about Michael Douglas," he said. "We won't talk to Michael Douglas, but I'll read about Michael Douglas instead of going out there and dealing with a video game."
