TITLE: USMNT vs. Haiti LIVE updates, lineups, score as Pochettino says farewell
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

CINCINNATI — Mauricio Pochettino will bid farewell to the U.S. men's national team on Tuesday, Nov. 17, as the U.S. hosts Haiti in the second leg of a Concacaf Nations League quarterfinal.
The USMNT hammered Haiti 6-0 in the first leg on Saturday, Nov. 14, all but clinching a spot in the semifinal before Tuesday's game even begins.
But Pochettino has vowed to take the second leg seriously, as the Argentine coaches his last game before departing to take over Manchester United.
USA TODAY Sports will have live coverage of the match from TQL Stadium in Cincinnati.
