TITLE: Juventus reignites interest in Manchester United star
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Juventus are once again interested in signing Joshua Zirkzee as they look to strengthen their attacking options during the January transfer window. The Dutch striker was among their targets last summer when he was open to leaving Manchester United in search of regular playing time.
The Bianconeri explored the possibility of signing him on loan, but they faced competition from several other clubs, including teams in the Premier League. At the time, Benjamin Sesko was identified as United's preferred striker, which appeared to leave Zirkzee's future uncertain.
However, none of the potential moves materialised, giving Juventus another opportunity to pursue the forward. The Old Lady are keen to take advantage of the situation and could return with a fresh approach when the transfer window opens.
Juventus revive their interest in Zirkzee
Juventus regard Zirkzee as one of the attractive options available in the market and would like to bring him to Turin. Nevertheless, his situation at Manchester United could depend on the plans of Mauricio Pochettino, who has recently been appointed as the club's manager.
The Argentine will first need to assess the players available to him before making decisions about their respective futures. Zirkzee's prospects could therefore become clearer once the new manager has evaluated his squad and decided which players fit into his plans.
A decision from Pochettino could have an important influence on Juventus' pursuit, particularly if the striker is allowed to leave in January.
Bianconeri want attacking reinforcements
According to Football Italia, Juventus remain interested in signing Zirkzee because they have not been satisfied with the performances of their current strikers.
The report adds that Juventus intend to recruit a new forward in January, with the United attacker among the players they would particularly like to add to their squad.
For Juventus, bringing in Zirkzee could provide another option in attack as they look to improve their attacking output. However, much will depend on Manchester United's willingness to sanction his departure and whether Pochettino sees a future for the Dutchman at Old Trafford.
A January move remains a possibility as Juventus continue to assess their options ahead of the transfer window.
