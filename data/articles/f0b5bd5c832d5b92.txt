GTA 6's Criminal Profiles Explained
Contains minor spoilers for "GTA 6"
Every game in the Grand Theft Auto series has been about crime, but "Grand Theft Auto 6" introduces some fundamental transformations to how the series approach its namesake activity. In "GTA 6," committing crime is costlier, more difficult, and totally more rewarding than it has been in any other GTA game. Rockstar has gone all out creating new ways for players to engage with crime in "GTA 6," and new ways for the game world to respond to players' many misdeeds.
From stick-ups to more complicated carjacking procedures, "GTA 6" adds a new level of mechanical depth and immersive realism to its criminal activities. The familiar star-rating Wanted system is back, but "GTA 6" also has a criminal profile system that changes everything about how the game plays. Profiles are formed based on how players choose to engage with the world around them in "GTA 6." That includes actions taken during crimes and side activities and choices that players make during all of the missions in "GTA 6."
Committing wanton violence will cause authorities to search for you with more drastic measures, while keeping a restrained profile might help you evade detection from police altogether. A difference in profiles might cause a fight between Jason and Lucia. In more ways than one, the criminal profile system helps to make "GTA 6" the most immersive game in the entire series.
What are the criminal profiles in GTA 6?
Criminal profiles in "GTA 6" are determined by how violent and how stealthy players are during crimes. Jason and Lucia can have different criminal profiles, and their profiles affect their relationship with each other and with other characters and NPCs in the game. Here are the five different criminal profiles in "GTA 6" and generally how players can go about earning them:
- Professional – Avoid killing, especially when not threatened, complete crimes without being detected
- Hardened – Be aggressive during crimes, kill enemies and get detected more often
- Violent – Attack civilians and kill cops
- Psycho – Regularly kill civilians, kill large amounts of cops, kill non-aggressive animals
- Irredeemable – Continue being needlessly violent and you may never come back
Actions that Jason and Lucia take during missions or side activities can factor into their criminal profile. Each character's profile might change throughout the game, especially once players learn that the profiles also come with small buffs.
What bonuses do criminal profiles give players?
The "GTA 6" criminal profiles system is a great way to immerse players in the narrative of the game. All the new crime mechanics add so much realism to the game that they make the most unrealistic detail in "GTA 6" stand out even more sharply (namely, the surprisingly low prices of various foods, drinks, and clothing options). In general, the criminal profile system ensures that even small actions players take during missions have the potential to influence the course of the game's story. That added sense of immersion is the primary selling point of the criminal profile system, but four of the five profiles also offer a small mechanical bonus. Here they are:
- Professional – Thoughtful actions and non-lethal takedowns grant focus
- Hardened – The same as Professional
- Violent – Headshots grant focus
- Psycho – Heads grant focus, you can't surrender to police
The bonuses associated with each profile lean into that profile's typical play style. It's up to players if they want to engage with the system on a role-playing or mechanical level. Like so many of the features in "GTA 6," criminal profiles are about adding realism to the game while increasing the number of ways players can interact with its world.
