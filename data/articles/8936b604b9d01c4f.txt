TITLE: Pochettino Returns to the Champions League With Manchester United
OUTLET: beIN SPORTS

Pochettino Returns to the Champions League With Manchester United
- The Argentine coach is back in European competition with the 'Red Devils' in crisis, four and a half years after his dramatic elimination against Real Madrid
The best moment of Mauricio Pochettino's managerial career was forged on a May night in 2019 in Amsterdam. The worst, or certainly one of the worst, came at the Santiago Bernabéu in March 2022. The Champions League has delivered contrasting emotions for the Argentine coach, who returns to European competition this Wednesday under unprecedented pressure at Manchester United.
Pochettino dreamed of managing the 'Red Devils', but he never imagined doing so with the team in the relegation zone, with just one league win in eleven games and with the Champions League as a strange salvation from a dismal start to the season.
This Wednesday they face Sporting CP and Pochettino returns to the Champions League four and a half years after the dramatic elimination against Real Madrid at the Bernabéu, when the Champions League of comebacks began. The Argentine witnessed the first glimpse of magic from Carlo Ancelotti's side that year, who overturned a 0-2 aggregate deficit in the space of half an hour thanks to a hat-trick from Karim Benzema. Months later, after barely a year and a half at the club, Pochettino was gone.
The peak with Tottenham and the lost final
The 'Spurs', mired in a trophy desert of more than two decades, knocked out Borussia Dortmund in the round of 16, thrashing the Germans, beat Manchester City in the quarter-finals amid huge controversy and with a goal helped by the hand of Fernando Llorente that Pep Guardiola still has not forgotten, and reached the semi-finals against Erik Ten Hag's Ajax.
The Londoners, with Harry Kane, Heung-min Son, Christian Eriksen and Dele Alli, lost 0-1 in London and were trailing 1-0 in the return leg when the Lucas Moura show began. In the best minutes of the Brazilian's career, he scored three goals, including one in the 96th minute, and thanks to the away goals rule -nonexistent today- he sent Tottenham to the final. Pochettino, embraced by his coaching staff, produced one of the best images of his life.
The story, however, could not be completed with joy. That Tottenham went to Madrid to fight for the Champions League against Liverpool and one of the worst finals in the history of the competition unfolded. Without a doubt the worst since the Champions League has been called the Champions League, perhaps only the 2003 final between Milan and Juventus can match it. A goal from Mohamed Salah in the first minute and another from Divock Origi dashed the title hopes of Pochettino's side, who also closed a circle that day.
Unprecedented pressure at Old Trafford
He was sacked months later, after a poor start to the league season, and left with the feeling that he had been treated unfairly. He made Tottenham Premier League runners-up and led them to their first Champions League final, and yet Daniel Levy handed him his dismissal within months. The results after Pochettino's spell confirmed that sacking him was a mistake.
This Wednesday he returns in a very different scenario, with a Manchester United in need of victories to avoid falling into an even greater crisis. Liverpool thrashed them this weekend; they cannot afford any more disasters. EFE
