TITLE: ‘GTA VI’ sales blew past $3 billion in its first day, making it one of the biggest entertainment launches ever
OUTLET: CNN

A video game that fans had waited more than a decade for generated more revenue in its first day on the market than a Taylor Swift tour and a Marvel “Avengers” movie.
“Grand Theft Auto VI” sales reached $3.79 billion after its first day of release, publisher Take Two Interactive and studio Rockstar Games announced on Friday, making it one of the biggest entertainment launches in history.
The “Grand Theft Auto” series was already among the top-selling video game franchises. But a 13-year wait and a massive production budget heightened expectations and anticipation among fans. The game generated $2.36 billion in revenue before its November 19 release date, according to market intelligence firm Sensor Tower.
Take Two said it sold 32.5 million pre-order copies, and the game has been available for preorder since June.
“Avengers: End Game” grossed $2.9 billion at the box office, and Taylor Swift’s Eras tour generated roughly $2 billion in sales.
The release has helped vault “GTA” from a video game to a “multigenerational franchise” on par with James Bond, Star Wars and Mickey Mouse, Omar Dessouky, a research analyst for Bank of America Global Research, wrote in a Friday research note.
Beyond sales, the game also shattered live-streaming records on Twitch, YouTube and Kick, with fans collectively spending more than 132 million hours watching the game on its launch day, according to analytics platform Stream Charts.
Set in “Vice City,” a fictionalized depiction of Miami, the game centers on two characters, Jason and Lucia, who find themselves in the center of a criminal conspiracy.
“GTA” games are known for their crime-ridden missions and storylines and graphic content, but their realism is also a major part of what makes them resonate with players. “GTA 6” is filled with mundane activities — from bird watching to hitting the gym and hanging drywall — that can make players feel more connected to the game’s world and its characters.
The game is expected to be a major driver of video game spending heading into the holiday season, with the only other major release in the fall timeframe being Nintendo’s “The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time” remake. More than 80% of those who preordered are splurging on the pricier $100 Ultimate Edition, which includes a range of extra cars and clothing and style customizations, compared to the $80 standard version, according to Sensor Tower.
Bank of America’s Dessousky believes “GTA 6” could draw new audiences into gaming while pleasing longtime fans.
“More mature storytelling should reengage lapsed gamers, including those who have not owned consoles in over a decade, while state of the art graphics and open world mayhem retain and attract younger audiences,” he wrote.
