TITLE: GTA 6 Increases physical game and console sales in the UK.
OUTLET: wolfsgamingblog.com

As expected, Grand Theft Auto 6’s impact is being felt across the industry, boosting both console and physical game sales numbers in the UK by significant amounts.
The data comes from Nielsen and covers the 15th Nov to 21st Nov, and the three-day GTA 6 launch period ending on Nov 21st. Nielsen kicks off the data by presenting us with this: boxed software rose a massive 409% over the previous week during the launch of GTA 6, making it the biggest weak for physical games since Christmas 2018, according to their data. And that’s despite the fact that GTA 6’s boxed copy doesn’t contain a disc – just a download code for the game, something which a lot of people didn’t seem to know and instead thought they were getting the game early.
GTA 6 accounted for £32 million in physical sales in the UK, which is around 74% of the UK’s total physical software. According to Neilsen, that makes GTA 6 number 17 in terms of launch week boxed sales.
It wasn’t just boxed copies GTA 6 boosted, though: both consoles saw pretty big jumps. PS5 sales rose by 46% week on week, while Xbox got catapulted by 75%.
Accessories saw gains, to: controllers were up 194% overall, gaming headsets were up 94% and even exterrnal console SSD sales jumped 30%. That last one might have something to do with the 212GB install size of GTA 6.
Mind you, none of this is as impressive as GTA 6’s sales numbers which have probably double or something insane since the last time I covered them.
