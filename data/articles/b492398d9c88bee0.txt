TITLE: Como and Napoli compete for Manchester United’s Joshua Zirkzee
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United's Joshua Zirkzee has constantly been linked with a move back to the Serie A over the last year or so. His situation at Old Trafford hasn't changed since then either, with the player struggling for game time constantly.
Gianluca Di Marzio has now reported that interest from Italy is once again growing in the Dutchman.
Como and Napoli are exploring a move, with both clubs needing a striker. Moise Kean is out until 2027 and this can force the lakeside club into signing the Manchester United man.
Napoli, on the other hand, expected more from Lorenzo Lucca after his return from the loan at Nottingham Forest. But the Italian has struggled, which means Max Allegri's side need another striker in January, with Zirkzee seen as a major option.
No discussions have been held with Manchester United yet, though. A lot will hinge on Mauricio Pochettino's thoughts about Zirkzee, who did come off against Liverpool at the weekend.
Player needs to follow Malen's example
Zirkzee is seeing his career wither away in England when Donyell Malen's is thriving in Italia. HHe needs to use his compatriot as an example and push for a move, while joining a stable footballing project like that of Como, which will bring most out of his skillset.
Manchester United have lacked stability for a while and this has constantly hindered the player.
