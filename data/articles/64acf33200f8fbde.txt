TITLE: GTA 6 players discover secret cutscene hidden behind ridiculous seven-minute climb
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Longtime fans of Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto series will know that it has a reputation for hiding ridiculous secrets, sometimes with little to no context. As it turns out, GTA VI is no exception, after a player discovered an unexpected cutscene following a ridiculously lengthy climb.
Following the release of GTA VI, the discovery was shared by X user xGarbett, who spent an estimated seven minutes climbing a communications tower in Leonida. To put that into perspective, that's considerably longer than the famous ladder climb in Metal Gear Solid 3: Snake Eater, which takes nearly two minutes while the iconic “Snake Eater” theme plays.
Let's be honest, with an open world as massive as GTA VI's, it would be easy to miss this particular secret. Though I'm sure many of you will have that instinct to climb any enormous tower you come across in a video game, just to see what's at the top. However, as shown in the clip shared by xGarbett, that curiosity certainly paid off.
GTA 6's secret cutscene is a hilarious callback to Vice City
So, what did xGarbett find, exactly? Well, at the top of the enormous communications tower is a bird's nest containing several large, colourful eggs, which quite literally look like Easter eggs.
However, these aren't just there for decoration. Players can pick up one of the eggs and throw it from the tower, triggering a secret cutscene.

The cutscene shows the egg spinning through the air like an NFL football before splattering over the car of an unsuspecting NPC, who looks like he's stepped straight out of 1980s Vice City.
The unfortunate driver is also busy talking on his mobile phone. I guess that's what you get for not paying attention to the road.
Speaking of 1980s Vice City, this secret appears to be a callback to the literal Easter egg hidden in 2002's GTA: Vice City.
That particular Easter egg could be found inside a secret room that was especially difficult to discover without knowing where to look. Back when the internet wasn't quite as accessible as it is today, chances are you either stumbled across it by accident or heard about it from someone who had.
How to find GTA 6's hidden Easter egg and trigger the secret cutscene
Unfortunately, xGarbett hasn't revealed the exact location of the tower at the time of writing. However, reports suggest it's one of the tallest communications towers in Leonida, which should help narrow down your search.
Once you've found the tower, here's what you need to do:
- Find the ladder and begin climbing the communications tower. According to xGarbett, reaching the top takes approximately seven minutes.
- Once you reach the summit, look for a bird's nest containing several large, colourful Easter eggs.
- Pick up one of the eggs and position yourself to throw it towards the road below.
- Throw the egg from the tower. If successful, a short cutscene will play, showing the egg striking a vehicle.

It's worth noting that the exact location and conditions needed to trigger the cutscene haven't been fully verified, so you may need to experiment with where you throw the egg.
There are also reports that players can reach the top by parachuting onto the tower, potentially saving themselves a lengthy climb. However, this apparently requires some very precise timing.
Knowing Rockstar Games, this probably won't be the last bizarre Easter egg that players discover in GTA VI. In fact, much like the Grand Theft Auto games that came before it, there's a good chance players will still be uncovering secrets years from now.
In the meantime, if you're planning on climbing every communications tower in Leonida looking for hidden surprises, you might want to have the Metal Gear Solid 3 soundtrack ready.
Grand Theft Auto VI is out now on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
