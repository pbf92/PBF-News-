TITLE: Israel row a 'defining moment for us' - Hallgrimsson
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
Republic of Ireland manager Heimir Hallgrimsson says the controversy surrounding the Nations League games against Israel has made "this group stronger".
The players took a vote on whether to play the two matches - in September and October - amid protests over the Israel-Gaza war, with goalkeeper Gavin Bazunu making himself unavailable for selection.
The Republic of Ireland won the first match 3-0 in Hungary before a 1-1 draw in Serbia.
Hallgrimsson was speaking after his side defeated 10-man Kosovo 3-0 in Dublin on Tuesday night to finish runners-up to Austria in Group B3.
The second place means the Republic of Ireland will be in pot two for the Euro 2028 qualifying draw next month and also ensures they have a promotion/relegation play-off in March against a League A team as they try to move up to the top tier for the first time.
"It tested everyone - physically, mentally and no I've learned a lot doubt and the players have learned a lot and grown," said Hallgrimsson.
"I am pretty sure in one year, two years when this team becomes successful we will look back and say that was the defining moment for us.
"I am pretty sure we will look back in hindsight and say this was a huge moment for us.
"Us as leaders sometimes artificially create us against the world to get the group to stick together and I have said we have got that environment for free for two or three weeks and we survived it and it made this team and this group stronger."
Troy Parrott scored two penalties and Jack Moylan also netted against a Kosovo team hit by the dismissal of Kreshnik Hajrizi after 26 minutes.
It brought to an end a campaign which saw the Republic of Ireland win two matches, with two drawn and two lost.
"We know we will never be perfect and we are trying to find a way to win with what we have," Hallgrimsson added.
"I think we are getting better and better on that. Like I said in the first meeting when I was here and was introduced, I said I want to build a team that is difficult to play against and I feel like we are getting closer and closer to that.
"We are now consistent in our selection of players. We've more or less found our squad, our group and with doing that on a consistent basis, playing the same players together, playing the same system, the connections between players are starting to emerge."
The promotion/relegation play-off draw take place on Friday with the Republic set to go up against one of Croatia, Serbia, Denmark or Wales.
"Everybody, and especially us, like to play the top teams. For one, to compare yourself with the best teams, that's one thing," he said.
"Second, those teams will expose your weaknesses and that helps you in your growth to play these teams and they will expose your weaknesses so we can continue our growth. We're already looking forward to March.
"[There are] exciting teams in the pot. Whoever we draw, it's going to be two tough games and it's good for this team at this time to play these matches instead of friendlies, for example.
"So we're really excited for March and looking forward to meeting the players again."
