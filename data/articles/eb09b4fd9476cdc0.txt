TITLE: Zirkzee To Lead The Line | 4-2-3-1 Manchester United Predicted Lineup Vs Sporting
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United will lock horns with Sporting CP at the Jose Alvalade Stadium this Wednesday night as they look to earn a good result on the road in the UEFA Champions League. The Red Devils suffered a dismal 4-0 loss at the hands of Liverpool recently, so they would be keen on securing a positive result this week.
Mauricio Pochettino will probably opt for the 4-2-3-1 formation and is expected to make some changes to his first team for this clash. Hence, Joshua Zirkzee could get a chance to lead the line for the Mancunian giants up top.
4-2-3-1 Manchester United Predicted Lineup To Face Sporting
Defence
Senne Lammens will feature in goal for Man United after conceding four goals in his last outing. Harry Maguire will play alongside Lisandro Martinez at the centre of Pochettino's defence as they hope to keep a good shape when defending inside their half.
Leny Yoro will try to prove his value as the right-back, while Diogo Dalot hopes to put in a good performance on the other flank as the left-back. Both of them will have to pick and choose their moments to join in on the attack.
Ayden Heaven and Noussair Mazraoui will be available for selection on the bench as they provide cover for the defensive positions.

Midfield
Youri Tielemans and Kobbie Mainoo will anchor the midfield for Man United as they look to win the 50-50 battles at the centre of the park and create some decent chances in the final third.
The gifted trio of Bryan Mbeumo, Bruno Fernandes and Patrick Dorgu will operate just ahead of them as part of Mourinho's attack. All three of them need to be at their best if the Red Devils are to secure a win this week.
Mason Mount and Amad Diallo will have to be content with a place on the bench and might come on to make an impact in the second half if required.
Attack
Joshua Zirkzee will spearhead Man United's attack up top. He has to figure out a way to be a constant menace in the final third.
Matheus Cunha will make up the numbers on the bench as he waits for his opportunity to shine in the latter stages of the contest if needed.
