Walrus: Supply Unlock Releases Up to 53.54 Million WAL - Apr 2027
Walrus plans to unlock up to 53.54 million WAL in April 2027, according to the circulation plan the project filed with Upbit. The planned release equals up to 1.86% of the circulating supply.
The unlock makes the released tokens available to trade. Holders can then put some or all of those tokens up for sale, which could add sell pressure.
