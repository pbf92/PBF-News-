Meta Platforms Pauses Class C Share Issuance Amid Delaware Litigation
Less than 1 min read
Meta Platforms agreed to pause Class C share issuance during pretrial and trial proceedings in shareholder litigation challenging its recapitalization.
Key Highlights:
- The Delaware Court of Chancery trial is scheduled to begin April 19, 2027.
- Mark Zuckerberg waived the deadline requiring the previously declared Class C stock dividend to be paid within five business days of the amended charter’s effectiveness.
- The waiver expires under specified conditions, including June 30, 2027, if the dividend has not been paid by then.
- Meta Platforms does not expect to launch its at-the-market program until after the stock dividend is paid and Class C shares begin trading.
Original SEC Filing: Meta Platforms, Inc. [ META ] - 8-K - Nov. 23, 2026
