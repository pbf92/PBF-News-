Maison Solutions Received Notice of Noncompliance
On November 24, 2026, Maison Solutions received a Nasdaq deficiency notice because its Audit Committee has two members, below the three-member minimum required by Nasdaq Listing Rule 5605(c)(2)(A). The company has until the earlier of its next annual shareholders' meeting or November 20, 2027, to regain compliance; if that meeting is held before May 19, 2027, the cure deadline is no later than May 19, 2027.
Original SEC Filing: Maison Solutions Inc. [ MSS ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
