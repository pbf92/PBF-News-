TITLE: Private rent price median and quartiles, England and Wales
OUTLET: Office for National Statistics

Dataset Private rent price median and quartiles, England and Wales
About this Dataset
The quartile and median rent prices in this dataset have been derived from the PIPR-model predicted prices in PIPR's monthly dataset, which approximates the private rental stock at a given point in time.
Edition in this dataset
Important notes and usage information
These monthly rent price estimates apply to a given monthly time period and are not directly comparable with estimates for other time periods because no adjustment has been made to account for changes in the rental sector or collected rents over time.
