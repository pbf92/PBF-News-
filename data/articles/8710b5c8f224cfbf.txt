What is Anthropic?
Anthropic is an artificial intelligence company founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers, including CEO Dario Amodei and President Daniela Amodei.
The company is best known for developing Claude, a family of large language models that compete directly with ChatGPT. Anthropic's products are used by enterprises, software developers, financial institutions, and governments for coding assistance, research, content generation, and workflow automation.
Major investors include:
	- Google.
- Amazon.
- Leading venture capital firms including Sequoia, Coatue, Dragoneer, and Greenoaks.
Is Anthropic profitable?
The company released its public S-1 filing  in October, which provided the first comprehensive look at profitability metrics.
Anthropic had revenue of $16.3 billion the first half of this year, a 14-fold increase from the first half of 2025. However, the filing shows the company had an $80.7 billion net loss in the first half of 2026, compared to a $13 billion net loss on $12 billion in revenue the first half of 2025.
While Anthropic's revenue growth has been extraordinary, AI model training remains enormously expensive due to computing infrastructure, chips, data centers, and research costs. Anthropic spent $8.9 billion on computing infrastructure the first half of 2026, according to the company.
Media reports indicate Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate climbed to roughly $47 billion by May 2026, up from approximately $14 billion just months earlier. However, like most frontier AI companies, it is widely believed to still be operating at a loss as it spends aggressively to maintain technological leadership.
What are the dangers of Anthropic tech?
Unlike many AI startups, Anthropic has positioned itself around "constitutional AI" and AI safety, arguing that advanced AI systems should be trained with explicit guardrails and oversight.
Still, there have been numerous instances where AI chatbots have broken into outside company data systems and attempted to cover its tracks. 
Anthropic CEO Amodei wrote in a Sept. 12 open letter: “But like many technologies before it, AI brings risks, and because it is such a powerful technology, these risks are serious.
“They include the risk of losing control of AI systems, misuse of AI for cyberattacks and bioterrorism, and serious economic disruption. A race to the bottom, spurred by commercial incentives, can make these risks more acute.”
Amodei has advocated “pacing the frontier” of AI development.
When is the Anthropic IPO Date?
The company plans to begin selling shares on the Nasdaq on Thursday, Nov. 19.
The initial price of shares is expected to be fixed on Nov. 18. The company is currently targeting between $70 and $78 per share.
Did Anthropic file for an IPO?
Yes. On June 1, 2026, Anthropic announced it had confidentially submitted a draft registration statement to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).
A confidential filing allows a company to work through SEC comments before publicly releasing financial information.
When will the Anthropic S-1 be public?
The S-1 public filing, the first comprehensive look at the company’s finances, was released in late October. Typically, confidential filings become public two weeks before an investor roadshow begins to market shares to institutional investors. 
Based on typical IPO timelines, the public offering could emerge in coming weeks.

Frequently Asked Questions about Anthropic IPO
Is Anthropic publicly traded right now?
No. Anthropic remains a private company, but intends to start publicly trading on Nov. 19, according to media report.
What are the risks of the Anthropic IPO?
Major risks include valuation concerns, competition, regulation, infrastructure costs, and potential AI market volatility. Also there are strong worries about proper safety guardrails for the AI industry.
Can I buy Anthropic stock on the NYSE or NASDAQ?
Not yet. Shares are not publicly listed, but will be listed on the Nasdaq exchange. Investors must wait for the IPO or seek access through private secondary markets.
Who owns the majority of Anthropic?
Anthropic is controlled primarily by its founders, employees, and institutional investors. Major strategic investors include Google and Amazon.
How do I sign up for Anthropic IPO allocations?
Investors should monitor major brokerages such as Fidelity, Charles Schwab, Robinhood, and E*TRADE, which sometimes participate in IPO share allocations.
Will the Anthropic IPO burst the AI bubble?
Probably not by itself. However, Anthropic's public debut may become one of the most important tests yet of whether public-market investors are willing to support trillion-dollar AI valuations. If demand is strong, it could reinforce the current AI boom. If demand disappoints, it could trigger a broader reassessment of AI-related stocks.
