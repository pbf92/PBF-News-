TITLE: Nasdaq opens higher in choppy trading; Nvidia earnings in focus
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Nov 17 (Reuters) - The Nasdaq opened higher on Tuesday in volatile trading, as investors looked ahead to Nvidia's earnings for fresh clues on the trajectory of the AI trade.
The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 221.2 points, or 0.43%, at the open to 51386.98. The S&P 500 fell 5.6 points, or 0.08%, at the open to 7411.02, while the Nasdaq Composite rose 18.3 points, or 0.07%, to 24528.057 at the opening bell.
(Reporting by Utkarsh Hathi in Bengaluru; Editing by Pooja Desai)
