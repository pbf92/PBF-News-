TITLE: Stock Market Today: Major Indexes Surge, on Track for Big Weekly Gains as AI Stocks Rally; Anthropic Jumps 8%
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

AI stocks are surging Friday morning at the end of another volatile week of trading.
The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite was up 1.6% recently, while the benchmark S&P 500 and the Dow Jones Industrial Average advanced 1% and 1.2%, respectively. Headed into yesterday's Thanksgiving break, stocks had been under pressure for two straight days, after posting massive gains on Monday following news that Jensen Huang was returning as CEO of AI bellwether Nvidia.
Each of the major indexes come into today's session up roughly 4% for the week. Stocks have bounced back this week from their worst weekly performance since April 2025, when President Trump's announcement of reciprocal tariffs sent financial markets into a tailspin. The Nvidia saga has unnerved investors, while concerns persist about the sustainability of the AI-related gains that have supported the market's record-setting performance in recent years.
Nvidia (NVDA) shares were down 1% in recent trading, extending a rough stretch for the chipmaker at the center of the AI boom. The stock has lost roughly 40% of its value since hitting a record high in early October.
But other chip stocks are rallying this morning. Memory chip maker Micron (MU) and Marvell Technology (MRVL) were both up 6%, while Broadcom (AVGO) and Intel (INTC) rose more than 3%, and Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) tacked on 2%. The iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) was up 3% and the Roundhill Memory ETF (DRAM) gained more than 6%.
Among other AI investor favorites, shares of memory device maker SanDisk (SNDK) were up nearly 14% to pace S&P 500 gainers, while rivals Western Digital (WDC) and Seagate (STX) both climbed 5%. Shares of Nvidia partners Coherent (CHRT) and Lumentum (LITE) were up 11% and 6%, respectively. Shares of Dell Technologies (DELL), another key player in the AI buildout, gained more than 6%.
Shares of Anthropic (ANT) were up 8% recently at just under $90, after closing below their $83 IPO price the previous two sessions. The AI lab carried out the largest IPO ever last week, rising as high as $115 on its first day of trading, but has been stuck below $90 this week.
Other mega-cap tech stocks are also gaining ground this morning. Tesla (TSLA) shares were up 5%, Amazon (AMZN) and Meta Platforms (META) rose 4%, and SpaceX (SPCX) added 3%.
In other markets, gold futures were recently up 2.7% at $3,630 an ounce, bouncing off a 14-month low, while WTI oil futures ticked higher to $83.80 per barrel. Bitcoin was trading at $60,100, down from an overnight high around $62,000. The yield on the 10-year Treasury, which affects interest rates on consumer loans, was at 4.91% recently, up from the previous session's close of 4.86%.
