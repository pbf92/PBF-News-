TITLE: Three Calgary police officers charged with manslaughter
OUTLET: Cochrane Eagle

Three Calgary police officers have been charged with manslaughter following an incident in a hotel lobby which claimed the life of a 42-year-old man.
According to a release from the Alberta Serious Incident Response Team (ASIRT), which investigates allegations of police misconduct throughout the province, there were sufficient grounds to charge Calgary Police Service (CPS) officers Const. Roy Braun, Const. Filip Poskovic and Const. Vincent Washington with manslaughter and assault causing bodily harm.
"The Alberta Serious Incident Response Team (ASIRT) investigation examined the circumstances surrounding the death of a 42-year-old man at a hotel in south Calgary on Sept. 17, 2024," ASIRT explained, while confirming the charges. "Staff at the hotel called police to ask for help removing a man from the lobby. ASIRT’s investigation found reasonable grounds to believe offences were committed. Following a review of the investigation and further review and advice from the Alberta Crown Prosecution Service that charges be laid, ASIRT executive director Matthew Block determined that the involved officers should be charged."
The three officers were arrested on Monday, Nov. 23 and were subsequently released on an undertaking. The case will be put over until Jan. 6, 2027 at the Alberta Court of Justice in Calgary.
