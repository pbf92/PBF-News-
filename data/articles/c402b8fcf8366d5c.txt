Over the weekend, USMNT coach Mauricio Pochettino announced he was leaving to become Manchester United's new manager in England. On Tuesday night, he coached his final game as the Americans played against Haiti at TQL Stadium in Cincinnati, Ohio.
Before the game, Pochettino posted a clip of his farewell video.
"Thank you so much from the bottom of my heart for all the fans," Pochettino said. "For all the people that was supporting us in the last two years. Special. Great memories of the World Cup and everything that we lived together. Always in my heart, always one of you. All the best and I wish everything good for the future."

He concluded his two-and-a-half-year coaching career with the national team on a postive note as the Americans defeated Haiti 2-0. It was also an aggravated 8-0 victory that included a 6-0 win on November 14. That helped the Americans advance to the CONCACAF Nations League.
Afterward, Steve Cherundolo will coach the Americans in the interim as the team begins its search for a new full-time coach this December.
During Pochettino's tenure, he led the USMNT team to three World Cup wins this past summer. Altogether, the Americans lost to Belgium in the Round of 16.
Pochettino, an Argentine native, played from 1989 to 2006. Additionally, Pochettino represented Argentina at the 2002 FIFA World Cup. He then began coaching in 2009, playing in the Premier League.
Now, Pochettino is set to take over one of the world's most storied soccer programs. A program that has collected around 60 trophies throughout all major European and English tournaments.
