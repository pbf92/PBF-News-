GridAI Power Acquires Pronghorn for 66 Million Common-Equivalent Shares
GridAI Power completed its acquisition of Pronghorn Resources through a share exchange, with consideration representing 66 million common-equivalent shares, and assumed Sigma9 Capital's rights and obligations under a Wyoming small modular reactor joint venture. The transaction makes Pronghorn a wholly owned subsidiary and gives GridAI Power the joint venture development rights. At closing, the company also amended Pronghorn's $5 million secured note to require cash repayment only, while its security interest remained in place.
Agreement 1: GridAI Power Acquires Pronghorn for 66 Million Common-Equivalent Shares
- Agreement type: Share exchange agreement
- Counterparty: Pronghorn Resources and its sellers
- Signed / Effective: Nov 12 2026 / Nov 22 2026
- Duration / Termination: Completed at closing
- Reason: Acquire Pronghorn and expand the company's energy business
Agreement 2: GridAI Power Takes Over Wyoming Reactor JV Rights From Sigma9
- Agreement type: Assignment and assumption of joint venture agreement
- Counterparty: Sigma9 Capital
- Signed / Effective: Nov 22 2026 / Nov 22 2026
- Duration / Termination: Ongoing under the joint venture agreement
- Reason: Secure rights to develop small modular reactor facilities in Wyoming
Agreement 3: GridAI Power Converts Pronghorn's $5 Million Secured Note to Cash-Only Repayment
- Agreement type: Amendment to secured convertible note
- Counterparty: Pronghorn Resources
- Signed / Effective: Nov 22 2026 / Nov 22 2026
