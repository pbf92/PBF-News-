TITLE: When Does GTA 6 Come Out? Release Date and Everything You Need to Know
OUTLET: Revista Merca2.0

The wait is almost over! Rockstar Games is set to launch GTA 6, the latest installment in its hit franchise, this week. The release marks the return of one of the most recognizable series in the video game industry. Here’s when you can start playing and everything you need to know before launch.

When Will GTA 6 Be Released?
GTA 6 is scheduled for release on November 19, 2026.
What time can I play GTA 6?
The game launches worldwide at midnight local time in each region.
Where Can I Play GTA 6?
GTA 6 will be available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The game is scheduled to launch on these consoles, allowing players on these platforms to access it when it is officially released. Availability on other systems will depend on future announcements from Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive.
How Much Does the GTA 6 Collector’s Edition Cost?
The GTA 6 Collector’s Edition is priced at $399.99. The package is aimed at fans looking for physical merchandise related to Grand Theft Auto VI, so the price covers only the collection of items included. The video game is sold separately and is not part of the collection.

What Is the GTA Video Game Series Like?
Grand Theft Auto is an open-world video game franchise developed by Rockstar Games. Its titles combine exploration, driving, combat, and side activities across large environments that players can freely explore.
The series is known for featuring stories centered on characters involved in various conflicts within fictional cities inspired by real locations in the United States. Players can travel on foot, drive different vehicles, interact with non-player characters, and complete missions that are part of the main campaign.
GTA also includes numerous activities outside the main missions, allowing players to explore the map and take part in different situations throughout the game world. Driving plays a major role, as do combat and interactions with other characters.
With GTA 6, Rockstar Games is preparing another installment built around this formula, once again set in a vast open world and featuring a story focused on new protagonists.
