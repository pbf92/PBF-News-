OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

US F-35 fighter jet vanishes mysteriously in Middle East as Iran war fears loom
A US Air Force F-35A fighter jet has vanished from flight-tracking radars after declaring an emergency over the United Arab Emirates
A US military aircraft mysteriously disappeared from flight radar systems over the Middle East on Monday, amid growing concerns that Donald Trump could order fresh strikes against Iran.
Flight tracking data reveals the F-35A fighter jet transmitted a squawk 7700 code, which is used to signal an emergency, over the United Arab Emirates, reports the Express.
The US Air Force warplane was last detected flying south of Abu Dhabi, according to a screenshot from aviation tracking website Flightradar24 that was shared across social media.
US Central Command, which oversees American military operations throughout the region, has been approached for comment.
The F-35 is a fifth-generation stealth fighter jet and is widely considered the most advanced of its kind in the world. Figures from 2022 placed the cost of the aircraft at $82.5 million — approximately £70 million.
On Sunday, US President Donald Trump stated that Iran has "launched more drones and missiles in the last week than at any time for over a year".
In a post on his Truth Social platform, he wrote: "Don't worry, they've all been knocked down, and done no damage."
In October and November, reported attacks in the Strait of Hormuz were greater in number than any previous months since the war began, according to data analysed by ABC News.
On Monday, Tehran declared it would use "all available means" to counter the US naval blockade of the country's ports.
