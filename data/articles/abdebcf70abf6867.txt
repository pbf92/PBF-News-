Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory Disclosed Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard
On November 24, 2026, Nasdaq notified the company that its closing bid price had been below $1.00 for 30 consecutive business days, failing Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2). The company has until May 24, 2027, to regain compliance by closing at or above $1.00 for at least 10 consecutive business days; the notice has no immediate effect on trading.
Original SEC Filing: Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory, Inc. [ RMCF ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
