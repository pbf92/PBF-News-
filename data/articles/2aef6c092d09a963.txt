TITLE: Wickenburg Community Hospital & Clinics joins Mayo Clinic Care Network
OUTLET: EurekAlert!

WICKENBURG, Ariz., and ROCHESTER, Minn. — Wickenburg Community Hospital & Clinics (WCH) and Mayo Clinic announced that WCH's hospital and its clinics have joined the Mayo Clinic Care Network.
Mayo Clinic Care Network members receive special access to Mayo Clinic's knowledge, expertise and resources, including digital solutions and capabilities from Mayo Clinic Platform. Members are carefully vetted, independent healthcare organizations.
Healthcare professionals from WCH can combine their understanding of their patients' medical needs with Mayo Clinic expertise, so patients get the care they need, close to home.
"Wickenburg Community Hospital & Clinics' interest and primary objective is to enhance the quality of care available to the local community while maintaining its independence as a rural Critical Access Hospital," says Jackie Lundblad, chief executive officer of WCH.
Through WCH's membership in the Mayo Clinic Care Network, its healthcare professionals have access to Mayo Clinic tools and services, including:
- AskMayoExpert: A point-of-care tool offering concise clinical information on hundreds of medical conditions, including medical protocols, treatment recommendations and medical references.
- eConsults: Connections to Mayo Clinic specialists for second opinions on specific patient cases.
- eBoards: Live, scheduled video conferences that enable medical teams at WCH to review and discuss complex cases with a Mayo Clinic multidisciplinary panel and other physicians in the Mayo Clinic Care Network.
- Health Care Consulting: Access to Mayo Clinic's extensive experience, knowledge and subspecialty expertise to support clinical goals.
"Our organization is excited to become a member of the Mayo Clinic Care Network," says Jodie Allen, chief nursing officer for WCH. "This collaboration reflects the dedication toward excellence that WCH commits to providing to our community. Through the Mayo Clinic Care Network, our local providers have access to Mayo Clinic experts when they feel it would benefit their patients. WCH is excited to begin this journey of excellence to further elevate the exceptional care we provide."
Staff from WCH can use Mayo Clinic educational materials designed for patients and access opportunities for professional development and continuous medical education.
"The Mayo Clinic Care Network brings Mayo Clinic expertise and resources closer to patients by helping healthcare organizations strengthen the care they provide locally," says Sameer Keole, M.D., medical director, Mayo Clinic Platform. "We welcome WCH to the network and look forward to working together to help more patients get the care they need close to home."
Founded in 2011, the Mayo Clinic Care Network reached a 15-year milestone as a global community of more than 50 member organizations, representing 80 hospitals and nearly 20,000 physicians.WCH and other Mayo Clinic Care Network members remain independent.

About Wickenburg Community Hospital and Clinics
Founded in 1926, Wickenburg Community Hospital & Clinics is a nonprofit organization dedicated to providing quality health and wellness services where the patient, family, and community come first. As a federally designated Critical Access Hospital and state-designated Level IV Trauma Center with three Rural Health Clinics, the organization serves residents across approximately 3,300 square miles of rural Arizona. Its services include emergency medicine, critical cardiac care, a Barrow Neurological Institute Telestroke Partnership, surgical and diagnostic services, a licensed 19-bed acute care unit, MRI, rehabilitation, infusion, interventional pain management, and more. To learn more, visit wickhosp.com.
