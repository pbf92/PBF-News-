3 Stocks With Rising Earnings Estimates to Buy Before 2027
Wall Street's earnings outlook is broadening beyond mega-cap technology, creating opportunities in industries ranging from banking and healthcare to electricity generation. With investors becoming more selective about valuations and earnings quality, companies attracting upward analyst estimate revisions could offer compelling investment opportunities heading into 2027.
Here, we have shortlisted three top-ranked stocks, Constellation Energy CEG, Citigroup C and Intuitive Surgical ISRG, with rising earnings estimates across different industries. These stocks are expected to offer investors exposure to improving earnings prospects and potential growth opportunities heading into 2027.
Earnings Momentum Defies Market Uncertainty
The latest earnings season supports this trend. According to our Earnings Preview, 468 S&P 500 companies reported third-quarter 2026 results through Nov. 20, with aggregate earnings increasing 28.9% year over year on 14.3% higher revenues. Moreover, 82.3% of reporting companies beat EPS estimates. Although fourth-quarter earnings growth is projected at 28.3%, recent estimate revisions have moderated, underscoring the importance of individual stock selection.
The macroeconomic backdrop remains supportive but complicated. The U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis’ latest estimate showed third-quarter U.S. GDP growth accelerating to 4.3% annualized, up from the previous 3.4% estimate. However, October PCE inflation climbed to 3.8% year over year, while core PCE inflation reached 3.3%. Markets are increasingly pricing in another Federal Reserve rate hike in December, potentially followed by further tightening in early 2027.
Against this backdrop, Goldman Sachs expects S&P 500 earnings growth to moderate to 11% in 2027 from approximately 30% in 2026, emphasizing earnings delivery over valuation expansion.
Our Picks
Constellation Energy: Rising electricity consumption from AI data centers, industrial electrification and power-intensive infrastructure is strengthening the outlook for electricity producers. Nuclear power operator Constellation Energy is positioned to benefit from growing demand for reliable, carbon-free electricity.
CEG reported third-quarter 2026 adjusted operating earnings of $5.06 per share, up from $3.04 a year earlier, exceeding the Zacks Consensus Estimate by 29.7%. For 2026, earnings estimates have moved upward by 24 cents over the past 30 days. The stock currently carries a Zacks Rank #2 (Buy).
Citigroup: Resilient economic activity, investment banking demand and trading opportunities are supporting diversified financial institutions. Citigroup's restructuring initiatives, expense discipline and capital redeployment toward higher-return businesses provide additional earnings levers.
C reported third-quarter 2026 EPS of $2.85 per share, up from $2.34 a year earlier. The company delivered an EPS surprise of 7.14%, with the consensus EPS estimate being $2.66. For 2026, earnings estimates have moved upward by 22 cents over the past 60 days. The stock also carries a Zacks Rank #2.
Intuitive Surgical: Robotic-assisted surgery remains an important healthcare growth opportunity as hospitals increasingly adopt minimally invasive procedures. Intuitive Surgical benefits from its expanding installed base, recurring instruments and accessories revenues, and growing adoption of its da Vinci and Ion platforms. Its installed base of Ion systems reached 1,152 units by September 2026, up 21% year over year, while third-quarter Ion procedures increased 31%.
ISRG reported third-quarter 2026 earnings of $3.00 per share, up from $2.40 a year earlier, exceeding the Zacks Consensus Estimate by 13.6%. The Zacks Consensus Estimate for 2026 earnings has increased 4.5% over the past 60 days, while next-year estimates have advanced 3.3% over the past 30 days. ISRG carries a Zacks Rank #2.
