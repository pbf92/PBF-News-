TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino: Yoro red card 'completely unfair situation' in Manchester United loss at Liverpool
OUTLET: NBC Sports

Mauricio Pochettino’s debut as Manchester United boss was anything but standard, so how will the new boss see things after a 4-0 loss to Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday?
MORE — Recap, video highlights | Player ratings
Leny Yoro was sent off for a controversial last man back foul in the very early stages of the match, Dominik Szoboszlai smashed in the resulting free kick, and United were down 2-0 before the game’s first quarter-hour was in the books.
The first half was a defensive quagmire as Liverpool ran through and around the Red Devils, and things didn’t improve a ton in the second as the 10-men of Man Utd packed into their own end.
Below are the thoughts of Kobbie Mainoo and his new boss Pochettino after the game.
Mauricio Pochettino reaction — How did Manchester United boss see debut, Yoro red card, 10-man performance?
Not the ideal start to your tenure? “Things like this happen. We need to adapt. It’s true that it’s a difficult decision, one that maybe we do not agree but there’s nothing to do now. We cannot talk too much about the game because 11v10 so early is so difficult. The way we wanted to play, this affects the preparation. This is so painful but we need to keep our heads up and of course translate all that anger and energy to start to go from tomorrow for the next game.”
Had to like the Man Utd fight after the red card? “It was a completely unfair situation but when a situation is like this to turn the negative to the positive, we need to make double effort. More concentration, more focus, care more about everything. In the 10-12 minutes after we concede a goal and had 10 men, the players were committed and tried everything. We made effort, we ran, we tried against a very difficult team.”
On the red card to Yoro: “We are in the era of technology and it’s obvious to the fans who love soccer, you need to see one minute and a half before, Jacquet and Mbeumo. You go to the next minute and see the action from Yoro and Isak. In the box, no? And close to our own box. I think it’s difficult to accept that because in any case it was foul in my view and for it was penalty. Look how it changes everything because of that action, Yoro on Isak. We need to review if everyone does not agree with me. It’s a normal situation when you don’t have control of the football and you retard your run [to get contact] with Yoro. If that was a red card, then Mbeumo was a clear penalty.”
Kobbie Mainoo upset with red card
Thoughts on the game? “This doesn’t define us. The game’s been taken out of our hands early by a red card that I don’t think is a red card. We have to come together because the games are coming thick and fast.”
Have you seen it back or was that just your read from the field? “No chance. The ball’s already with the keeper. They are both running full speed. What can you do?
“Anfield anyway is a tough game and you lose a game. It makes it hard but I can’t fault the boys for their fight. We have to take the pain and move on with it.”
Does the game plan go out the window with such an early red card? “I wouldn’t say it all goes out the window because you can still take things from training into the game but it makes it 10 times harder with one less man.”
How are things at the club right now? “It’s hard. It’s painful but no one’s coming to say us. We need to keep digging, keep working, keep fighting, and it will all come out.”
