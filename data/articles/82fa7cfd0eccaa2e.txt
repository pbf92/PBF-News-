TITLE: GTA VI Is Coming To PC Thanks To Major Leak
OUTLET: RockstarINTEL

GTA VI is getting a PC version very soon thanks to a major leak and AI tools. This is truly an unprecedented situation that continues to move fast.
Last week, notorious cryptobro Cyberleek published files for a build of GTA VI for XBOX dated November 2026. The only thing missing from the game being able to open was the executable file. Cyberleek promised it would be uploaded on Monday even if his memecoin does not reach the target market cap. Cyberleek stuck to his work and did share the .exe file needed for the game to boot. Now the floodgates have opened.
The exe is out. Always free. An early Christmas gift from CL. The torch passes to the open source recomp and emulation projects. Before trusting any file, verify it.
Every release was always free and always will be. If this work matters, support it. Every donation is a vote for more of this. Where the votes go, CL follows.
Cyberleek
Dozens of groups are currently competing with each other to be the first that gets GTA VI playable on PC. There’s even a dedicated website with a progress tracker for each of the groups with a finish line. At the time of writing, one group is nearly 70% of the way there with the game booting, the intro playing and the main menu working. The furthest it gets to before issues is the first mission title card and some lighting rendering alongside audio.
AI tools are being used to decompile the game and get it up and running on PC before Rockstar release their refined and expanded version at some point. To some, the leaked files and getting it working on PC is a middle finger to Rockstar. To others, this could allow for major exploits when the next GTA Online comes out; depending on how it works. The whole situation is giving bad actors a huge leg up.
Many of the groups are using Discord to communicate and work on their progress. However, the company are actively deleting the servers. Despite these hurdles, a fully playable PC version of GTA VI is inevitable. Perhaps it will take a few days or just mere more hours. No doubt Rockstar and Take-Two will try to take these attempts down but it will just be one big game of whack a mole.
