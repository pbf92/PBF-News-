Reliance Global Group Shares Sold to Executives for $514,553; Demands Withdrawn
Reliance Global Group executives purchased GNK Holdings’ 197,905 shares for $514,553, prompting the withdrawal of demands for a special shareholder meeting and records inspection.
Key Highlights:
- Chairman and CEO Ezra Beyman, COO Judah Korman and VP Mordechai Beyman bought the shares for $2.60 each.
- GNK Holdings and Gabrielle Philipson irrevocably withdrew their demands upon the November 20, 2026 closing.
- The company has no obligation to call a special meeting, provide shareholder records or respond further to the demands.
- For 18 months, GNK and its affiliates are barred from specified share acquisitions, proxy activity and board-related actions without company consent.
- The parties agreed not to publicly disparage the company, one another or their affiliates for 12 months after closing.
Original SEC Filing: Reliance Global Group, Inc. [ XGPU ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
