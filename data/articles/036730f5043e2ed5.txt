Vivakor to Acquire Direct Midstream at $40 Million Valuation
Vivakor entered into a Membership Interest Purchase Agreement with Chris Early to acquire all membership interests in Direct Midstream, subject to closing. The $40 million valuation consists of $1 million in Vivakor common stock, $29 million in Series B Preferred Stock, and a commitment to invest $10 million in Direct Midstream after closing. The purchase price may be adjusted based on Direct Midstream's performance during the first year after receiving the cash investment.
Agreement details:
- Agreement type: Membership Interest Purchase Agreement
- Counterparty: Chris Early
- Signed / Effective: Nov 21 2026 / N/A
- Duration / Termination: Through closing; purchase price subject to first-year performance adjustment
- Reason: Acquire Direct Midstream and expand Vivakor's midstream business
Original SEC Filing: Vivakor, Inc. [ VIVK ] - 8-K - Nov. 25, 2026
