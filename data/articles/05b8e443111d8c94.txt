TITLE: Bassey on Manchester United radar ahead of January transfer window
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Tom Masters talks through Calvin Bassey's possible move to Manchester United.
Fulham centre back Calvin Bassey is on the radar of Manchester United this January and has been added to the list of possible targets, according to the Daily Mail.
Manchester United have had a horrific start to the Premier League, winning just one of their first 10 matches and sitting 18th in the table.
That has seen Michael Carrick sacked from his role at Manchester United, with Mauricio Pochettino replacing him at Old Trafford.
United of course, had one of the lowest spends in the summer transfer window, signing only Andrey Santos from Chelsea, Youri Tielemans from Aston Villa and Carlos Baleba from Brighton and Hove Albion.
They made no new signings to their defence, which has comfortably been their weakest asset this season, as shown with disastrous defending in both of their two most recent matches: a 2-0 defeat to Chelsea and a 4-4 draw with Aston Villa.
Pochettino, you feel, is likely to want to make changes to the Manchester United back line, which of course primarily revolves around signing at least one centre-back, if not two.
Harry Maguire has struggled, as have Ayden Heaven, Lisandro Martinez and even Lenny Yoro.
Of course, it is possible it came down to the regime of Carrick and the tactics that were in play.
But there also does seem to be a general consensus that the Manchester United defence is not good enough compared to their attack, their midfield and their goals going forward.
On the list is Fulham defender Calvin Bassey, who of course has become an integral part of the Cottagers' defence.
Bassey has in fact become effectively the unofficial captain this season, with Joachim Andersen relegated to the bench in favour of David Affengruber and Tom Cairney rarely getting onto the pitch as the actual club captain.
His importance to Fulham is more or less unparalleled in the starting XI, and he would be a major loss should Manchester United decide to sign him.
The positive from a Fulham perspective is of course that Manchester United have other defenders on their list, not least Brentford defender Nathan Collins, as it would appear they're trying to stick to what was successful in their attacking approach in the summer before last when they signed proven Premier League talents.
That summer, they sign Matheus Cunha from Wolves and Bryan Mbeumo from Brentford, both of whom have become key members of the Manchester United team, and played a big role in securing third spot in the table for the Red Devils last season.
Arbeloa and Fulham will hope that Bassey does not leave the club to join Manchester United or should he do so it should at least be for a handsome fee.
