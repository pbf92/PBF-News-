Stocks Slump as Tech Selloff Deepens
The S&P 500 Index SPY is down by -2.88% today, the Dow Jones Industrial Average DIA is down -2.04%, and the Nasdaq 100 Index QQQ is down -4.29%. December E-mini S&P futures (ESZ26) are down -2.26%, and December E-mini Nasdaq futures (NQZ26) are down -3.25%.
US stock indexes are falling sharply today amid a global selloff in technology stocks. Stocks are continuing to unwind Monday’s surge after Nvidia’s CEO Huang was reinstated. Markets remain concerned about returns on vast AI buildout spending and the prospect of tighter regulations and higher borrowing costs. Chipmakers, AI-infrastructure stocks, and the Magnificent Seven technology stocks are all under pressure today, leading the broader market sharply lower.

Market sentiment has also weakened after the Wall Street Journal reported on Monday that the US is preparing to raise tariffs on China and other countries that the Trump administration argues are harming American companies by supporting their own domestic industrial production. US Trade Representative Greer is expected to unveil the results of a tariff investigation into industrial subsidies by China, the European Union, and 14 other countries. Greer said those subsidies and other government support have created excess industrial capacity in the targeted economies, allowing their firms to flood global markets with cheaper goods and undermine US companies.
Stock prices are retreating today, despite mostly better-than-expected US economic news that showed weekly jobless claims falling to a 4-month low and Q3 GDP being revised upward to show the economy expanding at the fastest pace in three years.
The selloff in global equity markets has spurred safe-haven buying of government bonds and knocked bond yields lower. The 10-year T-note yield, and the 10-year UK Gilt yield fell to 2-week lows today, and the 10-year German Bund yield and Japan 10-year JGB bond yield fell to 2.5-week lows.
US MBA mortgage applications rose +6.1% in the week ended November 20, with the purchase mortgage sub-index up +10.2% and the refinancing mortgage sub-index up +0.5%. The average 30-year fixed-rate mortgage rose +3 bp to 7.38% from 7.35% in the prior week.
US weekly initial unemployment claims fell -13,000 to a 4-month low of 189,000, showing a stronger labor market than expectations of 200,000.
US Oct personal spending rose +0.3% m/m, weaker than expectations of +0.4% m/m. Oct personal income rose +0.7% m/m, stronger than expectations of +04% m/m.
The US Oct core PCE price index, the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, rose +3.3% y/y, stronger than expectations of +3.2% y/y and the fastest pace of increase in nearly three years.
US Q3 GDP was revised upward to +4.3% (q/q annualized) from +3.4%, stronger than expectations of +3.5% and the fastest pace of growth in three years.
US Oct capital goods new orders nondefense ex-aircraft and parts rose +1.2% m/m, stronger than expectations of +0.7% m/m.
The markets are currently pricing in a 97% chance of a +25 bp rate hike at the next FOMC meeting on December 8-9. After that rate hike, the markets are pricing in at least one more +25 bp Fed rate hike in 2027.
Jan WTI crude oil prices (CLF27) are climbing today amid concerns about supply disruptions. Saudi Arabia said today it intercepted four drones fired by Houthi rebels at Ras Tanura, where the kingdom has a major oil refinery and Persian Gulf export facilities. Also, Iran has stepped up its attacks on shipping traveling through the strait. The markets remain on guard for a full resumption of US attacks after Iran over the weekend attacked an industrial facility in Bahrain, which hosts a major US military installation. In addition, Ukraine continues to ramp up attacks on Russian energy infrastructure and has attacked 15 Russian oil refineries since the start of November that have a combined capacity of more than 3.6 million bpd, over half of Russia's total capacity, according to Bloomberg calculations.
Stocks are supported by solid Q3 quarterly earnings. According to data compiled by Bloomberg Intelligence, Q3 S&P 500 earnings rose by +31% from a year earlier, much better than a July forecast of +22%. As of today, 477 of the S&P 500 companies have reported results, with 85% beating estimates.
Overseas stock markets are lower today. The Euro Stoxx 50 is down -0.74%. China’s Shanghai Composite fell to a 16.5-month low and closed down -2.03%. Japan's Nikkei Stock Index closed down -2.78%.
Interest Rates
December 10-year T-notes (ZNZ6) are up +15 ticks today, and the 10-year T-note yield is down -10.7 bp to 4.822%. Dec T-notes rallied to a 2-week high today, and the 10-year T-note yield fell to a 2-week low of 4.820%. Today’s stock weakness boosted safe-haven demand for T-notes. Also, lower inflation expectations are bullish for T-notes after the 10-year breakeven inflation rate fell to a 19.5-month low of 2.091% today.
European bond yields are moving lower today. The 10-year German bund yield fell to a 2.5-week low of 3.207% and is down -3.9 bp to 3.223%. The 10-year UK gilt yield slid to a 2-week low of 5.110% and is down -5.4 bp to 5.133%.
German Q3 GDP was revised downward to +0.1% q/q and +1.2% y/y from the previously reported +0.2% q/q and +1.3% y/y.
ECB Governing Council member Martin Kocher said, "Given significant upside risks to the inflation outlook, we should not fall behind the curve," suggesting he would back additional ECB interest rate hikes. He added that "it seems at the moment likely that the ECB's inflation projections in December will need to be revised upwards for 2027, given heightened forward energy prices."
The markets are pricing in a 94% chance of a +25 bp rate hike at the ECB’s next policy meeting on December 17.
US Stock Movers
Chipmakers and AI-infrastructure stocks are selling off today to weigh on the overall market. The iShares Semiconductor ETF SOXX is down more than -6%. Lam Research LRCX and Micron Technology MU are down more than -8%, and Applied Materials , SanDisk SNDK, and Advanced Micro Devices AMD are down more than -7%. Also, ARM Holdings Plc ARM, Marvell Technology MRVL, and Intel INTC are down more than -6%, and ASML Holding NV ASML, KLA Corp KLAC, Seagate Technology Holdings Plc STX, and Western Digital WDC are down more than -5%.
The Magnificent Seven technology stocks are retreating today, weighing on the broader market. Nvidia NVDA is down more than -8% to lead losers in the Dow Jones Industrials, and Alphabet GOOGL and Tesla TSLA are down more than -5%. Also, Amazon.com AMZN is down more than -4%, and Meta Platforms META and Microsoft MSFT are down more than -2%. In addition, Apple AAPL is down more than -1%.
Software stocks are sliding today after earnings results from Autodesk and Workday failed to impress. Datadog DDOG is down more than -6% and Palantir Technologies PLTR is down more than -5%. Also, Autodesk ADSK is down more than -4%, and Atlassian Corp TEAM, ServiceNow NOW, and Workday WDAY are down more than -3%. In addition, Intuit INTU and Salesforce CRM are down more than -2%.
Cloud infrastructure stocks are sinking today amid weakness in AI stocks. Nebius Group NV NBIS is down more than -8% to lead losers in the Nasdaq 100, and CoreWeave CRWV and Lumentum Holdings LITE are down more than -7%. Also, Applied Digital APLD and Oracle ORCL are down more than -6%, and Arista Networks ANET is down more than -5%.
Crypto stocks are retreating today, with Bitcoin IREN is down more than -11%, and BitMine Immersion Technologies BMNR and Bullish BLSH are down more than -8%. Also, Circle Internet Group CRCL, Strategy MSTR, and Riot Platforms RIOT are down more than -7%, and Mara Holdings MARA, Galaxy Digital Holdings GLXY, and Coinbase Global COIN are down more than -6%. In addition, Robinhood Markets HOOD is down more than -5%.
Defensive food producers are moving higher today amid the selloff in the broader market. Archer Daniels Midland ADM and Hershey Co HSY are up more than +2%, and General Mills GIS, Kraft Heinz Co KHC, and McCormick & Co MKC are up more than +1%.
Urban Outfitters URBN is down more than -10% after reporting Q3 comparable retail segment sales rose +4.4%, weaker than the consensus of +5.6%.
HP Inc HPQ is up more than +10% to lead gainers in the S&P 500 after reporting Q4 adjusted EPS of 83 cents, better than the consensus of 74 cents, and forecasting 2027 adjusted EPS of $3.15 to $3.45, the midpoint above the consensus of $3.18.
