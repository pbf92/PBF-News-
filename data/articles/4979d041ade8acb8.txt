News Corp Reports $588.5 Million Used Under $1 Billion Buyback Program
Less than 1 min read
News Corp reported approximately $588.5 million in repurchases to date under its authorized $1 billion share buyback program.
Key Highlights:
- The 2025 program authorizes up to $1 billion in repurchases of Nasdaq-listed Class A and Class B common stock.
- On November 24, News Corp repurchased 31,260 Class A shares for about $811,553 and 15,623 Class B shares for about $447,402.
- The program had repurchased approximately $588.5 million of Class A and Class B shares based on total consideration paid.
- Repurchases may occur in the open market or otherwise, subject to market conditions, share price and other factors; no ASX-listed CDIs will be repurchased.
- News Corp states the program is intended to enhance shareholder value; the purchase price is not predetermined.
Original SEC Filing: NEWS CORP [ NWSA ] - 8-K - Nov. 24, 2026
