Everything you need to know about Mauricio Pochettino's disastrous first match in charge of Manchester United
- Mauricio Pochettino endured a brutal Manchester United debut as Liverpool fans relentlessly taunted him during the heavy defeat, singing "You're getting sacked in the morning". The hostile chants compounded a miserable outing as United's defensive frailties were exposed on the pitch in the humiliating 4-0 loss.
- Liverpool's fourth goal meant Manchester United have now conceded 34 times across all competitions this season. This unwanted milestone leaves them with the worst defensive record in Europe's major leagues, one worse off than Crystal Palace and Union Berlin.
- Gary Neville expressed disbelief upon learning that United statistically boast the worst defence in Europe. He said: "That's absolutely incredible. 33 goals conceded, the worst defence in Europe. It's actually difficult to do that with the money spent on this squad."
- United's difficult afternoon was further marred by Leny Yoro's red card and growing concerns surrounding Matthijs de Ligt. Both Neville and Roy Keane agreed that the defender's struggles highlighted major issues at the back.
- Keane dismissed debates over using a back four or back five, insisting formations do not matter when players fail to do the basics. Even accounting for players returning from injury, he was amazed to see such fundamental errors at this level.
- The former United captain criticised the backline for failing to read the flight of the ball and neglecting to cover for one another. He echoed Neville's view that defenders must always anticipate the worst-case scenario rather than getting caught out.
- Pochettino was furious at the red card in the seventh minute, which put United on the back foot right away. Dominik Szoboszlai scored from the resulting free-kick before goals from Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz recorded an embarrassing loss for United.
