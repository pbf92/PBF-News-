GTA 6: A guide to every mission and chapter
With five chapters, 91 story missions and over 65 hours of gameplay, GTA 6 is a huge adventure. Here's every mission in the game, organised by chapter, to help you navigate the story.
GTA 6 is finally here, and there's a whole lot of Leonida to explore. Rockstar Games has delivered its biggest and most ambitious open-world adventure yet, packed with new gameplay features and a huge world to discover.
Below, you'll find the complete GTA 6 mission list, a breakdown of each chapter and an estimate of how long it'll take to finish the story.
- Spoiler warning: Even the mission names give away quite a bit about the plot. We’ve obscured major plot twists, but minor spoilers are unavoidable.
FAQs: Missions and chapters in GTA 6
01
GTA 6 playtime: How long does the game last?
You should allow at least 65 hours for the main story alone. Unsurprisingly, GTA 6 is significantly more extensive than its direct predecessor from 2013. In terms of scope, the open-world game even eclipses the Western adventure Red Dead Redemption 2.
But the main missions are, of course, only one side of the coin. A game like GTA naturally thrives on its side activities, and these are not only much more varied here, but also more extensive than before. If you include side missions and activities, you’ll quickly reach 90 to 100 hours. And if you really want to complete everything to 100 per cent, you’ll need to set aside over 200 hours.
The reason for this isn’t just the sheer number of story missions. Between chapters, the game world also changes noticeably: new areas, new contacts and new possibilities open up. So it’s well worth taking a spin around Leonida after each chapter change before you carry on with the story. 
The scope of GTA 6:
- Story chapters: 5
- Story missions: 91
- Main story: around 65 to 70 hours
- Including side content: approx. 90 to 100 hours
- 100 per cent completion: over 200 hours

Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise
At the start of GTA 6, the open-world game introduces the two protagonists, Lucia and Jason, separately before their paths cross. An interesting point: you can only switch freely between the two characters as the game progresses.
At the start of the game, Lucia is still in prison, so the opening is mainly focused on Jason. Small smuggling jobs, odd jobs, a few shady acquaintances: the first chapter deliberately starts off at a leisurely pace, allowing you to settle into Leonida at your own pace. At the same time, Lucia’s storyline unfolds, building up to her release from prison. With 22 missions, the opening chapter is already packed with content.

Chapter 2: Paradise Lost
Now Lucia takes the reins. The second chapter shifts the action to Port Gellhorn, where (after being released from prison on an electronic tag) she first has to find her feet. The missions revolve around everyday life, new friendships and petty crimes. With 11 missions, it’s the second-shortest chapter; towards the end, Jason rejoins her.

Chapter 3: Tits, Ass, Drug Money
Welcome to Vice City. Whilst the first two chapters focused primarily on the surrounding areas on the Leonida map, we now head into the big city, which – unsurprisingly – is naturally far livelier and more detailed than in the 2002 version of GTA Vice City.
From here on, things get wild: Jason and Lucia are out and about together in the metropolis, making new contacts and slipping ever deeper into the local criminal underworld. Drug deals, car thefts, bank robberies and, ultimately, the big cartel heist. With 26 missions, the third chapter is the most extensive in the entire game.

The finale of GTA 6: Chapter 5 – Top of the World, Ma
Anyone who’s reached GTA 6 Chapter 5 is slowly but surely heading towards the grand finale. And, in typical Rockstar fashion, it’s a real cracker. The finale is short, but incredibly intense. In just seven missions, Jason and Lucia set off together one last time: stealing cars, retrieving deliveries, hatching new plans. Naturally, we won’t reveal what happens in the final mission.
