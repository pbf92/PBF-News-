TITLE: IPO News - US IPO Weekly Recap: Anthropic seesaws in record debut
OUTLET: Renaissance Capital

One IPO debuted this week, and one IPO submitted initial filings. 
AI model developer Anthropic PBC (ANT) priced its upsized IPO above the range to raise $81.3 billion at a $1.4 trillion market cap. Anthropic PBC develops Claude, a family of frontier AI models with a focus on enterprise deployment and model safety. Its models are offered through a developer platform and apps such as Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and Claude.ai. Most of its revenue is consumption-based usage, with subscriptions adding the rest. As of September 30, 2026, more than 11,900 customers generated at least $100,000 in trailing-12-month revenue, including every Fortune 10 company and more than half of the Fortune 500. Anthropic’s IPO was the largest-ever in history, surpassing SpaceX (SPCX) by over $6 billion. Anthropic also raised $26 billion in a concurrent private placement at the offer price. 
Anthropic popped 17% in its first day of trading, but subsequently gave up most of those gains. It finished the week up 7%.
