Playboy to exit Russell 3000 in December reconstitution
FTSE Russell has released a preliminary list of additions and deletions to its Russell 3000 index. The newly reconstituted indexes take effect after the US markets close on December 11.
The Russell 3000 Index tracks the performance of the 3,000 largest U.S. companies and is designed to represent roughly 98% of the investable U.S. equity market.
