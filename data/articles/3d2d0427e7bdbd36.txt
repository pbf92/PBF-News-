The report arrives at a sensitive moment. Futures priced a 25 bp Fed hike at about 99% on 27 November (MarketScreener/Reuters). The FOMC blackout period starts on Saturday 28 November, so Fed officials stop speaking in public until after the meeting.
Three things usually draw the most attention on the day:
- The headline payrolls number against the 90K consensus.
- The revisions to September (+60K) and October (+165K).
- Average hourly earnings, together with the unemployment rate.

What happened in October
October was a large upside surprise. Payrolls rose by 165K against a 65K forecast (BLS). Private payrolls rose by 159K against 64K expected, according to Fox Business.

What Fed officials and economists have said
The blackout means the last public comments came before 28 November. Some views from November:
- Governor Waller called for a 50 bp move in December, as reported by Stocktwits on 10 November.
- Dallas Fed President Logan saw at least two more 25 bp hikes, according to the same report.

Can the jobs report change the December decision?
With the hike almost fully priced, one report may not change the December outcome. It could still shape the tone of the statement and the dot plot. Scenario 1: stronger than forecast
A print well above 90K would follow October's beat. It could confirm the hike that markets already price. Some traders could then look again at a 50 bp move or at more hikes in 2027. Beats have often lifted the dollar and Treasury yields, according to Forexuro.
The downside case matters too. A strong headline with weak wages could send mixed signals. Downward revisions to September and October could also dampen any first reaction.
Scenario 2: close to the consensus
A result near 90K would fit the "slower but far from weak" picture from Continuum Economics. The December hike would likely stay priced. Attention could move on to Warsh's guidance and the new projections on 9 December.
A quiet headline can still hide surprises. A jump in unemployment to 4.2% or a wage miss could move markets either way.
Scenario 3: weaker than forecast
A print below about 50K would point to cooling hiring. Core PCE at 3.3% suggests the December hike could still go ahead. Pricing for further hikes in 2027 could fall, which may weigh on the dollar.
There is a downside for stocks as well. Lower yields could help, though a sharp miss could raise growth worries. XTB noted on 27 November that markets already priced fewer hikes than two weeks earlier.

How to prepare for NFP: what traders often watch
Experienced traders often follow a routine around a big release. Common steps include:
- They check the release time in their own time zone.
- They compare the headline with the consensus range of 82K to 95K.
- They read the revisions to September and October.
- They look at wages and the unemployment rate after the headline.
- They watch how the dollar and Treasury yields react in the first minutes.
The Admirals guide to NFP trading explains how the release works in more depth.
NFP trading risk management
Volatility around 08:30 ET can be high. Spreads can widen, and prices can gap past stop orders. Some traders manage risk by reducing position size or by waiting for the first reaction to settle. Leverage magnifies losses as well as gains.
