Ezra J. Cattan Discloses Investment in NextNRG with 25% Stake
Ezra J. Cattan reported a 25% stake in NextNRG, Inc., based on shares he can vote or direct. His Schedule 13D, filed November 18, 2026, lists purchases for accounts connected to him and Saul Cattan on October 28 and November 10. The Cattans say they bought the shares as an investment because they believe NextNRG represents an attractive opportunity.
Investor Intent
The Cattans say they have no current plan or proposal for changing NextNRG. They may discuss the company’s business and strategic direction with its representatives and other shareholders, and they say they will continue to review their investment. They could later buy or sell shares or develop other plans, but the filing names no specific next step.
Investor's Background
Ezra J. Cattan works as an entrepreneur and investor, while Saul Cattan works as an entrepreneur. Both list New York business addresses and filed this report together. They say they used personal funds to buy NextNRG shares, which they view as an attractive investment opportunity.
Original SEC Filing: NEXTNRG, INC. [ NXXT ] - SCHEDULE 13D - Nov. 18, 2026
