TITLE: How to watch Manchester United vs Brentford live: Stream, TV, team news, prediction
OUTLET: NBC Sports

Manchester United host Brentford at Old Trafford as Mauricio Pochettino is desperate for a first win as United’s new boss.
After losing 4-0 at Liverpool last Sunday, United drew 2-2 at Sporting Lisbon on Wednesday in a wild Champions League clash as refereeing decisions against them were once again questionable. Pochettino says he is pleased with the speedy progress his new team are already making but he needs a win to try and drag them out of the relegation zone (still weird to type that) as soon as possible.
Brentford continue to soar under Keith Andrews and battered Everton 4-0 at home on Monday to move up to sixth in the table heading into Matchweek 12. After narrowly missing out on European qualification for the first time in their history last season, it seems like the hunger and belief that they can go one step further this campaign is well and truly there.

Manchester United team news
There are still a few injuries for Pochettino to deal with as Manuel Ugarte, Benjamin Sesko, Marcus Rashford and Carlos Baleba remain out. But Luke Shaw is back in the squad and Leny Yoro is available for this game after having his red card against Liverpool rescinded after United’s appeal.
Brentford team news
The Bees continue to buzz around and cause absolute havoc. Aaron Hickey, Nathan Collins, Rico Henry and Josh Dasilva remain out injured, while Igor Thiago is suspended and veteran Callum Wilson has led the line superbly in his absence.
Manchester United vs Brentford prediction
Given the defensive mistakes United keep making, and Brentford’s penchant for pressing high and forcing plenty of said mistakes, this is going to be chaos. Manchester United 3-2 Brentford.
