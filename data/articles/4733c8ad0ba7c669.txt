TITLE: Florida pastor convicted of role in Haitian president's assassination
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

Florida-based pastor Christian Emmanuel Sanon has been found guilty in a Florida court of his role in the 2021 assassination of Haitian President Jovenal Moïse.
The 67-year-old dual Haitian/US national has been found guilty on all eight charges laid against him, including conspiracy to kidnap or kill a person outside the United States.
He could face up to life in prison.
Records hosted by the Florida Baptist Historical Society indicate he was appointed Director of the Haitian Church Planting Department for the Florida Baptist Convention in 1997.
His roles included wording directly with churches to start new Haitian congregations and creating materials for Haitian churches.
Baptist Press has reported he last worked with the convention in 2001.
Premier Christian News has contacted the Florida Baptist Convention for comment.
Haiti has been thrown into turmoil since the assassination, with several gangs seizing large areas of the capital, Port-au-Prince.
Mr Moïse’s widow, Martine Moïse, has registered for Haiti’s presidential election next year.
The Caribbean nation has been without a President since 2021.
Posting on social media, and translated into English, Ms Moïse described the guilty verdict as a gift, saying, “…We will not stop saying thank you as we continue working for liberation. The Architect and our ancestors are leading the way to take us from desolation to a better life.”
