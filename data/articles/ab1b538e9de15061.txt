First US Bancshares, Inc. (FUSB) Extends Repurchase Program Through 2027
First US Bancshares extended its share repurchase program expiration from December 31, 2026, to December 31, 2027. The company has repurchased 2,122,972 shares to date, with 1,119,813 shares remaining available.

This extends a longstanding repurchase program originally approved in 2006, rather than introducing a new authorization. Actual purchases and their pace would need to be disclosed to establish the program's near-term impact.
