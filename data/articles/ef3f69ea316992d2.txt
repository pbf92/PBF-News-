SOBR Safe Received Notice of Delisting
On November 23, 2026, Nasdaq announced it would delist SOBR Safe's common stock and file a Form 25 with the SEC. Trading had been suspended on Nasdaq on September 16, 2026, after the company failed to meet the $1.00 minimum bid price requirement under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2); the company had also been ineligible for a 180-day compliance period because its reverse splits totaled 1-for-1,100 over two years.
Original SEC Filing: SOBR Safe, Inc. [ SOBR ] - 8-K - Nov. 24, 2026
