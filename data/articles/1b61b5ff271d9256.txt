TITLE: Rockstar Explains Why GTA VI Left Some Classic Features Behind
OUTLET: RockstarINTEL

SPOILER ALERT: This article discusses a major early-game twist and the events of GTA VI’s Chapter 1 finale. If you haven’t completed the first chapter yet, we recommend coming back to this article later!
Rockstar Games has made several changes to the Grand Theft Auto formula in GTA VI. While some players have been asking about missing features from previous games, the studio has now explained why certain decisions were made.
In a new interview with Video Games Chronicle, Rockstar North co-head and GTA VI producer Rob Nelson discussed some of the game’s biggest design choices, including cut content, the new Criminal Profile system, and the freedom players have during missions.
Why Some Features Didn’t Make It Into GTA VI
One of the biggest changes is the new Criminal Profile system. Some players have welcomed it as a new role-playing element, while others would rather cause chaos in Vice City without worrying about the consequences. Nelson said Rockstar expected these different reactions and explained that the system was designed to give players more choices rather than completely change how GTA works.
He also explained why some classic weapons are missing. For example, chainsaws can be found in the game world, but players cannot pick them up. According to Nelson, the weapon did not really fit Jason and Lucia’s characters or the tone Rockstar wanted to create for GTA VI.
Other familiar activities were also considered during development. Nelson mentioned golf, tennis, volleyball, and darts, explaining that the team would have liked to make more activities fully playable if they had unlimited time. However, Rockstar had to decide which features were important enough to include.
The same thinking applied to a possible parody of Disney World. Nelson confirmed that Rockstar considered the idea but decided against creating a fully functioning theme park. The team did not have a strong enough narrative reason to send Jason and Lucia there, and building such a location would have required a lot of work.
Rockstar Wanted More Freedom in Missions
Nelson also discussed Rockstar’s approach to mission design. The studio wanted to reduce unnecessary fail conditions and give players more freedom to complete objectives in different ways. He explained that allowing missions to unfold differently creates more work for the developers, but the team believes that freedom is worth the effort.
The interview offers an interesting look at the decisions behind GTA VI and shows how Rockstar wanted to move the series forward without simply repeating everything from previous games.
