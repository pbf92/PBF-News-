TITLE: Big Tech companies are the butt of the joke in 'GTA 6'
OUTLET: Business Insider

The "Grand Theft Auto" franchise has long skewered American life with a parade of double entendres.
Take "GTA 5" as an example. Rockstar Games built Los Santos as a dystopic Los Angeles look-alike, where the high-end Westwood Boulevard becomes "Morningwood Boulevard." Players strolling past upscale retailers can overhear NPCs bragging about their wealth and cosmetic procedures.
"GTA 6" is no different — but this time, Silicon Valley is the butt of the joke.
The new game, which launched on Thursday, is full of techie doppelgängers: delivery services, ride-hailing apps, and social platforms that look familiar, but now have overtly sexual names.
Here are a few:
RydeMe, an Uber/Lyft counterpart
SnapMatic, an Instagram-style social media app (also in GTA 5)
Sahara, an Amazon look-alike
WhatUp, a WhatsApp-like messenger
BuckMe, a CashApp-ish money transfer app
WAiNK, a (vaguely) Carvana-ish vehicle scanner
iFruit, an Apple iPhone-like smartphone (also in GTA 5)
