Free GTA 6 Codes on Kick and Twitch, How to Win a Copy
Kick and Twitch are both handing out free Grand Theft Auto 6 codes around the game’s 19 November launch, so viewers have two separate chances to get a copy without paying for it. Kick’s Sunset Reward Case has 10,000 GTA 6 Ultimate Edition codes up for grabs, and Twitch has promised daily code drops from launch until the end of November.
GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on 19 November, following Jason and Lucia through Vice City and the wider state of Leonida. Kick’s campaign began on 12 November and runs until 7 December. It covers both consoles, and you don’t need to buy anything or subscribe to enter. Twitch’s drops start at launch, but Twitch hasn’t said how many codes it has, which consoles they’re for, or what viewers need to do to get one.
How to Enter Kick’s Sunset Reward Case
Kick’s giveaway is based on watch time, and the stream you watch doesn’t have to be GTA content.
who wants a free copy of GTA VI? https://t.co/K1DZm6UkbS
— KICK (@kick) November 12, 2026
To earn a case:
- Sign in to Kick on the website or open the Kick app.
- Watch any livestream for 180 minutes.
- Open the Sunset Reward Case next to your avatar.
A case gives you a chance at a code, not a guaranteed one. A new case unlocks every day, and new Kick users get a free entry case once they finish onboarding. Kick’s official terms say entry requires no purchase, subscription, deposit, wager, or betting.
Cases unlock once a day rather than for every three hours watched, so watching past 180 minutes on the same day likely won’t earn you a second one. Across the full 12 November to 7 December window, that puts the limit at 26 earned cases per account, and it takes 78 hours of viewing to collect them all. New users get their onboarding case on top of that.
Kick’s GTA 6 Code Platforms, Regions, and Delivery
The 10,000 codes are digital copies of GTA 6 Ultimate Edition, split between PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Xbox codes work worldwide, subject to Microsoft’s platform restrictions. PS5 codes only work in supported PlayStation Network regions. Kick won’t issue any codes before GTA 6 launches on 19 November, so if you win from a case you open this week, you won’t get the code until the game is out.
New Zealand is a supported PlayStation Network region, so under the published terms, viewers here can win either the PS5 or the Xbox version.
Kick says it is running the promotion independently and that it isn’t affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Kick teased the campaign on X on 12 November with a short post asking who wanted a free copy of GTA VI, and the post has passed 3.9 million views. The giveaway is getting an offline push too: a truck-sized version of Kick’s loot case is driving around Los Angeles with a Lucia cosplayer at the wheel and a QR code that links to the campaign. Kick says the truck will travel through wider California while the promotion runs.
Twitch’s Daily GTA 6 Code Drops
Twitch announced its plans at the TwitchCon San Diego 2026 opening ceremony on 13 November. Its official blog post promises “surprise daily drops of GTA 6 game codes” from launch until the end of November, and tells viewers to tune in. That covers 12 days, from 19 November to 30 November.
And finally, we will be doing daily drops of GTA 6 game codes from launch through the end of November to show appreciation, so be sure to tune in.
— Twitch (@Twitch) November 13, 2026
That is all Twitch has shared so far. It hasn’t announced a total number of codes, a watch-time requirement, a list of participating channels, or which consoles the codes are for. Until those terms are published, Kick’s is the only one of the two giveaways with a known prize pool and a clear way to enter. If Twitch publishes its rules before launch, that gap could close quickly.
Twitch’s Other GTA 6 Rewards, From Emotes to Heist Trains
The code drops are part of a wider set of GTA 6 rewards on Twitch, all running from launch until 30 November:
- Sub Emotes and Badges: GTA-themed emotes and badges earned through Subs and Gift Subs.
- Evolving badge: a badge that levels up as you watch GTA streams. Collect every reward and fully evolve the badge to unlock a Golden Iguana Chat badge.
- Heist Trains: participating GTA 6 streams get a GTA-themed version of Hype Trains, the chat-wide event that starts when viewers subscribe or cheer in quick succession. These HEIST trains drop six exclusive emotes designed by streamer LumpyTouch.
- Stream Recaps: Twitch will occasionally drop Gift Subs in the channels of streamers who post Recaps of their GTA 6 streams, and will feature selected Recaps from around the world on the Twitch homepage.
