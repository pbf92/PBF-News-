Chevron nears $5B deal for Blackstone-backed Beacon Offshore - FT
Chevron CVX is closing in on a deal to buy a majority stake in oil producer Beacon Offshore, valuing the business at ~$5B, perhaps as soon as this week, as it seeks to expand its presence in the U.S. Gulf, the Financial Times reported Monday.
Chevron CVX reportedly has moved ahead of rival bidders in its pursuit of the Blackstone-backed BX company, which controls 70 deepwater leases across ~400K gross acres in the U.S. Gulf.
Chevron CVX has been expanding production in the Gulf, with start-ups at the Anchor, Whale, and Ballymore projects expected to boost output by 50% to 300K boe/day by the end of this year.
