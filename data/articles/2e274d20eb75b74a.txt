TITLE: Manchester United CEO Omar Berrada names 'priority' after Mauricio Pochettino appointment
OUTLET: Yahoo

Manchester United chief executive officer Omar Berrada has confirmed the club's commitment to supporting newly appointed head coach Mauricio Pochettino and his coaching staff following his arrival at Old Trafford.
Pochettino officially began his tenure at United on Wednesday, with Berrada addressing the appointment in an official statement released alongside the club's latest financial results covering the period from July to September this year.
The headline numbers revealed a revenue increase to £146.1 million ($191.2 million) over those three months, though Berrada once again emphasized the club's commitment to growing "sustainably", as per the Manchester Evening News.
"Our revenue remains strong and on track, while we continue to grow sustainably," he said. "We are pleased to welcome Mauricio Pochettino and his staff to the club and our priority is to support them as we look continuously to improve our performances on the pitch.
"We are pleased with the progress Eva Olid has made with our women's team and how well the team has adapted to her approach."
The financial results only cover the period through the end of September, meaning they do not account for the dismissal of Michael Carrick or the subsequent appointment of Pochettino and his backroom team.
Compensation figures relating to Carrick's departure and the costs associated with releasing Pochettino and his staff from their U.S. Soccer contracts will be disclosed in the club's next financial report.
