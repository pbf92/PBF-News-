TITLE: Sporting CP vs Manchester United player ratings — Tielemans sees red as Red Devils play out draw in Champions League
OUTLET: Sportstar

Mauricio Pochettino’s wait for a first win as Manchester United manager went on after it was held to a 2-2 draw by Sporting CP on Wedndesday.
The Argentine manager was on course to take all three points before a goalkeeping error by Senne Lammens allowed Luis Suarez to equalise in the 80th minute.
