TITLE: Pochettino criticises Premier League officiating after Yoro red card overturned
OUTLET: The Guardian football

Mauricio Pochettino has criticised officiating in English football, saying: “We have an unbelievable car, but we need to be in the level to drive [it],” after Leny Yoro’s Anfield red card was overturned on Tuesday.
Despite the Football Association ruling the Frenchman’s early sending-off in Sunday’s 4-0 defeat at Liverpool was “wrongful dismissal”, Manchester United remain furious at the decision, and at Pro Ref, the elite officials’ organisation.
Dominik Szoboszlai scored the subsequent free-kick which put Liverpool on the road to victory, and United were dismayed by Howard Webb, the Pro Ref chief, saying he could understand why Sam Barrott gave the red card and the video assistant referees backed the decision.
United remain insistent that Alexander Isak would not have reached the ball before their goalkeeper, Senne Lammens, as he was brought down by Yoro and they argue this can be objectively proved. The club also believe there was an error of process as the recording of the video officials shows a quick look at the incident with no due consideration and they have confirmed they will raise this with the Premier League and Pro Ref.
When asked about this, Pochettino also claimed Szoboszlai’s strike should not have stood as Bradley Barcola touched United’s wall. The head coach said: “We didn’t pay attention, but Bruno [Fernandes] was aware: no one talked about that the goal wasn’t legal. Should disallow the goal because Barcola was touching our wall, and maybe in the last two years maybe they changed the rule. Very disappointed because I think this type of thing never can happen.
“My question always was the same: OK, we can accept the referee can make a mistake because of the emotional relationship [the heat of moment] within the game.
“But if we have VAR, what were they doing? What were they doing? Because now, are we going to repeat the game? We need to take the time to review the situation. We have an unbelievable car, but we need to be at the level to drive [it]. We need to drive in the best way.
“We love the technology, yes? We accept that the technology is there, yes? [So] what are they doing?”
Pochettino also referenced Erling Haaland’s winner in Manchester City’s 1-0 win at United in September. Pro Ref later admitted it should not have been allowed after the video assistant referee ruled Enzo Fernández was not interfering with play in the buildup.
“It’s not only this mistake or the mistake against Haaland in Manchester City when I was a spectator. It’s how after it affects everything. I need to defend my players and my club, and that is to denounce and to say, yes, we were right, or the decision wasn’t good. And now, where are the three points? It’s a thing that we need to think a little bit more deep.”
Speaking before the card was rescinded, Webb said: “Isak’s got control of the ball, knocks it past Yoro. He’s moving at pace and a foul comes and knocks him down. There’s a question mark around whether or not he would get on to that ball before Lammens. Now, in the moment, the referee looked at it and felt he would. The VAR checks to see whether that decision was clearly and obviously incorrect, and formed the opinion it wasn’t.
“Most people would see that as a correct decision, with Isak moving quickly towards that ball. The alternative view is that Lammens might get there first, but it’s debatable. [It’s] certainly not clearly wrong.”
The Liverpool defeat came in Pochettino’s first match as the United head coach. His side are next at Sporting in Wednesday’s fifth Champions League group game, having suffered a 4-1 humiliation at Como in the previous one under Michael Carrick. Despite this and Sunday’s reverse, which left United second-bottom in the Premier League, Pochettino was bullish when asked about the comments of Paulo Fonseca, the Sporting coach, who dismissed any notion that the visitors are fragile.
He said: “What I found is that people [outside] want it to look like we’re fragile. I don’t say that we are fragile. We are strong, we have great mentality. There are things we need to improve, of course, many things that we need to improve, but it’s normal when you are in a position that we are in the Premier League.
“We are a strong team – they showed that last season, capable of finishing third.”
Fernandes, whose contract expires in the summer, has said discussions regarding new terms are paused because of the club’s predicament. If results improve, this may allow a fresh deal for the captain to be signed.
