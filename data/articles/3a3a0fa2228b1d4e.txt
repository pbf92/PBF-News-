Premier League: Matchday 11
One of the biggest games in the footballing calendar this weekend is Liverpool welcoming Manchester United to Anfield in a blockbuster clash in the Premier League.
Fresh off an international break, which saw Florian Wirtz hit top form once again for Germany and Alexander Isak being rested for one of the two games Sweden played, Andoni Iraola will be hoping his star men come back fit and ready for the challenge ahead.
As for Manchester United, they drew 4-4 with Aston Villa in their final fixture before the two-week break, with that being the final straw for the United hierarchy, who sacked Michael Carrick and have brought in Mauricio Pochettino as the Englishman's replacement.
Last Time Out: Crystal Palace v Liverpool
Liverpool secured a professional victory away at Selhurst Park against a struggling Crystal Palace side who couldn't keep the goals out.
Goals from Alexis Mac Allister, Florian Wirtz and Alexander Isak ensured that LFC went into the international break in the top four and with three more points.
And while it was a vintage showing for Iraola's side, the star quality prevailed. In those types of games, when the team as a whole isn't performing at their best, you need your superstars to get a result over the line.
This result would also result in Pierre Sage getting sacked after a poor start to the season in South London.
Manchester United's Form
The Red Devils have had another poor start to the season and are currently sitting in 18th place after 10 games, winning just one of those.
They have recorded humiliating defeats to Hull City, local rivals Manchester City and a 4-1 demolition away to Como in the Champions League.
This led to Michael Carrick being relieved of his duties, and Mauricio Pochettino has stepped into the high-pressure role with the belief that he will be the one to turn the club's fortunes around.
But he has a lot of work ahead of him, starting this weekend with a trip to the Red half of Merseyside.
