TITLE: Anthropic Closes Up 17% at $97.17 in Hot AI IPO Debut
OUTLET: The Tech Buzz

Anthropic wrapped its blockbuster Nasdaq debut with shares closing at $97.17, up 17% from the company's $83 IPO price and capping one of the most anticipated AI public offerings in years. The Claude AI maker's strong first-day performance signals that institutional and retail investors remain hungry for direct exposure to the generative AI boom, even as the company burns through nearly $900 million annually on compute costs.
Anthropic closed its first day as a public company at $97.17 per share, notching a 17% gain that validates the frenzied demand leading up to its Nasdaq debut. The closing bell marks a watershed moment for the AI sector, with the Claude maker now commanding a market capitalization exceeding $2 trillion.
The stock's performance caps a whirlwind 48 hours for the company. Just yesterday, Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share, above its marketed range of $70 to $78, after institutional orders poured in at levels not seen since the pandemic-era tech boom. Earlier today, shares surged past the $2 trillion market cap threshold in opening trades, briefly touching highs near $105 before settling into afternoon consolidation.
The 17% first-day pop puts Anthropic in rare company. It's the strongest IPO debut for a tech company of this scale since Rivian's 29% surge in 2021, and it signals that public market investors are willing to pay premium valuations for companies at the center of the AI infrastructure buildout. Trading volume was heavy throughout the session, with over 180 million shares changing hands - more than double the typical first-day turnover for large-cap tech debuts.
Retail investors drove much of the enthusiasm, with platforms like Robinhood and Fidelity reporting record order volumes ahead of the listing. The company's decision to allocate 15% of shares to retail buyers, rather than the typical 5-10%, helped fuel the broader participation. That move, combined with the company's consumer-facing Claude chatbot brand recognition, created a perfect storm of demand.
But the celebration comes with caveats. Anthropic is burning cash at an eye-watering rate - nearly $895 million annually on compute infrastructure alone, according to IPO filings. The company sold 825 million shares and secured a $26 billion private placement from Google, Nvidia, and AMD to fund its AI training runs and inference costs. Those economics mean Anthropic needs to rapidly scale revenue to justify today's valuation.
The IPO also reshapes the competitive landscape. OpenAI, still private but reportedly considering its own public listing, now faces a direct comparison point. Meta and Google, both of which have invested heavily in Anthropic, suddenly find themselves competing with a publicly traded rival that can use stock currency for acquisitions and talent retention.
Wall Street analysts who initiated coverage this morning set price targets ranging from $85 to $120, with the bull case hinging on Anthropic's ability to monetize its enterprise Claude offerings and capture share in the AI agent market. The company's "Constitutional AI" approach, which emphasizes safety and interpretability, has resonated with regulated industries like healthcare and finance - sectors that could drive high-margin revenue if adoption accelerates.
The seven-member founding team, which each cashed out $250 million in equal share blocks during the offering, retains control through a dual-class share structure. That setup mirrors Meta and Google, giving CEO Dario Amodei and his co-founders the ability to pursue long-term AI safety research without quarterly earnings pressure.
Today's close also sets the stage for a wave of AI IPOs in the pipeline. Companies like Nscale, the Nvidia-backed neocloud startup, are watching Anthropic's reception closely as they finalize their own public market timing. If ANT stock holds these levels or pushes higher in coming weeks, expect bankers to fast-track AI infrastructure deals that have been waiting in the wings.
The broader market context matters too. The Nasdaq Composite added 1.2% today, with AI-adjacent names like Nvidia up 3% and Microsoft gaining 2%. That rising tide helped lift Anthropic, but the stock's outperformance relative to peers suggests genuine enthusiasm rather than just sector momentum.
What happens next depends on Anthropic's ability to execute. The company needs to prove it can translate its $83 billion in contracted enterprise bookings into actual revenue, expand gross margins as it scales inference efficiency, and fend off competition from both big tech incumbents and nimble startups. Investors who bought at $97 are betting that Claude's technology lead and brand recognition will carry the day.
Anthropic's 17% first-day gain cements its status as the AI sector's most successful IPO to date, but the real test begins now. With a $2 trillion valuation and massive cash burn, the company must rapidly convert its technological edge into sustainable profits while navigating intensifying competition from OpenAI, Google, and Meta. For public market investors, ANT stock offers the purest play on generative AI infrastructure - but at a price that leaves little room for execution missteps. The coming quarters will reveal whether today's enthusiasm was justified or whether the market got ahead of the fundamentals.
