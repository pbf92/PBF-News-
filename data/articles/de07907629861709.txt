TITLE: A $4.2 Billion Reason Why Google Stock Is Up Today
OUTLET: TradingView

A $4.2 Billion Reason Why Google Stock Is Up Today
Alphabet GOOG shares rose 3% in Friday morning trading as the Google parent raised C$6 billion ($4.2 billion) through a Canadian bond sale.
The latest borrowing gives Alphabet another source of funding as major technology companies tap debt markets to finance their investments. The Thursday offering was split into four portions, with repayment dates ranging from three to 40 years.
The longest-dated notes accounted for C$1.25 billion and carried a yield premium of 1.85 percentage points over Canadian government securities. That was five basis points below the initial pricing discussions. Alphabet also dropped a proposed floating-rate component during the transaction.
The new financing follows an C$8.5 billion Canadian bond sale in May. Amazon.com (AMZN) later surpassed that offering with a C$14 billion transaction in June.
Foreign issuers have increasingly turned to Canada's debt market, where Canadian-dollar offerings, known as Maple bonds, have reached C$54 billion this year. That is more than three times the volume recorded in 2025, according to Bloomberg-compiled data.
