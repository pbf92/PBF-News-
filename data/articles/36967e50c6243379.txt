TITLE: Robert Pattinson wins the 007 role, becoming the seventh official Bond
OUTLET: PPC Land

Amazon MGM Studios today named Robert Pattinson as the next James Bond, the first time the role has been cast since Amazon took creative control of the franchise in 2025. Denis Villeneuve is directing the film from a script by Steven Knight, with Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing through Pascal Pictures and Heyday Films and Tanya Lapointe as executive producer, according to an announcement published on Amazon's corporate news site. The post, written by Emily Murray and filed under Entertainment, MGM and James Bond, gives no title, no release date, no production start and no other cast.
In Short
Amazon has picked Robert Pattinson to play James Bond in the next 007 film, which Denis Villeneuve will direct. It matters because Amazon now owns both the studio that makes Bond and Prime Video, where ads run by default and where all 25 earlier Bond films already stream. The lead role is settled, but with no title or date, the existing films and Pattinson's other work are what Amazon has to put in front of viewers and advertisers for now.
What Amazon announced
The announcement is short. According to Amazon, Pattinson "will take on the iconic role of James Bond in the studio's highly anticipated film, ushering in a new era for one of cinema's most celebrated characters." The page carries the headline "Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond", while its browser title reads "Robert Pattinson cast as James Bond for the next 007 film." The phrase "highly anticipated film" links to a running page on Amazon's site that tracks updates on the next Bond movie.

Amazon's page also lists Pattinson's representation: WME, 3 Arts Entertainment, Curtis Brown Group, Narrative, and Sloane, Offer, Weber & Dern.
The timing has a pattern. Amazon MGM Studios confirmed on May 14, 2026 that auditions had begun, with Nina Gold as casting director. Today's announcement arrives 186 days later, by PPC Land's count. Nobody else from the auditions is named. Villeneuve alludes to them only in passing.
What the studio and the team said
Five statements accompany the announcement, from the actor, two Amazon executives, the director and the producers.
"James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime. I'm deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn't be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team," he said.
Mike Hopkins, head of Prime Video and Amazon MGM Studios, framed the decision in terms of the studio rather than the film. "Today marks a defining moment for our studio and a significant milestone in the history of James Bond as we proudly introduce Robert Pattinson as the next 007," Hopkins said. He went on: "This iconic character has captivated audiences around the world for more than six decades, and there's no doubt that Robert's remarkable range, magnetic screen presence, and quiet intensity make him an exceptional choice to carry that legacy forward."
Hopkins holds both titles - streaming service and studio - which is itself part of the story for advertisers. One executive oversees the company that makes the film and the service on which its predecessors are already streaming with ads.
Courtenay Valenti, head of film at Amazon MGM Studios, spoke about the audience. "Few films carry the kind of anticipation that comes with James Bond, and we have strived to honor the passionate fans, the legacy, and the future of this iconic character," she said. "We are thrilled to officially welcome Robert Pattinson as our 007 and are confident he will bring something truly special to this role."
Villeneuve's statement is the only one that refers to the selection process. "Before anything else, I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007. I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin," he said.
Pascal and Heyman gave a joint statement. "James Bond is one of the most beloved, inspiring, iconic, and yet enigmatic characters in literature and movies. The legacy of this character is in great hands with Robert Pattinson. His fearlessness as an actor, his self-deprecating wit, and his ability to continually reinvent himself make him the perfect Bond for our times," the producers said.
Pattinson's record, by Amazon's account
Amazon describes a career "spanning more than two decades" that moves "seamlessly between independent cinema and major studio productions." Most of the films it cites were made by other studios. The page lists them in a mix of past and future tense, which, taken together, sets out a crowded calendar:
Two of those lines connect directly to the Bond team. Villeneuve is directing Pattinson in Dune: Part Three, which Amazon says is due later this year, and Heyman produced the Harry Potter film that first brought the actor attention. The connections are listed by Amazon, not inferred.
The page also borrows from reviews. It describes Pattinson's turn in The Odyssey as "endlessly entertaining to watch" and an "outright pantomime," and his performance in The Drama as "career-best," "sublime" and "exquisite." None of those phrases is attributed to a named critic or publication on Amazon's page.
One of those films has an Amazon angle of its own. The Odyssey is a Universal film that opened in cinemas on July 17, 2026, yet Amazon's page links its mention of the film to an Amazon guide to watching it on Prime Video. The Odyssey goes on sale on Prime Video in the US on November 17 for $19.99 - a TVOD purchase, not a subscription title. So the day after Amazon names its new Bond, Prime Video starts selling a film in which he plays a supporting villain.
How Amazon got control of Bond
The casting is the latest step in a sequence that began with a studio purchase. "In 2022, Amazon acquired MGM, including a vast catalog with more than 4,000 films and 17,000 TV shows," Amazon states today. PPC Land's report on the deal in March 2022 put the price at $8.5 billion and described the TV library as 17,000 episodes rather than shows.
Owning MGM did not give Amazon creative control of Bond. That sat with Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson. "In March 2025, Amazon MGM Studios closed a new joint venture with Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson, the terms of which call for Amazon MGM Studios to gain creative control of the James Bond intellectual property rights," according to Amazon. Amazon has not disclosed what it paid. Deadline put the additional payment at around $1 billion, on top of the MGM acquisition.
The franchise Amazon took over is long but slow-moving. All 25 official Bond films, from Dr. No in 1962 to No Time to Die in 2021, now stream on Prime Video, and Amazon's own guide to them, published on October 29, 2026, says six actors have played 007 across that run. Pattinson becomes the seventh in the official series. When his film arrives, the gap since No Time to Die will be at least five years, by PPC Land's count - and Amazon has given no date.
Why the casting matters for advertisers
For the marketing community, the interest is less in the actor than in where Bond now sits. Amazon owns the studio, the 25-film library and the streaming service that carries it, and that service is an advertising business.
Prime Video began showing ads in the US in January 2024, followed by the UK, Canada, Germany and Austria the next month, which made the default Prime Video experience an AVOD one for Prime members. In its fourth-quarter 2025 results, Amazon reported an average ad-supported audience of 315 million viewers globally, and by May 2026 the platform sold advertising in 16 countries. Viewers who want to avoid ads in the US pay extra: from April 10, 2026, the ad-free option became Prime Video Ultra at $4.99 a month or $45.99 a year, a 67% increase on the previous fee.
Amazon has also given advertisers tools that work at the level of individual titles. In November 2025, Amazon Marketing Cloud added Prime Video viewership signals in open beta in the US, Canada, Japan and Australia, covering engagement by title, content type and show. The 25 Bond films are part of Amazon's back catalogue, and title-level data of that kind is what would show whether a casting announcement lifts viewing of older films. Amazon has published nothing on how its Bond titles perform on the service.
The broader numbers set the scale. In the third quarter of 2026, Amazon's advertising revenue grew 11% to $19.64 billion, and the company said Amazon MGM Studios had passed $1.2 billion in global box office in 2026. Amazon does not break out Prime Video advertising revenue, so there is no way to isolate what any single franchise contributes.
What the casting changes is the calendar. Until today, Amazon's Bond assets were a library and a production without a lead. It now has a named star whose other films - one on sale on Prime Video from tomorrow, one due from Villeneuve later this year, one still in production - will keep his face in front of audiences while the Bond film is made. How many ad breaks sit around those 25 earlier films, and in which of the 16 advertising markets, Amazon has not said.
What the announcement does not say
Amazon gives no title for the film, no release date, no start of production, no shooting locations and no other cast. It does not say whether the film will open in cinemas first or how long it would wait before reaching Prime Video. Nor does it explain how Bond fits around The Batman Part II, which Amazon says Pattinson is currently filming, or Dune: Part Three. No budget, no brand partners and no plans for advertising around the existing Bond library are mentioned. PPC Land's analysis in May suggested the first realistic theatrical window would probably be 2028; nothing in today's announcement confirms or rules that out.
