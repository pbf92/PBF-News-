TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino’s first Man United match ends with 4-0 loss to Liverpool after early red card
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

So far, the grass is not looking greener for Mauricio Pochettino.
On Sunday, the former United States men's national team manager had his first game as head coach of Manchester United since his sudden departure from the USMNT. But Pochettino's return to the Premier League did not get off to a triumphant start: Man United fell 4-0 to Liverpool, with an early red card fueling the rout.
The Red Devils were forced down to 10 men in the eighth minute, after Leny Yoro was given a straight red. The card came after Yoro caught a streaking Alexander Isak from behind, denying the Liverpool forward from a clear goal-scoring opportunity.
The card gave the Reds a free kick just inside the penalty arc; Liverpool midfielder Dominik Szoboszla took the shot direct from the kick, sending it through the wall and past goalkeeper Senne Lammens to take things from bad to worse for United.
Just minutes later, Florian Wirtz grew Liverpool's lead with a spinning volley that went up and in to the goal.
It was not an auspicious first half in the slightest, with Pochettino accidentally making a bit of history: The combination of a red card and two concessions in the opening half of the manager's debut match is a first in Premier League history, per OptaJoe.

Víctor Muñoz gave Liverpool another goal in the 82nd minute, hitting a one-two with Wirtz and a beautiful finish off a poor defensive clearance from United. That was enough to seal the 4-0 rout and start Pochettino's tenure on a very sour note.
United's best chances of the game came in stoppage time, with Bruno Fernandes getting a breakaway and Harry Maguire getting the header on the subsequent corner kick.
But other than that, the Red Devils were shockingly ineffective on offense while down a player, recording 10 shots (four on target) — compared to a whopping 28 shots (11 on target) for Liverpool. The score could have been even worse, as Lammens picked up seven saves and forced Liverpool to miss six big chances allowed by the struggling United defense.
Taking over Manchester United was always going to be a tall challenge for Pochettino: The legendary club is starting the season in freefall, garnering only one win across the first 10 games of the season. Former manager Michael Carrick, a club legend who joined the team at the end of last season, has spent the entire start of the 2026-27 season on the hot seat.
Pochettino's name started to emerge as a coaching replacement before Carrick was even fired. But things began to move very quickly after Carrick was officially sacked, with the club reaching out to the USMNT to hire him away. Bringing Pochettino on board cost United $10 million, as part of a clause in the extension Pochettino had signed just a few months ago.
After Sunday's loss, United is currently sitting in 19th place out of 20 spots, and is in danger of relegation for the first time in the club's history if this skid continues. Including the four-goal drubbing, the Red Devils have already allowed 23 goals, second only to woeful 20th-place Crystal Palace.
