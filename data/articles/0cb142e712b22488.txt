American Resources Corporation Receives Nasdaq Staff Delisting Determination Letter
Company intends to appeal determination while continuing efforts to complete outstanding SEC filings and regain compliance
FISHERS, IN / ACCESS Newswire / November 24, 2026 / American Resources Corporation AREC ("American Resources" or the "Company") today announced that it received a Staff Determination Letter from The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC ("Nasdaq") dated November 18, 2026, notifying the Company that it has not regained compliance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5250(c)(1), which requires the timely filing of periodic financial reports with the Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC").
The determination follows the Company's failure to file its Quarterly Reports on Form 10-Q for the periods ended March 31 and June 30, 2026, by the November 16, 2026 expiration of its previously granted extension. In addition, as the Company has not yet filed its Quarterly Report on form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, this serves as an additional basis for delisting the Company's securities.
The Company intends to appeal Nasdaq's determination to a Nasdaq Hearings Panel by the November 25, 2026 deadline and request a stay of any suspension pending the hearing process. A timely hearing request provides an initial 15-day stay of suspension, while any additional stay is subject to approval by the Hearings Panel. If the Company does not timely appeal, trading in its common stock would be suspended at the opening of business on November 30, 2026, and Nasdaq would initiate delisting proceedings.
The Company continues to work closely with its accounting, audit and other external service providers to complete its outstanding SEC filings, including the accounting associated with the deconsolidation of former subsidiaries and presentation of discontinued operations. Management remains focused on completing all outstanding filings as expeditiously as practicable, utilizing the time available under the applicable Nasdaq hearing and compliance procedures to cure the filing deficiencies and regain compliance with Nasdaq's listing requirements.
The Company will provide further updates as appropriate. There can be no assurance that the Hearings Panel will grant an extended stay or that the Company will regain compliance and maintain its Nasdaq listing.
About American Resources Corporation
American Resources Corporation AREC operates a capital-efficient critical materials platform focused on sourcing, aggregating and supplying rare earth and critical mineral feedstocks to domestic and allied processing and refining markets. Through its Platform Orchestration strategy, the Company coordinates resource sourcing, feedstock origination, recycling, industrial processing and strategic commercial relationships to connect diverse mineral resources with advanced refining capabilities.
The Company's business model emphasizes disciplined capital allocation, diversified supply sources and strategic partnerships, supported by its ownership interest in Electrified Materials Corporation and strategic minority ownership interest in ReElement Technologies Corporation.
