TITLE: GTA 6 Producer Says Hyper-Violent Weapons Would Have Been “Too Much”
OUTLET: OpenCritic

GTA 6 is being celebrated for more reasons every day, and rightfully so. However, the most striking thing about the game, since it’s the first thing you see and it’s hitting you over the head every second you’re looking at it, is how stunning it is. That realism is one of the reasons why the option to make Jason and Lucia hyper-violent is taken out of the player’s hands.
Rockstar producer Rob Nelson revealed the level of thinking that went into not only some of the endless things you can do in Leonida, but also some of the things you can’t in an interview with VGC. One of the things Nelson discussed was the decision to not include weapons that would allow for a heightened level of violence that have been present in previous GTA entries, like chainsaws and katanas.
Despite a mountain of leaks, Rockstar managed to keep one of GTA 6’s biggest plot points a secret throughout all of its development.
