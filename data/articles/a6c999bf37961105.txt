TITLE: Two charged after man run over by van during flags incident in Birmingham
OUTLET: The Guardian

Two men have been charged following an incident in Birmingham, where a man was run over by a van after flags were removed from lamp-posts.
A man in his 30s was taken to hospital with leg injuries after the incident on Mayfield Road in the suburb of Stirchley, south-west Birmingham, on 14 May.
Mark Keating, 49, from Tamworth, has been charged with four counts of assault by beating and will appear before magistrates in Birmingham on 9 December, West Midlands police said on Monday.
Ryan Bridge, 45, from Bromsgrove, is charged with two counts of using threatening/abusive words/behaviour likely to cause harassment, alarm or distress. He will appear before the same court on 10 December.
Another man, 53, from Sutton Coldfield, remains on bail after being arrested on suspicion of wounding in May.
In a statement, West Midlands police said the van was recovered nearby, shortly after the incident.
“Officers have been carrying out a range of thorough enquiries to establish the full circumstances around what happened,” the statement read. “We took reports of four assaults relating to the same incident, but we understand that no one suffered serious injuries.”
