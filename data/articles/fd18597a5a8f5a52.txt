What Traders Will Look For in the FOMC Meeting Minutes
The FOMC meeting minutes use set words to show how widely a view was held. "Many" or "most" usually point to broad support. "Several" or "a few" usually point to a smaller group. In the September minutes, "most participants" saw another increase as likely appropriate.

How Markets Reacted to the 7 October Minutes
The September minutes came out on 7 October at 14:00 ET. EUR/USD edged higher, gave back part of the move and held near 1.12, as XTB reported at 18:07 GMT that day. The message matched what markets already expected. Our preview of those minutes explains the background.
The minutes of the 28-29 October 2025 meeting, released on 19 November 2025, show the other side. Participants "expressed strongly differing views" on December, and the Dollar Index firmed after the release. By the US close that day, money markets priced a December cut at roughly 24-26%, down from about 48%. A delay to the BLS data schedule announced the same day also weighed on the odds, per Newsquawk.
