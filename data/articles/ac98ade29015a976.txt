TITLE: Wall Street Closes Higher in a Short Black Friday Session
OUTLET: Stock Titan

On this page
The short answer. By the 1:00 p.m. ET close, QQQ had won back nearly all of its Tuesday and Wednesday fall, after winning back about three fifths by late morning. SPY, the fund that tracks the S&P 500 index of large US companies, ended above its Monday close, and Nvidia closed above its Wednesday close. Brazilian stocks were among the biggest gainers, after Bloomberg reported that Bradesco's chairman was named the country's finance minister. Bradesco later confirmed that he accepted.
Every company reporting next week, with its date and time, is in StockTitan's Earnings Calendar for the Week of November 30, 2026.
Live updates
US stocks closed higher in a short Black Friday session. They gave back much of their premarket gains after the open, then climbed again. Nvidia closed higher, and Brazilian stocks traded in the US were among the biggest gainers.
3:03 p.m. ET
SPY closes up 1.62%, and the Brazil fund EWZ closes up 9.81%
Closing prices are now in for more funds and stocks. SPY, the S&P 500 fund, closed up 1.62% from Wednesday's close. Its last trade at the 1:00 p.m. ET bell had shown about 1.7%. DIA, the fund that tracks the Dow Jones Industrial Average, closed up 1.26%. IWM, a fund of smaller US companies, closed up 1.83%.
The Brazil fund EWZ closed up 9.81%. Bradesco's US shares closed up 15.23%.
QQQ and SPY moved little after the close. QQQ was down 0.18% from its close at 3:00 p.m. ET. SPY was up 0.09% from its close at 3:01 p.m. ET.
1:03 p.m. ET
US stocks close higher, and QQQ ends almost back at its Monday close
US stocks rose further after the last update and closed higher when the short session ended at 1:00 p.m. ET. QQQ, the Nasdaq 100 fund, closed up 2.69% from Wednesday's close. That left it just below its Monday close. SPY, the S&P 500 fund, was up about 1.7% at the bell. CNBC reported at 12:42 p.m. ET that the S&P 500 was headed for its best week since April 2025.
Nvidia closed up 0.80%, above its Wednesday close. Anthropic closed up 10.98%, at $91.88.
Brazilian stocks traded in the US kept most of their jump. The Brazil fund EWZ was up about 9.8% at the bell.
11:02 a.m. ET
US stocks win back most of their early gains, and SPY tops its Monday close
US stocks climbed back after the last update and regained most of their premarket gains. QQQ, the Nasdaq 100 fund, was up 1.68% from Wednesday's close at 11:01 a.m. ET, a much bigger gain than at the last update. SPY, the S&P 500 fund, was up 1.15%, which put it above its Monday close. CNBC reported that technology stocks supported the bounce, with Meta Platforms and Amazon up about 3% each.
Nvidia was still down 1.28%, below its Wednesday close. Anthropic was up 8.47% at 11:01 a.m. ET.
Brazilian stocks traded in the US held their jump. The Brazil fund EWZ was up 10.41% at 11:01 a.m. ET, about the same as at the last update.
10:03 a.m. ET
Early stock gains shrink after the open, and Nvidia turns lower
US stocks kept only part of their premarket gains once regular trading began at 9:30 a.m. ET. QQQ, the Nasdaq 100 fund, was up 0.51% from Wednesday's close at 10:01 a.m. ET, a much smaller gain than at the last update. SPY, the S&P 500 fund, was up 0.45%. Nvidia was down 2.18%, below its Wednesday close. Among single US stocks, Vera Therapeutics was up 13.05% by then.
Brazilian stocks traded in the US kept most of their jump. The Brazil fund EWZ was up 10.36% at 10:01 a.m. ET, a smaller gain than before the open.
Ero Copper, which mines copper in Brazil, was down 5.03% at 10:00 a.m. ET. On Thursday night, it said all mining and processing at its Tucumã mine had stopped for now, under a notice from the environment agency of Pará state. The company said the agency found unusual readings in local water samples after heavy rain caused two overflows of mine water last week (Ero Copper release).
9:04 a.m. ET
Brazil's rally spreads to Nubank and Sabesp, and US stocks keep most of their gains
The jump in Brazilian stocks traded in the US spread further before the open, to a digital bank and to utilities. Nu Holdings, the parent of the digital bank Nubank, was up 11.60% at 9:01 a.m. ET premarket. Sabesp, the São Paulo water company, rose 14.26% by then, and Cemig, a power company, 12.71% by 8:59 a.m. ET. The Brazil fund EWZ was up 13.49% at 9:01 a.m. ET, a slightly smaller gain than at the last update.
US stocks kept most of their premarket gains. QQQ, the Nasdaq 100 fund, was up 2.18% at 9:01 a.m. ET, also a slightly smaller gain than at the last update. Separately, Altria said on Thursday that it was considering a possible offer for Imperial Brands, the UK tobacco company, and that any offer would likely be in cash. It added that an offer was not certain (Altria release). Altria's stock was down about 2.5% at 9:01 a.m. ET premarket.
8:04 a.m. ET
Brazilian stocks climb further, and Bradesco confirms the finance minister pick
Most Brazilian stocks traded in the US rose further before the open, and Bradesco said its chairman had agreed to serve as Brazil's finance minister. In a filing at 7:40 a.m. ET, the bank said Luiz Carlos Trabuco Cappi had accepted the president's invitation to take the post from 2027 (Bradesco filing). The gains also spread beyond the banks. Petrobras, the oil producer, was up 13.27% and Vale, the mining company, 10.40% by 8:01 a.m. ET premarket.
US stocks held their premarket gains, with QQQ, the Nasdaq 100 fund, still leading. Separately, CNBC reported on Friday, citing Bloomberg, that Nvidia Blackwell chips barred from shipment to China were installed at a Shanghai data center. CNBC said the site is linked to the founder of Megaspeed, a firm under scrutiny over concerns that Nvidia chips were diverted to China. Nvidia was still higher premarket.
6:45 a.m. ET
AI stocks bounce back before a short Black Friday session
US stocks are higher before the open, and the AI and chip stocks that fell on Tuesday and Wednesday are leading the way. At 6:37 a.m. ET premarket, SPY, the S&P 500 fund, was up 1.71% from Wednesday's close. Nvidia, Micron and SanDisk were all higher.
Brazilian stocks traded in the US rose the most. Bloomberg reported on Friday that Brazilian assets gained after President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva named Luiz Carlos Trabuco Cappi, the chairman of the bank Bradesco, as finance minister.
Vertex fell premarket. After Wednesday's close it said the FDA needs up to three more months to decide on its kidney drug (Vertex release). Alvotech fell too. On Thursday it said a US court had granted Janssen a preliminary injunction over its planned copy of the drug Simponi. That is a court order that holds while the case goes on, and Alvotech said it will appeal (Alvotech release). The market was closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving.
AI and chip stocks today
AI and chip stocks rose before the open after falling on Tuesday and Wednesday. They gave back much of that rise after the open, then climbed again into the close. QQQ, the Nasdaq 100 fund, ended almost back at its Monday close, and Nvidia closed higher.
The week began with a jump. On Monday, November 23, Nvidia's board brought back Jensen Huang as chief executive (Nvidia release), and its stock closed up 31.48% that day, as StockTitan's Monday live page shows. AI and chip stocks then gave back part of that gain on Tuesday and Wednesday, as the Wednesday live page shows.
Before Friday's open, the Nasdaq 100 fund had won back most of that fall. It kept only part of that gain once trading began, then climbed again. By the 1:00 p.m. ET close, QQQ had won back nearly all of what it lost on Tuesday and Wednesday, against about 81% at its 9:01 a.m. ET premarket price. SPY ended above its Monday close. Nvidia closed above its Wednesday close, and 8.8% below its Monday close.
Among the large chip stocks, SanDisk had the biggest gain. It closed up 15.96%, while Micron closed up 7.60%. AMD and Broadcom closed higher too.
Anthropic, the maker of the Claude chatbot, closed at $91.88. That left it above the $83.00 IPO price set in its final prospectus, after it closed just below that price on Wednesday.
Overseas, CNBC reported that Chinese chip stocks had their biggest one-day gain on record on Friday. It tied that rise to a rare state media summary of a government meeting. CNBC said the summary signaled that officials would do more to stop a long slide in Chinese stocks.
Why are Brazilian stocks up today
Brazilian companies listed in the US were among the day's biggest gainers at the close. Bloomberg reported on Friday that Brazilian assets gained after President Lula named Bradesco's chairman as finance minister. The bank Bradesco said that he accepted and will start in 2027.
Some single stocks rose even more than EWZ, the fund that tracks large Brazilian stocks. Bradesco's American depositary shares, the form its stock takes in New York, closed up 15.23% at 1:00 p.m. ET. XP, a Brazilian investment firm whose shares trade on Nasdaq, closed up 18.07%.
Bloomberg reported on Friday that Brazilian assets gained after President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva tapped Trabuco, Bradesco's chairman, as finance minister. It said a Tokyo fund that tracks Brazilian stocks rose as much as 8.3% in early Asian trading. Bradesco confirmed the choice in a filing at 7:40 a.m. ET, citing a release from the president's press office (Bradesco filing).
The gains reach across Brazil's banks and payment companies listed in the US. Inter&Co closed up 16.21%. Itaú Unibanco and StoneCo were higher too, and so were the oil company Petrobras and the miner Vale. Nu Holdings, the parent of Nubank, and the utilities Sabesp and Cemig were up as well.
