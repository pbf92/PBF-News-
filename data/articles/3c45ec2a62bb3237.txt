TITLE: Grand Theft Auto 6 Sells 32.5 Million Copies, Earns $3.79 Billion on Launch Day
OUTLET: GamingBolt

After days of estimates, official sales for Grand Theft Auto 6 are finally in, and they appear to be far better than expected. Rockstar’s open-world title has sold 32.5 million units and brought in $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales.
This isn’t just a new record for “entertainment across any medium”; GTA 6 is now the “biggest entertainment launch in history.” In a press release, Rockstar president and founder Sam Houser said, “It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto 6. We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world, and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”
These numbers are based on data through the end of day on November 19th worldwide, and absolutely trounce Grand Theft Auto 5’s launch record of $800 million. It also beats Red Dead Redemption 2, which earned $725 million in its first weekend. Even when compared to movies like Avengers: Endgame and Spider-Man: Brand New Day, GTA 6 is in its own league.
Interestingly, Newzoo estimated that it would reach $3.3 to $5.2 billion within its first week. Based on pre-order pace and an average selling price of $88 (a mix of Standard and Ultimate Edition sales), it estimated $4.5 billion in sales or about 51 million units sold during that period. However, those estimates may have been a bit conservative.
The sky is truly the limit for Rockstar, and there’s no telling how high Grand Theft Auto 6 may soar. Stay tuned for updates regardless and check out our review.
