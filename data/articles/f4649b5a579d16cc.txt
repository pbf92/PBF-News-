TITLE: New Poll: Majority of Trump 2024 Voters Who Abandoned Republicans Support Blocking Bombs To Israel
OUTLET: IMEU Policy Project

A poll released Wednesday, November 25, by IMEU Policy Project, first reported in Semafor and conducted by YouGov, found that 56% of Trump 2024 voters who abandoned Republicans in the midterms support blocking the transfer of bombs to Israel. 59% support reducing or ending all military aid to Israel.
42% say Trump has been too supportive of Israel, compared with just 12% who say he has not been supportive enough. Another 46% say support has been at the right level.¹
The poll surveyed 636 Trump 2024 voters who either did not vote in 2026 or cast a ballot for a candidate besides the Republican in at least one federal race. The survey was fielded from November 4-18 and has a margin of error of +/- 4.3%.
¹An IMEU Policy Project poll conducted by YouGov after the 2024 election found a majority (53%) of Biden 2020 voters who abandoned Democrats similarly believed President Biden had been too supportive of Israel, compared to just 6% who said he had not been supportive enough and 23% who said it was the right amount.
KEY FINDINGS:
- 59% support reducing or ending US military aid to Israel, including 33% who want it to end entirely. Just 5% want to increase military funding for Israel, while 20% favor maintaining it at current levels.
- 56% support Senate measures to block the transfer of bombs to Israel and 59% support measures to strip $3.3 billion in annual taxpayer funding to Israel. Just 24% and 25% of voters, respectively, disapprove of these measures.
- 42% say Trump has been too supportive of Israel, compared with just 12% who say he has not been supportive enough. Another 46% say support has been at the right level.¹
- Voters connect the Iran war with support for Israel, with 48% believing the war benefits Israel more than the United States. Just 20% believe the war benefits the United States more.
- 58% say the current US-Israel relationship benefits Israel more than the US, compared to 14% who say it benefits the US more.
- 52% identify the Iran war as one of the issues that most contributed to Republicans losing control of Congress, second only to the high cost of living (56%). Trump’s support for Israel was identified by 19%.
- Among respondents who said the economy played a significant role in their decision to abandon Republicans, 66% blamed rising gas prices on the Iran war.
- Among respondents who said relations with Israel played a significant role in their vote, 78% said the US should reduce support for Israel, while just 5% said we should increase support and 12% said that we should maintain support.
- Among respondents who said the Iran war played a significant role in their vote, 90% disapprove of the war.
