TITLE: Midterm news fills 12 of 28 debuts on Triton's chart as the top four hold
OUTLET: PPC Land

Triton Digital today released its Q3 2026 U.S. Podcast Ranker, a quarterly survey-based chart of the 200 US podcasts with the largest audiences, and the picture it draws is of a crowded quarter with a frozen summit. News programmes accounted for 12 of the 28 shows that debuted in the Top 200 between July and September, as campaigns built toward the November 3 midterm elections, according to Triton. Yet The Joe Rogan Experience, Crime Junkie, The Daily and Call Her Daddy held the first four places for a sixth consecutive quarter.
The audio measurement company, which operates in more than 80 countries, paired the survey list with genre data from its Demos+ product and with October download highlights from its Podcast Metrics service. Comedy and True Crime posted their highest monthly reach in six quarters. News, despite supplying the most new entries, slipped 1.6% against the second quarter. The release, issued from New York, presents that stability as a planning argument for holiday advertisers.
In Short
Triton Digital's survey of American podcast listeners for July to September 2026 found that a wave of election shows entered its Top 200 without dislodging the four biggest podcasts, which have held their places for six quarters. Comedy and True Crime drew their largest audiences in six quarters, while News lost a little ground even as it supplied the most new entries. For holiday advertisers, Triton's genre data links luxury shoppers to Technology shows and fast-fashion shoppers to Kids & Family, though the release does not say how many such listeners those genres hold.
The top of the chart did not move
The survey list, which carries the label "July 2026" and covers the reporting period July 1 to September 30, ranks 200 shows by the number of respondents who say they listened to or watched each one. Its first four places are identical to the second quarter's. According to Triton, the same four shows have occupied the top four since Q2 2025, and the five biggest genres have kept the same order over the same six quarters.
Source: Triton Digital, US Podcast Ranker, Top Podcasts By Listeners/Viewers (Survey), July 1 - September 30, 2026.
Below that fixed point, the chart churned. Triton counts 60 shows climbing and 28 debuting. A PPC Land count of the full list fills in the rest: 72 shows fell, 35 returned after missing the previous period, and five held their positions, the top four plus Casefile True Crime at No. 133.
Climbed and Debut figures match Triton's release; Fell, Unchanged and Return are a PPC Land count of the ranker document.
"Debut" has a narrower meaning than it suggests. According to the ranker's definitions, it "indicates that the show is new to the ranker, having made its debut for the first time within the last 13 months," while "Return" marks a show that came back "after having not appeared in the previous reporting period(s)." Several of the quarter's debuts are long-running television news brands rather than new productions.
"Campaign season brought a wave of new political shows to the rankings, and News supplied more debuts this quarter than any other genre, yet audiences didn't trade in the shows they already love," said Daryl Battaglia, SVP, Measurement Products & Strategy at Triton Digital. "The rankings barely moved, but the audience did. For advertisers, a steady lineup and a growing audience make podcasting a dependable foundation for holiday media spend, pointing brands to the genres that reach sought-after audiences such as luxury shoppers, moms and Gen Z."
Midterm season brings 12 news debuts
News ended the quarter with 43 shows in the Top 200, according to Triton. Television news brands feature heavily among its debuts. CNN Inside Politics entered at No. 126, The Fox News Rundown at No. 149, Anderson Cooper 360 at No. 169 and Deadline: White House at No. 171, according to the ranker. Commentary arrived from both sides of the aisle, in Triton's words: The Clay Travis and Buck Sexton Show debuted at No. 104, Victor Davis Hanson: In His Own Words at No. 144, Breaking News from Pod Save America at No. 184, #RolandMartinUnfiltered at No. 197 and The Rest Is Politics: US at No. 198. The ranker also marks Hang Out with Sean Hannity (No. 82), The Officer Tatum Show (No. 87) and Inside Trump's Head (No. 153) as debuts; the release does not say which genre Triton assigns to them.
Personality-led shows already on the chart kept rising. The Dan Bongino Show gained 18 places to No. 32 and has improved every quarter since its Q1 2026 debut, according to Triton. No Lie with Brian Tyler Cohen rose 59 places to No. 79, its second straight quarterly gain.
The genre as a whole did not grow, though. News reach fell to 25.3% from 25.7%, a relative decline of 1.6% and the only drop among the five largest categories.
Established titles slipped
The ranker shows a number of established news and political programmes losing ground in the same quarter, something the release does not discuss. Up First from NPR fell 38 places to No. 65. The Rachel Maddow Show dropped 24 places to No. 59, The Journal from The Wall Street Journal fell 79 places to No. 181, and The Best People with Nicolle Wallace slid 70 places into the last slot, No. 200. The Matt Walsh Show fell 76 places to No. 135, The Tucker Carlson Show 15 places to No. 24 and The Ben Shapiro Show 12 places to No. 38.
Timing shapes how far the data can be read. The survey window closed on September 30, about five weeks before the vote. Political money was moving through audio at the same time: AdImpact projected $11.6 billion in political ad spending for the 2026 midterm cycle, above the $11.2 billion of the 2024 presidential race, and DAX US put political podcast ad spending up 42% year over year. Meta blocked new US political ads from October 27 through election day. Did listeners turn toward political shows in the final weeks of the campaign? A quarterly survey that ends on September 30 cannot answer that.
Comedy and True Crime reach six-quarter highs
Comedy, News and Society & Culture remain the three largest categories by monthly reach, followed by Sports and True Crime, according to Triton. The methodology defines monthly reach as "the proportion of U.S. monthly podcast listeners/viewers who have consumed at least one podcast in that category." Because a listener can consume several genres, the five figures sum to more than 100%.
Source: Triton Digital Q3 2026 U.S. Podcast Ranker release. Point changes are a PPC Land calculation.
The changes Triton reports are relative, not percentage points. True Crime's 9.4% rise amounts to 1.6 points of reach; Comedy's 1.3% is 0.6 points. Comedy posted its third consecutive quarterly gain. True Crime is up 34% from Q3 2025, the largest year-over-year increase among the five biggest genres, according to Triton. By PPC Land's calculation from those two figures, True Crime's reach a year earlier stood at about 14.0%.
One presentation error sits in the release: its list of top categories by reach prints "Sports (19.5%)" twice. The quarter-on-quarter breakdown that follows confirms the order and the figures in the table above.
The ranking contrasts with the Latin American picture Triton published two days ago, in which news took 32% of podcast downloads across the region. The two figures measure different things - a share of downloads in one case, survey reach in the other - so they are not directly comparable, but in the US survey News trails Comedy by almost 20 points.
True Crime's strongest stretch
True Crime now has 25 shows in the Top 200, up from 19 in the second quarter, and nine in the Top 50, up from seven a year earlier, according to Triton. 48 Hours, at No. 21, has climbed for four straight quarters and sits 78 places higher than in Q3 2025. Rotten Mango (No. 13), Murder, Mystery & Makeup (No. 48) and My Favorite Murder with Karen Kilgariff and Georgia Hardstark (No. 37) all posted double-digit gains. Seven True Crime shows returned to the list, including Murder With My Husband (No. 76), Crime, Conspiracy, Cults and Murder (No. 93) and Park Predators (No. 103), while Allegedly debuted at No. 141.
The genre's audience composition has held steady too. True Crime ranks first among women overall at Index 145 and first in every female age group under 55: women 18-24 at Index 164, women 18-34 at 168 and women 35-54 at 158. Its index among women 18-34 has stayed between 165 and 172 in every quarter since Q2 2025, according to Triton.
"Comedy keeps setting new records, and True Crime is having its strongest stretch yet," Battaglia said. "With reach at a six-quarter high and nine shows in the Top 50, True Crime offers both scale and a consistently female audience."
Climbers, fallers and the publishers behind them
Triton's release lists ten climbers within the Top 50. The ranker document shows an eleventh with an equal claim: Armchair Expert with Dax Shepard rose 19 places to No. 28, the same gain as My Favorite Murder and more than two shows the release did include.
Source: Triton Digital release and ranker document. Armchair Expert appears in the ranker but not in the release's list.
Comedy supplies four of the ten names Triton chose. Huberman Lab's 38-place jump took it to No. 11, one position short of the top ten.
Further down, the biggest moves were larger still. Baby, this is Keke Palmer, the Wondery show that Apple named its 2026 Show of the Year on November 17, climbed 83 places to No. 75, according to the ranker. Pop Culture Happy Hour from NPR rose 82 places to No. 106 and Serial Killers: Real Life Documentaries 80 places to No. 119. Within the Top 50, the steepest falls belonged to Pardon My Take, down 29 to No. 47, and The Ramsey Show, down 20 to No. 36.
Publisher concentration is uneven. By a PPC Land count of the ranker, SiriusXM Podcasts has eight shows in the Top 50 - Call Her Daddy, SmartLess, Rotten Mango, Conan O'Brien Needs A Friend, MrBallen, The Mel Robbins Podcast, Morbid and The Megyn Kelly Show - and 12 in the full 200. Morbid, at No. 29 after an eight-place gain, moved to SiriusXM under an exclusive distribution and ad sales deal that began on September 1, 2025. iHeart Audience Network has the most entries overall, 24, but only four in the Top 50. Audioboom, including three shows listed as Crooked Media / Audioboom, has 10 entries and three in the Top 50. The weight at the top of the survey chart sits alongside a slower advertising picture at SiriusXM, where podcast advertising growth slowed to 20% in the third quarter.
Downloads tell a different story
The release also carries download highlights for October, measured directly by Triton's Podcast Metrics service. The top three sales networks by average weekly downloads were iHeart Audience Network, Audioboom and Cumulus Podcast Network. The top three podcasts were Pod Save America (Audioboom), Stuff You Should Know (iHeart Audience Network) and My Favorite Murder with Karen Kilgariff and Georgia Hardstark (iHeart Audience Network). The release gives no download figures.
That order differs from September. In Triton's September download ranker, iHeart averaged 67.3 million weekly downloads against Audioboom's 15.5 million, and Stuff You Should Know led Pod Save America. The October list puts Pod Save America first - the same show that slipped three places to No. 15 in the survey for July to September.
None of the survey's top four appears in the download highlights. The reason is structural rather than a verdict on popularity. Download rankings "require participating networks to opt into measurement by Triton Digital," according to the methodology, while the survey list is open to every show: "All podcasts are eligible to be reported in the ranker." The two lists also cover different months.
