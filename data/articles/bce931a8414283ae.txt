YZY: Team allocation unlock releases 29.17 million tokens - 12 Jun 2027
YZY is scheduled to unlock 29.17 million tokens for the team on 12 June 2027. The team allocation unlock represents 9.76% of the token's circulating supply and is part of a monthly release.
The unlock increases the token supply available to team members by an amount close to one-tenth of current circulating supply. This creates dilution and potential selling pressure if the team sells the newly unlocked tokens.
