Rockstar Games Announces Grand Theft Auto VI Now Available
Launches as the Highest Rated Game of All Time on PlayStation® 5 and Xbox Series X|S
    NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Nov. 19, 2026--
Rockstar Games®, a publishing label of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTWO), is proud to announce that Grand Theft Auto VI is now available for the PlayStation® 5 computer entertainment systems and the Xbox Series X|S games and entertainment system.
This press release features multimedia. View the full release here: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261119830927/en/
Rockstar Games®, publishing label of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTWO), is proud to announce that Grand Theft Auto VI is now available for the PlayStation® 5 computer entertainment systems and the Xbox Series X|S games and entertainment system.
Vice City, USA. Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida — forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive.
“Grand Theft Auto VI represents everything we have been working towards since we first started making games,” says Sam Houser, President and Founder of Rockstar Games. “Whether you can trace your journey to this game through the entire series or are experiencing Grand Theft Auto for the first time, we hope that we have created an experience that exceeds your expectations for what’s possible from this medium. We are incredibly grateful for everyone’s support, and we look forward to many more years of sharing these experiences with you.”

About Rockstar Games
Founded by Sam Houser in 1998, Rockstar Games pioneered the open-world genre of video games with the launch of Grand Theft Auto III in 2001. The series’ most recent iteration, Grand Theft Auto V, has sold-in nearly 235 million units alone. Alongside the other blockbuster entries in the series, including Grand Theft Auto: Vice City, Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, and Grand Theft Auto IV, Grand Theft Auto has become one of the biggest-selling and most critically acclaimed and influential properties in modern entertainment, selling nearly 480 million units. Rockstar Games’ blockbuster Western series, Red Dead Redemption, has sold-in nearly 118 million units worldwide to date, with its most recent release, Red Dead Redemption 2, breaking new ground in fidelity and immersion. The latest Rockstar Games release is Grand Theft Auto VI. Follow Rockstar Games on X, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Twitch, Discord, WhatsApp, TikTok, and BiliBili.
