Clinch Promotes Taylor West, Appoints Dean Jayson
The advertising technology company named West chief revenue officer and Jayson vice president of growth strategy.
Updated on Nov. 23, 2026 in Advertising
Clinch, an agentic operating system for omnichannel advertising, promoted Taylor West to chief revenue officer and appointed Dean Jayson as vice president of growth strategy. West joined the company in 2020 and has led partnerships with enterprise clients including Coca-Cola, JPMorgan, Sony, and Expedia.
Why it matters
The moves reflect a focus on expanding enterprise advertiser relationships as the company seeks consistent year-over-year revenue growth globally. Jayson brings experience from Universal McCann, part of Interpublic Group, where he managed major partnerships with Google, Meta, Amazon, and The Trade Desk.
West joined Clinch in 2020, and Jayson brings nearly 20 years of industry experience and leadership across more than 50 markets. The announcement did not disclose revenue growth rates or partnership values, leaving the financial impact of the appointments unquantified.

Clinch

Taylor West

Dean Jayson
Dean Jayson is an advertising executive with nearly 20 years of experience in media, technology integration, and business transformation.
The details
Taylor West, who joined Clinch in 2020 as global head of sales and client experience, has helped drive consistent year-over-year revenue growth globally. Dean Jayson joins from Universal McCann, where he served as EVP of enterprise transformation and operations.

Market Landscape
The hire of an executive from Universal McCann, part of Interpublic Group, into an independent agentic ad platform reflects agencies and ad-tech firms competing for leaders who manage Google, Meta, Amazon, and The Trade Desk relationships. Clinch's emphasis on enterprise partnerships places it in the contest to own omnichannel campaign management.
For advertisers, a stronger enterprise focus at Clinch could mean more dedicated account support for large brand campaigns. Clinch has open roles in client partnership, product management, and engineering for those interested in the company's work.
The takeaway
Leadership moves at ad-tech platforms signal where companies are investing, namely in enterprise client relationships and operational execution. Readers tracking the advertising industry can watch whether Clinch's revenue growth continues under its new leadership structure.
