TITLE: Can PINS's New Microsoft Advertising Deal Boost Its Competitive Edge?
OUTLET: TradingView

Can PINS's New Microsoft Advertising Deal Boost Its Competitive Edge?
Pinterest, Inc. PINS is expanding its advertising reach through a new partnership with Microsoft Corporation MSFT, enabling more advertisers to promote their products across PINS. The initiative is expected to help Pinterest attract more brands, increase monetization opportunities and connect businesses with users searching for ideas and products.
Under the agreement, Pinterest will allow eligible campaigns from Microsoft Advertising to access its Home Feed, Search Results and Related Pins through a custom bidding model. The integration uses Pinterest Intelligence, the company’s AI technology, along with search activity and user interests to display relevant ads. It will also enable advertisers to reach Pinterest users through their existing tools without requiring additional platforms.
The collaboration will also broaden the company’s access to advertisers across Microsoft’s media platforms, including Bing, MSN, Xbox, Microsoft Casual Games and Outlook. Pinterest has 661 million monthly active users and around 80 billion monthly searches, more than half of which have commercial intent. Additionally, more than 96% of text searches are unbranded, offering brands opportunities to engage consumers early in their purchasing journeys.
Initially launching in North America, Australia and New Zealand, the integration could increase advertiser participation, generate greater demand for Pinterest’s advertising inventory and strengthen its position in the digital advertising market.
How Are Competitors Performing in the Digital Advertising Market?
Pinterest faces competition from Snap Inc. SNAP and Meta Platforms, Inc. META.
SNAP is strengthening its digital advertising business through AI-powered tools and new advertising formats to help brands reach audiences more effectively. The company is expanding creator advertising solutions to connect brands with content creators and improve audience engagement. SNAP’s Commerce Power Pack introduces AI-driven tools to support product promotion, optimize campaigns and improve shopping conversions.
Meta is increasing its use of AI in advertising to help businesses improve campaign performance. The company introduced new tools that allow advertisers to create videos, manage campaigns and generate creative content more easily. Meta is upgrading its ad recommendation systems to show users more relevant ads and help businesses achieve better campaign results.
PINS’ Price Performance, Valuation & Estimates
Pinterest shares have plunged 27.5% over the past year compared with the industry’s decline of 7%.
From a valuation standpoint, Pinterest trades at a forward price-to-sales ratio of 1.97, below the industry tally of 3.54.
Earnings estimates for 2026 have decreased 3.4% to $1.99 over the past 60 days, while the same for 2027 have declined 4.3% to $2.25.
