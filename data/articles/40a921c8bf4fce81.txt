Planet Labs Stock Nears 52-Week Low - Here's Why
Planet Labs PL Planet Labs shares reversed Monday’s gains, closing sharply lower near their 52-week low despite announcing an expanded contract with a European defense and intelligence customer this week.
Markets tumbled Wednesday ahead of Thanksgiving, with the Nasdaq down 3.75% and the S&P 500 falling 2.5%, led by sharp losses in AI stocks.
October PCE inflation accelerated to 3.8%, while core PCE reached 3.3%, above estimates, fueling expectations of a December Fed rate hike.
Proposed tariffs on 14 trading partners, including an additional 20% levy on China, added to market concerns.
European Customer Contract
On Tuesday, Planet Labs expanded its existing agreement with a European defense and intelligence customer, building on a seven-figure, one-year contract signed in June.
Under the expanded deal, the customer will use PlanetScope Monitoring and high-resolution satellite imaging to improve maritime and land surveillance, strengthen situational awareness and protect critical infrastructure from emerging threats.
Planet said demand for its geospatial intelligence solutions is growing across Europe, following earlier agreements with another European defense customer and the Swedish Armed Forces.
