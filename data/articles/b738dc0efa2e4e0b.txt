TITLE: Miami Heat unveil 'A Night in Vice City' court, activities ahead of GTA VI release
OUTLET: ESPN

The Miami Heat have been carefully leaning into "Vice City" branding for years now, but 2026 will bring the team's most ambitious effort yet: Wednesday will be "A Night in Vice City" at the Kaseya Center.
With Grand Theft Auto VI's long-awaited release date set for Thursday, the Heat are partnering with Rockstar Games to offer a topical lead-up event the night before.
Miami is rolling out plenty of offerings, including before and during the game.
Fans are encouraged to arrive early with with the team's "Vice City" collection available starting two hours before tipoff. There will be GTA VI-inspired cars set outside Kaseya Center on the West Plaza plus a GTA IV logo next to the rides for pictures. Similar spots for photos will be scattered throughout the arena and East Plaza. Official music from the game is going to be played in-arena, while Cluckin' Bell and Burger Joint -- two fast food restaurants from the GTA series -- will be at select concessions in Kaseya Center.
The night will feature a special Vice-themed court and a reimagined edition of the team's popular alternate uniforms. Even the roof of Kaseya Center is going to be altered to read: "Welcome to Vice City."
Miami revealed the pink, white and blue hardwood court Tuesday, featuring "Vice City" in the center and "I heart Vice City" on a baseline. The "VI" logo also appears near the dunker spot on each side of the court.
The jerseys, unveiled on Nov. 6., build on the team's past "Vice" alternates with nods to Grand Theft Auto VI and Travis Scott's Cactus Jack brand. The black Swingman jersey features pink and white accents, "Vice City" across the chest, Scott's signature flipped Nike Swoosh and a "VI" nod to the game. Six pink stars and Cactus Jack branding appear at the hem alongside a tag featuring Rockstar Games, the Heat and Cactus Jack.
Both the on-court and Swingman versions will be available in limited quantities Wednesday.
According to a news release, the special occasion will "transform the Kaseya Center into Vice City -- showcasing the Miami-inspired setting of Grand Theft Auto VI -- for a first-of-its-kind sports crossover experience."
The Heat kicked off the promotion for the special event on Nov. 5., lighting up the "Welcome to Vice City" signage on the roof of its arena. Players have expressed excitement, including Giannis Antetokounmpo and Klay Thompson, an avid gamer.
"I understand the hype and feel like a lot of people in this locker room have played the game. They're excited for the game to come out," Antetokounmpo said Monday. "Just be able to be part of one of the most talked about video games. It's fun. I heard that we have special jerseys and special court, so I'm excited for that, excited for the energy."
Antetokounmpo added that the main thing for the Heat though is that they come out and win.
Thompson revealed Monday that he has been playing GTA since it was "the bird's-eye view," which was the set up for the earlier editions over 20 years ago. Therefore, even at 36 years old, Thompson said he will still be gaming once he gets this version and expressed excitement over the story mode. He also joked about potentially streaming himself playing GTA VI while wearing the special "Vice City" threads.
"I'm excited when that game does come out. ... It will feel much better to have a day of gaming when we get that victory on Wednesday. That will feel so much better," he said.
The Heat ring in "A Night in Vice City" with a matchup against the Milwaukee Bucks Wednesday on ESPN.
