TITLE: Arthur J. Gallagher VP and Chief Financial Officer Sold Shares Worth Over $2.8M
OUTLET: TradingView

Arthur J. Gallagher VP and Chief Financial Officer Sold Shares Worth Over $2.8M
Douglas K. Howell, VP and Chief Financial Officer, reported selling 5,000 shares of common stock on November 20, 2026, in four transactions at a weighted average price of $283.11, and 5,000 shares on November 24, 2026, at a weighted average price of $283.264. The total sale amount was $2,831,848. Following the transactions, Howell directly owned 77,939 shares and indirectly owned 3,678 shares: 3,187 held by his spouse and 491 in his Gallagher 401(k) plan account. Howell disclaimed beneficial ownership of the shares held by his spouse.
