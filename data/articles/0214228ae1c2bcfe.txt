TITLE: Girl rushed to hospital after air ambulance lands near busy Oldham road
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Girl rushed to hospital after air ambulance lands near busy Oldham road
Ripponden Road was cordoned off after the girl was hit by a car
A teenage girl was rushed to hospital after being hit by a car in Oldham.
The crash happened on busy Ripponden Road at around 2.10pm on Wednesday (November 25). Police and paramedics descended on the scene as the air ambulance also landed nearby.
A spokesperson for Greater Manchester Police said the girl, who was believed to be aged 15, was taken to hospital from the scene with serious injuries. No update has been issued on her condition at this stage.

No arrests were made. A cordon was put in place across a stretch of Ripponden Road as emergency crews responded.
The road was closed off between Keston Road and Broadbent Road. A white BMW car was pictured within the cordon.
