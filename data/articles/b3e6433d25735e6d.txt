Agenus Names Timothy Wright Audit and Governance Committee Chair
Less than 1 min read
Agenus appointed new committee leaders following the death of Lead Director Thomas L. Harrison.
Key Highlights:
- Harrison, a director since August 2024 and Lead Director since July 2025, passed away on November 17, 2026.
- The Board appointed current director Marco Tullio Marcucci to the Audit Committee to fill the vacancy created by Harrison’s passing.
- Timothy R. Wright was named Chair of both the Audit Committee and the Corporate Governance and Nominating Committee.
- The Board now has six directors and one Class III vacancy, which it intends to fill in due course.
