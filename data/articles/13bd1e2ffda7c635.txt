TITLE: GTA 6 can be boring but that’s not a bad thing - Reader's Feature
OUTLET: Metro.co.uk

It may be the biggest launch in entertainment history, but some have accused GTA 6 of being boring, and yet one reader feels that’s not actually an insult.
So we’ve seen a lot of talk this week about GTA 6 being ‘boring’, which is not something I expected. It started with an attempted backlash that dropped the game’s User Score on Metacritic to 5.7, which is obviously ridiculous.
It seemed to start off as people having a thing against Lucia, then it was people pretending they care about 30fps, and the Zelda fans got involved in… I don’t care, it’s obviously nonsense. But then the word boring came up again in GC’s review and I’ve heard it many times from other people talking about the game, even when they like it.
A lot of it is based on just the first few hours which, kind of obviously, start off slow. There’s a lot of stuff here that people assume is essential but it’s actually optional, like fixing up the house and buying Lucia a necklace. And while that is admittedly not very exciting (painting the wall with a roller gave me DIY flashbacks), it doesn’t last very long anyway.
But it’s not like the whole game is like that, optional or otherwise, and right from the beginning you can just go off and do whatever you want. What I will admit can be boring is driving, because you’re in a world of trouble, that takes a long time to get out of, if you smash into anyone or get caught speeding.
I think not having any kind of fast travel, except for paying a fortune for it via the Uber equivalent, is a mistake. You don’t need it to be easy or everywhere but just now I did a mission and it was driving for a good five minutes on the highway and absolutely nothing happened. Then I picked up a car from a garage and… drove it back to do the mission that was near where I started.
I don’t care how realistic the game is, that’s just not something that needs to happen to you as a player. It kind of works as a palette cleanser, for when the crazy stuff happens, but unless you happen to get a good random incident, or you know you’re doing a story mission next, it’s not guaranteed.
Where for me it definitely does work though, and overrides all the other problems, is it encourages you to spend time as a couple. Whether it’s sitting on a couch together, watching TV, or taking a shower together, doing ‘boring’ things with Lucia and Jason really makes the characters seem like real people, which no one in previous GTA games ever did, or were even really meant to.
For a character to feel real in a game they have to do things other than murder people, which is always going to seem more ‘boring’ compared to the action. But GTA 6 understands that the quiet, slow moments are what make the exciting ones work.
Yes, there are some issues with some of the things you’re meant to do, especially the long-distance driving, but beyond that the quiet, non-action stuff is some of the best there is in the game and what gives it the best story by far in the whole series.
Besides, for a game that apparently so many people find boring it does seem kind of successful and I definitely haven’t heard of people queuing up for refunds. Maybe they realised the truth of what I’m saying, that you’ve got to have lows for the highs to go as far as possible.
