TITLE: Prince Harry admits he would rejoin military to fight Russia if asked
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Prince Harry admits he would rejoin military to fight Russia if asked
Prince Harry has said he would be prepared to return to military service to confront Russia if the situation demanded it
The Duke of Sussex has revealed he would be willing to return to military service to face Russia should circumstances require it.
Excerpts from Prince Harry's interview came to light on the final day of the Prince and Princess of Wales's landmark tour of India. The duke took part in a discussion for The Telegraph's Ukraine: The Latest podcast during an unannounced visit to the war-torn country last weekend.
In footage released on the Telegraph's website on Wednesday afternoon, former soldier Harry, who spent a decade in the armed forces and served on the front line in Afghanistan, said: "There is a lot that we can learn from Ukraine. I think there's a lot that the whole world can learn from Ukraine.
"If asked to sign up again, if the need is there, then I will 100% be there to serve my country, alongside my fellow veterans and service personnel."
Harry suggested that Britain had been locked in a struggle with Russia for the past decade, telling the paper's Dominic Nicholls: "Just because there's not drones, bullets, and rockets flying at us doesn't mean that we're not at war."
On US President Donald Trump's position regarding the ongoing conflict, the duke noted: "I think there's been a big separation between what American society feels regarding support for Ukraine, which I think is incredibly high and always has been, compared to a certain group, or in, in this case, an administration that was perhaps thinking about it differently."
The duke undertook two operational tours in Afghanistan before leaving the Army in 2015. Meanwhile, William and Kate wrapped up the princess's first major international journey since her cancer diagnosis.
During their final day, Kate conducted an individual visit to a maternity facility in Mumbai, where she provided solace to a grieving mother.
The pair concluded their official duties by meeting Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi at his personal New Delhi home, at the request of the British government.
William has been estranged from his younger sibling, who came back to the UK this summer, for several years, with no sign that the division is likely to mend.
Reports of Harry's interview surfaced on Tuesday, just minutes before the Prince and Princess walked the green carpet at William's prestigious flagship event, The Earthshot Awards.
The complete podcast will be available from 00.01 on Thursday via Ukraine: The Latest's Youtube channel.
