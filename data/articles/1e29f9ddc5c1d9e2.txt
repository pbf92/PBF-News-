$3.79B First-Day Sales
Take-Two Interactive and Rockstar Games announced that Grand Theft Auto VI reached $3.79 billion in retail sales on its first day, positioning the launch as the biggest entertainment debut in history across any medium. Take-Two also said GTA 6 sold 32.5 million copies before release, including preorders, and the game’s first-day performance was framed as a record across all platforms. Sensor Tower estimated that GTA 6 generated $2.36 billion in revenue before its November 19 release date, and the same market-intelligence estimate put pre-release demand at 24.6 million copies.
Sam Houser, president and founder of Rockstar Games, said, "It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI," as the company highlighted the response from players around the world. The launch also came with a Metacritic score of 99 out of 100, and the game was described as setting audience records on Twitch at launch.
Digital Box, Retail Pain
Richard Tewksbury, owner of Super Game Shack in the Silver Arcade in Leicester, said he had seen "embarrassing" sales because GTA 6 was released without a disc version. Tewksbury told the BBC, "I've only sold four copies on PS5 and one copy on Xbox," and he added that customers were not buying the code-in-box format. Tewksbury said, "It's because it's all digital - no-one sadly wants to buy a code in a box," and he described the impact on smaller stores as "just not great."
GameStop CEO Ryan Cohen said the "decision to move to all digital has hurt consumers" and his comments were tied to the shift away from discs. The BBC also quoted Rob Spence, a physical media collector and TikToker from Rothley in Leicestershire, saying there was "outrage" over more games being released without a disc.
What Comes Next
Take-Two and Rockstar Games framed the launch as a new chapter, and Sam Houser said, "We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn't be more excited to share this moment with you." The sources tied the next phase of the business to a broader rollout, with the game’s online multiplayer component described as crucial to turning the title into a recurring revenue engine. The reporting said investors were waiting for Take-Two to outline plans for a PC launch of GTA VI and an online version rollout, with some industry analysts expecting the PC launch and online mode in 2027.
Sensor Tower estimated that more than 80% of preorders were for the $100 Ultimate Edition, compared with the $80 standard version, and the Ultimate Edition was described as including extra cars and clothing and style customizations. The sources also placed the game’s cultural reach alongside other entertainment benchmarks, with the launch compared to Taylor Swift’s Eras tour generating roughly $2 billion in sales and Avengers: Endgame grossing $2.9 billion at the box office.
