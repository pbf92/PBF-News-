TITLE: GTA 6 Vice City pre-order DLC now available for all, but there’s a catch
OUTLET: Polygon.com

Fans who pre-ordered Grand Theft Auto 6 prior to its Nov. 19 release were treated to a bundle of exclusive in-game goodies. If you bought the game after release day or just forgot to get your copy in advance, you sadly missed out on these bonuses. Rockstar Games, however, is now giving everyone a chance to grab the Vintage Vice City Pack regardless of when you bought the game. You will, of course, have to shell out some more money for it.
As of Nov. 20, Rockstar has made the Grand Theft Auto 6: Vintage Vice City pack available on Xbox and PlayStation as a stand-alone DLC purchase. The DLC costs $4.99 on both platforms. That isn't too much on its own, but considering players already shelled out $80 to $100 (depending on whether they bought the standard or ultimate edition of the game) for GTA 6, some may not want to pay more for a handful of bonus items.
Those who bought the ultimate edition of GTA 6 got their own special items, ranging from unique vehicles and weapons to exclusive hairstyles for Jason and Lucia. A lot of players are probably enjoying those benefits right now, considering Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick revealed prior to the game's release that the ultimate edition was actually outselling the standard edition.
All GTA 6: Vintage Vice City Pack DLC items
The Vice City DLC is themed around '80s-inspired retro aesthetics. Think the original Miami Vice TV series or the upcoming reboot rather than the 2000s Michael Mann film. Here's everything you get when you purchase the pack:
- '55 Vapid Stanier: Cruise Shore Drive in this classic sedan and store it just a stone's throw from the glistening sands of Ocean Beach in the Shore Court garage, complete with a weapon locker plus a secure place to deposit stolen goods to be fenced.
- Outfits and Hairstyles: Dress for excess with Jason’s effortlessly chic linen suit in vintage pastel, complemented by the cut and coif of the decade of decadence. Show everyone the world is yours in Lucia’s red sequin mini dress and curls.
- Exclusive Weapon Pattern: Adorn most guns with a tropical pattern inspired by Tommy Vercetti's iconic palm tree button-up.
Items in the Vintage Vice City Pack won't all be available as soon as the DLC is purchased; instead, they will become available as the main story progresses.
