TITLE: It Only Took 24 Hours For ‘GTA 6’ To Beat ‘Avengers: Endgame’s Lifetime Box Office
OUTLET: https://103weup.com/

Remember that protest, “no physical, no buy”? Well, it appears it didn’t work, and gamers put GTA 6 on a fast track to make it the most profitable piece of entertainment ever.
Take-Two Interactive confirmed the official sales numbers for GTA 6, revealing it moved 32.5 million units before its official release on Nov. 19 and brought in an estimated $3.79 billion in revenue through launch.
According to Rockstar Games, those numbers signify that its preorder sales record across all consoles made GTA 6 the “biggest entertainment launch in history.”
“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” said Rockstar Games president and cofounder Sam Houser in a press release. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world, and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”
GTA 6 Dominated Playtime on Both The PS5 & XBOX Series X/S Consoles
Circana’s senior director Matt Piscatella reports the game drew the spotlight for engagement on the PS5 and Xbox Series X/S during the first 24 hours after release.
Piscatella reports that 65 percent of PS5 players in the US played on launch day, along with 56 percent of Xbox gamers globally, and that players across both consoles averaged 494 minutes of playtime. Fortnite was the second-most-played game, with just 10 percent of active users across both PlayStation and XBOX.
“To put this into perspective, Grand Theft Auto IV sold 25 million units across five years. Grand Theft Auto V sold 11 million copies on its first day and generated $800 million,” Niko Partners analyst Daniel Ahmad wrote on X (formerly Twitter). “And that’s comparing it to GTA. Other games don’t come close.”
GTA 6 sold well on its first day, surpassing Hollywood’s biggest blockbuster, Avengers: Endgame, which currently holds the box office record at $2.94 billion following multiple theatrical runs.
The game is selling so well that its day-one revenue equals the combined box office of The Odyssey and Avengers: Infinity War.
