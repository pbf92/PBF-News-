BioCardia Disclosed Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard
On November 20, 2026, Nasdaq notified BioCardia that its stockholders' equity of $835,000 was below the $2.5 million minimum required by Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(b)(1). The company has until January 4, 2027, to submit a plan to regain compliance; if Nasdaq accepts it, the company may receive up to 180 calendar days from the notice date to demonstrate compliance. The notice does not immediately affect trading, and the stock will continue to trade on the Nasdaq Capital Market under BCDA.
Original SEC Filing: BioCardia, Inc. [ BCDA ] - 8-K - Nov. 20, 2026
