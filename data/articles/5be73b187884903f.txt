TITLE: Where to Find The Pegassi Toros in GTA 6—Randy’s Wishlist Car Locations
OUTLET: Insider Gaming

If you’re looking for the Pegassi Toros in GTA 6 to complete Randy’s wishlist, we have you covered. Luckily, the Pegassi Toros is one of the easiest cars to obtain on Randy’s wishlist because it doesn’t require any fighting, sneaking around, or anything of the like. Instead, it’s as simple as going to it, unlocking it, and driving off!
Pegassi Toros Location in GTA 6
The Pegassi Toros isn’t like most of the cars on Randy’s list; it’s a bit slower, so make sure your car doesn’t lose momentum while you’re driving, because it takes a while to accelerate and the tracker could trigger.
To find the Pegassi Toros location, head to the Pegassi Residences on Bayside Dr, Tequesta Retreat, Vice City, Vice-Dale County. You can see the location on the map below.
There will be some security in the area, and sometimes they might spot you, but in our case, we simply found the vehicle parked outside the building.
Clone the key, hop in, and drive off. It’s that simple!
As always with our car guides, make sure you repair the vehicle at the gas station across from Randy’s garage. Randy will give you a bonus of $7500 for the car in immaculate condition, so it’s well worth fixing it up beforehand!
