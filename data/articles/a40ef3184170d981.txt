TITLE: Should You Buy Anthropic Stock Now That It's Public?
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Anthropic (NASDAQ:ANT) is officially a publicly traded company. The artificial intelligence (AI) developer behind Claude made its Nasdaq debut Thursday, with shares beginning trading just before noon Eastern time at $111.50.
The company priced its initial public offering at $83 per share, but that demand pushed the opening price much higher.

I'm bullish on Anthropic's long-term prospects. I think it could be an outstanding investment, and I fully intend to own shares at some point. But am I buying today, or any time this week?
Absolutely not.
A great company doesn't guarantee a great investment in the early days
The problem with big tech stock IPOs is that the stock price can be very volatile in the first few days – or even weeks. And that can lead to some big losses for investors who can't wait to get a piece of a hot new tech stock.
Space Exploration Technologies (NASDAQ:SPCX) went public in June at $135 per share and opened trading at $150. Within days, shares surged above $225 before retreating. At this writing, it's trading close to $145. Meta Platforms (NASDAQ:META), then known as Facebook, went public at $38 in 2012. By September, shares had fallen to $17.55.
Image source: Getty Images.
I wouldn't be surprised if Anthropic suffered the same fate. And to be honest, Anthropic's Claude personal assistant agrees. I used my personal Claude account and asked it, "Based on your knowledge of how IPOs work, and how IPO stocks tend to move in the first few days of trading, do you think retail investors should buy Anthropic stock as quickly as they can when trading opens?"
Here's what I got back:
      "Anthropic makes me, so I'm biased, and I'm not a financial advisor. Historically, retail buyers at an IPO's open often pay post-pop prices, and hyped IPOs tend to underperform. If you want ANT, consider waiting for volatility to settle, using limit orders, and investing only what you can lose."
I have to agree.
The long-term investment picture is promising
Anthropic has loads of potential. It generated $17 billion in revenue during the third quarter, more than 12 times what it reported a year earlier. Its annual revenue run rate reached $81 billion in October.
Those numbers help explain why investors are so excited about Anthropic. Its Claude models have become enormously popular with businesses, particularly for software development, and the company has been growing at a pace few businesses have ever achieved.
