TITLE: Oxford beat Spurs U21s to seal EFL Trophy progress
OUTLET: BBC Sport

- Published
Aaron Ramsey's Oxford United beat Tottenham Hotspur Under-21s 2-1 to secure one of the final two Jersey Mike's Trophy knockout spots.
Oxford knew avoiding defeat by a margin of four goals or more would be enough to seal progression from Southern Group A alongside already-qualified Exeter City.
They trailed to Tyler Tingey's early opener on Tuesday, which gave Spurs' youngsters a glimmer of hope of pulling off that mini-miracle.
But Jun'ai Byfield's own goal and 18-year-old Jack Lee's late winner ensured ex-Arsenal man Ramsey got one over his old north London rivals as Oxford won to finish top of the group.
Elsewhere, Fleetwood's decisive Northern Group G match at home to Leeds United Under-21s was put back 24 hours because of a waterlogged pitch.
The two sides will now meet on Wednesday to decide who progresses alongside group leaders Oldham Athletic to complete the line-up for the knockout stages.
There were wins for League Two pair Tranmere Rovers and Salford City over Premier League sides in Tuesday's other two ties, which were virtually dead rubbers.
Tranmere, who had already sealed their last-32 spot, came from behind to beat eliminated Everton Under-21s 2-1 with goals from David Kamara and Charlie Veevers in Northern Group E.
Both Salford City and Sunderland Under-21s had already been eliminated from Northern Group A but goals from Paolo Mariani, Brandon Cooper and Kyrell Malcolm gave the Ammies a 3-2 win despite Jaydon Jones' Black Cats double.
Of the 16 Premier League under-21 sides to enter the competition, with one placed in each group, only six have progressed to the knockout rounds, although Leeds can still make that number seven.
The last-32 draw will take place on Wednesday at 16:30 GMT. Ties will take place in the week commencing 7 December.
All 16 group winners are guaranteed a home tie, with fixtures remaining regionalised until the semi-final stage.
