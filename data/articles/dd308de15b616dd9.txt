Anthropic IPO: the key numbers and timeline, from the $83 pricing to the first day of trading
A reference guide to the record listing of the Claude maker: what it sold, what it raised, how the stock traded and who owns it.
Key takeaways
- Anthropic sold 980 million Class A shares at $83 on Nov. 18, 2026, raising $81.34 billion, the largest IPO on record.
- The stock opened at $111.50 on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on Nov. 19 and closed its first session at $97.17, up 17.1%.
- Amazon and Google will hold 14.6% and 13.4% of Anthropic's capital stock, while a founders' entity controls 50.1% of the vote.
Anthropic, PBC, the developer of the Claude AI models, sold 980 million Class A shares at $83 each on Nov. 18, 2026, raising $81.34 billion in the largest initial public offering on record. The stock began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on Nov. 19 under the ticker ANT and closed its first session at $97.17, up 17.1%.
How was the deal sized and priced? Anthropic had marketed the shares at $70 to $78 and planned to sell 825 million of them, the Associated Press reported, before raising both the price and the share count. The final prospectus shows Anthropic itself sold 956,351,354 shares and selling stockholders sold 23,648,646. Each of the seven co-founders sold 3,378,378 shares. The AP said the deal topped the roughly $75 billion SpaceX raised in June.
Where did the money go? Proceeds to Anthropic before expenses were $78.84 billion, after an underwriting discount of about 56 cents a share, or $550 million in total. Selling stockholders received $1.95 billion. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley led a syndicate that also included J.P. Morgan, Citigroup, Barclays, Wells Fargo, BofA Securities, Deutsche Bank and RBC Capital Markets. The banks hold a 30-day option to buy up to 147 million more shares from Anthropic, which CNBC calculated would add about $12.2 billion.
Is there money beyond the IPO? Yes. Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom, Google, Micron Technology, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners agreed to buy $26.0 billion of non-voting Class N stock in a private placement at the $83 IPO price, scheduled to close right after the offering. Those shares are not registered and are not covered by the underwriters' lock-up agreements, but they are restricted securities under the Securities Act.
What is Anthropic worth? At $83 a share, the AP put the valuation at about $1.5 trillion. On a fully diluted basis the stock was worth nearly $2.01 trillion at the open and about $1.8 trillion at the close, CNBC reported; NBC News put the closing figure at about $1.76 trillion. CNBC said that made Anthropic the seventh most valuable US company.
How did the first day go? Trading opened at about 11:50 a.m. New York time at $111.50, 34% above the offer price, according to NBC News. More than 846 million shares changed hands by the close, CNBC said, more than SpaceX's 522 million on its first day. Retail investors received about 15% of the offering, below the roughly 20% Anthropic had targeted. The S&P 500, the Nasdaq and the Dow each closed more than 2% lower that day, and Anthropic slipped more than 2.5% in after-hours trading, NBC reported.
Who owns the company? After the offering, Amazon and Google will hold about 14.6% and 13.4% of Anthropic's outstanding capital stock, mostly as non-voting Class N shares, the prospectus says. New public investors hold about 7.4% of the Class A and Class N shares. Control sits elsewhere: a Founder LLC owned by the seven co-founders holds a single Class F share carrying 50.1% of the vote, and the Long-Term Benefit Trust holds Class T stock that lets it elect a majority of the board.
What was the timeline? Anthropic filed confidentially with the Securities and Exchange Commission on June 1, 2026, made its S-1 registration statement public on Oct. 26 and amended it on Nov. 2, Nov. 10 and Nov. 17. The SEC declared the registration effective on Nov. 18, the shares were priced that evening, trading began Nov. 19 and the offering was scheduled to close on Nov. 20, according to EDGAR records and the company's pricing announcement.
Questions answered
How much did Anthropic raise in its IPO?
Anthropic and selling stockholders sold 980 million Class A shares at $83 each, for $81.34 billion in gross proceeds, according to the final prospectus. A separate $26.0 billion private placement of non-voting Class N shares was sold at the same price to seven technology companies.
Where did Anthropic stock close on its first day of trading?
Anthropic closed at $97.17 on Nov. 19, 2026, 17.1% above the $83 IPO price, after opening at $111.50. CNBC put its fully diluted value at the close at about $1.8 trillion.
Who are Anthropic's largest shareholders?
Amazon and Google will hold about 14.6% and 13.4% of Anthropic's outstanding capital stock after the offering, mostly in non-voting Class N shares. Voting control rests with a Founder LLC owned by the seven co-founders, which holds 50.1% of the vote, and the Long-Term Benefit Trust, which elects a majority of the board.
