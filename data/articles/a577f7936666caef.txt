TITLE: 5 Game of the Year nominees are up to 57% off in Loaded's Black Friday sale: GTA 6, 007 First Light and more
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Anyone looking to catch up on the biggest games of 2026 ahead of The Game Awards next month, or simply hoping to buy GTA 6 at a cheaper price, can now pick up all but one of this year’s six Game of the Year nominees at a heavily discounted price, thanks to Loaded's Black Friday sale.
The digital gaming retailer, formerly known as CDKeys, has taken up to 57 per cent off five of the six titles competing for the prestigious award this year, of course including Grand Theft Auto VI, which hasn't even been out for a full week — but also top-tier triple-A titles from earlier in the year, like 007 First Light and Resident Evil Requiem.
Loaded's Black Friday sale across digital PC, PS5, and Xbox platforms is set to run until Tuesday, 1 December, with over 800 deals available to gamers in the UK, US, and other regions — across digital games, console memberships, and gift cards.
Five Game of the Year 2026 nominees are on sale at Loaded
Of the six games nominated for Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2026, Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is the only one missing from this particular selection of deals.
The remaining five are available at the following prices:
- Grand Theft Auto VI (Xbox Series X|S): from £55.99 / $67.49 at Loaded (20 per cent off £69.99 / 16 per cent off $79.99)
- Grand Theft Auto VI + Vice City Pack (PS5, UK): £64.99 at Loaded (7 per cent off £69.99)
- Grand Theft Auto VI + Vice City Pack PS5 (US): $69.99 at Loaded (13 per cent off $79.99)
- Grand Theft Auto VI Ultimate Edition (Xbox Series X|S): £69.99 / $85.99 at Loaded (22 per cent off £89.99 / 14 per cent off $99.99)
- 007 First Light: from £29.99 / $39.89 at Loaded (57 per cent off £69.99 / $93.09)
- Resident Evil Requiem: from £27.49 / $36.59 at Loaded (50 per cent off £54.99 / $73.09)
- Control Resonant: from £26.49 / $35.19 at Loaded (47 per cent off £49.99 / $66.49)
- Phantom Blade Zero: from £37.99 / $50.49 at Loaded (24 per cent off £49.99 / $66.49)

Although Rockstar's latest release leads this year's Game Awards nominations with eight in total, the biggest saving amongst the GOTY contenders comes from IO Interactive's 007 First Light, with as much as £40 knocked off the James Bond origin story.
As we mentioned for our original 007 First Light review back in May, the game is an ‘exquisite taste of slick spy action and blockbuster storytelling, and is fully what a Bond game should be’ — and already being able to grab it for under half its launch price only six months later feels insane.
Meanwhile, Capcom's return to Raccoon City in Resident Evil Requiem is now half-price for as little as £27.49, whilst Remedy Entertainment's supernatural action RPG, Control Resonant, is the cheapest of the five nominated games on offer — available for £26.49 on PC at the lowest.
In the case of GTA 6, PlayStation owners can actually save even more through deals on PlayStation gift cards, though buying the game directly does also come with the additional Vintage Vice City Pack.
Other major gaming discounts in Loaded's Black Friday sale
Outside of those competing for Game of the Year, several other big releases and established favourites have received sizeable discounts, including some of the year's newest sports, racing, and multiplayer games.
Amongst the other highlights of Loaded's sale are:
- Ready or Not: from £13.79 / $18.29 at Loaded (69 per cent off £44.99 / $59.79)
- NBA 2K27: from £20.99 / $28.99 at Loaded (65 per cent off £59.99 / 59 per cent off $69.99)
- Minecraft: Java & Bedrock Edition: from £11.99 / $15.99 at Loaded (52 per cent off £24.99 / $33.19)
- Star Wars: Galactic Racer: from £29.99 / $39.89 at Loaded (40 per cent off £49.99 / $66.49)
- Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4: from £42.99 / $57.19 at Loaded (39 per cent off £69.99 / 18 per cent off $69.99)
For anyone who missed last year's Game of the Year winner, Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is also available from only £16.99 / $22.59, with a 60 per cent discount bringing Sandfall Interactive's acclaimed RPG below £17.
Keep in mind that all of these games are digital downloads rather than physical copies, with the majority of the PC deals redeemable through Steam.
