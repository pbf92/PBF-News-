21Shares Forecasts Bitcoin to Hit New Highs in 2027 Predictions List
Bitcoin BTCUSD could reach a new all-time high in 2027 as institutional demand accelerates, while stablecoins, tokenized assets and privacy cryptocurrencies to emerge as major growth drivers, according to 21Shares’ 2027 Investment Outlook.
21Shares Predictions For 2027
21Shares outlined 10 predictions for digital assets, arguing that institutional adoption and improving regulatory clarity could support a broader market recovery.
- Bitcoin: Reaches a new all-time high without marking its cycle top.
- Regulation: 15 of 21 G20 members establish digital asset regulatory frameworks.
- Stablecoins: Monthly active addresses exceed 100 million, with annual card spending reaching $25 billion.
- DeFi: Real-world assets and fintech distribution contribute 15% of gross revenue, up from 8%.
- Tokenization: Trading volume climbs to $15 trillion from an estimated $6.3 trillion in 2026.
- On chain options: Annual trading volume surpasses $100 billion, compared with approximately $30 billion in 2026.
- Prediction markets: Annual volume could exceed $1 trillion, although growth slows without major sporting events.
- Privacy cryptocurrencies: Sector market capitalization triples from $31 billion to more than $100 billion. Since Bitcoin’s October 2025 peak, Zcash ZECUSD and Monero XMRUSD have gained 467% and 52%, respectively, while Bitcoin and Ethereum ETHUSD declined 32% and 43%, according to the report.
- AI and blockchain: Autonomous AI agents expand their use of on chain payments, lending and trading.
- Quantum security: Exchanges move at least 50,000 BTC to addresses that conceal public keys.
Bitcoin’s Strength Holds Firm
Bitcoin has fallen more than 50% from its October 2025 peak of almost $126,000 to a June 2026 low of $59,000.
Despite the downturn, ETFs, corporations and sovereign investors accumulated 1.75 million BTC since 2024, roughly 3.4 times the amount mined during that period.
Even during the bear market, institutional buyers absorbed 180,000 BTC against 122,000 newly mined coins.
21Shares expects Bitcoin to surpass its previous record in 2027 but does not anticipate a cycle peak until later, potentially following the 2028 halving.
