TITLE: Man Utd Hit With Bizarre Altay Bayındır Problem After One Appearance
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

How will Mauricio Pochettino handle his first test as Man Utd boss against Liverpool?
Mauricio Pochettino's arrival at Manchester United sparks the beginning of a new era at Old Trafford. The 54-year-old manager inherits a struggling 18th place Man United side ahead of game week 11 with much change needed to improve.
With the January transfer window fast approaching, questions grow over how active United will be with definite areas in the squad needing improvement and the club's finances at the forefront of many fans' minds.
The solution may lie in loan deals to provide a short term fix to United's long term problems giving Pochettino enough time until the summer, where United can make bigger moves in regard to both their incomings and outgoings.
However, one United loanee could see his time away cut short after very limited game-time leaving the loan meaningless in hindsight.
Bayindir Loan Could Be Terminated
After a total of 17 competitive appearances as United's backup goalkeeper, turned first choice, Altay Bayindir was clearly not the right man for the job. The Turkey international struggled on the big stage and found himself to be at the root of many defensive mistakes.
Following the arrival of Senne Lammens last season from Royal Antwerp, Bayindir was left searching for a new start after Lammens quickly made the starting goalkeeper position his own.
Faro de Vigo reports that Celta Vigo could see Altay Bayindir return to United in January after new plans for the squad have been formed.
The goalkeeper is not seen as an essential asset to the plan after a disappointing first six months, which is somewhat bizarre after the La Liga side recruited him as they were in desperate need for a new goalkeeper.
Bayindir landed on a move to Celta Vigo in the 26/27 summer window in the hopes to revive his confidence, but the move has been nothing short of underwhelming. The 6'6 giant made only one appearance for Celta Vigo this season, in a 5-0 win in the first round of the Copa Del Rey and could be on his way out already.
Man Utd Loanees Struggling
While some loan moves have been very beneficial to United players such as Amad Diallo to Sunderland or Marcus Rashford to Aston Villa and Barcelona, others have often struggled to return and claim a place in the squad.
This season alone has seen a series of failed loan spells at United with Bayindir's struggles and Chido Obi also finding first team football hard to come by during his loan at Willem II on loan.
The exciting young forwards has yet to start a game in the Netherlands and raises concerns for his future at United. Perhaps a new loan deal in English football would be more beneficial for him.
United need to be more considerate in their loan deals with conditions in place to maximise the benefit that their loanees will gain, particularly if United aim to make significant money from eventual permanent sales.
