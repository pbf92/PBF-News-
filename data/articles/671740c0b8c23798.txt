TITLE: Major update as firearms arrest made after homes evacuated in Droylsden
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Major update as firearms arrest made after homes evacuated in Droylsden
Police said 'enquiries have developed' in the probe
A man has been re-arrested on suspicion of possession of a suspected firearm as part of an investigation in Tameside.
The probe was launched on Wednesday (November 18) after 'potentially hazardous substances' were found at a house on Shelley Grove in Droylsden. Police said 23 homes were evacuated on the street as a cordon was put in place and bomb disposal experts attended the scene.
Searches took place at a property on the street over the weekend following the discovery of a suspected explosives stash. A man in his 20s, a man in his 70s and a woman in her 60s were taken into police custody.

Today (November 24), a spokesperson for Greater Manchester Police said the man, aged 29, has since been further arrested on suspicion of possession of a suspected firearm and is in police custody. He was previously arrested on suspicion of criminal damage, arson and possession of ammunition.
The man and woman arrested on suspicion of unlawfully possessing ammunition and explosive substances remain on police bail. The Manchester Evening News understands the incident is not being treated as terror-related.
Yesterday police confirmed residents had been able to return to their homes after the evacuation. Evidential searches will continue at the suspect's address over the coming days.
Detective Superintendent Rick Arthern said: "Our investigation is continuing and we have re-arrested the 29-year-old man as our enquiries have developed. This incident caused significant disruption to people in Droylsden and I would like to thank the community for their cooperation and patience with the emergency services during this investigation.
"Over six days, a total of 23 households were displaced from the area. All residents have now returned to their homes as the cordon has been lifted.
"Our evidential search of the suspects home address continues over the next few days, I would like to reassure the community that there is no wider risk and all items which resulted in the evacuation have been safely removed in conjunction with our partners.
"We will continue to investigate the circumstances of this incident and would encourage anyone who has information relating to it can contact us on 101, quoting the log number 1906 of 18/11/2026. Alternatively, you can contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111."
