TITLE: Manchester United CEO delivers double transfer update as Bruno Fernandes talks confirmed
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United CEO Omar Berrada has confirmed the club is in active negotiations with Bruno Fernandes over a new contract.
He also admitted United did "absolutely everything" to attempt to convince JJ Gabriel to stay at the club.
United captain Fernandes' current contract expires at the end of this season, and it has been reported that he has already rejected two offers of a new deal.
As the club has struggled in recent years, Fernandes has regularly been able to nick results with inspiring performances. Last season alone, he was responsible for nine goals and 21 assists in the Premier League.
As such, Berrada is desperate to tie him down to a new deal, but kept his cards close to his chest when discussing a potential new contract.
Speaking in a rare interview with Sky Sports, he said: "We have a tremendous amount of respect for Bruno. We're very fortunate to have him as a captain.
"What he's done for this team over the last six, seven years is absolutely incredible. Last year he won, I think, all the individual awards you can win.
"And we're very proud to have him as captain. It's true that we're having conversations about his new contract, but I prefer to keep that private. I think it's something that needs to be discussed behind the scenes between him and the club."
One player United have failed to convince to stay, meanwhile, is JJ Gabriel. The 16-year-old winger is widely considered to be one of the game's brightest talents, but left United stunned earlier this year when he lodged a surprise transfer request.
He has since signed with a new agency and said his goodbyes to United, with Real Madrid expected to win the race to take him on. Gabriel will leave United without having made a first-team appearance.
"We did everything. Absolutely everything," said Berrada, asked how United attempted to convince Gabriel to stay.
"Let's not forget with JJ, he's a very young boy with a lot of media attention around him. Our primary concern is his well-being.
"Credit to the academy and people that have been here for many years, they helped him in his development. We still think this is the best place for him to continue his career and to grow.
"Obviously, him and his family have taken the decision to leave. We can only wish him the best.
"We want players that want to be here, that feel being at Manchester United is a privilege."
