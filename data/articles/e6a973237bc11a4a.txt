TITLE: Enphase Energy Director Sold Shares Worth Over $23M
OUTLET: TradingView

Enphase Energy Director Sold Shares Worth Over $23M
Thurman J. Rodgers, a director, reported selling 774,137 shares through the Rodgers Massey Revocable Living Trust on Nov. 17, 2026, at a weighted average price of $23.3185 per share, and 225,863 shares through the trust on Nov. 18 at a weighted average price of $22.59 per share. The total sale amount was $23,154,808. After the transactions, Rodgers directly owned 4,464 shares and indirectly owned 301,719 shares through the Rodgers Massey Revocable Living Trust, 4,100 shares through the Valeta Massey 2012 Irrevocable Trust, 4,100 shares through the TJ Rodgers 2012 Irrevocable Trust, and 85,200 shares through the Rodgers Family Freedom and Free Markets Charitable Trust. Rodgers disclaimed beneficial ownership of the charitable trust shares except to the extent of his and his spouse’s pecuniary interest. The sales were undertaken to provide liquidity for capital commitments related to private portfolio funds.
