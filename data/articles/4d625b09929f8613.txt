TITLE: Shopify's Global Expansion Gains Pace: Can It Fuel More Growth?
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Shopify Inc. SHOP is strengthening its global revenue growth prospects as expanding international operations and localized commerce solutions help merchants reach new customers. International gross merchandise volume (GMV) increased 32% year over year in third-quarter 2026, while European GMV grew 33% in constant currency, compared with 28% growth in North America. Over the past three years, Shopify merchants' online GMV in Europe expanded at a 37% compound annual rate, significantly outpacing the region's 8% e-commerce market growth and doubling their share of European e-commerce.
The company is lowering merchant adoption barriers through local-currency subscription billing, introduced in 10 additional countries, and region-specific commerce solutions. During the quarter, Shopify launched WhatsApp sign-in and marketing tools, introduced a localized Shop app in Japan and expanded payment options through OXXO in Mexico. Shop Pay Installments also expanded into Australia. These initiatives should improve shopping convenience and support merchant acquisition across international markets.
International expansion also strengthens Shopify's transaction-based revenue model. Merchant Solutions revenues increased 35% year over year to $2.91 billion, supported by higher GMV and payments penetration. Foreign-exchange-related payment revenues also benefited from the company's expanding international scale.
However, currency fluctuations, international trade restrictions and evolving regulations could pose challenges to Shopify's global expansion and financial performance. Nevertheless, strong overseas GMV growth, localized commerce solutions and increasing payment monetization provide a solid foundation for sustained international growth. Reflecting these growth prospects, the Zacks Consensus Estimate projects revenues to increase 32.4% year over year in 2026 and 25% in 2027, indicating expectations of continued strong revenue momentum.  
How Are SHOP's Competitors Positioned in Global Commerce?
Block XYZ competes with Shopify through its Square commerce and payments ecosystem, supporting merchants across physical and digital channels. In third-quarter 2026, Block reported 26% year-over-year growth in Square's international gross payment volume, reflecting strong overseas merchant activity. Block is expanding distribution partnerships across Europe, Australia and Canada, intensifying competition for international merchants.
GoDaddy GDDY competes with Shopify through its website-building, online storefront and integrated commerce solutions. In third-quarter 2026, GoDaddy's Applications and Commerce revenues increased 9% year over year to $524.3 million, while international revenues rose 3.1%. GoDaddy is strengthening its AI-powered website capabilities through Airo, potentially attracting merchants seeking simplified digital commerce solutions and supporting its global expansion prospects.
