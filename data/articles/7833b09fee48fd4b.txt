TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino left 'disappointed' as Man Utd boss fumes at VAR after Leny Yoro red card U-turn
OUTLET: Yahoo News Singapore

Mauricio Pochettino voiced his anger at VAR following the reversal of Leny Yoro's early dismissal against Liverpool over the weekend.
The Manchester United defender received his marching orders after only eight minutes from referee Sam Barrott for fouling Alexander Isak. Liverpool proceeded to demolish their weakened bitter rivals 4-0 in Pochettino's debut match in charge, leaving the Red Devils stuck in the Premier League drop zone with just one victory from 11 matches.
The Football Association announced on Tuesday that Yoro's red card, issued for denying a clear goalscoring chance, had been rescinded "following a successful claim of wrongful dismissal".
And Pochettino voiced his anger at the damaging decision after Manchester United's successful challenge, questioning why VAR didn't step in at the time.
"Very disappointed because this kind of thing can never happen," said the Argentine following the outcome of Yoro's appeal in his pre-Sporting Lisbon press conference, as per The Mirror.
"We can accept the referee can make a mistake, but if we have VAR, what were they doing? Are we going to repeat the game 11 v 11? When we were, you need to take your time to review the situation."
Howard Webb, the chief of Ref Pro, defended Barrott's decision to send Yoro off despite it being rescinded, saying it was "not wrong" for him to do so. Speaking on 'Mic'd Up', he said: "We know it's clearly a foul. I don't think there's any dispute about that. And we also know that it's outside the penalty area. We're thinking about the sanction, aren't we? That's why we're talking about this.
"Is it a denial of an obvious goal scoring opportunity? Four considerations officials have to think about. direction of play, it's definitely going towards the goal. It's not going out wide. Proximity from goal, well, it' just outside the penalty area. the location of other defenders. Well, there's only Yoro there. The keeper is moving back towards his own goal.

"He has to put the brakes on and possibly come back out. the reason we're talking about it is the fourth consideration, that's control or likelihood of keeping on or gaining control of the ball. Isak's got control of the ball, he's got possession, he's got the touch, he knocks it past Yoro. He'smoving at pace at that moment, and a foul comes in and knocks him down.
"And there's a question mark, I guess, around whether or not he would get onto that ball before Senne Lammens. Now, in the moment, the referee looked at it and felt he would.
"That was the referee's call made on the field, and then the var checks that to see whether that decision made on the field was clearly and obviously incorrect, and formed the opinion that it wasn't, that if there was any doubt, then it was doubt.
"It wasn't going to be something that was clearly and obviously wrong and therefore allowed that decisive on-field decision to stand. and i think by and large, most people would see that as a correct decision, with Isak moving quickly on towards that ball. The alternative view is that Lammens might get there first, but i think it's a little bit debatable, I guess. Certainly not clearly wrong."
