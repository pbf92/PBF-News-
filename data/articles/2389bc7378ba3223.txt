TITLE: Life360 Director Sold Shares Worth Over $7.2M
OUTLET: TradingView

Life360 Director Sold Shares Worth Over $7.2M
Life360 Director Chris Hulls sold 130,000 shares directly on Nov. 24, 2026, at a weighted average price of $40.80 per share, and 50,000 shares directly on Nov. 25 at $39.25 per share. The total sale amount was $7,266,009. After the transactions, Hulls directly owned 374,511 shares, including restricted stock units, and indirectly owned 585,936 shares through the Rose Hulls 2023 Irrevocable Trust, the Robin Hulls 2023 Irrevocable Trust and the Mckenzie Hulls 2023 Irrevocable Trust, with 195,312 shares held by each trust.
