TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino still waiting for first win with United after Champions League draw
OUTLET: The Japan Times

Lisbon – Mauricio Pochettino must wait for his first win as Manchester United coach after a wild 2-2 Champions League draw with Sporting Lisbon on Wednesday, with the host equalizing thanks to a goalkeeping error from United’s Senne Lammens.
The Belgian stopper’s poor handling allowed Luis Suarez to complete a brace for Sporting, canceling out United playmaker Bruno Fernandes’ double against his former side.
United gave away two penalties in the first half, the second converted by Suarez, before Fernandes equalized from the spot early in the second half.
