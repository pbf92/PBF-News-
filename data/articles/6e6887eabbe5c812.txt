TITLE: Bitcoin sentiment hits new low as Jeff Park calls this cycle his toughest yet
OUTLET: TradingView

Bitcoin sentiment hits new low as Jeff Park calls this cycle his toughest yet
Bitcoin is down about 52% from its peak. Compared with past cycles, that is a mild decline.
That investor is Jeff Park. On November 25, 2026, he called the current stretch the hardest Bitcoin cycle he has lived through.
"I have to admit this is becoming psychologically the toughest bitcoin cycle yet for me."
The numbers explain part of the gloom. Bitcoin topped out at approximately $126,198 on October 6, 2025.
The bear market that followed has now run for over 411 days, the longest in Bitcoin's history.
The drawdown from that peak is about 52-56%, depending on where you mark the latest price. Recent lows landed around $55,254 as of November 21, 2026.
Park's frustration goes beyond simple losses. In his reading, the macro backdrop looks almost tailor-made for Bitcoin's original pitch.
Institutional trust is low, surveillance ambitions are high and markets are tightly correlated. Bitcoin advocates have long said those are the conditions in which a neutral, scarce asset would shine.
Bitcoin has not separated itself from other assets. Holders are watching the story they signed up for unfold while the asset itself sits on the sidelines.
Park pointed to the lack of returns and the absence of compelling alternatives as the main reasons the cycle feels so draining.
He has not given up on the case. Park reaffirmed that Bitcoin still offers an uncensorable exit route and a fixed supply. What it lacks right now, in his view, is price performance and a catalyst to change the narrative.
Sentiment data backs up Park's account. The Fear & Greed Index dropped to single digits or the low teens during mid-to-late November 2026.
Money has been leaving too. US spot Bitcoin ETFs have seen billions of dollars in outflows over the same period.
In October 2026, Galaxy Research argued that the bear market had ended back in June.
The firm has since retracted that view.
Galaxy Research now flags $58,525 as a key level. If Bitcoin falls below it, the firm warns the price could slide further toward $48,500.
With recent lows around $55,254, that threshold is not a distant hypothetical. The market has already been trading near it.
For traders, the Galaxy Research levels give a clear map. A sustained break below $58,525 could bring more selling, with $48,500 as the next reference point the firm has identified.
ETF flows are worth watching closely. A switch from outflows to inflows would be one of the clearest signs that institutional mood is turning.
For now, the market is caught between a thesis that looks increasingly relevant and a price chart that does not reflect it. That tension, more than the percentage decline, is what is wearing on holders like Park.
