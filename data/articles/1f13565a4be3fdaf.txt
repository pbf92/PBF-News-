TITLE: Pochettino slams 'driverless car' VAR and 'illegal' Liverpool goal vs Man Utd
OUTLET: The Sun

MAURICIO POCHETTINO unleashed his fury over the Leny Yoro farce.
After the FA overturned Yoro’s red card in Sunday’s 4-0 defeat at Liverpool, Manchester United last night launched a stinging attack on ProRef chief Howard Webb for supporting ref Sam Barrott’s call.
Despite winning their appeal for wrongful dismissal, furious Red Devils boss Pochettino slammed the Prem’s VAR system as a “car without drivers”.
Pochettino, who saw his team thrashed at Anfield on his United managerial debut following Yoro’s sending off in the eighth minute, said: “I’m very disappointed as I think this type of thing never can happen.
“OK, we can accept the referee can make a mistake because of the emotional relationship with the game.
“Sometimes the view, the position, obviously can be affected by that.

“But if we have VAR, what were they doing?
“Are we going to repeat the game? Are we going to have the possibility to play with similar tools, eleven v eleven? No.
“I think we need to take the time to review the situation.
“We have an unbelievable car, but we need to be in the level to drive. You know, we need to drive in the best way.
“We love the technology, yes. We accept that the technology is there, yes. What are they doing?”
At 0-0, French centre-back Yoro was red-carded after he brought down Reds striker Alexander Isak, despite the Swede taking a heavy touch and with doubt over whether the ball would run to visiting keeper Senne Lammens.
Webb said on-pitch referee Barrott had made “the correct decision” on his Mic’d Up show and United intend to raise his comments with the Premier League and ProRef directly.
The FA’s independent regulatory commission — made up of a three-man panel — felt Yoro had not deliberately denied a goalscoring opportunity after United lodged an appeal on Monday.
Red Devils sources fumed that the ProRef interpretation of the incident was wrong — and claimed it was a “process error and a failure to interpret the rules correctly”.
The club flagged that on the VAR audio, there was “no meaningful challenge or consideration” after Yoro’s foul and disputed Webb’s assertion it was a subjective decision.
United believe they could prove that Isak would not have reached the ball before Lammens if he had hit his top speed.
Webb felt Barrott’s call was not “clearly and obviously wrong”, so the VAR John Brooks allowed his decision to stand.
Webb added: “It’s a really decisive decision by the referee. Isak’s got control of the ball, he’s got possession and knocks the ball past Yoro.
“He’s moving at pace and a foul comes in and knocks him down. There’s a question mark, I guess, around whether or not he would have got to that ball before Lammens.

Pochettino, with 500 Prem games under his belt, was smacked the reality of the task he faces after Leny Yoro’s eighth minute red card killed his game plan.
United have the worst defence in Europe with 33 goals conceded in 16 games and the same individual mistakes and shambolic defending that Michael Carrick were at bay.
United are one point above rock-bottom Crystal Palace with just eight points out of a possible 33 and travel to Sporting CP in Europe on Wednesday.
