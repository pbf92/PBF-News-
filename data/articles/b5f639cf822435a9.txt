TITLE: Manchester United manager costs near £100m since Ferguson retired
OUTLET: Mezha.net

Manchester United manager costs near £100m since Ferguson retired
Photo source: BBC Sport
The club has paid £95m to hire and dismiss managers since Sir Alex Ferguson retired in 2013. The total is likely to exceed £100m after the costs of Mauricio Pochettino’s staff and Michael Carrick’s dismissal are included.
Manchester United borrowed another £10m three days after confirming Pochettino as their new head coach. In a filing accompanying its first-quarter financial results, the club said it would owe US Soccer £7.5m in compensation to hire the Argentine. United announced on 13 November that Pochettino had agreed to a two-and-a-half-year deal; the loan was taken out on 16 November.
This is reported by BBC.
The loan was one of three confirmed after the 30 September first-quarter reporting date, bringing the amount owed on United’s rolling credit limit to £240m.
Carrick was sacked last week, with the club in the relegation zone and enduring its worst start to a season in 40 years. The former midfielder had signed a two-year contract in the summer after a successful six-month spell as caretaker manager last season. The cost of hiring Pochettino’s staff and dismissing Carrick has yet to be added to the £95m already paid to hire and fire managers since Ferguson’s retirement.
Pochettino is United’s ninth full-time manager since Ferguson, counting Ralf Rangnick, appointed in November 2021. The club has changed managers midway through each of the past three campaigns.
United also confirmed spending £5.7m to acquire or extend registrations for “certain players and members of coaching staff”.
The filing showed that the club owes £417m in outstanding transfer fees, with £181m due after one year. On Thursday, United said its substantial $750m debt was equivalent to £577.4m on 30 September.
