TITLE: How Much Money Has GTA 6 Made So Far? Launch Sales Explained
OUTLET: VICE

Grand Theft Auto VI is finally out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the first sales estimates are already in. Rockstar’s latest game appears to have made more by the end of launch day than GTA 5 did in its first three days. Here’s how much money GTA 6 sales has made so far and how many copies it’s estimated to have sold on each platform.
How Much Money Has GTA 6 Made So Far?
GTA 6 made $3.79 billion in digital sales by Thursday evening, November 19, according to Take-Two. Rockstar’s parent company also confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI sold over 32.5 million copies of the game.
Videos by VICE
The GTA 6 sales includes digital pre-orders and purchases made on launch day and covers both PlayStation and Xbox storefronts.
GTA 6 Sales So Far: How Many Copies Has It Sold?
GTA 6 sold 32.5 million copies worldwide during its launch. According to Sensor Tower, PS5 accounted for roughly three out of every four copies sold. Here’s how Sensor Tower’s launch-day estimate breaks down:
- PS5: 18.44 million copies sold and $1.77 billion in sales
- Xbox Series X|S: 6.15 million copies sold and $588 million in sales
The total was already climbing fast on launch day. On Thursday morning, Sensor Tower had Grand Theft Auto VI at an estimated 19.1 million copies and $1.84 billion in sales. By that evening, its estimate had climbed by roughly 5.5 million copies and $520 million.
How Do GTA 6 Launch Sales Compare to GTA 5?
Back in 2013, Take-Two announced that Grand Theft Auto V had made more than $800 million in worldwide retail sales on its first day. The game went on to pass $1 billion in its first three days on PS3 and Xbox 360.
That puts GTA 6’s estimated launch-day digital sales at nearly three times the $800 million milestone GTA 5 hit on day one. We’ll have a clearer picture of how the two launches stack up once Take-Two shares its own numbers.
