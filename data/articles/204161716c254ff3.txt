Vulcan Infrastructure & Power Receives Final Regulatory Approval for MIG Financing
Vulcan Infrastructure & Power received the final regulatory approval required under its subscription agreement with MIG, enabling changes to its financing securities.
Key Highlights:
- The September 10 senior secured convertible note issued to MIG is now convertible into Vulcan’s Class A common stock.
- The warrant issued to MIG is no longer subject to its prior beneficial ownership limitation.
- The board has not yet been reconstituted; Vulcan is working with MIG and Atlas to meet the agreements’ eight-director requirement.
Original SEC Filing: Vulcan Infrastructure & Power Inc. [ VIP ] - 8-K - Nov. 24, 2026
