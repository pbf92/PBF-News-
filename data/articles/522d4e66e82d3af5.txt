TITLE: KFC Offers a Fried Chicken 'GTA 6' Replacement Disc
OUTLET: Hypebeast

KFC Spain is celebrating the highly anticipated launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 with a unique limited-edition fried chicken giveaway
The fast-food chain has created The Replacement Disc to fill the empty space in the physical editions of the new video game
Fans can claim the edible disc and exclusive Colonel Sanders packaging by bringing their game cases to select restaurants
KFC Spain has officially unveiled “The Replacement Disc,” offering fans a unique fried chicken alternative to celebrate the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6. As Rockstar Games transitions away from traditional physical media by including only a digital download code in the game’s physical edition, the fast-food chain is stepping in to give gamers a tangible—and edible—addition to their empty cases.
The limited-edition promotion playfully addresses the ongoing shift toward digital distribution while rewarding dedicated fans. KFC has crafted a custom piece of fried chicken designed to substitute the missing physical disc. Adding to the novelty of the gaming crossover, the fast-food giant has also designed exclusive packaging that features Colonel Sanders on the cover, making the meal resemble a traditional video game of its own.
According to KFC Spain, the initiative was created as a direct response to collectors and players debating the future of boxed video games. Rather than offering a standard promotional item, the brand is providing a humorous twist on the modern unboxing experience. To claim the quirky prize, fans must bring their original GTA 6 game case to a participating location and present it to the staff.
The Replacement Disc promotion runs at ten participating KFC restaurants across Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, Zaragoza, and Málaga.
