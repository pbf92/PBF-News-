TITLE: GTA 6 is here: What to know about reviews, voice actors, multiplayer and PC release
OUTLET: Good Morning America

The highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) has arrived, after years of publisher Rockstar Games teasing fans with details about the sixth numbered entry in the massively popular video game franchise.
Where can I play GTA 6?
GTA 6 is available on Sony PlayStation 5 and Microsoft Xbox Series X|S.
What is the game about?
The GTA games take place in heavily hyperbolic versions of American cities, and focuses on characters working their way through the city's worlds of crime.
GTA 6 takes place in Vice City and Leonida -- inspired by Miami and Florida -- and follow Bonnie and Clyde-style criminal duo Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Lucia is the series' first female protagonist.
"Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them," a brief synopsis of the game reads. "But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida -- forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive."
What can I do in the game?
GTA 6 is a third-person action-adventure game that allows players to explore a massive, detailed open world on foot and in a wide array of vehicles.
You can undertake specific missions (which typically involve some kind of criminal activity), explore the city and its surroundings, engage in a wider array of activities (including skydiving and wrestling) and take on law enforcement (who will call reinforcements as the situation escalates).
This freedom is central to the series' appeal -- a concept known as emergent gameplay. Players can do what they like rather than stick to a linear path, and discover fun opportunities along the way. Each gameplay session can be tailored to your mood; you might follow the story, play some mini games, wander the game world and cause chaos, or a combination.
"I think that's a very escapist fantasy for a lot of people that the game really gives them a lot of ownership over," Bloomberg video game reporter Cecilia D'Anastasio told "GMA" on Friday.
You can play as Jason and Lucia, each of whom has a customizable appearance.
Are GTA 6 reviews out?
The review embargo broke on Wednesday, and GTA 6 has been met with universal acclaim from critics:
IGN: 10/10
GameSpot: 10/10
Game Informer: 10/10
Eurogamer: 5/5
GamesRadar: 5/5
Bloomberg's Jason Schreier highlighted the breadth of activities available to the player.
"In this send-up of Florida and of American culture, you can doomscroll in bed, pressure-wash a boat, fix a roof, flip fish fillets on a grill, play through several entire video games inside the video game (yes, really), listen to podcasts, sip cocktails by the pool and deadlift with perfect form," he wrote.
Writing for The New York Times, Yussef Cole highlighted the strength of the relationship between the game's leads.
"Partway through the game, Lucia and Jason take a walk down their hotel's sandy-white beach. They, naturally, lock hands," he wrote. "The gunfights and car chases and cartoonish gangster shenanigans can wait. They're distractions from the game's true achievement: the well-earned satisfaction of an authentic human bond."
In a review for NPR, Jason Sheehan emphasized the game's sense of freedom.
"At almost any point, you can walk away from the narrative for a while, call your better half, hop in your car and just drive," he wrote. "Everywhere you go, Leonida is alive around you in a way that no game has ever been before. There are a million stories here just waiting to be discovered or created. Pick one. See where it leads you."
At time of writing, GTA 6 is the top-rated game of all time on review aggregation site Metacritic, standing alongside the 1998 Nintendo 64 classic The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. GTA 5's score is based on based over 150 reviews, while Zelda's is based on 22.
How long does it take to beat GTA 6?
It takes around 63 hours to complete the game's story, according to HowLongToBeat, a community-driven website that tracks average video game completion times.
Getting 100% will take considerably longer, with guide site PlayStation Trophies saying the Platinum will take "200+ hours, easily."
Why is GTA 6 such a big deal?
GTA 6 follows more than a decade of anticipation since the 2013 release of Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5), which has sold 235 million copies, franchise owner Take-Two Interactive said in its fall 2026 investor presentation.
The series started on the original PlayStation and PC in 1997, when it generated controversy due to its violence. In 2001, Grand Theft Auto III came to PlayStation 2. It brought the series into 3D and once again caused controversy with violence and sexual content.
These are all the mainline GTA games:
- Grand Theft Auto (1997)
- Grand Theft Auto 2 (1999)
- Grand Theft Auto III (2001)
- Grand Theft Auto: Vice City (2002)
- Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas (2004)
- Grand Theft Auto IV (2008)
- Grand Theft Auto V (2013)
GTA 6 is set in the same Miami-inspired location as 2002's "Vice City" but is far more detailed and includes a huge area outside that urban environment.
In total, the franchise has sold 480 million copies, Take-Two noted.
The sixth numbered entry will almost certainly be the top selling game of the year and could become one of the highest selling games on all time.
"Grand Theft Auto VI is expected to earn more money than 'The Odyssey' very quickly," D'Anastasio told "GMA."
Both Sony and Microsoft confirmed the game is the most preordered in the history of their respective digital stores, the PlayStation Store and Xbox Store.
What about the music?
Listening to the radio while driving around the colorful world is a huge part of the GTA experience, since most of the games include a huge library of licensed music. That includes pop, country, hip-hop, Latin, rock, metal and more, with real-life musicians like Bad Bunny and Lana Del Rey playing some of the DJs.
For the first time in the series, you'll also be able to listen to podcasts via the characters' phones.
A soundtrack, "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album," will be released Thursday alongside the game. It will include tracks from Yung Lean, Travis Scott and Rauw Alejandro, among others.
A 30-track score album -- "Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score" -- from French electronic music duo Justice is scheduled for Dec. 11. It will include the game's theme song, "Flower in a Hurricane," which features Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard.
Who are the voice actors in GTA 6?
Lucia is played by Manni L. Perez, who has appeared in TV shows like "Law & Order: Special Victims Unit," "Jessica Jones" and "Blindspot."
Dylan Rourke, who plays Jason, has been in "Modern Family," "Grey's Anatomy" and "Station 19." Tagging Perez, he posted a tribute to the developers on social media Thursday.
"Years witnessing the hardest working and most skilled artisans game making has to offer," Rourke wrote. "We are truly honored to collaborate on this worldwide testament to human creativity, ingenuity, and sheer willpower. So much learned and so much to learn, keep fighting for what you love, stay humble."
Jason's buddy Cal Hampton, whom you'll encounter early in the game, is voiced by Bobby Moynihan. You might know him from "Saturday Night Live," "Hoppers" and "Minions & Monsters"
The game has a huge cast of characters, so you'll likely recognize plenty of voices as you play.
How much does GTA 6 cost?
The new game costs $80, bucking the general new game standard by around $10.
Along with the standard version of game, you can also preorder Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition, which include additional in-game items. This version costs $100.
The ultimate edition "amplifies the deepest and most immersive GTA experience yet with an exclusive collection of premium vehicles, weapons, apparel, and action threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story," Rockstar said.
Unlike previous entries in the series, Grand Theft Auto VI won't be released on a disc, according to Rockstar. Physical versions of the game include a code in the box.
Why did it take so long?
GTA 6 is a massive, multifaceted game that was worked on by thousands of developers spread across the globe. Since 2013, Rockstar has released Red Dead Redemption 2 (in 2018) and upgraded GTA 5 for PC, PS4, Xbox One, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
It's also supported Grand Theft Auto Online, the multiplayer component of GTA 5 that uses the same map, with multiple content updates each year.
When does multiplayer come out for GTA 6?
At launch, GTA 6 will be a single player-only game. However, it's likely that a version of Grand Theft Auto Online that uses the new game's map will arrive sometime in the future.
The current version of Grand Theft Auto Online came out a few weeks after GTA 5. The GTA 6 version probably won't arrive as quickly, given that Rockstar hasn't announced any details, with Bloomberg reporting that it's expected to be released in 2027.
Is GTA 6 appropriate for kids?
No, the game is completely inappropriate for children due to its violence and depiction of the criminal underworld.
If you're looking for an alternative, Lego Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight might be a good option. It's set open-world Lego version of Gotham City, allows you to use vehicles for traversal and is available on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2 and PC.
When will GTA 6 be on PC?
Rockstar hasn't announced a PC release, but every other game in the series has come to the platform. GTA 5 arrived on PC in April 2015, more than a year and a half after its initial console release.
There's also no indication that Rockstar is planning a Switch 2 version.
