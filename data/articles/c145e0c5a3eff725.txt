TITLE: Because of JJ Gabriel: Manchester United ignite a revenge war against Real Madrid with a swift move
OUTLET: Goal.com

For years, Manchester United's youth academy saw JJ Gabriel as its great hope. He had spent a short period at Carrington.
The 16-year-old turned down a professional contract with the Red Devils and announced his exit on Tuesday evening. Everyone now knows where he's heading: Real Madrid have won the race to sign him.
United are deeply frustrated by the outcome and bitterly disappointed by how events have unfolded. They saw the attacking midfielder as a long-term investment.
Manchester United want to respond
That frustration could push United into a scathing response aimed at Real Madrid.
According to The Times, Manchester United's management are considering legal action. They'll open an investigation to establish whether any clear irregularities occurred during the transfer.

The English club will look into whether Real Madrid contacted the player last summer, which would breach FIFA rules on minors.
It's United's attempt to save face in a transfer that slipped entirely out of their control.
The Red Devils will receive just £300,000 from the deal, around €360,000, as training compensation.
JJ Gabriel visits Valdebebas
JJ Gabriel moved another step closer to Real Madrid on Wednesday.
According to Marca, the 16-year-old English footballer visited the club's training facilities at Valdebebas on Wednesday, getting a first look at what will soon be his new home.
Only Real Madrid's official announcement remains, now that the player has terminated his contract with Manchester United.
JJ Gabriel bids farewell to Manchester United
Young English talent JJ Gabriel has settled his future, officially announcing on Tuesday that he is leaving Manchester United. That ends a journey with the Red Devils which began when he joined the club's academy aged ten, with a move to Real Madrid in the upcoming winter transfer window drawing near.
The 16-year-old attacking midfielder confirmed his decision in a message on his social media accounts. He thanked Manchester United, the coaches, his team-mates and everyone who had helped him during his years at the club, but stopped short of naming his next destination.
Reports suggest Real Madrid have become the most likely destination, having moved ahead of several major European clubs interested in signing him, led by Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain and Bayern Munich. The Spanish club have reached an agreement with the player and his representatives over the move.
