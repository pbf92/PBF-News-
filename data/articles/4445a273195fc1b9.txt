Goosehead Insurance Received Notice of Noncompliance
On November 20, 2026, Goosehead Insurance received notice from Nasdaq that it is not in compliance with Listing Rule 5250(c)(1) because it has not filed its quarterly report for the period ended September 30, 2026. The notice does not immediately affect the listing or trading of its Class A common stock; the company has until January 19, 2027, to submit a plan to regain compliance, and Nasdaq may grant up to 180 days from the report's original due date if it accepts the plan.
Original SEC Filing: Goosehead Insurance, Inc. [ GSHD ] - 8-K - Nov. 24, 2026
