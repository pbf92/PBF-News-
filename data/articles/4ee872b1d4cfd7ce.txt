NetEase Stocks Plunge 11.39% as Games Revenue Slips 1.7%
NetEase NTES plunged 11.39% to $104.94 at the 4:00 p.m. ET close on November 19 after third-quarter revenue declined. The Chinese gaming giant was among the worst large-cap performers on a weak day for U.S.-listed tech.
Net revenue fell 1.8% to RMB27.9 billion, and games and related services slipped 1.7% to RMB22.9 billion on lower revenue from certain self-developed titles. Youdao fell 12.3%. Gross profit still rose 4.8% to RMB19.0 billion, and net income attributable to shareholders was RMB8.4 billion versus RMB8.6 billion a year earlier. NetEase raised its quarterly dividend to $0.585 per ADS, extended its $5 billion buyback to January 2029 and holds RMB175.2 billion of net cash.
Net cash of about $26.1 billion covers roughly 39% of NetEase's $66.84 billion market value on GuruFocus. The GuruFocus chart shows a GF Score of 94/100, signaling strong profitability and a robust balance sheet. GF Value is $129.04, an 18.7% discount to the close, which GuruFocus rates modestly undervalued. The balance sheet limits downside; the pipeline decides the upside. The test is whether titles in development can offset fading older games in the December quarter.
