TITLE: Pulisic Shines, USA Cruise in Pochettino’s Farewell
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

The United States men's national team gave Mauricio Pochettino a memorable sendoff Tuesday night, defeating Haiti 2-0 in Cincinnati to advance to the Concacaf Nations League Finals. Christian Pulisic delivered one of the night's biggest highlights with a spectacular bicycle kick, while 18-year-old Julian Hall added another goal to continue his impressive start with the senior team.
The victory completed an 8-0 aggregate triumph over Haiti after the Americans won the first leg 6-0 in Miami on Saturday. The United States also secured its place in the 2027 Concacaf Gold Cup, giving the team another important achievement as it closes out the year.
Pulisic Delivers A Moment To Remember
Pulisic gave the home crowd something special in the 25th minute. After helping build the attack, the AC Milan forward finished a cross from Weston McKennie with an acrobatic bicycle kick that sent the ball into the net.
The goal was Pulisic's 36th for the United States and his third in the two-match series against Haiti. He had scored twice in Saturday's opening leg, continuing a strong November run for the national team.
McKennie played a central role in both goals Tuesday. After assisting Pulisic, he found Hall with a perfectly placed pass behind Haiti's defense just before halftime. Hall controlled the ball and finished with the outside of his right foot in the 43rd minute.
The goal marked Hall's fifth in just six appearances for the senior national team. The 18-year-old New York Red Bulls forward continues to make a strong case for a larger role as the United States looks toward its next competitive cycle.
A Winning Sendoff For Pochettino
Tuesday's match also marked the end of Pochettino's time as head coach of the United States. The Argentine is leaving the national team to take over at Manchester United, closing his tenure with a sixth consecutive victory.
Pochettino finished with a 23-12-2 record since taking charge in October 2024. His final stretch included wins over Haiti, Martinique, Canada, Jamaica, Qatar and Mexico, giving the Americans considerable momentum heading into the next chapter.
The team also recorded its fourth consecutive clean sheet Tuesday. Goalkeeper Diego Kochen made two saves, while the defense limited Haiti to eight shots and just two on target.
The United States will now turn its attention to the Concacaf Nations League Finals, scheduled for March 2027 at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. The Americans have reached the final four for the fifth consecutive time and will look to reclaim a championship they have won three times.
For Pochettino, the victory provided a fitting conclusion. For the United States, it offered another encouraging performance and a reminder of the young talent emerging within the program.
