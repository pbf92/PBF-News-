TITLE: U.S. Stocks Mostly Lower After Nvidia Earnings Fail to Soothe Nerves
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance

Nvidia's blockbuster earnings failed to give tech stocks a lift, a sign that worries about the AI trade continue to haunt investors after days of drama over the chip maker's ousted CEO Jensen Huang.
The Dow is in the red, while the Nasdaq turned higher in the last hour. Nvidia shares are slipping despite the company posting another quarter of record revenue and upgraded guidance. The stock has lost more than a third of its value since early last month.

Other AI-linked stocks such as CoreWeave are down, but have pared losses. Investors will now turn to the next big test for the AI trade: Anthropic's mega-IPO, expected tomorrow.
Oil prices are falling as data showing growing U.S. inventories overshadow the latest flare-up of fighting in the Persian Gulf.
Fed governor Christopher Waller reiterated his call for a half-percentage point rate hike in December. Central bank minutes released at 2 p.m. ET did little to change traders' views that a rate hike is likely in December.
