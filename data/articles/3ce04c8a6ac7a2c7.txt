TITLE: Pre-ordering GTA 6 only got you an early seat and $5 in savings, as its supposedly exclusive bonus goes on sale for $4.99
OUTLET: TweakTown

Rockstar is now selling the GTA 6 pre-order bonus separately for $4.99, which means the supposedly "exclusive" Vintage Vice City Pack was really a $5 saving for anyone who pre-ordered. The pack is available on the PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

When pre-orders opened in June, Rockstar said anyone who bought GTA 6 before November 20 would get the Vintage Vice City Pack at no extra cost. The pack includes a '55 Vapid Stanier sedan, a garage in Ocean Beach, outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and a weapon pattern. Jason gets a vintage pastel linen suit, while Lucia gets a red sequin minidress and a curly hairstyle.
The Shore Court garage has a weapon locker and a place to store stolen goods before you sell them to a fence, and the weapon pattern is inspired by Tommy Vercetti's shirt in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. It's all cosmetic or convenience content, with no missions or story content, and it doesn't change how the game plays.
According to Insider Gaming, the pack went on sale on November 20, one day after the game launched, although the change was widely noticed on November 23. Anyone who bought the game before then already owns the pack, so check your add-ons before paying for it again. You'll also need the base game.
Rockstar never explicitly promised exclusivity, but many fans assumed the pack would be limited to pre-order customers. In a Reddit thread from around three months ago, one user wrote, "Preorder only. They said it," and another said they would "carefully assume that it's exclusive to pre-ordering," citing it as a reason to pre-order.

But before you start bashing Rockstar, there's one pre-order perk you do get to keep. The original promotion also included a free month of GTA+ for digital pre-orders through the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store, and that isn't included with the $4.99 pack. Players who already qualified can still redeem the free month under the original terms.
