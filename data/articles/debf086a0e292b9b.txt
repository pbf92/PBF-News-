OUTLET: Medical Dialogues

Exercise During Hemodialysis May Boost Physical Activity and Muscle Strength: Study
China: A systematic review and meta-analysis published in the American Journal of Nephrology found that intradialytic exercise can improve physical activity, peak oxygen consumption (VO₂ peak), and muscle strength among patients undergoing hemodialysis. The analysis suggested that combined aerobic and resistance training may offer particular benefits for physical activity and aerobic capacity, while exercise intensity and program duration may influence specific outcomes.
- Combined aerobic and resistance exercise significantly improved physical activity levels (SMD, 0.99) and VO₂ peak (SMD, 1.01).
- Resistance exercise was associated with improved muscle strength (SMD, 0.52).
- Moderate-intensity exercise improved physical activity (SMD, 0.55), VO₂ peak (SMD, 0.59) and muscle strength (SMD, 0.37).
- Higher-intensity exercise also produced a significant improvement in muscle strength (SMD, 1.02).
- Exercise programs lasting up to 3 months improved physical activity (SMD, 0.65) and VO₂ peak (SMD, 0.70).
- Programs extending to 6 months significantly improved muscle strength (SMD, 0.41).
