TITLE: Bow Wow Dismisses 'GTA VI' Relationship Storyline With Lucia: 'This Is Not "Love Island"'
OUTLET: Complex

Key Takeaways
- Bow Wow aired his frustrations about GTA VI’s romance options, questioning why Jason has the option to hold Lucia’s hand during a mission.
- The rapper-actor also dismissed buying flowers and rubbing her feet.
- Bow Wow previously said in a Dec. 2025 episode of Million Dollaz Worth of Game that he’s uninterested in getting married again.
Four days into the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, Bow Wow has gripes with the relationship between protagonists Jason and Lucia.
In an Instagram Story posted on Saturday (Nov. 21), the rapper and media personality said that after 48 hours of playing the latest Rockstar Games release, Lucia was starting to get on his “goddamn nerves."
His frustration came when the GTA VI gave him an option to hold her hand during a mission.
"Why I got to have an option to hold your hand on a mission? Baby, this a mission... this ain't date night,” he stressed.
Bow Wow also mentioned a couch scene where the game gave him the option to massage Lucia’s feet.
"I ain't rubbing your feet. No. I'm trying to get to the bag... rob a couple stores, get a couple cars, buy some mansions, and enjoy Vice City,” he continued. "Baby, this is not Love Island. Got to buy flowers... I mean, what's next? If I got to cook for her, I'm done, bro."
Hand-holding is a prompt available when Jason and Lucia are walking together and can increase their score on a four-level relationship scale, according to G2A.
Jason purchasing flowers is one of five optional errands in the "Coming Home" mission, which includes buying Lucia a necklace and picking up her clothes, and completing all five errands starts the couple at the third tier, “Crazy in Love.” The highest level is “Hot Together.”
IGN explains that players have a choice between keeping Jason and Lucia as partners in crime or spending time together to heighten their romantic bond. Scores go down when a player ignores Jason or Lucia’s texts, rejects their calls, or doesn’t verbally interact when they return home.
Bow Wow, who has two children from two previous relationships, said in a 2025 episode of Million Dollaz Worth of Game that he refuses to get married, adding, "I love being single. I'm at peace."
