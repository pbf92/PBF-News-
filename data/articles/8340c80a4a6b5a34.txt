Interparfums Projects 6% Sales Growth to $1.58 Billion in 2027
Less than 1 min read
Interparfums expects 2027 net sales to rise 6% to $1.58 billion and diluted EPS to increase 3% to $5.00.
Key Highlights:
- 2027 guidance excludes the $17.9 million, or $0.35 per diluted share, tariff-refund gain estimated for 2026.
- EPS growth is expected to reflect higher sales and operating efficiencies, partly offset by increased brand and marketing investment.
- Growth is expected from leading brands and planned launches, partly offset by softer smaller brands and expiring licenses.
- Interparfums expects an approximately 1% unfavorable foreign-exchange impact on 2027 net sales.
- Planned launches include new Lacoste, Coach and Montblanc franchises, Longchamp fragrances, and new PUMA fragrances.
Original SEC Filing: INTERPARFUMS INC [ IPAR ] - 8-K - Nov. 18, 2026
