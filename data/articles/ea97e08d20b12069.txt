CCTA-Guided PCI Noninferior to IVUS-Guided PCI in P4 Trial: NEJM
USA: The randomized P4 trial has demonstrated that coronary CT angiography (CCTA) can safely and effectively guide preprocedural planning for percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI), potentially reducing the need for invasive imaging during cardiac catheterization.
- At 12 months, target-vessel failure occurred in 23 patients (4.1%) in the CCTA-guided PCI group, compared with 25 patients (4.6%) in the IVUS-guided PCI group.
- The absolute risk difference between the groups was −0.42 percentage points.
- CCTA-guided PCI met the prespecified criteria for noninferiority compared with IVUS-guided PCI, indicating that it was not associated with an unacceptably higher risk of target-vessel failure.
- No statistically significant superiority was demonstrated for either approach.
- Clinical procedural complications were reported in six patients receiving CCTA-guided PCI and nine patients undergoing IVUS-guided PCI.
