OUTLET: India Today

Pochettino wants Manchester United transfer say, backs Bruno Fernandes as captain
Mauricio Pochettino has made his first big statement as Manchester United head coach, making it clear that he wants a say in transfer decisions while confirming that Bruno Fernandes will continue as club captain. The Argentine has signed a deal until 2029.
Mauricio Pochettino wants to have a say in Manchester United's transfer decisions despite being appointed head coach rather than manager. The Argentine, who has signed a deal until 2029, said his immediate priority is to coach the team and pick the starting XI, but he also hopes to be involved in decisions that shape the squad.
"I know that being a head coach, I am the guy that is going to decide the squad for Sunday and I'm going to pick the starting XI. Of course, in the future, I would like to be involved in all the main decisions that will happen on the squad," Pochettino said at his first press conference on Friday.
United have moved away from the traditional manager's role since sacking Erik ten Hag in October 2024. Ruben Amorim and Michael Carrick were both appointed head coaches, with director of football Jason Wilcox and the recruitment team handling much of the club's transfer business.
Pochettino, however, wants to be part of those discussions as he begins his new job at Old Trafford. The 54-year-old has had little time to settle in, having arrived in Manchester from the United States on Wednesday. He admitted that he had been sleeping for just three or four hours a night ahead of Sunday's clash against Liverpool at Anfield.
He took his first training session on Thursday and spent around three hours speaking to players individually. Pochettino said he wanted to understand their situations, both on and off the pitch, before getting down to work with the squad.
"Yesterday we spent three hours chatting in an individual way in my office with all the players," he said.
"Maybe I missed some injured players, but I think in the next few days we will have time to talk in an individual way with everyone."
BRUNO TO CONTINUE AS UNITED CAPTAIN
Pochettino also confirmed that Bruno Fernandes will continue as Manchester United captain. The midfielder had come under criticism for questioning Carrick's tactics during the final weeks of his reign, but the new head coach made it clear that he has no plans to take the armband away from him.
"I think Bruno is our captain and I think he's a very important player, but all the players need to feel that they are important, from the top to the bottom, or from the bottom to the top," Pochettino said.
"But Bruno is our captain and he's a very important player."
POCHETTINO ON US EXPERIENCE
Pochettino believes his time in charge of the United States national team has made him a better coach and prepared him for the pressure that comes with managing a club like Manchester United.
The Argentine left the US job this week to take over at Old Trafford. He spoke about the pressure of coaching the national team, particularly during its first World Cup game against Paraguay at SoFi Stadium in California.
"We are much better coaches now after work in the national team like USA because the expectation and the pressure was massive," he said.
"I remember in SoFi, our first game against Paraguay, the pressure was massive. There was more than 200 celebrity people expecting us to win and to play well and all the country was behind us."
Having previously managed Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea, Pochettino admitted that he had missed the daily routine of club football.
"We are animals of the grass. We like to feel the grass. We need to smell the grass and try to help the players to improve every day," he said.
His immediate task is to get United back on track. The club are 18th in the Premier League table and have managed just one win in their last 10 games. Sunday's trip to Liverpool will be a tough first test for the new head coach.
Pochettino knows there is little time to make changes but wants his players to understand and buy into his ideas quickly.
"It's a race to arrive in condition to be competitive against Liverpool," he said.
"We arrived with the idea to create something from scratch, from our own ideas and philosophy in the way that we love to work."
He added that United need to start getting results and return to the level expected of the club.
"Manchester United need to be in a different place than we are now."
