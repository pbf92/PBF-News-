GTA 6 collectible cards are getting physical versions “coming soon” in official collab with Rockstar Games
Grand Theft Auto VI players may be busy tracking down the game’s many collectible cards (an interactive GTA 6 map will be necessary to do so), but fans will soon be able to start collecting some in physical form. A collaboration between the sports trading card brand Panini America and Rockstar Games has been announced – official trading cards are “coming soon,” and a handful have already been revealed.

A week has now passed since GTA 6 launched, and gamers are still discovering new things within the vast world of Leonida. There are literally hundreds of cards scattered around the map, many of which are sports-focused. Based on what we know so far, there are 480 cards across 18 sets, according to the collectible cards menu provided by Rockstar, so this assumes extras aren’t hidden out there.

Eight official trading cards for GTA 6 revealed so far
Panini America nor Rockstar Games have listed any physical cards on their respective stores just yet, but it’s worth keeping an eye on both stores if you’re a card collector, based on how quickly the physical GTA 6 soundtrack album (on vinyl and CD) sold out. All we have so far is a post from Panini America, which reveals a selection of eight GTA 6 cards that’ll be available in physical form.
- Liberty City Wrath – Dartanyon Pearson
- Vice City Narcos – Carl Kasey
- Vice City Flamingos (Legends of PBL) – Elias Delgado
- Liberty City Wrath (Autographed) – Robbie Stine
- Vice City Manatees (Autographed) – Maleek Rivers
- Liberty City Swingers – Mario Rugama
- Vice City Club de Futbol – Anne Florence
- Vice City Flamingos – Charlie Spears
How many collectible cards are in GTA 6?
Fans have collectively been discovering collectible cards as part of the mammoth task of mapping out every aspect of Leonida. Luckily, once you’ve discovered a set, Rockstar reveals how many more you have remaining in the game’s menus.
Assuming there aren’t any hidden extras, sports teams make up 10 sets (246 cards), while there are a further 8 sets (234 cards), which totals 480 collectible cards in-game. We don’t yet know if Panini America or Rockstar Games will be releasing every single one of these in physical form.
