Pochettino determined to 'change things' at Man Utd
Manchester United are struggling at the wrong end of the table, but Mauricio Pochettino is ready to help change their fortunes.
Mauricio Pochettino believes it is a "mistake" to solely focus on Manchester United's current predicament, and vows to "change things" at the club.
Pochettino began his tenure as United boss on Wednesday, leaving his position as United States head coach after signing off with a win in the Concacaf Nations League quarter-finals against Haiti.
He is tasked with improving United's current position, with the team sitting 18th in the Premier League table after the first 10 games.
The Red Devils have won just one of their first 10 matches in the league (D5 L4), while they are winless in eight Premier League matches for just the second time, also doing so in April/May 2025.
By the time United kick off against Liverpool in Pochettino's first game in charge, they will have been in the drop zone for 15 consecutive days – their longest run of days in the top-flight drop zone since September/October 1986 (28 days).
But Pochettino does not want to focus on the negatives, instead choosing to be optimistic about what United could do.
"It's a mistake to see the situation," Pochettino told United's social channels. "We need to see the potential of this club.
"It's about to start to act and to change things, and to try to affect, in a good way, all the areas, to try to really believe that we can recover that feeling about to win.
"The most important thing is for us to start to work hard to recover that feeling. That feeling is about being winners, to win games, to perform. But that is not only to talk about winning."
Reports suggest Pochettino will have a say in whatever business United do in the January transfer window, but he has over a month until he can add reinforcements to his squad.
United have 10 games to play before the end of the calendar year, and the Argentinian is determined to get the best out of his current crop of players.
"The best request that we can have, the players that we have, and we need to love the players that we have," Pochettino added.
"We need to support the players; we need to provide all the tools for their performance.
"It's only an act of responsibility now to start to work and provide them the possibility to translate all their talent on the field."
Pochettino will be the first-ever United manager to see his first league game come against rivals, Liverpool.
Just one of the Red Devils' last nine managers (incl. caretakers) has lost his first Premier League game of a spell in charge of the club (W4 D4), with Erik ten Hag losing 1-2 to Brighton in August 2022.
Though Liverpool are the favourites to win – with the Opta supercomputer giving them a 57.3% chance of victory on Sunday – Pochettino has his eyes on three points.
"We are going to go there, to Anfield, with the idea to win and be brave and to be Manchester United," he said.
"We need to make sure that we are brave, and we are protagonists, and go there with no fears and challenge a very good team like Liverpool."
