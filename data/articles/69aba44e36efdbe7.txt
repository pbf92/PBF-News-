Arm stock rises 4.8% as Nvidia earnings spark sector rally

Arm Holdings PLC (NASDAQ: ARM) shares rose 4.81% to $263.12 on Thursday, extending gains from the previous session amid a broad semiconductor rally triggered by Nvidia’s strong results.
The movement follows Arm’s Wednesday gain of 4.76% in after-hours trading, which saw shares reach $263 after closing at $251.06 during regular hours.
Nvidia Earnings Drive Sector Rally
Arm’s ascent on Thursday was primarily driven by blowout second-quarter earnings from Nvidia Corporation (NASDAQ: NVDA). Nvidia reported revenue of $96.22 billion, up 106% year-over-year, beating the Street consensus estimate of $92.18 billion.
Adjusted earnings per share came in at $2.22, topping consensus estimates of $2.10. Data Center revenue surged 117% year-over-year to $89.0 billion. Gross margin reached 75.0%.
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated that AI has reached its inflection point and demand is accelerating. The company expects third-quarter revenue between $105.84 billion and $110.16 billion, representing 85.7% to 93.2% year-over-year growth. Nvidia also ended the quarter with $99.0 billion remaining under its share repurchase authorization.
Expanded Collaboration With IBM
The stock movement also follows the announcement of an expanded collaboration with IBM (NYSE: IBM). Under the deal, IBM will integrate Arm architecture into its future IBM Z and LinuxONE mainframe systems.
Mohamed Awad, Arm’s executive vice president of Cloud AI, stated that the partnership marks the first dual-architecture processor from the two firms. Each processor core is designed to natively execute both IBM and Arm instructions.
This allows Arm-native Linux environments to run alongside z/OS and existing Linux applications. IBM Z and LinuxONE are enterprise-grade server families designed for security, large-scale operations, and mission-critical computing.
Recent Financial Performance
The partnership announcement comes shortly after Arm reported stronger-than-expected first-quarter results in late July. The company beat analyst estimates for both revenue and earnings per share.
Strategic Developments
Arm is also a co-development partner with Meta (NASDAQ: META) on the Arm AGI CPU. This represents Arm’s first self-designed data center chip after 35 years as a pure licensing business.
Raymond James raised its price target on Arm to $641 from $565 on Tuesday, maintaining an Outperform rating.
