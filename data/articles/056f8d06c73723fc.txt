Falcon Finance: Supply unlock releases about 203 million FF - Aug 2027
Falcon Finance has scheduled an unlock of about 203.06 million FF tokens for August 2027. The release covers tokens allocated to the project's ecosystem and foundation, as well as to investors and the team.
The unlock makes tokens equal to 5.73% of the circulating supply available for sale. It adds a potential source of selling pressure if recipients choose to put some of the newly unlocked tokens on the market.
