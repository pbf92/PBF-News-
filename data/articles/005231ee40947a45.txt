TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino Offers Pep Guardiola Advice on USMNT Job Amid Rumors, 'It's the Perfect Moment'
OUTLET: Bleacher Report

Departing USMNT manager Mauricio Pochettino, amidst rumors that the U.S. Soccer Federation is hoping to replace him with Pep Guardiola, told reporters on Monday that he would recommend the job to the former Manchester City man.
"It's the perfect moment to arrive to the [U.S. Soccer] Federation," he said. "Everything is in the right direction. I think, of course, take [it]. Take it because he's going to find really good people, good players. The player can fit for his style; the National Training Center that wasn't [there] before."

Pochettino, 54, will be leaving the USMNT for Manchester United following Tuesday's Concacaf Nations League quarterfinal second leg against Haiti. But despite his departure, he believes the job he's leaving behind has become a prestigious one on the global stage.
"Now the vision from Europe, I promise, is completely different after the World Cup. Being head coach in USA now is prestigious," he told reporters. "It's Manchester United, the caliber of [Real] Madrid, Barcelona. Manchester United is coming for the coach of the USA. If you start to think that if you are another coach, maybe you are not working—[Antonio] Conte, or I don't know—you say, 'Oh, it's a great opportunity because it's not only the value of to be coach here. If Pochettino from Chelsea come here, [then goes to] Manchester United.' The vision is completely different."
Whether that's enough to attract the likes of Guardiola remains to be seen, though Jason Burt of the Telegraph reported that Pep will turn down any USMNT offers as he seeks to take a year away from the game. He added that "it has been suggested that Guardiola is open to meeting the US, but sources are adamant that he wants a year away from football and is not open to any offers."
So Guardiola probably won't be Poch's replacement, even if the latter spoke highly of the job on Monday.
