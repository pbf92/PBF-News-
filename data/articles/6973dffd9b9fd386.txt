Shopify, Costco, e.l.f.: Why One Writer Would Buy in a Crash
Motley Fool writer Jennifer Saibil says she would buy Shopify, Costco and e.l.f. Beauty if the S&P 500 sold off, citing fast sales growth but valuations of 53 to 75 times earnings. The index is up 7% this year.
Motley Fool writer Jennifer Saibil said she would buy Shopify, Costco Wholesale and e.l.f. Beauty if the stock market crashes. These are the 3 stocks she is ready to buy on a sell-off, because their businesses are strong but their share prices are high. This is one writer's view, not a forecast.
The S&P 500 is up about 7% this year. That is below the double-digit gain it showed earlier, which Saibil called "cautious optimism" given high inflation and rising interest rates. She said a crash is bound to come at some point, either before year-end or later.
Shopify: fast growth, 75 times earnings
Shopify's third-quarter revenue rose 32% from a year earlier. Its gross merchandise volume, the value of sales made on its platform by clients, grew 29% to $119 billion. Saibil said that topped Amazon's online and third-party sales of $116 billion.
The company now works with large retailers such as Lululemon Athletica and Estee Lauder's Clinique brand. Saibil said the price is what has held her back. Shopify trades at 75 times trailing 12-month earnings, the highest multiple of the three.
Costco: sales accelerate, valuation stretched
Costco's sales rose 11.2% in the fiscal fourth quarter, which ended August 30. Comparable sales grew 9.4%, and earnings per share rose from $5.87 to $6.75. Part of the gain came from gas stations as oil prices climbed.
Monthly sales rose 13% in both September and October. Costco also announced a $30 special dividend. Saibil said that would lift this year's effective yield to 3.2%, against a regular yield of 0.53%.
The stock trades at a price-to-earnings ratio of 53. Saibil said it is "priced for perfection."
E.l.f. Beauty: sales up 51%
In the fiscal second quarter, which ended September 30, e.l.f. sales rose 51%. Gross margin climbed 1.7 percentage points to 71%. The company recently bought Hailey Bieber's Rhode brand.
Saibil said tariff refunds helped margins, and the stock has nearly doubled since its low last June. It is the most popular cosmetics company in the Piper Sandler Taking Stock With Teens survey, with Rhode third.
E.l.f. trades at 72 times trailing 12-month earnings. If a sell-off pushed that multiple to 40 or 45, Saibil said she would be ready to buy it alongside Shopify and Costco.
