Coherent Stocks Jump 10.78% After $2 Billion Buyback Authorization
Coherent COHR shares jumped 10.78% to $235.79 at 10:00 a.m. ET on November 19 in regular trading, after the board authorized a share repurchase program of up to $2.0 billion on November 18. The program has no expiration date and no obligation to buy a set number of shares. CNBC linked the move to continued data center demand after Nvidia's results.
The operating trend is strong. Fiscal first-quarter revenue rose 50% to $2.37 billion, or 56% on a pro forma basis. Datacenter and Communications revenue climbed to $1.95 billion from $1.09 billion a year earlier, while Industrial revenue shrank. Non-GAAP EPS rose $0.93 to $2.09, and management expects fiscal 2027 pro forma revenue growth above 60% as AI data centers shift from copper to optical links.
The $2.0 billion authorization equals about 4.3% of Coherent's $46.45 billion market value. Yet the GuruFocus page shows a negative free cash flow margin, so buybacks may compete with capacity spending for cash. The GF Score is 78/100, with solid profitability and momentum and decent financial strength. Shares trade about 91% above the GF Value of $123.44, a significantly overvalued label. Watch whether free cash flow turns positive before the buyback is meaningfully used.
