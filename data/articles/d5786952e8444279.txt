TITLE: Sporting CP vs. Manchester United: Most Popular Betting Lines
OUTLET: news.bet365.com

Struggling Manchester United travel to Portugal in the UEFA Champions League to face Sporting CP.
Mauricio Pochettino took over as the new head coach of Manchester United last week after leaving the USMNT, and suffered a brutal 4-0 defeat against Liverpool in his first match in charge. United have been starring relegation from the Premier League right in the face, hence the managerial change, but have faired much better in the UEFA Champions League this season. 
United began the campaign with a 4-0 win over Sabah FK, then lost to Atletico Madrid and Como in their next two matches. Last time out, the Red Devils picked up a 3-2 win against Roma, and currently sit in a playoff position with six points. 
The English side will be up against a high flying Sporting CP side who currently sit third in Liga Portugal, and 12th in the Champions League with three wins and a loss. Sporting have defeated Galatasaray, Lens, and Shakhtar Donetsk, but lost to LASK in round 3. 
Pochettino will be trying to settle things down for Manchester United in his first UCL game since 2022, but he'll be facing a Sporting side that will be eager to take advantage of their opponents unsteadiness. Luis Suarez will particularly excited for this match after already having four goals under his belt in the Champions League.
