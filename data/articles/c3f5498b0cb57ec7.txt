What this release says
DXC Technology launched DXC Frontier Code Assurance, a managed cybersecurity service that uses Anthropic's Claude Mythos AI model to find, validate and help fix vulnerabilities in mission-critical software. The release says the offering is available now, with DXC specialists checking AI findings and building a prioritized remediation plan. For a shareholder, this is a product launch that extends an existing partnership, and the release gives no revenue, customer or financial figures for it.

FAQ
What does DXC Frontier Code Assurance do?
It uses Claude Mythos to find vulnerabilities in mission-critical software, has DXC cybersecurity specialists validate and rank them by business impact, and then helps customers remediate and patch them.
Is the service available now?
Yes, the release says the offering is available now, and interested parties can email FCA@DXC.com for more information.
How does DXC describe its role alongside the AI model?
The release says Claude Mythos finds the potential vulnerabilities, while DXC specialists decide what to do about them.
