Moderna Stock Pops: What's Going On?
Moderna Inc. MRNA shares are jumping Tuesday. Investors are shifting money away from AI and tech shares, which are volatile and retreating after Monday’s big rally, and toward biotech.
- Moderna shares are powering higher. Why is MRNA stock surging?
Moderna Rises as Investors Rotate into Biotech
The rotation traces back to Monday. Nvidia’s board restored Jensen Huang to the president and CEO posts and to its board with no delay, after Huang left earlier this month amid a scandal over national security worries. Nvidia shares jumped 31%, and AI and tech names rallied alongside.
Those groups are retreating Tuesday. Investors may be pulling money out of the choppy AI and tech trade, and part of it could be flowing into healthcare.
The Health Care Select Sector ETF (XLV) gained around 3% Tuesday, and several biotech stocks moved by more. Kymera Therapeutics rose roughly 13%, TG Therapeutics 10%, BridgeBio Pharma 9 and Revolution Medicines 7%.
Sanofi-Insmed Deal Fuels Biotech Strength
Takeover news seemingly anchored the group’s strength. Sanofi SA SNY agreed Tuesday to acquire Insmed Inc. INSM for roughly $41.0 billion in cash, or $175 a share, about 46% above Insmed’s unaffected close on Nov. 18. Press reports of a possible deal first appeared the following day.
Sanofi says Insmed deepens its respiratory disease and immunology business. The deal also brings two marketed therapies plus a Phase 3 candidate.
A takeover of that size could pull investors back toward the sector, which may benefit Moderna. Moderna has climbed approximately 841% in the last 12 months, the biggest gain among the stocks in this group, versus around 160% for Kymera and an approximate 17% decline for Insmed.
