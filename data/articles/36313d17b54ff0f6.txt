TITLE: Manchester United predicted XI vs Brentford: Senne Lammens keeps his place
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United host Brentford in the Premier League on Saturday as head coach Mauricio Pochettino weighs up his team selection following Wednesday's 2-2 Champions League draw with Sporting CP.
Goalkeeper and Defence
Senne Lammens should continue in goal. His errors against Sporting will inevitably create debate over whether a short break would help, but we think Pochettino will probably offer him an immediate opportunity to respond. Former United goalkeeper Peter Schmeichel has also argued that dropping Lammens may not help him rediscover his confidence.
United are expected to return to a back four after their European assignment. Leny Yoro and Harry Maguire could form the central partnership, with Lisandro Martinez moving across to left-back.
Using Martinez on that flank would allow Shaw, who returned to training earlier this week after a muscular problem kept him out for nine matches, to be reintroduced gradually from the bench rather than being asked to complete a full Premier League game immediately after his lay-off.
Patrick Dorgu is an alternative at left-back.
Noussair Mazraoui is the likely choice at right-back after playing reasonably well against Sporting. Diogo Dalot is the other option. Either of those could also switch to the left.
Midfield
The deeper midfield pairing is expected to remain unchanged. Kobbie Mainoo and Youri Tielemans should continue together after starting against Sporting.
Manuel Ugarte and Carlos Baleba's continued absence also reduces the scope for rotation in this area, although Andrey Santos is an option.
Attack
Fernandes should start as the number 10 if he recovers from illness. His two goals on Wednesday underlined his importance to a side desperately needing results, but his condition will have to be assessed before the team is finalised.
If he doesn't make it, either Josh Zirkzee or Mason Mount will likely come in as the number 10.
Amad is expected to continue on the right after a positive display against Sporting. Matheus Cunha should return on the left following his suspension for the Champions League fixture.
Bryan Mbeumo could lead the line while Sesko remains sidelined. The former Brentford forward would not necessarily stay fixed through the middle, however, with Amad and Cunha also capable of interchanging positions across the front three.

Final Thoughts
- Lammens is expected to keep his place despite his errors against Sporting.
- Fernandes must recover from illness to start as the number 10.
- Martinez could play at left-back as Shaw returns from the bench.
- Mbeumo is likely to lead a flexible front three.
