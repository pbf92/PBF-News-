TITLE: Scenes of 'madness' and homes evacuated as bomb squad scrambled to residential Trafford street
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

Scenes of 'madness' and homes evacuated as bomb squad scrambled to residential Trafford street
A 200m cordon was put in place following the discovery of a suspected WWII device
Emergency services were scrambled to a quiet residential street in Trafford following the discovery of a suspected explosive device today (November 24).
Police were called to the scene, on Firs Road, Sale, shortly before midday. A bomb disposal team was also called after the item, believed to be from World War II, was found at an address.
Homes were evacuated and a 200m cordon was established. It is understood the device has been taken to a nearby area in order to carry out a controlled explosion.

Residents described scenes of 'madness' to the Manchester Evening News. An elderly couple who were evacuated from their home on Firs Road said they were told by emergency services a 'three-inch mortar' had been found.
They added: “They said they were treating it as live and were looking for place to detonate, so we had to leave."
Another person living in the area said: “The important thing is that it’s been removed and everyone is safe.”
Members of the public were told to avoid the area while the operation to remove the device was underway. Videos from the scene showed a man in armed forces fatigues carrying a red box to a bomb disposal van waiting nearby at around 5pm.
A spokesperson for Greater Manchester Police told the MEN: "At 11.55am this morning (November 24), we were called to reports of a historic unexploded World War II device which was found at an address on Firs Road in Sale.
"Explosive Ordinance Disposal (EOD) attended the scene and are taking the device to a nearby area to carry out a controlled explosion.
"A 200m cordon has been put in place as a precaution. Please avoid the area and find alternative routes. Thank you for your cooperation."
