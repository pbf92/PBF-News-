TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino leaves USMNT with a victory in rare positive send-off
OUTLET: Sports Business Journal

Mauricio Pochettino wrapped up his tenure as coach of the USMNT with a victory Tuesday night, as the USMNT took down Haiti, 2-0, in the Concacaf Nations League quarterfinals. Pochettino, who is now the coach of Premier League club Manchester United, “gave hugs to just about everyone in sight” after the game: “players, coaches, even U.S. Soccer executives JT Batson and Oguchi Onyewu.” He also “took a photo with the entire USMNT staff.” As “send-offs go, Tuesday’s win over Haiti was about as good as could be expected for Pochettino’s last game in charge.” It was “an exit like no other manager” in USMNT history has experienced -- as none of Pochettino’s “full-time predecessors, at least in the modern era, left of their own accord” (ESPN.com, 11/17).
Pochettino said he thinks the team will “achieve big things” in the wake of his departure to Manchester United. After the match, Pochettino was “scheduled to fly on a private jet directly to Manchester, England, to take charge of his new club ahead of Wednesday’s training session.” Pochettino was asked if “he’ll be available to help the U.S. Soccer Federation pick his successor,” but he said that he “wouldn’t be participating in that process.” Pochettino: “It’s not my job. And for sure they have the capacity and the knowledge to find the right people with the right profile. I don’t know how to measure my colleagues and I think it’s not my job” (ESPN.com, 11/17).
Batson said in a prematch interview with TNT Sports that Pochettino “has shown everyone the benefits of coming to America as well as his confidence in being able to attract a world-class coach.” Batson: “For us to win, we need to be great at a lot of things, and we need someone who cares about youth development, cares about our youth national team programs, our relationships with our clubs and the success of the federation more broadly. We know that is a key part of our success, and it’s also a key part of how we’ve been able to attract great people because they get to do big things.” Pochettino said during a roundtable with reporters on Monday that former Manchester City manager Pep Guardiola “should be his successor if offered the job” (AP, 11/17).
Meanwhile, things continue on for the USMNT, which will face Mexico in the 2027 Concacaf Nations League semifinals on March 25 at SoFi Stadium (USA TODAY, 11/17).
