One day we’ll start a podcast with something other than Manchester United, but not today. Mauricio Pochettino’s side had a weird game in Lisbon, is the new manager just unlucky? United had two soft disallowed goals, one denying them victory. Is it unlucky to concede two penalties minutes apart or is that just bad?
Elsewhere, Liverpool eased past Brugge, Florian Wirtz and Alexander Isak with the goals. Bayern lost in Lille and PSG were held by Roma, which means Real Madrid are the only side with a 100% record. There were two goals for Barcelona’s Anthony Gordon and wins for Inter and Atléti.
Plus, we’ll discuss the Raheem Sterling case after he received a 12 month-suspended prison sentence and there’s a Premier League preview on a weekend featuring Arsenal v Manchester City and the Merseyside derby. All that and your questions answered.
