TITLE: The Witcher 4 Should Improve on Witcher 3 as Much as GTA 6 Did on GTA 5, Says CDPR
OUTLET: TechPowerUp

Tuesday, November 24th 2026
CD Projekt RED is not exactly shy when it comes to hyping up The Witcher 4. As much as critics and audiences alike loved The Witcher 3, its successor is already setting some pretty high expectations for itself. According to CDPR's Joint CEO Michał Nowakowski, the company expects The Witcher 4 to be as big a step up from The Witcher 3 as GTA 6 is from GTA 5. Nowakowski made the comments during the company's Q3 earnings call, after Erste analyst Krzysztof Tkocz asked whether a leap like the one Rockstar just delivered is possible for CDPR's next Witcher game. Nowakowski said the studio has indeed looked at "the game" and saw the differences, but he wasn't going to do a full GTA 5 versus GTA 6 breakdown. Here's what he did say, however:
Michał Nowakowski, Joint CEO CD Projekt RedDo we believe there's going to be a comparable leap between The Witcher 3 and The Witcher 4? I think so, yes. I mean, it's been 11 years. We are working on The Witcher 4 to make it significantly better looking and improved in terms of pretty much every possible facet of the game so yes, you can expect us to make a much bigger jump between The Witcher 3 and The Witcher 4.
That comparison is a huge one, and it might be extremely optimistic for CDPR to compare The Witcher 4 to GTA 6, given the reception Rockstar's game has already gotten. Nowakowski said during the call that GTA 6's release hasn't changed how CDPR approaches development, also noting that their games are ambition-driven rather than budget-driven. Still, given the records GTA 6 has already broken and what its trajectory looks like, CDPR seems to have set an extremely high bar for itself. The Witcher 4 is currently targeting a 2028 release window, so we will have to wait and see if CDPR can deliver on that promise.
