TITLE: Martinez To Start, Mazraoui On The Bench | 4-2-3-1 Manchester United Predicted Lineup Vs Liverpool
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United will lock horns with Liverpool at Anfield this Sunday as they look to secure a good result on the road in the Premier League. The Red Devils were held to a 4-4 draw by Aston Villa before the international break, so they will be eager to return to winning ways this weekend.
Mauricio Pochettino will set his side up in a 4-2-3-1 formation and is expected to make one change to his first team for this game. Lisandro Martinez could get a chance to impress from the start of this contest, while Noussair Mazraoui makes way for him and drops to the bench.
4-2-3-1 Manchester United Predicted Lineup To Face Liverpool
Defence
Senne Lammens will feature in goal for the Red Devils after conceding four times in his last appearance. Harry Maguire is a slight concern ahead of this game and could get the nod to play alongside Lisandro Martinez at the centre of the Man United defence as they look to keep a good shape when defending inside their half.
Leny Yoro will look to impress as the right-back, while Diogo Dalot hopes to prove his value on the other side as the left-back. Both of them will have to pick and choose their moments to join in on the attack.
Noussair Mazraoui will provide cover for the defensive positions on the bench.

Midfield
Kobbie Mainoo and Youri Tielemans will operate as the holding midfielders as they look to make life tough for the home side in the middle of the park.
The talented trio of Bryan Mbeumo, Bruno Fernandes and Amad Diallo will function just ahead of them as part of Pochettino's attack. All three of them need to combine well if they want to create enough chances for Man United to win this contest.
Mason Mount and Andrey Santos will have to be content with a spot on the bench as they await their chance to make an impact in the second 45 minutes if required.
Attack
Matheus Cunha will lead the line for the Mancunian giants on the offensive end of the field. He would be eager to get his name on the scoresheet and make a difference for the Red Devils up top.
Joshua Zirkzee will be named on the bench and might come on to influence the game in the latter stages if needed.
