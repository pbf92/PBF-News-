TITLE: Nafe Smallz Grand Theft Auto VI appearance has not 'sunk in'
OUTLET: BBC

Luton to Vice City: Rapper's GTA dream comes true
A rapper from Luton said it has "not fully sunk in" after his voice appeared on the soundtrack of Grand Theft Auto VI.
Nafe Smallz features on Personal, a track by house producer and DJ Jaden Thompson that can be heard in a nightclub in the game.
Smallz previously appeared in Grand Theft Auto V on Skepta's track Greaze Mode, which was added to the game in a 2019 update.
He said: "I don't think it's fully sunk in yet, I'm currently playing the game, I just got to Vice City in the game so I'm waiting to get to the club and hear it for myself - but yeah, it is super exciting."
The previous game sold about 235 million copies and was the fastest entertainment release to reach $1bn in sales.
Although Smallz has not made it far enough into the game to hear himself, friends have sent him clips and told him the track forms part of its story mode.
He said: "At first I thought it might just be on the radio or when you're playing online, but the fact that it's actually built into the default story mode is incredible.
"It just means hundreds of millions of people are going to be exposed to the song."
Smallz, a lifelong fan of the franchise who grew up playing previous titles Vice City and San Andreas, described the placement as a "full-circle moment" as he expands into house music.
He added: "It's just showing people the range of what we could do, it's not just the rap stuff.
"It's just a blessing, you know what I'm saying?"
