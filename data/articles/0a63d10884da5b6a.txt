Coast Copper Corp. (COCO) Raises Placement Target to $1.655M
Coast Copper increased its non-brokered private placement from up to $1.0M to up to $1.655M by offering up to 4,137,500 flow-through units at $0.40 each. The offering remains subject to customary closing conditions, including exchange approval.
