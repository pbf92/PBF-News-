TITLE: Pochettino begins Manchester United rebuild after U.S. team exit
OUTLET: Chosunbiz

"Manchester is blue? Give me time. It will definitely change."
Mauricio Pochettino, 54, Manchester United's new manager, responded confidently to a taunt aimed at Manchester City. After completing his final duties with the U.S. national team, he traveled straight to Manchester and began his official work.
The Guardian reported on the 19th (Korea time) that Pochettino spoke with former Manchester City player DaMarcus Beasley before saying goodbye to the U.S. national team.
Beasley taunted Pochettino, saying, "Manchester is blue. It always will be."
Pochettino smiled and shot back, "Absolutely not. You'll see. Give me time. It will definitely change."
"This is a really big challenge. But we have experience handling situations like this," he said. "The situation Manchester United is in now is not fitting for the club or its fans. United fans are truly amazing."
Pochettino was appointed Manchester United's new manager on the 13th. However, under the terms of his contract with the U.S. national team, he had to remain through the quarterfinals of the CONCACAF Nations League.
The United States won both legs of its quarterfinal matchup against Haiti. After the final game and his official news conference, Pochettino boarded a flight to Manchester.
Manchester United announced on its official website on the 19th that Pochettino had begun his official duties.
Pochettino traveled from Cincinnati, Ohio, to Manchester on the 18th and went straight to the Carrington training ground. He toured the club's facilities and met with some players and staff members.
The coaching staff also underwent a major overhaul. Five longtime associates joined him: Jesus Perez, Miguel D'Agostino, Toni Jimenez, Alex Scott and Sebastiano Pochettino.
Meanwhile, six people left the club, including former manager Michael Carrick, Travis Binnion, Jonny Evans, Steve Holland, Craig Mawson and Jonathan Woodgate.
Pochettino faces a daunting task. Manchester United is currently in the Premier League relegation zone and will travel to face Liverpool on the 22nd.
Pochettino has pledged to change Manchester's colors. His first test at United will be against rival Liverpool.
