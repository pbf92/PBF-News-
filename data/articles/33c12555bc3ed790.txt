TITLE: GTA 6 fashionistas are sharing their Lucia fits like it’s Animal Crossing, and our leading lady has never looked better
OUTLET: Yahoo Tech

GTA 6 has a nearly infinite amount of things to do, but fashionistas have found their new favorite hobby: dressing up Lucia. These elegant fans are sharing their best thrift finds on social media, and it's the Animal Crossing: New Horizons Turnip Craze all over again.
Different stores can carry different goods in GTA 6, so if you want a specific piece of clothing, you might have to run all around Leonida to find it. With users bombarding social media with Lucia's fantastic fits, the classic "I love this, where did you get it?" is overflowing into the game's universe.
Users have started sharing their favorite looks and how to compose them across social media, with threads devoted entirely to showcasing and replicating these combinations. They're happily trading fashion tips, and I've seen a true avalanche of Lucia's flawless style over the past few days.

There's a staggering variety in those outfits, from beach beauty to bank robber chic. One user swears by De'Luge swimwear for all of Lucia's sunbathing needs, while another presents so many different outfits, and all of them succeed at looking good.
Fans have even designed a website called GTAfits.com, which compiles hair, makeup, and clothing combinations, with the locations of where to find each piece. It's community-driven, allowing users to upload fits for both Jason and Lucia and tag where they found each piece. It seems to be a work in progress, especially with regard to being complete with its locations, but it's surprisingly thorough for the one-week-old game.
As an outsider in the two scenarios (PC master race?), this whole movement reminds me of the days of the Turnipmania in Animal Crossing: New Horizons during the pandemic. With turnip prices floating differently on each island, players would flock to locations with better prices, swapping hints about prime spots with their peers. It even sparked r/TurnipExchange, a subreddit made specifically for trading these valuable vegetables.
Players expected GTA 6 to have a cornucopia of dress options for both Jason and Lucia. In part, it's because GTA 5 already had a litany of options, and the 13-year gap between the two titles gave Rockstar a lot to add. Plus, if you kept up with marketing material before release, you probably noticed the sheer variety of clothes on Lucia and Jason, representing a microcosm of what to expect from the full version. Yet the game delivered a deluge of options.
Jason and Lucia's relationship is front and center in the game, with a bigger focus on the two protagonists' day-to-day lives than any other game in the franchise. Getting dressed is just one way for them (and the player) to express themselves, and with the avalanche of options available, you're probably robbing banks to support your fashion habit. The old adage rings true: fashion is the real endgame after all.
