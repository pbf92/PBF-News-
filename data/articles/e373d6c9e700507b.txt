TITLE: Manchester United’s four minutes of self-sabotage dissected
OUTLET: The Telegraph

After a calamitous opening to Mauricio Pochettino’s tenure at the weekend, Manchester United suffered four more minutes of madness in his second match in charge as his team conceded two penalties in a matter of minutes.
United have conceded the most penalties (five) and have the most red cards (four) among Premier League clubs in all competitions. Here is how the chaos unfolded at José Alvalade Stadium against Sporting Lisbon.
28mins 11secs
After a fairly even start, the madness started when Sergi Altimira spread the ball from left to right and found the feet of Geny Catamo, who made a yard of room and went down theatrically when Lisandro Martínez was close to him.
