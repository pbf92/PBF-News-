Thursday’s earnings calendar features major consumer and e-commerce, with Walmart (WMT), Alibaba (BABA), PDD Holdings (PDD) and Manchester United (MANU) reporting quarterly results before the market opens.
Consumer Demand and Margins in Focus
Thursday’s reports offer a useful snapshot of consumer spending and global e-commerce conditions, with Walmart providing insight into US household demand and Alibaba and PDD highlighting China’s competitive retail environment. Manchester United adds a different dimension, with investors watching its commercial recovery and financial discipline.
Earnings Calendar – Thursday
The key will be guidance rather than headline EPS alone. Strong revenue growth will need to translate into healthier margins and cash generation, particularly as investors remain selective about valuations and corporate spending. Any disappointing outlook from Walmart or China’s e-commerce giants could weigh on sentiment across their respective sectors.
– Walmart (NYSE: WMT) – Q3 FY2027 | Before Market Open
- – Expected EPS: $0.64
- – Market capitalisation: approximately $1.01 trillion.
- – Investors will focus on comparable-store sales, grocery demand, e-commerce growth and consumer spending trends.
- – Walmart’s ability to maintain margins while competing on prices will be particularly important as households remain sensitive to inflation and higher living costs.
- – Guidance on operating income and holiday-season demand could determine whether the stock can sustain its premium valuation.
– Alibaba Group (NYSE: BABA) – Q2 FY2027 | Before Market Open
- – Expected EPS: $11.03
- – Market capitalisation: approximately $213.58 billion.
- – Investors will monitor growth across its China e-commerce operations, cloud computing business and international commerce division.
- – Cloud revenue, investment spending and profitability remain important indicators as Alibaba attempts to strengthen its competitive position in China’s technology market.
- – Management commentary on consumer demand and its restructuring strategy could influence the stock’s next move.
– PDD Holdings (NASDAQ: PDD) – Q3 2026 | Before Market Open
- – Expected EPS: $18.84
- – Market capitalisation: approximately $110.26 billion.
- – Investors will look for updates on revenue growth, Temu’s international expansion and competitive pressures in China’s e-commerce market.
- – Rising overseas operating costs, tariffs and investments in logistics could weigh on margins, making profitability just as important as headline revenue growth.
- – Any signs of slowing consumer demand or increased competition could trigger renewed volatility.
– Manchester United (NYSE: MANU) – Q1 FY2027 | Before Market Open
- – Expected EPS: $0.06
- – Market capitalisation: approximately $3.76 billion.
- – Investors will focus on commercial revenue, broadcasting income, sponsorship agreements and matchday performance.
- – Financial discipline, operating costs and progress in the club’s restructuring efforts will remain important considerations.
- – Updates on the sporting and commercial outlook could influence investor sentiment beyond the headline earnings figure.
