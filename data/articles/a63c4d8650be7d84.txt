Target Raises Sales Outlook as November Trends Accelerate
Target Corp. TGT rose 0.88% in premarket after it reported third-quarter net sales up 7.1% to $27.1 billion, ahead of analysts' consensus of $26.5 billion, and GAAP and adjusted EPS of $2.65 against a $2.08 estimate. The results included $0.50 a share in tariff refund benefits.
Comparable sales rose 5.6%, driven by a 5.0% increase in traffic, with store comparable sales up 4.6% and digital up 9.8%. Hardlines sales grew nearly 27% and beauty more than 9%, while non-merchandise sales, including Roundel advertising, rose nearly 20%. Operating margin widened to 6.2% from 3.8%, with tariff refunds contributing 1.1 percentage points. CEO Michael Fiddelke said the company's investments are building on one another.
Target now expects full-year net sales growth of about 6%, one percentage point above its prior range, and EPS of $10.65 to $11.40. That includes about $2.14 in tariff refunds recognized so far but excludes any future refunds. The company said November sales to date are in line with its guidance and have accelerated from October, and it plans to spend $5 billion to $6 billion on capital projects in 2027.
