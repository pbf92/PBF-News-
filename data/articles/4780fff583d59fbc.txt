TITLE: EA Sports FC 27 Update 1.11 | 1.000.012 Kicks Out for Major Gameplay Changes on November 26
OUTLET: MP1st

EA Sports FC 27’s latest major patch is out on all platforms on November 26, and this brings a slew of gameplay changes. For console gamers, you’ll see this download as EA Sports FC 27 update 1.11 | 1.000.012 and this should be a hefty download.
For the complete list of changes made, read on for the patch notes that detail everything that got changed.
EA Sports FC 27 Update for November Brings Major Gameplay Changes
Gameplay
Made the following changes:
- Shorter players are not able to bump much taller opponents off the ball as easily as before.
  - Well-timed challenges will remain effective when a taller player leaves the ball exposed.
- [PC, PS5, XBS X|S Only] Adjusted left-stick dribbling responsiveness, first touch trapping, and skill move agility for taller players to make them more responsive and agile, especially in tight spaces.
- Goalkeepers will take up stronger starting angles and react more reliably against near-post threats.
- Slightly increased shot error and spread for shot attempts from difficult angles.
- Referee logic inside the penalty area has been improved so that light side-barges and minor stumbling after a fair challenge are less likely to result in a penalty kick.
- Slightly decreased interception reach and reaction range for players with lower defensive Attributes.
- Slightly adjusted the ball-recovery distance after intercepting a pass to give players a chance to fully control the ball before being tackled.
- When failing to trap a loose ball, the player will now release their focus on the ball, allowing a better positioned teammate to claim possession.
- Reduced the effectiveness of right stick flicks, especially when using them repeatedly in a short amount of time and reduced a player’s speed after performing a flick-up.
- Shoulder challenges initiated directly behind a dribbler will be less likely to force an unnatural stumble and turnover.
Addressed the following issues:
- Clean challenges could result in an unintended foul during the recovery animation.
- Defensive Midfielders were sometimes too slow to move up the pitch.
- In rare cases, shielding the ball in certain areas of the pitch could disable auto tackles for defenders.
- In rare cases, the ball could occasionally clip through a defender’s foot during an attempted tackle or interception.
- In some situations in The Grounds 1v1 small-sided Football, the goalkeeper could do a lob pass or clearance instead of a ground pass.
- In The Grounds 1v1 small-sided Football, players could be pulled against their inputs when the goalkeeper had the ball in their arms with the player nearby.
Ultimate Team
Changes:
Stay tuned for more info on further Ultimate Team updates kicking off in Season 3.
Addressed the following issues:
- In certain situations, when entering a match in UT, only UI indicators could be seen on a black screen instead of the pitch.
The Grounds
Made the following changes:
- The Winter Theme will appear in The Grounds.
  - This will be activated as part of a Chapter Update alongside the Title Update.
- Amps will now impact Street Football 1v1, 2v2, 3v3 and in-world Rush matches.
- When entering The Grounds, Archetype Level will be taken into consideration for The Grounds matchmaking.
  - This only applies to matchmaking when entering The Grounds and not to the individual game modes within.
Addressed the following issues:
- Addressed an issue in the Pitch Pong mini-game where target props did not change colour when hit.
- Some objectives were not tracking correctly.
- In Clubs matches, equipping an AI Facility perk incorrectly applied those attribute enhancements to human-controlled Virtual Pros as well.
- In rare scenarios, players from other concurrent Kickabout matches could occasionally appear on the pitch during the match.
- Sometimes, finishing a League match could cause the game to get stuck in an infinite loop of stadium spectating cutscenes.
- Addressed various issues with incorrect NPC behaviour.
- Sometimes in the Pitch Pong mini-game, when the ball went out of bounds, it would fail to reset.
- Addressed an issue where player avatars could go outside of the pitch boundary lines during the post-match cutscene.
- After disconnecting in The Grounds, menus could become inaccessible until exiting and reentering The Grounds.
- Player Avatar could unintentionally move outside the 2v2 pitch area after completing the 2v2 Street Football tutorial.
Career Mode
Made the following changes:
- Balanced transfer interest thresholds in Manager Career to ensure signing players when managing lower-league clubs is more achievable while still maintaining transfer market authenticity.
- Added the wage column to the Contract Renewal tab under Active Negotiations.
  - Calendar simulation now remembers your target date after an interruption, so you no longer have to set it again.
  - We also reduced how often the confirmation pop-up appears.
- In Manager Career, clubs can now trigger the Loan to Buy option to sign players they had loaned from other clubs permanently.
Addressed the following issues:
- Temporarily disabled player transfer requests.
  - This is to prevent further disruptions to your experience while we work on further investigations, scan the QR code on the right to learn more.
- Fixed an issue where the order of the players in team management is not kept after rearranging substitutes.
- In Manager Career, players could experience a complete lack of transfer or loan offers for lower-rated squad players and youth players, even when they are actively listed on the Transfer Hub.
- In Manager Career, high-rated young players transferred between CPU-controlled clubs were being signed on contracts with unrealistically low weekly wages.
- In Player Career, a specialisation remained equipped even after a player refunded the attribute points required to unlock it.
- Addressed an issue in Player Career where some training scenarios displayed incorrect images in the Training Hub.
- Addressed instances of misaligned text.
- Addressed instances of incorrect overlays and cinematics.
- In some instances in Manager Career, scouts return incorrect players in their reports.
- Addressed an issue in Manager Career where the player info tile was incorrectly displayed in the transfer shortlist screen for players who already had an active transfer offer.
- Addressed an issue in Manager Career where a player’s profile page incorrectly listed their status after they had already been sold for the following season.
- In Manager Career, a potential transfer fee was incorrectly displayed in the negotiation summary when setting up a loan deal with the Option to Buy set to No.
- Addressed various stability issues that could occur in Career Mode.
General, Audio, and Visual
Made the following changes:
- Updated some kits, crests, overlays, hair, audio, commentary, cameras and camera angles, boots, headbands, stadiums, lighting, banners, flags, scarves, logos, text, gloves, player images, cinematics, tops & celebrations
Addressed the following issues:
- Addressed various stability issues that could have occurred.
- In Kick-Off, some teams were missing broadcast intros.
- [PC Only] Addressed an issue where the game would not save or launch in the custom dimensions of a previously resized windowed-mode screen.
- [PC Only] Addressed an issue where, when using an analog keyboard with Steam Input disabled, menu button prompts could incorrectly switch from keyboard keys to controller icons when changing the active window.
- Sometimes, when re-entering the Warm-Up tutorial, looking-around prompts may incorrectly display mouse instructions instead of the active controller’s analogue stick prompts.
Don’t be surprised to see a hotfix or two be applied if something breaks.
