TITLE: GTA 6 Apocalypse Bunker Location & Entry Guide
OUTLET: DualShockers

What’s Grand Theft Auto 6 without a conspiracy theorist character? That’s exactly who Freddie is, a man with his own podcast, and he’s ready for the apocalypse in his big bunker. Unfortunately, you won’t know where to find Freddie as his location is somewhat obscure. There’s nothing to point you in the right direction, but thankfully, that’s why we’re here. We know exactly where to find this bunker, but be warned: it’s not as easy to breach as most hideouts.
Where is the Bunker Located?
The bunker in Grand Theft Auto 6 is a little trickier to find just because it doesn’t quite point you in the right direction. This isn’t part of a main quest, let alone a side quest, but instead is something that has to be found by exploring.
The bunker in question is located in North Leonard County. This section of the map can be found east of North Kelly County and northwest of Vice City itself. The biggest landmark you can look for on the map is Lake Leonida. The bunker can be found on the northwest road closest to it. You see several dark-colored buildings on the map.
Upon driving up to it, though, you will notice that it is completely fortified with an intercom and an automated message saying automated defenses will end you if you attempt to get in.
How to Get in the First Time
Being locked away, there will be the temptation to get in. Thankfully, the first time you do this, it’s relatively easy. If you head to the northeast corner of the bunker’s fence, you will find a water tower with a multi-ladder climb.
Climb up the first ladder and on the small platform, jump over the fence onto the shack below. Congratulations; you have successfully breached the defenses. Unfortunately, it’s not over quite yet. This is because there will be additional defenses set up.
Specifically, there will be landmines spread across the property. The best you can do is go down the road towards the bunker and keep an eye out on the ground. You will hear beeping and a red dot on the mine when you get too close.
When you get close to the bunker, drones will be activated. Simply shoot the two down before they get too close as they will explode and deal a good deal of damage. Afterward, jump down the small slope and walk up to the bunker.
The resident, Freddie, isn’t happy you have got as far as you have, but sees it as a challenge to up his defenses. He will say he will text you when he has installed a better system. Simply wait until he texts you before heading back.
How to Get in the Second Time
The second time is a little trickier to get in only because he has blocked ladder access to the water tower. Thankfully, he left some excavating tools out in the northwest of the perimeter.
There will be a truck with a large bed, and a bulldozer. Park the truck close to the electric fence and then use the bulldozer’s blade to lift the bed of the truck until it’s slightly above the fence. Jump on the truck and into the bed and then hop over the fence. It’s best to set this up near the water tower to avoid some mines.
Speaking of which, the mines are now a little harder to detect. This is because they’re buried slightly further underground, and you can’t see the red light when you get near them. You will still be able to hear it, but it’s best to use your Focus Mode to see them as they light up.
Moving down the road, avoiding the significantly more placed mines, the drones attack has also been adjusted. He will still send two waves of them, but instead of one at a time, the first wave has two drones and the second has three.
After taking care of them, jump down the slope again and approach the bunker for you to meet Freddie for the first time. Congratulations! You can now enter the bunker and even hang out with the apocalypse-ready Freddie.
