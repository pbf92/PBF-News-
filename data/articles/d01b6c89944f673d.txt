TITLE: GTA 6 Fan Finds a Bizarre Way to Stay Unemployed & Play All Day
OUTLET: PlayStation LifeStyle

A Grand Theft Auto VI fan has come up with an unconventional way to fund his gaming plans hours after the game’s launch. This plan allows him to skip the traditional method of earning money in a creative way.
Rather than relying on a regular salary, the GTA fan put out a video on Instagram to announce that he will be using a part of his body as an advertising space, allowing him to not only stay at home and enjoy the game but also earn some cash without going to an office.
His unusual plan to make money without working
In a viral Instagram Reel, creator hoodyirl laid out the pitch to potential advertisers. He said, “I’m selling ad space on my leg so I can stay unemployed and play GTA 6 all day long. My offer is very simple: you pay me money, and I will tattoo your logo or brand on my leg forever. This is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, so brands, tap in.”
In the clip, the GTA fan had also divided his leg into several sections, each carrying a different price tag. The hand-drawn layout listed individual spaces for $100, $200, and $300, offering potential sponsors different options to advertise their businesses. However, the unusual sponsorship idea completely depends on whether businesses are willing to pay for the opportunity.
The release of the sixth installment in the Grand Theft Auto franchise has been one of the most anticipated events in gaming. With the game arriving after a 13-year wait, many fans have prepared by clearing their schedules to spend hours exploring its world and experiencing its new features on PlayStation.
Several major companies have also introduced paid time off to let employees enjoy gaming. For instance, Logitech announced a paid day off called Logi Gaming Day for its approximately 7,000 employees, allowing them to choose a day to play any game of their choice.
