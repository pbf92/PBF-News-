Update 01.002.002 for GTA VI is currenty being rolled out
, by Thibaut
Rockstar Games has released a new patch for GTA VI. It's the free update 01.002.002, whose existence was revealed by PlayStation Game Size on X.
The update is 603 MB. We don’t know what it contains yet. It likely includes bug fixes and additional optimizations. Note that 33% of players can download it right now, and 66% will be able to get it in three hours. Everyone should have it within six hours.
