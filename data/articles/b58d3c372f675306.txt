TITLE: Manchester United Lose Their Rising Star as JJ Gabriel Confirms His Exit and Eyes Real Madrid
OUTLET: beIN SPORTS

Manchester United Lose Their Rising Star as JJ Gabriel Confirms His Exit and Eyes Real Madrid
- JJ Gabriel shared an emotional farewell message to Manchester United after six years at the club. The 16-year-old forward is ready to begin a new chapter in European football.
Manchester United are losing one of their brightest young talents. JJ Gabriel, the 16-year-old forward, confirmed his departure from Old Trafford with a video posted on social media, looking back on his best moments after six years at the club. "Manchester United will always be part of my story," the teenager said in his farewell message.
Gabriel joined the club in 2020, when he was just 10 years old, and quickly attracted attention with his performances in the academy. His achievements include winning the Jimmy Murphy Award, being named Premier League U-18 Player of the Year, and featuring in a preseason match with the first team.
Real Madrid Emerges as His Likely Next Destination
All signs point to Real Madrid as Gabriel's next destination following his farewell. According to reports from Sky Sports and The Mirror, the forward has reportedly reached an agreement to join the Spanish giants during the January 2027 transfer window. Barcelona and Bayern Munich were also reportedly interested in the youngster, but Real Madrid's project ultimately gained the upper hand.
The deal could include a three-year contract with a salary exceeding $1.1 million per year, according to figures published by The Mirror. Gabriel holds a European passport, which would allow him to join a club in the European Union before turning 18. Real Madrid reportedly intends to initially integrate him into its reserve team, Real Madrid Castilla, as he continues his development.
JJ Gabriel's Dream: Winning the Ballon d'Or
The young forward has big ambitions for his career. In comments reported by Marca, Gabriel admitted that his dream is to win the Ballon d'Or, highlighting the lofty goals he has set for himself. Joining Real Madrid would give him an opportunity to develop at a club that has invested heavily in young talent in recent years.
However, the road ahead will not be easy. Gabriel will have to adapt to a new country, a different competition, and the demands of one of the biggest clubs in the world. For now, his first challenge will be earning regular playing time and proving he can replicate in Spanish football what he showed during his time in England.
Manchester United Could Consider Legal Action Over His Departure
Gabriel's departure could still have another chapter off the pitch. Manchester United will retain his registration until the end of the season and, according to The Mirror, the club is considering possible legal action related to the move. United's hierarchy is reportedly unhappy with how the situation unfolded, particularly after the player requested to leave the club in September.
Reports suggest training compensation could range between $130,000 and $390,000, although no final amount has been confirmed. Manchester United are therefore losing one of their most promising academy prospects as Real Madrid prepares to welcome the teenager.
