Drone user reveals why he secretly filmed Mauricio Pochettino's Man United training session
Mauricio Pochettino's first training session with Manchester United had an unwelcome visitor
A drone operator who filmed Mauricio Pochettino's first Manchester United training session has insisted he meant no harm. The new head coach was on the training ground with his new squad for the first time on Thursday.
With limited time to get ready for Sunday's tough trip to Liverpool, Pochettino may have been troubled to learn that his session was monitored by a drone. A 12-minute video of the session in high definition has been posted to YouTube by an account named 'CC OVERVIEW'.
It captures Pochettino working with his new squad, alongside commentary from the drone pilot, who says: "I can get quite a bit closer without being a nuisance."
The footage shows players divided into smaller groups performing drills. On a separate full-size pitch, 11 players can be seen positioned across the field amongst mannequins. The group then gathers in a huddle to receive feedback from Pochettino.
At the close of the footage, the drone operator says: "I'm going to leave it there, I don't want to cause any disruption."
United have been approached for a comment, while the owner of CC OVERVIEW has stood by the recording. They told the Mirror: "[My intention was] just to capture Pochettino in his first training session.

"It was around 30 minutes into the session, so presumed it would not reveal any tactics or hints at team selection and just wanted to give United fans that first look.
"I could have stayed longer, I wasn't on United's property and at no point flew over it but had captured all I had intended to so wrapped up filming."
The outfield players taking part in the full-size pitch session with Pochettino were Harry Maguire, Kobbie Mainoo, Amad Diallo, Matheus Cunha, Bryan Mbeumo, Ayden Heaven, Andrey Santos, Joshua Zirkzee and Harry Amass.
Responding to concerns raised in the comments section, the channel's owner posted on YouTube: "For the people asking about legality, it is unrestricted airspace, meaning anyone can fly over it.
"The drone remained in line of sight at all times, it didn't fly over anyone or even near anyone so was a perfectly legal flight going by the CAA rules that regulates drone usage.
"The drone is correctly labelled and I hold all the relevant licences, public liability and insurance required. As for leaking team news/ tactics, it was half an hour into the manager's first training session, there are many media there so certainly won't be the only footage of training released."
Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) guidelines specify: "If you use these devices where people can expect privacy, such as inside their home or garden, you're likely to be breaking data protection laws."
