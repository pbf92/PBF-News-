There is no denying that Rockstar Games’ GTA 6 is a technical masterpiece and a testament to video game studios’ propensity to deliver greatness, but with their latest game, did that greatness come at a cost? The life of your base PS5 and XBOX Series S/X consoles.
What Exactly Is Happening?
While the issue does not seem to be widespread for the millions of GTA 6 players currently experiencing the madness around Leonida, there seems to be a growing number of complaints from others claiming the graphically demanding game is essentially bricking their gaming consoles.
At first, it seemed the issue was limited to the base PS5 console, but it appears the Xbox Series S/X is also having problems running the game.
Reports of PS5 consoles began hitting the internet with power measurements reaching 274.2 watts under heavy load, which isn’t outside Sony’s design ceiling for the console. Still, that number is without a doubt higher than typical game load.
Gamers took to social media, sharing that while playing they received error code CE-108255 after their games crashed, with some saying the crashes prevented them from progressing in the game and, in some cases, cost them hours’ worth of playtime.
Some gamers even claim GTA 6 bricked their consoles completely. Streamer Shiv’s PS5 literally died while she was streaming the game, and she claimed her GPU was fried.
XBOX Console Owners Spoke Too Soon
Immediately, the jokes began to fly, with many people calling out the fact that, due to PlayStation’s exclusive marketing agreement with Rockstar Games, the PS5 and PS5 Pro were the best place to experience GTA 6, with many calling into question that claim following the wave of PS5 owners experiencing issues.
Then there was also the Digital Foundry report that revealed the XBOX Series X handles the game better than the PS5, further adding to the hoopla surrounding the game.
Well, Team Green’s bragging was short-lived, as reports of Xbox Series S/X consoles having similar issues began popping up on technical forums.
Per Digital Trends:
Spotted by Notebookcheck, Polish repair technician modyfikatorcasper noted an unusually sharp increase in catastrophic Xbox failures after the GTA 6 launch. Although Xbox consoles are far less common than PlayStation systems in Poland, they now account for roughly two out of every ten units brought in for service. Reported problems range from random shutdowns, severe slowdowns, and failed updates to full hardware bricks where an Xbox Series X refuses to power on or displays no video signal.
The issue doesn’t seem to be widespread, but that could change as gamers log plenty of hours over the holiday weekend.
We recommend opening up those PS5 consoles, giving them a good cleaning, and making sure they are in a well-ventilated area so they stay cool and don’t overheat while running the game.
