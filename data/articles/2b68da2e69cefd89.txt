TITLE: GTA 6 Sells 32.5 Million Copies Before Launch And Beats Avengers: Endgame's Lifetime Box Office By Nearly $1 Billion In Just 24 Hours
OUTLET: Kotaku

Take-Two has confirmed the first official sales numbers for Grand Theft Auto 6, revealing 32.5 million units sold pre-release, leading to an estimated $3.79 billion in revenue through the end of its launch day on November 19.
That’s a new pre-order sales record across all consoles and solidifies GTA 6 as the “biggest entertainment launch in history,” according to Rockstar’s parent company. “It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” said Rockstar Games president and cofounder Sam Houser in a press release. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”
According to Circana senior director Mat Piscatella, the game dwarfed everything else in terms of engagement on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S during its first 24 hours. Roughly 61 percent of all PS5 players based in the U.S. played it on launch day, alongside about 56 percent of Xbox players globally. Players on both consoles averaged over 494 minutes of playtime. Fortnite was the second-most-played game that day with just 10 percent of active users across both platforms playing it at some point.
“To put this into perspective, Grand Theft Auto IV sold 25 million units across five years. Grand Theft Auto V sold 11 million copies on its first day and generated $800 million,” Niko Partners analyst Daniel Ahmad wrote. “And that’s comparing it to GTA. Other games don’t come close.”
The day-one revenue also dwarfs the highest grossing Hollywood blockbusters. Avengers: Endgame, which currently holds the lifetime box office record after multiple theatrical runs sits at $2.94 billion. Put it another way, GTA 6‘s day-one revenue is equal to the combined lifetime box offices for Avengers: Infinity War and The Odyssey.
This all reinforces that GTA 6 is as big as fans were expecting. Whether it’s as big as the market was hoping for remains to be seen. Some industry observers were forecasting 45 million copies sold in its first year. It looks like it might even have a shot of selling that many copies before the end of 2026. The company’s stock was trending upward following the news.
