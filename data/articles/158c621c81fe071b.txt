TITLE: GTA VI is finally here after 13 years. Here’s how much it costs
OUTLET: WAFB

BATON ROUGE, La. (WAFB) - The wait is finally over for millions of gamers.
Grand Theft Auto VI, commonly known as GTA VI, officially launched Thursday, Nov. 19, bringing an end to a 13-year wait for the next major installment in Rockstar Games’ franchise.
The game is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a starting price of $79.99.
It’s the first mainline Grand Theft Auto release since Grand Theft Auto V debuted in September 2013.
Here’s what to know about the release, pricing and where to play.
How much does GTA VI cost?
Rockstar Games is offering two editions of Grand Theft Auto VI:
- Standard Edition — $79.99: Includes the full game.
- Ultimate Edition — $99.99: Includes the full game and additional vehicles, weapons, clothing and other in-game content.
The Ultimate Edition includes exclusive content that becomes available as players progress through the game’s story.
Players who purchase the Standard Edition can upgrade to the Ultimate Edition later for an additional $20.
Prices are in U.S. dollars and do not include taxes.
What consoles can play GTA VI?
Grand Theft Auto VI is available to play on:
- PlayStation 5
- PlayStation 5 Pro
- Xbox Series X
- Xbox Series S
Xbox One owners can also access the game through Xbox Cloud Gaming, although it cannot run natively on the older console. Cloud streaming requires a separate game purchase and is subject to service availability and eligibility requirements.
GTA VI has not been released as a native PC game, and Rockstar has not announced a PC release date.
The game is not available natively on PlayStation 4.
Where can you buy GTA VI?
Players can purchase Grand Theft Auto VI digitally through the PlayStation Store, Xbox Store or Rockstar Games.
Boxed versions are also available through participating retailers, but there’s one major difference from traditional physical game releases.
Physical copies of GTA VI do not contain a game disc. Instead, the box includes a code to download the game digitally.
Rockstar began distributing the boxed versions ahead of launch to allow buyers to download the game in advance.
Are there any GTA VI launch bonuses?
Players purchasing the game by Thursday, Nov. 19, can receive the Vintage Vice City Pack, which includes bonus vehicles, clothing, hairstyles and a weapon design.
Eligible digital purchases also include one month of GTA+, Rockstar’s subscription service.
Players should review the terms of the subscription offer, including renewal conditions, before redeeming it.
What is GTA VI about?
Grand Theft Auto VI returns players to Vice City, a fictional destination inspired by Miami, Florida, in the fictional state of Leonida.
The story follows two protagonists, Jason and Lucia, who become caught in a criminal conspiracy following a robbery gone wrong.
The game features an open-world environment with cities, highways, beaches and other locations inspired by Florida.
Grand Theft Auto VI is rated M for Mature by the Entertainment Software Rating Board, meaning it is intended for players ages 17 and older.
Rockstar has also released a new collection of official screenshots, videos and artwork through its GTA VI website.
