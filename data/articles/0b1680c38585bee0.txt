TITLE: GTA VI Features a Hidden Memorial for Rockstar Developers’ Late Family Members
OUTLET: RockstarINTEL

Rockstar Games has included a special memorial in Grand Theft Auto VI to honor the late family members of some of its developers. Located in Leonida, the memorial features several names of people who passed away during the game’s development. According to a report by GAMER2GO, two Rockstar developers have shared the personal stories behind the tribute.
One of them is Matt Green, a Rockstar Games QA developer whose late mother, Bev Green, is among the names on the memorial. He shared a post on Instagram, explaining how much the tribute means to him.
Green revealed that his mother played an important role in his journey into game development. When he was younger, he asked her if he could play Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. Looking back, he believes that decision may have helped set him on the path toward eventually working at Rockstar Games.
Sadly, his mother passed away in March 2026, around eight months before GTA VI was released. Green explained that she was excited to see the game he had been working on, but he never got the chance to show her the finished project.
The memorial can be found just north of Hamlet in Leonida. Her name, Bev Green, appears alongside several other names on the wall.
Another Rockstar Developer Also Finds His Family Members
Matt Green is not the only Rockstar developer whose family members have been remembered in GTA VI. Production designer Terrance Radcliff also revealed that his late father and daughter were included in the same memorial.
Radcliff shared his reaction on X, explaining that he lost both of them during the game’s development. Their names, Elhuntry Radcliff and Jade Radcliff, appear next to each other on the memorial.
He described it as an honor to see his father and first daughter forever remembered in GTA VI. He also explained that having them connected to such an important moment in gaming history meant everything to him.
The full list of names on the memorial has not been explained yet, so it is possible that other Rockstar developers have also honored their loved ones in the same location.
While GTA VI is filled with hidden details, Easter eggs and references for players to discover, this memorial is something different. It carries a real personal meaning for the people who worked on the game, giving them a way to remember their loved ones inside a world they helped create.
