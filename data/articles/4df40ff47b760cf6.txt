How High Can ANT Stock Go? Wall Street Debut Sparks Valuation Debate – One Analyst Sees Amazon-Google Scale Opportunity In Anthropic
- Needham put Anthropic in the same league as Amazon, Google and Microsoft.
- Melius set the highest target at $155 over two years, nearly 60% above the debut close.
- NYU Stern's Aswath Damodaran valued Anthropic at about $76, citing higher data center costs and regulatory pressure.
Shares of Anthropic PBC (ANT) jumped 17% in their Wall Street debut on Thursday as analysts cast the Claude maker as a rare growth opportunity in the same league as Amazon and Google, with the most bullish target pointing to nearly 60% more upside.
ANT stock opened at $111.50 on Thursday before settling at $97.17, well above its $83 offer price even on a day the Nasdaq Composite fell over 2%.
The $81.34 billion offering overtook SpaceX's June listing as the largest IPO on record. Alongside a separate $26 billion private placement, Anthropic expected to receive $104.8 billion after fees and expenses, giving it cash to fund the next generation of Claude.
Analyst Puts Anthropic In Big Tech's League
Needham initiated coverage with a 'Buy' rating and a $150 target, implying 54% upside from current levels. "Growth opportunities like ANT rarely come along but we think history puts ANT in the same dominant bucket as fellow tech titans AMZN, GOOG, and MSFT," the firm said.
Needham estimates that AI could address a tenth of the $56 trillion global labor market. Anthropic already earns more when customers put Claude to work on more tasks. Usage-based charges accounted for 79% of revenue in the first nine months of 2026, according to its prospectus.
Needham expects revenue to grow more than 150% in fiscal 2027 and believes Claude's brand and Anthropic's scale could drive operating margins above 35% over time, even as the company spends tens of billions of dollars a year training models.
William Blair also expects Anthropic to win a larger share of corporate budgets. "We think AI is a generational shift in the tech landscape, and Anthropic is leading the charge in developing AI with a select handful of other companies," the firm said.
William Blair initiated coverage at 'Outperform,' calling the IPO valuation "very reasonable" at nine times estimated 2027 sales. It said a multiple of 17 to 20 times sales could put the stock at $130 to $150, or 34% to 54% above its debut close.
What It Would Take ANT To Hit $155
Melius initiated coverage at 'Buy' with a two-year target of $155, implying nearly 60% upside from Anthropic's $97.17 debut close. The firm expects customers to use Claude more often and buy more products as the cost per task falls. Over time, it sees earnings power above $5 a share and operating margins above 30%.
