Morten Christensen Qualifies for Third TRUMP Dinner After $6 Hedge Profit
Trader Morten Christensen qualified for the TRUMP dinner for the third time after hedging and making a $6 profit. According to Odaily, the third dinner was held on November 22 at Trump National Golf Club in Virginia. Christensen said his first two qualifying attempts cost about $1,200 and $500, respectively. A total of 130 wallets qualified, collectively holding about $8.6 million worth of TRUMP. Democratic U.S. senators have repeatedly requested documents related to Trump-associated tokens.
