TITLE: STAT+: White House pulls nomination of Heidi Overton to lead FDA
OUTLET: STAT News

WASHINGTON — The Trump administration is pulling its nominee to lead the Food and Drug Administration, Heidi Overton, according to a White House official and a source close to Overton.
The decision follows concerns that Overton, a preventive care doctor who previously served in the Trump White House, lacked sufficient support from Republicans on the Senate’s health panel, which had been expected to vote on her nomination Wednesday.
The news was first reported by Axios on Tuesday evening.
