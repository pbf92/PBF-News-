TITLE: Motorola Solutions Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $20M
OUTLET: TradingView

Motorola Solutions Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $20M
Gregory Q. Brown, Chairman and CEO and a director of Motorola Solutions, sold 35,000 shares in five transactions at a weighted average price of $534.26 per share, for a total of $18,699,060. Following the transactions, Brown directly owned 47,768 shares and indirectly owned 293,170 shares: 32 through the Motorola Solutions 401(k) Plan, 2,220 held by his wife, 40,500, 21,580, 18,747 and 17,650 through trusts, and 71,270, 39,671 and 81,500 through the 2025-1 Grantor Retained Annuity Trust, 2026-1 Grantor Retained Annuity Trust and 2026-2 Grantor Retained Annuity Trust, respectively.
Cynthia Yazdi, SVP and Chief of Staff to the Chairman and CEO of Motorola Solutions, sold 3,094 shares at $539.135 per share, for a total of $1,668,084. Following the sale, Yazdi directly owned 2,322 shares and indirectly owned 11 shares through the Motorola Solutions 401(k) Plan.
