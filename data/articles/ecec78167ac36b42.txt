TITLE: Ayden Heaven keen on Manchester United exit in January
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United defender Ayden Heaven wants to leave on loan in January, but would consider a permanent transfer amid concerns over his minutes.
The 20-year-old is keen to secure more regular first-team football and will assess his future in the winter window.
According to BBC Sport, Heaven fears that new manager Mauricio Pochettino 'will favour experienced defenders' as he looks to turn results around at Old Trafford. Heaven has been an unused substitute in each of Pochettino's first two games in charge.
Barcelona and Fulham are reportedly weighing up moves. The Spanish champions have emerged as surprise contenders as Hansi Flick looks for defensive cover. Barcelona want to sign a left-sided centre-back in the January window and have Heaven on their shortlist.
Fulham are also considering additions amid uncertainty surrounding Calvin Bassey. The Nigeria international's deal is up in the summer, and he has been linked with Manchester United.
Bassey has confirmed that he is in contract talks with Fulham over an extension, though no agreement has been reached. Heaven was born in London and came through the academy at Arsenal before moving to Manchester United in 2025.
