Marriott Pres. Greater China Sold Shares Worth Over $2.5M
Mao Yibing, Marriott’s Pres. Greater China and an officer of the issuer, sold 6,000 shares of Class A Common Stock at $428.41 per share for a total of $2,570,460. After the transaction, Mao Yibing directly owned 21,398 shares of Class A Common Stock, 2,285 Class A Common Restricted Stock Units, and 156 Class A Common Stock Deferred Stock Bonus Awards.
