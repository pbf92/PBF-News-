TITLE: Dead by Daylight: Terrifier’s New Killer is No Laughing Matter
OUTLET: Xbox Wire

Hope you’re not afraid of clowns. Putting the laughter in slaughter, The Terrifier steps into Dead by Daylight as the hit horror multiplayer’s latest Killer.
A serial murderer fueled by a sick sense of humor, Art the Clown met his end at the hands of Sienna Shaw. Rising from the dead with a thirst for vengeance, Art’s dogged pursuit of his favorite final girl has led the two straight into the claws of The Entity and Dead by Daylight’s endless cycle of Trials.
Known for his unpredictable nature and ecstatic violence, The Terrifier makes a clear fit for Dead by Daylight‘s fiendish roster. But with that chaos comes the challenge of making humor and horror blend together into a compelling gameplay loop for Killers and Survivors alike.
Players beware – it’s all fun and games until the clown pulls out a gun.
A Work of Art
Joining the likes of Jason Voorhees and Freddy Krueger, Art the Clown is the latest addition to Dead by Daylight‘s unmatched repertoire of horror legends. While past slashers were the killers that many grew up fearing, Art the Clown represents a more modern (and extreme) horror icon for a new generation of fans.
“I think the thing that attracts people to Art is how fun he is,” reflects Dave Richard, Dead by Daylight‘s Creative Director. “He’s an absolute menace taking his time to torture and tease his victims before going all in for a big kill. We really wanted to lean into that fantasy when designing his kit.”
With his Power, Bag of Toys, Art reaches into his trash bag for a random selection of weapons including a flail, a flamethrower, a firearm, a beer bottle full of acid, and even his signature bike horn. Each successful hit with a toy builds its deadliness, eventually letting Art down injured Survivors and execute them with murderous glee.
With a unique Mini-Mori for each weapon alongside two additional finishers, Art has a mind-blowing total of seven executions: a new record for the series! This clown’s creativity really knows no bounds.
Art’s kit is as unpredictable as his film counterpart’s antics, making you adapt on the fly to whichever tools you pull from his bag. The gun is great for shooting targets at a distance but is a bit shaky when aiming in close quarters. No choice is a bad choice, however, and in a skilled players hand, every tool can spell death for the most daring Survivors.
Clowning on Survivors
Never speaking a word in “The Terrifier” films, Art instead relies on big gestures and huge facial expressions, a signature part of his character. Principal Killer Designer Janick Neveu knew that incorporating Art’s overly emotive nature would be key to bringing him to life in-game.
“We knew right from the start that we wanted to have an emote system in place for Art. The idea of hooking a Survivor and then immediately pointing and laughing at them was too good to resist.”
A first for any Killer in the series, Art’s emotes range from a mocking laugh and an eerie wave to straight up flipping the bird at his opponents. More than just a means of self-expression, Art’s emotes play an essential part in his kit as well.
“Players can emote at any time as Art, but emoting at a Survivor after downing or hooking them will increase the deadliness of all his weapons,” explains Neveu. “This really puts them into a position where if they want to play optimally, they really have to get into the character’s mindset and act like Art.”
This extends to The Terrifier’s unique character Perks as well. Laughingstock, Kill Spree, and Funny Business all incentivize players to take big swings as they go over-the-top to get their last laughs. When you step into Art’s oversized shoes, you’d better be ready to act like a clown.
Delightfully devious, Art the Clown will have you going all out in every Trial. When your best jokes don’t land in the Trial, remember that sometimes the easiest way to crack someone up is to give their head a good beating with a hefty table leg.
Dead by Daylight: Terrifier is out on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One today.
