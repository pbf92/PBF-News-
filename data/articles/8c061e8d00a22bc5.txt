TITLE: Chinese pastor remains in custody after inconclusive trial
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

Chinese pastor Gao Quanfu and his wife have been left in limbo after their trials concluded without a verdict.
Gao Quanfu, 70, is currently facing fraud charges in the Beilin District People’s Court, in Xi’an.
If found guilty, he could face up to 13 years in prison.
He has been the lead pastor of the Light of Zion church in the inland Northern Chinese city for more than 40 years.
Quanfu was detained by authorities in May 2025.
His wife, Pang Yu, was arrested in September of last year.
Christian Solidarity Worldwide is calling on the Chinese government to release details about the location and condition of the pair.
“We’re calling for more information to be released by authorities or a statement on their location, condition and the results of the trial,” says CSW’s China researcher, who goes by ‘John’ for security reasons.
CSW says it holds significant concerns for the health of the pair, particularly Pany Yu, who has suffered heart disease and pain in her left leg.
“[It’s] an issue because she is forced to undertake manual labour in the prison, while standing for the duration.”
On top of the health concerns, John says he is worried the pair may not get a fair trial.
He says the lawyers have been pressured by the authorities, causing some of Quanfu’s representatives to drop the case.
“There’s been so many violations like that throughout this trial. The lack of a pre-trial hearing for Pastor Gao’s wife and Pastor Gao himself. His pre-trial lasted less than half a day. So, this is not a fair legal procedure and that’s what we’re calling out here at CSW.”
Pastor Gao is the most recent in a line of religious leaders in China detained over fraud charges.
“It’s an increasing trend to use criminal charges rather than ideological charges to charge and detain pastors and religious leaders of all faiths across China.”
Wang Yi, pastor of the Early Rain Covenant Church, was sentenced to 9 years in prison in 2019, in part due to “illegal business activity”.
