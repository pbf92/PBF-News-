ChainOpera AI: COAI supply unlock releases at least 11.25 million tokens - Feb 2027
ChainOpera AI has scheduled a COAI supply unlock for February 2027. The release will make at least 11.25 million tokens available to advisors, investors and the team under the announced schedule.
The minimum release equals 5.98% of the token's circulating supply. Once the unlock lifts the transfer restriction on these tokens, recipients can choose whether to hold or sell them.
