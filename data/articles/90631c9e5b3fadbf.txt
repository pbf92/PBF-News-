TITLE: INTERVIEW: GTA 4 Actor on GTA 6 Reviews: "It Needs To Succeed"
OUTLET: Insider Gaming

Grand Theft Auto 6 is being released in mere hours. It’s the final countdown to the most important video game launch in human history, and it’s the ultimate delivery on years of development, investment, and patience from an entire world of gamers.
In the run-up to GTA 6 hitting the market, I sat down with a very important guest for the most recent episode of Access Granted, which will soon be live in the Insider Gaming app. This episode features Jason Zumwalt, the actor behind Grand Theft Auto 4’s Roman Bellic, and naturally, we spoke about the imminent release of GTA 6.
Here’s a snippet of that interview.
GTA 4’s Roman Bellic Predicts GTA 6’s Review Score
Grand Theft Auto 4 is, by numbers, the highest-rated Grand Theft Auto game ever made. In fact, it’s the fourth highest-rated game of all time on Metacritic. That’s an impressive feat, and while other Rockstar titles have gotten close to GTA 4’s lofty heights, none of them have managed to steal the crown.
But some believe that Grand Theft Auto 6 will manage it.
On November 19, GTA 6 hits the global market, and millions upon millions of gamers will dive into the open world of Leonida to explore everything Rockstar Games has spent the last decade or so piecing together, bit by bit. It has been an extremely long road to get here, but we’re within spitting distance of the game.
I sat down with Jason Zumwalt, who you’ll know best as the legendary GTA character, Roman Bellic. We were introduced to Roman in the opening minutes of 2008’s Grand Theft Auto 4, and he stuck with us for the entire game, serving as one of the most memorable supporting characters in franchise history.
In one segment of our interview, which heavily leaned towards Rockstar Games’ iconic open-world series, I asked Jason where he thought GTA 6 would fall on the review score scale.
He said:
At this point, it’s tough. There’s politics involved, too.
GTA 6 kind of needs to succeed. I think the industry knows that it needs to succeed. Politics could come into play. But I also feel like when you have a number like 98, it’s a stiff hill to climb.
I think it’ll top out somewhere between 94 and 96, which is still amazing.
I’m wondering how much the AI, NPC aspect of the game is going to rub people the wrong way in the beginning.
That’s all you get to see! If you want to check out the full interview, you’ll be able to access it as part of the Insider Gaming app’s premium subscription platform tomorrow, November 18.
But Jason makes a great point. The industry has a lot riding on Grand Theft Auto 6. Numbers have shown that, if it wasn’t for GTA 6 coming out, console sales would be in a monumental slump. And in terms of software spending, GTA 6 sales will pump billions into the companies behind the game, and that’s before we talk about GTA 6 multiplayer or the PC version of the game.
GTA 6’s higher price point (even if it’s not that much higher) could set a new paradigm for AAA games, and it just received nominations at The Game Awards, including Game of the Year, before it has even been released.
Generational stuff.
