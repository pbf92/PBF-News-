Manchester United Activate Bruno Fernandes Contract Extension
Manchester. Captain Bruno Fernandes's contract with a one-year option has been activated by Manchester United. Sources have indicated that the 32-year-old player's contract, which was set to expire next summer, has now been extended until 2028.
Sources say this decision was made in the best interest of both parties. This move means that foreign clubs will not be able to have formal discussions with Fernandes when the transfer window opens on January 1.
Portugal's international player Fernandes had confirmed on Tuesday that discussions regarding the new contract were on hold. Sources say these talks will only resume after United's league position improves.
Since arriving at United from Sporting in January 2020 for £47 million, Fernandes has played 343 games and scored 115 goals. Last season, he broke the Premier League assist record and won all the individual player of the year awards, when United finished third.
Although questions about his long-term future still arise, the contract extension ensures that United will receive a transfer fee if Fernandes leaves the club in the summer.
In the summer of 2025, United was prepared to sell Fernandes when Saudi Pro League club Al Hilal made a big offer. He later stated that he was 'sad' that the club was willing to sell him, although he stayed at the club after discussing it with his wife. He reiterated on Tuesday that his ambition to win major trophies still remains.
Meanwhile, Fernandes's participation in Saturday's Premier League match against Brentford is uncertain due to a virus, after returning from the Champions League match against former club Sporting. The playmaker, who scored both goals in the 2-2 draw, did not participate in training on Friday, and head coach Mauricio Pochettino said he would make a decision on Saturday morning after assessing his condition.
'We will assess him throughout the day and see how he is when he wakes up tomorrow morning,' Pochettino said. 'We will not take any risks.'
