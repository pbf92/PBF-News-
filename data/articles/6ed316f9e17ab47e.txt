TITLE: GTA 6 Online Isn't Live Yet. Here's When Multiplayer Could Arrive
OUTLET: PCMag

After a lengthy hiatus, Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here, and it's mind-blowing. GTA 6 takes players to a vast fake Florida that would surely be fun to explore with friends. But when will the new Vice City hit Grand Theft Auto Online? While Rockstar has not announced anything official about GTA 6 Online, there's a lot of speculation. Here's what we know so far.
What Is GTA Online?
Grand Theft Auto 6 breaks a 13-year gap between GTA games, but the wait hasn’t felt quite as long as it has been. Why? Soon after GTA 5 launched in 2013, Rockstar released Grand Theft Auto Online. In this online multiplayer companion mode, up to 30 aspiring virtual criminals can team up to commit all sorts of sleazy shenanigans in the main game's expansive open world.
GTA Online has become an absolute juggernaut over the past decade, drawing in millions of players and billions of dollars. It even has its own subscription service. So, even though we haven’t had a numbered entry in a long time, thanks to GTA Online, Grand Theft Auto has only gotten bigger.
Can I Play GTA 6 Online Now?
No, GTA 6 Online is not currently available. Despite starring two playable characters that you'll switch between, the live version is strictly a single-player affair, like classic GTA games. If you want to explore Vice City with a friend, they’ll just have to watch you play through the story.
Is GTA 6 Getting an Online Mode?
We don’t know any details about when GTA 6 Online will officially launch, or what it will even look like. However, given how much GTA Online has become a vital source of revenue for Rockstar, it’s safe to assume the inevitable smash-hit GTA 6 will also get a lucrative online mode.
There’s an argument to be made that GTA Online is the more important product, and not launching the games simultaneously gives the developers the chance to make sure both games meet Rockstar's high-quality standards. GTA Online had an infamously rough launch before it became what it is today, so it makes sense that Rockstar would want to get it right this time. The game is reportedly the most preordered title in history, so the developers can afford to wait.
Details are scarce, but we have confidence GTA 6 Online is coming in the near future. If we had to guess, more information about an online mode will be shared alongside a future PC release. Right now, rumors are all over the map, from six months to another year or two before the game even comes to Windows. For now, we just have to stay patient.
Can I Still Play GTA Online When GTA 6 Comes Out?
Just because a new game is coming out doesn't mean GTA 5 Online is going anywhere. Even though GTA 6 is live, you can still play Grand Theft Auto Online as a separate release. You’ll just have to settle for driving around familiar Los Santos despite Vice City becoming the new hotness. Digital preorders for GTA 6’s $80 Standard Edition and $100 Ultimate Edition both come with a free one-month subscription for GTA+, which gives you benefits in GTA Online. It's clear Rockstar wants you to stay familiar with the online release while we wait for the next incarnation.
We'll share more details as they become available. In the meantime, make sure to choose the right console or take a trip down Vice City’s memory lane.
