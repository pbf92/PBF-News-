TITLE: Meta Platforms (META) Down 8.1% Since Last Earnings Report: Can It Rebound?
OUTLET: Yahoo! Finance Canada

A month has gone by since the last earnings report for Meta Platforms (META). Shares have lost about 8.1% in that time frame, underperforming the S&P 500.
Will the recent negative trend continue leading up to its next earnings release, or is Meta Platforms due for a breakout? Well, first let's take a quick look at the most recent earnings report in order to get a better handle on the recent catalysts for Meta Platforms, Inc. before we dive into how investors and analysts have reacted as of late.
META's Q3 Earnings Beat Estimates, Revenues Rise Y/Y on Ad Growth
Meta Platforms reported third-quarter 2026 adjusted earnings of $7.40 per share, which increased 2.1% year over year, beating the Zacks Consensus Estimate by 13.50%. Revenues jumped 26% year over year to $64.6 billion, surpassing the consensus mark by 1.88%.
Strong advertising demand, AI-driven engagement and improving monetization supported revenue growth. Ad impressions increased 10% year over year, while the average price per ad advanced 14%. However, higher infrastructure spending and legal expenses pressured operating profitability.  
META's Ad Business Fuels Revenue Growth
Family of Apps revenues, which accounted for 99.3% of total revenues, increased 26.4% year over year to $64.16 billion. Advertising revenues rose 25.9% to $63.06 billion, supported by healthy engagement, user growth and ad-load optimization across Meta's platforms.
Family of Apps other revenues surged 59% year over year to $1.1 billion, primarily driven by WhatsApp paid messaging and subscriptions. 
Reality Labs revenues declined 5.7% to $443 million as lower Quest headset sales more than offset strong growth in AI glasses revenues.  
Meta's AI Investments Boost User Engagement
Family daily active people averaged 3.64 billion in September, up 3% year over year. Average revenue per person increased to $17.72 from $14.46 in the year-ago quarter, reflecting improving monetization across the company's ecosystem.
Instagram global time spent per user reached an all-time high, growing at a double-digit rate. Facebook Reels engagement also achieved record levels, while Threads time spent per user increased more than 20%. AI-powered recommendation improvements lifted Instagram Reels viewing time by more than 4%.
Meta's advertising systems continued delivering measurable performance gains. The company's expanded GEM ranking model increased offsite campaign conversions by 1.7% and app advertising conversions by 9.6%. Nearly 10 million advertisers used generative AI creative tools during the reported quarter.
