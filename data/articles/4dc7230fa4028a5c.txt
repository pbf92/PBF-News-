Liverpool vs Manchester United: Bruno Fernandes Backed Ahead of Anfield Clash
Manchester United have given Mauricio Pochettino one of the least forgiving opening fixtures possible, Liverpool away. There is no easing into the job, no soft landing, no time for sentiment. United are in the relegation zone, they have one league win, and they have already changed manager after parting company with Michael Carrick during the international break.
That makes Sunday at Anfield significant straight away. Liverpool, under Andoni Iraola, are fourth and coming off a win over Crystal Palace. They are stable, organised and heading into a week that could shape the early direction of their campaign, with Everton next on the schedule after this one.
There is, however, one small detail in United's favour. Their last three visits to Anfield have ended without defeat, and they also completed a league double over Liverpool last season. That does not fix the wider problems, but it does mean there is recent evidence they can handle this fixture better than many expect.

Bruno Fernandes Remains Central
The first clear call from Pochettino concerns Bruno Fernandes. Despite criticism over his form this season, the new Manchester United manager has kept faith with the Portugal international and confirmed he will stay as captain. Pochettino said: "All the players, we were chatting, it was amazing from day one, Bruno is our captain, an important player. But all the players need to think they are important, from the bottom to the top.
"I spent three hours, chatting in my office, with all the players, maybe I miss some injured players. In the next few days, we will talk in an individual way with all of them.
"It's important to listen to what they need to say, understand the place they are. Not only on the field, off the field, what's going on. Some conversations, some collective way, meetings, but focus is on Sunday."
That is standard new-manager messaging, but it matters because Fernandes remains the one United player most likely to set the rhythm if Pochettino gets his pressing game going. He was highly productive last season, he has fallen short this term, and United need him functioning immediately.
Contract Situation Adds Pressure
There is another layer to the Fernandes situation. He has reportedly rejected a second contract offer and is due to be out of contract at the end of the season, although United hold an option to extend by another 12 months until 2028. The club want a fresh agreement rather than relying on the clause. If that fails, they are expected to trigger the extension before January 1.
For now, that is background noise. The immediate issue is football. Pochettino needs a response, Fernandes needs a performance, and Liverpool will look at this as a chance to expose a fragile side before the pressure rises again.
