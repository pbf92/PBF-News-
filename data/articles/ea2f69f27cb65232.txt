TITLE: GTA 6 has a hidden warehouse packed with loot worth $14,000
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

When you begin your GTA 6 story, making money doesn't come easy. For the most part, you'll be earning relatively small amounts before working your way up the criminal underworld and making even Scrooge McDuck jealous. Thankfully, you can earn around $14,000 quite early in the game by visiting a hidden location in Leonida, without even completing a mission.
I'm the kind of gamer who loves exploring open worlds to see what's around the next corner or over the next hill. That's how I stumbled across a remote warehouse near Hamlet in GTA VI, packed with valuable loot, including stolen goods, drugs and cash. It's a fantastic way to earn some extra money for Jason, especially if you want to spoil Lucia on her first day out of prison.
So, if you fancy making some easy cash in Rockstar Games’ highly anticipated entry in its Grand Theft Auto series without getting your hands too dirty, here's where to find the warehouse and how to get your hands on its valuable loot.
Where to find the hidden warehouse in GTA 6
Advert
The warehouse in question is Apollo Aggregates, located near Hamlet in South Vice-Dale County. It's easy enough to miss if you're simply driving past, but if you're anything like me and enjoy exploring every little corner of the map, it's certainly worth checking out.
How to make over $14,000 from the hidden warehouse
Now, this is where things get interesting, because the warehouse is packed with goods that you can sell for a tidy profit. However, don't expect to simply walk inside and find $14,000 lying around. You'll need to collect everything and find someone willing to take it off your hands.
- Enter the warehouse and search for valuable loot, including marked bills and drugs, weapons and a collectible card (you can sell or keep it).
- Check the offices, storage areas and nearby vehicles for anything you might have missed.
- Use a vehicle with enough storage space to transport everything to a drop-off for Brian to launder, and/or a pawn shop dealer (for the card) . You may need to make more than one trip.
- Take the stolen goods to a buyer, such as Donnie or Brian, to turn them into cash.

What else can you find inside the warehouse?
The money isn't the only reason to visit, because there are a few other goodies worth keeping an eye out for. Before you make your escape, be sure to have a proper look around. You never know what else you might find.
There are plenty of ways to make money in GTA VI, but stumbling across a warehouse packed with valuable loot is certainly one of my favourite discoveries so far. After all, earning around $14,000 without completing a single mission isn't a bad way to start your criminal career.
Grand Theft Auto VI is out now on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
