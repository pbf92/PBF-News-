TITLE: GTA 6 reportedly steals the launch-week streaming crown with 420 million hours watched
OUTLET: Digg

Dexerto, citing Streams Charts, reports that GTA 6 generated 420 million hours watched in its first week, making it the biggest game launch in livestreaming history. Source
Dexerto’s November 26 report says that total nearly tripled the launch-week record set by Valorant’s closed beta in April 2020. It also exceeded the combined opening-week viewing of Valorant, Elden Ring, Diablo IV and Lost Ark. A photo finish this was not. Source
The report also puts GTA 6’s concurrent audience at a launch-record 7.91 million viewers. Twitch led by total hours watched, followed by YouTube and Kick, which reportedly finished close together. Source
The hours-watched metric adds up viewing time, while concurrent viewership measures the audience watching at once. Those 420 million hours aren’t 420 million individual viewers, players or copies sold.
The underlying Streams Charts analysis has not been independently verified for this article. Dexerto’s account does not supply the precise tracking window or methodology behind the comparisons. Source
Three streamers, three different crowns
Dexerto reports these GTA 6 launch-week leaders: Source
- TheBurntPeanut led GTA 6 streamers by launch-week viewing time, with 11.43 million hours watched. YouTube supplied the majority of that total.
- IShowSpeed drew the highest concurrent audience on a single channel.
- IlloJuan recorded the highest combined peak audience across multiple platforms. Source
The report supplies no numerical peak audience for either IShowSpeed or IlloJuan. Source
The reported records extended well beyond those three names: 1,056 channels broke their all-time peak-viewership records while playing GTA 6. Of those, 193 reached at least ten times their previous record audience. Source
