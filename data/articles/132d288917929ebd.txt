TITLE: The Normal Person’s Guide To Understanding Grand Theft Auto 6
OUTLET: Kotaku

Grand Theft Auto 6 is everywhere you look. Every news channel is talking about it. Celebrities and musicians are playing GTA 6 or posting about its release. It’s apparently the biggest game ever and has sold nearly 40 million copies already. But if you aren’t a big gamer or not a video game fan at all, you might have questions. I have answers.
Perhaps someone sent you this link because you kept asking them GTA 6 questions. Maybe you did a Google search, made it past the AI results and found this blog explaining what GTA 6 is to people who don’t buy or play games. Whatever the case, welcome. I’m going to walk you through what is almost certainly the biggest video game launch of the decade, why people are going so wild, and what the game actually is and isn’t.
So what kind of game is GTA 6?
It is an open-world game that mixes together a lot of video game genres, like driving, shooting, and exploration. As with most open-world games, you can run around freely or do story missions. It’s up to the player. You control two characters, Jason and Lucia, who are criminals on the run in fictional versions of Florida and Miami named Leonida and Vice City, respectively.
Did it really take a decade to make this game?
More or less, yeah. The developer, Rockstar Games, has been working on this game since at least 2015 or so. Turns out creating a photorealistic recreation of a chunk of Florida that players can explore and do whatever they want in takes time!
Does this game actually cost $100?
No. You can buy it on either PS5 or Xbox for $80. The $100 version comes with a bunch of extra outfits, hairstyles, and other items players can wear or buy in-game using cash they collect from crimes, but it’s definitely not required.
Is it really as big as I’ve heard?
It took me about 90 hours to reach the end of the main story. I’ve since played over 150 hours, and I’m still finding new stuff, hearing new songs, and seeing new random events. Yeah, it’s big.
So why is everyone around me playing GTA 6 and going wild over it?
Well, a lot of people have been waiting for 13 years to play GTA 6. The last new GTA game, GTA V, was released back in 2013. So anticipation for this game has been building for a long time. And, surprisingly, GTA 6 lived up to the hype.
What’s up with all the reviews being super positive? Is it that good?
There are a few reasons for the glowing critical reception. The game’s narrative is really good, featuring two extremely well-written characters that develop a meaningful relationship over the course of its five chapters. Another big reason for the high review scores is just how expansive and reactive the world is. Characters in the game will comment on your clothes, respond to your actions, and remember things you did hours and hours ago. It’s also a lot of fun to play, with challenging but rewarding driving, combat, and police encounters. And it looks incredible, with some of the best graphics ever seen in a console game. Some critics are calling it the best game of the year. And many players are calling it one of the best games ever.
Is this like Vice City on PS2?
GTA: Vice City on the PS2 was much, much smaller and less advanced (though in some ways it was pretty advanced for the time) and was set in the 1980s. While Vice City is a major location in GTA 6, this is an unconnected and different version of the city from that seen in the original 2002 PS2 game.
What do you actually do in GTA 6?
So many things! Players can steal cars, drive boats, get into fist fights, shoot at the cops, go fishing, play mini-golf, hit the gun range, pet dogs, scuba dive, fly planes, explore the swamps, hunt deer, join in street races, visit the beach, get drunk, buy clothes, play fake video games inside the game, take a shower, eat food, work out, blow up cars, or just chill in a bar. You can also just play through the game’s main story, which is all about Jason and Lucia trying to make some money as criminals to build a life for themselves and their son.
Wait, they have a kid?!
Yes, Jason and Lucia have a child, a fact which was omitted from any pre-release marketing but is revealed in the first 20 minutes of the game. During the events of the game, he is living with his aunt while Lucia and Jason do whatever they can to make a good life for him and get him back.
Who is Mandy and why do so many people like her?
She is an attractive but disturbing character players meet very early in the game who may or may not be a serial killer or creepy stalker. Despite this, many people online still want to have sex with her. Some players also think Jason should hook up with Mandy and ditch Lucia. These people are wrong.
Is it true you have to be a good boyfriend and do chores?
No. While you can do that stuff, GTA 6 doesn’t force you to be a good boyfriend. You can be a real jerk to Lucia, and she can be a real asshole to Jason. Also, the chores players are talking about only make up a few minutes of the opening hours of GTA 6. The rest of the game has none of that stuff in it.
I read that you have to work out and shower all the time. Sounds boring!
Like the chores mentioned already, these elements are optional. If you constantly skip working out, your characters will eventually become less ripped and might even develop small bellies and thicker thighs. But you can still play all the missions and shoot cops.
I saw a video about how you can’t even shoot NPCs anymore?
What? You can totally shoot random people, cops, and more.
I saw a different Instagram Reel about how you can’t shoot women.
That’s not accurate. You can shoot, punch, tackle, burn, run over, drown, choke, and slap women.
Well, there was another video claiming you can’t steal cars anymore.
Uhh, you can steal cars. Some cars, like very fancy cars, have special security measures, so you can’t steal them as easily, but you can walk up to any person driving a car at a stoplight, rip them out and take their ride.
But a different clip on TikTok I saw-
Okay, so, something you need to understand about GTA 6 is that it is at the center of a massive online culture war. Conservative players, grifters, chuds, and other weirdos are currently spreading false information about this game online. There are also plenty of people online posting inflammatory nonsense to get clicks and engagement on sites like Twitter that reward that shit. So if you read something kind of wild about this game, just know it might be false. Check places like Kotaku or other major gaming sites to verify.
Oh, so the devs didn’t remove prostitutes?
Well, actually, Rockstar did remove sex workers you can pay to have sex with from GTA 6. But there are still strippers in the game.
Does GTA 6 have multiplayer?
Nope!
Wait really? But didn’t the last one have multiplayer?
Yes, GTA V has GTA Online, a very popular, successful, and still-ongoing multiplayer game with millions of players. This time around, it seems Rockstar wanted to focus all its resources on getting GTA 6 out the door without also having to finish a big online mode. That said, I find it very hard to believe that GTA 6 won’t get some kind of multiplayer mode in the future.
When is this getting ported to Switch 2?
Never. I mean, officially, Rockstar hasn’t said that, but based on how the game looks and how it runs on the Series S, the weakest console it is currently available on, I’m very doubtful a Switch 2 port is coming anytime soon.
My 12-year-old son wants to play GTA 6? Is this an appropriate game for a kid?
There is a lot of nudity in this game. You can shoot people’s heads off. You can burn them alive. A big part of the game involves robbing stores and selling drugs. I say all that not to suggest your kid or teenager shouldn’t play the game, but just to let you know what’s waiting for them in Vice City. Ultimately, it’s up to you and what you think your kid can handle. Either way, just know they are already totally seeing clips of this game online on places like TikTok.
