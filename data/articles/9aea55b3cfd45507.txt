TITLE: Did Anthropic’s IPO burst the AI bubble?
OUTLET: Yahoo Finance UK

Anthropic raised $81 billion, becoming the largest IPO in history and dethroning SpaceX. Anthropic share price jumped 34% on debut. Yet, the Nasdaq fell 10% for the week, the S&P 500 dropped 8.6%, and the Dow Jones lost 8%.
Why?
For one, a correction had been brewing for some time, amid growing concerns about the return on investment in AI for big tech companies and stretched overvaluations. Anthropic's IPO may simply have been a classic case of "buy the rumor, sell the news."
Fed officials discussing when and by how much to raise rates may have also weighed on sentiment, given that keeping rates unchanged was still on the table until recently.
Iran reporting US strikes on Qeshm Island, claiming to have shot down an MQ-1 Predator drone and attacked a US radar system at Sheikh Isa Air Base in Bahrain, didn't help the case neither. If tensions flare up again and energy prices take off, inflation could easily get another boost.
As for the "historic meeting" between Putin, Trump and Xi Jinping, they basically agreed to keep talking about nuclear risks, peace, UN reform and energy stability — nothing exciting for markets.
Then there was Nvidia. On the one hand, the company delivered strong results, with revenue up 103% to $115.8 billion, including $107.6 billion from data centers, and next-quarter guidance of $130 billion, well above expectations. On the other hand, the SEC reportedly opened an investigation into Nvidia's accounting, thus adding another layer of uncertainty for the sector.
U.S. stocks are trying to bounce back on Monday, with Nvidia among the premarket gainers, climbing over 30% following Jensen Huang's reinstatement as the company's CEO. The question is: without any improvement in fundamentals, particularly ROI, how long can this optimism last?
