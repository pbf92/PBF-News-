TITLE: Malick Thiaw Joins Lewis Hall as Major Manchester United Transfer Target
OUTLET: SB Nation

Manchester United has reportedly added Malick Thiaw to its list of defensive targets, with Newcastle United’s center-back viewed as an ideal fit for new head coach Mauricio Pochettino.
Thiaw joins fellow Magpie defender Lewis Hall in the list of Man Utd targets after the Red Devils were a bit late in their approach to Hall last summer, in a saga that ended with the England international signing a new contract with Newcastle United.
According to TEAMtalk’s Fraser Fletcher, Manchester United believes the Germany international is a player the club could realistically sign, although Newcastle’s position makes a January move extremely difficult.
“Man Utd sources believe that the Germany international defender is a player they could realistically land, although any January move faces significant obstacles,” Fletcher reported.
Thiaw has become a key figure at St James’ Park since arriving from AC Milan in 2025, with his physicality, composure and recovery pace attracting interest from several leading European clubs.
Newcastle, however, remains in a strong negotiating position. The 25-year-old is under contract until 2029, and TEAMtalk reports that the Magpies have previously placed an £80 million valuation on him.
“Many of the big boys have him on their radar,” sources told TEAMtalk, with Manchester United now firmly among those monitoring his situation.
That said, a departure is not expected to happen in January, with Fletcher reporting that the cost of completing a midseason deal would likely be beyond what Manchester United can manage at this stage. A move in summer 2027 is considered significantly more realistic, as was the one for Hall last summer.
TEAMtalk added that Thiaw’s position could become more interesting if multiple elite clubs make serious approaches after the season, with the defender potentially preferring to move on rather than remaining in Tyneside.
