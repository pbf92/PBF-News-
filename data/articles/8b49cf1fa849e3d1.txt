TITLE: GTA 6 Sets New Sales Record for Rockstar Just One Day In
OUTLET: Polygon.com

Grand Theft Auto 6 was setting records before it was even released. Now that it's out, don't expect that pace to slow down anytime soon. New estimates suggest GTA 6 is not just the fastest-selling video game ever, but the fastest-selling piece of entertainment ever.
How much money has GTA 6 made?
According to the latest estimates from market analysis firm Sensor Tower, which has been tracking GTA 6 preorders for some time, Rockstar's latest blockbuster game has sold 24.58 million copies. It has generated $2.3 billion, bringing the average GTA 6 sale price to $93.57. That means most players have purchased the more expensive Ultimate Edition ($99.99), although recent purchasers have gravitated toward the standard edition ($79.99). When GTA 6 crossed $1 billion in estimated revenue, the average sale was about $97.
Rockstar has not officially announced sales figures for GTA 6 yet. Grand Theft Auto 5 hit $1 billion in revenue in three days, and Rockstar parent company Take-Two said at the time it believed "this marks the fastest that any entertainment property, including video games and feature films, has reached this significant milestone." GTA 6 appears to have surpassed that success.
How much did GTA 6 cost to make?
Likewise, Rockstar has not confirmed how big GTA 6's development budget was. The studio has been focused on making the game since releasing Red Dead Redemption 2 in 2018. Rockstar previously shot down the idea that GTA 6 cost $1.5 billion to make, but the game likely cost over $1 billion to make, meaning it's the most expensive video game in history.
Players have been enjoying discovering all that GTA 6 has to offer, like Easter eggs, bodega cats, and the game's best-kept secret. GTA 6 is out now on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.
