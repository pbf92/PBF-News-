TITLE: Rockstar is handing out 100 of these gorgeous limited-edition GTA 6 PS5 consoles, and you can't buy one
OUTLET: TweakTown

Rockstar Games is not selling the custom GTA 6 PS5 Pro units. Instead, it is giving them away carefully and in very limited numbers. Reports confirm that at least 100 of these special consoles exist.

The machines feature a dark gray-and-black finish with etched palm-tree patterns across the side plates, the GTA 6 logo, and neon teal-to-magenta accents that reference Vice City's nightlife. Each unit carries a unique serial number under the Rockstar symbol.
The first wave went to Miami Heat players just before the November 19 release as part of the team's Vice City-themed collaboration. Those packages arrived in fitted hard cases that also included matching DualSense controllers and a physical copy of Grand Theft Auto 6.
A second batch is now being delivered to streamers and influencers. The streamer Davy Jones showed one of those consoles, with images shared by the videotech account on X. The styling matches the previous promotional units, confirming that Rockstar produced a deliberate, small run rather than one-off customs.
The limited-edition DualSense controllers are a different story. Sony and Rockstar released both black and white versions for retail sale at $84.99, and those have been available at major stores and PlayStation Direct.
Unfortunately, the console remains strictly promotional. Despite years of fan requests for a proper GTA PS5 Pro bundle or even just official faceplates, neither Sony nor Rockstar has provided anything for the public.
