Blink Charging Plans 1-for-30 Reverse Stock Split to Address Nasdaq Bid Price Requirement
Less than 1 min read
Blink Charging plans a 1-for-30 reverse stock split intended to help meet Nasdaq’s minimum bid price requirement.
Key Highlights:
- The split is expected to take effect at 12:01 a.m. ET on December 11, 2026, with split-adjusted trading expected at market open under BLNK.
- Every 30 outstanding common shares will combine into one; fractional shares will be rounded up to the next whole share, with no cash paid.
- Outstanding common shares are expected to fall from approximately 145.2 million to 4.84 million; authorized common shares will decrease to 16,666,667.
- The board approved the split, which does not require stockholder approval; preferred stock authorization will remain 40 million shares.
