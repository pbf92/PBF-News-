TITLE: New man admits sexual assaulting woman drugged and raped by her husband for more than 20 years
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

New man admits sexual assaulting woman drugged and raped by her husband for more than 20 years
Tommy Golden will next appear at Minshull Street Crown Court on December 17
Another man has admitted sexually assaulting a woman who was drugged and raped by her husband for over 20 years.
Tommy Golden, 59, of HMP Risley, appeared via videolink at Manchester Magistrates’ Court on Monday, November 23, wearing a navy tracksuit.
He pleaded guilty to sexual assault of the woman, namely sexual touching, on June 29, 2025. Golden, the former landlord of The Blarney Stone on Bradshawgate, in Bolton, was remanded into custody and was told he will be sentenced on December 17 this year.

The woman's husband - a man in his 60s from Stockport - previously pleaded guilty to 60 charges relating to the single complainant. He admitted counts of rape; assault by penetration; administering a substance with intent; sexual assault; attempted rape; and sharing intimate photographs or film.
The Manchester Evening News and other media organisations are not naming the husband in order to protect the anonymity of his wife. The law entitles her to anonymity for life as the victim of sexual offences.
Four other men previously pleaded guilty to offences in the case. Keith Fotheringham, 59, of Cleghorn Street, Dundee, admitted rape; assault by penetration; administering a substance with intent; and assault by penetration.
Graham Brougham, 74, of Birtwistle Road, Northwich, Cheshire, admitted rape earlier this month. Sean Peers, 38, formerly of Sandwich Road in Eccles, now of Stockport, has admitted assault by penetration.
Karl Lindsay, 56, of Wordsworth Drive in Taunton, pleaded guilty to conspiring to rape the woman between January 2015 and November 2025, and to conspiring to administer a substance to the woman in order to stupefy her, between the same dates. He also pleaded guilty to three counts of making indecent images of children, and two counts of possessing an extreme pornographic image, in separate proceedings in Taunton.

On Monday morning, District Judge Simon Blakeborough told Golden: “This case is too serious to be dealt with at the Magistrates’ Court. The next hearing will be on December 17 at Minshull Street Crown Court.
“You will be produced for that either over the videolink or in person."
