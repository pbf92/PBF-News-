TITLE: 10 of the Best 'GTA 6' Cheat Codes, and How to Use Them
OUTLET: Lifehacker

Cheat codes have long been a part of the Grand Theft Auto video game franchise, and the tradition continues with GTA 6. Not only does GTA 6 support cheats, but it also has a menu that lists all of them as well as instructions for unlocking them. However, you cannot fire up the game and start using cheats from the beginning. In fact, it requires quite a bit of work on your part. Here's how to unlock cheat codes in GTA 6, how to see the list of cheats, as well as the 10 best cheat codes in GTA 6.
How to unlock cheats in GTA 6
Gone are the good old days when you could fire up GTA, enable god mode, unlimited ammo, no reload, spawn a jetpack, and run around the entire city destroying things at will. In GTA 6, you'll need to complete the main story to unlock the ability to use cheat codes. On top of that, you'll have to complete challenges of various difficulty levels to access specific cheat codes. Some of these cheats unlock the moment you finish the main story. Others, however, are quite challenging or time-consuming. For instance, unlimited reload requires you to win 25 gold medals at shooting ranges.
Where to see a list of all cheats in GTA 6
Once you complete the main story in GTA 6, just go to in-game settings, and you'll see a new Cheats tab. This lists all 61 cheats and how to unlock them. You'll see the actual button combo for each cheat right after you complete the unlock requirement.
The consequences of using cheats in GTA 6
Unfortunately, there are some serious gameplay ramifications to using cheat codes in GTA 6. For starters, you can't save the game once you input even one cheat code. That's why it's best to save before you start using cheats, to avoid losing progress. Using cheat codes will also disable trophies and achievements in GTA 6.
How to use cheats in GTA 6
To start using cheat codes in GTA 6, you'll have to get your character to pull up the smartphone by pressing the up arrow on the D-Pad. Then, hold L1+R1, and press the cheat's key combination on the D-Pad.
10 of the best GTA 6 cheat codes
While there are a lot of cheat codes in GTA 6, these are 10 of the best, listed with the right key combination, and the condition to unlock them:
Unlimited money, in batches of $250,000
This cheat unlocks the moment you complete the main story in GTA 6. To use it, press up, right, down, left, up, left, down, right.
Raise heat level
You unlock this cheat code after losing a three-star wanted level 25 times. The key combination is left, down, right, down, up, down, up.
Raise wanted level
Lose a three-star wanted level 50 times to unlock this cheat. The key combination is left, down, right, down, up, right, up.
Unlimited focus
This cheat unlocks the moment you wrap up the main story. Press this to unlock: down, up, up, down, up, down, up, down, up, right.
Use super-powered punches
This is the third cheat that unlocks when you finish the main story. When you shoot or punch/kick characters, this cheat will launch them in the air. The key combination is: down, up, left, right, down, down, up, down
Unlimited ammo
If you win 25 silver medals at shooting ranges, you can unlock infinite ammo in GTA 6. The key combination is: down, up, right, down, left, left, up, down, down, down.
No reload needed
To stop needing reloads, you need to win 25 gold medals at shooting ranges in GTA 6. The key combination is: down, up, right, down, left, left, up, down, down, up.
Obtain body armor
If you complete 100 workouts during your GTA 6 playthrough, you'll unlock the cheat that lets you instantly obtain body armor. Press this key combination to make it work: down, up, left, up, up, up, up, up, down, down.
Change the weather
This GTA 6 cheat allows you to change the weather at any time, and it unlocks when you finish the main story. Press this key combination to toggle it: down, right, up, down, right, up, right.
Spawn an MTL Pumper (fire truck)
To spawn this cool emergency vehicle, you just need to discover the MTL Pumper in the game's progress menu. Use this key combination to spawn one: left, up, right, down, left, left, up, left.
