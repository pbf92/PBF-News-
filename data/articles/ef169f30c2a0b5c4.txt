Joshua Zirkzee could leave Manchester United as three Serie A clubs show interest
Joshua Zirkzee’s time at Manchetser United may be drawing to a close, with a move back to Italy on the cards, with three Serie A sides interested in the Dutch forward.
Since joining Manchester United in the summer of 2024, Zirkzee has not hit the heights that were expected of him after INEOS evaluated his 2023/24 season at Bologna.
During that season, Zirkzee scored 12 goals in 37 games, earning himself a place in the team of the season and the best U23 player.
The past 28 months have not been kind to Zirkzee. After joining from Bologna for £36.5m, the young Dutchman looked to have refound his footing at the highest level since leaving Bayern Munich in 2022.
Just a month earlier, he had earned his first call-up to the Netherlands squad, making his debut in a 2-1 European Championship quarter-final win over Turkey.
The signs were positive when he struck an 87th-minute winner against Fulham on his debut after coming off the bench. A euphoric introduction, yet over the following months, his form began to unravel.
Erik ten Hag would lose his job, and Zirkzee would drop to the bench as his minutes faltered.
A particularly grim moment came on 30 December 2024, when United fell 2-0 to Newcastle at Old Trafford, and the Dutchman faced boos as he left the pitch in the 33rd minute after a dismal display.
Now, the Dutchman looks set to leave, with Zirkzee wanting to forge his own path in the modern football landscape.
Three Italian sides interested
Speaking on his Italian YouTube channel, Fabrizio Romano claims Zirkzee is open to leaving United, and has three Italian clubs who would be interested in his services.
“A lot of you are asking us this evening about Joshua Zirkzee.
“The first point is that a new coach has arrived in Manchester. Mauricio Pochettino is there, and Zirkzee will now have to work out with Pochettino what Manchester United want to do, whether to let him go or not.
“So Pochettino will make a decision.”
The Italian journalist first mentioned Napoli, a club that United have a storied history with, especially in the last decade.
Numerous players have worn the red jersey of United and the blue of Napoli, with the likes of Scott McTominay, Romelu Lukaku, Rasmus Hojlund, and Edinson Cavani all doing so across the past 15 years.
“Zirkzee is certainly open to leaving. As we’ve told you, he would gladly return to Italy. He wants to feel important; he wants to feel like a protagonist. This is a guarantee
“We must also add that the clubs we’ve mentioned are Napoli, because they’re looking for a striker, and Zirkzee. His agents have an excellent relationship with Napoli.”
Romano went on to add another side, one that United have already played this season, in which Zirkzee scored his first Champions League goal for United.
“Como are also looking for a striker, as we’ve told you, and if Zirkzee were to leave on advantageous terms, rather than in a permanent deal, Como could consider him a possibility.
“And then we’re always keeping an eye on Juventus, because they want to sign a striker.”
Masterful at his feet, Zirkzee moves with rare grace for a 6ft 4in striker, gliding past defenders with subtle shifts and soft touches.
But aesthetic quality alone does not secure a starting berth at United.
A return to Italy may be the best deal on the cards for the Dutchman, unless new head coach Mauricio Pochettino can find a role for him at the club.
