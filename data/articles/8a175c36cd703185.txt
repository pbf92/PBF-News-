TITLE: ValueAct Capital Management Discloses Investment in Wix.com with 7% Stake
OUTLET: TradingView

ValueAct Capital Management Discloses Investment in Wix.com with 7% Stake
ValueAct Capital Management, L.P., which manages investment funds that hold Wix.com Ltd. shares, disclosed a 7% beneficial ownership stake in the company. The group reported owning 2,997,316 shares in a Schedule 13D filed on November 23, 2026. ValueAct says it sees the shares as undervalued and plans further discussions with Wix.com leaders about the company’s direction.
Investor Intent
ValueAct says it bought Wix.com shares because it considers them undervalued and an attractive investment. The firm expects further discussions with Wix.com officers and directors about management, operations, capital allocation, dividends, acquisitions, business strategy, executive pay and corporate governance. It may also speak with other investors and interested parties.
ValueAct plans to keep reviewing its investment, but it does not announce a specific board-seat request or proposed change at Wix.com.
Investor's Background
ValueAct Capital Management, L.P. is a Delaware limited partnership that provides management services to ValueAct Capital Master Fund and ValueAct Strategic Global Partners IV. Its general partner, ValueAct Capital Management, LLC, oversees the partnership. Other affiliated ValueAct entities also report beneficial ownership of these shares because they manage the funds or serve as their general partners.
Original SEC Filing: Wix.com Ltd. [ WIX ] - SCHEDULE 13D - Nov. 23, 2026
