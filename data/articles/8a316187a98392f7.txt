TITLE: Fermi Chief Site Development Officer Sold Shares Worth Over $1.7M
OUTLET: TradingView

Fermi Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $3.7M
Charles Lynn Hamilton, Fermi’s Chief Site Development Officer and a direct shareholder, sold 761,629 shares in two transactions on November 19, 2026, for a total of $1,791,155: 736,471 shares at a weighted average price of $2.36 per share and 25,158 shares at $2.11 per share. After the sales, Hamilton directly owned 4,764,281 shares and indirectly owned 3,825,000 shares through Gracious Endurance Trust and 3,825,000 shares through Steadfast Endurance Trust. He disclaimed beneficial ownership of the shares held by each trust except to the extent of his pecuniary interest.
Blanes Jacobo Ortiz, Fermi’s Chief Operating Officer and a direct shareholder, sold 847,810 shares on November 19, 2026, at a weighted average price of $2.35 per share, for a total of $1,993,830. After the sale, Ortiz directly owned 4,768,341 shares and may be deemed to beneficially own 7,875,000 shares held by Las Brisas Financial Services LLC; he disclaimed beneficial ownership of those shares except to the extent of his pecuniary interest.
