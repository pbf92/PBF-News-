ChainOpera AI: Unlocks at Least 11.25 Million COAI - Nov 2027
ChainOpera AI has scheduled a COAI supply unlock for November 2027. The release covers at least 11.25 million COAI, with tokens allocated to advisors, investors and the team.
The minimum release equals 5.98% of the circulating supply. The unlock removes the restriction that prevents recipients from selling those tokens. Once the tokens are released, each recipient can decide whether to sell their share.
