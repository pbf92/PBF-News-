TITLE: Pochettino confident Man United will turn things round
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Mauricio Pochettino is confident that Manchester United will turn things around and says he sees a united dressing room.
Pochettino's side drew 2-2 at Sporting CP in the Champions League on Wednesday night, as the Argentine earned the first positive result of his tenure.
Braces from Luis Suarez and Bruno Fernandes saw the points shared in Lisbon, a result that leaves United in the qualification places for the play-off round.
Pochettino's first fixture ended in a 4-0 defeat to Liverpool, but he saw improvement from his side at Sporting. United dealt with some questionable referring decisions in Portugal, which included conceding two penalties, a disallowed goal, and Youri Tielemans' red card.
Despite a tough start to his reign, Pochettino said he is full of motivation as he looks to get United back to the top of English and European football.
"I'm happy to be here. I am so excited to be in the front and be the face of representing the club," he said.
"I think we are here because the situation wasn't the situation that all the fans and the people expect, that is why we are here. We are here to take the responsibility from week one, and we are going to for sure improve and put again this team in the place [we belong], because of the history and everything we deserve to be.
"It's going to be tough, of course. Nothing is easy, but we have the energy and I think we have the capacity and, most importantly, we have the players and the power and the energy from our fans that is massive. And I think united all together, I think we will find a way to perform and win games."
