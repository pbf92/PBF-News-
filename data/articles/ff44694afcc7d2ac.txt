Olivier Goudet Discloses 22.3% Stake in Oatly Group AB
Olivier Goudet, through his Luxembourg investment company Platin S.à r.l., reported beneficial ownership of a 22.3% stake in Oatly Group AB. Platin bought additional ADSs in open-market trades on November 23 and 24, 2026, and filed the Schedule 13D on November 25. The reported position combines ADSs, convertible notes and a share-linked swap due to settle on January 27, 2027.
Investor Intent
Platin says it made the purchases and entered the swap because it views Oatly shares as an “attractive investment opportunity.” Goudet and Platin do not identify a specific plan to change Oatly’s business or management, though they say they may discuss proposals and reassess their investment.
Investor's Background
Platin S.à r.l. is a Luxembourg private limited liability company whose principal business is holding investments. Olivier Goudet majority-owns and controls Platin, which holds the Oatly securities reported in the filing. Goudet is a French citizen.
Original SEC Filing: Oatly Group AB [ OTLY ] - SCHEDULE 13D - Nov. 25, 2026
