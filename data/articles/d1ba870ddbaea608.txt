TITLE: Man Utd blow as Pochettino drops hint in Champions League squad
OUTLET: London Evening Standard

Manchester United are set to welcome Luke Shaw and Karl Darlow back into the fold for tomorrow night’s Champions League trip to Sporting, but they will be without Matheus Cunha, who was left out of the travelling cohort.
The Brazilian was withdrawn at half time in the 4-0 drubbing from Liverpool over the weekend, replaced by Joshua Zirkzee as the Red Devils trailed 2-0 and were down to ten men after Leny Yoro’s now-overturned red card.
The former Wolves forward is sidelined due to a suspension he picked up from his red card against Roma last time out in Europe.
The 31-year-old is yet to feature in the Champions League this season, rested for the 4-0 win in the opener over Sabah, before injury ruled him out of the next three fixtures.
The Welshman was called up for international duty a fortnight ago, playing the first game of the break against Norway before being rested for the second due to a knock.
