TITLE: Stock Market Today, November 17, 2026 : Nvidia Earnings Loom as Wall Street Awaits First Report Without Jensen Huan, Dow and Nasdaq Slide
OUTLET: Eurasia Business News

By William Collins, consultant in stock markets – Eurasia Business News, November 17, 2026. Article no 3272
U.S. stocks declined Tuesday, November 17, 2026, as investors awaited Nvidia’s first earnings report since Jensen Huang resigned. Selling extended across technology and other sectors, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average under particular pressure. Interim CEO Colette Kress faces questions extending beyond quarterly growth to succession, financing commitments and export restrictions.
At 9:33 p.m. Central European Time, or 3:33 p.m. Eastern, regular trading was still underway. Nvidia had not released its results. The figures below are intraday snapshots, not closing prices, and quotations from different times should not be treated as synchronized.
Stock Market Today: Dow, S&P 500 and Nasdaq Retreat
The supplied afternoon update placed the Dow approximately 1.5% lower, the S&P 500 down 1.3%, and the Nasdaq Composite off 1.4%. All 11 sectors were negative, with information technology leading the declines.
Published reporting confirmed that selling intensified after a mixed opening. By early afternoon, major indexes were down more than 1%, while Nvidia, Salesforce and Home Depot remained under pressure. Disney retained a modest gain in one afternoon snapshot.
The progression matters: early strength in selected stocks did not establish a sustained recovery. Investors remained reluctant to increase exposure before Nvidia’s report, which could influence sentiment across semiconductor manufacturers, cloud providers and other AI-linked businesses.
Nvidia Earnings: Strong Growth Meets Leadership Uncertainty
Nvidia’s official calendar schedules its fiscal third-quarter 2027 earnings call for Tuesday at 5 p.m. Eastern. Published estimates point to approximately $109 billion in revenue and adjusted earnings of $2.47 per share. These are expectations, not reported results.
The chipmaker previously guided to $108 billion in revenue, plus or minus 2%. Investors will compare the actual outcome and subsequent guidance with those expectations while examining profitability and customer demand.
Yet the leadership transition changes the discussion. Kress must explain how Nvidia will maintain decision-making and customer confidence while directors seek Huang’s permanent replacement. A revenue beat could support the commercial outlook without resolving those governance questions.
Nvidia Stock Remains Under Pressure
Nvidia fell 4.12% Monday, closing at $156.14. Tuesday’s early advance subsequently reversed; an afternoon report placed the shares 1.42% lower at 1:02 p.m. Eastern. The supplied later update described a decline exceeding 2%.
The stock’s recent losses reflect more than immediate earnings expectations. Investors are reassessing leadership continuity, regulatory exposure and the sustainability of spending throughout Nvidia’s ecosystem.
The company’s growth forecasts and share price can therefore move differently. Strong anticipated sales do not prevent investors from assigning a lower valuation when uncertainty increases. Tuesday’s results will test operating momentum, but management’s explanations may be equally consequential.
AI Dealmaking and Chip Restrictions Draw Scrutiny
The supplied Journal account says Kress is expected to restrain Nvidia’s aggressive dealmaking. That remains an expectation rather than a confirmed change in investment policy. Investors will seek clarity about existing commitments and future financing discipline.
Separately, the Commerce Department announced restrictions targeting overseas access to advanced American chips. Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Singapore and Brazil face additional shipment-licensing requirements intended to limit Chinese customers’ remote use of data centers there. Exceptions could apply to U.S. technology companies.
This is not simply a blanket ban on every overseas computing service. Licensing scope, implementation and exemptions will determine the commercial effect. Those details matter when assessing potential pressure on Nvidia and other chip suppliers.
Disney, Home Depot and Salesforce Move
Disney reported quarterly results above expectations, supported by improving streaming profitability and growth across other businesses. Its shares fluctuated before holding a 0.58% gain at 1:02 p.m. Eastern, illustrating the difference between the earnings reaction and broader market pressure.
Home Depot declined approximately 2% despite beating quarterly estimates. The retailer announced that finance chief Richard McPhail would replace Ted Decker as CEO on November 23. Investors also evaluated its updated outlook.
Salesforce suffered a steeper decline, falling 9.44% in the same afternoon snapshot. The supplied update also identified Reddit as a gainer, but a matching independently verified quotation was unavailable.
Stocks to Watch Wednesday, November 18
Wednesday’s watchlist combines Nvidia’s post-earnings reaction with consumer and enterprise-technology reports:
- Nvidia: assess whether results and Kress’s commentary change expectations across AI-linked stocks.
- Target: its quarterly conference call is scheduled for 7 a.m. Central, following its earnings release.
- TJX: the company confirms November 18 for its fiscal third-quarter 2027 release.
- Lowe’s: scheduled results provide another perspective on home-improvement demand after Home Depot.
- Palo Alto Networks: expected after the close, offering insight into enterprise cybersecurity spending.
The Fed’s meeting minutes, due Wednesday afternoon, add another potential market catalyst.
Oil Prices Today: Brent and WTI Recover
Crude strengthened Tuesday as tanker attacks and expectations of declining U.S. inventories supported prices.
At 12:07 p.m. Eastern, Brent futures traded at $93.33 per barrel, up $0.89, or 0.96%. WTI futures stood at $86.53, gaining $0.81, or 0.94%. These were midday quotations, not settlements available at the requested timestamp.
Projected excess supply limited the advance. Recovering Middle Eastern exports continued to compete with shipping-security concerns, leaving traders balancing immediate disruption risks against a potentially better-supplied market.
Gold Price According to Kitco
A Reuters report carried by Kitco quoted spot gold at $3,858.19 per ounce at 10:08 a.m. Eastern. December gold futures traded at $3,860.50, up 0.2%. Spot and futures prices represent different instruments.
Dollar strength, elevated yields and expectations for further monetary tightening constrained the recovery. Kitco’s report also described cash-raising pressure associated with the AI-equity selloff, showing why weaker stocks did not automatically produce stronger bullion.
Euro Against the Dollar
Together, currency strength, regulatory uncertainty and the approaching earnings announcement left Nvidia’s report positioned as a major test for an already fragile market.
The euro touched $1.0983 Tuesday before recovering to approximately $1.0992, Reuters reported. It remained near its weakest level since April 2025 as investors assessed relative interest-rate expectations.
