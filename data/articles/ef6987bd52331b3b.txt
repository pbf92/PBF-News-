TITLE: Meta Expands Family Of Apps With Localized Shopping Platform 11/23/2026
OUTLET: MediaPost

Meta is continuing to expand its consumer-facing app
ecosystem, releasing its eleventh app of the year -- a shopping platform called “Achados” that is live for Android users in Brazil.
The regional app combines local product listings
from other Meta-run platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, and Marketplace.
“One feed for what people near you are selling,” the app’s Google Play store description reads.
This includes “listings from Marketplace, posts from Facebook Groups, and items sellers have shared from Instagram, including listings you won’t find in Marketplace
search.” 
Similar to Pinterest’s recent chatbot
update, Achados offers users a price comparison feature, highlighting the various prices of the same products across Meta’s family of apps.

By creating an Achados profile,
users are also able to view seller profiles on Facebook and Instagram, as well as customer reviews and previous sales history. 
Over the past year, the tech giant has focused more effort
on developing and releasing new apps that utilize its evolving AI models, both to collect data from users for training purposes, to introduce generative-AI technology to more consumers, and establish
new revenue streams.
As the eleventh standalone app Meta has launched since the start of 2026, Achados follows mini-games app Pockets, video-generation app Meta Vibes, bedtime-story generator Storykit, a
Facebook creative companion app, the Meta AI and Muse chatbot apps,
Instagram Instants, kids app Waddle World, outfit planning app Style Sketch, and standalone apps connected to Groups and Marketplace.
  Meta is also working on a prediction-market app called Takes,
similar to Kalshi or Polymarket.
