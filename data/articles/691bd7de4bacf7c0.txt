TITLE: Just a day after its IPO, Anthropic's mathematician tells mathematicians around the world: Claude has 'so
OUTLET: The Times of India

Just days after AI giant Anthropic made its highly anticipated stock market debut, one of its mathematicians made an announcement that could have far-reaching implications for both artificial intelligence and mathematics: he believes that he has solved the 167-year-old Riemann Hypothesis with the help of Anthropic's AI model, Claude. As reported by Wall Street Journal, Levent Alpöge, a mathematician at Anthropic, recently revealed that he has created a proof for the Riemann Hypothesis, one of the most famous and elusive unsolved problems in mathematics, using assistance from Claude. If the proof withstands scrutiny from the mathematical community, it would mark one of the most significant achievements in the history of the field and a major milestone for artificial intelligence.
Why it matters, if it holds
Proposed in 1859, the hypothesis has defeated mathematicians for 167 years and is one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems. If the proof checks out, it would be the second of those problems solved with AI, after an OpenAI model, supported by millions of dollars in computing power, solved one in September. Humans alone have solved only one. For decades, mathematicians have viewed the problem as the discipline's "holy grail.
" Even advanced AI systems had previously failed to produce a complete proof. According to the report, Alpöge now believes that barrier has been broken.
Claude's role in the proof
Alpöge disclosed the findings saying the solution was reached with assistance from Claude. If verified, the breakthrough would become the second Millennium Prize Problem solved with the help of artificial intelligence, following an earlier AI-assisted breakthrough involving an OpenAI model.
The mathematician published the proof using Lean, a formal programming language designed to enable computers to verify mathematical arguments line by line. By releasing the proof publicly, he invited mathematicians worldwide to examine and validate the work.
A 250,000-page AI-generated argument
One of the most interesting aspects of the claim is the size of the proof produced by Alpöge. He stressed that the complete argument is "incredibly long," and described the AI-generated version as a "massive unreadable travesty of slop." As reported by WSJ, the AI-generated proof has more than 250,000 pages. The length of the proof also underlines both the power and limitations of modern AI-assisted mathematics. Where a machine can easily navigate through vast chains of reasoning, understanding and cross checking the arguments still remain a significant challenge for human researchers.
The record breaking IPO of Anthropic
Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share, above its marketed range of $70 to $78. The offering raised $81.3 billion, the largest on record, and values the company at about $1.5 trillion. Shares opened at $111.50 and rose to nearly $115. They closed 17.07% above the IPO price, then slipped 4.70% in after-hours trading. About 832 million shares changed hands, more than SpaceX’s first-day volume, making Anthropic the most actively traded stock on the Nasdaq.
Amazon and Google parent Alphabet are major investors. After the IPO, Amazon will reportedly hold 14.7% and Alphabet 13.7%, with Alphabet’s stake including $10 billion of shares bought in a private placement alongside the offering. Both companies also supply computing and other infrastructure to Anthropic.
