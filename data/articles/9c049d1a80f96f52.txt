TITLE: Bitcoin drops to lowest level in more than two years
OUTLET: TradingView

Bitcoin drops to lowest level in more than two years
Bitcoin BTCUSD fell to its lowest level in more than two years late Friday as a widespread selloff in artificial intelligence stocks amid concerns over another Fed rate hike weighed on the world's largest cryptocurrency by market value.
Bitcoin’s BTCUSD selloff has coincided with a broader decline in the tech sector as investors review their investments in riskier assets amid fears over another Federal Reserve rate hike next month.
The abrupt resignation of Nvidia NVDA CEO Jensen Huang, Anthropic’s ANT record-breaking IPO, and increasing regulatory risks for the cryptocurrency industry following the Democrats’ victory in the midterm elections have added another dimension to the bitcoin’s valuation debate.
