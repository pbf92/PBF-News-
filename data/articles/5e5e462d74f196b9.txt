BullFrog AI Announces 1-for-10 Reverse Stock Split to Address Nasdaq Compliance
Less than 1 min read
BullFrog AI announced a 1-for-10 reverse stock split intended to help regain compliance with Nasdaq’s minimum bid-price requirement.
Key Highlights:
- Split-adjusted trading is expected to begin November 30, 2026, under ticker BFRG and new CUSIP 12021E307.
- Every 10 outstanding shares will combine into one; shares are expected to fall from 18,693,280 to approximately 1.88 million.
- Fractional shares will be rounded up to one whole share, while authorized shares, par value and proportional ownership remain unchanged except for this treatment.
- Options, warrants and other convertible securities, along with equity-plan share reserves, will be adjusted proportionately under their terms.
- Nasdaq’s compliance deadline is February 8, 2027; the stock must close at or above $1.00 for at least 10 consecutive business days.
Original SEC Filing: BullFrog AI Holdings, Inc. [ BFRG ] - 8-K - Nov. 25, 2026
