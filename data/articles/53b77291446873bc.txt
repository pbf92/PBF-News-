Texas Capital calls Las Vegas Sands a casino stock sleeper
Las Vegas Sands LVS is on watch after Texas Capital started off coverage with a Buy rating.
Analyst David Bain and his team believe the market “overemphasizes” near-term, cyclical headwinds and “undervalues” the structural resilience and long-term earnings power of the casino giant's asset base in Macau and Singapore. Bain thinks Las Vegas Sands LVS has been strategic by acquiring its shares at single-digit EV/EBITDA multiple in a move that is seen as highly accretive to both long-term free cash flow per share and overall equity value.
Bain noted that Macau gross gaming revenue trends are cyclical, not secular. He noted Las Vegas Sands' LVS Macau positioning is special since competitors cannot replicate its scale, including 11K hotel rooms, as well as more than 2M square feet of retail and MICE on the Cotai Strip.
The bullish view on LVS also factors in that the market also “lazily” fails to unbundle Marina Bay Sand from Macau and the company's pursuit in Texas to open a destination resort with the potential for EBITDA in the billions.
Texas Capital assigned a price target of $59 to Las Vegas Sands LVS. The 52-week high for the casino stock is $70.45. Shares of LVS dipped 0.3% in premarket trading to $39.16.
