TITLE: Infinity Nikki Version 3.0 “Of Yesterday and Tomorrow” update live today on PS5
OUTLET: PlayStation Blog

Explore two new cities, discover new abilities and combat systems, and more.
Explore two new cities, experience nearly 10 hours of cinematic storytelling, discover new abilities and combat systems, and celebrate Nikki’s Birthday to enjoy 160 draws in total, plus 19 sets of clothes—all free.
Today we announced Infinity Nikki Version 3.0, a major update that takes Nikki and Momo’s adventure to the Twin Cities of Art: Cicia and Ceremius, which releases on PS5 later today. Featuring nearly 10 hours of immersive story with cinematic presentation, the update introduces new exploration abilities, expanded combat systems, diverse open-world activities, and a more lifelike city experience. Alongside the new content, Version 3.0 and Nikki’s Birthday bring 160 draws and 19 sets of clothes, as well as improvements that make the adventure more accessible to new and returning stylists.
Twin cities of art, a new chapter: Of yesterday and tomorrow
In Version 3.0, Nikki and Momo’s adventure unfolds across Cicia and Ceremius, two cities that share a common origin but could hardly be more different.
Beyond everyday academy life in Cicia, the capital of art, Nikki gets caught up in strange anomalies on the streets and an investigation into the Rogues. Crossing the mist into Ceremius, she explores where the city’s past meets its present crisis, drawing ever closer to the truth about the Goddess des Arts.
New abilities, combat systems, and ways to explore
Across the two cities, stylists will have access to four key abilities: Veiling, Alchemy, Enhanced Floating, and Gemwork, adding more possibilities for exploration and puzzle-solving.
With the Veiling Ability, stylists can throw the Shadow Beetle and choose when to teleport. When it hits certain objects, they can switch to the object’s view and control it. Combined with the city’s anomalies and mechanisms in dungeons, movement becomes part of how stylists observe their surroundings, find their way, and solve puzzles.
Through Alchemy, stylists can gather materials in the open world and brew potions with Basic Recipes, or develop new recipes through trial-crafting. Potions can be used in combat, environmental puzzles, scene creation, and NPC interactions, connecting gathering, crafting, and exploration.
The Enhanced Floating ability allows Nikki to jump again in midair for a nimble double jump and dash forward through the air, while the Gemwork ability lets her discover and appraise uncut gemstones across the open world, then work them into gems for outfit crafting and Alchemy.
Combat also gains two new systems: firearms and magic, alongside a build system that adds depth and variety. Stylists can switch between the short musket for rapid fire and the long musket for aimed shots, while the staff allows them to cast different Oracle Arts based on their selected Nature’s Breaths and chant tiers. Combining these new ways of fighting with the build system lets stylists customize their combat style.
A living open world: From the streets of Cicia to the mysteries of Ceremius
In Cicia, stylists can drive through the city, run their own shop, and enjoy colorful urban life. Drive a car from Brise Automobile to complete passenger commissions, run the Warmhearted Boutique and put together outfits for customers, or discover activities such as the Scoop & Smile Creamery ice cream shop and Fuse Bead Art.
Meanwhile, Ceremius lies hidden in the mist. Stylists can awaken their alchemy skills, brew potions, and explore its fantastical flora and fauna. Unique activities such as relic restoration, old photo restoration, and divination turn the local lore into hands-on play.
Across both cities, immersive puzzle dungeons draw on stylists’ exploration abilities, combining miniature spaces, gear mechanisms, Rogue-style binding, and potion use to create new challenges as the story progresses.
The new Star Crown outfit: as stunning as it is powerful
Version 3.0 introduces the new Star Crown Resonance outfit, “Where Stars Shine Again,” alongside a lineup of new 5-Star and 4-Star Resonance outfits that combine artistic beauty with powerful abilities.
The Star Crown outfit comes with two weapons, the musket and the staff. With either equipped, unused dashes can be stored and released all at once or used one at a time. The outfit also adds four step-up bonuses and a free trial entry in the event hall, giving stylists the opportunity to experience its abilities before purchasing.
The new 5-Star Resonance outfit “When Light Falls Upon” unlocks Air Jumps and Air Dashes when equipped with its matching jump booster, alongside a stunning jump synergy effect. Meanwhile, the 4-Star Resonance outfits “Game for the Immortal” and “Beyond the Silence” summon chess pieces and a phantom from a fairy tale for playful interactions.
More outfits in a variety of styles will also arrive through anniversary celebrations, the Heart of Infinity, direct purchases, and cumulative top-up rewards.
More accessible, more lifelike: Version 3.0 Optimizations
Version 3.0 introduces a more flexible starting experience for stylists. New, veteran, or returning: every stylist can head straight to Cicia. New and returning stylists can skip the standard beginner tutorial, experience a new transitional tutorial, and use chapter select to pick a starting point from any story chapter released so far.
The update also enlivens the open world with large ambient NPC crowds, smoothly flowing traffic, and richer interactions across the city. Stylists will encounter taxi passengers with stories to tell, street musicians, NPCs dancing together, alchemy antics, and memorable character moments.
Ray-traced visuals have also received a significant upgrade. On supported devices, crowds and vehicles are now visible in reflections and shadows at medium and long distances, while reflections on wet roads, glass façades, and puddles feature greater depth and detail. Together, naturally moving crowds, traffic, and refined lighting create a richer atmosphere for exploration and photography.
160 Free Draws & 19 sets of clothes to celebrate Version 3.0 and Nikki’s Birthday
160 Free Draws and 19 Sets of Clothes: all free. Version 3.0 and Nikki’s Birthday bring a wide range of rewards for stylists.
The First Top-Up Double Bonus resets. Miss it, and you’ll wait another year. The Black Friday Sale lets stylists top up to get Diamonds back and earn points to redeem for rewards.
Your past top-ups pay off the moment you log in: claim generous rewards, including two stunning 5-Star outfits.
Nikki’s birthday has more surprises in store: outfits and Diamonds, as our way of saying thank you. The celebration also comes to the open world through a special quest, where friends reveal Nikki’s birthday surprise step by step across the city.
With two cities to discover, nearly 10 hours of cinematic storytelling, new exploration and combat possibilities, and a wealth of outfits and rewards, Infinity Nikki Version 3.0 opens a new chapter in Nikki and Momo’s journey through Miraland.
