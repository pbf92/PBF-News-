TITLE: Man United talks with Mauricio Pochettino alternative soured over £25million contract
OUTLET: talkSPORT

Mauricio Pochettino wasn't the only man contacted by Manchester United to replace Michael Carrick as manager.
The Red Devils also got in touch with former France boss Didier Deschamps - but talks didn't end very well...
talkSPORT understands that negotiations with the World Cup winning coach were only brief and failed to progress.
Pochettino was, in the end, the man brought in to the Old Trafford dugout from the USA, rather than Deschamps.
The ex-Tottenham and Chelsea manager is expected to arrive in Manchester on Thursday and take charge of his first game as boss against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday afternoon.
Meanwhile, Deschamps may prefer a move to Saudi Arabia.
Why did United not pursue Deschamps?
Deschamps was approached by Man United and talks took place, but the Red Devils are said to have been left suspicious.
According to The Athletic, conversations between the two parties ended up with United feeling as if they were being used.
They believed that the legendary French manager and ex-player was looking to gain leverage for a move to Saudi Arabia.
It had been reported in France in September that Deschamps had been offered a three-year contract by Al Ahli.
That contract is said to have been worth around £8.3million per season, so a whopping total payout of £24.9m.
And United were suspicious that talks with the 58-year-old were only ongoing to encourage a big-money Saudi offer.
Who else was in the running for the job?
Deschamps was one of a few contenders to replace Carrick at Man United, albeit a short-lived one.
There were a couple of other big names that were linked with the job, including Antonio Conte.
Conte was linked even before Carrick was sacked, having to put out a statement to deny that he was in talks.
United are said to have shown some interest, however, they opted to not push for the former Chelsea and Spurs boss, going for another ex-manager of the two Premier League giants instead.
Eddie Howe was also in the running, but talkSPORT understands that he wasn't given an interview despite co-owner Jim Ratcliffe making a case for him to come in.
Meanwhile, former Germany manager Julian Nagelsmann was sounded out for the job in October.
A move wasn't pursued though, and eventually Pochettino was the man that United's chiefs settled on.
