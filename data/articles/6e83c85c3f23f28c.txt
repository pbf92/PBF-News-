TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino confirms major Man United injury scare
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Bruno Fernandes Injury Doubt Leaves Manchester United Waiting Before Brentford Test
Manchester United have a Bruno Fernandes problem, and for now there is no clear answer. Mauricio Pochettino cannot say whether his captain will be fit for the Premier League meeting with Brentford after the midfielder came through the 2-2 draw with Sporting carrying more than one issue.
The manager said: "Yes, we don't know. He's affected by a virus from Portugal. He finished the game a little bit with a problem, and, today, he cannot be part of the training session. We'll see. We are going to see during the day and tomorrow, how he wakes up, and we'll see. We're not going to take any risks, because you know when this type of situation happens, you don't feel [you can]. We are going to pay attention but, still, we decide nothing."
That is about as non-committal as it gets. Fernandes is ill, he has a physical issue, and United are waiting. There is no point dressing it up. If he is not right, he should not play.
Bruno Fernandes contract decision adds another layer
There is movement off the pitch too. United are set to trigger the one-year option in Fernandes' deal, extending his contract until 2028. That secures the club's position, though it does not automatically end talk of a summer departure. Modern contracts work like that. Protection for the club comes first, long-term certainty comes later, if at all.
What matters immediately is availability. Fernandes remains central to how United function, particularly in games where control can disappear quickly.

Manchester United injury list still looks heavy
Marcus Rashford and Carlos Baleba are out, and neither case sounds close to full resolution. Pochettino said: "Baleba was injured against Roma, medical people were talking about 10-12 weeks. Marcus is on the field, starting to feel much better. I think he [Rashford] start to feel much better. We'll see day by day, you know, working with the feeling that it's going to provide us after every single training session, no, that increasing, no, the progression of the recovery."
Luke Shaw is back in the squad after six weeks out, which helps. Benjamin Sesko and Manuel Ugarte remain longer-term absentees. Sesko is not expected back before the end of January, while Ugarte continues his recovery from a serious knee injury.
Brentford match could hinge on squad depth
Pochettino made his broader point clearly: "I am looking forward to working with all the players, the players that are injured. I hope that they can recover as soon as possible. I think to have the full squad fit and available to be selected, that is the most important thing for every single coaching staff."
That is obvious, but it is also true. United need bodies and they need reliability. There is some good news, with Leny Yoro available after his red card against Liverpool was overturned. Youri Tielemans is also free to play domestically despite his second yellow card against Sporting. For United, that matters, because right now they are counting every fit player.
