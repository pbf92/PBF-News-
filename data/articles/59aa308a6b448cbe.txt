Insight Molecular Diagnostics expands CFO's role to COO
- Insight Molecular Diagnostics appointed current chief financial officer Andrea James as chief operating officer, expanding her responsibilities as the company prepares for commercialization.
- In her expanded role, James will oversee human resources, compliance, the CLIA-certified laboratory, client care, market access, IT, facilities and enterprise operations, in addition to her existing financial responsibilities.
- The company aims to strengthen its operational capabilities and prepare for broader adoption of its GraftAssure transplant diagnostics across transplant centers.
