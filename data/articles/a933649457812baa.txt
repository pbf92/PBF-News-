Everything Is Breaking. Here’s the Cybersecurity Stock That Benefits
Hello, Reader.
If the recent technology outage stories were grouped together as an Oscar-winning motion picture, they would be Everything Everywhere All at Once.
This 2022 Best Picture winner explores the concept of the “multiverse”, the idea that essentially anything – or everything – is happening in a parallel timeframe, across different universes.
Over the past week, it felt like everything broke “all at once.”
- On November 12, a global outage hit Microsoft 365 apps for nearly a day across more than 70 countries. Outlook, Teams, Copilot, OneDrive, and SharePoint were all down.
- On November 13, the Federal Aviation Agency (FAA) ordered a nationwide ground stop and grounded all flights for four hours.
- On November 16, JPMorgan Chase & Co. (JPM), Bank of America Corp. (BAC), U.S. Bank, Capital One, Truist, and Wells Fargo & Co. (WFC) experienced service outages. United Airlines Holdings Inc. (UAL) had to delay flights nationwide.
- On November 17, the National Security Agency’s (NSA) classified internal communications network went down. Meanwhile, Apple Inc. (AAPL) saw its Apple Pay, the App Store, iCloud Mail, and more, disrupted.
Thankfully, the outages were not due to cyberattacks. Government officials and the impacted companies blamed outdated technology, software bugs to system patches, and scheduled maintenance.
That changed on November 19, when JPMorgan announced that its third-party vendors were victims of a cyberattack. “We believe the attackers accessed certain marketing data held by those vendors, which may include some customer names and email addresses,” they said.
So, it’s no surprise that the onslaught of outages and the latest cyberattack on JPMorgan have investors piling into cybersecurity stocks.
Between November 12 and November 19, Amplify Cybersecurity ETF (HACK),which tracks cybersecurity stocks, is up about 3.5%. Meanwhile, the S&P 500, Dow Jones Industrial Average, and Nasdaq Composite are all down more than 5%.
With all eyes on cybersecurity stocks right now, I’d like to use today’s Smart Money to share the cybersecurity stock I like… and how it relates to a bigger trend in the AI trade that I call “The Crossing.”
The Company Catching Cyberattacks in Real Time
That company is Fortinet Inc. (FTNT).
Fortinet is a Silicon Valley-based cybersecurity company that provides unified security solutions. Its products are deployed over digital networks to protect users against malware, spam, and network intrusions.
The company is particularly notable for pairing physical firewall products with network security services. By combining the best of traditional firewall firms like Palo Alto Networks Inc. (PANW) with the newer threat-detection capabilities of software companies like CrowdStrike Holdings Inc. (CRWD), Fortinet provides a one-stop shop for IT managers looking to consolidate their cybersecurity budget.
