META Rides on Strong Demand in Ad Business: Can It Beat RDDT and SNAP?
Meta Platforms META is benefiting from strong demand in its advertising business, driven by increasing user engagement, AI-powered advertising tools and improving monetization across Facebook, Instagram and Threads. The company’s extensive user base and advanced advertising capabilities strengthen its competitive position against Reddit RDDT and Snap SNAP in the digital advertising space.
In the third quarter of 2026, Meta Platforms reported advertising revenues of $63.1 billion, up 26% year over year. Total ad impressions increased 10%, while the average price per ad rose 14%, reflecting improving advertising performance and engagement. META’s Family of Apps reached 3.64 billion daily active people, highlighting its extensive advertising reach.
Meta Platforms’ growing investments in artificial intelligence (AI) are improving advertising targeting, recommendations, and conversions. In the third quarter of 2026, enhancements to its GEM advertising model increased app ad conversions by 9.6%, while improvements to Andromeda boosted Facebook conversions by 1.4%. Advertisers adopting Meta AI business assistant recommendations achieved nearly 5% more conversions at the same cost per conversion.
The growing adoption of generative AI advertising tools remains another key catalyst. Nearly 10 million advertisers used at least one such tool in the third quarter of 2026. Meanwhile, Meta Platforms’ partnership ads business grew more than 97% year over year, surpassing a $15 billion annual revenue run rate.
Meta Platforms expects continued gains from AI-powered recommendations and advertising optimization. With strong user engagement, expanding AI capabilities, and a massive advertiser base, META appears well-positioned to maintain its advertising momentum and stay ahead of Reddit and Snap. For the fourth quarter of 2026, META expects revenues between $73 billion and $76 billion.
How Competitors Fare Against META
Despite Meta Platforms’ expanding portfolio, the company faces stiff competition from Reddit and Snap. Both companies are expanding their footprint in the rapidly growing digital ad market.
Reddit is benefiting from robust advertising demand, improving monetization, AI-driven ad enhancements and growing adoption of its performance advertising solutions. The company’s investments in artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning and advertising automation are strengthening its competitive position. In the third quarter of 2026, Advertising revenues surged 54% year over year to $847 million, supported by higher pricing and impressions. Total revenues increased 53% to $895 million. Click volumes grew more than 50%, while conversion volumes more than tripled, highlighting improvements in advertising effectiveness.
SNAP is benefiting from strong direct-response advertising demand, improved AI-powered ad targeting and expanding monetization opportunities across Snapchat. In the third quarter of 2026, Snap’s advertising revenues grew 4% year over year, supported by robust performance in app and web purchase campaigns. App purchase revenues surged more than 54%, while web purchase revenues increased 11%. Sponsored Snaps revenues jumped 56% sequentially, reflecting growing advertiser adoption.
