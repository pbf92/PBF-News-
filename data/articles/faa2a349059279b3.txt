TITLE: Christian reportedly drowns in Uganda after being thrown off water taxi for preaching gospel
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

A Christian father of three in Uganda drowned this month after being thrown off a boat by a group of Muslim men for sharing the gospel, according to a Christian persecution news outlet.
Morning Star News have reported that Gilbert Musede, 32, of Kaliro Town Council, Kibuku District, was thrown into Lake Lemwa while getting a water taxi from Saaka to Kapyani, according to a passenger.
Passenger Anna Maria Nausimba said she met the father and committed Christian as she boarded the taxi. She added that he apparently began sharing the gospel with her when she met him on Thursday 5 November.
As a heavy storm developed halfway along the journey, Musede reportedly stood and prayed to God for Him to calm the storm.
After the storm cleared, it is said he began professing his faith without knowing that there were Muslims onboard.
A Muslim man reportedly then shouted "Allahu Akbar", "God is Great", in Arabic, and stood up to reference the biblical account of Jonah being swallowed by a great fish before being released alive three days later.
The group of Muslim men onboard then allegedly told Musede they wanted to throw the Christian believer into the lake as Jonah was, at which point the alleged eyewitness says Musede warned he was not as strong as Jonah.
The alleged aggressors are said to have reiterated that they wanted to see him "demonstrate the strength" of his faith, also calling him an "infidel" and accusing him of "preaching false teachings".
The passengers then took Musede by the legs and threw him into the lake – according to Nausimba. Terrified, she was grateful the group never asked her what her faith was.
After the Muslim group reportedly turned the boat around, Nausimba called people to arrange for them to meet the passengers when they came ashore
Men on motorcycles are said to have taken the five Muslim men away from the scene.
A rescue operation involving divers and fishermen found his body after six hours. Morning Star News reports that local police are yet to investigate the death.
Musede is said to have been a member of the Pentecostal Discipleship Church of Christ in Kaliro. He leaves behind his wife and three children.
Uganda is a majority Christian country, with 82% of citizens in the 2024 census identifying as Christian, Catholicism the leading denomination at 36%. Just over one-in-eight identified as Muslim.
The Uganda Police Force has been approached for comment.
