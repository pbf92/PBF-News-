Aston Villa analyst hit with four-game ban and huge fine after Man United misconduct
Aston Villa analyst Victor Manas has been handed a four-game ban following his conduct in a 4-4 draw against Manchester United
Aston Villa's head of performance analysis, Victor Manas, has been slapped with a four-game stadium ban and a fine following his conduct during a 4-4 draw against Manchester United earlier this month. The Spaniard admitted to two charges of acting improperly towards the match officials.
On November 8, United faced Villa at Old Trafford, and the contest ended in a draw. This match was Michael Carrick's final game in charge before he was sacked and replaced by Mauricio Pochettino.
During the fixture, Manas, who has worked under Villa manager Unai Emery for 18 years - including during his spells at Sevilla, Paris Saint-Germain and Arsenal - was cited for two separate incidents. Manas acted improperly towards the match officials, who were headed by referee Chris Kavanagh, around the Old Trafford tunnel area at half-time.
He then confronted officials again in the 96th minute, when United keeper Senne Lammens went up for a set piece and challenged Villa keeper Zion Suzuki in the air before Patrick Dorgu scored to make it 4–4. Reports suggest he was removed from the stands by stewards.
An independent Regulatory Commission has now imposed sanctions. A Football Association (FA) statement reads: "Victor Manas has been sanctioned for misconduct during Aston Villa's Premier League match against Manchester United on Sunday 8 November.

"It was alleged that at half-time, the Villa analyst acted in an improper manner towards the match official(s) around the tunnel area. It was further alleged that he acted in an improper manner towards the match official(s) around the 96th minute of the fixture.
"Victor Manas subsequently admitted the charges. An independent Regulatory Commission imposed a four-match stadium ban, a £6,000 fine, and a warning following a hearing, and the written reasons for its decision will be published in due course."
Manas was not shown a red card but was escorted away from the tunnel. This is the fourth time the analyst has landed himself in hot water during his time in English football.
In December 2024, Manas came to blows with former Newcastle United assistant coach Jason Tindall at half-time during a Premier League fixture. This sparked a mass confrontation and both Manas and Tindall were sent off.
Following the game, Manas was given a two-game stadium ban and a £4,000 fine - this was reduced from three games and a £6,000 fine after he admitted to the charges and apologised. One year prior, when Villa faced Emery's old side Arsenal, Manas was once again involved in a touchline altercation and his improper conduct earned him a three-match stadium ban and an £8,000 fine.
In 2018, Manas, working for Arsenal, picked up a minor charge after acting improperly around the tunnel area as the Gunners faced Crystal Palace at Selhurst Park. He was handed a one-match touchline ban and a £6,000 fine.
