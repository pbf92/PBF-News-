TITLE: GTA 6's Mandy actress issues statement while gamers divided over ‘fixing sink’ scene
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

GTA VI has finally arrived, and it's almost impossible to avoid on social media. Players are having a blast exploring Vice City, uncovering secrets, and embracing the chaos that only Rockstar Games can deliver. However, one character in particular has taken the internet by storm, and that's Mandy.
Mandy quickly became the talk of the internet after Jason was sent to collect her rent, only to end up fixing her kitchen sink. Let's just say Mandy made her feelings towards Jason quite clear, and some fans seem more interested in her than the game's main storyline.
Now, Lexie Shoaibi, the actress behind Mandy's voice and motion capture, has officially revealed her involvement in GTA VI, and her announcement certainly hasn't done much to calm her newfound legion of fans.
GTA 6's Mandy actress confirms her role in Instagram post
If Lexie Shoaibi wasn't previously familiar to gamers, there's a good chance she is now. While she didn't address that infamous kitchen sink scene in her Instagram video, the actress appeared delighted to finally reveal her involvement in GTA VI.
“Okay, now that Grand Theft Auto VI is finally out, I can share with you guys a secret, which is that I'm in it,” she said.

Shoaibi also described Mandy as “a lot of fun” and encouraged players to let her know if they find her in the game. Oh, I don't think that will be a problem.

GTA 6 fans are divided over Mandy's viral 'fixing sink' scene
Unsurprisingly, Mandy's newfound fame has attracted plenty of attention from the Grand Theft Auto community, and it's fair to say that the internet's finest gooners have been working overtime. However, not everyone is particularly happy with the attention surrounding the character and her actress.
“Her DMs are gonna take a huge beating,” declared Reddit user MachoManPissDrawer69.
“‘Let me know if you find me’ - bro, you're literally one of the first missions,” joked imtired-boss.
“Poor thing doesn't know what is coming her way; Mandy became an instant fan favourite,” said -Captain-.
However, some fans have expressed concerns that the attention directed towards Shoaibi could go too far.
“Watch the GTA fanboys start obsessing over her photos and videos or worse, try to stalk her. It's honestly creepy how fixated they are on fake 3D women in bikinis,” commented Sexy-Hot-Boy.
“There are already people in this thread talking about her ‘insane’ picture, and it’s literally just her in a bikini,” added TheConqueror74.

Regardless of where you stand on Mandy, there's no denying that she's become one of GTA VI's most talked-about characters, with fans seemingly unable to get enough of her.
Whether Rockstar Games will ever give players the option to pursue a romantic relationship with Mandy remains to be seen, although it seems unlikely given how central Jason and Lucia's relationship is to the story. For now, at least, Jason might want to stick to collecting the rent.
