Chip stocks are bouncing back from last week's AI-driven sell-off, and NVIDIA (NASDAQ:NVDA) is leading the group higher. NVIDIA stock is at $161.05, up 28% in morning trading. The rebound comes after a hard stretch for the AI chip leader. In breaking news, Yahoo Finance just reported that NVIDIA's board is reinstating Jensen Huang as the company's CEO.
Rival chipmaker Intel (NASDAQ:INTC) is moving higher alongside NVIDIA. Intel stock is at $58.20, up 13%. Advanced Micro Devices (NASDAQ:AMD) is climbing as well, though its gain is smaller. AMD stock is at $454.34, up 7%.
Major-index futures are rising as markets try to recover from last week's sell-off, which pushed the major indexes into correction territory. Whether the move in NVIDIA, Intel and AMD marks a relief rally or the start of a real recovery is the open question for chip investors.
Selling Pressure Gives Way to a Chip Rebound
Last week's selling hit AI and semiconductor names hard, and tech stocks were still under heavy pressure on November 20. The gains across the chip group point to returning risk appetite, with buyers stepping back into AI hardware after the slide. Among the three names, NVIDIA is posting the largest advance.
