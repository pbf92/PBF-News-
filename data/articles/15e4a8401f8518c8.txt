TITLE: GTA 6 Lacks Mission Replay Because “It Felt At Odds With the Story,” Says Rockstar
OUTLET: GamingBolt

With the player base having spent extensive time with Rockstar’s Grand Theft Auto 6, some notable omissions have come to light. Perhaps the most egregious is the lack of a Mission Replay, which GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 offered. Not that the development team cut it because of technical reasons – instead, it’s for the sake of the story.
Rockstar Games senior design director Scott Butchard told Kotaku that, “Mission replay was certainly something we looked at early on in development, and we do not currently support it. As the game came together it felt at odds with the story we were trying to tell and felt a bit alien and disconnected.”
He felt that “sitting outside of the game as it were and replaying missions really diminished the choices you had made.”
To be fair, Grand Theft Auto 6 still offers multiple save slots. If you’re unsure about a decision, you can always return to a previous point in the story, much like in Baldur’s Gate 3, The Outer Worlds 2, and so on. The difference is they’re limited. At some point, you’ll have to commit to a choice and see how it unfolds.
While Rockstar could always it later, this approach fits with how much the developer wants players to immerse themselves in Jason and Lucia’s lives. Believing in them and their connection is a core tenet of the experience, after all.
Despite this, Grand Theft Auto 6 is still a monstrous success. It topped 32.5 million pre-sales and quickly crossed 39 million unique players on PS5 alone. How much higher could it go when the rumored PC version hits, reportedly in Q1 2027? Stay tuned for updates until then.
