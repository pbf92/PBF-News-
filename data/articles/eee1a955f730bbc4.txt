GTA 6 Patch 01.002.001: The second update
- Version: 01.002.001
- Release date: November 16, 2026
- Platforms: PS5, Xbox Series X|S
- PS5 download: approx. 602 MB
- Download for Xbox Series X|S: approx. 1.6 GB
- Rollout: Phased
- Patch notes: Not published
The latest GTA 6 update, version 01.002.001, is significantly smaller than the previous patch. On PS5, the download is around 602 MB, while data miners suggest the Xbox Series X|S version is approximately 1.6 GB – around 1 GB larger than its PlayStation counterpart. These figures are based on user reports and have not been confirmed by Rockstar Games.
The update is being rolled out to players in phases, although it's unclear exactly what has changed compared to version 01.002.000. Rockstar has yet to publish official patch notes, so it's also unknown whether this will be the final launch-day patch.
What improvements does the GTA 6 update 01.002.001 offer? 
It's unclear, as Rockstar Games has not published any patch notes. It presumably contains the usual bug fixes, performance improvements and preparations for the game’s launch.
03
GTA 6 Patch 01.002.000: The first update
- Version: 01.002.000
- Release date: November 13, 2026
- Platforms: PS5, Xbox Series X|S
- PS5 download size: approx. 1.07 GB
- Download for Xbox Series X|S: approx. 2.18 GB
- Rollout: Staggered across two waves
- Patch notes: Not published
On November 13, the first GTA 6 update appeared in the PlayStation queue. Version 01.002.000 is around 1.07 GB in size on the PS5 and is marked as a mandatory update. You'll need to install it before playing. On Xbox, the download is even larger. A whopping 2.18 GB needs to be downloaded.
Interestingly, the rollout was staggered. According to a data miner, the first half of players received the patch first, with the second half following around eleven hours later. So if you didn’t see it straight away, that was perfectly normal.
What improvements does the GTA 6 Patch 01.002.000 bring?
Here, too, the developers are keeping quiet about the contents of the update. What is surprising, however, is the fact that the patch on Xbox Series X|S is over two times as large as on the PlayStation 5.

GTA 6 updates: How to keep your game up to date
To make sure you're always playing the latest version of GTA 6, follow these four simple steps to keep your game updated and ready to go.
- Pre-load the game. According to reports, pre-loading has been possible since November 12. Anyone who has installed the game will automatically be offered the patches.
- Enable automatic updates. Your console will then download both patches automatically as soon as they’re available to you.
- Check your download queue. If you haven’t seen the patch yet, check your console’s download queue. The phased rollout may take a few hours.
- Make sure you have enough storage space. The game itself requires around 212 gigabytes of hard-drive space. The patches will take up additional space. Clear some space in good time.
