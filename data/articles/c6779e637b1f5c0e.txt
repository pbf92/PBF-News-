Semaglutide Benefits Regardless of Baseline Frailty Index: JAMA
USA: In the SELECT trial, semaglutide improved a broad range of clinical outcomes regardless of baseline frailty index (FI). Health-related quality of life improvements appeared greater among patients with higher frailty.
- The risk of the primary cardiovascular outcome increased as baseline frailty became more pronounced.
- Semaglutide's effect on the primary outcome remained broadly consistent across frailty categories, with no significant evidence that baseline FI modified treatment efficacy.
- Semaglutide also reduced the composite heart failure outcome, all-cause hospitalization and all-cause mortality across different frailty levels.
- Improvements in health-related quality of life, assessed using the EQ-5D-5L score, appeared greater among participants with higher baseline frailty.
- Compared with placebo, semaglutide was associated with a greater likelihood of improvement in frailty status by week 104 (OR, 2.46; 95% CI, 1.80-3.37) and a lower likelihood of frailty worsening (OR, 0.47; 95% CI, 0.34-0.65).
- The relative risk of adverse events resulting in permanent treatment discontinuation appeared lower with increasing baseline frailty.
