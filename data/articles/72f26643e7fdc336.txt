TITLE: GTA 6 Officially Includes Unreleased Song From Music Legend D'Angelo
OUTLET: Polygon.com

Following Grand Theft Auto 6's record-breaking launch, players discovered a touching tribute to the late musician D'Angelo in the game's vast soundtrack, which spans more than 800 songs. While you're listening to the radio while driving around Vice City, you might hear an unreleased song from the late artist.
What D'Angelo song is in GTA 6?
A posthumously released D'Angelo song called "Kamikazi" can be heard on the Worldwide FM radio station in GTA 6. According to Rolling Stone, D'Angelo collaborator DJ Dojah confirmed that this was an official D'Angelo song, and at least one other track ("Lady") can also be heard on the radio's Honey FM channel.
It's a nice tribute for Rockstar Games to include an unreleased D'Angelo song in GTA 6, since the artist was a major collaborator on Red Dead Redemption 2, the studio's previous game. The soulful, somber "Unshaken" plays during a key story moment in that game, and has become quite iconic in the eight years since Red Dead Redemption 2's release. D'Angelo even performed it live in partnership with Rockstar Games at the 2021 Tribeca Film Festival.
D'Angelo died from pancreatic cancer in October 2025, more than a year before GTA 6's launch. While "Kamikazi" doesn't get the same focus in GTA 6 that "Unshaken" did in Red Dead Redemption 2, it's nice that Rockstar Games still found a way to include at least one more new song from him in the latest GTA game.
Rampant copyright strikes are taking down videos of the song on YouTube, so the best way to listen to it in full right now is to tune into GTA 6's Worldwide FM station and wait for "Kamikazi" to play it or find an unofficial upload of it on social media platforms like X. It's unknown how RCA, the Sony Music Entertainment label that D'Angelo was signed to, plans to officially release Kamikazi outside GTA 6.
