Bitcoin Price Technical Analysis: Will $60K Hold Heading Into December?
BTC has broken lower from the $70,000–$75,000 region and is consolidating near $64,000 after testing support around $62,000. The recent decline has produced a clear series of lower highs, with immediate resistance clustered near $68,000–$70,000 and a heavier supply zone around $75,000–$80,000.
Momentum indicators have moved into oversold territory on intermediate timeframes, a condition that has historically preceded sharp relief rallies once forced selling exhausts. In a standard recovery, a sustained weekly close back above $72,000–$75,000 would be an important first confirmation that the corrective phase is ending.
Under the extreme sovereign-reserve catalyst outlined above, those intermediate levels would be cleared rapidly, allowing measured-move extensions and multi-cycle projections from the current base to support a far more aggressive upside trajectory.
While the past few weeks have been painful and have tested investor conviction, Bitcoin has repeatedly shown it can rebound powerfully when structural demand returns. The combination of its established scarcity, growing institutional infrastructure, and the theoretical potential for a major shift in global reserve allocation creates a setup from which a powerful recovery remains possible.
