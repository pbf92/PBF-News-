TITLE: Smartphone Ownership at Age 12 Linked to Eating Disorder Symptoms, Suggests Study
OUTLET: Medical Dialogues

Smartphone Ownership at Age 12 Linked to Eating Disorder Symptoms, Suggests Study
USA: A US cohort study of adolescents has found that smartphone ownership at age 12 was associated with greater eating disorder (ED) symptoms. The findings suggest that the timing of smartphone ownership may be associated with ED risk, although the underlying mechanisms remain unclear and require further investigation.
- Smartphone ownership at age 12 was associated with a 61% higher prevalence of binge-eating symptoms at the same age (PR, 1.61).
- Smartphone ownership was associated with more than twice the prevalence of self-worth being tied to weight (PR, 2.21).
- The prevalence of any ED symptom was 36% higher among smartphone owners at age 12 (PR, 1.36).
- Longitudinally, smartphone ownership at 12 was associated with a 67% higher risk of binge-eating symptoms at age 14 (RR, 1.67).
- Smartphone ownership was also associated with higher risks of inappropriate compensatory behaviors (RR, 1.50), self-worth tied to weight (RR, 2.03), and any ED symptom (RR, 1.49) at age 14.
