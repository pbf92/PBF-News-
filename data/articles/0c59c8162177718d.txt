Mauricio Pochettino introduces sixth rule change as Man United players lift weights after game
Manchester United were held to a 2-2 draw by Sporting CP in the Champions League on Wednesday evening, with Mauricio Pochettino making players lift weights next to pitch
Karen Carney and Owen Hargreaves were left amazed as Manchester United players completed a strength and conditioning session on the touchline after Wednesday's clash with Sporting CP. The Reds were held to a 2-2 draw in the Champions League at the Estádio José Alvalade, in a game which was marred by controversial decisions from referee Szymon Marciniak.
Luis Suarez opened the scoring from the penalty spot 33 minutes into play, having been denied by Senne Lammens from 12 yards just moments earlier. Amad thought he had levelled matters headed into half-time with a close-range finish, only to see his goal chalked off as a result of a foul from Youri Tielemans in the build-up.
A Bruno Fernandes double early into the second half handed United a 2-1 lead, only for Suarez to equalise with 10 minutes to play with a bicycle kick, despite appeals from United players due to the centre-forward nudging Lammens, who spilt the ball when trying to collect a cross.
Moments later, however, the Reds saw another goal ruled out for a similar incident. Fernandes whipped a well-placed corner on top of Kaique Pereira, with the Sporting goalkeeper punching the ball into the back of his own net.
As a result of Patrick Dorgu knocking into the shot-stopper in the process, however, Marciniak denied United the lead. However, it wasn't just the scoreline which had TNT Sports pundits animated in the aftermath.

Shortly after full-time, United players who had seen limited minutes against Sporting were spotted putting in a heavy strength-and-conditioning session on the touchline. Players were spotted using weights as they were put through their paces, with Pochettino seemingly looking to improve fitness within the camp.
Speaking about the showing, Carney said: "By the way, as we're talking now, the Man United players who have played limited minutes are up and down the pitch and putting a shift in. I've never seen a strength and conditioning session like there.

"They're doing a full on weight session. So all the stuff we're saying about the athleticism, the fitness, which is probably the time they can before the next game.
"I haven't seen this, this is proper full-on so they're thinking, 'Okay, we need to get them fitter, stronger, faster,' and I haven't see that from any team actually. They need to do it, they need to compete and that's what we've criticised of Manchester United but they're going for it."
Ex-United star Hargreaves, meanwhile, added: "I have never see a weight session away in the Champions League after a game on the pitch, proper weights. I tell you what the kitman is devastated he's had to bring the weights with him all the way from Manchester."
It comes as the sixth rule change that Pochettino has implemented at United since taking the reins from Michael Carrick last week. The 54-year-old has made the decision to take away post-match days off in a bid to amp up fitness, as well as implementing body fat tests and mandatory pre-match meals for players.
The Argentine also sees himself as more of a manager than a 'head coach,' although his title remains the latter - as was the case for Carrick and Ruben Amorim before him. Finally, United have also changed the layout of their pre-match lineup graphics, listing their players in positional order rather than by squad number. That was the case for the Liverpool game on Sunday, as well as Wednesday's run out with Sporting CP.
