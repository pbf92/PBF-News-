Cathie Wood Just Bought $88 Million of Anthropic Stock
Cathie Wood's ARK Invest increased its Anthropic ANT position for a second consecutive session, purchasing 317,053 shares across six exchange-traded funds on Wednesday.
The trades were valued at about $26.2 million, using the Nov. 25 closing price of $82.79.
The largest portion went into the ARK Innovation ETF (ARKK), which added 214,603 shares. ARK also bought 29,969 shares through its ARK Next Generation Technology ETF and 26,569 through its ARK Genomic Revolution ETF. The remaining orders went through three other funds.
The latest transactions followed ARK's purchase of 751,710 Anthropic shares on Tuesday, valued at nearly $62 million. Combined, the two days of buying amounted to about $87.8 million, based on the figures reported by TipRanks. Across both sessions, ARK acquired more than one million shares in total.
Anthropic shares had fallen below their $83 initial public offering price on Tuesday. The reported transactions show ARK adding to its exposure during the pullback, but do not establish the firm's total Anthropic holdings or the size of its stake relative to the company.
