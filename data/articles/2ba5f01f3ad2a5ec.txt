Coherent stock jumps on $2B share buyback plan
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- Coherent has authorized a $2B share repurchase program, allowing the company to buy back its common stock through open-market purchases, negotiated transactions, accelerated buybacks, block trades, Rule 10b5-1 plans, put options and other derivative transactions.
- The program has no expiration date and does not obligate Coherent to repurchase any specific number of shares.
- Shares rose 7% premarket to $228.
