TITLE: How Long Does It Take To Run Across GTA 6's Map?
OUTLET: Kotaku

In some parts of the world, Grand Theft Auto 6 is out now on Xbox and PlayStation. And one of the biggest questions many players have is: How big is GTA 6‘s map? Well, I’ve walked, driven, and flown across the whole map to figure out just how long these journeys will take in Rockstar’s massive open-world sequel.
I doubt I need to explain what GTA 6 is or why so many people are excited to finally play it. So instead, let’s hop right into the numbers! First up, I walked across the whole map. To do this, I picked the most southern spot I could reach that was still on land and then made my way on foot as straight as possible without deviating. Sometimes, due to alligators or impossible-to-scale cliffs, I had to make small detours. And at first, I had to stick to highways as I worked my way up the islands that make up the bottom part of GTA 6‘s massive world. But once I got past that, I was able to mostly head north on foot without much issue. When I reached the furthest point north on the map, I stopped my timer.
It took me 3 hours, 21 minutes, and 20 seconds to walk across GTA 6. It was quite the journey, and during my walk I discovered areas I had never seen before and encountered new events, including a dude having sex with an anime character while wearing a VR headset.
After walking, I turned around and then ran across the map. I followed a similar route and also had to dodge alligators and other critters. Even running and sprinting constantly, it still took me 1 hour and 41 minutes. In comparison, running across GTA V‘s map takes about 30 minutes. And walking across it takes around two and a half hours. You might be wondering why I was able to run across GTA V‘s world in less than one-third the time I was able to run across GTA 6‘s, while walking didn’t result in quite so dramatic a discrepancy. I think it comes down to the fact that characters in GTA V can sprint forever and seem to move faster. In GTA 6, Jason and Lucia have a stamina bar that limits how far they can sprint at full speed and seem to move a bit slower.
Driving and flying across Leonida
Next up, I grabbed a mid-range sedan, hopped in as Lucia and, starting from the southernmost roadway on the map, I drove the highways and freeways all the way up to the most northern dirt road in the game. To drive across the map via the highway, it took 10 minutes and 53 seconds.
To be clear: I wasn’t following speed limits and I weaved around traffic like a lunatic. Still, it took nearly 11 minutes of highway driving at top speed to cross the whole map. In comparison, you can find videos of people driving quickly across GTA V‘s map in about eight minutes.
Finally, I grabbed a prop plane and flew from the furthest bit of land in the south to the furthest bit of land in the north of Leonida. This journey took 5 minutes and 51 seconds.
I imagine some people are going to use these numbers to try to compare this world to those of past GTA games. And some might be disappointed that GTA 6‘s map doesn’t take an hour to drive across. But trust me, as someone who has played over 130 hours of GTA 6, the map feels massive. I booted up GTA V recently to compare, and that game feels so tiny and empty in comparison.
These times also don’t do a good job of demonstrating how big GTA 6 is in every direction. There are more buildings to enter, underwater locations to explore, and large swaths of wilderness to explore. So while these numbers are interesting, they don’t tell the whole story.
If you were worried GTA 6 is small, just know that after playing 100 hours and finishing the story and most of the main side missions, I still have sections on my map I’ve yet to visit.
