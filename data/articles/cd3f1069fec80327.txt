Jaguar Health Received Notice of Delisting
On November 20, 2026, Nasdaq notified Jaguar Health that it had determined to delist the company's common stock under Listing Rule 5101, citing public-interest concerns related to the reverse split and subsequent preferred-stock dividend, as well as shareholder dilution. The company requested a hearing by November 27, which stays suspension pending the Panel's decision; it plans to apply to trade on OTCQB if the decision is unfavorable.
Original SEC Filing: Jaguar Health, Inc. [ JAGX ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
