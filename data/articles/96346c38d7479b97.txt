TITLE: The truth behind Man Utd triggering Bruno Fernandes contract extension
OUTLET: London Evening Standard

Standard Sport understands that Man Utd have activated their option to extend their captain’s deal by one year.
Fernandes’ current terms were due to expire at the end of the current season, but now run until the summer of 2028.
Fernandes confirmed last week that he had put contract negotiations with United on hold due to the club’s ongoing struggles on the pitch, having already rejected two offers of new terms.
Man Utd head into the Premier League weekend sitting in 19th place in the Premier League, and welcome sixth-placed Brentford to Old Trafford on Saturday in what is new manager Mauricio Pochettino’s first home game in charge.
Fernandes has struggled to reproduce the form that earned him a host of individual awards last season, and Standard Sport understands that both parties believe it is sensible to return to negotiations at a later date.

But that left Fernandes open to approaches for foreign teams with pre-contract offers ahead of the summer.
And that’s why Man Utd have acted.
To prevent any further distractions, Fernandes has been tied up for a further 12 months and will return to the negotiation table once the club are on more assured footing form-wise.
But an exit this summer can not yet be ruled out.
Fernandes may not be able to help his team-mates on Saturday, though, having been struck down by an illness which prevented him from training on Friday morning.
Confirming the issue later on Friday, Pochettino said of Fernandes: "He's affected by a virus from Portugal. He finished the game a little bit with a problem, and, today, he cannot be part of the training session.
"We'll see. We are going to see during the day and tomorrow, how he wakes up, and we'll see. We're not going to take any risks, because you know when this type of situation happens, you don't feel [you can]. We are going to pay attention but, still, we decide nothing."
