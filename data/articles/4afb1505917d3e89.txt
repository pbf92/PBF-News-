TITLE: GTA 6 update fixes crashes, autosaving and story-progress bugs
OUTLET: finchannel

The FINANCIAL — Rockstar Games released a GTA 6 update Monday addressing crashes, failed autosaves and phone calls that could prevent the story from advancing, according to its November 23 release notes. The company listed seven fixes for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
Gaming publication Push Square identified the PS5 download as version 01.002.002 and described it as the game’s first post-launch patch on that console. It reported a 603.8-megabyte PS5 download and a larger, approximately 1.6-gigabyte download on Xbox Series X and Series S.
The patch follows the November 19 release of “Grand Theft Auto VI” for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The game follows Jason and Lucia in Vice City and the fictional state of Leonida, where the pair become involved in a criminal conspiracy after an attempted score goes wrong.
Seven fixes include problems that blocked progress
The official launch-update notes say Rockstar corrected several causes of game crashes and an issue in which autosaving failed. Another set of fixes concerns phone calls that did not trigger properly, including calls needed to advance the story or unlock mission content.
Rockstar also addressed cases in which players could not clear their Wanted Level and weather remained unchanged for extended periods. The other two entries concern the gym’s unusable deadlift bar and partners failing to hand over bags that players needed to sell to fences.
The company groups all seven entries under general and miscellaneous changes. Its notice is dated November 23 and covers both supported console families. It gives no count of affected players, crash-frequency measurements or estimates of how much playing time the problems had disrupted.
Reported rollout details carry separate qualifications
Push Square’s initial report described a staged PS5 distribution, citing Rockstar dataminer Tex2. That account said access would expand from roughly one-third of players to two-thirds after three hours and to all players after six hours. Those figures were attributed to Tex2, rather than a distribution timetable published in Rockstar’s release notes.
The publication subsequently updated its report with the official seven-item list. Its earlier text saying patch notes were unavailable described the situation before that update. Rockstar’s support page now supplies the developer’s own account of the changes, while the download sizes and staged-rollout details come from the separate reporting.
In its Xbox report, Pure Xbox reproduced a platform-update notice listing version 1.0.2.2 for Xbox Series X|S, alongside 01.002.002 for PS5. The publication described the Xbox download as about 1.6 gigabytes. Rockstar’s seven-item support notice does not provide those platform-specific version numbers.
Pure Xbox said the official fixes did not specifically identify Xbox Series S performance or changes to brightness. It treated player suggestions about smoother performance and brighter images cautiously. Its report did not establish those impressions as measured improvements, and the published Rockstar list makes no claim of a frame-rate or HDR change.
How PS5 owners can check for the patch
Sony’s game-update instructions say PS5 owners can select a game tile, press the controller’s Options button and select Check for Update. If a download is available, the console provides installation instructions. Sony also says games and applications update to the newest available version when started.
If an update will not install, Sony’s initial troubleshooting steps include testing the internet connection, restarting the console and checking that its system software is current. The connection test is under Settings, then Network. These are Sony’s general troubleshooting instructions for game updates, separate from Rockstar’s description of the bugs fixed Monday.
The console’s operating-system version can be checked under Settings, System, System Software and Console Information. Sony says an Up to date label indicates the latest system software is installed. That information concerns the PS5 system itself; the game’s Check for Update command is accessed from the game or application.
Sony’s GTA VI support guide also explains background installation. Its instructions place automatic-update controls under Settings, Saved Data and Game/App Settings, then Automatic Updates. The guide tells users to enable Auto-Install in Rest Mode and the separate Stay Connected to the Internet setting in the console’s rest-mode options.
For a stalled GTA VI download already visible in Downloads/Uploads, Sony’s first instructions are to select the download, pause it and then resume it. Its checks also include using the account that ordered the game or redeemed its code, confirming an internet connection, checking available storage and keeping system software current.
The update download is smaller than the full installation
Rockstar’s installation requirements, last updated November 3, say GTA VI requires up to 212 gigabytes of available space, depending on the device. That requirement describes installing and playing the game, rather than the size of Monday’s separately reported patch download.
PS5 owners can inspect free storage through Settings and then Storage, according to Sony’s GTA VI guide. The same guide separates problems with downloading the game from problems within it. Missing or unreadable codes in a boxed purchase are directed to the retailer, while the console’s download checks concern account access, connectivity and storage.
Rockstar’s console-error guidance directs PS5 players to PlayStation’s error-code support and Xbox Series X|S players to Microsoft’s error-code search. The publisher maintains those troubleshooting routes alongside its launch-update notes, with a separate option to submit a support request.
For example, Sony defines PS5 error CE-108255-1 as a game or application error. Its help page recommends updating the game and directs users who need further assistance to PlayStation Repairs. The page provides general console guidance, without identifying a particular GTA VI mission or linking that code to one specific cause.
Edition content and technical fixes are documented separately
Rockstar’s edition and platform guide, updated November 18, lists Standard and Ultimate editions. Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade separately after redeeming their game code. The guide says the boxed version contains a download code, with no disc included, and that no physical Ultimate Edition is available.
The Ultimate package’s published contents include extra vehicles, weapons, clothing and locations, with bonuses distributed through chapters of Jason and Lucia’s story. These are described in the edition documentation. Monday’s launch-update notice separately lists the seven technical fixes and does not announce additional Ultimate Edition items or a new content package.
Rockstar’s platform guide also describes remote access separately from local installation. It says GTA VI supports Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles using Series S graphics settings, as well as Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox consoles. The company cautions that connection speeds and other technical factors can affect performance differently from local gameplay. Those qualifications concern the way the game is accessed; the November 23 patch notes cover the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions.
Sony’s U.S. product page lists the Standard Edition at $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition at $99.99. It describes a single-player game with offline play enabled and Remote Play supported. The page also identifies PS5 Pro enhancements and says the game is unavailable for PS4.
The advertised PlayStation features include Tempest 3D Audio, DualSense haptic feedback, adaptive-trigger resistance and sound through the controller’s speaker. For PS5 Pro, Sony lists PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution and ray-traced shadows, with a compatible display required. These are features described on the product page, separate from Monday’s published corrections.
The FINANCIAL’s earlier GTA VI launch coverage examined the editions, advance downloads and the wider gaming market before the November 19 release. Monday’s support notice adds a dated list of corrections affecting saving, story progression and other game functions after the launch.
Frequently asked questions
