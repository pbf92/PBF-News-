TITLE: How GTA VI’s Vice City stacks up to the real Florida
OUTLET: CNN

For all its violence, “Grand Theft Auto VI” is, really, a complex love story — between Floridian gamers and the franchise’s lightly fictionalized version of their home state.
The long-awaited crime epic is a shockingly faithful ode to the unique pains and pleasures of South Florida living: inexplicable changes in weather, sneaky tolls on palm tree-lined expressways, run-ins with American alligators or crocodiles near virtually any body of water. GTA VI is part heist adventure, part, as one player put it, “Florida simulator.”
Players are patently impressed with GTA VI’s extremely vivid recreation of Miami (called Vice City in the game), down to its manatee-shaped mailboxes and lack of reliable sidewalks. Like their Miami counterparts, many motorists in GTA’s Vice City possess a tenuous understanding of traffic law and can erupt into dangerous road rage. Players can stop in for a cafecito at a hole-in-the-wall Cuban cafe and captain an airboat through “the Grassrivers,” the swamp wilderness that surrounds Vice City. And before they can even get to the main story, they’ve got to drive across the picturesque ocean highway from the Keys, where they can scuba dive and drink at bars that include a dead ringer for Key Largo’s Caribbean Club.
Out-of-staters might assume that the dog who drives a jet ski to fetch players in open water is some fanciful invention. Nope, she’s a real Florida character named Loosy. (A giant mutant crab was invented for the game, though it’s not uncommon in Florida to receive unwanted crustacean guests.)
“I can’t emphasize enough how well GTA 6 gets South Florida,” comedy writer Mike Drucker posted on X. “But even things like the way the streets look and the weird little median turns and the random tacky businesses on the side. Feels like visiting home.” (Drucker hails from Coral Springs in Broward County.)
Previous GTA installments accurately captured the glitz and sleaze of South Beach, but what’s wowing players now is the game’s equal dedication to nailing the banal beauty south of downtown: Flat roads that go on for miles, suburban strip mall sprawl, weather-worn ranch houses. (Researchers from GTA developer Rockstar Games, based in the UK, told Love Magazine that they repeatedly drove across South Florida down to the Florida Keys to create Leonida, the game’s version of Florida.)
Miami celebrated the release by outfitting Miami Heat players in special Vice City uniforms and hosting a neon-drenched drone show over Biscayne Bay.
Some Floridians joked that GTA VI had rendered the state a bit too lovingly.
“Genuinely worried that GTA 6 might convince impressionable adults that Florida is fun enough to move to,” the streamer Cynthetic wrote. “Stay home, we’re full.”
Carjacking and looting across Vice City has made other ex-Floridians a little wistful for their home state — until they’re forced to pay a hidden toll on a desolate expressway.
