TITLE: GTA 6 Prologue Now Runs on PC with a Graphics Card Radeon RX 7800 XT at 2 FPS
OUTLET: GameGPU

The victory-amd project, developed by a developer nicknamed myokex, has been published on GitHub. It is a fork of the fxceless/victory-fork branch based on the VIctory compatibility layer. The main goal of the new branch is to address specific driver and compiler errors when running the console version of GTA 6 for Xbox Series X on AMD graphics cards. Windows 11. During benchmarking on a system with a video card Radeon RX 7800 XT With 16 GB of memory, a Ryzen 5 7500F processor, and 32 GB of RAM, the project managed to render the opening cutscene and the first playable room of the prologue, with character control, at a frame rate of 2 to 3 frames per second. Eight-minute sessions generally run without resetting the video driver, although one benchmark showed a DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_HUNG error after five and a half minutes, and some scenes featured white frames, dark lamps, or disappearing walls.
Technically, victory-amd introduces a number of targeted improvements on top of the parent fork, including splitting dispatching over 65,535 groups into two parts for correct operation at resolution 2560x1440, improved texture representation partitioning by memory footprint, and the removal of ray tracing shaders that previously caused the AMD compiler to freeze. The base fxceless/victory-fork, benchmarked on a configuration with an Intel Core i7-14700KF processor and NVIDIA RTX 4070 12GB graphics card, already delivers 2,5 to 4 frames per second in the prologue, stable menu operation at 30 frames per second, loading autosaves, and gamepad responsiveness. Thanks to the implementation of translated shader optimization passes, the number of instructions on the host was reduced by approximately 43%, saving approximately 20 ms of runtime. GPU for each frame.
Despite significant progress in rendering geometry, textures, skies, and sunlit areas, the project remains unplayable and requires approximately 24 GB of RAM while running. A key unresolved issue remains the lack of ray tracing: reflected light and lamp shadows are currently disabled, causing all areas not directly illuminated by the sun to appear completely black. Both forks were developed using the Claude Code tool, are licensed under the GPL-3.0 license, and do not contain any game files, requiring users to have their own copy of the distribution.
