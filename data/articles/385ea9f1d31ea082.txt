TITLE: Clint Dempsey delivers verdict on Thierry Henry as Mauricio Pochettino’s USMNT replacement
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

The Mauricio Pochettino chapter of the U.S. Men's National Team history is now over, following his move to Manchester United.
With the Argentine opting to step away from his recently signed four-year deal in favor of a return to club management with the struggling Red Devils, U.S. Soccer is now on the lookout for their next head coach.
Several high-profile figures have been touted as potential replacements, including Pep Guardiola, Roberto Martínez, and former New York Red Bulls star turned CBS analyst, Thierry Henry.
The latter, a FIFA World Cup winner with the French national team, has previously coached AS Monaco, CF Montréal, and France's Under-21s. While the 49-year-old may not have the most trophy-laden coaching resume, USMNT legend Clint Dempsey believes he would be a smart hire by U.S. Soccer.
"I'd buy him too, man. Just because of being around him. Just the way he sees the game," he said on Men in Blazers' 'The Deuce' podcast. "The confidence that he would give those players. I think when he's telling them stuff, they're going to listen.
"There's a reason why there's a statue outside of the Arsenal Stadium, the Emirates, of him, because of what he was able…he's one of the best players to have played the game.
"So, if he can figure out a way to give some of that confidence and that knowledge of the game to some of these players, I think it can make a big difference. I mean, he was able to, in the Olympics, to get France to a second-place finish versus Spain.
"He's someone that loves the game. He loves the US. He's passionate about it. He loves New York, the time that he spent there. So, I say, why not?

"Because someone of his pedigree, and being around the game and me watching the way that he watches the game and sees football, I think there's a lot of positives there. And I know that is a job that he would want to do because we've had conversations about it, and I think he's someone that could make a difference."
That being said, Dempsey also offered his support to several other candidates, namely Real Sociedad boss Pellegrino Matarazzo, Nashville SC's B.J. Callaghan, and U.S. Under-23s boss Steve Cherundolo. As for former Portugal and Belgium coach Martínez, Dempsey admitted he could see the arguments both for and against appointing the ex-Everton manager.
Looking forward, U.S. Soccer needn't rush to make an appointment, with their next friendly scheduled for December 13 against Jamaica, after which they aren't scheduled to return to action until March when they face Mexico in the CONCACAF Nations League semi-final. This comes after they beat Haiti 8-0 on aggregate in the recent quarter-final double-header, in what were Pochettino's final two games at the helm.
