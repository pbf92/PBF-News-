Burlington 4Q 2026: Revenue $2.94B, EPS $1.67 — 10-Q Summary
Burlington's revenue rose 8.4% and net income increased slightly year over year, as higher sales were partially offset by a lower gross margin rate. Sales growth reflected contributions from 119 net new stores and 1% comparable-store sales growth; gross margin was pressured by higher freight costs.
Financial Highlights
¹ Reported as “Total revenue”. ² Reported as “Net income”. ³ Reported as “Common stock - diluted”.
Business Highlights
- Comparable sales: Rose 1% in the quarter, or $19.7 million.
- Store growth: 119 net new stores opened since the end of the prior-year quarter contributed $206.5 million in net sales.
- Gross margin: Fell to 43.7% from 44.2%, driven by higher freight costs from higher diesel fuel rates; merchandise margin was flat.
- Store footprint: Opened 136 gross new stores, including 14 relocations, and closed four during the first nine months; ended October 31 with 1,330 stores.
