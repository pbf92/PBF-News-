TITLE: Comparison of GTA 6 with early trailers revealed significant differences in graphics
OUTLET: GameGPU

A debate has erupted on social media about how Grand Theft Auto 6's graphics compare to those seen in promotional trailers and the final version of the game, which was released on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The controversy stemmed from a post comparing frames from early demos with the final build. The author praised Rockstar Games for believing the final game is virtually identical to what was previously shown. The post has garnered over 70 views, but audience reactions were mixed.
Some users disagreed with this assessment, pointing to noticeable graphical changes. Examples include simplified lighting, less detailed environments, and differences in Lucia's appearance in the prison scene compared to the first trailer. Critics consider this a graphical downgrade. Other participants defended Rockstar, suggesting that the differences in user screenshots could be due to HDR settings on the console, TV, and in-game. Thus, the dispute concerns not only graphical quality but also the conditions under which the images are compared.
The discussion comes amid ongoing research into the technical features of the console versions of GTA 6. Previously, players and experts alike have focused on the image reconstruction from the base 1440p resolution and the frame rate cap at 30 FPS. These decisions are aimed at ensuring the stable operation of the massive open world on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. However, Rockstar Games has not yet officially commented on any possible graphical changes between early trailers and the final release.
