TITLE: Pochettino’s first training session filmed by drone in United security breach
OUTLET: The Telegraph

Mauricio Pochettino’s first Manchester United training session has been leaked online in a security breach at the club’s Carrington headquarters.
Pochettino worked with his players for the first time on Thursday and footage of 12 minutes of that session was uploaded to YouTube by a drone operator.
United have not commented on the footage, which has been viewed more than 60,000 times. It shows Pochettino addressing players and working on the club’s training pitches ahead of the Premier League fixture at Liverpool on Sunday.
Pochettino arrived in the UK this week after his final games as United States coach, with his first interview being broadcast by the club earlier on Thursday.
