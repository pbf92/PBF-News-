TITLE: GTA 6, Zelda and Resident Evil fans caught up in bizarre Metacritic ratings war
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

The console wars have been raging for decades, with Atari, SEGA and Nintendo among the early contenders before PlayStation and Xbox entered the battlefield.
Now, that rivalry has seemingly evolved into a bizarre battle over which game deserves to be crowned Game of the Year, with GTA VI, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time and Resident Evil Requiem caught in the crossfire.
This latest battle is taking place on Metacritic, where users have been handing out perfect 10/10 and rock-bottom 0/10 scores. Sure, some reviews will be genuine, but with questionable reasoning and a flood of extreme ratings appearing over the last few days, it's difficult not to question some of their intentions.
Arguing over Game of the Year or the greatest game of all time is nothing new. That's all part of the fun. However, some fans appear to be taking things to ridiculous extremes.

Arguably, two of the biggest contenders for Game of the Year at The Game Awards this December are GTA VI and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake.
Finding out exactly where this latest battle began would be like looking for a needle in a haystack. However, a glance at Metacritic reveals some rather interesting user reviews, particularly when it comes to Nintendo's beloved classic.
"Greatest game in history, peak graphics, sound, gameplay, combat, dungeons, atmosphere, amazing as well, incredible boss fights and story," said Nasagoal82, awarding the remake a perfect 10/10.
Another user, Mjbjenkins, was equally impressed, describing the remake as an improvement over the original before declaring that any score below 5/10 is an illegitimate troll rating.

However, not everyone shares that enthusiasm.
"More or less the same, a repeat of what was a version from a couple of years ago, it doesn't deserve to be nominated for GOTY," declared Walterwhite9, awarding the game a 0/10.
Meanwhile, SickickBH was even less impressed, describing Ocarina of Time as "a punishment disguised as entertainment" before awarding it the lowest possible score. Even the original Ocarina of Time in 1998 and the 2011 remaster have been catching stray bullets from the community.
As for GTA VI, we covered its apparent Metacritic review bombing the other day, although it's worth remembering that many players have legitimate complaints. Nevertheless, I've noticed another wave of extreme ratings appearing over the last 24 hours.
"Greatest Game of all time; people will tell you otherwise because of bugs and some missions being boring, don't listen to them," said Body_Hazem, awarding Rockstar Games' blockbuster a perfect 10/10.
At the opposite end of the scale, Mogli88 gave the game a zero, criticising its portrayal of the relationship between the main characters in rather colourful fashion.
Of course, there's nothing wrong with having different opinions, but the contrast between some of these reviews certainly raises a few eyebrows.

Resident Evil Requiem is seemingly getting caught in the crossfire
However, GTA VI and Ocarina of Time aren't the only games attracting extreme scores, with Capcom's Resident Evil Requiem also receiving its fair share of questionable user reviews.
Many would consider Resident Evil Requiem another major Game of the Year contender, and much like its competition, Metacritic users have been awarding it both perfect 10/10 ratings and rock-bottom 0/10 scores.
"Absolutely insane game, I love it so much. The graphics are insane in it, probably the best I've seen in 2026. I genuinely hope that it will be GOTY 2026. Either this or Phantom Blade," explained loyal872, awarding it a 10/10.
However, another user was far less complimentary, awarding the game a zero while complaining about its $70 price, relatively short campaign and the female characters players are tasked with protecting.
Whether these extreme ratings are connected to the ongoing GTA 6 and Zelda rivalry remains to be seen. However, with more games attracting such wildly different opinions, it's becoming increasingly difficult to separate genuine criticism from those who might simply be looking to stir up trouble.

Is the Game of the Year rivalry getting out of hand?
With The Game Awards 2026 just around the corner, it's possible that the upcoming Game of the Year announcement is adding fuel to this rivalry. However, it's worth remembering that Metacritic user scores don't directly determine which game takes home the prestigious award.
Of course, there's nothing wrong with wanting your favourite game to win, and a bit of friendly competition is all part of being a gamer. But if people are handing out perfect 10s and rock-bottom 0s simply to make one game look better than another, what's the point?
After all, user reviews should help gamers decide whether a game is worth their time and money. If these rating battles continue, genuine opinions could end up getting lost among the nonsense.
And let's face it, you don't have to hate one game to enjoy another. Sometimes, it's perfectly fine to admit that more than one game deserves a 10/10.
