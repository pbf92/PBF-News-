TITLE: USA 2-0 Haiti (Nov 17, 2026) Game Analysis
OUTLET: ESPN

USA v Haiti
A Christian Pulisic bicycle kick for a score and another goal from teenager Julian Hall saw the United States men's national team clinch its spot in the Concacaf Nations League semifinals in Mauricio Pochettino's final game as coach.
Already 6-0 up on Haiti from Saturday's first leg in Miami, the United States' place in March's final four had effectively been sealed before Tuesday's return match in Cincinnati.
But Pulisic and Hall ensured Pochettino a winning end to his two years in charge before taking over Premier League giant Manchester United. A 2-0 victory at TQL Stadium sets up a semifinal with either Jamaica or rival Mexico in the semifinals in Inglewood, California.
Pochettino departs with a 23-2-12 (W-D-L) record and with the team on a six-game winning streak following its World Cup exit at the hands of Belgium.
U.S. under-23 coach Steve Cherundolo will lead the team during December's training camp, with a permanent replacement yet to be announced.
Pulisic, who has at times had a fractious relationship with Pochettino, provided the highlight Tuesday, meeting Weston McKennie's cross with an acrobatic volley in the 25th minute. It was the AC Milan attacker's third goal of the two-match quarterfinal, following his double on Saturday.
McKennie delivered another assist, this time with a perfectly weighted pass over the Haiti defense, for Hall to double the lead with a smooth take and finish in the 43rd minute.
It was the 18-year-old New York Red Bulls striker's fifth goal in six appearances for the United States. Only Pulisic, with seven, has scored more goals before his 19th birthday for the Americans.
Pochettino, who named the same starting XI on Tuesday as in the first leg, received warm applause from the crowd of 24,001 at TQL Stadium. The 54-year-old Argentine begins his new job as Manchester United's coach on Wednesday after agreeing last Friday to replace Michael Carrick.
Goalkeeper coach Toni Jiménez, top assistant Jesús Pérez and fitness coach Sebastiano Pochettino -- Mauricio Pochettino's son -- have been in England since Sunday. Assistant coach Miguel D'Agostino, fitness coach Sylvia Tuyà Viñas and analyst Alex Scott stayed for Tuesday's match and are also headed to Manchester with Pochettino.
During Pochettino's tenure, 78 players earned caps, including 31 who made their national team debuts. Nine teenagers also earned playing time.
