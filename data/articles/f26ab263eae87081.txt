ChainOpera AI: COAI supply unlock releases about 6% of circulating supply - Oct 2027
ChainOpera AI has scheduled a supply unlock of at least 11.25 million COAI for October 2027. The tokens are set to go to advisors, investors and team members. The schedule gives a month but does not specify a day for the release.
The minimum amount due to unlock equals 5.98% of the circulating supply. Once the tokens are released, the recipients can sell them.
