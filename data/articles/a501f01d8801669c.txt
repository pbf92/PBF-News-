LayerZero: Foundation Stakes 100 Million ZRO on Zero - 23 Nov 2026
On Nov 23, 2026, the LayerZero Foundation stakes 100 million ZRO on Zero. The foundation splits the stake into two transactions of 50 million ZRO each. It stakes on the same terms as other participants on Zero.
The foundation commits the full stake to securing Zero. The lock applies to the tokens in both transactions and runs until March 31, 2027.
