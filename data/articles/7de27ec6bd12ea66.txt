International Paper Approves DS Smith Spin-Off, Sets Dec. 7 Distribution
Less than 1 min read
International Paper approved the spin-off of its EMEA Packaging Business as DS Smith, with distribution expected on December 7, 2026.
Key Highlights:
- Shareholders and depositary interest holders of record December 3 are expected to receive DS Smith shares, subject to applicable tax withholding.
- Expected distribution ratios are 0.36 DS Smith ADSs per International Paper share or 1.80 DS Smith ordinary shares per depositary interest.
- International Paper expects to retain about 19.9% of DS Smith; the distribution remains subject to customary conditions and regulatory approvals.
- The DS Smith distribution is expected to be taxable for U.S. federal income tax purposes; cash will replace fractional share entitlements.
- International Paper intends to delist from the London Stock Exchange on December 22, 2026, while remaining listed on the NYSE.
