TITLE: 'We know what this game means' - Virgil van Dijk previews Liverpool v Man Utd
OUTLET: Liverpool FC

Following the international break, the Reds return to action against Mauricio Pochettino’s United at Anfield on Sunday afternoon (4.30pm GMT kick-off).
Van Dijk previews the game in his notes for the matchday programme, writing: “After two weeks without Premier League football, we could not have a more exciting fixture with which to return to action.
“Some of the most memorable games I have been involved in as a Liverpool player have been against Manchester United, and I am looking forward to another special Anfield atmosphere.
“These are the games everyone wants to be involved in. I don’t need to talk too much about the history and the rivalry between the two clubs, I just know that you guys will be as excited as we players are to get out there and go to work.
“It will be a tough game, no doubt about that. We know we are facing a team with a new manager, a team that will be highly motivated to perform, and we need to be ready to work extremely hard if we want to come away with the result we all want.
“We went into the break on the back of a good, and important, away win against Crystal Palace, in which I believe we showed a lot of the qualities and characteristics required if we are to have a successful season.
“It wasn’t a perfect performance, by any means, but the way we stuck together, fought for everything and defended as a team for 90-plus minutes was pleasing, especially at the end of a testing run of games.
“Those are the standards we need to maintain going forward. I say it so often, but the best teams are the ones who can stay consistent, week after week. We are still working towards that, and Sunday is another good test in that regard, with more big games on the horizon.
“This is the busiest period of the season, without a doubt. Between now and the end of January we will play at least 19 games across four competitions, and that is something that everyone needs to be ready for. The demands are big but so are the rewards, and everyone inside the dressing room is ready for the challenges ahead.
“First and foremost, though, our focus is on Sunday. We know what this game means to the club, means to you all. We know how much you will all be up for it, and I can assure you that we will be too.
“Hopefully we can give you all another day to remember.”
