TITLE: Rockstar hints massive 'GTA 6' map saved room for future content
OUTLET: Mashable

Grand Theft Auto VI is out in some parts of the world (Editor's note: Who's to say you don't live in New Zealand, too?). The early reviewers all say it's incredible, and according to Rockstar, there may be more to come.
That piece of reporting comes courtesy of Kotaku, whose reviewer Zack Zwiezen noted that he had played more than 130 hours of the game without ever visiting certain parts of the enormous GTA 6 map. These areas include a zoo and two large casinos (where gambling is apparently not allowed), and are never mentioned in the main story or any side missions.
According to Rockstar Games VP of design Stephen Donkin, the in-game map was built for more than just this initial launch period.

"The map has been built for the long term and with many factors in mind,” Donkin told Kotaku. "So there are a few areas on the map that give us options in the future should we have a gameplay or narrative need for them."
Donkin's response seems to indicate that there will be more content coming to GTA 6 in the future, which is not surprising in and of itself. It's only mildly surprising that Rockstar would openly acknowledge that fact on launch day (in New Zealand, anyway). Where this gets interesting is that Donkin could be referring to a couple of different things, namely new single-player missions or a still-unannounced online mode.
GTA V rather infamously never got single-player DLC, despite Rockstar promising such a thing shortly after its launch in 2013. Instead, GTA Online became massively successful, and that took priority. GTA 6 could be an opportunity to right that wrong, or it could be a repeat of history.
How big is the GTA 6 map?
In September, Games Informer published interviews with Rockstar leaders that revealed just how gargantuan the map really is.
Rockstar confirmed it's the largest map it's ever made, and roughly twice as big as the GTA 5 map and three times as big as the Red Dead Redemption 2 map. But it's not just about size. Rockstar said it's also much denser and includes more interior spaces gamers can explore.
The map currently has six main regions: Vice City, Leonida Keyes, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga National Park, all inspired by different parts of Florida.
