TITLE: LEGO Announces 4 New Sets Based on the Harry Potter HBO Series
OUTLET: VGC / IGN

The LEGO Harry Potter theme has been a mainstay since the first set came out in 2001. Now that HBO has a new TV adaptation of the Wizarding World in the works (set to debut on December 25), LEGO has finally unveiled a new batch of sets based on the new series. Four new sets have been announced, two of which are available to preorder now. They're all out January 1. Let’s take a look.
Featured in this article
The four sets offer a nice selection of characters and locations from the first book, Harry Potter and the Sorcerer’s Stone, which is what the HBO show’s first season adapts.
Honestly, these sets don’t look all that distinct from the sets based on the movies. Then again, based on the TV show’s trailer, they didn’t really change much when adapting the franchise into a show.
LEGO Harry Potter: Privet Drive
It all starts on 4 Privet Drive. That’s where we find the home of the Dursley family, who adopt Harry Potter after you-know-who kills his parents. The Dursleys aren’t very nice to Harry, but this LEGO set lets you build their two-story house, complete with Harry’s cupboard under the stairs. This one’s available for preorder now.
LEGO Harry Potter: Gryffindor Common Room
The three main young wizards all end up in house Gryffindor, so they spend a lot of time in the common room. This set recreates that cozy space, complete with a fireplace, five minifigures, and a Quidditch table that’s unique to the show.
LEGO Harry Potter: Hogwarts Express & Platform 9¾
- Not available for preorder
There are so many now-iconic things in the first book that it must have been hard to pick what to base sets on. That said, it would be a shame if there wasn’t a new Hogwarts Express set. This set has platform 9¾, plus the bold red train. It also includes minifigures for Harry, Hermione, Ron, Draco Malfoy, and Molly Weasley — all characters that appear in or around the train in the book.
LEGO BrickHeadz: Harry, Draco and Hagrid Figures
- Not available for preorder
BrickHeadz are kind of like LEGO’s take on Funko Pops. They depict known characters from pop culture, but in a stylized design, and they’re meant for display. These seem like good, blocky renditions of the trio they depict.
