TITLE: Wales rugby star Louis Rees-Zammit ‘starstruck’ after meeting Manchester United pair
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Louis Rees-Zammit admitted he was "shaking" when he met Manchester United duo Bruno Fernandes and Diogo Dalot, mere days after helping Wales secure a momentous first victory against New Zealand in 73 years.
Zammit scored the decisive try in Cardiff on Saturday as Wales secured a memorable 35-28 win over the All Blacks.
Yet the "starstruck" Manchester United supporter admitted he lost his cool upon meeting the pair following Portugal's 3-2 Nations League win over Wales in Cardiff on Tuesday.
"I went to support Wales football and, although they lost, I thought it was a really good performance from them, going 2-1 up," said Rees-Zammit.
"After the game, being able to meet Bruno Fernandes and Diogo Dalot was really special.
"As a Manchester United fan, I was a bit starstruck, to be honest. I was shaking a little bit when I was shaking their hands.
"I was sat down waiting and I was shaking. I stood next to my brother and said, 'I'm just going to have to stand up and just try and calm down a little bit.'
"I'm just a massive fan and to have the opportunity to meet players like that is crazy.
"I actually spoke to Bruno about the New Zealand game and he said he'd seen the result online.
"Whether I believe him or not, I don't know, but he made it sound like he knew about it."
Wales return to Nations Championship action against Australia on Saturday still on a high after their historic All Blacks win
They remain fifth in the northern hemisphere standings but, having beaten Japan in their first autumn fixture, are chasing a third successive victory for the first time since the 2023 World Cup.
Rees-Zammit, 25, said: "We reviewed everything on Monday, but a lot of our focus and concentration has been put towards this Saturday.
"I think if we can back up what we did last weekend, it'll show the kind of journey we're on.
"Any game that we can win is an opportunity for us to go again the week after. Especially last week, getting back-to-back wins for us is huge. It's just about how we can carry on this momentum."
Rees-Zammit believes Wales are improving under Steve Tandy, with the 2027 World Cup now just over 10 months away.
He said: "In the Six Nations we started to build nicely. From start to finish, it was on the up.
"Come this autumn, I think we've really started to build some momentum in training weeks and, leading on to Saturdays, we're just full of confidence and belief."
