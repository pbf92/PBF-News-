Westlake Agrees to $199 Million in PVC Pipe Antitrust Settlements
Less than 1 min read
Westlake agreed to settlements totaling $199 million with non-converter seller and end-user classes in PVC pipe antitrust litigation, subject to court approval.
Key Highlights:
- Westlake agreed to pay $119.5 million to the non-converter seller settlement class and $79.5 million to the end-user settlement class.
- Both classes have agreed to release Westlake from certain claims relating to the litigation, subject to settlement conditions and court approval.
- Westlake expects to record a pre-tax charge for substantially all of the settlement amounts in the fourth quarter of 2026.
- The settlements do not constitute an admission of liability or wrongdoing by any party.
Original SEC Filing: WESTLAKE CORP [ WLK ] - 8-K - Nov. 20, 2026
