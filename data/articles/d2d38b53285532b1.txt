TITLE: December 11 is Suddenly an Exciting Day for GTA 6 Players
OUTLET: OpenCritic

Rockstar is finally releasing Grand Theft Auto 6's Original Score by Justice on December 11, giving fans something brand-new to look forward to. GTA 6's score is essential to setting the tone for Rockstar's latest epic, and it won't be long before players can check out the full album for themselves.
Music has often been one of the most important elements of the GTA formula over the years. Rockstar has always done an excellent job picking iconic songs for its soundtracks that match the feel of the game and help players form lasting memories with each GTA title. GTA 6 is no different, immersing players in the different areas of Leonida across 24 unique radio stations.
While Rockstar Games remains silent on GTA 6's PC release date, a few unofficial ports reach a major milestone by running the game at 24 FPS.
Gamers can pre-order the Grand Theft Auto 6: Original Score by Grammy-winning French duo Justice in three versions: a double CD, a triple black vinyl, and a limited edition triple crystal clear vinyl.
