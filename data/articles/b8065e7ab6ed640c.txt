GTA 6 Doesn't Let You Replay Missions, and Rockstar Says There's a Good Reason for That
One and done.
GTA 6 gives players a lot of freedom in how the story and quests play out, but it came at the cost of letting players replay missions, according to Rockstar.
When speaking with Kotaku, Rockstar Games senior design director Scott Butchard explained why the studio scrapped a mission replay menu in GTA 6. Both GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 allowed players to freely replay missions, but that's not the case in GTA 6. According to Butchard, they considered bringing the feature back, but cut it because it was "at odds" with the story.
“Mission replay was certainly something we looked at early on in development, and we do not currently support it,” said Butchard. “As the game came together it felt at odds with the story we were trying to tell and felt a bit alien and disconnected. Sitting outside of the game as it were and replaying missions really diminished the choices you had made.”
The lack of a mission replay option has been a point of contention for fans. GTA 6 has some of the best missions in the franchise, particularly the final mission of Chapter 1. Given that GTA 6 can take players anywhere from 50 - 100 hours to finish, most people probably won't start the entire game over just to re-experience their favorite mission. However, it's worth noting that Butchard says the game doesn't "currently support" this feature. Maybe it'll come in the form of an update later, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed that.
GTA 6's story and missions can deviate somewhat significantly based on your choices. Certain characters can be killed or spared, objectives can be completed in different ways, and the entire relationship between Jason and Lucia can vary based on your actions. You can get entirely different dialogue options and cutscenes based on these differences, which might be why a mission replay system isn't as straightforward as it was previous games.
However, Red Dead Redemption 2 also featured different choices that changed Arthur's character. It wasn't quite as nuanced as GTA 6's variations, though. Maybe Rockstar will figure out a way to do this, but it clearly fell low on the priority list, if it was on the list at all by the end of development. Even if Rockstar never adds it, modders will probably find a way to slot it in when the inevitable PC version releases.
