TITLE: The Salford acting school where Coronation Street and Hollyoaks stars show how it's done
OUTLET: Manchester Evening News

The Salford acting school where Coronation Street and Hollyoaks stars show how it's done
Actors of all ages have secured parts in commercials and TV shows
It started in a village hall in Boothstown and now a Salford acting school has helped secure parts on soap operas, commercials and more.
JM Casting first launched back in 2014 operating as Screenplay Acting from Boothstown Village Hall, in Boothstown, near Worsley.
It's since expanded to a dedicated studio in Media City that is fully equipped for studio filming and has become known for helping actors of all ages secure small parts in the likes of Emmerdale, Hollyoaks, CBBC and a host of TV adverts.

Landing a part is not a given, but it's something that delights owner Jade McCabe, who says it's 'amazing' seeing her actors on screen.
The 45-year-old decided to set up the business after working in casting for BBC's Waterloo Road and freelancing on commercials, where she 'saw so many incredible, experienced actors miss out in auditions simply because of nerves or a lack of familiarity with a studio environment'.
"I created these classes so actors could get comfortable in that environment, making their first or even 51st audition feel much less daunting," she said.
"Additionally, casting directors usually only invite actors in if they've seen them perform before, so our courses give actors a direct opportunity to perform in front of casting directors from Hollyoaks, Coronation Street, and EastEnders, as well as TV drama directors."
She says the thousands of adults and children who have attended over the years have gained invaluable insight from tutors and industry professionals who regularly attend the classes.
Classes are taught by James Boyland, who has been in Coronation Street and Emmerdale, while there are regular guest appearances from the likes of Hollyoaks actors including Ashley Taylor Dawson (Darren Osborne) and Isabelle Smith (Frankie Osborne).
Coronation Street casting director Joanne Moss and award-winning Hollyoaks casting directors Peter Hunt and Gill Charnock also attend some classes to watch the actors at work.
In September casting director Kate Bone (Game of Thrones, The Crown, The Responder, Star Wars, London Spy) attended a session and this month, director Jason Wingard (CBBC's The Dumping Ground, Coronation Street) is watching children perform on the kids course.
There have been numerous success stories over the years including Matthew Mainwaring, from Monton, who landed a seven-episode role playing John in Emmerdale just a week after auditioning for Emmerdale at Jade's classes.
Actress Chrissy Parle auditioned for Kate Bone in one of the classes and was cast alongside Martin Freeman in BBC One's The Responder.
"Kate specifically noted she chose Chrissy because of our workshops," said Jade, who grew up in Salford and now lives in Timperley.
Younger students have had similar success, including Zara Thurstan, who was spotted by the casting director for CBBC's 4 O'Clock Club at the classes and landed a lead role.
Another young actor, Inathi Rozani, was spotted by director Alex Jacob for a role in Casualty, and went on to play lead roles in Waterloo Road and CBBC's Biff & Chip.
"It feels amazing seeing our actors on screen," said Jade. "I know firsthand how tough this industry is to break into, so it's a huge achievement for them, and I'm over the moon that we - alongside their agents - could help them on that journey."
Her own son Harley, age 10, has attended the classes himself, and got signed by an agent and received 'fantastic feedback' from a Hollyoaks casting director. And her five-year-old daughter Saffie is already 'desperate to join in too'.
Children's classes are for ages 8-11 and 12–teens, while adult courses start from age 16.
"We welcome actors of all ages, our oldest student to date is 81," said Jade. "It's definitely never too late to start. That is the wonderful thing about acting, you can jump in at any age.
"Attending these classes can never guarantee a TV role and actors don't have to attend to be cast in soaps, but it's an incredible opportunity that opens doors and gives great experience and our students get a lot of enjoyment from simply taking part and building their confidence."
Children's classes start at £75 for a 10-week course and adult courses start at £145 for a 4-week course.
Alongside running the classes, Jade works as a casting director casting TV commercials and short films, with past projects including campaigns for adidas, Puma, McDonald's, Google, Disney, Nike and Smart Life Insurance.
JM Casting is at Studio 96, Unit 1A and 1B, Sundance Court, Kansas Avenue, Salford, Media City, M50 2GZ.
