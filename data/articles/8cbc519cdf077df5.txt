TITLE: 75% of Rockstar Games staff vote against union recognition
OUTLET: GosuGamers

Rockstar Games employees voted against unionising.
The Independent Workers' Union of Great Britain (IWGB) has confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI maker Rockstar Games’ employees voted against a deal that would recognise the Rockstar Game Workers Union. 93.2% of the company’s staff participated in the vote, and a total of 75% of voters were against recognising the union.
The union, if it had been approved, would have been the largest recognised workers’ union in the UK games industry.

Did Rockstar Games employees vote against unionising?
A vote held to recognise the Rockstar Game Workers Union has come to a close, with 25% of voters agreeing to recognise the union, while 75% voted against the union. The union will still organise and continue to exist, but Rockstar Games no longer has any obligation to recognise it in an official capacity, either as a representing entity for its staff, or by meeting it regularly.
While speaking to Eurogamer, Rockstar’s head of publishing Jennifer Kolbe said:
"We are extraordinarily proud of our team, the culture we've built together and the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6. This result reflects our team's strong support for continuing the direct relationships, collaboration and open dialogue that have helped make Rockstar Games so innovative and strong. As we look ahead, we're excited to build on all we've accomplished together."
The Rockstar Union also released a statement following the vote, confirming that employees at Rockstar Games will continue to organise:
"For anyone at Rockstar who believes in a fairer workplace, a fairer sector and a fairer society - this isn't a sign to retreat, it's a sign to double down on what we stand for. Hundreds of our coworkers voted in favour of our union at Rockstar, yet sadly we recognise we have more work to do to build understanding and win the trust of more of our colleagues. We're ready to put in the effort to make that happen.
"Whilst we haven't emerged with a recognition deal, this ballot has put our union on the radar of every worker, and opened the door for new kinds of conversation to start happening. People entirely unfamiliar with unions were for the first time debating employment conditions, collective bargaining rights, and our contracts. We heard from many more colleagues about what mattered to them, and we hope people will continue to engage so that we can see where these new discussions can lead us.
"A recognition agreement would have marked a path towards greater workplace democracy. But it's one route of many. At Rockstar we have already won, and will continue to win, tangible changes that improve our everyday lives. We'll keep fighting for a say. For pay transparency, contractual overtime pay, and protections against Al and layoffs.
"The power to shape our working lives has always been, and will always be, in our hands."
This outcome comes amid an ongoing Rockstar Games vs IWGB tribunal legal case, which revolves around the firing of employees who were found to be in a union Discord server. The affected employees allege that this was a matter of union busting by Rockstar, but Rockstar alleges that the firings occurred due to broken confidentiality agreements.
