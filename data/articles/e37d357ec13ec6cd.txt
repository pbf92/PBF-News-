TITLE: GTA 6 Pushing The Witcher 4 to Improve “Every Possible Facet” of The Witcher 3
OUTLET: Comic Book Resources

Gaming has been consumed by Grand Theft Auto 6 ever since its release last week, and its impressive depth is already exerting its influence on the development and business sides of the industry. The newest release from Rockstar Games rocketed its way to becoming the most lucrative media product of all time amid a near-perfect critical reception, and players are clearly reveling in what is arguably the most sophisticated open-world action game in history. GTA 6 has already set a bar that The Witcher 4 is being compared to.
The fourth installment of CD Projekt Red's fantasy RPG series, which itself made its mark on open-world gaming with 2015's The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, is set to be the Cyberpunk 2077 studio's next release in 2028. Given Cyberpunk 2077's esteem as boasting a top-notch game world, there are high hopes that The Witcher 4 will be on the cutting edge. Now that Grand Theft Auto 6 has now been met with a collective jaw drop, CD Projekt Red is being questioned about its ability to innovate.
Improvements From The Witcher 3 to 4 Will Mirror GTA 5 to 6, Says CDPR
During a recent earnings conference, CD Projekt Red IR (the media company's investor relations wing) was asked in a Q&A section about how big of a technological leap going from The Witcher 3 to The Witcher 4 will be. The question was put to co-CEO Michał Nowakowski, per GamesRadar, specifically in relation to Grand Theft Auto 6 and the massive improvements made in the 13 years since that series' last installment.
Nowakowski expects a "comparable" difference between TW3 and TW4, saying "we are working on The Witcher 4 to make it significantly better looking and improved in terms of pretty much every possible facet of the game, so yes, you can expect us to make a much bigger jump between The Witcher 3 and The Witcher 4." According to Nowakowski, CD Projekt Red developers "did look at" GTA 6 and assess its improvements.
Can The Witcher 4 Stand Up to GTA 6?
Nowakowski is obviously going to sing the praises of CDPR's work on The Witcher 4 to investors, but even with this expected optimism, it's still a lofty stance to take. Improving every facet of a game—one of the most beloved RPGs ever, no less—is difficult enough, but also taking a leap about as big as GTA 6's is a bold statement, and may unearth some bad memories of the promises made before Cyberpunk 2077's infamous launch.
Part of the problem is GTA 6's unparalleled budget. Rockstar hasn't given exact numbers, but estimates point to its full budget approaching $1.5 billion. CD Projekt Red has been very successful with The Witcher 3 and Cyberpunk 2077, and is a decently sized studio, but neither its operating budget nor its development team size come close to those that Rockstar enjoys. GTA 6 is a product that only Rockstar has the ability to make.
This isn't to belittle CD Projekt Red; TW3 and 2077 are two of the most impressive open-world games as well. But CDPR has been known to over-promise, and the depth to which Rockstar has reconfigured the GTA formula is quite astounding. A few The Witcher 4 tech demos have shown some promising glimpses of what CDPR is cooking, so here's hoping it really does blow The Witcher 3 out of the water.
