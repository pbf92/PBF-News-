TITLE: Mauricio Pochettino addresses Manchester United transfer plan with USMNT stars linked
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Incoming Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino has kept the door ajar on the possibility of recruiting players from the U.S. Men's National Team. Pochettino, who was officially unveiled as United's new boss last week, will step down from his role as head coach of the USMNT following their fixture against Haiti on Tuesday, before boarding a private jet to Manchester shortly after the final whistle.
The former Southampton, Tottenham Hotspur, and Chelsea manager will take over from Michael Carrick and faces the considerable challenge of transforming United's fortunes, with the Red Devils sitting deep in the relegation zone after their opening 10 Premier League fixtures. United are anticipated to be active in the January transfer window, with Pochettino expected to receive funds to bolster his squad with new signings.
Several USMNT internationals, including striker Folarin Balogun, midfielder Weston McKennie, and left-back Antonee Robinson, have been linked with a move to follow Pochettino, who declined to rule out potential reunions at Old Trafford. "Now it's not time [to speak about it]," the Argentine began via The Mirror.
"I know that you asked the players before. All good players always have dreams. To reach the last level, and I see United is one of the teams that is in the last level... it's a dream for many of the players. But now I see they need to be focusing in today, in tomorrow's game, in their day clubs.
"I know really well for more than two years every single player, and I see they are all players with an unbelievable talent. And of course, in the future, when that happens, we are going to pay attention."
McKennie was also asked about the possibility of USMNT players joining United, and the 28-year-old replied during a press conference in Cincinnati: "I think it's a potential. I think you know we have a lot of guys on our team that are due to make moves soon or within the next year.
"So I think it's kind of inevitable. But at the end of the day, you never know what's going on in coach's mind and what players he's actually looking for. I think he could potentially see some of the guys from here being talked about to go to Manchester United and potentially it happening."

Away from transfer speculation, Pochettino also disclosed that three members of his coaching staff have already departed the USMNT camp to begin preparations for their new roles in Manchester. His assistant manager, Jesús Pérez, and coach Toni Jiménez bid farewell following Pochettino's side's dominant 6-0 victory over Haiti in their CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final first leg.
Pochettino's son and fitness coach, Sebastiano, also made his exit. United's incoming coaching team have precious little time to settle in before their showdown with Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.
