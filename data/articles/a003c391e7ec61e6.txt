How To Stop Relationship Level Going Down in GTA 6
Conversely, do the opposite to make the relationship level go down: Ignore each other, don’t respond to texts or calls, and ghost your partner in GTA 6.
One thing I would love cleared up concerns Level 4 of Jason and Lucia’s relationship. There are times I’ve gotten it to max level, and despite not doing much wrong or avoiding ‘bad things’ to do, it can pop back down to Level 3?
I’ve seen other people theorize Level 4 is only a temporary boost, but it’s a bit ambiguous. However, these are things you definitely shouldn’t do to keep it at Level 3, and 100% shouldn’t do if you’re at Level 4, if you want to keep it that way:
- Constant time apart.
- Ignore texts and calls from your partner.
- Leave your partner whilst you’re out and about with them.
- Not showing up to see your partner after agreeing to see them.
- Refusing invitations to see each other.
- Staying out all night and not coming home to bed.
