Falcon Finance: Unlocks About 203 Million FF - Jul 2027
Falcon Finance is scheduled to unlock about 203.06 million FF in July 2027. The tokens are allocated to the ecosystem, the foundation, investors and the team. The release is equal to 5.73% of FF's circulating supply.
Once the tokens are unlocked, recipients can sell them. If recipients put some of their newly released FF on the market, those sales could add selling pressure.
