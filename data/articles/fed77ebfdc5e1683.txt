Manchester United return to Champions League action on Wednesday night as they travel to Portugal to take on Sporting CP.
The Red Devils will be looking to put Sunday's heavy 4-0 defeat to fierce rivals Liverpool behind them as Mauricio Pochettino prepares for his next outing in charge.
United have endured a difficult start to life under their new head coach, with just one win in their last 11 matches leaving them languishing in the Premier League's bottom three.
Their European form has offered some encouragement, however, with two victories from their opening four Champions League league-phase fixtures.
Both of those wins came at Old Trafford, against Atletico Madrid and Como, while they have struggled on their travels, losing to both sides away from home.
Sporting, meanwhile, have enjoyed an impressive start to their European campaign, winning three of their four matches so far. Their only defeat came against Austrian side LASK, giving United hope they can return from Lisbon with a positive result.

Sporting CP v Man Utd team news
Manchester United could welcome Luke Shaw back into the squad after the left-back returned to training ahead of Wednesday's trip to Lisbon.
Shaw has missed the last eight matches with a muscular injury but could be in contention to feature against Sporting.
