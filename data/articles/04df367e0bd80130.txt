TITLE: Man Utd news: Could Pochettino drop Lammens?
OUTLET: BBC

- Published
Former Manchester United winger Ashley Young said Senne Lammens may be one mistake from being dropped.
Lammens was at fault for Sporting's second goal on Wednesday, when he dropped the ball into the path of striker Luis Suarez, who scored with an overhead kick.
The goalkeeper's performance was not good and errors are beginning to mount, and it is not what new manager Mauricio Pochettino needs right now.
United showed faith in the Belgium international this summer by bringing in 36-year-old Karl Darlow as his understudy.
Darlow did end last season as first choice at Leeds United but started just 30 league games in his three seasons with the Elland Road club. Tom Heaton was also on the bench against Sporting, but the 40-year-old has not played in a senior game since February 2023.
Could Pochettino contemplate leaving Lammens out?
"This type of thing happens," said the Argentine. "That is football. Football is about making mistakes.
"I always want to support my player. I think we need to talk about responsibility. In this type of action our defensive line needs to cover the keeper better because it was very difficult.
"It's not to blame the defensive line, but it's two things that we need to improve - details that we need to work on. We need to manage his type of situation better."
