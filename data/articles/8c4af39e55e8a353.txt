TITLE: GTA 6 Mission Music: Songs, Cutscenes and How to Identify Them
OUTLET: RouteNote

Heard a brilliant song during a GTA 6 mission but cannot find it on a radio-station tracklist? The game has several different layers of music, and recognising which one you heard makes the search much easier.
GTA 6 combines licensed recordings, songs created for its companion album and an original adaptive score composed by Justice. A piece of music heard during a mission is not automatically a radio song.
What music plays during GTA 6 missions?
Mission audio can include a licensed track selected for a particular scene, music playing naturally from a car or venue, or instrumental score that responds to the action. Rockstar’s official credits identify the music and artists, but a credit does not always tell you precisely which scene uses a recording.
For that reason, a credible scene-by-scene soundtrack guide needs verified gameplay timestamps as well as the music credits. Claiming to have identified every mission song from a list of artists alone risks putting tracks in the wrong scenes.
How to identify a song from a GTA 6 cutscene
Start with the mission name, the point in the story and any memorable vocal line. Next, check whether the song has recognisable lyrics or is an instrumental cue. Search the official GTA VI credits for the artist or title, then compare the recording with your captured gameplay.
If the music is instrumental and follows the rhythm of combat, driving or dramatic changes, it may be part of Justice’s original score. The score is scheduled for a separate soundtrack release on 11 December 2026.
Is the GTA 6 theme song part of the score?
Yes. Justice’s ‘Starting from Zero’, featuring Michael McDonald, is credited in the game’s score material and is closely associated with its opening. The duo also created ‘Flower in a Hurricane’ with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard.
Those compositions sit alongside hundreds of licensed songs in the game’s wider musical world. Our GTA 6 theme song explanation covers the difference between score and soundtrack in more detail.
What about songs heard in trailers?
Trailer music should be catalogued separately from confirmed in-game mission placements. A song can appear in promotional footage without playing in the same form or scene during the finished game. The extended gameplay presentation included material from artists such as Birdman, Depeche Mode and Phil Collins, but the soundtrack context matters.
The best place to start
If you heard the music while driving, search the relevant station first. If you heard it in a mission or cutscene, use official credits and a gameplay timestamp. And if you want the original compositions, see the Grand Theft Auto VI album and score overview.
