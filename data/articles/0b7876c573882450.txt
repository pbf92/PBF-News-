TITLE: Man Utd news: Mauricio Pochettino's to-do list includes fixing leaky defence
OUTLET: BBC

- Published
Mauricio Pochettino has used his first interview as Manchester United head coach to outline his vision for the club.
There is a clear non-negotiable.
"To create the culture is the most important [thing]," he told United's in-house media in a 15-minute introductory chat.
One of the most immediate tasks for Pochettino will be to alter the mindset of players who seem to have become restricted by the pressure they have found themselves subjected to.
That said, there are some glaring issues that Michael Carrick was unable to fix and Pochettino must find an answer to. The most obvious is defence.
Who should the cornerstones of United's defence be?
The Carrick regime felt Leny Yoro was the player with the attributes - pace, strength and control - most suited to their needs. Yet, at 21, Yoro is prone to mistakes and he has also been needed at right-back because regular right-backs Diogo Dalot and Noussair Mazraoui have, at times, been required on the left.
Lisandro Martinez and Matthijs de Ligt have not been reliable fitness-wise. Pochettino knows the baggage attached to Martinez, given his post-match criticisms of the club hierarchy after the 2-0 defeat by Atletico Madrid, and the knowledge his fellow countryman's contract expires in the summer.
Harry Maguire has started all 10 league games this season but suffered a back spasm in the 4-4 draw with Aston Villa immediately before the international break and had to be replaced at half-time. Maguire has many attributes, but his presence does restrict the ability to press high up the field.
Ayden Heaven has made eight appearances but has previously been regarded as being behind Yoro in terms of his overall development.
Then there is Dalot.
The 27-year-old was consoled by Joshua Zirkzee when he was replaced against Villa, such was the negative fan reaction to his performance, which included conceding a penalty at the start of the second half.
It can justifiably be argued if Dalot has the talent to match the 260 club appearances he has now made.
However, for his faults, coaches love his attitude.
