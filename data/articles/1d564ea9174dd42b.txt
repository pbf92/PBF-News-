TITLE: GTA 6 confirms what happened to Michael and Jimmy after GTA 5 in nostalgic easter egg
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Grand Theft Auto VI is packed to the rafters with easter eggs and secrets - and while it feels as if players have already discovered hundreds of examples, we’ll probably still be finding more five years from now.
You can be taken to Hell to fight Satan, dropped into a battle royale that’s equal parts The Hunger Games and Saw, or stumble across Mount Chiliad 2.0. There’s no shortage of surprises in Leonida.
This latest find, however, is a tad more wholesome than some of those examples we’ve listed above, because GTA VI also confirms what happened to Michael and Jimmy following the events of Grand Theft Auto V.
GTA V can end a number of different ways, but Michael’s canon ending sees him leave behind his life of crime in pursuit of a career in the Vinewood film industry as a producer.

As for Jimmy, well, his canon ending also sees him survive and eventually move out of his parents house, much to Michael’s relief, we’re sure.
Jimmy quite prominently features within GTA VI - more on that later - but it’s a team-up between the duo that’s really made us nostalgic.
Father and son duo Michael and Jimmy go into business together in GTA VI
As shared by DiscussingFilm and found by X (formerly Twitter) user CaptainGlack, Michael and Jimmy went into production together on a film following the events of GTA V.
Across Vice City in GTA VI, you should be able to find film posters for ‘Righteous Slaughter Overkill’.
CaptainGlack was able to zoom in to see that the film credits both Michael De Santa and Jimmy De Santa, although it’s not clear whether they’re producers, directors, or something else.
Even still, it’s nice to see proof that Michael achieved his dream and even turned it into a family business.
If ‘Righteous Slaughter Overkill’ is ringing a bell, that’s because it appears across the Grand Theft Auto franchise a couple of times.
It’s actually GTA’s spoof version of Call of Duty. You can find characters wearing ‘Slaughter Hood Ops’ t-shirts in GTA VI, while in GTA V, Michael can watch Jimmy play ‘Righteous Slaughter 7’.
It’s pretty funny to think then that Jimmy showed his now-producer dad his favourite video game and got it turned into a major Hollywood - or Vinewood - blockbuster.

How to find Jimmy De Santa in GTA VI
It’s at this point that we’ll offer a spoiler warning in case you want to discover GTA VI’s secrets and cameos at your own pace. You’ve been warned.
Jimmy actually shows up in GTA VI as a “crypto influencer” and self-declared “nepo baby”.
You’ll find him at the Jack of Hearts club in Vice City with Danny Temberelli returning to voice the character.
It’s a very brief interaction but it’s still a blast to run into this returning familiar face.
