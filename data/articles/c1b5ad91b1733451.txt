The broader NASDAQ indices open higher but move into negative territory. Nvidia also gives early gains after earnings beat
Summary
- What technical levels are in play for the NASDAQ composite, the NASDAQ 100 and shares of Nvidia after their earnings beat.
After the close yesterday,Nvidia announce earnings and they topped expectations on both earnings and revenue in its fiscal third quarter. Revenue more than doubled from a year ago, led by the Data Center business, and the company began shipping its next-generation Vera Rubin NVL72 systems. investor.nvidia.com
- Adjusted EPS came in at $2.68 vs. $2.48 expected (BEAT).
- Revenue came in at $115.8 billion vs. $109.0 billion expected (BEAT), up 20% from the previous quarter and 103% from a year ago.
- Data Center revenue reached $107.6 billion, up 110% from a year ago. investor.nvidia.com Consensus estimates
Fourth-quarter sales guidance tops estimates, but margins are expected to narrow
Nvidia expects fourth-quarter revenue of $130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%, vs. $125.3 billion expected. The quarter includes an extra week, and the outlook assumes zero Data Center compute revenue from China. Nvidia sees adjusted gross margin easing to 71%–72% from 74.9% in the third quarter. investor.nvidia.com Guidance consensus
Nvidia gives up early gains as the Nasdaq indices find support
Nvidia shares initially moved higher after the earnings beat and held those gains in premarket trading. That changed after the US open. The stock gave up its early gains and moved into negative territory.
The Nasdaq Composite and Nasdaq 100 followed a similar path, opening higher before reversing lower. Both indices tested key technical targets at their session lows and found willing buyers. That bounce took both indices back into positive territory. The question now is whether buyers can keep the price above those levels on a retest.
In the video above, I look at the key levels in the Nasdaq Composite, the Nasdaq 100 and Nvidia. In a volatile market, those levels tell traders where buyers and sellers are making a stand. They also give traders a level to lean against to keep risk close and a roadmap for the next targets if those levels break.
