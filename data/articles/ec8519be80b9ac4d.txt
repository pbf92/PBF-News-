Glassnode: Bitcoin Continues to Find Buying Support After Retesting June Low
Bitcoin is still holding above this year's summer low, Glassnode said. According to Odaily, the August breakout has been fully retraced, bringing the price back near the June low. Although Bitcoin briefly dipped below that level twice, every daily candle closed above it, indicating buyers continued to defend the key support.
