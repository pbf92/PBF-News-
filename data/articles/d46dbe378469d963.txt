Start the Conversations Early: Opening Networking Blitz at The Folly
The business conversations do not have to wait until the conference doors open. Tomorrow, on 23 November, the Opening Networking Blitz at The Folly brings confirmed Finance Magnates Summit London 2026 attendees together ahead of the main event.

A successful Summit often begins before the main programme starts. The Opening Networking Blitz on 23 November gives confirmed attendees a dedicated opportunity to reconnect with existing contacts and meet new people ahead of the conference and exhibition at Old Billingsgate the following day.
Hosted at The Folly in London, the Blitz creates a different setting from the busy Summit floor. Conversations can begin without competing directly with the conference sessions or booth visits, giving attendees more flexibility to introduce themselves, discuss priorities and identify people they would like to meet again on 24 November.
For professionals travelling to London, the early start can help make the entire visit more productive. Arriving with a few introductions already made can bring greater focus to the main Summit day. Access to the Opening Networking Blitz is included for confirmed Summit registrants; the separate VIP Pre-Blitz Reception operates by invitation and confirmation only.
