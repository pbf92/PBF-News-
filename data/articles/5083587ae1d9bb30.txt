Has Man United team for Liverpool been leaked? What secret drone footage captured
Footage of Manchester United's first session with Mauricio Pochettino has been shared on YouTube
Manchester United's trip to Liverpool on Sunday looks tough enough without the possibility of sensitive team information being leaked by a drone operator. Mauricio Pochettino's maiden training session as United boss has been slightly overshadowed after clandestine footage, gleaned from a drone, was shared by a channel called 'CC OVERVIEW'.
For a pesky YouTuber chasing their five minutes of fame (well, 12 minutes in this case) to secure such access is an unwanted embarrassment for the club amid the largely positive vibes of Pochettino's first day with the players.
But more importantly, what did it actually tell us about Pochettino's team? The answer is not particularly much.
Ten outfield players were seen working with Pochettino, but it's hard to see them all starting. Of the group, only Harry Maguire, Kobbie Mainoo, Amad Diallo, Matheus Cunha and Bryan Mbeumo are regular starters.
Ayden Heaven and Andrey Santos have been in and out of the side, while Joshua Zirkzee and Harry Amass haven't started a Premier League match so far this season.
As much as Pochettino likes to give youth a chance, Sunday at Anfield probably isn't the time or place. Experienced heads will be needed.

Elsewhere at Carrington, captain Bruno Fernandes could be seen playing a game of rondos, which sees two players in the middle trying to intercept the passes of the five players in a circle. Shea Lacey, who could have a role to play at Anfield, Dan Armer and Bendito Mantato were also part of the game.
Another group was made up of Lisandro Martinez, Matthijs de Ligt, Youri Tielemans, Mason Mount, Leny Yoro, Noussair Mazraoui and Diogo Dalot. You can't make out all the players in the clip, but the club has confirmed Patrick Dorgu played a part, encouraging news after he was ruled out of Denmark duty with a knock.
There was some shape work, but nothing that's going to blow the lid off Pochettino's plans for Sunday. Besides, you don't need to be James Bond to know his preferred formation. Pochettino has been a 4-2-3-1 or a 4-3-3 man during his career.
And most United fans would agree that the problem this season hasn't been down to formation or necessarily tactics. It's been more about the players' form and/or attitude.
That said, United will presumably take action to ensure other drone-flying chancers don't get near Carrington in the future. The operator defended their actions, telling the Mirror: "[My intention was] just to capture Pochettino in his first training session.
"It was around 30 minutes into the session, so presumed it would not reveal any tactics or hints at team selection and just wanted to give United fans that first look.
"I could have stayed longer. I wasn't on United's property and at no point flew over it but had captured all I had intended to so wrapped up filming."
