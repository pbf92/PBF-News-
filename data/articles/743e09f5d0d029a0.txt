The AI selloff has a fresh leg this morning, and it runs straight through NVIDIA (NASDAQ:NVDA | NVDA Price Prediction). As of 8:20 a.m. ET, the stock is down about 8% in morning trading to around $142. That adds to a 30% drop over the past month, even though the company just posted a record quarter. Technology is the weakest sector before the bell, so the Nasdaq is set to fall while the Dow points higher.
Pre-Market Snapshot: Tech Sinks as the Dow Pushes Higher
The VIX closed Monday down from 36[1] the previous Friday. It is still in the high range, sitting at the 95th[1] percentile of the past year.
NVIDIA’s Blowout Quarter Is Losing Ground to AI Doubts
NVIDIA’s latest quarter showed demand still climbing. Revenue came in at $116 billion[2], up 103% from a year earlier, and management forecast to $130 billion for the next quarter. The worries come from elsewhere. That guidance assumes no data center revenue from China, and the filing flags an ongoing independent board investigation. The BBC reported overnight that the U.S. attorney general dodged questions about the Nvidia investigation.
NVIDIA is the largest stock in the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq. That weight is why the two indices are pointing lower and the Dow and Russell 2000 are not. Money is moving into defensive sectors. Health care is up about 3% and consumer staples about 2%, while technology is down about 3%. The selling has reached China too. Data center operator GDS Holdings (NASDAQ:GDS) is down 40% over the past month.
Deere and China ADRs Move on Fresh Earnings
Deere (NYSE:DE) is down about 3% after earnings of $3.89[3] per share missed the $4.05 consensus. Its chief executive quoted “early signs of improvement in large agriculture, led by North America.”[3]
Li Auto (NASDAQ:LI) is up about 7% after gross margin recovered to 17%[4] from 11% the prior quarter. Kanzhun (NASDAQ:BZ) is up about 5%.
Falling Yields Have Not Rescued Growth Stocks
The 10-year Treasury yield eased to 4.9% from 5.1% a week earlier, but tech stocks kept falling anyway. If you own an S&P 500 index fund, a single chipmaker is setting your daily results. Take a look at how much AI exposure your portfolio holds across all of your funds, and look past the chipmakers to the power, cooling, and networking names behind (we covered seven of them in a free AI infrastructure report).
Next up are the PCE inflation data and the Federal Reserve release at 2 p.m. ET. Traders are preparing for a December Fed hike. A hotter inflation reading would make that case firmer.
