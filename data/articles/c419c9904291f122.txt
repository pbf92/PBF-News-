TITLE: Manchester United and Barcelona Look for Additions
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

Manchester United are expected to be active in the transfer market as new head coach Mauricio Pochettino looks to improve the squad.
Sky Sports reports that Everton defender Jarrad Branthwaite and Fulham's Calvin Bassey are among the center-backs United are monitoring. Fulham left-back Antonee Robinson is another option, while Barcelona's Alejandro Balde could return to consideration if circumstances allow.
United are looking at several positions, including left-back, center-back and forward. Pochettino is expected to have input into recruitment decisions, although the club has not confirmed any specific transfer targets.
McKennie And Bruno Fernandes Also Attract Interest
United have also been linked with Juventus midfielder Weston McKennie. The American has a strong relationship with Pochettino from their time together with the United States men's national team, prompting speculation that the new United manager could pursue a reunion.
Reports from Italy have suggested United could consider an offer in the region of €40 million. However, McKennie recently extended his Juventus contract through 2030 and has publicly indicated that he is happy in Turin. Any potential move remains speculative.
Meanwhile, Bruno Fernandes is reportedly attracting interest from AC Milan and Galatasaray ahead of next summer. According to reporting from Italian journalist Nicolo Schira, both clubs are monitoring the Manchester United captain as a possible free-agent signing. United have offered Fernandes a contract extension through 2029, but an agreement has not yet been reached.
Barcelona Look To Strengthen Their Defense
Barcelona are also preparing for potential defensive additions. According to Mundo Deportivo, the club is considering Inter Milan's Alessandro Bastoni, Borussia Dortmund's Nico Schlotterbeck and Manchester United's Lisandro Martinez as possible center-back targets.
Barcelona would prefer an experienced, left-footed defender, although financial constraints may affect which player they pursue.
The club is also reportedly interested in Brighton defender Luka Vuskovic. GOAL reports that Barcelona has renewed contact over the 19-year-old Croatian, who has also attracted attention from Real Madrid and Liverpool. Brighton, however, are in a strong position after signing him to a long-term contract.
With January approaching, several major clubs are already evaluating their options. Forbs is among the emerging names attracting attention, and his latest performance for Portugal has given Premier League scouts another reason to keep watching.
