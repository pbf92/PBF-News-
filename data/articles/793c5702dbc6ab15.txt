Primo Brands 10% Owner Sold Shares Worth Over $614M
Tony W. Lee, a 10% Owner and director of Primo Brands Corp, reported selling 26,366,806 shares of Class A Common Stock on November 18, 2026, at $23.32 per share, for a total sale amount of $614,873,916. The shares were held by Triton Water Equity Holdings, LP and Triton Water Forward Holdings, LP. Lee and Scott Spielvogel share voting and investment discretion over the shares through their roles as managing members of ORCP III DE TopCo GP, LLC. Following the sale, Lee indirectly beneficially owned 69,433,660 shares: 50,839,931 held by Triton Water Equity Holdings, LP and 18,593,729 held by Triton Water Forward Holdings, LP. Lee disclaimed beneficial ownership except to the extent of his pecuniary interest.
