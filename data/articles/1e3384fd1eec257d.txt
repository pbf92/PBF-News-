Katahdin Bankshares Corp. (KTHN) Raises Dividend 36.3% Year Over Year
Katahdin Bankshares Corp. declared a $0.276-per-share cash dividend for Q4 2026, payable Dec. 22 to shareholders of record Dec. 15. The dividend is up 36.3% from Q4 2025 and 20% from last quarter.
