TITLE: SalesCloser Launches Live AI Video Sales Agent for Shopify Stores
OUTLET: The Manila Times

App puts a lifelike AI avatar on a merchant’s online storefront that shoppers can see, talk to or chat with to find products, add them to the cart and check out
Extends SalesCloser’s live AI sales conversations from sales calls into e-commerce, a new market reachable through a self-serve, no-code app
Vancouver, British Columbia, Nov. 17, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- SalesCloser Technologies Ltd. ("SalesCloser” or the "Company”) (TSXV: SCAI) (OTCQB: SCTLF) (FSE: MJ5), a pioneer in autonomous AI sales technology, today introduced "SalesCloser: AI Chat & Video”, an app for Shopify merchants that places a lifelike AI sales agent on a merchant’s online store. Shoppers can see the agent as a video avatar, talk to it by live voice or chat with it by text, and switch between the three in one conversation, at any hour. The app is available today on the Shopify App Store.
The agent is designed to work the way an in-store sales associate does. It greets shoppers, answers their questions, recommends products from the store’s live catalog with product cards showing price and stock, adds items to the shopper’s cart and takes them to checkout.
The app runs on both SalesCloser’s own avatar technology and its patent-pending approach to carrying a single AI conversation across text chat, voice and avatar channels, as previously announced on August 26, 2026. SalesCloser believes it is one of a limited number of apps on the Shopify App Store that give shoppers a live video avatar sales agent on the storefront. According to Shopify Inc.’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025, Shopify’s platform facilitated gross merchandise volume of US$378.4 billion in 2025, an increase of 29% from 2024, and millions of merchants from more than 175 countries were using the platform as of December 31, 2025.
Merchants get up and running from their Shopify dashboard, with no code. The agent carries the store’s own brand, with a stock avatar or the merchant’s own face and voice, and store staff can step in live at any moment. Plans are billed through Shopify.
"In the past, when you visited an e-commerce website, you saw live chat. Now shoppers can see, talk to or chat with an AI sales agent instead,” said Ali Tajskandar, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of SalesCloser. "It is built to sell. Through the Shopify App Store, millions of merchants can find and install our agent without a sales call, and e-commerce becomes a new market for the platform we have built.”

About SalesCloser
SalesCloser.ai is a Vancouver-based AI software company focused on automating and scaling revenue generation through conversational AI. The Company’s platform enables businesses to deploy AI-powered virtual sales agents that engage prospects and customers across the sales lifecycle. SalesCloser’s agents conduct real-time, personalized interactions across voice, video, and digital channels, including lead qualification, product demonstrations, follow-ups, and meeting scheduling. By augmenting core sales functions, the platform is designed to help organizations increase capacity, accelerate pipeline velocity, and improve conversion rates without a corresponding increase in headcount. The platform integrates with existing CRM and business systems, supports multilingual deployment, and is intended to deliver consistent customer interactions across industries. SalesCloser operates under a subscription-based SaaS model, generating recurring revenue, while continuing to develop its AI capabilities. The Company’s technology is supported by a growing portfolio of patents and patent applications focused on improving the performance of AI-driven conversational workflows. SalesCloser.ai is listed on the TSX Venture Exchange under the ticker "SCAI”, on the OTCQB Venture Market under the ticker "SCTLF”, and on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange under the ticker "MJ5”.
