Aptera Motors Sets Date and Format for 2026 Annual Meeting
Less than 1 min read
Aptera Motors will hold its 2026 Annual Meeting on December 9, with an in-person voting session and a separate online session.
Key Highlights:
- The business session starts at 1:00 p.m. Pacific in Carlsbad and is limited to eligible Class A holders of record as of November 20.
- Eligible Class A holders may vote only in person by written ballot; no proxies or remote access will be available.
- The general session begins online at approximately 1:30 p.m. Pacific and is open to Class A and non-voting Class B holders.
- Stockholders may email questions by 5:00 p.m. Pacific on December 4; live questions will not be taken.
Original SEC Filing: Aptera Motors Corp [ SEV ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
