American Atomics (NUKE) Plans Private Placement for Up to C$1.05M
American Atomics plans a non-brokered private placement of 10M to 15M units at C$0.07 each, for gross proceeds of up to C$1.05M. It may also settle up to C$200K of debt through units on the same terms.
