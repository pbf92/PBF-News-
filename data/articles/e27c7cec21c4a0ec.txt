GTA 6 Chapter 1's Ending Has A Major Twist You Need To Watch Out For
Contains spoilers for "GTA 6"
After waiting years for the release, gamers are now tearing through the "Grand Theft Auto VI" story at full speed. They're fixing up projects at Jason's house and building up a nest egg with a combination of drug trafficking and petty crime. There are unlimited activities to do in "GTA 6," but players who are moving at warp speed through the early game should be aware of a big twist that comes at the end of Chapter 1.
Without getting into spoilers, we'll just say that the ending of Chapter 1 causes players to lose their house, cars, and all of their money. The beginning of the story sets up Jason's connection to Brian Heder and his relationship with Lucia, while it also introduces several important side characters and mechanics that players will be engaging with throughout the game. The acts you take matter, but the "GTA 6" Chapter 1 ending will act as a soft reset and it sends Jason and Lucia on a new, unexpected path. Read on if you want to know what causes the dramatic loss at the end of "GTA 6" Chapter 1 and to find out what bits of player progression survive the purge.
What happened at the end of GTA 6 Chapter 1?
Jason and Lucia are constantly trying to avoid the police and the dreaded six-star wanted level in "GTA 6," but at the end of Chapter 1, their luck runs out. In the middle of an important and potentially lucrative job, our leads get arrested by undercover cops and pulled into a makeshift interrogation room. While Jason and Lucia are being questioned, Officer Aldo Méndez enters the room, and it immediately becomes apparent he's on a different wavelength than the other cops.
Méndez kills the detective interrogating Jason and Lucia, and tries to kill them. In the process of making their escape, the duo also gets out with a bag containing an encrypted hard drive that Méndez badly wants. Thugs show up at Jason's house and burn the place down, and then Méndez and his loyalists frame the two of them for the murder he committed. Because of that, Jason and Lucia are broke, on the run, and wanted by people on both sides of the law.
How to get your stuff back after the GTA 6 Chapter 1 ending
The ending of "GTA 6" Chapter 1 acts like a soft reset. Players lose their home, cars, and money, but they don't lose everything. The actions players take throughout Chapter 1 still matter, and parts of their progress carries over into Chapter 2. The "GTA 6" criminal profile system has a constant effect on the game, so Jason and Lucia will keep the profiles player the helped them develop in Chapter 1. The leads will maintain whatever relationship status they've achieved by the end of the first act of the game, too, so all the early game chores and flirting still matters.
Players wanting to know how to get their stuff back after the "GTA 6" Chapter 1 ending get a small piece of good news. Some things remain at the charred remnants of Jason's house. Players who go back will find that Jason's beloved pet snake survived the blaze, and they'll be able to dig Jason's metal stash box out of the ashes to regain a small amount of cash. Since players can also keep their side quest and collectibles progressions, the "GTA 6" Chapter 1 ending isn't a total loss.
