TITLE: GTA 6 fans list every 'legitimate' complaint as GOTY gets reviewbombed
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

Critics have cemented Grand Theft Auto VI as the greatest game of all time, matching the 99/100 record set by The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time in 1998.
The reviews have, in our opinion, crowned it as the best media product in the last 30 years, as Rockstar Games' masterpiece has sold unfathomable numbers around the globe.
In fact, GTA VI has even devoured Avengers: Endgame as the best-selling piece of entertainment, earning $3.79billion in sales on the opening day alone (over $850million more than Endgame's entire Box Office run).
Yet, that 99/100 score opens the door to the one percent club who have some gripes.

And, of course, the vocal minority have found their voice.
Despite the record sales, record scores, and record views on Twitch, GTA VI has been review-bombed in the user scores, dipping as low as 3.7/10 before inching back up to a current rating of 5.5.
While many of these outrageous reviews stem from questionable takes on Lucia and Jason's dynamic, others have pointed out legitimate reasons to dislike GTA VI.
The reasons why GTA 6 isn't winning over the final 1% of gamers
There's no denying that GTA VI has tried to be slower than its previous instalments, putting less emphasis on the mainline heisting and allowing you to really find your place within the Miami-esque city.
However, this pacing, especially during the opening 15 hours of gameplay, has begun to irk players.
Combined with a string of new mechanics, such as the altered weapon system, there is enough for some players to take a slight disliking to the sixth instalment of a franchise known for its unreasonable chaos.
This was epitomised by a reasonable review posted on social media, which steers clear of any misogynistic rants and instead focused on the gameplay.
"Why am I limited to two guns? Why am I tethered to an El Camino where I keep all my shit like it's my horse from RDR2? The game is literally called Grand Theft Auto and I can't change my main vehicle," one user wrote on X.

"Why can't I return to my literal safe house when I have a wanted level? Why can't I "own" a weapon that I steal from a dead guy? The series used to be total freedom and chaos with very minimal realism.
"Now it's like a playable movie about being lower class and it's constantly trying to make you do regular boring adult shit: fix your leaky roof, lift weights, eat a burrito, open a bank account, get a haircut, take your girlfriend on a walk so she can talk your ear off.
"It's has become like The Sims but with firearms. Visually the game is gorgeous and the city feels very alive. It is a technical marvel. NPC interaction is great, But it also is trying too hard to be a life simulator."

The post has earned more than 50,000 likes of support, showing that certain mechanical changes to GTA VI have made a negative impact on player experience.
Of course, the more we play GTA VI, the more options we'll get to forgo the simulations and truly marvel in a lawless paradise.
But so far, the world-building and weapon usage has left small margins for improvement.
