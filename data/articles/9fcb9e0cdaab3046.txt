TITLE: Christian woman takes prayer to the front line of violent Paris protests
OUTLET: Premier Christian News

A Christian woman has been taking her faith to the front line of protests in Paris, praying for police officers, protesters and the protection of the city amid weeks of unrest.
Tens of thousands of students and union members marched through the centre of Paris on Saturday, the latest demonstration in two months of unrest.
Ahead of the masses of protesters, the line of police and the journalists, a petite woman led the way with arms outstretched.
Speaking with Premier Christian News on Saturday, Suzana Esparon said she was there to pray for those attending.
“I've prayed so that the police officers are protected, I've prayed so that the homes are protected.”
As she walked ahead of the march, the Brazilian native issued blessings to all she passed, police officers, journalists, and curious civilians come to watch.
A small sticker on her coat marked out her religion; in French: ‘Jesus loves you. John 3:16.’
A line of riot police dictated the pace of the protest throughout the afternoon, halting at regular intervals to keep the protesters at a crawling pace.
During one stop, a beer bottle was thrown from an unseen hand within the crowd, shattering on the bitumen just beyond the officers. A command was barked, and the police donned their helmets.
But Esparon said her fears were for the violence later in the evening.
“I come to these demonstrations to pray for the police officers, to talk with the young people, and to tell them that it's not through violence that we'll get there, but that Jesus Christ is the only, unique one who can save us, who can change our current situation, who can transform us, who can transform this nation.”
The protests began in September with students demanding better schooling conditions, but quickly expanded to those demanding relief from cost of living pressures.
Esparon says the violence comes from groups who aren’t there to protest peacefully or make demands of the government. Through murmured prayers she wishes for divine protection over police and buildings targeted during the unrest.
“If we Christians do our part, the darkness retreats. So that's our goal, our calling, to be the light in the midst of darkness.”
