Anthropic IPO Price May Reach $85, Deal Size Poised to Surpass SpaceX Factoring in Private Placement
Anthropic is considering raising its IPO price range to $80 to $85 per share ahead of its scheduled Nasdaq listing on November 19, Eastern Time, driven by robust institutional and retail demand. Combined public and private placement proceeds could reach $96.1 billion, surpassing SpaceX's total transaction size. The valuation is underpinned by explosive revenue growth, with nine-month revenue surging 1,200% year-over-year to $33.24 billion. As the first frontier AI model developer to go public, Anthropic's higher pricing tests the valuation premium public investors are willing to pay for leading AI growth prospects.
TradingKey - Anthropic, the developer of Claude, is considering pricing its IPO at $80 to $85 per share, above its previous range of $70 to $78, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter. Even without increasing the number of shares offered, at $85 per share, the combined size of the public offering and concurrent private placement would reach approximately $96.1 billion. The final pricing has not yet been set, and the company is expected to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19, Eastern Time, under the ticker symbol ANT.
Strong subscription demand provided room for the price hike. According to earlier reporting by CNBC, when Anthropic closed its order books, institutional investor demand totaled approximately $400 billion, with retail demand approaching $100 billion.
According to Anthropic's prospectus, the base public offering totals 825 million shares, of which approximately 801 million are newly issued by the company and the remaining approximately 23.6 million are being sold by existing shareholders. Assuming the number of shares offered remains unchanged, based on a price range of $80 to $85 per share, the public offering size would be approximately $66 billion to $70.1 billion. Proceeds from the sale of secondary shares will go to the selling shareholders and will not be included in the company's proceeds.
In addition, investors including Nvidia, AMD, Google, and Microsoft have agreed to participate in concurrent private placements totaling $26 billion at the IPO offering price. Combined, the total transaction size would be approximately $92 billion to $96.1 billion, surpassing SpaceX's offering size of approximately $86.2 billion after accounting for the over-allotment option.
However, comparing only the base public offerings yields the exact opposite conclusion: even if priced at $85, Anthropic's offering size of approximately $70.1 billion remains below SpaceX's $75 billion. Therefore, what Anthropic is poised to surpass SpaceX in is the total transaction value of the public offering plus concurrent private placements, rather than setting a new record for a standalone public offering.
The basis for Anthropic's ability to attract capital is revenue growth driven by Claude, particularly its coding tool Claude Code. In the nine months through the end of September, the company's revenue reached $33.24 billion, up approximately 1,200% year-over-year. By the end of October, annualized revenue extrapolated from recent usage and subscription fees was approximately $81 billion, an increase of $10 billion from the end of September. This metric reflects the pace of expansion, rather than realized full-year revenue.
If it goes public as planned, Anthropic will become the first frontier AI model developer to list, facing public market pricing ahead of rival OpenAI. For retail investors, what is more immediate than fundraising records is the purchase price: if ultimately set at $85, the per-share price will be approximately 9% higher than the original offering range ceiling of $78. Investors are buying into the same company's growth prospects, but the price they pay for that growth is already different.
