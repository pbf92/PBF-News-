EnergyVision Raises 2027 EBITDA Growth Guidance to at Least 70%
EnergyVision agreed to acquire OCTA+'s electricity and gas activities, adding approximately 130,000 supply points. It raised 2027 underlying EBITDA growth guidance to at least 70% from at least 40% previously and targets at least €90M underlying EBITDA.
