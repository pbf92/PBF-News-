TITLE: Drone footage of Pochettino's first Man Utd training session leaked online

Drone footage of Pochettino's first Man Utd training session leaked online
Photo source: Sky Sports
The 12-minute clip from Pochettino's first Manchester United training session today shows tactical drills ahead of Sunday's match against Liverpool at Anfield.
Footage of Mauricio Pochettino's first Manchester United training session today was leaked online in a major security breach. The 12-minute drone video, filmed over the club's Carrington training ground, was uploaded to YouTube.
This is reported by Sky Sports.
It shows Pochettino putting his new team through tactical drills as they prepare to play Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday. The training session was closed and not open to the media.
Signs around Carrington say drone use is "strictly prohibited" and that anyone caught will "be reported for prosecution and subject to civil action". The uploaders, CC Overview, claimed they had not broken any laws. In the video, they wrote:
For the people asking about legality. It is unrestricted airspace, meaning anyone can fly over it, the drone remained in line of sight at all times, it didn't fly over anyone or even near anyone so was a perfectly legal flight going by the CAA rules that regulates drone usage.
- CC Overview
The drone is correctly labelled and I hold all the relevant licences, public liability and insurance required.
- CC Overview
As for leaking team news/ tactics, it was half an hour into the manager's first training session, there are many media there so certainly won't be the only footage of training released.
- CC Overview
Manchester United declined to comment when contacted by Sky Sports News.
