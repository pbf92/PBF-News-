TITLE: We Got GTA 6 Online Before GTA 6 Online
OUTLET: TechPowerUp

Friday, November 27th 2026
Progress on GTA 6 PC ports is being made at an astonishing pace, mainly thanks to AI models doing most of the grunt work. However, it can't be denied that, even though most of the GTA 6 PC ports currently are "vibe-coded," they are doing something unprecedented in the gaming space. We covered yesterday that some GTA 6 PC recompilation efforts have reached 20 FPS during gameplay, and many others are claiming even higher numbers. Building on that momentum, a developer going by TeK has shown GTA 6 multiplayer running on PC. The short clip was posted by TeK on X and shows two player characters together on an airport tarmac, with a red sports car being driven around by one of them. 
The clip isn't long, but TeK claims the game runs at a stable 25-30 FPS, even though we can't verify that claim directly since there's no framerate counter visible. The clip is running relatively smoothly, but we don't know the hardware behind it, and the scene is mainly just an empty runway. Performance aside, the fact that someone got multiplayer running in a game that isn't even released on PC is quite something. Moreover, the poster claims that proximity chat also works, which gives the multiplayer claim some more credence. PortWatch VI, a community site tracking these GTA 6 PC projects, has independently verified the video's authenticity and also confirmed that proximity chat is indeed working.According to PortWatch, TeK's group is working on a private fork of the CGBJ build, which is built on the open-source VIctory and XCL projects. The code is private for now, so there's no way for anyone else to test it. Of course, this is nowhere near what Rockstar would be planning for GTA Online, but it's a demonstration of how far these fan-made ports have come. I imagine Take-Two Interactive won't be too happy seeing this, and Rockstar will probably be rethinking their PC port and GTA Online plans as well. As always, readers are advised to not download any unofficial "GTA 6 PC ports," as they have been known to hide malware.
