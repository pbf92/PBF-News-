TITLE: Stock market today: Dow and S&P 500 futures slip, Nasdaq futures rise in countdown to Nvidia earnings
OUTLET: AOL.com

US stock futures fell in the lead-up to Nvidia (NVDA) first earnings release without Jensen Huang at the wheel, which carries high stakes for the rest of the AI trade.
Futures on the Dow Jones Industrial Average (YM=F) slipped 0.9%, while those on the S&P 500 (ES=F) fell 0.2%. Contracts for the Nasdaq-100 (NQ=F) rose 0.2% following a rough sell-off that kicked off the week.
The focus is on Nvidia's (NVDA) earnings report after the bell in what will likely be one of the biggest events of the week — eclipsed only by Anthropic's (ANTH.PVT) IPO on Thursday. Nvidia's former CFO and now interim CEO Colette Kress is no stranger to speaking on the earnings call, but she'll do so in a new capacity as the company's leader following Jensen Huang's abrupt resignation last week amid affair allegations.

In other earnings news, Disney (DIS) reported an earnings beat and continued growth in the company's parks and streaming businesses in its fiscal fourth quarter. The Home Depot (HD) also reported an earnings beat and named its CFO Richard McPhail as the company's new CEO starting next week.
Investors were also treated to a bevy of minor economic data. Retail sales rose 0.2% month over month in October, which was above economists’ estimates.
