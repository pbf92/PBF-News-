TITLE: Alto Neuroscience Chief Operating Officer Sold Shares Worth Over $2M
OUTLET: TradingView

Alto Neuroscience Chief Operating Officer Sold Shares Worth Over $2M
Jr. Michael Conick Hanley, Chief Operating Officer, sold 75,000 shares of Alto Neuroscience common stock for a total of $2,070,389 under a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan adopted on August 14, 2026. On November 16, he sold 70,000 shares in three transactions at a weighted average price of $27.68; on November 17, he sold 2,104 shares at $26.816; and on November 18, he sold 2,896 shares at $26.48. He no longer directly owns Alto Neuroscience common stock.
