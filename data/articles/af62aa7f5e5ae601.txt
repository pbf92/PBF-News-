Healthcare Triangle Received Notice of Delisting
On November 24, 2026, Nasdaq's Hearings Panel denied the company's request to remain listed and determined to delist its securities after a failure to meet the $1.00 minimum bid price requirement under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2). Trading is scheduled to be suspended on November 26, 2026; the company intends to seek review within 15 days and expects its stock to be quoted on OTC Pink while pursuing OTCQB quotation.
Original SEC Filing: Healthcare Triangle, Inc. [ HCTI ] - 8-K - Nov. 25, 2026
