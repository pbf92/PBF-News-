TITLE: Pochettino asks for time as he starts Man Utd job
OUTLET: Dave.Sport

Mauricio Pochettino said Manchester United deserve better than their place in the relegation zone when he spoke to TNT Sports US on Tuesday night, minutes after his final match as USA head coach.
Less than a day later he was at Carrington, where he described joining United as “an honour” and “a privilege”. He takes charge of a side 18th in the Premier League and without a league win in eight matches. His first game is at Anfield on Sunday.

What Pochettino told TNT after USA beat Haiti
The USA had just beaten Haiti 2-0 in Cincinnati when Pochettino was asked about the job awaiting him in Manchester. “We know that the situation is not a situation that a club like Manchester United deserves,” he said.
Pochettino said the challenge brought the kind of adrenaline he wanted to feel. Asked how he would approach the job, he told TNT: “We need to bring our common sense for the way that we are going to approach things.” NBC Sports carried his post-match comments.
‘Give me time’ – Pochettino’s reply to DaMarcus Beasley
DaMarcus Beasley, the former USA international who played on loan for Manchester City, told Pochettino on TNT that “Manchester’s blue and always will be blue”.
“No way. You will see. Give me time,” Pochettino replied. “Give me time and that is going to start to change.”
Pochettino also spoke about the United supporters he had met since his appointment. In United’s account of his final night with the USA, he estimated that nine in every 10 people he passed in the street in America supported the club.
Pochettino’s first interview and the build-up to Liverpool
Pochettino flew overnight from Cincinnati to Manchester. By the time he arrived on Wednesday, Jesús Pérez, Toni Jiménez and Sebastiano Pochettino had already led training for the players who had stayed at Carrington during the international break. Read more about Pochettino’s coaching staff.
The players away with their countries are due back on Thursday, when they are expected to meet their new head coach, The Guardian reports. United say his full first club interview will be available on Thursday morning.
