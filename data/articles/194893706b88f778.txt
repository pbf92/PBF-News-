TITLE: Shopify Rebellion sign Spiker and Bae94 from Wildcard
OUTLET: SiegeGG

Shopify Rebellion have announced the signings of Brady "Spiker" Lukens and Jacob "Bae94" Dowling. Both players had been playing together for Wildcard since August 2024, when Bae94 joined the roster before the start of the BLAST R6 North America League 2024.
The now former Wildcard players have signed for Shopify Rebellion to replace Troy "Canadian" Jaroslawski and Richie "Rexen" Coronado. In the last year, Bae had been Wildcard's best player with a SiegeGG rating of 1.10. Meanwhile, Spiker had averaged a SiegeGG rating of 0.99.
Alongside Wildcard, the Americans claimed back-to-back Top 4 finishes at RE:L0:AD and the BLAST R6 Major Munich as well as a Top 6 finish at the Six Invitational 2026. In the first international event of Year 11, both players helped Wildcard to claim a Top 8 finish at the BLAST R6 Major Salt Lake City. Two months later, the Americans won the BLAST R6 North America League 2026 Stage 1 and reached the Rainbow Six Siege Esports World Cup 2026 Top 8.
It's also worth noting that Bae94 had been called to represent the United States of America at the Esports Nations Cup 2026. However, due to the ongoing conflict in the Middle East, the event had to be postponed. The new date is November 2027.
Shopify Rebellion's next appearance will be at the Six Invitational 2027. The additions of Spiker, who will likely become the team's IGL, and Bae94 make this roster extremely dangerous. If they successfully integrate into the roster alongside William "Spoit" Löfstedt, Jaylen "Ambi" Turk, and Damian "Surf" Medina, Shopify Rebellion could be one of the strongest sides at the Six Invitational 2027.
