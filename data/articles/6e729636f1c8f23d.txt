TITLE: How much does it cost to play GTA 6? Here's what you'll need to spend
OUTLET: NBC Connecticut

It’s that time of year again. Kids are probably getting ready to ask Santa for the latest toys, gadgets, and video games.
The adults are probably asking Santa for this one, too: "Grand Theft Auto VI."
It’s been 13 years since Rockstar Games released "Grand Theft Auto V" in 2013. In that time, a lot has changed in the world of gaming, including the impact on our wallets.
So, how much will you have to spend to live the Vice City life?
First, you’ll need the game.
Unlike previous Grand Theft Auto releases, GTA 6 is digital-only, meaning there’s no physical disc to insert into your console.
You can purchase the game through an online retailer or directly through the Xbox or PlayStation digital storefronts.
According to Rockstar Games, GTA 6 comes in two editions:
- Standard Edition: $79.99
- Ultimate Edition: $99.99
Both prices are before tax.
The Standard Edition includes the full game, while the Ultimate Edition comes with additional in-game content, including special vehicles, garages, hairstyles, clothing, exclusive weapons, and two additional side missions.
If you decide to purchase the Standard Edition first, you can upgrade to the Ultimate Edition later for $20. Either way, you’re spending about $100 if you want all the additional content.
But here’s a caveat: even though GTA 6 is digital-only, you can still buy it in person at a store. By doing that, you’ll get a GTA 6 box, a double-sided promotional poster, and a digital redemption code instead of a disc.
There’s even a space inside the box where a disc would normally go…but no disc.
Next, you’ll need a console that can play it.
Already have an Xbox or PlayStation? Great! But that doesn’t necessarily mean you’re ready to play GTA 6.
The game can only be played on the current generation of Xbox and PlayStation consoles.
Four console models can play the game at launch, with prices ranging from about $499 to $899.
Here’s how they compare, from least to most expensive.
- Xbox Series S: $499
- PlayStation 5: $599
- Xbox Series X: $749
- PlayStation 5 Pro: $899
These are general retail prices before sales or discounts, per the Xbox and PlayStation websites. Note: There are different versions of these consoles, including models with disc drives and options with different storage capacities. Those differences can affect how much you’ll pay.
As a general rule of thumb, the more powerful the console, the better the graphics and potential performance. But that extra power comes at a price.
The PlayStation 5 Pro offers the most powerful hardware of the four consoles, while the Xbox Series S is the most affordable option.
If you’re looking to save some money, buying a used or refurbished console could also help bring down the total cost.
Don’t forget about storage.
GTA 6 is a massive game.
According to the game’s listed storage requirements, it takes up approximately 212 gigabytes of space.
For comparison, the consoles mentioned above come with varying storage capacities, generally ranging from 512 gigabytes to 1 terabyte, although some models offer as much as 2 terabytes. The more storage you want, the more you may have to spend.
If you already have a compatible console but don’t have enough available space, you may need to delete other games or invest in additional storage. External storage devices vary in price depending on their capacity and features. On current-generation consoles, not every external drive can run games directly, so shoppers should check compatibility before buying.
But, there’s technically one more console that can play GTA 6: the Xbox One.
First released in 2013, the Xbox One is considerably older than the current-generation consoles. These consoles are no longer sold new, but purchasing used and refurbished models can cost significantly less.
The thing is, you wouldn’t actually be running GTA 6 on the Xbox One itself--you’d be streaming the game through Xbox Cloud Gaming, with the game running on remote servers and the video being streamed to your console. That means your internet connection becomes a major factor. Xbox says connection speeds, network stability, and other technical limitations can affect cloud gaming performance in ways that wouldn’t necessarily affect a game running directly on your console.
So, while cloud streaming could offer a less expensive way to play without purchasing a new console, there are trade-offs.
All in all, on the high end, the total could easily exceed $1,000.
On the low end, buying the game and a new console will cost around $579.
But if you shop for used or refurbished hardware, you could potentially spend considerably less, possibly under $300, depending on the console and the deal you find.
And if you already own a compatible console with enough storage, then all you need is the game.
So, are you ready to start living the Vice City life?
