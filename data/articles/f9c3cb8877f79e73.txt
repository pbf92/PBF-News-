TITLE: GTA 6 obliterates box office records as fans spot impressive details about figures
OUTLET: Indy100

Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar Games' parent company, has confirmed GTA 6 has absolutely smashed entertainment box office records.
The most highly anticipated game of all time finally released on 19 November on PS5 and XBOX Series X/S and fans have been getting deeper into exploring Leonida.

That means GTA 6 is already in the top 30 best selling games of all time and has generated more revenue than whatSpider-Man: Brand New Day, the highest grossing film of 2026, managed in its entire cinema run.
