TITLE: 'People Are Doing It Too Quickly Now' — The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping Director Speaks Out on YA Franchise Reboots
OUTLET: VGC / IGN

I got the chance to sit down with the director of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, Francis Lawrence, who opened up about the amount of remakes happening in Hollywood and how he would feel if The Hunger Games series ever got rebooted.
Francis Lawrence has directed all but one movie in The Hunger Games series. He’s been with the franchise for over a decade; the first film, directed by Gary Ross, released in 2012, after which Lawrence took up the mantle for all subsequent movies.
It’s one of several iconic movie franchises that defined the YA boom of the 2000s - mid-2010s. And unlike other series from this time period, it has neither languished nor suffered a reboot before its corpse is even cold.
HBO’s forthcoming Harry Potter series is perhaps the most egregious example of such a reboot. The last Harry Potter movie released in 2011, and its cast defined an entire generation of fans who grew up watching their adventures at Hogwarts. It seems almost insulting to reimagine the world that was so painstakingly built over the course of a decade — but that’s just my opinion.
I’m not alone in this line of thinking, though. When asked about his feelings on reboots, Lawrence agreed that they’re happening too soon, bringing up Warner Bros.’ thankfully canceled idea for an I Am Legend reboot in 2014, just seven years after the original film was released.
“I mean, I do feel like people are doing it sort of too quickly now,” he mused. “I agree with you on that. People started talking about that with I Am Legend. I don't remember how long ago, but it felt like 10 years had gone by or something, and somebody was thinking of remaking it already at Warner. I'm like, ‘Really? 10 years, that's it?’”
Given the imminent release of HBO’s Harry Potter series on Christmas day, I had to bring up the very real possibility that The Hunger Games sees a reboot in the next decade or so — an idea that Lawrence wasn’t sure would get Suzanne Collins’ blessing.
“I don't know how I would feel about it,” he admitted. “I also don't know if Suzanne has anything in place to sort of protect against that, but that'd be really, really interesting to figure out. But I think it's a tough one. I mean, if Suzanne backed it, I think some spinoffs, and obviously if she writes other books, things like that could be interesting, as long as she backs it and comes up with it and has the thematic reasonings and philosophies that she usually does, that would be cool. But I don't know how you go back and remake the original movies and it not be [Jennifer Lawrence]."
To his point, The Hunger Games franchise has managed to stay in the public consciousness, owing in part to its later release during the YA boom (the final film in the original series came out in 2015) and its two prequel movies that rocked theaters in the 2020s. The latest movie, Sunrise on the Reaping, received solid reviews from critics, earning an 8 out of 10 from IGN.
