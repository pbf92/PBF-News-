TITLE: Manchester United Are Keeping Tabs On This Fulham Defender: Good Choice For Pochettino?
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

In a recent report, journalist Graeme Bailey mentioned that Manchester United are keeping tabs on Fulham defender Calvin Bassey. It has been claimed that the Red Devils are showing interest in recruiting the Nigerian centre-back next year.
Bassey's Impressive Form In The Premier League
Bassey has enjoyed a good run of form at the London club this season as he has put in a series of impressive displays for them on the defensive end of the field. The 26-year-old has made 13 appearances for Fulham in this campaign so far in all competitions.
The Nigerian talent is currently one of the most talented centre-backs in the Premier League. Therefore, it is no wonder that Man United are hoping to land him in 2027.
His current contract at Craven Cottage will expire at the end of this campaign, which could open the door for the Red Devils to sign him on the cheap this January or on a free transfer next summer.

Will Bassey Be A Good Choice For Manchester United Boss Mauricio Pochettino?
Bassey is a good tackler of the ball and can sense the danger well to secure possession back for his team inside his half. He usually puts his foot through the ball when needed and is an accurate passer as well from the back.
Standing at 1.85m, the Nigerian sensation can even use his strong 1.85m frame to dominate the opposition attackers in the air. Bassey is primarily a centre-back but can also serve as a left-back if asked to do so by his manager. He has already proven himself in the Premier League and won't take any time to adjust to life at Man United if they manage to get him on board.
We can expect Bassey to bring more quality and depth to Mauricio Pochettino's attack. He has what it takes to fight for a regular starting spot at Old Trafford.
At 26, Bassey has his peak years ahead of him, which makes him a decent choice for the Mancunian giants to consider this January or at the end of this season. He will also help the Red Devils compete across all fronts in the coming years. Therefore, Pochettino should focus on going all out to sign him in 2027.
