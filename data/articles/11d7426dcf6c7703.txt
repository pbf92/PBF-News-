Palantir Insiders Sold Shares Worth Over $23M
Ryan D. Taylor, Chief Revenue Officer and Chief Legal Officer, sold 47,319 Palantir Technologies Class A common shares under a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan to cover tax withholding obligations related to restricted stock unit vesting. He sold 19,164 shares on November 20, 2026, at a weighted average price of $146.84 per share and 28,155 shares on November 23, 2026, at a weighted average price of $158.10, for total proceeds of $7,265,437. After the transactions, Taylor directly owned 438,556 shares.
David A. Glazer, Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer, sold 40,687 Palantir Technologies Class A common shares under a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan to cover tax withholding obligations on vested restricted stock units. He sold 16,478 shares on November 20, 2026, at a weighted average price of $146.84 per share and 24,209 shares on November 23, 2026, at a weighted average price of $158.10, for total proceeds of $6,247,149. After the transactions, Glazer directly owned 638,671 shares.
Shyam Sankar, Chief Technology Officer and Executive Vice President, sold 62,355 Palantir Technologies Class A common shares under a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan to cover tax withholding obligations upon restricted stock unit vesting. He sold 25,253 shares on November 20, 2026, at a weighted average price of $146.84 per share and 37,102 shares on November 23, 2026, at a weighted average price of $158.10, for total proceeds of $9,574,092. After the transactions, Sankar directly owned 795,261 shares and held 599,899 shares of record as co-trustee of the Sankar Irrevocable Remainder Trust; he disclaimed beneficial ownership of the trust shares except to the extent of his pecuniary interest.
