TITLE: ANT IPO News - Anthropic prices upsized IPO at $83, above the range; raises $81B in the largest US IPO ever
OUTLET: Renaissance Capital

Anthropic PBC, a developer of frontier AI models known for its Claude product line, raised $81.3 billion by offering 980 million shares (2% secondary) at $83, above the $70 to $78 range. The company offered 155 million more shares than anticipated. Certain investors and affiliates had agreed to purchase $26 billion worth of shares in a concurrent private placement, and board members had indicated on $303 million worth of shares in the offering (0.4% of the deal). At pricing, the company commands a fully diluted market value of $1.38 trillion. 
At pricing, Anthropic has officially completed the largest US IPO ever by proceeds raised, beating out the record set by SpaceX (SPCX; $75B) just this past June.
Anthropic PBC develops Claude, a family of frontier AI models with a focus on enterprise deployment and model safety. Its models are offered through a developer platform and apps such as Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and Claude.ai. Most of its revenue is consumption-based usage, with subscriptions adding the rest. As of September 30, 2026, more than 11,900 customers generated at least $100,000 in trailing-12-month revenue, including every Fortune 10 company and more than half of the Fortune 500. On Monday, the company released its latest models, Mythos 5.2 and Fable 5.2.
The San Francisco, CA-based company will trade on the Nasdaq under the symbol ANT. Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, J.P. Morgan, Citi, Barclays, Wells Fargo Securities, BofA Securities, Deutsche Bank, RBC Capital Markets, UBS Investment Bank, BMO Capital Markets, BNP Paribas, Credit Agricole CIB, Jefferies, Mizuho Securities, MUFG Securities America, TD Securities, BTIG, Cantor Fitzgerald, Capital One Securities, Macquarie Capital, and Societe Generale acted as joint bookrunners on the deal.
