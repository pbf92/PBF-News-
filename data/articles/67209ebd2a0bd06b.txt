Interactive Strength Received Notice of Noncompliance
After director Kirsten Bartok Touw resigned effective November 25, 2026, Interactive Strength no longer meets Nasdaq requirements for audit committee composition, compensation committee composition, or a majority-independent board. The company notified Nasdaq on November 27 and intends to use the applicable cure periods, which generally run until the earlier of its next annual meeting or one year after the vacancy, subject to a 180-day period in certain cases; it plans to appoint a new independent director.
Original SEC Filing: Interactive Strength, Inc. [ TRNR ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
