Dear Ed
Since your letter in May 2025, there has been constructive engagement between colleagues in the Office for Statistics Regulation (OSR) and the Office for National Statistics (ONS) regarding the accreditation status of the Producer Price Index (PPI) and Services Producer Price Index (SPPI). Following our recent discussions, we are now in a position to provide an indication of when we believe we will have completed the work required to meet your conditions for re-accrediting PPI and SPPI statistics. We welcome the positive discussions we have had over recent months on this work and the support provided by OSR.
Since accreditation was suspended, ONS has addressed the chain-linking issue that led to the suspension and introduced additional quality assurance controls across production. We resumed publication of producer prices indices in October 2025. We have also made substantial progress against the requirements identified through OSR's Spotlight on Quality review. As a result, we believe the processes supporting these statistics are now significantly stronger than they were when we wrote to you suggesting that the accreditation status be suspended.
We recognise, however, that following the error identified in 2025, OSR expects further action to strengthen resilience and address the remaining vulnerabilities before reaccreditation can be considered. We agree that there is further work to do, particularly in relation to evidencing our quality framework, completing work on sample sustainability, and managing the risks associated with legacy systems and longer-term operational sustainability.
Given the progress already made and the clear timetable for completing the remaining work, our current expectation is that the key elements of this programme will be completed by summer 2027. At that point, we would expect to seek accreditation of PPI and SPPI again through the appropriate process and we would be in a position to provide the evidence needed to demonstrate that the relevant accreditation requirements have been addressed and that appropriate mitigations are in place for any residual risks.
We therefore ask OSR to work with us towards reaccreditation of PPI and SPPI and to develop an efficient process that allows for the OSR to validate our progress and, when satisfied, reaccredit the statistics. We would welcome continued engagement with OSR colleagues as individual elements of the work are concluded, allowing evidence to be shared and discussed progressively and helping ensure that any issues can be identified and addressed at an early stage.
We remain fully committed to seeking accredited official statistics status for these statistics again when the necessary work has been completed, and to maintaining transparency with users and stakeholders throughout this process.
I would like to thank you and your colleagues for your continued support, challenge and engagement. We look forward to continuing to work together over the coming months.
Yours sincerely
Grant Fitzner
