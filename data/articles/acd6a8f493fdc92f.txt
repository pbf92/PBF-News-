TITLE: Blackthorn Publishing Launches Free ADA Website Compliance Audits for Los Angeles Small Businesses
OUTLET: FinancialContent

Los Angeles web design agency Blackthorn Publishing is offering free ADA website compliance audits that show small businesses which accessibility barriers to fix, in plain English, at no cost and with no obligation.
-- Blackthorn Publishing, a Los Angeles web design and digital marketing agency, today announced free website audits focused on ADA website compliance for small businesses across Los Angeles and the San Fernando Valley. The audit gives business owners a clear picture of where their website stands on accessibility, at no cost and with no obligation.
Many small business websites have accessibility problems their owners never see: images without descriptions, buttons that do not say what they do, forms a screen reader cannot follow, and text that is hard to read. These barriers can keep customers with disabilities from booking, buying or getting in touch, and websites that cannot be used with assistive technology have become a growing source of legal demand letters for businesses in California.
"Most business owners want their website to work for everyone, they just don't know where the problems are," said Matthew Tropp, founder of Blackthorn Publishing. "The free audit shows them exactly what to fix, in plain English, so they can make an informed decision about ADA website accessibility instead of guessing."
What the free audit for any platform includes
Each audit reviews a business's homepage and one key page, such as a contact or booking page, against the WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines, the standard most often referenced for web accessibility. Business owners receive:
- A plain-English list of the accessibility issues found, ranked by priority
- An explanation of why each issue matters to real visitors
- An optional 15-minute call to walk through the results
- The free audit is available for websites on any platform, including Wix, Shopify, WordPress, Squarespace, GoDaddy and custom-built sites.
If a business wants help fixing what the audit finds, Blackthorn Publishing provides a quote for accessibility remediation. There is no obligation to hire the agency, and owners are free to use the report with their own web team.
Real fixes, not quick patches
Blackthorn Publishing's website accessibility services focus on fixing issues in the website itself, including image descriptions, heading structure, form labels, color contrast and keyboard navigation. The agency advises business owners to be cautious of one-line accessibility widgets or overlays sold as an instant fix, since they do not correct the underlying code.
"An audit is a starting point, not a guarantee," Tropp added. "We tell people honestly what we find and what it would take to fix it. For legal questions, we always recommend they speak with an attorney who specializes in accessibility."
Business owners can request a free audit on the Blackthorn Publishing accessibility remediation page.
