TITLE: GTA 6 has a hidden first-person mode waiting to be unlocked - here's how
OUTLET: GAMINGbible

GTA VI is a game meant to be played in third-person, but players have already found a way of switching their perspective. The best part? It works just as well as Red Dead Redemption 2.
During pre-release interviews and previews, Rockstar Games explained that the world of GTA VI was designed for a third-person camera.
This is why there’s no official first-person mode at launch like there was with Red Dead Redemption 2, but this could always change in a future update.
Adding some credibility to this tin-foil hat rumour is a new exploit that unlocks a hidden first-person mode, and it’s almost flawless.
How to get a fixed first-person camera in GTA 6
This was discovered by MuteSpotted74 on Reddit, and after trying to replicate it on my own game, I can confirm it works.
Advert
To do this, you need to navigate the game’s controller settings and set the camera choice button to either the PS5 touchpad, or the back button if you’re on Xbox.
Once you’ve done this, get into any car and pull out your phone. Unlike GTA V, when you use your phone in the game, your perspective will switch to first-person mode for optimal doomscrolling.
Next, put your phone away, but as you do so, spam your respective camera shift button. You might need to give it a few goes as the right timing is vital, but when you’ve finally done it your camera should be stuck in first-person.
You can then get out of the car and explore Leonida at your leisure – now from an all-new perspective, or stay behind the wheel for some immersive driving as I did.
Obviously this isn’t an intentional feature. Rockstar Games is probably going to fix this exploit in a future update so if you want to give it a go before then, now’s the time.
Why would Rockstar remove this though? Sure, it’s not harming anyone and it certainly doesn’t break the game, but again it’s not the intended way to play.
It’s also not perfect anyway. I had fun running around Vice City, robbing people and escaping the fuzz through Lucia’s actual eyes for a change, but it’s not as coherent as the official first-person modes Rockstar has endorsed.

When will first-person mode come to GTA 6?
GTA V got its first-person mode when it was re-released on ‘next-gen’ consoles, which at the time was the Xbox One and PlayStation 4 (we feel so old now). Red Dead Redemption 2, however, had first-person mode at launch.
For GTA VI, Rockstar could be waiting for Xbox Project Helix and the PlayStation 6. Given the studio’s close relationship with both Xbox and Sony there’s bound to be an enhanced port on the way. Perhaps one that can actually run at 60fps.
The other option is first-person mode will be released as a free add-on when the unannounced PC port drops, or GTA 6 Online.
