“I think I prefer RDR2’s rivers”: Former Rockstar artist looks at GTA 6’s world
A former Rockstar environment artist has praised the world of GTA 6, while admitting there is one part of Red Dead Redemption 2 he still prefers: its rivers.
David O’Reilly, who worked on water as an artist on Red Dead Redemption 2, shared his impressions while exploring GTA 6 on his Game World Art channel. He was clear that his comments were his own perspective, not an account of Rockstar’s internal development process.
“I think I prefer RDR2’s rivers,” he said, adding that he planned to explain his reasons in a future video. His broader view of GTA 6 was much more positive: he described its presentation as almost flawless and said its world looked beautiful.
The comparison comes from a water artist’s perspective
O’Reilly said he had specialized in water while working as an environment artist on Red Dead Redemption 2. In the video, he wondered whether GTA 6’s river water moved a little too slowly, while acknowledging how much work goes into making convincing water in a large game world.
He also pointed out something he liked: water continued to react to the player and objects in it, even when the player stopped moving. That is his observation while playing, not a result from a separate MIXED test.
Later, O’Reilly suggested that rivers were a greater emphasis in Red Dead Redemption 2, while GTA 6 puts more attention into its urban environments. That is an artist’s interpretation of two very different settings, not an official explanation from Rockstar about its design priorities.
He is careful about taking individual credit
O’Reilly said he left Rockstar in 2023 and cautioned viewers not to treat the video as an insider guide to how the studio makes its games. He also noted that work on a large world changes over time and involves contributions from many people.
His walk through Leonida is spoiler-light in its presentation and gives players an interesting reason to slow down and look at the landscape. It does not amount to a technical comparison or a verdict that one entire game looks better than the other.
