OPHC files for mixed shelf offering of $100M
Less than 1 min read
Key points:
- Florida based OptimumBank Holdings filed for a mixed shelf registration with the SEC on Wednesday for up to $100M.
- The bank intends to offer and sell securities like common stock, preferred stock, debt securities, depositary shares, warrants, purchase contracts, purchase units, subscription rights, and units.
- OPHC intends to use the net proceeds from the sales of the securities for purposes including general corporate requirements.
