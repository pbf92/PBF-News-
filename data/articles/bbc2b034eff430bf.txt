TITLE: GTA 6 Is Finally Here — and It’s Already Threatening Relationships, Productivity, and Your Weekend Plans
OUTLET: WKMG

(CBS NEWS) – The wait is over. After 13 years of fan theories, leaked trailers, and countdown clocks, Grand Theft Auto VI is officially out — and it’s already reshaping how people spend their time, money, and emotional energy.
“I’ve been waiting for this day for 13 years of my life,” one gamer said at launch. “This is one of the biggest days of my life.”
Workers Are Skipping Out — and Not Just Calling In Sick
The release isn’t just a cultural moment. It’s a workforce event. According to human resources data, nearly 1 in 5 salaried workers — 18% — said they planned to skip work for the GTA VI launch. Half of those planned to call out sick.
Even the U.S. military got in on the action. Sergeant Jamire Smith is one of 43 Army battalion members at Fort Stewart, Georgia, who recently re-enlisted for six years — in exchange for a $66,000 signing bonus and four days off to play the game.
“I think it was a cherry on top … those four days,” Smith said. “And I’m definitely going to be using my work hours to be playing GTA.”
$1.65 Billion in Pre-Sales — Before Anyone Played a Single Level
The numbers behind GTA VI are staggering. The game topped $1.65 billion in pre-sales ahead of launch, making it one of the biggest entertainment releases in history.
To put that in perspective, gaming business expert and economist Joost van Dreunen offered a comparison: “Just to give you a perspective, $2 billion is what the entire ERAS Tour for Taylor Swift made over the course of two years.”
The standard game costs around $80, but developer Rockstar Games and parent company Take-Two Interactive have built multiple revenue streams into the experience — premium editions, optional in-game purchases, and a subscription service called GTA Plus.
A Love Story at the Center of It All
This installment puts emotional connection at its core. At the heart of GTA VI’s story are Jason and Lucia — two partners in crime whose relationship goes beyond robbing banks. Players can build their romance through in-game dates, shared experiences, and even text messages.
“You can socialize over time,” van Dreunen said. “I think that’s where Take-Two has really put the emphasis in — we want to give you the richest experience as possible.”
Relationships Everywhere Are Being Put on Notice
For the people who don’t play — partners, roommates, and families of GTA fans — the message from social media has been clear.
“I’ve already accepted I’m not going to see my fiancé for the next month,” one TikTok user said.
Another offered a blunter reminder: “Remember who you were before your man entered a relationship with a video game.”
One gamer even prepared a GTA 6 care basket for his girlfriend — apparently a preemptive peace offering.
