Silver Bow Mining Proposed EPA Settlement Requires $799,799 Plus Interest
Silver Bow Mining and four affiliates proposed a $799,799 settlement with the EPA, plus interest, to resolve alleged liability for past Superfund response costs.
Key Highlights:
- The proposed agreement covers past response costs at three operable units of the Silver Bow Creek/Butte Area Superfund Site.
- The Settling Parties would pay EPA $799,799 plus interest accrued on the unpaid principal.
- Payment would be due within 30 days after the agreement’s effective date, following the public comment process.
Original SEC Filing: SILVER BOW MINING CORP. [ SBMT ] - 8-K - Nov. 27, 2026
