TITLE: Michael Burry flips mostly long as tech stocks slide
OUTLET: TradingView

Michael Burry flips mostly long as tech stocks slide
Michael Burry (Trades, Portfolio), the investor known for "The Big Short," said that he has closed his put options on the Nasdaq 100 (NDX) and now holds more long positions than short ones.
Put options are contracts that gain value when a stock falls. Burry said elevated volatility has made them expensive, leaving outright short selling as the main way to express a bearish view.
For now, Burry said, his portfolio is nearly all long, except for put options on Palantir Technologies PLTR and MetLife (MET).
About 25% of his portfolio is in cash, which he wants to put to work.
Burry also holds Nvidia NVDA call options bought last Friday, which have already doubled in value. He plans to short the chipmaker again closer to $200 a share during a period of market optimism, but said that moment has not arrived.
He has also scaled back bearish bets on Nvidia and the VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) as tech stocks fell over the past six weeks.
