TITLE: Manchester United have the worst defense in Europe: How does Mauricio Pochettino improve it?
OUTLET: Yahoo Sports

After their 4-0 hammering at Liverpool on Sunday Manchester United have conceded more goals, 34, than any other team in Europe's top five leagues in all competitions so far this season.
Let that stat sink in for a moment.

One of the richest teams in the world, who have incredible resources and are full of international players, have the worst defensive record in all of Europe.
Once again, to hammer this home: out of 96 teams across Europe's top five leagues, United have conceded the most goals.

How on earth does Mauricio Pochettino improve this record and make United better defensively?
What Pochettino has to do to improve Man United's defense
First up, Pochettino has now seen the awful mistakes and defending himself up close at Anfield and it's clear the defenders he has aren't good enough.
This drubbing may not be the worst thing to happen to United if Pochettino is truly going to turn this around.
In January new additions should arrive straight away to improve things. United's hierarchy can't just tell Poch to use the players he has. They aren't good enough. It's clear.
But between now and then, how does Pochettino make United more solid?
He has very little time on the training ground to work on things with the festive period about to arrive and games coming thick and fast in the league and the UEFA Champions League.
But cutting out poor individual mistakes has to be the first port of call. It sounds easy enough to do but with all of Pochettino's defenders low on confidence and looking slow and not able to defend as a unit, it will be an almighty task to stop United giving away soft goals.
Accountability and nastiness has to arrive. United are feeling sorry for themselves and are giving away goals through lapses in concentration and a lack of care. Pochettino will ask them all to do their job properly and focus solely on that as individuals. That is the first step.
Back three is the way to go
With the defenders United currently have, a back three is clearly the way to go to shore things up as quickly as possible.
United have to keep it tight and Pochettino will probably go with experience in Maguire, De Ligt and Martinez at center back, even if De Ligt is clearly nowhere near full sharpness after a year out injured. Going to a back three suits those experienced center back.
That formation suits their full backs better too as Luke Shaw, Patrick Dorgu, Noussair Mazraoui and Diogo Dalot are all better as wing backs, while Amad Diallo can also play in that role.
This is probably not the kind of situation for young defenders to be thrown into, even if Leny Yoro and Ayden Heaven are both good prospects and the former has been unlucky with certain decisions going against him this season.
Pochettino must go back to basics and get as many of his best pure defenders into this team as possible. He has to somehow create a more solid foundation to keep United in games.
Sorting midfield and the press out is just as important
We've all seen that central midfielders Andrey Santos and Youri Tielemans have struggled since arriving, with energy and pressing a particular problem.
Kobbie Mainoo can do it, while Carlos Baleba is very good at doing it and will be straight in when he returns from injury. But Pochettino has to improve the pressing out in midfield to stop his defenders having to defend so much.
It may make a lot of sense to have someone like Mason Mount in midfield alongside Tielemans and Mainoo in the coming games for his energy and to help United close down space and win the ball back more often.
If that means taking an extra attacker out of the team then so be it and Matheus Cunha and even Bruno Fernandes will be looking on nervously. Pochettino has to sort the balance out and United have to stop conceding goals.
Play a 3-4-2-1. Get experienced defenders on the pitch for now. Play with wing backs because you don't have full backs who are good enough to defend. And become tougher to beat and don't give up silly goals. This has to be the starting point for Pochettino to turn around this woeful defense.
