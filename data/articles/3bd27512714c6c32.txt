Complete GTA 6 Chapter 5 Mission List in Order
Chapter 5 features seven main story missions. The complete list is:
Chapter 5
- Bird in the Hand
- Taking Liberties
- VC Baby
- Doggie Bag
- Operation Savage Glory
- Protective Custody
- The End of the Road
What Is the Point of No Return in GTA 6 Chapter 5?
The End of the Road is the final mission and the main point of no return in GTA 6 Chapter 5. It brings the central storyline to its conclusion, with important decisions influencing the ending. You should absolutely make a manual save before the start of The End of the Road.
Protective Custody is also an important mission because it introduces a significant ending-related decision, so we’d reccomend using another of your manual saves here.
What Should You Do Before Finishing Chapter 5?
Before completing GTA 6’s final chapter, consider these tips:
- Complete any remaining side activities you want to experience.
- Make a manual save before Protective Custody.
- Create another save before The End of the Road.
- Explore Leonida before watching the final credits.
