TITLE: Keck Hospital of USC receives “A” grade for patient safety
OUTLET: EurekAlert!

LOS ANGELES — Keck Hospital of USC earned an "A" Hospital Safety Grade from The Leapfrog Group for fall 2026, marking its 13th top safety grade since 2019. The grade is based on hospital safety, quality and error prevention data.
"Every patient who comes through our doors expects exceptional care, and we strive to exceed those expectations,” said Marty Sargeant, MBA, chief operating officer of Keck Medicine of USC and CEO of Keck Medical Center of USC, which includes Keck Hospital. "This recognition reflects the skill, expertise and teamwork of our physicians, nurses and staff dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of patient safety.”
Keck Hospital is a 343-bed acute care hospital known for its highly specialized clinical care. In addition to earning a fall 2026 “A” grade from The Leapfrog Group, the hospital was recently recognized by Vizient Inc. as a 2026 top performer, placing it among the top hospitals in the country for excellence in delivering high-quality care.
“As part of Keck Medicine, Keck Hospital truly encompasses the distinct strengths of an academic health system — from pioneering research and state-of-the-art facilities, to highly specialized clinical expertise — and patient safety remains at the center of every decision we make,” said Steven D. Shapiro, MD, executive vice president of health affairs at USC and president and CEO of Keck Medicine of USC.
The Leapfrog Group assigns "A" through "F" grades to general acute care hospitals nationwide based on measures of patient safety and a hospital's overall performance in preventing medical errors and harm. The grading system is peer-reviewed, fully transparent and free to the public. Grades are updated twice annually, in the fall and spring.
To see Keck Hospital’s full Leapfrog grade details and access hospital safety tips for patients, please visit HospitalSafetyGrade.org.
