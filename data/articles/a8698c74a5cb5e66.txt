TITLE: US Stocks Soar On Jensen Return, Dow Jones And S&P 500 Surge
OUTLET: BusinessToday Malaysia

Stocks soared on Monday as investors cheered Nvidia’s board reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO. It marked a major rebound on Monday as buyers stepped back in for the holiday-shortened week after a bruising sell-off.
The S&P 500 rose 4.5% following seven straight days of declines. The Dow Jones Industrial Average jumped more than 1,200 points to recover by 2.5% following its worst week since the COVID-related sell-off in 2020. The Nasdaq Composite rallied 7%.
Nvidia stock soared more than 30% Monday morning after Nvidia announced it has reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and board director, effective immediately.
“The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers,” Nvidia’s board chair Harvey Jones said.
The move came after President Trump renewed his call to reinstate the chipmaker’s founder, Jensen Huang, as CEO amid a series of controversies.
